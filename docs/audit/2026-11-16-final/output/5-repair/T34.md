# T34: Changes table keeps kind, direction, cause and effect

Finding: S2-007 (Medium). No merged ids.

Base: `audit/repair-3` tip `99ceeed`. Worktree branch: this worktree's own branch (see the commit).

## Cause

`ChangesTable` built its head from the measure and the two period labels only, so the Table side of the
Changes presentation drew none of a change's kind, direction words, cause or effect, all of which the
Cards side draws (CM-053, CM-198, CM-025).

## Files changed

- `web/src/pages/library/parts/ChangesTable.tsx`: three columns added after the two values, in the
  card's order: `Kind` (the delivered kind), `Direction` (the delivered direction words, missing where
  the change has none) and `Cause and effect` (the cause then the effect, joined by one space as the
  card draws them). The new columns read from the left; the cause and effect column wraps inside a
  minimum width (`WORDS_CLASS`, through `TableCard`'s existing `tableClass`, the same mechanism
  `EvidenceRecord` and `chartTableStyle` use). The kit's table (`ChartTable`) is untouched. A blank
  delivered word becomes missing (`word`), because the kit's table throws on an empty-string cell and
  the reader permits an empty `kind`, `cause` or `effect`.
- `web/src/pages/library/parts/ChangesTable.columns.test.tsx` (new): the test below.
- `web/src/pages/library/Changes.test.tsx`: one existing assertion changed (below).

No change to `ChangeCard`, `slice.ts`, `read.ts`, `components/`, fixtures, handoffs or Signal.

## Test

`web/src/pages/library/parts/ChangesTable.columns.test.tsx`, five cases through the Changes route with the
Table side chosen, expected values written out from the delivered fixture words (not computed):

1. the heads are `Change, 30 Jun 2026, 30 Sep 2026, Kind, Direction, Cause and effect, Sources`, and the
   Net leverage, Rating outlook and Refinancing rows carry their kind, direction and cause-and-effect text;
2. Interest cover (no direction words) draws `n.a.` for direction, not zero, with its cause and effect;
3. the 2026-03-31 run: a withheld current value and a zero stay distinct beside the same words;
4. the wrapper carries the left-align classes for columns 4 to 6 and the wrap class for column 6, and each
   of the six rows keeps its one trace control;
5. a blank kind, cause and effect draw as not available and the page stays.

Failing then passing: before the repair `npx vitest run src/pages/library/parts/ChangesTable.columns.test.tsx`
exited 1 with 4 of 4 failing (the then-existing four cases; heads were three columns and `Sources`,
`expected '' to be 'Value'`, no left-align class). After the repair: 5 passed (exit 0). Case 5 was added
after the guard and was checked separately: with `word` replaced by the identity it fails with
`row "Net leverage" has an empty-string cell; a missing figure is null`, and passes with the guard.
