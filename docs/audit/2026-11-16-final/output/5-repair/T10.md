# T10: abandoned forms must not use up the 50 unfinished drafts (C2-008)

Model: Sonnet, medium effort. BASE: 6aea8e5230e6e32ce8070b0e9cd3b351570f6e02.

## Cause
`submissions.new()` refuses at `MAX_OPEN = 50` unsealed folders; only the 24-hour `STALE_SECONDS` sweep removes
abandoned ones. The page makes a draft per form (`POST /api/submissions`) and the service has no route to release
one (`service.py` is outside this task's files), so the page cannot release its draft.

## Repair (service only, `submissions.py`)
At the ceiling, `new()` first removes the oldest drafts idle for `IDLE_SECONDS` (2 hours), as many as needed, then
refuses if still full. Never removed: a sealed (submitted) folder (not counted open at all), a draft with an
upload in flight, a draft with an `issued` approval (it reached the review), any draft touched in the last 2 hours.
`reading()` now touches the draft, as uploads and removals already do, so a form that is reading its documents is
live. Submit's idempotence is unchanged (sealed/issued records are not touched). The limit stays at 50 and still
refuses when 50 drafts are live. Why this and not a page change: no delete route exists and the page cannot be
changed without one; counting liveness by idle time needs no new surface and only acts when the limit is hit.
Residual: a form idle for over 2 hours at the limit, before reaching the review, can lose its draft (then
"unknown submission"); a drafts that reached the review are kept until the 24-hour sweep.
