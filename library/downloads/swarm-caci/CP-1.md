## Findings

### Bottom line

CACI's reported leverage is 3.92x total debt to LTM EBITDA at 30 September 2026, down from 4.18x at fiscal 2026 year-end but above the 3.02x of fiscal 2025, so creditors carry a debt-funded acquisition whose earnings are only partly in the numbers.

EBITDA covers cash interest 5.60x on the same basis against 6.67x at fiscal 2025 year-end, but only 4.21x against the quarter's interest expense annualised, and cash plus the undrawn revolver gives USD 1,674.39 million of liquidity against USD 46.75 million of fiscal 2027 principal scheduled at 30 June 2026. The leverage ratios use company-defined EBITDA with no pro forma credit for the acquired business, so they probably overstate run-rate leverage, and none of the ratios is a covenant measure.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_caci-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 3, 8–9, 21; EX-99.1_2026-10-21_fy27-q1caci20261021ex991.htm.jsonl, page 10; 10-K_2026-06-30_caci-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 66–67, 89; calculation C7; calculation C10; calculation C13; calculation C9; calculation C12; calculation C15; calculation C25; calculation C31

### Key findings

Leverage has eased from the acquisition peak but remains well above its pre-acquisition level. Total debt to EBITDA was 3.02x at fiscal 2025 year-end, 4.18x at fiscal 2026 year-end after the debt-funded ARKA purchase and 3.92x on the last twelve months to 30 September 2026, while net debt to EBITDA was 2.91x, 4.01x and 3.69x. Net debt fell by USD 153.08 million in the September 2026 quarter as the revolver was repaid and cash rose.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_caci-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 3, 8–9; 10-K_2026-06-30_caci-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 66, 86–87; calculation C7; calculation C8; calculation C10; calculation C11; calculation C13; calculation C14; calculation C26

Interest cover has thinned since the acquisition, and the trailing cash figure flatters the current run-rate. EBITDA covered cash interest 6.67x in fiscal 2025, 5.49x in fiscal 2026 and 5.60x on the last twelve months to 30 September 2026, because cash interest paid rose from USD 145.04 million in fiscal 2025 to USD 213.64 million in fiscal 2026 on the larger debt stack. Cash interest paid was only USD 42.62 million in the September 2026 quarter because the notes' semi-annual coupons fall outside it, while interest expense and other, net was USD 73.24 million against USD 46.17 million a year earlier; four times that expense is USD 292.96 million, which would put cover at 4.21x.
Basis: 10-K_2026-06-30_caci-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 66–67; 10-Q_2026-09-30_caci-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 4; 10-K_2026-06-30_caci-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 89; calculation C9; calculation C12; calculation C15; calculation C27; calculation C31

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against reading leverage as still elevated is that the trailing figures carry the acquired business's debt for the whole period but only part of its earnings, since the acquisition closed on 9 March 2026 and no pro forma figures are supplied. A full-year earnings figure for the acquired business would lower both leverage ratios, and the company's guidance of at least USD 900 million of free cash flow for fiscal 2027, set against USD 725.96 million over the last twelve months, would cut net debt faster if delivered. The view would worsen if the receivables-sale facility lapsed without replacement, because up to USD 350.0 million of financing would leave the liquidity position, or if quarterly EBITDA stopped growing while cash interest stayed near its current level.
Basis: 10-K_2026-06-30_caci-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 79–81; EX-99.1_2026-10-21_fy27-q1caci20261021ex991.htm.jsonl, pages 4–5, 11; 10-Q_2026-09-30_caci-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 8; calculation C24

### Limitations

- Trailing leverage and interest cover set the full acquisition debt against part-period earnings of the acquired business, so they overstate run-rate leverage by an amount that cannot be sized from the supplied documents.
Basis: 10-K_2026-06-30_caci-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 81; calculation C13
- The maturity ladder is drawn at 30 June 2026, so the fiscal 2027 and later amounts do not reflect the quarter's repayments, and no ladder at 30 September 2026 can be stated.
Basis: 10-K_2026-06-30_caci-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 89; 10-Q_2026-09-30_caci-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 9
- The second-quarter fiscal 2026 EBITDA is derived from nine-month and quarterly figures because no release for that quarter is supplied, so the quarterly series carries one calculated point.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-04-22_fy26-q3caci20260422ex991.htm.jsonl, page 11; calculation C20
- No balance sheet at 30 September 2025 is supplied, so balance-sheet ratios cannot be compared with the same date a year earlier.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_caci-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 3
- Covenant definitions are not supplied, so the leverage and cover ratios here are not the tests in the credit agreement and say nothing about headroom.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_caci-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 9
