## Findings

### Bottom line

CACI's net leverage fell to 3.69x at 30 September 2026 from 4.63x at 31 March 2026, and liquidity covers near-term maturities, but both trailing ratios mix a full debt load with part-year earnings and interest. EBITDA holds only part of a year of ARKA, so leverage is overstated, while cash interest holds only part of a year of its debt cost, so interest cover is overstated.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_caci-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 2–4; 10-Q_2026-09-30_caci-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 8–9; EX-99.1_2026-10-21_fy27-q1caci20261021ex991.htm.jsonl, page 10; calculation C11; calculation C14

Liquidity is cash of USD 279.39 million plus an undrawn revolver of USD 1,395.00 million, against scheduled maturities of USD 46.75 million in the year to 30 June 2027 and no large maturity before fiscal 2031. Net debt of USD 4,556.67 million does not count USD 160.72 million of collections owed to the receivables purchaser, which the balance sheet carries as a current liability.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_caci-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 3, 8, 20–21; 10-K_2026-06-30_caci-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 89; calculation C5

### Key findings

Net debt rose from USD 2,811.76 million at 30 June 2025 to USD 5,022.58 million at 31 March 2026 because the ARKA acquisition was funded with debt, then fell to USD 4,556.67 million at 30 September 2026 as EBITDA grew and cash built. Net leverage on trailing EBITDA moved from 2.91x to 4.63x, then to 4.01x at 30 June 2026 and 3.69x at 30 September 2026. Part of the improvement is mechanical: the acquisition debt was in place by mid-March 2026 while ARKA earnings enter the trailing total only from the closing on 9 March 2026, so each further quarter adds ARKA earnings to the denominator and lowers the ratio without any debt repayment.
Basis: 10-K_2026-06-30_caci-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 65–66, 79; 10-Q_2026-03-31_caci-20260331.htm.jsonl, pages 2–3; 10-Q_2026-09-30_caci-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 2–3; calculation C1; calculation C3; calculation C5

EBITDA covered cash interest 5.60x over the last twelve months to 30 September 2026, against 6.67x at 30 June 2025, but cash interest paid understates the run-rate cost of the post-acquisition debt. The income statement charge for interest and other was USD 73.24 million in the September 2026 quarter against USD 46.17 million a year earlier; as an analyst sensitivity, annualised it is USD 292.96 million and gives cover of 4.21x on trailing EBITDA. The 2033 Notes pay semi-annually, so quarterly cash interest is uneven and the trailing figure holds only some of the new coupons.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_caci-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-K_2026-06-30_caci-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 89; calculation C14; calculation C26; calculation C27

Operating cash flow was USD 886.71 million in fiscal 2026 against USD 547.01 million in fiscal 2025, and issuer-defined free cash flow was USD 735.43 million, 62.65% of EBITDA. In the September 2026 quarter operating cash flow was USD 161.31 million against USD 171.07 million a year earlier because working capital absorbed cash, and issuer-defined free cash flow was USD 133.49 million against USD 142.96 million. Sales of receivables added USD 44.62 million to fiscal 2026 operating cash flow, and the programme is now at its ceiling, so it cannot add to cash flow from here.
Basis: 10-K_2026-06-30_caci-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 67, 85; 10-Q_2026-09-30_caci-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 3–4, 8; EX-99.1_2026-08-05_fy26-q4caci20260805ex991.htm.jsonl, page 13; EX-99.1_2026-10-21_fy27-q1caci20261021ex991.htm.jsonl, page 11; calculation C37

Liquidity at 30 September 2026 was USD 1,674.39 million, made up of USD 279.39 million of cash and USD 1,395.00 million of undrawn revolver, with USD 605.00 million of the revolver drawn. Scheduled maturities are USD 46.75 million in the year to 30 June 2027 and USD 62.38 million in the following year; the revolver and Term Loan fall due in November 2030, inside the year to 30 June 2031 in which USD 1,706.75 million matures. The receivables purchase agreement is not counted as liquidity because it sells receivables rather than extending credit, and its stated term ends on 18 December 2026.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_caci-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 3, 8–9, 20–21; 10-K_2026-06-30_caci-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 85, 88–89; calculation C25

Two balances that are not debt change how much cash is free. Collections held for the receivables purchaser rose from USD 110.13 million at 30 June 2026 to USD 160.72 million at 30 September 2026 and sit in other accrued liabilities. Current contract liabilities, the advances and deferred revenue that fund operations, rose from USD 190.40 million at 30 June 2025 to USD 294.06 million at 30 June 2026 and USD 337.94 million at 30 September 2026, and they unwind as work is performed.
Basis: 10-K_2026-06-30_caci-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 84–85; 10-Q_2026-09-30_caci-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 7–8

Obligations outside the debt figure at 30 June 2026 are USD 483.02 million of operating lease liabilities, all of the company's leases being operating leases, USD 119.23 million of supplemental retirement savings plan obligations against USD 104.88 million of related assets, USD 6.97 million of unfunded post-retirement benefits, USD 8.32 million of contingent acquisition consideration and the receivables sold under the purchase agreement. The company reports no material off-balance-sheet financing, and the USD 42 million judgment in the Al Shimari action carries no recognised liability and was vacated on 21 October 2026, although the plaintiffs may seek Supreme Court review.
Basis: 10-K_2026-06-30_caci-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 66, 85–86, 90, 92, 97, 99; 10-Q_2026-09-30_caci-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 8, 13, 22

Every ratio uses the issuer's unadjusted EBITDA, which adds depreciation held within direct costs, USD 8.20 million more than the income statement charge in fiscal 2026. It does not add back the USD 22.5 million of acquisition-related costs expensed in fiscal 2026, so it is conservative on that item. The issuer states no adjusted EBITDA, net debt or leverage, and debt is measured at carrying value, USD 28.38 million below principal at 30 September 2026.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-08-05_fy26-q4caci20260805ex991.htm.jsonl, page 12; 10-K_2026-06-30_caci-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 66, 81; 10-Q_2026-09-30_caci-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 9; calculation C24

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

Two points cut against the not-like-for-like reading. If ARKA earned little before its closing, a full-year figure would barely lower leverage, and if cash interest in the December 2026 quarter, when the semi-annual coupons fall due, comes in near a quarter of the trailing total, the overstatement of cover is small. Deleveraging is also real: net debt fell USD 153.08 million in the September 2026 quarter and the issuer guides to free cash flow of at least USD 900 million in fiscal 2027. The reading would be confirmed if working capital keeps absorbing cash, as in the September 2026 quarter, or if the receivables programme lapses at the end of its stated term on 18 December 2026 without renewal.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_caci-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 2–4, 21; EX-99.1_2026-10-21_fy27-q1caci20261021ex991.htm.jsonl, pages 4–5; 10-K_2026-06-30_caci-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 85; calculation C28

### Limitations

The filings state no adjusted EBITDA, net debt or leverage, so every ratio is calculated on the issuer's unadjusted EBITDA and carrying-value debt and is not a covenant figure, because the covenant definitions are not established.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-08-05_fy26-q4caci20260805ex991.htm.jsonl, page 12; 10-Q_2025-12-31_caci-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 12

Trailing figures at interim dates add the latest full year to the current stub and subtract the prior-year stub, and the second quarter of fiscal 2026 is derived because no release for that quarter is in the set.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-04-22_fy26-q3caci20260422ex991.htm.jsonl, page 11; calculation C6; calculation C9

ARKA's earnings before its closing are not printed, so no pro forma leverage is stated, and the change of fiscal year end to 31 December means the next reported period is a six-month transition period ending 31 December 2026 that will not compare with a full quarter.
Basis: 10-K_2026-06-30_caci-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 81; 10-Q_2026-09-30_caci-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 13, 32
