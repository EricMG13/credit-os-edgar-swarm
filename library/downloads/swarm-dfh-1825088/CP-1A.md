## Findings

### Bottom line

Dream Finders is a founder-controlled, asset-light homebuilder whose USD 2.2 billion Beazer acquisition adds debt, 12.0% preferred stock and land bank funding while margins fall, and governance gives creditors little independent restraint. The founder holds about 84% of the votes and had pledged 61.50% of his Class B stock for margin loans at the end of 2025, against 30.32% a year earlier, and a fall below 50% of votes or economic interest would let Series B holders demand cash.
Basis: 10-K_2024-12-31_dfh.htm.jsonl, page 47; 10-K_2025-12-31_dfh.htm.jsonl, page 48; 10-K_2025-12-31_dfh.htm.jsonl, page 49; 10-Q_2026-09-30_dfh.htm.jsonl, page 26; 10-Q_2026-09-30_dfh.htm.jsonl, page 29; 10-Q_2026-09-30_dfh.htm.jsonl, page 66; calculation C2; calculation C5; calculation C11

Offsetting this, the land model limits owned land, no common dividend has ever been paid and the board added homebuilding leadership in 2026, so governance risk is medium rather than high; pro forma debt and liquidity are not established.

### Key findings

Control is concentrated in one person and part of it is financed by pledged shares, so a market-driven forced sale is the main route by which control could change. The founder holds all Class B stock and about 84% of the votes. The share of his Class B stock pledged for margin loans rose from 30.32% at the end of 2024 to 61.50% at the end of 2025, and a further 5.20% was under prepaid forward sale contracts. The lender, loan size and trigger prices are not disclosed. On a simple count of common shares he could part with about 21.58% of his Class B stock before his holding fell to 50% of common shares outstanding, which is well below the share pledged.
Basis: 10-K_2024-12-31_dfh.htm.jsonl, page 47; 10-K_2024-12-31_dfh.htm.jsonl, page 48; 10-K_2025-12-31_dfh.htm.jsonl, page 48; 10-K_2025-12-31_dfh.htm.jsonl, page 49; 10-K_2025-12-31_dfh.htm.jsonl, page 50; 10-Q_2026-09-30_dfh.htm.jsonl, page 2; calculation C2; calculation C5; calculation C6; calculation C7

The Series B preferred stock ties the founder's holding to a cash claim on the company. Holders can require redemption in cash at the greater of the as-converted value and the liquidation preference, plus accrued dividends, if the founder ceases to own at least 50% of both the voting power and the economic interest, or on a change of control. The face value is USD 675.0 million and the 12.0% cumulative dividend is USD 81.0 million a year, against USD 13.5 million a year on the redeemed 9% Series A stock. The company may defer dividends, which protects cash but compounds the claim at 12.0%. The stock is classified as mezzanine equity and ranks ahead of common stock.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a cautious view is structural. The company has never paid a common dividend and the option model keeps owned land low. The insider trading policy was tightened between 2024 and 2025 so that pledges now need board approval without the requesting person's vote. The Series B stock lets the company defer dividends to protect cash. Management states the purchase price was an implied 0.8x of Beazer's book value and expects over USD 100 million of annual run-rate cost synergies, and net sales were a record for the first nine months of 2026.
Basis: 10-K_2024-12-31_dfh.htm.jsonl, page 47; 10-K_2025-12-31_dfh.htm.jsonl, page 49; 10-K_2025-12-31_dfh.htm.jsonl, page 53; 10-K_2025-12-31_dfh.htm.jsonl, page 7; 10-Q_2026-09-30_dfh.htm.jsonl, page 25; EX99.1_2026-06-30_dfh_ex-991pressreleasexmergera-htm.htm.jsonl, page 1; EX99.1_2026-06-30_dfh_ex-991pressreleasexmergera-htm.htm.jsonl, page 3; EX99.1_2026-09-30_dfh_dfh-q32026xer-htm.htm.jsonl, page 1

The view would move toward lower risk if the company disclosed pro forma debt and liquidity that match the 18 to 24 month deleveraging commitment, margin loan terms with ample headroom, a board majority confirmed as independent, or a lower pledge. It would move toward higher risk if pledged shares were sold or called, if the Credit Agreement were drawn heavily at closing, if the land pipeline review produced large abandonment charges, or if gross margin fell further below the 14.5% reported for the first nine months of 2026.
