## Findings

### Bottom line

PBF Holding ended Q3 2026 in net cash of USD 897.6 million with debt at 0.40x LTM EBITDA, so near-term leverage risk is low, but the ratio rests on a refining upswing and insurance gains, as FY2025 EBITDA was only USD 311.3 million. Net debt was USD 1,645.3 million, or 5.29x EBITDA, at the end of FY2025 after negative EBITDA in FY2024, so leverage can reverse quickly if margins fall.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_pbf-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 7; 10-Q_2026-09-30_pbf-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 59; 10-K_2025-12-31_pbfh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 123; calculation C2; calculation C5; calculation C8; calculation C24; calculation C27

After the October 2026 redemption of the last of the 2030 senior notes, net cash is about USD 827.2 million and no drawn debt matures before 2032. The reported debt figure is a carrying value that sits below principal, and the parent's own debt figure for the same date is higher and not reconciled.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_pbf-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 33; 10-Q_2026-09-30_pbf-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 17; EX-99.1_2026-10-29_a991pressreleaseq3-26.htm.jsonl, page 8; calculation C17

### Key findings

- Leverage fell from stress to negligible in three quarters because debt was repaid while earnings recovered: debt was USD 2,802.3 million at 2026-03-31 against LTM EBITDA of USD 1,089.4 million, or 2.57x, and USD 1,613.0 million against USD 4,072.7 million, or 0.40x, at 2026-09-30. LTM EBITDA covered cash interest 25.09x against 2.05x in FY2025.
  Basis: 10-Q_2026-03-31_pbf-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 7; 10-Q_2026-09-30_pbf-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 7; calculation C6; calculation C8; calculation C25; calculation C27

- The earnings behind that ratio are cyclical and partly non-recurring, which is the main reason to hold the conclusion loosely: FY2024 EBITDA was negative USD 245.4 million, FY2025 EBITDA of USD 311.3 million included an insurance gain of USD 832.5 million and was negative USD 9.5 million before special items, and the first nine months of 2026 include an insurance gain of USD 356.5 million. The issuer's adjusted LTM EBITDA is USD 3,505.2 million.
  Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_pbfh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 123; 10-K_2025-12-31_pbfh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 154; 10-Q_2026-09-30_pbf-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 8; 10-Q_2026-09-30_pbf-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 59; calculation C11

- Operating cash flow was lifted by growth in accrued liabilities, not only by profit: in the nine months of 2026 operating cash flow was USD 3,070.6 million after accrued expenses added USD 981.1 million while receivables absorbed USD 904.3 million and inventories USD 339.0 million. Inventory-related accruals were USD 2,378.3 million at 2026-09-30 against USD 1,351.5 million at 2025-12-31, and the filings do not say what they are owed to, so a reversal would draw on the cash balance.
  Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_pbf-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 9-10; 10-Q_2026-09-30_pbf-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 16; 10-K_2025-12-31_pbfh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 172

- Liquidity is ample: cash was USD 2,510.6 million and revolver availability about USD 3,100.0 million at 2026-09-30, after the revolver was extended to 2031-09-30 and enlarged to USD 4,000.0 million. The issuer states availability only as an approximate figure, and letters of credit outstanding were about USD 157.2 million.
  Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_pbf-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 24; 10-Q_2026-09-30_pbf-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 66

- The maturity wall is distant: the debt table at 2026-09-30 lists USD 800.0 million due in 2030, USD 550.0 million of exchangeable notes due in 2032 and USD 500.0 million due in 2034, with the revolver undrawn. The 2030 notes were redeemed on 2026-10-26 for about USD 853.4 million, which leaves pro forma net cash of USD 827.2 million, or 0.20x EBITDA, before accrued interest on the redemption.
  Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_pbf-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 17; 10-Q_2026-09-30_pbf-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 33; calculation C17

- Reported debt understates the contractual claim: principal at 2026-09-30 was USD 1,850.0 million against a carrying value of USD 1,613.0 million, mostly a USD 174.0 million discount created when the exchange feature of the 2032 notes was valued separately as a liability of USD 197.6 million, which sits outside the debt line. Leverage on principal is 0.45x.
  Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_pbf-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 17; 10-Q_2026-09-30_pbf-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 29; calculation C15; calculation C16

### Limitations

- The 2026 columns are unaudited and no auditor review is stated, so ratios for 2026 rest on management's figures; the FY2024 and FY2025 columns are audited.
  Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_pbfh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 152; 10-Q_2026-09-30_pbf-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 7

- Reported EBITDA is the issuer's own unadjusted EBITDA and includes special items; the lenders' covenant definitions are not supplied, so no covenant headroom can be stated from these ratios.
  Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_pbf-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 58-59

- Revolver availability is stated only as an approximate figure, so operational liquidity of over USD 5.6 billion is a rounded issuer statement and not a figure rebuilt from the borrowing base.
  Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_pbf-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 66
