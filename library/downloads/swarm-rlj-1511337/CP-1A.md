## Findings

### Bottom line

RLJ is a publicly held lodging REIT with no sponsor named, so creditors depend on diversified hotel cash flows run by third-party managers under a few brands, and protection comes from asset quality and mostly unsecured funding rather than from an owner's support.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_rlj.htm.jsonl, pages 5–10; 10-K_2025-12-31_rlj.htm.jsonl, page 55; 10-K_2025-12-31_rlj.htm.jsonl, page 114

The filings document no owner extraction, creditor-adverse transaction or covenant relief: the common dividend was USD 0.60 per share in FY2025, three hotel sales raised USD 73.70 million and the 2026 refinancing extended maturities. Ownership percentages, board independence and the legal room to pay distributions are not established because the proxy statement and executed debt documents are not supplied.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_rlj.htm.jsonl, page 62; 10-K_2025-12-31_rlj.htm.jsonl, page 115; 10-K_2025-12-31_rlj.htm.jsonl, page 124; 10-K_2025-12-31_rlj.htm.jsonl, page 78

### Key findings

Ownership is dispersed and no sponsor is named, so creditors have neither a sponsor to support them nor a sponsor whose extraction they must price, but percentage holdings by shareholder are not established. The Trust is the sole general partner of the operating partnership and owns 99.5% of its units, a small group of other limited partners holds the rest, and the annual report caps any holder at 9.8% of the shares.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_rlj.htm.jsonl, page 9; 10-K_2025-12-31_rlj.htm.jsonl, page 55; 10-K_2025-12-31_rlj.htm.jsonl, page 56; 10-Q_2026-09-30_rlj.htm.jsonl, page 20

Cash flows are spread across many hotels but are concentrated in three brand families, two managers and a few markets: 83 of the 93 hotels at the end of 2025 carry Marriott, Hilton or Hyatt brands, Aimbridge and Hilton run 51.09% of the consolidated hotels, and the three largest markets produced 35.85% of FY2025 revenue, of which the two California markets produced 24.84%. A brand or manager dispute, or a California event, therefore reaches many hotels at once.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_rlj.htm.jsonl, pages 7–8; 10-K_2025-12-31_rlj.htm.jsonl, page 22; 10-K_2025-12-31_rlj.htm.jsonl, page 105; 10-K_2025-12-31_rlj.htm.jsonl, page 114; calculation C3; calculation C2; calculation C5

The owner has limited control over operations and fees come ahead of owner cash flow: independent managers control hotel operations, rooms generate 80.99% of revenue, and management and franchise fees took 7.61% of FY2025 revenue, while the Trust guarantees the obligations under each franchise agreement. Fee levels and brand standards therefore sit largely outside the owner's day-to-day control.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_rlj.htm.jsonl, page 50; 10-K_2025-12-31_rlj.htm.jsonl, page 52; 10-K_2025-12-31_rlj.htm.jsonl, page 87; 10-K_2025-12-31_rlj.htm.jsonl, page 114; 10-K_2025-12-31_rlj.htm.jsonl, page 115; calculation C1; calculation C4

Funding is mostly unsecured and guaranteed within the group, which leaves most hotels unencumbered: the annual report describes unsecured senior notes, unsecured credit agreements and mortgage loans secured by certain hotels, mortgage loans encumber eight hotels, and the senior notes carry the guarantee of the Trust and certain operating partnership subsidiaries. This module relies on that statement that the senior notes are unsecured; Financial foundation lists their security as not stated, and the annual report controls.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_rlj.htm.jsonl, page 9; 10-K_2025-12-31_rlj.htm.jsonl, page 106; 10-Q_2026-09-30_rlj.htm.jsonl, page 13

Capital allocation has been measured and has recycled assets rather than added leverage: the common dividend was USD 0.60 per share in FY2025 and USD 0.45 per share for the first nine months of 2026, three hotels were sold for USD 73.70 million in 2025 and eight for USD 123.20 million in 2026, and USD 28.60 million of shares were repurchased in 2025 and none in the first nine months of 2026, under programmes that each authorise up to USD 250.00 million. The credit agreements restrict dividends only while an event of default exists and the indenture limits restricted payments on terms not supplied, so the payout looks like policy rather than a covenant constraint, but that is not tested.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_rlj.htm.jsonl, page 56; 10-K_2025-12-31_rlj.htm.jsonl, page 62; 10-K_2025-12-31_rlj.htm.jsonl, page 115; 10-Q_2026-09-30_rlj.htm.jsonl, page 20; 10-Q_2026-09-30_rlj.htm.jsonl, page 28; calculation C6; calculation C7

Disclosure is adequate for monitoring but governance detail sits outside the supplied filings: the statements carry an auditor's report and quarterly reports follow, the board is a small group of trustees led by an executive chairman, trustees can be removed only for cause by a two-thirds vote, and covenant compliance is reported each period as met. Trustee independence, pay, committee roles and related-party dealings are in the proxy statement, which is not supplied.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_rlj.htm.jsonl, page 34; 10-K_2025-12-31_rlj.htm.jsonl, page 78; 10-K_2025-12-31_rlj.htm.jsonl, page 82; 10-Q_2026-09-30_rlj.htm.jsonl, pages 11–13

Contractual claims on hotel cash flow beyond debt are modest but real: 12 consolidated hotels sit on ground leases, one of which expires in 2029 and would revert to the lessor if not extended, and restricted cash is reserved for capital spending under management, franchise and mortgage terms. These claims are fixed before owner cash flow and matter more when hotel earnings fall.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_rlj.htm.jsonl, page 21; 10-K_2025-12-31_rlj.htm.jsonl, page 113; 10-Q_2026-09-30_rlj.htm.jsonl, page 18

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest case against a benign reading is that no sponsor or controlling holder stands behind creditors while distributions continue and buybacks are authorised: net income attributable to common shareholders fell from USD 42.91 million in FY2024 to USD 3.39 million in FY2025 and preferred dividends alone were USD 25.12 million, so the payout exceeds earnings and rests on operating cash flow, a new authorisation of USD 250.00 million is in place until May 2027, and the Trust has no cyber insurance while relying on managers' systems. The view would turn adverse if a repurchase or special distribution were funded with borrowing, if a mortgage cash trap or a covenant waiver appeared, if a manager or brand relationship ended across many hotels, or if the proxy statement showed concentrated holdings or related-party fees; it would turn favourable if the executed debt documents confirmed wide covenant headroom and tight limits on restricted payments.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_rlj.htm.jsonl, page 12; 10-K_2025-12-31_rlj.htm.jsonl, page 30; 10-K_2025-12-31_rlj.htm.jsonl, page 88; 10-K_2025-12-31_rlj.htm.jsonl, page 115; 10-Q_2026-09-30_rlj.htm.jsonl, page 13; 10-Q_2026-09-30_rlj.htm.jsonl, page 44; calculation C8

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

The February 2026 refinancing recast the Revolver to February 2030, upsized one term loan to USD 569.00 million, added a USD 150.00 million seven-year term loan and extended two mortgage loans to April 2029, at a cost of USD 14.00 million of lender fees and legal costs. During 2026 another previously unencumbered hotel was added to a mortgage loan for USD 23.40 million of extra proceeds, so the secured claim grew while unsecured maturities were pushed out.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_rlj.htm.jsonl, page 124; 10-Q_2026-09-30_rlj.htm.jsonl, page 10; 10-Q_2026-09-30_rlj.htm.jsonl, pages 12–13

No bonds were issued in 2026: the 3.75% notes due 2026 were repaid at maturity on 2026-07-01 from the refinancing proceeds, and the next scheduled maturity after extension options is in 2029.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_rlj.htm.jsonl, page 10; 10-Q_2026-09-30_rlj.htm.jsonl, page 11

After the quarter end the sale of seven hotels near Midway Airport closed on 2026-10-08 for about USD 110.00 million, of which about USD 90.00 million was cash and about USD 20.00 million was seller financing with a three-year term and one extension option; the buyer assumed the franchisors' key money obligations. The use of the cash is not stated.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_rlj.htm.jsonl, pages 9–10; 10-Q_2026-09-30_rlj.htm.jsonl, page 25
