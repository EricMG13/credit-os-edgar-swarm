## Findings

### Bottom line

Entegris is a semiconductor consumables supplier whose creditor risk is demand cyclicality and customer concentration, not governance: no sponsor or controlling holder is identified and the company is repaying debt rather than distributing cash.
Basis: calculation C-30; 10-K_2025-12-31_entg-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 18; 10-K_2025-12-31_entg-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 95

The top ten customers were 50% of FY2025 net sales, and nine-month 2026 growth of 13.8% is led by Equipment and Engineering customers at 42.0%, the most cyclical category. Governance risk is Low but provisional, because the proxy statement, the credit agreement and the indentures were not supplied; finance leadership changed during 2026 and three insider trading plans were omitted from earlier disclosure.
Basis: calculation C-11; calculation C-16; 10-K_2025-12-31_entg-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 18; 10-Q_2026-09-26_entg-20260926.htm.jsonl, page 10; 10-K_2025-12-31_entg-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 89–90; Source readiness (CP-0)

### Key findings

The business is a consumables supplier tied to semiconductor volumes, which makes earnings cyclical but less so than an equipment maker. Management describes revenue as predominantly unit-driven or recurring, and Advanced Purity Solutions, the larger segment at 58.4% of segment sales, earns 67.6% of segment profit at a 31.4% margin against 21.1% for Materials Solutions. Growth of 13.8% over nine months and 24.6% in Q3 came mainly from volume, which added USD 341.2 million over nine months, but customers give no long-term purchase commitments, so the recovery can reverse as quickly as it came.
Basis: calculation C-03; calculation C-07; calculation C-10; calculation C-09; calculation C-11; calculation C-12; 10-Q_2026-09-26_entg-20260926.htm.jsonl, pages 14–15; 10-Q_2026-09-26_entg-20260926.htm.jsonl, page 23; 10-K_2025-12-31_entg-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 9; 10-K_2025-12-31_entg-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 18

Customer and regional concentration is the main business risk. The top ten customers were 50% of FY2025 net sales, up from 43% in 2023, with TSMC at 16%; Taiwan, the largest region, was 26% of nine-month 2026 sales, while China, at 18%, was flat and is exposed to trade restrictions. Equipment and Engineering customers grew 42.0% against 10.2% for fabs, so the growth is skewed to capital-spending-linked demand, which falls first in a downturn.
Basis: calculation C-16; calculation C-18; 10-K_2025-12-31_entg-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 18; 10-Q_2026-09-26_entg-20260926.htm.jsonl, page 10; 10-Q_2026-09-26_entg-20260926.htm.jsonl, pages 23–24

Governance risk is Low but provisional. No sponsor, parent or controlling holder is named in the documents supplied, and the documented behaviour is creditor-aligned: USD 450.0 million of term loan repaid in nine months 2026 from USD 606.0 million of operating cash flow, no share repurchase programme, and a quarterly dividend of USD 0.10 per share that absorbed 8.8% of FY2025 operating cash flow. Ownership and board detail sit in a proxy statement that was not supplied, and legal capacity for distributions, investments or priming cannot be assessed without the credit agreement and indentures, so the level cannot be firmer than Low.
Basis: calculation C-30; calculation C-27; 10-Q_2026-09-26_entg-20260926.htm.jsonl, page 7; 10-K_2025-12-31_entg-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 65–66; 10-K_2025-12-31_entg-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 95; Source readiness (CP-0)

Finance leadership and disclosure controls are the monitoring points. The Chief Financial Officer position moved from a separation agreement on 2026-01-19, through an interim officer from 2026-03-01, to a new officer who signed the second-quarter report on 2026-08-04, while the revolver was being refinanced; three insider trading plans entered in the first quarter of 2025 were omitted from earlier disclosure through an administrative oversight, which the company says it has corrected. Neither is a financial misstatement and reporting has been regular, but they bear on the quality of lender reporting if stress returns.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-03-28_entg-20260328.htm.jsonl, page 35; 10-Q_2026-06-27_entg-20260627.htm.jsonl, page 38; 10-Q_2026-06-27_entg-20260627.htm.jsonl, page 39; 10-K_2025-12-31_entg-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 89–90

Financial policy is deleveraging, and no new debt was issued in 2025 or in 2026 to date. The revolver was upsized by USD 175.0 million to USD 750.0 million and extended to 2031-04-29, removing a 2027 maturity, while the 2023 and 2024 divestitures, including USD 675.3 million for Electronic Chemicals and USD 263.2 million gross for PIM, left a simpler group. Nine-month capital expenditure of USD 124.0 million is 49.6% of the USD 250.0 million 2026 plan, so part of the cash available for debt repayment is timing.
Basis: calculation C-33; calculation C-29; 10-Q_2026-09-26_entg-20260926.htm.jsonl, page 12; 10-K_2025-12-31_entg-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 5; 10-K_2025-12-31_entg-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 82

Supply, trade and the accounting estimate are the other operating risks. Certain filtration and chemical inputs come from a single source or limited suppliers, tariffs and export controls remain a stated risk with China sales flat, and the January 2026 useful-life change cut nine-month depreciation by USD 55.6 million, which lifts operating income and net income but not EBITDA, so margin gains at the operating income level overstate the cash improvement.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_entg-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 22; 10-Q_2026-09-26_entg-20260926.htm.jsonl, pages 19–20; 10-Q_2026-09-26_entg-20260926.htm.jsonl, page 9

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a benign view is that the demand recovery is recent and concentrated. Nine-month net sales grew 13.8% and Q3 grew 24.6%, but customers can cancel orders, the top ten customers account for half of sales and equipment-linked sales grew 42.0%, so a reversal of fab capital spending would hit revenue and the margin gains together. A second challenge is that the Low governance level rests on absence of evidence: without the proxy statement or the debt documents, a large holder, wide restricted-payment baskets or a priming right cannot be excluded. The view would change if the proxy statement named a holder with board control, if the credit agreement showed broad capacity to pay dividends or move assets outside the creditor group, if net sales fell back while capital expenditure caught up with the plan, or if further finance leadership or disclosure lapses followed.
Basis: calculation C-11; calculation C-12; calculation C-16; calculation C-29; 10-K_2025-12-31_entg-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 18; 10-Q_2026-09-26_entg-20260926.htm.jsonl, page 10

- The conclusion that distributions are not a creditor risk rests on observed behaviour only; without the credit agreement and indentures the restricted-payment, investment, priming and asset-transfer capacity is not established, and wide capacity would weaken it.

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

No new debt was issued in 2025 or the first nine months of 2026, and no acquisition or sponsor transaction is reported in that period. The company amended its senior secured revolving credit facility on 2026-04-29, raising commitments from USD 575.0 million to USD 750.0 million and extending the maturity to 2031-04-29 from 2027-07-06, with margins, commitment fees and certain negative covenants revised; it paid USD 2.0 million of debt issuance costs in 2026. It repaid its whole term loan, USD 450.0 million, in three steps of USD 50.0 million, USD 200.0 million and USD 200.0 million across the first three quarters of 2026, including a USD 25 million voluntary payment on 2026-07-31. The remaining debt is four series of notes issued under indentures dated between 2020 and 2022, with supplemental indentures dated 2022-07-06; the exhibit index also lists an equal priority intercreditor agreement of that date between the collateral agents of the senior credit facilities and of the notes, which links the credit facilities and the 2029 secured notes but was not supplied. Earlier transactions were portfolio pruning: the sale of QED for USD 134.3 million in 2023, termination of an alliance agreement for USD 191.2 million of net proceeds in 2023, the sale of the Electronic Chemicals business for USD 675.3 million in 2023 and the sale of the PIM business for USD 263.2 million gross in 2024.
