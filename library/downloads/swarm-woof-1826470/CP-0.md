## Findings

### Bottom line

The six supplied filings and releases are enough to build Petco's financial foundation, business and governance picture and fundamental credit view through the quarter ended August 1, 2026, but not to test covenant headroom or guarantor structure. The issuer is the listed operating company itself, the borrower under the term loan and the asset-based revolver and the issuer of the secured notes, so no parent-versus-subsidiary split is needed, and its sponsors control it from above.
Basis: 10-K_2026-01-31_woof-20260131.htm.jsonl, page 1; 10-K_2026-01-31_woof-20260131.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-K_2026-01-31_woof-20260131.htm.jsonl, page 150; 10-Q_2026-08-01_woof-20260801.htm.jsonl, pages 10–11

The capital structure changed on February 2, 2026, the day after fiscal 2025 closed. The January 31, 2026 balance sheet still carries the old USD 1,500.0 million term loan, while the August 1, 2026 filing shows USD 897.8 million of term loan and USD 600.0 million of 8.25% secured notes, so the two balance sheets are not like for like. The USD 75.0 million prepayment in September 2026 is a subsequent event, no pro forma balance sheet is supplied, and nothing dated after September 4, 2026 is supplied, so events up to the November 18, 2026 cutoff are not established.
