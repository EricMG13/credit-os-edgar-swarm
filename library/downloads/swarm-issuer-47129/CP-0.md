## Findings

### Bottom line
The five Hertz filings are enough to run the financial, business and fundamental credit work on The Hertz Corporation, but only inside stated limits, so creditors should not rely on any leverage, covenant or ratings conclusion that ignores them.

Three limits matter most. The annual report states Adjusted Corporate EBITDA on the earlier definition while the 2026 quarterly reports recast only 2025 interim periods on a revised one, so no consistent twelve-month figure is established. The parent company's debt and results differ from the issuer's, so only the issuer's own statements should carry debt and earnings. The Oro Mobility joint venture and a segment revision due in the fourth quarter of 2026 fall after the latest balance sheet, and the newest filing is 13 days older than the as-of date. No executed agreements, ratings or dated debt quotes were supplied.
