## Findings

### Bottom line

ESAB has no sponsor, and insiders took USD 125.0 million of the Eddyfi equity on the same terms as outsiders, so governance risk is moderate; the exposure is financial policy, since equity funded only 21.73% of the price. Creditors should watch whether further acquisitions or dividend increases come before the balance sheet is repaired.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-10-02_esab-20261002.htm.jsonl, page 23; calculation A1; calculation A2

The business is consumables-led, with consumables at 60.96% of nine-month net sales and 60.40% of sales in EMEA & APAC, about 5% in Russia and an acquired inspection business that raises the equipment share. Board composition, ownership percentages and pay are not in the supplied filings, and the chief financial officer and chief accounting officer both changed in 2026, so governance conclusions rest on disclosed actions rather than structure.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a moderate governance rating is the pace and direction of financial policy: ESAB bought four businesses in 2025 and a USD 1.5 billion business in 2026, raised its dividend as the deal closed, and granted management options that pay only on a much higher share price, all while its finance leadership changed and one acquired unit's controls were still being brought into scope at the end of 2025. Insider participation in the preferred stock can also be read the other way, since the Rales-affiliated buyers took a 6.50% cumulative claim that ranks ahead of common equity and the placement terms were negotiated with the unaffiliated investors rather than set by the company alone. The view would move to high if a further large acquisition were funded mainly with debt before leverage falls, if the dividend rose again, or if the missing proxy statement showed a small board majority tied to the Rales Holders. It would move to low if the proxy statement showed an independent board and no concentrated holder, the credit agreement showed distribution limits that bind, and the reason for the finance leadership change proved routine.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_esab-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 42; 10-K_2025-12-31_esab-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 122; 10-Q_2026-10-02_esab-20261002.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-Q_2026-10-02_esab-20261002.htm.jsonl, page 22; 10-Q_2026-10-02_esab-20261002.htm.jsonl, page 23

### Limitations

- Ownership percentages and the Rales Holders' stake are not stated, so the conclusion that no controlling holder is extracting value cannot be tested and stays a statement about documented actions only.
- Directors' independence, committees and pay sit in a proxy statement that was not supplied, so the moderate governance rating is provisional and could move either way once board structure is known.
- No credit agreement or indenture was supplied, so what distributions, acquisitions and debt incurrence the documents permit is not established; the financial-policy conclusion rests on what ESAB has done, not on what it may do.
- The reasons for the 2026 finance and Fabrication Technology leadership changes are not stated, so the finding on integration execution risk could be too cautious if the changes were planned.
- Eddyfi's stand-alone results and margins are not supplied beyond its sales contribution, so the statement that the acquisition raises the equipment share and adds inspection technology is not matched by any view of its profitability.

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

The defining transaction is the acquisition of Eddyfi Holding Inc., a Québec-incorporated inspection and monitoring technology business. ESAB signed the share purchase agreement on January 31, 2026 at about USD 1.45 billion, completed it on June 1, 2026, and reports consideration of about USD 1.5 billion net of cash received, with goodwill of USD 811.2 million and intangibles of USD 752.0 million on a preliminary allocation that can still change within a year. Eddyfi's 2025 revenue was about USD 240 million.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_esab-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 52; 10-Q_2026-10-02_esab-20261002.htm.jsonl, page 9; 10-Q_2026-10-02_esab-20261002.htm.jsonl, page 10; 10-Q_2026-10-02_esab-20261002.htm.jsonl, page 35
