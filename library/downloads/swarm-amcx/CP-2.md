## Findings

### Bottom line

AMC Global Media has a weakening fundamental credit profile: no maturity falls due before 2029 and cash is USD 464.0 million, but earnings are falling, secured debt now dominates the stack and no committed facility remains, so creditors need earnings to stabilise.

Net debt of USD 1,197.0 million is 3.82x last twelve months adjusted operating income and 8.73x reported EBITDA, adjusted operating income fell 46.21% in the first half of 2026, and cash interest absorbs 48.28% of adjusted operating income. Free cash flow of USD 190.5 million over the last twelve months and cash that exceeds the USD 420.5 million due in 2029 are the main supports; the unsecured 2029 notes rank behind USD 1,315.1 million of secured notes.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); Business & governance (CP-1A); calculation C2; calculation C3; calculation C7; calculation C19; calculation C15

### Key findings

The strongest support is time and liquidity, not earnings: there is no maturity until the 2029 notes, all debt is fixed rate, and cash of USD 464.0 million already exceeds the USD 420.5 million of 2029 principal by USD 43.5 million. Free cash flow of USD 190.5 million over twelve months, struck after cash interest, is 60.86% of adjusted operating income because capital needs are small and content payments run below amortization, which gives the issuer room to meet the 2029 notes from internal cash if earnings stabilise.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); calculation C14; calculation C15; calculation C12; 10-Q_2026-06-30_amcx-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 16; 10-Q_2026-06-30_amcx-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 65

The weakest dimension is earnings durability, and leverage looks acceptable only on the issuer's own adjusted measure. Net debt is 3.82x adjusted operating income but 8.73x reported EBITDA, and 4.63x if only the USD 97.78 million impairment and the restructuring charges are added back, an Analyst Judgement; first-half adjusted operating income of USD 230.1 million annualised would put net debt at 5.20x. Adjusted operating income fell 57.88% in the second quarter, revenue fell 5.68% in the half and operating income fell USD 48.6 million on a revenue decline of USD 52.5 million, which shows how little of lost revenue the cost base absorbs.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); Business & governance (CP-1A); calculation C2; calculation C3; calculation C6; calculation C21; calculation C8; calculation C9; calculation C33; calculation C34

The capital structure has been lengthened at a cost to the unsecured creditors. Secured debt rose from 52.36% of principal at the end of 2024 to 76.36% at the end of 2025, and after the 2026 exchange the 10.50% notes due 2032 alone are 75.77% of principal, the annual cash coupon is USD 156.0 million against cash interest paid of USD 151.15 million over twelve months, and the unsecured 2029 notes of USD 420.5 million sit behind that secured debt. The issuer's own fair value estimates show the market differentiates: the secured notes are valued at 102.25% of principal while the unsecured senior notes are valued at 88.00%, and the convertible notes, valued at 106.98% of principal, would need a share price rise of 44.77% from the May 2026 price to convert, so they should be treated as cash-settled debt of USD 143.75 million.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); Business & governance (CP-1A); calculation C35; calculation C36; calculation C13; calculation C17; calculation C23; calculation C24; calculation C25; calculation C26; 10-Q_2026-06-30_amcx-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 20–21; 10-Q_2026-06-30_amcx-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 58

Governance risk is Medium and mainly structural: the Dolan family controls about 79% of the votes, the company is a controlled company, and capital returns are modest but continuing, with a covenant basket of USD 50 million for buybacks agreed in 2026 and no common dividend. The consent solicitation also tightened trademark and investment protections, so the documented behaviour is mixed rather than extractive; the gap is that no executed indenture or proxy statement was supplied, so capacity cannot be separated from willingness.
Basis: Business & governance (CP-1A); 10-K_2025-12-31_amcx-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 72–74; 10-Q_2026-06-30_amcx-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 17

Liquidity is adequate only while earnings hold, because cash is the single buffer. The revolver of USD 175 million was cancelled in May 2026, part of the cash is held abroad, program rights obligations of USD 381.7 million are interest-free funding that fell over the half, and amortization of program rights exceeded payments by USD 72.4 million, a gap that can reverse. The Netflix licence adds about USD 100 million a year of cash from 2027 to 2030 on fees of USD 500 million, equal to 64.12% of the annual coupon, which helps but does not offset the earnings decline.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); calculation C28; calculation C29; calculation C18; 10-Q_2026-06-30_amcx-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 17; 10-Q_2026-06-30_amcx-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 55; 10-Q_2026-06-30_amcx-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 68

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The case for a stronger view is that the debt has been reduced by USD 633.8 million of principal since the end of 2024, the secured 2029 maturity has been moved to 2032, interest is fixed, free cash flow remains positive after cost-cutting and the issuer's own leverage of 4.1x, on principal, leases and adjusted operating income, is not far above the net debt multiple shown here of 3.82x. If adjusted operating income stopped falling in the second half, cash interest would remain covered 2.07x and the 2029 notes could be met from cash. The view would worsen if the first-half pace persisted, since annualised adjusted operating income of USD 230.1 million covers the annual coupon only 1.48x, if cash fell below the 2029 principal, or if buybacks or a dividend were funded from cash while earnings fall.
Basis: calculation C27; calculation C4; calculation C22; calculation C20; Financial foundation (CP-1)

### Limitations

- Ratings, market quotes and peer data are not in the sources, so the relative standing of the profile against other cable programmers and the market's view of the notes beyond the issuer's own fair values are not established.
- Management's forward guidance is referred to but not quantified in the sources, so the outlook rests on the stated trend and the Netflix cash schedule, and a forward earnings figure is an assumption, not a source fact.
- The annualised first-half figure is an arithmetic illustration, not a forecast, because second-half seasonality and the timing of content deliveries are not modelled, so the downside conclusion indicates direction and scale only.
- The fair values of the notes are the issuer's own Level 2 estimates at one date, so they indicate relative pricing of the secured and unsecured notes but are not executable prices.

### Strengths and weaknesses

Strengths. First, maturities are distant and fixed: the earliest is the 2029 notes of USD 420.5 million, so refinancing risk is back-ended and covered by cash of USD 464.0 million on today's balance sheet, which lowers near-term default probability. Second, free cash flow is positive and small capital needs mean a falling fee base has so far reduced cash flow less than earnings, with free cash flow at 60.86% of adjusted operating income, which preserves debt service capacity. Third, streaming is now 29.28% of revenue and growing on price, and the Netflix licence adds contracted cash, which diversifies away from linear affiliate fees. Fourth, the audit opinion is unqualified and reporting is timely, so figures can be relied on for monitoring.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); Business & governance (CP-1A); calculation C14; calculation C15; calculation C12; calculation C32; 10-K_2025-12-31_amcx-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 131–132

Weaknesses. First, affiliate and advertising revenue are in structural decline, and with a largely fixed content cost base adjusted operating income fell 46.21% in the half, which compresses coverage and leverage headroom. Second, one customer is about a fifth of revenue and unnamed, which concentrates renewal and collection risk. Third, the secured share of debt is 75.77% after rising from 52.36% at the end of 2024, so unsecured creditors carry the weakest recovery position if earnings keep falling. Fourth, the revolver is gone, leaving cash alone as the buffer for content payments and interest. Fifth, family control with overlapping affiliates leaves creditors no governance lever and the indentures were not seen.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); Business & governance (CP-1A); calculation C7; calculation C13; 10-Q_2026-06-30_amcx-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 17; 10-Q_2026-06-30_amcx-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 33

### Company description and ownership

AMC Global Media owns and distributes entertainment content through five domestic linear networks, seven streaming services, an in-house studio, a film distribution arm and international channels in more than 100 countries and territories. Revenue is 62.86% subscription, 25.12% advertising and 12.02% content licensing, with streaming now the largest domestic line, so cash flow visibility rests on multi-year distributor contracts that are shrinking with the cable subscriber base and on a streaming book that grows by price. The company is controlled by the Dolan family, which holds all Class B shares and about 79% of the votes, and the subsidiaries that guarantee the notes are domestic, with foreign subsidiaries outside the guarantee; the structure supports strategic continuity but gives creditors no governance lever and leaves foreign cash only partly available to service debt.
Basis: Business & governance (CP-1A); 10-K_2025-12-31_amcx-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 6–7; 10-K_2025-12-31_amcx-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 72–73; 10-Q_2026-06-30_amcx-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 60

A refinancing event is not expected before 2029, but the group has a record of liability management: the 2025 secured issue funded a discounted purchase of unsecured notes and the 2026 exchange extended secured notes to 2032 with a premium, which reduces maturity risk and raises priority risk for the unsecured notes.
Basis: Business & governance (CP-1A); 10-K_2025-12-31_amcx-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 110; 10-Q_2026-06-30_amcx-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 17–19

### Revenue, cost and cash conversion

Revenue durability is structurally declining in linear and growing only on price in streaming. Affiliate revenue fell 13% in 2025 and 17% in the second quarter of 2026 on basic subscriber losses, advertising fell on ratings and pricing, and streaming subscribers were flat to lower, so volume is not offsetting the loss; renewals with four of the five largest distributors protect carriage but not rates. The credit consequence is a revenue base that is shrinking at a mid-single-digit rate, 5.68% in the first half of 2026, before any recovery in streaming scale or licensing.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-02-11_amcx-21126xex991.htm.jsonl, pages 3–4; EX-99.1_2026-07-30_amcx-73026ex991.htm.jsonl, pages 1–3; 10-Q_2025-09-30_amcx-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 52; 10-Q_2026-06-30_amcx-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 43–44

Cost flexibility is limited by content: technical and operating expenses were about half of revenue and program rights are funded at least nine months ahead of airing, so the fixed cost base turned a revenue decline of USD 52.5 million into an operating income decline of USD 48.6 million in the second quarter. Restructuring, including a voluntary buyout and an international plan, is under way but savings are not quantified. Cash conversion is better than earnings suggest because capital expenditure is small, and the free cash flow of USD 190.5 million over twelve months absorbs restructuring cash, cash taxes and cash interest.
Basis: calculation C33; calculation C34; calculation C12; 10-K_2025-12-31_amcx-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 106; 10-Q_2026-06-30_amcx-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 46–47

### Competitive forces, outlook and sector

Competitive rivalry and substitution are the main forces: subscription streaming services and virtual distributors give audiences an alternative to the cable bundle, which cuts affiliate fees and ratings; distributors also favour their own affiliated networks and have more bargaining power over carriage, and programme suppliers with studios compete for content. Entry is limited by content cost, but the large studios and platforms already have the scale. Regulation and technology matter less to credit than these forces; the main sector headwind is the continued decline of the cable subscriber base, and the main company tailwinds are price increases on streaming and the Netflix licence. Over twelve to twenty-four months the likely path is continued linear decline offset in part by streaming price and licensing cash; over three to five years the issuer must replace affiliate fees with owned franchise licensing and streaming scale, which the sources do not quantify.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_amcx-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 21–23; 10-Q_2026-06-30_amcx-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 43–44; EX-99.1_2026-07-30_amcx-73026ex991.htm.jsonl, pages 1–4

### Qualitative downside scenario

The initiating condition is a faster loss of affiliate and advertising revenue than the first half, with no offsetting growth in licensing. The first operational break is a further fall in adjusted operating income, since annualised first-half adjusted operating income of USD 230.1 million is already well below the last twelve months figure. Financially, net debt over that annualised measure is 5.20x, adjusted operating income covers the annual coupon 1.48x, and free cash flow, which is already struck after interest, would fall further from its present level. Cash of USD 464.0 million would remain enough to meet the 2029 notes of USD 420.5 million only if buybacks stop and content payments are managed. Management levers are lower buybacks, slower content spending and restructuring; the observable triggers are a third-quarter 2026 adjusted operating income below the second quarter, falling cash, or a restricted payment beyond the amended basket. The sources do not support an assumption of default, and resilience appears sufficient to avoid liquidity distress through 2028 on current cash.
Basis: calculation C20; calculation C21; calculation C22; calculation C15; Financial foundation (CP-1)

### Overall credit view

Overall, AMC Global Media presents a weak business profile supported by distant maturities, positive free cash flow and cash that exceeds the 2029 principal, but credit quality is constrained by a falling earnings base, a secured share that has risen sharply since 2024 and the loss of the revolver. Cash-flow durability appears adequate today but weakening, because free cash flow of USD 190.5 million depends on content payment timing and on stable cash interest of USD 151.15 million. Refinancing capacity is likely to depend on stabilisation of adjusted operating income before 2028, when the 2029 notes become current, and on the market's appetite for another secured extension. The primary downside path is affiliate and advertising revenue decline compressing adjusted operating income faster than cash and free cash flow can cover the unsecured 2029 notes. Further analysis would require the executed indentures, ratings, market quotes and management guidance.
