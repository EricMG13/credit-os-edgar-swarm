## Findings

### Bottom line

AMC Global Media is a Dolan-controlled programmer whose cash flows still rest on shrinking cable affiliate fees, so creditors carry business-model decline and family control, but documented financial policy has been mixed rather than extractive, which supports a Medium governance risk level.

Streaming is now the largest domestic revenue line at USD 677.0 million, 29.28% of revenues in 2025, yet affiliate revenue fell 13% in 2025 and 17% in the second quarter of 2026, and adjusted operating income fell 46.21% in the first half of 2026. The controlling family holds about 79% of the votes, no common dividend is paid, and the main creditor-relevant behaviour is a mix of deleveraging, maturity extension into secured notes and modest buybacks.
Basis: calculation C1; calculation C25; calculation C12; EX-99.1_2026-02-11_amcx-21126xex991.htm.jsonl, page 4; EX-99.1_2026-07-30_amcx-73026ex991.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-K_2025-12-31_amcx-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 72–73

### Key findings

The business is in managed decline at its core, and streaming growth is price-led rather than volume-led. Affiliate revenue, the contract-based fee stream, falls with basic subscriber losses, advertising follows ratings down, and streaming revenue growth of 12% in 2025 and 8.4% in the first half of 2026 comes from price increases while subscriber counts are flat to lower. Consolidated revenue fell 5.68% in the half and operating income fell USD 48.6 million in the second quarter on a revenue decline of USD 52.5 million, so the cost base does not flex with the fee base and lost revenue falls through to creditors' cover.
Basis: calculation C11; calculation C42; calculation C41; EX-99.1_2026-07-30_amcx-73026ex991.htm.jsonl, pages 2–3; 10-Q_2026-06-30_amcx-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 43–47; 10-K_2025-12-31_amcx-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 103

Customer and distributor concentration is high and partly unexplained. One domestic customer accounted for about 18% of revenues in 2025 and about 20% in the first half of 2026, the filings do not name it, and a domestic customer filed for bankruptcy protection on 2026-06-30 with the issuer expecting to collect in full. The renewals the issuer has completed with four of the five largest domestic distributors in the last twelve months reduce near-term carriage risk, but affiliate revenue is falling on subscriber losses and, to a lesser extent, lower contractual rates agreed at renewal.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_amcx-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 200; 10-Q_2026-06-30_amcx-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 33; 10-Q_2026-06-30_amcx-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 57–58; EX-99.1_2026-07-30_amcx-73026ex991.htm.jsonl, page 1; 10-Q_2025-09-30_amcx-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 52

Control sits with the Dolan family and it is complete, which supports stability but limits creditor leverage over governance. The family group holds all Class B shares and about 79% of the votes, Class B elects most directors, the company uses the controlled-company exemption from independence requirements, and several directors and executives also serve at the affiliated Sphere and Madison Square Garden companies. Related-party flows disclosed are small, at USD 4.8 million of revenue and USD 1.9 million of charges in 2025, and an independent committee reviews affiliate transactions under a written policy, so the documented conflict risk is structural rather than a realised leakage.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_amcx-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 72–76; 10-K_2025-12-31_amcx-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 195–196

Financial policy is mixed: it has cut debt and removed the 2029 maturity wall, but it has done so by raising the secured share of the stack and it keeps returning small amounts of cash to shareholders. Principal fell by USD 633.8 million from USD 2,369.4 million at the end of 2024 to USD 1,735.6 million at 2026-06-30, helped by buying back unsecured notes at a discount of USD 111.0 million on a USD 600 million tender; the 10.50% secured notes due 2032 are now USD 1,315.1 million, 75.77% of principal. Against that, buybacks were USD 18.0 million in 2025 and USD 30 million in 2026, the consent solicitation widened the buyback covenant to USD 50 million in aggregate, and USD 87.4 million of the board authorization remains.
Basis: calculation C18; calculation C17; 10-K_2025-12-31_amcx-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 110; 10-Q_2026-06-30_amcx-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 16–18; 10-Q_2026-06-30_amcx-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 58

Disclosure quality is adequate for monitoring but incomplete for legal analysis. The auditor's opinion on the 2025 statements is unqualified and internal control over financial reporting was effective, the issuer reconciles its non-GAAP measures, and it files each quarterly report on the day of its earnings release; however, no executed indenture, no covenant certificate and no numeric guidance were supplied, and the guarantor summary describes the guarantees as unsecured while the annual report describes the 2032 notes as secured.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_amcx-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 122; 10-K_2025-12-31_amcx-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 131–132; 10-K_2025-12-31_amcx-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 171; 10-Q_2026-06-30_amcx-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 60

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence for a lower risk level is that nothing in the sources shows value being moved away from creditors: there is no common dividend, no sponsor or shareholder fee is stated, no transfer of assets to unrestricted subsidiaries is described, and the 2026 consent solicitation tightened the trademark and investment protections at the same time as it widened the buyback basket. The licence agreement with Netflix adds fixed cash of about USD 100 million a year from 2027 to 2030 on fees of USD 500 million, and four of the five largest domestic distributors have renewed. The view would move to High if debt-funded buybacks or dividends exceeded the amended covenant basket, if assets were moved to unrestricted subsidiaries, or if affiliate and advertising declines accelerated without cost action; it would move toward Low if affiliate declines flattened, if the remaining unsecured notes were refinanced without raising secured debt, and if the full indentures confirm the creditor protections the filings summarise.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_amcx-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 17–18; 10-Q_2026-06-30_amcx-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 68; EX-99.1_2026-07-30_amcx-73026ex991.htm.jsonl, pages 1–4

### Limitations

- No proxy statement was supplied, so board composition, committee independence, compensation and the approval record for related-party transactions are not examined, and the Medium governance level rests on risk-factor and note disclosure alone.
- No executed indenture was supplied, so legal capacity for restricted payments, investments and asset transfers cannot be separated from willingness, and the behaviour flags describe actions taken rather than actions permitted.
- The filings do not name the customer that accounts for about a fifth of revenue or say whether it is the customer that filed for bankruptcy protection, so the concentration conclusion cannot be tied to a named counterparty.
- Ownership is stated for the family group in aggregate only, with no named holder percentages beyond the trust share of Class B, so control conclusions do not extend to the individual holders.

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

There is no acquisition, sponsor or change-of-control transaction in the sources; the transactions that matter to creditors are financing and capital-return actions.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_amcx-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 6; 10-Q_2026-06-30_amcx-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 17–19

In July 2025 the issuer sold USD 400.0 million of 10.50% senior secured notes due 2032 for net proceeds of USD 394.5 million and used them with cash to buy back USD 600 million of its 4.25% senior notes at a discount of USD 111.0 million, a secured-for-unsecured swap that cut gross debt and raised the secured share. In October 2025 it amended its credit agreement, lowering the minimum interest coverage ratio to 1.50x and extending part of the revolver to 2030.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_amcx-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 110–111; 10-K_2025-12-31_amcx-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 171

From February to April 2026 it exchanged 2029 secured notes for additional 2032 secured notes: USD 861.3 million of principal was tendered and USD 915.1 million of notes were issued, which carried a premium of USD 53.8 million of principal, and the remaining USD 13.7 million was redeemed at 105.125%. It also obtained consents to let it repurchase up to USD 50 million of equity while limiting trademark transfers and investments in unrestricted subsidiaries. On 2026-05-12 it repaid the USD 80.0 million term loan and cancelled the revolving credit facility, and on 2026-05-08 it agreed a USD 30 million accelerated share repurchase.
Basis: calculation C19; calculation C20; calculation C21; 10-Q_2026-06-30_amcx-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 17–19; 10-Q_2026-06-30_amcx-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 58

On 2026-07-30 it announced a licence agreement under which Netflix pays USD 500 million over five years for co-exclusive streaming rights to The Walking Dead Universe, about USD 25 million in 2026 and about USD 100 million a year from 2027 to 2030; this is a licence of rights, not a financing, and it is not yet in any balance sheet figure.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_amcx-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 68; EX-99.1_2026-07-30_amcx-73026ex991.htm.jsonl, page 4
