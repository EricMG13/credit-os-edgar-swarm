## Findings

### Bottom line

Leverage on reported earnings rose to 3.92x net debt to EBITDA at September 30, 2026 from 3.75x at the end of 2025, and interest cover fell to 2.93x from 3.43x, so creditors have a thinner earnings cushion even though liquidity improved.

Debt did not rise: net debt fell by USD 246.5 million over nine months as dealership sale proceeds came in, but last-twelve-month EBITDA fell by USD 104.4 million. Charging floor plan interest against earnings lifts net leverage to 4.38x. The company's own year-end ratio of 3.2x cannot be extended to September 2026 because none is stated for that date, and covenant levels are not printed.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_abg_abg-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 3, 5–6, 55–56; 10-K_2025-12-31_abg_abg-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 14, 96; calculation C2; calculation C4; calculation C7; calculation C9; calculation C10

### Leverage and coverage

The reading depends on which earnings and which debt are used. Net debt to EBITDA, with EBITDA built from income from operations plus depreciation and amortization, was 3.37x at the end of 2024, 3.75x at the end of 2025 and 3.92x for the twelve months to September 30, 2026; total debt to EBITDA was 3.45x, 3.79x and 4.02x. Interest cover on total cash interest paid, floor plan interest included, was 3.38x, 3.43x and 2.93x. The weakening comes from lower earnings: twelve-month EBITDA of USD 838.6 million compares with USD 943.0 million for FY2025, while net debt of USD 3,285.1 million is below the USD 3,531.6 million at year end.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_abg_abg-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 96, 98–99, 131; 10-Q_2026-09-30_abg_abg-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 2–3, 5–6; calculation C1; calculation C2; calculation C3; calculation C4; calculation C5; calculation C6; calculation C7; calculation C10

Adding back asset impairments, a non-cash charge of USD 141.0 million in FY2025, brings FY2025 net leverage to 3.26x and the twelve months to September 2026 to 3.43x; the first is close to the company's own transaction-adjusted ratio of 3.2x at the end of 2025, which the filing says is calculated as set out in its credit facility and which cannot be reproduced here. Treating floor plan interest as a cost of the business instead lifts net leverage to 3.74x for FY2024, 4.15x for FY2025 and 4.38x for the twelve months to September 2026. Floor plan interest of USD 91.2 million in FY2025 is charged below income from operations, so EBITDA before it flatters the cover of the debt that is counted. No covenant level is printed, so none of these figures can be set against a breach point.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_abg_abg-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 14, 96; 10-Q_2026-09-30_abg_abg-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 3, 56; calculation C8; calculation C9

### Cash flow quality

Operating cash flow held up better than earnings but is not a clean measure, because trade floor plan changes sit inside it and non-trade floor plan flows sit in financing. Reported operating cash flow was USD 617.7 million for nine months 2026 against USD 623.3 million a year earlier; the company's own adjusted measure that brings floor plan flows into operations was USD 525.2 million against USD 542.6 million. Capital expenditure including real estate was USD 248.0 million, up from USD 104.4 million, leaving USD 369.7 million of free cash flow on the reported measure, against USD 518.9 million in the prior nine months.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_abg_abg-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 5–6, 58–59; calculation C14

Dealership sale proceeds of USD 397.2 million and share repurchases of USD 277.6 million were the largest other items, so the fall in net debt rests on divestitures as well as on operations. Repurchases were USD 50.0 million in the prior nine months; the authorization has USD 322.4 million remaining, and the filings state that buybacks and dividends are subject to covenant compliance.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_abg_abg-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 5–6, 9, 56

### Liquidity and maturities

Stated liquidity of USD 1.03 billion at September 30, 2026 compares with USD 927.1 million at the end of 2025, but USD 160.6 million of the total is cash held in floor plan offset accounts and USD 38.9 million of balance-sheet cash sits in the insurance subsidiary outside the guarantor group, so the cash and facilities that are freely available to the debt holders are smaller than the headline. Revolver availability of USD 794.3 million is USD 109.9 million below the commitment less letters of credit and drawings, and the filing does not explain the gap. Principal falling due is concentrated in 2026 (USD 484.4 million at year-end 2025, including the 2021 real estate facility of USD 401.7 million that matures in December 2026 and that management expects to refinance) and in 2028 to 2030, with USD 610.2 million, USD 837.7 million and USD 482.9 million.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_abg_abg-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 74, 131; 10-Q_2026-09-30_abg_abg-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 18–19, 55–56; calculation C12; calculation C15

### Definitions and data quality

Four definitions move the leverage figures. Floor plan notes payable of USD 1,764.3 million at September 30, 2026 and loaner vehicle notes of USD 250.4 million sit outside total debt, which is the carrying value of current and long-term debt only; deferred revenue of USD 891.3 million is a non-debt funding float that has grown from USD 828.2 million at the end of 2025; the cash figure includes the insurance subsidiary's cash; and carrying value at year-end 2025 is USD 21.4 million below contractual principal because of issuance costs net of premiums. The statements also print small rounding differences, so FY2025 net cash change and FY2024 and FY2025 total liabilities are withheld rather than forced.
