## Findings

### Bottom line

Upbound is a public, scaled lease-to-own operator with no identified controlling sponsor, so creditors rely on debt-document protections and cash generation rather than owner support, and the dominant business risks are falling Acima volume and unresolved state attorney general matters.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_upbd-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 89; 10-Q_2026-09-30_upbd-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 34; calculation CALC-03

Acima supplies 52.05% of nine-month 2026 revenue and 51.95% of segment operating profit while its merchandise volume fell 9.3%; Rent-A-Center adds 39.87% of revenue from a shrinking store base, and Brigit, at 6.17% of revenue, is growing with rising advance losses.
Basis: calculation CALC-01; calculation CALC-02; calculation CALC-03; calculation CALC-04; calculation CALC-05

Financial policy is mixed rather than adverse: dividends paid in FY2025 were 119.97% of net earnings and the Brigit purchase was partly debt-funded, yet no liability management, priming or debt-funded recapitalisation is documented. Ownership, board and related-party detail sit in a proxy statement that is not supplied, so the sponsor and governance risk level is Medium on incomplete evidence.
Basis: calculation CALC-06; 10-K_2025-12-31_upbd-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 76; 10-K_2025-12-31_upbd-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 178

### Key findings

- Revenue and profit are concentrated in Acima, whose retailer-channel volume is now shrinking. Acima was 53.51% of FY2025 revenue and 50.80% of segment operating profit; its volume fell 9.3% in nine months and 11.0% in the third quarter, and revenue fell 2.37%. Margin held at 12.27% against 11.66% because fewer customers used early purchase options and lease charge-offs eased to 8.8% of revenue from 9.3%, so profit is being protected by mix rather than growth. Lower volume releases merchandise cash today but shrinks the base that services the debt.
  Basis: calculation CALC-01; calculation CALC-02; calculation CALC-03; 10-Q_2026-09-30_upbd-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 52; 10-Q_2026-09-30_upbd-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 56
- Rent-A-Center is a large but contracting store franchise. Store count fell 5.92% in nine months, 69 stores were closed in the second quarter, revenue slipped 0.72% and segment margin fell to 12.26% from 13.11% as lease charge-offs rose to 5.0% of revenue from 4.7%. The closures are intended to lower fixed cost, but the savings are not quantified, so creditors should treat the segment as stable cash generation with no growth.
  Basis: calculation CALC-04; calculation CALC-02; 10-Q_2026-06-30_upbd-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 26; 10-Q_2026-09-30_upbd-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 53
- Brigit adds growth and margin but a different, more regulated credit risk. Revenue rose 53.87% (part of that is nine months against eight months) at a 16.05% segment margin, while net advance losses reached 3.9% of advances against 2.8%, and the advances are funded from consolidated cash with no matched funding disclosed. Regulation of earned wage access is changing state by state, so the segment can add earnings but also event risk.
  Basis: calculation CALC-05; calculation CALC-02; 10-Q_2026-09-30_upbd-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 54; 10-K_2025-12-31_upbd-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 24
- Regulatory and legal exposure is the main source of claims outside debt and of earnings volatility. Estimated legal accruals were USD 57.9 million, down 19.58% since year-end; a non-binding agreement in principle exists with the multistate group of 43 states, and on 2026-08-27 the court let the New York Attorney General's case proceed except for one allegation, with settlement talks active. Any settlement above the accrual, or injunctive terms that change Acima's practices, would reach the largest profit source, and no range is estimated for unaccrued matters.
  Basis: calculation CALC-07; 10-Q_2026-09-30_upbd-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 32; 10-Q_2026-09-30_upbd-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 33; 10-Q_2026-09-30_upbd-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 34
- Management has been refreshed at the top and governance detail is not supplied. The Chief Executive Officer role passed on 2025-06-01 to the former Chief Financial Officer, a new Chief Financial Officer joined on 2025-11-10 and a Chief Growth Officer on 2025-09-18, while the auditor changed to Deloitte & Touche LLP for FY2025 with no disagreements reported and Brigit excluded from the internal control assessment. New finance leadership during settlements and integration raises execution risk, which creditors should monitor through reporting quality rather than assume.
  Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_upbd-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 91; 10-K_2025-12-31_upbd-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 117–118; 10-K_2025-12-31_upbd-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 176; 10-K_2025-12-31_upbd-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 177
- Shareholder distributions are modest in relation to cash flow (36.81% of FY2025 operating cash flow less capital expenditure) but exceeded earnings in FY2025, and acquisitions have been debt-assisted with no creditor-adverse transaction documented. Dividends paid were 119.97% of FY2025 net earnings and 75.81% in nine months of 2026, the annual run rate at the current share count is USD 90.95 million, and no repurchase has occurred since 2023 although USD 235.0 million of authorisation remains. The Brigit deal left USD 37.5 million of deferred consideration and an earnout of up to USD 60 million outside debt. Distribution and acquisition capacity cannot be tested because the debt documents are not supplied.
  Basis: calculation CALC-06; calculation CALC-09; 10-K_2025-12-31_upbd-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 89; 10-K_2025-12-31_upbd-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 76; 10-Q_2026-09-30_upbd-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 9
- Supply and control terms are secondary vulnerabilities. Three suppliers account for 58% of merchandise purchases with no minimum-purchase contracts, a cybersecurity incident drove fraudulent lease contracts of about USD 13.3 million in the second quarter of 2026 with the investigation ongoing, and a third party acquiring 40% of the votes is an event of default under the bank facilities. These raise operating and control-change risk but do not by themselves threaten debt service.
  Basis: calculation CALC-08; calculation CALC-11; 10-K_2025-12-31_upbd-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 18; 10-Q_2026-06-30_upbd-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 26; 10-K_2025-12-31_upbd-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 81

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a cautious reading is that the earnings engine is improving: Acima's segment operating profit rose 2.74% on lower volume, Rent-A-Center remains profitable, Brigit adds a high-margin segment, legal accruals fell 19.58% and the company carries no sponsor that could extract value. Distributions fell to 75.81% of net earnings in nine months of 2026 and no creditor-adverse transaction is documented.
Basis: calculation CALC-03; calculation CALC-05; calculation CALC-07; calculation CALC-06

That reading would win if Acima volume stabilised, the multistate and New York matters settled within the accrued amount without changes to Acima's practices, and the proxy statement and credit agreements showed independent directors and tight restricted-payment baskets. The cautious view would strengthen if a settlement exceeded the accrual, fraud losses recurred, Brigit advance losses kept rising or the dividend were maintained while earnings fell below it.
Basis: calculation CALC-03; calculation CALC-07; 10-Q_2026-09-30_upbd-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 34
