## Findings

### Bottom line

Mercer has no sponsor to inject equity, so creditor protection rests on its own capital allocation and on a restructuring in which senior noteholders holding about 87% of the notes would receive most of the reorganized equity.
Basis: calculation A1; calculation A9; 10-Q_2026-09-30_Q3-2026-Quarterly-Report.pdf, pages 7, 8, 12

The business is a commodity pulp and lumber producer: pulp was 69.85% of FY2025 revenue and fiber about 55% of pulp cash cost, which ties earnings to prices Mercer does not set. Governance evidence is mixed rather than adverse: dividends of USD 10,039 thousand were paid in 2025, a year in which Operating EBITDA was negative, and were then suspended on 2025-07-31, and no uptier, priming or fee leakage is documented. Board and shareholder detail was not supplied, so the governance conclusion is provisional.
Basis: calculation A2; calculation A8; 10-K_2025-12-31_2025-Annual-Report-on-Form-10-K.pdf, pages 19, 57, 63, 124; 10-K_2024-12-31_2024-Annual-Report-on-Form-10-K.pdf, page 94; 10-Q_2026-09-30_Q3-2026-Quarterly-Report.pdf, page 8

### Key findings

Control is dispersed and unquantified, so no sponsor support should be assumed. The annual report says a few significant shareholders own a substantial percentage of the shares and can influence elections and major transactions, but no holder or percentage is named in the supplied reports. The only disclosed shareholder-linked commercial tie is a surety bond facility of up to USD 30,000 thousand with an entity owned by a major shareholder, on standard commercial premiums and nominally used. Creditors therefore cannot rely on a parent or fund to cure a shortfall, and the only new money now offered comes from the noteholders themselves.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_2025-Annual-Report-on-Form-10-K.pdf, page 51; 10-Q_2026-09-30_Q3-2026-Quarterly-Report.pdf, pages 12, 17; Source readiness (CP-0)

Capital allocation was growth-led and then defensive, with mixed creditor impact. Since 2021 Mercer invested about USD 396.6 million to widen solid wood, including the Torgau facility in 2022 for about USD 263.2 million and the Conway and Okanagan facilities in 2023 for about USD 82.1 million, while the funding mix of those purchases is not stated in the supplied reports. It also paid dividends of USD 10,039 thousand in 2025 when operating cash flow of USD 8,587 thousand did not cover them, then suspended the dividend and cut nine-month capital spending to USD 38,674 thousand from USD 74,430 thousand. The pattern shows dividends continuing into a year in which Operating EBITDA ended negative, which is a modest early-warning signal for creditors, offset by the suspension in July 2025.
Basis: calculation A8; 10-K_2025-12-31_2025-Annual-Report-on-Form-10-K.pdf, pages 9, 12, 57, 95; 10-Q_2026-09-30_Q3-2026-Quarterly-Report.pdf, page 6

The operating model is cyclical and fiber-dependent, with energy sales a small cushion. Pulp products were 69.85% of FY2025 revenue and fiber was about 55% of pulp cash production cost and 75% of lumber cash cost, so a rise in fiber prices or a fall in pulp prices hits margins directly; surplus energy and chemicals in the pulp segment were 4.38% of revenue. Sales are spread across Germany at 25.44%, China at 29.45% and the United States at 16.00% of FY2025 revenue, and no customer exceeded 10% of revenue, so concentration is low but price exposure is global.
Basis: calculation A2; calculation A4; calculation A7; 10-K_2025-12-31_2025-Annual-Report-on-Form-10-K.pdf, pages 9, 19, 124, 128

The restructuring is creditor-led in direction but untested. Under the support agreements senior noteholders would receive 91.5% of the new common stock, 56.5% of the new convertible preferred stock and USD 150,000 thousand of takeback notes, and could take part in a rights offering of USD 420,000 thousand of new senior secured notes backstopped by an ad hoc group, while existing holders would keep 8.5% of the new common stock. The existing holders' 8.5% is diluted only by the management incentive plan and by conversion of the preferred stock, whereas the noteholders' 91.5% is also diluted by the rights offering stock, a term that favours the equity class relative to the noteholders (Analyst Interpretation). Existing equity keeping a protected residual stake while the senior notes are impaired is a point creditors should test against the executed documents, which were not supplied.
Basis: calculation A9; 10-Q_2026-09-30_Q3-2026-Quarterly-Report.pdf, pages 8, 12, 43

Disclosure is adequate for financial monitoring and thin for governance. The FY2025 audit opinion was unqualified with effective internal control, and the interim reports give facility availability, covenant status and waiver terms; but board composition, committee independence, pay and related-party review are incorporated by reference to a proxy statement that was not supplied, and the ownership share of a logging and chipping operation is stated as 50% in the FY2024 report and 20% in the FY2025 report without explanation.
Basis: 10-K_2024-12-31_2024-Annual-Report-on-Form-10-K.pdf, page 120; 10-K_2025-12-31_2025-Annual-Report-on-Form-10-K.pdf, pages 83, 85, 89, 121; 10-Q_2026-09-30_Q3-2026-Quarterly-Report.pdf, pages 13, 17, 50

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against reading the outcome as creditor-led is that the plan is conditional on court confirmation and that holders of about half of the common stock, whose claims rank behind notes that the issuer's own fair value disclosure puts at 29.06% of principal, are party to the agreements and keep a residual stake. If the executed documents show releases for shareholders, a management incentive plan that dilutes the noteholders' 91.5%, or consent terms that favour consenting holders over the rest, the alignment view would weaken. In the other direction, governance behaviour has been more cautious than the business's leverage suggests: the dividend was suspended in July 2025, the waiver bars distributions to the parent until 2026-09-30, and general unsecured creditors are unimpaired under the plan. A proxy statement showing a controlling holder, any evidence that the 2025 dividends or the acquisitions were debt-funded against creditor interests, or a plan outcome on different terms after 2026-11-05 would change the view.
Basis: calculation A9; calculation A15; 10-Q_2026-09-30_Q3-2026-Quarterly-Report.pdf, pages 8, 12, 13, 26, 51; 10-K_2025-12-31_2025-Annual-Report-on-Form-10-K.pdf, page 57

### Limitations

Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_2025-Annual-Report-on-Form-10-K.pdf, pages 83, 85
- The executed indentures, waiver letter and restructuring support agreements were not supplied, so what the documents permit for distributions and how equity and noteholders are treated rest on the issuer's summaries; this limits the creditor-alignment conclusion and the view of legal capacity.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_Q3-2026-Quarterly-Report.pdf, pages 12, 13, 53
- No document dated after 2026-11-05 was supplied, so the petition date, any debtor-in-possession financing and the outcome of the extended interest grace period are not established; this limits whether the restructuring timetable behind the view still holds at the cutoff.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_Q3-2026-Quarterly-Report.pdf, pages 12, 44, 54
- The 2022 and 2023 annual reports were not supplied, so how the Torgau, Conway and Okanagan acquisitions were funded is not established; this limits the conclusion that growth was debt-funded and the reading of financial policy.

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

The central transaction is the prepackaged restructuring under the support agreements signed on 2026-11-02. Mercer and one non-operating subsidiary intend to file chapter 11 cases in the Southern District of Texas; the operating subsidiaries are not filing and the company expects to complete the restructuring by the end of 2026. Senior noteholders holding about 87% of the notes principal and holders of about 50% of the common stock have agreed to vote for the plan.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_Q3-2026-Quarterly-Report.pdf, pages 7, 8, 12

Under the plan, holders of the USD 400,000 thousand 2028 notes and USD 875,000 thousand 2029 notes would receive 91.5% of the new common stock, 56.5% of USD 325,000 thousand of new convertible preferred stock and USD 150,000 thousand of takeback senior secured notes, plus the right to join a rights offering of USD 420,000 thousand of new senior secured notes for USD 400,000 thousand of cash, fully backstopped by members of an ad hoc group. The terms also include put option notes of USD 56,000 thousand, linked to a put option premium that the summary does not describe further. All new secured notes would sit under one indenture, mature four years and 364 days after issue and have no scheduled amortization, with interest payable in kind at the issuer's option for the first two years.
Basis: calculation A9; 10-Q_2026-09-30_Q3-2026-Quarterly-Report.pdf, pages 8, 12

Existing common stock would be cancelled, and holders would receive 8.5% of the new common stock and 3.5% of the new convertible preferred stock. On the effective date Mercer would enter a new asset-based revolving facility of up to USD 300,000 thousand, repay and terminate the German Facility and cut the Canadian Facility by at least USD 41,000 thousand, subject to any amendment extending its maturity by five years. Trade and other general unsecured creditors would be unimpaired.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_Q3-2026-Quarterly-Report.pdf, pages 8, 12, 43

The earlier debt actions are smaller. In October 2024 Mercer added USD 200,000 thousand of 12.875% notes due 2028 at 103.000% of principal and redeemed USD 300,000 thousand of notes due January 2026. In 2026 it obtained a waiver of the German Facility leverage covenant dated 2026-05-04, did not pay about USD 25,800 thousand of interest on the 2028 notes due 2026-10-01, and obtained majority consent on 2026-10-30 to lengthen the grace period from 30 days to 90 days. Government support for the Peace River mill in August 2026 comprised C$18.1 million of grants and a repayable contribution of up to C$19.0 million.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_2025-Annual-Report-on-Form-10-K.pdf, page 107; 10-Q_2026-09-30_Q3-2026-Quarterly-Report.pdf, pages 9, 12, 13

### Overall governance view

Overall, Mercer presents medium sponsor and governance risk. The key supported behavior is a dividend of USD 10,039 thousand paid in 2025 and suspended on 2025-07-31, followed by a covenant waiver, a deferred coupon and a noteholder-led restructuring plan, which matters because cash left while liquidity tightened and now creditors rather than a sponsor decide the outcome. Countervailing evidence is the absence of a sponsor, no documented priming, uptier or fee leakage, and unimpaired general unsecured creditors. Further analysis requires the proxy statement, the executed agreements and any filings after 2026-11-05.
