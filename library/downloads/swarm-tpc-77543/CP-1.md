## Findings

### Bottom line

Debt is modest against earnings and covered by cash, but the cushion is thinner than the headline suggests. Gross debt at 2026-09-30 is 1.02x last-twelve-months EBITDA built from statement lines, net debt is below zero, and USD 504,218 thousand of the USD 1,111,049 thousand reported cash is available for general corporate purposes, the rest being held for joint ventures.

FY2024 EBITDA was negative, so leverage that year is not meaningful, and the 2026 refinancing moved the next large maturity to 2033 with the revolver at USD 350,000 thousand undrawn. Customer billings in excess of costs of USD 2,044,207 thousand are the largest liability line on the balance sheet and would reverse if backlog shrank.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_tpc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 3, 10, 14–18; calculation C5; calculation C11

### Key findings

Leverage has fallen from not meaningful to modest as earnings recovered and debt was repaid. On FY2025 EBITDA of USD 281,776 thousand gross debt was 1.45x EBITDA and the company held net cash of 1.16x EBITDA, and on last-twelve-months EBITDA of USD 396,576 thousand at 2026-09-30 gross debt is 1.02x and net cash is 1.78x. Gross debt carrying value fell from USD 534,138 thousand at the end of FY2024 to USD 403,641 thousand at 2026-09-30, while income from construction operations swung from a loss of USD 103,753 thousand in FY2024 to a profit of USD 231,961 thousand in FY2025 and USD 302,336 thousand in the nine months of 2026.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_tpc-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 70–71; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tpc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 2–3, 14; calculation C1; calculation C2; calculation C4; calculation C5; calculation C9; calculation C10; calculation C11

Interest cover on the latest full year is comfortable but the latest twelve months cannot be tested. FY2025 EBITDA covered cash interest paid of USD 50,419 thousand by 5.59x, against -0.68x in FY2024 when EBITDA was negative. The 2026 refinancing replaced the 2029 senior notes with the 2033 senior notes at a lower coupon, and the company states annualised cash interest savings of USD 21.0 million; the interim reports state no cash interest paid, so cover at 2026-09-30 is not calculable on the method's definition.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_tpc-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 84, 101; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tpc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 15, 62; calculation C2; calculation C14

Cash generation is strong but is flattered by customer funding. Operating cash flow was USD 748,065 thousand in FY2025 and USD 665,063 thousand in the nine months of 2026, against capital expenditure of USD 180,854 thousand and USD 91,265 thousand, but billings in excess of costs, the customer advances that fund projects, rose from USD 1,216,623 thousand at the end of FY2024 to USD 1,838,610 thousand at the end of FY2025 and USD 2,044,207 thousand at 2026-09-30. That float is more than five times gross debt and reverses if new awards stop replacing revenue, and backlog of USD 20,353.3 million at 2026-09-30 is below the USD 21.6 billion of a year earlier, so operating cash flow of this size is not a stable base.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_tpc-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 71–72; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tpc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 3–4, 49–50; calculation C15; calculation C16

Liquidity is ample against near-term maturities. Cash available for general corporate purposes of USD 504,218 thousand plus the undrawn USD 350,000 thousand revolver is USD 854,218 thousand, against current maturities of debt of USD 4,563 thousand. Most of the reported cash, USD 606,831 thousand, is joint venture cash available only for joint venture uses, so net debt on consolidated cash overstates what creditors can reach; on general-purpose cash alone the company still has net cash of USD 100,577 thousand, or 0.25x EBITDA.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_tpc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 3, 10, 18, 63; calculation C12; calculation C17; calculation C18

EBITDA in this work is derived, not issuer-stated: income from construction operations plus depreciation and amortisation, before other income, net, and after share-based compensation of USD 150,002 thousand in FY2025, equal to 53.23% of that year's EBITDA. The issuer's own covenant ratios at 2026-09-30, total net leverage of -0.22x and interest cover of 17.14x, follow credit agreement definitions that are not supplied, so they are not comparable with the figures above. The first lien leverage test used until the first quarter of 2026 was replaced from the second quarter of 2026, so the covenant history is not one series.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_tpc-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 71, 120; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tpc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 17, 66; calculation C2; calculation C19

The refinancing of 2026-07-02 is inside the 2026-09-30 balance sheet but after the FY2025 balance sheet, so FY2025 net debt and the year-end maturity ladder, which still shows USD 402,223 thousand maturing in 2029, pre-date it. The only dated development after 2026-09-30 in the supplied reports is an October 2026 court ruling with no stated effect on net debt, so no post-balance-sheet adjustment is made.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_tpc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 100; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tpc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 15, 26; calculation C21

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest case against the comfortable reading is the Philadelphia hotel litigation: the court awarded the developer approximately USD 175 million, the company's building subsidiary has appealed, and management describes a material adverse impact as neither probable nor remote, against USD 504,218 thousand of general-purpose cash. A second weakness is the earnings record, since FY2024 EBITDA was negative and FY2025 EBITDA includes USD 150,002 thousand of share-based compensation that moves with the share price.
The view would turn if the award were upheld in full on appeal, if billings in excess of costs fell while backlog shrank, or if cash available for general corporate purposes fell back toward the USD 265,647 thousand of FY2024.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_tpc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 10, 25–26; 10-K_2025-12-31_tpc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 78; calculation C19
