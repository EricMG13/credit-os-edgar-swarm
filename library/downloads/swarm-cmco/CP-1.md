## Findings

### Bottom line

Net debt is 6.95x issuer-adjusted EBITDA for the twelve months to September 2026, above the 4.9x the issuer reports on an annualised basis, so creditors should not rely on the lower figure until Kito Crosby has been owned for a full year. Net debt of USD 2,301.41 million (and USD 2,303.44 million after the dividend payable on 2026-11-16) has not fallen since March 2026, and reported EBITDA of USD 10.03 million over the same twelve months, after a USD 200 million goodwill impairment and deal costs, is too small to give a meaningful ratio.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-11-03_exhibit9911132026.htm.jsonl, page 14; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cmco-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 4; calculation C03; calculation C06; calculation C13; calculation C15

Liquidity of USD 531.98 million and no large maturity before fiscal 2029 give time, but adjusted cash interest cover is 2.75x and the AR securitization lender had to waive its delinquency tests at September 2026.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_cmco-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 27; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cmco-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 55; 10-K_2026-03-31_cmco-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 115; calculation C08; calculation C09

### Key findings

The apparent fall in leverage is a denominator effect, not repayment. Net debt was USD 2,296.45 million in March 2026, USD 2,278.37 million in June and USD 2,301.41 million in September, so net debt over issuer-adjusted EBITDA moved from 12.66x to 8.76x and 6.95x only because Kito Crosby's earnings entered the twelve-month figure. The issuer's own 4.9x Credit Agreement ratio is lower again because its earnings add USD 132.39 million of annualised acquisition earnings and expected synergies, and its net debt excludes the AR securitization facility.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-11-03_exhibit9911132026.htm.jsonl, page 14; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cmco-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 4; calculation C03; calculation C06; calculation C15

Reported earnings are not a usable base. EBITDA before issuer adjustments was negative at USD 68.42 million for fiscal 2026 because the goodwill impairment, deal costs, inventory step-up and refinancing costs run through it. Issuer-adjusted EBITDA of USD 331.19 million for the twelve months to September 2026 therefore rests on add-backs the issuer defines: fourteen items added a net USD 249.8 million in fiscal 2026, led by the USD 200 million impairment, with a USD 103.31 million gain on a business sale deducted.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-06-04_exhibit9913312026.htm.jsonl, pages 13–14; EX-99.1_2026-11-03_exhibit9911132026.htm.jsonl, page 13; calculation C05; calculation C12; calculation C21

Cash generation does not yet support the debt. Operating cash flow was negative at USD 125.39 million and free cash flow negative at USD 153.19 million over the twelve months to September 2026, dominated by fiscal 2026 when deal and integration costs were paid; in the first half of fiscal 2027 operating cash flow was USD 21.07 million and free cash flow USD 4.6 million. Capital expenditure is light at 1.57% of net sales, so deleveraging depends on operating cash flow recovering as integration costs fade.
Basis: 10-K_2026-03-31_cmco-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 60; 10-K_2026-03-31_cmco-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 77; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cmco-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 7; EX-99.1_2026-11-03_exhibit9911132026.htm.jsonl, page 13; calculation C06; calculation C07

Interest cover is thin and the trailing figure flatters it. Issuer-adjusted EBITDA covered cash interest paid 2.75x over the twelve months to September 2026, but that period holds only part of a year of interest on the new debt. Interest and debt expense in the September quarter was USD 48.05 million against quarterly issuer-adjusted EBITDA of USD 111.21 million, a cover of 2.31x, before any benefit from the 50 bp term loan margin cut that began on 2026-09-21.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_cmco-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 5; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cmco-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 27; EX-99.1_2026-11-03_exhibit9911132026.htm.jsonl, page 13; calculation C06; calculation C17

Liquidity is adequate for now but fell in the quarter. Cash of USD 110.9 million and revolver availability of USD 421.08 million make USD 531.98 million, down USD 35.17 million since June as revolver borrowings rose from USD 12.84 million to USD 59.64 million. The annual report's schedule shows only USD 17.98 million due in fiscal 2027 and USD 16.5 million in fiscal 2028 before USD 69.9 million in fiscal 2029 and USD 2,320.12 million after fiscal 2030, and the issuer plans to repay about USD 150 million in the next twelve months, already classed as current.
Basis: 10-K_2026-03-31_cmco-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 115; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cmco-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 27; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cmco-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 29; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cmco-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 55; calculation C08; calculation C09; calculation C19

The covenant package gives little comfort that can be measured. The key financial covenant is a first lien leverage ceiling of 7.75x that steps down to 6.25x and is tested only when revolver borrowings exceed 30% of commitments, a trigger that USD 59.64 million drawn against USD 500 million, or 11.93%, does not reach. The first lien ratio itself is not reported, so headroom is not established. The AR securitization lender waived its delinquency and default ratios at 2026-09-30 and the facility was amended again on 2026-10-29, and the documents do not say why.
Basis: 10-K_2026-03-31_cmco-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 35; 10-K_2026-03-31_cmco-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 113; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cmco-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 27; calculation C19; calculation C20

Claims outside total debt add to the burden. The USD 800 million of 7% convertible preferred shares sold to the sponsor are carried in equity at USD 818.4 million, including payment-in-kind dividends, and would convert into about 43% of the equity; operating lease liabilities were USD 95.72 million and the pension plans were underfunded by USD 78.25 million at March 2026, general and product liability reserves were USD 31.87 million before USD 19.74 million of expected insurance recoveries at September 2026, and USD 20 million is payable to the former owner of Kito Crosby.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_cmco-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cmco-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 31; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cmco-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 33; 10-K_2026-03-31_cmco-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 109; 10-K_2026-03-31_cmco-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 119; 10-K_2026-03-31_cmco-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 149; calculation C18

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest contrary evidence is operating momentum and liquidity. The issuer-adjusted EBITDA margin was 20.8% in the first half of fiscal 2027 against 14.7% a year earlier, backlog at March 2026 was USD 519.6 million against USD 322.5 million a year before, and the issuer's annualised measure is 4.9x with cost synergies still to come. Liquidity is also well above scheduled maturities, and the key financial covenant is untested at current revolver use.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-11-03_exhibit9911132026.htm.jsonl, pages 13–14; EX-99.1_2026-06-04_exhibit9913312026.htm.jsonl, page 10; calculation C15; calculation C19

The view would improve if actual twelve-month issuer-adjusted EBITDA, once Kito Crosby is in for a full year, brings net leverage clearly below the current figure while net debt falls, if free cash flow turns positive after integration costs and cash interest, and if the AR securitization facility passes its tests without waivers. It would worsen if revolver borrowings keep rising toward the 30% trigger, if the waiver is repeated, or if quarterly adjusted EBITDA stops covering interest and debt expense by a wide margin.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_cmco-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 27; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cmco-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 55; calculation C17; calculation C19

### Financial interpretation

Leverage trajectory and drivers. Debt rose from USD 470.98 million in March 2025 to USD 2,393.01 million in March 2026 because Kito Crosby was bought with a new term loan, USD 900 million of senior secured notes due 2033 and USD 800 million of preferred equity, and then rose a further USD 19.3 million to USD 2,412.31 million by September 2026. Net debt over reported EBITDA is 229.41x in September and not meaningful in March and June because reported EBITDA is negative; the issuer-adjusted ratios of 12.66x, 8.76x and 6.95x fall only as acquired earnings are added to the twelve-month window.
Basis: 10-K_2026-03-31_cmco-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 74; 10-K_2026-03-31_cmco-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 109; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cmco-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cmco-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 26; calculation C02; calculation C03; calculation C11

Coverage. The quarterly view, 2.31x on interest and debt expense, is the better indicator of the current burden. No funds-from-operations measure is stated by the issuer and none is built here.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_cmco-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 5; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cmco-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 8; EX-99.1_2026-11-03_exhibit9911132026.htm.jsonl, page 13; calculation C06; calculation C17
