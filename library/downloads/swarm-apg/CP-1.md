## Findings

### Bottom line

Acquisitions and share repurchases lifted leverage in 2026: total debt reached 3.58x trailing reported EBITDA and net debt 3.21x at 2026-09-30, against 3.13x and 2.10x at the 2025 year end, while liquidity fell to USD 1,032 million.

Cash interest cover rose to 7.74x as earnings grew and cash interest paid fell, but interest expense in the third quarter of 2026 was USD 45 million against USD 34 million a year earlier. The USD 100 million drawn on the revolver after quarter end is not reflected in net debt, and its effect on net debt is not established. Trailing EBITDA carries acquired businesses only from their closing dates and the filings state no pro forma earnings, so run-rate leverage is not established.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_apg-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-Q_2026-09-30_apg-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 22–23; 10-Q_2026-09-30_apg-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 24; calculation CALC-3; calculation CALC-6; calculation CALC-15

### Key findings

Net debt rose from USD 1,847 million to USD 3,201 million between 2025-12-31 and 2026-09-30, an increase of USD 1,354 million. Acquisitions of USD 1,356 million and share repurchases of USD 252 million over nine months exceeded free cash flow of USD 317 million, and the gap was funded by the 2034 senior notes, revolver borrowing and cash on hand. Added debt raises leverage unless acquired earnings arrive quickly, and the filings state no pro forma earnings for the three large acquisitions.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_apg-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 4–5; 10-Q_2026-09-30_apg-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 8–9; calculation CALC-6; calculation CALC-11; calculation CALC-12

Cash flow quality weakened even as earnings grew. Operating cash flow was USD 393 million against USD 377 million a year earlier, but the working capital outflow widened to USD 312 million from USD 218 million, and free cash flow equalled 35.38% of adjusted EBITDA against 41.15%. Working capital absorbed cash because receivables and contract assets grew with revenue, so cash conversion depends on collections keeping pace with growth.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_apg-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 4–5; EX-99.1_2026-10-29_apg-20260930xexx991.htm.jsonl, page 11; calculation CALC-10; calculation CALC-11; calculation CALC-16

Liquidity fell by USD 625 million to USD 1,032 million, made up of cash of USD 373 million and undrawn revolver capacity of USD 659 million after USD 6 million of letters of credit. The revolver is drawn USD 335 million, and a further USD 100 million was drawn after quarter end, so availability is USD 559 million at 2026-10-29. Apart from USD 5 million of short-term loans and scheduled term loan amortization, nothing matures before 2029, which limits near-term refinancing pressure, but the 2031 revolver is the only committed facility.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_apg-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 24; 10-Q_2026-09-30_apg-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 56; calculation CALC-13; calculation CALC-14

The adjusted figures rest on large add-backs. Systems and business enablement costs of USD 82 million equal 9.15% of nine-month adjusted EBITDA and were USD 61 million a year earlier, so a cost the issuer calls non-recurring has been recurring across periods. Reported EBITDA is therefore the primary denominator, and the adjusted ratios of 3.00x total and 2.69x net are shown beside it.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_apg-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 54–55; EX-99.1_2026-10-29_apg-20260930xexx991.htm.jsonl, page 11; calculation CALC-17

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest contrary evidence is earnings momentum: nine-month adjusted EBITDA rose to USD 896 million from USD 746 million, nine-month operating cash flow rose, and the nearest large maturity is the 2029 notes. Adjusted leverage of 3.00x total and 2.69x net at 2026-09-30 is below reported leverage and would fall further if earnings from acquisitions closed during the year were annualised, but the filings do not state those earnings. A partial revolver repayment from operating cash flow, or disclosed pro forma earnings for the acquisitions, would move the view toward lower leverage; further debt-funded acquisitions or a larger working capital outflow would move it the other way.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_apg-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 8–9; 10-Q_2026-09-30_apg-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 22–23; calculation CALC-4

### Limitations

The fiscal-year EBITDA figures are calculated from statement lines because the annual report states only adjusted EBITDA. Cash interest is reported net of interest income, and carrying value is stated only in total, not by instrument. The most recent maturity ladder in the filings predates the 2026 amendment, so the ladder shown is built from instrument maturity dates and puts scheduled term loan amortisation at final maturity. The term loan's security and ranking are not stated, so it is left out of the debt stack.
