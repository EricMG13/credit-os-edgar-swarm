## Findings

### Bottom line
ACCO is a publicly listed branded-products business whose credit risk comes from shrinking demand, tariff exposure and customer concentration rather than sponsor behaviour, but the 2025 covenant amendment shows the limits of its cushion. No sponsor or controlling holder is identified in the supplied filings, and shareholder payouts of USD 42.1 million in FY2025 were below free cash flow of USD 50.8 million.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_acco_acco-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 38; 10-K_2025-12-31_acco_acco-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 39; 10-K_2025-12-31_acco_acco-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 63; 10-K_2025-12-31_acco_acco-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 75; calculation C06

The governance risk level is Medium on evidence that is incomplete rather than adverse: the proxy statement that holds director, pay and ownership detail was not supplied, and the credit agreement that sets payout capacity is summarised only in the filings.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_acco_acco-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 108–109; 10-K_2025-12-31_acco_acco-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 75–76; Source readiness (CP-0)

### Key findings
- Demand, not governance, is the main operating risk. Comparable net sales fell 9.3% in FY2025 after a fall of 8.0% in FY2024 and fell 3.7% in the nine months to 2026-09-30; the issuer attributes FY2025 volume losses to lower consumer and business demand and tariff disruption, and 2026 declines to technology peripherals. Reported sales rose 3.9% in 2026 only because EPOS and currency added to them. Falling volume reaches creditors through lower earnings on a largely fixed cost base, which the cost programme has so far offset: the issuer states about USD 90.0 million of annualized savings realized against a target of about USD 100.0 million by the end of 2026.
Basis: 10-K_2024-12-31_acco_acco-20241231.htm.jsonl, page 40; 10-K_2025-12-31_acco_acco-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 41–42; 10-Q_2026-09-30_acco_acco-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 30–31; 10-Q_2026-09-30_acco_acco-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 36
- Customer concentration is material. The five largest customers took USD 484.7 million, or 31.79% of FY2025 net sales, and the largest, Amazon, took USD 158.1 million, or about 10%; their receivables were USD 117.0 million, or 32.53% of year-end accounts receivable. Management itself says the largest customers have significant negotiating leverage and that concentration raises customer credit risk, and a reserve of USD 4.8 million for a customer liquidation was booked in the third quarter of 2026, which shows the receivable exposure is real.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_acco_acco-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 12; 10-K_2025-12-31_acco_acco-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 62; 10-K_2025-12-31_acco_acco-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 106; 10-Q_2026-09-30_acco_acco-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 31; calculation C01; calculation C09
- Strategy is moving toward technology peripherals through small, cheap acquisitions, and the funding of the latest is not stated. EPOS was bought on 2026-01-30 for a net USD 1.1 million after cash acquired and produced a bargain purchase gain of USD 33.8 million because it was losing money, so integration and restructuring cash, not the price, is the cost. The Trust acquisition completed on 2026-10-01 at an unadjusted price of about USD 57 million plus USD 5 million of adjustments, after the latest balance sheet, with no financing disclosed.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_acco_acco-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 7–9
- Payout policy is mixed rather than extractive. The quarterly dividend per share has been unchanged for three years and cost USD 27.0 million in FY2025; with USD 15.1 million of buybacks, nearly all in the first quarter of 2025, that was 82.9% of free cash flow. After the July 2025 covenant amendment, 2026 dividends plus buybacks are capped at the greater of USD 40.0 million or 1% of consolidated total assets, which is the USD 40.0 million figure at the 2025 year-end balance sheet; no shares were repurchased in the nine months to 2026-09-30, when dividends took 23.4% of free cash flow and 52.0% of the cap. The filings do not say whether the wider standing baskets, which allow a further USD 75.0 million a year at 0.25x inside the covenant and unlimited payments at 3.25x or lower, apply again after 2026.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_acco_acco-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 39; 10-K_2025-12-31_acco_acco-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 62; 10-K_2025-12-31_acco_acco-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 63; 10-K_2025-12-31_acco_acco-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 76; 10-Q_2026-09-30_acco_acco-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 4–5; 10-Q_2026-09-30_acco_acco-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 10; 10-Q_2026-03-31_acco_acco-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 4; calculation C06; calculation C08
- Seasonality moves the revolver through the year. Sales are lowest in the first quarter, back-to-school lifts the second and third quarters, and operating cash flow arrives in the second half, so revolver availability fell to USD 204.8 million at 2026-06-30 before recovering to USD 253.7 million at 2026-09-30. The quarterly path, not the year-end snapshot, is what to monitor.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_acco_acco-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 6; 10-Q_2026-06-30_acco_acco-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 32; 10-Q_2026-09-30_acco_acco-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 34
- Exposure outside the United States is 57.5% of net sales and sits in euro, Australian dollar and other currencies, matched in part by euro and Australian dollar borrowings; about 40% of products are made in the issuer's own plants and 60% are sourced, mainly from Asia, so tariff and currency changes reach gross margin directly. Net tariff refunds added USD 15.1 million to third-quarter 2026 operating income and may not recur.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view
The strongest evidence against a cautious reading is financial: the issuer reports a covenant leverage ratio of 3.60x against a 4.25x maximum, liquidity has rebuilt to USD 428.4 million, capital expenditure is only about 1.17% of net sales, no payout exceeds free cash flow, and about USD 90.0 million of cost savings are already realized. The Medium level would move down to Low if the proxy statement showed independent control and no related-party payments and if the Trust purchase proved to be funded from cash on hand; it would move up to High if payouts after 2026 drew on the wider baskets while leverage stayed above 3.25x, if a top customer was lost, or if fourth-quarter cash flow failed to repay the revolver drawings that peaked in mid-year.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_acco_acco-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 11; 10-Q_2026-09-30_acco_acco-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 34; 10-K_2025-12-31_acco_acco-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 63; 10-K_2025-12-31_acco_acco-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 76; 10-Q_2026-09-30_acco_acco-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 36; calculation C04; calculation C06; calculation C07

### Limitations
- The proxy statement was not supplied, so director independence, committee structure, executive pay and share ownership are not established; the Medium governance level could move either way once it is read.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_acco_acco-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 108–109; 10-K_2025-12-31_acco_acco-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 2
- The executed credit agreement and indenture were not supplied, so what the company may pay out, borrow or transfer is known only from the filings' summaries, which omit the definitions behind the baskets; this limits any conclusion on legal capacity for distributions and does not support a finding of willingness.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_acco_acco-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 10–11; 10-K_2025-12-31_acco_acco-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 76; 10-K_2025-12-31_acco_acco-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 111–112

Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_acco_acco-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 106
- The funding, cash acquired and earnings contribution of the Trust acquisition are not stated, so its effect on leverage and on the technology-peripherals strategy is not established.

### Transaction summary and new debt issues
No new debt was issued in the periods the filings cover and no refinancing is pending; the financing event that matters is the July 2025 covenant amendment, which bought covenant room at the price of a USD 35.0 million term loan prepayment and a cap on 2026 payouts, so the risk is headroom rather than new secured debt ranking ahead of existing creditors. The amendment raised the maximum leverage ratio to 4.50x for the third and fourth quarters of 2025, 4.75x for the first and second quarters of 2026 and 4.25x for the third and fourth quarters of 2026, with the original 4.50x and 4.00x levels returning afterwards, and it changed lien, debt and restricted-payment baskets through 2026-12-31. The reported ratio was 4.13x against a 4.50x maximum at 2025-12-31 and 3.60x against 4.25x at 2026-09-30. The secured credit agreement, reduced in October 2024 to a EUR 184.8 million term loan facility and a USD 467.5 million revolver, falls due on 2029-10-30, with a requirement to refinance the USD 575.0 million of 4.25% senior unsecured notes due March 2029 by September 2028.

Acquisitions are small and cash-settled. Buro in Australia and New Zealand closed on 2025-02-28 for USD 10.1 million, EPOS on 2026-01-30 for EUR 6.5 million at closing plus up to EUR 3.0 million of contingent consideration, and Trust on 2026-10-01 for about USD 57 million plus USD 5 million of preliminary adjustments, the last after the latest balance sheet and with no financing, cash acquired or earnings contribution disclosed, so its effect on leverage is not established.
