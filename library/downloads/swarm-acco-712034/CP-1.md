## Findings

### Bottom line
ACCO Brands carries USD 878.1 million of balance-sheet debt, which is 5.60x last-twelve-months EBITDA derived from reported results to 2026-09-30 and well above the 3.60x covenant ratio the issuer reports, so headroom depends on a covenant definition the filings do not give. Net debt is USD 703.4 million, or 4.49x, and derived EBITDA covers cash interest 3.63x.

### Financial interpretation

Leverage on carrying-value debt and derived EBITDA was 5.07x at FY2025 and is 5.60x on a last-twelve-months basis at 2026-09-30, because carrying debt rose from USD 836.8 million to USD 878.1 million while derived EBITDA fell from USD 165.1 million to USD 156.7 million. The FY2024 ratio of 23.12x is not a trend point: it is struck after a non-cash impairment of USD 165.2 million, and before it the same ratio is 4.15x. The issuer's covenant ratio of 3.60x against a 4.25x maximum is 2.00x below the derived figure, and without the covenant EBITDA the difference cannot be bridged, so the covenant figure is quoted but not used as a leverage measure here.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_acco_acco-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 62–63; 10-Q_2026-09-30_acco_acco-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 3–5; 10-Q_2026-09-30_acco_acco-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 34; calculation C01; calculation C05; calculation C07

Free cash flow after capital expenditure is USD 114.0 million over the last twelve months, before dividends of USD 20.8 million in the nine months.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_acco_acco-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 63; 10-Q_2026-09-30_acco_acco-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 4–5; 10-Q_2026-09-30_acco_acco-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 36–37; calculation C04; calculation C05
