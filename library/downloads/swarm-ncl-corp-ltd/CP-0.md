## Findings

### Bottom line

The supplied filings are sufficient for Financial foundation, Business & governance and Fundamental credit work on the issuer's own statements, but without the credit agreement and indentures covenant protection cannot be tested, and the October 2026 refinancing postdates the latest balance sheet.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_nclc-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 41; 10-Q_2026-09-30_nclc-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, pages 11–12; 10-Q_2026-09-30_nclc-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 50

The issuer is a wholly owned subsidiary of Norwegian Cruise Line Holdings, so its own annual and quarterly reports must carry every debt, earnings and liquidity figure. The three results releases describe the parent group and differ from the issuer on net income, cash and debt. The October 2026 refinancing is described in amounts only, so issuer-level pro forma debt and leverage are not established. The annual report gives levels for three covenant tests but none for the net funded debt to capitalization test, and the amendment that changed the covenant definitions is not supplied.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_nclc-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 2; EX-99.1_2026-10-29_exh_991.htm.jsonl, page 1; calculation K3; calculation K4; calculation K5
