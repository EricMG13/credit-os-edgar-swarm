## Findings

### Bottom line

Post's gross leverage is 5.52x reported EBITDA, little changed in nine months, because USD 916.1 million of repurchases absorbed free cash flow and divestiture proceeds; creditors rely on cash generation and revolver access, not debt reduction.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_post-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 3–4; calculation C47; calculation C35; calculation C98

Interest cost is the pressure point: interest expense, net rose to USD 419.1 million over the last twelve months from USD 361.4 million in fiscal 2025, and reported EBITDA covered fiscal 2025 cash interest 3.59x. Liquidity is adequate at USD 1,242.4 million of cash and undrawn revolver, with the next debt maturity, USD 576.3 million, in fiscal 2027.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_post-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 94; 10-Q_2026-06-30_post-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 20–21; calculation C68; calculation C52; calculation C72; calculation C54

### Key findings

Gross debt at carrying value rose to USD 7,632.6 million at June 30, 2026 from USD 7,422.9 million at September 30, 2025, while last-twelve-months reported EBITDA rose to USD 1,383.0 million from USD 1,330.1 million. Gross leverage therefore eased from 5.58x to 5.52x and net leverage from 5.45x to 5.33x. The larger step was in fiscal 2025, when net leverage rose from 4.76x to 5.45x as USD 920.3 million of acquisitions and USD 709.0 million of repurchases were paid from cash and borrowings.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_post-20250930.htm.jsonl, pages 94–96; 10-Q_2026-06-30_post-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 3; calculation C47; calculation C35; calculation C48; calculation C36; calculation C34; calculation C76

The fiscal 2026 refinancings raised principal debt rather than holding it flat. USD 1,300.0 million of 6.50% notes due 2036 and USD 600.0 million of additional senior notes due 2034 repaid USD 1,235.0 million of 5.50% notes due 2029 and the revolver, which lengthened the profile at higher coupons, but principal still rose by USD 223.8 million to USD 7,676.0 million, and net debt rose USD 120.8 million over nine months despite USD 424.2 million of disposal proceeds.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_post-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 17–22; calculation C77; calculation C75

Coverage is thinner than leverage suggests. Reported EBITDA covered cash interest 3.59x in fiscal 2025 against 3.94x in fiscal 2024, and interest expense, net is up USD 57.7 million over the last twelve months on higher average debt, a higher average rate and lower interest income, so cover on that basis eased from 3.68x to 3.30x. Cash interest paid is not printed for any fiscal 2026 period, so interim cash cover is not established.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_post-20250930.htm.jsonl, pages 94, 128; EX-99.1_2026-08-06_ex99-1q32026er.htm.jsonl, page 5; calculation C52; calculation C51; calculation C78; calculation C79; calculation C80

Free cash flow improved because capital expenditure fell, not because operations improved. Over nine months operating cash flow was USD 691.3 million against USD 697.0 million, additions to property were USD 289.8 million against USD 360.5 million, and free cash flow was USD 401.5 million against USD 336.5 million. Cash paid for repurchases of USD 916.1 million was more than twice that free cash flow.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_post-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 3–4; calculation C84; calculation C83; calculation C98

Liquidity is the offset. Cash of USD 265.6 million plus USD 976.8 million of available revolver capacity gives USD 1,242.4 million, against USD 714.4 million at September 30, 2025, because the March 2026 notes repaid the revolver. No debt falls due before fiscal 2027, when USD 576.3 million matures, led by USD 575.0 million of convertible notes due in August 2027; the revolver matures in 2029. The one financial covenant tests secured net leverage and applies only when revolver usage passes 30% of commitments, so it is untested at current usage.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_post-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 17–23; calculation C72; calculation C73; calculation C54

These ratios are not the issuer's own. The issuer reports net leverage of 4.6x on principal debt of USD 7,676.0 million less cash, over Adjusted EBITDA of USD 1,597.3 million after credit-agreement adjustments of USD 18.6 million. Issuer add-backs are 16.84% of reported EBITDA, which is why adjusted leverage of 4.56x net and reported leverage of 5.33x net differ so widely. The secured net leverage ratio the covenant tests is not reported, so covenant headroom is not established.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-08-06_ex99-1q32026er.htm.jsonl, pages 21–22; 10-Q_2026-06-30_post-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 23; calculation C50; calculation C48; calculation C89; calculation C90; calculation C91

Obligations outside the debt figure are mostly commitments, not borrowings. At September 30, 2025, operating lease liabilities of USD 277.9 million would add 0.21x to gross leverage if counted as debt, signed leases not yet started carry USD 138.3 million of future payments, and minimum purchase commitments are USD 7,382.7 million, mainly egg contracts and open purchase orders. The balance sheet carries pension assets of USD 151.6 million and pension and retiree benefit obligations of USD 57.3 million, lease guarantees on the former Bob Evans restaurants carry USD 13.5 million of annual rent, and litigation is not expected to be material. None of these is in Total Debt.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_post-20250930.htm.jsonl, pages 80, 128, 131; 10-K_2025-09-30_post-20250930.htm.jsonl, pages 142–143; calculation C97

Subsequent events are kept out of the period figures. Repurchases of USD 39.3 million after June 30, 2026 lift pro forma net leverage to 5.36x, and two facility sales expected in the second half of calendar 2026 are not reflected because no proceeds are stated. Several events carry more than one figure, and the definition register sets each out with its role: the 8th Avenue acquisition, the pasta sale and the Crystal Farms sale. No supplier finance, factoring or deferred-revenue float appears in the statements.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-08-06_ex99-1q32026er.htm.jsonl, page 6; 10-Q_2026-06-30_post-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 9–12; calculation C57; calculation C58

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest case against a cautious view is liquidity and a clear runway: USD 1,242.4 million of cash and undrawn revolver, no revolver drawings, and no maturity before fiscal 2027. It would win if the next two quarters show repurchases curtailed and free cash flow, USD 553.1 million over the last twelve months, applied to debt, so that net debt fell below USD 7,246.2 million. The view would turn more negative if fourth-quarter Adjusted EBITDA lands in the USD 369.5 million to USD 379.5 million range the fiscal 2026 outlook implies, against USD 425.4 million a year earlier, because last-twelve-months EBITDA and cover would then fall without any reduction in debt.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-08-06_ex99-1q32026er.htm.jsonl, pages 6–7; calculation C72; calculation C71; calculation C87; calculation C88

### Limitations

The latest balance sheet is dated June 30, 2026 and nothing later is supplied, so every position and ratio describes that date, and the fiscal 2026 year-end is not covered.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_post-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 3

Reported EBITDA is calculated from net earnings, interest, tax and depreciation and amortization because the issuer prints no unadjusted EBITDA; the issuer's Adjusted EBITDA appears only in the earnings releases, from fiscal 2025 onward.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-08-06_ex99-1q32026er.htm.jsonl, page 22; calculation C3

Instrument carrying values, the secured net leverage ratio, interim cash interest and a year-by-year maturity table at June 30, 2026 are not printed, so the debt stack and ladder use principal and the leaseback balance is placed at the end of its term.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_post-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 17
