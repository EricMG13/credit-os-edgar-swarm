### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a constrained view is that growth, margins and visibility are improving together: second-quarter organic growth was 24.4% by the company's account, adjusted EBITDA margin has held near 30%, the free cash outflow of the first half of 2026 was smaller than a year earlier, the 2026 outlook was raised in March, May and August 2026, and the backlog of USD 1.3 billion is about 2.21x twelve-month revenue. If the outlook is met and net debt stays at its June level, net leverage on adjusted EBITDA would fall to 3.22x, the repricing already lowers interest by about USD 3,820 thousand a year, and liquidity covers 4.06 years of the current cash outflow. The view would improve if operating cash flow turns positive after capital expenditure, if contract assets stop growing faster than revenue, if pro forma earnings show the acquired businesses covering their debt, and if the control weaknesses are reported as remediated. It would worsen if the Walker acquisition is funded with revolver or term loan before earnings, if a top-three customer delays or loses a program, if the revolver is drawn above the covenant test threshold, or if restated figures follow from the control weaknesses.

### Limitations

- No peer, rating or market-pricing information is supplied, so the finding that leverage is high rests on the company's own history and the disclosed covenant level, and it could prove softer or harder against comparable issuers.
- The 2026 outlook and the organic growth rate are the company's own statements and the filings define neither the acquired-revenue split nor the outlook beyond excluding future acquisitions, so the outlook-implied leverage and the durability of growth rest on management's figures.
- Pro forma earnings are not stated for any acquisition, so the pro forma net leverage uses year-end earnings with none of the acquired business and overstates run-rate leverage by an amount that is not established.
- The credit agreement is not supplied, so covenant headroom, the springing-test threshold, guarantor scope and debt ranking cannot be tested, and the liquidity finding counts the whole revolver as available.
- The as-of date falls after the third quarter of 2026 but the documents end at 30 June 2026, so third-quarter results, the Walker closing and any later financing are not captured and could change the leverage and cash conversion findings.
- The company dropped the word funded from its backlog label in the annual report and now defines backlog as the total or current estimated value of contracts less amounts invoiced, with no restatement and no split of funded from unfunded work, so how much of the USD 1.3 billion is funded is not established and the revenue visibility finding cannot be turned into expected cash.

### Company description

Karman Holdings Inc. designs, tests and manufactures payload protection, aerodynamic interstage and propulsion systems for missile defense, tactical missile and space launch programs and sells mainly to prime contractors. Revenue was USD 471,500 thousand in 2025, split 31.81%, 31.78% and 36.41% across hypersonics and strategic missile defense, space and launch, and tactical missiles and integrated defense systems, and the maritime end market opened in 2026 with the Seemann acquisition. The company has grown through nine acquisitions since formation, selecting engineered products with intellectual property that can be used across programs, and it leases all of its facilities, so growth needs capital spending and lease capacity while the programs it serves are long-lived and recurring. The credit linkage is positive for cash-flow visibility and negative for liquidity preservation, because contract assets, fixed-price work and debt-funded acquisitions absorb the cash that the visibility should produce.

### Revenue drivers and pricing power

Revenue is driven by production volumes on missile, interceptor and launch programs, by new content on existing programs and by acquisitions, with the company reporting second-quarter 2026 organic growth of 24.4% against total growth of 58.18%. Revenue is recurring and visible because programs are long-lived and requalification is costly, but it is concentrated in three customers at 51.5% of revenue, depends on U.S. government budgets and is partly fixed-price, so cost overruns and delays sit with the company and pass-through of tariff costs is not assured. Pricing power is therefore moderate: the company can defend its sole-source positions but cannot assume price increases, which supports stable gross margins but not margin expansion beyond operating leverage. For creditors this means revenue is durable in volume terms and exposed in timing and cost terms.

### Porter's five forces
