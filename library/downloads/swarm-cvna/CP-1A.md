## Findings
### Bottom line
Carvana's creditors are exposed to loan-sale market access and to related-party contracts as much as to vehicle demand, so funding access and control terms matter more than growth. Gains on loan sales supplied 64.1% of other sales and revenues in Q3 2026, and revenue from related parties supplied 21.9% of them in the nine months to September 2026.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_cvna-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 18–19; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cvna-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 70; calculation C05; calculation C07

The online used-car retailer is controlled by the Garcia Parties, Ernest Garcia II, Ernest Garcia III and their entities, who held about 83% of the voting power at the 2025 year-end, and a tax receivable agreement liability of USD 2.2 billion, USD 1.7 billion of it owed to related parties, accelerates on a change of control. Sponsor and governance risk is Medium: no dividend or debt-funded distribution is documented and the Garcia Parties committed up to USD 126 million of equity in 2023, but control, related-party dependence and missing ownership detail keep it above Low.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_cvna-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 48–50; 10-K_2025-12-31_cvna-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 55; 10-K_2025-12-31_cvna-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 62; 10-K_2025-12-31_cvna-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 129; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cvna-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 17

### Key findings
- Loan-sale funding is the dependency that matters most. Gains on loan sales were USD 381 million in Q3 2026, 64.1% of other sales and revenues, which carry a 100% gross margin, and other sales and revenues were 39.4% of gross profit in the nine months to September 2026. Pricing depends on investor demand and credit spreads, so wider spreads would cut gross profit directly. The gain earned per dollar of receivables sold fell from about 10.0% to about 7.4% over the year. Committed capacity is large but finite: the unused commitment of Ally, its committed forward-flow buyer, lasts 9.0 months at the Q3 pace against 9.8 months of remaining term, and USD 8.3 billion of fixed-pool capacity remains.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_cvna-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 18–19; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cvna-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 60; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cvna-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 70; 10-K_2025-12-31_cvna-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 43–44; calculation C04; calculation C07; calculation C10
- Control rests with one controlling group, and the contracts route services and cash to it. The Garcia Parties hold ten votes per Class B share while their as-exchanged Class A ownership stays at or above 25%, can elect every director and own substantially all of DriveTime. The charter waives their duty to avoid competing businesses. Controlled-company exemptions from board independence rules are available but not used, and holders of LLC units other than Carvana Co. own 34.9% of the operating group.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_cvna-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 51; 10-K_2025-12-31_cvna-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 55–56; 10-K_2025-12-31_cvna-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 58; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cvna-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 8; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cvna-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 30
- The tax receivable agreement is a debt-like claim that competes for cash and accelerates on a change of control. The liability was USD 2,228 million at 2025-12-31, equal to 43.6% of balance-sheet debt at that date, and USD 1,721 million of it was due to related parties. Payments of USD 37 million in the nine months to September 2026 were small, but a merger, breach or early termination makes the obligation immediately payable, and the Senior Secured Notes also carry a change-of-control offer at 101%.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_cvna-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 48–50; 10-K_2025-12-31_cvna-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 100–101; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cvna-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 17; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cvna-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 25; calculation C09
- DriveTime is an embedded related-party provider, and the dependence is rising. Vehicle service contract commissions on contracts administered by DriveTime were USD 349 million in the nine months to September 2026, and revenue from related parties rose from 17.4% of other sales and revenues in FY2024 to 20.0% in FY2025 and 21.9% in the nine months, when it equalled 8.6% of gross profit. DriveTime also administers the limited warranty, services receivables and leases reconditioning centres, and the annual report says these arrangements cannot be assumed to be arm's length.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view
The strongest evidence against a Medium level is the documented record. No dividend or debt-funded distribution appears in the sources, the Garcia Parties committed up to USD 126 million of equity in 2023, USD 539 million of public equity was raised in Q3 2025, the 2028 secured notes were redeemed early and the August 2026 loan ranks pari passu with no maintenance covenant. Related-party revenue was 8.6% of gross profit in the nine months to September 2026 and related-party payables are only USD 35 million. Loan-sale capacity is committed with four counterparties, Ally for up to USD 8.0 billion and three fixed-pool purchasers for up to USD 12.5 billion in total, and management reports eight consecutive quarters of retail unit growth of about 40%.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_cvna-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 62–63; 10-K_2025-12-31_cvna-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 129; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cvna-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 17; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cvna-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 18–19; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cvna-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 26; EX-99.1_2026-10-28_ex99_1q32026.htm.jsonl, page 1; calculation C08

The level would fall to Low if the proxy statement showed a board with a majority of independent directors and arm's-length pricing for the DriveTime contracts, and if the indentures limited restricted payments and affiliate transactions tightly. It would rise to High on a distribution, a new unrestricted-subsidiary designation of a core asset, a shortening of the loan-sale commitments, gain on loan sales falling well below the Q3 2026 level, or a sell-down that took the Garcia Parties below the 25% ownership test for ten-vote shares.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_cvna-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 55; 10-K_2025-12-31_cvna-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 58; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cvna-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 28; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cvna-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 18–19

### Transaction summary and new debt issues
The main new debt issue is the Term Loan B of USD 1.66 billion, entered on 2026-08-14 and fully drawn at closing at an issue price of 99.75%. It matures on 2033-08-14, amortises by USD 4 million a quarter from the March 2027 quarter, bears interest at Term SOFR plus 2.25% and ranks pari passu with the Senior Secured Notes on a first-priority lien over substantially all assets. It has no financial maintenance covenants, and a 1.00% premium applies to repricing prepayments until 2027-02-14.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_cvna-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 26–27

The loan proceeds and cash on hand redeemed the 2030 Senior Secured Notes of USD 1,660 million for USD 1,711 million, including a USD 50 million premium and USD 1 million of accrued interest, and Q3 2026 carries a loss on debt extinguishment of USD 53 million. The 2031 Senior Secured Notes of USD 2,269 million remain as the other first-priority instrument, and the refinancing replaced a 9% cash coupon with a loan whose effective rate is about 6.1%.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_cvna-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 24–27; calculation C12

Other financing events were smaller. A transportation fleet loan agreement of up to USD 250 million, signed on 2025-10-20, had USD 142 million outstanding at 2026-09-30 against USD 23 million at 2025-12-31. Ally raised its commitment to buy finance receivables to USD 8.0 billion on 2026-07-27, one fixed-pool agreement was increased by USD 500 million in Q3 2026, and one USD 600 million finance receivable facility was extended to October 2027 after the Q3 balance-sheet date. Two franchise dealerships were bought in 2026 for USD 40 million after five in 2025 for USD 160 million, and the Trade Floor Plan Facility that finances their new-vehicle inventory is now USD 300 million.
