## Findings

### Bottom line

Sponsor and governance risk is medium on the evidence supplied: Range has no sponsor and no documented creditor-adverse action, but the all-stock EQT merger signed after quarter end leaves post-merger control and creditor treatment unstated, so creditors cannot yet price the surviving obligor. Distributions are modest against operating cash flow, and the filings supplied document no debt-funded recapitalisation or liability-management exercise.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_rrc.htm.jsonl, pages 4, 18–19, 46; 10-K_2025-12-31_rrc.htm.jsonl, pages 109, 154; calculation C-05

The business is a single-basin gas producer: substantially all reserves and production are in the Marcellus Shale, reserves equal 22.23 years of FY2025 production, and fixed transportation and gathering commitments of USD 5,857.88 million at FY2025 year end exceed USD 794.00 million of debt principal at 2026-09-30, so volume and price shocks reach cash flow quickly.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_rrc.htm.jsonl, pages 14, 81, 92; 10-Q_2026-09-30_rrc.htm.jsonl, page 8; calculation C-01; calculation C-13; calculation C-07

### Key findings

Range has no sponsor or controlling shareholder in the supplied documents, so the extraction risk that dominates sponsor-owned credits is absent, but ownership percentages, the full board composition and independence, and related-party dealings are not established because the annual report takes them from a proxy statement that was not supplied. The company is a listed public company whose share count at 2026-10-23 equals shares issued less treasury shares at 2026-09-30, so the equity base is consistent across the filings.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_rrc.htm.jsonl, pages 75, 134, 154, 156–157; 10-Q_2026-09-30_rrc.htm.jsonl, pages 1, 3; calculation C-10

Documented financial policy is creditor-neutral to supportive. Shareholder returns in 9M 2026 were USD 105.48 million of buybacks and USD 70.83 million of dividends, USD 176.31 million together and 15.18% of operating cash flow of USD 1,161.75 million; in FY2025 they were USD 316.25 million, or 27.00% of operating cash flow of USD 1,171.32 million. The board raised the repurchase authorization to USD 1,500.00 million in February 2026 and USD 1,421.65 million remained at 2026-09-30, which is larger than the USD 794.00 million of debt principal outstanding, so a debt-funded acceleration is possible and the limit on it is not established because the credit agreement was not supplied.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_rrc.htm.jsonl, pages 4–5, 8, 35, 49; 10-K_2025-12-31_rrc.htm.jsonl, page 109; calculation C-04; calculation C-05; calculation C-14; calculation C-15; calculation C-16

The EQT merger moves control of Range's capital structure to the combined company: Range shareholders would own about 27% and EQT shareholders about 73% on a fully diluted basis, each Range share converts at a fixed exchange ratio, closing is expected in Q1 2027, and until then the agreement restricts Range's corporate transactions without EQT's consent. For creditors the open points are that the filing does not say whether Range survives as a subsidiary, whether the change-of-control repurchase right at 101% of principal applies to the notes, or whether the revolver is refinanced. Termination fees of USD 423 million payable by Range and USD 1,148 million payable by EQT make a failed deal costly for Range.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_rrc.htm.jsonl, pages 9, 18–19, 43, 46

Range is a concentrated producer with a large fixed cost base. Substantially all reserves and production are in the Marcellus Shale in Pennsylvania, product sales in 9M 2026 were 57.78% natural gas, 36.45% NGLs and 5.78% oil, and transportation, gathering, processing and compression was USD 990.08 million, or 54.86% of costs and expenses. Minimum transportation and gathering commitments of USD 5,857.88 million at FY2025 year end, which rose by about USD 1,500 million in 9M 2026, are a fixed element of that cost, so lower prices cannot be offset by cutting it quickly, and cash flow is sensitive to price and to the availability of third-party pipelines.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_rrc.htm.jsonl, pages 14, 57, 92; 10-Q_2026-09-30_rrc.htm.jsonl, pages 3, 17, 23; calculation C-03; calculation C-09; calculation C-13

A legacy obligation sits outside the debt measures: the 2020 sale of the North Louisiana assets left gathering, transportation and processing contracts that give no future benefit, with a balance of USD 241.48 million at 2026-09-30 and obligations that extend into 2030. Natural gas hedges run through 2029 and basis swaps through 2030, with shorter NGLs and oil hedges, and stabilise some cash flow; the net derivative position is USD 126.22 million, but hedges cover only part of output and their fair value is not cash.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_rrc.htm.jsonl, pages 10–11, 16; calculation C-08; calculation C-11

Disclosure supports monitoring of public financial statements but not of lender terms. The auditor, in place since 2003, gave an unqualified opinion on the FY2025 statements and an opinion that internal control was effective, and management concluded that disclosure controls were effective at 2026-09-30; compliance with the credit agreement and notes is stated without figures, and compliance certificates, basket usage and the credit agreement are not supplied.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_rrc.htm.jsonl, pages 103–107; 10-Q_2026-09-30_rrc.htm.jsonl, pages 8, 34, 41

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence for a lower risk level is behavioural: Range has no sponsor, no documented amendment, waiver or liability-management exercise, a record of retiring notes below par or at maturity and of calling the 2029 notes at a small premium, and distributions that are small against operating cash flow. The level would fall to low if the merger documents show that Range's debt is assumed, refinanced or left in place on terms at least as protective as today's. It would rise to high if the merger agreement or credit agreement allows debt-funded distributions or leaves Range's creditors behind a more heavily indebted parent, or if buybacks outran free cash flow and the revolver funded them.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_rrc.htm.jsonl, pages 4, 9, 18, 46, 49; 10-K_2025-12-31_rrc.htm.jsonl, pages 81, 131

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

The only transaction of scale is the EQT merger. On 2026-10-25 Range entered into an agreement to combine with EQT Corporation in an all-stock merger at a fixed exchange ratio, after which EQT shareholders would own about 73% and Range shareholders about 27% of the combined company on a fully diluted basis. Both boards approved the merger unanimously; it needs the approval of each company's shareholders, expiry or termination of the Hart-Scott-Rodino waiting period, effectiveness of EQT's registration statement and New York Stock Exchange listing of the new shares, and it is expected to close in Q1 2027. Either party may terminate if the merger has not closed within 12 months of the agreement, extended automatically to 18 months if only antitrust or legal-restraint conditions remain. In specified terminations Range would pay EQT USD 423 million and EQT would pay Range USD 1,148 million.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_rrc.htm.jsonl, pages 18–19, 42, 46

The filings supplied describe no new senior notes. The exhibit index in the annual report lists no indenture after the one dated February 2022 for the 4.75% senior notes due 2030. The new debt arrangements are the amended and restated revolving credit facility entered into in October 2025, which extends maturity to 2030-10-02, raises commitments from USD 1,500 million to USD 2,000 million and keeps a borrowing base of USD 3,000 million, and the January 2026 redemption of the 8.25% senior notes due 2029 at 101.375% of par using revolver borrowings. The filings state no cash consideration, debt assumption or refinancing in the merger.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_rrc.htm.jsonl, pages 131, 155; 10-Q_2026-09-30_rrc.htm.jsonl, pages 9, 18, 34
