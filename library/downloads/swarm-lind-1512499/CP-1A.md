## Findings

### Bottom line
Lindblad is a premium, deposit-funded expedition and land-travel business whose creditors depend on a National Geographic brand license that can end on a change of control, while ownership and board control are not established.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_lindb20251231c_10k.htm.jsonl, page 5; 10-K_2025-12-31_lindb20251231c_10k.htm.jsonl, page 16; 10-K_2025-12-31_lindb20251231c_10k.htm.jsonl, page 42; 10-K_2025-12-31_lindb20251231c_10k.htm.jsonl, page 93; Source readiness (CP-0)

Financial policy on the record is neutral to supportive: no dividends have been paid, the 2025 refinancing repaid the earlier notes in full and moved the maturity to 2030, and the preferred stock converted into common stock in February 2026. The leakage to watch is cash spent on acquisitions and on minority interests, which took 56.31% of free cash flow from FY2024 to September 2026, including USD 66.6 million for White Desert and Echo Charlie. A sale of the company, a lost or re-priced license, or a weak booking season would change the view.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_lindb20251231c_10k.htm.jsonl, page 66; 10-K_2025-12-31_lindb20251231c_10k.htm.jsonl, page 86; 10-K_2025-12-31_lindb20251231c_10k.htm.jsonl, page 117; 10-Q_2026-09-30_lindb20260930c_10q.htm.jsonl, page 12; 10-Q_2026-09-30_lindb20260930c_10q.htm.jsonl, page 19; calculation C08; calculation C10

### Key findings

The business is premium-priced and sold well ahead, which gives revenue visibility and a deposit float. Guests pay a deposit at booking and the balance 60 to 120 days before departure, and Lindblad segment occupancy rose from 78% in FY2024 to 88% in FY2025 and reached 92% over nine months of 2026, with net yield per available guest night up 14.10% in FY2025. The consequence for creditors is earnings that follow occupancy and price, with cash collected before the voyage that must be delivered afterwards.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_lindb20251231c_10k.htm.jsonl, page 16; 10-K_2025-12-31_lindb20251231c_10k.htm.jsonl, page 81; 10-K_2025-12-31_lindb20251231c_10k.htm.jsonl, page 82; EX-99.1_2026-11-03_ex_1021914.htm.jsonl, page 9; calculation C03

Concentration is in a brand and in permits, not in customers. National Geographic can end the brand license, which runs through 2040, on a change of control, on a failure to reach growth or capacity benchmarks by the end of 2032, or on product quality scores, and the royalty is a percentage of ticket revenues with minimums from 2026. The Galápagos vessels operate under permits held on 20-year terms that the government can end early for non-compliance. A lost license or permit would remove the demand channel and the capacity the cash flow relies on.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_lindb20251231c_10k.htm.jsonl, page 42; 10-K_2025-12-31_lindb20251231c_10k.htm.jsonl, page 54; 10-K_2025-12-31_lindb20251231c_10k.htm.jsonl, page 126

Two segments make the earnings, and the land business earns a similar margin on a smaller base. The Lindblad segment was 64.28% of FY2025 tour revenues and Land Experiences 35.72%, while the Adjusted EBITDA margin was 16.10% and 16.87%. The White Desert and Echo Charlie purchase sits in Land Experiences and adds Antarctic and aviation products, so creditors gain a second profit pool but also integration and provisional-valuation risk.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_lindb20251231c_10k.htm.jsonl, page 81; 10-K_2025-12-31_lindb20251231c_10k.htm.jsonl, page 133; 10-Q_2026-09-30_lindb20260930c_10q.htm.jsonl, page 18; 10-Q_2026-09-30_lindb20260930c_10q.htm.jsonl, page 19; calculation C01; calculation C02

Ownership and board control are not established, and that is the main governance gap. The annual report incorporates directors, pay and ownership by reference to a proxy statement that is not supplied; what is supplied is a staggered board, anti-takeover provisions, a cap on non-U.S. citizen ownership of 22%, and no dividend policy. No sponsor is identified and no holder above a threshold is named, so conduct is judged from the issuer's actions, not from who owns it.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_lindb20251231c_10k.htm.jsonl, page 58; 10-K_2025-12-31_lindb20251231c_10k.htm.jsonl, page 62; 10-K_2025-12-31_lindb20251231c_10k.htm.jsonl, page 66; 10-K_2025-12-31_lindb20251231c_10k.htm.jsonl, page 71; 10-K_2025-12-31_lindb20251231c_10k.htm.jsonl, page 93; Source readiness (CP-0)

Documented conduct is creditor-neutral to supportive but cash-hungry. The notes issued in 2025 repaid the 6.75% and 9.00% notes in full and cut the annual coupon cost by USD 1,800 thousand, though principal rose by USD 39,974 thousand to pay premiums and fees. The preferred stock, which ranked ahead of common equity, converted in February 2026. Against that, cash purchases of businesses and minority interests of USD 121,166 thousand since 2024 have used most of the free cash flow, and minority put options remain outstanding.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against caution is that the documented conduct is not extractive and the business is performing: occupancy and yield are at their highest in the periods supplied, no dividend has been paid and no stock was bought under the repurchase plan in 2025, the preferred stock is gone, and the notes are not due until September 2030. The view would turn more favourable if a proxy statement showed dispersed ownership, an independent board and no related-party fees, or if the debt documents showed a change-of-control put and tight limits on restricted payments. It would turn adverse if National Geographic amended or ended the license, if a related-party transaction larger than the USD 4.0 million Terra & Tu payment appeared, or if bookings slowed and deposits reversed while acquisition and put-option cash calls continued.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_lindb20251231c_10k.htm.jsonl, page 42; 10-K_2025-12-31_lindb20251231c_10k.htm.jsonl, page 66; 10-K_2025-12-31_lindb20251231c_10k.htm.jsonl, page 87; 10-Q_2026-09-30_lindb20260930c_10q.htm.jsonl, page 20; 10-Q_2026-09-30_lindb20260930c_10q.htm.jsonl, page 39; EX-99.1_2026-11-03_ex_1021914.htm.jsonl, page 3; EX-99.1_2026-11-03_ex_1021914.htm.jsonl, page 9
