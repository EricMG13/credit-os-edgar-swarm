## Findings

### Bottom line

Fundamental credit quality is adequate but leveraged: recurring leased-machine and iGaming earnings and liquidity of USD 1,059 million support creditors, while unadjusted net leverage of 4.24x and USD 2,568 million of principal due in 2029 constrain them.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); Business & governance (CP-1A); EX-99.1_2026-11-04_lnw09302026erandtables.htm.jsonl, page 18; 10-K_2025-12-31_lnw-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 165; calculation CALC-5

Net leverage is 3.3x on the issuer's adjusted measure and falling, but add-backs of USD 355 million, a shrinking social casino business, legal cash costs and discretionary repurchases could stall deleveraging. The primary downside path is a casino capital-spending pause that cuts machine sales while social casino keeps shrinking, ahead of the 2029 refinancing.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); Business & governance (CP-1A); EX-99.1_2026-11-04_lnw09302026erandtables.htm.jsonl, pages 5, 18; calculation CALC-5; calculation CALC-14

### Key findings

- The business profile is adequate: recurring and leased revenue anchors cash flow, but one segment carries most of it. Recurring or leased revenue was at least 63.73% of nine-month revenue, gaming operations revenue rose 17.63% and the North American premium installed base grew for a 25th consecutive quarter, and Gaming provided 74.33% of segment adjusted EBITDA. Leased units earn daily fees through the casino capital cycle, so they support debt service when machine sales fall, but group cash flow still depends on one segment and its customers' spending.
Basis: Business & governance (CP-1A); EX-99.1_2026-11-04_lnw09302026erandtables.htm.jsonl, page 1; 10-Q_2026-09-30_lnw-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 14–17

- Earnings are growing faster than revenue, so margin and mix, not volume, carry the credit. Revenue for the twelve months to September 30, 2026 was 0.48% above FY2025 and Q3 revenue fell 2.02%, while adjusted EBITDA rose 3.81% to USD 1,498 million at a 44.98% margin, services revenue rose 5.90% and products revenue fell 11.51% in nine months. Selling, general and administrative costs fell to 26.22% of FY2025 revenue from 27.35%, but flat revenue leaves less room for margin gains to keep funding deleveraging.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); 10-K_2025-12-31_lnw-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 139; 10-Q_2026-09-30_lnw-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 9–10; EX-99.1_2026-11-04_lnw09302026erandtables.htm.jsonl, page 18; calculation CALC-6; calculation CALC-7; calculation CALC-8; calculation CALC-9

- Leverage is moderate on the issuer's measure and high on unadjusted earnings, so creditors should size the risk on the higher figure. Net debt was 4.24x unadjusted EBITDA and 3.3x on the issuer's adjusted measure at September 30, 2026, against 4.60x unadjusted at the end of 2025, and add-backs of USD 355 million are 23.70% of adjusted EBITDA. Legal and related costs inside the add-backs were USD 79 million in 2024, USD 130 million in 2025 and USD 179 million over the last twelve months, so they recur.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); EX-99.1_2026-11-04_lnw09302026erandtables.htm.jsonl, page 18; calculation CALC-5

- Cash flow covers scheduled debt service with room, and it does so after legal settlements. Operating cash flow less capital expenditures was USD 596 million over the last twelve months against scheduled 2026 principal and interest of USD 369 million, 1.62x, and USD 369 million in the first nine months of 2026 against USD 257 million a year earlier, after USD 137 million of settlements paid in the first quarter. Shareholder returns of USD 193 million in the nine months compete for the same cash, and capital spending of USD 339 million over twelve months absorbs part of operating cash flow before any debt repayment.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_lnw-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 126; 10-Q_2026-09-30_lnw-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 11; EX-99.1_2026-11-04_lnw09302026erandtables.htm.jsonl, page 18; Financial foundation (CP-1); calculation CALC-1; calculation CALC-2; calculation CALC-3; calculation CALC-4

- Liquidity is strong, and the refinancing wall is the real test. Liquidity of USD 1,059 million is 1.23x the USD 859 million of principal due before 2029 on the year-end ladder, and revolver borrowings of USD 70 million sit far below the USD 300 million at which the revolver's 4.50x first lien test applies. Business & governance reads the maintenance test as inactive while the revolver is undrawn; that holds for the revolver only, because the Term Loan A carries the same test every quarter, and the issuer states compliance without printing a covenant figure. USD 2,568 million, 49.32% of principal, falls due in 2029, 59.06% of principal is variable rate, swaps of USD 700 million end in April 2027, and the issuer puts a 1% rate move at USD 30 million of interest before swaps. The 2033 notes show market access at a lower coupon, but no agency rating or market price was supplied.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); 10-K_2025-12-31_lnw-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 60, 165, 167, 169; 10-Q_2026-09-30_lnw-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 21, 22, 58; calculation CALC-13; calculation CALC-12; calculation CALC-14; calculation CALC-11

- Financial policy is the swing factor, and it has turned toward debt reduction for now. Business & governance rates sponsor and governance risk Medium: no sponsor or controlling holder is identified and no dividend has been paid, but repurchases were USD 1,495 million from 2024 to September 30, 2026. None were made in Q3 2026, USD 180 million of program capacity remains and the issuer aims for net debt leverage below 3.0x in the first half of 2027; the pause is a board choice and not a contractual limit.
Basis: Business & governance (CP-1A); 10-Q_2026-09-30_lnw-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 59; EX-99.1_2026-11-04_lnw09302026erandtables.htm.jsonl, pages 4, 5

- Social casino, legal exposure and cyclical sales are the main earnings risks. SciPlay revenue fell 9.68% in nine months with fewer monthly active users and a lower payer conversion rate than a year earlier, gaming machine sales and systems fell 8.18% and 18.27% and together were 29.06% of revenue, and the issuer expects full-year adjusted EBITDA growth toward the lower end of a mid-to-high single digit range because of social casino weakness and delayed customer investment. Gaming machine sales rose 5.29% in the third quarter, three class actions are open and the UK online gambling duty rose to 40% from April 1, 2026.
Basis: Business & governance (CP-1A); 10-Q_2026-09-30_lnw-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 14, 51; EX-99.1_2026-11-04_lnw09302026erandtables.htm.jsonl, page 5; 10-K_2025-12-31_lnw-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 26; calculation CALC-18

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest case for a better view is deleveraging momentum and earnings quality: principal fell USD 188 million since year-end, the issuer's net debt leverage fell from 3.5x to 3.3x with a target below 3.0x in the first half of 2027, adjusted EBITDA rose 3.81%, the revolver is almost undrawn, and the 2033 notes refinanced at a lower coupon. That case wins if legal costs fall back toward the USD 79 million of 2024, social casino stabilises, the buyback pause holds and the 2029 maturities are addressed well before the Term Loan B nears maturity. The view would turn more cautious if revenue fell again after Q3, if revolver borrowings approached USD 300 million, if repurchases resumed before the target is met, or if a debt-funded acquisition followed Grover.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); Business & governance (CP-1A); EX-99.1_2026-11-04_lnw09302026erandtables.htm.jsonl, pages 1, 5, 18; 10-Q_2026-09-30_lnw-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 21; calculation CALC-17; calculation CALC-7; calculation CALC-12

### Limitations

- The analysis of earnings changes and the peer comparison were not supplied, so margin durability rests on one year of reported segment margins and relative strength against sector peers is not established.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_lnw-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 15–17; 10-K_2025-12-31_lnw-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 139

- No agency rating, bond price or loan trading level was supplied, so the view that refinancing capacity is adequate rests on the 2025 issuance and not on current market pricing.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_lnw-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 167; EX-99.1_2026-11-04_lnw09302026erandtables.htm.jsonl, page 11

- The downside sensitivity is an illustrative 10% fall in adjusted EBITDA chosen by the analyst, with the whole shortfall taken to cash flow; it shows direction and not a forecast of stressed leverage.
Basis: calculation CALC-15; EX-99.1_2026-11-04_lnw09302026erandtables.htm.jsonl, page 18

### Company description

Light & Wonder is a cross-platform games company with three segments: Gaming, which sells and leases machines, systems and table products and includes the Grover charitable gaming business; SciPlay, a social casino and casual games business; and iGaming, which supplies content and platforms to online operators. Revenue was USD 3,314 million in FY2025 and customers outside the United States gave 31.50% of it, which ties cash flow to casino capital budgets, consumer spending on mobile platforms and online gambling rules in several jurisdictions. Recurring or leased revenue supports visibility, while sales of machines and systems add cyclicality, so the model supports debt service capacity but not a steady growth path.

### Ownership and group structure

The company is publicly traded as depositary interests on the ASX since November 2025 and quoted over the counter in the United States; no sponsor or controlling holder is named, and holder percentages and board composition sit in a proxy statement that was not supplied. All reported debt sits in a wholly owned subsidiary, Light and Wonder International, Inc., guaranteed by the parent and by wholly owned domestic subsidiaries and secured on first-priority liens for the credit facilities, while the notes are senior unsecured and structurally behind the liabilities of non-guarantor subsidiaries, whose size is not disclosed. Without a sponsor, leverage tolerance is judged from actions: repurchases since 2024, a debt-funded acquisition and a pause in repurchases in the third quarter of 2026.

### Revenue drivers and pricing power

Revenue is driven by leased machine volumes and daily fees, new machine shipments, social casino payer numbers and iGaming wagers. Revenue is therefore recurring and visible in the leased and iGaming lines, cyclical in machine and systems sales, and structurally declining in social casino so far; pricing power is shown only by a higher average sales price per new unit in Q3 2026, and no customer concentration is disclosed.

### Porter's Five Forces, credit-translated

Rivalry is intense in every segment: the issuer names Aristocrat, IGT and Konami in machines and systems, Playtika and others in social casino and Evolution, Pragmatic Play and Playtech in iGaming, so share losses in a segment hit pricing and the margin that supports debt service. New entrants are a threat mainly in social casino, which the issuer calls a business with low barriers to entry, so SciPlay's margin is the least protected. Substitutes are other forms of play and leisure that compete for player time, which pressures social casino user numbers.

Supplier power sits with app store platforms, whose payment processing fees sit in cost of services, and with Asian component suppliers exposed to tariffs; the direct-to-consumer route reached 28.84% of SciPlay revenue against 17.20% a year earlier, which reduces platform dependence and supports the segment margin.

### Downside scenario

A realistic downside starts with casino operators delaying capital spending, which the issuer already cites, while social casino keeps shrinking at the nine-month pace. Gaming machine sales and systems, 29.06% of revenue, fall first, and fixed costs turn that into a faster fall in adjusted EBITDA; a 10% fall in adjusted EBITDA would lift the issuer's leverage ratio to 3.62x and unadjusted net leverage to 4.88x (calculation CALC-15). ### Overall credit view

Overall, Light & Wonder presents an adequate business profile supported by recurring leased and iGaming earnings, large liquidity and a stated priority on deleveraging, but credit quality is constrained by leverage that is high on unadjusted earnings, a concentrated 2029 maturity wall and discretionary shareholder returns. Cash-flow durability appears adequate: operating cash flow less capital expenditures covers scheduled debt service 1.62x after legal settlements. Refinancing capacity is likely to depend on reaching the leverage target before the 2028 and 2029 maturities and on market conditions when the swaps end. The primary downside path is a casino capital-spending pause with continued social casino decline, followed by weaker earnings against a concentrated maturity wall. Further analysis would require the executed debt agreements, the proxy statement, guarantor information, agency ratings, market pricing and a peer comparison. Top risk: refinancing the 2029 maturities if deleveraging stalls.
