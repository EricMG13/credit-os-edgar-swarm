## Findings

### Bottom line

The eight filings and releases support a credit snapshot of Phinia with limits, but not a test of covenant headroom, because the executed financing documents were not supplied. Statements, the debt note and liquidity run through 30 September 2026; governance, rating-agency and market evidence are absent.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_phin-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 73–74; 10-Q_2026-09-30_phin-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 2–3; 10-Q_2026-09-30_phin-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 14–15; 10-K_2025-12-31_phin-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 127–128

Liquidity of USD 813 million at 30 September 2026 was struck before the USD 136 million stoba purchase price was paid on 1 October 2026, and no source restates it after closing.
