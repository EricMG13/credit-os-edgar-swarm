## Findings

### Bottom line

Creditor risk comes from the business and its capital returns, not from a sponsor: Molina is a listed pure-play government managed-care operator with no controlling holder disclosed, and we assign medium sponsor and governance risk.
Medicaid is 74.89% of premium revenue, three states each supply about 10% or more, and the state contracts run three to five years and can be ended by either side, so revenue visibility is moderate. Membership has fallen 11.46% since year-end.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_moh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 8; 10-K_2025-12-31_moh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 14; 10-K_2025-12-31_moh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 121; 10-Q_2026-09-30_moh-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 31; calculation C1; calculation C4

The parent bought back USD 1,000 million of stock in each of 2024 and 2025, equal to 211.86% of FY2025 net income, while borrowing USD 1,100 million in the first nine months of 2025. Nothing was bought in 2026, but USD 500 million of authority runs to 2026-12-31, so a resumption before earnings recover is the main creditor-adverse event to watch.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_moh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 135; 10-Q_2026-09-30_moh-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 40; 10-Q_2026-09-30_moh-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 43; calculation C6; calculation C7

### Key findings

There is no sponsor to model, and that cuts both ways. Fourteen registered holders of record (including the depository nominee) hold the common stock, which is listed on the New York Stock Exchange, no cash dividend has ever been paid, and the supplied documents disclose no controlling holder, fee arrangement or related-party payment, so extraction by an owner is not evidenced. The same absence means no sponsor equity stands behind the notes: the parent's own USD 608 million of cash and investments at 2026-09-30 is the only identified buffer, and ownership percentages and board independence are held in a proxy statement that was not supplied.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_moh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 79; 10-K_2025-12-31_moh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 149; 10-Q_2026-09-30_moh-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 36; 10-Q_2026-09-30_moh-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 1

Revenue is concentrated in one program and four states, and the contract model gives it little protection. Medicaid was USD 32,240 million of FY2025 premium revenue, and the Texas, California, Washington and New York Medicaid contracts together were USD 17,320 million, or 40.23% of all premium revenue. State contracts run three to five years, can be terminated by either party with or without cause, and are rebid; the Virginia contracts ended on 2025-06-30 after Molina was not an awardee. A loss of any large state contract would remove revenue that fixed costs and USD 3,771 million of parent debt service rely on.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_moh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 8; 10-K_2025-12-31_moh-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 14–16; 10-K_2025-12-31_moh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 12; 10-K_2025-12-31_moh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 121; calculation C3

The operating model turns on one ratio the company does not set. Premiums are fixed per member by each state and must be actuarially sound, while the company bears medical cost risk, so cost trend above rate updates lifts the medical care ratio: 92.1% for the nine months of 2026 against 90.8% a year earlier. The reported ratio flatters the trend, because favourable reserve releases of USD 352 million helped it; on current-year costs alone the ratio was 93.22% against 91.15%. With last-twelve-months premium revenue of USD 41,588 million, each percentage point of the ratio is worth USD 415.88 million of earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortization.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_moh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 16; 10-Q_2026-09-30_moh-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 14; 10-Q_2026-09-30_moh-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 24; calculation C5; calculation C12

Membership and revenue are contracting while new contracts are still to arrive. Total membership at 2026-09-30 was 13.61% below the same date a year earlier, with Marketplace down 59.24% since year-end through a deliberate repricing and Medicaid down 4.29%. Management expects the 2025 federal budget law to cut Medicaid premium growth by 2% to 3% a year through 2029 and a further USD 1 billion of Marketplace premium in 2027. New Florida, Illinois and Wisconsin awards start between 2026-10-01 and 2027-01-01 and the Georgia and Texas awards slipped to late 2027 or 2028, so the timing of replacement revenue is uncertain.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_moh-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 28–31; calculation C4

Capital allocation favoured buybacks in a year of heavy borrowing and falling earnings, and has since reversed. Share purchases of USD 500 million in the first quarter of 2025 came at an average of USD 297.83 a share and USD 500 million in the third quarter at USD 175.50, in a year when the company also borrowed USD 650 million in the first quarter and USD 1,100 million over the first nine months; the filings do not link the two. Acquisitions were modest by comparison: ConnectiCare for USD 350 million of cash. In FY2025 the parent's buybacks (USD 1,000 million), the acquisition (USD 350 million) and subsidiary contributions (USD 439 million) came to USD 1,789 million against USD 1,823 million of subsidiary dividends and net note proceeds, which left little over for its other outflows, and its cash and investments ended at USD 223 million. In 2026 the company stopped repurchasing, which is creditor-aligned, but an amendment on 2026-02-04 cut the minimum interest cover test to 1.75x for the rest of 2026 for reasons the filings do not give.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_moh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 12; 10-K_2025-12-31_moh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 123; 10-K_2025-12-31_moh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 132; 10-K_2025-12-31_moh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 135; 10-K_2025-12-31_moh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 143; 10-Q_2026-03-31_moh-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-Q_2026-09-30_moh-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 4; calculation C5; calculation C7; calculation C8

Disclosure is adequate for monitoring but incomplete for covenant work. The annual accounts carry an unqualified audit opinion and a critical audit matter on the reserve for claims incurred but not paid, which was USD 3,211 million of USD 4,887 million of medical claims payable; management reported effective internal controls with the ConnectiCare business excluded from the assessment. Covenant ratios are stated as met but never printed, and the 2025 securities class action and the related shareholder derivative suit were both dismissed with prejudice in October 2026 with no loss estimate recorded.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_moh-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 103–104; 10-K_2025-12-31_moh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 140; 10-K_2025-12-31_moh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 146; 10-Q_2026-09-30_moh-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 18–19; 10-Q_2026-09-30_moh-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 15

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a cautious view is that nothing here shows value moved to owners at creditors' expense beyond buybacks, and that the buybacks have stopped. Regulated subsidiaries paid USD 985 million of dividends to the parent in FY2025 out of cash above their capital needs, parent cash and investments rose to USD 608 million, the notes were termed out to 2031, and the revolver is undrawn. Management also says it has won 90% of Medicaid re-procurements and 80% of new contracts since 2019, which is its own characterisation and cannot be tested here. The view would improve if buybacks stay suspended after the authority lapses on 2026-12-31 and the medical care ratio falls back toward its FY2025 level of 91.7%. It would worsen if purchases resume while the ratio is above its FY2025 level, if a large-state contract is lost, or if the proxy statement shows a holder with control and a distribution appetite.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_moh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 10; 10-K_2025-12-31_moh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 13; 10-Q_2026-09-30_moh-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 36; 10-Q_2026-09-30_moh-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 40; 10-Q_2026-09-30_moh-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 24

### Limitations

- The proxy statement is not supplied, so holder percentages, board independence and related-party dealings are not established; the finding that no controlling holder exists would change if a large holder appears there.
  Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_moh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 149
- The credit agreement and indentures are not supplied, so legal capacity for buybacks and other restricted payments is not established; the judgement that buybacks are a mixed signal rests on conduct, not on what the documents permit.
  Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_moh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 132
- The premium figure for the new Florida award is hard to reconcile with its stated enrolment and with the full-year guidance, so any growth view that relies on that award is bounded by management's own description.
  Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_moh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 81; EX-99.1_2026-10-21_moh3q26_er.htm.jsonl, page 2; calculation C9
- Earlier acquisitions are described only by cash paid, so no pattern of acquisition behaviour or integration record is established beyond the single ConnectiCare transaction.
  Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_moh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 92

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

There is no sponsor transaction. The new debt in the period is the November 2025 refinancing: the company sold USD 850 million of senior unsecured notes due 2031 in a private offering on 2025-11-20, used the proceeds to repay USD 740 million of term loan debt and for general corporate purposes, and replaced its revolving facility with a USD 1.25 billion facility ending 2030-11-20 under a new credit agreement whose terms the company calls substantially similar except for covenants amended in its favour.
The credit agreement was amended on 2026-02-04 to lower the minimum interest cover test temporarily, and no further debt was issued in 2026. The only acquisition in the period was ConnectiCare, bought for USD 350 million of cash and closed on 2025-02-01.
