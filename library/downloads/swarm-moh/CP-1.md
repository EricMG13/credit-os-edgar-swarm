## Request

Financial foundation for MOLINA HEALTHCARE, INC. (issuer id moh), as of 2026-11-05, on the credit snapshot pathway. The scope is the consolidated statements in US dollars for FY2023 to FY2025 and the nine months to 2026-09-30, the debt, cash and liquidity tables, and the leverage and cover ratios built from them, with the parent-only statements used for the perimeter of the senior notes. Where the filings print no EBITDA, it is built from operating income and depreciation and amortization and labelled as calculated.

## Findings

### Bottom line

Earnings, far more than debt, drive the credit risk: last-twelve-months EBITDA of USD 333 million against USD 3,771 million of parent senior notes is reported leverage of 11.32x, up from 1.54x at FY2024, so creditors rely on cash and distant maturities.
The group holds net cash on its balance sheet (cash of USD 4,479 million against debt of USD 3,771 million), but the notes sit at the parent, which held only about USD 608 million, while USD 6,129 million of claims and government settlements are owed out of group cash and investments.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_moh-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 2–4; 10-Q_2026-09-30_moh-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 36; 10-K_2025-12-31_moh-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 105–106; calculation C3; calculation C13; calculation C17; calculation C18

No note matures before June 2028 and the USD 1,250 million revolver is undrawn, so the near-term question is whether earnings recover, not refinancing; interest cover on the covenant definition cannot be tested because the filings do not give it.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_moh-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 14; 10-Q_2026-09-30_moh-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 15; 10-Q_2026-09-30_moh-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 40; 10-K_2025-12-31_moh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 130

### Key findings

Reported leverage has risen mainly because earnings fell, with new debt a smaller cause. EBITDA, which the issuer does not print and which is built here as operating income plus depreciation and amortization, fell from USD 1,893 million in FY2024 to USD 976 million in FY2025 and to USD 333 million over the last twelve months. Debt rose from USD 2,923 million at FY2024 to USD 3,771 million at 2026-09-30, USD 848 million more after the senior notes due 2031 were issued in November 2025, but holding debt at its FY2024 level would still give 8.78x on the latest EBITDA, so most of the rise to 11.32x comes from the denominator.
Basis: calculation C1; calculation C3; calculation C33; 10-K_2025-12-31_moh-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 105–106; 10-K_2025-12-31_moh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 131; 10-Q_2026-09-30_moh-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-09-30_moh-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 14

Revenue has not fallen far (USD 40,650 million in FY2024, USD 45,426 million in FY2025 and USD 44,127 million over the last twelve months), so the damage is margin. The medical care ratio, medical costs as a share of premium revenue, was 92.1% for the nine months of 2026 against 90.8% a year earlier and 89.1% in FY2024. Because EBITDA is a thin residual of USD 41,588 million of last-twelve-months premium revenue, an increase of 0.80 percentage points in that ratio with premiums unchanged would remove all of it, and each percentage point is worth USD 415.88 million (an analyst sensitivity, not an issuer figure). Creditors therefore carry one cost-trend ratio that state rate-setting, not the company, largely controls.
Basis: calculation C2; calculation C10; calculation C25; 10-Q_2026-09-30_moh-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 24; 10-K_2025-12-31_moh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 81; EX-99.1_2026-10-21_moh3q26_er.htm.jsonl, page 1

The group reports net cash, but the claim noteholders hold is on the parent. Cash and cash equivalents of USD 4,479 million exceed the USD 3,771 million of debt by USD 708 million. The notes are obligations of the parent corporation, which held about USD 608 million of cash and investments at 2026-09-30 (USD 223 million at 2025-12-31), so parent debt exceeded parent liquid holdings by USD 3,163 million. The rest of the cash sits in regulated health plans that must hold minimum capital of about USD 3.0 billion and may pay only about USD 170 million of dividends without regulatory approval.
Basis: calculation C13; calculation C17; 10-Q_2026-09-30_moh-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 36; 10-Q_2026-09-30_moh-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 39; 10-K_2025-12-31_moh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 142

Much of the cash is working-capital float rather than free liquidity. Medical claims payable of USD 4,988 million and amounts due to government agencies of USD 1,141 million, together USD 6,129 million, are non-interest-bearing obligations that premium cash is held to meet. Against unrestricted cash and investments of USD 8,579 million they leave USD 2,450 million, compared with USD 2,473 million at FY2024 and USD 2,043 million at FY2025. Membership fell 11.46% over the nine months while claims payable rose, so cash would leave to settle older claims if claims payable fell in step with enrolment.
Basis: calculation C18; calculation C19; calculation C28; 10-Q_2026-09-30_moh-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-09-30_moh-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 31; 10-K_2025-12-31_moh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 105

Interest is serviced by the parent from cash passed up by regulated subsidiaries, and that support has thinned. In FY2025 the parent received USD 985 million of dividends and contributed USD 439 million, a net USD 546 million, which was 2.84x its interest expense of USD 192 million; for the nine months of 2026 the net was USD 199 million against USD 161 million of interest, or 1.24x. The two figures are not defined identically (the quarterly report counts dividends and returns of capital) and dividends are lumpy within a year, so the comparison shows direction, not a precise change.
Basis: calculation C20; 10-K_2025-12-31_moh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 143; 10-Q_2026-09-30_moh-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 35

Cash generation improved in 2026 for reasons other than earnings. Operating cash flow was USD 629 million for the nine months against an outflow of USD 237 million a year earlier, yet net income fell to USD 112 million from USD 632 million: working-capital movements turned from an outflow of USD 1,082 million to an inflow of USD 297 million, and the issuer attributes this mainly to smaller settlements with government agencies (USD 185 million paid against USD 467 million). The inflow came early: operating cash flow was USD 1,082 million in the first quarter, then an outflow of USD 294 million in the second and USD 159 million in the third, and cash and cash equivalents fell from USD 5,314 million at 2026-03-31 to USD 4,479 million at 2026-09-30. FY2025 operating cash flow was an outflow of USD 535 million (working capital an outflow of USD 1,289 million) and free cash flow after USD 101 million of capital spending an outflow of USD 636 million. Financing outflows of USD 31 million in 2026 were only stock withheld for taxes and other items, so no shares were repurchased, whereas USD 1,000 million was bought in the first nine months of 2025, which removes the biggest discretionary outflow of 2025.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_moh-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 3–4; 10-Q_2026-09-30_moh-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 38; 10-K_2025-12-31_moh-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 106–107; EX-99.1_2026-04-22_moh1q26_er.htm.jsonl, page 5; EX-99.1_2026-07-22_moh2q26_er.htm.jsonl, page 2; calculation C7; calculation C9; calculation C31; calculation C32

All debt is five senior unsecured notes of the parent, with USD 3,800 million of principal and USD 3,771 million of carrying value. Principal falls due as USD 800 million in 2028, USD 650 million in 2030, USD 850 million in 2031 and USD 750 million in each of 2032 and 2033, so nothing is due in 2026 or 2027; the USD 1,250 million revolver is undrawn and ends on 2030-11-20. Finance lease liabilities of USD 179 million are carried outside debt and are excluded from every ratio; adding the USD 209 million of finance leases at FY2025 would lift leverage to 4.07x from 3.86x. No pension, retiree-medical, factoring or supplier-finance obligation is described, and management states that its other agreements hold no material non-cancelable commitments.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest contrary evidence is that the weakest quarter is about to leave the last-twelve-months window. EBITDA for the fourth quarter of 2025, calculated as FY2025 less the nine months, was an outflow of USD 118 million against USD 122 million, USD 185 million and USD 144 million in the first three quarters of 2026, and 2026 guidance implies fourth-quarter 2026 net income of roughly USD 10 million at its minimum against a loss of USD 160 million a year earlier, so last-twelve-months EBITDA should rise at the next report even though the new Florida contract is guided to cost USD 1.50 per share in that quarter. The USD 93 million impairment in the first quarter also depresses the period: before it, last-twelve-months EBITDA would be USD 426 million and EBITDA over accrued interest 2.00x. No note matures before 2028, the USD 1,250 million revolver is undrawn, the securities class action was dismissed with prejudice on 2026-10-20, and the issuer states that every covenant was met at 2026-09-30.
Basis: calculation C26; calculation C27; calculation C30; EX-99.1_2026-04-22_moh1q26_er.htm.jsonl, page 5; EX-99.1_2026-07-22_moh2q26_er.htm.jsonl, page 5; EX-99.1_2026-10-21_moh3q26_er.htm.jsonl, page 2; EX-99.1_2026-10-21_moh3q26_er.htm.jsonl, page 9; 10-Q_2026-09-30_moh-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 18–19; 10-Q_2026-09-30_moh-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 15; 10-Q_2026-09-30_moh-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 9

The view would improve if the medical care ratio falls back toward the FY2025 level, if parent cash and net upstream cash keep rising from the USD 608 million and USD 199 million now reported, or if the credit agreement shows that its interest cover definition adds back items that leave a wide margin over the 1.75x minimum that applies through the end of 2026. It would worsen if the ratio keeps rising, if the float of claims and government settlements unwinds faster than cash is replenished, or if parent cash falls while dividends above about USD 170 million need regulatory approval. EBITDA over accrued interest of 1.56x is not the covenant measure and must not be read as headroom.
Basis: calculation C5; calculation C12; calculation C18; 10-Q_2026-09-30_moh-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 15; 10-Q_2026-09-30_moh-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 39; 10-K_2025-12-31_moh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 132

### Limitations

EBITDA is constructed from operating income and depreciation and amortization because the issuer prints none; it includes investment income earned on premium cash, carries the first-quarter impairment, and will differ from any EBITDA defined in the credit agreement.
Basis: calculation C1; 10-Q_2026-09-30_moh-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 2; EX-99.1_2026-10-21_moh3q26_er.htm.jsonl, page 8

Cash interest for the nine months of 2026 and 2025 is not printed, so interest cover on cash paid exists for FY2024 and FY2025 only; the latest-period cover shown uses accrued interest expense.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_moh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 107; 10-Q_2026-09-30_moh-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 4

The balance sheet is stated at three dates, the credit agreement and indentures are not supplied, and lease liabilities for the latest date are partly unprinted, so debt-like claims outside the notes are incomplete.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_moh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 127; 10-Q_2026-09-30_moh-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 2

Parent-only statements are annual, so the net cash passed up to the parent for 2026 rests on a quarterly report description that is defined differently from the annual figure.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_moh-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 142–143; 10-Q_2026-09-30_moh-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 35
