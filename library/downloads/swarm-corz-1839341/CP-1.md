## Findings

### Bottom line

Leverage and interest cover cannot be measured on trailing earnings: reported EBITDA is negative for the twelve months to 2026-09-30, while USD 4,302,175 thousand of carrying-value debt, most of it issued in 2026, rests on contracted colocation capacity still being delivered.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_corz.htm.jsonl, page 5; 10-Q_2026-09-30_corz.htm.jsonl, pages 21–22; calculation K19; calculation K43

The latest quarter shows the ramp: Adjusted EBITDA of USD 133,568 thousand on revenue of USD 232,866 thousand covers the coupon interest of USD 67,388 thousand incurred in the quarter 1.98x, but the issuer added add-backs to Adjusted EBITDA during 2026, so no trailing figure is comparable. The first coupon on the 2031 secured notes fell due on 2026-11-15, after the last document supplied.

### Credit analysis

Net debt rose by USD 2,749,287 thousand in nine months, from USD 748,947 thousand at 2025-12-31 to USD 3,498,234 thousand at 2026-09-30, because the 7.75% secured notes added USD 3,300,000 thousand of principal in May 2026 while the USD 1,000,000 thousand term loan drawn in March was repaid from the proceeds. The secured notes are three quarters of contractual principal, while at the end of 2025 all of the debt was convertible notes, so the debt now rests mainly on the completion and leasing of the data centres the secured notes fund.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_corz.htm.jsonl, page 5; 10-Q_2026-09-30_corz.htm.jsonl, pages 21–24; 10-K_2025-12-31_corz.htm.jsonl, page 99; calculation K44; calculation K2; calculation K43

Reported EBITDA, which the issuer does not print and which is derived here as operating income plus depreciation and amortization, is negative for FY2024, FY2025 and the twelve months to 2026-09-30, so total debt and net debt multiples are not calculable and the interest cover is negative. On the issuer's own Adjusted EBITDA, FY2024 shows total debt of 6.93x and net debt of 1.61x, but FY2025 is negative and the twelve months to 2026-09-30 are not comparable because the annual figure uses the earlier definition and the nine-month figures the wider one. Against that, contracted operating lease payments expected to be received total USD 7,484,422 thousand, before the leases that have not yet commenced.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_corz.htm.jsonl, page 21; calculation K19; calculation K27; calculation K28; calculation K12; calculation K11

Cash interest paid of USD 19,229 thousand over the twelve months to 2026-09-30 predates the first coupon on the secured notes, so it understates the burden: the annual coupons on the secured notes and the 2029 convertible notes are USD 269,550 thousand. Against the quarter's incurred coupon interest, Adjusted EBITDA gives 1.98x and reported EBITDA gives 1.34x; both are quarterly figures and are not annualised.
Basis: calculation K20; calculation K37; calculation K29; calculation K30; 10-Q_2026-09-30_corz.htm.jsonl, page 22

Operating cash flow of USD 296,243 thousand for the nine months is not a clean measure of earnings power, because it includes USD 286,765 thousand of proceeds from selling bitcoin earned by self-mining, and the stock of bitcoin is now almost exhausted. Purchases of property, plant and equipment of USD 1,494,979 thousand alone exceeded operating cash flow, leaving free cash flow of negative USD 1,198,736 thousand before USD 676,881 thousand of land and power acquisitions, so the build-out is funded by the new debt rather than by operations.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_corz.htm.jsonl, page 7; 10-Q_2026-09-30_corz.htm.jsonl, page 5; calculation K34; calculation K33

Customer prepayments are a non-debt funding float: deferred revenue stood at USD 573,571 thousand at 2026-09-30 against USD 555,851 thousand at 2025-12-31. It funds construction and unwinds as capacity is delivered, so it is an obligation to deliver rather than free cash; the documents read do not state refund terms if delivery slips.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_corz.htm.jsonl, pages 66–67; calculation K39; calculation K38; calculation K40

Liquidity is cash of USD 803,941 thousand plus an undrawn USD 100,000 thousand revolver, against restricted cash of USD 837,603 thousand held in utility and development escrows and the debt service reserve. Expected purchase and construction commitments are about USD 4.4 billion, substantially all within two years, and the company expects to issue project-level bonds within twelve months to fund part of the AMD and Neocloud build-out.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_corz.htm.jsonl, page 5; 10-Q_2026-09-30_corz.htm.jsonl, pages 23–25; 10-Q_2026-09-30_corz.htm.jsonl, page 32; EX99.1_2026-09-30_corz_q32026corescientificinc-ea-htm.htm.jsonl, page 1; calculation K31

The main data-quality risks are that FY2024 was restated for improperly capitalised assets committed to demolition, that the control weakness behind it was unremediated at 2026-09-30, and that the 2026 statements are unaudited. The main definition risks are the Adjusted EBITDA change, three liquidity measures that differ across releases, and interest shown as expense, as incurred coupon and as cash paid. Each conflict is logged with both figures.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_corz.htm.jsonl, page 118; 10-Q_2026-09-30_corz.htm.jsonl, pages 72–74; EX99.1_2026-06-30_corz_q22026corescientificinc-ea-htm.htm.jsonl, page 9; 10-Q_2026-03-31_corz.htm.jsonl, page 46; EX99.1_2026-03-31_corz_q12026corescientificinc-ea-htm.htm.jsonl, page 2; EX99.1_2026-06-30_corz_q22026corescientificinc-ea-htm.htm.jsonl, page 1; EX99.1_2026-09-30_corz_q32026corescientificinc-ea-htm.htm.jsonl, page 1

No event after the balance-sheet date of 2026-09-30 is stated in the documents read, so no pro forma adjustment is made. Holders of the 2029 convertible notes could convert in the fourth quarter of 2026 because the share-price condition was met in each quarter of 2026, and none had elected to by 2026-09-30, so the 2029 maturity is not the only way those notes can leave the debt stack.

## Gaps

- Reported EBITDA, derived as operating income plus depreciation and amortization, is negative for FY2024, FY2025 and the twelve months to 2026-09-30, so total debt and net debt multiples on reported EBITDA are not calculable and interest cover is negative; this restricts every leverage conclusion, it is a high-severity gap, and no action closes it until operating earnings turn positive.
- The twelve-month Adjusted EBITDA to 2026-09-30 is not comparable, because FY2025 is on the earlier definition, the nine-month figures are on the wider 2026 definition and no Q4 2025 figure on the wider definition is printed; this blocks a leverage multiple on the issuer's own measure, it is a high-severity gap, and the action is to obtain FY2025 Adjusted EBITDA on the 2026 definition.
- No standalone or restricted-group statements are supplied for Core Scientific Finance I LLC and its five subsidiary guarantors, so debt, cash and earnings are consolidated while the secured notes' covenants bind only that group; this restricts any coverage or structural conclusion for the notes, it is a high-severity gap, and the action is to obtain issuer-level accounts.
- Cash interest paid for the twelve months to 2026-09-30 predates the first coupon on the secured notes, so the cover for that period understates the interest burden; this is a medium-severity gap and the action is to recompute cover once the first coupon, due 2026-11-15, is reported.
- Undrawn committed facilities are not supplied for the balance-sheet dates before 2026-09-30 because no source states one; this is a low-severity gap and the action is to confirm from later filings that none existed.
- Cells the filings print as a dash are shown as Not supplied rather than zero, as the method requires, so line-by-line sums are not checked for those columns; this is a low-severity gap with no action beyond reading the filings.
- The cash flow row for proceeds from sales of digital assets is printed with four figures for three columns in the 2025 annual report, so the FY2024 and FY2025 values are not supplied; this is a low-severity gap and the action is to check the filing's layout.
- The legal ranking of the revolver, the letter of credit facility and the two convertible notes against the secured notes is not stated in any document, so only the secured notes are ranked in the debt stack; this is a medium-severity gap and the action is to obtain the credit agreement and the indentures.
