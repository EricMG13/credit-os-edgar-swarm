## Findings

### Bottom line

Leverage on carrying debt fell from 5.37x of reported EBITDA at year-end 2025 to 3.52x at September 30, 2026 on a last-twelve-months basis, but the pending buyout leaves the term loan's treatment open for creditors. Net debt over reported EBITDA fell from 4.90x to 2.95x, and the issuer's adjusted measure gives 2.62x gross.
Basis: calculation C09; calculation C10; calculation C14

Part of the improvement is lower stock compensation, a non-cash charge that depresses reported EBITDA, so cash generation is the firmer test: cash interest cover was 4.98x on the same basis. The debt is one floating-rate term loan maturing in 2032 and an undrawn revolver maturing in 2029, subject to a springing 2028 maturity that the filings state but do not settle.
Basis: calculation C11; calculation C17; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ccc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 18

### Financial interpretation

Leverage on carrying debt rose from 3.49x of reported EBITDA at the end of 2024 to 5.37x at the end of 2025, then fell to 3.52x on a last-twelve-months basis at September 30, 2026. The 2025 rise came from USD 525.0 million of new term loans, which funded the EvolutionIQ acquisition and share repurchases, while cash fell from USD 398.983 million to USD 111.192 million; the 2026 fall comes from higher earnings, scheduled amortisation and a rebuilt cash balance, with no further term-loan issuance. Net leverage followed, from 4.90x to 2.95x. For creditors the balance sheet has absorbed the 2025 shareholder-return programme, but the pending buyout can reset the debt regardless of this trajectory.
Basis: calculation C01; calculation C02; calculation C09; calculation C10; 10-K_2025-12-31_ccc-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 158–161; 10-K_2025-12-31_ccc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 132; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ccc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 8

Reported EBITDA is the primary denominator here and is depressed by non-cash stock compensation, which the issuer adds back to reach adjusted EBITDA; on the issuer's measure leverage is 2.62x gross and 2.20x net at September 30, 2026. About 44% of the nine-month rise in reported EBITDA comes from lower stock compensation rather than higher adjusted earnings, so the reported deleveraging overstates operating progress and the adjusted view is the cleaner read of cash earnings. The add-backs are management-defined and are not classified as realised or unrealised in the sources, so creditors should treat the adjusted ratios as a supporting view only.
Basis: calculation C06; calculation C13; calculation C14; calculation C17; 10-K_2025-12-31_ccc-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 110–111; EX-99.1_2026-10-29_ccc-ex99_1.htm.jsonl, pages 11–12

Cash interest cover on reported EBITDA improved from 3.57x for 2025 to 4.98x for the twelve months to September 30, 2026, because cash interest paid rose only modestly while earnings grew. The term loan is floating-rate and the swaps that fix USD 750.0 million of it at about 3.94% run only to July 31, 2027, so the cover benefits from a hedge that is finite; the sensitivity after that date is not established here and belongs to Rates, FX & hedging.
Basis: calculation C07; calculation C11; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ccc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 18–19; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ccc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 46

Cash generation is solid after capital spending: free cash flow was USD 254.508 million for 2025 and USD 311.375 million for the twelve months to September 30, 2026. Working capital was a drag in 2026: deferred revenues, which are customer advances that fund operations without interest, fell from USD 72.793 million at year-end to USD 54.917 million, a decrease the quarterly report attributes to the timing of revenue recognition. If billing terms shortened, that float would absorb cash ahead of any earnings decline, so Earnings quality and Liquidity should watch it.
Basis: calculation C16; calculation C18; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ccc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 5; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ccc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 47
