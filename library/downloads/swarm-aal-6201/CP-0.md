## Findings

### Bottom line

The five supplied filings are sufficient to analyse American Airlines Group's finances, liquidity and business, but not its creditor protections, because no executed debt documents, agency ratings or market prices were supplied.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_aal_aal-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 266–311; 10-Q_2026-09-30_aal_aal-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 71–73

The FY2025 year-end statements are audited and present fairly in the auditor's opinion, and the quarterly statements are unaudited. The latest filing was signed on 22 October 2026, 27 days before the as-of date, so later events are not covered. Debt and balance-sheet figures agree between the annual and quarterly reports and the liquidity table adds up, which supports the Financial foundation, Business & governance and Fundamental credit work with limitations. Legal & covenants, Recovery & fulcrum and Market-implied risk work are blocked until executed documents and prices are supplied, and board, ownership and rating-level detail stay unsupported.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_aal_aal-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 140–142; 10-Q_2026-09-30_aal_aal-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 6–8, 59–60, 73; calculation C1; calculation C2; calculation C4; calculation C6
