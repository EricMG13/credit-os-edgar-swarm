## Findings

### Bottom line

Leverage has eased since the acquisition financing, with net debt at 2.73x reported EBITDA at 2026-08-01 against 2.99x at the year end, but earnings include the acquired businesses only part-period, so the improvement is incomplete.

Cash interest cover of 10.49x flatters the position because cash interest paid over the last twelve months only partly reflects the larger borrowings; reported EBITDA covered interest expense by 7.64x in the first half of fiscal 2027. The revolver was undrawn with USD 746.4 million of availability and cash was USD 340.08 million, while the credit agreement was not supplied, so covenant headroom is not established.
Basis: calculation C29; calculation C27; calculation C33; calculation C34; 10-Q_2026-08-01_dy-20260801.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 21; Source readiness (CP-0)

### Key findings

Gross debt to reported EBITDA fell from 4.00x at the fiscal 2026 year end to 3.11x at 2026-08-01 while carrying-value debt was almost unchanged, so the whole improvement is earnings growth rather than repayment. Net debt rose over the same period because cash fell, yet net leverage still fell from 2.99x to 2.73x, which means EBITDA grew faster than net debt. Last-twelve-months EBITDA includes Power Solutions only from 2025-12-23 and National Technology Integrators only from 2026-07-02, so leverage on a full-period basis would be lower, but the sources state no pro forma EBITDA and none is worked out here.
Basis: calculation C27; calculation C29; calculation C15; calculation C17; 10-Q_2026-08-01_dy-20260801.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 27

Issuer-adjusted EBITDA is higher than reported EBITDA in each of the four ratio periods and gives 2.94x gross and 2.58x net leverage at 2026-08-01, but its add-back set changed between fiscal 2026 and fiscal 2027, so the adjusted figures are a mixed definition and the ratios here use reported EBITDA.
Basis: 10-K_2026-01-31_dy-20260131.htm.jsonl, page 70; 10-Q_2026-08-01_dy-20260801.htm.jsonl, page 44; calculation C21

Operating cash flow converts weakly in the first half of each year: it was 0.14x reported EBITDA in the first half of fiscal 2027 and 0.01x a year earlier, against 0.91x for fiscal 2026, because receivables and contract assets absorb cash as revenue grows. The issuer describes its results as seasonal, so the pattern is not new, but the larger revenue base makes the working capital build larger and leaves cash generation weighted to the second half.
Basis: calculation C43; calculation C42; calculation C41; 10-Q_2026-08-01_dy-20260801.htm.jsonl, pages 4, 49; 10-K_2026-01-31_dy-20260131.htm.jsonl, page 53

Supplier finance obligations of USD 367.4 million at 2026-08-01, up from USD 226.4 million at the year end, sit inside accounts payable rather than debt and equal 60.22% of accounts payable. They fund working capital outside the leverage ratios, and if the third-party program ended suppliers could require faster payment, a liquidity call that debt measures do not capture.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-08-01_dy-20260801.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 29; calculation C44

The revolver is undrawn with USD 746.4 million of availability. Apart from scheduled amortization the first maturity is USD 500.0 million of 2029 notes in April 2029, followed by USD 1,540.0 million of term loan A in December 2030 and USD 800.0 million of term loan B in January 2033. Funded secured debt is 2.58x last-twelve-months reported EBITDA and ranks ahead of the unsecured notes, which bring total principal to 3.13x.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-08-01_dy-20260801.htm.jsonl, pages 18–21; calculation C36

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest contrary evidence is that leverage may already be lower than shown: Building Systems earned a higher Adjusted EBITDA margin than Communications in the first half of fiscal 2027 and contributed to only part of the last twelve months, and total backlog rose between the year end and 2026-08-01. An issuer-stated pro forma EBITDA, or the report for the quarter ended 2026-10-31 that falls after the as-of date, would show whether the improvement holds. A draw on the revolver, a further working capital build or a covenant ratio close to its limit would reverse the view.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-08-01_dy-20260801.htm.jsonl, pages 45, 54–55

### Limitations

The credit agreement and the 2029 notes indenture were not supplied, so covenant definitions, scheduled amortization and baskets are not established and no covenant headroom is stated. Maturities are shown by final maturity because amortization is not itemized, and the cash interest base is built from year-to-date disclosures that only partly reflect the larger borrowings. The Adjusted EBITDA add-back set changed between fiscal 2026 and fiscal 2027, fiscal 2026 had 53 weeks, and the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026 is not tabulated as a discrete quarter.
Basis: Source readiness (CP-0); 10-K_2026-01-31_dy-20260131.htm.jsonl, pages 70, 86
