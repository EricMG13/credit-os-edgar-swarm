## Findings

### Bottom line

Newmark's business is diversified across service lines and clients, so creditors' main exposure is governance: a Cantor-controlled owner group with 58.6% of the vote, heavy related-party dealing and a chief executive transition at the end of 2026.

The largest service line is 39.57% of nine-month 2026 revenue and the top ten clients were 9.1% of FY2025 revenue, so a single client or line is unlikely to impair debt service. The risks that matter to a bondholder come from the controlling holder: Cantor holds USD 125.0 million of the USD 600.0 million senior notes, 98.48% of the shares repurchased in the third quarter came from the chief executive and his foundation, and the Audit Committee is the stated check on related-party deals. The chief executive is contracted as chief executive only through 2026-12-31, and the sources name no successor.

### Key findings

Control sits with one family through Cantor and is larger than its economic interest, which makes the board and borrowing decisions subject to one group's preferences. Class B shares carry ten votes each, and as of 2026-09-30 Mr. Brandon Lutnick beneficially controlled 58.6% of the vote, while Cantor Fitzgerald, L.P. holds 11.7% of the economics and 56.5% of the voting power and CFGM, Cantor's managing general partner, holds 0.8% of the economics and 1.2% of the voting power. The stated protection for other holders is Audit Committee approval of matters that need it, and a Lutnick family voting agreement that directs the family's votes, so control is stable rather than contestable. Newmark is a controlled company under Nasdaq rules and could opt out of the majority-independent-board requirement, which it has not done to date.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_nmrk-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 129; 10-Q_2026-09-30_nmrk-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 133; 10-Q_2026-09-30_nmrk-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 134; 10-K_2025-12-31_nmrk-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 104; 10-K_2025-12-31_nmrk-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 53–54; 10-K_2025-12-31_nmrk-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 110; calculation C17

Related-party dealing with Cantor is extensive and touches creditors directly, because Cantor is both the controlling holder and a lender and service provider. Cantor holds USD 125.0 million of the USD 600.0 million of senior notes due 2029, which is 20.83% of the issue, and its affiliate CF&Co acts as placement agent and makes a market in the notes; Cantor also supplies administrative services, can borrow from and lend to Newmark under a credit agreement of up to USD 250.0 million with no balance outstanding, and is owed a tax receivable liability. These arrangements are approved by the Audit Committee on terms stated to be no less favorable than third parties, but the agreements themselves were not supplied.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_nmrk-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 68; 10-Q_2026-09-30_nmrk-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 78; 10-Q_2026-09-30_nmrk-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 80; 10-Q_2026-09-30_nmrk-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 83; 10-Q_2026-09-30_nmrk-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 84; calculation C13

Capital returns are heavy and tilted toward insiders, so shareholder distributions are competing with creditors for the same cash. In the third quarter 98.48% of the shares repurchased were bought from the chief executive and his foundation, the quarterly dividend is USD 0.06 per share against USD 0.03 a year earlier, and the repurchase authorization was raised to USD 400.0 million of which USD 189.5 million remained at 2026-09-30. The repurchases were approved by the Audit and Compensation Committees and paid for while the revolver drawings rose, which is the pattern a bondholder should watch.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_nmrk-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 49; 10-Q_2026-09-30_nmrk-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 22; 10-Q_2026-09-30_nmrk-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 62; 10-Q_2026-09-30_nmrk-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 70–71; 10-K_2025-12-31_nmrk-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 262; calculation C16

Leadership continuity is the near-term governance risk: the chief executive is contracted as chief executive only through 2026-12-31, after which he becomes Chairman of the operating subsidiary Newmark & Co., and the sources name no successor; the Board retained a leadership advisory firm in July 2025 and says it is assessing long-term options, so a process exists but its outcome is not disclosed. The Board chair is also the chief legal officer, a director is the son of the former executive chairman and now Chief Strategy Officer, and a Chief Operating Officer was appointed in 2025, so management depth exists but the outcome of the succession process is not disclosed.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a governance concern is procedural and structural, and the company states that it has not relied on any controlled-company exemption from Nasdaq governance standards to date. Related-party transactions need Audit Committee approval and the authorizations state arm's-length price terms, the former executive chairman completed the divestiture of his holdings and holds no voting or dispositive power, revenues are spread over three service lines and many clients, and the quarterly report shows no outstanding balance under the Cantor credit agreement. The view would turn more positive if the proxy materials show a majority of independent directors and an independent chair, if the issuer names a chief executive successor with an orderly handover, and if insider repurchases stop; it would turn more negative if Cantor sold its notes at a discount or the revolver were drawn to fund further insider buybacks. The Board has also had a leadership advisory firm assisting with long-term succession planning since July 2025, which lowers the risk that the end-2026 transition is unplanned. A single Class B voting block can also be an asset to creditors if it is a stable, long-horizon holder, which the sources neither confirm nor rule out.
