## Findings

### Bottom line

The five supplied Terex filings support a credit snapshot as of 2026-09-30, with limits: the REV merger of February 2026 breaks comparability with earlier periods, so creditors should not read year-on-year or LTM figures across it as like-for-like.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_tex.htm.jsonl, page 8; 10-K_2025-12-31_tex.htm.jsonl, page 9

Cash of USD 407 million, total debt of USD 2,578 million and an undrawn revolver of USD 800 million are printed for that date. Covenant headroom, LTM Adjusted EBITDA, ratings, market prices and anything dated after 2026-10-29 are not established from the supplied documents.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_tex.htm.jsonl, page 5; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tex.htm.jsonl, pages 24–25; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tex.htm.jsonl, page 47

### Key findings

The group a lender faces today is larger and different from the one the audited annual report describes. The REV merger closed on 2026-02-02 and created a fourth segment, Specialty Vehicles. REV contributed net sales of USD 1,744 million and net income of USD 17 million from the closing date to 2026-09-30. Unaudited pro forma figures are printed only for net sales, net income and earnings per share, so no combined EBITDA or leverage can be taken from the filings.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_tex.htm.jsonl, page 8; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tex.htm.jsonl, pages 21–22; 10-K_2025-12-31_tex.htm.jsonl, page 9

Two reporting differences matter more than their size suggests. From 2026-01-01 the company shows amortisation of customer relationships and trade names as an operating expense instead of cost of goods sold, and it recast only the 2025 quarterly comparatives, by USD 52 million for the first nine months; the audited FY2025 and FY2024 statements print no separate amortisation line, so gross profit and the operating expense lines do not line up across the two kinds of filing while operating profit does. The REV purchase price is also printed twice, USD 3,262 million in the annual report and USD 3,384 million in the quarterly reports, and the gap equals the USD 122 million of REV debt repaid at closing.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_tex.htm.jsonl, page 8; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tex.htm.jsonl, page 19; 10-K_2025-12-31_tex.htm.jsonl, page 94; 10-K_2025-12-31_tex.htm.jsonl, page 137; calculation C1

Debt and liquidity are well documented at the latest date. Three funded instruments remain: senior notes due 2029, senior notes due 2032 and a term loan due 2031, with the USD 800 million revolver, drawn USD 170 million at 2026-03-31 and USD 110 million at 2026-06-30, undrawn at 2026-09-30 and expiring on 2029-10-08. The only maintenance covenant is a first lien net leverage test of 3.00x on the revolver, applied only when more than 30% of it is used; the company says it complies but prints no ratio, and the credit agreement itself was not supplied. The 2024 annual report stated a 2.5x net leverage target through the cycle; the 2025 annual report drops the number.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-03-31_tex.htm.jsonl, page 20; 10-Q_2026-06-30_tex.htm.jsonl, page 23; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tex.htm.jsonl, pages 24–27; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tex.htm.jsonl, page 47; 10-K_2024-12-31_tex.htm.jsonl, page 11; 10-K_2025-12-31_tex.htm.jsonl, page 11

Claims outside the debt figure are only partly refreshed. The latest operating lease liabilities, USD 129 million, are dated 2025-12-31 before the merger, and the latest pension funded status is dated 2024-12-31. The 2026 reports add repurchase commitments with a maximum contingent liability of USD 460 million, performance, bid and specialty bonds of USD 619 million, short-term customer advances of USD 271 million, long-term customer advances of USD 176 million and receivable sales of about USD 562 million in the first nine months; none of these is inside the debt figure.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_tex.htm.jsonl, page 133; 10-K_2024-12-31_tex.htm.jsonl, page 131; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tex.htm.jsonl, page 5; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tex.htm.jsonl, pages 29–30; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tex.htm.jsonl, page 48

Adjusted EBITDA exists only in the 2026 quarterly reports: USD 727 million for the first nine months of 2026 against USD 495 million for the same months of 2025. The annual reports print no EBITDA reconciliation, so a twelve-month Adjusted EBITDA to 2026-09-30 cannot be built from the supplied documents, and it would blend pre-merger and post-merger perimeters. The 2025 annual report does print operating profit for each 2025 quarter, including USD 137 million for the fourth quarter, and a 2026 outlook of USD 930 million to USD 1 billion of EBITDA that includes REV from closing and is not updated in the 2026 reports.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_tex.htm.jsonl, pages 45–46; 10-K_2025-12-31_tex.htm.jsonl, page 59; 10-K_2025-12-31_tex.htm.jsonl, page 61

After the quarter end the third-quarter report prints three events: completion on 2026-10-23 of the sale of two Specialty Vehicles businesses for about USD 33 million, a further share repurchase authorisation of up to USD 500 million in October 2026, and a USD 0.17 per share dividend payable on 2026-12-18. The latest document is dated 2026-10-29, so the period to the as-of date of 2026-11-18 is not covered.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_tex.htm.jsonl, pages 33–34; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tex.htm.jsonl, page 53

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest case against a ready-with-limits view is that the combined business has three quarters of history, a provisional purchase price allocation and no pro forma EBITDA, which could make any leverage reading of the new group unreliable. The filings point the other way on two counts: the company recasts its 2025 quarters and discloses REV’s own contribution, and the REV goodwill estimate rose by USD 19 million in the second quarter and fell by USD 42 million in the third, from USD 1,451 million at the first quarter to USD 1,428 million at the third.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-03-31_tex.htm.jsonl, page 17; 10-Q_2026-06-30_tex.htm.jsonl, page 20; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tex.htm.jsonl, pages 20–21

The executed credit agreement and indentures would turn the covenant and guarantor statements into testable terms, and a FY2025 Adjusted EBITDA reconciliation or REV’s own annual report would allow a leverage measure on one perimeter. A larger revision of the REV accounting, or a revolver draw above 30%, would raise the weight of the covenant gap.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_tex.htm.jsonl, pages 84–88; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tex.htm.jsonl, page 27; 10-K_2025-12-31_tex.htm.jsonl, page 9

### Limitations

This view concerns what the filings contain and where, not credit quality: it makes no leverage, coverage or recovery judgement, and the arithmetic it carries ties printed figures to their printed parts. Page references point to positions in the supplied text files, which flatten tables into lines, so each figure was read against its row label; they are not the filings’ printed page numbers.

The 2026 reports are unaudited and the REV accounting stays provisional until its measurement period closes, so figures that depend on it can still change.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_tex.htm.jsonl, page 20
