## Findings

### Bottom line

ZipRecruiter is a short-cycle, founder-controlled online hiring marketplace whose revenue fell by nearly a third between 2023 and 2025 and is only now recovering, so noteholders depend on management restraint over buybacks that nothing visible in these filings constrains.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_zip-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 98; 10-Q_2026-09-30_zip-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 7, 16, 29 and 90; calculation A2

No sponsor owns it: the chief executive officer holds 78.9% of the votes with 15.7% of the shares, and sponsor and governance risk is Medium. Management retired USD 299.3 million of the notes at a discount in 2026 and has never paid a dividend, but it spent USD 102.1 million on share repurchases in FY2025 while free cash flow was USD 3.5 million.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_zip-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 21 and 90; 10-K_2025-12-31_zip-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 84, 100 and 101; calculation A3; calculation A4

### Key findings

- The business is short-cycle and cost-flexible, which gives creditors little revenue visibility but a lever to protect cash. Subscription plans run from a day to a year, performance-based campaigns run for one to three months, the company reports no material remaining performance obligations, and sales and marketing absorbs about half of revenue, most of it advertising that management says it can scale back quickly.
  Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_zip-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 14, 16, 17, 31 and 36; 10-K_2025-12-31_zip-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 10; calculation A5
- Demand fell sharply and is only now turning. Revenue declined 26.59% in FY2024 and 5.28% in FY2025, and in Q3 2026 revenue rose 8.23% and Quarterly Paid Employers 10.41%, but revenue per paid employer fell 1.98%, so the recovery rests on employer count rather than spend per employer.
  Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_zip-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 7 and 28; calculation A2; calculation A6
- Control sits with the founder, not a sponsor, and the board's independence cannot be tested. The chief executive officer is the sole Class B holder with 78.9% of the votes and 15.7% of the shares, the board is classified with staggered terms, and director and officer detail sits in a proxy statement that is not supplied.
  Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_zip-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 90 and 93; 10-K_2025-12-31_zip-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 59 and 147; calculation A3
- Capital allocation favoured shareholders until 2026. Share repurchases were USD 147.6 million in FY2023, USD 40.3 million in FY2024 and USD 102.1 million in FY2025, with USD 642.7 million of the USD 750.0 million authorisation used by 2026-09-30, while free cash flow in FY2025 was only USD 3.5 million.
  Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_zip-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 84, 100 and 101; 10-Q_2026-09-30_zip-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 24 and 96; calculation A4; calculation A8
- The 2026 note repurchase shows willingness to use cash for creditors when debt trades at a discount. USD 299.3 million of principal, 54.4% of the original USD 550.0 million, was retired for USD 236.8 million, and share repurchases fell to USD 14.0 million for nine months, yet the revolving facility was left to lapse, so no committed liquidity backstop remains.
  Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_zip-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 10, 21 and 24; calculation A8; calculation A9
- No dividend, distribution recapitalisation, creditor exchange or priming has been disclosed, so the supported conduct is mixed rather than adverse, and the sponsor and governance risk level is Medium on limited evidence.
  Basis: 10-K_2024-12-31_zip-20241231.htm.jsonl, page 61; 10-Q_2026-09-30_zip-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 21 and 23

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against caution is conduct since mid-2026: management retired more than half of the notes below par, share repurchases dropped below free cash flow, no dividend has ever been paid and no creditor-adverse transaction is disclosed. That evidence would win if the indenture proves to restrict share repurchases tightly enough to make the FY2025 level impossible to repeat, or if free cash flow stays near the USD 27.4 million trailing level while repurchases stay near USD 14.0 million a nine-month period.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_zip-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 10, 21 and 24; 10-K_2025-12-31_zip-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 101; calculation A8

The view would worsen if repurchases resume at the FY2025 pace while earnings are thin, if the remaining USD 107.3 million of authorisation is drawn quickly, or if the chief executive officer's voting control is used to approve a distribution or a transaction that changes the notes' protection; the repurchase programme is already the subject of a stockholder suit filed on 2026-06-17.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_zip-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 23, 24 and 96

### Limitations

- The indenture is not supplied, so restricted payment capacity is not established and the conclusion that noteholders depend on management restraint cannot be tested against actual covenant limits.
- The proxy statement is not supplied, so board independence, director and officer ownership and related-party transactions are not established, and the Medium governance level could move either way once they are known.
- The relationship of the Institutional Venture Partners affiliated holders that sold shares back to the company in FY2025 is not described, so that private repurchase is not classified as related-party conduct.
- No investor presentation or earnings release is supplied, so management's own description of the recovery and of buyback intentions is limited to the filings' wording, which lowers confidence in the demand turn.
- Nothing dated after the quarterly report signed on 2026-11-09 is covered, so a buyback or note transaction in the last weeks before the as-of date would not appear and the 2026 pattern of restraint could already have changed.

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

No new debt was issued in the periods covered, and no acquisition or sponsor transaction is pending in the supplied filings; the transactions that changed the creditor position were a lapse of the revolving facility and repurchases of the notes. The USD 290.0 million credit facility expired unrenewed on 2026-04-30 with nothing drawn, the company retired USD 294.6 million of the 5% notes due 2030 in June 2026 through privately negotiated agreements with certain holders for USD 232.8 million and a further USD 4.7 million in the open market in September 2026 for USD 4.0 million, which left USD 250.7 million of principal.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_zip-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 21 and 22; 10-Q_2026-06-30_zip-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 20; calculation A9

The last completed acquisition was Breakroom, a UK employee review platform bought on 2024-07-23 for USD 13.3 million, of which USD 12.4 million was cash and USD 0.9 million a holdback paid in the third quarter of 2025; goodwill of USD 6.794 million arose. The notes were issued on 2022-01-12 in a private placement, and the supplied filings do not state what the proceeds were used for.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_zip-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 120 and 128
