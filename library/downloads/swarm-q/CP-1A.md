## Findings

### Bottom line

Qnity is a volume-led electronics materials supplier with no sponsor identified in its filings, so creditor risk sits in uncapped legacy-liability indemnities to DuPont, Asian customer concentration and shareholder returns; sponsor and governance risk is Medium. Net sales were USD 4,754 million in 2025 and rose 20.65% in the first nine months of 2026, essentially all from volume, while the top ten customers took 34% of 2025 sales and Asia Pacific 78.82%.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_q-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 17; 10-K_2025-12-31_q-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 106; 10-Q_2026-09-30_q-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 11; 10-Q_2026-09-30_q-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 35; calculation BG2; calculation BG4

Legacy liabilities allocated to Qnity were USD 218 million at 2026-09-30, with up to about USD 100 million more reasonably possible, and DuPont alone decides how the underlying claims are settled. The separation was financed with USD 4,100 million of debt whose proceeds were paid to DuPont as a cash dividend, and buybacks and dividends took USD 125 million in nine months, 35.01% of free cash flow.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_q-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 119; 10-K_2025-12-31_q-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 120; 10-Q_2026-09-30_q-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 6; 10-Q_2026-09-30_q-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 21; calculation BG7; calculation BG9; calculation BG10

### Key findings

- Business risk is moderate and recurring: over 90% of 2025 net sales came from products consumed in chip and board manufacturing or built into devices, nine-month 2026 net sales rose 20.65% with volume up 21% and price and mix flat, and segment Adjusted Operating EBITDA margins were 35.41% in Semiconductor Technologies and 29.54% in Interconnect Solutions.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_q-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 7; 10-Q_2026-09-30_q-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 11; 10-Q_2026-09-30_q-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 29; 10-Q_2026-09-30_q-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 35; calculation BG4; calculation BG5
- Customer and regional concentration are the main business risks and cannot be diversified away on the evidence supplied: the top ten customers took 34% of 2025 sales, Samsung and TSMC took 10% each in the nine months to 2026-09-30, Asia Pacific took 78.82% and China 33.02% of 2025 sales, and top customer contracts carry no long-term purchase commitments.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_q-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 17; 10-K_2025-12-31_q-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 33; 10-K_2025-12-31_q-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 106; 10-Q_2026-09-30_q-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 11; calculation BG2; calculation BG3

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence for a lower level is that no sponsor extracts value, the franchise is growing on volume, and the legacy liabilities are small against earnings: the accrued USD 218 million is 14.74% of last-twelve-month reported EBITDA, and 21.50% if the reasonably possible range is added. Management has raised full-year guidance at each of the last three results releases, and most lenders rolled their term loan balances, 84.60% of the repriced principal, when the margin was cut.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); 10-Q_2026-09-30_q-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 20; EX-99.1_2026-05-12_exhibit991supplementalsche.htm.jsonl, page 2; EX-99.1_2026-08-04_exhibit991supplementalsche.htm.jsonl, page 2; EX-99.1_2026-11-04_exhibit991supplementalsche.htm.jsonl, page 2; calculation BG8; calculation BG11

Against that, the level is Medium rather than Low because the one documented value transfer was large, DuPont and the trust hold rights that creditors cannot contest, and customer and regional concentration leave sales exposed to one large customer decision or one export-control change.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_q-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 17; 10-K_2025-12-31_q-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 48; 10-K_2025-12-31_q-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 119; calculation BG2

The level would rise to High if executed documents show restricted-payment baskets that allow materially larger distributions, if Qnity adds debt for an acquisition or a special dividend, or if DuPont settlements lift the accrual well beyond the reasonably possible range. It would fall to Low if the proxy statement confirms dispersed ownership with no holder above 5%, if the legacy indemnities end or are capped, and if indenture terms limit distributions.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_q-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 21; 10-K_2025-12-31_q-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 84; Source readiness (CP-0)

### Limitations

- The proxy statement that carries holders above 5%, director holdings and board independence was not supplied, so the finding that no controlling holder exists could be overturned by a large holder and would then raise the governance level.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_q-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 84
- The credit agreement, its first amendment and both indentures were not supplied, so the finding that shareholder returns are moderate rests on observed behavior and not on legal capacity; larger restricted-payment baskets would move the level toward High.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_q-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 119; 10-Q_2026-09-30_q-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 46
- Legacy liability sizing rests on DuPont estimates that Qnity does not control, so the USD 218 million accrual and the reasonably possible range of about USD 100 million are the least firm inputs to the Medium level.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_q-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 21; 10-K_2025-12-31_q-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 121
- Revenue is disclosed by segment, region and the top two customers only, so the finding that concentration is customer and regional rather than product-level cannot be tested for individual product lines.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_q-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 17; 10-K_2025-12-31_q-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 106
- No ratings, market prices or executed customer contracts were supplied, so the view that demand is recurring rests on the company description of long customer relationships.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_q-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 17

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

The separation was a spin-off and not a sponsor transaction: DuPont announced the plan on 2024-05-22 and completed it on 2025-11-01 by distributing one Qnity share for every two DuPont shares, and Qnity common stock began trading on the New York Stock Exchange on 2025-11-03. No acquirer, purchase price, equity contribution or entry multiple exists, and the sources and uses of the separation are not stated beyond the debt below.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_q-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 6; 10-Q_2026-09-30_q-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 8

Qnity borrowed USD 4,100 million before the distribution: USD 1,000 million of secured notes due 2032 and USD 750 million of unsecured notes due 2033 issued on 2025-08-15, and a USD 2,350 million term loan due 2032 funded on 2025-10-31, with the term loan and the notes proceeds paid to DuPont as a cash dividend. The secured debt is 81.71% of the total, and a USD 1,250 million revolver maturing in October 2030, undrawn at 2026-09-30, adds committed capacity under a first lien net leverage test of 4.50x.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_q-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 116; 10-K_2025-12-31_q-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 117; 10-K_2025-12-31_q-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 119; 10-K_2025-12-31_q-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 120; 10-Q_2026-09-30_q-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 20; calculation BG7

On 2026-07-01 the term loan was repriced: the margin fell by 25 bp to 1.75% over term SOFR, USD 360 million of principal was repaid and reissued and the rest rolled over, and the issuer expects annual cash interest to fall by about USD 6 million. Both series of notes can be redeemed before 2028-08-15 only at par plus a make-whole premium, or in part from equity offerings.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_q-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 20; 10-Q_2026-09-30_q-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 34; 10-K_2025-12-31_q-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 117; calculation BG8

No new debt issuance is stated in the nine months to 2026-09-30: the only change to debt terms was the repricing, and cash went to buybacks of USD 75 million and dividends of USD 50 million in nine months. Legacy payments to DuPont are not debt but are claims outside it, at USD 218 million accrued, and the executed documents that fix distribution and lien capacity were not supplied.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_q-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 6; 10-Q_2026-09-30_q-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 21; 10-Q_2026-09-30_q-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 23; calculation BG9; calculation BG10
