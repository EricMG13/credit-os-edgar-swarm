## Findings

### Bottom line

The seven supplied Hudbay filings are enough for a consolidated credit snapshot to 30 June 2026, but not for covenant, ranking or guarantor-level work, so creditors should read every leverage and liquidity figure as a consolidated, company-defined measure.
Basis: 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-06-30_exhibit99-1.htm.jsonl, pages 1, 18–19; 6-K-EX-99.3_2026-06-30_exhibit99-3.htm.jsonl, pages 6–7

The audited FY2025 statements, the annual management discussion and the annual information form are listed as exhibits to the annual report cover but were not supplied, and no credit agreement, indenture, rating or market quote was supplied, so covenant compliance rests on the company's own statement that it was in compliance. No document in the set is dated after 29 July 2026.
Basis: 40-F_2025-12-31_form40f.htm.jsonl, pages 2–3; 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-06-30_exhibit99-1.htm.jsonl, page 19; 6-K-EX-99.3_2026-06-30_exhibit99-3.htm.jsonl, page 1

Two definitions need care. The company's headline liquidity and net debt count cash held by the Copper World project entity, USD 334.5 million at 30 June 2026, which is contractually reserved for that project, and the fourth-quarter 2025 release calls USD 1,008.6 million total principal debt when that is the balance-sheet carrying value and principal was USD 1,014.9 million.
Basis: 6-K-EX-99.3_2026-06-30_exhibit99-3.htm.jsonl, page 7; 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-06-30_exhibit99-1.htm.jsonl, page 12; 6-K-EX-99.3_2025-12-31_exhibit99-3.htm.jsonl, page 22; 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-03-31_exhibit99-1.htm.jsonl, page 13; calculation CALC-3; calculation CALC-6
