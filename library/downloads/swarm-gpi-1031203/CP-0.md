## Findings

### Bottom line

The five Group 1 Automotive filings support a filing-based credit snapshot at 30 September 2026, but not ratings, legal-document, market-pricing or post-acquisition work, so creditors should treat every balance-sheet figure as before the USD 1.3 billion Hennessy purchase.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_gpi-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 13–14

The filings print no credit rating beyond a statement that the debt is rated just below investment grade, no executed debt agreements, no dated bond quotes beyond quarter-end fair values of the notes and no peer financials, and the company reports no EBITDA, so those items are not established and leverage has to be derived from statement lines or taken from the company's own covenant ratios.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_gpi-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 23; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gpi-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 18; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gpi-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 60

The third-quarter cash flow statement covers nine months only, so a standalone quarter needs the six-month statement subtracted, and the latest source was signed 20 days before the as-of date with nothing later supplied.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_gpi-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 7–8; 10-Q_2026-06-30_gpi-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 7; calculation C7
