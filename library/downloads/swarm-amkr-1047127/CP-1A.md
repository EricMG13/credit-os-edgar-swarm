## Findings

### Bottom line
Amkor is a scaled but concentrated contract manufacturer under family influence, so creditors depend on one dominant customer relationship and on payout and capital spending choices made while the Arizona build absorbs cash. One customer, Apple, supplied 29.8% of FY2025 net sales and the ten largest customers 72%. The Kim family and affiliates hold about 49.5% of the shares, and the part of that stake above an aggregate 41.6% of voting shares is voted neutrally under an agreement. Governance risk reads Medium rather than Low because dividends of USD 62.1 million and buybacks of USD 32.3 million continued while capital spending is funded with debt, but the sources document no related-party leakage, extraction or restructuring.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_amkr_amkr-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 23; 10-Q_2026-09-30_amkr_amkr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 74; 10-Q_2026-09-30_amkr_amkr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 37; 10-Q_2026-09-30_amkr_amkr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 83

### Key findings
Customer concentration is the largest business risk. Apple accounted for 29.8% and Qualcomm for 11.1% of FY2025 net sales and the ten largest customers for 72%. In the three months to September 30, 2026 the communications end market fell from 51% to 37% of net sales, down 28%, because of the timing of transferring system-in-package programs from Korea to Vietnam, while the computing and the automotive and industrial end markets each grew 37%. Factory loading follows a few customers' program timing, so a program transfer or a lost design moves utilization and margin quickly. The implication is volatile quarterly earnings around a growing base: gross margin of 18.3% against 14.3% a year earlier came from a mix with lower material content and may not persist.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_amkr_amkr-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 23; 10-Q_2026-09-30_amkr_amkr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 12; 10-Q_2026-09-30_amkr_amkr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 30; 10-Q_2026-09-30_amkr_amkr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 28

Capital intensity is rising and the payback is distant. Amkor expects about USD 2.5 billion of capital spending in 2026, mainly to build the Arizona facility, whose first phase is not expected to start manufacturing until the first half of 2028. Government support is conditional: a direct funding award of up to USD 407 million had produced no cash by September 30, 2026 and carries milestones and clawback rights, and the investment tax credit rate rose from 25% to 35% for property placed in service after 2025. Cash is committed years before revenue, so creditors should expect a long stretch of spending funded by debt and customer advances until the facility ramps.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_amkr_amkr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 37; 10-K_2025-12-31_amkr_amkr-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 65; 10-Q_2026-09-30_amkr_amkr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 35; 10-K_2025-12-31_amkr_amkr-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 62; 10-K_2025-12-31_amkr_amkr-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 75

Control sits with the Kim family, which holds about 49.5% of the shares and, through the Chairman, can substantially influence shareholder and board decisions. A voting agreement requires the part of the stake above an aggregate 41.6% of voting shares to be voted neutrally, which limits control of that part but not of the rest. For creditors the mechanism is that a recapitalisation or takeover needs family consent and the family's incentives are tied to the franchise; the sources show a regular dividend, not special distributions, so no extraction is documented.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_amkr_amkr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 74; 10-Q_2026-09-30_amkr_amkr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 76

Financial policy has been to refinance and fund growth with new debt while keeping a regular dividend. In 2025 the 2027 senior notes were redeemed with the proceeds of USD 500.0 million of 2033 senior notes and a term loan under a USD 1.0 billion secured revolving facility, and in May 2026 Amkor issued USD 1.15 billion of convertible notes with a capped call that cost USD 56.4 million. Payouts of USD 62.1 million in dividends over nine months and USD 32.3 million of repurchases in the third quarter under a USD 300.0 million authorization are small beside capital spending, but they continue while debt funds the build, which is why governance risk is Medium.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_amkr_amkr-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 119; 10-K_2025-12-31_amkr_amkr-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 118; 10-K_2025-12-31_amkr_amkr-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 75; 10-Q_2026-09-30_amkr_amkr-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 19–20; 10-Q_2026-09-30_amkr_amkr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 20; 10-Q_2026-09-30_amkr_amkr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 37; 10-Q_2026-09-30_amkr_amkr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 83; 10-Q_2026-09-30_amkr_amkr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 83

Customer advance payments are becoming part of how the build is funded: Amkor expects about USD 1.9 billion within twelve months and, under an October 2026 agreement, about USD 1.3 billion in 2027. This is a non-debt source of funds that must be earned by delivering capacity and is partly backed by standby letters of credit, so it lowers the borrowing need only if customer demand and Amkor's delivery both hold.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_amkr_amkr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 36; 10-Q_2026-09-30_amkr_amkr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 36; 10-Q_2026-09-30_amkr_amkr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 10

Disclosure is adequate for monitoring but incomplete on governance. The annual statements are audited by the same firm since 2000 and the latest quarterly report states effective disclosure controls, but board independence, related-party transactions, compensation and the ownership table are incorporated by reference to a proxy statement that is not supplied, and the credit agreement and indentures were not read, so restricted-payment capacity cannot be tested against the payouts.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_amkr_amkr-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 90; 10-Q_2026-09-30_amkr_amkr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 42; 10-K_2025-12-31_amkr_amkr-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 131

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view
The strongest case for a better governance view is that family ownership aligns long-term incentives with a capital-intensive franchise, payouts are small beside capital spending, and no related-party leakage, restructuring or creditor-adverse transaction appears in the sources read, while customers are prepaying about USD 1.9 billion for capacity. The view would worsen on a debt-funded special distribution, related-party dealings disclosed in the proxy statement, a revolver draw to fund payouts, or loss of the largest customer's programs; it would improve if the proxy statement shows a majority of independent directors and no related-party payments and the debt documents show tight restricted-payment limits.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_amkr_amkr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 36; 10-Q_2026-09-30_amkr_amkr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 76; 10-K_2025-12-31_amkr_amkr-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 131

### Transaction summary and new debt issues
In May 2026 Amkor issued USD 1.15 billion of convertible senior notes due July 15, 2031 under an indenture dated May 5, 2026, including the full exercise of a USD 150 million option for additional notes. The notes carry a 0.00% coupon, are senior unsecured obligations guaranteed by Guardian Assets, Inc., cannot be redeemed before May 15, 2029 and are settled in cash up to principal with cash or shares for the excess. The conversion price is about USD 106.37 per share and none of the conditions for early conversion had been met at September 30, 2026. Alongside the issue Amkor bought capped calls for USD 56.4 million to reduce dilution; the notes' proceeds funded the capped calls and general corporate purposes including capital spending.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_amkr_amkr-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 19–20; 10-Q_2026-09-30_amkr_amkr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 20; 10-Q_2026-09-30_amkr_amkr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 19

In 2025 Amkor replaced its bank and note funding: it entered a USD 1.0 billion senior secured revolving credit facility in May, added a USD 500.0 million term loan tranche in June, issued USD 500.0 million of senior unsecured notes due 2033 in September, and redeemed the remaining 2027 senior notes in July and October with the proceeds. The revolver and term loan are guaranteed by subsidiaries and the notes by Guardian Assets, Inc.; the executed agreements are not supplied.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_amkr_amkr-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 75; 10-K_2025-12-31_amkr_amkr-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 119; 10-K_2025-12-31_amkr_amkr-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 119; 10-K_2025-12-31_amkr_amkr-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 118

A July 2026 Korean cash support agreement allows grants of up to about USD 110 million subject to performance conditions, of which about USD 11 million had been received.
