## Findings

### Bottom line

Reported leverage is high and rising: net debt of USD 5,749 million is 7.10x last-twelve-months reported EBITDA of USD 810 million at 2026-09-30, so creditors depend on liquidity and asset sales rather than earnings to service debt.
Basis: calculation C55; calculation C24; calculation C72; 10-Q_2026-09-30_whr-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 5–7

Cash of USD 1,103 million and USD 2,193 million of undrawn committed facilities, before an unstated borrowing-base cap, are set against only USD 14 million of debt due within twelve months. Cash interest paid for 2026 and a debt table by instrument after the June 2026 refinancing are not printed, so interest cover is stated for fiscal years only.
Basis: calculation C79; 10-Q_2026-09-30_whr-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 20–21; 10-Q_2026-09-30_whr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 54; 10-K_2025-12-31_whr-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 77

### Key findings

Leverage rose because earnings fell while debt stayed near USD 6,852 million. Total debt over reported EBITDA was 5.54x for FY2025 and is 8.46x on the last twelve months, as EBITDA fell from USD 1,176 million to USD 810 million while operating profit fell from USD 838 million to USD 428 million. The reported measures carry one-offs: the last twelve months include gains on disposals of USD 389 million and restructuring costs of USD 154 million. Excluding both, an Analyst Judgement measure, EBITDA is USD 681 million and net debt is 8.44x of it.
Basis: calculation C65; calculation C71; calculation C18; calculation C42; calculation C39; calculation C91; 10-Q_2026-09-30_whr-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 5–7; 10-K_2025-12-31_whr-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 75–77

Interest cover on cash interest paid was 3.36x for FY2025, against 1.35x for FY2024. No cash interest figure is printed for any 2026 period, so cover on a cash basis cannot be stated for the last twelve months. Income statement interest expense of USD 326 million gives a proxy cover of 2.48x. After the June 2026 refinancing, third-quarter interest expense was USD 102 million, an annual rate of USD 408 million, which would be 1.99x of last-twelve-months EBITDA (a derived illustration, not a reported figure).
Basis: calculation C74; calculation C73; calculation C89; calculation C88; calculation C90; 10-K_2025-12-31_whr-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 77; 10-Q_2026-09-30_whr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 5

Cash flow is the pressure point. Operating cash flow was an outflow of USD 826 million in the nine months of 2026, against an outflow of USD 669 million a year earlier, and free cash flow after capital expenditure of USD 255 million was an outflow of USD 1,081 million. Payables fell by USD 306 million and accrued advertising and promotions by USD 318 million, the seasonal unwind of supplier and customer funding that lower earnings did not replace. The February 2026 equity issuance of USD 1,081 million net and asset sales funded the outflow, not operations.
Basis: calculation C85; calculation C87; 10-Q_2026-09-30_whr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 7; 10-Q_2026-03-31_whr-20260331.htm.jsonl, pages 6–7

Liquidity at 2026-09-30 was USD 1,103 million of cash and USD 2,193 million of undrawn commitments, USD 3,296 million together, before any cap from the borrowing base and before letters of credit, neither of which is stated. The new asset-based facility cut undrawn commitments by USD 1,239 million from year-end 2025, when the previous revolver left USD 3,432 million undrawn. Cash of USD 1,239 million at 2026-06-30 included USD 212 million restricted for the July 1, 2026 debt payment.
Basis: calculation C80; calculation C81; calculation C76; 10-Q_2026-09-30_whr-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 20–21; 10-Q_2026-09-30_whr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 54; EX-99.1_2026-08-03_exhibit991pressrelease0630.htm.jsonl, page 8

The year-end 2025 maturity ladder, USD 6,168 million of contractual maturities including USD 586 million in 2026 and USD 702 million in 2027, no longer describes the debt. The June 2026 refinancing repaid the 2026 and 2027 notes and the unsecured revolver with two USD 1,000 million second-lien issues and borrowings under the new facility. The balance sheet now shows USD 14 million of debt due within twelve months, but the instrument-level table after the refinancing is not printed, so the maturity ladder here is drawn at year-end 2025 and the later profile must be rebuilt and labelled as built.
Basis: calculation C61; 10-K_2025-12-31_whr-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 100–101; 10-Q_2026-09-30_whr-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 17–22; 10-Q_2026-09-30_whr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 6

Definitions matter for every ratio here. The issuer states no EBITDA, so reported EBITDA is operating profit plus depreciation and amortisation, which keeps restructuring, impairments and disposal gains in it. Debt is balance-sheet carrying value including notes payable, with lease liabilities and supplier finance kept outside it. Supplier finance obligations of USD 650 million at 2026-09-30 sit in accounts payable, and the 2025 annual report prints USD 763 million at year-end 2025 where the 2026 quarterly reports open at USD 772 million.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_whr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 12; 10-K_2025-12-31_whr-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 86; 10-Q_2026-03-31_whr-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 11
