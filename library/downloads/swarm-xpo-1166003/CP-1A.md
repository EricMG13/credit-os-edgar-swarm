## Findings

### Bottom line

XPO is a listed, asset-based freight operator with no sponsor identified and a creditor-aligned recent record, so governance adds little risk beyond the freight cycle and the unresolved future of the European business.

North American LTL supplies 60.53% of nine-month 2026 revenue of USD 6,823 million and 90.22% of adjusted EBITDA. This year the issuer has repaid USD 275 million of term loans from cash and bought back USD 250 million of shares, and it has never paid a cash dividend.

Control and legal capacity are not established: ownership above the registered-holder count and the credit agreements are not in the sources.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_xpo-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 9; 10-Q_2026-09-30_xpo-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 17; 10-K_2025-12-31_xpo-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 51; calculation C1; calculation C2; Source readiness (CP-0)

### Key findings

North American LTL is the credit. It earned USD 1,070 million of adjusted EBITDA on USD 4,130 million of revenue in nine months 2026, a 25.91% margin against 23.37% a year earlier, helped by yield growth of 4.0% and a fuel surcharge pass-through. It has no union representation in North America and a young fleet (average tractor age 3.7 years), and management says the network has more than 30% spare door capacity, so a volume recovery should lift margin without a matching rise in fixed cost. The weakness is that fuel surcharge revenue is now 20.29% of segment revenue, which inflates revenue and cost together.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_xpo-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 9–10; 10-Q_2026-09-30_xpo-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 24–25; 10-Q_2026-09-30_xpo-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 31; 10-K_2025-12-31_xpo-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 17; calculation C3; calculation C4

European Transportation is the low-margin, saleable part. It earns 4.79% adjusted EBITDA margin over nine months 2026, funds part of its receivables through a fully used securitisation programme, relies on subcontractors, and is covered 85% by collective agreements. The board authorisation to divest it remains in effect with no terms disclosed, so a sale would raise group margin but remove 39.48% of revenue and the proceeds' use is not established.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_xpo-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 9–10; 10-Q_2026-09-30_xpo-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 25; 10-Q_2026-09-30_xpo-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 35; 10-K_2025-12-31_xpo-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 17; 10-K_2025-12-31_xpo-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 38; calculation C1; calculation C3

Financial policy is creditor-aligned on the evidence. The issuer has never paid a cash dividend, funded buybacks from cash while repaying term debt, and used the May 2026 refinancing to move 2028 term loan maturities to 2029 and 2031. No exchange offer, priming or other liability management transaction is described in the sources.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_xpo-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 51; 10-Q_2026-09-30_xpo-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 15–17; calculation C5

Governance has changed shape and the sources do not explain why. The founder, listed as executive chairman in the FY2024 annual report, is absent from the FY2025 annual report, and the chief executive is now also chairman, with a lead independent director in place. A new agreement of 2026-10-29 keeps the chief executive without a fixed term and adds enhanced change-of-control severance.
Basis: 10-K_2024-12-31_xpo-20241231.htm.jsonl, pages 21–22; 10-K_2025-12-31_xpo-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 22; 10-K_2025-12-31_xpo-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 131; 10-Q_2026-09-30_xpo-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 41

A legacy Con-way insurance dispute is the one contingent claim named. A final judgment was entered in May 2026 in line with a USD 35 million accrual and is under consolidated appeal with no schedule; the company says it is unrelated to current operations.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_xpo-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 21

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest case against a low governance risk is cash returned to shareholders against deleveraging. Buybacks of USD 250 million and USD 124 million of tax withholdings on vested awards took 69.65% of nine-month free cash flow of USD 537 million, and half of the USD 750 million repurchase authorisation is still unused. The chairman and chief executive roles are now combined, and the chief executive also sits on the board of QXO, which the FY2024 annual report says the former executive chairman leads, a link the sources do not explain.

The view would change on a debt-funded buyback or distribution, on use of any European sale proceeds for shareholders instead of debt reduction, on a disclosed large holder or control right, or on credit agreements that allow wide restricted payments. A rise in capital expenditure towards the top of the USD 500 million to USD 600 million guide would also reduce the cash left for debt repayment.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_xpo-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-Q_2026-09-30_xpo-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 17; 10-Q_2026-09-30_xpo-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 39; 10-K_2025-12-31_xpo-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 22; calculation C6

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

No acquisition, sponsor transaction or new bond issue is reported for 2026. The financing events are a May 2026 term loan refinancing and voluntary term loan repayments. In May 2026 the issuer entered a USD 500 million term loan A due 2029-05-29 and a USD 385 million term loan B-4 due 2031-02-01, and used the USD 885 million proceeds to repay all of the prior term loan B. Term loan A carries an interest margin of 1.25% over Term SOFR and term loan B-4 1.50%, both with a 0.125% step-down on a leverage test, and both are secured on substantially all assets.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_xpo-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 15–16

Cash prepayments of term loan B totalled USD 275 million year to date to 2026-10-29: USD 100 million before the May refinancing, USD 100 million in July 2026 and USD 75 million in October 2026. The earlier history is senior notes issued in May 2023 (USD 830 million secured and USD 450 million unsecured) and December 2023 (USD 585 million), the February 2025 term loan refinancing, and the Yellow service center purchase in December 2023, which added 26 service centers and two assumed leases.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_xpo-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 17; 10-K_2025-12-31_xpo-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 104–105; 10-K_2024-12-31_xpo-20241231.htm.jsonl, pages 6–7; calculation C5
