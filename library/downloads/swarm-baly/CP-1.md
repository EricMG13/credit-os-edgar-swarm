## Findings

### Bottom line

Bally's Corporation's debt is not covered by its reported earnings: at 2026-06-30 net debt is 8.03x issuer-adjusted last-twelve-month EBITDA, and reported EBITDA is negative over the same twelve months, so repayment depends on asset sales, new financing and the lenders' conditional covenant waiver.
Basis: calculation C16; calculation C05; calculation C06; 10-Q_2026-06-30_baly-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 38

The issuer's adjusted measure is stated after triple-net rent and, in the 2026 half-year, adds back fair-value losses and other non-operating items while removing a sale-leaseback gain, so it is the usable denominator; on it, total debt is 8.79x and cash interest cover is 1.15x. The latest quarterly report states substantial doubt about continuing as a going concern, and the statements carry no adjustment for it. The FY2025 column covers only the Successor period from 2025-02-08, so year-on-year comparisons are not like-for-like.
Basis: calculation C15; calculation C17; 10-Q_2026-06-30_baly-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 10–11; 10-K_2025-12-31_baly-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 107

### Key findings

On the issuer-adjusted measure, total debt to EBITDA was 6.70x at FY2024, 9.42x at FY2025 and 8.79x at 2026-06-30, and net debt to EBITDA was 6.35x, 7.75x and 8.03x. Debt rose faster than earnings because Queen joined the group in February 2025 and the debt of Bally's Intralot was consolidated from October 2025, and the FY2025 ratio sets year-end debt against a Successor period shorter than a year; adding the Predecessor period's adjusted EBITDA lowers FY2025 total leverage to 8.96x. On reported EBITDA the ratios are 28.45x at FY2024 and 111.47x at FY2025 and are not calculable at 2026-06-30 because last-twelve-month reported EBITDA is negative, so the leverage assessment rests on the issuer's add-backs, none of which is stated as realised or unrealised.
Basis: calculation C15; calculation C16; calculation C18; calculation C21; 10-K_2025-12-31_baly-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 80; 10-Q_2026-06-30_baly-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 60–61

In the six months to 2026-06-30 the issuer reaches adjusted EBITDA of USD 247,318 thousand from reported EBITDA of USD 76,346 thousand through net add-backs of USD 170,972 thousand, led by USD 186,005 thousand of non-operating items that include fair-value and debt-extinguishment losses and partly offset by the removal of a USD 105,845 thousand sale-leaseback gain. Transaction costs of USD 28,568 thousand and development costs of USD 33,264 thousand in the half-year are added back in every period shown, so creditors should treat adjusted EBITDA as a ceiling on recurring earnings rather than a settled figure.
Basis: calculation C37; 10-Q_2026-06-30_baly-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 60–61; 10-K_2025-12-31_baly-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 80

Cash interest cover on adjusted EBITDA fell from 1.58x at FY2024 to 1.40x at FY2025 and 1.15x at 2026-06-30, and on reported EBITDA it is 0.37x, 0.12x and -0.10x. Last-twelve-month cash interest of USD 444,640 thousand now absorbs most of adjusted EBITDA of USD 512,810 thousand, and the 2026 term loans carry a margin of 750 bp over term SOFR with a floor of 300 bp, so a modest fall in earnings leaves little room above break-even cover.
Basis: calculation C17; calculation C20; calculation C07; 10-Q_2026-06-30_baly-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 34

Operating cash flow was USD -11,014 thousand in the FY2025 Successor period and USD -265,936 thousand in the first half of 2026, so free cash flow after capital expenditure and capitalised software is USD -532,312 thousand over the last twelve months. The 2026 half-year includes up-front licence payments of USD -98,906 thousand and a working-capital change of USD -159,699 thousand, and it excludes an acquisition of gaming licences of USD -502,000 thousand, so the group is funding development and licences from asset sales and borrowing: the half-year took in USD 685,000 thousand of sale-leaseback proceeds and repaid a net USD 49,087 thousand of debt.
Basis: calculation C33; calculation C39; 10-K_2025-12-31_baly-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 109–110; 10-Q_2026-06-30_baly-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 5–6

Reported liquidity is thin and shrinking. Cash was USD 390,184 thousand at 2026-06-30 against USD 798,423 thousand at FY2025, and the revolver had USD 195.8 million available against USD 588.1 million. The filing says total revolver availability will fall to about USD 319 million from October 2026; read as the commitment limit, and with USD 303,750 thousand drawn, it would leave about USD 15.25 million undrawn if the drawn balance were unchanged and no letters of credit were outstanding. The as-of date falls after that step-down and no supplied document shows the position since. Restricted cash of USD 97,587 thousand, which includes player deposits, is excluded throughout, and the sources do not split consolidated cash between Bally's Corporation's own group and Bally's Intralot.
Basis: calculation C11; calculation C23; 10-Q_2026-06-30_baly-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-06-30_baly-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 33; 10-K_2025-12-31_baly-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 64

The consolidated figures combine two obligor groups that are insulated from each other: Bally's Intralot does not guarantee Bally's Corporation's debt and a breach under the Bally's Corporation revolver has no implications under Bally's Intralot's debt documents, so the principal debt of Bally's Intralot of USD 2,011,800 thousand and the USD 2,898,268 thousand of revolver, term loan and note principal at Bally's Corporation sit in separate groups. Splitting last-twelve-month adjusted EBITDA by segment, which is analyst judgement and not an issuer measure, Bally's Intralot's segments earn USD 391,035 thousand and carry principal debt of 5.14x that amount, while the Bally's Corporation group earns USD 121,775 thousand after triple-net rent and carries principal debt of 23.80x that amount. The consolidated 8.79x therefore understates the leverage faced by lenders to Bally's Corporation, because it counts Bally's Intralot's earnings, which are shared with its 42.1% minority holders and, as far as the sources show, reach Bally's Corporation only through dividends.
Basis: calculation C24; calculation C25; calculation C26; calculation C15; 10-Q_2026-06-30_baly-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 8; 10-Q_2026-06-30_baly-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 11

Carrying value of debt is USD 403,368 thousand, or 8.22%, below contractual principal because fair-value adjustments of USD -351,399 thousand and unamortised discount of USD -51,969 thousand are netted, so leverage measured on principal would be higher than every ratio quoted here. Operating lease liabilities of USD 2,392,267 thousand sit outside debt; adding them to debt and measuring against adjusted EBITDAR gives 9.54x, an analyst judgement because EBITDAR adds back only triple-net rent. At the 2025 year-end a New York gaming licence fee of USD 500,000 thousand and contingent consideration of USD 115,000 thousand, together USD 615,000 thousand, sat in accrued liabilities outside debt and were paid in the first quarter of 2026, and deferred payable arrangements of USD 27.7 million at 2026-06-30, against USD 47.0 million at 2025-12-31, are also carried outside debt.
Basis: calculation C13; calculation C14; calculation C35; calculation C30; calculation C40; 10-Q_2026-06-30_baly-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 31; 10-Q_2026-06-30_baly-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 41; 10-K_2025-12-31_baly-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 162

In the quarter to 2026-06-30 revenue rose 20.49% to USD 792,234 thousand while adjusted EBITDA changed by -4.03% to USD 124,034 thousand, the first quarter to carry the UK gaming tax increase. In May 2026 lenders conditionally waived the net leverage covenant, subject to a minimum liquidity requirement whose level is not stated; a fifth amendment to the credit agreement is dated 2026-07-29 and its terms are not described; in July the company signed a non-binding term sheet for a Bronx loan and in August a letter of intent with a potential equity investor, neither with an amount stated; and on 2026-07-27 Bally's Intralot signed GBP 261.8 million of new facilities and paid a dividend of which USD 13.9 million went to minority holders, lifting pro forma net debt to USD 4,130,416 thousand.
Basis: calculation C34; calculation C31; 10-Q_2026-06-30_baly-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 11; 10-Q_2026-06-30_baly-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 13; 10-Q_2026-06-30_baly-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 37; 10-Q_2026-06-30_baly-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 38; 10-Q_2026-06-30_baly-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 74; EX-99.1_2026-08-14_ex991q22026earningsrelease.htm.jsonl, pages 2–3

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest contrary evidence is operational: revenue is growing, adjusted EBITDA of USD 512,810 thousand over twelve months is above the FY2025 Successor figure of USD 478,009 thousand, the Twin River sale-leaseback released USD 685,000 thousand of cash, and Bally's Intralot's own principal leverage of 5.14x is moderate. A binding Bronx loan or equity sale that extends liquidity beyond the October 2026 step-down, filings after 2026-06-30 showing liquidity above the waiver minimum, or positive last-twelve-month reported EBITDA would each move this view toward manageable leverage; a further fall in quarterly adjusted EBITDA, a revolver drawn close to its reduced commitments, or an adverse change in the waiver would move it the other way.
Basis: calculation C06; calculation C26; 10-Q_2026-06-30_baly-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 11; 10-Q_2026-06-30_baly-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 33

### Limitations

These statements are consolidated and mix a Predecessor and a Successor basis, so FY2024 and FY2025 are not comparable and the short Predecessor period from 2025-01-01 to 2025-02-07 sits outside every column. Reported EBITDA is derived from net loss because the issuer states only an adjusted measure, and no covenant EBITDA, covenant level or minimum liquidity amount is supplied, so no covenant headroom is stated. The latest balance sheet is 128 days before the as-of date, the newest document is 83 days old, and the interim statements are unaudited.
Basis: Source readiness (CP-0); calculation C41; 10-Q_2026-06-30_baly-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 2
