## Findings

### Bottom line

The fundamental credit profile is weak and liquidity-driven: adjusted EBITDA of USD 512,810 thousand covers cash interest only 1.15x, free cash flow is deeply negative, and going-concern doubt means creditors depend on asset monetisation and refinancing rather than internally generated cash.
Basis: calculation B01; calculation B04; Financial foundation (CP-1); 10-Q_2026-06-30_baly-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 11

Scale across casinos, online gaming and lottery, a proven ability to sell property for cash and lenders' willingness to waive support the credit; the October 2026 revolver step-down, an unfunded development programme and net debt of USD 4,116,516 thousand, or USD 4,130,416 thousand pro forma for the Bally's Intralot dividend paid on 2026-07-27 with pro forma net leverage not established, weaken it.
Basis: calculation B02; calculation B06; Financial foundation (CP-1); 10-Q_2026-06-30_baly-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 33; 10-Q_2026-06-30_baly-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 11

### Key findings

Liquidity is the binding weakness and it is shrinking on a known date. Cash was USD 390,184 thousand and the revolver had USD 195.8 million available at 2026-06-30, together USD 585,984 thousand, and the filing says total revolver availability falls to about USD 319 million from October 2026, which would leave about USD 15.25 million undrawn if read as the commitment limit with the drawn balance unchanged. The company states that without its planned financings it does not project meeting the minimum liquidity requirement of the waiver or the reinstated leverage covenant, so a revolver whose access depends on a test creditors cannot see is the first thing that breaks; pro forma cash after the 2026-07-27 dividend is not established.
Basis: calculation B06; calculation B07; 10-Q_2026-06-30_baly-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 11; 10-Q_2026-06-30_baly-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 33; 10-Q_2026-06-30_baly-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 38; Financial foundation (CP-1)

Operations do not fund the business, so every obligation is met from asset sales or borrowing. After cash interest, capital expenditure and capitalised software, adjusted EBITDA leaves USD -128,393 thousand, free cash flow over the last twelve months is USD -532,312 thousand, and capital spending absorbs 38.33% of adjusted EBITDA before rent timing, working capital or licence payments. In the first half of 2026 the group received USD 685,000 thousand from the Twin River sale-leaseback and repaid a net USD 49,087 thousand of debt, which shows that deleveraging so far comes from monetising property into fixed rent rather than from earnings.
Basis: calculation B03; calculation B04; calculation B05; calculation B15; Financial foundation (CP-1)

Leverage is high on any measure and the parent group carries the worse half. Total debt is 8.79x issuer-adjusted EBITDA and net debt 8.03x, with pro forma net leverage not established, and reported EBITDA is negative over the last twelve months, so no reported-basis leverage can be stated; splitting segment earnings, which is analyst judgement, puts the principal debt of Bally's Corporation at 23.80x its share of adjusted EBITDA against 5.14x at Bally's Intralot. Lenders to the parent group therefore rely on property monetisation and parent-level financing rather than on the Intralot earnings that sit in the consolidated ratio.
Basis: calculation B02; calculation B10; Financial foundation (CP-1)

Refinancing capacity is weaker for the unsecured notes than for the secured loans, and the real wall is 2029. The disclosed fair value of the 2029 notes is 69.64% of principal and of the 2031 notes 63.00%, against 99.14% for the 2026 term loans, and the term loans mature on 2029-03-01 if the 2029 notes remain outstanding, so USD 1,859,518 thousand, or 37.87% of principal, falls on one refinancing date; 29.14% of principal falls due by the end of 2029. The unsecured notes can only be refinanced on better terms if earnings recover or an equity investor joins, which is why the Bronx financing outcome matters beyond the Bronx.
Basis: calculation B08; calculation B09; 10-Q_2026-06-30_baly-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 31; 10-Q_2026-06-30_baly-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 32; 10-Q_2026-06-30_baly-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 37

Revenue is durable at the regional casinos but margin is not. Casinos & Resorts revenue rose 2.0% and segment Adjusted EBITDAR 3.4% in the second quarter of 2026 and rated visitation rose 4.3%, yet the adjusted EBITDA margin fell from 20.71% in the first half of 2025 to 15.98% in the first half of 2026 and quarterly adjusted EBITDA changed by -4.03% on revenue growth of 20.49%. Triple-net rent takes 29.06% of Adjusted EBITDAR and the UK gaming tax increase cost about USD 39 million gross in the quarter with close to 65% offset by management action, so fixed claims and regulation, not demand, set the margin.
Basis: calculation B11; calculation B12; calculation B13; calculation B14; Business & governance (CP-1A); Financial foundation (CP-1)

Financial policy and governance are average at best: a controlling holder with 67.1% of the common stock has received no dividends and no documented fees, but no sponsor equity support is documented while the company buys more Bally's Intralot shares and pursues the evoke offer. Policy therefore adds growth demands to a business that cannot fund its present commitments, and creditor protection rests on documents and lender waivers rather than on sponsor support.
Basis: calculation B17; Business & governance (CP-1A); 10-Q_2026-06-30_baly-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 5; 10-Q_2026-06-30_baly-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 28

In a downside, the first break is the October 2026 revolver step-down and the minimum liquidity test, not an operating collapse. If the Bronx loan and equity do not close, development spending and cash interest continue to draw on a smaller revolver and falling cash; a failed liquidity test ends the waiver and reinstates the leverage covenant, which the company already projects it could not meet, and the filings do not state how the term loans and notes cross-default. The observable triggers are revolver availability, cash, waiver status and the Bronx financing, and the analysis does not state a run-out date because no forecast is supplied.
Basis: calculation B06; calculation B07; calculation B16; 10-Q_2026-06-30_baly-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 11; 10-Q_2026-06-30_baly-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 38; 10-Q_2026-06-30_baly-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 66

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a weak view is that creditors and landlords keep funding the company: lenders conditionally waived the leverage covenant, GLPI is advancing Chicago construction costs, the Twin River sale-leaseback realised USD 700.0 million, Bally's Intralot has signed GBP 261.8 million of new term facilities at 5.14x principal leverage on segment earnings, revenue rose 20.49% in the quarter and casinos are growing slowly but positively. A binding Bronx loan with third-party equity, an extension of the revolver beyond October 2026, an equity sale or a second property monetisation at a similar price would move the view toward average; termination of the waiver, availability near USD 15.25 million, a failure to refinance the 2029 notes or a further fall in quarterly adjusted EBITDA would move it to a distressed view.
Basis: calculation B07; calculation B10; calculation B13; 10-Q_2026-06-30_baly-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 37; 10-Q_2026-06-30_baly-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 39; 10-Q_2026-06-30_baly-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 40

### Limitations

- The downside is described qualitatively with no stress figures and no run-out date, so the weak liquidity conclusion rests on direction and on the known October 2026 step-down, and a quantified forecast could bring the date forward or push it back.
- The outlook for UK online trading and lottery contract wins comes from management statements in the second-quarter release, so a stronger recovery than stated would lift the margin finding and a weaker one would deepen it.
- Casinos are assessed from segment totals because property-level results are not supplied, so a weak property inside a stable segment would not show and the revenue-durability finding could be too favourable.
- The ranking of strengths and weaknesses is analyst judgement among supported drivers, and a documented revolver extension or Bronx financing would reorder the top weaknesses rather than add new ones.
- The parent and Bally's Intralot comparison depends on the issuer's segment boundaries, so an intragroup charge or cost allocation not shown would shift the finding that the parent group carries the weaker credit.

### Strengths and weaknesses

The strongest credit supports are scale and spread of earnings, asset value and a willing lender group. Revenue is USD 2,957,917 thousand over the last twelve months across US casinos, online gaming and lottery, so no single property or product dominates; the Twin River sale-leaseback shows that real estate can be sold for USD 700.0 million, and lenders have waived rather than enforced.
Basis: calculation B13; calculation B17; 10-Q_2026-06-30_baly-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 39; 10-Q_2026-06-30_baly-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 38; Financial foundation (CP-1)

The weakest points are liquidity, cash conversion and the unfunded development programme. The revolver is the only undrawn source stated for the parent group and it steps down in October 2026, free cash flow is USD -532,312 thousand, rent absorbs 29.06% of Adjusted EBITDAR, and the Bronx project of USD 4.0 billion has only a non-binding term sheet and a letter of intent. Opportunities that would improve credit quality are a binding Bronx financing, the 2027 Chicago opening and the 2028 Las Vegas stadium, and threats are a failed liquidity test, a further UK tax or regulatory change and a Bally's Intralot acquisition that adds debt.
Basis: calculation B04; calculation B07; calculation B11; calculation B16; EX-99.1_2026-08-14_ex991q22026earningsrelease.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-06-30_baly-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 11

### Company and ownership in credit terms

Bally's Corporation is a casino, interactive and lottery group whose 2025 acquisitions of Queen and a controlling 57.9% of Bally's Intralot lifted revenue to USD 2,957,917 thousand over twelve months, with casinos at 50.44% and Bally's Intralot at 41.14% of first-half 2026 revenue. Cash-flow visibility is moderate because casino revenue is regional and discretionary while lottery contracts are multi-year, but the group is held by a controlling holder with 67.1% of the common stock and by two separate obligor groups, so visibility at the parent group is lower than the consolidated figures suggest.
Basis: calculation B13; calculation B17; Business & governance (CP-1A)

### Revenue durability, margins and cash conversion

Revenue visibility is highest at the regional casinos and at lottery contracts and lowest in UK online gaming, where tax rose from 21% to 40% from 2026-04-01 and about USD 39 million of the quarter's earnings was hit before offsets. Operating leverage is high because rent, interest and development spending are fixed, so a small fall in segment earnings removes the cushion after cash interest; capital spending of 38.33% of adjusted EBITDA is only partly discretionary because Chicago is contractually committed.
Basis: calculation B03; calculation B05; calculation B14; Financial foundation (CP-1); EX-99.1_2026-08-14_ex991q22026earningsrelease.htm.jsonl, page 3

### Competitive and external forces

Competitive rivalry is regional, and management named elevated competition in Atlantic City and East Saint Louis as a pressure in the second quarter; entry and substitution risk from online play is not evidenced beyond the company's own strategy of expanding interactive gaming; buyers in lottery are public bodies under long contracts, which supports visibility but needs up-front investment; and the landlord GLPI is the most powerful supplier because rent is fixed and, after a credit agreement refinancing, the Chicago lease cross-defaults to the master leases. Regulation matters to credit through the UK gaming tax increase and through the Rhode Island regulatory agreement, which caps distributions and new debt above a leverage of 5.50x on its own definition; foreign exchange matters because Bally's Intralot's debt is in sterling and euros.
Basis: calculation B14; Business & governance (CP-1A); EX-99.1_2026-08-14_ex991q22026earningsrelease.htm.jsonl, page 3; EX-99.1_2026-08-14_ex991q22026earningsrelease.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-K_2025-12-31_baly-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 5; 10-K_2025-12-31_baly-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 16; 10-K_2025-12-31_baly-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 49

### Financial profile and credit quality

The financial profile is weak on liquidity, free cash flow and margin stability, average on scale and diversification, and average on financial policy because no value leaves the group but no equity enters it. The trend is negative: adjusted EBITDA margin fell to 15.98% in the first half of 2026 from 20.71% a year earlier, net leverage on adjusted EBITDA is 8.03x with pro forma net leverage not established, and the key missing datapoints are covenant levels, the minimum liquidity requirement and any forecast.
Basis: calculation B02; calculation B12; Financial foundation (CP-1); Business & governance (CP-1A)

### Outlook

Over the next one to two years the Chicago permanent casino targets an early 2027 opening, the Las Vegas ballpark is due before the 2028 season, the revolver steps down in October 2026 and the Bronx financing must close; trading tailwinds are rated visitation, UK online growth and new lottery contracts, headwinds are the UK tax and rent growth. Over three to five years the Bronx project of USD 4.0 billion, due to open by 2030, decides whether the group becomes self-funding or carries a second large unfunded commitment.
Basis: calculation B14; calculation B16; EX-99.1_2026-08-14_ex991q22026earningsrelease.htm.jsonl, page 2; EX-99.1_2026-08-14_ex991q22026earningsrelease.htm.jsonl, page 3

### Overall credit view

Overall, Bally's presents a not fully assessable business profile supported by scale, diversification and asset value, but credit quality is constrained by liquidity, negative free cash flow and fixed landlord claims. Cash-flow durability appears weak because capital spending and cash interest together exceed adjusted EBITDA. Refinancing capacity is likely to depend on the Bronx financing, a revolver extension and the pricing of the 2029 refinancing. The primary downside path is a failed liquidity test after the October 2026 step-down. Further analysis would require covenant levels, the minimum liquidity requirement, a forecast and filings after 2026-08-14. Fundamental credit review completed with limitations. Top risk: liquidity.
Basis: calculation B03; calculation B06; calculation B07; Financial foundation (CP-1); Business & governance (CP-1A); Source readiness (CP-0)
