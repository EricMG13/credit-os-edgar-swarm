### Limitations

- The credit agreement definitions and compliance certificates were not supplied, so covenant headroom is not calculable and the leverage-tolerance view rests on the quarterly maximums alone; a definition that adds back more earnings than reported EBITDA would make the position stronger than shown.
- Antares Vision earnings before 2026-03-31 and the fourth-quarter 2025 release were not supplied, so the company's 2.5x cannot be rebuilt; if it were confirmed, the house ratio would overstate leverage by about 0.51x and the cautious view would soften.
- The credit agreement summary does not date the completion of the Antares Vision purchase, so the covenant steps are placed by reading it as 2026-03-31; if the first phase of 2025-12-15 counted instead, each maximum would fall one quarter earlier, to 4.75x at 2026-09-30 and 4.25x from Q1 2027, and the step-down risk would arrive sooner.
- Agency ratings are stated only as of 2025-12-31, before the Antares Vision second phase of 2026-03-31, so the market-access conclusion rests on a dated opinion; a later downgrade would weaken the refinancing view.
- The fourth-quarter estimates are derived from approximate outlook ranges and assume Antares Vision adds the same sales as in Q3, so they show direction only; a firmer outlook could reverse the implied fall in security sales.
- Segment results exist on two structures and FY2025 is not recast, so the margin trend of the payments and inspection business is read from nine-month comparisons only and the fourth-quarter security estimate assumes that segment is unchanged; a recast could change the size of the decline and of the implied fourth-quarter fall.

### Competitive forces

Rivalry is real, since the company calls its markets highly competitive and says it is a principal competitor in most of them, but it cites technological differentiation in anti-counterfeiting materials and authentication software, which supports pricing but not volume in a weak cycle. ### Downside scenario

The realistic downside starts with a banknote award that slips out of the year while payments and inspection volumes stay weak. Security and authentication carries the profit gain, so a delay cuts currency volumes first; its nine-month operating profit rose USD 40.4 million, and if that gain were lost with net debt unchanged, last-twelve-months reported EBITDA would be USD 333.0 million, net debt over reported EBITDA would rise to 3.40x and EBITDA cover of cash interest would fall to 5.02x. Liquidity would hold, with USD 821.2 million available and no bank maturity before 2030-12-15, and interest would stay covered, so resilience appears sufficient to avoid liquidity distress. The year-end deleveraging target would be missed while the permitted maximum steps down to 4.75x at 2026-12-31, and floating-rate debt at 60.94% would add cost if rates rose; management levers are capital expenditure timing, the dividend of USD 0.18 per share a quarter, cost actions and integration savings, and whether these are enough against a covenant whose definition is not supplied is not established.

### Overall credit view

Overall, Crane NXT presents an adequate business profile supported by specialised security and authentication technology, an order book covering about two years and strong cash generation, but credit quality is constrained by acquisition-led leverage of 3.03x reported EBITDA, a shrinking payments and inspection segment and award concentration. Cash-flow durability appears adequate because free cash flow is 63.07% of reported EBITDA, although it depends on lumpy awards. Refinancing capacity is likely to depend on deleveraging towards the company's reported 2.5x on a verified basis, the agencies' view after the Antares Vision financing and covenant headroom through the step-downs. The primary downside path is a delayed banknote award that holds leverage above 3.03x as the covenant maximum falls. Further analysis would require the credit agreement and compliance certificates, Antares Vision earnings before 2026-03-31, the fourth-quarter 2025 release and current ratings.
