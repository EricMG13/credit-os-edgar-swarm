## Findings

### Bottom line
Oceaneering carries little financial leverage and holds more cash than debt, so creditors face low near-term refinancing and coverage risk: debt is 1.27x last-twelve-month reported EBITDA, cash exceeds it by USD 243.52 million and the only maturity is the senior notes due in 2034.

The July 2026 refinancing was leverage-neutral but not cost-neutral: principal stayed at USD 500 million, the coupon rose by 87.5 bp and a USD 19.03 million loss on extinguishment was booked, which is why reported EBITDA of USD 385.96 million trails adjusted EBITDA of USD 408.03 million. Free cash flow is seasonal and guided lower for the fourth quarter, so the cash cushion is the point to monitor.
Basis: calculation N4; calculation N5; calculation N7; calculation N11; calculation N15; Source readiness (CP-0); 10-Q_2026-09-30_oii-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 16

### Key findings
- Leverage is low and net debt is negative. Debt at carrying value of USD 491.42 million is 1.27x reported EBITDA and 1.20x adjusted EBITDA for the last twelve months, and cash of USD 734.94 million leaves net cash of USD 243.52 million, up from USD 15.51 million at the end of 2024 and USD 201.46 million at the end of 2025. The improvement came from cash build, not repayment, because the debt is one bullet note whose carrying value moves only with the amortization of costs.
Basis: calculation N4; calculation N5; calculation N7; calculation N10; 10-Q_2026-09-30_oii-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-09-30_oii-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 16
- Reported EBITDA understates earnings momentum because of the refinancing. Fiscal 2025 reported EBITDA of USD 404.23 million compares with USD 385.96 million for the last twelve months, yet adjusted EBITDA rose from USD 401.47 million to USD 408.03 million; the USD 22.07 million gap between the two measures is mostly the USD 19.03 million loss on extinguishment of debt booked in the third quarter.
Basis: calculation N1; calculation N2; calculation N4; calculation N5; calculation N16; EX-99.1_2026-10-21_exhibit_991x8kx3qx2026.htm.jsonl, page 10
- Coverage is ample on every measure the sources allow. Fiscal 2025 reported EBITDA covered cash interest paid 12.63x, last-twelve-month EBITDA covers interest expense 10.91x, and the annual coupon on the new notes of USD 34.38 million is covered 11.23x by reported EBITDA. Cash interest paid is not stated for interim periods, so the coupon cover is the only forward-looking measure available.
Basis: calculation N4; calculation N11; calculation N12; 10-K_2025-12-31_oii-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 108; 10-K_2025-12-31_oii-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 83; 10-Q_2026-09-30_oii-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 3
- The refinancing extended maturities at a higher cost. Principal stayed at USD 500 million, the coupon rose by 87.5 bp and annual coupon interest rises by USD 4.38 million, while the revolving facility grew from USD 215 million to USD 345 million and now matures in 2031; no debt falls due before 2034.
Basis: calculation N11; 10-Q_2026-09-30_oii-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 18; 10-Q_2026-09-30_oii-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 19; 10-K_2025-12-31_oii-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 64

Basis: calculation N13; calculation N15; EX-99.1_2026-10-21_exhibit_991x8kx3qx2026.htm.jsonl, page 10; 10-Q_2026-09-30_oii-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-Q_2026-09-30_oii-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 4

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view
The strongest evidence against the benign view is that net cash is a seasonal high that may not persist. Cash fell by USD 81.40 million in the first quarter of 2026, nine-month free cash flow of USD 97.43 million came after receivables and contract assets absorbed USD 139.06 million, so a slip in collections would cut cash directly, and guidance implies fourth-quarter free cash flow of only USD 2.58 million to USD 22.58 million. Management also continues to repurchase shares and guides Offshore Projects Group operating income lower for the fourth quarter.

The view would change if a cash drain of the size of the net cash, USD 243.52 million, became plausible: a fourth-quarter free cash flow below the guided range, a further receivables build, or debt-funded repurchases or acquisitions would be the observable signs. It would strengthen if year-end cash holds above the current balance.
Basis: calculation N13; calculation N15; calculation N18; calculation N19; EX-99.1_2026-10-21_exhibit_991x8kx3qx2026.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-09-30_oii-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 4

### Limitations
- Cash interest paid is stated only for fiscal years and rounded to USD millions, so interim cash interest cover is not stated and the interest-expense ratio is an accrual proxy that includes non-cash amortization; coverage conclusions for the last twelve months are approximate.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_oii-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 108; 10-K_2024-12-31_oii-20241231.htm.jsonl, page 109; calculation N12
- Fiscal 2024 EBITDA is calculated with an amortization figure printed only to USD 0.1 million and the issuer states no fiscal 2024 adjusted EBITDA, so the year-on-year trend to fiscal 2025 carries that imprecision and has no adjusted comparison for 2024.
Basis: calculation N3; 10-K_2025-12-31_oii-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 66; 10-K_2024-12-31_oii-20241231.htm.jsonl, page 109
- The security, guarantors and ranking of the senior notes are not stated, so no ranked debt stack is drawn and the notes are not placed against the secured revolving facility; leverage is on total carrying-value debt without seniority splits.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_oii-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 16; 10-Q_2026-09-30_oii-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 18; 10-Q_2026-09-30_oii-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 19
- Total letters of credit are not stated after 2025-12-31, so liquidity is cash plus undrawn commitments and is not shown net of any bonding or self-insurance lines outside the facility.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_oii-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 109; 10-Q_2026-09-30_oii-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 6
