## Findings

### Bottom line

Reported leverage is low and the maturity ladder is long, so the balance sheet puts little near-term pressure on creditors: total debt was 1.28x last-twelve-months EBITDA at 26 September 2026 and the only drawn debt, the senior notes, falls due in 2034.
Basis: calculation K77; 10-Q_2026-09-26_dorm.htm.jsonl, pages 3, 7, 26

Three qualifications matter. EBITDA is derived because the issuer reports none, and it includes a USD 56.7 million goodwill impairment, so leverage excluding it would be 1.10x. Customer-sponsored receivable sales are a debt-like funding source of USD 970.9 million outside the balance sheet; adding them to debt puts leverage near 3.57x on EBITDA before factoring costs. The senior notes have paid no coupon yet, so last-twelve-months cash interest understates the run rate, and whether the notes are secured is not established.
Basis: calculation K81; calculation K84; calculation K93; calculation K94; 10-K_2025-12-31_dorm.htm.jsonl, page 71; 10-Q_2026-09-26_dorm.htm.jsonl, pages 7, 26; Source readiness (CP-0)

### Leverage and coverage trajectory

Total debt to EBITDA fell from 2.14x at the end of fiscal 2023 to 1.38x at the end of fiscal 2024 and 1.24x at the end of fiscal 2025, and was 1.28x on a last-twelve-months basis at 26 September 2026; net debt to EBITDA moved from 2.00x to 1.21x, 1.10x and 0.79x. Carrying-value debt fell from USD 575.6 million to USD 440.7 million, while EBITDA rose from USD 269.5 million to USD 355.3 million and then slipped to USD 344.8 million on a last-twelve-months basis; that figure still carries the goodwill impairment of the fourth quarter of 2025. Interest cover on cash interest paid rose from 5.44x to 17.49x, but the last-twelve-months denominator holds no coupon on the senior notes, which first pay in December 2026. The notes carry a fixed 6.25% on USD 450.0 million, an annual coupon of USD 28.1 million, against a floating rate of 5.07% on the term loan at the end of 2025, so cash interest will step up although debt is unchanged; last-twelve-months EBITDA covers that coupon 12.26x. The creditor consequence is a comfortable cushion that the derived EBITDA and the coupon step both qualify.
Basis: calculation K62; calculation K77; calculation K79; calculation K85; calculation K86; 10-K_2025-12-31_dorm.htm.jsonl, pages 71, 85; 10-Q_2026-09-26_dorm.htm.jsonl, page 7

### Cash-flow quality

Operating cash flow was USD 208.8 million in fiscal 2023, USD 231.0 million in fiscal 2024 and USD 113.6 million in fiscal 2025, when inventories absorbed USD 250.4 million of cash to meet customer demand and pay higher tariffs; after USD 38.0 million of capital expenditure, free cash flow converted only 0.21 of EBITDA. In the nine months to 26 September 2026 operating cash flow rebounded to USD 260.9 million as inventories released USD 177.6 million and USD 99.7 million of tariff refunds and related interest were received, so last-twelve-months free cash flow of USD 269.2 million (conversion 0.78) rests on an inventory unwind and refunds that may not repeat. The credit implication is that cash generation is lumpy and tariff-dependent: net debt fell by USD 116.8 million between the end of 2025 and 26 September 2026 even after USD 113.8 million of share repurchases, which shows how much cash management can return to shareholders.
Basis: calculation K56; calculation K57; calculation K92; 10-K_2024-12-31_dorm.htm.jsonl, page 77; 10-K_2025-12-31_dorm.htm.jsonl, page 72; 10-Q_2026-03-28_dorm.htm.jsonl, pages 3, 4; 10-Q_2026-06-27_dorm.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-Q_2026-09-26_dorm.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-K_2025-12-31_dorm.htm.jsonl, page 63; 10-Q_2026-09-26_dorm.htm.jsonl, page 28

### Liquidity and maturities

At 26 September 2026 cash was USD 167.0 million and USD 798.9 million of the USD 800.0 million revolver was available after USD 1.1 million of letters of credit, for liquidity of USD 965.9 million against total debt of USD 440.7 million. Drawn debt is the senior notes alone, due in 2034; the revolver matures in 2031 and is undrawn, and the term loan that was due in October 2027 under the old agreement was repaid in June 2026. The credit implication is that no principal falls due before 2031, so refinancing is not the near-term risk; the exposure lies in the working-capital programs below and in covenant terms that are not supplied.
Basis: calculation K95; calculation K87; 10-Q_2026-09-26_dorm.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 7, 26; 10-K_2025-12-31_dorm.htm.jsonl, pages 62, 85

### Receivable sales as non-debt funding

Customer-sponsored programs let the company sell receivables without recourse: USD 1,387.8 million were sold in fiscal 2025, 65.15% of net sales, and USD 843.6 million in the nine months to 26 September 2026, 51.59% of net sales and 18.1% less than a year earlier, at a cost of USD 36.1 million recorded in selling, general, and administrative expenses. At 26 September 2026 USD 970.9 million of additional receivables would otherwise have been outstanding. The programs are run by financial institutions for customers, can be modified or terminated, and are priced off Term SOFR, so a withdrawal would move receivables back onto the balance sheet while higher tariff payments are made before the goods are sold; the USD 970.9 million is about equal to total liquidity of USD 965.9 million. Counting the receivables as debt-like funding gives 3.57x on EBITDA before factoring costs (Analyst Judgement); the reported ratios exclude them.
Basis: calculation K84; calculation K90; calculation K91; calculation K94; 10-K_2025-12-31_dorm.htm.jsonl, pages 33, 62; 10-Q_2026-09-26_dorm.htm.jsonl, pages 6, 25, 26

### Subsequent events and one-offs

Tariff refunds are the main one-off. After the Supreme Court ruling of 20 February 2026, which the fiscal 2025 report records as a subsequent event, the company recorded refunds of USD 98.1 million in 2026, of which USD 2.1 million was still receivable at 26 September 2026, and received USD 99.7 million in cash including interest; the second-quarter release shows a USD 44.4 million benefit to second-quarter gross profit and USD 11.3 million to six-month gross profit. These are one event in different roles, kept together in the definition conflict register. Period EBITDA is not adjusted for them because the issuer states no adjusted measure, so second-quarter and nine-month earnings and operating cash flow are flattered and last-twelve-months coverage should be read with that in mind. No event after 26 September 2026 that changes net debt is stated in the sources.
Basis: calculation K92; 10-K_2025-12-31_dorm.htm.jsonl, page 101; 10-Q_2026-09-26_dorm.htm.jsonl, pages 9, 28; EX99.1_2026-06-27_dorm_dorm-20260627xexx991-htm.htm.jsonl, page 9

### Data quality and definition risks

Four points qualify every ratio. EBITDA is derived as income from operations plus depreciation and amortization because the issuer reports none, and the annual figures include accretion in depreciation. Fiscal 2022 was a 53-week year, so growth in fiscal 2023 compares unlike lengths. The first-quarter report prints other income with a negative sign and the third-quarter report separates interest expense from interest income; both were restated to one basis and tie to the printed totals. The covenant tests, secured net leverage not above 3.50x and interest coverage not below 2.00x, are summarised without their definitions, so no headroom is stated and none can be inferred from the ratios in this report.
Basis: calculation K20; 10-K_2025-12-31_dorm.htm.jsonl, page 72; 10-K_2024-12-31_dorm.htm.jsonl, page 57; 10-Q_2026-09-26_dorm.htm.jsonl, pages 5, 7, 26; 10-Q_2026-03-28_dorm.htm.jsonl, page 2
