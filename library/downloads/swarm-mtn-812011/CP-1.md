## Findings

### Bottom line

Reported leverage at Vail Resorts rose to 4.23x total debt to EBITDA at the 2026-07-31 year end from 3.70x a year earlier while debt was flat, and cash interest cover fell to 4.04x from 5.52x, leaving creditors less cushion after a record-low snowfall season.
Basis: 10-K_2026-07-31_mtn-20260731.htm.jsonl, pages 101, 103; 10-K_2026-07-31_mtn-20260731.htm.jsonl, pages 112, 132, 133, 135; calculation C25; calculation C21; calculation C29

The ratios use carrying-value debt and the issuer's own Total Reported EBITDA with no add-backs. Net of cash the year-end ratio is 3.92x, and the effect of the dividend payable on 2026-10-27 is not established. At the April 2026 quarter-end the ratio was 4.03x on last-twelve-months EBITDA, using cash that the issuer later said was overstated, and no cash interest cover can be stated for that date.
Basis: 10-K_2026-07-31_mtn-20260731.htm.jsonl, pages 112, 172; 10-Q_2026-04-30_mtn-20260430.htm.jsonl, page 49; calculation C26; calculation C23; Source readiness (CP-0)

### Credit analysis by dimension

Leverage rose because EBITDA fell, not because debt grew. Total debt at carrying value was USD 3,194.274 million at the 2025 year end and USD 3,186.368 million at the 2026 year end, while Total Reported EBITDA fell 12.72% to USD 753.042 million as skier visits fell 13.4% in a record-low snowfall season. The composition of debt changed more than its size: the convertible notes were repaid in cash at maturity on 2026-01-02, funded by a delayed draw term loan and cash on hand, and USD 180.000 million was drawn on the revolver at the year end against nothing a year earlier. The consequence for creditors is that the same debt is now serviced by a smaller and more seasonal EBITDA base, which moves total debt to EBITDA from 3.70x to 4.23x.
Basis: 10-K_2026-07-31_mtn-20260731.htm.jsonl, pages 101, 112, 114, 132, 156, 162; 10-K_2025-07-31_mtn-20250731.htm.jsonl, page 95; calculation C30; calculation C25; Source readiness (CP-0)

Interest cover fell from 5.52x to 4.04x because cash interest paid rose 19.15% to USD 186.308 million while EBITDA fell. After capital expenditures of USD 231.625 million, EBITDA covered cash interest only 2.80x against 4.01x a year earlier, so a larger share of earnings now goes to lenders before any reinvestment. The amended credit agreement lowered the margin on the USD 1,423.125 million drawn under it, which should ease interest cost, but the filings print no figure for the saving.
Basis: 10-K_2026-07-31_mtn-20260731.htm.jsonl, pages 114, 133, 135, 158; calculation C31; calculation C29; calculation C40

Free cash flow after capital expenditures was USD 248.001 million in fiscal 2026 against USD 319.679 million in fiscal 2025, and it did not cover USD 317.131 million of dividends and USD 45.000 million of share repurchases, leaving a shortfall of USD 114.130 million met from cash and the revolver. Operating cash flow fell USD 75.244 million, and deferred revenue absorbed USD 39.564 million of cash against a USD 26.436 million inflow a year earlier. For creditors the dividend is the flexible use of cash, and the issuer states that it can respond to a weak season by managing capital expenditures, variable operating expense and the dividend.
Basis: 10-K_2026-07-31_mtn-20260731.htm.jsonl, pages 117, 135; calculation C17; calculation C34; calculation C32

Liquidity at the 2026 year end was USD 231.349 million of cash plus USD 513.300 million of undrawn revolver commitments, USD 744.649 million in total, against USD 83.908 million of debt due within one year, and no large maturity falls due before fiscal 2030, when USD 1,739.211 million matures. The cash balance sits near the seasonal low, because the issuer states that cash is lower at the end of the first and fourth fiscal quarters than at the end of the second and third. USD 37.112 million of short-term certificates of deposit sit outside the cash figure and would add to liquidity if counted.
Basis: 10-K_2026-07-31_mtn-20260731.htm.jsonl, pages 102, 112, 114, 132, 167; calculation C35

Customer prepayments are a float that funds operations before winter revenue arrives. Short-term deferred revenue, mostly pass products sold before the season, was USD 566.5 million at the 2026 year end against USD 602.1 million a year earlier, a decline of 5.91%, and pass sales for the 2026/2027 season through 2026-09-18 were down in units by approximately 12% and in dollars by approximately 6%. A second weak pass campaign would therefore reduce the cash available to service debt in fiscal 2027, and the Epic Coverage refund option adds to that exposure if another weak season follows.
Basis: 10-K_2026-07-31_mtn-20260731.htm.jsonl, pages 101, 148, 150; calculation C44

Three data limits qualify every ratio. The issuer states that cash at both 2026 interim quarter-ends was overstated by USD 38.8 million and USD 38.4 million because certificates of deposit were classified as cash, so interim ratios use cash as reported and are labelled. The EBITDA measure is the issuer's own and excludes the USD 19.239 million fair-value charge on the Park City contingent consideration, which is a participating payment to the landlord, so a ratio that counted it would be higher. The issuer's net debt of USD 2,917.907 million also deducts USD 37.112 million of certificates of deposit that this module keeps outside cash.
Basis: 10-K_2026-07-31_mtn-20260731.htm.jsonl, pages 112, 172; 10-Q_2026-04-30_mtn-20260430.htm.jsonl, page 2; calculation C42; calculation C41; Source readiness (CP-0)

The strongest contrary evidence is that the weakness is weather-driven and the maturity profile is comfortable. The issuer reports compliance with all financial covenants at 2026-07-31 and expects compliance for the next twelve months, almost all of its debt falls due in fiscal 2030 or later, and fiscal 2026 was a record-low snowfall year. That view would win if pass sales stabilise and EBITDA returns toward its fiscal 2025 level, which would bring leverage back toward its fiscal 2025 level. The filings print no covenant levels, so the margin of safety under the covenants cannot be measured here.
Basis: 10-K_2026-07-31_mtn-20260731.htm.jsonl, pages 101, 116, 120, 167; Source readiness (CP-0)
