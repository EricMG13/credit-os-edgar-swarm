## Findings

### Bottom line
Flutter is an industry-leading operator by size, but its creditor risk is concentrated in regulation and contingent claims outside management's control: higher gaming taxes, the Brazilian ban and a Fox option over 18.6% of FanDuel priced at USD 4.8 billion and rising 5% a year.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_flut-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 29; 10-Q_2026-09-30_flut-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 34; 10-Q_2026-09-30_flut-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 48–49

Revenue is broad, with the United States the largest at 39.20% of nine-month 2026 revenue, so no single market is dominant, and no shareholder is named as a controller. Against that, nine-month adjusted EBITDA fell 25.53% and a new chief executive took over on 2026-10-01; the company has paused buybacks and extended its credit facilities, which supports creditors.
Basis: calculation C02; calculation C07; EX-99.1_2026-08-05_d127369dex991.htm.jsonl, page 1; 10-Q_2026-09-30_flut-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 75

### Key findings

- Revenue is diversified by geography but the two biggest 2026 shocks hit different regions at once. The United States was 39.20% of nine-month 2026 revenue and the United Kingdom and Ireland region 22.07%, while Brazil was only 1.65%; the UK remote gaming duty rose from 21% to 40% in April 2026 and Brazil was banned on 2026-09-25, so scale cushions any single shock but not simultaneous ones.
Basis: calculation C02; 10-Q_2026-09-30_flut-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 48–49
- Earnings erosion comes mainly from taxes and investment, with player numbers also lower after the India and Brazil closures. Nine-month adjusted EBITDA fell 25.53% while revenue grew, as the UK duty increase cut International margin and US earnings were reduced by marketing for prediction markets, new markets and the World Cup, so debt service depends on cost programmes the company sizes at more than USD 300 million and on the UK restructuring.
Basis: calculation C07; calculation C08; calculation C12; EX-99.1_2026-11-04_d141532dex991.htm.jsonl, pages 1, 4
- The Fox option is the largest contingent claim outside the debt table. Fox may buy 18.6% of FanDuel for a cash price of USD 4.8 billion at 2025-12-31, growing 5% a year, until December 2030, in full only and only if Fox is licensed; the liability is carried at USD 100 million, so a cash call of that order would compete with creditors for liquidity and is not funded in the filings.
Basis: calculation C09; 10-K_2025-12-31_flut-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 29; 10-Q_2026-09-30_flut-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 34
- Control and governance information is thin and mostly fixed by the documents supplied, not by evidence of behaviour. No holder is named above any threshold, the board and compensation items are incorporated by reference to a proxy statement not supplied, and the leadership changed in May and October 2026, so sponsor-style conduct cannot be assessed and the governance risk level is medium on the evidence of bridge-funded acquisitions followed by a buyback pause.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_flut-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 216; EX-99.1_2026-05-06_d46069dex991.htm.jsonl, page 1; EX-99.1_2026-08-05_d127369dex991.htm.jsonl, page 1; calculation C10
- Capital allocation moved from acquisition and buybacks to deleveraging. The Snai and Boyd transactions of 2025 were funded with bridge loans that new 2031 notes and a 2032 term loan B repaid, repurchases reached USD 1,372 million of the USD 5,000 million authorisation, and in the third quarter of 2026 the company paused repurchases to prioritise deleveraging, so shareholder distributions are no longer competing with debt reduction.
Basis: calculation C11; calculation C10; 10-Q_2026-09-30_flut-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 25, 75

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view
The strongest evidence against a cautious view is the breadth of the business and the company's response: no region is above 40% of nine-month revenue, the company reports compliance with its debt covenants, it has extended its revolver and term A maturities and paused buybacks, and it guides to adjusted EBITDA of USD 2,495 million at the midpoint for 2026. The view would soften if Brazil's Congress lets the ban lapse, if Fox does not exercise by December 2030, or if cost programmes lift margins above the guided 14.17%; it would harden if the Fox option is exercised before the cash is arranged or if further tax increases follow the UK and state-level changes.
Basis: calculation C06; 10-Q_2026-09-30_flut-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 27, 42, 49, 75; 10-K_2025-12-31_flut-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 29

### Limitations
- Ownership and board information beyond the notes is not supplied, so the conclusion that no shareholder controls Flutter rests on non-disclosure and would change if the proxy statement names a holder with consent or appointment rights.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_flut-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 216
- The Brazilian measure needs Congressional approval or amendment within 120 days, and no later outcome is stated, so the conclusion that Brazil earnings are lost is provisional and would reverse if the ban lapses.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_flut-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 49
- The Fox option price is given at stated dates and no exercise intention or funding plan is disclosed, so the size and timing of any cash call on FanDuel are not established and the contingent-claim conclusion is a range of exposure, not an expected outflow.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_flut-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 29; 10-Q_2026-09-30_flut-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 34
- Legal capacity for distributions, investments and priming sits in the credit agreement and indenture, which are not supplied, so sponsor-style behaviour tests can separate neither capacity nor willingness and the medium governance level could change.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_flut-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 216–218

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

The 2025 acquisitions were large and the two biggest were debt-funded. Snai was acquired on 2025-04-30 for about USD 2,600 million with a EUR 2.5 billion bridge loan, NSX on 2025-05-14 for USD 674 million with options over the rest, and the Boyd 5% stake in FanDuel on 2025-07-31 for USD 1,553 million plus USD 205 million to end the market access agreements, with a USD 1.75 billion second bridge loan; the 2031 notes and a 2032 term loan B repaid both bridges in full. Those transactions took the company to full ownership of FanDuel, subject to the Fox option, and added the new debt that the Financial foundation module tabulates.
Basis: calculation C11; 10-K_2025-12-31_flut-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 102–103, 190; 10-Q_2026-09-30_flut-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 25–26

The credit agreement was amended and restated on 2026-10-30, extending the term A and revolver maturities to 2032-05-03 and replacing the net total leverage covenant with a net first lien leverage covenant at 5.70:1.00; the covenant definition is in the credit agreement, which is not supplied, and the amendment's effect on net debt is not stated.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_flut-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 42, 74–75
