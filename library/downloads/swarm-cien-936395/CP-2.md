## Findings

### Bottom line

Ciena presents a strong near-term credit profile that rests on cyclical, concentrated earnings, so creditors are well covered today and exposed to an order reversal that the pace of shareholder returns would not cushion. Net leverage is 0.86x last-twelve-months EBITDA, or 0.90x pro forma for USD 36.0 million of repurchases to August 28, 2026, and cash alone covers the 2030 notes 6.11x. Two cloud customers supply 41.7% of third-quarter revenue, and nine-month shareholder returns exceeded free cash flow by USD 127.54 million.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); Business & governance (CP-1A); EX99.1_2026-08-01_cien_ex9912026q3earningspressre-htm.htm.jsonl, page 2; calculation C6; calculation C12

EBITDA fell 39.95% in fiscal 2024 when revenue fell 8.47%, which took gross leverage to 5.15x, so a reversal would reopen leverage quickly. Covenant headroom, legal capacity and ratings are not established because those documents are not supplied.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); Source readiness (CP-0); calculation C2; calculation C3

### Key findings

Leverage is low on trailing earnings, but the improvement came from earnings rather than debt reduction, and those earnings are calculated and recent. Last-twelve-months EBITDA of USD 914.04 million is 2.71x the fiscal 2025 figure, while gross debt more than doubled after the June 2026 convertible issue, so gross leverage is 3.53x against 4.55x at the fiscal 2025 year-end and net leverage is 0.86x (0.90x pro forma for the August 2026 repurchases) against 1.31x. The issuer states EBITDA only quarterly, so a fall in the calculated trailing figure would raise leverage faster than any repayment could lower it.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); calculation C1; calculation C21; calculation C22

Liquidity is the strongest support. Cash of USD 2,445.71 million (the cash after the August 2026 repurchases is not established) and an undrawn revolver of USD 259.3 million after letters of credit cover the USD 400.0 million of 4.00% senior notes due 2030 6.11x, and cash plus investments of USD 2,843.55 million equal 0.87x the USD 3,275.0 million of note principal. The cover exists because the convertible proceeds were kept, so it holds only while the cash is not spent on repurchases, capacity investment or supplier prepayments.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); 10-Q_2026-08-01_cien.htm.jsonl, page 21; 10-Q_2026-08-01_cien.htm.jsonl, page 23; calculation C6; calculation C8; calculation C21

Cash conversion is good but depends on working capital. Free cash flow was USD 814.36 million over the last twelve months, 0.89x EBITDA, and the fiscal 2025 conversion of 1.97x was lifted by a payables inflow, while in the nine months of fiscal 2026 working capital absorbed USD 410.39 million net, mainly receivables, inventories and prepaid items. A slowdown would first release working capital, but USD 3.3 billion of purchase commitments could keep cash tied up in inventory and prepayments.

Gross margin also includes tariff recoveries of an amount not stated, so a pause by either customer would cut EBITDA by more than its share of revenue.

The strongest evidence against caution is that the demand base may be structural rather than cyclical: backlog was USD 5.0 billion at the 2025 year-end against USD 2.1 billion a year earlier, remaining performance obligations were USD 2.5 billion at August 1, 2026, and management raised guidance after each quarter. A repeat of the fiscal 2024 percentage fall in EBITDA from the trailing level would leave EBITDA of USD 548.89 million, gross leverage of 5.88x and net leverage of 1.43x (1.49x pro forma for the August 2026 repurchases), with cash still more than six times the 2030 notes.

### Limitations

- Ratings are not supplied, so the strong liquidity and leverage reading cannot be set against an agency view or a market-access test, and the refinancing conclusion rests on the June 2026 issue alone.
- Covenant definitions sit in a credit agreement that is not supplied, so the stated compliance with the revolver tests cannot be turned into headroom; the leverage conclusions describe reported ratios, not distance to a covenant.
- No earnings module or peer set is supplied, so margin durability is judged from Ciena's own history and the issuer's guidance, and the comparison with other leveraged issuers is not established.
- Fiscal 2026 year-end balances are not reported, so leverage and liquidity are measured at August 1, 2026 and the pro forma figure covers only repurchases to August 28, 2026.

Cash of USD 2,445.71 million (the cash after the August 2026 repurchases is not established) and investments of USD 397.85 million sit against USD 3,275.0 million of note principal, of which 87.79% is zero-coupon convertible notes due in 2031; free cash flow over the last twelve months was USD 814.36 million; and remaining performance obligations of USD 2.5 billion support near-term revenue. Management calls Ciena the only pure-play optical systems and interconnects provider, which is management's own characterization and is not tested against a peer set.

Two cloud customers supply 41.7% of third-quarter revenue, EBITDA fell 4.72x as fast as revenue in fiscal 2024, nine-month shareholder returns exceeded free cash flow by USD 127.54 million, and the firm part of USD 3.3 billion of purchase commitments is not stated. ### Company description

Ciena sells optical transport and interconnect systems, routing and switching platforms, and network software and services to cloud providers, service providers and other network operators; Optical Networking was 71.0% of nine-month fiscal 2026 revenue and Networking Platforms 81.0%. Revenue follows customer capital spending, so cash flow is strong in an upturn and exposed in a downturn, and remaining performance obligations of USD 2.5 billion give visibility into the next year. The model supports debt service today but leaves little recurring revenue to fall back on, because the two software segments were 7.56% of nine-month revenue.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-08-01_cien.htm.jsonl, page 31; 10-Q_2026-08-01_cien.htm.jsonl, page 13; Business & governance (CP-1A); calculation C25

Ownership is public with no sponsor or controlling holder identified, so financial policy is set by management and the board, whose chair is separate from the chief executive; governance risk is medium because shareholder returns take most operating cash flow. The revolver is described as senior secured and the 2031 notes as senior unsecured with the guarantors of the 2030 notes, but the security and rank of the 2030 notes and the intercreditor terms are not stated, so structural priority is not established.

### Revenue drivers and pricing power

Volume from cloud capital spending drives revenue: third-quarter revenue rose 37.0%, and cloud customers supplied 41.7% of it. Pricing power is not evidenced beyond management's reference to pricing optimization within the margin gain, revenue is cyclical, as the fiscal 2024 fall of 8.47% showed, and supply constraints keep orders above revenue. Recurring revenue is small, so a customer pause would reach revenue directly and counterparties are unnamed in the 2026 reports.

### Cost structure and margin resilience

Operating leverage works in both directions: operating expense was 27.4% of revenue in the third quarter against 35.2% a year earlier because spending rose 6.6% while revenue rose 37.0%, and gross margin was 45.4% against 41.3%, helped by cost reductions, pricing, mix and tariff recoveries. Research and development of USD 236.67 million a quarter is largely fixed investment, so margins can be defended in an upturn but not in a volume decline, as the fall of EBITDA 4.72x as fast as revenue in fiscal 2024 showed. The tariff share of the gain is not quantified.

### Capital intensity and cash conversion

Capital purchases of USD 194.9 million in nine months, 104.35% above the prior year and mainly for supply chain equipment, and research and development are the main cash uses; cash taxes were USD 84.58 million and cash interest USD 58.71 million. Capital spending above maintenance is discretionary and can be cut in a downturn, while research and development is largely fixed. EBITDA converts to free cash flow at 0.89x over the last twelve months, but that depends on working capital, so conversion is adequate rather than strong.

### Competitive forces

Rivalry is intense: Ciena names Nokia, Huawei, Cisco, Hewlett Packard Enterprise and ZTE, several with greater resources, and says rivals use pricing pressure to displace incumbents, which limits pricing power. New entrants and substitutes arrive through the data center, where Marvell, Credo and Broadcom compete in interconnect and disaggregated pluggable modems let customers buy modems and line systems from different vendors. Buyer power is high because two cloud customers supply 41.7% of third-quarter revenue and cloud providers heavily influence architecture, and supplier power is high because key components are often available only from sole or limited sources with no guarantee of supply. Together these forces cap margins and can shift cash to customers or suppliers in a squeeze.
Basis: 10-K_2025-11-01_cien.htm.jsonl, page 5; 10-K_2025-11-01_cien.htm.jsonl, page 9; 10-K_2025-11-01_cien.htm.jsonl, page 17; 10-K_2025-11-01_cien.htm.jsonl, page 29; EX99.1_2026-08-01_cien_ex9912026q3earningspressre-htm.htm.jsonl, page 2

### Macro and trade factors

Trade policy is material to the credit: approximately 72% of fiscal 2025 revenue came from the United States, products are built or introduced in Canada, Mexico, Thailand and Vietnam and sourced partly from China, and the Supreme Court ruling of February 20, 2026 on part of the tariffs raised the prospect of refunds that the third-quarter margin already cites without an amount. Cloud capital spending is the economic driver and it sits with few buyers, and the move toward disaggregated interconnect is both the growth market and a source of new competitors. Foreign exchange is not a primary driver, as about 9% of nine-month revenue was not in US dollars with minimal currency impact.

### Outlook, tailwinds and headwinds

Over the next 12 to 24 months the evidence points to continued growth: management guides fourth-quarter revenue to USD 1.75 billion plus or minus USD 50 million, adjusted gross margin to 45% plus or minus 50 bp and adjusted operating margin to 20% plus or minus 50 bp, and orders exceed revenue. Tailwinds are AI-driven network spending, a growing order book and expanding supply capacity; headwinds are supply constraints, customer concentration, tariff policy and cash committed to suppliers. The sources give no evidence beyond management's strategy statements for the next three to five years, so the medium-term outlook is not established, and a normalization of cloud spending would press revenue, margin and deleveraging together.

### Downside scenario

The downside is a pause in orders from one of the two cloud customers. Revenue would fall while research and development of USD 236.67 million and operating expense of USD 458.09 million a quarter largely continue, so EBITDA would fall faster than revenue, as in fiscal 2024. A repeat of that percentage fall would leave EBITDA of USD 548.89 million, gross leverage of 5.88x and net leverage of 1.43x (1.49x pro forma for the August 2026 repurchases). Liquidity would hold on reported balances, with cash of USD 2,445.71 million (the cash after the August 2026 repurchases is not established) against USD 400.0 million of debt before 2031, but cash could be tied up if the USD 3.3 billion of supplier commitments proved firm. Management can stop repurchases, cut capital purchases, cancel or reschedule part of the supplier orders and draw the undrawn revolver of USD 259.3 million. Resilience looks sufficient to avoid liquidity distress through 2030; the exposure is the 2031 principal, which is 87.79% of note principal and would need cash that was retained.

### Overall credit view

Overall, Ciena presents a strong but cyclical business profile supported by liquidity, cash generation and a long-dated, zero-coupon maturity profile, but credit quality is constrained by customer concentration, shareholder returns above free cash flow and thin evidence on legal capacity. Cash-flow durability appears adequate because conversion depends on working capital, and refinancing capacity is likely to depend on how much of the convertible proceeds is retained and on earnings holding at the trailing level. The primary downside path is a pause in cloud customer orders while repurchases and supplier commitments continue. Further analysis would require the indenture, credit agreement, ratings, proxy statement and the fiscal 2026 year-end results. Fundamental credit completed. Top risk: loss of cloud customer orders while shareholder returns continue.
