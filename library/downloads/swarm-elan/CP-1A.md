## Findings

### Bottom line

Elanco shows no documented shareholder extraction and no identified controlling holder, so creditor risk sits in business durability: five product lines supply about 38% of revenue, one distributor about 12%, and the Seresto collar loses its United States formulation patent in September 2027.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_elan-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 9; 10-K_2025-12-31_elan-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 13; 10-K_2025-12-31_elan-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 17; calculation C09

Governance risk is rated medium because the evidence is incomplete, not adverse: the proxy statement, credit agreement and indenture are not supplied, so board independence, ownership percentages and legal capacity for distributions or priming are not established. Behaviour so far is creditor-aligned, with divestiture proceeds applied to term debt, no dividends and a longer maturity profile, though other financing claims have grown: a USD 295 million royalty sale, a headquarters lease with a USD 250 million purchase option and deferred and contingent payments for the April 2026 AHV acquisition.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_elan-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 70; 10-K_2025-12-31_elan-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 75–76; 10-K_2025-12-31_elan-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 121; 10-K_2025-12-31_elan-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 144; 10-Q_2026-06-30_elan-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 10–11; 10-Q_2026-06-30_elan-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 17; Source readiness (CP-0)

### Key findings

- Earnings depend on a handful of product lines and one channel partner rather than on any segment or country. Five products and product families supplied about 38% of FY2025 revenue, the Advantage Family alone about 10%, and the largest customer, an affiliate of Cencora, about 12%, with a second distributor at about 7%. The same customer owed USD 131 million at 2026-06-30, about 12.16% of receivables, so a change in that distributor's ordering or payment terms would move cash flow quickly.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_elan-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 9; 10-K_2025-12-31_elan-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 13; 10-Q_2026-06-30_elan-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 10; calculation C09
- Revenue durability is weakest where patent cover is ending. The Seresto formulation patent expires in the United States in September 2027, its European formulation patents expired in June 2025, with supplementary protection certificates in some countries running to August 2026, and certain Advantage Family products, Rumensin and Maxiban and Monteban have no patent protection. Volume growth of 7% in the half-year to 2026-06-30 came from new products, led by Zenrelia and Credelio Quattro, so creditors are relying on launches replacing eroding franchises.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_elan-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 9; 10-K_2025-12-31_elan-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 16–17; 10-Q_2026-06-30_elan-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 31–32; calculation C06
- Business risk is balanced between pets and livestock. Pet Health supplied 48.78% and Farm Animal 50.10% of FY2025 revenue, and the United States 47.38%, but pet health was 52.14% of revenue in the half-year and 51.62% of FY2025 revenue fell in the first half, so cash flow and working capital swing with the flea-and-tick season.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-02-24_elanco-12312025exhibit991.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-K_2025-12-31_elan-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 8; 10-K_2025-12-31_elan-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 12; 10-Q_2026-06-30_elan-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 9; calculation C01; calculation C02; calculation C04; calculation C05
- Behaviour is creditor-aligned on the evidence available. The 2024 sale of the aqua business raised USD 1,294 million in cash, the vast majority used to repay term loan debt, the October 2025 refinancing extended the maturity profile, no dividends are anticipated and the credit facilities restrict them. The documented exceptions are other financing claims: the USD 295 million royalty sale, now a liability of USD 315 million, the headquarters finance lease and the deferred and contingent AHV payments.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_elan-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 50; 10-K_2025-12-31_elan-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 75–76; 10-K_2025-12-31_elan-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 105; 10-Q_2026-06-30_elan-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 10–11; 10-Q_2026-06-30_elan-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 17; calculation C12; calculation C13; calculation C10
- No sponsor, fund vintage or sponsor fees apply because the issuer is a listed company, and no holder above five percent is identified in the filings supplied. Control, board independence and related-person dealings sit in a proxy statement that is not supplied, so governance is described from the annual report only: eleven directors signed the annual report, the chair is not the chief executive, the auditor concluded that the statements present fairly and management assessed internal control over financial reporting as effective.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_elan-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 70; 10-K_2025-12-31_elan-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 138–139; 10-K_2025-12-31_elan-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 143–144; 10-K_2025-12-31_elan-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 149–150
- Litigation is extensive but has not produced a recorded liability. A securities class action over Zenrelia safety, labelling and launch disclosures was dismissed with prejudice on 2026-03-26 and is on appeal, the related derivative suits are stayed, and the Tevra antitrust verdict for the issuer was affirmed on 2026-07-06; the issuer states it has established no material liability for these matters.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_elan-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 19–22

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against concern about concentration is momentum from new products: second-quarter organic constant-currency revenue growth of 8%, innovation revenue of USD 892 million in FY2025 against a raised 2026 target of USD 1,250 million, and Zenrelia described by the issuer as a blockbuster in the year to July. If these launches keep volume growth near the 7% of the half-year, the Seresto expiry is more likely to be absorbed than to be a cliff, which is an analyst interpretation. The view would change toward higher risk if volume growth fades before September 2027, if a generic Seresto entrant appears in the United States, or if the largest distributor shifts volume; it would change toward lower risk if the proxy statement and legal documents confirm independent directors, no holder above five percent and tight limits on distributions and priming.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-02-24_elanco-12312025exhibit991.htm.jsonl, page 1; EX-99.1_2026-08-05_elanco-20260630exhibit991.htm.jsonl, pages 1–2; 10-K_2025-12-31_elan-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 17; 10-Q_2026-06-30_elan-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 31–32

### Limitations

- The proxy statement is not supplied, so board independence, committee membership, ownership percentages and related-person transactions are not established; this keeps the governance risk level at medium instead of low.
- The credit agreement and indenture are not supplied, so the legal capacity for dividends, additional debt, liens and asset transfers is not established; the creditor-alignment conclusion rests on observed behaviour alone and cannot say what the issuer is permitted to do.
- Product revenue is given only for the top product families and product categories, so the concentration finding rests on the issuer's approximate shares and not on a full product schedule.
- Management characterisations of the innovation pipeline and the productivity programme are the issuer's own and are labelled as management language; they support the contrary view but do not change the evidenced risk.
