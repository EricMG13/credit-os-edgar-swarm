## Findings

### Bottom line

Financial foundation shows low leverage and ample liquidity at September 30, 2026: net debt of USD 1,134.05 million is 0.65x last-twelve-month EBITDA, but free cash flow is thin because capital spending absorbs most operating cash flow.

Total debt is USD 1,634.35 million against liquidity of USD 2,499.9 million, EBITDA covers cash interest 20.17x, and no note falls due before 2029. The ratios use the issuer's EBITDA measure, which excludes a noncontrolling interest that debt and cash include, so they are slightly conservative; two source conflicts remain open, the 2042 notes coupon and the total debt label. The 2026 rise in EBITDA reflects higher realized prices, and free cash flow over the last twelve months is USD 240.61 million after USD 1,526.64 million of capital spending.
Basis: calculation C2; calculation C3; calculation C5; calculation C6; calculation C15; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mur-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 14, 45, 47

### Key findings

Net debt rose from USD 1,007.88 million at year-end 2025 to USD 1,134.05 million at September 30, 2026, yet net leverage fell from 0.85x to 0.65x because last-twelve-month EBITDA rose from USD 1,191.7 million to USD 1,740.6 million. Total debt grew by USD 249.27 million: the January 2026 refinancing added USD 272.51 million of notes net of redemptions, finance leases rose USD 82.07 million with the Vietnam floating storage vessel lease, and repaying the USD 100.0 million revolver borrowing offset part of it. Taken alone, the largest event after year-end is the dividend paid on March 2, 2026, which lifts year-end net debt to USD 1,058.06 million, or 0.89x.
Basis: calculation C2; calculation C10; calculation C11; calculation C15; 10-K_2025-12-31_mur-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 112; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mur-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 13–15, 32

The rise in EBITDA came from higher realized prices in the United States and Canada and lower lease operating expenses, partly offset by lower volumes, and it lifted cash interest cover from 13.52x at year-end 2025 to 20.17x. Cash interest paid is steady at about USD 86.31 million over the last twelve months, so cover moves with EBITDA and not with the cost of debt. On the issuer's adjusted measure, which also adds back asset retirement accretion, leverage is 0.92x gross and 0.64x net.
Basis: calculation C4; calculation C12; calculation C15; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mur-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 35, 45, 49

Cash-flow quality is the weak point. Capital spending absorbed 48.92% of last-twelve-month revenue, so free cash flow was only 0.14x EBITDA, and the issuer's own adjusted free cash flow, after dividends, distributions to the noncontrolling interest and acquisitions, was negative USD 32.2 million for the nine months. Cash still rose over the nine months because the refinancing raised more than it repaid, so a fall in prices would reach net debt quickly while dividends and a capital programme guided at USD 1,500 million to USD 1,600 million for the year remain to be funded.
Basis: calculation C5; calculation C6; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mur-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 45–46, 52; EX-99.1_2026-11-04_mur-2026q3xex991.htm.jsonl, pages 3, 10

Liquidity is USD 2,499.9 million, or 152.96% of total debt: USD 500.3 million of cash and USD 1,999.6 million undrawn on the USD 2,000.0 million revolver, which expires in January 2031. No note principal falls due before 2029, and the larger series fall due in 2032, 2034 and 2042. The total of letters of credit outstanding is stated only at year-end 2025, so liquidity is net only of the USD 0.4 million drawn on the revolver.
Basis: calculation C3; calculation C15; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mur-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 14, 47; 10-K_2025-12-31_mur-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 74, 113

Four definition and source conflicts are logged. The issuer's EBITDA excludes the noncontrolling interest while consolidated debt and cash include it, and on a consolidated house-derived basis net leverage is 0.61x against 0.65x, so the reported ratio is the more conservative. The note line labelled total debt omits the revolver printed beneath it. The 2042 notes carry different coupons in the 2025 annual report and the third quarter report, and the issuer's weighted average coupons imply the change fell between the first and second quarters of 2026. The 2025 comparatives in the segment note differ from the statement of operations by up to USD 2.4 million.
Basis: calculation C7; calculation C8; calculation C14; Source readiness (CP-0); 10-K_2025-12-31_mur-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 112–113; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mur-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 13–14, 48

No non-debt funding float is stated: the filings show no supplier finance, factoring, deferred revenue or customer deposits. An October 2026 agreement for a deepwater drillship, with initial-term consideration of USD 225 million to USD 325 million gross, is not recognised in the statements and is kept out of period figures, as are the exploration items announced after the quarter.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_mur-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 32, 34

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest contrary evidence to a comfortable credit view is cash generation, not leverage: after USD 1,526.64 million of capital spending, dividends and distributions, adjusted free cash flow was negative USD 32.2 million for the nine months, and the 2026 recovery in EBITDA rests on realized prices that the filings describe as volatile and exposed to regional conflict. If prices fell back toward the 2025 level, EBITDA would drift toward the year-end 2025 figure of USD 1,191.7 million, net leverage would return to 0.85x or higher as net debt rose, and cover would fall toward 13.52x. The view would strengthen if capital spending came in below the guided range, or if the executed indentures show the notes rank equally with the revolver.
Basis: calculation C5; calculation C6; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mur-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 45, 50–52; EX-99.1_2026-11-04_mur-2026q3xex991.htm.jsonl, pages 3, 10
