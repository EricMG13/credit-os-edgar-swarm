## Findings

### Bottom line

Ashland is widely held and no sponsor is identified, so creditor protection rests on its own cash-funded financial policy, and governance risk is medium rather than low because the filings leave control, related-party and policy questions open.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_ash_ash-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 39; 10-K_2025-09-30_ash_ash-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 41; calculation C-06; calculation C-19

The business is a specialty-ingredients portfolio whose earnings rest on two product families and two high-margin segments, and its earnings base has shrunk after disposals, with sales of USD 1,824 million in fiscal 2025 against USD 2,191 million in fiscal 2023. Management guides to full-year Adjusted EBITDA of USD 385 million to USD 400 million, at or below the USD 401 million of fiscal 2025, and the legacy asbestos and environmental claims remain the largest balance-sheet claim outside debt.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_ash_ash-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 122; 10-Q_2026-06-30_ash_ash-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 68; 10-K_2025-09-30_ash_ash-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 74; calculation C-01; calculation C-16; calculation C-17

### Key findings

- No sponsor or controlling shareholder is identified, which removes the extraction and support questions a sponsor-owned issuer raises but leaves discipline to the board and to public shareholders. The company is listed, has a widely spread share register and a combined chair and chief executive, and expects quarterly dividends comparable to past ones to continue, at the board's discretion; the proxy statement that would show holders and related-party dealings is not supplied, so the control and related-party picture is incomplete rather than clean.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_ash_ash-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 39; 10-K_2025-09-30_ash_ash-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 41; 10-K_2025-09-30_ash_ash-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 55
- Earnings rest on two product families and on the two segments that earn the most. Cellulosics and polyvinylpyrrolidones were 64% of nine-month sales, and Life Sciences and Personal Care earned Adjusted EBITDA margins of 33.3% and 29.0% in Q3 2026 against 14.7% for Specialty Additives and 10.8% for Intermediates. A raw-material or supply shock to cellulose or vinyl pyrrolidone chemistry would therefore hit most of the group at once, and the weaker segments give little offset.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_ash_ash-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 29; 10-Q_2026-06-30_ash_ash-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 57; 10-Q_2026-06-30_ash_ash-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 58; 10-Q_2026-06-30_ash_ash-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 60; 10-Q_2026-06-30_ash_ash-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 61; 10-K_2025-09-30_ash_ash-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 26; calculation C-09; calculation C-10

Sales fell 16.75% and Adjusted EBITDA 12.64% between fiscal 2023 and fiscal 2025 after the Nutraceuticals and Avoca sales and the cellulose product-line actions, which cut fiscal 2025 sales by about USD 208 million and Adjusted EBITDA by USD 45 million; in the nine months to 2026-06-30 the same actions cost only USD 11 million of sales and USD 1 million of Adjusted EBITDA. A smaller business carries the same debt, so scale is lower while the obligations are not.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest case that governance risk is low rests on conduct that is on record: the filings read show no related-party payments, no priming or other liability-management transaction, no distribution funded by debt, buybacks halted in 2026, covenant ratios of 2.3x and 6.9x well inside the 4.0x and 3.0x limits, and detailed reporting. That case wins if the proxy statement shows an independent board, ordinary related-party arrangements and no controlling holder, and if the fiscal 2026 annual report confirms that buybacks stay suspended while Adjusted EBITDA is at or above USD 385 million.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_ash_ash-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 67; 10-Q_2026-06-30_ash_ash-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-K_2025-09-30_ash_ash-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 41; 10-Q_2026-06-30_ash_ash-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 68; calculation C-08

The view moves toward high if repurchases resume while Adjusted EBITDA falls below the guided range, if a debt-funded acquisition or distribution appears, if asbestos or environmental reserves are raised again, or if the board and finance team thin further; it moves toward low if the proxy statement and the credit documents show no capacity for distributions or priming beyond ordinary baskets.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_ash_ash-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 68; 10-Q_2026-06-30_ash_ash-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 21–22; calculation C-03

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

The only debt financing transaction in the period is the Second Amended and Restated Credit Agreement of 2026-05-28, which replaced the USD 600 million 2022 revolver with a USD 500 million five-year unsecured revolver for Ashland and its Swiss subsidiary, with a USD 125 million letter of credit sublimit. It prices at 137.5 bp over Term SOFR or EURIBOR to begin with, moving between 125 bp and 175 bp with net leverage, against an initial 125 bp before, and carries an unused fee of 17.5 bp rising to a range of 12.5 bp to 27.5 bp; Ashland paid USD 2 million of issuance costs and keeps financial covenants of a maximum 4.0x net leverage and a minimum 3.0x interest coverage. Committed liquidity is USD 100 million lower, the facility is unsecured, and the obligations of the Swiss borrower are guaranteed by Ashland.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_ash_ash-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 14–15; 10-K_2025-09-30_ash_ash-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 150; 10-Q_2026-06-30_ash_ash-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 67; calculation C-14

Carrying debt rose by USD 35 million in fiscal 2025 and fell by USD 10 million in the nine months, so the transactions of the period did not add leverage.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_ash_ash-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 149; 10-Q_2026-06-30_ash_ash-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 13; calculation C-19
