## Findings

### Bottom line

CoreWeave's credit quality depends on a handful of very large customer contracts, a founder-controlled share structure and uninterrupted access to capital markets, while the business is scaling faster than its financial controls.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_crwv-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 15; 10-Q_2026-06-30_crwv-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 141–142; 10-Q_2026-06-30_crwv-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 59

The three largest customers provided 72% of second-quarter 2026 revenue, and one customer alone provided 67% of FY2025 revenue, although contracted obligations of USD 103.7 billion give long visibility. Under current terms the Co-Founders hold all Class B shares with ten votes each until at latest May 31, 2032, and management reports that material weaknesses in internal control persist, so creditors rely on reported figures produced under controls that management itself rates as not effective.
Basis: calculation C1; 10-Q_2026-06-30_crwv-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 14–15; 10-K_2025-12-31_crwv-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 145; 10-Q_2026-06-30_crwv-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 142

### Key findings

- Customer concentration has eased but remains the dominant business risk. The largest customer fell from 67% of FY2025 revenue to 40% of first-half 2026 revenue, yet the three largest customers still produced 72% of second-quarter 2026 revenue, so a contract dispute or credit event at one counterparty would hit both revenue and the contracts that secure the delayed draw loans.
Basis: calculation C1; 10-K_2025-12-31_crwv-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 145; 10-Q_2026-06-30_crwv-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 14–15
- Contract structure supports cash-flow visibility. Committed take-or-pay contracts were 98% of second-quarter 2026 revenue, with a weighted-average duration of about five years at December 31, 2025 and USD 103.7 billion of unsatisfied obligations at June 30, 2026, so near-term revenue depends on delivering capacity on schedule more than on winning new demand.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_crwv-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 14–15; 10-K_2025-12-31_crwv-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 8
- Control sits with the Co-Founders and no sponsor stands behind the credit. Class B shares carry ten votes against one for Class A, all Class B shares are held by the Co-Founders, and each can exchange newly vested Class A shares for Class B, so voting power can rise over time; creditors have no sponsor equity backstop and rely on public equity markets, where the issuer raised USD 2,000 million from NVIDIA in January 2026 and USD 1,000 million in April 2026.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_crwv-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 141–142; 10-Q_2026-06-30_crwv-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 45; 10-Q_2026-06-30_crwv-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 56
- The control environment is a live governance weakness. Management concluded that disclosure controls were not effective at June 30, 2026 because of material weaknesses in information technology general controls, segregation of duties and accounting staffing, with no misstatement identified, so covenant compliance reporting and non-GAAP measures rest on controls still being remediated.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_crwv-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 59–60
- Growth is financed with debt in large steps and a first move into self-built capacity. In the first half of 2026 the issuer put in place USD 8,500 million and USD 3,100 million of new delayed draw facilities and issued USD 10,279 million of unsecured and convertible notes, and it is committed to up to USD 1,700 million of investment in two data centre joint ventures, which adds construction risk to a business that has so far relied on third-party sites.
Basis: calculation C2; 10-Q_2026-06-30_crwv-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 16–17; 10-Q_2026-06-30_crwv-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 28
- Supplier dependence is absolute at the chip level. All graphics processors used are NVIDIA parts and customers have contractually specified them, so any supply or allocation constraint at one vendor delays capacity delivery and the revenue and lender cash flows that depend on it.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_crwv-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 64–65
- Litigation and insider selling are present but not yet quantified. A securities class action filed January 12, 2026 and related derivative actions seek unspecified damages, no loss is accrued, and officers including a Co-Founder have adopted share sale plans, so the disclosed legal exposure is open-ended and the cases may divert management attention.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_crwv-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 25–26; 10-Q_2026-06-30_crwv-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 151–154

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a cautious view is that contract counterparties are described as largely investment grade, that the issuer's lenders hold security over specific contracts and equipment, that customer concentration is falling as new commitments from Meta and OpenAI are added, and that equity investors, including NVIDIA, continue to fund the company. The view would improve if the largest customer share fell further while remaining under contract, if remediation of the control weaknesses were reported complete, and if the board and officer information deferred to the proxy statement showed independent oversight of financing decisions. It would worsen if a major customer delayed payment or sought to reduce its commitment, if a new material weakness appeared, or if equity or debt market access closed before capacity under construction reached revenue.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_crwv-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 56; 10-Q_2026-06-30_crwv-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 72; 10-Q_2026-06-30_crwv-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 45

### Limitations

- The proxy statement is not supplied, so board composition, independence and committee oversight are not established, which limits the governance conclusion to control structure and disclosed conduct.
- No customer contract or order form is supplied, so termination rights, payment security and take-or-pay terms for the largest customers are taken from risk-factor summaries, which limits how far counterparty strength can support the lenders' contract security.
- Customer labels in the filings change between periods, so the concentration trend compares customers that may not be the same entity, which limits the claim that concentration is falling.
- The latest source is dated August 11, 2026, so any later customer, financing or control developments are outside these conclusions.

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

In the first half of 2026 CoreWeave put in place USD 8,500 million of delayed draw capacity in March for a non-recourse subsidiary and USD 3,100 million in May for a guaranteed subsidiary borrower, and by June 30, 2026 had drawn USD 2,837 million and USD 1,101 million of them, leaving USD 7,662 million undrawn that can be used only against eligible assets and project conditions. It issued four unsecured note series totalling USD 10,279 million in April and June 2026, including a euro series of EUR 2,000 million at 8.50%, and bought capped calls for USD 492 million against the convertible issue.
Basis: calculation C2; calculation C3; 10-Q_2026-06-30_crwv-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 27–33

Equity also funded the build: USD 2,000 million from NVIDIA in January 2026 at USD 87.20 a share and USD 1,000 million in April 2026 at USD 109.00 a share, after the March 2025 initial public offering. Acquisitions have been paid mainly in stock: Weights and Biases, the one with disclosed terms, cost USD 1,029 million of consideration of which only USD 96 million was cash, so acquisitions have added little cash leverage. No change of control, sponsor transaction or dividend recapitalisation appears in the sources read.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_crwv-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 20; 10-Q_2026-06-30_crwv-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 56; 10-Q_2026-06-30_crwv-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 10
