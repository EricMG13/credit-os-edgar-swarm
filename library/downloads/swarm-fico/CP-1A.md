## Findings

### Bottom line

Fair Isaac is a high-margin scoring franchise that depends on three distributors, and its board has chosen to fund very large share repurchases with new borrowing, so financial policy is the main risk for creditors, ahead of business risk.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_fico-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 13; 10-Q_2026-06-30_fico-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 15; EX-99.1_2026-07-29_exhibit991erq32026.htm.jsonl, page 5; calculation CALC-02; calculation CALC-03

Scores produced 66% of nine-month revenue at a 90% segment margin, but its growth leaned on a higher mortgage score unit price, and three consumer reporting agencies paid 63% of third-quarter revenue. Total debt rose USD 2,526.70 million in nine months as repurchases of USD 3,045.99 million reached 4.06x free cash flow of USD 750.04 million, and no sponsor or controlling holder is established to support equity. Governance and financial-policy risk is medium, moving toward high if borrowing-funded repurchases continue as the leverage covenant ceiling, raised from 3.5x to 4.5x by the June 2026 amendment, steps back to 4.0x on 2026-12-31 and 3.5x on 2027-12-31.

### Key findings

Scores is the credit, and it is performing: it produced 66% of nine-month revenue at a 90% segment margin and 95% of the nine-month revenue increase, so earnings quality is high but rests on one product line. Operating cash flow was USD 777.88 million for nine months and capital spending was only 1.48% of revenue, so cash conversion is not the constraint.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_fico-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 25; 10-Q_2026-06-30_fico-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 32–33; 10-Q_2026-06-30_fico-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 4; calculation CALC-01; calculation CALC-08

Concentration among distributors is rising and is, in our view, the largest business risk. Experian, TransUnion and Equifax together paid 63% of third-quarter revenue, up from 51% in fiscal 2025 and 41% in fiscal 2023, and they established VantageScore, a joint venture that sells a competing score. These distributors can ask to change the terms on which they offer the FICO Score, and the company says it has limited visibility until their royalty reports arrive, so a change in one relationship would reach the highest-margin revenue first.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_fico-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 15; 10-K_2025-09-30_fico-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 15; 10-K_2025-09-30_fico-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 17; 10-K_2025-09-30_fico-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 37; 10-K_2025-09-30_fico-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 38; 10-K_2025-09-30_fico-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 53

Growth in Scores leans on price, which can reverse. The company attributes the third-quarter business-to-business increase to a higher mortgage origination scores unit price, its risk factors warn that limits on score fees could constrain further price increases, and the Federal Housing Finance Agency director announced in July 2025 that mortgage originators may choose the credit score they submit to Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac. A price or policy reversal would fall almost directly to profit because the segment's costs are small, which is our interpretation of its 91% third-quarter margin.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_fico-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 26; 10-Q_2026-06-30_fico-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 32; 10-K_2025-09-30_fico-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 36; 10-K_2025-09-30_fico-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 46; EX-99.1_2026-07-29_exhibit991erq32026.htm.jsonl, page 1

Software diversifies the business only slowly. Third-quarter software revenue rose 2%, and annual recurring revenue grew 10% to USD 815.8 million because platform products grew 62% while non-platform recurring revenue fell 17% with net retention of 82%. Segment margin fell to 26% from 32% as point-in-time licence sales declined and hosting costs rose.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_fico-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 24; 10-Q_2026-06-30_fico-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 25; 10-Q_2026-06-30_fico-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 26; 10-Q_2026-06-30_fico-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 32

Financial policy is the binding risk. Between June 2025 and June 2026 the board approved three successive repurchase programmes, each larger than the last, and nine-month repurchases of USD 3,045.99 million were 4.06x free cash flow of USD 750.04 million, so lenders and noteholders funded the gap. The USD 1.5 billion accelerated repurchase was paid on the day the USD 1.5 billion term loan was drawn, and total liabilities of USD 6,134.51 million are now 3.01x total assets of USD 2,037.37 million.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_fico-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 62; 10-Q_2026-06-30_fico-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 11; 10-Q_2026-06-30_fico-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 13; 10-Q_2026-06-30_fico-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 35; 10-Q_2026-06-30_fico-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 36; 10-Q_2026-06-30_fico-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-06-30_fico-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 4; EX-99.1_2026-07-29_exhibit991erq32026.htm.jsonl, page 5; calculation CALC-03; calculation CALC-05; calculation CALC-07

Governance is only partly evidenced, and no sponsor or controlling holder is established, so there is no identified party to inject equity or cure a covenant breach. Board composition, committees, ownership and related-party dealings sit in a proxy statement that was not supplied, which leaves the board's alignment with creditors to be judged from its recorded actions, and those actions include the borrowing-funded repurchases described above.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_fico-20250930.htm.jsonl, pages 131–132; 10-Q_2026-06-30_fico-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 1; Source readiness (CP-0)

A surviving antitrust claim is a live but unquantified risk. The Sherman Act Section 2 claim and accompanying state law claims against the company continue through discovery after the court dismissed all other claims on 2024-11-24, and the filings state no material exposure for matters with a reasonable possibility of loss and give no estimate. An adverse outcome could reach the pricing and distribution practices behind Scores growth, which is an analyst interpretation.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_fico-20250930.htm.jsonl, pages 60–61; 10-Q_2026-06-30_fico-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 20; 10-Q_2026-06-30_fico-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 43

Disclosure is adequate on terms but thin on risk. The quarterly reports state each debt instrument, the leverage covenant steps and covenant compliance, but the annual risk factors contain no stand-alone risk factor on the company's own indebtedness, and the third-quarter report says risk factors had no material changes although total debt had risen by USD 2,526.70 million since the year-end.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a cautious view is cash generation and market access. Free cash flow was USD 750.04 million in nine months, the Scores margin reached 91% in the third quarter, capital spending is light, and the company placed USD 1.5 billion of senior notes in May 2025 and USD 1.0 billion in March 2026 while the disclosed fair value of its senior notes was 98.04% of face value on 2026-06-30. The revolving line runs to 2030-05-13, covenant compliance is reported, no cash dividend has been paid since May 2017, and the filings record no priming, uptier or other action against existing lenders. Repurchase programmes are open-ended authorisations, not commitments, so the board can slow them at any time; only the accelerated repurchase is contractually fixed.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-07-29_exhibit991erq32026.htm.jsonl, page 5; 10-Q_2026-06-30_fico-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 12; 10-Q_2026-06-30_fico-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 11; 10-K_2025-09-30_fico-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 61; calculation CALC-06

Risk would fall if the board commits repurchases to free cash flow, states a leverage target, or the proxy statement shows a stable holder base and independent oversight of financial policy. It would rise if repurchases again exceed free cash flow after the covenant ceiling steps down to 4.0x on 2026-12-31, if the board starts a further programme funded by borrowing, if a consumer reporting agency or the federal housing regulator changes how the FICO Score is priced or used in mortgages, or if the antitrust claim produces a remedy that touches score pricing.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_fico-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 11; 10-K_2025-09-30_fico-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 36; 10-K_2025-09-30_fico-20250930.htm.jsonl, pages 60–61
