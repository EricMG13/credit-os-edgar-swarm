## Findings

### Bottom line

Valaris is a widely held offshore driller with no controlling shareholder disclosed in the supplied documents, so creditors' main exposure is event risk from the pending all-stock sale to Transocean and from the ARO joint venture, not sponsor extraction. A change of control may let holders of the USD 1,100.0 million second lien notes require repurchase at 101.0% of principal.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_val-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 11–12; 10-K_2025-12-31_val-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 127; 10-K_2025-12-31_val-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 70

The operating base supports debt service for now: contract backlog was USD 4,750.1 million on 2026-11-02, all ten active drillships are on contract, and no shares have been repurchased in 2026 after USD 425.0 million between 2023 and 2025. Four customers each supplied at least 10% of Q3 revenue, and Middle East conflict costs cut Q3 operating income by about USD 24.0 million.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-11-02_a09302026ex991pressrelease.htm.jsonl, page 1; EX-99.1_2026-11-02_a09302026ex991pressrelease.htm.jsonl, page 11; 10-Q_2026-09-30_val-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 30; 10-Q_2026-09-30_val-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 34; calculation C6; calculation C5

### Key findings

- Ownership offers creditors neither a sponsor backstop nor sponsor leakage. Valaris emerged from bankruptcy in 2021, has paid no dividends, had 63 holders of record in February 2026, and the supplied documents describe no dividend recapitalisation, liability management transaction or related-party fee other than its lease agreements with ARO; the repurchase programme used USD 425.0 million of its USD 600.0 million authorisation, and the source does not state how the repurchases were funded.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_val-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 70; 10-K_2025-12-31_val-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 71; 10-K_2025-12-31_val-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 107; 10-Q_2026-09-30_val-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 16; 10-Q_2026-09-30_val-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 23; calculation C6
- The pending Transocean combination is the principal credit event, and its effect on the notes is not established. The all-stock agreement signed on 2026-02-09 values Valaris at about USD 5.8 billion at announcement, leaves Valaris holders with about 47% of the combined company on a fully diluted basis, and is scheduled to close on or about 2026-12-01; until then the agreement restricts new debt, dividends, repurchases, encumbrances and disposals, and Valaris owes Transocean USD 173.0 million if it ends in specified circumstances. The supplied documents do not say how Valaris debt is treated, and the notes' quoted fair value at 2026-09-30 was 102.3% of principal, above the 101.0% repurchase price, so a repurchase offer would help holders only if prices fall.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against the view that extraction risk is low is the USD 425.0 million repurchased since 2023 while USD 1,100.0 million of secured notes stayed outstanding, together with USD 175.0 million of programme capacity that remains authorised; the funding source is not stated and the combined company's capital policy is not established. The view would also change if the indenture shows that the notes are repaid, exchanged or left behind a larger secured claim after closing, if a change of control triggering event occurs, if ARO calls capital or its notes receivable are not extended, or if repurchases resume after closing.

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

Valaris and Transocean Ltd. signed a business combination agreement on 2026-02-09 under which Transocean will acquire all Valaris common shares for 15.235 Transocean shares each, by court-approved scheme of arrangement under Bermudian law; the value was about USD 5.8 billion at announcement and Valaris holders would own about 47% of the combined company on a fully diluted basis. Regulatory approvals have been received, shareholder meetings are scheduled for 2026-11-24, a Bermuda court hearing for 2026-11-26 and closing on or about 2026-12-01, and either party may end the agreement if it has not closed by 2027-02-09, subject to extension. Until closing the agreement restricts new debt, dividends, repurchases, encumbrances and disposals, and Valaris owes Transocean USD 173.0 million if the agreement ends in specified circumstances, with reimbursement of up to USD 58 million of Transocean expenses if Valaris holders fail to approve.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_val-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 22; 10-K_2025-12-31_val-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 24; 10-K_2025-12-31_val-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 154; 10-Q_2026-09-30_val-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 11–12; 10-Q_2026-09-30_val-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 32

No new debt was issued or repaid in 2026. The capital structure is the USD 1,100.0 million 8.375% second lien notes due 2030-04-30, issued in April and August 2023 by Valaris Limited and Valaris Finance Company LLC and guaranteed by certain subsidiaries, and an undrawn USD 375.0 million revolver due 2028-04-03. Two supplemental indentures were signed on 2026-07-16 and 2026-09-14 and filed as exhibits, naming further Valaris subsidiaries as parties; the documents do not state their purpose.
