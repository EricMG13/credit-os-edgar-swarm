## Findings

### Bottom line

Business and governance risk is medium: a founder-led single-basin producer is funding four 2026 acquisitions largely with borrowings while raising its dividend, so creditors rely on integration succeeding and on a stated debt repayment plan.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_mtdr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 34; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mtdr-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 13–14; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mtdr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 17; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mtdr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 23; calculation C5; calculation C7

The company was founded in 2003 and is led by one Chairman and Chief Executive Officer, and 72% of 2025 oil, natural gas and NGL revenue came from three purchasers. San Mateo is 51% owned and consolidated but carries its own non-recourse debt, and about 49% of its distributions go to the partner, so cash leaves the group before it can service parent debt. Director independence, ownership and related-party details sit in a proxy statement that was not supplied, which limits the governance view.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a medium risk view is the stated deleveraging intent and the operating record: management says it expects to repay between $350 million and $400 million of Credit Agreement borrowings by year-end, of which $150 million was repaid by November 3, 2026, third-quarter capital spending of $381 million was 10% below guidance, Fitch upgraded the company to BB in 2025, and the March 2026 notes refinancing retired the 2028 notes through a cash tender and redemption rather than an exchange. The supplied documents show no drop-down, priming transaction or exchange of notes, and the three supplemental indentures dated November 3, 2026 appear in the exhibit list for the notes after the Paloma closing, which is consistent with the acquired entities joining the notes' guarantee group, although their terms were not supplied.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_mtdr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 17; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mtdr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 19; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mtdr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 53; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mtdr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 74; EX-99.1_2026-11-03_a20260930mtdr8ker-ex991.htm.jsonl, page 1; 10-K_2025-12-31_mtdr-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 12

The view would improve to low if the repayment plan is met without a further debt-funded acquisition, the proxy statement shows a board with a majority of independent directors and no related-party leakage, and the San Mateo term loan due July 30, 2027 is refinanced on a long-term basis. It would worsen to high if shareholder payouts continue to rise while Credit Agreement borrowings stay near $2.25 billion, if the term loan cannot be refinanced and members must fund it, or if the governing documents show capacity for drop-downs or priming that the filings do not describe.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_mtdr-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 17–18; 10-K_2025-12-31_mtdr-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 245

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

Matador completed or agreed four acquisitions in 2026 and financed them with a mix of cash, borrowings and new debt. In May 2026 it bought 5,154 net undeveloped acres in the Delaware Basin through the federal lease sale for about $1.16 billion; on July 31, 2026 San Mateo bought the operating subsidiaries of Cardinal Midstream Partners for $761.0 million in cash, funded by a new $650.0 million term loan due July 30, 2027, capital contributions of $51.0 million from Matador and $49.0 million from Five Point, and cash on hand; and on October 1, 2026 Matador closed the purchase of Paloma Permian for an as-adjusted price of about USD 1,255 million, against USD 1,275 million agreed on July 22, 2026, using cash and Credit Agreement borrowings. The Ridge Runner purchase of 13,600 net acres in the Woodford play was agreed on July 22, 2026 with subsidiaries of Ridge Runner Resources II, an EnCap portfolio company, expected to be funded by cash on hand and Credit Agreement borrowings, and is stated to have closed in October 2026, with no price disclosed. The price of Cardinal was stated as $752.0 million in the second quarter report and as $761.0 million in the third quarter report, both subject to post-closing adjustments.
