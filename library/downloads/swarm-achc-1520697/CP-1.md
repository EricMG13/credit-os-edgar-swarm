## Findings

### Bottom line

Reported EBITDA is negative for 2025 and the last twelve months, so reported leverage is not measurable; after excluding impairment and legal settlements, net leverage is 4.96x at 2026-09-30, a house-normalised figure that is not the covenant measure.
Basis: calculation C10; calculation C8; calculation C7; 10-K_2025-12-31_achc_achc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 122; 10-Q_2026-09-30_achc_achc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 3

Net debt fell to USD 2.19 billion at 2026-09-30 from USD 2.37 billion at the end of 2025 as capital spending was cut and revolver borrowings were repaid, and USD 710.1 million of the revolver was undrawn. Cash interest cover was 5.36x in 2024 and is not calculable for the last twelve months because the quarterly reports print no cash interest.
Basis: calculation C4; calculation N2; calculation N3; calculation C16; 10-Q_2026-09-30_achc_achc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 4–5; 10-Q_2026-09-30_achc_achc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 22

### Key findings

Leverage was moderate before 2025 and is high on any basis that can be measured. At the end of 2024 total debt was 3.22x and net debt 3.09x reported EBITDA. Reported EBITDA then turned negative because the company took non-cash impairment charges of USD 1.01 billion in 2025, almost all of it goodwill, so the 2025 and last-twelve-month ratios on the reported basis are not calculable. Excluding impairment, legal settlements, debt extinguishment and property sale results, net debt was 3.01x EBITDA at the end of 2024, 5.72x at the end of 2025 and 4.96x at 2026-09-30, with gross debt at 5.33x.
Basis: calculation C9; calculation C10; calculation N1; 10-K_2025-12-31_achc_achc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 88; 10-K_2025-12-31_achc_achc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 122

The step-up in 2025 came from gross debt rising by USD 543.06 million while capital spending of USD 571.81 million far exceeded operating cash flow. Debt has since fallen by USD 147.61 million to USD 2.35 billion at 2026-09-30, and house-normalised EBITDA rose from USD 413.58 million for 2025 to USD 441.51 million for the last twelve months; both moves, in roughly equal measure, took the house-normalised net ratio from 5.72x to 4.96x.
Basis: calculation N5; calculation N6; calculation N4; calculation N12; calculation N20; calculation N21; 10-K_2025-12-31_achc_achc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 123; 10-Q_2026-09-30_achc_achc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-09-30_achc_achc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 4

Free cash flow was an outflow of USD 560.69 million in 2024, when the January 2024 payment of USD 400.0 million under the Desert Hills settlement agreements fell within a USD 420.89 million cash outflow in accounts payable and other accrued liabilities, and an outflow of USD 439.91 million in 2025. In the first nine months of 2026 free cash flow was positive at USD 155.62 million, against an outflow of USD 260.43 million a year earlier, because cash paid for capital expenditures fell to USD 165.86 million from USD 478.62 million while operating cash flow rose to USD 321.48 million from USD 218.19 million. Over the last twelve months free cash flow is still slightly negative, so the improvement depends on capital spending staying low.
Basis: calculation C12; calculation N8; calculation N18; calculation N7; calculation N9; calculation N10; calculation N11; calculation N13; calculation N14; 10-K_2024-12-31_achc_achc-20241231.htm.jsonl, page 125; 10-K_2025-12-31_achc_achc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 123; 10-Q_2026-09-30_achc_achc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 4–5

Cash paid for interest was USD 124.66 million in 2025 against interest expense, net of USD 138.86 million. Reported EBITDA covered cash interest 5.36x in 2024 and was negative 5.92x in 2025; on the house-normalised basis cover was 5.51x in 2024 and 3.32x in 2025, so cover weakened sharply as debt rose and earnings fell.
Basis: calculation C11; calculation N15; calculation N16; 10-K_2025-12-31_achc_achc-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 122–123

Liquidity at 2026-09-30 was USD 873.41 million of cash plus the undrawn revolver, after standby letters of credit rose to USD 15.9 million from USD 1.2 million three months earlier. Term-loan repayments are modest, but the revolver and the term-loan balance fall due together in 2030 (USD 745.05 million of principal), with the 2028 notes and term-loan repayments (USD 494.73 million) and the 2029 notes (USD 536.10 million) ahead of them and the 2033 notes of USD 550.00 million last.
Basis: calculation C16; calculation N17; calculation C13; calculation N22; calculation N23; calculation N24; calculation N25; 10-Q_2026-09-30_achc_achc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 20–23; 10-Q_2026-06-30_achc_achc-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 22

Revenue grew 5.04% in 2025 and 3.96% in the first nine months of 2026, but same-facility revenue growth slowed to 4.0% in the nine months from 7.7% in 2024, and the payor mix shifted toward Medicaid, which reached 61.8% of revenue from 56.5% in 2024 as the commercial share fell to 21.4% from 26.0%. A larger government-payor share exposes earnings to state Medicaid funding and rate decisions.
Basis: calculation C17; 10-K_2025-12-31_achc_achc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 85; 10-K_2025-12-31_achc_achc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 134; 10-Q_2026-09-30_achc_achc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 10; 10-Q_2026-09-30_achc_achc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 38

The house-normalised figure adds back a net USD 1,151.41 million in 2025, an Analyst Judgement that moves 2025 EBITDA from negative to USD 413.58 million. Debt at carrying value is USD 14.14 million below principal of USD 2,366.50 million; leases sit outside debt, and redeemable noncontrolling interests of USD 229.88 million sit outside both debt and equity.

### Limitations

- Cash paid for interest and for income taxes is printed only in the annual reports, so interest cover for the last twelve months and cash taxes for 2026 are not calculable.
- Covenant earnings and ratios are not printed, so the house-normalised ratios are not covenant measures and no headroom is stated.
- The quarterly balance sheets before 2026 and the fourth quarter of 2025 are not in the filings, so quarterly leverage before 2026 is not calculated and the fourth quarter is derived.
- Per-share data and weighted-average shares are not tabulated.
