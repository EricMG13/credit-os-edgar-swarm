## Findings

### Bottom line

Eldorado is a listed producer with no controlling shareholder disclosed and conventional governance, so creditor risk comes from capital allocation: two new mines are consuming debt-funded capital while dividends and buybacks continue under a newly installed chief executive.

No distressed amendment, waiver or exchange is disclosed and the Skouries term debt is non-recourse to the parent, but no source ranks the instruments, so legal protection is not established. The sponsor and governance risk level is Medium.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against concern is that the operating mines fund themselves: the issuer's free cash flow excluding Skouries and McIlvenna Bay was US$202.1 million for nine months, the Foran deal was paid in shares, the new chief executive has put capital discipline and balance-sheet strength first, and the issuer reports compliance with every covenant at 2026-09-30. Skouries produced its first concentrate on 2026-09-08 and was connected to grid power on 2026-09-28, so the main construction milestones are behind the project, although commissioning continues. Taken together these would support a Low risk level.
