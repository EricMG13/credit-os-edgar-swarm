## Findings

### Bottom line

Net debt was USD 18,152 million at 2026-09-30, 7.73x last-twelve-month reported EBITDA or 4.81x excluding the Q1 2026 goodwill impairment, so creditors remain exposed to a heavily levered structure that depends on continued refinancing access. Reported cash interest cover was 1.73x in FY2025, and cover for the last twelve months is not established because interim cash interest is not stated.
Basis: calculation C7; calculation C8; calculation C9; calculation C13; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bhc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 5; 10-K_2025-12-31_bhc-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 319–320.

Cash of USD 2,485 million and undrawn facilities of USD 1,135 million exceed principal due before the end of 2027 of USD 716 million, but Bausch + Lomb holds USD 515 million of group cash and USD 666 million of the undrawn facilities, and neither is generally available to other group entities.
Basis: calculation C10; calculation C14; calculation C19; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bhc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 127; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bhc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 131.

### Key findings

Reported net leverage rose from 6.23x at FY2025 to 7.73x at 2026-09-30 even though net debt fell by USD 1,356 million, because last-twelve-month reported EBITDA fell from USD 3,131 million to USD 2,347 million after the USD 1,426 million goodwill impairment booked in Q1 2026. The impairment is non-cash and does not change debt service, and without it net leverage is 4.81x against 5.95x at FY2025 on the same basis, so leverage on a cash-earnings basis is falling while the reported ratio overstates the burden.
Basis: calculation C3; calculation C7; calculation C8; calculation C12; calculation C13; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bhc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 6.

Net debt fell because cash rose by USD 1,176 million to USD 2,485 million while gross debt fell only USD 180 million, so the improvement came from cash generation rather than debt repayment. Operating cash flow was USD 1,714 million in the nine months against purchases of property, plant and equipment of USD 252 million, but it excludes USD 158 million of contractual interest that the issuer allocates to debt premiums and reports in financing, so free cash flow of USD 1,462 million is flattered by that amount.
Basis: calculation C12; calculation C16; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bhc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 7–8; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bhc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 41; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bhc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 126.

Cover is thin: FY2025 reported EBITDA was 1.73x cash interest paid of USD 1,810 million, and cash interest exceeded income statement interest expense of USD 1,604 million by USD 206 million, partly because USD 276 million of interest paid on secured notes carried at a premium reduces the premium instead of expense. Income statement interest therefore understates the cash burden, and cover for the last twelve months is not established because the quarterly filing prints no total interest paid.
Basis: calculation C1; calculation C9; 10-K_2025-12-31_bhc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 219; 10-K_2025-12-31_bhc-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 319–320.

Maturities are light until 2028, with USD 15 million of principal due in the rest of 2026 and USD 701 million in 2027, then USD 3,766 million in 2028 and USD 1,667 million in 2029, rising to USD 4,123 million in 2030 and USD 3,896 million in 2031, so refinancing is the central risk once cash is used. The approximately USD 643 million of 8.5% notes due 2027 are scheduled for redemption on 2026-11-30 from cash on hand, and the effect on net debt and pro forma net leverage is not established because the redemption price and accrued interest are not stated.
Basis: calculation C11; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bhc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 42; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bhc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 76.

Of principal debt of USD 20,168 million, USD 5,065 million sits at Bausch + Lomb, so Bausch Health itself carries USD 15,103 million against cash of USD 1,973 million, and the Bausch Health maintenance covenant excludes Bausch + Lomb earnings and net debt. Consolidated ratios therefore blend two credit groups with separate collateral and separate cash.
Basis: calculation C18; calculation C19; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bhc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 40; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bhc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 127–128.

Reported EBITDA for FY2024 and FY2025 is calculated here from audited components because no source states it, and adjusted EBITDA is stated only for quarter and nine-month periods, such as USD 3,323 million for nine months 2026, so adjusted leverage on a twelve-month basis is not established.
Basis: calculation C1; calculation C2; EX-99.1_2026-10-28_ex991-q32026.htm.jsonl, page 24.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest contrary evidence is earnings momentum: issuer-adjusted EBITDA for the nine months was USD 3,323 million against USD 2,565 million a year earlier, the issuer guides to USD 4,315 million to USD 4,415 million of adjusted EBITDA for 2026, and cash of USD 2,485 million exceeds principal due before the end of 2027. That reading would win if a twelve-month adjusted EBITDA near the guidance range were confirmed, because net leverage on that basis would sit well below the 7.73x reported ratio; it would fail if the USD 3,766 million due in 2028 proved hard to refinance. A full-year 2025 adjusted EBITDA or the fourth-quarter 2025 release would let an adjusted twelve-month ratio be built and settle the question.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-10-28_ex991-q32026.htm.jsonl, page 24; EX-99.1_2026-10-28_ex991-q32026.htm.jsonl, page 4; calculation C14; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bhc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 42.

### Limitations

- Full-year adjusted EBITDA and nine-month total interest paid are not stated, so adjusted leverage and last-twelve-month interest cover are not established.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-10-28_ex991-q32026.htm.jsonl, page 24; 10-K_2025-12-31_bhc-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 319–320.

- Reported EBITDA for FY2024 and FY2025 is calculated from audited components rather than stated by the issuer, and no reported fourth quarter of 2025 is supplied.
Basis: calculation C1; calculation C2.

- Revolver availability at 2024-12-31 and any balance sheet at 2025-09-30 are not supplied, so liquidity and debt trends cover FY2025 and 2026-09-30 only.

- The lien and legal rank of the 14.00% second lien notes are not described in any filing and the notes are left out of the debt stack; executed debt documents are not supplied, so covenant headroom is not established.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_bhc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 27; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bhc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 34; 10-K_2025-12-31_bhc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 284.

- Consolidated debt and cash blend Bausch Health and Bausch + Lomb, whose collateral pools and cash are separate, and the debt stack gives each pool first-priority debt the same rank without a legal ranking across the pools.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_bhc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 36; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bhc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 127.
