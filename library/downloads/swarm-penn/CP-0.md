Source readiness is adequate for a credit snapshot of PENN Entertainment, Inc.: the audited 2025 annual report and every 2026 quarter to the cutoff support work on debt, liquidity and lease claims, but covenant headroom, ratings, market pricing and board-level governance cannot be established.
