## Findings

### Bottom line

The four supplied filings are enough to start the financial foundation, the business and governance review and the fundamental credit synthesis for Genmab on its September 2026 position, but not to establish debt terms or audited full-year 2025 statements. Creditors can see how much debt there is but not its shape: carrying amounts of the term loans, secured notes and unsecured notes are printed, while coupons, maturity dates, guarantors, collateral and covenant levels are not.
Basis: 20-F_2025-12-31_gmab-20251231_d2.htm.jsonl, pages 130–131; 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-11-05_genmabinterimreportq32026.htm.jsonl, pages 45–46

Borrowings were USD 4,992 million and cash USD 1,860 million at 30 September 2026, but the maturity wall, cash interest by instrument and covenant headroom must stay gaps until the credit agreement, the indentures and the statutory annual report are supplied. All three requested analyses can run with these limits; none is blocked.
Basis: 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-11-05_genmabinterimreportq32026.htm.jsonl, page 5; calculation C2
