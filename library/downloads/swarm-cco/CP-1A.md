## Findings

### Bottom line

Creditors face an ownership change they cannot yet price: a pending take-private by funds advised by Mubadala Capital, with creditors' change-of-control protection waived, while the acquirer's financing and sponsor behaviour are not disclosed, so governance risk is Medium on incomplete evidence.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_cco-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 8; 10-Q_2026-06-30_cco-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 16–17; 10-K_2025-12-31_cco-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 4

Operating leverage is real: site lease cost of USD 624.2 million was 38.91% of 2025 revenue and is partly fixed by minimum guarantees, while no single advertiser is material and digital reached 44.1% of revenue. Board composition, ownership above 5% and the Merger financing are not in the documents supplied.
Basis: calculation C4; 10-K_2025-12-31_cco-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 11; 10-K_2025-12-31_cco-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 16; 10-K_2025-12-31_cco-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 57; 10-K_2025-12-31_cco-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 64

### Key findings

- The Merger changes who controls the issuer without, on the documents supplied, changing who is owed what: the secured notes and credit agreements were amended so that the Merger is not a change of control, the amendments become operative only on completion, and conditional redemption notices cover the 2028 and 2029 senior notes. Creditors have already consented, so the protection they would normally hold on a takeover is gone, while the size and ranking of the acquirer's own debt are not established.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_cco-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 16–17; 10-Q_2026-06-30_cco-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 34; 10-K_2025-12-31_cco-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 24

- Ownership is dispersed today and the future owner is only partly identified. The company has one hundred and sixty-one holders of record and no holder above 5% is named in the documents supplied, and the consortium is described with TWG Global in the annual report and the first-quarter documents but without it in the second-quarter documents. At the deal price the equity is worth about USD 1,236.9 million on shares outstanding at 30 June 2026, against company-defined net debt of USD 4,915.49 million, so debt is 79.90% of implied enterprise value and the creditors, not the shareholders, carry most of the claim.
Basis: calculation C2; calculation C3; 10-K_2025-12-31_cco-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 51; 10-K_2025-12-31_cco-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-Q_2026-06-30_cco-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 8

- Governance disclosure is adequate for monitoring but silent on control rights. The auditor gave unqualified opinions on the statements and on internal control, covenant compliance is stated every quarter, and the Merger Agreement bars dividends and new borrowing without the acquirer's consent, which protects creditors until closing; directors, fees and related-party dealings are not in the documents supplied.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_cco-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 77; 10-K_2025-12-31_cco-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 133–134; 10-K_2025-12-31_cco-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 25; 10-K_2025-12-31_cco-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 51; 10-Q_2026-06-30_cco-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 34

- Financial policy has favoured creditors on deleveraging but not on cost. Disposals of the international businesses raised USD 607.8 million in 2025, USD 375.0 million of which prepaid the term loan of a former subsidiary, and the company repurchased USD 229.7 million of senior notes at a discount, yet the August 2025 refinancing lengthened maturities at a higher weighted average cost of debt (7.6% against 7.4%). No dividend has been paid and none is intended.
Basis: calculation C6; calculation C7; 10-K_2025-12-31_cco-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 66; 10-K_2025-12-31_cco-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 67; 10-K_2025-12-31_cco-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 73; 10-K_2025-12-31_cco-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 102; 10-K_2025-12-31_cco-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 51

- The business is a fixed-cost concession model with long commitments. Street furniture contracts run five to fifteen years and airport concessions five to ten, awarded by competitive bid with minimum fees, and the New York transit contract alone carries USD 390.0 million of minimum lease payments over fifteen years. That locks in cost and creates renewal risk, but digital growth and low advertiser concentration support revenue visibility.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_cco-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 16; 10-K_2025-12-31_cco-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 118; 10-K_2025-12-31_cco-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 32; 10-K_2025-12-31_cco-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 11

- Seasonality is steep and favours the fourth quarter: Q1 2025 Adjusted EBITDA was 48.19% of Q4 2025, which leaves first-quarter debt service resting on the weakest earnings of the year.
Basis: calculation C8; 10-K_2025-12-31_cco-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 16

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against caution is that creditors were asked, and consented, before closing: the requisite holders and lenders approved the change-of-control amendments, the receivables facility will be extended to five years from completion with commitments raised from USD 200.0 million to USD 250.0 million, and the company has no dividend history in the documents supplied. The Merger Agreement also bars new borrowing and dividends until closing, and stockholders approved it on 12 May 2026. These facts point to a negotiated, creditor-visible transaction rather than a surprise.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_cco-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 8; 10-Q_2026-06-30_cco-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 16–17; 10-K_2025-12-31_cco-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 25

The view would move to Low if the acquirer's debt financing, equity contribution and the treatment of the 2028 and 2029 senior notes were disclosed and left secured creditors no worse placed, and it would move to High if the financing primed existing secured debt or distributions were funded from the company. Completion or termination of the Merger, which the documents supplied do not reach, would replace the public-company governance assessed here, with four days to the outside date from the as-of date.
Basis: calculation C9; 10-K_2025-12-31_cco-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 24; 10-Q_2026-06-30_cco-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 34

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

The pending take-private is an Agreement and Plan of Merger signed on 9 February 2026 with Madison Parent Inc. and Madison Merger Sub Inc., vehicles of an investor consortium of affiliates and funds advised by Mubadala Capital. Stockholders approve USD 2.43 per share in cash, the antitrust waiting period expired on 9 April 2026, stockholders voted yes on 12 May 2026, and completion still needs regulatory approvals including review by the interagency committee on foreign investment. The company has to pay a USD 39.8 million fee in some terminations, or USD 19.9 million if a superior proposal arrives during the go-shop, and the acquirer would pay USD 92.9 million in others. The outside date is 9 November 2026, extendable to 9 February 2027, and the documents supplied stop before it. The acquirer's equity and debt financing, sources and uses and any ratings effect are not in the documents supplied.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_cco-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-K_2025-12-31_cco-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 23; 10-K_2025-12-31_cco-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 24; 10-K_2025-12-31_cco-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 54; 10-Q_2026-06-30_cco-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 8; calculation C11; calculation C2

The only new debt in the period supplied is the August 2025 refinancing, which replaced USD 2,000.0 million of secured notes due 2027 and 2028 with USD 2,050.0 million of secured notes due 2031 and 2033, extending the earliest note maturity to 2028, with USD 62.4 million of premium and fees. In 2026 the company obtained consents instead of issuing: the secured notes indentures, the senior secured credit agreement and the receivables-based credit agreement were amended so that the Merger is not a change of control, and conditional redemption notices were issued for the 2028 and 2029 senior notes. The first group of amendments is effective but operative only on completion, while the receivables facility amendment, which extends maturity to five years from completion and lifts commitments to USD 250.0 million, takes effect on completion.
Basis: calculation C7; 10-K_2025-12-31_cco-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 102; 10-Q_2026-06-30_cco-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 16–17; 10-Q_2026-06-30_cco-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 34

The company has sold its businesses in Mexico, Peru and Chile, the Europe-North segment for a purchase price of USD 625.0 million and net proceeds of USD 572.0 million, and Brazil for about USD 15.0 million, and on 4 August 2026 it completed the Spain sale for about USD 132.3 million, with net proceeds not yet stated and intended for debt reduction subject to the Merger. These sales left the company a focused United States business and are the source of its deleveraging in 2025.
Basis: calculation C6; calculation C10; 10-K_2025-12-31_cco-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 94; 10-Q_2026-06-30_cco-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 9
