## Findings

### Bottom line

Moog's four-segment aerospace, defense and industrial business gives creditors durable demand and a large order book, but governance risk is medium because dual-class control, trust-held votes and an unremediated reporting-control weakness limit creditor protection.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-27_mog-20250927.htm.jsonl, page 94; 10-K_2025-09-27_mog-20250927.htm.jsonl, page 99; 10-Q_2026-06-27_mog-20260627.htm.jsonl, pages 39–40

No sponsor owns Moog. In fiscal 2025 shareholder returns were 1.40x free cash flow and net debt rose, while the trustees of company-funded trusts vote 29.47% of the Class B shares that elect most of the board.
Basis: calculation C26; calculation C34; calculation C17

### Key findings

- The business is diversified by segment and weighted to defense, which steadies cash flow for creditors. The largest segment, Space and Defense, was 28.83% of fiscal 2025 net sales and the smallest, Military Aircraft, 23.00%; defense was 52% of 2026 sales, and aftermarket support was 14% of fiscal 2025 sales.
Basis: calculation C01; calculation C02; 10-K_2025-09-27_mog-20250927.htm.jsonl, page 10; 10-Q_2026-06-27_mog-20260627.htm.jsonl, page 35
- Customer concentration is moderate but real. Boeing was 10.28% of fiscal 2025 net sales, U.S. Government contracts 37.85%, and the five largest customers about 31%, so a Boeing production cut or a defense funding change would reach sales and cash flow directly.
Basis: calculation C10; calculation C09; 10-K_2025-09-27_mog-20250927.htm.jsonl, page 10; 10-K_2025-09-27_mog-20250927.htm.jsonl, page 16
- The order book supports near-term revenue. The twelve-month backlog was USD 3.0 billion at the 2025 year end, up 20%, and USD 3,250 million at June 27, 2026, up 23%, which reduces the chance that margin gains reverse quickly.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-27_mog-20250927.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-Q_2026-06-27_mog-20260627.htm.jsonl, pages 27–28
- Control sits in the share structure and the identity of the controlling holders is not established. Class A shares carry one-tenth of a vote and Class B one vote, Class B holders elect the balance of the board after Class A elects at least 25%, and trustees of the stock employee compensation trust and the supplemental retirement trust vote all trust-held shares: 29.47% of Class B shares outside treasury, or 18.67% of all votes on a calculated basis.
Basis: calculation C17; calculation C22; 10-K_2025-09-27_mog-20250927.htm.jsonl, page 94; 10-K_2025-09-27_mog-20250927.htm.jsonl, page 99
- Shareholder returns have run ahead of free cash flow, but the pattern reversed in 2026. Dividends plus treasury purchases were 1.40x free cash flow in fiscal 2025 and 1.57x in fiscal 2024, and 0.60x over the first nine months of fiscal 2026; the difference was funded by debt, which raised net debt over fiscal 2025.
Basis: calculation C26; calculation C29; calculation C32; 10-K_2025-09-27_mog-20250927.htm.jsonl, pages 56–57; 10-Q_2026-06-27_mog-20260627.htm.jsonl, pages 4–5
- Acquisition appetite is modest. The only acquisition in the period, COTSWORKS on July 1, 2025, cost 1.59% of fiscal 2025 net sales and was paid in cash and Class A shares, so it did not strain liquidity.
Basis: calculation C35; 10-K_2025-09-27_mog-20250927.htm.jsonl, page 70
- Disclosure quality is the weakest governance point. The 2025 annual report revised fiscal 2023, fiscal 2024 and interim results, the auditor gave an adverse opinion on internal control while giving an unqualified opinion on the statements, and the latest quarterly report says the weakness is not yet remediated, so reported earnings carry added estimation risk until it is.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-27_mog-20250927.htm.jsonl, pages 105–106; 10-K_2025-09-27_mog-20250927.htm.jsonl, pages 125–126; 10-Q_2026-06-27_mog-20260627.htm.jsonl, pages 39–40
- The 2026 financing actions were neutral to positive for creditors. The 4.25% notes were redeemed at par with the proceeds of the 5.50% notes due 2034 and cash, the revolver maturity moved to 2031, and no premium or extraction is documented.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-27_mog-20260627.htm.jsonl, pages 16–17

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a medium governance view is that no sponsor owns Moog, the chairman and chief executive are different people, the auditor's opinion on the statements is unqualified, leverage is low, and shareholder returns were covered by free cash flow at 0.60x in the first nine months of fiscal 2026. The view would move to low if the control weakness is confirmed remediated, the proxy statement shows an independent board and names the trustees, and distributions stay below free cash flow. It would move to high if returns or acquisitions were again funded by debt while the weakness remains open, or if the trustees' votes were used to change creditor terms.
Basis: calculation C32; 10-K_2025-09-27_mog-20250927.htm.jsonl, page 140; 10-K_2025-09-27_mog-20250927.htm.jsonl, pages 125–126; 10-Q_2026-06-27_mog-20260627.htm.jsonl, page 40

### Limitations

- The proxy statement is not supplied, so board independence, beneficial ownership above five percent and the identity of the trustees are not established; the governance view therefore rests on share structure and disclosed conduct and could move either way once those are known.
- The executed loan agreement and indentures are not supplied, so the legal capacity for distributions and acquisitions is not established; the shareholder-behaviour finding describes conduct, not what the documents permit.
- Customer and programme detail beyond Boeing and U.S. Government shares is not supplied, so concentration inside the five largest customers is not established; the durability conclusion is limited to those shares.
- The trust vote shares are calculated from reported share tables and assume treasury shares carry no vote; the filings state no voting percentages, so the control finding is an estimate within a few points.

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

No sponsor transaction, buyout or recapitalisation is described in the supplied documents; the transactions that matter to creditors are the financing events of fiscal 2025 and 2026 and one small acquisition. On February 26, 2026 Moog entered into an eighth amended and restated loan agreement that extended the revolving loan from 2027 to 2031 and kept a term loan maturing in 2031, with commitments of USD 1,100 million on the revolver.
