## Findings

### Bottom line

Total debt to reported EBITDA rose from 2.83x at year-end 2025 to 3.31x at 2026-09-30 after the Off-Highway purchase, so creditors now depend on acquired earnings and further debt repayment, although this ratio overstates run-rate leverage.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_alsn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 19; calculation LT-6; calculation TD-5

Net debt is USD 2,120 million higher than at year-end 2025 and cash interest cover fell from 8.50x to 6.55x, yet nine-month free cash flow of USD 662 million, debt repayment of USD 410 million and liquidity of USD 1,493 million show the debt is being serviced and reduced.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_alsn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 3, 44; calculation ND-6; calculation FC-1; calculation DR-1; calculation LQ-1

No pro forma EBITDA is supplied, so combined leverage is not established; net debt is 2.46x adjusted EBITDA, and only USD 320 million of debt matures through 2027.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_alsn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 9; EX-99.1_2026-11-02_d187329dex991.htm.jsonl, page 9; calculation LD-2; Source readiness (CP-0)

### Key findings

Reported-basis leverage peaked one quarter after closing and has fallen since: total debt to EBITDA was 4.23x at 2026-03-31, 3.80x at 2026-06-30 and 3.31x at 2026-09-30, against 2.83x at year-end 2025. Debt fell by USD 255 million over the last two of those quarters while last-twelve-months reported EBITDA rose from USD 1,009 million to USD 1,211 million as acquired quarters replaced pre-closing ones, so part of the improvement is arithmetic and continues only if the acquired business keeps earning at the 2026 rate.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-03-31_alsn-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-09-30_alsn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 2; calculation TD-6; calculation LT-4; calculation LT-6

Interest cover is the pressure point. EBITDA to cash interest paid was 8.50x in FY2025 and 6.55x over the last twelve months, and net interest expense of USD 51 million in Q3 alone annualises to USD 204 million, which would put run-rate cover at 5.94x on reported-basis EBITDA and 7.01x on adjusted EBITDA. Floating-rate term debt of USD 1,699 million carries the rate exposure, and the twelve-month cash interest figure still includes a quarter before the acquisition debt was drawn.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_alsn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 19–20; calculation IC-1; calculation IC-2; calculation IC-3; calculation FR-1

Free cash flow of USD 662 million in the nine months, 65.74% of reported EBITDA, covered USD 410 million of debt repayment and USD 195 million returned to shareholders; the twelve-month figure is USD 831 million. Cash generation is therefore the deleveraging engine, so a working-capital reversal or faster buybacks would slow it.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_alsn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 3; calculation FC-1; calculation FC-2; calculation FC-3; calculation DR-1; calculation CR-1

Liquidity at 2026-09-30 is USD 1,493 million, made up of USD 497 million of cash and USD 996 million of undrawn revolver commitments, against USD 4 million of debt due in the rest of 2026 and USD 316 million in 2027. Most of the cash is offshore: USD 361 million, or 72.64%, is held by foreign subsidiaries, so domestic cash is about USD 136 million and the cushion available in the United States is the revolver, which matures in January 2031.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_alsn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 21, 44; calculation LQ-1; calculation LQ-2; calculation LQ-3

Maturities are back-ended. Only USD 320 million falls due before 2028 and 78.29% of principal matures in 2031 or later, so the first bond maturity of consequence is the USD 500 million of 2029 notes, which falls in a year with USD 516 million of principal due.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_alsn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 19–21; calculation LD-1; calculation LD-2; calculation LD-3

Adjusted EBITDA of USD 1,166 million in the nine months is USD 159 million, or 13.64%, above reported-basis EBITDA of USD 1,007 million. Most of the gap is purchase accounting (USD 64 million of inventory step-up) and acquisition costs (USD 30 million), but the issuer states no realisation status for any add-back and defines the Other line of USD 7 million by an agreement that is not supplied, so adjusted measures are shown beside the reported basis, not in place of it.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-11-02_d187329dex991.htm.jsonl, page 9; calculation BR-1; calculation BR-2

Mix dilutes margins. Allison Off-Highway earned a nine-month adjusted EBITDA margin of 14.03% against 37.47% for Allison Transmission, and the consolidated margin fell to 25.88% from 38.06% a year earlier, so coverage now rests partly on a lower-margin business whose agriculture and industrial end markets the issuer calls subdued. Allison Transmission's own net sales rose 8.84%.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-11-02_d187329dex991.htm.jsonl, pages 1, 8, 10; calculation SG-1; calculation SG-2; calculation SG-3; calculation SG-4; calculation GR-2

The acquisition closed on 2026-01-01 and its financing, a USD 1,200 million incremental term loan and a USD 300 million revolver draw, is dated 2026-01-02, both after the 2025-12-31 balance sheet. Year-end 2025 net debt of USD 1,395 million (1.37x) is therefore not a guide to current leverage, and no event after 2026-09-30 changes net debt in the sources read.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_alsn-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 65, 118; 10-Q_2026-09-30_alsn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 6–7; calculation ND-2

Customer-funded float is small: deferred revenue was USD 179 million at 2026-09-30, mostly extended transmission coverage, against USD 4,012 million of debt, and no supplier finance or receivables sale programme appears in the filings read.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_alsn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 23, 25; calculation DF-1; calculation TD-5

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest case that leverage is lower than it looks: the reported-basis ratio uses a twelve-month period that includes a quarter before the acquired business, net debt is 2.46x adjusted EBITDA, and the first lien net leverage ratio the issuer reports is 0.84x against a 5.50x maximum. If the acquired business sustains its nine-month margin and free cash flow stays near USD 662 million for nine months, leverage on a full-year basis would fall faster than the last-twelve-months ratios show.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_alsn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 22; calculation FC-1; calculation LT-6

The view turns more cautious on an Off-Highway margin below the 14.03% earned in the nine months, a redraw of the revolver or repayment slower than the USD 255 million of debt reduction over the last two quarters, buybacks above free cash flow, or cash interest running at the annualised USD 204 million rather than the last-twelve-months USD 185 million.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-11-02_d187329dex991.htm.jsonl, page 10; calculation SG-2; calculation TD-6; calculation IC-1

### Limitations

- No pro forma adjusted EBITDA or pre-closing quarterly EBITDA for the acquired business is supplied, so combined leverage and cover are not established and every last-twelve-months ratio combines a quarter before the acquisition with quarters after it.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_alsn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 9; 10-K_2025-12-31_alsn-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 117
- Reported EBITDA is calculated from components because the issuer states only an adjusted measure defined by a credit agreement that is not supplied, so the covenant ratio of 0.84x cannot be reproduced from the sources.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_alsn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 22
- The maturity ladder is calculated from stated maturities and minimum quarterly payments, and the ranking of the notes below the secured facilities rests on their stated unsecured status, because no printed ladder at 2026-09-30 and no ranking statement is supplied.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_alsn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 19–22
- The first-quarter earnings release disagrees with the first-quarter quarterly report on total debt of USD 4,292 million against USD 4,267 million and on total assets of USD 8,749 million against USD 8,703 million; the quarterly report is used.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-05-04_d138525dex991.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 8; 10-Q_2026-03-31_alsn-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 2; calculation RL-1; calculation RL-2
- Interim statements are unaudited, purchase accounting stays preliminary until the one-year measurement period ends, and the fourth quarter of 2025 is constructed as the year less nine months because no fourth-quarter release is supplied.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_alsn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 7–8; calculation Q4-1
