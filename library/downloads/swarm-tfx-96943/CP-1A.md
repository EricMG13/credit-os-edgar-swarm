## Findings

### Bottom line

Teleflex is shrinking into a narrower vascular, interventional and surgical device group, and creditors now depend on new leadership splitting sale proceeds between bank debt repayment and share buybacks, the two uses management named.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_tfx.htm.jsonl, page 61; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tfx.htm.jsonl, page 22; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tfx.htm.jsonl, page 52

The retained franchise is profitable: nine-month segment operating profit rose 20.0% and the bank facilities now run to 2031. Against that, 2026 growth was bought through the BIOTRONIK acquisition, the chief executive, Chair and board all changed during 2026, an activist wants strategic alternatives reviewed, and the Acute Care and Interventional Urology sale has slipped towards 2027.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_tfx.htm.jsonl, pages 48–49; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tfx.htm.jsonl, pages 20–21; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tfx.htm.jsonl, page 41; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tfx.htm.jsonl, page 60; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tfx.htm.jsonl, page 12

### Key findings

The business creditors keep is smaller, and about three quarters of its 2026 revenue growth was bought rather than earned. Continuing operations were 60.44% of FY2025 combined net revenues, with the other 39.56% sold or held for sale. Net revenues rose 18.4% in the nine months of 2026, but USD 198.1 million of the USD 262.7 million increase, or 75.41%, came from the BIOTRONIK business bought on 2025-06-30, and third-quarter revenue rose only 0.2%.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_tfx.htm.jsonl, page 124; 10-K_2025-12-31_tfx.htm.jsonl, page 127; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tfx.htm.jsonl, page 44; calculation C01; calculation C04

Interventional is the growth engine and the weak point. It supplied 37.17% of nine-month segment revenue after the acquisition, yet its segment margin was 10.37% against 14.10% a year earlier, its third-quarter revenue fell 2.6%, and its fair value exceeded carrying value by only about 15%, a cushion that a 1% rise in the discount rate would cut to below 5%. A further shortfall in the acquired business would first appear as an impairment signal.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_tfx.htm.jsonl, page 37; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tfx.htm.jsonl, pages 48–49; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tfx.htm.jsonl, pages 41–42; calculation C03; calculation C05

Governance changed on every front in 2026, and the filings do not yet state how the new board will weigh creditors against shareholders. The Chairman, President and Chief Executive Officer left on 2026-01-08, an interim chief executive served for five months and a new one started on 2026-06-08; an independent Chair was appointed on 2026-01-08 and replaced on 2026-05-15; two directors left on 2026-05-15 and three joined, on 2026-05-15, 2026-06-08 and 2026-09-08; and an activist called for board changes and a review of strategic alternatives on 2026-03-27. No leverage target or credit rating is stated, and the proxy statement with board and ownership detail was not supplied.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_tfx.htm.jsonl, page 8; 10-Q_2026-03-31_tfx.htm.jsonl, page 28; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tfx.htm.jsonl, page 41; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tfx.htm.jsonl, page 60; Source readiness (CP-0)

Management has said shareholders will share in the sale proceeds, and its 2026 actions are mixed rather than adverse. The 2025 annual report and the first-quarter 2026 report said the expected net after-tax proceeds of about USD 1.8 billion would be used "primarily to return capital to shareholders through share repurchases and pay down debt", and the later reports do not repeat the statement. After the OEM sale closed on 2026-08-03 the company repaid USD 1,190.0 million of bank debt, and from 2026-07-01 to the report date it also repurchased USD 350.0 million of stock, so repayment was 3.40 times repurchases in that window. Since 2026-01-01 repurchases total USD 600.0 million, USD 400.0 million of the USD 1.0 billion authorisation remains, and the dividend is USD 0.34 per share a quarter.

The Acute Care and Interventional Urology sale, worth approximately USD 530 million gross, was first expected in the second half of 2026, then in the fourth quarter and now by the end of 2026 with closing possibly in 2027. ### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against caution is what management has done with cash. It repaid the USD 700.0 million Term A-2 loan and USD 490.0 million of the revolver within weeks of the OEM closing, extended the revolver and Term A-1 to 2031, redeemed the 2027 notes, and raised segment operating profit 20.0% in nine months with Vascular up 16.5% and Surgical up 34.3%. No acquisition, extraordinary dividend or creditor-adverse transaction is disclosed after 2025, repurchases to date are smaller than debt repayment, and the dividend is unchanged at USD 0.34 per share.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_tfx.htm.jsonl, page 22; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tfx.htm.jsonl, pages 20–21; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tfx.htm.jsonl, pages 48–49; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tfx.htm.jsonl, page 5; calculation C07

The view would turn more cautious if the Acute Care and Interventional Urology proceeds, or the remaining USD 400.0 million authorisation and a larger one, went mainly to buybacks while the fourth-quarter taxes are drawn on the revolver; if the Palette claim succeeds above the contingent consideration liability; if the Acute Care sale slips beyond 2027 or fails; or if the review of strategic alternatives the activist wants ends in a sale of the company, which could engage the notes' repurchase right on a change of control if a ratings downgrade accompanies it. It would improve if the new chief executive states a leverage policy, if sale proceeds are applied to debt, and if the guarantor perimeter and segment reporting stop changing.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_tfx.htm.jsonl, page 52; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tfx.htm.jsonl, page 54; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tfx.htm.jsonl, page 35; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tfx.htm.jsonl, page 12; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tfx.htm.jsonl, page 60; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tfx.htm.jsonl, pages 22–23

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

Three transaction streams define the credit. In 2025 Teleflex bought the BIOTRONIK vascular intervention business, completed on 2025-06-30 for a net initial cash payment of EUR 704.3 million, or USD 825.2 million, against an agreed initial price of EUR 760 million before adjustments, funded with USD 700 million of delayed draw term loan borrowings and USD 140 million drawn on the revolver. On 2025-12-09 it signed agreements to sell the OEM business to Montagu and Kohlberg and the Acute Care and Interventional Urology businesses to Intersurgical for USD 2.0 billion in cash in total, about USD 1.5 billion and USD 530 million respectively. In 2026 it refinanced its bank debt and its 2027 notes and repaid bank debt from the OEM proceeds.
Basis: 10-K_2024-12-31_tfx.htm.jsonl, page 63; 10-K_2025-12-31_tfx.htm.jsonl, page 62; 10-K_2025-12-31_tfx.htm.jsonl, page 124; 10-K_2025-12-31_tfx.htm.jsonl, page 8

The OEM sale closed on 2026-08-03 for net cash proceeds of USD 1.5 billion, USD 1.3 billion after tax, and a pre-tax gain of USD 1.0 billion; the June report had put the after-tax figure at approximately USD 1.2 billion and the later report controls. Ancillary agreements with the buyers run for up to 24 months and their results sit in selling, general and administrative expenses. Cash taxes on the gain are due in the fourth quarter of 2026 and will be funded with the revolver and cash.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_tfx.htm.jsonl, page 11; 10-Q_2026-06-30_tfx.htm.jsonl, page 34; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tfx.htm.jsonl, page 54

The Acute Care and Interventional Urology sale had not closed at the report date. Gross cash proceeds of approximately USD 530 million are expected, subject to working capital adjustments and transaction costs, closing may extend into 2027, and the transition agreements are to be executed at closing. The held-for-sale assets carry a valuation allowance of USD 758,859 thousand at 2026-09-30, which shows the agreed price is far below the carrying value of the businesses.
