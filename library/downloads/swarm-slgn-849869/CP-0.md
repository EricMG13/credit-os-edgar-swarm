## Gaps

No executed credit agreement, amendment or indenture is in the source set. The annual report lists them as exhibits and the first-quarter report lists the sixth amendment dated March 6, 2026, but the run settings declare no legal documents, so covenant definitions, baskets, guarantor and security terms and the call and change-of-control terms cannot be read from the governing documents. Legal & covenants, Covenant capacity, Structural priority, Intercreditor control and Recovery & fulcrum work would rest on filing summaries alone and cannot run until the documents are supplied. This is a material gap.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_slgn.htm.jsonl, pages 91–96; 10-Q_2026-03-31_slgn.htm.jsonl, page 25

The filings name two financial covenants, Interest Coverage and Total Net Leverage, and state that the company is in compliance, but print no covenant level, no step-down and no reported ratio. Covenant headroom is therefore not established, and the credit agreement's own definition of Total Net Leverage is not available to compare with any leverage measure built from the statements. This is a material gap.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_slgn.htm.jsonl, page 76; 10-K_2025-12-31_slgn.htm.jsonl, page 118; 10-Q_2026-09-30_slgn.htm.jsonl, page 27

The announcement of October 19, 2026 is described only in one note to the third-quarter report: a partnership with an investment vehicle affiliated with funds managed by American Industrial Partners, the contribution of the Metal Containers segment and the food and beverage flat cap closures business, about USD 2.3 billion of cash to the company intended to retire existing debt, a 60% controlling interest for the company and 40% for the partner, about USD 1.0 billion of partner cash, about USD 4.9 billion of new secured term loans and a USD 1.0 billion secured revolver at the partnership, and the proposed acquisition of Trivium Packaging B.V. for about USD 327.5 million in cash plus about USD 3.1 billion of assumed debt, expected to close in mid-2027 subject to regulatory approvals and works council consultation.
