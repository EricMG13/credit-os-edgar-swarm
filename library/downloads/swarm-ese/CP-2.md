## Findings

### Bottom line

ESCO's standalone credit profile is strong, with net debt at 0.04x EBITDA on 2026-06-30, but the pending Megger acquisition would lift it to 2.98x pro forma, a step-up creditors cannot size because Megger's earnings, debt and the new facility's covenants are not supplied.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); Business & governance (CP-1A); 10-Q_2026-06-30_ese-20260630x10q.htm.jsonl, page 19; 10-Q_2026-06-30_ese-20260630x10q.htm.jsonl, page 12; calculation C01; calculation C02; calculation C03; calculation C04

Net debt was USD 11.76 million at 2026-06-30, USD 13.86 million after the dividend paid on 2026-07-17 and USD 13.84 million after the one scheduled for 2026-10-15, both 0.05x, and USD 911.76 million pro forma for the Megger cash price of USD 0.9 billion, with closing expected in the first quarter of fiscal 2027. Cash and the undrawn revolver of USD 515.54 million cover total debt of USD 85 million, or 6.07x.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); 10-Q_2026-06-30_ese-20260630x10q.htm.jsonl, page 11; 10-Q_2026-06-30_ese-20260630x10q.htm.jsonl, page 19; calculation C01; calculation C02; calculation C03; calculation C04; calculation C10; calculation C11

### Key findings

Standalone leverage and coverage leave a wide cushion before any acquisition debt. Total debt was USD 85 million, or 0.28x last-twelve-months EBITDA of USD 305.68 million, against 0.76x at 2025-09-30, and EBITDA covered cash interest 20.42x against 14.09x in fiscal 2025. Debt fell because the VACCO proceeds were used primarily to pay down borrowings after the Maritime purchase, so the acquisition debt did not stay in place, and the ratios use reported EBITDA with Maritime counted only from 2025-04-25.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); 10-K_2025-09-30_ese-20250930x10k.htm.jsonl, page 32; 10-Q_2026-06-30_ese-20260630x10q.htm.jsonl, page 3; calculation C05; calculation C41

Liquidity is ample against the debt, and no maturity of size falls before 2028-08-30. Cash of USD 73.24 million and the undrawn USD 442.3 million of the revolver give USD 515.54 million, 6.07x total debt and 25.78x the USD 20 million due within twelve months, with USD 45 million of revolver borrowings and USD 12.7 million of letters of credit already deducted. Liquidity is USD 50.71 million lower than at 2025-09-30, which reflects repaid term debt and the cash tax on the VACCO gain, not operating weakness; the revolver and term loan A both mature on 2028-08-30.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); 10-Q_2026-06-30_ese-20260630x10q.htm.jsonl, page 11; 10-Q_2026-06-30_ese-20260630x10q.htm.jsonl, page 25; calculation C07; calculation C10; calculation C11; calculation C12; calculation C39; calculation C40

Cash generation is good, but part of it is timing. Free cash flow was USD 258.10 million over the last twelve months, 84.44% of EBITDA against 60.41% in fiscal 2025 and 41.04% in fiscal 2024, helped by customer advances: contract liabilities rose 33.04% to USD 288.14 million since 2025-09-30. A full reversal of the USD 71.55 million increase would use 97.70% of cash but only 13.88% of liquidity, so the timing benefit is not a liquidity risk on its own, but part of the improvement in conversion will not repeat.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); 10-Q_2026-06-30_ese-20260630x10q.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-Q_2026-06-30_ese-20260630x10q.htm.jsonl, page 24; calculation C06; calculation C16; calculation C15; calculation C14; calculation C21; calculation C09; calculation C17; calculation C18; calculation C19

The Megger acquisition is the dominant credit event and cannot yet be sized. Net debt to EBITDA would rise from 0.04x to 2.98x pro forma for the cash price, with closing expected in the first quarter of fiscal 2027, and that figure uses ESCO's own EBITDA only, with no Megger earnings and no Megger debt. The total price of about USD 2.35 billion is 7.69x ESCO's last-twelve-months EBITDA and 97.11% of its total assets, the cash part alone is 2.94x, and the equity part, 59.57% of the price, adds no debt but adds a shareholder. As an illustration, interest on USD 0.9 billion at the existing facility's recent average rate of 5.18% would be about USD 46.62 million a year, 15.25% of last-twelve-months EBITDA, but the new facility's pricing is not supplied.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); Business & governance (CP-1A); 10-Q_2026-06-30_ese-20260630x10q.htm.jsonl, page 11; 10-Q_2026-06-30_ese-20260630x10q.htm.jsonl, page 19; calculation C22; calculation C23; calculation C24; calculation C25; calculation C26; calculation C27

Revenue is visible but concentrated in defense. Backlog of USD 1,540.5 million is 1.19x last-twelve-months net sales and about 59% of it is expected to convert within twelve months; entered orders were 1.43x net sales over nine months. The A&D segment, which serves Navy and aerospace customers, was 49.29% of nine-month net sales and 71.56% of backlog, government customers were 31.63% of fiscal 2025 sales, government contracts can be terminated for convenience, and funding delays on submarine programs can last more than a year.
Basis: Business & governance (CP-1A); 10-K_2025-09-30_ese-20250930x10k.htm.jsonl, page 10; 10-K_2025-09-30_ese-20250930x10k.htm.jsonl, page 18; 10-Q_2026-06-30_ese-20260630x10q.htm.jsonl, page 16; 10-Q_2026-06-30_ese-20260630x10q.htm.jsonl, page 21; calculation C08; calculation C32

Margins are stable to rising and cost flexibility is moderate. EBITDA margin was 23.68% over the last twelve months against 22.40% in fiscal 2025 and 21.58% in fiscal 2024, A&D earned a segment margin of 28.41% in the nine months, the company attributes higher segment margins to volume leverage and price increases partly offset by inflationary pressures, and capital expenditure is only 3.32% of sales. A fixed-cost share is not disclosed, and fixed-price contracts expose margin to cost overruns, with over-time revenue at 45.18% of fiscal 2025 sales, so cost estimates at completion are the main margin risk.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); Business & governance (CP-1A); EX-99.1_2026-08-06_tm2621646d1_ex99-1.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-K_2025-09-30_ese-20250930x10k.htm.jsonl, page 18; 10-K_2025-09-30_ese-20250930x10k.htm.jsonl, page 53

Financial policy has favored creditors so far, with acquisitions the main use of cash. Dividends took 4.12% of fiscal 2025 operating cash flow, no shares were bought after fiscal 2024 despite an authority of up to USD 200 million, and the VACCO proceeds went to debt. Against that, acquisition cash over fiscal 2023 to 2025 was 1.95x free cash flow, so acquisitions are financed beyond internal cash generation, which is the pattern behind the current transaction; governance risk is rated medium because the legal documents and the new holder's rights are not available.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); Business & governance (CP-1A); 10-K_2025-09-30_ese-20250930x10k.htm.jsonl, page 32; 10-K_2025-09-30_ese-20250930x10k.htm.jsonl, page 40; 10-K_2025-09-30_ese-20250930x10k.htm.jsonl, page 81; calculation C29; calculation C30; calculation C31

Refinancing is not an issue before the transaction, and the transaction replaces the facility. The existing revolver and term loan A mature on 2028-08-30, with USD 65 million left after the USD 20 million current portion; the new facility signed on 2026-05-29 provides a five-year revolver and term loan A and a seven-year term loan B, USD 1.5 billion in total, effective on conditions that include the Megger closing. Market access is untested in the sources: no rating, quote or bond is supplied, and the credit agreement terms that decide headroom are not available.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); 10-K_2025-09-30_ese-20250930x10k.htm.jsonl, page 80; 10-Q_2026-06-30_ese-20260630x10q.htm.jsonl, page 11; 10-Q_2026-06-30_ese-20260630x10q.htm.jsonl, page 12; Source readiness (CP-0)

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The view could be too cautious. The equity part of about USD 1.4 billion is 59.57% of the price and adds no debt, the financing is committed, total debt at 2026-06-30 was below its USD 122 million level before the Maritime purchase, and Megger's own earnings, which would lower the 2.98x figure, are not counted in it. It could also be too optimistic: reported EBITDA includes Maritime from 2025-04-25 and a customer-advance benefit, backlog conversion depends on government funding, and the covenant levels and new facility terms are unseen.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); Business & governance (CP-1A); 10-Q_2026-06-30_ese-20260630x10q.htm.jsonl, page 12; 10-Q_2026-06-30_ese-20260630x10q.htm.jsonl, page 19; calculation C25

The view would improve if Megger's earnings and debt, once supplied, showed combined leverage at or below the pro forma figure, and if the covenant package left wide headroom. It would worsen if the term loans were drawn above the cash price, closing slipped, backlog stopped growing, free cash flow fell back toward the 60.41% of EBITDA of fiscal 2025, or distributions rose after closing.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); 10-Q_2026-06-30_ese-20260630x10q.htm.jsonl, page 12; calculation C15

### Limitations

- Covenant levels, definitions and the terms of the new facility are not supplied, so liquidity and refinancing are judged on drawn and undrawn amounts only, and a tight covenant would turn the liquidity reading from ample to constrained.
  Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_ese-20250930x10k.htm.jsonl, page 80; 10-Q_2026-06-30_ese-20260630x10q.htm.jsonl, page 11; 10-Q_2026-06-30_ese-20260630x10q.htm.jsonl, page 12; Source readiness (CP-0)
- The pro forma net leverage excludes Megger's earnings, debt and cash and the fees of the deal, so it is a mechanical figure on ESCO's own EBITDA and the leverage after closing could be higher or lower; the downside conclusion therefore rests on direction, not size.
  Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); 10-Q_2026-06-30_ese-20260630x10q.htm.jsonl, page 19
- The latest balance sheet is dated 2026-06-30, so year-end fiscal 2026 cash, debt and results are not established, and the standalone strength reading could move if the fourth quarter differed from the guidance range.
  Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_ese-20260630x10q.htm.jsonl, page 3; EX-99.1_2026-08-06_tm2621646d1_ex99-1.htm.jsonl, page 3
- No rating, price quote, peer set or multi-year projection is supplied, so the assessments are absolute, not relative to peers, and the outlook beyond fiscal 2026 is judgement rather than sourced.

### Strengths and weaknesses

The first credit strength is low standalone leverage with high coverage. Total debt of USD 85 million is 0.28x EBITDA and cash interest is covered 20.42x, so a large fall in earnings would still leave interest comfortably covered: halving last-twelve-months EBITDA would still give 10.21x cover. That keeps default probability low on the standalone business.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); calculation C28

The second is liquidity with no near maturity. Cash plus the undrawn revolver of USD 515.54 million is 6.07x total debt, and the facility matures on 2028-08-30, so there is no refinancing pressure on the existing structure before the Megger closing.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); 10-Q_2026-06-30_ese-20260630x10q.htm.jsonl, page 11; calculation C10; calculation C11

The third is visible demand and rising margins. Backlog of USD 1,540.5 million is 1.19x annual sales, orders exceed sales, and EBITDA margin rose from 21.58% in fiscal 2024 to 23.68% over the last twelve months, which supports debt service through a cycle.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); 10-Q_2026-06-30_ese-20260630x10q.htm.jsonl, page 21; calculation C08; calculation C32

The fourth is a financial policy that has repaid debt and kept distributions small. Dividends took 4.12% of operating cash flow and the VACCO proceeds went primarily to debt, which lowers leakage and improves recovery prospects.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); 10-K_2025-09-30_ese-20250930x10k.htm.jsonl, page 32; 10-K_2025-09-30_ese-20250930x10k.htm.jsonl, page 40; calculation C29

The fifth is a spread across three segments and end markets with no customer above 10% of sales: A&D, USG and Test earned segment margins of 28.41%, 22.78% and 14.22% in the nine months, so a problem in one segment does not stop the group from servicing debt.
Basis: Business & governance (CP-1A); 10-K_2025-09-30_ese-20250930x10k.htm.jsonl, page 86

The first weakness is the Megger transaction: net debt would rise to USD 911.76 million pro forma, 2.98x ESCO's own EBITDA, and the price is 97.11% of total assets, so the credit moves from almost debt-free to acquisition-levered on terms that are not visible.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); Business & governance (CP-1A); calculation C04; calculation C24

The second is dependence on government programs: A&D is 49.29% of nine-month sales, government customers 31.63% of fiscal 2025 sales, and funding delays on Navy submarine programs can last more than a year, so a budget change would hit the largest and highest-margin segment first.
Basis: Business & governance (CP-1A); 10-K_2025-09-30_ese-20250930x10k.htm.jsonl, page 10; 10-K_2025-09-30_ese-20250930x10k.htm.jsonl, page 18

The third is an asset base weighted to acquired intangibles: goodwill and intangible assets are 58.87% of total assets, so recovery value rests on the earnings of acquired businesses rather than on tangible collateral.
Basis: Business & governance (CP-1A); 10-Q_2026-06-30_ese-20260630x10q.htm.jsonl, page 3

The fourth is a cash-flow benefit from customer advances, which would reverse if deliveries outpace new payments, and a margin exposure to fixed-price contract estimates.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); 10-Q_2026-06-30_ese-20260630x10q.htm.jsonl, page 24; 10-K_2025-09-30_ese-20250930x10k.htm.jsonl, page 53; calculation C17

The fifth is the missing legal and ownership detail: covenant levels, the new facility terms and the Shareholder Agreement with TBG AG are not supplied, so creditor protection after closing cannot be assessed.
Basis: Business & governance (CP-1A); 10-Q_2026-06-30_ese-20260630x10q.htm.jsonl, page 12; 10-Q_2026-06-30_ese-20260630x10q.htm.jsonl, page 30

### Competitive forces, external factors and opportunities and threats

### Downside scenario

A realistic downside starts with a delay or cut in U.S. Government funding for Navy submarine programs, which Globe and Westland depend on and which the company says can extend beyond a single year, arriving while Megger has closed and the new term loans are drawn. The first operational break is in A&D, the largest segment at 49.29% of nine-month sales: program deliveries slip, backlog converts later, and customer advances stop growing, so the USD 71.55 million build in contract liabilities starts to reverse. EBITDA and operating cash flow fall together, free cash flow conversion drops back toward the 60.41% of fiscal 2025, and on today's liquidity of USD 515.54 million, 7.21x the contract liability build, the reversal alone would be absorbed, although liquidity after closing depends on the new revolver and the cash used for the price, which are not established.
