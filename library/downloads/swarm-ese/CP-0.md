## Findings

### Bottom line

The seven supplied documents support a credit snapshot of ESCO as of the quarter ended June 30, 2026, but not its position at the 2026-11-05 cutoff, because nothing after August 2026 is supplied. That matters because the USD 2.35 billion Megger acquisition was signed but not closed at the latest balance sheet date, its new bank facilities replace the existing facility only on closing, and no supplied document says whether it has since closed.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_ese-20260630x10q.htm.jsonl, page 12; 10-Q_2026-06-30_ese-20260630x10q.htm.jsonl, page 19; calculation C1; calculation C2

Statements, debt balances, liquidity inputs, segment data and backlog are present for the Financial foundation, Business & governance and Fundamental credit work, on a fiscal year ending September 30. Covenant levels, executed credit agreements, the proxy statement and any rating or market data are not supplied, so those points stay explicit gaps rather than assumptions.
