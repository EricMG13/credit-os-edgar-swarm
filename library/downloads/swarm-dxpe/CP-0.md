## Findings

### Bottom line

The seven supplied filings and releases are sufficient and current to support a credit snapshot of DXP ENTERPRISES INC as of 2026-11-05, with limits on legal, market and peer evidence that bound what creditors can conclude about covenants, pricing and relative value.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_dxpe-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 37; EX-99.1_2026-11-05_earningsrelease9302026.htm.jsonl, page 1; calculation C1

The latest quarter ended 36 days before the as-of date, and the annual report, three quarterly reports and three releases give full statements, debt, liquidity and segment disclosure for the consolidated company. Total debt of USD 839.5 million agrees between the quarterly report and the release, and cash plus revolver availability recomputes to the stated liquidity of USD 411.0 million.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_dxpe-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 11–14; 10-Q_2026-09-30_dxpe-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 33; EX-99.1_2026-11-05_earningsrelease9302026.htm.jsonl, page 2; calculation C2; calculation C4; calculation C5

One conflict remains open: the annual report schedules the secured leverage covenant at 5.50x for the June and September 2026 tests, while both later quarterly reports state 5.75x, and the executed credit agreements that would settle it are not supplied. Peers, market quotes, a portfolio, numeric management guidance and the proxy statement are also absent, and the Moody's rating is dated 2025-12-31.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_dxpe-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 84; 10-Q_2026-06-30_dxpe-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 11; 10-Q_2026-09-30_dxpe-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 12; 10-K_2025-12-31_dxpe-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 54
