## Findings

### Bottom line

The supplied filings support a credit snapshot of TransAlta Corporation as at the end of Q3 2026, but not an annual FY2025 comparison, instrument-level debt terms or covenant headroom, so creditor conclusions must stay at the level of consolidated leverage, liquidity and operations.
Basis: 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-11-03_q32026newsreleasecombined.htm.jsonl, pages 67–71; 40-F_2025-12-31_tac-20251231_d2.htm.jsonl, page 2

The Form 40-F is only a cover document: the audited statements, annual management discussion and annual information form it incorporates are not supplied, so year-end 2025 appears only as comparatives inside the 2026 interim filings. The three quarterly management discussion exhibits repeat text already inside the combined news releases, so the evidence is four distinct filings, not seven files.
Basis: 40-F_2025-12-31_tac-20251231_d2.htm.jsonl, pages 2–5; 40-F_2025-12-31_tac-20251231_d2.htm.jsonl, page 10; calculation C05

Debt is known by category and maturity year, not by instrument, and covenants are described only as customary and complied with. The October 2026 acquisition and minority sale fall after the balance sheet date and no pro forma leverage is supplied, so reported period figures and the effect of those transactions cannot be combined.
Basis: 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-11-03_q32026newsreleasecombined.htm.jsonl, page 87; 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-11-03_q32026newsreleasecombined.htm.jsonl, page 89; 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-11-03_q32026newsreleasecombined.htm.jsonl, page 39; 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-11-03_q32026newsreleasecombined.htm.jsonl, page 98
