## Findings

### Bottom line

Leverage is moderate and cash-backed, but the 3.56x gross and 2.56x net multiples of last-twelve-months EBITDA at 2026-09-30 are flattered by working-capital timing and equity income, so creditors should rely on the long maturity runway rather than the multiple.

Gross debt is USD 2.02 billion, almost all fixed-rate or zero-coupon, with nothing due before 2028 and the USD 1,250 million revolver undrawn. EBITDA covered cash interest 13.84x over the last twelve months against 7.88x in FY2025, because the zero-coupon convertible notes replaced bank debt. The medical business sale of up to USD 800 million is pending and net debt after closing is not established.
Basis: calculation C22; calculation C23; calculation C24; calculation C21; calculation C42; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bwxt_bwxt-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 36–41; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bwxt_bwxt-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 15–16

### Key findings

Leverage peaked at the end of FY2025 and has eased since. Gross debt over derived EBITDA was 2.26x at FY2024, 3.92x at FY2025 and 3.56x at 2026-09-30, and net debt over EBITDA was 2.10x, 2.95x and 2.56x. Gross debt almost doubled in FY2025 as the convertible notes and the Kinectrics purchase were financed, and EBITDA growth has since absorbed part of it.
Basis: calculation C16; calculation C17; calculation C19; calculation C20; calculation C22; calculation C23; 10-K_2025-12-31_bwxt_bwxt-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 80–81; 10-K_2025-12-31_bwxt_bwxt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 111

Interest cover improved sharply because the main new debt carries no coupon. EBITDA covered cash interest 6.44x in FY2024, 7.88x in FY2025 and 13.84x over the last twelve months, and the issuer's own schedule puts contractual interest due within a year at USD 33.0 million, which last-twelve-months EBITDA covers 17.22x. Cash interest paid over the last twelve months of USD 41.1 million still includes pre-refinancing months, so run-rate cover is higher than the last-twelve-months figure.
Basis: calculation C18; calculation C21; calculation C24; calculation C30; calculation C49; 10-K_2025-12-31_bwxt_bwxt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 73

Earnings quality needs care because EBITDA includes equity income of joint ventures. That income is 15.93% of last-twelve-months EBITDA and was only partly received in cash: income of investees net of dividends was a USD 31,573 thousand deduction from operating cash flow in the nine months of 2026, which means the joint ventures retained more than they paid out.
Basis: calculation C11; calculation C12; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bwxt_bwxt-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 2–3; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bwxt_bwxt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 6

Cash generation looks stronger than it is. Last-twelve-months operating cash flow equals 101.81% of EBITDA for a business spending 5.73% of revenue on capital expenditure, because accounts payable rose USD 150,334 thousand on vendor payment timing and advance billings rose USD 123,057 thousand to USD 428.3 million. Free cash flow of USD 372.3 million over the last twelve months is therefore partly borrowed from suppliers and customers and can reverse.
Basis: calculation C31; calculation C41; calculation C48; calculation C50; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bwxt_bwxt-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 6–7; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bwxt_bwxt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 45

Liquidity is strong and the first maturity is distant. Liquidity of USD 1.82 billion is 0.90x gross debt, the revolver has USD 1,248.6 million available after letters of credit, and the first maturity is the USD 400 million senior notes due in 2028, which cash on hand already covers.
Basis: calculation C35; calculation C36; calculation C44; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bwxt_bwxt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 38; 10-K_2025-12-31_bwxt_bwxt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 112

The convertible notes behave like straight debt. The conversion price of about USD 262.51 a share is far above the USD 152.39 average price paid for shares withheld in September 2026, and the notes' fair value of USD 1,092.0 million sits below their USD 1,250.0 million principal, so creditors should expect to be repaid in cash and no equity credit is warranted.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_bwxt_bwxt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 22; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bwxt_bwxt-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 40–41; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bwxt_bwxt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 47

Claims outside the debt figure are modest against EBITDA but real. Pension and retiree medical plans were underfunded by USD 127.1 million at FY2025, the quarterly report states underfunded plan obligations of approximately USD 149.0 million at 2026-09-30, surety bonds of USD 361.0 million and bilateral letters of credit of USD 37.6 million are contingent, and no purchase commitments, factoring or supply-chain finance are described.
Basis: calculation C34; calculation C46; 10-K_2025-12-31_bwxt_bwxt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 119; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bwxt_bwxt-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 43–44

The medical business is already separated as held for sale, with assets of USD 700.4 million, but the benefit to creditors depends on proceeds that are not stated. The agreement values the business at up to USD 800 million, the closing is expected in 2026 or the first quarter of 2027, and the part of EBITDA it contributes is not stated.
Basis: calculation C32; calculation C47; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bwxt_bwxt-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 15–16

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest case against caution is visibility. Backlog of USD 8,556.2 million is 2.38x last-twelve-months revenue, most of the Government Operations work is for the US Government, and the credit facility covenants (maximum leverage 4.00 times, minimum interest coverage 3.00 times) leave wide room against the 2.56x derived net multiple, with compliance reported at every date read. The view would soften if cash flow held up after the vendor timing benefit reverses, if the medical business sale closed at close to its headline value, or if EBITDA growth continued without new debt.

The view would harden if the revolver were drawn for an acquisition, if advance billings or vendor payables unwound while EBITDA stalled, if the sale failed to close, or if a rating action changed the lien position of the credit facility.
Basis: calculation C37; calculation C23; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bwxt_bwxt-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 36–38; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bwxt_bwxt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 47

### Limitations

- EBITDA is derived because the issuer states none, so every leverage and cover multiple depends on operating income plus depreciation and amortization; an issuer-adjusted figure could move the multiples in either direction.
- Covenant headroom is not computed because the credit agreement's EBITDA definition is not supplied, so the statement that covenants leave room rests on the reported compliance alone.
- The 2026-09-30 balance sheet separates the medical business as held for sale and earlier balance sheets are not recast, so comparisons of assets and liabilities with year-end mix the reclassification with trading.
- The maturity ladder is drawn at 2025-12-31 because the latest report gives no principal total, so a change in principal since then is not visible except that no borrowings were reported.
- No source states the legal rank or security of the senior notes or convertible notes, so the debt stack shows the revolver only and no structural or recovery conclusion should lean on it.
