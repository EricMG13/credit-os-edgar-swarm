## Findings

### Bottom line

The five Fortrea filings support Financial foundation, Business & governance and Fundamental credit, but they carry no reported covenant ratios, guarantor and collateral terms or adjusted earnings, so creditors should treat covenant headroom and debt ranking as unverified. The set runs from the 2024 annual report to the quarterly report for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, signed 20 days before the run date, so it covers the October 2026 acquisition as a subsequent event but nothing filed afterwards.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_ftre-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 25; 10-K_2025-12-31_ftre-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 188–189; calculation C8

The main limits are the missing executed credit agreement, indenture and receivables purchase agreement, the missing 2026 proxy statement, the absence of any earnings release, rating, quote or peer data, and a label conflict over whether the 2030 notes are secured. The first limit keeps Legal & covenants blocked, and the others narrow Business & governance, Financial foundation and Fundamental credit without stopping them.
