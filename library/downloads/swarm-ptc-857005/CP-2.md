## Findings

### Bottom line

Ptc Inc. has a strong fundamental credit profile, built on recurring software revenue and low leverage, so creditors are well covered today, but the buyback policy and a single fiscal 2028 maturity window are the real risks to that position.

USD 1,393.7 million of USD 1,425.0 million of scheduled principal falls due in fiscal 2028. Guided FY2026 repurchases of USD 1,625 million exceed guided free cash flow and sale cash by USD 251.7 million.

The revenue base is recurring and diversified, which makes debt service predictable. About 95% of revenue is recurring, no customer exceeded 10% of revenue, about 75% of sales are made directly, and annual run rate excluding the divested businesses grew 7% to USD 2,412.4 million at 30 June 2026. Management expects churn to remain low, but a retention rate is not disclosed.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a positive view concerns policy, not performance. Management showed within nine months that it will add debt to buy back stock: the Q3 2026 repurchase of USD 525 million was more than twice the quarter's earlier target, and the July 2026 repurchase was financed mainly by new borrowing. If that continued until the revolver was used up, total debt would reach 1.79x last-twelve-months EBITDA on the amounts stated at 30 June 2026, and a repeat of the Q3 2026 revenue shortfall would raise the ratio further because operating expenses did not flex. Operating expenses were also held flat partly by transition services income, which the issuer provides for up to 12 months from the sale.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_ptc-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 11; 10-Q_2026-06-30_ptc-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 19; 10-Q_2026-06-30_ptc-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 21; 10-Q_2026-06-30_ptc-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 24; 10-Q_2026-06-30_ptc-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 28; EX-99.1_2026-07-29_ptc-ex99_1.htm.jsonl, page 2; calculation C32; calculation C14; calculation C35

Evidence in the other direction is that cash conversion stayed high through the divestiture year and that the issuer repaid debt before the repurchase wave. The view would improve if repurchases are held to free cash flow and net debt returns near its FY2025 level, and would worsen if guided annual run rate growth of 9% to 9.5% is missed, if net borrowing funds repurchases beyond the guided USD 1,625 million, or if a covenant amendment or waiver appears.

About 95% of revenue is recurring, no customer exceeded 10% of revenue, and free cash flow of about USD 850 million is guided for FY2026 even after about USD 100 million of divestiture-related cash taxes. Debt principal equals 1.66 years of FY2025 free cash flow, so all of the debt could be repaid from internal cash flow in under two years if repurchases and other uses stopped.

### Company description

Ptc Inc. sells design and product lifecycle software to manufacturers through one reportable segment, and the subscription model supports cash-flow visibility and debt service. Product lifecycle management was 62.27% and computer-aided design 37.73% of FY2025 software revenue, and the Americas, Europe and Asia Pacific produced 48.45%, 36.33% and 15.22% of FY2025 revenue, so the business is spread across two product groups and three regions, although lifecycle management is the larger group. End markets are industrials, aerospace and defense, electronics, automotive and medical technology.

### Revenue drivers and pricing power

Revenue is predictable in its recurring part and lumpy in its license part. Support and cloud services were 53.63% of FY2025 revenue and are recognised over the contract term, while license revenue was 42.45% and is recognised when a contract starts, so it moves with contract timing: it grew 44.10% in FY2025 on larger and notably longer contracts and fell 18% in Q3 2026 on one renewal of shortened duration. Revenue grew 19.18% in FY2025, against annual run rate growth of 9.93%, and 11.64% in the nine months to June 2026, and the issuer cites commercial optimisation as a driver, but a price against volume split is not disclosed, so pricing power is not established.

### Downside scenario

The realistic downside starts with tariff-driven weakness among manufacturing customers, who delay purchases and shorten renewals. License revenue, which is recognised when a contract starts, falls first, as it did in Q3 2026 when revenue fell 6.82% and operating income fell 20.65% because operating expenses did not flex. Free cash flow then weakens, annual billing in advance means less cash is collected from smaller contract values, and if repurchases continue beyond free cash flow the revolver supplies the difference; drawing all of the amount available to borrow would take total debt to 1.79x EBITDA on the June 2026 amounts. Management can stop repurchases, which are discretionary, and capital expenditure is small, so resilience looks sufficient to avoid liquidity distress unless repurchases continue into the 2027 refinancing window.

Overall, Ptc Inc. presents a strong business profile supported by recurring subscription revenue, high margins and low leverage, but credit quality is constrained by debt-funded repurchases and a single fiscal 2028 maturity window.
