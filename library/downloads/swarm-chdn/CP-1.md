### Financial interpretation

Leverage has improved because debt fell while earnings rose. Total debt at carrying value fell from USD 5,130.2 million at 2025-12-31 to USD 4,722 million at 2026-09-30, mainly because revolver borrowings were repaid from operating cash flow of USD 660 million in nine months after USD 176 million of capital expenditure and USD 44 million of buybacks, while last-twelve-months reported EBITDA rose from USD 1,063.2 million to USD 1,157.2 million. The gain rests on restrained spending: capital project expenditure was USD 119 million against USD 172 million a year earlier, and buybacks were USD 44 million against USD 396 million, so a return to the pace of 2025 would slow deleveraging.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_chdn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 2–4; 10-Q_2026-09-30_chdn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 13; 10-K_2025-12-31_chdn-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 76–79; calculation C01; calculation C04; calculation C10

Cash interest cover improved to 4.22x from 3.61x because cash interest paid fell to USD 274.5 million on a last-twelve-months basis from USD 294.5 million as the revolver was paid down. That benefit is exposed to the October 2026 redemption of the USD 600 million 5.50% 2027 notes with revolver borrowings: the weighted average rate on the Credit Agreement was 5.48% at 2026-09-30, so the fixed coupon is replaced by a floating rate of similar cost, and the issuer states that a one-percentage-point rise in the reference rate would cost USD 12 million on USD 1.6 billion of variable debt before this redemption. The filing prints no post-redemption variable balance, so the new sensitivity is not established.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_chdn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-Q_2026-09-30_chdn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 28; 10-Q_2026-09-30_chdn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 47; 10-Q_2026-09-30_chdn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 49; calculation C05

Cash generation is of reasonable quality but depends on the equity investees. Distributions from unconsolidated affiliates were USD 106 million in nine months 2026 against equity income of USD 115 million, so cash receipts track reported earnings, and free cash flow after total capital expenditure was USD 484 million against USD 449 million in nine months 2025. Cash taxes paid rose to USD 65 million from USD 17 million, which absorbed part of the interest saving. Contract liabilities of USD 60 million at 2026-09-30 against USD 80 million at 2025-12-31 relate largely to advance Kentucky Derby receipts; they are working-capital float that unwinds after the spring and is not debt, but it flatters March quarter-end net debt.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_chdn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-Q_2026-09-30_chdn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 17; 10-Q_2026-09-30_chdn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 27; calculation C10

Liquidity at 2026-09-30 was unrestricted cash of USD 167 million plus about USD 1,111 million of undrawn revolver capacity after USD 10 million of letters of credit; restricted cash of USD 92 million is excluded. The strongest counter to the improving picture is the structure of the bank facilities: they were extended to September 2031 but carry a springing maturity if the notes maturing earlier are not refinanced at least 91 days before they mature, so the USD 700 million 2028 notes are the first practical test for the revolver and term loan A. What would change this view is a revolver draw after the October redemption that is larger than the principal redeemed, a resumption of large buybacks under the new USD 750 million authorization, or covenant ratios, which are not printed for 2026.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_chdn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 14; 10-Q_2026-09-30_chdn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 28; 10-Q_2026-09-30_chdn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 43; calculation C07

Two definition risks limit comparison with the issuer's own measures. The company prints Adjusted EBITDA only, which includes its share of the EBITDA of equity investees whose cash arrives as distributions, so leverage on Adjusted EBITDA (3.79x total) looks better than on reported EBITDA (4.08x), and the quarterly releases quote net bank leverage of 3.8x without a definition. The quarterly debt table labelled Net debt is the non-current debt balance before cash, not net debt in the usual sense.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-10-28_ex991pressrelease10-28x26.htm.jsonl, page 1; 10-Q_2026-09-30_chdn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 27; 10-Q_2026-09-30_chdn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 44; calculation C05; calculation C15
