## Findings

### Bottom line

Advanced Drainage Systems is releveraging: net debt reached 1.60x trailing reported EBITDA at 2026-09-30 from 0.92x at FY2025, and the pending StormTrap purchase and continuing buybacks would lift it further, so creditors face rising leverage on a business that covers cash interest 9.46x.

Liquidity of USD 891.88 million and no scheduled principal above USD 32,675 thousand a year before fiscal 2031 on the March 2026 ladder limit near-term risk. What the supplied filings leave open is how StormTrap will be funded and whether the 4.25x springing covenant is then tested, because the credit agreement and the covenant calculation are not supplied.

### Key findings

Leverage rose because the NDS acquisition was funded from cash on hand and new borrowing while reported earnings barely moved. Between FY2025 and FY2026 gross debt at balance-sheet value rose USD 350,300 thousand and cash fell USD 240,307 thousand, which lifted net debt from 0.92x to 1.59x of reported EBITDA, while EBITDA itself grew only 0.72%. At 2026-09-30 the multiple was 1.60x on trailing EBITDA of USD 912,734 thousand: gross debt was little changed at USD 1,611,197 thousand against USD 1,611,823 thousand in March, but cash was USD 69,767 thousand lower.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a cautious view is cash generation and the unused revolver. Trailing free cash flow of USD 529,952 thousand is 5.49 times trailing cash interest paid, the USD 750 million revolver is undrawn, and the issuer states that it complied with its covenants at 2026-09-30. The one maintenance test, a 4.25x senior secured net leverage ratio, applies only when revolver exposure exceeds 35% of commitments, so it does not apply while exposure sits far below that level. Reported EBITDA was also held back by restructuring and transaction costs of USD 48,299 thousand and USD 40,805 thousand in FY2026; on issuer-adjusted EBITDA net debt is 1.40x and Adjusted EBITDA grew 8.29% in FY2026.
Basis: calculation C21; calculation C66; calculation C83; calculation C40; 10-K_2026-03-31_wms_wms-20260331.htm.jsonl, pages 59–60; 10-K_2026-03-31_wms_wms-20260331.htm.jsonl, pages 66–67; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wms_wms-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 38–39

A stronger view would need evidence that NDS and StormTrap earnings are large enough to absorb the extra debt, that buybacks slow from USD 372,916 thousand in six months, and that the organic sales decline in the latest quarter is temporary. A weaker view would follow if StormTrap were funded mainly from the revolver: cash of USD 153,245 thousand at 2026-09-30 would, if no further cash were generated, leave USD 376,755 thousand to borrow, above the USD 262.5 million of revolver exposure at which the 4.25x test starts to apply, and the filings give neither the covenant definition nor the issuer's ratio, so headroom could not be confirmed.
