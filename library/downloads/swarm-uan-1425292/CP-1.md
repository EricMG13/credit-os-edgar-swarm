## Findings

### Bottom line

Leverage is moderate and the only borrowed debt matures in 2028, but EBITDA swings with fertilizer prices and plant downtime and most cash is distributed, so creditor protection rests on the cash balance and a small asset-based facility.
Net debt was USD 411.55 million at September 30, 2026, or 1.83x last-twelve-months EBITDA of USD 224.73 million against 2.37x at the end of 2025, and total debt was USD 569.74 million. Quarter-end cash of USD 158.19 million was lifted by customer prepayments, and the declared distribution of USD 18.9 million would take net leverage to 1.92x once paid on November 23, 2026.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_uan.htm.jsonl, page 12; 10-Q_2026-09-30_uan.htm.jsonl, page 6; calculation K1; calculation K2; calculation K9; calculation K16

### Key findings

Total debt to EBITDA was 1.95x in FY2023, 3.18x in FY2024, 2.70x in FY2025 and 2.54x for the last twelve months to September 30, 2026, and net debt to EBITDA was 1.79x, 2.67x, 2.37x and 1.83x. Debt moved by only USD 22.43 million over the period, almost all of it finance lease obligations first recorded in 2024, so the swings come from EBITDA, which fell from USD 281.1 million in FY2023 to USD 178.9 million in FY2024 before recovering. The notes carry a fixed coupon, so EBITDA to cash interest of 8.25x, 5.24x, 5.74x and 6.10x moved with EBITDA alone: creditors carry commodity-cycle risk on a stable debt balance.
Basis: 10-K_2024-12-31_uan.htm.jsonl, page 83; 10-K_2025-12-31_uan.htm.jsonl, page 87; 10-Q_2026-09-30_uan.htm.jsonl, page 6; 10-K_2025-12-31_uan.htm.jsonl, page 76; calculation K1; calculation K2; calculation K16

The third quarter of 2026 shows how quickly cover can fall: EBITDA was USD 19.72 million against USD 70.62 million a year earlier, with turnaround expenses of USD 26.84 million against USD 1.23 million, yet nine-month EBITDA of USD 204.56 million stayed above USD 190.68 million. The East Dubuque turnaround ended in mid-October 2026, so the lost output is behind the company, but the issuer expects the expanded capacity to be tested in the fourth quarter. The fourth quarter of 2025, derived at USD 20.17 million of EBITDA, also carried a turnaround, at Coffeyville, so two of the four quarters in the last-twelve-months figure were weak and that figure already absorbs both.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_uan.htm.jsonl, page 7; 10-Q_2026-09-30_uan.htm.jsonl, page 30; 10-Q_2026-09-30_uan.htm.jsonl, page 15; 10-Q_2026-09-30_uan.htm.jsonl, page 19; 10-K_2025-12-31_uan.htm.jsonl, page 79; calculation K17

Operating cash flow for the nine months of 2026 was USD 258.39 million, USD 87.15 million above the prior year, and USD 70.58 million of that rise came from working capital, chiefly customer prepayments recorded as deferred revenue and payables on turnaround projects. After cash capital expenditures of USD 64.51 million the free cash flow of USD 193.88 million funded distributions of USD 110.45 million and left cash USD 88.95 million higher. The working-capital inflow reverses as product ships, and the filings do not say how much of the cash balance is customer advances.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_uan.htm.jsonl, page 8; 10-Q_2026-09-30_uan.htm.jsonl, page 34; calculation K6; calculation K7

Deferred revenue, a non-interest-bearing float that funds operations, stood at USD 83.93 million at September 30, 2026 against USD 43.6 million at the end of 2025. Customers have prepaid against future deliveries, so part of the cash buffer is owed in product rather than available to creditors.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_uan.htm.jsonl, page 6; 10-K_2025-12-31_uan.htm.jsonl, page 87; calculation K8

Liquidity of USD 203.4 million at September 30, 2026 is cash of USD 158.2 million plus USD 45.2 million of availability under the asset-based facility, which has nothing drawn and matures in September 2028. Calls on it in the next quarter are the distribution of USD 18.9 million, fourth-quarter capital spending that the full-year estimate implies at USD 29.11 million to USD 38.11 million (the release outlook is USD 26 million to USD 34 million), and the semi-annual note coupon due in December. No principal on the notes or the facility falls due within twelve months: the notes mature in June 2028 and have been callable at par since June 15, 2026.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_uan.htm.jsonl, page 31; 10-Q_2026-09-30_uan.htm.jsonl, page 17; 10-Q_2026-09-30_uan.htm.jsonl, page 32; 10-K_2025-12-31_uan.htm.jsonl, pages 104–105; calculation K9; calculation K13

The debt total depends on perimeter. Total debt of USD 569.74 million includes finance lease obligations of USD 20.86 million, and the issuer's own long-term debt line for the notes alone is USD 548.88 million, which would read as 2.44x rather than 2.54x total debt to EBITDA. Capital expenditures also carry two bases, USD 64.51 million paid in cash and USD 65.89 million on the table basis, and every ratio here uses carrying value and the cash basis unless stated.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_uan.htm.jsonl, page 12; 10-Q_2026-09-30_uan.htm.jsonl, page 31; 10-Q_2026-09-30_uan.htm.jsonl, page 8; calculation K3; calculation K10; calculation K12

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against caution is the balance sheet: net leverage of 1.83x is below the 2.67x of FY2024 and the 2.37x of FY2025, interest cover of 6.10x is above FY2024 and FY2025, and liquidity of USD 203.4 million is well above USD 117.1 million at the end of 2025. That view would win if the third-quarter earnings loss proves a one-off turnaround effect and the East Dubuque expansion lifts output as planned.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_uan.htm.jsonl, page 6; 10-Q_2026-09-30_uan.htm.jsonl, page 31; calculation K1; calculation K2

The view would turn negative on a change of control, which the 2025 annual report says could follow a sale of the Icahn interest in CVR Energy: the 101% repurchase offer on the notes would call for about USD 555.5 million before accrued interest, against liquidity of USD 203.4 million, and the executed indenture that would settle the terms is not in the evidence. A fourth-quarter EBITDA that fails to recover from the USD 19.72 million of the third quarter, or capital spending at the top of the implied USD 38.11 million, would also draw down the cash that supports liquidity.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_uan.htm.jsonl, page 47; 10-Q_2026-09-30_uan.htm.jsonl, page 31; calculation K11; calculation K13

### Limitations

The executed indenture, ABL agreement and intercreditor agreement are not in the evidence, so security, guarantor scope and covenant levels are taken from filing summaries only. The three 2026 quarterly reports are unaudited, the fourth quarter of 2025 is not stated on its own, and last-twelve-months figures are derived by addition and subtraction rather than reported.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_uan.htm.jsonl, pages 172–175; 10-Q_2026-09-30_uan.htm.jsonl, page 7

Undrawn facility capacity at the end of 2023 is stated by the issuer to one decimal only, the borrowing base behind the facility's availability is not disclosed, and interim income tax lines are printed as dashes that this analysis does not read as zero. No adjusted EBITDA is stated apart from EBITDA, so every ratio uses the issuer's single EBITDA measure.
Basis: 10-K_2024-12-31_uan.htm.jsonl, page 74; 10-Q_2026-09-30_uan.htm.jsonl, page 12; 10-Q_2026-09-30_uan.htm.jsonl, page 7
