## Findings

### Bottom line

The eight filings and releases support a credit snapshot of Orion Group Holdings Inc as of 2026-11-18, but only on reported figures: no executed credit agreement is supplied, so covenant headroom, guarantor names and debt amortisation are not established for creditors.

The evidence runs from the 2024 annual report to the third-quarter 2026 report signed on 2026-10-28 and covers both the December 2025 refinancing and the February 2026 acquisition of J.E. McAmis. Reported debt, liquidity and covenant levels can be read directly. Leverage and coverage on the company's adjusted EBITDA need a full-year 2025 reconciliation that none of the documents contains.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_orn-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, pages 14–15; EX-99.1_2026-10-27_orn-ex99d1.htm.jsonl, pages 8–9; calculation CALC-01

### Key findings

The documents form a continuous record for one reporting entity, Orion Group Holdings, Inc. and its subsidiaries, from the 2022 fiscal year to the nine months to 2026-09-30, and nothing in them is dated after the cutoff. The three-year statements in the two annual reports agree on every overlapping figure tested, and the three 2026 quarters add to the nine-month revenue printed in the latest report.
Basis: 10-K_2024-12-31_orn-20241231x10k.htm.jsonl, pages 72–74; 10-K_2025-12-31_orn-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, pages 71–74; 10-Q_2026-03-31_orn-20260331x10q.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-Q_2026-06-30_orn-20260630x10q.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-Q_2026-09-30_orn-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 3; calculation CALC-10; calculation CALC-11; calculation CALC-13

Debt sits with one borrower and consists of a secured bank facility led by UMB Bank, a subordinated seller note, and lessor-financed equipment and sale-leaseback obligations. The documents show no bonds, indenture or co-issuer, so there is no second issuer perimeter to reconcile. Guarantors are described as every domestic subsidiary and are not named.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_orn-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, pages 14–16; 10-K_2025-12-31_orn-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, pages 87–89; 10-K_2025-12-31_orn-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 101

Reported liquidity and debt figures can be taken as stated, and they tie out. Borrowings under the UMB facility were USD 83.9 million, availability under the revolver was about USD 28.0 million, and the two maintenance covenants are a fixed charge coverage floor of 1.20x and a senior leverage cap of 3.00x, with compliance stated at 2026-09-30. The debt table, the balance sheet and the quarterly narrative agree exactly.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_orn-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, pages 14–15; 10-Q_2026-09-30_orn-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 32; calculation CALC-01; calculation CALC-02; calculation CALC-03; calculation CALC-04

Four things cannot be established. Covenant headroom needs the executed credit agreement's definitions and the ratios the company reports, and neither is in the documents. The repayment profile of the term loans is not disclosed beyond the December 2030 maturity, so a maturity wall by year is incomplete. Last-twelve-month adjusted EBITDA on the company definition needs a full-year 2025 reconciliation, and no document holds one. Surety capacity was last stated at the end of 2024, at least USD 1.1 billion with about USD 588 million bonded.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest argument that the set is ready without qualification is its recency and internal consistency: it runs to 2026-09-30, every tie-out performed agrees exactly, and compliance with all covenants is stated at each of the last four reporting dates. That is enough for a reader who needs only reported figures. It is not enough for covenant headroom, a full maturity wall or last-twelve-month adjusted EBITDA. The view would move to ready if the executed credit agreement with the latest reported ratios, the term loan repayment schedule and the full-year 2025 adjusted EBITDA reconciliation were added.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_orn-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 89; 10-Q_2026-03-31_orn-20260331x10q.htm.jsonl, page 13; 10-Q_2026-06-30_orn-20260630x10q.htm.jsonl, page 15; 10-Q_2026-09-30_orn-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 15; calculation CALC-01
