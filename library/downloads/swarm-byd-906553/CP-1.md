## Findings

### Bottom line

Net leverage is moderate at 2.17x trailing reported EBITDA, but net debt rose USD 685.8 million in nine months and USD 800.9 million of facility debt is now current ahead of the 2027 notes.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_byd_bgc20260930-10q.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 19–21; calculation F1; calculation F2; calculation E3

The rise reflects USD 475.0 million of share repurchases and USD 456.0 million of capital spending against operating cash flow of only USD 313.4 million after USD 364.2 million was paid for 2025 tax credits. Interest is not the pressure: trailing EBITDA of USD 1,094.1 million covers trailing cash interest of USD 111.7 million 9.79x, and the exposure is refinancing the 2027 notes and the springing maturity.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_byd_bgc20260930-10q.htm.jsonl, pages 6, 15, 21; calculation F3; calculation F4; calculation F5; calculation F7; calculation F10; calculation F11

### Key findings

Net debt rose from USD 1,692.2 million at December 31, 2025 to USD 2,377.9 million at September 30, 2026, so net leverage on trailing reported EBITDA moved from 1.61x to 2.17x and total debt leverage from 1.95x to 2.45x. The year-end 2025 position looked lighter than it was because the USD 1,758.0 million proceeds of the 2025 FanDuel sale had already repaid most of the facility and a USD 371.3 million payable for 2025 tax credits sat in accrued liabilities, outside debt, until it was paid in 2026.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_byd_bgc20260930-10q.htm.jsonl, pages 14, 19; 10-K_2025-12-31_byd_bgc20251121-10k.htm.jsonl, page 113; calculation N3; calculation N6; calculation F12; calculation F13; calculation F25; calculation F26

Operating cash flow was USD 313.4 million in the first nine months of 2026 against USD 701.4 million a year earlier because USD 364.2 million was paid for the 2025 tax credits; before that payment, operating cash flow would have exceeded capital expenditures of USD 456.0 million, not fallen USD 142.6 million short of them. Buybacks of USD 475.0 million and dividends of USD 43.4 million were paid for alongside USD 625.9 million of net facility borrowings, which is how net debt rose.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_byd_bgc20260930-10q.htm.jsonl, pages 6, 15, 58–59; calculation F3; calculation F4; calculation F5; calculation F6; calculation F7; calculation F8; calculation F9; calculation F23; calculation F24

Trailing reported EBITDA of USD 1,094.1 million covers trailing cash interest of USD 111.7 million 9.79x, up from 6.96x in fiscal 2025, because the third-quarter 2025 repayment cut interest and every quarter in the trailing figure falls after it. The facility has since grown from USD 160.7 million to USD 800.9 million, so trailing interest understates the current run-rate, and cover would fall if that borrowing is carried for a full year.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_byd_bgc20251121-10k.htm.jsonl, page 85; 10-Q_2026-09-30_byd_bgc20260930-10q.htm.jsonl, pages 6, 19; calculation E5; calculation F2; calculation F10; calculation F11; calculation F33

Reported EBITDA is calculated because the issuer prints none: it is operating income plus depreciation and amortization, so fiscal 2025 carries USD 128.4 million of impairments. The issuer's own Adjusted EBITDAR adds back master lease rent, which this module deducts, giving house-adjusted trailing EBITDA of USD 1,209.1 million and net leverage of 1.97x, USD 115.0 million above reported EBITDA. The lender-defined earnings measure and the covenant ratio are not printed.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_byd_bgc20251121-10k.htm.jsonl, page 143; 10-Q_2026-09-30_byd_bgc20260930-10q.htm.jsonl, page 36; calculation E2; calculation E4; calculation F14; calculation F15; calculation F28

Cash of USD 297.2 million plus USD 1,834.9 million of contractual availability gives USD 2,132.1 million, but all facility borrowings are current and USD 800.0 million of that availability is a delayed-draw term loan that can be drawn only until July 1, 2027.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_byd_bgc20260930-10q.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 20–21; calculation F16; calculation F17; calculation F18; calculation F34

Maturities are concentrated in 2027, when USD 1,800.9 million falls due: the facility at its September 1, 2027 springing maturity and the USD 1,000.0 million notes due December 1, 2027, against USD 900.0 million in 2031. The issuer states that facility availability is enough to retire the 2027 notes and lift the springing maturity, which would leave net debt unchanged but move USD 1,000.0 million from fixed-rate notes to floating-rate facility borrowings that carried a blended rate of 5.1%.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_byd_bgc20260930-10q.htm.jsonl, pages 21, 61; 10-K_2025-12-31_byd_bgc20251121-10k.htm.jsonl, pages 116–117; calculation F19; calculation F20; calculation F21; calculation F35

Obligations outside the debt figure are large: operating lease liabilities of USD 612.2 million, mostly the master leases whose undiscounted payments were USD 537.1 million at the 2025 year-end, and the Norfolk resort, for which the company expects USD 750.0 million of spending including USD 275.0 million in 2026, to be funded from cash on hand, the credit facility and operating cash flow. Leverage here excludes the lease liabilities, the Norfolk commitment and the small assessment arrangements, which sit on separate lines or outside the balance sheet.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_byd_bgc20251121-10k.htm.jsonl, page 65; 10-Q_2026-09-30_byd_bgc20260930-10q.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 19, 26, 64; calculation F37; calculation F38; calculation F39; calculation F40

In the third quarter the company repurchased shares for USD 163.9 million, sold Sam's Town Shreveport on August 1, 2026 and declared a dividend of USD 0.20 per share; the quarterly report identifies no subsequent event requiring disclosure, and the dividend payable on October 15, 2026 is the only dated post-quarter cash payment it states.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_byd_bgc20260930-10q.htm.jsonl, pages 27–28, 34, 39; calculation F31; calculation F41

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest case against concern is that leverage is still modest: net leverage of 2.17x is below the 2.37x of fiscal 2024, trailing interest cover is 9.79x, the issuer states that availability suffices to retire the 2027 notes, and the USD 364.2 million tax-credit payment settled a 2025 purchase. That case wins if buybacks slow and EBITDA holds; it fails if repurchases continue at the 2026 pace, because each USD 100 million of further net debt adds about 0.09x of net leverage on trailing reported EBITDA.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_byd_bgc20260930-10q.htm.jsonl, pages 19–21; 10-K_2025-12-31_byd_bgc20251121-10k.htm.jsonl, page 82; calculation F22; calculation F30

Trailing reported EBITDA is USD 43.0 million higher than in fiscal 2025, but only because impairments were smaller: house-adjusted EBITDA is USD 30.9 million lower, and Las Vegas Locals and Online earned less in the first nine months of 2026 than a year earlier. Weaker operations would change the view faster than the debt structure would.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_byd_bgc20260930-10q.htm.jsonl, pages 36, 38; calculation F30; calculation F36

### Limitations

Reported EBITDA is calculated from operating income and depreciation and amortization because the issuer prints no EBITDA, so it includes impairment charges and project costs that the issuer excludes from its own measure.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_byd_bgc20251121-10k.htm.jsonl, pages 82, 143

Trailing figures at March 31, June 30 and September 30, 2026 are derived from annual and stub-period statements, not reported, and a statement dash is shown as not supplied rather than zero.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_byd_bgc20260930-10q.htm.jsonl, pages 3–4; calculation E3

Debt is at carrying value and excludes operating lease liabilities, which the issuer carries on separate lines, so leverage here is not a lease-adjusted measure.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_byd_bgc20260930-10q.htm.jsonl, pages 2–3, 19
