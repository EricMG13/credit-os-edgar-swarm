## Findings

### Bottom line

The five supplied filings give creditors a primary-source base for financial, business and fundamental credit work on Ziff Davis, Inc., but they cannot test covenant headroom, ratings or legal priority, so those questions stay open until further documents arrive. Financial foundation, Business & governance and Fundamental credit can proceed on these filings alone, each with the limitations below.

Full-year 2025 figures on the continuing basis are therefore not printed.
