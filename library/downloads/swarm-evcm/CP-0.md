## Findings

### Bottom line
Source coverage is sufficient for a credit snapshot through the quarter ended June 30, 2026, but covenant, security and control conclusions are limited by missing legal documents and an unremediated weakness in revenue-accounting controls.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_evcm-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 56; 10-K_2025-12-31_evcm-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 204–206; 10-K_2025-12-31_evcm-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 208–209

Four discrete quarters, the audited 2025 statements and the debt note tie to one another on one consolidated perimeter, so leverage, coverage and liquidity work can proceed. The credit agreement, the terms of the chief executive transition, the letters of credit issued under the revolver and every period after June 30, 2026 are missing from the set. The main contrary point is that the filings report covenant compliance and no revolver borrowings, so these gaps limit confirmation of protections rather than signal stress. Supplying the credit agreement and the filings for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 would resolve the covenant, security and timeliness limits.
Basis: calculation C01; calculation C02; calculation C03; calculation C04; calculation C05; 10-Q_2026-06-30_evcm-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 20–21; 10-Q_2026-06-30_evcm-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 54
