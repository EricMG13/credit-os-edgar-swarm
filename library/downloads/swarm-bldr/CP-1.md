## Findings

### Bottom line

Creditor protection has weakened: total debt was 5.11x last-twelve-month reported EBITDA at 2026-09-30, against 3.22x at FY2025, because earnings fell while debt grew, and EBITDA covered cash interest only 3.18x.
Basis: calculation C61; calculation C55; calculation C63

The issuer's own adjusted measure looks milder, at 4.1x net debt to adjusted EBITDA, because its adjusted EBITDA is USD 224.70 million above reported EBITDA, mostly technology implementation and stock compensation add-backs. No note or revolver matures before 2030 and liquidity is USD 1,514.11 million, so the exposure is further earnings erosion rather than a near-term refinancing.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-10-29_bldr-ex99_1.htm.jsonl, page 3; EX-99.1_2026-10-29_bldr-ex99_1.htm.jsonl, page 13; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bldr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 27; calculation C78; calculation C45; calculation C48

### Key findings

Debt rose while earnings fell. Total debt at carrying value was USD 4,619.16 million at 2026-09-30, USD 177.80 million above FY2025, after the revolving facility was drawn for USD 197.00 million. Last-twelve-month reported EBITDA was USD 904.40 million, 34.35% below the USD 1,377.71 million of FY2025, so net debt of USD 4,564.15 million stands at 5.05x. The earnings fall, not new borrowing, explains most of the rise: had EBITDA stayed at the FY2025 level, total debt would be 3.35x EBITDA.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_bldr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bldr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 13; 10-K_2025-12-31_bldr-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 72; calculation C91; calculation C9; calculation C94; calculation C37; calculation C92

The fall is operational. Nine-month 2026 net sales were 7.01% lower than a year earlier, gross margin fell from 30.54% to 28.06% of sales and income from operations dropped from USD 724.50 million to USD 244.11 million, because selling and administrative costs fell only 1.57% while sales and commodity prices fell. The issuer attributes the decline to lower housing starts: core organic sales fell 6.40% and commodity deflation took 2.00%, partly offset by acquisitions. The quarterly sales decline narrowed from 10.13% in the first quarter to 2.21% in the third, but third-quarter gross margin of 27.82% was the lowest of the three quarters of 2026.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_bldr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bldr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 25; calculation C93; calculation C70; calculation C74; calculation C75

The gap between the two leverage views is the add-backs. Technology implementation expense is USD 149.00 million of the USD 224.70 million by which adjusted EBITDA exceeds reported EBITDA, or 13.20% of adjusted EBITDA, and it rose from USD 93.50 million in the first nine months of 2025 to USD 106.90 million in 2026; in this analyst's judgement a cost that recurs and grows is not a one-off. Total debt over adjusted EBITDA is 4.09x and net debt over adjusted EBITDA 4.04x, so last-twelve-month leverage runs from 4.04x to 5.11x depending on the definitions, and the issuer's headline of 4.1x uses principal debt.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-10-29_bldr-ex99_1.htm.jsonl, page 13; calculation C78; calculation C95; calculation C64; calculation C65; calculation C39

Interest cover has thinned sharply. Cash interest paid was USD 284.75 million over the last twelve months, up from USD 260.52 million in FY2025 and USD 188.45 million in FY2024, while reported EBITDA cover fell from 11.45x to 5.29x to 3.18x, or 3.97x on adjusted EBITDA. The interest bill is structural: the 6.75% 2035 notes issued in May 2025 added USD 750.00 million of debt, and the issuer also cites interest on other finance obligations after purchase options were exercised.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_bldr-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 73; 10-K_2025-12-31_bldr-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 92; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bldr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bldr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 26; calculation C54; calculation C57; calculation C63; calculation C66; calculation C11

Cash conversion has fallen sharply. Operating cash flow was USD 302.03 million in the first nine months of 2026 against USD 1,021.09 million a year earlier, as lower earnings met a working-capital build in receivables and inventories that higher payables only partly offset. Free cash flow on the issuer's definition was USD 150.88 million, 21.83% of nine-month reported EBITDA against 63.44% for FY2025. Buybacks of USD 303.52 million, almost all in the first quarter, were 2.01x free cash flow, and acquisitions took USD 116.68 million, so the revolver draw financed shareholder returns and deals, not operations.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_bldr-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 3–4; 10-Q_2026-03-31_bldr-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 3; calculation C25; calculation C96; calculation C4

Customer deposits are a small float and trade credit a large one. Contract liabilities, which hold customer advances and deposits and are recognised as revenue within the year, were USD 181.88 million at 2026-09-30, against USD 168.44 million at FY2025, so they unwind into sales rather than into a cash claim. Accounts payable rose USD 185.48 million to USD 900.19 million and supplied USD 178.06 million of nine-month operating cash flow; that support would reverse, and liquidity would fall, if suppliers shortened terms or purchases slowed.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_bldr-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 2–4; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bldr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 10; 10-K_2025-12-31_bldr-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 72; calculation C100

Liquidity is adequate but has narrowed. Cash of USD 55.01 million and net borrowing availability of USD 1,459.10 million give USD 1,514.11 million, down from USD 1,709.05 million at FY2025 and USD 1,784.62 million at FY2024. Availability is capped by a borrowing base of USD 1,731.70 million, not the USD 2,200.00 million of commitments, which leaves USD 468.30 million unusable. The fixed charge coverage test of 1.00x applies only if availability falls below USD 173.20 million; that is a trigger level, not a measure of covenant headroom.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_bldr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bldr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 27; 10-K_2025-12-31_bldr-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 63–64; calculation C41; calculation C43; calculation C45; calculation C47; calculation C97

The debt is long-dated and mostly fixed rate, which limits refinancing risk. Five senior unsecured note series total USD 4,300.00 million, maturing between 2030 and 2035; the only debt stated to be secured is the revolving facility, drawn for USD 197.00 million and due 2030-05-20. No note or revolver principal falls due before 2030; USD 747.00 million falls due in 2030, followed by USD 2,000.00 million in 2032. The notes rank behind the secured revolver and behind the non-guarantor subsidiaries, whose size is not disclosed, and USD 161.17 million of other finance obligations on leased properties sit in debt with no stated ranking.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_bldr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 9; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bldr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 13; 10-K_2025-12-31_bldr-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 93–94; calculation C48; calculation C50

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest contrary evidence is that the structure buys time. No note or revolver matures before 2030, the notes are fixed rate, liquidity is USD 1,514.11 million and free cash flow remains positive. The issuer guides full-year free cash flow of USD 400.00 million to USD 450.00 million, which would imply USD 249.13 million to USD 299.13 million in the fourth quarter; that arithmetic is the analyst's, not the issuer's. Sales declines have also narrowed, with core organic sales down 3.90% in the third quarter against 8.30% in the first.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-10-29_bldr-ex99_1.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bldr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 24; 10-Q_2026-03-31_bldr-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 18; calculation C98; calculation C45

The view would improve if fourth-quarter adjusted EBITDA reaches the range implied by the issuer's full-year outlook of USD 1,075.00 million to USD 1,125.00 million, which is USD 220.80 million to USD 270.80 million against USD 274.90 million derived for the fourth quarter of 2025, and if free cash flow repays revolver borrowings. It would worsen if availability falls toward the USD 173.20 million trigger, if buybacks or acquisitions are again funded on the revolver, or if gross margin falls below the third-quarter level.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-10-29_bldr-ex99_1.htm.jsonl, page 4; EX-99.1_2026-10-29_bldr-ex99_1.htm.jsonl, page 13; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bldr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 27; calculation C99; calculation C31

Adjusted EBITDA exists only in the issuer's releases and is issuer-defined; the FY2025 figure is derived and the FY2024 figure is not supplied, so adjusted leverage before the first quarter of 2026 rests on one derived point.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-04-30_bldr-ex99_1.htm.jsonl, page 11; EX-99.1_2026-10-29_bldr-ex99_1.htm.jsonl, page 13; calculation C29; calculation C31

The maturity ladder at 2026-09-30 is built from each instrument's stated maturity year, and the repayment schedule of USD 161.17 million of other finance obligations is printed only at 2025-12-31.
