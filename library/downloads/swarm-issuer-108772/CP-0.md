## Findings

### Bottom line

The five supplied filings support the credit snapshot for Xerox Corp, but creditors cannot yet see whether the debt exchange expected on or about November 13, 2026 closed, because nothing after the November 5, 2026 filing is supplied.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_xrx-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 56; 10-Q_2026-09-30_xrx-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 115; calculation C6; calculation C7

The filings are combined reports of Xerox Holdings Corporation and Xerox Corporation, and the management discussion covers both registrants together while stating some liquidity figures for the parent alone, so issuer-level debt, cash and earnings must be taken from the separate Xerox Corporation statements. Those statements show USD 1,676 million of debt owed to the parent at September 30, 2026, inside total debt of USD 4,220 million.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_xrx-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-Q_2026-09-30_xrx-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 7; 10-Q_2026-09-30_xrx-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 43; calculation C1; calculation C3

Financial foundation, business and governance, and fundamental credit can all run now with stated limits. Executed credit agreements, agency ratings, market prices and the board and ownership detail held in the proxy statement are not supplied, so covenant terms, rating triggers and governance depth rest on the filings' own summaries.
