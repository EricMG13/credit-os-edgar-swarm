## Findings

### Bottom line

Xerox Corp is a shrinking print business whose parent has cut shareholder payouts and made debt reduction its priority, but recent financings that ring-fenced the brand and added secured debt keep governance risk at Medium for creditors.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_xrx-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 7; 10-Q_2026-03-31_xrx-20260331.htm.jsonl, pages 9–11; 10-Q_2026-09-30_xrx-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 56; 10-Q_2026-09-30_xrx-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 61

No sponsor or controlling holder is identified: Xerox Holdings Corporation owns all of the shares of Xerox Corporation and is itself publicly held, so creditors depend on the parent's choices on distributions, guarantees and exchanges. Xerox Corporation sent USD 45 million to the parent in the first nine months of 2026 while its own equity fell to a deficit of USD 492 million, and the proxy statement that would show the board and large holders is not supplied.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_xrx-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 255; 10-Q_2026-09-30_xrx-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 8; 10-Q_2026-09-30_xrx-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 51; calculation C10

### Key findings

- The business is a large print annuity that is shrinking: post sale revenue is 70% of revenue and Print and Other produces 91% of it in the first nine months of 2026, but pro forma revenue fell 5.0% in that period after falling 7.6% in 2025, so earnings recovery rests on cost savings rather than growth.
  Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_xrx-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 65–67; 10-K_2025-12-31_xrx-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 84; calculation C2; calculation C3
- Profitability is recovering from a low base through integration savings and cost discipline, with adjusted operating income of USD 399 million in the first nine months of 2026 against USD 243 million a year earlier on a pro forma basis, yet the gain sits in Print and Other, with segment profit of USD 443 million, while IT Solutions earned USD 21 million and corporate costs took USD 65 million.
  Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_xrx-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 65; 10-Q_2026-09-30_xrx-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 112; calculation C4
- Asset-value support is weaker than the earnings recovery suggests, because the Print and Other business has been written down twice in two years: goodwill impairments of USD 1,058 million in the third quarter of 2024 and USD 959 million in the third quarter of 2026, together USD 2,017 million, the latest driven by lower projections and rising memory prices.
  Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_xrx-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 70; 10-K_2025-12-31_xrx-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 99; 10-Q_2026-09-30_xrx-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 59; 10-Q_2026-09-30_xrx-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 61; calculation C6

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a Medium risk level is that behaviour has turned creditor-friendly: dividends are being suspended, debt reduction is the stated first priority with a target below 3.0x total debt to EBITDA, notes were repurchased at 50.5% of par, adjusted operating income has grown year over year for three consecutive quarters, and no sponsor is taking fees or recapitalising the company. The Joint Venture lenders also have no recourse to the general credit of Xerox Corporation or the parent beyond the contracted support, and warrants exercised with notes retired about USD 8 million of convertible notes in the third quarter.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_xrx-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 7; 10-Q_2026-03-31_xrx-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 10; 10-Q_2026-09-30_xrx-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 40; 10-Q_2026-09-30_xrx-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 56; 10-Q_2026-09-30_xrx-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 59; calculation C13

The view would move to Low if the exchange closes as described, further discounted repurchases follow, the proxy statement shows an independent board with no large holder able to direct payouts, and the executed documents show tight limits on distributions and asset transfers. It would move to High if further brand or asset financings, first lien issuance or distributions to the parent continue while operating cash flow stays near the lowered outlook of USD 145 million to USD 195 million for 2026, or if the legal documents show that the Joint Venture support obligations limit recovery for unsecured creditors.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_xrx-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 97; 10-Q_2026-03-31_xrx-20260331.htm.jsonl, pages 10–12

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

Xerox Corporation completed the acquisition of Lexmark from Ninestar Group Company Limited on July 1, 2025 for total consideration of USD 749 million after a USD 19 million working capital reduction. It was financed mainly with new debt: second lien notes of USD 500 million in total, released from escrow at closing, and 13.00% notes of the parent of USD 250 million due 2030, while the USD 400 million of first lien notes issued in April 2025 mainly redeemed a parent note that matured in 2025.
Basis: 10-K_2024-12-31_xrx-20241231.htm.jsonl, page 183; 10-K_2025-12-31_xrx-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 218; 10-K_2025-12-31_xrx-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 219; 10-K_2025-12-31_xrx-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 220; 10-Q_2026-09-30_xrx-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 26; calculation C17

On February 17, 2026 Xerox Corporation contributed the Xerox brand trademarks and related intellectual property to a new entity, XRX Brandco Holdings LLC, whose term loans of USD 405 million and Class A Units of USD 45 million were funded by investors including funds managed by Angelo, Gordon & Co., L.P. After USD 46 million of closing costs the net proceeds were USD 404 million, distributed to Xerox Corporation for general corporate purposes. The group pays the entity a royalty of 2.0% of specified revenue, and both instruments amortize by 4.50% of the closing amount each quarter after September 30, 2026, so the USD 450 million counted as debt falls by USD 81 million a year before a final payment. The entity's creditors have recourse to its assets and to the guarantees and collateral support in the licence agreement, but not to the general credit of Xerox Corporation or Xerox Holdings Corporation.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-03-31_xrx-20260331.htm.jsonl, pages 8–12; 10-Q_2026-09-30_xrx-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 39; calculation C12; calculation C21

In October 2026 the company agreed an exchange with certain holders of Xerox Holdings Corporation's 5.50% notes due 2028: about USD 174 million of those notes for about USD 120 million of new 10.25% first lien senior secured notes due 2030 of Xerox Corporation, about 3.9 million shares of common stock and accrued interest in cash. It was expected to close on or about November 13, 2026, would cut debt by up to USD 54 million and would move USD 120 million of maturity from 2028 to 2030, but the new notes rank ahead of the unsecured and second lien debt of Xerox Corporation, and completion is not confirmed in the supplied documents.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_xrx-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 56; 10-Q_2026-09-30_xrx-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 61; calculation C15; Source readiness (CP-0)

Other funding actions in 2026 are the sale of rights to tariff refund receivables carrying USD 105 million for about USD 80 million, treated as financing with imputed interest of about USD 25 million; repurchases of USD 194 million of the parent's 2028 notes and USD 6 million of second lien notes for about USD 101 million; continued sales of finance receivables to funding partners, with net proceeds of USD 122 million in the first nine months; and Amendment No. 2 to the First Lien Term Loan Credit Agreement, filed with a current report dated October 29, 2026, whose terms are not supplied.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_xrx-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 34; 10-Q_2026-09-30_xrx-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 40–41; 10-Q_2026-09-30_xrx-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 114; calculation C13; calculation C14
