## Findings

### Bottom line

The supplied filings name no sponsor, parent or controlling shareholder, and documented behaviour toward creditors is neutral to supportive, so sponsor and governance risk is Medium, held there mainly by missing ownership evidence.

Shareholder returns are covered by free cash flow but appear to be rising: repurchases and share settlement taxes took 68.80% of free cash flow in the nine months to September 30, 2026, against 52.16% in FY2025. The larger creditor exposure is the business itself: connected television is 53% of revenue, the issuer generally has no long-term contracts with buyers or sellers, and two buyers owed 40% and 12% of receivables at year-end FY2025.

### Key findings

No controlling holder is named, but the filings do not rule one out. The annual report defers its ownership and board items to a proxy statement that is not among the supplied documents, so the only ownership fact is about 42 holders of record at February 19, 2026, and holders in street name are not counted. Takeover defences, including a classified board, removal of directors only for cause by a supermajority vote, no action by written consent and the Delaware business-combination statute, make a hostile change of control harder; that protects management continuity, but no change-of-control protection for creditors can be read because the credit agreement is not supplied.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_mgni-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 80; 10-K_2025-12-31_mgni-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 178; 10-K_2025-12-31_mgni-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 75–76

Shareholder returns are the main discretionary use of cash and are paid from operating cash flow. Magnite has never declared a dividend and the credit facility restricts them, but a repurchase plan approved on February 23, 2026 allows up to USD 200.0 million through February 29, 2028, of which USD 55.1 million had been used and USD 144.9 million remained at September 30, 2026. Repurchases plus taxes paid on net share settlement were 68.80% of free cash flow in the nine months to September 30, 2026, up from 52.16% in FY2025; repurchases alone were 44.57%. The revolver was undrawn at September 30, 2026, although the purpose of the second-quarter draw is not stated, so the mechanism is a smaller cash cushion, not higher debt, and the nine-month ratio is likely to fall as seasonally strong fourth-quarter cash flow arrives.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a Medium rating is favourable: free cash flow in the nine months to September 30, 2026 comfortably covers the return programme, debt has been repaid rather than added, and the only change to lender terms on record is repricing in the borrower's favour. A Low rating becomes arguable once the proxy statement shows dispersed ownership and independent oversight and the credit agreement shows restricted payments that are bounded.
Basis: calculation A01; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mgni-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 18; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mgni-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 19

The strongest evidence for a worse view is the direction of returns: they rose from 52.16% of free cash flow in FY2025 to 68.80% in the nine months to September 30, 2026, USD 144.9 million of authority remains, and the baskets that would limit further returns are not in the supplied documents. The view would move to High if returns were funded by revolver borrowing, if a controlling holder with distribution rights emerged, or if any exchange, priming or asset-transfer transaction appeared; the first observable warning would be returns that exceed free cash flow for a full year.
Basis: calculation A06; calculation A05; calculation A10; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mgni-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 20

### Governance view

Overall, Magnite presents Medium sponsor and governance risk. The key supported behaviour is cash-funded shareholder returns alongside full repayment of the convertible notes from cash, which matters because it keeps debt falling while using free cash flow that could otherwise build liquidity, and implies stable creditor treatment for as long as returns stay within free cash flow. Countervailing evidence is the repricing of the term loan in the borrower's favour, which lowers lender yield without changing priority. Further analysis requires the proxy statement and the credit agreement. This governance review is completed with limitations, and the sponsor and governance risk level is Medium.
