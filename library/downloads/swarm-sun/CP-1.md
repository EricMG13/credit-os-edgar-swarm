## Findings

### Bottom line

Sunoco LP's net debt was USD 12,403 million at September 30, 2026, or 3.51x trailing issuer-adjusted EBITDA, which leaves creditors with moderate leverage and ample interest cover, but the roughly USD 600 million Offen purchase that closed after the quarter already raises it.
Basis: calculation C03; calculation C05; 10-Q_2026-09-30_sun-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 10

The fall from the 6.11x of year-end 2025 is mostly a comparison effect, because that year held only two months of Parkland earnings against a full balance sheet, and the issuer's own covenant ratio was 4.03x. Trailing adjusted EBITDA covers cash interest 4.87x, and trailing reported EBITDA of USD 3,624 million includes net inventory valuation gains of USD 258 million that adjusted EBITDA removes.
Basis: calculation C02; calculation C05; 10-K_2025-12-31_sun-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 251; 10-Q_2026-09-30_sun-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 5, 22

### Key findings

Net debt to issuer-adjusted EBITDA was 5.07x at year-end 2024, 6.11x at year-end 2025 and 3.51x at September 30, 2026, and on calculated reported EBITDA 3.42x at the latest date. The year-end 2025 jump came from carrying the debt of the Parkland acquisition against EBITDA that held two months of it. The 2026 fall came from the trailing figure holding about eleven months of Parkland, while carrying-value debt moved only from USD 13,389 million to USD 13,163 million.
Basis: calculation C03; calculation C05; 10-K_2025-12-31_sun-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 7, 247

Cash interest paid over the trailing year was USD 726 million, so adjusted EBITDA covers it 4.87x and reported EBITDA 4.99x; after total capital expenditure of USD 738 million the cover is 3.98x on reported EBITDA. Cover was 4.72x on adjusted EBITDA in FY2025 and 4.30x in FY2024, so it has edged up from FY2025 and is above FY2024, with both resting on acquired earnings.
Basis: calculation C02; calculation C05; 10-K_2025-12-31_sun-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 243; 10-Q_2026-09-30_sun-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 11

Operating cash flow of USD 2,251 million in the first nine months of 2026 included a USD 316 million working-capital release, so operating cash flow before working capital of USD 1,935 million is the cleaner anchor. After capital expenditure of USD 579 million, free cash flow was USD 1,672 million, against distributions of USD 880 million to common, Class D and preferred unitholders and USD 356 million paid for acquisitions.
Basis: calculation C06; 10-Q_2026-09-30_sun-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 5, 6

Liquidity at September 30, 2026 was USD 760 million of cash plus USD 2,300 million of unused revolver, or USD 3,060 million, which is 23.25% of total debt. The revolver matures in June 2030, and USD 1,049 million of 2027 notes are classified as long-term only because management intends to refinance them.
Basis: calculation C07; 10-Q_2026-09-30_sun-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 14, 15

Senior unsecured notes make up USD 12,213 million of USD 13,243 million of principal, so the drawn revolver of USD 111 million is the only debt stated as secured, and the structure leans on subsidiary guarantees and equal-ranking unsecured claims. Maturities run USD 1,049 million in 2027, USD 900 million in 2028 and USD 2,723 million in 2029.
Basis: calculation C08; 10-K_2025-12-31_sun-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 249, 250; 10-Q_2026-09-30_sun-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 13, 14

Two events after the quarter are stated but not in any period figure: the Offen purchase of approximately USD 600 million, closed on 2026-10-27, and the third-quarter distribution of USD 267 million payable on 2026-11-19. Net debt alone after each would be USD 13,003 million (3.59x reported EBITDA) and USD 12,670 million (3.50x). How Offen was funded and what it earns are not established.
Basis: calculation C09; 10-Q_2026-09-30_sun-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 10, 44

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest contrary evidence is that trailing issuer-adjusted EBITDA still holds a one-time first-quarter inventory gain of USD 102 million; without it net debt to adjusted EBITDA is 3.61x rather than 3.51x. Trailing reported EBITDA is flattered by net inventory valuation gains of USD 258 million, and the refinery segment contributed USD 238 million of adjusted EBITDA in the third quarter against USD 456 million across nine months, a pace that the first-quarter turnaround makes hard to extrapolate. The view would weaken if the Offen purchase was debt funded and earns little in its first year, or if inventory and refinery margins normalise; it would strengthen if the next quarterly report shows leverage near the current level after Offen is included.
Basis: calculation C10; calculation C02; EX-99.1_2026-05-05_ex991sunerq12026.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-09-30_sun-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 5, 22, 40

### Limitations

- Reported EBITDA is not stated by the issuer; it is calculated here as net income plus income tax, net interest and depreciation, amortization and accretion, and the issuer's only EBITDA measure is its adjusted one.
Basis: calculation C01; 10-Q_2026-09-30_sun-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 22
- Carrying values by instrument are not disclosed, so the debt instrument table and debt stack use principal, with one reconciling financing-cost line to carrying value.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_sun-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 13, 14
- The maturity ladder at September 30, 2026 is built from the maturity year in each note's title, because the quarterly report prints no schedule; USD 597 million of lease-related and other subsidiary debt has no stated maturity.
Basis: calculation C08; 10-Q_2026-09-30_sun-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 13
- The rank of the GoZone Bonds and of lease-related financing obligations is not stated, so they are outside the debt stack.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_sun-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 13, 14
- Offen's earnings and funding are not stated, so no pro forma leverage includes its EBITDA.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_sun-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 10
- Year-end 2025 ratios are not comparable with the trailing figure, because FY2025 holds two months of Parkland.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_sun-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 7

## Gaps

- The issuer states no unadjusted EBITDA, so reported EBITDA for every period is calculated from net income, income tax, net interest and depreciation, amortization and accretion; this restricts any ratio on a reported basis to a calculated denominator; severity Medium; the action is to confirm the definition with the issuer or a lender presentation.
- Several statement lines print a dash or have no line in some reports (the West Texas Sale in 2025 and 2026, the preferred and Class D allocations before September 2025, acquisition and financing lines in other periods, accounts receivable from affiliates before 2026, and refinery operating data before the acquisition); these are shown as not supplied rather than zero; severity Low; no action is needed.
- The interim reports do not break net interest expense into gross interest, financing fee amortization and interest income, which the annual report gives for fiscal years only, and they print cash flow on a year-to-date basis, so third-quarter 2026 cash flow and cash interest were not constructed; severity Low; the action is to obtain the quarterly breakdown.
- Cash taxes paid are stated for fiscal years only and not for the nine-month periods, so trailing cash taxes are not calculable; severity Low; the action is to obtain interim cash tax data.
- Unused revolver availability at December 31, 2024 is not stated, so the 2024 liquidity point shows cash only; severity Low; the action is to obtain the 2024 annual report.
- Carrying values by instrument are not disclosed, so the debt instrument table carries principal with one financing cost adjustment; debt by seniority at carrying value is therefore not stated and senior secured, senior unsecured and subordinated debt rows of the balance sheet are not supplied; severity Medium; the action is to obtain the indentures and debt note detail.
- The rank and security of the GoZone Bonds and of lease-related financing obligations and other subsidiary debt are not stated, so they are left out of the debt stack, covering USD 919 million of principal; severity Medium; the action is to obtain the governing agreements.
- The margin in force on the Credit Facility and its reference rate for the Canadian dollar drawings are not stated; severity Low; the action is to obtain the credit agreement.
- Offen Petroleum's earnings and the funding split between cash and revolver are not stated, so the pro forma net leverage excludes its EBITDA and may overstate the effect; severity High for the post-quarter view; the action is to obtain the issuer's Offen disclosure or the next quarterly report.
- The maturity ladder at September 30, 2026 is built from note titles and treats the Credit Facility as maturing in 2030; USD 597 million has no stated maturity and the current maturities of USD 7 million are not placed in a year; severity Medium; the action is to obtain a dated schedule.
- The covenant Net Leverage Ratio is reported at 4.03x and 3.5x but its definition, with acquisition pro forma effects and cash netting, is not reproducible from the sources, so a gap to the calculated ratios cannot be fully explained; severity Medium; the action is to obtain the credit agreement.
- Maintenance and growth capital are stated only on an accrual basis for FY2025 and nine months 2026, not on a cash basis for other periods; severity Low; the action is to obtain a cash split.
