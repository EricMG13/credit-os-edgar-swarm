## Findings

### Bottom line

Sunoco LP's creditors face medium governance and structure risk: an Energy Transfer-controlled general partner holds broad discretion over borrowing, distributions and acquisitions, while the notes are guaranteed by only part of a group that earns nearly half its profit outside the guarantor perimeter.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_sun-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 91, 92, 155; 10-Q_2026-09-30_sun-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 46, 47, 48; calculation C09

The business itself is diversified and fee-supported: fuel distribution earned about 53.86% of nine-month 2026 segment Adjusted EBITDA, with pipelines, terminals and the acquired refinery supplying the rest. Distributions have risen eight quarters in a row and the third-quarter payout was covered 2.20x by adjusted distributable cash flow, and recent acquisitions were funded partly with preferred and Class D units rather than debt alone, which tempers the risk.
Basis: calculation C04; calculation C06; 10-Q_2026-09-30_sun-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 20, 22; EX-99.1_2026-11-03_ex991sunocoandsunococorper.htm.jsonl, pages 3, 7

### Key findings

Energy Transfer controls the borrower through its general partner, and the partnership agreement lets that general partner weigh Energy Transfer's interests against those of the partnership and its unitholders. Energy Transfer owns all of the general partner, 20.79% of the common units and every incentive distribution right, and its power to appoint the board runs through SunocoCorp under a delegation agreement. Five of the seven directors were independent at year-end 2025 and an independent audit committee oversees reporting, but the annual report describes no creditor vote, and the general partner alone decides borrowing, reserves, asset sales and whether spending counts as maintenance.
Basis: calculation C01; 10-K_2025-12-31_sun-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 91, 92, 93, 155, 156, 193

Related-party dealings are large in volume but small against cost of sales, and they rose sharply in 2026. Purchases of motor fuel from Energy Transfer affiliates were USD 1,249 million in FY2025, 5.57% of cost of sales, and USD 1,760 million in nine months 2026, 4.60% of cost of sales, while sales to affiliates reached USD 884 million against USD 15 million a year earlier. Reimbursements to the general partner were USD 43 million in FY2025 with no cap in the partnership agreement, so the exposure is leakage and pricing discipline rather than funding, and the pricing basis is not established.
Basis: calculation C05; 10-K_2025-12-31_sun-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 94, 195, 196, 256; 10-Q_2026-09-30_sun-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 16

Distributions take a large and rising share of cash flow, but coverage is ample and growth is deliberate policy. Distributions paid were USD 880 million in nine months 2026, 39.09% of operating cash flow, and the third-quarter distribution per unit is 10.28% above a year earlier, the eighth consecutive increase against management's stated multi-year distribution growth rate of at least 5%. Adjusted distributable cash flow covered the USD 267 million third-quarter payout 2.20x, but incentive distribution rights take 28.46% of it, and the partnership agreement requires distribution of available cash, so growth capital and acquisitions rely mainly on external financing.
Basis: calculation C03; calculation C04; 10-K_2025-12-31_sun-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 94, 122; 10-Q_2026-09-30_sun-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 5, 6, 20; EX-99.1_2026-11-03_ex991sunocoandsunococorper.htm.jsonl, pages 3, 7

Acquisition appetite is high and recent funding mixed debt with equity-like claims. Since 2024 the group closed the NuStar, Parkland, TanQuid and Delta purchases, and the Offen purchase of approximately USD 600 million closed after the quarter. Parkland was paid for with cash and Class D units issued to SunocoCorp, the cash raised through USD 1.9 billion of new senior notes and USD 1.47 billion of Series A preferred units, which keeps part of the burden outside debt but adds preferred distributions of about USD 118.1 million a year that rank ahead of common units. Management says leverage returned to its target in less than one year, but the target level is not stated.
Basis: calculation C02; calculation C07; 10-K_2025-12-31_sun-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 7, 8, 249; 10-Q_2026-09-30_sun-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 10, 20; EX-99.1_2026-11-03_ex991sunocoandsunococorper.htm.jsonl, page 2

The business is diversified but still earns about half its segment profit from motor fuel distribution. In nine months 2026 fuel distribution produced 53.86% of segment Adjusted EBITDA, pipelines 18.52%, terminals 11.84% and the refinery 15.78%, and the refinery's quarterly Adjusted EBITDA rose from USD 43 million in the first quarter of 2026, hit by a turnaround, to USD 175 million and then USD 238 million. Contractually committed revenue of USD 2,038 million at September 30, 2026 gives some visibility, and 89.26% of FY2025 revenue was in the United States before a full year of Parkland's Canadian and Caribbean operations.
Basis: calculation C06; calculation C10; 10-Q_2026-09-30_sun-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 16, 22; 10-K_2025-12-31_sun-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 258, 279; EX-99.1_2026-05-05_ex991sunerq12026.htm.jsonl, page 2; EX-99.1_2026-08-04_ex991sunerq22026.htm.jsonl, page 2

Creditors rely on guarantees from only part of the group. In nine months 2026 the guarantor issuer group had USD 22,894 million of revenue and USD 758 million of net income, against USD 20,080 million and USD 667 million for the non-guarantor subsidiaries, whose long-term assets were USD 11,730 million; the notes are structurally subordinated to the non-guarantors' debts. Before USD 5,510 million of investments in non-guarantors, the guarantor group's liabilities exceeded its assets by USD 2,031 million, so note holders depend on the value of those subsidiaries.
Basis: calculation C09; 10-Q_2026-09-30_sun-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 46, 47, 48

Exchange and domicile changes lighten outside governance checks. Sunoco converted from a Delaware to a Texas limited partnership under a plan dated 2026-07-02, and its units are now listed on the Texas Stock Exchange, which does not require a majority-independent board or compensation and nominating committees for a partnership; the general partner's own agreement still requires at least three independent directors.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_sun-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 50; 10-Q_2026-09-30_sun-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 3, 50, 51; 10-K_2025-12-31_sun-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 155

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a medium risk view is that equity-like funding carried the largest deal. Class D units valued at USD 2,547 million and USD 1.47 billion of preferred units went into Parkland, leverage on the issuer's own covenant basis fell from 4.03x at year-end 2025 to 3.5x at September 30, 2026, distributions are well covered and the board has a majority of independent directors. That would point to low risk if repeated, and it wins if the next acquisitions are equity funded and distribution growth is paced to deleveraging.
The view would worsen if the Offen purchase was debt funded without deleveraging, if related-party sales keep growing from USD 884 million, or if the delegation agreement or the listing change were used to reduce creditor information or consent rights.
Basis: calculation C02; calculation C05; 10-K_2025-12-31_sun-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 197, 251; 10-Q_2026-09-30_sun-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 10, 15

### Limitations

- The partnership agreement, delegation agreement, omnibus agreement, credit agreement and indentures were not supplied, so the conclusion that the general partner holds broad discretion rests on the annual report's summaries and could prove narrower or wider in the documents themselves.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_sun-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 91, 92, 93, 197
- Energy Transfer's share of SunocoCorp's economics is not stated, so the sponsor's total economic interest in the borrower is not established and the control conclusion rests on legal rights rather than on economic exposure.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_sun-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 155, 193
- Customer concentration, a rating and the pricing basis of related-party fuel trade are not stated, so the diversification conclusion cannot be tested against a top-customer share and the leakage reading cannot be priced.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_sun-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 195, 256
- Offen's earnings and funding are not stated, so the acquisition-appetite conclusion covers the funding mix of earlier deals only, and the use of the 2024 asset-sale proceeds is not stated.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_sun-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 10; 10-K_2025-12-31_sun-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 249
- Management's leverage target is not quantified, so the statement that leverage returned to target cannot be checked and the leverage view rests on the covenant ratio alone.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-11-03_ex991sunocoandsunococorper.htm.jsonl, page 2

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

The defining transaction is the Parkland acquisition, which closed on 2025-10-31: Sunoco paid Parkland shareholders cash and issued Class D units to SunocoCorp, whose units were exchanged for Parkland shares, and it assumed or exchanged about USD 3.65 billion of Parkland debt into its own notes. The financing before closing was USD 1.0 billion of 5.625% notes due 2031 and USD 900 million of 5.875% notes due 2034 in September 2025, alongside 1.5 million 7.875% Series A preferred units with net proceeds of about USD 1.47 billion.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_sun-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 7, 8, 89, 237, 249; 10-Q_2026-09-30_sun-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 20

After Parkland the group completed TanQuid on 2026-01-16, with equity consideration of EUR 206 million and assumed debt of EUR 298 million, and Delta on 2026-04-01 for about USD 81 million, funded from cash and the Credit Facility. In March 2026 it issued USD 600 million of 5.375% notes due 2031 and USD 600 million of 5.625% notes due 2034 and used part of the proceeds to redeem USD 500 million of 2026 notes and USD 600 million of 2027 notes, then redeemed the remaining Parkland notes and, by August 2026, its Canadian-dollar notes. The Offen Petroleum acquisition, closed on 2026-10-27 for approximately USD 600 million in cash, is after the quarter end and not in any reported balance.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_sun-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 9, 10, 14; EX-99.1_2026-05-05_ex991sunerq12026.htm.jsonl, page 2

The registrant also converted from a Delaware to a Texas limited partnership, with the Plan of Conversion dated 2026-07-02, and moved its listing to the Texas Stock Exchange; the related current report and the new partnership agreement are not in the documents supplied.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_sun-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 50; 10-Q_2026-09-30_sun-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 3, 51
