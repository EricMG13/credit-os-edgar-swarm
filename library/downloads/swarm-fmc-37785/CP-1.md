## Findings

### Bottom line

Earnings no longer carry the debt: last-twelve-months adjusted EBITDA was negative USD 53.5 million at 2026-09-30, so leverage cannot be measured and creditors depend on liquidity, covenant relief and pending asset sales. On adjusted EBITDA, total debt over EBITDA rose from 3.73x at FY2024 to 5.78x at 2026-06-30 before the third-quarter loss, and reported EBITDA is negative in every period after FY2024.
Basis: calculation C-LA5; calculation C-LA4; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fmc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 51

Net debt was USD 3,441.9 million at 2026-09-30 against USD 3,490.4 million at 2025-12-31, held down by USD 403.2 million of equity proceeds and a USD 200.0 million licensing prepayment rather than by operations, whose cash flow was negative USD 285.5 million for nine months. Liquidity reached USD 2,397.2 million, and the interest coverage covenant passed at 2.28x against a 2.00x minimum on a definition the filings do not print.
Basis: calculation C-N5; calculation C-O1; calculation C-LQ5; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fmc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 6, 24

### Leverage and coverage

Leverage worsened and then became unmeasurable because the earnings base collapsed, not because debt grew. Total debt over adjusted EBITDA was 3.73x at FY2024, 4.84x at FY2025, 5.70x at 2026-03-31 and 5.78x at 2026-06-30, while total debt at carrying value was USD 3,365.3 million, USD 4,074.9 million, USD 4,533.6 million and USD 4,280.6 million, and USD 4,177.7 million at 2026-09-30. At 2026-09-30 last-twelve-months adjusted EBITDA is negative USD 53.5 million because the third quarter alone was negative USD 558.6 million, so neither the adjusted nor the reported ratio can be stated. For creditors this means the debt can no longer be sized against earnings: what decides whether leverage returns to a measurable range is the recovery of earnings, and the negative third quarter stays in the last-twelve-months figure until the third-quarter 2027 report.
Basis: calculation C-LA5; calculation C-Q5; calculation C-G5; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fmc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 51

Coverage deteriorated in step with it: adjusted EBITDA over cash interest was 3.89x at FY2024, 3.55x at FY2025, 3.25x at 2026-03-31 and 2.83x at 2026-06-30, and negative 0.21x at 2026-09-30, while EBITDA (Reported) over cash interest is negative from FY2025. The issuer nevertheless reports interest coverage of 2.28x against a 2.00x minimum for the four quarters to 2026-09-30, so the covenant definition of adjusted earnings must differ materially from the issuer's own Adjusted EBITDA; the filings do not print that definition, so no covenant headroom is inferred here.
Basis: calculation C-LA5; calculation C-LI5; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fmc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 24

The strongest contrary evidence is that the third-quarter loss is more than accounted for by charges of about USD 671.6 million for pricing and rebate adjustments on inventory held by channel customers, which the cash flow statement adds back as non-cash. Before those charges, third-quarter adjusted EBITDA would have been USD 113.0 million and last-twelve-months adjusted EBITDA USD 618.1 million, giving total debt over EBITDA of 6.76x, a normalisation that is an Analyst Judgement and not an issuer measure. That view would win if accrued customer rebates, which rose from USD 417.4 million at 2025-12-31 to USD 811.9 million at 2026-09-30, were settled by credit notes rather than cash; if they are paid in cash they exceed the USD 735.8 million of cash held, so the earnings loss converts into a cash call.
Basis: calculation C-M1; calculation C-M2; calculation C-D9; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fmc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 4–5, 45–47

### Cash-flow quality

Operating cash flow was negative in both nine-month periods and the 2026 improvement came from sources that do not repeat. Net cash from operating activities was negative USD 285.5 million for nine months 2026 against negative USD 717.0 million for nine months 2025. The 2026 figure includes the USD 200.0 million prepayment from Corteva for rimisoxafen supply, recorded as deferred revenue, so without it operating cash flow would have been negative USD 485.5 million, and income taxes paid fell to USD 36.8 million from USD 156.3 million. A prepayment is an obligation to deliver product, not earned cash, so it supports liquidity without supporting debt service from earnings.
Basis: calculation C-O1; calculation C-O2; calculation C-O3; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fmc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 5–7, 14

Seasonal working capital is the other swing: advance payments from customers fell by USD 452.6 million in nine months 2026, almost the same as in nine months 2025, and the revolver was drawn to USD 1,109.5 million at 2026-03-31, leaving only USD 666.9 million available at that date. If the seasonal pattern repeats, the first quarter of 2027 would again draw heavily on the revolver.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-03-31_fmc-20260331.htm.jsonl, pages 16, 55; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fmc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 5

### Liquidity and refinancing position

Liquidity at 2026-09-30 is high against the earlier dates but was rebuilt with one-off sources. Cash was USD 735.8 million and revolver funds available USD 1,661.4 million at 2026-09-30, a total of USD 2,397.2 million against USD 1,057.8 million at 2026-03-31, the highest of the five balance-sheet dates shown. The rebuild came from USD 1,200.0 million of Senior Secured Notes due 2031 issued in June 2026, which redeemed the notes due October 2026 and repaid revolver borrowings, from USD 403.2 million of equity proceeds in September 2026 and from the licensing prepayment. Of the cash, USD 486.1 million is held by foreign subsidiaries; only USD 72.8 million of debt other than the revolver borrowing falls due within one year and the revolving credit facility commitment matures in 2028.
Basis: calculation C-LQ5; calculation C-LQ3; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fmc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 22–23, 69

The refinancing risk has moved from the 2026 maturities to the covenant package and the secured structure: the leverage covenant is not applied for the first three quarters of 2026 and resumes at a maximum of 6.75x from the quarter ending 2026-12-31, while the new secured notes carry first-priority liens on substantially all assets of the company and its main guarantors. The filings state no security for the senior notes or the subordinated notes and do not state how the secured revolver ranks against the new secured notes, so the position of those notes in a recovery is not established here; the net proceeds of the India sale, which is pending regulatory approval, are directed by Amendment No. 8 to repaying the revolver or to general and working capital needs.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_fmc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 23–24, 49

### Non-debt funding float

Supplier finance and customer-funded items carry more of working capital than the debt tables show. Obligations confirmed under the supply chain finance program were USD 213.1 million at 2026-09-30 against USD 109.5 million at 2025-12-31, an increase of USD 103.6 million that sits in trade payables and is paid out of operating cash flow; guarantees of vendor financing for customers were USD 51.6 million. Accrued customer rebates of USD 811.9 million exceed cash of USD 735.8 million. If suppliers stopped using the program or customers drew down rebates in cash, working capital would absorb cash that the revolver would have to fund.
Basis: calculation C-S1; calculation C-D9; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fmc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 4, 41–42

Customer advance payments are a seasonal float that ran from USD 453.1 million at 2025-12-31 to USD 67.8 million at 2026-09-30, and the licensing prepayment of USD 200.0 million sits in other long-term liabilities. Receivables sold under securitization programs, mainly in Brazil, were USD 134.4 million in nine months 2026 and are not in debt.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_fmc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 4, 14, 17

### Subsequent events and multi-figure events

Two events after 2026-09-30 are flagged and kept out of the period figures: the cash dividend of USD 12.6 million paid on 2026-10-15, which raises pro forma net debt to USD 3,454.5 million, and Amendment No. 8 to the credit agreement dated 2026-10-21, which requires the net proceeds of the India sale to repay revolver debt or fund general or working capital needs and conditionally releases some guarantors and collateral. The India sale for USD 252 million and the Newark, Delaware property sale were not complete at the report date, so neither is in net debt.
Basis: calculation C-P5; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fmc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 24, 31, 49, 85

The India business appears with different figures in different roles: sale consideration of USD 252 million subject to adjustments, net assets held for sale of USD 282.6 million, a carrying value of USD 260.0 million after a USD 22.6 million translation gain, an impairment of USD 57.1 million in nine months 2026, and a USD 145.2 million India line in the Adjusted EBITDA reconciliation. All are kept, each with its role, and none is chosen as the single India figure; the consideration is USD 30.6 million below net assets held for sale.
Basis: calculation C-R1; calculation C-R2; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fmc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 10–11, 21, 51

### Data and definition risks

The main data risk is definitional: the issuer's Adjusted EBITDA differs from reported EBITDA by USD 2,508.9 million for the last twelve months to 2026-09-30, the covenant definition of adjusted earnings is not printed, and the India business is in adjusted EBITDA before Q3 2025 but out of it afterwards. Senior notes appear as one line in every debt note, so the instrument table and the maturity ladder after 2029 rest on aggregates, and only the Senior Secured Notes have a stated legal rank. Interim last-twelve-months figures are built from year-to-date stubs and the 2026-03-31 figure mixes India perimeters for one quarter.
Basis: calculation C-D3; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fmc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 22–24, 50–51
