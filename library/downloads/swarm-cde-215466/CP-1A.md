## Findings

### Bottom line

Coeur Mining is a North American precious-metals producer with no controlling holder identified in the supplied documents and a record of creditor-aligned financial policy, so sponsor and governance risk is low, although the missing proxy statement and financing documents bound that view.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_cde.htm.jsonl, pages 17–21; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cde.htm.jsonl, page 57; 10-K_2025-12-31_cde.htm.jsonl, pages 178–179

Two share-funded acquisitions in 2025 and 2026 turned a five-mine business into seven mines across the United States, Mexico and Canada, and no mine supplied more than 22.04% of nine-month revenue. The creditor risks sit in integration and structure, not owner conduct: the full-year gold expectation fell by 8.70% between August and November, to the low end of its range, and the Mexican mines that earned 34.95% of revenue are not among the named note guarantors.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_cde.htm.jsonl, pages 10–11; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cde.htm.jsonl, page 14; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cde.htm.jsonl, pages 31–32; EX99.1_2026-06-30_cde_a2q26earningsreleaseex-991-htm.htm.jsonl, page 2; EX99.1_2026-09-30_cde_a3q26earningsreleaseex-991-htm.htm.jsonl, page 2; calculation K1; calculation K6; calculation K10

### Key findings

The business is diversified by mine, metal and country, which limits single-site outage risk but not metal-price risk. In the first nine months of 2026 no mine exceeded 22.04% of revenue, gold supplied 62.35%, silver 32.54% and copper 5.11%, and the United States, Mexico and Canada supplied 33.36%, 34.95% and 31.69%. A mine outage therefore costs a fraction of revenue, while a fall in gold or silver prices reaches 94.89% of it.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_cde.htm.jsonl, page 14; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cde.htm.jsonl, page 40; calculation K1; calculation K2; calculation K3; calculation K4

Both large acquisitions were paid in shares, so no acquisition debt entered the capital structure, but integration is now the main operating risk. The New Gold purchase price was USD 6,938,701 thousand in newly issued shares, the company assumed debt with a fair value of USD 414,767 thousand, and transaction and integration costs reached USD 39.3 million. The two Canadian mines supplied 31.69% of nine-month revenue, yet the full-year gold expectation was cut by 8.70% between August and November, to the low end of its range, in a quarter with a conveyor failure at New Afton and heavy rain at Rainy River and Palmarejo.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_cde.htm.jsonl, pages 10–11; EX99.1_2026-06-30_cde_a2q26earningsreleaseex-991-htm.htm.jsonl, page 2; EX99.1_2026-09-30_cde_a3q26earningsreleaseex-991-htm.htm.jsonl, page 2; calculation K2; calculation K10

No controlling holder is identified in the documents supplied, but the documents that would show board independence and holder concentration are not supplied. Holders of New Gold stock received 37.96% of the shares outstanding at 2026-03-31, the chairman also serves as chief executive, and the annual report leaves board, pay and ownership disclosure to a proxy statement that is not in the source set. Creditor risk from owner conduct cannot be tested beyond documented actions.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_cde.htm.jsonl, page 10; 10-Q_2026-03-31_cde.htm.jsonl, page 2; EX99.1_2026-09-30_cde_a3q26earningsreleaseex-991-htm.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-K_2025-12-31_cde.htm.jsonl, pages 178–179; calculation K7

Financial policy is conservative on leverage and generous to shareholders, which is creditor-aligned today and the main behaviour to monitor. The long-term target is zero net debt to trailing adjusted EBITDA, the revolving facility was repaid by USD 195.0 million net in 2025, USD 39.5 million of finance lease principal was prepaid in 2026, and USD 221,232 thousand of share repurchases plus USD 20,645 thousand of dividends were paid in nine months. Repurchases took 46.13% of third-quarter free cash flow and USD 519.4 million of the USD 750 million authorization remains, so distributions are funded from operations while the contractual limits on them are not established.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_cde.htm.jsonl, page 57; 10-K_2025-12-31_cde.htm.jsonl, page 104; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cde.htm.jsonl, page 21; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cde.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cde.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cde.htm.jsonl, page 60; EX99.1_2026-09-30_cde_a3q26earningsreleaseex-991-htm.htm.jsonl, page 2; calculation K8; calculation K9

The note guarantee structure appears to leave the Mexican operations outside the guarantor group, which puts a large share of revenue behind subsidiary creditors. Summarized guarantor revenue was USD 1,902,992 thousand of USD 2,925,400 thousand, leaving USD 1,022,408 thousand or 34.95% outside the group, within USD 1 thousand of the two Mexican mines' combined USD 1,022,409 thousand. The named guarantors include no Mexican operating subsidiary, 47.14% of employees work in Mexico, and a USD 29.6 million Mexican value-added tax receivable has been written down since 2021.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_cde.htm.jsonl, page 14; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cde.htm.jsonl, pages 31–32; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cde.htm.jsonl, page 34; 10-K_2025-12-31_cde.htm.jsonl, page 7; calculation K2; calculation K6; calculation K11

Two streaming agreements take a share of production at fixed prices, which caps upside on those ounces and works like a senior claim on output. At Palmarejo, 50% of gold production is sold to a Franco-Nevada subsidiary for the lesser of USD 800 or spot per ounce, and at Rainy River 6.5% of gold and 60% of silver production go to Royal Gold at 25% of the spot price. In a price rally these ounces earn far less than the realized price, so group margins rise more slowly than prices.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_cde.htm.jsonl, page 34; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cde.htm.jsonl, page 36

Disclosure quality is adequate for monitoring: the statements carry an unqualified audit opinion and effective internal control, but the finance leadership changed in August. A new chief accounting officer took office on 2026-08-10, the former controller moved to an accounting role ahead of an intended retirement in early 2027, and disclosure controls were reported effective at 2026-09-30.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_cde.htm.jsonl, page 76; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cde.htm.jsonl, page 77; 10-K_2025-12-31_cde.htm.jsonl, page 124; 10-K_2025-12-31_cde.htm.jsonl, page 177

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a low-risk view is execution. The expectation moved from the midpoint to the low end of the guidance ranges within one quarter, quarterly silver production fell 12% year on year, and the operating permit for the Las Chispas mine, which earned 16.43% of third-quarter revenue, was set to expire in November 2026 with no later update in the filings. A further guidance cut, a lapse of that permit, or a debt-funded acceleration of the USD 750 million repurchase program would move the view toward medium risk. A proxy statement showing independent board control and financing documents that cap restricted payments would support the low reading.
Basis: EX99.1_2026-09-30_cde_a3q26earningsreleaseex-991-htm.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-K_2025-12-31_cde.htm.jsonl, page 7; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cde.htm.jsonl, page 40; calculation K5; calculation K10

### Limitations

- The proxy statement is not in the source set, so board independence, holder concentration, executive pay and related-party dealings are not established, and the low sponsor and governance reading rests on documented actions alone.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_cde.htm.jsonl, pages 178–179
- The credit agreement and indentures are described only in the issuer's own notes, so the exact room for dividends, repurchases, debt, liens and asset transfers is not established, and the view that distributions are creditor-safe rests on current net cash, not on documented limits.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_cde.htm.jsonl, pages 20–21
- The two Canadian mines have been in the group for about two quarters and the supplied filings give only a Rainy River mine life to 2035 and a New Afton resource estimate, not reserve estimates, so the finding that integration is the main operating risk rests on a short operating record and incomplete mine-life evidence.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_cde.htm.jsonl, pages 10–12; 10-Q_2026-03-31_cde.htm.jsonl, page 37
- No synergy target or integration budget is stated, so the finding that no acquisition debt was added cannot be matched against any measure of the benefit the shares paid for.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_cde.htm.jsonl, pages 10–11

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

The central transaction is the acquisition of New Gold Inc., agreed on 2025-11-02 and completed on 2026-03-20 by court-approved plan of arrangement. New Gold shareholders received a fixed fraction of a Coeur share for each New Gold share, for a purchase price of USD 6,938,701 thousand, so the deal added no acquisition debt. The preliminary allocation assigns USD 9,585,545 thousand to property, plant and equipment and USD 2,793,678 thousand to deferred tax liabilities, and leaves reclamation, unrecognized tax benefits and deferred taxes open for up to one year.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_cde.htm.jsonl, pages 10–11

Two debt events followed. First, the company replaced its 2017 credit agreement with a new USD 1,000 million senior secured revolving facility maturing in March 2031, with no draws at 2026-09-30. Second, it exchanged USD 385.8 million of the USD 400.0 million New Gold senior notes due 2032 for USD 385,774 thousand of its own senior unsecured notes due 2032 plus USD 771,600 of cash, settled on 2026-04-22, and obtained consents that removed restrictive covenants from the notes that stayed outstanding, about USD 12.6 million at 2026-09-30. Supplemental indentures added New Gold and two related entities as guarantors of the 2029 notes on 2026-04-30 and clarified the first interest date on the 2032 notes on 2026-10-07.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_cde.htm.jsonl, pages 18–21; EX99.1_2026-03-31_cde_ef20071153-ex99-1-htm.htm.jsonl, page 1

The earlier SilverCrest acquisition closed on 2025-02-14 and was also paid in shares, at an implied equity value of USD 1.58 billion, and the company repaid USD 195.0 million net under the revolving facility in 2025. Shareholder returns began in 2026 with an expanded USD 750 million repurchase program announced on 2026-03-23, an inaugural dividend of USD 0.02 per share paid on 2026-06-10 and a second dividend declared on 2026-11-04 for payment on 2026-12-16.
