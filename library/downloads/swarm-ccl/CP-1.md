## Findings

### Bottom line

Carnival's statement-based leverage fell from 4.48x total debt at the end of fiscal 2024 to 3.27x at 2026-08-31 because debt was repaid while EBITDA grew, which strengthens creditors' position; interest cover and issuer-adjusted ratios cannot be extended past fiscal 2025.
Basis: 10-K_2025-11-30_ccl-20251130.htm.jsonl, pages 63–64; 10-Q_2026-08-31_ccl-20260831.htm.jsonl, pages 2–3; calculation V1; calculation V9

Debt at carrying value fell from USD 26,640 million at 2025-11-30 to USD 23,912 million at 2026-08-31 and cash from USD 1,928 million to USD 1,220 million, leaving net debt of USD 22,692 million, or 3.10x of last-twelve-months EBITDA of USD 7,311 million. Cash from operations of USD 5,303 million in nine months covered capital expenditure of USD 2,139 million, dividends of USD 618 million and repurchases of USD 929 million, so part of the gain is being distributed. Net debt after repurchases since 2026-08-31 is not established.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-08-31_ccl-20260831.htm.jsonl, pages 2–4; 10-Q_2026-08-31_ccl-20260831.htm.jsonl, pages 9–10; calculation N5; calculation L3; calculation V10

### Leverage trajectory and drivers

Total debt over statement-based EBITDA improved from 4.48x at fiscal 2024 to 3.66x at fiscal 2025 and 3.27x at 2026-08-31, and net debt over EBITDA from 4.28x to 3.40x and 3.10x. The drivers are repayment of debt, which cut carrying value by USD 2,728 million in nine months, and EBITDA growth from USD 6,131 million to USD 7,311 million over the last twelve months. Net debt over EBITDA was 3.09x at 2026-05-31 and 3.10x at 2026-08-31, so the latest quarter added no further improvement because cash fell by USD 1,023 million. The issuer's own adjusted ratio of 3.1x at Q2 2026 uses a different EBITDA and agrees only within rounding.
Basis: 10-K_2025-11-30_ccl-20251130.htm.jsonl, pages 63–64; 10-Q_2026-08-31_ccl-20260831.htm.jsonl, pages 2–3; EX-99.1_2026-06-23_a20262qearningsrelease8-k.htm.jsonl, page 3; calculation V1; calculation V2; calculation V3; calculation V4; calculation V7; calculation V8; calculation V9; calculation V10

### Coverage

Interest cover on statement-based EBITDA over cash interest paid rose from 3.83x in fiscal 2024 to 6.06x in fiscal 2025, helped by interest expense falling from USD 1,755 million to USD 1,349 million. Cash interest is printed to the nearest USD 100 million and no interim figure is stated, so cover after fiscal 2025 is not established; interest expense net of capitalized interest was USD 862 million over nine months 2026 against USD 1,034 million a year earlier.
Basis: 10-K_2025-11-30_ccl-20251130.htm.jsonl, pages 63–64; 10-K_2025-11-30_ccl-20251130.htm.jsonl, page 98; 10-Q_2026-08-31_ccl-20260831.htm.jsonl, page 2; calculation C4; calculation C5

### Cash flow quality

Free cash flow, cash from operations less capital expenditure, was USD 2,607 million in fiscal 2025 and USD 3,164 million in nine months 2026, which is 0.36 of EBITDA in fiscal 2025. Customer deposits added USD 318 million to operating cash in nine months 2026, so part of operating cash flow is advance sales collected ahead of sailings and reverses if bookings slow. Capital expenditure fell from USD 4,626 million in fiscal 2024 to USD 3,611 million in fiscal 2025 as ship deliveries slowed.
Basis: 10-K_2025-11-30_ccl-20251130.htm.jsonl, page 60; 10-K_2025-11-30_ccl-20251130.htm.jsonl, page 61; 10-K_2025-11-30_ccl-20251130.htm.jsonl, page 64; 10-Q_2026-08-31_ccl-20260831.htm.jsonl, pages 3–4; calculation F1; calculation F3; calculation M4

### Liquidity and funding float

Liquidity at 2026-08-31 was cash of USD 1,220 million plus a revolving facility with USD 4.5 billion available, about USD 5,720 million, with USD 10.7 billion of undrawn export credit facilities that fund ship deliveries only. Customer deposits of USD 7,639 million are an interest-free funding float that exceeds cash several times over; it is repaid in sailings rather than cash, which supports liquidity in a normal booking cycle and becomes an obligation if sailings are cancelled. Principal falling due is USD 301 million in the rest of 2026 and USD 2,517 million in 2027.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-08-31_ccl-20260831.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-08-31_ccl-20260831.htm.jsonl, page 10; 10-Q_2026-08-31_ccl-20260831.htm.jsonl, page 25; EX-99.1_2026-09-29_a20263qearningsrelease8-k.htm.jsonl, page 9; calculation F2; calculation P2
