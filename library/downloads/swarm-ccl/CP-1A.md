## Findings

### Bottom line

Carnival is a scaled, diversified cruise operator with no sponsor identified and now one Bermuda company, so creditor risk rests on demand, fuel and shareholder returns; legal capacity to distribute value is not established because debt agreements were not supplied.
Basis: 10-K_2025-11-30_ccl-20251130.htm.jsonl, pages 7–10; 10-Q_2026-08-31_ccl-20260831.htm.jsonl, pages 1–5; Source readiness (CP-0)

Shareholder returns are now material: dividends of USD 618 million and repurchases of USD 929 million over nine months 2026 took 29.2% of cash from operations of USD 5,303 million, and the company targets distributing more than 40% of operating cash flow to 2029. Debt is falling and no secured debt remains, which supports creditors, but the shift from deleveraging to returns is the main governance change to monitor.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-08-31_ccl-20260831.htm.jsonl, pages 3–4; 10-Q_2026-08-31_ccl-20260831.htm.jsonl, page 28; EX-99.1_2026-03-27_a20261qearningsrelease8-k.htm.jsonl, page 3; EX-99.1_2026-09-29_a20263qearningsrelease8-k.htm.jsonl, page 3; calculation B1

### Key findings

- Scale and diversification are the core strengths: 94 ships and eight cruise lines, with North America 66.1% and Europe 31.8% of fiscal 2025 revenue and guests from the United States 55.8% of revenue, so demand and currency exposure is concentrated in US and European leisure spending.
Basis: 10-K_2025-11-30_ccl-20251130.htm.jsonl, page 10; 10-K_2025-11-30_ccl-20251130.htm.jsonl, page 92; 10-K_2025-11-30_ccl-20251130.htm.jsonl, page 94; calculation A1; calculation A2
- The corporate structure was simplified in May 2026: the dual listed arrangement of Carnival Corporation and Carnival plc ended in favour of one Bermuda company, Carnival Corporation Ltd., with plc shareholders exchanging one for one. The dual listed arrangement had each company cross-guarantee the other's debt; whether those cross-guarantees remain after the termination agreement is not established, because the filings say only that debt is issued or guaranteed by substantially the same entities.
Basis: 10-K_2025-11-30_ccl-20251130.htm.jsonl, page 66; 10-Q_2026-08-31_ccl-20260831.htm.jsonl, page 5; 10-Q_2026-08-31_ccl-20260831.htm.jsonl, page 10; 10-Q_2026-08-31_ccl-20260831.htm.jsonl, page 26
- Financial policy moved from deleveraging towards returns: the dividend was reinstated at USD 0.15 per share in December 2025 and a USD 2.5 billion repurchase program was approved in March 2026, alongside debt repayment of USD 2,186 million in nine months 2026 and the redemption of the 7.00% notes due 2029.
Basis: 10-K_2025-11-30_ccl-20251130.htm.jsonl, page 62; EX-99.1_2026-03-27_a20261qearningsrelease8-k.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-Q_2026-08-31_ccl-20260831.htm.jsonl, pages 3–4; 10-Q_2026-08-31_ccl-20260831.htm.jsonl, page 10; EX-99.1_2026-09-29_a20263qearningsrelease8-k.htm.jsonl, page 3
- Security and guarantees were released alongside the rating upgrades: the 7.88% 2027 and 4.00% 2028 notes ceased to be secured, certain notes and export credit facilities lost subsidiary guarantees, and the company says it has no secured debt after the S&P upgrade, so every creditor now sits in unsecured debt whose legal ranking and release terms are in documents that were not supplied.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-08-31_ccl-20260831.htm.jsonl, page 9; 10-Q_2026-08-31_ccl-20260831.htm.jsonl, page 10; EX-99.1_2026-09-29_a20263qearningsrelease8-k.htm.jsonl, page 3
- The business depends on advance bookings and carries a funding float: customer deposits were USD 7,639 million at 2026-08-31, new-ship commitments total USD 18.3 billion over the years to 2030 and beyond, and a 10% change in fuel cost per metric ton moves fourth-quarter adjusted net income by USD 59 million.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-09-29_a20263qearningsrelease8-k.htm.jsonl, page 5; EX-99.1_2026-09-29_a20263qearningsrelease8-k.htm.jsonl, page 9; 10-Q_2026-08-31_ccl-20260831.htm.jsonl, page 13; calculation B2

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a benign view is that capital returns are rising while the company's own net debt to adjusted EBITDA was 3.1x at the second quarter of 2026 against its target of 2.75x, new-ship commitments of USD 18.3 billion remain, and security and guarantees have been released without the governing documents being available. A debt-funded distribution, a reversal of the release of security, a rating downgrade, or a booking slowdown that shrinks customer deposits would move the view toward higher governance and financial-policy risk; continued repayment alongside returns, with leverage reaching the target, would move it toward lower risk.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-03-27_a20261qearningsrelease8-k.htm.jsonl, page 3; EX-99.1_2026-06-23_a20262qearningsrelease8-k.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-Q_2026-08-31_ccl-20260831.htm.jsonl, page 13; 10-Q_2026-08-31_ccl-20260831.htm.jsonl, page 9

### Limitations

- Ownership percentages, board composition and compensation are incorporated by reference to a proxy statement that was not supplied, so the finding that no sponsor or controlling holder is identified rests on the documents' silence and could change if one holds a large stake.
- The indentures, the revolving credit agreement and the export credit agreements were not supplied, so how far debt, liens, restricted payments and releases of guarantees are permitted, and why security was released, are not established; this limits the financial-policy conclusion to observed behaviour.
- The guarantee structure after the unification is described only in general terms and the relationship of Carnival UK Ltd. to Carnival plc is not stated, so which entities stand behind the existing debt is not established.
- No document is dated after 2026-09-29, so events in the 37 days to the as-of date, including whether the planned shelf registration was filed, are not covered.

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

The main structural transaction was the unification of the dual listed company under Carnival Corporation Ltd. and the move of its legal incorporation from Panama to Bermuda, completed in May 2026; plc shareholders received Carnival Corporation Ltd. shares one for one and the company reports no material change to its business. A termination agreement between Carnival Corporation and Carnival plc is dated 2026-05-06.
Basis: 10-K_2025-11-30_ccl-20251130.htm.jsonl, page 67; 10-Q_2026-05-31_ccl-20260531.htm.jsonl, page 5; 10-Q_2026-08-31_ccl-20260831.htm.jsonl, page 5; 10-Q_2026-05-31_ccl-20260531.htm.jsonl, page 31

The balance sheet was reshaped by a refinancing program that the 2025 annual report says was completed in December 2025: in 2025 the company issued six senior unsecured note series and borrowed under three unsecured term loans, and used the proceeds with cash on hand to prepay debt of USD 11.6 billion. In December 2025 it settled the 2027 convertible notes with 69.1 million shares and USD 500 million in cash, and in the third quarter of 2026 it redeemed USD 500 million of 7.00% notes due 2029 with cash on hand. No new debt was issued in nine months 2026; the cash flow statement prints a dash for issuance proceeds.
Basis: 10-K_2025-11-30_ccl-20251130.htm.jsonl, page 50; 10-K_2025-11-30_ccl-20251130.htm.jsonl, pages 80–81; 10-Q_2026-08-31_ccl-20260831.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-Q_2026-08-31_ccl-20260831.htm.jsonl, page 10; EX-99.1_2026-09-29_a20263qearningsrelease8-k.htm.jsonl, page 3
