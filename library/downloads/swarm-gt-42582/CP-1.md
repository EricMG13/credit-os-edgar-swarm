## Findings

### Bottom line

Net leverage on reported EBITDA has jumped to 6.62x at 30 September 2026 from 3.98x at year-end 2025, because earnings fell while borrowing funded negative free cash flow, so creditors now carry more debt against a smaller earnings base. Net debt rose by USD 1,160 million in nine months to USD 6,557 million, while last-twelve-months reported EBITDA fell to USD 990 million from USD 1,357 million in 2025.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-K_2025-12-31_gt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 92; 10-K_2025-12-31_gt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 91; calculation C02; calculation C03; calculation C07; calculation C08

Reported EBITDA is derived and distorted by one-off gains and impairments, so a segment-based proxy is shown beside it, and on that proxy net leverage still rises to 3.89x from 2.57x. The company states that liquidity is adequate for the next twelve months, but USD 1,339 million of debt falls due within a year, including the two 2027 notes.
Basis: calculation C18; calculation C15; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 55; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 22

### Key findings

Leverage worsened from both sides of the ratio. Total debt rose by USD 1,171 million to USD 7,369 million, including the USD 1,050 million senior notes issued on 2026-06-04 and USD 676 million drawn on the two secured revolvers, while last-twelve-months reported EBITDA fell, so total debt over EBITDA reached 7.44x against 4.57x at year-end 2025.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 22; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 23; calculation C01; calculation C04; calculation C26; calculation C08

Cash generation, not interest, is the weak link. Free cash flow for the first nine months of 2026 was negative USD 1,245 million, made up of an operating outflow of USD 764 million and capital expenditure of USD 481 million, against negative USD 1,365 million a year earlier, and net borrowings of USD 1,209 million funded the gap. Receivables absorbed USD 711 million in the period; the company expects working capital to be neither a significant source nor use of cash over the full year, so the fourth quarter shows whether the outflow is timing or structural.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 4–5; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 55; calculation C12; calculation C14

Reported EBITDA is a poor guide to run-rate earnings here because Goodyear states none and the derived figure keeps one-offs: 2025 includes USD 816 million of gains on asset sales and a USD 674 million goodwill impairment, and nine months 2025 includes USD 700 million of gains. On a proxy of segment operating income plus depreciation and amortization, which strips those items but also omits unallocated corporate costs, last-twelve-months earnings were USD 1,684 million against USD 2,102 million in 2025, a fall of USD 418 million, so both measures point to weaker earnings.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_gt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 91; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 19–20; calculation C06; calculation C17; calculation C19

Liquidity, meaning cash plus unused long term committed arrangements, fell to USD 4,138 million from USD 4,946 million at year-end 2025, mainly because the two revolvers, undrawn at year-end, carried USD 676 million at 30 September 2026. Of the USD 1,339 million due within a year, the two 2027 notes are USD 818 million due on 2027-03-15; the company intends to repay them with the proceeds of the 2026 notes, which it temporarily applied to repay revolver borrowings, so repayment depends on revolver and cash capacity rather than set-aside cash.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 40–41; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 22; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 57; 10-K_2025-12-31_gt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 60; 10-K_2025-12-31_gt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 134; calculation C10; calculation C11; calculation C16; calculation C15

Reported EBITDA covered cash interest 3.28x in 2025 and 3.30x in 2024. Cash interest paid for the nine months is not printed, so cover at 30 September 2026 is not established; interest expense for the last twelve months was USD 415 million against USD 445 million in 2025, and the 2032 notes carry the highest coupon of any note, so new unsecured debt is costing more.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_gt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 116; 10-K_2025-12-31_gt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 91; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 22; calculation C09; calculation C07

Funding outside the debt figure is material. Receivables of USD 844 million were sold off balance sheet and supplier-finance programmes confirmed USD 589 million, up from USD 551 million at year-end 2025 and included in trade payables, while operating lease liabilities of USD 1,000 million sit outside Total Debt. A withdrawal of factoring or supplier-finance capacity would draw on liquidity before it shows in reported debt.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 26; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-K_2025-12-31_gt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 141; calculation C25

Other claims also sit outside the debt figure and outside the leverage ratios. Pension and retiree medical plans were net underfunded by USD 369 million at year-end 2025, with non-U.S. pensions USD 215 million and retiree medical USD 231 million in deficit and a U.S. pension surplus of USD 77 million; binding purchase commitments were about USD 5,500 million against USD 2,600 million a year earlier, including a 15-year take-or-pay chemical supply agreement; and reserves for asbestos and product liability were USD 435 million at 30 September 2026.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_gt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 148; 10-K_2025-12-31_gt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 163; 10-K_2024-12-31_gt-20241231.htm.jsonl, page 173; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 32; calculation C21

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest offset is management's statement that liquidity is adequate for the next twelve months, its expectation that working capital will be neutral over 2026, which implies a fourth-quarter inflow, and the savings it expects from the transformation plan. Against that, the same outlook names higher raw material costs, unabsorbed overhead and inflation in the fourth quarter, and nine-month operating cash flow was negative. A positive fourth-quarter operating cash flow with the 2027 notes repaid from cash, or segment operating income recovering toward its 2025 level, would improve the view; a further revolver draw to meet the 2027 maturity would worsen it.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 41; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 55

### Limitations

- Goodyear states no EBITDA, so the figure used is derived and unadjusted and keeps impairments, gains and restructuring inside it; no covenant EBITDA or adjusted EBITDA is available to set against it.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_gt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 91; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 61–62
- Cash interest paid is printed for full years only, and the five-year maturity ladder stops at 2030 and is drawn at year-end 2025, so interest cover at 30 September 2026 and the reconciliation of contractual principal to carrying value are not established.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_gt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 116; 10-K_2025-12-31_gt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 142
- The revised 2023 balance sheet is not in the supplied filings, so net debt and leverage are stated for 2024, 2025 and 30 September 2026 only; the 2024 and 2023 accounts were revised for a currency remeasurement error and the revised figures are used.

## Gaps

- High: The maturity ladder is printed only for 2026 to 2030 and only at year-end 2025, with no total contractual principal and no ladder at 30 September 2026. Contractual principal and its reconciliation to carrying value are therefore not stated, and the debt maturing after 2030 is not quantified; the two 2027 notes and the 2032 notes can be read from the instrument table, but a rolled-forward ladder was not built because it would require estimates. Closing it needs the executed agreements or a later annual report ladder.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_gt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 142; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 22
- Medium: Goodyear states no EBITDA, adjusted EBITDA or add-back schedule, so the leverage and cover figures use a derived, unadjusted EBITDA that includes impairments, restructuring and asset-sale gains. This restricts any conclusion on run-rate leverage and means no bridge from reported to adjusted EBITDA can be stated; an earnings release with company reconciliations would close it.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_gt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 91; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 2
