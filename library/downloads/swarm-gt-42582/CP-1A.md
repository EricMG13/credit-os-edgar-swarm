## Findings

### Bottom line

Goodyear is a widely held listed tire manufacturer with no sponsor or controlling shareholder identified, so creditors face operating and funding risk, not extraction risk, and the 2026 turn from debt repayment to net borrowing is the concern.
No common dividend has been paid since at least 2022, and the three 2025 divestitures raised about USD 2,220 million of gross proceeds that were applied to debt reduction. In the first nine months of 2026 the group borrowed USD 1,209 million net, including USD 1,050 million of senior notes due 2032, while segment operating income fell to USD 305 million from USD 641 million. Sponsor and governance risk is medium because owners, directors and legal capacity are not established, not because of adverse conduct.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_gt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 60; 10-K_2025-12-31_gt-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 43–44; 10-K_2025-12-31_gt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 65; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 40–41; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 62–63; calculation C5

### Key findings

No controlling shareholder or sponsor is identified, so sponsor extraction is not a documented risk, but ownership is unverified rather than disproved. The company is listed on Nasdaq with several thousand holders of record, and the proxy statement that would name large holders, directors and related-party dealings was not supplied.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_gt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 41; 10-K_2025-12-31_gt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 169; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 1

Earnings have shifted from the Americas to Asia Pacific, which now carries the group. In the first nine months of 2026 the Americas produced 56.2% of net sales but 40.3% of segment operating income, down from 78.3% a year earlier, while Asia Pacific produced 11.5% of sales and 61.0% of segment operating income, up from 21.7%. Group segment operating margin was 2.4% against 4.8%.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 19–20; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 48; calculation C2; calculation C3

Demand risk sits in the replacement market, which is the larger and weaker part of the business. Replacement tires were 71.3% of 2025 units, and replacement volume fell 10.7% worldwide in the first nine months of 2026 while original-equipment volume rose 4.5%; management ascribes part of the fall to planned rationalization of lower-tier lines.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_gt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 5; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 44; calculation C4

The 2025 portfolio sales reduced debt but left continuing obligations to each buyer. The sales of the off-the-road tire business, the Dunlop brand and the polymer chemicals business carry supply, licence, offtake or take-or-pay terms, binding purchase commitments were about $5.5bn at year-end 2025, and management expected the Dunlop and chemical sales to reduce 2026 segment operating income by about USD 185 million.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_gt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 19; 10-K_2025-12-31_gt-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 43–44; 10-K_2025-12-31_gt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 45; 10-K_2025-12-31_gt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 163; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 40

The June 2026 notes issue is meant to take out the 2027 maturities but did not set cash aside for them. The USD 1,050 million notes due 2032 are intended to repay the two 2027 notes, which total USD 818 million, yet the proceeds were first applied to revolver balances rather than held in cash, and the two revolvers carried USD 362 million at 30 June 2026 and USD 676 million at 30 September 2026. The annual coupon on the new notes is USD 93.19 million against USD 43.05 million on the two 2027 notes, an increase of USD 50.14 million, partly because the issue is larger than the notes it is meant to retire.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 40–41; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 22; 10-Q_2026-06-30_gt-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 22; 10-K_2025-12-31_gt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 134; calculation C7; calculation C8

Noteholders rely on a guarantor group whose summarized net sales equal 50.8% of group net sales and which ranks behind secured lenders and behind the foreign subsidiaries' own creditors. The senior notes are unsecured, guaranteed by wholly-owned United States and Canadian subsidiaries, and effectively subordinated to secured debt; the summarized guarantor group had net assets of USD 522 million at 30 September 2026 against USD 956 million at year-end 2025, and two older dollar notes totalling USD 268 million have no guarantee described. Net assets of USD 741 million, including USD 195 million of cash, sit in countries that require approval for transfers.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 63–66; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 55–56; 10-K_2025-12-31_gt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 135; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 22; calculation C9; calculation C10

Shareholder distributions are not a documented risk, and capital spending is being cut rather than financed. Capital expenditure fell to USD 826 million in 2025 from USD 1,188 million in 2024, and 2026 spending is now expected at about USD 675 million against about USD 825 million expected in February; management believes its capital spending relative to sales is below that of its principal competitors, which is an underinvestment risk. The credit facilities permit dividends and repurchases if no default exists and financial tests are met, and management states no current plans for either.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_gt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 60; 10-K_2025-12-31_gt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 21; 10-K_2025-12-31_gt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 58; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 55; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 62–63; calculation C11

Restructuring is the main operating lever and carries a near-term cash cost. The Fayetteville plant closure approved on 2026-07-16 has total pre-tax charges of USD 535 million to USD 565 million, of which USD 190 million to USD 210 million is cash, and rationalization payments are expected to be about USD 210 million in 2026. A new master labour contract ratified on 2026-08-23 runs to 2029-04-28 for the three plants it covers; the filings do not reconcile its coverage with the earlier master agreement that was due to expire in July 2026.

Receivables sold off balance sheet and supplier finance confirmed together were USD 1,433 million at 30 September 2026 against USD 1,443 million at year-end 2025, and the confirmed supplier amounts sit in trade payables, not in debt.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest case against a benign governance reading is that risk is untested, not low: the proxy statement is missing, the credit facilities allow distributions when tests are met, and nine months of borrowing show leverage rising with no distribution to explain it. The strongest case for a benign reading is that unused availability under all credit arrangements was USD 3,639 million at 30 September 2026, management states liquidity is adequate for twelve months, and no extraction, priming, exchange offer or covenant waiver is described in any of the five filings. A holder with board or consent rights in the proxy statement, any dividend or buyback, a transfer of assets outside the guarantor group, repayment of the 2027 notes from revolver draws, or a covenant waiver would move the view toward high; repayment of the 2027 notes from cash and a return of segment operating income in the Americas would move it toward low.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 61–62; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 40–41; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 54–55; 10-K_2025-12-31_gt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 169

### Limitations

- The proxy statement and the exhibit that names the guarantor subsidiaries were not supplied, so the finding that no controlling holder exists means none identified, and the guarantor perimeter is described by class only; a holder with board rights or an unlisted guarantor would change both findings.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_gt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 169; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 63
- The executed credit agreements and indentures were not supplied, so restricted-payment, debt and lien capacity is stated only as the filings summarize it; a broad basket or an unrestricted-subsidiary right would move the governance rating toward high even though no distribution has been made.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 61–62; 10-K_2025-12-31_gt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 138
- Comparisons of 2026 with 2025 mix retained and divested operations because no pro forma accounts were supplied, so the fall in group sales and the shift of earnings toward Asia Pacific overstate or understate the underlying trend by an amount not quantified here.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 9; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 42–45
- No customer, channel or distributor share of sales is stated, so the conclusion that revenue is diversified by region and product does not extend to customers; a dominant vehicle maker or distributor would raise business risk.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_gt-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 6–8

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

The only new debt issue in 2026 is USD 1,050 million of senior notes due 2032-07-15, issued on 2026-06-04 at par. They are unsecured senior obligations of the parent, guaranteed by the United States and Canadian subsidiaries that also guarantee the first lien revolver, callable from 2029-07-15 at a premium that falls to par from 2031-07-15, and the indenture limits liens, sale and leaseback transactions and mergers. Management intends to use the proceeds to repay, redeem or repurchase the two notes due 2027-03-15, USD 700 million and USD 118 million as printed, at or before maturity; meanwhile the proceeds were applied to revolver balances, which stood at USD 362 million at 30 June 2026 and USD 676 million at 30 September 2026. The new coupon is higher than the coupons on the notes it is meant to retire, by USD 50.14 million a year on the stated principal amounts.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 22–23; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 40–41; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 57; 10-Q_2026-06-30_gt-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 22; 10-K_2025-12-31_gt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 134; calculation C7; calculation C8
