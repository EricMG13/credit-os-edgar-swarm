## Findings

### Bottom line

The five filings support a credit snapshot of the consolidated Goodyear group through 30 September 2026 with limitations, so creditors can test liquidity and maturities from the filings but not covenant headroom or collateral priority against the governing documents. No executed debt agreements, credit ratings, current market prices or company-defined EBITDA were supplied.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 61–62; 10-K_2025-12-31_gt-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 171–174

The 2025 annual report revised 2024 earnings downward, so the revised figures, not those in the 2024 annual report, govern any comparison. The latest evidence is the third-quarter 2026 report, signed on 2026-11-05, thirteen days before the run date.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_gt-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 166–167; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 75; calculation C1; calculation C2

### Key findings

The statements can be rolled to a last-twelve-months view at 30 September 2026 without a missing period, because the third-quarter report carries nine-month 2025 comparatives and the 2025 annual report carries the full year; only the fourth quarter of 2025 has to be derived by subtraction.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 2–5; 10-K_2025-12-31_gt-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 91–94

The issuer is one registrant, The Goodyear Tire & Rubber Company, reporting consolidated figures for itself and its subsidiaries. The filings give no standalone accounts for the parent or a borrower group, only a summary of the parent and its guarantor subsidiaries, and the list of guarantors sits in an exhibit that was not supplied. The euro notes are issued by the European subsidiary with the parent as guarantor, so the issuer differs by instrument even though the consolidated figures are the only complete perimeter.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_gt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-K_2025-12-31_gt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 172; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 63–66

The third-quarter report prints each note series and the main credit facilities with amount, rate and maturity year, the borrowing-base shortfall on the first lien revolver, the covenant tests with a statement of compliance, receivables sold and supplier-finance balances, and the 2025 annual report adds a five-year maturity table. Liquidity and refinancing work therefore have what they need from the filings, though not from the agreements themselves.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 22–26; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 61–62; 10-K_2025-12-31_gt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 142

The main claims outside reported debt each have their own disclosure in the filings: operating and finance leases, pension and retiree plans, receivables sold off balance sheet, supplier-finance programmes and the asbestos and product-liability reserves. Whether each sits inside or outside the debt figure is a question for Financial foundation, not for this readiness view.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 22; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 26; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 29–30; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 32–35

No filing prints a group EBITDA, an adjusted EBITDA or the result of a covenant ratio; the covenants are described by name and threshold only, and their definitions sit in the credit agreements. Any EBITDA-based leverage or coverage figure will therefore be built from the statements and will not reconcile to the lenders' definition.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 61–62; 10-K_2025-12-31_gt-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 63–64

Business and governance work has the annual report's description of the operations, the three regional segments and the executive officers, and the quarterly segment tables, but no proxy statement, so director, shareholder and pay evidence is not established; the annual report defers those items to a proxy statement.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_gt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-K_2025-12-31_gt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 168; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 19–20; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 39

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest case that the filings are enough for covenant and priority work is that they describe the first lien revolver's financial covenant, the guarantee and security terms of each facility and a summary of guarantor financials. That case fails where a creditor needs it most, because a filing's summary cannot show the definitions, baskets and carve-outs that decide headroom, or who is paid first in a default. Supplying the executed credit agreements and indentures, or current ratings and prices, would move covenant, priority and market-implied work from blocked to ready; a filing dated after 2026-11-05 that changed the debt stack would reopen the readiness of every module that relies on the third-quarter figures.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 23–24; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 61–66

### Limitations

- The figures were read from page-text extracts, not from the original formatted filings, and the extract of the 2024 annual report splits table cells across lines, so every figure carried into later work must be opened at its cited page.
Basis: 10-K_2024-12-31_gt-20241231.htm.jsonl, pages 104–105
- Only the two annual reports carry an audit opinion; the three quarterly reports are unaudited, so the nine-month 2026 position has a lower evidential standard than the year-end 2025 position.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-K_2025-12-31_gt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 91
- No document dated after the third-quarter report was supplied, so any financing, rating or trading event between 2026-11-05 and the 2026-11-18 cutoff is not established.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_gt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 75; calculation C1
