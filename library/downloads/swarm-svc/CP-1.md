## Findings

### Bottom line

Reported leverage overstates the deterioration in creditor protection: total debt was 22.72x unadjusted EBITDA at 2026-09-30 against 10.35x at 2025-12-31 because of impairment and extinguishment charges, while the analyst-normalised ratio eased to 9.21x from 10.05x as debt fell.
Basis: calculation C5; calculation C7; calculation C8; 10-Q_2026-09-30_svc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 2–5

Even normalised, leverage above nine times and cash interest cover of 1.46x (0.59x unadjusted) over the last twelve months leave creditors reliant on asset sales and refinancing. Liquidity rests on an undrawn revolving credit facility of USD 650 million that matures on June 29, 2027, and 34.13% of principal falls due by the end of 2028.
Basis: calculation C6; calculation C8; calculation C11; calculation C16; 10-Q_2026-09-30_svc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 20; 10-Q_2026-09-30_svc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 48–49

Revenue fell as hotels were sold, so the ratios rest on a smaller earnings base than at the year end.
Basis: calculation C2; 10-K_2025-12-31_svc-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 171–172; 10-Q_2026-09-30_svc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 3
