## Findings

### Bottom line

Arcosa's net leverage was 1.91x at 30 June 2026 on a continuing-operations basis, but that figure reflects barge sale cash held on the balance sheet, not earnings growth, so the creditor cushion is only as durable as that cash. Only USD 83.0 million of the USD 429.9 million net sale proceeds repaid debt, leaving USD 432.1 million of cash against USD 1,436.7 million of carrying debt, with a pro forma net leverage of 1.92x after the dividend paid on 31 July 2026. Continuing operations used USD 68.9 million of cash in the first half after capital expenditures, and cash interest cover for 2026 cannot be stated because the quarterly reports give no interest paid.

### Key findings

Net leverage peaked at 3.82x at the end of 2024 because the Stavola acquisition put its full financing on the year-end balance sheet while contributing only one quarter of earnings, then eased to 2.32x at the end of 2025 as earnings rose and USD 168.7 million of debt was repaid; the 2025 figure still includes a barge business that earned USD 53.6 million of EBITDA and has since been sold.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_aca_aca-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 94; 10-K_2025-12-31_aca_aca-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 104; 10-K_2025-12-31_aca_aca-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 109; calculation C-V2; calculation C-V3; calculation C-X6

Cash interest cover fell from 16.10x in 2023 to 7.81x in 2024 and 5.48x in 2025 as interest expense rose from USD 28.1 million to USD 108.8 million on the acquisition financing. The first half of 2026 carried USD 9.7 million less interest expense than the first half of 2025, which gives 5.30x of EBITDA over interest expense for the twelve months to June 2026; that is an accrual-based proxy, not cash cover.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_aca_aca-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 93; 10-K_2025-12-31_aca_aca-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 94; 10-K_2025-12-31_aca_aca-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 64; 10-Q_2026-06-30_aca_aca-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 25; calculation C-I1; calculation C-I2; calculation C-I3; calculation C-P5; calculation C-X7

Earnings growth is modest: last-twelve-month EBITDA to June 2026 is USD 14.0 million, or 2.74%, above the 2025 figure rebuilt without the barge business, and 14.99% of first-half operating profit came from other operating income such as asset sale gains, so the debt reduction rests on disposal proceeds rather than on trading.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_aca_aca-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 109; 10-Q_2026-06-30_aca_aca-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 2; calculation C-X17; calculation C-X14; calculation C-R4; calculation C-LB4

Cash flow quality is the weaker side: receivables rose USD 146.4 million in the first half of 2026, continuing operations used USD 68.9 million after capital expenditures against USD 43.1 million used a year earlier, and USD 84.7 million went on three acquisitions and USD 17.5 million on share repurchases, while investing cash flow was positive only because discontinued operations brought in USD 428.5 million. Over the full years operating cash flow less capital expenditures was USD 312.3 million in 2024 and USD 175.5 million in 2025.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_aca_aca-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-06-30_aca_aca-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 3–4; 10-K_2025-12-31_aca_aca-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 94; calculation C-X13; calculation C-F2; calculation C-F3; calculation C-F4; calculation C-F5

Liquidity is ample on the balance sheet but incompletely evidenced: cash was USD 432.1 million at 30 June 2026 and the revolver commitment is USD 700.0 million with no loans outstanding, yet the June report states neither the undrawn amount nor letters of credit, so the facility figure for that date is left as not supplied. There is no debt maturity before the 2029 senior notes apart from the term loan amortization of USD 7.0 million a year.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_aca_aca-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-06-30_aca_aca-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 16; 10-Q_2026-06-30_aca_aca-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 18; calculation C-D1

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest case against the cautious reading is the size of the cash buffer: USD 432.1 million of cash and a USD 700.0 million revolver with no loans outstanding cover the 2029 notes of USD 400.0 million, and net debt fell by USD 303.6 million in six months. The view would improve if the third-quarter report shows operating cash flow covering capital expenditures and the proceeds still unspent; it would worsen if they were spent on acquisitions, buybacks or a merger-related payment, or if the amendment of July 2026 changed the covenants.
