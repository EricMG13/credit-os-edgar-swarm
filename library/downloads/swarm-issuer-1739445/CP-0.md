## Findings

### Bottom line

The four filings support a dated credit read of Arcosa as of 30 June 2026 but not a current one, because nothing dated after 5 August 2026 was supplied and a cash takeover by CRH was then pending. For creditors that means leverage, liquidity and covenant headroom can only be stated as June positions: the shareholder vote scheduled for September, any third-quarter results, the July amendment to the credit agreement and the indentures behind the 101% change-of-control repurchase offer on the senior notes are all outside the evidence.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_aca_aca-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 5–7; 10-Q_2026-06-30_aca_aca-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 40; 10-Q_2026-06-30_aca_aca-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 45; 10-Q_2026-06-30_aca_aca-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 18; calculation C-02

The statements that were supplied are usable and tie to each other, but the 2025 annual figures still include the barge business that the 2026 quarterly reports treat as discontinued; it was 13.29% of 2025 revenue, so any trailing-twelve-month measure needs a rebuilt perimeter.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_aca_aca-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 109; 10-Q_2026-06-30_aca_aca-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 11; calculation C-03; calculation C-06; calculation C-09; calculation C-10
