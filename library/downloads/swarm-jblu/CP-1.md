## Findings

### Bottom line
JetBlue's earnings no longer service its debt: derived EBITDA of USD 115 million for the twelve months to September 2026 covers cash interest paid of USD 493 million only 0.23x, so creditors depend on liquidity and aircraft collateral rather than operating cash flow.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_jblu-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 6–7; 10-K_2025-12-31_jblu-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 113–114; calculation C04; calculation C05; calculation C18

Net debt rose to USD 7,135 million at September 30, 2026 from USD 6,552 million at the 2025 year-end because cash fell by USD 688 million while carrying debt eased by USD 105 million; on the derived measure that is 62.04x EBITDA.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_jblu-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 5–6; 10-K_2025-12-31_jblu-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 112–113; calculation C14; calculation C15; calculation C19; calculation C17

The nearest large maturity is the USD 1,835 million due in 2029, set against USD 1,626 million of cash and investment securities and a USD 600 million undrawn line; cash and investments fell by USD 851 million in nine months.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_jblu-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 5, 11, 14; calculation C20; calculation C22

### Financial interpretation

Leverage has risen because the earnings base has shrunk, not because borrowing has grown. Carrying debt moved only from 8,539 at the 2024 year-end to 8,498 at the 2025 year-end and 8,393 at September 30, 2026, while derived EBITDA fell from 320 in FY2025 to 115 for the twelve months to September 2026 because nine-month fuel expense rose by 798 and total operating expenses by 1,172 against a revenue rise of 931. With a debt stack of this size on that earnings base, scheduled repayments are being met from the balance sheet and new secured borrowing rather than from operations, which is why the ratios sit at several tens of turns.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_jblu-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 112–114; 10-Q_2026-09-30_jblu-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 5–7; calculation C14; calculation C04; calculation C16; calculation C35

Coverage tells the same story. Cash interest paid, net of interest income, rose from 230 in FY2024 to 427 in FY2025 as the 2024 TrueBlue financing, equipment notes and finance leases became full-year costs, and reached 493 for the twelve months to September 2026 while EBITDA fell, so cover is 0.23x. Interest is therefore a first call on cash that the operating business does not currently generate.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_jblu-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 89, 126–131; 10-Q_2026-09-30_jblu-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 6–7; calculation C05; calculation C18

Cash-flow quality is weak. Operating cash flow was negative at -273 for nine months 2026 and capital expenditures plus pre-delivery deposits were 624, so free cash flow was negative before debt service; the gap was met by running down cash and investment securities and by raising 495 of new aircraft-secured debt against 658 of repayments. Air traffic liability, a non-debt float that funds operations, rose to 2,696 from 2,373, which flatters operating cash flow and would reverse if bookings weakened.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_jblu-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 5–7; calculation C12; calculation C32; calculation C11

Liquidity is adequate near term but thin against the 2029 wall. Cash and investment securities were 1,626 at September 30, 2026 with a 600 undrawn Citibank line, against scheduled maturities of 152 for the rest of 2026, 477 in 2027 and 582 in 2028, which total 1,211, before the 1,835 in 2029. The strongest contrary evidence is management's statement that unencumbered assets reached about 7bn, which would support further secured borrowing, and an additional 250 of aircraft-secured financing that the company expects to fund in the fourth quarter. That view would win if fuel recapture and management's goal of operating profitability in 2027 are delivered and the new financing closes; it fails if cash burn continues at the 2026 pace, because cash and investments fell by 851 in nine months.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_jblu-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 5, 11, 14, 44; EX-99.1_2026-10-26_ex991-earningsreleaseq32026.htm.jsonl, page 2; calculation C20; calculation C22

Several definitions change the reading. The company's third-quarter liquidity headline adds the undrawn line that earlier quarters left out, so the apparent stability hides a fall of 542 on the earlier basis.
