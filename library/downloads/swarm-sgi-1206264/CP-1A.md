## Findings

### Bottom line

Somnigroup is a listed company with no sponsor or controlling holder named in its filings, so creditors rely on its own financial policy, and two acquisitions since 2025-02-05, funded by debt and shares, show that policy has favoured scale over deleveraging.

The acquisitions lifted net sales from USD 4,930.9 million in FY2024 to USD 7,844.2 million over the trailing twelve months and total debt from USD 3,809.9 million to USD 5,381.6 million, while dividends stayed small at USD 108.1 million in nine months 2026 and no shares were repurchased under the programme. The main risks are integration of Leggett & Platt, whose gross margin is 17.7% against 42.02% for the group, and a Mattress Firm segment whose net sales fell 5.8% in the third quarter; the filings name no sponsor fees, related-party payments or creditor-adverse transactions.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_sgi.htm.jsonl, pages 72–73; 10-Q_2026-09-30_sgi.htm.jsonl, pages 6, 9, 19, 35–36; calculation C1; calculation C13

### Key findings

The business is now a vertically integrated bedding group in which company-owned stores are the largest route to the consumer, which ties cash flow to consumer traffic but not to any one customer. Direct sales were 63.48% of net sales in FY2025 against 24.93% in FY2024 because Mattress Firm is a retailer, and the group states that no customer exceeds 5% of global sales. The store network carries operating lease cost of USD 489.5 million, 6.55% of FY2025 net sales, so a fall in traffic cuts earnings faster than sales, and Mattress Firm net sales fell 5.8% in the third quarter of 2026 on softer online sales with slightly lower same-store sales.
Basis: 10-K_2024-12-31_sgi.htm.jsonl, page 11; 10-K_2025-12-31_sgi.htm.jsonl, pages 47–48, 103; 10-Q_2026-09-30_sgi.htm.jsonl, pages 34–35; calculation C8; calculation C14

Acquisition policy, not sponsor behaviour, is the main driver of creditor risk. Total debt rose USD 1,571.7 million, 41.25%, between 2024-12-31 and 2026-09-30, while net sales over the trailing twelve months were 59.08% above FY2024. Mattress Firm cost USD 5,408.4 million, of which 57.16% was cash and 41.51% shares, and the USD 2,896.5 million drawn or released from escrow at closing, equal to 93.69% of that cash, also had to repay Mattress Firm's debt and pay fees; Leggett & Platt cost USD 1,289.6 million, 98.78% in shares, but it brought USD 1,500.0 million of notes with it. The second deal therefore added less new borrowing but the same direction of travel, and the issuer's own leverage target of 2.0x to 3.0x is on the covenant measure, not on reported earnings.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_sgi.htm.jsonl, pages 92–93; 10-Q_2026-09-30_sgi.htm.jsonl, pages 16–17, 19, 21, 45; calculation C1; calculation C2; calculation C3

Ownership gives creditors no sponsor to lean on and none to fear, so support for creditors cannot be inferred from who owns the company. The filings name no controlling holder and no sponsor, and the new shares issued to the sellers of the two acquired companies equal 23.50% of shares outstanding on 2026-11-04. The equity cushion is thinner than it looks: goodwill and intangible assets of USD 7,608.4 million exceed stockholders' equity of USD 4,633.6 million, so tangible equity is negative USD 2,974.8 million.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_sgi.htm.jsonl, pages 1, 6–7, 15; 10-K_2025-12-31_sgi.htm.jsonl, pages 92–93; calculation C4; calculation C10; Source readiness (CP-0)

Distributions are modest and growing, and no leverage test in the debt documents currently stops them. The dividend per share rose from USD 0.44 in 2023 to USD 0.52 in 2024 and USD 0.60 in 2025 and is USD 0.17 a quarter in 2026, USD 0.68 a year, and the USD 108.1 million paid in nine months 2026 was 12.99% of operating cash flow. No shares were repurchased under the programme in 2025 or 2026, with USD 774.5 million of authorisation unused. The issuer's covenant ratio of 2.69x is below both the 3.75x and 3.50x levels under which restricted payments are unlimited, so restraint is the board's choice, and the builder basket grows by 50% of adjusted net income each quarter.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_sgi.htm.jsonl, pages 36–37, 41, 74; 10-Q_2026-09-30_sgi.htm.jsonl, pages 9, 20, 45, 47, 52–53; calculation C5

Reporting is timely, audited and detailed on covenants, but control coverage of the acquired businesses is incomplete. The annual report was signed 58 days after year-end with an unqualified audit opinion, and the third-quarter report 40 days after quarter-end with a quarterly covenant reconciliation. Mattress Firm's internal controls are still being brought into the assessment for 2026, Leggett & Platt is excluded from that assessment, and the Dreams ERP system went live in the third quarter with modified controls while Dreams net sales fell 34.7%.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_sgi.htm.jsonl, pages 70, 72, 120; 10-Q_2026-09-30_sgi.htm.jsonl, pages 35, 55, 58; calculation C11

Integration evidence is positive at Mattress Firm and unproven at Leggett & Platt. The covenant add-back for expected Mattress Firm cost savings fell from USD 100.0 million at FY2025 to USD 70.0 million at 2026-09-30, and the issuer states that Mattress Firm synergies added 420 bp to North America gross margin in the third quarter and 570 bp over nine months, so those savings are being realised; the USD 75.0 million Leggett & Platt add-back is unrealised and its stub gross margin is 17.7%. Excluding Leggett & Platt, adjusted operating income fell 5.91% in the third quarter, and pro forma net sales including Leggett & Platt fell 6.30%.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_sgi.htm.jsonl, page 62; 10-Q_2026-09-30_sgi.htm.jsonl, pages 18, 33, 36, 41, 49–53; calculation C6; calculation C7; calculation C13

Leggett & Platt's debt is structurally separate, so which creditors stand where cannot be established from these filings. Its three notes of USD 1,500.0 million carry no guarantee and are obligations of Leggett & Platt, Incorporated, while the guarantors of the credit facilities and of the 2029 and 2031 notes are not named; the company is designated a restricted subsidiary, which brings its earnings into the covenant measure. Whether its assets support the secured facilities or the other notes is not stated, and that question decides how the added debt ranks against the existing creditors.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_sgi.htm.jsonl, pages 19, 21–23, 53; 10-K_2025-12-31_sgi.htm.jsonl, pages 99, 101; Source readiness (CP-0)

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against reading acquisition appetite as a creditor risk is that Leggett & Platt was 98.78% paid in shares, that the filings show no debt-funded distribution or sponsor extraction, that the issuer reports covenant leverage of 2.69x against a 2.0x to 3.0x target, and that the Mattress Firm synergy add-back has fallen by USD 30.0 million as savings were achieved. The view would improve on a printed full-period result for Leggett & Platt that supports the USD 330.5 million pre-acquisition add-back, on a guarantor list showing the new subsidiaries stand behind the senior secured and note creditors, and on continued debt repayment ahead of dividends. It would worsen on another debt-funded acquisition, on a rise in distributions while reported leverage stays high, or if third-quarter sales weakness at Mattress Firm and International continues.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_sgi.htm.jsonl, page 62; 10-Q_2026-09-30_sgi.htm.jsonl, pages 15–16, 45, 47, 52–53; calculation C3; calculation C6

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

The two acquisitions since 2025-02-05 define the credit. Mattress Firm closed on 2025-02-05 for total consideration of USD 5,408.4 million: USD 3,091.5 million of cash, USD 2,245.1 million of shares at USD 65.65 each and USD 71.8 million of settled pre-existing balances, less USD 267.0 million of cash acquired. The cash was funded by USD 625.0 million of the Delayed Draw Term A Loan, USD 679.5 million of revolver borrowing and about USD 1,592.0 million of Term B Loan proceeds released from escrow, where they had been held since 2024-10-24, and the same proceeds also repaid Mattress Firm's debt and paid fees.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_sgi.htm.jsonl, pages 92–93, 97; calculation C2

Leggett & Platt closed on 2026-08-26 for total consideration of USD 1,289.6 million, almost all in new shares valued at USD 64.09 each, with net consideration of USD 730.0 million after USD 560.2 million of cash acquired; its three senior notes of USD 500.0 million each remain outstanding at Leggett & Platt, Incorporated with no guarantee, and the debt assumed was recorded at USD 1,283.6 million. No new bonds were issued in either transaction.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_sgi.htm.jsonl, pages 15–17, 21–23; calculation C3

The financing around the second deal was a refinancing, not new money for the purchase. Amendment No. 5 of 2026-07-27 added a USD 1,200.0 million Term A Loan and USD 510.0 million of revolving commitments, took revolver commitments to USD 1,700.0 million, extended both to 2031-07-27, and repaid the earlier term loans, and USD 700.0 million of the Term B Loan was prepaid with the remaining proceeds. A further USD 200.0 million of the Term B Loan was prepaid on 2026-10-01 with cash acquired and cash from operations, after the balance-sheet date.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_sgi.htm.jsonl, pages 20–21, 46
