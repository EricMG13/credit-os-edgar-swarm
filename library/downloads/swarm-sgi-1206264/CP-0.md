## Findings

### Bottom line
The five supplied filings support a credit snapshot of Somnigroup International as of 2026-11-18 on reported balances and results, but creditors cannot verify covenant definitions, ratings or market pricing because no executed debt documents, agency ratings or price quotes were supplied. Financial foundation, Business & governance and Fundamental credit can proceed on this evidence, with the limitations recorded here; supplying the executed credit agreement and note indentures would remove the main one.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_sgi.htm.jsonl, pages 56–58

The latest quarter changed the perimeter: Leggett & Platt joined on 2026-08-26, so the September balance sheet carries its debt while the income statement carries only about five weeks of its results. Total debt was USD 5,627.0 million and liquidity USD 1,794.8 million. The covenant leverage the issuer reports, 2.69x, counts USD 475.5 million of add-backs beyond reported adjusted EBITDA of USD 1,389.3 million, so it should be read as a covenant-defined measure, not as leverage on reported earnings.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_sgi.htm.jsonl, pages 10, 19, 47, 52–53; calculation C1; calculation C4; calculation C6; calculation C7
