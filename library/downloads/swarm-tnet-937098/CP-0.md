## Findings

### Bottom line

The five filings support the financial foundation, business profile and fundamental credit work selected for this run, but not covenant headroom, note pricing, rating triggers or recovery, so creditor protection beyond reported covenant compliance cannot be judged from them.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 25; 10-K_2025-12-31_tnet.htm.jsonl, pages 120–121; 10-K_2025-12-31_tnet.htm.jsonl, pages 133–134

The evidence is one reporting entity current to 2026-09-30: debt of USD 896 million on the balance sheet against USD 900 million of note principal, unrestricted cash of USD 328 million and a USD 700 million revolver with no borrowings outstanding. The covenant level, revolver availability after letters of credit, ratings, quotes, guarantor names and the executed debt documents are not in the set, and each limits only the conclusion that depends on it.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 25; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 28; calculation C02; calculation C03

### Key findings

The set is one reporting entity across five primary filings: the annual reports for 2024 and 2025 and the quarterly reports for March, June and September 2026. The newest was signed 20 days before the cutoff, so events between 2026-10-29 and 2026-11-18 are not established.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 46; calculation C01

The statements support a financial foundation: audited annual statements for 2023 to 2025, and for 2026 the first quarter, six months and nine months, each with income statement, balance sheet, cash flow and a reconciliation of net income to adjusted earnings. The 2025 interim periods exist only as comparatives inside the 2026 reports, so discrete 2025 quarters are not available as filed, and the quarterly worksite-employee charts in the annual report carry no figures in the text.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_tnet.htm.jsonl, pages 93–95; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tnet.htm.jsonl, pages 27–29; 10-K_2025-12-31_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 74

Debt instruments are identifiable: a USD 500 million senior unsecured note due March 2029, a USD 400 million senior unsecured note due August 2031, and a USD 700 million revolver with no borrowings outstanding since July 2025. None of the three facilities matures within twelve months of the cutoff, but the revolver maturity of August 2028 is stated only in the 2024 annual report. Rates, effective rates and issuance costs are printed only in the annual report. The quarterly reports give principal and the balance-sheet figure of USD 896 million but no instrument table, and the principal of USD 900 million exceeds that figure by USD 4 million without a printed explanation.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_tnet.htm.jsonl, pages 118–119; 10-K_2024-12-31_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 126; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 25; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 28; calculation C02; calculation C03

Corporate liquidity can be built from the filings: unrestricted cash was USD 328 million at 2026-09-30, and the filings separate it from payroll and insurance-collateral funds that are restricted. The undrawn amount of the revolver after letters of credit is stated only at year-end, USD 699 million of USD 700 million at 2025-12-31, so the September 2026 amount is not established.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_tnet.htm.jsonl, pages 21–22; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 28; 10-K_2025-12-31_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 120

Claims outside debt are identifiable and sit outside the debt figure: operating lease liabilities of USD 47 million at 2025-12-31, purchase obligations of USD 136 million, workers' compensation obligations of USD 151 million, and the payroll and tax liabilities held for worksite employees, which the filings show offset by matching client-related current assets. No pension, retiree medical, factoring, supply-chain finance or consent decree obligation is described, and litigation is accrued at amounts the filings call immaterial.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_tnet.htm.jsonl, pages 80–81; 10-K_2025-12-31_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 118; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 21; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tnet.htm.jsonl, pages 38–39

The revolver carries a financial covenant on maximum total net leverage, and compliance is stated at every period end supplied. The maximum level, any step-down, the definition and the reported ratio are not printed, so headroom is not calculable. The restrictions in the note indentures on liens, subsidiary guarantees and mergers are described only in summary.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 25; 10-K_2025-12-31_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 121; 10-K_2025-12-31_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 83

The run settings supply no ratings, quotes, deal terms, peer set, portfolio, management bridge or executed debt documents, and the filings name no rating of the issuer. Ratings & triggers, Peer benchmark, Security selection, Legal & covenants and Recovery & fulcrum therefore have no evidence in this set, and Market-implied risk has only the pricing-service fair values of the two notes.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_tnet.htm.jsonl, pages 120–121; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 37

Ownership evidence is partial: the largest stockholder, an investment firm affiliated with Atairos, held about 38% at 2026-01-31 and keeps a board nomination right while it holds at least 15%, but directors, officers and compensation sit in a proxy statement that was not supplied.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 62; 10-K_2025-12-31_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 2

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest case against a ready-with-limitations verdict is that the missing items sit exactly where a creditor needs precision: the leverage covenant is the one maintenance test the filings mention, and the revolver is the one committed bank facility they describe. Supplying the credit agreement and indentures would let covenant headroom, availability after letters of credit, guarantee status and lien limits be tested, and Legal & covenants and Covenant capacity could move out of blocked. A filing, release or rating action dated between 2026-10-29 and 2026-11-18 could also change the view, because a later financing, amendment or acquisition would sit outside every figure in this set.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 25; 10-K_2025-12-31_tnet.htm.jsonl, pages 133–134

Consistent compliance statements in all five filings, and no revolver borrowings since July 2025, point the other way: they suggest the missing covenant level matters less to the selected modules than to a covenant or recovery analysis. They do not replace the level.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 25; 10-K_2025-12-31_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 121; 10-K_2024-12-31_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 128; 10-K_2025-12-31_tnet.htm.jsonl, pages 133–134

### Limitations

The verdicts rest on filing text only. Tables are flattened into lines, charts are not retained as figures, and a page reference in this work is a text chunk, not a printed page. Absent items are never treated as zero: the 2025 interim filings as filed, the covenant level, letters of credit at 2026-09-30, the proxy statement, every guarantor name, any filing after 2026-10-29 and every external input. The revolver maturity is stated only in the 2024 annual report. The Cocoon closing day and the 2023 adjusted EBITDA margin differ between filings and are preserved, not reconciled. File integrity and text extraction were verified before this module ran, and no result is recorded here.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 74; 10-K_2024-12-31_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 126; 10-Q_2026-03-31_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 40; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 43
