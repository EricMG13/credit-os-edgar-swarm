## Findings

### Bottom line

Mueller Water names no sponsor or controlling shareholder in the supplied filings, and its financial policy returns modest cash to shareholders without debt funding, so governance adds little creditor risk beyond a concentrated distributor base.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_mwa-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 53; 10-Q_2026-06-30_mwa-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 4; calculation K21

Sponsor and governance risk is Low. Dividends and buybacks took 43.67% of free cash flow in nine months 2026, the only waiver the documents record lasted 57 days after a cyber incident, and the cash flow statements show no acquisition spending from FY2023 to nine months 2026. The unnamed transaction behind up to USD 22.0 million of nine-month charges and the unsupplied proxy statement are the open points.
Basis: 10-K_2024-09-30_mwa-20240930.htm.jsonl, page 116; 10-Q_2026-06-30_mwa-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 8–9; calculation K21; calculation K41; calculation K34

### Key findings

Ownership gives creditors no sponsor to extract value, but also no controlling holder to defend the company against a change of control. The filings name no sponsor or large holder, list 78 holders of record, and leave beneficial ownership to a proxy statement that is not supplied. The notes carry a put at 101% on a change of control only when a ratings decline accompanies it, and the indenture's definition of that decline is not supplied, so a takeover without it would leave noteholders with no put. The notes were rated Ba1 and BB+ at 2026-06-30.

Shareholder returns are modest and covered by free cash flow today, but in the weakest year they exceeded it. Dividends and repurchases were USD 48.3 million in nine months 2026, 43.67% of free cash flow of USD 110.6 million, against 33.08% in FY2025 and 26.07% in FY2024, and the quarterly dividend per share rose 4.48%. No borrowing appears in the financing sections from FY2022 to nine months 2026, and USD 49.5 million of the USD 250.0 million repurchase authorization remains.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a Low rating is that nothing in the supplied documents shows a structural barrier to a debt-funded transaction. No controlling holder is named, the direction of the unnamed transaction is not stated, and cash of USD 495.3 million plus USD 163.7 million of ABL availability could fund a deal without a new issue. The change-of-control put needs a ratings decline, and the indenture and credit agreement that set the baskets for payments, investments and added debt are not supplied, so what the documents permit is not established and the rating rests on documented behaviour. Customer concentration is the largest business risk, with two customers at 37% of gross sales. The view would worsen if the transaction proves debt funded or large against earnings, if a holder emerges with a stake that changes control, if payouts exceed free cash flow, or if a second waiver is needed. It would improve if the transaction is a small cash-funded disposal or bolt-on and the proxy statement shows dispersed ownership.
