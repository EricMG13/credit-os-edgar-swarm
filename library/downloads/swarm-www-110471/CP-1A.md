## Findings

### Bottom line

Wolverine is a widely held public company with no sponsor or controlling holder in its filings, so owner extraction is not the main creditor risk; business concentration, tariff-exposed Asian sourcing and legacy environmental claims are, and governance risk is moderate. The Active Group supplies 75.1% of revenue and 71.4% of segment profit before corporate costs, while the Work Group is flat and losing margin.
Basis: 10-K_2026-01-03_www-20260103.htm.jsonl, pages 107–108; 10-Q_2026-10-03_www-20261003.htm.jsonl, page 28; calculation K1; calculation K3; calculation K7

Shareholder payouts are recurring but moderate against operating cash flow, debt has fallen by USD 100.6 million since the end of fiscal 2024, and no acquisition or new debt issue is described in the filings. The elimination of the Active Group president role in 2026 and the missing proxy statement and credit agreement leave board oversight and payout capacity unproven.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-10-03_www-20261003.htm.jsonl, page 14; 10-Q_2026-10-03_www-20261003.htm.jsonl, page 27; 10-K_2026-01-03_www-20260103.htm.jsonl, page 87; calculation K10; calculation K11

### Key findings

No owner other than public shareholders is visible, so there is no sponsor to extract cash and none to supply a cure. The registrant is a Delaware corporation listed in New York with a few hundred holders of record, and no parent, fund or controlling shareholder is named in any of the five filings. The beneficial ownership table sits in a proxy statement that is not supplied, so a large holder or activist cannot be ruled out, although a classified board with one-third of directors elected each year makes a sudden change of control harder.

Governance disclosure is adequate for monitoring reported results but thin for creditor protection. ### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a moderate reading is conduct while debt was outstanding. The November 2025 repurchase and a dividend held at USD 0.10 per share each quarter were paid while revolver balances were outstanding at the quarter-ends reported, and the credit agreement and indenture that limit dividends and buybacks are not supplied, so payout capacity could be wide. Turnover in senior management when the Active Group carries most of the profit, a Work Group whose margin is falling and a pending environmental suit add to the case for a higher risk reading.
Basis: 10-K_2026-01-03_www-20260103.htm.jsonl, page 44; 10-K_2026-01-03_www-20260103.htm.jsonl, page 52; 10-Q_2026-10-03_www-20261003.htm.jsonl, page 14; 10-Q_2026-10-03_www-20261003.htm.jsonl, page 22; 10-Q_2026-10-03_www-20261003.htm.jsonl, page 27; 10-Q_2026-10-03_www-20261003.htm.jsonl, page 40

Against that, the filings print no related-party payments, no waiver, exchange offer or restructuring of any debt, a USD 100.6 million fall in debt since fiscal 2024, an undrawn revolver and unqualified audit opinions. A credit agreement with a wide restricted-payment basket, a large new holder or activist, a debt-funded acquisition or buyback, or a proxy statement showing related-party payments would move the view to higher risk. A proxy statement showing independent committees, together with tight payment limits in the credit agreement, would move it to lower risk.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-10-03_www-20261003.htm.jsonl, page 14; 10-Q_2026-10-03_www-20261003.htm.jsonl, page 15; 10-K_2026-01-03_www-20260103.htm.jsonl, pages 111–114; calculation K10

### Limitations

- Board independence, committee composition, compensation and related-party matters sit in a proxy statement that is not supplied, so the moderate governance reading cannot be tightened to low and related-party leakage is not ruled out.
- Beneficial ownership is not supplied, so the finding that no controlling holder exists rests on the absence of any named sponsor or parent in the filings; a large holder would change the ownership and control reading.
- The credit agreement and indenture are not supplied, so the capacity of the company to pay dividends, repurchase shares, add debt or move assets is not established; the moderate risk level rests on conduct, not on legal capacity.
- Brand revenue and profit are printed only as changes, so the dependence of the Active Group on Saucony and Merrell, and the share of Work Group revenue from the licensed Cat and Harley-Davidson brands, are stated by direction and not by size.
- No document after the quarterly report signed on 2026-11-12 is supplied, so executive, ownership or financing changes since that date could change the governance reading.
