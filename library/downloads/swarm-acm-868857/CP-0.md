## Findings

### Bottom line

The five supplied filings support a credit snapshot of Aecom through the third quarter of fiscal 2026, but not covenant headroom, ratings, market pricing or any event after that quarter, so creditor conclusions must stay provisional until those are added.

Financial foundation can run in full, because the statements, debt note and liquidity discussion are present for the latest quarter and two annual periods. Business & governance and Fundamental credit can run with stated limits: no proxy statement, no executed credit documents, no printed covenant ratio, and no result later than the filing signed 2026-08-11, which is 99 days before the as-of date.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-07-03_acm_acm-20260703.htm.jsonl, pages 2–6; 10-K_2025-09-30_acm_acm-20250930.htm.jsonl, pages 77–80; calculation C1

### Key findings

The file set is two annual reports (fiscal 2024 and fiscal 2025) and three quarterly reports (the first to third quarters of fiscal 2026), all primary filings by the registrant. Aecom issues the senior notes itself and reports consolidated statements; the subsidiaries that guarantee the notes appear only in summarised combined information, not in separate statements.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_acm_acm-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 1; 10-Q_2026-07-03_acm_acm-20260703.htm.jsonl, page 22; 10-Q_2026-07-03_acm_acm-20260703.htm.jsonl, pages 63–64

The filings use a 52 or 53 week calendar and present every period as if it ended on a calendar quarter end, so the cover of the third-quarter report names a period ending 2026-07-03 while its statements are dated 2026-06-30. Each figure in later work should carry the statement date, with the true period end noted once.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-07-03_acm_acm-20260703.htm.jsonl, page 1; 10-Q_2026-07-03_acm_acm-20260703.htm.jsonl, page 7

The bank debt terms changed twice in fiscal 2026: an amendment in March 2026 replaced the revolver and both term loans, and a new agreement in June 2026 added a second revolving facility. The annual reports and the first-quarter report therefore describe facilities that have been superseded, and the third-quarter report controls for current terms.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-07-03_acm_acm-20260703.htm.jsonl, page 20; 10-Q_2026-04-03_acm_acm-20260403.htm.jsonl, page 20; 10-Q_2026-01-02_acm_acm-20260102.htm.jsonl, page 19; 10-K_2025-09-30_acm_acm-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 104

The filings state the maintenance test, a consolidated leverage ratio capped at 4.00x and tested quarterly (the term loan B is exempt), and they say the company complied with its credit agreement covenants at each period end. None prints the ratio, its EBITDA definition or any headroom, and no file uses the term EBITDA, so covenant headroom is not calculable from this file set.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-07-03_acm_acm-20260703.htm.jsonl, page 21

No file mentions a credit rating or a rating agency, and the run settings supply no quotes, peers or portfolio. Ratings & triggers and Market-implied risk therefore cannot be run from this file set, and relative value lies outside the requested scope.

Business description, risk factors, segments and remaining performance obligations are well covered. The annual reports incorporate director, compensation and ownership information by reference to a proxy statement that is not in the file set, so governance can be described only from what the annual reports themselves print. The latest annual report gives both backlog and remaining performance obligations and states that they differ by USD 20.0 billion; the quarterly reports give only the obligations.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_acm_acm-20250930.htm.jsonl, pages 4–16; 10-K_2025-09-30_acm_acm-20250930.htm.jsonl, pages 17–40; 10-K_2025-09-30_acm_acm-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 13; 10-K_2025-09-30_acm_acm-20250930.htm.jsonl, pages 123–125; 10-K_2025-09-30_acm_acm-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 128; 10-K_2024-09-30_acm_acm-20240930x10k.htm.jsonl, page 129; 10-Q_2026-07-03_acm_acm-20260703.htm.jsonl, page 15

The third-quarter report carries two items that bear directly on later credit work: a loss of USD 336.8 million on one Construction Management project, and management's expectation of net cash outflows of USD 600 million to USD 800 million to complete two such projects, of which USD 175 million to USD 225 million falls in the fourth quarter. The filing quantifies these items but gives no later update.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-07-03_acm_acm-20260703.htm.jsonl, page 16; 10-Q_2026-07-03_acm_acm-20260703.htm.jsonl, page 55

The latest filing was signed 99 days before the as-of date, and the as-of date falls 49 days after the fiscal 2026 year-end as presented, the same lag at which the fiscal 2025 annual report was signed. The fiscal 2026 annual report may therefore be close but is not in the file set, and nothing in the file set reports the fourth quarter.
Basis: calculation C1; calculation C2; calculation C3; 10-Q_2026-07-03_acm_acm-20260703.htm.jsonl, page 7

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The best case that the file set is stronger than it looks is that the primary filings state the covenant level, the facility terms and the project exposures in detail, so Financial foundation and Fundamental credit can proceed without the missing documents. The view would improve if the fiscal 2026 annual report, the executed credit agreement or a compliance certificate were supplied, and would weaken if a later filing revised the third-quarter figures or the project loss estimate, which the third-quarter report itself says may change.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-07-03_acm_acm-20260703.htm.jsonl, page 16; 10-Q_2026-07-03_acm_acm-20260703.htm.jsonl, page 21

### Limitations

The assessment rests on the five filings and the run settings only; no outside source was consulted, and no judgement on credit quality is made here.

The quarterly statements are unaudited and omit footnotes that the annual reports carry.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-07-03_acm_acm-20260703.htm.jsonl, page 7
