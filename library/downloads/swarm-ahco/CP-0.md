## Findings

### Bottom line

The seven supplied AdaptHealth documents are enough for the requested credit snapshot, but no single document gives trailing twelve-month earnings on one business perimeter, so creditors should treat any leverage figure as provisional until that basis is fixed. The Diabetes Health business, now being sold, sits inside the 2025 annual figures and the first-quarter 2026 documents but outside the second- and third-quarter 2026 documents, so every comparison across them needs an explicit perimeter label.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_ahco-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 98; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ahco-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 27; EX-99.1_2026-08-04_ahco-20260804x8k_ex991.htm.jsonl, page 3; calculation C4

The executed credit agreement and indentures are not supplied, so covenant levels and reported covenant ratios are not established; the filings say only that the covenants were met at September 30, 2026. No credit rating, market quote, peer set or portfolio input is supplied either, so ratings, market-implied and portfolio work is not supported by this source set.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_ahco-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 41; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ahco-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 42
