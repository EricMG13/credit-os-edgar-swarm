## Findings

### Bottom line

Block carries medium governance and sponsor risk for creditors: there is no financial sponsor to support them, a Class B holder group casting 52.41% of the votes decides stockholder matters, and protection rests on the issuer's own cash and covenants.
Basis: calculation VOTE-20261030; 10-Q_2026-09-30_xyz-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 138; 10-K_2025-12-31_xyz-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 99

The exposure that matters most is Cash App lending, where loan losses rose 167.78% in nine months, while bitcoin supplies 28.33% of revenue but only 2.07% of gross profit. Creditors hold notes of USD 5,775 million whose indentures, as the issuer summarises the 2030, 2032 and 2033 series, limit liens, certain subsidiary debt, sale-leasebacks and asset transfers, and a USD 764 million regulatory accrual has no stated payment date.

### Key findings

Control sits with a Class B holder group and not with a sponsor, and its vote far exceeds its economic stake: Class B shares are 9.92% of shares outstanding but cast 52.41% of the votes, so Class B holders can approve or block any matter put to stockholders. The issuer names Jack Dorsey as the controlling shareholder for the bank regulator's purposes and says the Class B group includes executive officers, employees and directors, but the proxy statement that would name holders and stakes is not supplied. No equity injection, cure, dividend recapitalisation, sponsor fee or related financing appears in any supplied document, so willingness to support creditors has not been tested either way.
Basis: calculation VOTE-20261030; 10-K_2025-12-31_xyz-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 92, 99, 214; 10-Q_2026-09-30_xyz-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 138

Shareholder returns are large but cash-funded and slowing: repurchases were 23.35% of operating cash flow in the first nine months of 2026 against 78.65% a year earlier, no dividend has ever been declared, and no shares were repurchased in the third quarter, with USD 4.6 billion of the USD 9 billion authorization still available. The program is discretionary and can resume at any time, so it is capacity for cash leakage rather than a commitment, and the credit agreement's customary limits on dividends and repurchases are the only brake the supplied documents describe, at a level not printed.
Basis: calculation BBOCF-9M2026; calculation BBOCF-9M2025; calculation BBPROG-20260930; 10-Q_2026-09-30_xyz-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 8, 31, 43, 143; 10-K_2025-12-31_xyz-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 71, 107

Gross profit is concentrated in Cash App and in lending-linked products: Cash App supplied 63.92% of nine-month 2026 gross profit and Square 35.43%, and the Financial solutions line, which carries the lending, supplied 41.39% of gross profit against 36.13% a year earlier. Square gives a steadier base, because no customer exceeded 5% of Square payment volume in 2025, 2024 or 2023, but Square gross profit grew 12.11% against 32.11% for Cash App, so the faster-growing franchise also carries most of the retained loan book, 78.22% of loans held for investment at amortized cost.

Block also holds a long-term bitcoin investment valued at USD 771.9 million whose fair-value changes run through net income, with a loss of USD 31.6 million in the nine months, which adds earnings volatility that is not operating cash.

The compliance record is a clear governance weakness. The board formed a special litigation committee in April 2026 and the derivative actions reached a settlement in principle that includes USD 150 million from insurers, so a response is visible, but it does not remove the cash call, which equals 7.59% of total liquidity at September 30, 2026.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a cautious reading is conduct. Block has never declared a dividend, repaid USD 1,575 million of 2026 maturities in cash without any conversion, issued no new notes in the nine months, kept the USD 900 million revolver undrawn, reports compliance with its financial covenants, and had its Securities and Exchange Commission inquiry closed in March 2026 with no enforcement action recommended. Control by the Class B holders has so far coincided with creditor-neutral or creditor-supportive actions, and the nine-month repurchase pace fell from 78.65% to 23.35% of operating cash flow. The issuer also states ratings of BBB- at Fitch, Ba1 at Moody's and BB+ at S&P for 2025, although no agency document is supplied.
Basis: calculation REPAY-2026; calculation BBOCF-9M2026; calculation BBOCF-9M2025; 10-K_2025-12-31_xyz-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 107; 10-Q_2026-09-30_xyz-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 27–28, 34, 58

The view would turn adverse on a debt-funded distribution or repurchase while the Department of Justice payment is outstanding, a final settlement above the USD 764 million accrued or with operating restrictions, consumer delinquency moving beyond the 4.13% now reported, a leverage covenant level that leaves little headroom once disclosed, a use of the Class B vote to change the capital structure or related-party dealings, or parent cash committed to the bank subsidiary beyond the USD 50 million reserve. It would improve if the proxy statement showed board-level control of capital allocation that is independent of the Class B holders and if the final settlement matched the accrual.

### Limitations

- The 2026 proxy statement is not supplied, so named holders, Class B holdings by holder, director independence, committee membership and executive pay are not established; the control conclusion rests on the issuer's aggregate voting statement and cannot say who would act or how.
- The indentures and the restated credit agreement are not supplied, so limits on shareholder payments, investments and new debt are read only from the issuer's summaries; the conclusion on protection against value extraction is a statement of unknown capacity, not of limited capacity.
- No dates, prices or funding for the Afterpay acquisition are supplied and TIDAL has no acquisition history, so the view on acquisition appetite rests on the absence of any material acquisition in 2025 and 2026 and not on a funding history.
- Customer and activity counts are management's unaudited measures given only for December 2025 and September 2026, so operating durability is judged on reported direction and not on cohort or retention data.
- The Department of Justice resolution is an agreement in principle, so the USD 764 million accrual may change and its effect on liquidity cannot be timed; the governance conclusion on compliance is provisional until the final agreement is signed.
- No market, peer or rating-agency document is supplied, so how lenders price governance and control is not assessed and the stated ratings rest on the issuer's own statement.
