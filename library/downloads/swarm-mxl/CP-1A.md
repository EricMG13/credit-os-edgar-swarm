## Findings

### Bottom line

MaxLinear is a publicly held chip designer with no declared sponsor, so creditors face operating and litigation risk rather than owner extraction; the open exposure is an unquantified Silicon Motion damages claim that could be funded with debt. Sponsor and governance risk is medium because holder percentages, board independence and credit agreement terms are not supplied.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_mxl-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 38, 43; 10-K_2025-12-31_mxl-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 103, 127–128; Source readiness (CP-0)

Revenue grew to USD 524.1 million in the first nine months of 2026, 52% of it from the infrastructure line, but operating cash flow was an outflow of USD 17.7 million as supply commitments reached USD 347.6 million and the revolver was drawn by USD 40.0 million.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_mxl-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 4–7, 19–20, 34–35, 37, 62; calculation CALC-01; calculation CALC-02; calculation CALC-08

### Key findings

Owner-extraction risk is low on the evidence supplied: no sponsor, parent or controlling holder is named, no cash dividend has ever been declared, and the only shareholder distribution in the statements supplied, which start in 2023, was a share repurchase of USD 20.0 million in November and December 2025, in a year when free cash flow after capital spending was an outflow of USD 176 thousand and cash and restricted cash fell by USD 18,191 thousand. No repurchase followed in the first nine months of 2026, and USD 55.0 million of the USD 75.0 million authorisation remains available until November 2028. Whether the credit agreement would allow a larger distribution is not established.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_mxl-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 103–104, 137–138; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mxl-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 44, 65; calculation CALC-07

The terminated Silicon Motion merger is the transaction that matters most to creditors because its cost is still open. MaxLinear says it owed no break-up fee after terminating on July 26, 2023, while Silicon Motion seeks the termination fee plus damages in a confidential Singapore arbitration, and three suits by Silicon Motion stockholders name the company and certain officers. The company has accrued nothing and says no loss can be estimated, and its own risk disclosure warns that a payment could require drawing all lines of credit. An award would compete for the same USD 81.1 million of cash and USD 90.0 million of undrawn revolver that stand behind USD 165.0 million of debt, and the failed deal has already cost a USD 18.3 million financing ticking fee.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_mxl-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 14, 38–44, 57; 10-K_2025-12-31_mxl-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 38, 108; calculation CALC-03; calculation CALC-04

Financial policy in 2026 has favoured liquidity over distributions, at a price. The company made no repurchase and no acquisition outflow in the nine months, settled USD 32,463 thousand of bonuses in shares rather than cash, received USD 8.0 million for an interest in certain claims, and used two credit agreement amendments to push the revolver maturity to March 2028 and lift commitments from USD 100.0 million to USD 200.0 million. The revolver margin over SOFR rose to 2.25% from a range of 1.00% to 1.75%, and the October amendment cut the liquidity covenant minimum from USD 80 million to USD 60 million, a relaxation granted while the revolver was already drawn.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_mxl-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 6–7, 21–24, 44–45, 48; EX-99.1_2026-04-22_a03312026exhibit991.htm.jsonl, page 2; calculation CALC-03

The operating model is concentrated in customers, supply and geography, so one program loss or supply disruption would hit revenue and margin faster than the broad recent growth suggests. The ten largest customers were 56% of nine-month 2026 revenue (65% in 2025), one unnamed supplier provided 54% of inventory purchases (32% in 2025), 85% of revenue shipped to Asia with 50% to Hong Kong, and customers buy on purchase orders with no long-term commitments, while the company places firm orders with suppliers up to 26 weeks ahead, in some cases without a customer purchase order.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_mxl-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 34, 46–47; 10-K_2025-12-31_mxl-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 21–23, 183

Growth is being pre-funded, which makes cash conversion the main near-term operating risk for creditors. Operating cash flow was an outflow of USD 17.7 million in the nine months because working capital absorbed approximately USD 137.6 million, mostly wafer prepayments and inventory; free cash flow after capital spending and intangible purchases was an outflow of USD 32,688 thousand; and supply and software commitments rose from USD 209.6 million to USD 347.6 million, of which USD 311,376 thousand falls due between October 2026 and December 2027 against cash plus undrawn revolver of USD 171,085 thousand. If demand slipped, the commitments and inventory would remain while revenue fell.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_mxl-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 4–7, 37, 58–59, 62; calculation CALC-05; calculation CALC-06; calculation CALC-08

Governance gives adequate reporting but little creditor-specific visibility. The statements carry an unqualified audit opinion with effective internal control, one person holds the chairman and chief executive roles with a lead director named, and the classified board and Delaware takeover provisions reduce the odds of a hostile leveraged transaction. Board independence, committee composition, executive pay and related-party dealings sit in a proxy statement that is not supplied, and the credit agreement is known only through the company's summaries, which describe the financial covenants differently across the 2026 filings, so restricted-payment, investment, debt and lien capacity cannot be tested against behavior.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_mxl-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 87–88, 125, 127–128, 133, 135–136; 10-Q_2026-06-30_mxl-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 22–24; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mxl-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 23–24; EX-99.1_2026-10-22_a09302026exhibit991.htm.jsonl, page 1; Source readiness (CP-0)

Management incentives point to growth and equity value rather than creditor protection. The performance awards described vest on revenue and non-GAAP operating income, the annual report says pay is weighted to equity more heavily than at peers, most bonuses are settled in shares, and shares outstanding rose by 5.26% in nine months, so cash is conserved at the cost of dilution; none of the performance metrics described is tied to leverage or cash flow.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_mxl-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 4–5, 25–28; 10-K_2025-12-31_mxl-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 32; calculation CALC-09

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against caution is the speed and breadth of the recovery and the company's own liquidity. Revenue of USD 168.8 million in the second quarter and USD 218.1 million in the third landed inside the outlooks of USD 160 million to USD 170 million and USD 210 million to USD 220 million that management gave a quarter earlier, the fourth-quarter outlook is USD 240 million to USD 260 million, gross margin was 58.7% in the third quarter, and infrastructure revenue grew 168% in nine months while the broadband line fell 3%. At the balance sheet date cash plus undrawn revolver of USD 171,085 thousand was well above the USD 80.0 million covenant minimum, and still above it by USD 81,085 thousand if the whole USD 10.0 million letter of credit commitment were used; the company's own fair value estimate puts the term loan at 98.72% of par, and no sponsor, dividend recapitalisation or debt-funded acquisition appears in the record.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-04-22_a03312026exhibit991.htm.jsonl, page 2; EX-99.1_2026-07-23_a06302026exhibit991.htm.jsonl, page 1; EX-99.1_2026-10-22_a09302026exhibit991.htm.jsonl, pages 1–2; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mxl-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 20, 22–24, 50–51; calculation CALC-05; calculation CALC-10; calculation CALC-11

The view would move toward lower risk on an executed credit agreement with tight restricted-payment and investment baskets, a proxy statement showing independent directors, dispersed holdings and no related-party dealings, and a Silicon Motion settlement or award that cash on hand could fund. It would move toward higher risk on a damages award or settlement larger than available liquidity, resumed repurchases or a debt-funded acquisition while the revolver is drawn, a further covenant relaxation, the loss of a top customer, a hyperscale capital spending pullback that leaves USD 347.6 million of commitments unsupported, or an inventory write-down.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_mxl-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 37, 43, 59; Source readiness (CP-0)

### Limitations

Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_mxl-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 127–128; Source readiness (CP-0)
- The executed credit agreement and its three amendments are not supplied, only the company's summaries, so capacity for restricted payments, investments, additional debt and liens is not established and willingness to extract cannot be separated from legal capacity.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_mxl-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 21–24; Source readiness (CP-0)
- Silicon Motion's termination fee and damages are not quantified and the arbitration is confidential, so the size of any payment that could be debt-funded is not established and the creditor view can change sharply on an award or settlement.

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

MaxLinear issued no new bond, note or term loan in the period covered. Its funded debt is the senior secured term B loan of USD 125.0 million due June 23, 2028, unchanged since December 2025, and borrowings of USD 40.0 million under the revolving facility of the same June 23, 2021 credit agreement, which was undrawn at the March and June quarter ends. The revolver was drawn in the third quarter, after USD 60.0 million of borrowing and USD 20.0 million of repayment in the nine months.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_mxl-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 6, 19–21; 10-Q_2026-06-30_mxl-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 6, 23; calculation CALC-04

The credit agreement was amended twice in 2026. On April 22, 2026 the revolver maturity moved from June 23, 2026 to March 23, 2028, commitments rose by USD 30.0 million to USD 130.0 million, a USD 10.0 million letter of credit commitment was added, the SOFR margin on revolver loans became 2.25% and the financial covenants became a total net leverage ratio of at most 3.50x and unrestricted cash plus undrawn commitments of at least USD 80.0 million. On October 16, 2026, after the balance sheet date, commitments rose by USD 70 million to USD 200 million and the liquidity minimum fell to USD 60 million. The effect of the October amendment on cash and borrowings is not established, because no borrowing under the new commitments is stated.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_mxl-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 22–24, 44–45, 48; EX-99.1_2026-04-22_a03312026exhibit991.htm.jsonl, page 2; calculation CALC-03

Two financing-type items sit outside the debt figure: USD 8.0 million received in 2026 for selling an interest in certain claims, carried as a long-term liability until the claims are resolved, and USD 15.0 million of jointly funded research and development receipts deferred because repayment conditions are unresolved. Neither is included in the USD 165.0 million of debt principal.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_mxl-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 24, 38, 59; calculation CALC-04

No acquisition is pending. The terminated Silicon Motion merger was to be financed under commitment letters with Wells Fargo and other lenders that lapsed on termination, at a cost of a USD 18.3 million ticking fee paid in August 2023, and the committed amount is not stated in the supplied documents.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_mxl-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 108, 131, 158

At the as-of date the revolver and the term loan mature in about 16 months and about 19 months.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_mxl-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 23, 44, 65; calculation CALC-12
