## Findings

### Bottom line

The eleven supplied documents support a current credit snapshot of Sensata through the third quarter of 2026, but creditors cannot test their own protections because no executed credit agreement or indenture was supplied. Covenant, guarantee and security terms rest on the issuer's own summary in the 2025 annual report.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_st.pdf, pages 85–90; 10-K_2025-12-31_st.pdf, pages 110–113

The latest documents are dated 2026-10-27, 22 days before the as-of date, and together they print the statements, debt tables, revolver availability, agency ratings and Adjusted EBITDA the snapshot needs. Financial foundation and Fundamental credit can run on this evidence. Business & governance can run, but ownership and board evidence is thin because the proxy statement was not supplied. Recovery analysis and portfolio fit are not supported, and market pricing is limited to quarter-end fair values of the notes.
