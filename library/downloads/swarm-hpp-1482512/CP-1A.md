## Findings

### Bottom line

Hudson Pacific is a widely held Class-A office and studio landlord with no controlling owner identified in the filings, so unsecured creditors depend on leasing recovery and asset sales, not on owner support, to meet near-term maturities.

Office space was 85.6% occupied at 2026-09-30 on a smaller in-service portfolio, 32.3% of office annualized base rent expires in 2027 and 2028, and four tenants provide 28.6% of it. Management has suspended common dividends, raised equity in 2025 and sold properties to retire debt, which is creditor-aligned, but the filings give no board or shareholder detail beyond signatures and cover pages.
Basis: calculation K09; calculation K08; 10-Q_2026-09-30_hpp-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 51, 54, 58

### Key findings

Control appears dispersed, which removes sponsor extraction risk and also any sponsor backstop. The charter caps any holder at 9.8% of the shares unless the board exempts it, 75 holders of record were reported in February 2026, and the company owns about 96.7% of the operating partnership that issues the debt, the rest being held by executives, directors and affiliates. Creditors therefore cannot count on an equity cure from an owner; the equity cushion is the market value of the stock and the USD 425.0 million Series C preferred, which ranks ahead of the common and became redeemable by the company on 2026-11-16. Total debt was 73.8% of total market capitalization at 2026-09-30.
Basis: calculation K21; calculation K23; 10-K_2025-12-31_hpp-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 45, 70; 10-Q_2026-09-30_hpp-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 3, 42, 74

Leasing risk is concentrated in a few years and a few tenants. Office annualized base rent is USD 460,366,732 on HPP's share, of which 12.4% expires in 2027 and 19.9% in 2028, including the Google expirations in 2028 and 2029, and the four largest tenants provide 28.6%. Vacancy at 2026-09-30 included most of Skyport Plaza, which was sold on 2026-10-15. The announced wind-down of Weil, Gotshal & Manges on 2026-10-28 puts a lease running to 2038, about 0.8% of rent on the company's basis, at risk of non-payment.
Basis: calculation K09; calculation K08; calculation K20; 10-Q_2026-09-30_hpp-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 47, 54–55, 58–59

The operating model is an office business with a small, volatile studio segment. Office provided 81.74% of revenue and studio 18.26% in nine months 2026, but studio provided only 2.98% of segment profit, and the studio segment lost money in FY2025. Same-store office occupancy rose by 9.8 percentage points to 84.9% between the third quarters of 2025 and 2026 and same-store NOI rose 8.45%, so the recovery is real where it is measured, while total NOI fell 0.3% because asset sales and the expiry of the PayPal lease at Fourth and Traction removed income.
Basis: calculation K01; calculation K02; calculation K07; calculation K18; calculation K19; 10-Q_2026-09-30_hpp-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 44–45, 62–63

Capital allocation is creditor-aligned on the evidence but has shifted risk toward unsecured holders. The company suspended common dividends, raised gross equity proceeds of USD 689.3 million in June 2025, sold properties for USD 247.0 million in FY2025 and USD 90.5 million in nine months 2026, and paid down debt. Over the same period it repaid unsecured Series B, C and D notes of USD 465.0 million and raised CMBS loans on the Office Portfolio and 1918 Eighth properties, so secured debt rose from 41.85% of unsecured and secured principal at 2024-12-31 to 51.01% at 2025-12-31 and 50.90% at 2026-09-30, which narrows the unencumbered pool behind the unsecured notes.
Basis: calculation K10; calculation K11; calculation K12; calculation K13; calculation K14; calculation K26; 10-K_2025-12-31_hpp-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 76–77, 98; 10-Q_2026-09-30_hpp-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 26, 41, 56

Contingent exposure sits in the joint ventures. The company has given recourse carve-out guarantees of USD 190.5 million on three unconsolidated venture loans and states the likelihood of loss as remote, but the Bentall Centre and Sunset Glenoaks Studios loans mature in 2027 and the Sunset Pier 94 Studios loan in 2028, so ventures that cannot refinance will test partner and lender support. The completion guarantee for Sunset Pier 94 Studios was terminated in October 2026.
Basis: calculation K16; 10-Q_2026-09-30_hpp-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 23, 75

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a cautious view is the same-store recovery and leasing volume: the company leased 39.78% more square feet in nine months 2026 than a year earlier, same-store office occupancy and NOI rose, general and administrative expense fell 38.44% and the operating partnership reports no event of default. No controlling owner or sponsor has extracted value, and the company has used equity and sales, not creditors, to fund deleveraging.
Basis: calculation K17; calculation K18; calculation K19; calculation K22; 10-Q_2026-09-30_hpp-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 28, 59, 62–63

That view would win if same-store gains persist while the 2027 and 2028 expiries roll at current rents, the December 2026 revolver tranche is replaced and the Hollywood Media Portfolio loan is refinanced in 2027. It would lose if the Google, Weil or other large expiries are not backfilled, if sales continue to shrink the portfolio faster than leasing improves it, or if a venture loan cannot be refinanced and the guarantees are called.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_hpp-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 27–28, 54–55, 58

  Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_hpp-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 45, 70, 109

  Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_hpp-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 51–53, 62; 10-K_2025-12-31_hpp-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 89

  Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_hpp-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 23

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

In FY2025 the issuer refinanced, repaid and shrank. It obtained the Office Portfolio CMBS of USD 475.0 million in March 2025 at SOFR plus 4.15%, used the proceeds to repay USD 259.0 million drawn on the revolver and the USD 168.0 million Element LA loan, and prepaid USD 206.3 million of it after selling Element LA in the fourth quarter. It refinanced 1918 Eighth with a USD 285.0 million CMBS loan at 6.16% maturing 2030-09-11, repaid its Series B, C and D notes of USD 465.0 million, amended the revolver twice, and raised USD 689.3 million of gross equity in June 2025.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_hpp-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 76–77, 98, 166–167

In 2026 the issuer amended the Hollywood Media Portfolio CMBS to mature on 2027-11-09 with a swap fixing SOFR at 3.50% and a USD 20.0 million leasing reserve, sold 2001 Gateway Place and 875/899 Howard for USD 90.5 million, and drew nothing on the revolver through nine months. After the quarter end it retired USD 100.0 million each of its 2027 and 2028 senior notes by cash tender on 2026-10-14, sold Skyport Plaza and an Albuquerque studio for USD 84.9 million gross on 2026-10-15, and had USD 30.0 million drawn on the revolver at 2026-11-06. No new registered notes were issued in the period covered, and nothing after 2026-11-06 is supplied.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_hpp-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 27–28, 47, 56, 60; calculation K14; calculation K15
