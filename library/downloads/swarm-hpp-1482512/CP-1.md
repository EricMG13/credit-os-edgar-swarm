## Findings

### Bottom line

Hudson Pacific's net leverage is 9.27x last-twelve-months EBITDA but about 11.61x on the annualised third-quarter 2026 run-rate, so creditors face the 2027 maturity wall with thinner earnings than the trailing figure suggests.

The trailing figure includes fourth-quarter 2025 EBITDA of USD 140.57 million, about twice a 2026 quarter, which the Element LA lease termination fee tied to its sale probably lifted. Net leverage fell from 13.55x at the end of FY2024 mainly because debt was repaid from the June 2025 equity raise and property sales rather than from EBITDA growth; cash interest cover is 2.60x.
Basis: calculation C31; calculation C35; calculation C13; calculation C29; calculation C34

### Key findings

Net leverage on reported EBITDA fell from 13.55x at the end of FY2024 to 9.92x at the end of FY2025 and 9.27x on the trailing basis at 2026-09-30. Total debt at carrying value, joint venture partner debt included, fell from USD 4,242.98 million to USD 3,417.59 million in FY2025, a fall of USD 825.39 million, and was USD 3,413.60 million at 2026-09-30. The FY2025 repayment came from the equity raise and sales, not from earnings: net debt repayment was USD 719.22 million against net equity proceeds of USD 661.84 million, and EBITDA rose only from USD 308.55 million to USD 330.41 million. Debt carrying value barely moved in 2026, so further deleveraging has to come from earnings or sales.
Basis: calculation C29; calculation C30; calculation C31; calculation C70; calculation C61; calculation C62; calculation C71; 10-K_2025-12-31_hpp-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 98, 124–125, 128–129

Fourth-quarter 2025 EBITDA of USD 140.57 million, derived as FY2025 less nine months 2025, compares with USD 67.75 million, USD 72.62 million and USD 70.07 million in the first three quarters of 2026, an excess of USD 70.50 million over the latest quarter. The filing says termination fee income was received at Element LA in connection with its sale in the fourth quarter of 2025 and cites other termination fees, but states none of the amounts, so the quarter's excess cannot be sized exactly. Annualising the third quarter gives EBITDA of USD 280.30 million, net leverage of 11.61x and cash interest cover of 2.07x, which is the better guide to the run-rate that creditors are actually lending against. EBITDA is calculated from the statements because the issuer states none.
Basis: calculation C13; calculation C03; calculation C04; calculation C05; calculation C72; calculation C15; calculation C35; calculation C36; 10-K_2025-12-31_hpp-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 76, 93, 101

Cash interest paid, net of capitalized interest, fell from USD 170.98 million in FY2024 to USD 164.02 million in FY2025 and USD 135.17 million on the trailing basis, so cover rose from 1.80x to 2.01x and 2.60x. Cash interest paid fell by USD 28.86 million between nine months 2025 and nine months 2026, more than the USD 22.04 million fall in gross interest expense, so part of the cash saving may be timing, and capitalized interest fell by USD 13.04 million as development projects were completed, which pushes more interest into expense. The issuer's own covenant cover, adjusted EBITDA to fixed charges, is 1.7x against a minimum of 1.5x, on a definition the filings do not print.
Basis: calculation C32; calculation C33; calculation C34; calculation C73; calculation C74; 10-Q_2026-09-30_hpp-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 29–30, 47

Impairment charges were USD 149.66 million in FY2024, USD 299.32 million in FY2025 and USD 198.27 million in nine months 2026, including USD 147.83 million in the third quarter on the Hill7 and Skyport Plaza office properties after their expected holding periods were shortened. They sit below the operating line, so EBITDA does not fall, but they show asset values dropping: total equity fell by USD 382.09 million from the end of FY2025 to 2026-09-30, and a net-loss-based EBITDA would be negative USD 45.39 million for FY2025, which would make leverage meaningless.
Basis: calculation C69; calculation C58; 10-Q_2026-09-30_hpp-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 7, 65; 10-K_2025-12-31_hpp-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 125

Operating cash flow of USD 77.65 million in nine months 2026 did not cover additions to real estate of USD 83.57 million and to other property of USD 5.81 million, so free cash flow was negative USD 11.74 million before preferred dividends of USD 15.27 million, and negative USD 27.01 million after them. Cash still rose from USD 138.36 million to USD 160.53 million, helped by USD 80.87 million of real estate sale proceeds and USD 15.33 million from selling equipment as parts of the Quixote production services business are wound down. In nine months 2026 the company funded its capital spending and preferred dividends from asset sales rather than from operations.
Basis: calculation C45; calculation C47; calculation C68; 10-Q_2026-09-30_hpp-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 9–11, 75

The issuer's own fair value estimate, a model value on prevailing rates for similar instruments rather than a quote, puts the unsecured notes at USD 1,528.54 million against principal of USD 1,650.00 million, or 92.64% of par, and secured debt at 99.55% of principal, so the unsecured creditors carry the discount. The 2027 maturities are USD 1,479.77 million, or 43.19% of contractual principal of USD 3,426.49 million, made up of the 3.95% notes due 2027 and the Hollywood Media Portfolio CMBS. Revolver commitments of USD 333.3 million mature on 2026-12-21, so the undrawn USD 795.25 million shown at 2026-09-30 falls to USD 462.0 million before any new borrowing; at 2026-11-06 USD 30.0 million was drawn. After the balance-sheet date the issuer repurchased USD 100.0 million each of the 2027 and 2028 notes on 2026-10-14 with cash and revolver borrowing, at a price the filing does not state, and sold two properties on 2026-10-15 for USD 84.90 million gross, which would lower net debt of USD 3,253.06 million to USD 3,168.16 million.
Basis: calculation C48; calculation C51; calculation C56; calculation C40; calculation C77; calculation C78; 10-Q_2026-09-30_hpp-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 27–28, 35, 47

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest case that credit quality is improving is that EBITDA for the third quarter of 2026 of USD 70.07 million was 1.63% above the third quarter of 2025, nothing was drawn on the revolver through nine months 2026, and the operating partnership reports compliance with every financial covenant. Occupied in-service office space was 85.6% at 2026-09-30 against 76.3% at 2025-12-31, but the in-service office perimeter fell from 39 to 35 properties as properties were sold or moved to repositioning and held for sale, so that rise is not like for like. Revenue is still falling: it was 4.18% lower in nine months 2026 than a year earlier.
Basis: calculation C60; calculation C63; 10-Q_2026-09-30_hpp-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 28–29, 51; 10-K_2025-12-31_hpp-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 89

The view would improve if quarterly EBITDA stayed above USD 70.07 million while the 2026-12-21 revolver tranche is extended or replaced and the 2027 maturities are refinanced without a rise in cash interest. It would worsen if EBITDA reverts toward the pre-termination level, if the December 2026 tranche is not replaced, or if the 3.95% notes and the Hollywood Media Portfolio CMBS due in 2027 meet a closed refinancing market for office-weighted collateral. The Hill7 CMBS also starts to accrue a further 2.00% a year, added to principal, from 2026-11-06.
Basis: calculation C15; 10-Q_2026-09-30_hpp-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 27–28

### Limitations

Every leverage and cover figure rests on EBITDA calculated from the statements as revenue less operating expenses plus depreciation and amortization, because the issuer states no EBITDA and no adjusted EBITDA. Interim statements are unaudited, the fourth quarter of 2025 is derived as the year less nine months, and the termination fee income inside it is not quantified in the filings.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_hpp-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 6–7; 10-K_2025-12-31_hpp-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 93, 125

The executed credit agreement and indentures are not supplied, so covenant definitions, the rank and guarantee terms of each instrument and any intercreditor terms are taken from the filing summaries. Debt held by unconsolidated ventures, letters of credit and the use of the October 2026 sale proceeds are outside the debt figures, and no filing after 2026-11-06 is available.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_hpp-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 27–29, 46–47, 74–75
