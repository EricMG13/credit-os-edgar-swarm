## Findings

### Bottom line

Earnings no longer cover the debt: reported EBITDA for the twelve months to 2026-09-30 is negative USD 100.36 million, so leverage cannot be stated and creditors depend on liquidity of USD 277.0 million against net debt of USD 470.97 million.
Basis: calculation C03; calculation C01; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 54; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 62

Reported EBITDA of USD 366.24 million for FY2025 is inflated by a USD 418.6 million litigation gain and would be negative USD 52.36 million without it, so the reported leverage of 1.21x for that year is not a usable signal. Net debt is USD 470.97 million, against USD 281.66 million at FY2024.
Basis: calculation C05; calculation C04; 10-K_2025-12-31_wnc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 125

### Key findings

Underlying EBITDA has fallen in each year since FY2023. Reported EBITDA was USD 357.27 million in FY2023, negative USD 299.12 million in FY2024 and USD 366.24 million in FY2025, but FY2024 carries a USD 450 million product liability charge and FY2025 a USD 418.6 million gain on the same matter. Excluding both items, EBITDA falls from USD 357.27 million to USD 150.88 million to negative USD 52.36 million, as net sales fall from USD 2,536.50 million to USD 1,946.74 million to USD 1,542.75 million.
Basis: calculation C02; calculation C05; 10-K_2025-12-31_wnc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 96; 10-K_2025-12-31_wnc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 125

The loss is narrowing from a low base. Reported EBITDA was negative USD 44.92 million in the fourth quarter of 2025, negative USD 37.33 million in Q1 2026, negative USD 11.69 million in Q2 2026 and negative USD 6.42 million in Q3 2026, so the trend is better but the latest quarter is still below zero. The twelve month figure of negative USD 100.36 million still carries the weak fourth quarter of 2025.
Basis: calculation C02; calculation C03; calculation C08; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 40

Cash flow, not only earnings, is draining the balance sheet. Operating cash flow was negative USD 65.82 million for the nine months to 2026-09-30 even after payables and accruals rose USD 99.11 million, and capital spending of USD 9.19 million was small; USD 39.72 million of asset sale proceeds and the convertible notes bridged the gap. Cash interest of USD 22.82 million over the twelve months is a fixed claim on that same cash.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_wnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 4; calculation C03; calculation C12

Debt at carrying value is USD 540.26 million, USD 9.74 million below principal of USD 550.0 million, and consists of USD 400.0 million of 4.50% unsecured senior notes due 2028, USD 150.0 million of 4.00% convertible notes due 2032 issued in July 2026 and an undrawn revolver. Net debt rose USD 60.04 million in the nine months because cash burn, fees and dividends exceeded asset sale proceeds; the convertible notes repaid revolver borrowings and changed the debt mix more than the net total.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_wnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 17; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 21–22; calculation C01; calculation C07; calculation C13

Maturities are back-ended but the revolver springs. No principal falls due before 2028-10-15, when USD 400.0 million of senior notes mature; the revolver was cut from USD 350.0 million to USD 300.0 million and extended to 2031 in August 2026, but it matures 91 days before the senior notes or the convertible notes mature, so the 2028 refinancing governs access to the only committed liquidity.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_wnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 18–20; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 54

Liquidity of USD 277.0 million is the issuer's cash plus available revolver capacity, of which USD 69.29 million is cash; it was USD 165.1 million at 2026-03-31 and USD 235.3 million at FY2025, and rose after the convertible notes repaid revolver borrowings rather than because operations produced cash.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-03-31_wnc-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 43; 10-K_2025-12-31_wnc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 85; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 54; calculation C06

Funding that is not debt is mixed. Customer deposits fell from USD 26.45 million at FY2025 to USD 17.99 million and chassis converter pool financing fell from USD 59.60 million to USD 21.73 million, both inside other accrued liabilities, while accounts payable rose from USD 145.74 million to USD 246.18 million, so suppliers are funding more of the business than customers are.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_wnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 28; calculation C14

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence for a turn is order intake and a narrowing loss: total backlog is USD 1,198 million at 2026-09-30 against USD 705 million at FY2025, trailer shipments for the nine months rose year on year, and quarterly EBITDA losses have narrowed for three consecutive quarters. That evidence would win if quarterly EBITDA turns positive while the backlog converts at stable prices, and it would lose if the cost base and tariffs keep gross margin thin or if the early orders prove to be pull-forward ahead of price increases, which the issuer itself notes. Two quarters of positive reported EBITDA would justify a leverage reading; a further fall in liquidity would confirm the negative one.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_wnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 66–67; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 49; calculation C02

### Limitations

- Reported EBITDA is derived from statement lines because the five filings state no EBITDA or non-GAAP reconciliation; impairments and asset sale gains stay in it, and the issuer's own fixed charge coverage definition cannot be rebuilt.
  Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_wnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 2–3; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 21
- The 2025 product liability gain is disclosed for the year but not by quarter, so the twelve month windows ending 2026-03-31 and 2026-06-30, which still hold part of it, cannot be normalised exactly.
  Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_wnc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 125; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 47
- Interim figures are unaudited, and the 2025 interim comparatives come from the 2026 reports because the 2025 quarterly reports were not supplied.
  Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_wnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 2–3
