## Findings

### Bottom line

Leverage fell to 2.07x total debt and 1.32x net debt over EBITDA in fiscal 2026, but free cash flow was negative and debt rose in the latest quarter, so creditors depend on EBITDA growth, not cash generation, to hold leverage down. The EBITDA is derived from statement lines because the issuer states none, and it includes net one-off gains of USD 70.34 million.
Basis: 10-K_2026-06-30_iivi-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 86, 88–89, 104; 10-Q_2026-09-30_iivi-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 3–5, 10; calculation C01; calculation C02; calculation C03; calculation C06; calculation C10

Cash and the undrawn revolver total USD 2.22 billion at 2026-09-30 against vendor purchase commitments of USD 11.8 billion, and USD 0.80 billion of the debt is foreign facilities, most of it drawn this quarter, that the company and its other subsidiaries do not guarantee.

### Financial interpretation

The leverage fall in fiscal 2026 came from earnings and equity, not from operating cash. Derived EBITDA grew by 75.07% to USD 1.56 billion while total debt fell by USD 0.46 billion: the issuer made USD 509 million of term loan payments, USD 502 million of them voluntary, while raising USD 2.00 billion from a placement of common shares. Leverage was 4.14x total debt and 3.12x net debt in fiscal 2025. In the quarter to 2026-09-30 total debt rose by USD 0.78 billion to USD 4.00 billion as foreign facilities were drawn, so leverage on last-twelve-months EBITDA was 2.33x total debt and 1.43x net debt.
Basis: 10-K_2026-06-30_iivi-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 78, 88–89, 104; 10-Q_2026-09-30_iivi-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 3–5, 10; calculation C01; calculation C03; calculation C04

Interest cover improved to 8.24x in fiscal 2026 from 3.47x because derived EBITDA rose while cash interest paid fell from USD 256.70 million to USD 189.26 million; on the twelve months to 2026-09-30 it was 9.65x. After capital spending the cover is lower, 2.41x in fiscal 2026, which shows how much of the earnings the build-out absorbs before tax and working capital.
Basis: 10-K_2026-06-30_iivi-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 88–89; 10-Q_2026-09-30_iivi-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 4–5; calculation C03; calculation C04
