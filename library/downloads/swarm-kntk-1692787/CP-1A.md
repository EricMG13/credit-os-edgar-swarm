## Findings

### Bottom line
Kinetik is a concentrated Permian midstream business whose creditors rely on a few producers and on equity payouts that currently exceed free cash flow, so sponsor and governance risk is medium, not low.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_kntk-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 8; 10-K_2025-12-31_kntk-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 44; 10-K_2025-12-31_kntk-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 104; calculation K1; calculation K15

Three customers produced 61.80% of 2025 revenue, and contractually committed revenue of USD 418.80 million is about 1.05x one year of service revenue, so volumes depend on producer drilling more than on contracts. In the nine months to 2026-09-30, cash distributions of USD 394.89 million exceeded free cash flow of USD 266.62 million, and net borrowing of USD 131.80 million covered the gap.

### Key findings

Revenue concentration is high and contractual protection is thin, so creditor cash flow follows a few producers' drilling decisions. Three customers each exceeded 10% of revenue and together produced 61.80% of 2025 revenue, against 71.27% in 2024 and 57.04% in 2023, and the largest alone produced 26.56%. Contractually committed revenue at fixed price and volume is USD 418.80 million at 2026-09-30, about 1.05x one year of last-twelve-months service revenue of USD 398.82 million, and minimum volume deficiency payments were only USD 4.6 million in the nine months. The customers are not named, so their credit quality is not established.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_kntk-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 104; 10-K_2024-12-31_kntk-20241231.htm.jsonl, page 111; 10-Q_2026-09-30_kntk-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 13; calculation K1; calculation K2; calculation K3; calculation K4; calculation K10

Reported service revenue understates the fee base, because part of the fee revenue is now booked inside cost of sales, so the apparent move toward commodity-linked revenue is overstated. In the nine months to 2026-09-30 service revenue fell 13.57% and product revenue rose 27.43%, but fee revenue booked inside cost of sales rose USD 89.90 million; counting it, fee revenue grew 7.61%. Product sales are still 80.15% of revenue and carry commodity price and hedging exposure, with hedging losses of USD 49.90 million against a gain of USD 13.40 million a year earlier.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_kntk-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 13; 10-Q_2026-09-30_kntk-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 37; 10-Q_2026-09-30_kntk-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 40; calculation K7; calculation K8; calculation K9

A quarter of adjusted EBITDA comes from pipelines that Kinetik owns only in part and does not operate, so that cash reaches creditors as distributions rather than under Kinetik's control. Proportionate EBITDA of the equity-method pipelines was 26.23% of adjusted EBITDA in the nine months to 2026-09-30, down from 35.80% a year earlier after the 2025 sale of the EPIC interest, while the Midstream Logistics segment grew its adjusted EBITDA 26.90% and the Pipeline Transportation segment's fell 16.29%.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_kntk-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 30–32; 10-K_2025-12-31_kntk-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 9–10; 10-K_2025-12-31_kntk-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 13; calculation K12; calculation K13; calculation K14

Control rests with two large holders, and no conduct adverse to creditors is documented, which supports a medium governance risk rather than a high one. Entities controlled by Blackstone and I Squared Capital held a majority of the voting shares when the 2025 annual report was filed, and each may designate directors while it holds at least 10% of the common stock; their current holdings and any fees are not stated. The only related-party flow disclosed is pipeline capacity and demand fees of USD 28.55 million in 2025, after the former related party Apache sold its last shares by 2024-03-18.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_kntk-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 8; 10-K_2025-12-31_kntk-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 44; 10-K_2025-12-31_kntk-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 144; calculation K23

Equity payouts have run ahead of free cash flow and borrowing or asset sales have covered the difference, which is the main way ownership decisions reach creditors. In the nine months to 2026-09-30, cash distributions were USD 394.89 million against free cash flow of USD 266.62 million, so coverage was 0.68x, up from 0.28x a year earlier, and net borrowing under the revolver and the receivables facility was USD 131.80 million. In 2025, distributions and share repurchases of USD 676.06 million exceeded free cash flow of USD 74.45 million, alongside USD 504.18 million of proceeds from selling the EPIC interest; no shares were repurchased in the nine months to 2026-09-30.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_kntk-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 7–8; 10-Q_2026-09-30_kntk-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 20; 10-K_2025-12-31_kntk-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 96–97; calculation K15; calculation K16; calculation K17; calculation K19

Acquisitions have been funded partly with units, and unit holders can redeem into Class A shares at no cash cost to the Partnership, so economic ownership is moving from units into Class A shares without new debt. About 45.56% of the USD 785.67 million Durango price was cash and 53.87% was Common Units and deferred Class C shares, and redemptions raised the parent's share of Class A and Class C shares from 39.64% at 2025-12-31 to 49.55% at 2026-09-30; the identities of the redeeming holders are not stated.
Basis: 10-K_2024-12-31_kntk-20241231.htm.jsonl, page 102; 10-K_2024-12-31_kntk-20241231.htm.jsonl, page 104; 10-Q_2026-09-30_kntk-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 7; 10-Q_2026-09-30_kntk-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 20; calculation K20; calculation K21

A contract dispute with Energy Transfer is partly settled and partly open, so event risk around the NGL purchase agreements remains until the court rules. In October 2026 the parties confirmed that the term of one NGL purchase agreement ends on 2026-12-31, while two lawsuits over two long-term NGL purchase agreements remained pending after a bench trial in March and April 2026; accrued reserves are immaterial, and the filing does not say whether the settlement ends the lawsuits or what the earnings effect of the term ending is.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_kntk-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 27

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a medium reading is operating and financial improvement. Adjusted EBITDA of the Midstream Logistics segment rose 26.90% in the nine months to 2026-09-30, free cash flow rose to USD 266.62 million from USD 104.80 million a year earlier, and no shares were repurchased in the nine months to 2026-09-30. Kinetik typically takes title to the gas and products it processes and sells them to downstream buyers it describes as large investment grade entities, deducting its fees before remitting the balance, which on its account limits credit risk from producers. Unit redemptions cost no cash, and the filings disclose no priming, drop-down, exchange or sponsor fee transactions.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_kntk-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 31–32; 10-Q_2026-09-30_kntk-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 7–8; 10-Q_2026-09-30_kntk-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 20; 10-K_2025-12-31_kntk-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 104; calculation K14; calculation K15; calculation K16

The view would move to low if distributions were covered in full by free cash flow for several consecutive quarters, if sponsor holdings and any fees were disclosed with no leakage, and if committed revenue grew. It would move to high if repurchases resumed with borrowed money, if a sponsor or related-party payment appeared, if one of the three largest customers cut volumes, if the court ruled against Kinetik on the NGL agreements, or if the debt documents showed room to move assets away from creditors.

### Limitations

- The sponsors' current holdings, fund terms and any fees are not stated in the supplied filings, and the 2026 unit redemptions are not matched to named holders, so the medium sponsor and governance level could move either way once voting power and sponsor economics are known.
- The stockholders agreement, indentures and credit agreements were not supplied, so what Kinetik is permitted to pay out, as distinct from what it has paid, is not established; the payout conclusion rests on cash actually paid.
- Customers are unnamed and, apart from a range of remaining terms on contracts acquired in business combinations, their contract tenors, pricing and volume levels are not given, so the concentration conclusion cannot say how fast a top-three customer's revenue could be lost or replaced.
- Fee revenue booked inside cost of sales is stated only to one decimal in millions of dollars, so the underlying fee growth in the revenue conclusion is approximate.
- The outcomes of the Energy Transfer lawsuits and of the Kings Landing and EPIC earnouts are not stated, so the event-risk and cash-inflow conclusions exclude them.

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

Kinetik has no pending acquisition, merger or sponsor transaction in the supplied filings; its history is a series of completed purchases and sales. The Durango Acquisition closed on 2024-06-24 at an adjusted purchase price of about USD 785.67 million, made up of cash of USD 357.97 million, Common Units of USD 148.20 million, deferred Class C consideration of USD 275.00 million and contingent consideration of USD 4.50 million. The Barilla Draw Acquisition closed on 2025-01-14 for USD 175.50 million in cash. Kinetik sold its 16% interest in GCX on 2024-06-04 for an adjusted price of USD 524.40 million, and sold its 27.5% interest in EPIC on 2025-10-31 for USD 504.20 million upfront plus a possible earnout of about USD 96.00 million.

On 2025-05-30 a USD 1.15 billion term loan maturing in 2028 and a USD 1.60 billion revolving facility maturing in 2030 replaced the 2022 facilities.
