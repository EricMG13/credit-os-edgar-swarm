## Findings

### Bottom line

Governance risk to creditors is medium and comes from financial policy rather than an owner: the company is widely held with no sponsor or controlling holder named, but fiscal 2025 shareholder payouts exceeded free cash flow in a year of net new debt financing.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_enr.htm.jsonl, page 52; 10-K_2025-09-30_enr.htm.jsonl, page 46; 10-K_2025-09-30_enr.htm.jsonl, page 76; 10-K_2025-09-30_enr.htm.jsonl, page 101; calculation C03; calculation C08

Payouts of USD 176.8 million were 2.80 times free cash flow of USD 63.2 million in fiscal 2025, but in the nine months to 2026-06-30 payouts of USD 69.9 million were 0.67 times free cash flow of USD 103.9 million while the company repaid a net USD 83.5 million of debt. Project Momentum, now in a fourth year, has raised its restructuring cost range to USD 65.0 million to USD 75.0 million, so margin recovery is still being paid for. Ownership percentages, board detail and the executed financing documents are not supplied, so the risk level is provisional.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_enr.htm.jsonl, pages 3–4; 10-Q_2026-06-30_enr.htm.jsonl, page 10; 10-Q_2026-06-30_enr.htm.jsonl, page 48; calculation C03; calculation C08

### Key findings

The business is a mature, seasonal battery franchise whose growth was bought with price. Batteries were 76.11% of fiscal 2025 net sales and North America 55.88%, one retailer supplied 12.8% of sales, and fiscal 2025 organic growth of 0.7% combined volume growth of 1.5% with pricing declines of 2.2%. In the nine months to 2026-06-30 organic sales fell 2.3%, so earnings depend on cost savings rather than growth.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_enr.htm.jsonl, page 6; 10-K_2025-09-30_enr.htm.jsonl, pages 65–66; 10-K_2025-09-30_enr.htm.jsonl, page 113; 10-Q_2026-06-30_enr.htm.jsonl, page 44; calculation C01; calculation C02

Margins are the central operating risk. Segment profit fell 9.46% to USD 425.7 million in the nine months to 2026-06-30, with Auto Care down 26.82% and Batteries & Lights down 5.92%, and gross margin was 36.9% reported and 39.2% adjusted against 43.8% and 41.9% a year earlier, driven by unfavourable mix and production inefficiencies and cushioned by a USD 64.1 million tariff refund benefit that may not repeat. Lower segment profit shrinks the cash available to service debt.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_enr.htm.jsonl, pages 12–13; 10-Q_2026-06-30_enr.htm.jsonl, page 27; 10-Q_2026-06-30_enr.htm.jsonl, page 41; 10-Q_2026-06-30_enr.htm.jsonl, pages 44–46; calculation C06

Restructuring has become a recurring charge, not a one-off. Project Momentum cost USD 59.7 million, USD 91.7 million and USD 68.7 million in fiscal 2023, 2024 and 2025 and USD 76.8 million in the nine months to 2026-06-30, and its year-four range rose from a band of USD 35.0 million to USD 40.0 million to a band of USD 65.0 million to USD 75.0 million. Management reports about USD 206 million of savings, which is management language and not independently evidenced, so adjusted earnings that add these costs back depend on a programme that keeps recurring.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_enr.htm.jsonl, pages 60–61; 10-Q_2026-06-30_enr.htm.jsonl, page 10; calculation C05

Ownership is public and dispersed, so the creditor question is the Board's financial policy, not a sponsor's. No sponsor, controlling holder, owner fee or related-party leakage appears in the supplied pages, nine directors sign the annual report including an independent chairman, shares outstanding fell 4.85% between the November 2024 and November 2025 cover dates, and takeover protections make a leveraged change of control less likely. The absence of evidence is not evidence of absence, because the proxy statement that carries ownership and pay detail is not supplied.
Basis: 10-K_2024-09-30_enr.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-K_2025-09-30_enr.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-K_2025-09-30_enr.htm.jsonl, pages 45–46; 10-K_2025-09-30_enr.htm.jsonl, page 52; 10-K_2025-09-30_enr.htm.jsonl, page 162; calculation C04

Capital allocation swung from shareholder-heavy in fiscal 2025 to deleveraging in fiscal 2026. Payouts exceeded free cash flow by USD 113.6 million in fiscal 2025, a year of USD 155.9 million of net new debt financing, whereas in the nine months to 2026-06-30 payouts were 0.67 times free cash flow, USD 90.0 million of term loan was prepaid and net debt financing was a repayment of USD 83.5 million. Acquisitions stayed small at USD 44.8 million across three bolt-ons, so they add integration risk rather than leverage.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_enr.htm.jsonl, page 76; 10-K_2025-09-30_enr.htm.jsonl, page 101; 10-K_2025-09-30_enr.htm.jsonl, page 115; 10-Q_2026-06-30_enr.htm.jsonl, pages 3–4; 10-Q_2026-06-30_enr.htm.jsonl, page 8; 10-Q_2026-06-30_enr.htm.jsonl, page 17; 10-Q_2026-06-30_enr.htm.jsonl, page 48; calculation C03; calculation C07; calculation C08

Disclosure supports monitoring of reported performance but not of lender protections. The audited annual report with effective internal control, three quarterly reports and three earnings releases are regular, yet covenants are described only as customary with compliance stated without levels, and the executed credit agreement, the indentures and the four supplemental indentures dated 2026-01-02 are not supplied, so the company's legal capacity to pay shareholders or add debt cannot be separated from its willingness.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_enr.htm.jsonl, page 154; 10-Q_2026-06-30_enr.htm.jsonl, page 18; 10-Q_2025-12-31_enr.htm.jsonl, page 47

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence for a higher risk level is that fiscal 2025 payouts of USD 176.8 million exceeded free cash flow of USD 63.2 million, in a year when net new debt financing was USD 155.9 million, which would be creditor-adverse if repeated without covenant limits that cannot be seen. The strongest evidence for a lower level is that fiscal 2026 payouts fell to 0.67 times free cash flow, USD 90.0 million of term loan was prepaid, maturities were extended to 2030, 2032 and 2033, and no sponsor, fee or related-party leakage appears in the supplied pages. The view would move to low if the fiscal 2026 annual report shows continued repayment and the executed documents show tight restricted-payment limits, and to high if payouts again exceed free cash flow or the supplemental indentures show weaker note priority.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_enr.htm.jsonl, page 76; 10-K_2025-09-30_enr.htm.jsonl, page 101; 10-K_2025-09-30_enr.htm.jsonl, pages 137–138; 10-Q_2026-06-30_enr.htm.jsonl, page 17; 10-Q_2026-06-30_enr.htm.jsonl, page 48; calculation C03; calculation C08

### Limitations

- Ownership percentages, board committee detail and pay sit in a proxy statement that is not supplied, so the finding of dispersed ownership rests on a count of holders of record and cover-page share counts, and the governance risk level stays provisional.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_enr.htm.jsonl, page 52; 10-K_2025-09-30_enr.htm.jsonl, page 156
- The purpose and terms of the four supplemental indentures dated 2026-01-02 are not in the supplied documents, so whether guarantor coverage or note priority changed, which would alter the reading of the financing behaviour, is not established.
Basis: 10-Q_2025-12-31_enr.htm.jsonl, page 47; 10-Q_2026-03-31_enr.htm.jsonl, page 55
- The funding of fiscal 2025 payouts is inferred from the cash flow statement, not stated payment by payment, so reading the payouts as met from new borrowing is an inference and could overstate the link.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_enr.htm.jsonl, page 76; calculation C08
- Project Momentum savings and tariff mitigation are management statements without independent support, so the margin-recovery finding rests on management language rather than evidenced results.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_enr.htm.jsonl, pages 60–61; 10-Q_2026-06-30_enr.htm.jsonl, page 37

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

Two financings in fiscal 2025 reshaped the debt without changing leverage. In March 2025 the credit agreement was amended and restated, extending the USD 760 million senior secured term loan to 2032 and the USD 500 million revolving facility to 2030 for debt issuance costs of USD 8.0 million. In September 2025 the company issued USD 400.0 million of 6.00% senior notes due 2033, guaranteed on an unsecured basis by certain domestic restricted subsidiaries, redeemed USD 300 million of 6.50% notes due 2027 with a premium of USD 4.9 million, and added USD 100.0 million to the term loan to repay revolver borrowings. Together the fiscal 2025 financings cost USD 13.6 million of debt issuance costs plus the premium, and extended the note maturity wall at a lower coupon.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_enr.htm.jsonl, pages 137–138; 10-K_2025-09-30_enr.htm.jsonl, page 160

Acquisitions have been small and cash-funded: the APS business in Europe for USD 16.7 million, Centralsul in Brazil for a preliminary USD 16.5 million including an earnout, and Belgian battery assets for USD 11.6 million. In the nine months to 2026-06-30 the company prepaid USD 90.0 million of the term loan, completed the buy-out of the U.K. pension plan with a non-cash settlement loss of USD 26.3 million, and extended Project Momentum to a fourth year. Four supplemental indentures dated 2026-01-02 are listed as exhibits but their terms are not supplied, and the governing credit agreement and indentures are not in the supplied documents.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_enr.htm.jsonl, page 8; 10-Q_2026-06-30_enr.htm.jsonl, page 17; 10-Q_2026-06-30_enr.htm.jsonl, page 20; 10-K_2025-09-30_enr.htm.jsonl, page 115; 10-Q_2025-12-31_enr.htm.jsonl, page 47; calculation C07
