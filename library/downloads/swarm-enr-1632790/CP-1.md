## Findings

### Bottom line

Reported leverage rose over the nine months, narrowing the cushion beneath creditors: net debt of USD 3,162.2 million is 7.70x trailing reported EBITDA at 2026-06-30, against 5.54x at 2025-09-30, because net debt barely fell while trailing EBITDA dropped.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_enr.htm.jsonl, page 3; EX99.1_2026-06-30_enr_enrpr63026-htm.htm.jsonl, pages 16–17; calculation C02; calculation C05

The issuer's adjusted EBITDA fell much less, from USD 623.6 million to USD 575.4 million, putting adjusted net leverage at 5.50x at 2026-06-30 against 5.12x at 2025-09-30, so most of the reported deterioration comes from items the issuer treats as one-off. The declared dividend adds USD 20.5 million of net debt, or 7.75x pro forma, and the tariff refund received after quarter end lowers it by USD 11.0 million, or 7.67x pro forma, each taken alone. Fiscal 2025 cash interest cover was 3.83x on reported EBITDA, and interim cover is not established because interim cash interest paid is not printed.
Basis: EX99.1_2026-06-30_enr_enrpr63026-htm.htm.jsonl, pages 16–17; 10-Q_2026-06-30_enr.htm.jsonl, page 27; 10-Q_2026-06-30_enr.htm.jsonl, page 48; 10-K_2025-09-30_enr.htm.jsonl, page 99; 10-K_2025-09-30_enr.htm.jsonl, page 151; calculation C06; calculation C11

### Financial interpretation

Net debt rose by USD 204.7 million during fiscal 2025 and has eased by USD 33.3 million since, so the leverage swings come mainly from the EBITDA denominator rather than from borrowing. Net leverage on reported EBITDA was 9.00x at 2024-09-30, 5.54x at 2025-09-30 and 7.70x at 2026-06-30. Fiscal 2024 reported EBITDA of USD 332.4 million carried a USD 110.6 million intangible impairment, fiscal 2025 reported EBITDA of USD 577.2 million benefited from USD 78.0 million of retroactive production credits, and the trailing figure of USD 410.9 million carries USD 99.6 million of restructuring cost and a USD 26.3 million pension settlement loss.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_enr.htm.jsonl, page 66; 10-K_2025-09-30_enr.htm.jsonl, page 99; 10-K_2024-09-30_enr.htm.jsonl, pages 104–105; EX99.1_2026-06-30_enr_enrpr63026-htm.htm.jsonl, page 17; calculation C02; calculation C03; calculation C05

The issuer's adjusted EBITDA fell from USD 623.6 million to USD 575.4 million, which gives net leverage of 5.12x and 5.50x. The add-backs the issuer applies rose from USD 46.4 million to USD 164.5 million, so USD 118.1 million of the USD 166.3 million fall in reported EBITDA reflects higher add-backs rather than lower adjusted earnings. The strongest contrary evidence is that the adjusted view flatters the latest figure: it keeps USD 64.1 million of tariff refund benefit recorded in the nine months to 2026-06-30, only about USD 11.0 million of which had been collected after quarter end, and excluding that benefit alone would put adjusted net leverage at 6.18x (Analyst Judgement, not an issuer figure). A fourth-quarter result without further add-backs would favour the adjusted view; further restructuring cost or a delay in refund collection would favour the reported one.
Basis: EX99.1_2026-06-30_enr_enrpr63026-htm.htm.jsonl, page 2; EX99.1_2026-06-30_enr_enrpr63026-htm.htm.jsonl, pages 16–17; 10-Q_2026-06-30_enr.htm.jsonl, page 27; 10-Q_2026-06-30_enr.htm.jsonl, page 34; calculation C06; calculation C13
