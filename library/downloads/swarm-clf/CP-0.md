## Findings

### Bottom line

The seven supplied Cleveland-Cliffs filings and releases support a credit snapshot through the quarter ended September 30, 2026, but no debt agreements, agency ratings, debt trading levels or proxy statement were supplied, so creditor-protection terms, ratings and ownership stay unverified.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_clf-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 3–5; 10-K_2025-12-31_clf-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 109–111
