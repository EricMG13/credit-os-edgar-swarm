## Findings

### Bottom line
Edgewell's liquidity of USD 815.9 million and the absence of bond maturities before fiscal 2028 protect creditors for now, but weaker earnings leave refinancing of the 2028 notes dependent on a recovery that the nine-month results do not yet show.
Basis: calculation C15; Financial foundation (CP-1); 10-Q_2026-06-30_epc.htm.jsonl, pages 43, 46

Net debt was USD 1,187.1 million at 2025-09-30, or 6.40x reported EBITDA of USD 185.6 million, and USD 848.2 million pro forma for the sale proceeds received on 2026-02-02; pro forma net leverage is not established. Net debt was USD 882.1 million at 2026-06-30, but no earnings base for the retained business supports a current ratio, so the judgement rests on liquidity, a fixed-rate debt stack and management's outlook of adjusted EBITDA of USD 250 million to USD 260 million.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence for a better view is that the retained business has stabilised: third-quarter organic net sales grew 1.1% with North America up 3.0%, Sun and Skin Care organic net sales grew 5.0%, interest expense fell to USD 16.7 million in the quarter, and management guides to adjusted EBITDA of USD 250 million to USD 260 million, which implies USD 72.3 million to USD 82.3 million in the fourth quarter against USD 78.9 million in the third. The view would turn favourable if fourth-quarter adjusted EBITDA lands inside that range, Wet Shave profit stops falling and the 2028 notes are reduced or refinanced before 2028-03-02. It would turn adverse if adjusted EBITDA falls below USD 250 million, if buybacks resume ahead of free cash flow, or if the next annual report shows covenant headroom narrower than the add-backs suggest.
Basis: calculation C58; calculation C59; EX99.1_2026-06-30_epc_q3fy26-epcexhibit991-htm.htm.jsonl, pages 2–5, 14; 10-K_2025-09-30_epc.htm.jsonl, pages 64, 66

### Limitations

- Net leverage and interest cover for the retained business after the sale cannot be computed from the supplied statements, so the improvement from FY2025 is shown by net debt and liquidity and would be overturned if trailing earnings of the two continuing segments prove well below the outlook.
- No rating, bond price, peer leverage or sector spread is supplied, so the judgement that the business profile is average and refinancing capacity adequate rests on the maturity profile and liquidity and not on a market comparison, and a weak refinancing market for consumer credits would lower it.
- The fourth-quarter outlook is management's own and was issued on 2026-08-05, so the view that earnings recover in the last quarter of fiscal 2026 is untested and would change with the first results published after that date.
- Covenant definitions are described but not tested because compliance certificates are not supplied, so the conclusion that near-term default risk is low rests on reported compliance plus liquidity and would weaken if the definitions allow smaller add-backs than the figures imply.
- Fixed overhead that was shared with Feminine Care is not separated in any source, so the conclusion that the sale cost little earnings would weaken if stranded costs prove large.

### Business model and credit linkage

Edgewell is a branded and private-label personal care manufacturer with two continuing segments, Wet Shave and Sun and Skin Care, sold mainly through mass merchants, food, drug and convenience retailers and online. Revenue is spread across razors and blades, sun care and grooming and skin care products rather than one product, which supports cash-flow visibility, but one retailer accounts for about 17.4% of net sales and orders are shipped within a month, so there is no contracted backlog to support debt service in a downturn.
Basis: Business & governance (CP-1A); 10-K_2025-09-30_epc.htm.jsonl, pages 10–14

The business is seasonal: sun care sells from late winter to mid-summer, so the first quarter consumes cash (operating cash outflow of USD 125.9 million against USD 115.6 million a year earlier, consolidated) and the revolver was drawn to USD 277.0 million at 2025-12-31 before the sale. Cash plus revolver availability fell USD 139.4 million in that quarter to USD 365.8 million, before the sale proceeds, so the seasonal cycle can consume a large part of the USD 815.9 million shown at 2026-06-30.
Basis: calculation C18; calculation C19; calculation C17; calculation C20; calculation C77; calculation C15; EX99.1_2025-12-31_epc_epcexhibit99112312025-htm.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-Q_2026-06-30_epc.htm.jsonl, page 43; 10-K_2025-09-30_epc.htm.jsonl, page 14

### Ownership and financial policy

No sponsor or controlling holder is identified, so decisions on payouts and disposals rest with the board, and the record is mixed: shares outstanding fell 7.29% in two years through buybacks, FY2025 returns exceeded free cash flow, but the proceeds of the Feminine Care sale first repaid the revolver. Ownership percentages and the credit documents are not supplied, so legal capacity to make further payouts is not established.

### Revenue durability

Revenue is stable rather than growing: net sales were USD 2,251.6 million, USD 2,253.7 million and USD 2,223.5 million in FY2023 to FY2025, and organic net sales fell 1.3% in FY2025 on lower North America volumes in Wet Shave, Feminine Care and Sun Care. In fiscal 2026 so far organic growth was negative in the first two quarters (0.5% and 2.4% declines) and positive 1.1% in the third, so durability depends on the third-quarter recovery in North America holding and on Wet Shave supply constraints easing.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_epc.htm.jsonl, pages 58, 89; EX99.1_2025-12-31_epc_epcexhibit99112312025-htm.htm.jsonl, page 2; EX99.1_2026-03-31_epc_epcexhibit99103312026-htm.htm.jsonl, page 1; EX99.1_2026-06-30_epc_q3fy26-epcexhibit991-htm.htm.jsonl, page 2; Financial foundation (CP-1)

### Margin resilience

Margins are the weakest dimension: FY2025 gross margin was 41.60% against 42.41%, adjusted gross margin fell 110 bp (20 bp excluding currency) as productivity savings of 270 bp were outweighed by 150 bp of core inflation including tariffs, 75 bp of mix, 45 bp of promotions and 20 bp of absorption, and in nine months 2026 gross margin fell 3.17 percentage points to 41.02%. Advertising and overhead rose at the same time (advertising and promotion 14.59% of third-quarter net sales against 13.56%), so operating leverage is working against the company while volumes are flat.
Basis: calculation C49; calculation C50; calculation C51; calculation C47; calculation C46; calculation C48; calculation C52; calculation C53; 10-K_2025-09-30_epc.htm.jsonl, page 58; EX99.1_2026-06-30_epc_q3fy26-epcexhibit991-htm.htm.jsonl, pages 2–3

Reported EBITDA of USD 97.4 million in nine months 2026 against USD 158.0 million a year earlier (a fall of USD 60.6 million) is on the continuing perimeter, and adjusted EBITDA of USD 177.7 million includes add-backs equal to 45.19% of itself and transition services income that lasts twelve months, which was 9.76% of third-quarter adjusted EBITDA. ### Competitive forces

Rivalry is intense in Wet Shave, where The Procter & Gamble Company leads, BIC is expanding and Dorco competes in private label, and online and direct-to-consumer start-ups compete for shelf space; this explains the 7.2% fall in North America Wet Shave organic sales in FY2025 and caps pricing, so margin recovery depends on productivity rather than price. Substitutes and new entrants matter mainly through private label and direct brands, which is why lower private label sales from supply constraints in the third quarter hurt Wet Shave.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_epc.htm.jsonl, pages 12, 60; EX99.1_2026-06-30_epc_q3fy26-epcexhibit991-htm.htm.jsonl, page 4

Buyer power is the largest structural force: one retailer is about 17.4% of net sales and a few retailers account for a large share, so shelf-space or terms changes pass quickly into volume and margin. Supplier power is moderate because the principal inputs of steel, plastic resins, fabrics and chemicals are mostly available from several sources, but tariffs and inflation cost 160 bp of gross margin in the third quarter, which shows inputs still move earnings.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_epc.htm.jsonl, page 14; EX99.1_2026-06-30_epc_q3fy26-epcexhibit991-htm.htm.jsonl, page 2

### Macro, regulatory and currency factors

Tariffs and trade policy matter to credit because they are in the margin: core inflation and net tariffs cost 160 bp of gross margin in the third quarter, USD 5.3 million of tariff refunds was received in the quarter after the court rulings, and no asset is recognised for further refunds, so any refund is an unbudgeted gain and not a base case. Sun Care is a regulated category and reformulation, recall and destruction costs of USD 3.4 million were incurred in nine months 2026, and the Middle East conflict disrupted international sales in the third quarter; currency was a small favourable factor in the third quarter, so it is not a primary driver.
Basis: EX99.1_2026-06-30_epc_q3fy26-epcexhibit991-htm.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-06-30_epc.htm.jsonl, pages 28–29, 46

### Financial profile and credit quality

Competitive advantage is average because brands are established but North America Wet Shave volumes fell. Business diversification is average: two segments and a split of U.S. and international sales of 54.15% and 45.85%, but one retailer at about 17.4%. Cost and capex flexibility is average: capital expenditures are 3.46% of net sales and shareholder payouts are discretionary, but restructuring cash costs are committed.

### Downside scenario

The initiating condition is a Wet Shave supply disruption that lasts longer than management expects: plant consolidation continues to constrain private label and international supply, a retailer reallocates shelf space to a competitor, and tariff costs remain. The first operational break is lost Wet Shave volume in North America, where organic sales already fell 7.2% in FY2025, with promotions rising to defend share. Financially, Wet Shave profit, which already fell USD 31.3 million in nine months, falls further and adjusted EBITDA lands below USD 250 million, so net leverage rises on an unchanged debt stack.
Basis: calculation C39; 10-K_2025-09-30_epc.htm.jsonl, page 60; EX99.1_2026-06-30_epc_q3fy26-epcexhibit991-htm.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 4–5

Liquidity absorbs the shock before leverage forces a decision: the first-quarter seasonal outflow, about USD 20.1 million of remaining restructuring costs and the cash taxes on the gain draw on cash, and a revolver that is used in the seasonal trough narrows the cushion for the 2028 notes. Management levers are to stop buybacks, cut capital expenditures and phase the remaining restructuring spend, and resilience appears sufficient to avoid liquidity distress before the 2028 notes fall due, on the liquidity shown at 2026-06-30, but a covenant test measured on a weaker EBITDA is the observable trigger because its headroom is not calculable. The primary downside path is a Wet Shave earnings decline that leaves the company refinancing the 2028 notes at higher leverage.

### Overall credit view

Overall, Edgewell presents a not fully assessable business profile that is average on scale and diversification, supported by liquidity of USD 815.9 million and a fixed-rate debt stack with no maturity before fiscal 2028, but credit quality is constrained by a reported EBITDA margin of 8.35% in FY2025, Wet Shave margin compression and payouts that exceeded free cash flow. Cash-flow durability appears weak because free cash flow was 22.31% of reported EBITDA in FY2025, and refinancing capacity is likely to depend on Wet Shave earnings, liquidity at the seasonal trough and the springing maturity date of 2028-03-02. The primary downside path is Wet Shave earnings falling while the 2028 notes approach maturity. Further analysis would require trailing continuing-operations EBITDA, covenant calculations and peer data. Top risk: Wet Shave earnings decline before the 2028 refinancing.
