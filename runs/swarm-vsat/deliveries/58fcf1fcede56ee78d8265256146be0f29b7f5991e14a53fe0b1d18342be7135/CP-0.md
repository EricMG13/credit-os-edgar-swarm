## Findings

### Bottom line

The supplied documents support a credit snapshot of Viasat on reported figures through June 30, 2026, but not on covenant headroom, governance or later events, because no legal documents, proxy disclosure or filing after August 2026 was supplied.
Basis: 10-K_2026-03-31_vsat-20260331.htm.jsonl, pages 119–124; 10-Q_2026-06-30_vsat-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 81

Reported statements, debt tables and liquidity disclosures are complete enough for leverage, liquidity and maturity views to the latest quarter end, which falls 128 days before the as-of date, so later financing or operating events cannot be confirmed. Covenant levels are printed in no filing and issuer-defined adjusted earnings appear in no document, so covenant headroom is not established and earnings measures rest on reported statement lines.
Basis: 10-K_2026-03-31_vsat-20260331.htm.jsonl, pages 119–120; 10-Q_2026-06-30_vsat-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 2–4; calculation C1
