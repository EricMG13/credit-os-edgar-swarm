## Findings

### Bottom line

Mativ is a listed specialty materials producer with no sponsor, parent or controlling shareholder named in the supplied documents, so creditor risk rests on operating execution and shareholder-return policy rather than on sponsor extraction.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_matv-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-K_2025-12-31_matv-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-K_2025-12-31_matv-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 53; calculation C16

The diversified customer base, with no customer at 10% of sales, supports cash flow, and the dividend of USD 17.0 million paid in the first nine months of 2026 was 18.78% of free cash flow of USD 90.5 million. Against that, two goodwill write-offs totalling USD 812.9 million, a July 2026 tornado that contributed to a 10.9% fall in third-quarter sales in Sustainable & Adhesive Solutions, and management's stated pivot to growth keep the case open.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_matv-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 11; 10-K_2025-12-31_matv-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 58; 10-Q_2026-09-30_matv-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-Q_2026-09-30_matv-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 24; EX-99.1_2026-11-04_ex991earningsrelease2026q3.htm.jsonl, page 11; calculation C6; calculation C14

### Key findings

Ownership gives creditors no sponsor whose exit or fee incentives must be underwritten, and no sponsor to rely on for support: the company has been listed since its 1995 spin-off from Kimberly-Clark, and directors and officers together hold about 0.40% of the shares on the run's own arithmetic. The holdings of large institutions are not established because the proxy statement is not supplied, so the absence of a controlling holder is the filings' silence, not a confirmed fact.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_matv-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-K_2025-12-31_matv-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-K_2025-12-31_matv-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 53; 10-K_2025-12-31_matv-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 127; calculation C16; Source readiness (CP-0)

The business is diversified by segment, product and customer, which supports revenue durability, but the lower-margin segment carries most of the sales. Filtration & Advanced Materials was about 39% of 2025 sales and Sustainable & Adhesive Solutions about 61%; nine-month 2026 gross margin was 22.81% in the first and 17.41% in the second, and no product category exceeded 30% of nine-month sales. The United States was 56.17% of 2025 sales, so American demand carries most of the revenue.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_matv-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 10; 10-K_2025-12-31_matv-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 12; 10-K_2025-12-31_matv-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 90; 10-Q_2026-09-30_matv-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 22; 10-Q_2026-09-30_matv-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 25–26; calculation C1; calculation C3; calculation C4

The company's history is acquisition followed by write-down and pruning, and that is the main governance fact for creditors. The 2022 merger with Neenah created the group; goodwill impairments of USD 401.0 million in 2023 and USD 411.9 million in 2025 show that purchase value did not hold, and all goodwill of the Filtration & Advanced Materials unit was written off after its discount rate was raised to 14%. The 2023 sale of the Engineered Papers business for USD 620.0 million in cash repaid about USD 641.2 million of debt, and the supplied filings disclose no acquisition since. Equity of USD 467.1 million rests on goodwill and intangible assets: tangible equity is negative at USD 48.7 million.

Shareholder distributions are modest and were cut, so they are not the main risk, but they continue while reported net leverage is 3.8x. A further USD 0.10 per share payable on December 18, 2026 is about USD 5.52 million on the run's arithmetic.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a cautious reading is operating momentum and deleveraging. Nine-month adjusted EBITDA was USD 184.8 million, 7.94% above the prior year, free cash flow was USD 90.5 million, reported net leverage fell from 4.2x at the end of 2025 to 3.8x, and the company reports compliance with every covenant. The company also describes reducing leverage as a priority and says it expects inventory losses from the tornado to be fully recoverable.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-11-04_ex991earningsrelease2026q3.htm.jsonl, page 10; EX-99.1_2026-11-04_ex991earningsrelease2026q3.htm.jsonl, page 11; EX-99.1_2026-05-06_ex991earningsrelease2026q1.htm.jsonl, page 1; EX-99.1_2026-11-04_ex991earningsrelease2026q3.htm.jsonl, page 1; 10-K_2025-12-31_matv-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 63; 10-Q_2026-09-30_matv-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 28; 10-Q_2026-09-30_matv-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 29; calculation C5; calculation C13; calculation C22

The view would turn more cautious if the growth pivot becomes a debt-funded acquisition, if the dividend is raised or repurchases resume while reported net leverage stays near 3.8x, if the USD 38.0 million insurance receivable is not collected, or if the Sustainable & Adhesive Solutions goodwill of USD 56.2 million is impaired, since its fair value exceeded carrying value by only about 6% at the March 2025 test. It would turn more positive if the proxy statement shows diversified holders and the executed documents show tight limits on restricted payments, additional debt and asset transfers.

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

The group in its present form came from the merger completed on July 6, 2022 of Schweitzer-Mauduit International and Neenah, after which Neenah survives as a direct wholly owned subsidiary and the parent was renamed Mativ Holdings. The group was then reshaped by the sale of the Engineered Papers business to Evergreen Hill Enterprise for USD 620.0 million in cash on November 30, 2023, which carried a gain of USD 176.3 million, repaid about USD 641.2 million of debt and ended participation in tobacco-based markets. The consideration and financing of the 2022 merger are not stated in the supplied documents.
