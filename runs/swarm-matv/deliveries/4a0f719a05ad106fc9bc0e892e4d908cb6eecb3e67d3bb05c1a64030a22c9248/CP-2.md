### Company description and ownership

Mativ makes engineered media for filtration, tapes and liners, specialty paper and packaging, advanced films and healthcare products, and sells in two segments: Filtration & Advanced Materials, which was about 39% of 2025 net sales and sells largely direct to system integrators, and Sustainable & Adhesive Solutions, about 61% and sold through distributors, converters and retailers. Products are qualified by customers over long cycles, no customer reaches 10% of sales, and the United States is 56.17% of sales, which supports cash-flow visibility and refinancing confidence but leaves the group tied to construction, automotive and consumer cycles. The group was assembled by the 2022 merger with Neenah, shaped by the 2023 sale of the Engineered Papers business, and weakened by goodwill write-offs in 2023 and 2025.

### Revenue drivers and pricing power

Revenue is stable but not growing: net sales were USD 2,026.0 million in 2023, USD 1,981.1 million in 2024 and USD 1,987.0 million in 2025, and USD 1,510.9 million in nine months 2026, down 0.9%, with volume and mix down 4.4%, price up 2.5% and currency up 1.0%. Organic growth was negative 0.4% in the first quarter, positive 1.7% in the second and negative 2.3% in the third, so volume has not yet recovered and price is the driver. Filtration & Advanced Materials grew 10.2% in the third quarter on aerospace, defense and transportation demand, which management calls its strongest sales growth, while the other segment fell 10.9%. Revenue is cyclical in construction, infrastructure and automotive end markets, partly recurring through qualified products, and exposed to substitution by lower-cost substrates in less demanding uses. Pricing power is demonstrated in 2026 but untested in a volume downturn, which makes the margin gain less certain to persist.

### Cost structure and margin resilience

Cost of products sold was 80.4% of net sales in nine months 2026, so the base is mostly variable but leaves little buffer: resin is the largest single raw material cost in Filtration & Advanced Materials and wood pulp in Sustainable & Adhesive Solutions, and a 10% change in resin and pulp prices would move annual pre-tax earnings by about USD 32.3 million with no price pass-through, which is 16.26% of trailing reported EBITDA. Inventories of 60 to 80 days create a lag before cost changes reach the income statement, specialty inputs come from few suppliers, and nonmanufacturing costs fell 4.7% under the realignment plan, with a further USD 15.0 million to USD 20.0 million of savings targeted by the end of 2026. Margins can be defended against input shocks only if price follows cost; they are weakest against volume loss in the lower-margin segment.

### Competitive forces, external factors and SWOT

Rivalry is moderate: the annual report names large competitors such as 3M, Covestro, Ahlstrom and Mondi and says some are larger and some, in China, may have lower costs, but products compete on features, innovation and service. Entry is slowed by qualification cycles and over 1,100 patents and applications, substitutes threaten only the less demanding uses, buyers are dispersed with retail and distributor channels in the larger segment, and suppliers of specialty resins and mercerized pulp are few, which gives them leverage over cost. Among external factors, tariffs, currency and European energy cost matter to credit: tariffs have raised cost of goods sold, the Supreme Court ruling of February 20, 2026 on emergency-powers tariffs had a not significant net refund effect to September 30, 2026, a large share of sales is in euros and sterling, a 100 basis point rise in rates would have cost USD 1.5 million a year after hedges at the end of 2025, and at September 30, 2026 swaps covered 66.70% of floating-rate principal. ### Downside scenario

The initiating condition is another loss of volume or distribution in Sustainable & Adhesive Solutions, as the July 27, 2026 tornado showed, with no insurance recovery beyond the write-off.
