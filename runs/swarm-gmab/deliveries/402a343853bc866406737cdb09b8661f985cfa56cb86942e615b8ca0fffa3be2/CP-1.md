## Findings

### Bottom line

Genmab has cut net debt from USD 3,559 million at the end of 2025 to USD 3,132 million at 30 September 2026, but a leverage ratio on EBITDA cannot be stated because 2025 depreciation and amortization were not supplied. Against last-twelve-months operating profit of USD 1,044 million, a proxy that sits below EBITDA, total debt is 4.78x and net debt 3.00x.
Basis: calculation C2; calculation C4; calculation C15; calculation C16

Nine-month EBITDA of USD 1,068 million was 4.53x cash interest of USD 236 million, and USD 288 million of term loan principal was repaid, but operating cash flow fell to USD 574 million from USD 885 million, so further repayment depends on cash conversion recovering.
Basis: calculation C8; calculation C11; calculation C13; 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-11-05_genmabinterimreportq32026.htm.jsonl, page 35

### Key findings

Borrowings did not exist before December 2025, stood at USD 5,274 million at the end of 2025 after the Merus acquisition was financed, and fell to USD 4,992 million by 30 September 2026 as USD 288 million of term loan principal was repaid. Cash rose by USD 145 million to USD 1,860 million, so net debt on carrying values fell by USD 427 million to USD 3,132 million, paid for out of operating cash flow and existing cash rather than new funding.
Basis: 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-11-05_genmabinterimreportq32026.htm.jsonl, page 26; 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-11-05_genmabinterimreportq32026.htm.jsonl, page 45; calculation C4; calculation C14

Leverage on EBITDA cannot be stated at any date because 2025 depreciation and amortization were not supplied. A proxy on last-twelve-months operating profit puts total debt at 4.95x at the end of 2025, 4.93x at 31 March 2026 and 4.78x at 30 September 2026, and net debt at 3.34x, 3.49x and 3.00x. Because operating profit sits below EBITDA, true EBITDA leverage is lower by an amount that is not established. Net leverage rose in the first quarter, when operating cash flow was only USD 3 million and USD 96 million went on share purchases.
Basis: calculation C15; calculation C16; 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-05-07_genmabinterimreportq12026.htm.jsonl, page 3; 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-05-07_genmabinterimreportq12026.htm.jsonl, page 27

Nine-month EBITDA of USD 1,068 million was 4.53x cash interest of USD 236 million, and first-quarter EBITDA of USD 209 million was 3.43x cash interest of USD 61 million. Borrowing interest has no 2025 comparison because the debt was raised in December 2025, and the two cover figures span different durations. The June 2026 amendment cut the Term B margin from 3.00% to 2.00%, and interest expense on borrowings eased from USD 89 million in the first quarter to USD 82 million in the third, which lowers the burden if reference rates hold; how much of the floating-rate debt is hedged is not established.
Basis: calculation C7; calculation C8; calculation C10; calculation C11; calculation C13; 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-11-05_genmabinterimreportq32026.htm.jsonl, page 46; 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-11-05_genmabinterimreportq32026.htm.jsonl, pages 42–43; 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-05-07_genmabinterimreportq12026.htm.jsonl, page 33

Operating cash flow was USD 574 million in nine months against USD 885 million a year earlier, because working capital absorbed USD 354 million against USD 28 million a year earlier and interest on borrowings took USD 229 million, partly offset by taxes paid falling to USD 97 million from USD 317 million. The path was uneven: USD 3 million in the first quarter, a derived USD 51 million in the second and USD 520 million in the third. Free cash flow of USD 555 million was 51.97% of EBITDA, and principal repayments of USD 288 million and share purchases of USD 96 million were paid from it.
Basis: calculation C18; calculation C20; calculation C21; calculation C22; 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-11-05_genmabinterimreportq32026.htm.jsonl, page 35

Revenue grew 21.64% to USD 3,238 million in nine months, led by royalties of USD 2,699 million, but operating profit fell to USD 986 million from USD 1,007 million because operating expenses rose faster, including USD 87 million of acquisition and integration charges, so the operating margin fell to 30.45% from 37.83%. Merus is consolidated only from December 2025 and the 2025 comparatives exclude it, so the margin comparison is not like for like.
Basis: calculation C19; 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-11-05_genmabinterimreportq32026.htm.jsonl, page 34; 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-11-05_genmabinterimreportq32026.htm.jsonl, page 39

The strongest evidence against a durable deleveraging reading is weak operating cash flow: net debt was higher at 31 March 2026 at USD 3,687 million than at the end of 2025 at USD 3,559 million, and nine-month operating cash flow was below the prior year. The view would change with the Annual Report 2025 (EBITDA leverage), the debt documents (maturities, covenant levels and the revolving facility) and the first-half 2026 report (second-quarter cash flow and balance sheet).
Basis: calculation C3; calculation C2; 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-05-07_genmabinterimreportq12026.htm.jsonl, page 27; 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-11-05_genmabinterimreportq32026.htm.jsonl, page 35
