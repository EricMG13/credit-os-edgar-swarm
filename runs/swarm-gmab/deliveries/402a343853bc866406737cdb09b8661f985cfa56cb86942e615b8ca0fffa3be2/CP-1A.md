## Findings

### Bottom line

Genmab's creditors rely on a concentrated royalty stream, two thirds of revenue from DARZALEX, whose patents expire from 2029, to service the USD 5.5 billion of debt that financed the Merus acquisition, with no sponsor behind them and no disclosed holder above 5.80%.
Basis: 20-F_2025-12-31_gmab-20251231_d2.htm.jsonl, pages 10, 91, 150; 6-K-EX-99.1_2025-11-06_genmabinterimreportq32025.htm.jsonl, page 3

Governance is conventional for a listed company: a nine-member board with a majority of independent directors, an independent audit and finance committee chair, audited accounts, quarterly reporting, no dividends and no material related-party transactions. Creditor protection therefore comes from the credit agreement and the indentures, whose terms are not supplied, and not from a sponsor; share purchases of USD 96 million in 2026 continued while debt was repaid.
Basis: 20-F_2025-12-31_gmab-20251231_d2.htm.jsonl, pages 144, 151–152; 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-11-05_genmabinterimreportq32026.htm.jsonl, page 45

### Key findings

Revenue is concentrated in one product family and one partner. DARZALEX royalties were USD 2,057 million of USD 3,238 million of nine-month 2026 revenue, or 63.53%, and revenue from J&J was 66.99%; the annual filing states 66% for 2025. The royalty tiers run from 12% to 20% of annual net sales, are reduced for biosimilars and patent expiry, and J&J can terminate on 150 days' notice. Patent rights expire in 2029 in the U.S., 2030 in Japan and 2031 in Europe, and the filing expects royalties to decline materially from 2029, so debt has to be reduced well before then.
Basis: 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-11-05_genmabinterimreportq32026.htm.jsonl, pages 39–40; 20-F_2025-12-31_gmab-20251231_d2.htm.jsonl, pages 10, 90–91, 127; calculation C2

Diversification is real but slow. Net product sales rose to USD 410 million from USD 280 million, led by EPKINLY in the U.S. and Japan at USD 381 million, and Kesimpta royalties were USD 408 million, yet royalties still made up 83.35% of nine-month revenue. The 2026 guidance of USD 4,325 million to USD 4,525 million of revenue keeps the mix royalty-led, so new products reduce but do not remove the dependence on DARZALEX within the debt tenor.
Basis: 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-11-05_genmabinterimreportq32026.htm.jsonl, pages 6–7, 39–40; calculation C1

The Merus acquisition changed the creditor profile from net cash to leveraged. Genmab paid USD 97.00 per share, about USD 8.0 billion in cash, closed on 12 December 2025 and funded it with about USD 5.5 billion of new debt plus cash on hand. Management targets gross leverage below 3x within two years of closing. The asset acquired, petosemtamab, is in Phase 3, and acquired in-process research and development of USD 8,237 million sits on the balance sheet at 30 September 2026, so the acquisition adds costs before it adds cash and creditors rely on the royalty business to carry the debt while the asset matures.
Basis: 6-K-EX-99.1_2025-11-06_genmabinterimreportq32025.htm.jsonl, pages 3, 40; 20-F_2025-12-31_gmab-20251231_d2.htm.jsonl, pages 125–126; 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-11-05_genmabinterimreportq32026.htm.jsonl, pages 6, 40

Governance gives creditors visibility but no external support. There is no sponsor, the largest disclosed holders are BlackRock and Orbis at 5.80% each, management holds 1.27%, and the board is a majority independent with an independent audit and finance committee chair. The auditor's opinions are unqualified and internal control was effective, but Merus, 59.6% of total assets, was excluded from that assessment. The credit agreement and indentures restrict dividends and other distributions, and no dividends have been paid.
Basis: 20-F_2025-12-31_gmab-20251231_d2.htm.jsonl, pages 144, 150–152, 173, 185

Legal exposure is unquantified and involves a key partner. AbbVie's trade secret case over Rina-S resumed in the district court after its import-exclusion complaint to the trade commission ended in July 2026, and Chugai's three Japanese lawsuits over four patents on EPKINLY remain open; no provision is recorded for either. The amounts are not stated, so the exposure cannot be sized, and AbbVie is also Genmab's partner on epcoritamab, so the dispute touches a relationship that carries net product sales and profit sharing.
Basis: 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-11-05_genmabinterimreportq32026.htm.jsonl, pages 33–34, 51–52; 20-F_2025-12-31_gmab-20251231_d2.htm.jsonl, page 88

Capital allocation has been moderate since the acquisition. USD 288 million of term loan principal was repaid in nine months, the buy-back programme took USD 96 million, and acquisition and integration charges were USD 87 million. Whether continued share purchases are consistent with creditor protection depends on the restricted-payment baskets in the credit agreement and indentures, which are not supplied.
Basis: 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-11-05_genmabinterimreportq32026.htm.jsonl, pages 35, 44–45

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a cautious view is that the royalty base is still growing: royalties rose to USD 2,699 million from USD 2,219 million, partner sales of DARZALEX grew 19%, the company has no dividend, no sponsor and no related-party leakage, and three Phase 3 trials read out positively in October 2026, which supports the value of the assets that creditors rely on. A listed company with an independent board and quarterly reporting is also easier to monitor than a sponsor-owned borrower.
Basis: 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-11-05_genmabinterimreportq32026.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 4, 39; 20-F_2025-12-31_gmab-20251231_d2.htm.jsonl, pages 144, 151–152

The view would turn more favourable if the credit agreement and indentures show wide covenant headroom, long maturities and tight restricted-payment baskets, and if successful launches of epcoritamab, Rina-S or petosemtamab add royalty-independent cash flow before 2029. It would turn less favourable if DARZALEX royalties erode earlier than the patent dates imply, if the AbbVie or Chugai cases are decided against Genmab, or if share purchases accelerate while leverage stays above the management target.
Basis: 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-11-05_genmabinterimreportq32026.htm.jsonl, pages 4, 51–52; 6-K-EX-99.1_2025-11-06_genmabinterimreportq32025.htm.jsonl, page 3

### Limitations

- The credit agreement, the two indentures and the supplemental indentures are not supplied, so the conclusion that creditors have no sponsor support cannot be tested against guarantor coverage, collateral or the restricted-payment baskets, and the view on creditor protection stays provisional.
- The ownership percentages come from Schedule 13G filings dated March 2025 and September 2025 and from the table as of February 2026, so the statement that no disclosed holder exceeds 5.80% may be out of date.
- Integration evidence for Merus is limited to USD 87 million of nine-month charges, with no synergy, pro forma or sources-and-uses figures, so the conclusion that the acquisition adds cost before cash is directional.
- The legal matters are read from the annual filing and the interim notes; the Annual Report 2025 note on claims is not supplied, so the conclusion that the exposure cannot be sized may change if that note quantifies it.

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

On 12 December 2025 Genmab completed the acquisition of Merus N.V. for USD 97.00 per share in cash, a transaction value of about USD 8.0 billion, accounted for as an asset acquisition because substantially all of the value is petosemtamab. The agreement was signed on 29 September 2025 and the tender offer began on 21 October 2025.
Basis: 6-K-EX-99.1_2025-11-06_genmabinterimreportq32025.htm.jsonl, pages 3, 40, 47; 20-F_2025-12-31_gmab-20251231_d2.htm.jsonl, page 125

The acquisition was funded with about USD 5.5 billion of new debt and cash on hand: a USD 1 billion Term Loan A, a USD 2 billion Term Loan B, a USD 500 million revolving credit facility, USD 1.5 billion of senior secured notes and USD 1.0 billion of senior unsecured notes, with Genmab Finance LLC named alongside Genmab A/S in the debt documents. At 30 September 2026 the four drawn instruments carried USD 926 million, USD 1,674 million, USD 1,439 million and USD 953 million, and the Term B margin had been cut from 3.00% to 2.00% in June 2026. Coupons, maturities, guarantors and collateral are not supplied, and management's target is gross leverage below 3x within two years of closing.
Basis: 20-F_2025-12-31_gmab-20251231_d2.htm.jsonl, pages 126, 180–182; 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-11-05_genmabinterimreportq32026.htm.jsonl, pages 45–46; 6-K-EX-99.1_2025-11-06_genmabinterimreportq32025.htm.jsonl, page 3
