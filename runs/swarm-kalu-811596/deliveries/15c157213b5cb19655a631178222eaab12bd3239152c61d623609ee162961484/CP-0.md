## Findings

### Bottom line

The five supplied filings, the latest for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, support the requested credit snapshot of Kaiser Aluminum Corp with limitations, so creditors can rely on reported debt, liquidity and earnings but not on covenant headroom, guarantor coverage or ratings.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_kalu-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 3, 14–15, 29, 31; calculation CALC-2; calculation CALC-5

Three limits shape how far the figures can be used. The 2025 annual report adjusts 2024 and 2023 for an inventory accounting change, so the 2024 annual report is not comparable for those years. The quarterly reports do not state the inventory basis of their 2025 comparatives. The credit agreement and indentures are not supplied, so the level of the springing fixed charge coverage test and the list of guarantors are not established.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_kalu-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 54, 116–117; 10-K_2024-12-31_kalu-20241231.htm.jsonl, page 61; 10-Q_2026-09-30_kalu-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 32

## Gaps

- Covenant terms are not established. No supplied filing prints the level of the springing fixed charge coverage test, the availability level that switches it on, or any indenture covenant level, and the credit agreement and indentures are not supplied. Every filing was searched for fixed charge coverage, availability threshold, financial covenant, springing and ratio-level wording; management says only that it does not expect to trigger the test in the next 12 months. Covenant headroom cannot be calculated and the legal and covenant analysis cannot run.
  Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_kalu-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 32; 10-K_2025-12-31_kalu-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 37, 61

- The guarantors of the Senior Notes are not named. The 2024 annual report says certain subsidiaries are not guarantors of the Senior Notes or the Revolving Credit Facility, the 2025 annual report drops that statement, and neither names the guarantors or prints guarantor and non-guarantor financial information. Only the four revolver borrowers are named, so structural priority and asset coverage by entity cannot be established.
  Basis: 10-K_2024-12-31_kalu-20241231.htm.jsonl, page 42; 10-Q_2026-09-30_kalu-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 14; 10-K_2025-12-31_kalu-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 120

- No credit rating is stated in any filing or in the run settings. Ratings appear only as a factor in financing terms, in the credit thresholds that set derivative collateral for counterparties and in the change-of-control offer to repurchase the 5.875% notes, which applies when the change is accompanied by a ratings decline. Rating-based pricing and triggers cannot be assessed.
  Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_kalu-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 37, 100, 102

- The 2024 annual report and the 2025 annual report give different 2024 amounts because the company changed its inventory valuation method on 2025-01-01. Net income for 2024 is USD 46.8 million in the first and USD 65.7 million as adjusted in the second; Adjusted EBITDA for 2024 is USD 216.5 million in the first and USD 241.0 million as adjusted in the second. Had the former method continued, 2025 would have shown a net loss of USD 29.1 million instead of net income of USD 112.5 million. The 2025 annual report is the later filing and controls FY2025, FY2024 and FY2023; FY2022 exists only in the 2024 annual report on the former method, so any series that reaches back to FY2022 mixes bases.
  Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_kalu-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 58, 116–117; 10-K_2024-12-31_kalu-20241231.htm.jsonl, pages 61, 78

- The three 2026 quarterly reports do not say which inventory method their 2025 comparative figures use, and the 2025 quarterly reports are not supplied. No stand-alone fourth quarter 2025 statement is printed, so it has to be derived from the full year and nine months, and a trailing twelve months figure at 2026-09-30 rests on a nine months 2025 basis that is not confirmed.
  Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_kalu-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 3, 29; 10-K_2025-12-31_kalu-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 58

- Ownership, directors, officers and governance are in the proxy statement, which the annual report incorporates by reference and which is not supplied. Shareholder concentration, board independence and management incentives cannot be described.
  Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_kalu-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 119

- Purchase and capacity commitments are described in words only; no amount is printed in any supplied filing after searching for purchase obligation, purchase commitment, capital commitment and non-cancelable wording. The size of these claims outside debt cannot be stated.
  Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_kalu-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 62

- Nothing dated after the latest quarterly report of 2026-10-22 is supplied: no current report, earnings release or lender presentation. Events between that date and the 2026-11-18 cutoff cannot be checked, and management's outlook is available only as written in the quarterly reports.
  Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_kalu-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 31, 39
