## Findings

### Bottom line

Net debt is 1.98x trailing reported EBITDA at September 30, 2026 and liquidity is USD 687.2 million, so creditors have no near-term refinancing pressure, but the earnings recovery leans on metal price timing and a USD 157.2 million payables build.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_kalu-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 3, 14, 29, 31; calculation CALC-30; calculation CALC-56; calculation CALC-86
