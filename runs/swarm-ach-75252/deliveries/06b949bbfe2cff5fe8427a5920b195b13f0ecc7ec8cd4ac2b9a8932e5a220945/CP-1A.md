## Findings

### Bottom line

Sponsor and governance risk is Medium: no controlling owner or extraction is documented, but equity is a USD 568.23 million deficit under two NYSE notices and a new chief executive started on 2026-10-05, so creditors carry the outcome.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_ach_ach-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ach_ach-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 26; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ach_ach-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 42; Source readiness (CP-0)

Revenue fell 10.24% in the first nine months of 2026, nearly all of it from commercial payor contracts that were 12% of 2025 revenue. Management paid no dividend and stopped share repurchases, but it paid USD 80 million to end the Rotech acquisition and moved nearly all unsecured notes into secured notes in June 2026. The missing proxy statement and executed debt documents mean this view rests on the filings alone.

### Key findings

No sponsor or controlling shareholder is identified, so governance risk comes from the issuer's own decisions and from the incentives of an out-of-the-money equity, not from a financial owner's conduct. The common stock is listed and held by record holders, the filings name no parent, and ownership above the share count sits in a proxy statement that was not supplied, so this is a finding that none is documented, not that none exists.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_ach_omi-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 60; 10-K_2025-12-31_ach_omi-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 2; Source readiness (CP-0)

Equity holders have little left to lose, which matters because decisions taken for their benefit would be paid for by creditors. The total deficit is USD 568.23 million, the average market value of the stock stayed below USD 50 million over 30 trading days and the average closing price stayed below USD 1.00. Management plans a reverse split of 1-for-20 to 1-for-30 for the price notice and is still weighing alternatives for the equity notice, whose cure period is 18 months.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_ach_ach-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ach_ach-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 42; calculation C14

A new chief executive and a new commercial leader arrive after the payor loss, which raises execution risk but keeps board continuity. The new chief executive has been a director since March 2022, the former chief executive left the board and will advise for up to 30 months, and a newly created chief commercial officer joined the same day.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_ach_ach-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 26

Revenue durability is the main business risk and it is concentrated in a few payors, not spread across products. In 2025 the two largest commercial payors were 23% and 14% of revenue and Medicare and Medicaid 19%, and the terminated commercial contracts were 12% of revenue and nearly all capitation revenue. In the first nine months of 2026 those contracts explain USD 205 million, or about 97.49%, of the decline, while sleep therapy fell 16.2% and home respiratory therapy 14.8%.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_ach_omi-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 24; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ach_ach-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 27; calculation C05; calculation C07

Operating leverage is working against the business: revenue is falling faster than cost, and the cost ratios are rising. Cost of net revenue fell 4.3% against revenue down 10.24%, so it rose to 56.4% of revenue from 52.9%, and selling, general and administrative cost rose to 40.6% of revenue from 38.8%. The issuer attributes the cost ratio to lower collection rates, equipment depreciation fell only 3.3%, and no quantified targets are given for its value creation plan, so the path back to profit is stated but not measured.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_ach_ach-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 28; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ach_ach-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 36; calculation C12; calculation C13

Part of 2026 cash comes from selling assets, which cannot recur at this scale (an inference). In nine months the company received USD 125 million from selling patient service equipment, USD 85 million of it in connection with the payor termination, and USD 33 million from a non-core asset, against capital expenditure of USD 131 million, and it states recurring losses and cash outflows from operations in 2026.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_ach_ach-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 35; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ach_ach-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 36

Strategy has swung from acquisition to disposal within about 18 months, which makes the plan harder to underwrite and puts weight on the new chief executive's first plan (an inference). The Rotech acquisition for USD 1.36 billion in cash was agreed in July 2024 and ended in June 2025 after a USD 80 million payment, and the sale of Products & Healthcare Services completed on 2025-12-31 for USD 375 million in cash, which is 36.14% of the net assets that were held for sale at the end of 2024.
Basis: 10-K_2024-12-31_ach_omi-20241231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-K_2025-12-31_ach_omi-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 63; 10-K_2025-12-31_ach_omi-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-K_2025-12-31_ach_omi-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 101; calculation C18; calculation C20

Creditor treatment in the June 2026 exchange was consensual in form but left holdouts behind, and it is classed as mixed. Holders of 99.9% of the 2029 notes and 99.2% of the 2030 notes tendered into first lien notes due 2032 and second lien notes due 2033 (USD 539 million and USD 698 million outstanding), which moved maturities out and added security at higher coupons; the unsecured notes left, USD 0.3 million and USD 4.2 million, are effectively subordinated.

Disclosure quality is a strength for monitoring, with one past accounting error corrected. The auditor gave unqualified opinions on the 2025 statements and on internal control, disclosure controls were judged effective at 2026-09-30, and a USD 21 million over-accrual of accounts payable built up over several years was corrected through opening equity after the issuer judged it not material.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_ach_omi-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 79; 10-K_2025-12-31_ach_omi-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 119; 10-K_2025-12-31_ach_omi-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 85; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ach_ach-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 41

Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_ach_omi-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 15

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a Medium rating is that nothing in the supplied filings shows value leaving for owners. No dividend appears in the statements, repurchases stopped after USD 10.14 million, the exchange was agreed with existing creditors who accepted it almost unanimously, the auditor's opinions on the statements and on internal control are unqualified, and the new chief executive is an existing director, so continuity is likelier than a break. The payor step-down is also complete: the terminated contracts produced revenue only in the first quarter of 2026 and none in the third, and in May 2026 management said it did not expect material effects on operating income from the remaining terminations, so later comparisons lose that drag.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_ach_ach-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 21; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ach_ach-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 26; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ach_ach-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 43; 10-K_2025-12-31_ach_omi-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 79; 10-Q_2026-03-31_ach_ach-20260331x10q.htm.jsonl, page 23; calculation C21

The strongest case for High is that equity is a deficit, the listing is under notice and the only stated cure for the price notice is a reverse split that needs a shareholder vote, so a restructuring or an equity raise on terms that favour new capital over existing creditors becomes more likely (an inference). The Series C preferred stock and the rights agreement of 2026-08-10 are unexplained in the filings and could change control.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_ach_ach-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 42; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ach_ach-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 44

The view would move to High on any of these: a further exchange or lien step that treats holders of the same debt differently, an issue of equity or preferred stock that dilutes or outranks existing creditors, a delisting, covenant waivers without deleveraging, or a payment to shareholders. It would move to Low only on evidence not supplied: a proxy statement showing an independent board and no dominant holder, executed debt documents with tight restricted payment limits, and a quantified plan whose first results show revenue and the cost ratios stabilising.

  Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_ach_omi-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 80; 10-K_2025-12-31_ach_omi-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 2

  Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_ach_ach-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 44

- Payor shares are stated for 2025 only, and the revenue still earned from the payor that ended contracts is not stated, so the durability finding cannot say how much of the remaining revenue depends on that payor; it holds only for the part already lost.
  Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_ach_omi-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 24; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ach_ach-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 26

- The issuer gives no quantified targets for its value creation plan, so the finding that cost ratios are worsening cannot be tested against management's expected recovery; it stays a description of the first nine months of 2026.
  Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_ach_ach-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, pages 36–37

- No executed credit agreement or indenture was supplied, so the capacity to pay dividends, make investments, grant liens or move assets is not established; the finding that no extraction is documented describes behaviour only and says nothing on what the documents allow.
  Basis: Source readiness (CP-0)

- Each behaviour, an abandoned acquisition, a disposal and an exchange offer, appears once in the filings, so no pattern is inferred; acquisition appetite and creditor treatment stay at mixed and are not read as tendencies.
