## Findings

### Bottom line

The seven supplied CACI filings and releases support the financial foundation, business and governance, and fundamental credit work through the quarter ended 30 September 2026, but they cannot support any covenant, ratings, market-pricing or portfolio conclusion. Creditors should treat covenant headroom, guarantee scope and recovery as not established until executed debt documents are supplied.
Basis: 10-K_2026-06-30_caci-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 66–67; 10-Q_2026-09-30_caci-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 3–4, 8–9, 21

The statements are those of CACI International Inc consolidated, which is also the borrower or issuer of every printed debt instrument. The latest balance sheet, debt table and undrawn revolver of USD 1,395.0 million are filed figures, so liquidity can be built from the filings.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_caci-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 3, 8–9, 21; calculation C1

### Key findings

Coverage of the latest period is complete for the statements the selected work needs. The quarter ended 30 September 2026 has a balance sheet, cash flow statement, segment results and a six-instrument debt table in the quarterly report, the earnings release repeats the statements, and the four discrete quarters from December 2025 to September 2026 are all present, which supports a last-twelve-months view. Revenue for the four fiscal 2026 quarters sums to the filed annual figure of USD 9,567.8 million.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_caci-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 3–4, 8–9, 12; EX-99.1_2026-10-21_fy27-q1caci20261021ex991.htm.jsonl, page 1; 10-K_2026-06-30_caci-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 66; calculation C7

Debt can be listed in full but cannot be tested against its covenants. Every instrument is printed with maturity, rate and balance at 30 September 2026 and 30 June 2026, and the principal amounts sum to the stated totals. The documents state compliance with all financial covenants at each balance-sheet date but print no covenant level, no reported ratio and no headroom, and only the December 2025 quarterly report even names the two tests, a maximum total net leverage ratio and a minimum interest coverage ratio.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_caci-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 8–9; 10-Q_2025-12-31_caci-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 12; 10-K_2026-06-30_caci-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 90; calculation C3; calculation C4; calculation C5

The perimeter is a holding company whose subsidiary guarantors are not named. CACI International Inc conducts operations through subsidiaries, the secured debt carries guarantees and collateral, and the guarantors appear only by reference to exhibits that are not supplied. The consolidated statements are therefore the only perimeter available, and structural priority cannot be read from the filings.
Basis: 10-K_2026-06-30_caci-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 5, 89, 104

Fiscal 2026 earnings carry only part of the ARKA acquisition, and no pro forma figures are supplied. The acquisition closed on 9 March 2026 and was funded from increased borrowings and cash on hand, while the annual report states that pro forma results are not material and reports the acquired revenue only together with a smaller purchase. A trailing-twelve-month measure to September 2026 will therefore set the full acquisition debt against part-period earnings and must be labelled or adjusted.
Basis: 10-K_2026-06-30_caci-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 79–81, 89; EX-99.1_2026-04-22_fy26-q3caci20260422ex991.htm.jsonl, page 5; calculation C6

The latest quarterly report omits two items that the annual report prints. It gives no maturity schedule at 30 September 2026, so the schedule at 30 June 2026 is the latest, and it does not state the term of the receivables-sale facility, which the annual report says runs to 18 December 2026. Near-term liquidity work must label both as older evidence.
Basis: 10-K_2026-06-30_caci-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 85, 89; 10-Q_2026-09-30_caci-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 8

Period labels need care because the Board approved a change to a calendar year on 20 October 2026. A six-month transition report to 31 December 2026 will follow, none is in the supplied set, and no supplied figure is affected. Labels must follow the company's June fiscal years until then.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_caci-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 13

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest case against calling the set limited is that the company states compliance with every financial covenant at each period end, shows USD 1,395.0 million undrawn on the revolver with no borrowings on the swing line or stand-by letters of credit, and prints a complete debt table, so a reader could say covenant levels add little. That case wins if the next filing prints the covenant levels and reported ratios, or if the executed credit agreement is supplied and shows ample headroom, in which case covenant capacity and structural priority move from not established to supported. The view would also change if a rating report or market quotes were supplied, because ratings and market-implied work would then become possible.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_caci-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 8–9, 21; calculation C1

### Limitations

- No executed credit agreement, indenture, supplemental indenture or receivables purchase agreement is supplied, so any conclusion on covenant capacity, guarantee scope, collateral or recovery stays not established and is never assumed from the filings' summaries.
Basis: 10-K_2026-06-30_caci-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 88–89, 104–105
- No credit ratings, market prices, quotes or peer data are supplied, so ratings, market-implied and peer-relative conclusions are not supported; only the change-of-control ratings-decline repurchase right is stated in the filings.
Basis: 10-K_2026-06-30_caci-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 89
- No fund portfolio or mandate is supplied, so any portfolio-fit conclusion is outside what these sources can support.
- The earnings releases are issuer-prepared and their EBITDA, adjusted net income and free cash flow follow company definitions; they cannot override the filed statements, so conclusions on earnings quality rest on the filings first.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-10-21_fy27-q1caci20261021ex991.htm.jsonl, pages 9–11
- The proxy statement is not supplied, so governance conclusions on directors, executive officers, compensation and ownership are limited to the annual report's business and risk disclosures.
Basis: 10-K_2026-06-30_caci-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 103
