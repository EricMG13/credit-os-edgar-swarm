## Findings

### Bottom line

The seven supplied filings and releases support a consolidated credit snapshot of DAVITA INC. through Q3 2026, but creditor protections beyond the filings’ own summaries, agency ratings and market pricing cannot be assessed from them.

The annual report is audited with an unqualified opinion; the three quarterly reports and three earnings releases are unaudited. The overlapping figures tested agree, apart from two net debt definitions printed in each release. The latest source was published seven days before the as-of date.
