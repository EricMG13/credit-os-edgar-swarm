## Findings

### Bottom line

Leverage is edging up on broadly flat earnings: carrying-value debt is 3.82x last-twelve-months reported EBITDA at 2026-09-30, against 3.72x at 2025-12-31, so creditors hold a slightly thinner cushion than at year end.
Basis: calculation C04; calculation C05; 10-Q_2026-09-30_dva-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 3

Net of cash the ratio is 3.64x, and 3.68x after October share repurchases; the issuer’s adjusted measure gives 3.54x on carrying debt. Share repurchases and distributions to minority partners exceeded cash flow after capital expenditure, so debt rose while cash fell. Maturities are light until 2030, when 76.95% of principal falls due across 2030 and 2031, and the revolver and Term Loan A-2 fall due in March 2030 if the 2030 senior notes are still outstanding.
Basis: calculation C02; calculation C05; calculation C07; calculation C12; 10-Q_2026-09-30_dva-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 11; 10-Q_2026-09-30_dva-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 12

### Financial interpretation

Leverage rose through 2025 and again in 2026. Carrying-value debt over reported EBITDA moved from 3.36x at the end of 2024 to 3.72x at the end of 2025 and 3.82x at 2026-09-30, and net of cash from 3.07x to 3.48x and 3.64x. Debt grew faster than earnings because borrowing and cash funded buybacks, distributions to minority partners and a 200.000 US$m minority investment in Elara Caring beyond free cash flow, while the 62.176 US$m impairment in the third quarter held last-twelve-months EBITDA to 2818.746 US$m. After the October repurchases the net ratio is 3.68x on the same earnings base.
Basis: calculation C02; calculation C04; calculation C05; calculation C07; 10-Q_2026-09-30_dva-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-09-30_dva-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-Q_2026-09-30_dva-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 19

The issuer measures leverage differently, and its measure is the one the credit agreement uses. Its ratio of 3.38x uses principal, cash including short-term investments and Consolidated EBITDA of 3042 USD millions, so it sits 0.26x below the house net figure of 3.64x and 0.44x below the house total-debt figure of 3.82x, and it stays inside the 3.0x to 3.5x range the issuer calls its target. The annual report says the credit agreement lifts many limits on investments, share repurchases, dividends and further debt while the ratio is below 4.00x and sets the maximum at 5.00x through 2028 and 4.50x afterwards; the third-quarter release still prints 5.00x as the maximum, but neither quarterly report restates the 4.00x test or the step-down after the June 2026 amendment. The 0.62x between the issuer’s ratio and 4.00x is therefore a reading of a printed ratio against earlier text, not a verified covenant calculation, and no headroom is read from the house figures.
