## Findings

### Bottom line

NCL Corporation's total debt stood at 5.51x last-twelve-months adjusted EBITDA at 30 September 2026, up from 5.34x at year-end, and free cash flow is negative, so creditors rely on export-credit funding and revolver access.
Basis: calculation L1; calculation L2; calculation F1

Reported EBITDA gives a lower 4.94x because it includes non-cash gains, so the adjusted measure is the safer yardstick. Cover of EBITDA over cash interest was 2.81x in FY2025, and no cash interest is stated for 2026. Liquidity of USD 1,350.7 million at 30 September 2026 is close to the USD 1,344.3 million of principal due by the end of 2027. The October 2026 refinancing is not in these figures, and its effect on net debt is not established.
Basis: calculation L3; calculation C1; calculation Q1; calculation Q2; 10-Q_2026-09-30_nclc-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 11; 10-Q_2026-09-30_nclc-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 38

### Leverage and coverage

Adjusted leverage rose during 2026 while the unadjusted figure fell. Total debt was 5.51x last-twelve-months adjusted EBITDA at 30 September 2026, against 5.34x at 31 December 2025 and 5.43x at 31 December 2024: debt at carrying value grew from USD 14,583.4 million to USD 14,884.5 million while adjusted EBITDA slipped from USD 2,731.4 million to USD 2,700.1 million, because the nine months to September 2026 earned less adjusted EBITDA than the same months of 2025. The effect for creditors is that deleveraging is not yet visible in the issuer's own statements.
Basis: calculation D2; calculation D5; calculation A3; calculation L1; calculation L2

On the unadjusted measure the ratio fell to 4.94x, but that fall comes from other income of USD 434.5 million in the last twelve months, mainly non-cash gains on the conversion options in the exchangeable notes and on the remeasurement of euro debt. Those gains reverse when the share price or the exchange rate moves back, so they do not service debt, and creditors should anchor on the adjusted ratio. The strongest evidence against this view is that adjusted EBITDA now covers interest expense, net 3.59x over the last twelve months, up from 2.81x in FY2025; but FY2025 carried a USD 202.5 million loss on extinguishment of debt that is not repeated, and the October 2026 notes pay a higher coupon than the 2028 notes they replaced, so the view would change only if adjusted EBITDA recovers or debt falls.
Basis: calculation L3; calculation E3; calculation A3; 10-Q_2026-09-30_nclc-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 36; 10-Q_2026-09-30_nclc-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 11; 10-K_2025-12-31_nclc-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 150

Cover on cash interest was 2.81x in FY2025 and 3.06x in FY2024. The annual report says those cash interest amounts include early redemption premiums, so recurring cover is better than stated, and no cash interest is stated for any 2026 period, so last-twelve-months cover on cash interest is not calculable. Interest expense, net for FY2025 includes a loss on extinguishment of debt, which is why the proxy cover was 2.81x for FY2025 against 3.59x for the last twelve months.
Basis: calculation C1; 10-K_2025-12-31_nclc-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 169; 10-K_2025-12-31_nclc-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 121

### Cash flow quality

Operating cash flow of USD 2,169.0 million over the last twelve months did not cover capital additions of USD 2,729.7 million, leaving a free cash flow deficit of USD 560.7 million, against a deficit of USD 1,182.3 million in FY2025 and a surplus of USD 841.1 million in FY2024. The deficit is the newbuild programme: the delivery of Norwegian Luna in March 2026 and of Norwegian Aqua and Oceania Allura in 2025 were funded mainly by newbuild loans backed by export-credit financing and by revolver borrowings, so debt rises with the fleet and creditors carry delivery and financing-execution risk rather than relying on internally generated cash. The sources split capital spending into maintenance and growth only in guidance for the parent group, so that split is not established for the issuer.
Basis: calculation F1; 10-Q_2026-09-30_nclc-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-Q_2026-09-30_nclc-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 40; 10-K_2025-12-31_nclc-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 123

### Liquidity and maturities

Liquidity, measured as cash plus revolver availability, fell from USD 1,604.0 million at 31 December 2025 to USD 1,350.7 million at 30 September 2026, and is close to the USD 1,344.3 million of principal scheduled through the end of 2027, which includes the 2027 exchangeable notes that the issuer expects to repay in cash or refinance. The largest scheduled amount falls in 2030, when the revolver and the 2030 exchangeable notes mature. The October 2026 refinancing replaced the 2028 notes with notes due 2031, repaid part of the revolver and raised revolver commitments, which the group release puts at USD 2.1 billion of pro forma liquidity for the group; the issuer's own pro forma figures are not established, and export-credit facilities that fund ships are excluded from liquidity.
Basis: calculation Q1; calculation Q2; calculation M1; 10-Q_2026-09-30_nclc-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 12; 10-Q_2026-09-30_nclc-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 38; EX-99.1_2026-10-29_exh_991.htm.jsonl, page 3

### Customer deposits and subsequent events

Customer deposits held as advance ticket sales were USD 3,142.0 million at 30 September 2026, against USD 3,200.6 million at 31 December 2025 and USD 3,106.0 million at 31 December 2024, and they are many times cash of USD 250.7 million. This float funds operations without interest, so a fall in bookings, or a card-processor reserve requirement (none was required at 30 September 2026), would drain cash quickly; it is working-capital funding, not payables. Events after 30 September 2026 are kept out of the period figures: the October 2026 notes issue, redemption of the 2028 notes, revolver and export-credit repayments and the revolver amendment, and the sale agreement for Oceania Insignia with closing in September 2027.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_nclc-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-K_2025-12-31_nclc-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 122; 10-Q_2026-09-30_nclc-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 23; 10-Q_2026-09-30_nclc-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 24
