## Findings

### Bottom line
The five filed reports support a credit snapshot of BWX Technologies, Inc. through the quarter ended 2026-09-30, but covenant, ranking and market-based conclusions rest on filing summaries because no executed debt documents, ratings or prices were supplied.

The latest filing was signed 16 days before the as-of date and the quarter ended 49 days before it, so the evidence is current. The main limitation is the Medical business sale and its held-for-sale reclassification in the latest quarter, which makes the quarter's balance sheet and the earlier periods not like for like. The filings also word the release of liens on the credit facility differently.
