## Gaps

- The executed merger agreement, the two voting and support agreements, the equity commitment letters, the debt commitment letter and the Thirteenth Amendment to the U.S. credit agreement are named in the third quarter report but are not in the source set. The take-private terms used here are the issuer's own summaries. The merger agreement's outside date, the split of new debt between a bridge facility, new subordinated notes and the credit agreement increase, and the debt outstanding after closing are not established. Remediation: add the October 15, 2026 current report and its exhibits.
- No executed credit agreement or indenture is in the set, and the run settings declare no legal documents. The filings name the covenant tests of the U.S., U.K. and property lease agreements and state compliance at September 30, 2026, but none prints a threshold, a step-down or a reported covenant ratio, so covenant headroom cannot be calculated. Searches of all eight documents for covenant ratio levels found none for the issuer's own agreements; the only covenant-style level printed is the 3.0 to 1.0 debt-to-equity test in the Penske Transportation Solutions distribution terms. Remediation: add the credit agreements and the indenture.
- The set contains no credit rating, rating-agency report or rating trigger; the run settings list none, and the filings mention credit ratings only as a risk of the merger announcement.
- No market prices for the senior subordinated notes, the bank debt or the common stock were supplied, and the run settings list no quotes. The values printed are the USD 212.00 per share offer and the issuer's own fair value of its notes, which it labels as based on quoted prices for the identical liability.
- No peer financial data are supplied and the run settings name no peers; the 2025 annual report names five peer companies only for its stock performance graph.
- The acquisition of Penske Motor Group on November 19, 2025 was accounted for as a common-control transaction and all comparative periods from the 2025 annual report onward were recast, so the 2024 annual report is not on the same basis: the calculations size the difference for 2024 revenue, net income, total assets and debt. Fiscal 2022 exists only on the original basis. The 2025 quarterly reports themselves are not supplied, so 2025 quarters appear only as recast comparatives.
- The U.S. credit agreement's expiry is printed as September 30, 2027 in the 2024 annual report, September 30, 2028 in the 2025 annual report and the first two 2026 quarterly reports, and September 30, 2029 in the third quarter report, which does not say which amendment moved it. The latest printed term is used and the unexplained step is kept as a conflict.
- The third quarter report prints no maturity schedule; the last schedule is at December 31, 2025. A schedule at the latest balance sheet date is not established.
- Directors, executive officers, compensation and security ownership are incorporated by reference to a proxy statement, and no proxy statement is in the set. The 2025 annual report states approximate ownership of 52% by Penske Corporation affiliates and 20% by Mitsui.
- No stand-alone financial statements of Penske Transportation Solutions, the 28.9% equity-method investment that makes up almost all of the equity-method balance, are in the set. The 2025 and 2024 annual reports print combined condensed income statement and balance sheet information for all equity-method investees, and the quarterly reports print none, so interim earnings, assets and debt of the partnership are not established; only the issuer's share of earnings, distributions received and carrying value are printed for the quarters.
- The releases and reports give qualitative outlook text but no quantified management guidance for 2026 or 2027, apart from the estimated annualized revenue of recently acquired dealerships stated in the first and second quarter releases, and those releases mention investor presentations that are not supplied.
- Nothing dated after October 29, 2026 is in the set, so events between that date and the as-of date are not covered; the revolver balance at October 12, 2026 is a post-quarter figure kept apart from September 30 figures.
