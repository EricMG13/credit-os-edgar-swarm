## Findings

### Bottom line
The five supplied Amkor filings are sufficient for the requested credit snapshot through the quarter ended September 30, 2026, but creditors should treat covenant levels, executed debt terms and anything after October 27, 2026 as unverified. The FY2024 and FY2025 annual statements carry audit opinions and the 2026 quarterly statements are unaudited. Where filings overlap they agree: FY2024 results match across both annual reports and reported debt of USD 2,467.2 million ties between the balance sheet and the debt note. The FY2025 and 2026 filings name a minimum interest coverage test and a maximum leverage test but print no level, and the latest filing was signed 22 days before the as-of date.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_amkr_amkr-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 34–36; 10-K_2025-12-31_amkr_amkr-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 90; calculation C1; calculation C2; calculation C5; calculation C7

### Key findings
The sources cover the registrant, Amkor Technology, Inc., on a consolidated basis with its subsidiaries: two annual reports and the first, second and third quarter 2026 reports. Together they give income statements for four fiscal years, balance sheets at each December 31 from 2023 to 2025 and at each 2026 quarter end, and cash flow statements, so the requested work can be sourced from primary filings. The filings name Amkor Technology Singapore Holding Pte. Ltd. and Guardian Assets, Inc. as guarantors of certain debt, and no separate issuer or parent perimeter applies.
Basis: 10-K_2024-12-31_amkr_amkr-20241231.htm.jsonl, pages 86–89; 10-K_2025-12-31_amkr_amkr-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 90–93; 10-Q_2026-03-31_amkr_amkr-20260331.htm.jsonl, pages 4–6; 10-Q_2026-06-30_amkr_amkr-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 4–7; 10-Q_2026-09-30_amkr_amkr-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 1, 4–8, 19, 21

The numbers reconcile wherever the filings overlap. FY2024 and FY2023 income statement lines, including net sales and net income attributable to Amkor, are identical in both annual reports, total assets at December 31, 2025 agree between the FY2025 annual report and the latest quarterly report, cash including restricted cash ties to the cash flow statement at both dates, and debt on the balance sheet ties to the debt note and to the liquidity discussion. No restatement of earlier statements was found in any of the five files.
Basis: calculation C2; calculation C3; calculation C4; calculation C5; calculation C6; calculation C7; 10-K_2024-12-31_amkr_amkr-20241231.htm.jsonl; 10-K_2025-12-31_amkr_amkr-20251231.htm.jsonl; 10-Q_2026-03-31_amkr_amkr-20260331.htm.jsonl; 10-Q_2026-06-30_amkr_amkr-20260630.htm.jsonl; 10-Q_2026-09-30_amkr_amkr-20260930.htm.jsonl

The debt note lists senior notes due 2033, convertible senior notes due 2031, a senior secured revolving facility with USD 1.0 billion available, term loans and subsidiary loans, and states that USD 845.8 million of debt is collateralized. The parent's debt is structurally subordinated to subsidiary liabilities, so consolidated statements alone do not show which claims sit where. The FY2025 annual report and the 2026 quarterly reports name a minimum interest coverage ratio and a maximum consolidated leverage ratio but print no required level, and the executed credit agreement, its amendment and both indentures are not supplied, so headroom against covenants is not established.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_amkr_amkr-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 17–21, 35–36; 10-K_2025-12-31_amkr_amkr-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 134, 136

The latest filing was signed on October 27, 2026, 22 days before the as-of date, and no earnings release or current report is supplied for the period after it. That filing itself discloses an October 2026 customer advance payment agreement under which about USD 1.3 billion is expected in 2027. Any other event between October 27 and November 18, 2026 is not established.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_amkr_amkr-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 36, 84; calculation C1

Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_amkr_amkr-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 50, 70

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view
The strongest case that the sources are sufficient is that they are primary filings, audited for annual periods, internally consistent and recent, so covenant levels and later events would refine a snapshot rather than overturn it. The strongest case that they are not is the debt structure: the parent's debt ranks behind subsidiary liabilities, part of the debt is secured, and guarantees come from named subsidiaries, so consolidated statements cannot show claim priority. The executed credit agreement, its amendment and both indentures would let covenant levels, guarantors and collateral be read from executed terms, and a filing dated after October 27, 2026 would show whether the October 2026 customer agreement or any new financing has changed the picture.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_amkr_amkr-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 21, 35–36
