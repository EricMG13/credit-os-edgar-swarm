## Findings

### Bottom line

The seven supplied filings and earnings releases are enough to build a financial and business snapshot of Advance Auto Parts through the quarter ended October 10, 2026, but not enough to assess legal priority, covenant capacity or market-implied risk.

The annual report gives audited statements, the note-by-note debt table and the maturity ladder; the three quarterly reports and three earnings releases add interim balance sheets, liquidity and the company's own leverage reconciliation. No credit agreement, indenture, rating, market price, peer set or portfolio limit was supplied, so creditor protections rest on the company's own summaries of those documents. The run settings label the current period as the second quarter of 2027, which conflicts with the filings; the filings control.
