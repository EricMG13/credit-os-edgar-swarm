## Findings

### Bottom line

Century Communities is a large, affordability-focused US homebuilder with no sponsor and no controlling holder named in the filings, so creditors depend on management discipline and public-company controls and not on equity support, and this module rates governance risk as medium.

The main creditor risks sit in the business and in capital allocation, not in ownership: prices and margins fell through 2026, West inventory was impaired, a third cash acquisition closed on 2026-10-07 and shareholder payments continued while the revolver was drawn.

### Key findings

The business is a high-volume, affordability-weighted homebuilder, so creditor cash flow depends on selling prices, incentives and inventory turns and not on contracted revenue. The company builds in 16 states under two brands, about 94% of 2025 deliveries were priced below the Federal Housing Administration loan limits, and about 99% were move-in ready homes that shorten the cash cycle but leave buyers dependent on mortgage affordability.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_ccs-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 5; 10-K_2025-12-31_ccs-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 6

Regional concentration is moderate, but the West is the weak spot. No homebuilding segment exceeded 23.13% of 2025 revenue, and financial services and multi-family rentals together contributed only 4.45%. In the first nine months of 2026 West revenue fell 17.91%, its pre-tax income fell to USD 6.62 million from USD 67.59 million, and it carried USD 19.1 million of the USD 19.6 million of inventory impairment recorded in the third quarter.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_ccs-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, pages 120–121; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ccs-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, pages 9–10; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ccs-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 21; calculation CALC-1; calculation CALC-3; calculation CALC-11

Price has fallen faster than volume, which is why margins are under pressure. The average price of new homes delivered fell 10.2% in the third quarter of 2026 to USD 345.0 thousand while deliveries slipped only 1.77%, homebuilding gross margin fell to 15.0% from 17.9%, and the company says it raised incentives to lift sales pace. Orders equalled 1.05x deliveries over nine months and backlog value is flat at USD 413.96 million against USD 416.92 million a year earlier, so the volume base is holding but at lower prices.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_ccs-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 21; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ccs-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 37; calculation CALC-4; calculation CALC-5

The land position gives flexibility but is long relative to sales pace. Lots owned and controlled covered about 6.10 years of trailing deliveries at 2026-09-30, against 5.86 years at 2025-12-31, after the position shrank 24.5% during 2025 and with 45.1% held under options. Options limit capital at risk, but terminated contracts cost USD 11.2 million in 2025 and Texas holds the largest share of lots.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_ccs-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 8; 10-K_2025-12-31_ccs-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 61; 10-K_2025-12-31_ccs-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 68; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ccs-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 47; calculation CALC-6; calculation CALC-12

Growth has become acquisition-led, which adds integration and funding risk to a cyclical business. Three cash acquisitions closed since January 2024 for roughly USD 320.6 million, the latest being HistoryMaker Homes on 2026-10-07 for initial cash of USD 160.2 million. It adds 29 communities, about 8.45% of the company selling communities, and management says it triples Dallas deliveries, but purchase accounting is unfinished and the filings state neither how it was funded nor what it earns.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_ccs-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 121; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ccs-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 7; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ccs-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 37; EX-99.1_2026-10-28_ccs-20261028xex99_1.htm.jsonl, page 1; calculation CALC-7

Ownership is public with founder-managers holding a minority stake, which keeps incentives with shareholders but gives creditors no sponsor or parent behind the notes. The Executive Chairman and the Chief Executive Officer are brothers and founders, sit on the board and together held 13.9% of the stock at 2025-12-31, and the company itself says they have significant influence. Other large holders, board composition and related-party dealings are not established because Part III is incorporated by reference to a proxy statement.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_ccs-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 48; 10-K_2025-12-31_ccs-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, pages 98–99; 10-K_2025-12-31_ccs-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 104

Capital allocation is the clearest area to monitor, because shareholder payments have continued while operating cash flow is negative. In 2025 repurchases and dividends of USD 178.37 million exceeded full-year operating cash flow of USD 153.08 million. In the first nine months of 2026 they were USD 98.72 million against an operating outflow of USD 267.48 million, with the revolver up by a net USD 404.5 million; part of the outflow is seasonal, since the same period of 2025 also showed one of USD 57.65 million, but the 2026 gap is far larger. The dividend was raised 10% to USD 0.32 a quarter and 28.77% of the repurchase authorisation is unused.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_ccs-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, pages 108–109; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ccs-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, pages 3–4; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ccs-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 25; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ccs-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 36; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ccs-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 73; calculation CALC-8; calculation CALC-9; calculation CALC-10

The control environment is a relative strength. The auditor has served since 2013, gave an unqualified opinion and found internal control over financial reporting effective, and legal proceedings are described as ordinary course. The one critical audit matter is the judgement on inventory impairment indicators, which is exactly where the 2026 charge arose, so reported earnings carry estimate risk even with sound controls.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_ccs-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 54; 10-K_2025-12-31_ccs-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, pages 96–97; 10-K_2025-12-31_ccs-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, pages 105–106; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ccs-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 21

Debt sits partly outside the note guarantee group, so structure matters as much as size. Substantially all wholly-owned operating subsidiaries guarantee the notes, the financial services subsidiaries do not, and the mortgage repurchase facilities are not guaranteed by the parent, while the parent guarantees portions of the Century Living construction loans, which stood at USD 129.45 million at 2026-09-30.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_ccs-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 76; 10-K_2025-12-31_ccs-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 78; 10-K_2025-12-31_ccs-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 82; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ccs-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 14

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest case against a medium rating is that governance looks cleaner than the business. Controls are effective, the audit opinion is unqualified, no sponsor extracts fees or dividends, the 2025 refinancing replaced the 2027 notes at par with notes due 2033, and the revolver was enlarged and extended on 2026-09-30 with new lenders, which signals lender support. The case for a higher rating is the 2026 pattern of paying shareholders and buying another builder while operating cash flow was negative. The view would move to low if operating cash flow again covered shareholder payments and the HistoryMaker purchase proved leverage-neutral, and to high if repurchases continued as liquidity tightened, if the covenants revised on 2026-09-30 proved tighter than the filings imply, or if the proxy statement showed value passing to insiders.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_ccs-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 75; 10-K_2025-12-31_ccs-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, pages 105–106; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ccs-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, pages 16–17; calculation CALC-8; calculation CALC-9

### Limitations

- The ownership conclusion rests on one risk-factor sentence dated 2025-12-31, so the view that no single holder controls the company would change if a holder above 5% exists or the stake of the founders has moved since then.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_ccs-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 48; 10-K_2025-12-31_ccs-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 99

- The description of the business, its affordability focus and the benefits of HistoryMaker are management statements that the filings do not test against outside data, so the conclusion that the model suits a downturn is a reading of company claims.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_ccs-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 5; EX-99.1_2026-10-28_ccs-20261028xex99_1.htm.jsonl, page 1; EX-99.1_2026-10-28_ccs-20261028xex99_1.htm.jsonl, page 2

- The 2026 operating figures cover three seasonal quarters, so the conclusions on price, margin and West weakness describe direction and could reverse in a stronger fourth quarter.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_ccs-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 11; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ccs-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 37

- The HistoryMaker purchase is described only by cash price, community count and a delivery comment, so the conclusion that it raises integration and funding risk cannot be sized without its earnings, funding and debt effect.

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

The latest transaction is the HistoryMaker Homes asset acquisition, completed on 2026-10-07 after the balance sheet date for initial cash consideration of about USD 160.2 million, subject to customary holdbacks and post-closing adjustments. It adds 29 active communities in the greater Dallas area to the Texas segment, and the company states that the purchase accounting is not final. The filings do not state how the cash was funded or what the business earns.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_ccs-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 7; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ccs-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 34; EX-99.1_2026-10-28_ccs-20261028xex99_1.htm.jsonl, page 1; EX-99.1_2026-10-28_ccs-20261028xex99_1.htm.jsonl, page 2

The revolving credit facility was amended on 2026-09-30, raising commitments from USD 1.0 billion to USD 1.2 billion. Participating lenders extended maturity, so USD 1.1 billion now matures on 2030-11-01 and USD 100.0 million stays at 2028-11-01. The amendment also added lenders, revised certain financial covenants whose levels are not printed, and removed a 0.10% spread adjustment on SOFR-based borrowings. Drawings were USD 456.0 million at 2026-09-30 against USD 51.5 million at 2025-12-31.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_ccs-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, pages 16–17; 10-K_2025-12-31_ccs-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 77

The only new bond issue in the period is the 2033 senior notes of USD 500.0 million, sold at par in September 2025 with net proceeds of USD 493.1 million. In the same month the company extinguished USD 500.0 million of 2027 senior notes at par, a payment of USD 511.4 million including accrued interest, so the refinancing extended maturity without adding principal. The new notes are unsecured, are guaranteed by the operating subsidiaries and give holders a put on a change of control.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_ccs-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 75; 10-K_2025-12-31_ccs-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 82

Earlier transactions shaped the current group. The company bought Landmark Homes of Tennessee on 2024-01-22 for about USD 33.4 million and Anglia Homes on 2024-07-31 for about USD 127.0 million, both in cash, raised the revolver from USD 900.0 million to USD 1.0 billion on 2025-04-22, and sold a mortgage servicing portfolio for USD 46.9 million in cash in 2025. No sponsor transaction, leveraged buyout or debt-funded dividend appears in the filings.
