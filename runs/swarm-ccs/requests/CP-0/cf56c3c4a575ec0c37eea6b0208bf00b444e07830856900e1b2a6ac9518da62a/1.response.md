## Findings

### Bottom line

The seven supplied filings and releases support the requested financial, business and credit work on Century Communities, Inc. through the quarter ended 2026-09-30, but not covenant or ratings conclusions, so every later view rests on the issuer's own disclosure alone.

The reporting entity is the consolidated registrant itself, so there is no parent or group perimeter to separate. The binding constraints are that no executed debt document, rating, dated market quote or proxy statement was supplied, and that the HistoryMaker acquisition of October 2026 sits outside every reported balance.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_ccs-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 1; 10-K_2025-12-31_ccs-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 5; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ccs-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 7.

### Key findings

The documents cover fiscal 2025, the first three quarters of 2026 and the nine months to 2026-09-30, each with year-ago comparatives, so quarter and year-to-date figures can be kept apart. The fourth quarter of 2025 is not printed on its own; it can be obtained only by subtracting nine-month 2025 from full-year 2025, and the readiness tests show that route works for net income and that the three 2026 quarters add up to the nine-month total.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_ccs-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 107; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ccs-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 3; calculation CALC-1; calculation CALC-3.

Debt can be listed instrument by instrument from the notes: two fixed-rate senior note series, a senior unsecured revolving credit facility, secured construction loans, mortgage repurchase facilities and other financing obligations, and the listed instruments add up to the balance sheet debt lines at both 2026-09-30 and 2025-12-31. The contract terms behind them, meaning covenant levels, redemption schedules and guarantor identities, are not in the set because no executed credit agreement or indenture was supplied.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_ccs-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, pages 14–17; 10-K_2025-12-31_ccs-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 74; calculation CALC-5; calculation CALC-6.

Debt sits in separate perimeters. The senior notes are guaranteed by the operating subsidiaries but not by the financial services subsidiaries, the mortgage repurchase facilities belong to the financial services business and are not guaranteed by the company, and the company's own leverage measure excludes both construction loans and repurchase facilities, so consolidated debt and homebuilding debt differ by design.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_ccs-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 82; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ccs-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, pages 17–18; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ccs-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 66.

The newest events are dated and must stay apart from period figures. The revolving credit facility was enlarged and extended on 2026-09-30, the last day of the quarter, and the HistoryMaker asset acquisition closed on 2026-10-07, after the balance sheet date, with purchase accounting unfinished and no pro forma balance sheet in the set.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_ccs-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 7; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ccs-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 16.

Comparability rests on definitions that changed during 2025. Adjusted EBITDA gained two add-backs, lot option abandonments from the third quarter and stock-based compensation from the fourth quarter, and inventory impairment moved into cost of home sales, each with earlier periods recast; each supplied document states its comparatives on the recast basis, so a series drawn only from them is internally consistent.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_ccs-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, pages 61–62; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ccs-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 6; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ccs-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 66.

Governance evidence is thin. Directors, executive officers, compensation and ownership are incorporated by reference to a 2026 proxy statement that was not supplied, which leaves the officers named on the signature pages and releases and a risk factor stating that the Executive Chairman and the Chief Executive Officer, who are brothers, together held 13.9% of the common stock on 2025-12-31.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_ccs-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, pages 98–99; 10-K_2025-12-31_ccs-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 48; 10-K_2025-12-31_ccs-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 103.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The case that the set is stronger than it looks is that the filings describe the debt terms, the guarantor structure and the covenant types in detail and report compliance with every covenant, so the missing documents may confirm rather than change the picture. The case against is that covenant levels, step-downs and the financial covenants revised by the September 2026 amendment are not printed, so headroom cannot be measured. The executed credit agreement and amendment, the two indentures, the 2026 proxy statement, a rating report and dated quotes would move the conditional modules to ready, and a leverage or coverage test with thin headroom would make the covenant detail the first priority.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_ccs-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 16; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ccs-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 17; 10-K_2025-12-31_ccs-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 43.
