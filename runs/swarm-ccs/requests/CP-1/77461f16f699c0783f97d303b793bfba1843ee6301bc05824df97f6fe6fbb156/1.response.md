## Findings

### Bottom line

Leverage has risen sharply since FY2024 as earnings fell and borrowing grew: total debt is 7.68x last-twelve-months reported EBITDA at 2026-09-30, against 5.07x at FY2025 and 2.83x at FY2024, which leaves creditors reliant on liquidity and equity rather than earnings cover.

Interest cover on reported EBITDA fell from 6.61x in FY2024 to 3.50x in FY2025 and cannot be measured on a cash basis for 2026, while nine-month operating cash flow was an outflow of USD 267.48 million funded by the revolver. The cushions are USD 744 million of undrawn revolver capacity and no senior note maturity before 2029; the HistoryMaker acquisition of 2026-10-07 adds about USD 160.2 million of net debt that the ratios do not yet reflect.

### Key findings

Leverage is driven by falling earnings and a working-capital build. Total debt rose by USD 369.44 million from USD 1,443.15 million at FY2025 to USD 1,812.59 million at 2026-09-30, while last-twelve-months reported EBITDA of USD 236.05 million is 54.77% below the USD 521.9 million of FY2024. Net debt is 7.31x that EBITDA, and total debt is 6.16x issuer-adjusted EBITDA, so the conclusion survives the issuer add-backs.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_ccs-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 14; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ccs-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 65; calculation NAR-DEBT; calculation NAR-EBITDA; calculation LTM-E-9M; calculation LTM-A-9M; calculation ND-9M2026

Cash flow quality deteriorated sharply. Operating cash flow was an outflow of USD 267.48 million in nine months 2026 against an inflow of USD 153.08 million in FY2025, because inventories absorbed USD 403.41 million after releasing USD 41.13 million in FY2025. The revolver rose by a net USD 404.5 million while USD 27.52 million of dividends and USD 71.2 million of share repurchases were paid, which turns working capital into debt.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_ccs-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, pages 108–109; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ccs-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, pages 3–4; calculation NAR-REV; calculation FCF-9M

Cover on cash interest paid was 6.61x in FY2024 and 3.50x in FY2025, on the approximate annual figures of USD 78.9 million and USD 81.3 million that the annual report debt note prints. No interim filing prints interest paid, so interim cover rests on a labelled proxy using interest capitalised on the notes and revolver, which fell from 7.25x in FY2024 to 3.07x for the twelve months to 2026-09-30; the proxy overstates cover because it omits interest on construction loans, repurchase facilities and other debt.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_ccs-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 130; 10-K_2025-12-31_ccs-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 43; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ccs-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 18; calculation INT-9M; calculation LTM-E-9M

Liquidity is adequate but lower than at the year end. Undrawn revolver capacity was USD 744 million at 2026-09-30 against USD 948.5 million at FY2025 even after the commitment was raised from USD 1,000 million to USD 1,200 million, and cash and cash equivalents were USD 86.09 million. The repurchase facilities of USD 191.27 million and construction loans of USD 129.45 million serve the financial services and multi-family businesses and are not counted as corporate liquidity.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_ccs-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 16; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ccs-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ccs-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, pages 14–15; calculation UD-9M2026; calculation UD-FY2025

Near-term maturities are mostly secured facilities that roll. The annual report ladder at 2025-12-31 shows USD 385.76 million due in 2026, nothing in 2027, USD 51.5 million in 2028, USD 515.33 million in 2029 and USD 500 million after 2030; the 2029 senior notes of USD 500 million are the first long-dated bullet and the 2033 senior notes the last. The September 2026 amendment moved most revolver commitments to 2030-11-01, so the 2028 bar is superseded, and no ladder at 2026-09-30 is printed.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_ccs-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 130; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ccs-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 16; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ccs-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, pages 14–15

The leverage perimeter and the EBITDA definition both matter. Consolidated total debt includes USD 191.27 million of repurchase facilities, matched by USD 186.51 million of mortgage loans held for sale, and USD 129.45 million of construction loans, while the issuer homebuilding debt of USD 1,491.87 million excludes both; its net homebuilding debt to net capital rose from 30.5% at Q1 2026 to 34.9% at Q3 2026. Reported EBITDA also adds back USD 64.13 million of interest charged through cost of sales over the last twelve months, 27.17% of the total, so a reader who treats that interest as an operating cost would see lower EBITDA and weaker cover.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_ccs-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 14; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ccs-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 66; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ccs-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 37; calculation HB-9M2026; calculation CFL-1; calculation NAR-NHD

Three data points need care. The fourth quarter of 2025 is not printed and is derived by subtraction, giving revenue of USD 1,233.58 million. The EBITDA definitions changed twice in 2025 and every supplied period is stated on the recast basis. Source readiness stated that no cash paid for interest figure exists in the sources; that is wrong for fiscal years, because the annual report prints approximately USD 78.9 million for 2024 and USD 81.3 million for 2025 in its debt note, though not in the cash flow statement and not for interim periods.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_ccs-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, pages 91–92; 10-K_2025-12-31_ccs-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 130; 10-K_2025-12-31_ccs-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 43; calculation Q4-R; Source readiness (CP-0)

The revolving credit facility was amended on 2026-09-30, raising commitments from USD 1,000 million to USD 1,200 million and extending most of them to 2030-11-01, which the liquidity at 2026-09-30 reflects. The HistoryMaker acquisition closed on 2026-10-07 for initial cash consideration of about USD 160.2 million and is excluded from every period figure; adding it to net debt gives USD 1,886.7 million and 7.99x last-twelve-months reported EBITDA, which overstates leverage because the acquired earnings are not in the denominator.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_ccs-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 7; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ccs-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 16; calculation PF-ND; calculation PF-LEV

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest case for a better credit view is asset and equity cover: inventories of USD 3,744.02 million are 2.07x total debt, equity is USD 2,561 million, the issuer net homebuilding debt to net capital is 34.9%, covenants are reported as met, no senior note matures before 2029 and the revolver was enlarged and extended. This case wins if inventories turn to cash at book value without further impairment, which needs margin to stabilise: GAAP homebuilding gross margin was 15.0% in Q3 2026 against 17.9% a year earlier, after USD 19.61 million of inventory impairment. Another quarter of negative operating cash flow with the revolver rising again would confirm the bearish view, and a recovery in deliveries and margin that lifts EBITDA while inventory shrinks would reverse it.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_ccs-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ccs-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 17; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ccs-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 37; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ccs-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 66; calculation NAR-INV

### Limitations

- Cash interest paid is available only as approximate annual figures printed outside the cash flow statement, so interim cover is a proxy and the fiscal-year cover ratios carry the rounding of those figures.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_ccs-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 130
- No stand-alone fourth quarter of 2025, no maturity ladder at 2026-09-30 and no pro forma balance sheet for the HistoryMaker acquisition are printed, so the quarterly series, the maturity chart and the post-balance-sheet view rest on subtraction, on the annual ladder or on stated consideration.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_ccs-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 130; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ccs-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 7
- Statements are in USD thousands as printed and the money tables in USD millions; a line a filing prints as a dash is treated as not supplied rather than as zero, and a line a filing does not print is not supplied for that filing.
