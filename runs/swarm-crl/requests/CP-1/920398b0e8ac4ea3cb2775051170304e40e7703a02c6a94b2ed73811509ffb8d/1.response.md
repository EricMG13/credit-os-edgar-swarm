## Findings

### Bottom line

Reported net debt is 5.06x LTM EBITDA at the third quarter of 2026, but impairments depress that EBITDA; before them it is 2.61x, so underlying leverage is moderate yet rising, and creditors are exposed to a revolver drawn for acquisitions.
Basis: calculation C10; calculation C14

Reported EBITDA covers cash interest 4.32x on an LTM basis, the 2028 notes and the 2029 revolver and notes are the maturities to watch, and contract EBITDA and covenant compliance at the third quarter are not established.
Basis: calculation C15; calculation C19; 10-K_2025-12-27_crl-20251227.htm.jsonl, page 152; 10-Q_2026-09-26_crl-20260926.htm.jsonl, pages 25–26

### Key findings

Reported leverage has risen sharply, and two causes are mixed together. Reported total debt to EBITDA went from 3.81x at the 2024 year-end to 4.99x at the 2025 year-end and 5.51x on an LTM basis at the third quarter of 2026, and net debt to EBITDA went from 3.48x to 4.49x and 5.06x. Reported EBITDA fell from USD 589,088 thousand to USD 428,474 thousand in fiscal 2025 mostly because goodwill and long-lived asset impairments rose from USD 266,825 thousand to USD 424,080 thousand, and carrying-value debt then rose by USD 306,825 thousand in the first nine months of 2026 as acquisitions, buybacks and deferred payments exceeded free cash flow and disposal proceeds.
Basis: calculation C10; calculation C11; calculation C12; calculation C13; 10-K_2025-12-27_crl-20251227.htm.jsonl, pages 115–118; 10-Q_2026-09-26_crl-20260926.htm.jsonl, pages 5–7

Before impairments the picture is milder. The house measure adds back the goodwill and long-lived asset impairment lines of the cash flow statement and puts net debt at 2.39x, 2.26x and 2.61x of EBITDA at the same three dates, so underlying leverage rose by about a third of a turn in 2026 where the reported measure rose by more than half a turn. It is an analyst measure, not the issuer's and not the covenant's, and it leaves in a gain of $38.5 million on a Wilmington property sale and a $44.6 million reserve release inside 2026 operating income while leaving out restructuring and deal costs.
Basis: calculation C8; calculation C9; calculation C14; 10-K_2025-12-27_crl-20251227.htm.jsonl, pages 117–118; 10-Q_2026-09-26_crl-20260926.htm.jsonl, page 7; EX-99.1_2026-11-04_crl3q26earningsrelease.htm.jsonl, pages 13–14

Interest cover on reported EBITDA is adequate but not wide. EBITDA covered cash interest paid 4.77x in fiscal 2024, 4.21x in fiscal 2025 and 4.32x on an LTM basis, and after capital expenditure the reported ratio is 2.06x for fiscal 2025. The weighted average interest rate on the debt edged up from 4.05% to 4.11% between the 2025 year-end and the third quarter, and cash interest paid was barely higher in the first nine months of 2026, USD 84,528 thousand against USD 83,407 thousand, while the issuer puts the rise in interest expense mainly on non-cash accretion of deferred purchase consideration.
Basis: calculation C15; calculation C16; 10-K_2025-12-27_crl-20251227.htm.jsonl, page 143; 10-Q_2026-09-26_crl-20260926.htm.jsonl, page 21; 10-Q_2026-09-26_crl-20260926.htm.jsonl, page 26; 10-Q_2026-09-26_crl-20260926.htm.jsonl, page 45

Cash generation is solid but is being spent on acquisitions and buybacks faster than it is earned. Operating cash flow was USD 474,887 thousand in the first nine months of 2026 against USD 590,126 thousand a year earlier, lower because of bonus payments and professional fees, and free cash flow after capital expenditure of USD 133,823 thousand was USD 341,064 thousand. Against that, the company paid USD 467,254 thousand for acquisitions, USD 324,105 thousand for share repurchases and USD 111,394 thousand of deferred and contingent consideration, and received USD 176,563 thousand from disposals, so the revolver absorbed the gap.
Basis: calculation C17; 10-Q_2026-09-26_crl-20260926.htm.jsonl, page 7; 10-Q_2026-09-26_crl-20260926.htm.jsonl, pages 46–47

Reported liquidity is cash of USD 200,381 thousand plus undrawn revolver commitments of USD 1,056,826 thousand, but usable liquidity is lower. Restricted cash of USD 48,700 thousand is excluded from cash, letters of credit of $21.7 million may or may not use the facility and the facility's leverage and cover tests may constrain drawing, and the sources do not say either way. The revolver was drawn to USD 1,165,073 thousand at the first quarter and has since been reduced to USD 943,174 thousand.
Basis: calculation C5; calculation C6; calculation C18; 10-Q_2026-09-26_crl-20260926.htm.jsonl, page 22; 10-Q_2026-09-26_crl-20260926.htm.jsonl, page 26; 10-Q_2026-03-28_crl-20260328.htm.jsonl, page 23; 10-Q_2026-06-27_crl-20260627.htm.jsonl, page 24

The maturity wall is concentrated in fiscal 2028 and 2029. The 2028 senior notes of USD 500,000 thousand fall due first, and in fiscal 2029 the drawn revolver and the 2029 senior notes together are USD 1,443,174 thousand at the third quarter, against USD 1,117,807 thousand printed for fiscal 2029 at the 2025 year-end. The ladder the annual report prints places USD 501,167 thousand in fiscal 2030, which is consistent with the 4.00% notes that are named as due in 2031, and the sources do not give the notes' exact maturity dates.
Basis: calculation C19; calculation C20; 10-K_2025-12-27_crl-20251227.htm.jsonl, pages 152–153; 10-Q_2026-09-26_crl-20260926.htm.jsonl, pages 25–26

Reported EBITDA and several quarters are constructed, not printed. The issuer states no EBITDA, so reported EBITDA is operating income plus depreciation and amortization, with third-quarter depreciation and amortization taken from the release schedule. A stand-alone fourth quarter of 2025 is not reported, so it is fiscal 2025 less nine months: operating income was a loss of USD 283,440 thousand and EBITDA a loss of USD 205,163 thousand, which is where the impairments fell.
Basis: calculation C1; calculation C7; EX-99.1_2026-11-04_crl3q26earningsrelease.htm.jsonl, page 13; 10-K_2025-12-27_crl-20251227.htm.jsonl, pages 115–118

Definitions differ and some are unresolved. Reported total debt to EBITDA is above the 4.25x covenant ceiling at both the 2025 year-end and the third quarter even though the company stated compliance at the 2025 year-end, so the contract EBITDA must be defined differently, and the size of that difference is not established. The balance-sheet debt line leaves out a current portion of USD 3,118 thousand, the Cambodian acquisition is priced at $510.0 million in the annual report and $507.3 million in the quarterly report, and the divestiture of the CDMO and Cell Solutions businesses carries a possible funding obligation of up to $45.0 million.
Basis: calculation C4; calculation C10; 10-K_2025-12-27_crl-20251227.htm.jsonl, page 137; 10-K_2025-12-27_crl-20251227.htm.jsonl, page 152; 10-Q_2026-09-26_crl-20260926.htm.jsonl, page 26; 10-Q_2026-09-26_crl-20260926.htm.jsonl, page 37

Events after the 2025 year-end are in the 2026 figures and are not blended into the 2025 column. The Cambodian primate supplier was acquired in January 2026 for $335.0 million paid at closing, PathoQuest was taken to full ownership in April 2026, and the CDMO, Cell Solutions and certain European Discovery Services businesses were sold in May 2026. No event after 2026-09-26 that changes net debt is stated in the sources, so no pro forma adjustment is made to the third-quarter figures.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-27_crl-20251227.htm.jsonl, page 137; 10-Q_2026-09-26_crl-20260926.htm.jsonl, pages 14–17; 10-Q_2026-09-26_crl-20260926.htm.jsonl, pages 36–37

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest case against the milder house view is that impairments recur: goodwill impairments of USD 215,000 thousand in fiscal 2024 and USD 165,000 thousand in fiscal 2025, restructuring charges of $99.8 million in fiscal 2025 and $107.0 million in fiscal 2024, and a Manufacturing goodwill balance that already carries impairments of $380 million. If further write-downs follow, the add-back becomes harder to defend and net debt to EBITDA nearer the reported 5.06x would be the better anchor than 2.61x.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-27_crl-20251227.htm.jsonl, pages 115–118; 10-Q_2026-09-26_crl-20260926.htm.jsonl, page 25; 10-Q_2026-09-26_crl-20260926.htm.jsonl, page 36; calculation C9; calculation C10

The view would change with the credit agreement's definition of EBITDA and a compliance certificate, which would show how far the contract measure sits from the reported one, with a stand-alone fourth quarter of 2025 and the issuer's non-GAAP operating income for fiscal 2025, which would allow an LTM issuer-adjusted ratio, and with any acquisition or buyback after the third quarter that is funded by further revolver drawings.

### Limitations

Every statement figure is read from page-numbered text extracts of the filings, not from the original documents, so a figure printed only in a table image or footnote could be missed, and the statements are in thousands of US dollars while the money tables are restated in millions at three decimals without rounding.

Reported EBITDA is built from operating income and depreciation and amortization, the three quarterly reports are unaudited with no auditor review report in the files, and the third-quarter columns take depreciation and amortization from the earnings release, so the EBITDA series is only as reliable as those components.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-11-04_crl3q26earningsrelease.htm.jsonl, page 13; 10-Q_2026-09-26_crl-20260926.htm.jsonl, pages 5–7

The house measure and every ratio on it are analyst judgement, they exclude restructuring, acquisition and other costs the issuer adds back, and they cannot be compared with the issuer's own adjusted figures, which the files give for 2026 only.

No ratio here is a covenant test, and no covenant headroom is stated or implied.
