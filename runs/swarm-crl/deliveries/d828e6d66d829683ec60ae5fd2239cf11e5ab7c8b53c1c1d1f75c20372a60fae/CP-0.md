## Findings

### Bottom line

The seven supplied filings and releases support the requested financial, business and fundamental credit work on a current base, but no loan documents, ratings or market data were supplied, so covenant, security and valuation views stay unassessed.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-27_crl-20251227.htm.jsonl, pages 179–180; 10-Q_2026-09-26_crl-20260926.htm.jsonl, pages 25–26

The third-quarter balance sheet is 40 days old at the as-of date, and total debt has grown USD 304.3 million since the 2025 year-end, so the third-quarter filing, not the annual report, must anchor every debt figure.
Basis: calculation C1; calculation C6; 10-Q_2026-09-26_crl-20260926.htm.jsonl, pages 25–26

### Key findings

Coverage of the requested periods is complete. The annual report for fiscal 2025 and the three 2026 quarterly reports each carry full statements and notes, and each quarterly report has a matching earnings release, so the first, second and third quarters of 2026, the nine months of 2026 and their 2025 comparatives are all printed. A stand-alone fourth quarter of 2025 is not supplied; it is the annual figure less the nine-month comparative, so Financial foundation can derive it and must label it as derived.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-27_crl-20251227.htm.jsonl, pages 115–118; 10-Q_2026-09-26_crl-20260926.htm.jsonl, pages 5–7; EX-99.1_2026-11-04_crl3q26earningsrelease.htm.jsonl, pages 9–13

The debt evidence is internally consistent. The third-quarter debt note foots to its printed total and bridges exactly to the balance-sheet line, and the year-end note foots to the principal maturity table, so the debt build can start from printed figures. The note lists one revolving facility, three senior note series and small other debt and finance leases, and the balance-sheet line leaves out the current portion, so total debt must be taken from the note and not from the balance sheet.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-26_crl-20260926.htm.jsonl, pages 25–26; 10-K_2025-12-27_crl-20251227.htm.jsonl, pages 152–153; calculation C3; calculation C4; calculation C5

Contract terms are known only second-hand. The annual report describes two maintenance tests, a minimum interest cover of 3.50x and a maximum leverage of 4.25x, and collateral over substantially all assets of the company, with compliance stated only at the 2025 year-end. The three 2026 quarterly reports and releases say nothing on covenants, and no credit agreement or indenture was supplied, so covenant definitions, guarantors and security cannot be read from the contracts and compliance at the third quarter is not established.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-27_crl-20251227.htm.jsonl, page 152; 10-K_2025-12-27_crl-20251227.htm.jsonl, pages 179–180

Nothing was supplied to price, rate or rank the credit. The files contain no ratings, trading levels, peer set, fund mandate or earlier run, so Security selection, Market-implied risk, Peer benchmark, Ratings & triggers and any portfolio-fit work cannot run on evidence and each must show the item as not established.

Comparability changed during 2026. The company acquired assets of a Cambodian non-human primate supplier in January 2026, took full control of PathoQuest in April 2026, and sold certain European Discovery Services businesses, the CDMO business and the Cell Solutions business in May 2026, so 2026 and 2025 revenue and segment figures are not like for like. The third-quarter release gives organic growth bridges that Financial foundation and Fundamental credit should use for any trend statement.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-11-04_crl3q26earningsrelease.htm.jsonl, pages 15–16; 10-Q_2026-09-26_crl-20260926.htm.jsonl, pages 12–17

Adjusted measures can be traced but not tied to contract definitions. The third-quarter release reconciles adjusted operating income and earnings per share to the GAAP figures by segment, so each adjustment can be read item by item, but the contractual EBITDA definition is not in the files, so any adjusted EBITDA used in a ratio is the analyst's own construction and must be labelled so.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-11-04_crl3q26earningsrelease.htm.jsonl, pages 12–15; 10-K_2025-12-27_crl-20251227.htm.jsonl, page 152

Governance evidence is partial. The annual report names the executive officers and the January 2026 leadership transition, but board composition, committee membership and pay sit in the 2026 proxy statement, which was not supplied, so Business & governance will rest on the annual and quarterly reports alone.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-27_crl-20251227.htm.jsonl, page 37; 10-K_2025-12-27_crl-20251227.htm.jsonl, page 176

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The filings are detailed enough that a covenant headroom estimate from printed figures looks possible, which argues against treating the loan documents as essential. It is not safe to do so: the test definitions of EBITDA, indebtedness and cash interest are contractual, the company's adjusted measures differ from them in ways the files do not show, and a headroom figure built from the filings would carry no authority, so none is produced here.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-27_crl-20251227.htm.jsonl, page 152; EX-99.1_2026-11-04_crl3q26earningsrelease.htm.jsonl, pages 12–13

The view would change if the credit agreement, the indentures or a compliance certificate were supplied, which would move Legal & covenants and Covenant capacity from conditional to ready, or if the text extracts were found to differ from the original filings, which would reopen every figure taken from them. A rating report or trading levels would likewise move Security selection and Market-implied risk.

### Limitations

Each source was supplied as page-numbered text extracted from the original filing, not as the original document, so table layout and footnote marks could not be checked against the printed page, and every page reference is an extract page, not a printed page number.

The dates for the three quarterly reports are signature dates, because the reports do not state when they were filed, so publication dates in the event table are signature dates.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-03-28_crl-20260328.htm.jsonl, page 50; 10-Q_2026-06-27_crl-20260627.htm.jsonl, page 54; 10-Q_2026-09-26_crl-20260926.htm.jsonl, page 56
