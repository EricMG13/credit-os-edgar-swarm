## Findings

### Bottom line
The seven supplied JetBlue filings and releases are enough for a consolidated financial, business and credit snapshot to the third quarter of 2026, but covenant levels, ratings and market pricing are not in them, so covenant headroom cannot be measured. Statements, debt, liquidity and obligations are printed for every quarter and for FY2025, so period comparisons need no outside data.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_jblu-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 112–115; 10-Q_2026-03-31_jblu-20260331.htm.jsonl, pages 5–8; 10-Q_2026-06-30_jblu-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 5–9; 10-Q_2026-09-30_jblu-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 5–8; 10-Q_2026-09-30_jblu-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 11–12; 10-Q_2026-09-30_jblu-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 46

The main trap is a changed liquidity definition. The second-quarter figure of $2.2bn excluded the undrawn $600 million Citibank line, while the third-quarter figure of $2.2bn includes it; on the earlier basis the third quarter is $1.6bn, which is $542 million below the second quarter. Liquidity trends should therefore be read on the earlier basis.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_jblu-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 32–33; 10-Q_2026-09-30_jblu-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 5, 14, 33, 44; EX-99.1_2026-10-26_ex991-earningsreleaseq32026.htm.jsonl, page 2; calculation C01; calculation C02; calculation C03; calculation C04

Executed credit agreements, the guarantor list and rating reports are not supplied, the notes describe the TrueBlue covenants without stating levels, and governance detail beyond executive officers sits in a proxy statement that is not supplied.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_jblu-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 59, 165–167, 168–180; 10-Q_2026-09-30_jblu-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 47
