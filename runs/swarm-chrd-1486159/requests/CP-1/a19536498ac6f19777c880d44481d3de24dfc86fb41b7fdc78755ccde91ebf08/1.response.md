## Findings

### Bottom line

Chord's reported balance sheet is lightly levered and the fall in leverage over 2026 is real, so creditors hold thick earnings cover over debt that matures no earlier than 2030. Net debt was 0.22x last-twelve-months EBITDA at 2026-09-30, against 0.77x at FY2025 and 0.36x at FY2024, with USD 2,774.4 million of liquidity.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_chrd_chrd-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 5; 10-Q_2026-09-30_chrd_chrd-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 22; 10-Q_2026-09-30_chrd_chrd-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 46–47; 10-K_2025-12-31_chrd_chrd-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 125–127; calculation C1; calculation C2

The comfort rests on derived EBITDA at oil prices well above 2025 levels, and on cash that includes a refundable sale deposit. EBITDA is derived because the issuer states none, interim cash interest is not printed, and covenant ratios are not reported, so headroom is not established.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_chrd_chrd-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 6; 10-Q_2026-09-30_chrd_chrd-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 8; 10-Q_2026-09-30_chrd_chrd-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 23; 10-Q_2026-09-30_chrd_chrd-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 37; 10-K_2025-12-31_chrd_chrd-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 164; Source readiness (CP-0)

### Analysis

Leverage is low and the improvement is not an artefact of the 2025 impairment. Excluding that non-cash charge, net debt over EBITDA was 0.58x at FY2025, still more than twice the 0.22x at 2026-09-30. Gross debt is almost unchanged since FY2025 while cash rose, so the fall comes from cash build and a larger EBITDA base, not from repayment.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_chrd_chrd-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 126–127; 10-Q_2026-09-30_chrd_chrd-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 5; 10-Q_2026-09-30_chrd_chrd-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 22; calculation C3; calculation C4

Interest cover is very high, though the FY2025 cash-basis figure of 32.26x understates the cost of the 2025 notes, which were outstanding for only part of that year. On last-twelve-months EBITDA the full coupons on the two notes are covered 32.27x and interest expense is covered 28.88x.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_chrd_chrd-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 128; 10-Q_2026-09-30_chrd_chrd-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 24; calculation C5; calculation C6

Cash flow converts well and funds shareholder returns without new debt. Free cash flow of USD 1,307.73 million in nine months 2026 was about half of EBITDA, and buybacks plus dividends took 48.77% of it, so the remainder built cash. Working capital was neutral: receivables rose while payables rose by a similar amount.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_chrd_chrd-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 8; calculation C7; calculation C8

Liquidity is ample and maturity risk is distant. Undrawn commitments of USD 1,969.7 million sit on top of cash, no debt falls due before the 2030 notes, and the October 2026 amendment moved the revolver maturity to 2031, with a springing maturity ahead of the 2030 notes only if more than USD 100.0 million remains and utilisation exceeds 80%.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_chrd_chrd-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 23; 10-Q_2026-09-30_chrd_chrd-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 46–47; 10-Q_2026-09-30_chrd_chrd-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 57; calculation C1

Definitions change the headline in three places. Cash includes a refundable USD 55.0 million deposit for the Marcellus Shale sale, which would lift net leverage to 0.24x if excluded. Derivative settlements sit in investing activities and are outside the derived EBITDA, so cash earnings after hedge payments were lower than EBITDA suggests. Carrying-value debt is USD 17.755 million below principal because of unamortised financing costs.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_chrd_chrd-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 5; 10-Q_2026-09-30_chrd_chrd-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 8; 10-Q_2026-09-30_chrd_chrd-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 21–22; calculation C9; calculation C10

A non-debt funding float is growing: royalties payable and revenue suspense rose 30.84% from FY2025 to 2026-09-30. It costs no interest but is owed to royalty owners and partners and tracks revenue, so it would unwind if revenue fell.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_chrd_chrd-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 147; 10-Q_2026-09-30_chrd_chrd-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 13; calculation C16

Data quality limits three conclusions. Nine-month 2026 net income includes an out-of-period tax benefit of USD 41.8 million that does not touch EBITDA; the issuer prints no EBITDA, adjusted EBITDA or covenant ratio; and interim cash interest paid is not printed, so cover on a cash basis is available only for full years.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_chrd_chrd-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 10; 10-Q_2026-09-30_chrd_chrd-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 26; 10-K_2025-12-31_chrd_chrd-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 164; Source readiness (CP-0)

After the balance-sheet date, share repurchases of USD 91.0 million through 2026-11-04 would lift net debt to pro forma levels with net leverage of 0.25x. The pending sale of the Marcellus Shale interest, with estimated gross proceeds of USD 550.0 million, cannot be reflected because closing, net proceeds and the EBITDA sold are not stated.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_chrd_chrd-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 21; 10-Q_2026-09-30_chrd_chrd-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 29; 10-Q_2026-09-30_chrd_chrd-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 37
