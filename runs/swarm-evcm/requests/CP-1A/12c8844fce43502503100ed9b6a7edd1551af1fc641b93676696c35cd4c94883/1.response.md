## Findings

### Bottom line
EverCommerce is a diversified, mostly recurring small-business software company whose main credit risk is control rather than customers: two sponsors hold about 85.7% of the stock with consent rights over key decisions, while growth is slowing and spending is outrunning revenue.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_evcm-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 95–98; 10-Q_2026-06-30_evcm-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 45–46

No sponsor action adverse to creditors is documented: the company has never paid a dividend, its refinancings extended maturities and cut margins, and the latest acquisition was paid in cash. Against that, the issuer reports an unremediated revenue-accounting control weakness and refers to a new chief executive without saying who, when or on what terms, so management continuity is a question, not a finding.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_evcm-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 114; 10-K_2025-12-31_evcm-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 186–187; 10-Q_2026-06-30_evcm-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 12; 10-K_2025-12-31_evcm-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 204–206; EX-99.1_2026-08-05_evcmq226earningsrelease.htm.jsonl, page 2

### Key findings

Revenue is broad and mostly recurring, so near-term cash flow does not depend on a few customers. About 93% of customers each contributed less than USD 2 thousand of revenue in fiscal 2025, and about 96% of revenue was recurring or re-occurring. The mechanism is dispersion across a large base, which supports debt service at a moderate leverage level. The issuer also warns of key customers and a more pronounced concentration in certain markets without sizing them, so dispersion is established by customer count, not by revenue share.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_evcm-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 9; 10-K_2025-12-31_evcm-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 53; 10-K_2025-12-31_evcm-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 120; calculation C01

Growth is modest and retention is slipping, which is the main operating threat to coverage. Revenue grew 3.2% in the first half of 2026 against 4.8% in fiscal 2025, and annualized net revenue retention was about 94% in the second quarter against about 97% a year earlier. A customer base that sheds revenue faster than it adds it forces more sales spending just to hold earnings steady.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_evcm-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 11; 10-Q_2026-06-30_evcm-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 33; 10-Q_2026-06-30_evcm-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 45

Spending is growing faster than revenue, so the earnings cushion is thinning. In the first half of 2026 sales and marketing rose 12.5% and product development 7.9%, together more than the whole revenue increase, and Adjusted EBITDA fell to USD 85.21 million from USD 89.90 million. If revenue growth does not recover, the same spending base erodes cash interest cover before it threatens liquidity.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_evcm-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 45–46; EX-99.1_2026-08-05_evcmq226earningsrelease.htm.jsonl, page 11; calculation C03

The acquisition programme has slowed and the latest purchase is dilutive in the near term. The company has acquired 54 companies since inception, paid about USD 36.0 million in cash for ZyraTalk in September 2025 with up to USD 6.5 million more contingent over three years, and added no debt to do so. In the first half of 2026 ZyraTalk contributed USD 1.8 million of revenue against USD 5.3 million of post-acquisition operating expense, so it adds to the earnings drag until it scales.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_evcm-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 20; 10-Q_2026-06-30_evcm-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 12; 10-K_2025-12-31_evcm-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 185; 10-Q_2026-06-30_evcm-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 45–47; calculation C06

Control sits with two sponsors whose consent rights reach the decisions creditors care about, but no creditor-adverse use of them is documented. While the sponsors together hold at least 30% of the stock they must consent to a change of control, to any acquisition or disposal above USD 500.0 million, to hiring or removing the chief executive and to insolvency filings, and each can nominate directors while it holds at least 5%. This gives them the discretion to change capital policy without creditor consent unless the credit agreement, which is not supplied, restrains it.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_evcm-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 95–98; 10-K_2025-12-31_evcm-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 114; 10-K_2025-12-31_evcm-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 208–209

Reporting quality is the weakest part of governance. The issuer has disclosed the same revenue-accounting control weakness in its fiscal 2023, 2024 and 2025 annual reports and its disclosure controls were not effective at June 30, 2026. The statements still carry a clean audit opinion, so the risk is that revenue, receivables and deferred revenue are less reliable than their precision suggests.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_evcm-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 204–206; 10-Q_2026-06-30_evcm-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 56

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a cautious view is the sponsors' conduct on the record: no dividend has ever been paid, the only shareholder payments are cash-funded share repurchases, and the 2024 and 2025 amendments pushed the term loan to July 2031 and lowered its margin. A business with hundreds of thousands of customers and recurring revenue can absorb slower growth without a funding event.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_evcm-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 114; 10-K_2025-12-31_evcm-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 186–187; 10-K_2025-12-31_evcm-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 9

A second counterpoint to the diversification finding is the issuer's own warning of key customers and more concentration in certain markets, which the filings do not size; a disclosed share above a tenth of revenue would weaken that finding. The view would improve if the control weakness is reported remediated, if retention and growth recover, and if the proxy statement and credit agreement show no related-party fees or distribution capacity beyond what the filings describe. It would worsen if the chief executive change proves disorderly, if the sponsors sell down or the board approves a debt-funded payment to shareholders, or if buybacks again exceed the cash the business generates.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_evcm-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 53; EX-99.1_2026-08-05_evcmq226earningsrelease.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-06-30_evcm-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 56; 10-K_2025-12-31_evcm-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 208–209

### Limitations

- The proxy statement that holds related-party transactions, sponsor fees, executive pay and each sponsor's individual percentage was not supplied, so the finding that no creditor-adverse sponsor action is documented covers only the annual and quarterly filings and releases read.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_evcm-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 206–207; 10-K_2025-12-31_evcm-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 98
- Revenue by vertical, the size of the key customers the issuer warns about and any investor presentation were not supplied, so the diversification finding rests on customer counts and revenue type, not on business-line or customer shares.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_evcm-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 14; 10-Q_2026-06-30_evcm-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 28; 10-K_2025-12-31_evcm-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 9; 10-K_2025-12-31_evcm-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 53
- The only reference to a chief executive transition is one sentence in the latest release, so the management-continuity point is an open question and no view is taken on who leads or when.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-08-05_evcmq226earningsrelease.htm.jsonl, page 2
- The credit agreement that limits dividends, investments and asset sales was not supplied, so what the sponsors may do with cash, as opposed to what they have done, is not assessed.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_evcm-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 208–209; 10-K_2025-12-31_evcm-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 114

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

No new debt was issued in the period read. The company repriced and extended its term loan and revolver three times: an amendment on December 13, 2024 cut the margin and removed the credit spread adjustment; one on June 10, 2025 reduced the revolver to USD 155.0 million and extended USD 125.0 million of it; and one on July 29, 2025 pushed the term loan maturity to July 6, 2031 and the extended revolver to July 29, 2030. For creditors this removed any maturity before 2030 and lowered cash interest, and it shows lenders were willing to extend.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_evcm-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 186–187; 10-Q_2026-06-30_evcm-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 19–20

The company completed one acquisition and two disposals. ZyraTalk, an AI customer-engagement business, was bought on September 15, 2025 for about USD 36.0 million in cash, with up to USD 6.5 million of contingent consideration over three years. The marketing technology solutions business was sold on October 31, 2025 for about USD 45.0 million in cash and fitness solutions were sold in 2024. The disposals shrink revenue and are presented as discontinued operations, so fiscal and quarterly comparisons exclude them.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_evcm-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 12–13; 10-K_2025-12-31_evcm-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 9–10
