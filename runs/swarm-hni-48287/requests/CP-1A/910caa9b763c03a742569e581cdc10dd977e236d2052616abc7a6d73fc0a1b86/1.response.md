## Findings

### Bottom line

Creditors face a listed issuer with no identified sponsor or controlling holder, so Board discipline is the control, and it is untested: net debt of USD 1,244.2 million is above year-end while shareholder returns were widened, so risk is Medium.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-10-03_hni-20261003.htm.jsonl, pages 3, 21, 37; 10-K_2026-01-03_hni-20260103.htm.jsonl, pages 42, 74; calculation A1; calculation A2

Net debt was USD 1,083.9 million at year-end, and the nine months since brought free cash flow below dividends, USD 46.4 million of dealer investments and a USD 56.0 million repayment of loans against company-owned life insurance. Governance is otherwise conventional and transparent: the audit opinion is unqualified, acquisition accounting is updated each quarter, and the five filings document no priming, drop-down or related-party extraction. The gaps are provisional purchase accounting, a control review that excluded Steelcase, which is 63% of total assets, and credit documents that were not supplied.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-10-03_hni-20261003.htm.jsonl, pages 4–5, 25, 42; 10-K_2026-01-03_hni-20260103.htm.jsonl, pages 61, 69–73; calculation A2; calculation A3; calculation A24; calculation A36

### Key findings

No sponsor means no sponsor behaviour to score, and that is not an adverse finding. The filings describe one class of common stock listed on the New York Stock Exchange with no preferred stock outstanding, no private-equity owner and no controlling holder identified. Former Steelcase shareholders were issued shares equal to 34.87% of the shares now outstanding, two of their directors joined a twelve-member Board and three named Steelcase shareholders signed voting support agreements for the merger, which points to larger holders among them, but the stakes are set out in a proxy statement that was not supplied and concentration is not established.
Basis: 10-K_2026-01-03_hni-20260103.htm.jsonl, pages 43, 67, 74, 92; 10-Q_2026-10-03_hni-20261003.htm.jsonl, pages 1, 22; calculation A14

The Steelcase acquisition is a transformational, debt-funded step and the main governance risk. Total consideration was USD 1,922.3 million, of which cash was 44.97% and shares and replacement awards the remaining 55.03%, and USD 865 million of new secured facilities were drawn; total debt rose from USD 344.6 million at the end of FY2024 to USD 1,293.1 million at the end of FY2025, an increase to 3.75x the earlier level. Paying most of the price in shares limited the debt needed, but it also issued a new shareholder group a third of the equity.
Basis: 10-K_2026-01-03_hni-20260103.htm.jsonl, pages 74, 90–93, 103–104; 10-K_2024-12-28_hni-20241228.htm.jsonl, page 64; calculation A4; calculation A7; calculation A8; calculation A9; calculation A10; calculation A11; calculation A12

Financial policy has not yet shown deleveraging: net debt rose USD 160.3 million from year-end to 2026-10-03. Nine-month operating cash flow of USD 163.3 million less capital expenditure of USD 91.1 million left USD 72.2 million, which is below dividends paid of USD 77.6 million, and cash also went to USD 46.4 million of dealer investments, USD 24.1 million to acquire a dealer and a USD 56.0 million loan repayment. The company says earnings and cash are weighted to the second half, so nine months is a seasonally weak window, but the paydown that would reassure creditors is not yet visible.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-10-03_hni-20261003.htm.jsonl, pages 3–5, 29, 36; calculation A3; calculation A24; calculation A25

Shareholder returns were widened at the same time, a modest negative because they compete with debt reduction while the covenant tightens. On 2026-08-17 the Board declared a quarterly dividend of USD 0.35 per share, up 2.9%, and added USD 200 million to the repurchase authorization, which stood at USD 277.9 million at 2026-10-03 against USD 5.6 million actually spent in nine months. The company says it is committed to maintaining or modestly increasing the dividend, so the dividend is a hard-to-cut claim on cash, while the maximum net leverage ratio steps down from 4.25x to 4.00x and then to 3.50x within seven full quarters of closing.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-10-03_hni-20261003.htm.jsonl, pages 5, 18, 21, 37, 43; calculation A33; calculation A34; calculation A35

The record after the 2023 Kimball International acquisition was creditor-aligned, which is the best evidence of how the Board treats acquisition debt. Total debt fell USD 91.2 million to USD 344.6 million during FY2024 as USD 100 million of the acquisition term loan was prepaid, even though dividends of USD 63.6 million and share purchases of USD 65.8 million continued. It was a smaller deal than Steelcase, so it supports intent rather than proving capacity.
Basis: 10-K_2024-12-28_hni-20241228.htm.jsonl, pages 64, 77–78; 10-K_2026-01-03_hni-20260103.htm.jsonl, pages 75–76, 106; calculation A4; calculation A5; calculation A6

Disclosure is detailed, but the control picture is incomplete for the largest asset base. KPMG gave unqualified opinions and found internal control effective, yet management and the auditor excluded Steelcase, which is 63% of total assets and 7% of net sales, and the auditor named the preliminary valuation of acquired property and intangible assets as a critical audit matter. The allocation has already moved: goodwill fell USD 121.3 million and intangible assets rose USD 281.0 million, so assets and amortization can still change when the allocation is finalised in the fourth quarter of 2026.
Basis: 10-K_2026-01-03_hni-20260103.htm.jsonl, pages 61, 69–73; 10-Q_2026-10-03_hni-20261003.htm.jsonl, pages 10–11, 42; calculation A30; calculation A31; calculation A36

Creditor treatment so far is mixed rather than adverse, and legal capacity cannot be tested. The exchange offer moved USD 351 million of Steelcase notes into new HNI senior secured notes and left USD 99 million, 22.00% of the notes, as unsecured obligations of a subsidiary, so the two groups of holders now hold different claims; the June 2026 term loan B refinancing lowered the borrowing rate with other terms substantially unchanged. No uptier, drop-down, coercive exchange or collateral release appears in the five filings, but the credit agreement limits debt, liens, investments and restricted payments on customary terms whose baskets were not supplied.
Basis: 10-K_2026-01-03_hni-20260103.htm.jsonl, pages 37, 102–106; 10-Q_2026-10-03_hni-20261003.htm.jsonl, pages 15–18; calculation A28; calculation A29

The earnings base is still being reset around one integrated furniture business. Workplace Furnishings is 88.99% of nine-month net sales and Steelcase alone is 55.55% of consolidated sales, while Residential Building Products earns 31.19% of segment operating income on 11.01% of sales. The Workplace Furnishings margin was 4.86% against 9.93% a year earlier after USD 110.9 million of Steelcase purchase accounting and USD 35.9 million of restructuring, and management calls synergies slightly ahead of expectations without stating an amount.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-10-03_hni-20261003.htm.jsonl, pages 11, 25, 30; calculation A16; calculation A17; calculation A18; calculation A19; calculation A20; calculation A23

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a Medium level is that most of the Steelcase price was paid in shares, that the Board repaid acquisition debt after the Kimball acquisition, and that operations are improving: third-quarter net income was USD 90.0 million against USD 41.2 million a year earlier, and the company states it was in compliance with its financial covenants at 2026-10-03. If the second-half cash flow and synergy delivery hold, net debt should fall in the fourth quarter and the level would move toward Low.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-10-03_hni-20261003.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 18, 30; 10-K_2024-12-28_hni-20241228.htm.jsonl, pages 77–78; 10-K_2026-01-03_hni-20260103.htm.jsonl, page 106; calculation A11

The view would move toward High if the Board funded repurchases or dividends with new borrowing while the covenant ladder tightens, if a waiver or amendment became necessary, if a single holder emerged with board control, or if the executed documents showed wide capacity to move assets or priority away from lenders. Receipt of the credit agreement, the indenture and the proxy statement would settle the legal-capacity and ownership questions in either direction.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-10-03_hni-20261003.htm.jsonl, pages 18, 37; 10-K_2026-01-03_hni-20260103.htm.jsonl, page 37

### Limitations

- The proxy statement, which carries the beneficial ownership table, board independence and executive pay, was not supplied, so the finding that no sponsor or controlling holder exists rests on the annual report alone and would change if a large holder or special board rights were disclosed.
- The credit agreement and the indenture were not supplied, so legal capacity for dividends, repurchases, investments and new debt is not established; the Medium level rests on documented behaviour, and wide baskets would raise the risk of extraction without any change in conduct.
- Steelcase purchase accounting is provisional and its controls were outside the management and audit assessments, so the integration-risk reading could change when the allocation is final in the fourth quarter of 2026.
- Synergy delivery is described in words only, with no amount or target in the filings, so the contrary case that integration is ahead of plan rests on management language and cannot move the level on its own.
- No filing dated after 2026-11-03 was supplied, so shareholder-return or financing decisions taken since then are not established and could change the net debt trend that the Medium level relies on.

### Sponsor and governance risk level

The risk level is Medium, driven by repeated debt-funded acquisitions, widened shareholder returns while net debt has not fallen, and a control and accounting position that is not yet final, because together they slow deleveraging and leave creditors dependent on Board choices that cannot be checked against the credit documents. Countervailing evidence is the share-funded 55.03% of the Steelcase price, the debt repaid after the Kimball acquisition, an unqualified audit opinion and the absence of any documented priming or extraction.
Basis: 10-K_2026-01-03_hni-20260103.htm.jsonl, pages 61, 69–73, 90–93; 10-K_2024-12-28_hni-20241228.htm.jsonl, pages 77–78; 10-Q_2026-10-03_hni-20261003.htm.jsonl, pages 5, 21, 37; calculation A3; calculation A11

Sponsor / Governance Risk Level: Medium

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

The Steelcase merger was agreed on 2025-08-03 and completed on 2025-12-10 for total fair value of USD 1,922.3 million, made up of cash of USD 864.5 million, shares valued at USD 1,012.5 million and replacement awards valued at USD 45.4 million. Holders received USD 7.20 in cash plus a fixed fraction of an HNI share per Steelcase share, or an election that was prorated back to that mix, and the Board was enlarged by two to twelve seats for two Steelcase directors. Purchase accounting is provisional, with the allocation expected to be final in the fourth quarter of 2026.
Basis: 10-K_2026-01-03_hni-20260103.htm.jsonl, pages 90–93; 10-Q_2026-10-03_hni-20261003.htm.jsonl, pages 9–11; calculation A9

The cash price was financed with new senior secured facilities of USD 865 million under a credit agreement dated 2025-09-05, which equals the revolver, term loan A and term loan B balances at year-end, and with Steelcase cash that left a net cash outlay of USD 390.3 million. A further USD 351 million of Steelcase 5.125% notes due 2029 were exchanged into new HNI senior secured notes, leaving USD 99 million as unsecured obligations of Steelcase. In June 2026 the term loan B was refinanced with a USD 499 million tranche due 2032, and in September 2026 a dealer acquisition added USD 39.2 million of debt at 2026-10-03.
Basis: 10-K_2026-01-03_hni-20260103.htm.jsonl, pages 63, 92, 102–106; 10-Q_2026-07-04_hni-20260704.htm.jsonl, page 14; 10-Q_2026-10-03_hni-20261003.htm.jsonl, pages 7–8, 14, 15–18; calculation A12; calculation A13; calculation A27

The filings do not give a sources and uses table, the terms of the indenture or the Amendment No. 3 margin, so the split of the facilities between price, repayment of existing debt and fees is not established, and the lien and guarantee terms of the secured notes are not known.
Basis: 10-K_2026-01-03_hni-20260103.htm.jsonl, pages 92, 102–106; Source readiness (CP-0)
