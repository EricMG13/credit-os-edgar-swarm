## Findings

### Bottom line

KBR is a government-weighted services group whose creditor perimeter is about to shrink: the Mission Technology Solutions business to be spun off supplied 65.2% of nine-month revenue and 56.8% of Adjusted EBITDA, so creditors depend on a debt reduction that is not yet quantified.
The remaining group, Sustainable Technology Solutions plus corporate costs, would carry USD 336 million of nine-month Adjusted EBITDA if corporate costs stayed in full, 43.2% of the consolidated figure.
Basis: calculation M1; calculation M3; 10-Q_2026-10-02_kbr-20261002.htm.jsonl, page 33; 10-Q_2026-10-02_kbr-20261002.htm.jsonl, page 52

Operations are steady rather than strained: U.S. government customers were 57% of fiscal 2025 revenue, backlog is USD 17,718 million, and no sponsor or extraction behaviour is documented. Governance detail for creditors is thin because board and ownership information sits in a proxy statement that is not supplied.
Basis: 10-K_2026-01-02_kbr-20260102.htm.jsonl, page 15; 10-Q_2026-10-02_kbr-20261002.htm.jsonl, page 46; 10-K_2026-01-02_kbr-20260102.htm.jsonl, page 2

### Key findings

- Government demand is the anchor and the main exposure. U.S. government customers produced 57% of fiscal 2025 revenue and U.K. government customers 9%, and cost-reimbursable work was 38.2% of nine-month revenue in 2026, which passes cost overruns to customers but ties revenue to appropriations; a continuing resolution funds the U.S. government only through 2026-12-11 and no fiscal 2027 budget is enacted, so award timing is the near-term risk.
  Basis: 10-K_2026-01-02_kbr-20260102.htm.jsonl, page 15; 10-Q_2026-10-02_kbr-20261002.htm.jsonl, page 37; calculation M6
- The two segments are diverging, and the faster-growing one is the one that stays. Mission Technology Solutions revenue fell 4.3% in the nine months on lower NASA-related programme revenue and lower European command activity, while Sustainable Technology Solutions revenue rose 11.1% and its backlog rose 35.2% to USD 5,828 million, although its operating income fell 8% on licensing mix.
  Basis: 10-Q_2026-10-02_kbr-20261002.htm.jsonl, page 44; 10-Q_2026-10-02_kbr-20261002.htm.jsonl, page 46; calculation M7
- The spin-off, not operations, is the main credit event. The business to be spun off supplied 65.2% of revenue and 56.8% of Adjusted EBITDA, segment-level corporate costs of USD 71 million sit with neither segment today, and the filings say only that it will pay KBR a cash distribution from its own financing to reduce debt; the amount, the instruments and the debt each company keeps are not stated, so the remaining group's leverage cannot be measured.
  Basis: 10-Q_2026-10-02_kbr-20261002.htm.jsonl, page 52; EX-99.1_2026-10-29_q326kbr-exhibit991x10022026.htm.jsonl, page 14; calculation M1; calculation M3
- Shareholder returns are material but have been funded from operating cash flow, and the sources name no sponsor or controlling holder. Repurchases and dividends took 74.1% of fiscal 2025 operating cash flow from continuing operations and 27.8% in the nine months to 2026-10-02, as repurchases fell to USD 29 million with USD 402 million of authorisation remaining.
  Basis: 10-K_2026-01-02_kbr-20260102.htm.jsonl, page 80; 10-Q_2026-10-02_kbr-20261002.htm.jsonl, page 58; calculation M8
- Joint ventures and performance guarantees create exposure outside reported debt. Equity in earnings of unconsolidated affiliates was 31.0% of nine-month operating income, services to joint ventures were USD 710 million of fiscal 2025 revenue, joint venture partners are usually jointly and severally liable for performance guarantees with no maximum estimable, and a USD 41 million Kuwait court judgment against KBR is unaccrued and under appeal; the Aspire Defence bonds are non-recourse to KBR.
  Basis: 10-K_2026-01-02_kbr-20260102.htm.jsonl, page 148; 10-Q_2026-10-02_kbr-20261002.htm.jsonl, page 24; 10-Q_2026-10-02_kbr-20261002.htm.jsonl, page 54; calculation M10; calculation M12
- Disclosure is adequate for performance but thin for governance. Internal control over financial reporting was audited as effective at fiscal year-end 2025 and the auditor has served since 2005, but board composition, ownership and pay sit in a proxy statement that is not supplied, 52 shareholders of record are reported, and a multi-phase systems implementation is altering controls.
  Basis: 10-K_2026-01-02_kbr-20260102.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-K_2026-01-02_kbr-20260102.htm.jsonl, page 80; 10-K_2026-01-02_kbr-20260102.htm.jsonl, page 180; 10-Q_2026-10-02_kbr-20261002.htm.jsonl, page 58

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest case against caution is that the spin-off is built to cut debt and the business that keeps the name has the faster-growing order book: backlog covers 2.27x trailing revenue, Sustainable Technology Solutions backlog rose 35.2%, the issuer reports net leverage of 2.1x, and KBR intends to apply the cash distribution to reduce indebtedness. The view would turn constructive if the registration statement shows separation financing large enough to retire debt in line with the 56.8% of Adjusted EBITDA that leaves, and it would worsen if the distribution is small, because the remaining group would then carry most of the existing debt on 43.2% of earnings at best. A lapse in U.S. government funding after 2026-12-11 or a further decline in NASA-related work would weaken the Mission Technology Solutions earnings that support the spin-off financing.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-10-02_kbr-20261002.htm.jsonl, page 46; EX-99.1_2026-10-29_q326kbr-exhibit991x10022026.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-10-02_kbr-20261002.htm.jsonl, page 52; calculation M1; calculation M3; calculation M9

### Limitations

- The registration statement on Form 10 and the proxy statement are not supplied, so the post-separation debt allocation, the governance of both companies and ownership detail are not established, which limits the perimeter conclusion to the pre-separation group.
- Beneficial ownership is not stated in the sources, so the absence of a controlling holder rests on the filings naming none and on 52 holders of record, and it would change if a large holder were disclosed.
- Segment results sit on two bases, the fiscal 2025 alignment in the annual report and the 2026 alignment with recast comparatives, so the segment-mix conclusions use only the nine-month 2026 and 2025 figures.
- Backlog is the issuer's own measure and includes its share of unconsolidated joint ventures, so the coverage figure overstates consolidated revenue visibility.
- No investor presentation is supplied, so the strategy language comes from filings and releases and is treated as management language.
