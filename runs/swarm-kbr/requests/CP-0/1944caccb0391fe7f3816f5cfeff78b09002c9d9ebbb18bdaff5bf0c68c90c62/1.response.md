## Findings

### Bottom line

The supplied filings support a credit snapshot of KBR as of 2026-11-05 only for the group before the planned January 2027 spin-off of Mission Technology Solutions, so creditors cannot yet see the debt each company will carry.
The filings say only that the business being spun off will pay KBR a cash distribution from its own financing, to be used to reduce debt, and the amount and the debt each company keeps are not established.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-10-02_kbr-20261002.htm.jsonl, page 33; 10-Q_2026-10-02_kbr-20261002.htm.jsonl, page 52; EX-99.1_2026-10-29_q326kbr-exhibit991x10022026.htm.jsonl, page 6

For the existing group, the latest quarterly report was published 7 days before the as-of date, and total debt of USD 2,541 million and the covenant levels are stated in the notes. No credit agreement, indenture, rating, market price, separation registration statement or proxy statement is supplied, so covenant headroom, tranche-level maturities and post-separation leverage are not established.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-10-02_kbr-20261002.htm.jsonl, page 17; 10-Q_2026-10-02_kbr-20261002.htm.jsonl, page 19; calculation C1; calculation C8
