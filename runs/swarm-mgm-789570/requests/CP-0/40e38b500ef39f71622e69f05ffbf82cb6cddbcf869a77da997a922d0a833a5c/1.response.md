## Findings

### Bottom line

The supplied annual reports, quarterly reports and earnings releases are sufficient for the historical financial, business and fundamental credit work this snapshot asks for, but not for covenant, rating or market-pricing conclusions, which remain gaps.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_mgm-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 2–4; 10-K_2025-12-31_mgm-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 92–96; EX-99.1_2026-10-28_mgmex991q32026earningrelea.htm.jsonl, pages 10–11; calculation C1; calculation C7; calculation C10

Creditors can use the audited statements to 2025-12-31 and the unaudited statements to 2026-09-30 for the consolidated group, including the Macau subsidiary's debt. They cannot test covenant headroom or security, because no credit agreement or indenture is in the set and the filings name covenants without printing levels. Ratings, market prices and the terms of the amended voting agreement announced on 2026-10-28 are also not established.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_mgm-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 134–139; 10-K_2025-12-31_mgm-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 115; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mgm-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 13; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mgm-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 14; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mgm-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 35; EX-99.1_2026-10-28_mgmex991q32026earningrelea.htm.jsonl, page 1
