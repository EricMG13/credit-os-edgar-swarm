## Findings

### Bottom line

Net leverage fell to 1.47x adjusted EBITDA at 2026-09-30 from 1.72x at year-end, helped by asset-sale proceeds and free cash flow, while adjusted EBITDA over the last twelve months is below FY2025 and reported EBITDA is flattered by a one-off gain.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_mgm-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 2–4; 10-K_2025-12-31_mgm-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 94–96; calculation C6; calculation C7; calculation C11

Creditors should read the improvement as balance-sheet repair rather than earnings growth: cash interest cover on adjusted EBITDA slipped to 5.82x from 6.23x, and the cushion is cash and an undrawn parent revolver, with nothing maturing before 2027. Lease obligations, rent and the MGM Osaka commitment sit outside Total Debt, and MGM China debt and cash sit in a subsidiary owned about 56% by MGM.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_mgm-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 13; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mgm-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 35; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mgm-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 36; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mgm-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 6; calculation C7; calculation C12

### Leverage trajectory and drivers

Net debt fell by USD 894.118 million between 2025-12-31 and 2026-09-30, to USD 3,273.029 million, because gross debt at carrying value fell by USD 561.155 million after the parent repaid USD 400 million of notes at maturity with cash, MGM China cut its revolving borrowings by USD 156.876 million and refinanced its USD 750 million of maturing notes with new 2033 notes, and the Northfield Park sale brought in USD 513.462 million, while cash rose by USD 332.963 million. Net leverage on last-twelve-months adjusted EBITDA is 1.47x, down from 1.72x, and total leverage is 2.54x against 2.57x. The denominator moved the other way: adjusted EBITDA for the twelve months is USD 2,228.592 million against USD 2,425.623 million for FY2025, a change of -8.12%, so the lower ratios depend on the debt reduction holding while earnings stabilise.
Basis: calculation C6; calculation C7; calculation C11; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mgm-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mgm-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 13

Reported EBITDA, the method's primary denominator, gives 1.39x net and 2.41x total leverage at 2026-09-30, lower than the adjusted ratios of 1.47x and 2.54x. The reason is that reported EBITDA, operating income plus depreciation and amortization, includes a net gain on property transactions of USD 256.229 million over the twelve months (mainly the gain on the Northfield Park sale and on the partial termination of the VICI lease) and is reduced by a goodwill impairment of USD 133.813 million, while the issuer's adjusted measure removes both. Creditors should use the adjusted ratios for the trend and treat the reported ratios as the stated primary basis, not as evidence of stronger earnings.
Basis: calculation C2; calculation C8; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mgm-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 12; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mgm-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 31

### Coverage trends

Cash interest cover on adjusted EBITDA is 5.82x for the twelve months to 2026-09-30, against 6.23x for FY2025 and 5.93x for FY2024, because cash interest paid is little changed (USD 382.798 million for the twelve months against USD 389.128 million) while adjusted EBITDA is lower. On reported EBITDA cover rose to 6.14x from 5.19x for the reasons given above. Adjusted EBITDA is struck after triple net lease rent expense of USD 1,665.718 million in the nine months of 2026, so cover is already a rent-burdened measure; the interest-paid figure is consolidated and includes MGM China.
Basis: calculation C7; calculation C4; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mgm-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mgm-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 20

### Cash-flow quality

Operating cash flow was USD 1,659.066 million in the nine months of 2026 against USD 1,874.383 million a year earlier, a change of -11.49%, while capital expenditure fell to USD 617.706 million from USD 772.472 million, so operating cash flow after capital expenditure was USD 1,041.360 million against USD 1,101.911 million. That cash went to USD 262.499 million of share repurchases, USD 138.866 million of distributions to noncontrolling owners and USD 154.917 million of investment in unconsolidated affiliates, mainly MGM Osaka; the sale proceeds of USD 513.462 million and the retained balance paid down debt. Buybacks are the discretionary item to watch, because they compete with deleveraging while adjusted EBITDA is falling.
Basis: calculation C13; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mgm-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mgm-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 34

### Liquidity adequacy

Cash of USD 2,395.957 million, of which MGM China held USD 598 million, and USD 2,275.000 million undrawn on the parent revolving facility after USD 25 million of letters of credit compare with no debt maturing before 2027, USD 1,425.000 million maturing in 2027 and USD 750.000 million in 2028. Against that the issuer expects cash interest of USD 315 million to USD 335 million over the next twelve months, annual cash rent of USD 1.8 billion, remaining 2026 capital expenditure of USD 360 million to USD 410 million and about USD 2.1 billion still to fund for MGM Osaka through 2028, part of which it expects to finance with the yen facility. Liquidity covers the maturities comfortably, but fixed claims are large, MGM China cash is held in a 56%-owned subsidiary and reaches the parent through dividends, and the undrawn MGM China facility is not counted because its USD amount is not stated.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_mgm-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 35; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mgm-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 18; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mgm-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 13; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mgm-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 17; calculation C12

### Data-quality and definition risks

Four risks bear on how the figures should be used. Reported and adjusted EBITDA differ by the one-off items above, and neither is a covenant measure because the credit agreements are not in the set. Balance-sheet cash differs from the cash-flow closing balance by MGM China's restricted cash. The printed net change in cash includes a held-for-sale cash line, so the fixed Net Cash Change row is withheld where it applies. Debt at carrying value omits operating lease liabilities of USD 23.9 billion and finance lease liabilities, which the issuer presents on their own lines, so lease-burdened leverage is far higher than the ratios shown and is not calculated here.
Basis: calculation C9; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mgm-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 10; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mgm-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 15; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mgm-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 16; calculation C8

### Subsequent and multi-figure events

One event after the balance-sheet date changes net debt: the October 2026 funding of JPY19.4 billion, about USD 123 million, of the MGM Osaka commitment, which would lift net debt to USD 3,396.029 million and net leverage to 1.45x on reported EBITDA; the day is not stated. The agreement to sell MGM Springfield for USD 443 million is described as subject to approvals and other conditions, with no closing date, so no pro forma effect is stated. The Northfield Park sale is one economic event with several figures: a gross price of USD 546 million, net consideration of USD 541 million in the second-quarter report and USD 540 million in the third-quarter report, a gain of USD 255 million and then USD 253 million, and cash proceeds of USD 513.462 million in the cash flow statement, which is close to the net consideration less the USD 26 million of cash derecognized with the business although the filings give no reconciliation; the later report's figures are used.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_mgm-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 17; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mgm-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 13; 10-Q_2026-06-30_mgm-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 12; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mgm-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 4; calculation C12

### Non-debt funding float

Customer-funded contract liabilities, which carry no interest, were USD 1,244.996 million at 2026-09-30 against USD 1,280.725 million at 2025-12-31, led by customer advances and other (USD 851.424 million), which include casino front money and advance deposits. They are credit-relevant working-capital float rather than ordinary payables: they fund operations while guests hold deposits, chips and loyalty credits, and they can reverse quickly if play or confidence weakens, which would draw on cash that net debt already nets off. The float is about 0.38x net debt, a size that deserves monitoring even though it fell slightly in 2026.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_mgm-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 11; 10-K_2025-12-31_mgm-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 113; calculation C14
