## Findings

### Bottom line

The five filings are enough for a first-pass credit view of Installed Building Products, Inc., with two limits that matter to creditors: the latest balance sheet predates a USD 250.0 million term loan add-on, and no debt agreements or proxy statement were supplied.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_ibp-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 33–34; 10-K_2025-12-31_ibp-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 157

Two annual reports and three quarterly reports describe one consolidated registrant and the comparatives checked agree, so period and perimeter are not in doubt. Debt changed materially in 2026, with the 2028 notes replaced by 2034 notes and the revolver enlarged, so year-end and September figures must be kept apart. Governance depth and covenant thresholds cannot be tested beyond what the filings print.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_ibp-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ibp-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 11–13; 10-K_2025-12-31_ibp-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 101–102; 10-K_2025-12-31_ibp-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 124; 10-K_2025-12-31_ibp-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 157; 10-K_2024-12-31_ibp-20241231.htm.jsonl, pages 98–99
