## Findings

### Bottom line

DXP is an acquisitive, listed industrial distributor with no sponsor or parent named in the sources, so creditors rely on management restraint: USD 153.6 million went on acquisitions in nine months 2026 and a USD 150.0 million buyback was authorized on 2026-10-28.
Basis: calculation A01; calculation A08; 10-Q_2026-09-30_dxpe-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 18; 10-Q_2026-09-30_dxpe-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 21

Operating risk is moderate: no customer reaches 10% of sales, nine-month organic growth was 9.3%, and USD 411.0 million of liquidity sits against USD 839.5 million of debt. The view stays cautious because the chief executive's holding and the board's independence are not established and acquired earnings are not disclosed, so leverage after the deals cannot be tested.
Basis: calculation A04; calculation A11; 10-K_2025-12-31_dxpe-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 13; 10-Q_2026-09-30_dxpe-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 11; 10-Q_2026-09-30_dxpe-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 33; Source readiness (CP-0)

### Key findings

Growth is partly bought, which makes acquisition discipline the central credit variable. Sales rose 13.19% in nine months 2026 and organic sales rose 9.26%; acquired businesses supplied 8.27% of sales and 33.88% of Innovative Pumping Solutions sales in the third quarter, and 18 businesses cost USD 407.7 million across 2024, 2025 and nine months 2026.
Basis: calculation A03; calculation A04; 10-K_2025-12-31_dxpe-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 94; 10-K_2025-12-31_dxpe-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 96; 10-Q_2026-09-30_dxpe-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 18; 10-Q_2026-09-30_dxpe-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 20

Acquisitions outran free cash flow and were met from cash that the December 2025 term loan increase appears to have supplied. Free cash flow of USD 70.5 million covered 55.84% of the USD 126.2 million spent in nine months 2026 on acquisitions net of cash acquired, and the USD 205.0 million of new term loan commitments preceded the spending. The filings do not state what the loan proceeds were for, so this link is an analyst interpretation.
Basis: calculation A02; calculation A08; 10-K_2025-12-31_dxpe-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 83; 10-Q_2026-09-30_dxpe-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 33

Acquired earnings are not disclosed, so the leverage effect of the acquisition program cannot be tested from the filings. The acquisition notes give consideration, goodwill, intangibles and sales but no acquired EBITDA, and management's covenant EBITDA of USD 270.0 million includes pro forma acquisition effects that the filings do not bridge; the company's 2.30x secured leverage rests on that figure.
Basis: calculation A11; 10-K_2025-12-31_dxpe-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 85; 10-Q_2026-09-30_dxpe-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 12; EX-99.1_2026-11-05_earningsrelease9302026.htm.jsonl, page 2; Source readiness (CP-0)

Shareholder returns have been restrained so far, but the new authorization is large against free cash flow. Repurchases fell from USD 54.7 million in 2023 to USD 28.8 million in 2024, USD 17.0 million in 2025 and nothing in nine months 2026, and USD 68.0 million of the earlier authorization was left unused when that program ended in August 2026. The new USD 150.0 million program equals 142.91% of twelve-month free cash flow of USD 105.0 million and 68.55% of cash, and the term loan limits restricted payments with some baskets open only below stated leverage levels, but the basket sizes and thresholds are not supplied, so whether covenants would limit it is not established.
Basis: calculation A08; 10-K_2025-12-31_dxpe-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 84; 10-K_2025-12-31_dxpe-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 92; 10-Q_2026-09-30_dxpe-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 19; 10-Q_2026-09-30_dxpe-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 21; 10-Q_2026-09-30_dxpe-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 35–36; Source readiness (CP-0)

Control and governance cannot be verified because the proxy statement is not supplied. The chief executive has been Chairman, President and Chief Executive Officer since 1996 and acquired a controlling interest in the predecessor company in 1986, but his current holding, the board's independence and its committee membership are not stated. Related-party dealings are small: USD 2.3 million of 2025 lease expense went to entities the chief executive and family control, 0.50% of selling, general and administrative expense, and the Audit Committee reviews related-party transactions above USD 120,000.
Basis: calculation A10; 10-K_2025-12-31_dxpe-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 16; 10-K_2025-12-31_dxpe-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 100; 10-K_2025-12-31_dxpe-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 102

Operating risk is moderate and sits with suppliers and end markets, not customers. No customer reached 10% of sales in three years, over 90% of sales are products and 96.18% of 2025 sales were in the United States, but manufacturers can cancel distribution authorizations on little or no notice, part of revenue depends on oil, gas and petrochemical spending, and Supply Chain Services contracts can be cancelled on little or no notice. Backlog in Innovative Pumping Solutions was USD 325.0 million at the end of 2025, 11.2% above a year earlier.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against caution is balance-sheet strength. Liquidity of USD 411.0 million is 48.47x the USD 8.5 million of scheduled principal due in each of 2027 to 2029, the company's own secured leverage of 2.30x is far below its maximum of 5.50x or 5.75x, S&P raised the rating to B+ in July 2026, no common dividend is paid, and total debt fell by USD 7.3 million in nine months 2026 even as acquisitions continued. Organic growth of 9.26% also shows the acquired businesses are not masking weak demand.
Basis: calculation A04; calculation A11; 10-K_2025-12-31_dxpe-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 42; 10-K_2025-12-31_dxpe-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 84; 10-Q_2026-09-30_dxpe-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 12; 10-Q_2026-09-30_dxpe-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 33; 10-Q_2026-06-30_dxpe-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 32

The view would soften if a proxy statement showed a majority-independent board and a modest insider holding, if acquired EBITDA and purchase multiples showed deals priced below current leverage, if the executed agreements showed restricted-payment baskets too small to take the USD 150.0 million program at current leverage, and if repurchases stayed inside free cash flow. It would harden if repurchases were paid from cash while acquisitions continued, if leverage rose without matching earnings, if a fall in acquisition sales exposed weaker organic growth, if a major manufacturer withdrew an authorization, or if the baskets proved large enough to fund the whole program while leverage stayed where it is.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_dxpe-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 26; 10-K_2025-12-31_dxpe-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 34; 10-Q_2026-09-30_dxpe-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 21; Source readiness (CP-0)

### Limitations

- The proxy statement is not supplied, so the chief executive's holding, the board's independence and any other large holder are not established; a large insider stake would raise key-person and related-party risk while an independent board would lessen it, so the governance conclusion could move either way.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_dxpe-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 102; Source readiness (CP-0)
- Acquired EBITDA, purchase multiples and per-deal terms are not disclosed, so the conclusion on acquisition discipline rests on spending and sales contribution only; deals bought at high multiples or with weak margins would make it adverse.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_dxpe-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 94; 10-Q_2026-09-30_dxpe-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 18
- The executed credit agreements are not supplied, so the size of restricted-payment, investment and incremental-debt baskets and the leverage levels that open them are unknown; the shareholder-return conclusion treats the USD 150.0 million program as unconstrained by anything quantified, and a tight basket would lessen the concern.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_dxpe-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 34; 10-K_2025-12-31_dxpe-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 84; 10-Q_2026-09-30_dxpe-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 21; Source readiness (CP-0)
- The use of the December 2025 term loan proceeds is not stated, so the link between that debt and the 2026 acquisitions is an interpretation; a different purpose would weaken the finding that growth is financed with secured debt.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_dxpe-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 83
- End-market and product-category shares appear in charts that the text does not carry and supplier concentration is not quantified, so the finding that risk sits with suppliers and end markets cannot be sized.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_dxpe-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 8; 10-K_2025-12-31_dxpe-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 12; 10-K_2025-12-31_dxpe-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 26
- The filings name no sponsor, parent or fee arrangement, so the conclusion that no sponsor is present rests on silence; an undisclosed large holder with sponsor-like control would change the governance and shareholder-behavior conclusions.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_dxpe-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 42; 10-K_2025-12-31_dxpe-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 100
- The 2026 quarterly reports give no segment backlog and no results for individual acquired businesses, so the operating conclusion for Innovative Pumping Solutions rests on reported sales and income only.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_dxpe-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 18; 10-Q_2026-09-30_dxpe-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 25
