## Findings

### Bottom line

The supplied RLJ Lodging Trust filings support a credit snapshot with limitations, but creditor conclusions on covenant headroom, structural priority and recovery cannot rest on them because no executed debt documents were supplied.

Audited annual statements run through FY2025 and unaudited quarterly statements run to September 30, 2026, with debt detail by instrument plus covenant-compliance and hedging notes, so Financial foundation, Business & governance and Fundamental credit can proceed on this evidence.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_rlj.htm.jsonl, pages 87–90; 10-Q_2026-09-30_rlj.htm.jsonl, pages 2–6; 10-Q_2026-09-30_rlj.htm.jsonl, pages 10–13

Governance detail, any balance sheet after the October 2026 sale of seven hotels, credit ratings, market quotes, a peer set and management guidance are not established from the supplied set, so any conclusion that needs them stays open.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_rlj.htm.jsonl, page 78; 10-Q_2026-09-30_rlj.htm.jsonl, pages 9–10; 10-Q_2026-09-30_rlj.htm.jsonl, page 25
