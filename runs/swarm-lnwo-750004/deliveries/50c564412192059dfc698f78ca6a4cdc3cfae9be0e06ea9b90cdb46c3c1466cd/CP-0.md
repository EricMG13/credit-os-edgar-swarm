## Findings

### Bottom line

The eight supplied filings and earnings releases give a current, consolidated record of Light & Wonder's accounts and debt through September 30, 2026, enough for a credit snapshot, but creditors cannot test covenant, control or rating questions from them.

Every document is dated on or before November 4, 2026, ahead of the November 18, 2026 cutoff, and together they cover two fiscal years and three quarters of 2026. The debt sits at a wholly owned subsidiary and is guaranteed by the parent and its domestic subsidiaries, yet no borrower-group or guarantor financial statements were supplied, so leverage can only be read on the consolidated perimeter. Covenant levels rest on the issuer's own summary in the annual report because no executed credit agreement or indenture was supplied, and ownership and board detail sits in a proxy statement that is not in the supplied set.
