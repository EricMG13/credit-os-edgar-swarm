## Findings

### Bottom line

On unadjusted earnings, net debt was 4.24x EBITDA at September 30, 2026, well above the 3.3x the issuer reports on adjusted earnings, so creditors should size the risk on the higher figure while recognising that it has fallen from 4.60x at the end of 2025.

The fall came from a USD 182 million cut in carrying debt and from earnings growth, and available liquidity of USD 1,059 million leaves no near-term funding pressure. The gap between the two measures rests on USD 244 million of restructuring and other costs over twelve months, of which legal costs have recurred, and the USD 2,568 million due in 2029 is the next refinancing test.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_lnw-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 10–11, 21, 54; EX-99.1_2026-11-04_lnw09302026erandtables.htm.jsonl, pages 5, 18; calculation CALC-34; calculation CALC-42; calculation CALC-32; calculation CALC-46

### Key findings

- Unadjusted leverage jumped in 2025 and has since eased. Net debt was 3.42x unadjusted EBITDA at the end of 2024, 4.60x at the end of 2025 and 4.24x at September 30, 2026, because carrying debt rose by USD 1,293 million in 2025 while the issuer spent USD 861 million on acquisitions, chiefly Grover, and USD 880 million on share repurchases, and unadjusted EBITDA barely moved, from USD 1,075 million to USD 1,085 million. Since the year-end, carrying debt has fallen by USD 182 million and face value by USD 188 million, USD 153 million of it in the third quarter.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_lnw-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 140–142, 166; 10-Q_2026-09-30_lnw-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 10–11, 21; calculation CALC-53; calculation CALC-34; calculation CALC-35; calculation CALC-36; calculation CALC-2

- The issuer's lower leverage rests on add-backs, and the largest recurs. Adjusted EBITDA for the twelve months to September 30, 2026 was USD 1,498 million, USD 355 million above the unadjusted USD 1,143 million, and restructuring and other accounted for USD 244 million of the gap. Legal and related costs inside that line were USD 79 million in 2024, USD 130 million in 2025 and USD 179 million over the last twelve months, so a measure that excludes them flatters the debt a creditor must service in cash.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_lnw-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 148, 154; 10-Q_2026-09-30_lnw-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 18; EX-99.1_2026-11-04_lnw09302026erandtables.htm.jsonl, pages 16, 18; calculation CALC-31; calculation CALC-32; calculation CALC-33

- Cash generation improved but is flattered by timing. Operating cash flow was USD 616 million in the first nine months of 2026 against USD 475 million, USD 141 million more, and after capital expenditures of USD 247 million it left USD 369 million against USD 257 million. The nine-month figure also benefits from USD 137 million of legal settlements all paid in the first quarter that the issuer adds back to its own measure and from USD 46 million released from restricted cash, which counts in the issuer's free cash flow but not in operating cash flow.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_lnw-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 11, 56–57; EX-99.1_2026-05-06_lnw03312026erandtables.htm.jsonl, page 18; EX-99.1_2026-08-04_lnw06302026erandtables.htm.jsonl, page 22; EX-99.1_2026-11-04_lnw09302026erandtables.htm.jsonl, page 18; calculation CALC-37; calculation CALC-38; calculation CALC-39; calculation CALC-40

- Interest cover looks stronger on cash than on expense. Cash interest paid was USD 288 million over the last twelve months, for cover of 3.97x on unadjusted EBITDA, but interest expense was USD 328 million, for cover of 3.48x. The gap probably reflects payment timing: the third quarter shows cash interest of only USD 63 million against USD 89 million a year earlier, and the 2033 notes pay on April 1 and October 1, so the October payment falls outside the third quarter.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_lnw-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 167; 10-Q_2026-09-30_lnw-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 10–11; calculation CALC-29; calculation CALC-30; calculation CALC-54

- Liquidity is adequate and the first large maturity is in 2029. Cash of USD 139 million and undrawn revolver commitments of USD 920 million gave USD 1,059 million at September 30, 2026, against USD 927 million at year-end, with USD 70 million drawn on a USD 1,000 million revolver that matures in 2030. At year-end the principal due in 2029 was USD 2,568 million, the Term Loan B of USD 2,068 million and the 2029 notes of USD 500 million, which is about half of USD 5,207 million of principal, and the ladder has not been printed since.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_lnw-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 21, 54; 10-K_2025-12-31_lnw-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 125, 165–166; calculation CALC-42; calculation CALC-43; calculation CALC-46; calculation CALC-45

- Capital returns and a thin equity base weigh on creditors. The issuer spent USD 193 million on share repurchases and trust purchases in the first nine months of 2026 against USD 380 million a year earlier, with no repurchases under the program in the third quarter, and total equity of USD 423 million is small beside goodwill and intangible assets of USD 4,053 million. No supplier finance, factoring or deposit float was found, and contract liabilities are small.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_lnw-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 10, 14, 55; EX-99.1_2026-11-04_lnw09302026erandtables.htm.jsonl, page 5; calculation CALC-41; calculation CALC-56; calculation CALC-55

- No event after the September 30, 2026 balance sheet changes net debt in the sources read, so the net debt table carries no pro forma figure. The Grover price is stated four ways in the annual report, USD 850 million upfront, USD 858 million of cash after adjustments, USD 56 million of contingent consideration at fair value and USD 914 million in total, and the cash flow figure of USD 861 million is the one used for cash; up to USD 200 million more may be paid on revenue targets.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_lnw-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 141, 159–160; 10-Q_2026-09-30_lnw-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 12; calculation CALC-52

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest case against the cautious reading is the trend: unadjusted net leverage fell from 4.60x to 4.24x in nine months, the issuer targets below 3.0x on its own measure in the first half of 2027, the revolver covenant is tested only when borrowings exceed 30% of commitments and USD 70 million of USD 1,000 million was drawn, and the issuer reports compliance with its financial covenants. That case wins if the legal and related add-backs fall back towards the USD 79 million of 2024, the fourth-quarter interest payment does not lift cash cover toward expense cover, and debt reduction continues at the third-quarter pace rather than yielding to share repurchases.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-11-04_lnw09302026erandtables.htm.jsonl, pages 5, 18; 10-Q_2026-09-30_lnw-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 22; 10-K_2025-12-31_lnw-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 169; calculation CALC-33

Three observations would change the view toward more caution: a rise in restructuring and other beyond the USD 244 million of the last twelve months, a draw on the revolver that approaches the covenant test, or no refinancing plan for the 2029 maturities by the time the Term Loan B becomes current in 2028.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_lnw-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 165–166; calculation CALC-32; calculation CALC-46

### Limitations

The statements are consolidated and no borrower-group, guarantor or restricted-group figures were supplied, so leverage cannot be read where the debt sits or the covenant is tested, and covenant headroom is not established.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_lnw-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 169; 10-Q_2026-09-30_lnw-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 22

The issuer prints no unadjusted EBITDA, so the base measure is calculated here and includes other income and the loss on debt financing; interim ratios use twelve-month figures built from unaudited stubs.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_lnw-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 9–10; calculation CALC-8; calculation CALC-27
