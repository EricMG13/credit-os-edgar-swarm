## Findings

### Bottom line

Governance risk is medium: no sponsor or controlling holder is identified and no dividend has been paid, but USD 1,495 million of share repurchases since 2024 alongside a debt-funded acquisition show that shareholders compete with creditors for cash.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_lnw-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 100, 128; 10-Q_2026-09-30_lnw-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 26; EX-99.1_2026-11-04_lnw09302026erandtables.htm.jsonl, page 5; calculation CALC-15

Revenue is mostly recurring or leased, USD 1,555 million or 63.73% of nine-month revenue, which steadies cash flow, but social casino revenue is falling and three class actions are open. Repurchases stopped in the third quarter of 2026 in favour of debt reduction, and the test is whether they stay stopped as leverage nears the issuer's 3.0x target.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_lnw-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 14; EX-99.1_2026-11-04_lnw09302026erandtables.htm.jsonl, page 5; calculation CALC-6

### Key findings

- Ownership and control are not established, so sponsor behaviour is judged from actions and not from identity. The common stock has had its sole primary listing as depositary interests on the ASX since November 2025, no holder or board majority is named, and director and ownership information sits in a proxy statement that was not supplied; no fee, dividend or sponsor-led recapitalisation is evidenced, but no outside backstop is evidenced either.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_lnw-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 62, 100, 134, 135; 10-Q_2026-09-30_lnw-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 2

- Financial policy has favoured shareholders alongside leverage, and willingness to pay out is shown more clearly than capacity. The issuer repurchased USD 462 million of shares in 2024, USD 877 million in 2025, including USD 500 million in the fourth quarter, and USD 156 million in the first nine months of 2026, while borrowing a USD 800 million Term Loan A to buy Grover. The debt agreements were not supplied, so how much more could be paid out is not established.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_lnw-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 101, 128, 166; 10-Q_2026-09-30_lnw-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 26; calculation CALC-15; calculation CALC-12

- The 2026 pause in repurchases is real but discretionary. No shares were repurchased under the program in the third quarter, net revolver repayments were USD 160 million in nine months, and the issuer says it put deleveraging ahead of buybacks and aims for net debt leverage below 3.0x in the first half of 2027 on its own measure, from 3.3x at September 30, 2026. USD 180 million of program capacity remains, the program runs to June 12, 2027, and the employee share trust still bought USD 30 million of depositary interests in the quarter.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_lnw-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 11, 21, 59; EX-99.1_2026-11-04_lnw09302026erandtables.htm.jsonl, pages 4, 5; calculation CALC-11

- Refinancing has been creditor-aligned. The 2033 notes, at a coupon 0.75 percentage points below the 2028 notes they replaced, repaid those notes and the revolver in September 2025; the revolver was enlarged to USD 1.0 billion and extended to 2030, and the January 2026 Term Loan B margin cut saves about USD 5 million a year. The Term Loan B was also repriced twice in 2024, cutting its rate by 85 basis points in total. No exchange, priming or waiver is described, although the executed amendments were not supplied.
Basis: 10-K_2024-12-31_lnw-20241231.htm.jsonl, page 102; 10-K_2025-12-31_lnw-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 127, 166, 167; 10-Q_2026-03-31_lnw-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 19; EX-99.1_2026-05-06_lnw03312026erandtables.htm.jsonl, page 5; calculation CALC-13

- The operating base is mostly recurring, but growth comes from leased machines and iGaming while digital and cyclical lines weaken. In the first nine months of 2026 gaming operations revenue rose 17.63% and iGaming 14.75%, while SciPlay fell 9.68%, machine sales fell 8.18% and systems fell 18.27%. Gaming earned a 54.91% segment adjusted EBITDA margin against 37.71% for SciPlay and 36.79% for iGaming, so Gaming provided 74.33% of segment adjusted EBITDA and group cash flow rests on the gaming machine business.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_lnw-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 14, 15, 16, 17; calculation CALC-3; calculation CALC-7

- Exposure outside the United States is material, and receivables are more international than revenue. Customers outside the United States gave 31.50% of FY2025 revenue, and international receivables were 47.53% of the total at September 30, 2026.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_lnw-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 149; 10-Q_2026-09-30_lnw-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 18; calculation CALC-5; calculation CALC-14

- Controls and legal exposure keep the risk from being low. The year-end internal-control assessment left out Grover, about 14% of total assets and 3% of net revenues, and two consumer class actions against SciPlay and a shareholder class action in Australia are open with no damages estimate; accrued legal liabilities fell by USD 90 million in nine months and USD 137 million of settlements were paid in the first quarter.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_lnw-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 131; 10-Q_2026-09-30_lnw-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 28, 36, 37, 40; EX-99.1_2026-05-06_lnw03312026erandtables.htm.jsonl, page 4; calculation CALC-10

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a medium rating is behavioural: no dividend has ever been paid, no liability management transaction is described, the 2033 refinancing cut the coupon, revolver borrowings were only USD 70 million against USD 1.0 billion of commitments, repurchases paused in the third quarter, and the issuer reports net debt leverage of 3.3x on its own measure, down from 3.4x. A proxy statement showing an independent board and no related-party dealings, together with tight restricted-payment limits in the executed agreements, would move the rating to low. It would move to high if repurchases resumed before the 3.0x target is reached, if another debt-funded acquisition followed Grover, or if an amendment loosened payment limits. Gaming machine sales also rose 5.29% in the third quarter after falling over nine months, which hints at a recovering replacement cycle for the business that carries group earnings.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_lnw-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 100, 127, 169; 10-Q_2026-09-30_lnw-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 14, 21, 59; EX-99.1_2026-11-04_lnw09302026erandtables.htm.jsonl, page 5; calculation CALC-13; calculation CALC-9

### Limitations

- No proxy statement was supplied, so board independence, holder percentages, insider holdings and related-party dealings are not established; the medium rating rests on actions and would be revisited if a controlling holder or fees to a holder were disclosed.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_lnw-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 135

- The executed credit agreements and indentures were not supplied, so the payment, investment and borrowing room left under the restricted-payment and debt limits is not established; willingness is read from what the issuer did, and capacity is not tested.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_lnw-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 60, 100, 169

- The recurring share of revenue of 63.73% is a floor: systems maintenance and table rental revenue that the issuer also counts as recurring sits inside lines that are not separated, so durability is understated by an amount that cannot be measured.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-11-04_lnw09302026erandtables.htm.jsonl, page 6; 10-Q_2026-09-30_lnw-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 14; calculation CALC-6

- The class action damages and the repurchase funding source are not stated, so the legal and financial policy conclusions give a direction and not a size.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_lnw-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 36, 37, 40; 10-K_2025-12-31_lnw-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 128
