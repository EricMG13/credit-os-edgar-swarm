## Findings

### Bottom line

Governance risk for creditors is medium: Nexstar directs cash to debt repayment, but the debt-funded TEGNA acquisition sits under a court order barring integration, so creditors must rely on cash flow, not synergies, for deleveraging.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_nxst-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 13; 10-Q_2026-09-30_nxst-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 33; EX-99.1_2026-11-05_nxst-ex99_1.htm.jsonl, page 4; Source readiness (CP-0)

The acquisition cost USD 3,657 million for the equity and lifted carrying debt from USD 6,333 million at year-end 2025 to USD 11,409 million at 2026-09-30. Nexstar repaid USD 786 million in nine months against USD 171 million of dividends, and USD 525 million more after quarter end, yet kept its dividend at $1.86 per share. No controlling holder is named, so risk comes from the founder-led management, contract-based control of independent station owners and dependence on regulators and courts.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_nxst-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 17; 10-Q_2026-09-30_nxst-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 43; EX-99.1_2026-11-05_nxst-ex99_1.htm.jsonl, page 4; calculation C05; calculation C08; calculation C09

### Key findings

- Ownership gives creditors no sponsor to rely on or to fear: the stock is a single class with one vote per share, no preferred stock is outstanding and no controlling holder is named, but holder percentages and board composition are not established because they sit in a proxy statement that is not supplied.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_nxst-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 54; 10-K_2025-12-31_nxst-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 78; 10-K_2025-12-31_nxst-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 91; 10-K_2025-12-31_nxst-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 135; Source readiness (CP-0)

- Management continuity is a strength with a key-person edge: the founder has led the company as Chairman and Chief Executive Officer since 1996, his term runs to 2029-03-31, and the issuer itself lists his loss as a risk, so execution of the acquisition rests heavily on one executive.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_nxst-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 44; 10-K_2025-12-31_nxst-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 79

- The TEGNA acquisition is the dominant governance fact: it was funded by new debt, carrying debt rose by USD 5,076 million in nine months, and a court order in force since 2026-04-21 stops integration, so the synergies that justified the price cannot be relied on before the trial on 2027-07-06.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_nxst-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 12; 10-Q_2026-09-30_nxst-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 17; 10-Q_2026-09-30_nxst-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 33; EX-99.1_2026-11-05_nxst-ex99_1.htm.jsonl, page 4; calculation C08

- Capital allocation has turned toward deleveraging without pausing the dividend: debt repayment was 4.60x dividends in nine months and no shares were repurchased, but the board declared the unchanged dividend on 2026-10-30 and USD 1.4 billion of repurchase authority remains, so repayment discipline is behavioural and not contractual.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence for lower risk is cash generation and visible deleveraging: Adjusted Free Cash Flow was USD 1,101 million in nine months, debt repayments were USD 786 million, the company reports compliance with its financial covenants, and the issuer's first-lien ratio of 3.03x sits well inside the 4.75x test with total net leverage of 4.04x. Both ratios use credit agreement definitions that average two years of EBITDA and exclude TEGNA synergies from the second quarter, so they are not comparable with leverage on reported figures and do not by themselves lower the view.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-05-07_nxst-ex99_1.htm.jsonl, page 7; EX-99.1_2026-11-05_nxst-ex99_1.htm.jsonl, page 1; EX-99.1_2026-11-05_nxst-ex99_1.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-Q_2026-09-30_nxst-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 26; calculation C18

The view would improve to low if the appeal or the 2027 trial lifted the injunction, repayment continued at the nine-month pace while the dividend stayed flat, and no divestiture or forfeiture followed. It would worsen to high if the courts ordered divestitures or a longer hold-separate period, if a challenge to the ownership-rule repeal undermined the waiver, if repurchases resumed before leverage fell, or if the WPIX proceeding ended in a forfeiture and a divestiture order.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_nxst-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 13; 10-Q_2026-09-30_nxst-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 34; 10-Q_2026-09-30_nxst-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 37; EX-99.1_2026-11-05_nxst-ex99_1.htm.jsonl, page 4

### Limitations

- Directors, committees, pay and holders above any reporting threshold are in a proxy statement that is not supplied, so the conclusion that no controlling holder is named cannot be confirmed, and a concentrated holder would raise the governance risk level.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_nxst-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 78; 10-K_2025-12-31_nxst-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 82; Source readiness (CP-0)

- The purchase price allocation is preliminary for up to twelve months after closing, so the goodwill share of the price and the identifiable intangible assets behind the new debt could still move.

- The executed credit agreements and indentures are not supplied, so dividend, repurchase and investment capacity under them is not established; the capital-allocation view rests on observed behaviour only and would change if the baskets proved wide.

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

Nexstar completed its cash acquisition of TEGNA on 2026-03-19 at $22 per share, a purchase price of USD 3,657 million for the equity and unvested awards. It added 64 television stations in 51 markets, the Premion advertising platform, two multicast networks and a podcast network, and the FCC approved the merger the same day with binding conditions. The conditions include a commitment to divest six stations within two years if the local ownership waiver is still needed and an offer to extend existing retransmission agreements at current rates until 2026-11-30. The annual report for 2025 values the transaction, agreed on 2025-08-18, at USD 6.2 billion including refinancing of certain TEGNA debt, fees and costs, against committed financing of up to USD 5.725 billion and a USD 125 million termination fee payable by Nexstar in specified cases. Those headline figures and the USD 3,657 million price measure different things, and the documents give no sources-and-uses table.
