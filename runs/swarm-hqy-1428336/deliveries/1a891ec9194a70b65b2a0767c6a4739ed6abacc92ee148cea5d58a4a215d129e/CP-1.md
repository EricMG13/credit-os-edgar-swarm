## Findings

### Bottom line

Healthequity's reported net debt is 1.32x last-twelve-months EBITDA at July 31, 2026, and no debt falls due before fiscal 2030, so creditors face low leverage and little refinancing pressure; capital returns that outran free cash flow in the latest half-year are the pressure point.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-07-31_hqy-20260731.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-07-31_hqy-20260731.htm.jsonl, pages 11–14; calculation C17; calculation C21; calculation C22

Net debt to reported EBITDA fell from 2.34x at fiscal 2025 to 1.34x at fiscal 2026 as EBITDA grew and USD 100 million of debt was repaid, and interest cover is 10.79x on the last twelve months. Reported EBITDA is calculated here because the issuer prints only an adjusted figure, on which net debt to EBITDA is 1.11x. Net debt rose by USD 36.61 million in the first half of fiscal 2027.
Basis: calculation C21; calculation C18; calculation C23; 10-K_2026-01-31_hqy-20260131.htm.jsonl, page 92; 10-Q_2026-07-31_hqy-20260731.htm.jsonl, page 2

### Key findings

- Reported net debt to EBITDA fell from 2.34x at fiscal 2025 to 1.34x at fiscal 2026 and was 1.32x on a last-twelve-months basis at July 31, 2026, and total debt to EBITDA fell from 3.25x to 2.01x and 1.83x. Reported EBITDA grew by 46.83% in fiscal 2026 while total debt fell from USD 1,056.30 million to USD 957.38 million, so the fall in leverage comes from earnings growth and repayment together. On the issuer's adjusted EBITDA net leverage is 1.13x at fiscal 2026 and 1.11x at July 31, 2026, so the improvement does not depend on which EBITDA is chosen.
Basis: calculation C21; calculation C22; 10-K_2026-01-31_hqy-20260131.htm.jsonl, page 90; 10-Q_2026-07-31_hqy-20260731.htm.jsonl, page 2
- EBITDA to cash interest paid rose from 5.55x at fiscal 2025 to 9.14x at fiscal 2026 and 10.79x on a last-twelve-months basis, because EBITDA grew and cash interest paid fell as debt was repaid; on adjusted EBITDA cover is 12.83x. The revolving credit facility floats, at 5.08% at July 31, 2026, while the 2029 notes are fixed, so only the revolver's USD 335 million of debt is exposed to a rise in rates.
Basis: calculation C21; 10-K_2026-01-31_hqy-20260131.htm.jsonl, page 92; 10-Q_2026-07-31_hqy-20260731.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-Q_2026-07-31_hqy-20260731.htm.jsonl, page 13; 10-Q_2026-07-31_hqy-20260731.htm.jsonl, page 48
- Free cash flow was 85.57% of reported EBITDA in fiscal 2026, but share repurchases absorbed 73.24% of it, and in the first half of fiscal 2027 repurchases were 114.60% of free cash flow, so net debt rose to USD 675.06 million at July 31, 2026 from USD 638.45 million at January 31, 2026 even though the revolver balance fell to USD 335 million. For creditors, equity returns now take more of the surplus cash than debt reduction does.
Basis: calculation C22; calculation C18; 10-Q_2026-07-31_hqy-20260731.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-K_2026-01-31_hqy-20260131.htm.jsonl, page 91
- Liquidity at July 31, 2026 is USD 256.00 million of cash plus USD 665 million undrawn on the revolving credit facility, before letters of credit, or USD 921.00 million in all, against USD 935 million of debt that falls due in fiscal 2030; no debt matures in fiscal 2027, 2028 or 2029, so the maturity wall is more than three years away.
Basis: calculation C19; calculation C20; 10-Q_2026-07-31_hqy-20260731.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-07-31_hqy-20260731.htm.jsonl, pages 11–13
- Reported EBITDA is calculated here from the issuer's reconciliation lines, and the issuer's adjusted figure is 18.53% above it at fiscal 2026, mainly stock-based compensation, which creditors should treat as a real cost; neither is the covenant measure, so no covenant headroom is stated. Custodial revenue, which moves with interest rates, was 48.48% of fiscal 2026 revenue and 49.66% in the first half of fiscal 2027.
Basis: calculation C22; 10-K_2026-01-31_hqy-20260131.htm.jsonl, pages 76–77; 10-Q_2026-07-31_hqy-20260731.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-Q_2026-07-31_hqy-20260731.htm.jsonl, page 14

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

- Leverage is low and falling, but the earnings behind it are exposed to interest rates: custodial revenue was 49.66% of first-half revenue in fiscal 2027, and the issuer says a sustained decline in rates would reduce the yield and the custodial revenue it earns, although its contract structure mitigates the short-term effect. Cash taxes also rose to USD 35.59 million in the first half of fiscal 2027 from USD 6.51 million a year earlier, which weighs on free cash flow.
Basis: calculation C22; 10-Q_2026-07-31_hqy-20260731.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-Q_2026-07-31_hqy-20260731.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-Q_2026-07-31_hqy-20260731.htm.jsonl, page 47
- The view would weaken if repurchases keep exceeding free cash flow and are funded from cash and the revolver, which would push net debt to EBITDA back toward the 2.34x of fiscal 2025, or if reported EBITDA falls with rates. It would strengthen if free cash flow again exceeds repurchases and the revolver is repaid.
Basis: calculation C21; calculation C22
