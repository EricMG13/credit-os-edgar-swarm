## Findings

### Bottom line
The five filings support a credit snapshot of Trimas Corp as of 2026-11-18, but only the 2025 annual report and the three 2026 quarterly reports show the business that remains after the Aerospace sale, so creditor metrics must use them. The sale closed in March 2026, and the 2025 annual report already presents Aerospace as discontinued operations.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_trs.htm.jsonl, page 10; 10-K_2025-12-31_trs.htm.jsonl, page 6

The 2024 annual report still includes Aerospace in continuing results, so its sales of USD 925.01 million are not comparable with the later filings, and the 2025 annual report restates that year to USD 630.80 million. The executed credit agreement and notes indenture are not supplied, so covenant terms can be read only as the quarterly reports summarise them. Financial foundation, Business & governance and Fundamental credit can proceed, with no proxy statement for board and compensation detail.
