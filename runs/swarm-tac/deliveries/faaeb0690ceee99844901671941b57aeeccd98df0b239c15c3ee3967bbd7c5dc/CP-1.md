## Findings

### Bottom line

Leverage on issuer-adjusted EBITDA rose to 4.19x gross and 3.85x net at the end of Q3 2026 from 3.93x and 3.75x at year-end 2025, because EBITDA fell faster than debt, while liquidity of CAD 1,867 million covers scheduled maturities through 2027 more than four times.

Nine-month issuer-adjusted EBITDA was 3.80x cash interest paid, and the Sept. 30, 2026 balance sheet excludes the October 2026 Colorado acquisition, which brings US$750 million of assumed project debt, with no pro forma leverage stated.
Basis: calculation C11; 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-11-03_q32026newsreleasecombined.htm.jsonl, pages 70–71; 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-11-03_q32026newsreleasecombined.htm.jsonl, page 98

### Key findings

Last-twelve-months issuer-adjusted EBITDA was CAD 1,006 million at Sept. 30, 2026 against CAD 1,104 million for FY2025, a fall of CAD 98 million, while gross debt at carrying value fell only CAD 123 million to CAD 4,220 million. The EBITDA fall is the same CAD 98 million nine-month decline: the Energy Transition segment, where Centralia stopped coal generation, lost CAD 90 million and Hydro lost CAD 48 million, partly offset by Gas, which gained CAD 32 million. Hydro and Gas revenues fell on lower Alberta spot and ancillary prices, net of favourable hedge pricing, while debt service is fixed, so leverage rises when merchant earnings soften.
Basis: calculation C01; calculation C02; calculation C03; calculation C04; calculation C14; 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-11-03_q32026newsreleasecombined.htm.jsonl, page 3; 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-11-03_q32026newsreleasecombined.htm.jsonl, page 12

Net debt fell CAD 263 million to CAD 3,875 million because cash rose CAD 140 million to CAD 345 million and no cash is drawn on the committed credit facilities, against CAD 95 million drawn at year-end. The June 2026 share offering raised CAD 338 million net and was used partly to repay those drawings, so the improvement came from new equity rather than from earnings; the offering was raised to fund the cash price of the Colorado acquisition, which was paid after the balance sheet date. The issuer's own leverage measure, which adds half of its preferred shares, was 4.1x against a long-term target of 3.0x to 4.0x.
Basis: calculation C05; calculation C06; calculation C15; 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-11-03_q32026newsreleasecombined.htm.jsonl, pages 40–41; 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-11-03_q32026newsreleasecombined.htm.jsonl, page 92; 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-11-03_q32026newsreleasecombined.htm.jsonl, page 5; 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-11-03_q32026newsreleasecombined.htm.jsonl, page 98

Nine-month issuer-adjusted EBITDA of CAD 759 million was 3.80x cash interest paid of CAD 200 million, and a twelve-month cover cannot be built because FY2025 cash interest paid is not supplied. Interest expense fell to CAD 241 million from CAD 266 million after 2025 refinancings at lower rates, which supports cover, but the CAD 98 million fall in EBITDA is nearly four times the CAD 25 million interest saving.
Basis: calculation C11; calculation C18; calculation C26; 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-11-03_q32026newsreleasecombined.htm.jsonl, pages 41–42; 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-11-03_q32026newsreleasecombined.htm.jsonl, page 67; 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-11-03_q32026newsreleasecombined.htm.jsonl, page 71

Liquidity of CAD 1,867 million is cash of CAD 345 million plus CAD 1,522 million of undrawn committed capacity after letters of credit issued under both committed and non-committed lines; the syndicated and bilateral committed lines were extended in July 2026 to 2030 and 2028. Scheduled debt maturities are CAD 54 million in the rest of 2026 and CAD 338 million in 2027, and CAD 3,150 million falls after 2030, including CAD 750 million of exchangeable securities that Brookfield may convert but cannot call for repayment. The working capital deficit of CAD 219 million is mainly that exchangeable line classed as current.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a weakening credit is liquidity and tenor: no cash is drawn on committed facilities, only about 26% of principal falls due by 2030, and the company reaffirmed its 2026 outlook of CAD 950 million to CAD 1,050 million of adjusted EBITDA, which needs only CAD 191 million in the fourth quarter after CAD 759 million in nine months to reach the low end. If fourth-quarter 2026 adjusted EBITDA exceeds the CAD 247 million of the fourth quarter of 2025, last-twelve-months EBITDA would rise and the 4.19x gross leverage would ease; weaker Alberta prices, the Colorado debt or further mothballing of Alberta units would push it the other way.
Basis: calculation C01; calculation C02; calculation C27; calculation C28; 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-11-03_q32026newsreleasecombined.htm.jsonl, pages 23–24; 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-11-03_q32026newsreleasecombined.htm.jsonl, page 5
