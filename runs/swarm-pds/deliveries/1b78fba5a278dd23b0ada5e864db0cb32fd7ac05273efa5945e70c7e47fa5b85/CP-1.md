## Findings

### Bottom line

Leverage is moderate but has not improved on a net basis: net debt was 1.28x last-twelve-months EBITDA at September 30, 2026 against 1.21x at December 31, 2025, while total debt eased to 1.33x from 1.39x, so further deleveraging depends on cash generation.
Basis: calculation C14; calculation C7; calculation C13; 6-K-EX-99.2_2026-10-27_exh_992.htm.jsonl, page 1

Refinancing risk is low for now because only a small secured facility is drawn and the large unsecured notes mature in January 2029, but cash of CAD 23 million is thin after share buybacks and capital spending, and a disclosed Canadian tax reassessment of up to CAD 155 million excluding interest is not provided for. Full-year 2025 statements were not supplied, so fiscal 2025 earnings are rebuilt from the four reported quarters and the year-end balance sheet comes from the 2026 comparatives.
Basis: 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-10-27_exh_991.htm.jsonl, pages 12–13; 6-K-EX-99.2_2026-10-27_exh_992.htm.jsonl, pages 12–13; calculation C1

### Key findings

Gross debt fell by CAD 50 million over nine months to CAD 629 million, but net debt rose by CAD 12 million to CAD 606 million because cash fell from CAD 86 million to CAD 23 million, by more than debt did, and LTM EBITDA slipped to CAD 473 million from CAD 490 million. The company repaid CAD 75 million of facility borrowings net of new draws, repurchased CAD 47 million of shares and saw cash fall by CAD 63 million, against free cash flow of CAD 56 million; a weaker Canadian dollar added CAD 23 million to the carrying value of the U.S. dollar debt. Creditors therefore see lower gross debt than at the start of the year without a better net position.
Basis: calculation C17; calculation C16; calculation C19; calculation C18; calculation C30

EBITDA recovered to CAD 125 million in the third quarter from CAD 97 million in the second, but nine-month EBITDA of CAD 346 million is below the CAD 363 million of a year earlier, because of lower upfront capital payments in Canada, higher U.S. and international operating costs and higher share-based compensation. U.S. operating margin per utilization day fell to US$7,653 in the third quarter from US$8,700 a year earlier, and management guides to US$9,500 to US$10,500 for the fourth quarter, which is the main earnings assumption behind any deleveraging.
Basis: 6-K-EX-99.2_2026-10-27_exh_992.htm.jsonl, page 1; 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-10-27_exh_991.htm.jsonl, pages 4, 7; calculation C30

Cash generation is positive but thin after growth capital: nine-month cash from operations of CAD 280 million less capital expenditures of CAD 224 million leaves free cash flow of CAD 56 million, and the 2026 capital budget was raised to CAD 275 million to CAD 285 million, which implies CAD 51 million to CAD 61 million in the fourth quarter, with CAD 143 million of purchase commitments running to 2028. Cash interest paid was CAD 47 million for the nine months, which EBITDA less capital expenditures covers 2.63x.
Basis: calculation C19; calculation C20; calculation C30; 6-K-EX-99.2_2026-10-27_exh_992.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 11

Liquidity is adequate: cash of CAD 23 million plus CAD 439 million of undrawn facilities gives CAD 462 million, consistent with the company's own statement of more than CAD 460 million. The secured Senior Credit Facility has US$46 million drawn against a US$375 million commitment that matures in October 2028, of which US$43 million expires in June 2027, and the company reports senior debt at 0.14x covenant EBITDA against a limit of 2.50x and covenant interest cover of 10.27x against a floor of 2.50x. The US$400 million unsecured senior notes mature in January 2029 and are the only large maturity, so the 2029 wall is the refinancing question.
Basis: 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-10-27_exh_991.htm.jsonl, pages 4, 12–13; 6-K-EX-99.2_2026-10-27_exh_992.htm.jsonl, page 9; calculation C21; calculation C30

Claims outside the debt figure are large against cash: lease obligations of CAD 71 million, capital commitments of CAD 143 million, cash-settled share-based compensation liabilities of CAD 40 million that move with the share price, and a tax reassessment dispute with a stated maximum of CAD 155 million excluding interest, half of which the company must pay once reassessments are issued, which is up to CAD 77.5 million before interest. The company expects no effect on taxes payable until the 2024 to 2027 tax years and has recognised no liability.
Basis: calculation C23; calculation C24; calculation C25; 6-K-EX-99.2_2026-10-27_exh_992.htm.jsonl, pages 11–13

The company prints one EBITDA measure and calls it Adjusted EBITDA; it excludes disposal gains, foreign exchange and investment items but adds back no restructuring or share-based compensation. Unadjusted EBITDA would be CAD 128 million for the third quarter against the printed CAD 125 million, a difference of 2.07%, so the measure used here is the more conservative of the two.
Basis: calculation C26; calculation C30; 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-10-27_exh_991.htm.jsonl, page 17

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest case against caution is the company's own reporting: senior debt is 0.14x covenant EBITDA, covenant interest cover is 10.27x and available liquidity exceeds CAD 460 million, with U.S. margins guided up for the fourth quarter and Canadian heavy oil activity strong, so net leverage could fall quickly if guidance is met. The view would improve if fourth-quarter U.S. operating margin per utilization day reaches the guided US$9,500 to US$10,500 and cash is rebuilt while buybacks continue. It would worsen if capital spending reaches the top of the revised budget without EBITDA growth, if reassessments for further tax years are issued, or if Middle East disruption cuts international margins further.
Basis: 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-10-27_exh_991.htm.jsonl, pages 4, 6–7, 13; 6-K-EX-99.2_2026-10-27_exh_992.htm.jsonl, pages 9, 12–13

### Limitations

Interest cover is shown on period-to-date figures, not last-twelve-months, because cash interest paid for the fourth quarter of 2025 is not supplied, and quarterly interest paid is uneven, so the three cover figures are not comparable with one another.
Basis: calculation C29; 6-K-EX-99.2_2026-10-27_exh_992.htm.jsonl, page 2

Undrawn facilities are established only at September 30, 2026 and March 31, 2026, using the closing exchange rates the company states; the year-end and June 2026 figures are not established.
Basis: calculation C22; 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-03-31_exh_991.htm.jsonl, pages 9–10

Ranking and security in the debt stack follow the company's own secured and unsecured labels; the credit agreement, the note indenture and any guarantee terms were not supplied, and the interim statements are unaudited.
Basis: 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-10-27_exh_991.htm.jsonl, pages 12–13

The interest rate swap the company mentions has no stated notional, rate or maturity, so floating-rate exposure beyond the drawn facility cannot be sized.
