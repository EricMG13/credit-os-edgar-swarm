## Findings

### Bottom line
Kodiak's seven supplied filings and releases are enough to start a credit snapshot through the quarter ended June 30, 2026, but without executed credit documents, ratings or as-of-date market prices creditors cannot test covenant mechanics, guarantor coverage or priority of claims.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_kgs-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 46–47; 10-K_2025-12-31_kgs-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 91–94; 10-Q_2026-06-30_kgs-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 19–25

The debt stack, liquidity, leases, purchase commitments and contingencies are all printed with locators, so financial foundation, business and fundamental credit work can proceed on that basis. Two limits cap how far any conclusion can be pushed: no source is dated after August 7, 2026, leaving the latest balance sheet 128 days old at the as-of date, and the lenders' EBITDA definition behind the reported 3.2x covenant leverage is not supplied, so that ratio cannot be recomputed. Governance depth is also limited because the proxy statement is missing.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_kgs-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 66; EX-99.1_2026-08-06_kgsq22026earningsrelease.htm.jsonl, page 5; 10-K_2025-12-31_kgs-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 89; calculation C8; calculation C12
