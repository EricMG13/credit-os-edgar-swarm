## Findings

### Bottom line
At June 30, 2026 total debt is 4.52x reported EBITDA and 3.56x issuer-adjusted EBITDA, so creditors should underwrite the higher reported figure until the lenders' covenant definition is supplied, because the gap between the two is add-backs the issuer chooses.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_kgs-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 19–20; 10-Q_2026-06-30_kgs-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 56; calculation C05; calculation C06; calculation C15

Near-term liquidity is adequate: cash and undrawn ABL commitments of about USD 1.6 billion face no material maturity until 2030. Free cash flow was negative in the second quarter of 2026 as growth spending on power generation rose, and the share issue raised more than the cash paid for the acquisition, so net debt rose only slightly over the half-year. The covenant leverage the company reports, 3.2x, cannot be recomputed from the filings, and four-quarter earnings carry one quarter of the acquired business against its full financing.
