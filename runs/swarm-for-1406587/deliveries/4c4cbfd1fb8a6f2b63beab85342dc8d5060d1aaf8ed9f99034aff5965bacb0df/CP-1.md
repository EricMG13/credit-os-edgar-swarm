## Findings

### Bottom line
Forestar's debt is moderate and its liquidity is large relative to it, so creditors face little refinancing pressure before March 2028, but interest cover cannot be shown on cash because the filings print no cash interest paid. Total debt of USD 793.8 million was 3.13x last-twelve-months EBITDA of USD 253.7 million at June 30, 2026 and net debt of USD 398.9 million was 1.57x, on an EBITDA this analysis calculates because the issuer prints none.
Basis: calculation K03; calculation K05; calculation K06; 10-Q_2026-06-30_for-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 2–4

The pressure point is cash generation: operating cash flow was negative in fiscal 2024 and 2025 while inventory grew, and turned positive at USD 27.6 million only in the nine months to June 30, 2026. Cash and undrawn revolver capacity of USD 1,064.8 million cover debt 1.34x, but USD 65.0 million of that capacity was scheduled to mature on October 28, 2026 with no outcome established.
Basis: calculation K10; calculation K12; calculation K11; 10-Q_2026-06-30_for-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 24

### Key findings

Net leverage rose from 0.33x at the 2023 year end to 1.71x at the 2025 year end because the company turned cash into inventory, not because earnings fell: real estate grew from USD 1,790.3 million to USD 2,645.1 million while cash fell from USD 616.0 million to USD 379.2 million, fiscal 2025 added a net USD 100.0 million of debt, and EBITDA was USD 237.8 million in fiscal 2023 and USD 247.2 million in fiscal 2025. By June 30, 2026 net leverage had eased to 1.57x as inventory growth slowed: cash put into real estate was USD 69.3 million in the nine months against USD 561.0 million a year earlier. Leverage here is therefore set by how fast the company invests in land, which management controls, and it will rise again if the fiscal 2025 pace of investment returns without matching earnings growth.
Basis: calculation K04; calculation K06; calculation K18; calculation K23; 10-K_2025-09-30_for-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 59; 10-K_2024-09-30_for-20240930.htm.jsonl, page 55; 10-Q_2026-06-30_for-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 3–4

EBITDA was USD 237.8 million in fiscal 2023, USD 281.4 million in fiscal 2024 and USD 247.2 million in fiscal 2025. The fiscal 2025 fall came from selling, general and administrative expense rising from USD 118.5 million to USD 154.4 million and from gross margin slipping from 23.80% to 21.87%, only partly offset by revenue growth to USD 1,662.4 million. In the nine months to June 30, 2026 revenue rose 6.29% on tract and other land sales of USD 90.4 million against USD 14.6 million, while residential lot sales revenue slipped from USD 977.3 million to USD 963.9 million and gross margin eased to 20.80%; EBITDA grew only from USD 121.2 million to USD 127.7 million. The earnings base that supports the debt has not recovered to the fiscal 2024 level, and the land sales that drive growth are lumpy.
Basis: calculation K01; calculation K13; calculation K14; calculation K25; 10-K_2025-09-30_for-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 59; 10-Q_2026-06-30_for-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 2

Cash interest cover cannot be calculated: the cash flow statements print no interest paid, and all interest incurred is capitalized to real estate and released to cost of sales as lots are sold. Interest incurred rose from USD 32.6 million in fiscal 2024 to USD 45.5 million in fiscal 2025 after the 6.5% 2033 notes replaced the 3.85% 2026 notes. On that accrual proxy EBITDA cover fell from 8.63x to 5.43x and is 5.01x for the twelve months to June 30, 2026; the two notes alone carry USD 47.5 million of annual coupons, which twelve-month EBITDA covers 5.34x. The cash figure may differ because coupons are paid semiannually: accrued interest was USD 14.4 million at June 30, 2026 against USD 2.5 million at September 30, 2025. Cover stays at or above 5.01x on these proxies, but the direction since fiscal 2024 is down and no cash evidence confirms the level.
Basis: calculation K07; calculation K08; calculation K24; 10-K_2025-09-30_for-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 60; 10-K_2025-09-30_for-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 67; 10-Q_2026-06-30_for-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 7; 10-Q_2026-06-30_for-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 9

Operating cash flow was USD 364.1 million in fiscal 2023, when real estate fell by USD 206.3 million, then an outflow of USD 158.4 million in fiscal 2024 and USD 197.7 million in fiscal 2025 as real estate grew. EBITDA therefore overstates cash available for debt service in investment years and understates it in harvest years: free cash flow before debt service was USD 362.8 million in fiscal 2023 and an outflow of USD 199.9 million in fiscal 2025. Seasonality matters for the current year: the nine months to June 30, 2026 show free cash flow of USD 27.3 million, but the twelve months to that date show USD 282.9 million because the fiscal fourth quarter is the strongest delivery quarter.
Basis: calculation K15; calculation K03; calculation K23; 10-K_2025-09-30_for-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 60; 10-Q_2026-06-30_for-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 3–4; 10-Q_2026-06-30_for-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 27

At June 30, 2026 the company held USD 394.9 million of cash and USD 669.9 million of undrawn revolver capacity after USD 45.1 million of letters of credit, a total of USD 1,064.8 million or 1.34x total debt. All debt is senior unsecured and ranks equally: USD 300.0 million of 5.0% notes due 2028, USD 500.0 million of 6.5% notes due 2033 and an undrawn revolver, with no principal due for 20 months after the balance-sheet date. Availability is limited by a borrowing base tied to the book value of real estate and unrestricted cash, which the filings do not quantify, and USD 65.0 million of the commitments was scheduled to mature on October 28, 2026; without it availability would be USD 604.9 million and liquidity USD 999.8 million.
Basis: calculation K09; calculation K10; calculation K11; 10-Q_2026-06-30_for-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 7; 10-Q_2026-06-30_for-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 8; 10-Q_2026-06-30_for-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 23–24

Several claims sit outside the debt figure. Earnest money deposits of USD 202.2 million, 25.47% of total debt and up from USD 121.4 million at the 2023 year end, are customer funds that finance inventory and are released as lots are sold, and they are subject to mortgages secured by the real estate under contract; the filings do not name the lienholder, but such liens would sit ahead of the unsecured notes and revolver on those lots to the extent of the deposits. Surety bonds of USD 874.3 million and letters of credit of USD 45.1 million support development obligations, lease liabilities are USD 16.4 million, and a pledged reimbursements liability of USD 22.4 million is matched by an asset of the same size with no recourse to the company. Land purchase contracts carry a remaining price of USD 615.9 million against deposits of USD 33.7 million and are options, not obligations.
Basis: calculation K17; calculation K24; 10-K_2025-09-30_for-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 49; 10-K_2025-09-30_for-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 62; 10-K_2025-09-30_for-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 68; 10-Q_2026-06-30_for-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 13–15

The one price indication in the filings shows no stress: the fair value of debt was USD 807.8 million against principal of USD 800.0 million, 100.98% of principal, on quotes from inactive markets. This is not a market-implied risk assessment, but it gives no sign that lenders discount the notes.
Basis: calculation K19; 10-Q_2026-06-30_for-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 16

EBITDA is the main definition risk because the issuer prints none. This analysis adds interest charged to cost of sales and depreciation to income before income taxes and removes interest and other income. Without the interest add-back, twelve-month EBITDA would be USD 221.3 million and total debt 3.59x, so the add-back moves leverage by 0.46x; a reader who treats capitalized interest as an operating cost should use the lower EBITDA.
Basis: calculation K01; calculation K20; 10-K_2025-09-30_for-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 67; 10-Q_2026-06-30_for-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 7

No event after June 30, 2026 that changes net debt is stated in the filings, which end with the quarterly report signed on July 22, 2026; the only dated forward item is the USD 65.0 million revolver tranche due October 28, 2026, which does not change net debt because the revolver is undrawn. Earlier events were kept out of period figures: the October 30, 2025 increase of USD 25.0 million in revolver commitments, the December 2025 repayment of the USD 9.9 million other note payable, which lowered debt and cash together and left net debt unchanged, and the March 2026 increase of USD 50.0 million in commitments.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_for-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 69; 10-Q_2026-06-30_for-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 8; 10-Q_2026-06-30_for-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 10; calculation K10; calculation K26

The retirement of the 2026 notes shows one event in several figures: principal retired was USD 400.0 million (USD 329.4 million tendered and USD 70.6 million redeemed), cash paid was USD 405.0 million including USD 5.2 million of accrued interest, the loss recorded was USD 1.2 million, and the repayment of debt line of USD 680.0 million in the cash flow statement also holds USD 280.0 million of revolver repayments. The principal serves the debt history, the cash flow line serves cash movements and the loss serves earnings.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view
The strongest evidence against caution is the issuer's own measure: net debt to total capital was 17.7% at June 30, 2026 against a stated long-term aim of approximately 40% or less, no covenant breach is reported, and contracted lot sales to the controlling shareholder have a remaining sales price of USD 2,095.1 million against earnest money of USD 195.3 million. The cautious reading would fall away if covenant levels and the borrowing base proved wide, if cash interest paid matched the accrual proxy, and if operating cash flow stayed positive through the fiscal fourth quarter. It would harden if the annual report for fiscal 2026 showed operating outflows of the fiscal 2025 size with EBITDA below USD 247.2 million, or if the October 28, 2026 revolver tranche lapsed without replacement.
Basis: calculation K16; calculation K24; calculation K11; 10-Q_2026-06-30_for-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 15; 10-Q_2026-06-30_for-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 23

Dash cells in the statements are treated as missing, not zero, and the guarantor list is not printed.
