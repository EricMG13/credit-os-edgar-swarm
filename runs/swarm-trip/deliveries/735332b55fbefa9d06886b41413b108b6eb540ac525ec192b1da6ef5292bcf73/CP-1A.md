## Findings

### Bottom line
Experiences has become the credit, because the legacy Hotels and Other franchise is eroding with search traffic and no controlling shareholder or sponsor stands behind the company; creditors depend on how management uses the USD 741.0 million TheFork proceeds.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_trip-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 17, 23; EX-99.1_2026-11-05_trip-ex99_1.htm.jsonl, pages 2–3; calculation C04

Experiences revenue growth slowed from 10% in FY2025 to 1% in the first nine months of 2026 and was negative in the third quarter, while nine-month adjusted EBITDA fell 34.8% to USD 163.9 million. Management says it expects to prioritize debt reduction with the sale proceeds but has not committed to it.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-11-05_trip-ex99_1.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 14, 16; 10-Q_2026-09-30_trip-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 35, 65; calculation C03

### Key findings
The earnings base is moving from Hotels and Other to Experiences because the old franchise is shrinking, not because the new one is accelerating. Hotels and Other was 44.8% of FY2025 continuing revenue but 69.4% of adjusted EBITDA, and its nine-month revenue fell 21.0% as Google results, AI overviews and weaker click-through cut free traffic; the USD 369.5 million goodwill impairment in the third quarter followed that decline, and USD 44.3 million of goodwill remains in the unit. Booking and Expedia together supplied about 21% of consolidated revenue in FY2025, nearly all of it in this segment, so a single partner decision can move earnings.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_trip-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 12, 23; 10-Q_2026-09-30_trip-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 22, 46; EX-99.1_2026-11-05_trip-ex99_1.htm.jsonl, pages 3, 14, 16; calculation C03; calculation C04

Experiences growth has stalled in 2026 and is not yet profitable enough to replace the earnings being lost. Bookings rose about 6% over nine months and gross booking value about 5%, yet segment revenue rose only 1.5% and adjusted EBITDA fell 34.8%, because marketing absorbed 48.8% of third-quarter revenue against 43.3% a year earlier and cancellations rose after Middle East, Hawaii and Mexico disruption. The marketplace also runs on traveler prepayment, with USD 396.8 million of deferred merchant payables, so cash is strongest in the first half and reverses in the second.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_trip-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 3, 43, 48; EX-99.1_2026-11-05_trip-ex99_1.htm.jsonl, pages 1, 14; 10-K_2025-12-31_trip-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 11; calculation C03; calculation C07

Ownership is now diffuse, so creditors have no sponsor behind them and no controlling holder restraining the board. The merger with Liberty TripAdvisor Holdings closed on 2025-04-29, ended controlled-company status and left one class of common stock; the company then spent USD 501 million of cash on that purchase and on share repurchases in FY2025, against USD 341 million of net Term Loan B proceeds, and it reported USD 3.3 million of shareholder-activism costs in the first quarter of 2026. No dividends were paid in 2023 to 2025 and the credit agreement limits them, but the capacity under the credit agreement is not established.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_trip-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 17, 59, 67, 93, 109; 10-Q_2026-03-31_trip-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 24; calculation C05

The TheFork sale turns a small earner into cash that is several times net debt, but its use is a management decision. TheFork contributed about 6.6% of FY2025 as-filed adjusted EBITDA, and the USD 741.0 million net cash received on 2026-11-01 is 35.3 times that figure; management says it expects to prioritize reducing long-term debt, but no repayment, tender or capital-return decision has been disclosed.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_trip-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 186; 10-Q_2026-09-30_trip-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 11, 65; EX-99.1_2026-11-05_trip-ex99_1.htm.jsonl, page 2; calculation C06

The last two years of transactions were refinancings and shareholder repurchases, not leveraged acquisitions, and the debt structure is now simple. The Term Loan B of 2024 was enlarged by USD 350.0 million in 2025, the 2026 convertible notes were repaid from cash in April 2026, and the USD 500.0 million revolver is undrawn and matures on 2028-06-29.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_trip-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 160, 163; 10-Q_2026-09-30_trip-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 25, 62; calculation C13

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view
The strongest evidence against a cautious view is that the balance sheet is not stretched: net debt was USD 116.1 million at 2026-09-30 before the sale, the proceeds are several times that, there is no history of sponsor-driven distributions or creditor-adverse amendments in the sources, and Experiences scale is still growing in bookings and booking value. Cost actions of at least USD 85 million of annualized gross savings from the November 2025 plan and about USD 40 million more from the November 2026 plan could also hold adjusted EBITDA up if traffic stabilizes.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_trip-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 3, 24, 43; 10-K_2025-12-31_trip-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 71; EX-99.1_2026-11-05_trip-ex99_1.htm.jsonl, pages 2–3

The view would turn more favorable if the proceeds repay the Term Loan B and Experiences returns to revenue growth with a recovering margin. It would turn less favorable if the proceeds fund buybacks or acquisitions, if further goodwill charges follow, or if a search or partner change takes more Hotels and Other traffic, because that segment still produces most of the cash earnings.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_trip-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 22, 65; EX-99.1_2026-11-05_trip-ex99_1.htm.jsonl, page 16; calculation C04

### Transaction summary and new debt issues
The shareholder transaction of the period was the acquisition of Liberty TripAdvisor Holdings, agreed on 2024-12-18 and closed on 2025-04-29. Liberty TripAdvisor Holdings held 26.8 million Tripadvisor shares and, through the earlier governance arrangements, control; the aggregate price was USD 437 million, of which USD 411 million was cash, and the company lent USD 327 million to Liberty TripAdvisor Holdings in March 2025 so it could settle its exchangeable debentures before closing. The shares were retired, net shares outstanding fell by about 23.8 million, the company stopped being a controlled company and it moved its incorporation to Nevada on the same day.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_trip-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 17, 72, 93, 111–113; calculation C12

The debt raised in the period was a refinancing, not acquisition debt. In July 2024 the company issued a USD 500.0 million Term Loan B at SOFR plus 2.75% and used it to redeem the USD 500 million 2025 senior notes; in March 2025 it added USD 350.0 million at 98.56% of par to refinance the 2026 convertible notes, which it repaid at maturity on 2026-04-01 for USD 345.4 million from cash on hand. The Term Loan B amortizes at 1.00% a year and falls due on 2031-07-08, and the USD 500.0 million revolver, amended in 2023 and 2025, matures on 2028-06-29 with no borrowings.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_trip-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 160, 163–164; 10-Q_2026-09-30_trip-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 25, 62; calculation C13

The sale of TheFork to American Express was announced as a put option on 2026-06-14 at USD 700.0 million, exercised on 2026-08-01, documented by an equity purchase agreement on 2026-08-02 and completed on 2026-11-01. The company received about USD 741.0 million of net cash after working-capital, cash and debt adjustments and expenses, expects a gain of about USD 487.0 million in the fourth quarter of 2026, and will provide transition services for up to 12 months; the amount may change with final adjustments and TheFork is shown as a discontinued operation.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_trip-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 38; 10-Q_2026-09-30_trip-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 11–12; calculation C14

Two restructuring programs frame the operating response. The November 2025 actions target at least USD 85 million of annualized gross savings, with costs of about USD 33 million in the fourth quarter of 2025 and USD 4 million after, and the plan initiated on 2026-11-04 targets about USD 40 million of annualized gross savings at a cost of USD 15 million to USD 20 million, mainly in the fourth quarter of 2026.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_trip-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 71; EX-99.1_2026-11-05_trip-ex99_1.htm.jsonl, page 3
