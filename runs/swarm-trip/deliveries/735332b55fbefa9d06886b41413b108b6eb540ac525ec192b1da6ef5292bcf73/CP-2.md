## Findings

### Bottom line
Tripadvisor is financially safe today but a weakening business: low leverage and the TheFork cash protect creditors, while Hotels and Other earnings are eroding and Experiences growth has stalled, so creditors depend on the sale proceeds being used for debt.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); Business & governance (CP-1A); calculation C02

Net debt was USD 116.1 million at 2026-09-30 and negative USD 624.9 million pro forma for the TheFork sale completed on 2026-11-01, while last-twelve-months adjusted EBITDA fell to USD 207.3 million from USD 294.7 million in FY2025. Management says it expects to prioritize debt reduction but has not committed to it.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); 10-Q_2026-09-30_trip-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 65; calculation C02

### Key findings
Revenue durability is weak because the larger earnings engine is shrinking and the growth engine has stalled. Hotels and Other revenue fell 21.0% in the first nine months of 2026, which the company attributes to search engines and AI overviews displacing free traffic, yet the segment still produced 69.9% of nine-month adjusted EBITDA, so group earnings fall with a business that is no longer growing. Experiences revenue growth slowed from 10% in FY2025 to 1% over nine months and was negative 3% in the third quarter after conflict, flooding and unrest in key destinations raised cancellations.
Basis: Business & governance (CP-1A); EX-99.1_2026-11-05_trip-ex99_1.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 14, 16; 10-Q_2026-09-30_trip-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 41, 43; calculation C01

Margins have little cushion because the largest cost is the cost of buying traffic that used to arrive free. Adjusted EBITDA margin fell to 13.6% in nine months of 2026 from 19.0% a year earlier, marketing took 48.8% of third-quarter revenue against 43.3%, and third-quarter adjusted EBITDA fell 34% while revenue fell 11%. Cost programs of at least USD 85 million and about USD 40 million of annualized gross savings are management targets that have so far shown up mainly as lower personnel cost at 21.5% of revenue against 25.2%.
Basis: Business & governance (CP-1A); EX-99.1_2026-11-05_trip-ex99_1.htm.jsonl, pages 1–2, 16; calculation C12

Cash conversion is the weakest dimension, and the second half of each year is built to be cash-negative. Last-twelve-months free cash flow was negative USD 17.3 million, nine-month free cash flow of USD 98.6 million compares with USD 289.3 million a year earlier, and the fourth quarter of 2025 alone was negative USD 116.1 million because traveler prepayments reverse in the second half. Capital spending is light at 2.9% of last-twelve-months revenue, so the pressure is working capital and earnings, not investment.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); 10-K_2025-12-31_trip-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 11; calculation C09; calculation C10

Leverage and liquidity are the main support. Net debt of USD 116.1 million at 2026-09-30 compares with negative USD 624.9 million pro forma for the sale completed on 2026-11-01, and net leverage on adjusted EBITDA of 0.56x becomes negative 3.01x on the same basis, with gross leverage on carrying debt at 3.97x. Cash plus the undrawn revolver of USD 1,204.5 million overstates freely usable cash because USD 396.8 million of it is traveler money awaiting payment to operators, leaving USD 310.0 million of cash after that float; the pro forma cash after the sale is not established.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1)

Debt service is adequate and refinancing is not urgent, but cover is falling. Adjusted EBITDA covers cash interest 3.50x against 5.41x in FY2025, and adjusted EBITDA less capital spending covers cash interest plus scheduled amortization 2.40x. The Term Loan B pays a floating rate of SOFR plus 2.75%, amortizes USD 8.5 million a year and falls due on 2031-07-08, and the undrawn USD 500.0 million revolver matures on 2028-06-29, so the first date that tests market access is the revolver renewal; no rating or market price is supplied to show how lenders see the credit.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); Business & governance (CP-1A); calculation C06

Financial policy is the swing factor for creditors, and it is unresolved. With no controlling holder since the 2025-04-29 merger and activism costs of USD 3.3 million in the first quarter of 2026, the company spent USD 501 million of cash on the merger and share repurchases in FY2025 against USD 341 million of net Term Loan B proceeds, and USD 110.0 million of repurchase authorization remains unused. Against that, no dividends were paid in 2023 to 2025, and management says it expects to prioritize reduction of long-term debt with the USD 741.0 million of net sale proceeds.
Basis: Business & governance (CP-1A); 10-Q_2026-09-30_trip-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 11, 63, 65; calculation C11

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view
The strongest evidence against a cautious view is the sale: net proceeds of USD 741.0 million are 6.4 times net debt of USD 116.1 million at 2026-09-30 and 88.9% of the Term Loan B principal, so the debt could be almost cleared with cash the company already has. Experience bookings also still grew about 6% over nine months, and cost programs could hold earnings if search traffic stabilizes. On this evidence the credit question becomes whether earnings stop falling, not whether debt can be refinanced.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); Business & governance (CP-1A); 10-Q_2026-09-30_trip-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 11, 48; calculation C03; calculation C04

The view would improve if the proceeds repay most of the Term Loan B and Experiences returns to revenue growth with a recovering margin. It would weaken if the proceeds fund repurchases or acquisitions, if adjusted EBITDA falls again by the same 29.7% that separates FY2025 from the last twelve months to 2026-09-30, which would take it to USD 145.8 million and lift gross leverage to 5.64x with cover of 2.46x, or if a further goodwill charge follows, because the Hotels and Other unit's carrying amount now approximates its fair value.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_trip-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 22, 46, 65; calculation C02; calculation C05

Basis: Source readiness (CP-0)

Basis: calculation C05

### Strengths and weaknesses
The strongest support is low leverage backed by a large cash receipt. Net debt of USD 116.1 million is small against adjusted EBITDA of USD 207.3 million, the USD 741.0 million of net proceeds received on 2026-11-01 would turn net debt negative at USD 624.9 million pro forma, and no maturity falls before 2028-06-29, so creditors have time even if earnings keep falling.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); Business & governance (CP-1A)

A second strength is a modest cost of staying in business. Capital spending is 2.9% of last-twelve-months revenue and the Term Loan B amortizes only USD 8.5 million a year, so the cash needed to stand still is small, and the company can cut marketing, personnel and repurchases before it needs to touch debt. That flexibility is what lets adjusted EBITDA cover cash interest 3.50x even after a fall.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); calculation C06; calculation C09

A third strength is the scale and early cash of the Experiences marketplace. Experiences supplied 61% of nine-month 2026 continuing revenue, it is paid by commission with traveler cash collected before operators are paid, and bookings still rose about 6%, so a market that the company's cited third-party research puts at roughly 30% online penetration offers room to recover growth.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-11-05_trip-ex99_1.htm.jsonl, page 14; 10-Q_2026-09-30_trip-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 48; 10-K_2025-12-31_trip-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 6

The biggest weakness is dependence on a shrinking franchise. Hotels and Other revenue fell 21.0% in nine months of 2026, yet it produced 69.4% of FY2025 adjusted EBITDA and 69.9% of the nine-month figure, so each further point of traffic loss removes disproportionate earnings and, with them, cover and debt capacity.
Basis: Business & governance (CP-1A); calculation C01

A second weakness is thin and negative cash conversion. Last-twelve-months free cash flow was negative USD 17.3 million, a third of adjusted EBITDA is the stock-compensation add-back, and the seasonal reversal of the traveler float draws cash down in the second half, so adjusted EBITDA overstates the cash that reaches creditors.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); calculation C07; calculation C10

A third weakness is unproven financial policy. The company has no controlling holder, spent more cash on equity in FY2025 than it raised in new term debt, and has not said how it will use the sale proceeds, so the main support for creditors depends on a decision that has not been taken.
Basis: Business & governance (CP-1A); calculation C11

A fourth weakness is the concentration of income on two partners and one traffic source. Booking and Expedia together supplied about 21% of FY2025 consolidated revenue, nearly all in Hotels and Other, and Google is a significant traffic source that promotes its own competing products, so a single partner or search-engine decision can move earnings sharply.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_trip-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 12, 23

### Financial profile and credit quality
Of the nine dimensions of financial profile, one is Strong, five are Average and three are Weak, and the weak ones are the dimensions that drive cash. Liquidity is Strong even after the traveler float is removed, because USD 396.8 million of reported cash belongs to travelers until operators are paid, yet cash after the float of USD 310.0 million plus the undrawn revolver of USD 497.7 million comfortably exceeds the scheduled amortization of USD 8.5 million a year; the revolver's covenant is not supplied, so its availability is assumed. Ability to refinance is Average because leverage is low and the first maturity is the revolver on 2028-06-29, but market access is untested. Financial policy and governance is Average because dividends are absent and management states a debt priority, while the absence of a controlling holder and the USD 501 million of FY2025 cash to equity leave the outcome open.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); Business & governance (CP-1A)

Margin stability, free cash flow stability and business diversification are the three Weak readings. Adjusted EBITDA margin fell to 13.6% from 19.0%, last-twelve-months free cash flow was negative USD 17.3 million, and one segment still produced 69.9% of nine-month adjusted EBITDA while Booking and Expedia supplied about 21% of FY2025 revenue. Scale, competitive advantage, and cost and capex flexibility are the other three Average readings: the Experiences marketplace is large relative to a fragmented field and capital spending is only 2.9% of last-twelve-months revenue, but the Hotels and Other brand advantage is being bypassed by search engines.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); Business & governance (CP-1A); EX-99.1_2026-11-05_trip-ex99_1.htm.jsonl, pages 14, 16; 10-K_2025-12-31_trip-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 12; calculation C01; calculation C09

The trend is downward in earnings and upward in balance sheet strength. Adjusted EBITDA is 29.7% lower over the last twelve months than in FY2025, while net debt as filed moved from negative USD 228 million at the end of FY2024 to USD 137 million at the end of FY2025 and USD 116.1 million at 2026-09-30, or negative USD 624.9 million pro forma for the sale completed on 2026-11-01. The key missing datapoints are the covenant ratio and headroom, the use of the sale proceeds, any rating, and company guidance.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); calculation C02

### Company, ownership and revenue durability
The business is an experiences marketplace attached to a hotel-search advertising franchise, and the two earn money in different ways. Experiences, the Viator and Tripadvisor tours, activities and attractions marketplace, earns commission on completed experiences, so revenue follows travelers' spending and cancellations and cash is collected before operators are paid. Hotels and Other earns click and impression advertising, mainly from Booking and Expedia, so revenue follows free search traffic and partner budgets; that makes it more discretionary and more exposed to substitution than a contracted revenue stream. TheFork, a European restaurant-reservation platform, was sold on 2026-11-01 and is outside the continuing figures.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_trip-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 5–6, 12; Business & governance (CP-1A)

Ownership no longer includes a controlling holder, which frees management and removes a stable anchor for creditors. The merger with Liberty TripAdvisor Holdings closed on 2025-04-29 at an aggregate price of USD 437 million, of which USD 411 million was cash, leaving a single class of common stock with no sponsor. Creditors therefore face activism and repurchase risk in place of a sponsor that could provide support, and the only restraint named in the sources is a credit agreement whose terms are not supplied.
Basis: Business & governance (CP-1A); 10-K_2025-12-31_trip-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 17, 59, 72, 93

Revenue is discretionary, partly concentrated and partly structurally declining. Hotels and Other revenue is falling 21.0% for nine months and its adjusted EBITDA fell 41% in the third quarter, while Experiences revenue growth went from 10% in FY2025 to negative 3% in the third quarter and its adjusted EBITDA fell 27%. Experiences still has volume growth, with bookings up about 6%, but the issuer reports that disruption lifted cancellation rates across all points of sale, and revenue is recorded net of cancellations, so volume gains reach revenue late and incompletely.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-11-05_trip-ex99_1.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 14; 10-Q_2026-09-30_trip-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 43, 48

### Cost structure and cash conversion
Margins can be defended only partly, because the largest costs follow traffic and people. Marketing was 43.7% and personnel 31.6% of FY2025 as-filed costs, and marketing rose to 48.8% of third-quarter revenue as traffic acquisition replaced free search traffic, while personnel cost fell to 21.5% of revenue after the first cost program. Marketing can be managed brand by brand and market by market, but a cut also reduces the traffic that drives revenue, and personnel savings are limited by the restructuring cash they require, USD 15 million to USD 20 million for the November 2026 plan alone.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_trip-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 11, 107; EX-99.1_2026-11-05_trip-ex99_1.htm.jsonl, pages 1, 3; calculation C08

Adjusted EBITDA converts weakly into cash, so debt capacity rests on the balance sheet more than on flow. Free cash flow was 58.8% of adjusted EBITDA in FY2025 on the continuing perimeter, 60.2% over nine months of 2026 and negative 8.3% over the last twelve months, because the second half reverses the first-half working-capital gain and earnings fell. Capital spending is not the constraint at 2.9% of last-twelve-months revenue, and restructuring, cash interest and taxes sit between adjusted EBITDA and cash.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); 10-K_2025-12-31_trip-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 11; calculation C09; calculation C10

### Industry forces and macro factors
Rivalry and substitution are the forces that move credit quality. In experiences the company competes with specialist marketplaces and with operators seeking direct bookings, and in hotels with general travel agencies, metasearch providers, search engines and AI travel planners that the company itself lists as competitors, many with larger resources. The mechanism is that rivals and search engines take the traffic and conversion that Hotels and Other monetizes, which cuts revenue and forces higher marketing spend, so cash earnings and cover fall even if debt does not rise.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_trip-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 12, 29

Buyers and suppliers are less threatening than rivals but both are concentrated in places. Booking and Expedia, the two largest partners, bought about 21% of FY2025 revenue and can reduce spending at short notice because many agreements are terminable at will, while no single experience operator exceeded 10% of revenue, so supplier power in Experiences is low. New entrants are cheap to add because metasearch lowers the cost of reaching consumers, which keeps pressure on marketing spend.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_trip-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 12–13, 29

Macro and technology factors are material and PEST analysis is run for them. Conflict in the Middle East, flooding in Hawaii and unrest in Mexico hit Experiences bookings, revenue and cancellations from late February 2026, and the company says continuing tensions may weigh on the fourth quarter. Technology change, in the form of AI overviews, lower click-through and platform-based search, is the factor most likely to reduce Hotels and Other earnings further, while regulatory and tax changes are described only as possible effects on consumer spending and are not quantified.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_trip-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 41, 43; 10-K_2025-12-31_trip-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 70

### SWOT
Strengths are low leverage, a large cash receipt from the sale, no maturity before 2028-06-29, light capital needs of 2.9% of last-twelve-months revenue, and a growing Experiences volume base, which together give creditors time and flexibility. Weaknesses are the 21.0% revenue fall in Hotels and Other, a 13.6% adjusted EBITDA margin against 19.0% a year earlier, negative free cash flow over the last twelve months, adjusted EBITDA that includes a stock-compensation add-back worth a third of the total, and partner concentration of about 21%.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); Business & governance (CP-1A); calculation C01; calculation C07; calculation C09

Opportunities that could lift credit quality are repayment of the Term Loan B with the sale proceeds, a return of Experiences growth as the online share of a large market rises, and savings from the two cost programs. Threats that could weaken it are further search and AI traffic losses, repeated travel disruption in key destinations, repurchases or acquisitions funded from the proceeds, a further goodwill charge on the Hotels and Other unit, and a rise in the floating rate on the Term Loan B.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); Business & governance (CP-1A); 10-Q_2026-09-30_trip-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 22, 65

### Outlook
Over the next twelve to twenty-four months the debt position improves while earnings are unlikely to recover quickly. The tailwinds are the sale proceeds, lower interest burden if used to repay debt, the savings from the November 2025 and November 2026 cost programs, and the possibility that Experiences volumes recover after the 2026 disruption. The headwinds are the continuing decline in Hotels and Other revenue, higher traffic-acquisition cost, the company's own statement that geopolitical tension may still weigh on the fourth quarter, and a seasonal cash outflow in the second half. Net effect: liquidity supports the credit, earnings and cash conversion pressure it, and cover of 3.50x has little room to fall without debt repayment.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); Business & governance (CP-1A); 10-Q_2026-09-30_trip-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 43, 65

Over three to five years the outcome depends on whether Experiences becomes large enough to replace the earnings of a shrinking Hotels and Other unit. The company cites third-party research that the online share of the experiences market is about 30% and rising, which is a sector tailwind from outside the company, but its own Experiences adjusted EBITDA fell 27% in the third quarter, so scale has not yet turned into profit. If growth returns at a better margin the credit improves; if search and AI substitution reaches Experiences distribution as well, the credit would lose both engines together.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_trip-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 6, 29; EX-99.1_2026-11-05_trip-ex99_1.htm.jsonl, page 2

### Downside scenario
The realistic downside starts with search and AI displacement continuing at the 2026 pace while travel disruption lingers into the 2027 season. The first operational break is another year of falling Hotels and Other click revenue and elevated Experiences cancellations, with marketing rising above 48.8% of revenue as the company buys the traffic it used to receive free. The financial transmission is a second fall in adjusted EBITDA of the same 29.7% as between FY2025 and the last twelve months, to USD 145.8 million, which would lift gross leverage on carrying debt of USD 822.9 million to 5.64x and cut cover of cash interest to 2.46x, with a seasonal fourth-quarter outflow similar to the negative USD 116.1 million of 2025 deepening the cash drain.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); EX-99.1_2026-11-05_trip-ex99_1.htm.jsonl, pages 1–2; calculation C02; calculation C05

Management has levers, and resilience appears sufficient to avoid a liquidity problem so long as the proceeds are not distributed. Cost programs of at least USD 85 million and about USD 40 million, an unused repurchase authorization of USD 110.0 million that can simply be left unused, and a Term Loan B of which the net proceeds of USD 741.0 million could repay 88.9% are the levers, and no debt matures before 2028-06-29. The path to distress would require the proceeds to be spent on shareholders or acquisitions first and earnings to fall again afterwards, and the first observable sign would be the next quarterly report's statement on the use of proceeds and the rate of Hotels and Other decline.
Basis: Business & governance (CP-1A); 10-Q_2026-09-30_trip-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 63, 65; calculation C03

### Overall credit view
Overall, Tripadvisor presents an adequate but weakening business profile supported by low leverage, the TheFork cash and no maturity before 2028-06-29, but credit quality is constrained by falling Hotels and Other earnings, stalled Experiences growth and negative last-twelve-months free cash flow. Cash-flow durability appears weak because last-twelve-months adjusted EBITDA is 29.7% below FY2025 and a third of it is a stock-compensation add-back. Refinancing capacity is likely to depend on the use of the sale proceeds, the renewal of the revolver due 2028-06-29 and how lenders price the credit, none of which is established. The primary downside path is continued loss of search traffic combined with sale proceeds spent on shareholders. Further analysis would require the credit agreement and covenant ratio, a rating, company guidance and the decision on the proceeds. Top risk: erosion of Hotels and Other earnings before the proceeds are applied to debt.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); Business & governance (CP-1A); calculation C02; calculation C07
