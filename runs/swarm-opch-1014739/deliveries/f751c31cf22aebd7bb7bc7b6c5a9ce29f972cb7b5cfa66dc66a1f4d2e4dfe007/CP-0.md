## Findings

### Bottom line

The eight supplied filings and releases are enough for a financial, business and fundamental-credit review of Option Care Health, but they cannot support creditor-protection conclusions, because the debt documents and merger financing terms are absent.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_bios-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 21–23; 10-K_2025-12-31_bios-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 75–78

Statements, the debt note and the liquidity disclosure through 30 September 2026 can each be traced to a page. On 2026-10-05 the company agreed to a take-private by affiliates of Clayton, Dubilier & Rice and McKesson at $32.05 per share in cash, expected to close in the first half of 2027. Neither the merger agreement nor its debt financing is supplied, so post-closing leverage, the treatment of the existing term loan and notes, and covenant headroom are not established. Adjusted EBITDA for FY2025 is also not printed, which rules out a trailing twelve-month company-defined figure, and no agency rating is supplied.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_bios-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 21; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bios-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 22; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bios-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 37; EX-99.1_2026-11-04_exhibit991-q32026.htm.jsonl, page 6
