## Findings

### Bottom line

The five supplied Bristow filings support the requested financial, business and fundamental credit work on the consolidated company, so creditor conclusions can rest on reported statements through the third quarter of 2026 but cannot cover covenant headroom, ratings or market pricing.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_vtol_vtol-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 1; 10-K_2025-12-31_vtol_vtol-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 93–96

Two annual reports and three quarterly reports give four discrete quarters, nine-month periods and a trailing twelve-month view from printed figures. The gaps that matter to creditors are the absence of any indenture or credit agreement, no printed maintenance covenant test, and an ABL availability figure that does not reconcile to commitments less letters of credit.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_vtol_vtol-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 52–53; 10-Q_2026-09-30_vtol_vtol-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 13–14; 10-Q_2026-09-30_vtol_vtol-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 33; calculation C4
