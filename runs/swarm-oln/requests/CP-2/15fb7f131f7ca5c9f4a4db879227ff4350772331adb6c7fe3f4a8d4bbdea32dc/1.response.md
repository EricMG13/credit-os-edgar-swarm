## Findings

### Bottom line

Olin's fundamental credit profile is stretched but liquid, with net leverage of 7.76x on reported EBITDA and 5.21x on adjusted EBITDA, so creditors depend on the 2027 covenant and merger milestones rather than on near-term maturities.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); calculation C3; calculation C4

Cash and the undrawn revolver of USD 1,244.4 million cover the first maturity, a USD 465.0 million receivables facility due in November 2027, about 2.68x, and Winchester earnings are rising. Against that, trailing interest cover on reported EBITDA is only 2.12x and covenant levels are not printed, so the view is a moderate-risk credit with a defined time window, not a distressed one.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); calculation C11; calculation C5

### Key findings

Leverage is the weakest point and is driven mainly by lower earnings, with net debt also higher. Net debt rose by USD 138.6 million in nine months while trailing reported EBITDA fell to USD 360.8 million, so net leverage on reported EBITDA moved from 4.89x at the end of FY2025 to 7.76x and on adjusted EBITDA from 4.08x to 5.21x. No pro forma adjustment for the October 2026 credit amendment is established, because that amendment takes effect only at the merger closing. Because the covenant levels are not printed, how much of the revolver stays usable without the relief is not established.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); calculation C1; calculation C2; calculation C3; calculation C4

Cash flow is seasonal and less weak than the nine-month figure suggests. Free cash flow was negative at USD 36.9 million in the nine months of 2026, yet FY2025 free cash flow was USD 247.9 million and trailing free cash flow is USD 221.5 million, because the fourth quarter of 2025 alone produced about USD 321.2 million of operating cash flow as working capital reversed. The first nine months absorbed a Shintech payment of about USD 114 million, so the fourth quarter of 2026 decides whether the pattern repeats, and the issuer guides the fourth quarter lower than the third.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); Business & governance (CP-1A); calculation C6; calculation C7; calculation C8; calculation C9; 10-Q_2026-09-30_oln-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 25 and 45

Liquidity is adequate until the covenant relief period ends. Cash of USD 214.8 million and an undrawn revolver of USD 1,029.6 million total USD 1,244.4 million against USD 1,134.3 million of debt due through 2029, and nothing falls due before November 2027. The receivables facility has only USD 34.0 million of spare capacity limited by its borrowing base, so it is not a reserve, and the revolver and the term loan both mature in March 2030.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); calculation C5; calculation C10; calculation C11

The test is the end of covenant relief, no later than 2027-09-30. The credit agreement carries a net leverage ceiling and an interest coverage floor that were relaxed in February 2026, and the ratio levels are not printed, so the headroom cannot be measured; the issuer states compliance and no borrowing limit at 2026-09-30. If the merger closes in the first quarter of 2027 the new facility replaces these terms, so the merger is the planned way past the cliff, and a delay would leave tighter covenants against leverage above the FY2025 level.
Basis: Business & governance (CP-1A); 10-Q_2026-09-30_oln-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 8–9 and 13–14

Earnings concentration is moving from chemicals to Winchester. Chlor Alkali Products and Vinyls was 47.85% of nine-month sales against 55.28% a year earlier, its sales fell 14.17% and its segment earnings fell USD 190.6 million, with the Freeport vinyl chloride monomer outage costing USD 72.2 million, while Winchester sales rose 14.14% against an order backlog of USD 1,331 million. Winchester therefore props up cash flow while commodity chemical pricing stays weak, but about 13% of FY2025 sales are to the U.S. government and the fourth quarter is seasonally weakest.
Basis: Business & governance (CP-1A); calculation C12; calculation C13; calculation C14; calculation C15

Governance and financial policy are average, with no sponsor. The dividend of USD 0.20 per share a quarter continues, repurchases are not printed for 2026 and USD 1,948.9 million of authority remains, so the board can restart distributions at any time while leverage is elevated. Ratings are Fitch BB+ on Rating Watch Positive, Moody's Ba2 with a Negative outlook and Standard & Poor's BB on Credit Watch Developing, a split that followed the merger announcement.
Basis: Business & governance (CP-1A); 10-Q_2026-09-30_oln-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 49–53

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The best case against a cautious view is that the nine-month numbers overstate the weakness, which also qualifies the earlier statement that the dividend runs against negative free cash flow, true for nine months only. FY2025 free cash flow was positive after dividends, trailing free cash flow after dividends is USD 130.4 million, the issuer's own adjusted net leverage of 5.2x would fall toward 5.10x or 4.92x if the guided fourth quarter adjusted EBITDA of USD 80 million to USD 100 million were delivered with net debt unchanged, and after the merger announcement one agency moved to a positive watch and another to a developing watch. A first quarter 2027 closing on the stated facility, with combined leverage no higher than Olin's today, would move the view to average. A further fall in chemicals earnings, a repeat Freeport outage, an adverse appeal in the Shintech case or a closing that slips past the relief period would move it to weak.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); calculation C8; calculation C16; calculation C17; 10-Q_2026-09-30_oln-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 25 and 53

### Limitations

- Covenant levels and compliance certificates are not supplied, so the conclusion that liquidity is adequate until 2027 assumes revolver access holds; if a test binds earlier, usable liquidity is lower than the USD 1,244.4 million shown.
- Huntsman's debt and earnings are not supplied, so the view that the merger helps creditors is limited to Olin's own summary of the terms and could reverse if the combined obligor is more leveraged.
- Cash-flow seasonality rests on one prior year of quarterly pattern, so the conclusion that free cash flow stability is weak but not deteriorating could change if the fourth quarter release of working capital does not recur.
- Ratings are quoted as the issuer prints them with no agency report, so the market access conclusion rests on filing disclosure and not on agency reasoning.

### Strengths and weaknesses

The main credit strength is liquidity and maturity structure. Cash and the undrawn revolver of USD 1,244.4 million cover the first maturity, a USD 465.0 million receivables facility due in 2027, about 2.68x, and the revolver, term loan and 2030 notes fall due in 2030 or later, so refinancing pressure arrives only after the covenant relief and merger milestones.
Basis: calculation C5; calculation C11; 10-Q_2026-09-30_oln-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 12–14

A second strength is Winchester, whose nine-month sales rose 14.14%, whose segment earnings rose to USD 77.1 million from USD 67.1 million and whose backlog of USD 1,331 million provides visibility, which cushions weak chemical earnings.
Basis: Business & governance (CP-1A); calculation C14

A third strength is scale and integration: the largest chlor alkali capacity in North America by the issuer's own account, captive chlorine outlets in epoxy and chlorinated organics, and a diversified product list, which spreads fixed cost and gives more than one outlet for the same electrolysis output.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_oln-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 5 and 14

The main weakness is leverage against falling earnings, with net leverage of 7.76x on reported EBITDA and trailing interest cover of 2.12x, which leaves little room for another outage or price decline.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); calculation C3; calculation C9

A second weakness is concentration of operating risk at Freeport and in commodity chemicals, where an outage cost USD 72.2 million in nine months and subsidized Asian competition keeps epoxy and caustic pricing under pressure.
Basis: Business & governance (CP-1A); 10-Q_2026-09-30_oln-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 32–33

A third weakness is cash claims outside the debt and a distribution that does not flex: the Shintech judgment, environmental liabilities of USD 156.8 million and dividends of USD 68.3 million in nine months all call on cash while covenant relief is temporary.
Basis: Business & governance (CP-1A); 10-Q_2026-09-30_oln-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 25 and 46

### Company, ownership and operating model

Olin is a vertically integrated chlor alkali, vinyls and epoxy producer with a leading U.S. ammunition business, and its sales are 54.34% chlor alkali and vinyls, 20.23% epoxy and 25.43% Winchester in FY2025. About 32.37% of sales are outside the United States, no customer exceeds 10% of sales, and all three segments are capital intensive, so cash generation depends on keeping fixed-cost plants full at acceptable prices. The company is widely held with no sponsor, so financial policy is set by the board and the creditor risk is the dividend and the merger, not an owner's recapitalisation.
Basis: Business & governance (CP-1A); calculation C12

Revenue durability is average. Winchester has contracted backlog and a U.S. Army plant contract, while chlor alkali and epoxy sell at commodity prices that fell: chemicals sales are cyclical and tied to the Electrochemical Unit price, and management says it prioritises unit margin over volume, which protects price but gives up sales. Group sales were down 0.84% in the nine months with volume lost to the end of the Blue Water Alliance trading venture and the Freeport outage.
Basis: Business & governance (CP-1A); calculation C13; EX-99.1_2026-10-29_exhibit991q32026earningspr.htm.jsonl, page 2

Margin resilience is weak. Gross margin was 8.25% in the nine months of 2026 against 9.19% a year earlier, and the issuer's reported EBITDA margin fell with it. Electricity is the largest raw material, about three quarters of it from natural gas or hydroelectric sources, and about 73% of salt is internal supply, which helps cost but leaves earnings exposed to power and natural gas prices, which the third quarter report cites as a cost headwind.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); calculation C18; 10-K_2025-12-31_oln-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 6; 10-Q_2026-09-30_oln-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 32

Capital intensity is high but flexible at the margin. Capital spending is guided to about USD 200 million for 2026, against depreciation and amortization of about USD 470 million, so the company is spending well under its depreciation and cutting from USD 226.3 million in FY2025, which preserves cash but defers asset renewal. EBITDA converted to free cash flow at 45.54% in FY2025 and at a negative 9.53% in the nine months of 2026, and cash interest alone absorbs 47.17% of trailing reported EBITDA.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); calculation C7; calculation C8; calculation C19; calculation C20; 10-Q_2026-09-30_oln-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 45

### Market frameworks

Rivalry is the force that matters most. The issuer describes global overcapacity, low-priced imports across the epoxy range and subsidized Asian exports, and the owner of a large North American competitor changed in January 2026, so price discipline in chlor alkali and epoxy is outside Olin's control and caps margin recovery. Buyer power is limited by a spread of customers with none above 10% of sales, and supplier power is a risk because the issuer relies on a limited number of suppliers for specified feedstock and services and on third-party transportation.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_oln-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 14–15; EX-99.1_2026-10-29_exhibit991q32026earningspr.htm.jsonl, page 6

Regulatory and policy factors are material to credit. Environmental liabilities of USD 156.8 million at 2026-09-30 are a long-dated cash claim, the Section 45V hydrogen tax credit supported earnings by USD 5.0 million in the third quarter of 2026 against USD 32.0 million a year earlier, and U.S. government contracts carry standard termination rights. Social and technology factors are not primary credit drivers on the supplied documents.
Basis: Business & governance (CP-1A); 10-Q_2026-09-30_oln-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 46; EX-99.1_2026-10-29_exhibit991q32026earningspr.htm.jsonl, page 2

In credit terms the strengths are liquidity, maturities beyond 2027, Winchester and integration, the weaknesses are leverage, Freeport concentration, commodity exposure and cash claims outside the debt, the opportunities are a merger-date facility and recovery in chemicals pricing, and the threats are covenant tightening in 2027, a failed merger and a loss on appeal.
Basis: Business & governance (CP-1A); 10-Q_2026-09-30_oln-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 8–9

### Financial profile and credit quality

Scale and market position is strong on capacity, since the issuer reports the largest chlor alkali capacity in North America, although current earnings are weak. Competitive advantage is average, because integration lowers cost but commodity pricing sets returns. Business diversification is average, with three segments but chemicals still 47.85% of nine-month sales. Cost and capex flexibility is average, because spending was cut but depreciation and fixed cost dominate. Margin stability is weak, free cash flow stability is weak because of the swing between the nine-month and annual results, ability to refinance is average because maturities run to 2029 and beyond but leverage is high and ratings are a notch or more below investment grade, liquidity is average, and financial policy and governance is average. Overall the profile is average to weak, with a deteriorating trend in 2026 and a recovery case that rests on the fourth quarter and on the merger.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); Business & governance (CP-1A); calculation C3; calculation C5; calculation C12

### Outlook

Over twelve to twenty-four months the headwinds are elevated year-end customer destocking, higher feedstock costs, subsidized Asian exports and planned turnarounds, which the issuer says will push the fourth quarter below the third, with adjusted EBITDA guided to USD 80 million to USD 100 million. The tailwinds are normal operations at the vinyl chloride monomer plant, a recovering epoxy segment, Winchester backlog and the merger. Over three to five years the cost savings programme the issuer calls Beyond250 and a larger combined company are claimed benefits that are management language and are not quantified in the supplied documents.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_oln-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 45; EX-99.1_2026-10-29_exhibit991q32026earningspr.htm.jsonl, pages 2–3

### Downside scenario

The realistic downside starts with a second outage at Freeport or a deeper year-end destocking in chlor alkali, which cuts chemicals sales and margin in the fourth quarter and into the first half of 2027. Lower earnings reduce operating cash flow just as the Shintech appeal, environmental spending and the USD 0.20 quarterly dividend continue, so net borrowing rises and trailing adjusted leverage stays above 5x. The first financial break is not liquidity but the covenant tests: if they bind before relief ends, revolver and receivables availability shrink while USD 465.0 million is due in November 2027. Management levers are capital spending, the dividend, the paused repurchases and merger timing. Liquidity of USD 1,244.4 million appears sufficient to avoid distress through 2027 without covenant limits, so the observable triggers are a closing that slips, a failed refinancing of the receivables facility and any restriction on borrowing.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); calculation C5; calculation C11; 10-Q_2026-09-30_oln-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 13–15 and 45

### Issuer matrix

Revenue durability is average, margin resilience is weak, free cash flow conversion is weak, the liquidity buffer is average, refinancing capacity is average, and governance and financial policy is average. The primary downside path is a further chemicals earnings decline while covenant relief ends and the merger is delayed.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); Business & governance (CP-1A); calculation C3; calculation C11

### Overall credit view

Overall, Olin presents an adequate business profile supported by scale, integration, Winchester and a maturity wall that starts in 2027, but credit quality is constrained by leverage of 5.21x on adjusted EBITDA and 7.76x on reported EBITDA and by exposure to Freeport and to commodity chemicals pricing. Cash-flow durability appears weak to adequate because of the seasonal working capital swing and cash claims outside the debt. Refinancing capacity depends on the merger closing and on the covenant terms that follow it. The primary downside path is a further earnings decline reaching the end of covenant relief. A definitive opinion needs the executed credit agreement, Huntsman's financial statements and the proxy statement. The top risk is covenant tightening in 2027 against elevated leverage.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); Business & governance (CP-1A); calculation C3; calculation C4
