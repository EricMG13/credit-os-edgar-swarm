## Findings

### Bottom line

Olin's net debt of USD 2,798.3 million is 7.76x trailing reported EBITDA, up from 4.89x at the end of 2025, and only the issuer's own add-backs bring it down to 5.2x, so creditor protection rests on those add-backs being non-recurring. Net debt rose USD 138.6 million in nine months while trailing reported EBITDA fell from USD 544.4 million to USD 360.8 million, so the deterioration comes from earnings, not from borrowing.

Reported EBITDA covers cash interest only 2.12x, and nine-month operating cash flow of USD 69.7 million funded neither capital spending of USD 106.6 million nor dividends of USD 68.3 million; net borrowings of USD 185.3 million covered the gap. Liquidity of USD 1,244.4 million and no maturity before November 2027 give time.

### Key findings

#### Leverage trajectory and drivers

Net debt to trailing reported EBITDA rose from 3.17x at the end of 2024 to 4.89x at the end of 2025 and 7.76x at 30 September 2026, and total debt to EBITDA reached 8.35x. Net debt was USD 2,666.6 million, USD 2,659.7 million and USD 2,798.3 million on those dates, so the ratio moved with earnings: reported EBITDA fell from USD 840.6 million in 2024 to USD 544.4 million in 2025 and to USD 360.8 million for the twelve months to September 2026. The issuer's adjusted measure gives 4.1x at the end of 2025 and 5.2x at September 2026; the reported measure leads here because the add-backs are not shown to be non-recurring.
Basis: calculation cp1-1; calculation cp1-2; calculation cp1-5; calculation cp1-7; calculation cp1-8; calculation cp1-9; calculation cp1-14; calculation cp1-15; calculation cp1-16; EX-99.1_2026-10-29_exhibit991q32026earningspr.htm.jsonl, page 11

The gap between the two measures is USD 175.8 million over the last twelve months and USD 81.7 million in the first nine months of 2026, which comprises restructuring charges of USD 21.3 million, legacy litigation of USD 36.1 million and merger-related costs of USD 24.3 million. Restructuring has been charged in every period presented, so it behaves as a running cost rather than a one-off, and the fiscal 2025 reconciliation that would show what was added back in the fourth quarter is not supplied.
Basis: calculation cp1-19; calculation cp1-28; EX-99.1_2026-10-29_exhibit991q32026earningspr.htm.jsonl, page 10; 10-K_2025-12-31_oln-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 81

#### Coverage

Cash interest paid was USD 170.2 million over the last twelve months, so reported EBITDA covers it 2.12x, down from 4.65x in 2024 and 3.06x in 2025; on the issuer's adjusted EBITDA the cover is 3.15x. Interest expense, net was USD 130.9 million in the first nine months of 2026 against USD 138.1 million a year earlier, so cover is falling because earnings fall, not because the cost of debt rises.
Basis: calculation cp1-11; calculation cp1-17; 10-Q_2026-09-30_oln-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 3, 5

#### Cash-flow quality

Operating cash flow was USD 69.7 million in the first nine months of 2026 against USD 153.0 million a year earlier. After capital spending of USD 106.6 million, free cash flow was negative USD 36.9 million, and after dividends of USD 68.3 million the shortfall was USD 105.2 million; net borrowings of USD 185.3 million funded it and cash rose USD 47.2 million. Receivables absorbed USD 133.4 million of cash.
Basis: calculation cp1-20; calculation cp1-21; 10-Q_2026-09-30_oln-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 4–5

#### Liquidity and maturities

Liquidity at 30 September 2026 was USD 1,244.4 million: cash of USD 214.8 million plus undrawn revolver commitments of USD 1,029.6 million. Receivables financing headroom of USD 34.0 million is additional but limited by the borrowing base. No debt matures before the USD 465.0 million receivables financing in November 2027; the next maturities are USD 669.3 million in 2029, USD 1,213.4 million in 2030 and USD 600.0 million in 2033, and the recovery zone bonds of USD 83.0 million have no stated maturity year.
Basis: calculation cp1-22; calculation cp1-24; 10-Q_2026-09-30_oln-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 12–15

#### Debt composition

Total debt of USD 3,013.1 million is carrying value after USD 17.6 million of deferred issuance costs, against instrument principal of USD 3,030.7 million. Of that principal, USD 1,246.1 million is at variable rates, and the issuer estimates that a 100 bp rise in the reference rate adds USD 12.5 million to annual interest expense. Operating lease liabilities and the accrued pension liability, together USD 584.3 million, sit outside net debt.
Basis: calculation cp1-25; calculation cp1-27; 10-Q_2026-09-30_oln-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 12, 54

#### Segment drivers and outlook

Third-quarter 2026 sales were USD 1,747.7 million against USD 1,713.2 million a year earlier, but chlor alkali and vinyls segment results fell to USD 71.3 million from USD 127.6 million. Epoxy earned USD 0.6 million against a loss of USD 32.2 million, and Winchester earned USD 33.8 million against USD 19.3 million. The issuer expects fourth-quarter 2026 adjusted EBITDA of USD 80 million to USD 100 million, against USD 67.7 million calculated for the fourth quarter of 2025.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-10-29_exhibit991q32026earningspr.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 8; calculation cp1-13

#### Data quality and subsequent events

Gross debt, cash and net debt agree between the quarterly reports and the issuer's releases at each 2026 quarter end and at 31 December 2025. The issuer's headline liquidity of about USD 1.3 billion is not itemized, and neither a fiscal 2025 reconciliation nor a fourth-quarter 2025 release was supplied, so fourth-quarter 2025 sales of USD 1,665.1 million and EBITDA are calculated. On 27 October 2026 Olin signed a second amendment to its credit facility that provides a senior secured facility of USD 2,500.0 million only if the Huntsman merger closes; it changes no net debt today and is not reflected in any figure here.
Basis: calculation cp1-13; calculation cp1-22; 10-Q_2026-09-30_oln-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 8–9, 13; EX-99.1_2026-10-29_exhibit991q32026earningspr.htm.jsonl, page 3

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest case against reading leverage at 7.76x is that reported EBITDA is depressed by an outage and by charges the issuer treats as temporary: higher costs and lost sales at the Freeport vinyl chloride monomer plant cost the chlor alkali and vinyls segment USD 32.1 million in the third quarter of 2026, and the issuer guides fourth-quarter adjusted EBITDA to USD 80 million to USD 100 million, above the USD 67.7 million of the fourth quarter of 2025. If that recovery holds and the add-backs stop, trailing adjusted EBITDA would rise from USD 536.6 million and the issuer's 5.2x would be the better measure; if restructuring and litigation charges continue, the reported 7.76x is the better guide, and a covenant relief period that ends no later than 30 September 2027 makes the answer matter.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-10-29_exhibit991q32026earningspr.htm.jsonl, pages 1–2; 10-Q_2026-09-30_oln-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 13; calculation cp1-19; calculation cp1-13
