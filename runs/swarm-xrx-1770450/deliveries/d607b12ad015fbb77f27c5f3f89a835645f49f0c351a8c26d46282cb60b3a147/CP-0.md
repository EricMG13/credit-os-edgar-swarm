## Findings

### Bottom line

The five supplied filings support the requested credit snapshot of Xerox Holdings Corporation to 30 September 2026 from primary evidence, but covenant, priority, rating and pricing views cannot be drawn from them, so a creditor should treat those as open. The set is two annual reports and three quarterly reports of the combined filing with Xerox Corporation, and the latest was signed 13 days before the as-of date.
Basis: 10-K_2024-12-31_xrx-20241231.htm.jsonl; 10-K_2025-12-31_xrx-20251231.htm.jsonl; 10-Q_2026-03-31_xrx-20260331.htm.jsonl; 10-Q_2026-06-30_xrx-20260630.htm.jsonl; 10-Q_2026-09-30_xrx-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 1, 115; calculation C2

Three limits matter. An exchange of 2028 senior notes was expected to close on or about 2026-11-13, 5 days before the as-of date, and nothing supplied confirms it closed. The executed indentures and credit agreements are not supplied, so covenant and security terms rest on the filings' own summaries. The quarterly reports print neither cash interest paid nor EBITDA, so trailing interest coverage can only be derived from statement lines and must be labelled as derived.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_xrx-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 37–38, 56; 10-Q_2026-03-31_xrx-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 87; 10-K_2025-12-31_xrx-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 7, 227; calculation C3
