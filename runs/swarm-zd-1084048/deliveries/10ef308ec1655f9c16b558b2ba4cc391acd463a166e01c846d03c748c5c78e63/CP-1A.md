## Findings

### Bottom line

Ziff Davis is a publicly listed, advertising-led media company with no controlling shareholder or sponsor stated, so creditors rely on its own cash and earnings rather than owner support, and governance risk rests on how freely it returns cash to shareholders. At September 30, 2026 cash of USD 1,407.7 million exceeded debt principal of USD 872.3 million after the sale of Connectivity for USD 1,200 million, but continuing operations lost USD 75.4 million at the operating line over nine months and USD 265.0 million of cash went to share repurchases.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against caution is the balance sheet and the absence of an owner with an extraction incentive. Cash exceeded debt principal by USD 535.39 million, the USD 350.0 million revolving facility was undrawn with USD 348.9 million available, no dividend was paid in 2023, 2024 or 2025, free cash flow was USD 287.87 million in FY2025 with Connectivity included, controls over financial reporting were effective with an unqualified audit opinion, and the related-party note discloses one fund investment that needs no further capital. The view would improve if management committed the sale proceeds to debt reduction or a stated minimum cash balance, or if recast continuing results showed stable advertising revenue and positive free cash flow. It would weaken if repurchases continue at the nine-month pace, because finishing the remaining authorization at September's average price would use USD 327.84 million, 61.23% of the cash above debt principal, while taxes of USD 130.8 million are payable and the revolving facility matures on 2027-06-18, or if acquisitions return to the 2024 scale funded from sale cash.

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

On 2026-03-02 Ziff Davis agreed to sell its Connectivity business, the Ookla group of broadband testing and insight brands, to Accenture Inc. for an aggregate cash price of USD 1,200 million, subject to customary adjustments, and completed the sale on 2026-06-17. Total proceeds were USD 1,216.1 million, being USD 1,179.1 million at closing plus USD 37.0 million in escrow, and cash received at closing net of USD 45.0 million of cash divested was USD 1,134.1 million. The escrow holds USD 12.0 million for working-capital adjustments, to be settled within 120 days of closing and still a receivable at September 30, 2026, and USD 25.0 million for indemnifications, payable on the earlier of settlement of indemnification matters or three years after closing.
