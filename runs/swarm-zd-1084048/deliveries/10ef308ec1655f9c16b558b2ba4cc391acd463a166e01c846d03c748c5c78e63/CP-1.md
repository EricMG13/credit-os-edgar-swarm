## Findings

### Bottom line

Ziff Davis held more cash than debt at the end of the third quarter of 2026 after selling Connectivity, which cushions creditors near term, but earnings-based leverage on the continuing business cannot be calculated from the filings. Cash was USD 1,407.7 million against carrying-value debt of USD 868.2 million, a net cash position of USD 539.5 million, and the revolving credit facility was undrawn with USD 348.9 million available. At the 2025 year end, before the sale, net debt was USD 259.5 million, or 0.63x EBITDA of USD 411.8 million, derived from operating income and depreciation and including Connectivity.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_zd-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-09-30_zd-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 27; 10-Q_2026-09-30_zd-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 34; 10-K_2025-12-31_zd-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 128–129; calculation C60; calculation C61; calculation C62; calculation C63; calculation C64

The cushion is not recurring cash generation: free cash flow for nine months 2026 was an outflow of USD 49.3 million on a combined basis, and income taxes payable of USD 130.8 million were owed at quarter-end. The 1.75% convertible notes of USD 149.1 million were repaid after quarter-end, leaving notes of USD 723.2 million due in 2028 and 2030.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_zd-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-Q_2026-09-30_zd-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-09-30_zd-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 48; calculation C70; calculation C68; calculation C65; calculation C87

### Key findings

- The sale of Connectivity turned net debt into net cash but cut reported earnings: EBITDA on the continuing basis was USD 62.0 million for nine months 2026 against USD 188.6 million a year earlier, after goodwill impairments of USD 84.4 million and USD 17.6 million that are not added back, on revenue of USD 843.7 million against USD 874.2 million. Proceeds net of cash divested were USD 1,134.1 million.
  Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_zd-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-Q_2026-09-30_zd-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 4; calculation C73; calculation C74; calculation C75; calculation C76; calculation C77; calculation C78; calculation C69
- Liquidity is strong at the balance sheet date but rests on the sale: over the same nine months the company repurchased shares for USD 265.0 million and bought property and equipment for USD 91.1 million, and combined operating cash flow in the third quarter was an outflow of USD 77.1 million.
  Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_zd-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-Q_2026-06-30_zd-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 4; calculation C72; calculation C84; calculation C71
- Debt is simple: after the repayment of the 1.75% notes the remaining principal is the 2028 convertible notes of USD 263.1 million and the 2030 senior notes of USD 460.0 million, all fixed rate and unsecured; cash at quarter-end, before the repayment of the 1.75% notes and before taxes payable, exceeded that principal by USD 684.5 million. The revolver commitment of USD 350.0 million is secured, undrawn and matures seven months after the as-of date with no extension disclosed.
  Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_zd-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 27; 10-Q_2026-09-30_zd-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 28–33; 10-Q_2026-09-30_zd-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 77; calculation C66; calculation C67; calculation C86; calculation C85
- A non-debt float is part of the cash story: settlement payables to gift-card customers sit inside accounts payable, not debt, and fell from USD 469.1 million at the 2025 year end to USD 199.6 million at quarter-end, a fall of USD 269.5 million; the filing does not say how much cash is held against them.
  Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_zd-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 8–9; calculation C81; calculation C80; calculation C82

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against comfort is on earnings, not on the balance sheet: the continuing business reported an operating loss of USD 75.4 million for nine months 2026, net advertising revenue retention fell in all three advertising segments between the third quarters of 2025 and 2026, and subscription customer counts fell in all three subscription segments. The net cash position is a one-time result of the sale, so a durable view needs last-twelve-months EBITDA on the continuing basis, which no filing prints yet. The view would improve if recast full-year 2025 figures show EBITDA that comfortably covers the remaining notes and interest, and would weaken if buybacks, acquisitions or the tax payment on the sale gain used up the cash balance, which stood at USD 1,407.7 million at quarter-end before the 1.75% repayment.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_zd-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-Q_2026-09-30_zd-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 54–55; calculation C79; calculation C60
