## Findings

### Bottom line

Clean Harbors is a widely held public company with no controlling shareholder or sponsor named in the filings, so creditors depend on management and board discipline rather than owner support, and 2026 shows debt-assisted acquisitions running beside continued share repurchases.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_clh.htm.jsonl, page 61; 10-Q_2026-09-30_clh.htm.jsonl, page 48; calculation C09

The business is a North American hazardous-waste and used-oil network. Environmental Services supplies 83.44% of nine-month 2026 revenue, while the oil segment margin rose to 31.4% on product prices that can reverse. Five 2026 purchases total USD 1,158.0 million, the largest, EnviroServe at USD 470 million, paid from new notes, and the proxy statement that would show board independence and holdings is not supplied.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_clh.htm.jsonl, page 10; 10-Q_2026-09-30_clh.htm.jsonl, page 19; 10-Q_2026-09-30_clh.htm.jsonl, page 35; calculation C01; calculation C09

### Key findings

No controlling holder or sponsor is identified, so the question for creditors is the discipline of the board and management, not owner behaviour. The annual report counts about two hundred holders of record and well over one hundred thousand beneficial holders, gives no holder percentage, and leaves ownership, independence and committee detail to a proxy statement that is not supplied.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_clh.htm.jsonl, page 61; 10-K_2025-12-31_clh.htm.jsonl, page 159

Business risk is diversified by customer and service line but concentrated in capital-intensive disposal assets. No customer exceeded 10% of revenue, the top ten industries supplied about 80% of 2025 revenue and no service line exceeded 29.64% of nine-month 2026 revenue, but ten incinerators and seven landfills carry fixed costs, so margins depend on keeping throughput high.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_clh.htm.jsonl, page 8; 10-K_2025-12-31_clh.htm.jsonl, page 10; 10-K_2025-12-31_clh.htm.jsonl, page 106; 10-Q_2026-09-30_clh.htm.jsonl, page 10; calculation C04; calculation C19

Earnings are moving toward the price-driven oil segment, so part of the 2026 improvement should be treated as cyclical. The segment margin was 31.4% in nine months 2026 against 16.8% a year earlier and its share of segment Adjusted EBITDA rose from 9.64% to 18.37%, but oil product revenue fell 15.10% in 2025 before rising 26.70% in 2026 and the issuer ties the strength to global shortages; the larger Environmental Services margin was steadier at 26.3% against 25.9%.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_clh.htm.jsonl, page 66; 10-K_2025-12-31_clh.htm.jsonl, pages 67–68; 10-Q_2026-09-30_clh.htm.jsonl, page 10; 10-Q_2026-09-30_clh.htm.jsonl, pages 34–35; EX99.1_2026-09-30_clh_clh-q32026pressrelease-htm.htm.jsonl, pages 2–3; calculation C06; calculation C07; calculation C08

Acquisitions are now a repeated, debt-assisted strategy priced richly before synergies, so creditors rely on synergy delivery that the filings do not evidence. ### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against reading Clean Harbors as a disciplined public-company borrower is the pace of 2026: five purchases in nine months for USD 1,158.0 million, the last paid with new notes at an expected 17.41x before synergies, alongside USD 75.0 million of repurchases. If debt-funded purchasing continues into 2027, or if the USD 25 million and USD 4 million of expected synergies are not delivered, creditors would be financing a roll-up whose returns rest on issuer estimates.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_clh.htm.jsonl, pages 11–14; 10-Q_2026-09-30_clh.htm.jsonl, page 18; 10-Q_2026-09-30_clh.htm.jsonl, page 48; EX99.1_2026-06-30_clh_enviroservepressrelease-htm.htm.jsonl, page 1; EX99.1_2026-03-31_clh_terranovapressrelease-htm.htm.jsonl, page 1; calculation C09; calculation C11

Evidence for the view is also strong: no dividend has ever been paid, repurchases come from cash and can stop, covenant compliance and effective internal control are reported, and the 2025 refinancing extended maturities without adding secured debt to the notes. The view would turn to a higher-risk reading if the proxy statement showed a holder with board seats or veto rights, if no independent Chair is appointed, if the debt documents allowed large restricted payments, or if acquired earnings fall short of the issuer expectations of USD 27 million and USD 15 million; it would turn to a lower-risk reading if an independent Chair is confirmed, covenant levels show wide headroom and the 2026 purchases are integrated without further debt-funded deals.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_clh.htm.jsonl, page 61; 10-K_2025-12-31_clh.htm.jsonl, page 135; 10-K_2025-12-31_clh.htm.jsonl, page 136; 10-K_2025-12-31_clh.htm.jsonl, page 155; 10-Q_2026-09-30_clh.htm.jsonl, page 48; EX99.1_2026-03-31_clh_amckimpressrelease-htm.htm.jsonl, page 1

A reversal in oil prices would change the earnings-mix conclusion: the oil segment margin was 16.8% a year earlier and 16.4% in 2025, so a return towards those levels would reverse much of the 2026 improvement in that segment.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_clh.htm.jsonl, page 35; 10-K_2025-12-31_clh.htm.jsonl, page 68; calculation C08; calculation C23
