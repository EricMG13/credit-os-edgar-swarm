## Findings

### Bottom line

The eleven supplied files support a credit snapshot of Clean Harbors Inc through the quarter ended September 30, 2026, but not covenant, legal-priority, ratings or market-pricing conclusions, so creditors should treat those areas as unevidenced.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_clh.htm.jsonl, pages 2–4, 16–19; calculation C1

Both annual reports, the three 2026 quarterly reports and six press releases give audited annual and unaudited quarterly statements from 2024 to September 30, 2026, and the newest source is dated 21 days before the 2026-11-18 cutoff. The 2034 unsecured notes of USD 600 million issued on October 1, 2026 and the EnviroServe acquisition of about USD 470 million completed on October 2, 2026 appear only as post-quarter disclosures, so quarter-end debt, cash and leverage exclude them and no pro forma figure is printed.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_clh.htm.jsonl, pages 11, 18–19; calculation C3

No debt ratings, executed indentures or credit agreements, market quotes, peer set or portfolio data were supplied, and the proxy statement that holds board and compensation detail is absent, so covenant and governance findings can rest only on filing summaries.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_clh.htm.jsonl, pages 159, 161–164; 10-Q_2026-09-30_clh.htm.jsonl, pages 42, 48, 53
