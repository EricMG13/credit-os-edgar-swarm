## Findings

### Bottom line

Clean Harbors carries moderate leverage, with net debt at 1.86x last-twelve-months reported EBITDA at September 30, 2026, but the October 2026 EnviroServe purchase lifts that to 2.22x pro forma before any earnings from the target, so creditors should anchor on the higher figure.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_clh.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 16–19; calculation C22; calculation C08; calculation C30

Gross leverage fell from 2.49x at the end of 2025 to 2.18x as last-twelve-months EBITDA grew faster than debt, and cash interest paid is covered 9.61x, but the gain leans on high re-refined product prices and one emergency response event worth about USD 120 million of revenue, either of which can reverse.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_clh.htm.jsonl, pages 4, 16–19; EX99.1_2026-09-30_clh_clh-q32026pressrelease-htm.htm.jsonl, pages 2–3; calculation C17; calculation C08; calculation C10

The issuer states only adjusted EBITDA, so reported EBITDA is derived from the statements; on the issuer's adjusted measure net leverage is 1.77x, or 2.11x pro forma.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_clh.htm.jsonl, page 76; 10-Q_2026-09-30_clh.htm.jsonl, page 42; calculation C36; calculation C30

### Leverage and coverage

Net leverage on reported EBITDA moved from 1.96x at the end of 2024 to 1.75x at the end of 2025 and 1.86x on the twelve months to September 30, 2026, while gross leverage moved from 2.61x to 2.49x and 2.18x. Earnings growth lowered gross leverage in 2026, but net leverage rose because USD 689,044 thousand of acquisitions and USD 77,133 thousand of share repurchases helped cut cash by USD 402,771 thousand over nine months; the October 2026 purchase was then financed from the proceeds of new notes rather than from cash on hand.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_clh.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 4; calculation C19; calculation C22; calculation C08

Cash interest paid of USD 137,780 thousand over the last twelve months is covered 9.61x by reported EBITDA against 7.10x for 2025. The comparison flatters the latest cover because coupon dates moved with the October 2025 refinancing, and the issuer guides 2026 interest expense, net of interest income, of USD 155 million to USD 160 million, which is above the cash interest paid in the latest twelve months even before interest income is added back.
Basis: EX99.1_2026-09-30_clh_clh-q32026pressrelease-htm.htm.jsonl, page 5; 10-K_2025-12-31_clh.htm.jsonl, page 135; calculation C10

### Cash flow quality

Operating cash flow of USD 664,673 thousand for nine months 2026, against USD 511,632 thousand a year earlier, funded capital spending of USD 397,480 thousand, but a USD 276,946 thousand build in receivables as revenue grew absorbed part of it. The issuer's adjusted free cash flow of USD 382,981 thousand is higher than operating cash flow less capital spending by USD 115,788 thousand because the issuer adds back USD 107,305 thousand of strategic growth investment and USD 8,483 thousand of asset sale proceeds.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_clh.htm.jsonl, page 4; EX99.1_2026-09-30_clh_clh-q32026pressrelease-htm.htm.jsonl, page 5; calculation C40

### Liquidity and maturities

Cash and cash equivalents were USD 423,544 thousand at September 30, 2026, with a further USD 93,010 thousand of short-term marketable securities, and USD 335.5 million was available under the USD 600.0 million revolving facility after a USD 120.0 million borrowing and USD 144.5 million of letters of credit. Apart from term loan amortization of USD 12.6 million a year, the earliest scheduled maturity is the 2029 notes, so scheduled refinancing needs before 2029 are limited to that amortization; the revolver borrowing was repaid in October 2026 and the facility runs to June 2029.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_clh.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 18, 48; 10-K_2025-12-31_clh.htm.jsonl, pages 95, 138; calculation C26

### Subsequent events and pro forma net debt

After the balance sheet date the issuer sold USD 600.0 million of 2034 unsecured senior notes on October 1, 2026, paid about USD 470 million in cash for EnviroServe on October 2, 2026 and repaid the USD 120.0 million revolver borrowing on October 16, 2026. These events are kept out of the period figures; they raise net debt by about USD 470 million and put pro forma net leverage at 2.22x on reported EBITDA and 2.11x on adjusted EBITDA, before any earnings from EnviroServe, which the issuer expects to be about USD 27 million of Adjusted EBITDA a year before cost synergies.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_clh.htm.jsonl, pages 11, 18–19; EX99.1_2026-06-30_clh_enviroservepressrelease-htm.htm.jsonl, page 1; calculation C28; calculation C30

### Data quality and definitions

Reported EBITDA is derived because the issuer states none, and the issuer says its loan covenants are based on Adjusted EBITDA without printing a covenant level, so no headroom can be stated. Finance lease liabilities of USD 229,317 thousand at the end of 2025 sit outside total debt and would raise gross leverage from 2.49x to 2.69x; the definition register and the gaps below list the other limits.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_clh.htm.jsonl, pages 75, 150; 10-Q_2026-09-30_clh.htm.jsonl, page 42; calculation C38
