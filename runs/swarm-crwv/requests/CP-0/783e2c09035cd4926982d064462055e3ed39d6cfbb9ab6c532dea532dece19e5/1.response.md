## Findings

### Bottom line

The supplied filings and earnings releases are sufficient for a credit snapshot of CoreWeave, Inc. through the quarter ended June 30, 2026, but not for covenant, ranking or recovery conclusions, and no supplied document is dated after August 11, 2026. Creditors should treat later conclusions as dated to the second quarter, with the August 2026 financings as subsequent events.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_crwv-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 5; 10-Q_2026-06-30_crwv-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 46

Basis: EX-99.1_2026-08-11_coreweave2q26earningspress.htm.jsonl, page 7; 10-Q_2026-06-30_crwv-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 154–155
