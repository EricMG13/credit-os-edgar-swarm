## Findings

### Bottom line

Aecom's fundamental credit is adequate but weakening: two delayed projects and shareholder returns have raised leverage sharply, leaving creditors a thinner cushion through fiscal 2027 but no material refinancing pressure before 2031.

Net leverage was 2.31x at 2026-06-30 against 1.11x at 2025-09-30, or 2.36x pro forma for the dividend paid on 2026-07-17 and 2.24x if the USD 58.5 million settlement agreed in July 2026 is received in full, each taken alone. Cash plus undrawn revolvers covers the expected USD 600 million to USD 800 million of project outflows 3.45x at the top of the range, and scheduled maturities to fiscal 2030 are only USD 117.8 million. The main risks are claims left uncollected, outflows above the range and repurchases resuming.

### Key findings

The business profile is adequate to good for creditors, because scale, a government-funded client base and a large order book give revenue visibility that the third-quarter loss does not undermine. Governments supply 50% of revenue, no client reaches 10%, backlog was $39.7bn at 2025-09-30 and remaining performance obligations were $20.4bn at 2026-06-30, about 60% of them due within twelve months. The work is fee-based and asset-light, so a downturn in one market is cushioned by the others.
Basis: Business & governance (CP-1A); 10-K_2025-09-30_acm_acm-20250930.htm.jsonl, pages 10–13; 10-Q_2026-07-03_acm_acm-20260703.htm.jsonl, page 15; calculation B18

The financial profile has deteriorated in fiscal 2026 because earnings fell, not because Aecom borrowed more. Calculated EBITDA for the last twelve months is 29.63% below FY2025 while gross debt is almost unchanged, so total leverage rose to 3.21x from 2.26x, interest cover on accrued interest fell to 4.18x from 6.52x, and free cash flow fell to 0.24x of EBITDA from 0.57x. Cash interest cover for the latest period is not established because the quarterly reports print no cash interest.

Guaranteed-maximum-price and fixed-price work is 61.61% of revenue, so cost overruns land on Aecom, and the projects now account for more than the whole 12-day rise in days sales outstanding to 86 days.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a weakening view is that the damage is concentrated and may prove temporary. Before the USD 336.8 million loss, last-twelve-months EBITDA would have been close to the FY2025 level, so net leverage on that basis would be 1.65x, well below the reported figure; the International segment margin rose to 10.35% from 8.79%; two legacy claims have already been collected or settled; repurchases are paused; and claims of about $620 million are 1.03x the low end of the expected outflows, so full recovery would roughly offset the cash cost, an analyst inference and not a management position.

### Business description and ownership

Aecom supplies planning, design, engineering, program management and construction management services to governments and private clients in transportation, facilities, water, environment and energy, and earns its income mainly by billing employee time, so cash generation depends on utilisation and collections and not on capital spent. Americas supplies most of revenue (22.39% comes from International) and AECOM Capital is a real estate investment segment with negligible revenue but continuing guarantees. The model supports debt service because most costs flex with staff and subcontractors, but it weakens liquidity when projects carry price risk, as the two Construction Management projects show.
Basis: Business & governance (CP-1A); 10-K_2025-09-30_acm_acm-20250930.htm.jsonl, pages 4–5; calculation B21

Ownership is widely dispersed with no sponsor, parent or controlling holder disclosed, so financial policy is set by the board and management, and creditor protection rests on covenants and on the company's own cash flow, not on sponsor support. The guarantors are wholly owned subsidiaries; the bank lenders hold liens on substantially all assets of the company and the guarantors, the 2033 notes are unsecured, and the parent and guarantor group is smaller and weaker than the consolidated company. Holder percentages, board independence and related-party dealings are not established because the proxy statement is not supplied.
Basis: Business & governance (CP-1A); 10-Q_2026-07-03_acm_acm-20260703.htm.jsonl, page 21; 10-Q_2026-07-03_acm_acm-20260703.htm.jsonl, pages 63–64

### Revenue, margin and cash conversion

Revenue is predictable and largely public-sector funded, but its quality is lower than headline revenue suggests. Pass-through subcontractor cost was 53% of fiscal 2025 revenue, work is awarded project by project, and government clients can terminate or renegotiate contracts at their convenience with many contracts renewed annually. Backlog of $39.7bn is gross of pass-through costs and $20.0bn above the obligations the accounting standard counts, so the credit consequence is good visibility but limited pricing power, because the markets are fragmented and entry barriers are low.
Basis: Business & governance (CP-1A); 10-K_2025-09-30_acm_acm-20250930.htm.jsonl, pages 13–16; 10-K_2025-09-30_acm_acm-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 53; calculation B18

Margins can be defended against volume pressure but not against project overruns. Costs flex with staff and subcontractors, yet the nine-month Americas margin fell to 3.66% from 6.79% on one project while International rose to 10.35% from 8.79%, so margin stability depends on execution risk and not on demand. Restructuring spend of about USD 25 million is expected in fiscal 2026 and is meant to support margin improvement.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-07-03_acm_acm-20260703.htm.jsonl, page 37; 10-Q_2026-07-03_acm_acm-20260703.htm.jsonl, pages 54–55; calculation B11; calculation B19

Cash conversion is the weakest part of the profile. Capital expenditure is only 1.05% of last-twelve-months revenue, so capex is not the problem, but free cash flow fell to 0.24x of EBITDA from 0.57x because claims and receivables absorbed cash, and the two projects explain the whole rise in days sales outstanding to 86 days. EBITDA turns into durable free cash flow for debt service only if the claims are collected.

### Competitive forces, policy and credit-translated SWOT

Rivalry is high and barriers to entry are low. The filing describes highly fragmented markets with many competitors, some larger or more specialised, and services that need little upfront capital, so Aecom competes on reputation, cost, client relationships and its network. Buyer power is strong because governments supply 50% of revenue and can terminate or renegotiate contracts at their convenience, although no single client reaches 10%. Supplier power matters through subcontractors, whose cost is passed through on cost-reimbursable work but not on fixed-price or guaranteed-maximum-price work, and technology change is addressed through investment in digital and AI tools. These forces cap margins and mean credit quality relies on scale, contract discipline and client relationships.
Basis: Business & governance (CP-1A); 10-K_2025-09-30_acm_acm-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 6; 10-K_2025-09-30_acm_acm-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 10; 10-K_2025-09-30_acm_acm-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 14; 10-K_2025-09-30_acm_acm-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 16; calculation B18

Policy and funding are material to credit because half of revenue is public. Funded programs such as the Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act and the One Big Beautiful Bill Act in the United States, federal programs in Canada and the ten-year infrastructure strategy in the United Kingdom support demand, while government shutdowns, changes in administration and funding directives, and trade policy are named risks, and International is 22.39% of revenue and exposed to currency moves. A funding delay would slow billings and add to working capital, which is the channel through which policy risk reaches debt service.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_acm_acm-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 44; 10-Q_2026-07-03_acm_acm-20260703.htm.jsonl, page 39; 10-Q_2026-07-03_acm_acm-20260703.htm.jsonl, page 44; calculation B21

The factors that could improve credit quality are collection of claims, continued growth in backlog and a sustained International margin, and the factors that could weaken it are further loss estimates, client claims, a large tax adjustment and resumed repurchases.

### Overall credit view

Overall, Aecom presents an adequate business profile supported by scale, client diversification and a large order book, but credit quality is constrained by fixed-price project exposure, weak cash conversion and shareholder returns that exceeded cash flow. Cash-flow durability appears adequate in a normal year and weak this year, because free cash flow fell to 0.24x of EBITDA while the projects absorb cash. Refinancing capacity is likely to depend on claims being collected, repurchases staying paused and leverage returning toward 1.11x, and maturities are not pressing. The comparison with typical leveraged-credit issuers in the sector is not established because no peer set is supplied. Further analysis would require the covenant ratio and definition, project-level data, the fiscal 2026 annual report and the proxy statement. The top risk is the cash cost and contingent exposure of the two Construction Management projects.
