## Findings

### Bottom line

The five SEC filings are sufficient for a credit snapshot of Alta Equipment Group Inc. to September 30, 2026, giving creditors primary-source debt, liquidity and maturity evidence but no covenant-headroom or intercreditor test, because no credit agreement or indenture was supplied.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_altg_altg-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 17–18; 10-K_2025-12-31_altg_altg-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 113–114

Three limits qualify that view. The filings state covenant compliance but print no covenant level, so headroom cannot be measured. Reported borrowing availability of USD 413.5 million is USD 117.4 million below what the printed facility limits imply, and the borrowing base is not printed. Board, ownership and compensation detail sits in a proxy statement that was not supplied. No source document is dated after the November 9, 2026 quarterly report, so the cutoff is respected.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_altg_altg-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 18, 55; 10-K_2025-12-31_altg_altg-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 110–111; calculation C3
