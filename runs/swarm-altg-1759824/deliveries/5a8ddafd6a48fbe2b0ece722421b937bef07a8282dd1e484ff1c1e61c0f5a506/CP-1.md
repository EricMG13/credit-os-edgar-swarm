## Findings

### Bottom line

Alta Equipment Group Inc. is a stretched credit: total debt was 4.65x last-twelve-month EBITDA at September 30, 2026, up from 3.47x at FY2023, and EBITDA covered cash interest only 2.02x, so creditors depend on refinancing the 2029 maturities rather than on deleveraging.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_altg_altg-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 4, 18; 10-K_2025-12-31_altg_altg-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 70–74; calculation C82; calculation C80; calculation C85

The ABL facility and the second lien notes, USD 692.9 million together, fall due on the same day in 2029, against cash of USD 21.8 million and reported availability of USD 413.5 million that includes floor plan lines. Counting floor plan payables as debt lifts leverage to 6.78x. Equity is a deficit of USD 47.1 million and no covenant level is printed, so headroom is not established.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_altg_altg-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 5, 17–18, 55; calculation C60; calculation C86

### Key findings

Leverage rose because earnings fell while debt grew. Reported EBITDA dropped from USD 192.1 million in FY2023 to USD 159.5 million in FY2024 and was USD 163.2 million in FY2025, while total debt grew from USD 667.0 million to USD 735.0 million, taking total debt to EBITDA from 3.47x to 4.50x. Rental revenues, which cost only USD 20.0 million before depreciation in FY2025, fell from USD 203.4 million to USD 179.8 million and rental equipment sales fell from USD 138.0 million to USD 109.1 million, so the fleet-funded earnings stream is shrinking and creditors hold a larger claim on a smaller earnings base.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_altg_altg-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 70–74; calculation C80; calculation C81; calculation C1; calculation C2; calculation C3

Interest takes about half of EBITDA. Cash interest paid rose from USD 53.6 million in FY2023 to USD 76.4 million in FY2024 and USD 83.4 million in FY2025, so EBITDA cover fell from 3.58x to 1.96x. The last twelve months to September 30, 2026 lifted cover only to 2.02x, because cash interest eased to USD 75.6 million while EBITDA slipped to USD 152.8 million. At cover near 2x a modest earnings decline would cut quickly into the cash left after interest.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_altg_altg-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 73–74; 10-Q_2026-09-30_altg_altg-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 7–8; calculation C83; calculation C84; calculation C85; calculation C32

Free cash flow after rental and other capital expenditure was an outflow in each of FY2023, FY2024 and FY2025, of USD 16.2 million, USD 13.5 million and USD 18.0 million. The positive USD 64.2 million over the last twelve months rests on lower capital expenditure, USD 28.5 million against USD 51.0 million in FY2025, and on working capital rather than on higher earnings: nine-month 2026 operating cash flow of USD 58.8 million includes a USD 30.8 million inflow from manufacturer floor plan payables, against an outflow of USD 28.3 million in nine months 2025. Floor plan payables are repaid as the financed equipment is sold, so part of the improvement is borrowed and can reverse.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_altg_altg-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 7–8, 16–17; 10-K_2025-12-31_altg_altg-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 73–74; calculation C50; calculation C51; calculation C52; calculation C53

Liquidity is adequate in the near term, but how much of it can fund debt service is not established. Cash is USD 21.8 million and management reports USD 413.5 million of available borrowings under the ABL and floor plan facilities combined, and the floor plan facilities primarily finance equipment inventory. The printed facility limits imply USD 530.9 million, USD 117.4 million more than reported, and the filings do not print the borrowing base. No principal falls due before June 2029, when USD 692.9 million of ABL and second lien note principal matures on one day, so a single refinancing decides the credit.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_altg_altg-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 4, 17–18, 55; calculation C58; calculation C59; calculation C60

Leverage depends on the debt perimeter. Total debt of USD 710.7 million counts finance leases of USD 32.0 million but not floor plan payables of USD 325.7 million or operating lease liabilities of USD 116.0 million. With floor plan payables counted, total debt to EBITDA is 6.78x rather than 4.65x. The issuer's own Adjusted EBITDA deducts floor plan interest on new equipment as an operating cost, which is consistent with leaving floor plan payables out of debt, so a committee should read 4.65x as the issuer-consistent measure and 6.78x as the conservative bound.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_altg_altg-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 4–5, 16–20, 35; calculation C55; calculation C67; calculation C86

Customer deposits and deferred revenue, which fund operations without interest, were USD 33.4 million at September 30, 2026, down from USD 37.8 million at FY2023. The float is small against total debt of USD 710.7 million, so it neither supports liquidity materially nor hides a large claim; the obligation is to deliver equipment or service, and it is not a debt claim.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_altg_altg-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 4–5; 10-K_2024-12-31_altg_altg-20241231.htm.jsonl, pages 70–71; calculation C68; calculation C71

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The best case against a harsher view is the direction since June: leverage fell from 4.91x at June 30, 2026 to 4.65x, and net debt fell from USD 716.4 million at FY2025 to USD 688.9 million. On the issuer's own Adjusted EBITDA, total debt is 4.38x and cover is 2.15x. No principal falls due before June 2029 and reported availability is USD 413.5 million. The view would improve if leverage keeps falling from 4.65x toward the 3.47x of FY2023 while cover moves above 2.02x, and it would weaken if reported availability fell below the USD 373.8 million of June 30, 2026 or the borrowing base proved smaller than the printed facility limits.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_altg_altg-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 4, 18, 55; 10-Q_2026-06-30_altg_altg-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 55; calculation C87; calculation C88; calculation C66

### Limitations

Reported EBITDA is calculated from net income, interest, tax and depreciation because the issuer states only Adjusted EBITDA, and it adds back floor plan interest that the issuer treats as an operating cost.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_altg_altg-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 35–36; calculation C73

Interim ratios rest on last-twelve-month figures built from year-to-date statements, and they carry no statement tie.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-03-31_altg_altg-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 5; 10-Q_2026-06-30_altg_altg-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 5; 10-Q_2026-09-30_altg_altg-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 5

Covenant levels, the borrowing base and the maturities of individual floor plan facilities are not printed, so covenant headroom and facility-level availability are not established.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_altg_altg-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 16–18

Cash taxes paid are not printed for the 2026 quarters, and statements before FY2023 are not extracted.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_altg_altg-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 7–8; 10-K_2025-12-31_altg_altg-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 98–99
