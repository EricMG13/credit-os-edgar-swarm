## Findings

### Bottom line

The four filings support a source-based credit snapshot of Vail Resorts through the year ended 2026-07-31, but not a view of creditor protection, because no debt agreement was supplied and the filings name the covenants without printing their levels. Ratings, trading quotes, peer data and a first-quarter 2026 interim report are also absent, so those inputs stay unestablished rather than assumed.
Basis: 10-K_2026-07-31_mtn-20260731.htm.jsonl, page 120; 10-K_2026-07-31_mtn-20260731.htm.jsonl, pages 192, 195; 10-Q_2026-04-30_mtn-20260430.htm.jsonl, page 62

Two points need care before any figure is used. The run settings name the third quarter of 2026 as the current period, yet the sources include the annual report for the year ended 2026-07-31, signed 2026-09-28, which is 51 days before the as-of date. The issuer also states that cash at the January and April 2026 quarter-ends was overstated by USD 38.8 million and USD 38.4 million because certificates of deposit were classified as cash. Both points are carried as open items, not reconciled here.
Basis: 10-K_2026-07-31_mtn-20260731.htm.jsonl, pages 1, 172, 196; calculation C1
