### Company description

Eldorado is a gold, copper and base metals producer that sells gold as doré and gold, silver, lead, zinc and copper in concentrate from mines in Turkiye, Canada and Greece. Four mines produce today: Kisladag and Efemcukuru in Turkiye, the Lamaque Complex in Canada and Olympias in Greece. Skouries in Greece and McIlvenna Bay in Saskatchewan, the latter acquired with Foran on 2026-04-14, are ramping up toward commercial production expected in late fourth quarter 2026. Revenue of US$1,531.744 million for nine months came 45.20% from Canada, 36.27% from Turkiye and 18.53% from Greece. The credit linkage is that cash flow is visible only as far as the gold price and the Lamaque mine allow, and debt service on the build depends on projects that have not yet produced steady cash.

### Revenue drivers and pricing power

Revenue is price-driven and cyclical. Third-quarter revenue rose 17.74% to US$511.860 million although gold produced fell 7.67%, because the realized gold price rose 22.65% to US$4,326 per ounce, and royalties rose with price to US$31.8 million in the quarter. Gold is 90.35% of contract revenue and the Lamaque mine 46.83% of quarterly revenue, so revenue is concentrated and Eldorado is a price taker with no pass-through. Concentrate is sold under offtake agreements, and the release says copper-gold concentrate markets are highly competitive, so treatment terms are a second price risk once Skouries produces. The effect on credit is that interest cover is strong at today's price but offers no visibility beyond the next price move.

### Cost structure and margin resilience

Costs are mostly fixed at mine level and rise with volume handled and with royalties. Production costs rose to US$199.8 million in the third quarter from US$164.1 million, total cash costs rose to US$1,415 per ounce sold and all-in sustaining cost to US$2,050, yet the margin over sustaining cost still rose to US$2,276 per ounce. Kisladag is the weak point: lower grade and slower mining lifted its total cash cost 77.85% and its sustaining cost 83.30%, and higher-grade ore stacked on the heap leach pad will show in production only toward the end of the fourth quarter and into 2027 because of a leach cycle of about 300 days. Margins can therefore be defended against moderate volume pressure but not against a combination of falling price and rising unit cost at Kisladag.

### Competitive forces

Rivalry is price-based and gives no pricing power; position depends on cost, where Lamaque at US$1,436 per ounce of sustaining cost sits well below Kisladag at US$2,832. New entrants face high capital and permitting barriers, which Skouries shows: a project with a 2022 feasibility study reached first concentrate in September 2026 at a capital cost that rose in two revisions, so supply is slow to respond and incumbents hold their assets. There is no substitute for gold as a store of value, though copper and zinc by-products face industrial demand cycles. Buyer power is limited for doré but real for concentrate, because the company relies on a limited number of smelters and offtakers and Skouries volumes are contracted only for the next two years. Supplier power showed in the project overruns, where contractor workforce levels, temporary power generation and other execution costs raised capital, and in royalty burdens that rise with price. The credit consequence is that value accrues to cost position and execution, not to market structure.

### Macro, FX and regulation

These factors are material for a producer in three jurisdictions with euro and Canadian dollar costs. The Turkish corporate tax rate change produced a US$47.4 million deferred tax gain in nine months, foreign exchange translation of deferred tax balances produced a US$41.5 million loss, and royalties rose to US$115.7 million from US$79.6 million with price. ### Downside scenario

The scenario that fits this issuer is a commissioning slip combined with a lower gold price. Resilience appears sufficient to avoid liquidity distress within 12 months because liquidity covers principal due 2.37x, but not to meet the 2029 maturities from liquidity alone, so refinancing capacity would decide the outcome.
