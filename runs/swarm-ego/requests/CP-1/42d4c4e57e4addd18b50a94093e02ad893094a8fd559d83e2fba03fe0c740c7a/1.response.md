## Findings

### Bottom line

Eldorado's earnings cover its cash interest comfortably today, but net debt has risen from US$405.70 million at the end of 2025 to US$1,588.60 million at 2026-09-30 as Skouries and McIlvenna Bay absorb cash, so creditors are underwriting project completion.
Basis: 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-09-30_unauditedcondensedconsolid.htm.jsonl, page 1; calculation C05; calculation C08; calculation C51

Reported EBITDA of US$873.69 million for the nine months was 11.72x cash interest paid of US$74.55 million. Liquidity is US$396.83 million of unrestricted cash plus US$274.7 million available on the Credit Facility, and a further US$71.90 million is restricted for Term Facility repayments that begin on 2026-12-31. A leverage multiple on a full year of earnings cannot be calculated because the 2025 annual statements are not in the files supplied, and no source ranks the debt instruments against each other.
