## Findings

### Bottom line

Constellium is an NYSE-listed French aluminium converter with no sponsor or controlling shareholder in the supplied filings, so creditors depend on board policy; governance risk is moderate, because buybacks and a 1.5x to 2.5x leverage target coexist with falling leverage and a debt paydown.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_cstm-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 38–40, 48, 126, 128–129; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cstm-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 43; EX-99.1_2026-10-28_a2026-q3xearningspressre.htm.jsonl, page 2

Management says metal cost is passed through to customers, so margin follows product mix and volume, but the business is concentrated: packaging rolled products are 44.63% of FY2025 revenue and the two largest customers 21.25%. In 9M 2026 free cash flow of USD 223 million covered buybacks of USD 78 million 2.86x, leverage was 1.6x, and the July 2026 redemption cut the 2028 notes to USD 225 million.

### Key findings

No sponsor or controlling shareholder appears in the supplied filings, so sponsor extraction, the usual leveraged-credit hazard, is not indicated, but the board alone decides shareholder returns. Four holders of record hold the shares, and one United States record holder acting through the registrar holds 93.77% of them, so beneficial owners cannot be identified; the annual report incorporates its beneficial-ownership table by reference to a proxy statement that was not supplied. A holder crossing 30% of capital or voting rights must offer for all shares under the articles, and the senior notes carry a repurchase offer at 101% on a change of control, although the indenture definition of a change of control is not supplied.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_cstm-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 38–39, 48, 108–109, 126, 128–129; calculation C24

Prices are set at metal cost plus a conversion margin, so earnings should follow margin and volume, but the business is concentrated by product and customer. Packaging rolled products are 44.63% of FY2025 revenue, the packaging and automotive rolled segment is 59.97% of external revenue, and the two largest customers are 21.25% of revenue, with the ten largest around 56%. Management says the largest aerospace contracts run for several years and general industrial contracts for a year or less, so contract visibility is strongest in the segment with the highest margin, 17.23% in FY2025 against 6.95% for packaging and automotive sheet.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_cstm-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 7, 13, 93–94, 97; calculation C1; calculation C4; calculation C5; calculation C11

The 2026 earnings step-up mixes base-case and temporary drivers, which matters because buybacks are paid from it. Revenue rose 25.61% in 9M 2026 while shipments fell 0.88%, so growth came from revenue per tonne, which the company ties to higher metal prices, not from volume, and Adjusted EBITDA of USD 1,074 million includes a non-cash metal price lag of USD 222 million, or 20.67%. Management credits automotive sheet shortages in North America, favourable scrap and strong recycling, and says its 2028 targets of Adjusted EBITDA of USD 900 million and free cash flow of USD 300 million, both before metal price lag, leave out the scrap environment and the 2026 shortage benefit; the 2026 guidance of USD 1,030 million to USD 1,060 million is above that EBITDA target, and guidance was raised at each of the three 2026 reports.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a moderate risk level is the direction of travel: carrying debt fell from USD 1,944 million at December 2025 to USD 1,817 million at September 2026, leverage fell from 2.2x to 1.8x to 1.6x over the three 2026 quarters, free cash flow covered buybacks 2.86x, and no dividend, sponsor or creditor-adverse transaction appears. On behaviour alone the risk would be low.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_cstm-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 15; EX-99.1_2026-04-29_a2026-q1xearningspressre.htm.jsonl, page 2; EX-99.1_2026-07-29_a2026-q2xearningspressre.htm.jsonl, page 2; EX-99.1_2026-10-28_a2026-q3xearningspressre.htm.jsonl, page 2; calculation C14; calculation C21

The view moves to low if the indentures and credit agreement, once supplied, show restricted-payment and debt capacity tight enough to keep distributions inside free cash flow, or if the board adopts a leverage ceiling below 2.5x. It moves to high if buybacks continue while cash from operations again fails to cover capital expenditure, as in FY2024, if leverage climbs toward the top of the 1.5x to 2.5x range while repurchases continue, or if a holder approaches the 30% bid threshold without a stated creditor protection.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_cstm-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 38, 51, 64, 79; EX-99.1_2026-10-28_a2026-q3xearningspressre.htm.jsonl, page 2; calculation C13

### Limitations

- Beneficial ownership and the votes attached to each share are not stated in the supplied filings, so the finding that no shareholder controls the company could be reversed if the proxy statement shows a holder near the 30% takeover-bid threshold or a group acting in concert.
- The note indentures, the credit agreement for the US asset-based facility and the guarantor list are not supplied, so the moderate risk level rests on observed behaviour and cannot be tied to the distribution, debt or lien capacity the documents allow; that capacity could move the risk level either way.
- Executive pay, board independence, related-party dealings and director holdings are in the proxy statement that the annual report incorporates by reference, so the finding that incentives follow relative share performance rests on the equity plan note alone.
- Customer names, contract expiry dates and the segment of each of the two largest customers are not stated, so the concentration finding rests on revenue shares and cannot show which renewals fall inside the life of the notes.
