## Findings

### Bottom line

Constellium's leverage has fallen to 1.56x net debt to last-twelve-month Segment Adjusted EBITDA at September 30, 2026, from 2.53x at year-end 2025, and no senior note matures before 2028, so creditors face low near-term refinancing risk.

The improvement is real but flattered on reported measures: reported EBITDA of USD 1,254 million includes a non-cash metal price lag gain of USD 289 million over the last twelve months, so net leverage is 1.32x on that basis against 1.56x on the issuer's definition. Free Cash Flow of USD 333 million over the same period is only 0.27x reported EBITDA because working capital absorbed USD 488 million in the first nine months of 2026. Cash interest of USD 100 million is covered 12.54x by reported EBITDA and 10.65x by Segment Adjusted EBITDA. The October 22, 2026 amendment of the Pan-U.S. ABL falls after the balance sheet date and is not reflected in debt or liquidity.

### Analysis by dimension

Leverage has fallen because earnings grew while net debt eased, and the issuer's own definition confirms the level. Net debt at carrying value, gross debt less cash, was USD 1,824 million at year-end 2025 and USD 1,659 million at September 30, 2026, a fall of USD 165 million, while Segment Adjusted EBITDA over the last twelve months rose from USD 720 million to USD 1,065 million on favourable price, mix and metal costs in packaging and automotive rolled products and higher aerospace volumes. That is 2.53x falling to 1.56x, and the issuer states 1.6x at September 30, 2026 on the same definition, with 2.2x at March 31, 2026 and 1.8x at June 30, 2026.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_cstm-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 3–4, 9; EX-99.1_2026-10-28_a2026-q3xearningspressre.htm.jsonl, pages 1, 4, 16; EX-99.1_2026-07-29_a2026-q2xearningspressre.htm.jsonl, page 1; EX-99.1_2026-04-29_a2026-q1xearningspressre.htm.jsonl, page 1; 10-K_2025-12-31_cstm-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 95; calculation C42; calculation C45; calculation C70; calculation C46; calculation C47

Reported EBITDA overstates earnings power, so creditors should anchor on Segment Adjusted EBITDA. The issuer states no unadjusted EBITDA; the figure derived here from income before tax, net finance costs and depreciation is USD 1,254 million over the last twelve months, USD 189 million above Segment Adjusted EBITDA, because it carries the non-cash metal price lag of USD 289 million, partly offset by USD 100 million of net other items. The lag is a timing gain that reverses when aluminium prices fall, and it was already negative in the third quarter of 2026, so net leverage of 1.32x on reported EBITDA is the favourable end of the range and 1.56x is the better guide.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_cstm-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 41; EX-99.1_2026-10-28_a2026-q3xearningspressre.htm.jsonl, pages 3, 6; calculation C11; calculation C17; calculation C23; calculation C69; calculation C78; calculation C79; calculation C48

Interest cover is ample and has improved mainly through earnings, because cash interest has barely moved. Cash interest paid over the last twelve months was USD 100 million against USD 104 million in FY2025, so cover rose from 8.14x to 12.54x on reported EBITDA and from 6.92x to 10.65x on Segment Adjusted EBITDA. Every senior note carries a fixed coupon and the Pan-U.S. ABL carried no drawing at September 30, 2026, so a rise in interest rates would not change cash interest until the notes are refinanced in 2028 and 2029.

Free cash flow is real but modest against EBITDA because working capital absorbs the rise in metal prices. Free Cash Flow was USD 223 million in the first nine months of 2026 against USD 68 million a year earlier and USD 333 million over the last twelve months, which is 0.27x reported EBITDA. Working capital absorbed USD 488 million in the nine months against USD 161 million a year earlier as inventories and receivables rose with metal prices, and capital expenditure net of inflows was USD 197 million. Cash also funded buybacks of USD 78 million and debt repayment of USD 106 million, including the USD 100 million partial redemption of the 2028 notes, so gross debt fell USD 127 million and cash rose USD 38 million.

Liquidity is adequate and the next principal maturities are distant. The issuer states total liquidity of USD 992 million at September 30, 2026: USD 158 million of cash, USD 541 million available under the Pan-U.S. ABL, USD 114 million under the French inventory facility and USD 179 million under factoring programs, so USD 655 million is undrawn committed lending. No senior note matures before June 2028, when USD 225 million remains after the July 2026 partial redemption, and the 2029 notes total USD 841 million, so the refinancing wall is USD 1,066 million across 2028 and 2029; the French facility, undrawn, expires in December 2027.

### Strongest contrary evidence

The case for improving credit quality weakens if aluminium prices fall: the metal price lag would reverse and remove part of the USD 289 million that reported EBITDA carries, although working capital would then release some of the USD 488 million it absorbed in the first nine months of 2026. Shareholder returns also compete with deleveraging: a new buyback program of up to USD 300 million has USD 257 million remaining, and the issuer's target leverage range of 1.5x to 2.5x leaves room to add debt at 1.56x. The view would strengthen if net debt keeps falling while the lag stays flat, and would weaken if net leverage on Segment Adjusted EBITDA returned to the 2.19x of March 31, 2026.

## Gaps

- The maturity ladder is drawn at December 31, 2025 because the quarterly reports print no maturity table; it does not reflect the July 28, 2026 redemption of USD 100 million of the 2028 notes or later currency movements on the euro notes, so the debt maturities chart overstates 2028 by that amount and the principal reconciliation covers year-end only. Severity: Low. Action: use the maturity table of the next annual report or an issuer schedule at September 30, 2026.
- The senior notes are guaranteed by certain subsidiaries that the documents do not name, the indentures are not supplied, and the margins, reference rates and maturities of the credit facilities, finance leases and other loans are not stated, so the obligor scope and pricing of each facility in the debt stack and debt instruments tables are incomplete and no structural-priority view can be drawn. Finance lease liabilities of USD 27 million and other loans of USD 28 million are carried inside total debt but left out of the debt stack because no source states their legal rank, so the stack covers USD 1,762 million of USD 1,817 million. Severity: Low. Action: supply the indentures, the guarantor list and the lease and loan terms.
- The October 22, 2026 amendment of the Pan-U.S. ABL falls after the balance sheet date and is excluded from September 30, 2026 debt and liquidity; the sources do not state availability, borrowing base, covenant terms or whether the springing maturity remains after the amendment, and the fixed charge coverage covenant level and letters of credit drawn on the facility are not stated at all, so liquidity after the amendment is not established, covenant headroom is not calculable and no pro forma figure is given. Severity: Low. Action: obtain the credit agreement and the text of the amendment.
- Lines the statements print as a dash are carried as not supplied, not as zero: collection of deferred purchase price receivable (9M 2026), acquisition of subsidiaries (FY2025), proceeds from disposals (FY2024 and 9M 2025), other investing activities (FY2024 and 9M 2026), payment of financing costs (FY2025), proceeds from issuance of long-term debt (FY2025), impairment of assets (9M 2025), unrealized exchange gains (FY2025) and other adjustments (9M 2025); the nine-month statements also do not print proceeds from issuance of long-term debt, payment of financing costs or acquisition of subsidiaries. The sums hold when these are read as nil. Severity: Low. Action: confirm against the filed tables.
- Some prior-period items are not in the sources: the balance sheet at September 30, 2025, so debt, cash and leverage at that date are not calculable and the nine-month 2025 column is omitted from the balance sheet; the face value of debt and the availability under the facilities at December 31, 2024, so the principal reconciliation and undrawn committed facilities for FY2024 are not supplied; receivables derecognized under factoring at September 30, 2025; and the issuer's stated leverage at year-end dates and at September 30, 2025. Severity: Low. Action: supply the September 30, 2025 quarterly report and the prior annual report.
- Some items are not separately disclosed: maintenance against growth capital expenditure, so cash conversion before growth projects cannot be measured; interest income, which is inside finance costs - net, so net interest expense cannot be separated from the cash interest paid used for cover; and the carrying values of the euro senior notes, which the filings print in dollars only. Severity: Low. Action: none needed for the dollar ratios shown; obtain management's capital expenditure split if conversion before growth is wanted.
- The sources do not say whether the factoring programs are committed, and USD 417 million of receivables derecognized under them at September 30, 2026 sits outside net debt, so the funding risk from a withdrawal cannot be sized. Severity: Low. Action: obtain the program terms.
- EBITDA (Reported) is not stated by the issuer and is derived from income before tax, net finance costs and depreciation and amortization; it carries the non-cash metal price lag of USD 289 million over the last twelve months and unrealized derivative results, so net leverage on it is 1.32x against 1.56x on Segment Adjusted EBITDA.
