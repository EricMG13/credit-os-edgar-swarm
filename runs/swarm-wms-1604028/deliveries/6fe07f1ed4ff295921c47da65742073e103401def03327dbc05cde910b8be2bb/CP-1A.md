## Findings

### Bottom line

Advanced Drainage Systems has no named sponsor, so creditors depend on management's own financial policy, and that policy now spends more than the business generates, on acquisitions and shareholder returns, which makes covenant terms the filings do not show the real protection.
Basis: calculation A55; calculation A51; 10-K_2026-03-31_wms_wms-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 90; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wms_wms-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 6–7; 10-K_2026-03-31_wms_wms-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 42

The operating base is strong, with segment margins of 28.89% in Stormwater and 43.59% in Wastewater, but growth is bought: net sales rose 5.03% in fiscal 2026 and 1.54% without the two acquisitions, and fell 4.50% in the September 2026 quarter without NDS. Two distributors take 25.8% of sales, and the pending USD 530 million StormTrap purchase and the USD 114.1 million of shares bought back since quarter end together equal 72.22% of the cash and revolver availability at 2026-09-30.
Basis: calculation A32; calculation A33; calculation A01; calculation A02; calculation A04; calculation A61; 10-K_2026-03-31_wms_wms-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 99; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wms_wms-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 15; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wms_wms-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 28

### Key findings

Governance risk is medium, and it comes from financial policy rather than control. No named sponsor and a dispersed holder base remove the usual extraction risk, but a policy that spends more than free cash flow, a repeating acquisition pace and missing board and credit documents keep it from low.
Basis: calculation A55; 10-K_2026-03-31_wms_wms-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 42; 10-K_2026-03-31_wms_wms-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 75

No investor controls the company and no sponsor is named, so sponsor extraction and sponsor support are not in play and the discipline that matters is the board's own. Directors, officers and principal stockholders together hold about 16% of the shares and about 22% of the votes counting the employee retirement plan, and the filings name Canada Pension Plan Investment Board as party to 2021 shareholder and registration rights agreements without stating its holding or rights.
Basis: 10-K_2026-03-31_wms_wms-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 42; 10-K_2026-03-31_wms_wms-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 77; 10-K_2026-03-31_wms_wms-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 2

Financial policy is elastic rather than conservative, and no stated leverage target bounds it. Over fiscal 2025 and fiscal 2026 acquisitions and shareholder returns together took 159.53% of free cash flow while the cash balance fell, and in the first half of fiscal 2027 buybacks and dividends alone took 112.22%, against 26.01% in fiscal 2026.
Basis: calculation A55; calculation A51; calculation A48; 10-K_2026-03-31_wms_wms-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 88; 10-K_2026-03-31_wms_wms-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 135; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wms_wms-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 39

Reported growth overstates demand: fiscal 2026 net sales rose 5.03% but 1.54% once NDS and the full-year effect of Orenco are removed, and the first half of fiscal 2027 rose 2.28% without NDS, made up of a rise of 9.22% in the June quarter and a fall of 4.50% in the September quarter, when volume in pipe and allied products fell while price and mix improved. The filings do not explain the swing, so the September decline cannot yet be called a trend.
Basis: calculation A01; calculation A02; calculation A05; calculation A03; calculation A04; 10-Q_2026-06-30_wms_wms-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 24–25; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wms_wms-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 29–33

The business is moving from resin-linked pipe toward allied products and wastewater, which should dampen resin exposure if acquired margins hold. Domestic pipe fell from 50.87% of segment sales in fiscal 2025 to 43.50% in the first half of fiscal 2027, while allied products, which now include NDS, rose to 29.73%; Wastewater earned a segment margin of 43.59% against 28.89% in Stormwater in fiscal 2026 and supplied 30.70% of segment profit on 22.70% of segment sales, so a weak housing market would cut earnings by more than its sales share suggests.
Basis: calculation A08; calculation A18; calculation A19; calculation A33; calculation A32; calculation A36; calculation A16; 10-K_2026-03-31_wms_wms-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 49

Customer concentration is the structural business risk: two distributors took 25.8% of fiscal 2026 net sales and 27.0% in fiscal 2025, most sales run through distributors, and demand is seasonal, higher between April and September. A change in volumes or terms at one of them would reach earnings before any offset, and the filings give no contract terms.
Basis: 10-K_2026-03-31_wms_wms-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 19; 10-K_2026-03-31_wms_wms-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 20; 10-K_2026-03-31_wms_wms-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 99

Acquired assets are intangible-heavy and partly for resale, so little of the price is backed by physical assets. Goodwill and identified intangibles are 78.51% of the USD 984.9 million paid for NDS, five NDS entities were held for sale on acquisition, two were sold in July 2026, and the first half carried a USD 29,818 thousand loss from classifying the rest as held for sale.
Basis: calculation A62; 10-K_2026-03-31_wms_wms-20260331.htm.jsonl, pages 105–106; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wms_wms-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 13–14; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wms_wms-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 15–16

The StormTrap purchase is the next test of that policy. At approximately USD 530 million it equals 59.43% of the cash and revolver availability at 2026-09-30, and 72.22% together with the USD 114.1 million of shares bought back after quarter end; the funding split between cash and the revolver, the agreement terms and any closing are not in the supplied filings.
Basis: calculation A60; calculation A61; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wms_wms-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 15; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wms_wms-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 28; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wms_wms-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 38; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wms_wms-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 43

Reporting quality supports monitoring, with one open point: the annual report was signed 51 days after year end, the audit opinions were unqualified and internal control was effective, yet NDS, which carried 23% of total assets, was left out of the control assessment, so the largest recent addition has not been through a full control cycle.
Basis: calculation A68; 10-K_2026-03-31_wms_wms-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 84; 10-K_2026-03-31_wms_wms-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 87; 10-K_2026-03-31_wms_wms-20260331.htm.jsonl, pages 74–75

Capacity to add debt grew in February 2026 and willingness to spend cash is shown by action, but the size of the capacity is not supplied. The amendment allows incremental facilities of up to the greater of USD 350 million or 100% of consolidated EBITDA and amended certain baskets, while the definitions that size them are missing, so capacity is described and not calculated; no priming, exchange or other creditor-adverse transaction is documented.
Basis: 10-K_2026-03-31_wms_wms-20260331.htm.jsonl, pages 119–120

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against caution is cash generation and balance-sheet room. Free cash flow was USD 569,288 thousand in fiscal 2026, liquidity at 2026-09-30 was USD 891,880 thousand with the revolver undrawn, no bond matures before 2030, and shareholder payouts in the first half of fiscal 2027 were met from cash and operating cash flow with no revolver borrowings. A cut in buybacks, or a StormTrap closing funded mainly from cash flow with the revolver largely undrawn, would move the view toward low governance risk.
Basis: calculation A46; calculation A59; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wms_wms-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 38; 10-K_2026-03-31_wms_wms-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 121; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wms_wms-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 7

What would worsen the view is a debt-funded buyback or a revolver draw large enough to take exposure above 35% of commitments, which switches on the springing covenant; a further large acquisition before StormTrap is integrated; another quarter of falling volume excluding NDS; or credit documents showing wide payout and debt baskets. What would improve it is a stated leverage target, a proxy statement showing an independent board with credit-relevant oversight, and credit documents that cap payouts and additional debt.
Basis: 10-K_2026-03-31_wms_wms-20260331.htm.jsonl, pages 66–67

### Limitations

- Sales growth excluding acquisitions is approximate because NDS and Orenco sales are printed in millions to one decimal and River Valley Pipe's sales are not separated; the claim that growth is bought changes only if the true figures differ by more than a few tenths of a percentage point.
  Basis: calculation A02; calculation A04; 10-K_2026-03-31_wms_wms-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 54

- Statements that the company leads its markets, is the only national corrugated HDPE pipe producer and holds a cost advantage are management language that the filings do not test with market share data; they support the business-quality view only as management's own claim.
  Basis: 10-K_2026-03-31_wms_wms-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 21

- End-market mix is shown in charts that the extracted text does not carry, so exposure to housing, non-residential construction and agriculture is argued from segment and customer evidence; a printed split could raise or lower the cyclicality judgement.
  Basis: 10-K_2026-03-31_wms_wms-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 7

- Ownership percentages are as of 2026-05-14 and the share count has fallen 3.61% since, so insider and plan percentages may now be higher; the conclusion that no holder controls the company assumes they persist, and the stake of Canada Pension Plan Investment Board is not stated.
  Basis: calculation A67; 10-K_2026-03-31_wms_wms-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 42; 10-K_2026-03-31_wms_wms-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wms_wms-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 1

- Because board and holder-level documents are absent, the governance risk level cannot be refined either way; it would move to low or high only on board independence facts, the size of the payout and debt baskets, or the terms of the 2021 agreements.
  Basis: 10-K_2026-03-31_wms_wms-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 75; 10-K_2026-03-31_wms_wms-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 77
