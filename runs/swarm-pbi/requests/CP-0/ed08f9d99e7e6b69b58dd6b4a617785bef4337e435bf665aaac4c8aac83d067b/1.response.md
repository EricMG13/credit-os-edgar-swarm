## Findings

### Bottom line

Source coverage supports the short credit snapshot with stated limitations: statements, debt, liquidity and covenant disclosures cover year-end 2025 and the first three quarters of 2026, but security, guarantees and covenant definitions rest on filing summaries, not executed documents. The year-end 2025 balance sheet in the annual report agrees with the comparative column of the September 2026 quarterly report on the five lines compared.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_pbi-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 66; 10-Q_2026-09-30_pbi-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-Q_2026-09-30_pbi-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 19–20; calculation C3.

Four limitations decide how far the analysis can be pushed. The annual report supplied is the original Form 10-K, while every 2026 filing and release cites a Form 10-K/A for the same year that is not in the set. No supplied document publishes company-level adjusted earnings or adjusted free cash flow for full-year 2025, and the 2026 adjusted measures exclude pension expense that prior periods still carry. Debt, cash and capital spending are reported for the consolidated group only, with no finance-versus-industrial split. Credit ratings and market quotes were not supplied.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_pbi-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 1; 10-Q_2026-09-30_pbi-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 5; EX-99.1_2026-10-28_ex991q32026earningspress.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-Q_2026-09-30_pbi-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 9; 10-Q_2026-09-30_pbi-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 11.
