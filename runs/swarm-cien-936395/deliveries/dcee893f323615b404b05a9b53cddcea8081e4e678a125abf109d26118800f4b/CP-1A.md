## Findings

### Bottom line

Ciena presents medium governance risk for creditors: it has no identified sponsor and clean audit opinions, but most of its operating cash flow now goes to share repurchases and two cloud customers buy a large share of revenue. In the first nine months of fiscal 2026, repurchases and tax withholding purchases took USD 616.25 million, or 90.15% of operating cash flow, and USD 140.0 million of the June 2026 convertible proceeds bought back shares at pricing. Two cloud providers bought 41.7% of third-quarter revenue.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-08-01_cien.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-Q_2026-08-01_cien.htm.jsonl, page 18; EX99.1_2026-08-01_cien_ex9912026q3earningspressre-htm.htm.jsonl, page 2; calculation C26; calculation C27

The consequence for creditors is that debt service rests on management's continued choice to retain cash and on those customers' orders. Restricted-payment limits cannot be tested because the indenture and credit agreement are not supplied, and board independence, pay and large holders are not established because the proxy statement is not supplied.
Basis: 10-K_2025-11-01_cien.htm.jsonl, page 121; Source readiness (CP-0)

### Key findings

Ownership is public with no identified controlling holder, so creditors carry no sponsor-extraction risk but also have no sponsor to support a stressed balance sheet. The documents name no parent, sponsor or holder above any threshold, and the 2025 annual report places beneficial ownership, board independence and related-party dealings in a proxy statement that is not supplied, so who influences the equity is not established.
Basis: 10-K_2025-11-01_cien.htm.jsonl, page 43; 10-K_2025-11-01_cien.htm.jsonl, page 121; 10-K_2025-11-01_cien.htm.jsonl, page 122; Source readiness (CP-0)

The business is a cyclical hardware franchise whose profit now rides on cloud capital spending: Optical Networking was 71.0% of nine-month revenue, and revenue rose 36.6% over the nine months after an 18.79% rise in fiscal 2025 and an 8.47% fall in fiscal 2024. A swing of that size within two years means the cash flow that services the debt can reverse well before the 2030 and 2031 maturities.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-08-01_cien.htm.jsonl, page 31; 10-Q_2026-08-01_cien.htm.jsonl, page 32; 10-K_2025-11-01_cien.htm.jsonl, page 85; calculation C11; calculation C12

Customer concentration is the main business risk and it is rising: the customers above 10% of revenue, both cloud providers, took 36.86% of nine-month revenue against 26.32% a year earlier, when they were one cloud provider and one service provider, and cloud provider A alone supplied 47.19% of the nine-month revenue increase. Neither cloud provider is named in the quarterly report, so their ability to keep ordering cannot be assessed, and a pause in either order book would reach revenue before it reaches costs.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-08-01_cien.htm.jsonl, page 12; EX99.1_2026-08-01_cien_ex9912026q3earningspressre-htm.htm.jsonl, page 2; calculation C6; calculation C7; calculation C10

The conversion price of USD 746.66 is 84.58% above the August 2026 average repurchase price of USD 404.52, so repayment in cash at maturity, not conversion, is the planning case for creditors.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a medium rating is restraint in form and financial strength: Ciena has never paid a cash dividend, the repurchase authorization has USD 299.0 million left, the new notes carry no coupon and fall due in 2031, and the revolver had no borrowings at August 1, 2026 with covenant compliance stated. If the proxy statement shows an independent board and no related-party dealings, and the indenture and credit agreement restrict shareholder payments tightly, the level would move to low.
Basis: 10-K_2025-11-01_cien.htm.jsonl, page 43; 10-Q_2026-08-01_cien.htm.jsonl, page 23; 10-Q_2026-08-01_cien.htm.jsonl, page 28; calculation C29

Evidence that would raise the level to high is a repurchase or acquisition funded by new borrowing beyond the USD 140.0 million seen in June, a new authorization once the current one is used, or a cut in orders by either cloud provider while USD 3.3 billion of purchase commitments stay in place.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-08-01_cien.htm.jsonl, page 18; 10-Q_2026-08-01_cien.htm.jsonl, page 28; 10-Q_2026-08-01_cien.htm.jsonl, page 24

### Limitations

- The proxy statement is not supplied, so board independence, committee composition, executive pay, related-party dealings and large holders are not established; the medium level rests on documented behavior and can move either way once the proxy is read.
- The indenture and credit agreement are not supplied, so restricted-payment capacity and the security of the revolver after the term loan repayment are not established; the finding on shareholder returns describes what management has done, not what the documents allow.
- The two cloud customers are unnamed in the 2026 quarterly reports, so the concentration finding measures size, not whether those customers can keep ordering.
- No document is dated after September 3, 2026 and no fiscal 2026 year-end result is supplied, so repurchases and orders after August 28, 2026 are not captured and the repurchase pace is measured only to the third quarter.
- The firm part of the USD 3.3 billion purchase commitments is not stated, so the supply finding gives a ceiling on the claim, not an amount.
