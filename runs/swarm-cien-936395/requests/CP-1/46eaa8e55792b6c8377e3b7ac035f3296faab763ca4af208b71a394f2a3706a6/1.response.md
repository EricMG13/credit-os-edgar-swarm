## Findings

### Bottom line

Ciena can carry the June 2026 convertible refinancing from earnings and cash on the reported figures: at August 1, 2026 net debt was 0.86x last-twelve-months EBITDA, gross debt 3.53x and cash interest cover 12.08x. The EBITDA is calculated from the statements because the issuer states it only quarterly, and the balance sheet predates the fiscal year-end.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-08-01_cien.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-Q_2026-08-01_cien.htm.jsonl, page 19; 10-Q_2026-08-01_cien.htm.jsonl, page 21; calculation C14; calculation C27; calculation C28

At the fiscal 2025 year-end gross debt was 4.55x EBITDA and cash interest cover 3.96x, so the improvement is recent and rests on three quarters of earnings growth. The revolver is undrawn with USD 259.3 million available after letters of credit, the only debt maturities are in 2030 and 2031, and no result for the fiscal year that closed on October 31, 2026 is in the sources.
Basis: 10-K_2025-11-01_cien.htm.jsonl, pages 66–67; 10-Q_2026-08-01_cien.htm.jsonl, page 23; 10-Q_2026-08-01_cien.htm.jsonl, page 19; calculation C39; calculation C17

### Leverage and coverage

Net leverage fell from 1.31x at the fiscal 2025 year-end to 0.86x at August 1, 2026, and gross leverage from 4.55x to 3.53x, because last-twelve-months EBITDA more than doubled while the convertible issue added gross debt and cash together. The cover ratio of 12.08x is conservative for the debt now outstanding because last-twelve-months cash interest includes term loan interest that stopped in June 2026, but the EBITDA behind it depends on a small number of cloud customers, so the creditor consequence is that deleveraging is earnings-led rather than repayment-led.
Basis: calculation C14; calculation C11; calculation C27; calculation C28; EX99.1_2026-08-01_cien_ex9912026q3earningspressre-htm.htm.jsonl, page 2

The strongest contrary evidence is that last-twelve-months EBITDA is built from a fiscal 2025 total that carries a USD 89.100m non-cash abandonment charge and from stubs that are cumulative, so EBITDA falling back toward the fiscal 2025 level would lift gross leverage on the larger debt sharply; the observable trigger is a quarter in which reported EBITDA falls back toward the fiscal 2025 quarterly run-rate.
Basis: 10-K_2025-11-01_cien.htm.jsonl, page 60; 10-K_2025-11-01_cien.htm.jsonl, page 67; calculation C3

### Cash flow quality

Operating cash flow of the last twelve months exceeded reported EBITDA, and free cash flow after capex was USD 814.36 million, but the fiscal 2025 conversion was lifted by a large inflow from payables and accruals and the nine months of 2026 were held back by receivables and inventory build, so conversion is working-capital dependent rather than structural. The credit consequence is that cash generation can reverse quickly if customer payment terms lengthen or supply commitments are paid ahead of sales.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-08-01_cien.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-K_2025-11-01_cien.htm.jsonl, page 69; calculation C34; calculation C31

### Liquidity and debt structure

Cash and cash equivalents of USD 2,445.71 million at August 1, 2026 and an undrawn revolver of USD 259.3 million leave net debt at USD 784.14 million against gross debt of USD 3,229.84 million at carrying value, and the debt is two instruments that mature in 2030 and 2031, so there is no maturity before the 2030 senior notes. The risk mechanism is concentration of repayment in two dates, and the credit consequence is that the first refinancing test is the 4.00% senior notes; the convertible notes pay no interest and may convert earlier if the share price reaches the stated trigger.
