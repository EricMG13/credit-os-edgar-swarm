### Leverage trajectory and drivers

Leverage on the company's own debt definition fell in two steps. Total debt, which is long-term debt net of financing costs and excludes finance leases, went from USD 917.5 million at September 30, 2024 to USD 832.2 million a year later and USD 716.6 million at June 30, 2026, while last-twelve-month reported EBITDA moved from negative USD 13.7 million in fiscal 2024 to USD 118.0 million in fiscal 2025 and USD 216.6 million. Reported EBITDA carries non-cash charges, USD 191.0 million of impairments in fiscal 2024 and USD 53.7 million in fiscal 2025, so reported leverage is not calculable for fiscal 2024 and is 7.05x for fiscal 2025. Taking only the fiscal 2025 impairment out of EBITDA would give 4.85x, so about 2.20x of the 3.74x fall to 3.31x is that charge leaving the base, and the rest is debt paydown and earnings growth. On the issuer-adjusted measure, which adds the charges back, gross leverage was 4.45x, 4.19x and 3.07x and net leverage at June 30, 2026 is 2.83x, matching the company's own 2.8x. Creditors should therefore anchor on adjusted leverage near 3x and reported leverage near 3.3x, and treat any reading of a seven-turn improvement as an artefact of the base year.

### Coverage and cash interest

Reported EBITDA covered cash interest paid 1.99x in fiscal 2025 and 5.21x over the last twelve months, but the second figure overstates cover. The 2030 notes pay on January 1 and July 1 and their first coupon fell due on January 1, 2026, so the twelve months to June 30, 2026 hold one coupon, not two. Cash interest paid was USD 41.6 million against interest expense of USD 67.9 million, and the half-year coupon of USD 26.0 million fell due the day after the quarter end, with accrued interest of USD 26.4 million on the balance sheet. On interest expense, reported EBITDA covers 3.19x and adjusted EBITDA 3.43x. The 2030 notes alone cost USD 52.0 million a year in cash, so cash cover should be expected to fall back toward the expense-based figure once two coupons fall in a twelve-month window; creditors should use 3.19x.

### Cash-flow quality

Capital expenditure is a total only; no maintenance and growth split is stated, so the part of spending that is discretionary is not established.

### Liquidity and maturity profile

Liquidity is USD 328.1 million at June 30, 2026 (cash USD 56.3 million plus USD 271.8 million of undrawn revolver), against USD 364.6 million at September 30, 2025 and USD 189.9 million at September 30, 2024. The revolver (commitments of USD 325.0 million, borrowings of USD 35.0 million and letters of credit of USD 18.2 million) falls due in May 2028, about 22 months after the balance sheet date, and is the nearest maturity; the AR facility's USD 43.0 million follows in March 2029 and the USD 650.0 million 2030 notes after that, which is 89.29% of principal. No principal is due in fiscal 2026 or fiscal 2027. Refinancing risk is therefore not near, but one bullet carries almost nine-tenths of the debt and the revolver is the only committed backstop. The AR facility's undrawn capacity (up to USD 100.0 million, subject to conditions, with USD 43.0 million drawn) is not stated and is excluded, and USD 14.5 million of the cash is held by foreign subsidiaries. The 2030 notes become callable at 104.0% of principal from July 1, 2027, falling to par on or after July 1, 2029, so a refinancing before 2029 would carry a call premium of up to 4.0%.

### Strongest contrary evidence

The strongest case against caution is that the company's own measures are consistent and improving. Adjusted EBITDA rose to USD 233.2 million over the last twelve months from USD 198.8 million in fiscal 2025, net leverage is 2.83x, S&P is reported by the company to have raised the rating to B+, and fiscal 2026 guidance of USD 218 million to USD 242 million (midpoint USD 230 million) implies earnings hold near the last-twelve-month level while the nearest maturity is 22 months away. That case wins if net leverage stays near 3x at the December 31, 2026 balance sheet, when working capital is at its seasonal high, and if cover on interest expense holds near 3.19x once two coupons fall in one twelve-month window. Caution wins if adjusted EBITDA slips below the guidance midpoint, if cash add-backs recur (restructuring of USD 4.3 million and recall costs of USD 2.1 million in fiscal 2025), or if revolver borrowings rise above USD 35.0 million at a seasonal low.

### Data quality and definition risks

Management reports material weaknesses in internal control over financial reporting that are still being remediated, and the auditor gave an adverse opinion on internal control at September 30, 2024 and September 30, 2025, so the figures carry a reliability risk even though the auditor's opinion on the fiscal 2025 statements says they present fairly. The cross-checks that could be run agree: fiscal 2024 sales, net loss, EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA and total debt are the same in the 2024 and 2025 annual reports, and the nine-month and quarter figures in the June 2026 quarterly report agree with the third-quarter release for sales, operating income, net income, EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA and net debt. The differences found are presentational: finance leases moved to their own balance sheet lines and a term loan repayment was reclassified inside financing cash flows. Three debt bases coexist for one date (total debt of USD 716.6 million, principal of USD 728.0 million and carrying value with finance leases of USD 728.1 million), and the covenant tests use first lien debt at principal and a consolidated EBITDA that may include run-rate cost savings, neither of which is supplied, so no headroom is computed. The 2030 notes carry two maturity dates and two cost figures in the filings, and the debt table captions senior notes as subordinated, which is why ranking follows the notes' own description.
