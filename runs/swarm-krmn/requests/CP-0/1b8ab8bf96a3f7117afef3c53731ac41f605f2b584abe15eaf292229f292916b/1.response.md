## Findings

### Bottom line

The seven filed documents support Business & governance, Financial foundation and Fundamental credit on Karman Holdings Inc., with limits: evidence ends at 30 June 2026 and no executed credit agreement is supplied. Creditors should treat covenant headroom, collateral and any post-June leverage view as not established.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest case for higher readiness is that the filings agree with themselves on the figures that drive leverage and liquidity: the debt note ties to the balance sheet at year-end 2025 and at June 2026, and the quarterly chains tie to the annual totals. That case does not reach covenant headroom, guarantor and collateral coverage or anything after June 2026, which the filings cannot settle. Readiness would rise with the credit agreement and amendments, the third-quarter 2026 report and a report on the Walker closing and funding. It would fall if the third-quarter report restated earlier periods or revised the Seemann purchase accounting again, or if the credit agreement defined the covenant differently from the filings' summary.
Basis: calculation C1; calculation C3; calculation C5; 10-Q_2026-06-30_krmn-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 17; 10-Q_2026-06-30_krmn-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 19
