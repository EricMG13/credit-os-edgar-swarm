## Findings

### Bottom line

The five supplied RXO filings support a credit snapshot of the consolidated company through 2026-09-30, but not covenant, ratings or market-based work, so any view of creditor protection while the C.H. Robinson takeover is pending remains provisional.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_rxo.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-09-30_rxo.htm.jsonl, page 16; 10-K_2025-12-31_rxo.htm.jsonl, page 103

Both annual reports and the three 2026 quarterly reports give statements, debt notes and liquidity disclosure that agree where they overlap, and the quarters add up to the nine-month totals, apart from two small differences logged under Gaps. The takeover agreement signed on 2026-10-04 is known only from a summary in the latest quarterly report. The agreement itself, the credit agreement and note indenture, the proxy statement and any rating or market data were not supplied, so how the debt is treated on a change of control, the covenant levels and board governance are not established.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_rxo.htm.jsonl, pages 66–69; 10-K_2024-12-31_rxo.htm.jsonl, pages 72–75; 10-Q_2026-09-30_rxo.htm.jsonl, pages 3–4; 10-Q_2026-09-30_rxo.htm.jsonl, page 16; calculation C1; calculation C2; calculation C7

The strongest counterweight is that the latest quarterly report does state the takeover consideration, closing conditions and a USD 175 million termination fee, which narrows the gap on equity terms but says nothing about the debt.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_rxo.htm.jsonl, page 16; 10-Q_2026-09-30_rxo.htm.jsonl, page 31
