## Findings

### Bottom line

Reported leverage is low at 1.60x total debt and 0.71x net debt to EBITDA at July 31, 2026, but EBITDA fell for a third year and the fiscal fourth quarter was 42.17% below a year earlier, so creditors rely on a shrinking earnings base.
Basis: calculation C04; calculation C08; calculation C12; calculation C19; calculation C21; calculation C22; calculation C23

Liquidity cushions the decline: the asset-based facility had about USD 815 million available at year-end, the first sizeable maturity is the USD 500 million 2029 notes, and cash fell 17.83% over the year while dividends and buybacks exceeded free cash flow. EBITDA is built from reported lines because the issuer prints none, and USD 60,839 thousand of short-term financial obligations sit outside debt; counting them lifts total debt to 1.71x EBITDA.
Basis: 10-K_2026-07-31_tho.htm.jsonl, page 118; calculation C36; calculation C28; calculation C27; calculation C33; 10-K_2026-07-31_tho.htm.jsonl, page 93

### Analysis by dimension

Debt reduction, not earnings, has kept total debt over EBITDA near 1.5x to 1.6x. Total debt at carrying value fell from USD 1,133,915 thousand at July 31, 2024 to USD 922,979 thousand a year later and USD 867,959 thousand at July 31, 2026, as term-loan repayments of USD 205,000 thousand and USD 56,264 thousand were made in fiscal 2025 and 2026, while reported EBITDA fell from USD 714,655 thousand to USD 615,839 thousand and USD 541,912 thousand. The ratio therefore moved only from 1.59x to 1.50x and 1.60x. Repayments slowed sharply in fiscal 2026 while EBITDA kept falling, so another earnings decline without a further paydown would lift leverage; net debt over EBITDA already rose from 0.55x to 0.71x as cash fell and EBITDA declined.
Basis: calculation C01; calculation C02; calculation C04; calculation C10; calculation C11; calculation C12; 10-K_2026-07-31_tho.htm.jsonl, page 96

Coverage improved from 8.27x to 10.50x and 12.42x of EBITDA over cash interest, but the gain came from the liability side: cash interest paid fell from USD 86,421 thousand to USD 58,626 thousand and USD 43,633 thousand as the term loan was repaid and repriced, while EBITDA fell. The senior unsecured notes due 2029 pay a fixed coupon and the term loan floats on SOFR and EURIBOR, so cover is sensitive to base rates on the floating term loan and to earnings throughout.
Basis: 10-K_2026-07-31_tho.htm.jsonl, page 96; 10-K_2026-07-31_tho.htm.jsonl, page 117; calculation C12

Cash-flow quality weakened in fiscal 2026. Operating cash flow fell from USD 577,923 thousand to USD 321,223 thousand, with inventories absorbing USD 73,282 thousand, and free cash flow after purchases of property, plant and equipment was USD 168,836 thousand against USD 454,936 thousand. Dividends and share repurchases of USD 223,891 thousand exceeded it, so cash fell, even with USD 67,875 thousand of asset-sale proceeds in investing flows; free cash flow was 31.16% of EBITDA against 73.87% a year earlier.
Basis: calculation C25; calculation C26; calculation C27; 10-K_2026-07-31_tho.htm.jsonl, page 95; 10-K_2026-07-31_tho.htm.jsonl, page 96

Liquidity is adequate against the debt schedule. Cash plus the issuer-stated unused availability under the asset-based facility was USD 1,296,988 thousand at July 31, 2026 and USD 1,240,176 thousand at January 31, 2026, the lowest cash of the dates shown, when cash was USD 242,176 thousand. Maturities are small until the senior unsecured notes due 2029 (USD 502,814 thousand in fiscal 2030 including other debt) and the term loan due November 15, 2030 (USD 356,219 thousand in fiscal 2031). The facility that supplies the availability expires on November 15, 2028, before either, and its availability follows a borrowing base, with letters of credit outstanding not stated. Its only maintenance test is a springing fixed-charge coverage covenant of 1.0x that applies only when adjusted excess availability falls below the greater of 10% of the lesser of the borrowing base availability or the commitment and USD 60,000 thousand; the issuer states no reported ratio or headroom, so none is worked out here. Outside debt, standby repurchase obligations on dealer floor-plan financing were USD 3,315,731 thousand at July 31, 2026, of which USD 1,981,105 thousand expires within a year; they are contingent and not in total debt.
Basis: 10-K_2026-07-31_tho.htm.jsonl, page 74; 10-K_2026-07-31_tho.htm.jsonl, page 76; 10-K_2026-07-31_tho.htm.jsonl, page 118; 10-Q_2026-01-31_tho.htm.jsonl, page 50; calculation C32; calculation C30

Definition and data-quality risk sits in the earnings measure. Reported EBITDA is calculated and keeps other income, net; the cash flow statement shows fiscal 2026 gains of USD 36,696 thousand on disposals and USD 22,235 thousand on investments, and without those total debt over EBITDA would be 1.80x (Analyst Judgement, not applied to the ratios). Short-term financial obligations of USD 60,839 thousand are excluded from debt because no note describes them. The twelve months to April 30, 2026 show EBITDA of USD 636,712 thousand, but the fourth quarter that followed produced USD 130,004 thousand against USD 224,804 thousand a year earlier, so the April ratios are stale.
Basis: calculation C34; calculation C33; calculation C15; calculation C18; 10-K_2026-07-31_tho.htm.jsonl, page 95

The March 2024 hail event is one economic event with three figures: property losses and expenses of USD 69,822 thousand, insurance proceeds of USD 81,975 thousand net of the deductible and a fiscal 2025 gain of USD 12,153 thousand, of which USD 5,837 thousand is business interruption; the gain sits in other income, net and therefore in fiscal 2025 reported EBITDA. On non-debt funding, accounts payable rose by USD 90,409 thousand in fiscal 2025, which management attributes to extended vendor payment terms on certain raw material purchases, and were then flat in fiscal 2026; no supplier-finance program and no defined-benefit pension or retiree medical obligation was found by search. The only dated event after the year-end is the September 10, 2026 announcement of a unified North American operating group, which changes no fiscal 2026 figure and states no debt or cash effect, so it is excluded from period figures.
Basis: 10-K_2026-07-31_tho.htm.jsonl, page 131; 10-K_2026-07-31_tho.htm.jsonl, page 75; 10-K_2026-07-31_tho.htm.jsonl, page 132

## Gaps

- Reported EBITDA is calculated in every period because none of the four filings prints EBITDA, adjusted EBITDA or covenant EBITDA; this restricts leverage and coverage to a reported, unadjusted basis and leaves covenant EBITDA unknown. Severity: Medium. Action: obtain earnings releases or the compliance definition in the credit agreement.
- Other income, net sits inside reported EBITDA and includes gains on disposals and unrealized investment gains that the cash flow statement quantifies only in part (USD 58,931 thousand in fiscal 2026); the rest of the line is not quantified, which restricts the underlying earnings run rate. Severity: Medium. Action: obtain the breakdown of other income, net.
- Short-term financial obligations of USD 60,839 thousand at July 31, 2026 and USD 60,112 thousand a year earlier have no explanatory note, so whether they are debt-like is not established and they are excluded from total debt; this restricts total debt, net debt and every leverage ratio. Severity: Medium. Action: obtain the issuer description of the line.
- The debt stack is not drawn: the filings do not state which of the term loan and the asset-based facility are secured, how they rank against the senior unsecured notes or who guarantees them, and the credit agreements and indenture are not supplied; this restricts any statement of structural priority and cumulative leverage. Severity: High. Action: obtain the executed agreements, the indenture and the subsidiary list.
- Debt by seniority is not disclosed beyond the senior unsecured notes, so senior secured and subordinated debt are not supplied in any period. Severity: Medium. Action: as for the debt stack.
- The amount of letters of credit outstanding under the asset-based facility is not stated, and unused availability is the issuer approximation under a borrowing base; this restricts the undrawn figure in the liquidity table. Severity: Low. Action: obtain the borrowing base certificate.
- Unused asset-based facility availability at July 31, 2024 is not stated in the supplied filings, so the undrawn figure and the liquidity ratio for fiscal 2024 are not supplied. Severity: Low. Action: obtain the fiscal 2024 annual report.
- The statements print dashes or omit lines that other periods carry: asset impairment charges for fiscal 2024, borrowings on term-loan facilities for fiscal 2025 and 2026, payments of debt issuance costs for fiscal 2025 and 2026, revolving facility borrowings and repayments for fiscal 2025, business acquisitions for fiscal 2025 and nine months 2025, and several lines the nine-month statements do not print; none is treated as zero. Severity: Low. Action: none needed for the credit metrics.
- Fiscal 2025 and 2024 cash-flow lines were reclassified in the fiscal 2026 report, while the nine-month columns follow the quarterly report classification, so line-level comparisons across those columns are not like for like even though operating cash flow is unchanged. Severity: Low. Action: use the fiscal 2026 classification for the annual columns.
- No fourth-quarter figures are reported and no balance sheet at October 31, 2025 is supplied, so quarterly series are calculated by subtraction and labelled calculated. Severity: Low. Action: obtain the quarterly report for the quarter ended October 31, 2025 if that balance sheet is needed.
- The twelve months to April 30, 2026 are shown because the run settings name the third quarter of 2026 as current, but the fiscal 2026 annual report is later; interim ratios are stale against fiscal 2026, with fourth-quarter EBITDA 42.17% lower than a year earlier. Severity: Medium. Action: the run owner confirms the current period.
- Lease liabilities are not on their own balance-sheet lines; operating lease payments of USD 52,220 thousand undiscounted and finance lease payments of USD 940 thousand are in the contractual obligations table, so they are outside total debt and not stated at present value. Severity: Low. Action: none needed for the headline ratios.
- The Euro term loan is printed in US dollars only, so its Euro principal and the exchange rate are not established; total Euro-denominated debt of USD 325,768 thousand moves with the exchange rate and is reconciled to the Euro term loan, the Euro unsecured note and other debt. Severity: Low. Action: obtain the term loan agreement or the Euro balance.
- Interest income and exceptional items are not presented as separate income statement lines; interest is shown net, and impairments, gains and insurance settlements appear in the cash flow statement and in other income, net. Severity: Low. Action: none needed for the headline ratios.
- Segment structure may change from fiscal 2027 after the September 10, 2026 announcement; fiscal 2026 segment data follow the three reportable segments in the annual report. Severity: Low. Action: label segment figures as the fiscal 2026 structure.
- Maintenance and growth capital expenditure are not disclosed, so only total purchases of property, plant and equipment are carried. Severity: Low. Action: none needed for the headline ratios.
