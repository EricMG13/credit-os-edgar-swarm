## Findings

### Bottom line

Leverage rose to 3.42x total debt to LTM EBITDA at 30 June 2026 from 2.88x at the 2025 year end as borrowing rose and EBITDA fell, and a USD 86.0 million legal payment due after quarter end adds to net debt, thinning creditor cushion.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_brbr_brbr-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 2–3, 10–13, 16; 10-K_2025-09-30_brbr_brbr-20250930.htm.jsonl, pages 75–77, 92; calculation C29; calculation C30; calculation C31; calculation C49

EBITDA is derived as operating profit plus depreciation and amortization because the issuer states none, so every ratio rests on that definition. Cash interest cover was 5.51x in fiscal 2025 and is not calculable for later periods, because cash interest paid is printed only for fiscal years. Liquidity at 30 June 2026 was USD 248.0 million, of which USD 197.6 million is undrawn revolver, and after the legal payment net debt would be USD 1,170.9 million, or 3.53x LTM EBITDA.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_brbr_brbr-20250930.htm.jsonl, pages 75, 90; 10-Q_2026-06-30_brbr_brbr-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 12–13, 16, 25; calculation C32; calculation C38; calculation C47; calculation C48; Source readiness (CP-0)

### Credit analysis

Total debt to EBITDA rose from 1.96x at the 2024 year end to 2.88x at the 2025 year end and 3.42x on a last-twelve-month basis at 30 June 2026. The fiscal 2025 step came from carrying debt rising from USD 833.1 million to USD 1,084.3 million while EBITDA fell from USD 424.2 million to USD 376.0 million after a USD 69.0 million legal provision. The later step came from debt reaching USD 1,185.0 million in March 2026 and last-twelve-month EBITDA falling to USD 310.7 million before recovering to USD 331.9 million, as the weak third quarter of fiscal 2025, which carried the provision, left the window. Set aside that provision and fiscal 2025 leverage would have been 2.44x, so underlying leverage has risen by nearly one turn against a reported rise of about half a turn. The mechanism is a lower gross margin combined with share repurchases that exceeded free cash flow and were part funded by the revolver, which leaves less cushion for the legal payment now due.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_brbr_brbr-20250930.htm.jsonl, pages 75–77, 92; 10-Q_2026-06-30_brbr_brbr-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 2–3, 10, 22; calculation C28; calculation C29; calculation C52; calculation C53; calculation C58; calculation C59

Cash interest paid rose from USD 59.6 million in fiscal 2024 to USD 68.3 million in fiscal 2025, and EBITDA cover fell from 7.12x to 5.51x. Interest expense, net was USD 60.0 million in the nine months to 30 June 2026 against USD 49.3 million a year earlier because revolver borrowing rose, and the weighted-average rate on total debt was 6.7% in the third quarter. The 7.00% senior notes carry a fixed cost, so the floating revolver, at 5.62% on the drawn amount at 30 June 2026, is the variable element. Cash interest paid is printed only for fiscal years, so cover for the last twelve months is not established.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_brbr_brbr-20250930.htm.jsonl, pages 75, 90; 10-Q_2026-06-30_brbr_brbr-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 12, 24; calculation C32

Operating cash flow was USD 65.0 million in the nine months to 30 June 2026 against USD 91.5 million a year earlier, because inventory absorbed USD 150.7 million of cash and earnings fell. Free cash flow of USD 56.9 million covered less than half of the USD 137.2 million spent on share repurchases; the rest came from USD 50.0 million of net revolver borrowing and a USD 38.1 million fall in cash including restricted cash. Capital expenditure is small, at USD 8.1 million in the nine months, so the lever available to creditors is repurchase policy rather than capital spending.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_brbr_brbr-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 3, 19, 24; calculation C45; calculation C46

Liquidity at 30 June 2026 was USD 248.0 million, made up of USD 50.4 million of unrestricted cash and USD 197.6 million undrawn on the USD 500.0 million revolver after USD 2.4 million of letters of credit, against USD 320.8 million at the 2024 year end. The USD 86.0 million of legal settlement payments due by 10 August 2026 equal 34.68% of that liquidity, and net debt would rise to USD 1,170.9 million, or 3.53x last-twelve-month EBITDA, once they are paid. No facility matures within twelve months of the as-of date, because the notes fall due on 15 March 2030 and the revolver on 22 August 2030, or 14 December 2029 if the notes are not refinanced, so the near-term pressure is on liquidity rather than refinancing.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_brbr_brbr-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 10–13, 16, 25; 10-K_2025-09-30_brbr_brbr-20250930.htm.jsonl, pages 93, 95; calculation C38; calculation C34; calculation C51; calculation C47; calculation C48

The strongest contrary evidence is that net sales still grew in the nine months to 30 June 2026, last-twelve-month EBITDA recovered in the third quarter as the prior-year provision left the window, no debt matures before December 2029, and the revolver still has USD 197.6 million undrawn. That evidence would win if gross margin recovers toward its fiscal 2025 level and repurchases slow, which would reverse the rise in leverage; it loses if inventory keeps absorbing cash and the legal payment is funded by further borrowing.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_brbr_brbr-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 12–13, 22; 10-K_2025-09-30_brbr_brbr-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 75; calculation C45

EBITDA is derived, not issuer-stated, so every ratio inherits the definition of operating profit plus depreciation and amortization, and the debt in each ratio is carrying value, USD 4.7 million below principal at 30 June 2026. Restricted cash, USD 0.6 million at 30 June 2026 and USD 17.3 million at 30 September 2025, is excluded from cash.
