## Findings

### Bottom line

BellRing's creditors face a concentrated, asset-light brand business whose main governance risk is shareholder returns, not an owner: repurchases outran free cash flow while revolver debt rose, and the discretion to repeat that decides how fast leverage moves. Three customers bought 74.0% of fiscal 2025 net sales without material long-term supply agreements, one contract manufacturer made 46.3% of Premier Protein shake supply, and repurchases of USD 474.9 million in fiscal 2025 were 185.58% of free cash flow of USD 255.9 million.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_brbr_brbr-20250930.htm.jsonl, pages 10, 12, 14, 76–77; calculation C1; calculation C8; calculation C9

Post sold its last shares in 2022, so no sponsor is identified behind the credit, but the chairman still holds a Post role, the chief executive gave notice of retirement, a different person certifies the latest report and securities suits are pending. Sponsor and governance risk is Medium: the evidence is mixed and the proxy statement that would show board independence and large holders is missing.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_brbr_brbr-20250930.htm.jsonl, pages 10, 45, 104–105; 10-Q_2025-12-31_brbr_brbr-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 29; 10-Q_2026-03-31_brbr_brbr-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 35; 10-Q_2026-06-30_brbr_brbr-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 17, 34; Source readiness (CP-0)

### Key findings

Customer concentration is the main business risk behind the credit. Three customers took 74.0% of fiscal 2025 net sales (34.3%, 27.5% and 12.2%), the issuer has no material long-term supply agreements with customers and customers frequently reevaluate the products they carry, so one retailer's decision on shelf space or on a competing brand can remove a large share of sales. Costs do not flex in step: take-or-pay purchase commitments were USD 1,362.9 million at 30 September 2025, with USD 602.7 million due in fiscal 2026. How much of that commitment could be resized is not stated, so the link from lost sales to cash flow is an analyst inference. The issuer attributes the 16.05% growth in fiscal 2025 net sales to distribution gains and promotion, so growth and concentration have come together.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_brbr_brbr-20250930.htm.jsonl, pages 12, 31, 64, 66–67, 87; calculation C1; calculation C2

Supply is concentrated and part of it now comes from the former parent. One contract manufacturer made 46.3% of Premier Protein shake supply in fiscal 2025 and a single facility of it made 28.0%, one supplier provides the majority of milk-based protein, and a sole supplier provides the aseptic packaging for the 11 ounce shake. Purchases of shakes from Comet, a subsidiary of Post, rose from USD 8.6 million in fiscal 2024 to USD 62.2 million in fiscal 2025 and USD 80.6 million in the nine months to 30 June 2026, which is 6.60% of nine-month cost of goods sold against 4.07% a year earlier, and the payable to Post and its subsidiaries reached USD 15.0 million. The filings say licences with Post are priced as arm's-length transactions but give no pricing basis for the Comet purchases, so related-party pricing is not established.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_brbr_brbr-20250930.htm.jsonl, pages 14, 23–26, 87; 10-Q_2026-06-30_brbr_brbr-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 3, 7; calculation C6; calculation C7

Shareholder returns, not an owner, are the governance risk that moves creditor outcomes. Cash repurchases were USD 474.9 million in fiscal 2025, or 185.58% of free cash flow of USD 255.9 million, and USD 137.2 million in the nine months to 30 June 2026, or 241.12% of free cash flow of USD 56.9 million. Across fiscal 2025 and the nine months repurchases exceeded free cash flow by USD 299.3 million while net revolver borrowing was USD 300.0 million, and in August 2025 the lenders agreed to a USD 500.0 million revolver with broadened exceptions for stock repurchases. The issuer does not link the borrowing to the repurchases, so that funding is an analyst inference from the cash flows. Board authorisations rose from USD 300.0 million to USD 400.0 million and then USD 600.0 million between March and November 2025, and USD 506.9 million of the latest remained at 30 June 2026.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_brbr_brbr-20250930.htm.jsonl, pages 61, 65, 76–77, 93; 10-Q_2025-12-31_brbr_brbr-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 28; 10-Q_2026-06-30_brbr_brbr-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 3–4, 33–34; calculation C8; calculation C9; calculation C10; calculation C11; calculation C20

No sponsor or controlling holder is identified in the filings, but overlap with Post persists. Post held about 14.2% after the March 2022 spin-off and sold its last shares on 25 November 2022; the chairman of the board still serves as an officer and director of Post, the charter lets Post and its affiliates compete and renounces corporate opportunities for overlapping directors while any Post-affiliated person serves, and a tax matters agreement obliges BellRing to indemnify Post for tax liabilities that follow from its own actions. With no sponsor there is no named source of equity support in the filings, and with the proxy statement missing the independence of the board and the presence of any large holder are not established.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_brbr_brbr-20250930.htm.jsonl, pages 9–10, 45–48, 104–105; 10-Q_2026-06-30_brbr_brbr-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 7; calculation C18

Management and legal events add event risk without yet showing a failure of controls. The chief executive gave notice of retirement on 2 February 2026, effective at the earlier of a successor's appointment or the end of fiscal 2026 on 30 September 2026, and the chief executive certification of the report signed on 4 August 2026 is given by a different person whose title and appointment date are not stated, so a change of chief executive between May and August 2026 is likely but not documented; the end of fiscal 2026 itself fell 49 days before the as-of date. A securities class action and a consolidated derivative action allege misstatements between August 2024 and May 2026; the court granted in part and denied in part the motion to dismiss on 28 July 2026 and the company cannot estimate a loss. The older Joint Juice litigation is settled for USD 90.0 million, of which USD 86.0 million remained to be paid on or before 10 August 2026, and the provision sits in fiscal 2025 results.
Basis: 10-Q_2025-12-31_brbr_brbr-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 29; 10-Q_2026-03-31_brbr_brbr-20260331.htm.jsonl, pages 18, 35; 10-Q_2026-06-30_brbr_brbr-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 16–18, 34; 10-K_2025-09-30_brbr_brbr-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 98; calculation C15; calculation C16

The operating model is asset-light, but growth and margin have turned. Net sales growth slowed from 19.76% in fiscal 2024 and 16.05% in fiscal 2025 to 2.28% in the nine months to 30 June 2026, because Premier Protein sales rose USD 6.4 million on 5% higher volume offset by lower prices from promotion, while Dymatize sales rose 14%. Cost of goods sold rose from 65.06% of net sales in the nine months to 30 June 2025 to 71.53%, a rise of 6.47 percentage points that includes a USD 11.3 million charge for an ingredient that failed quality requirements and a USD 10.0 million inventory reserve. Capital spending was only 0.20% of net sales in fiscal 2025, so cost pressure lands on cash flow, not on capital outlay.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_brbr_brbr-20250930.htm.jsonl, pages 64, 76, 86; 10-Q_2026-06-30_brbr_brbr-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 3, 6, 19, 22, 25; calculation C2; calculation C12; calculation C14

Board structure gives creditors little protection beyond the debt documents, and those documents are not supplied. The board is split into three classes until the 2027 annual meeting, directors can be removed only for cause, preferred stock can be issued without a stockholder vote and an exclusive Delaware forum applies, which together make a board reset slow. The restated charter and bylaws were filed with the Form 8-K of 31 January 2025, while the Credit Agreement and indenture appear only as exhibit references, so how much more stock the company may buy back under its restricted-payment terms is not established.
Basis: 10-K_2024-09-30_brbr_brbr-20240930.htm.jsonl, page 52; 10-K_2025-09-30_brbr_brbr-20250930.htm.jsonl, pages 54, 95, 105, 107

Overall, sponsor and governance risk is Medium. The behaviour that matters is repurchases above free cash flow funded in part by the revolver, which matters because it raises debt while the company faces a USD 86.0 million legal payment and slowing sales, and it implies higher refinancing and liquidity risk if repeated. Countervailing evidence is that there is no sponsor extracting value, no cash dividend has ever been paid, the services agreement with Post ended with its functions transferred and repurchases paused in May and June 2026. Further analysis requires the proxy statement, the executed credit documents and the reports for fiscal 2026.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_brbr_brbr-20250930.htm.jsonl, pages 53, 76–77, 102; 10-Q_2026-03-31_brbr_brbr-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 32; 10-Q_2026-06-30_brbr_brbr-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 7, 16, 33; calculation C9; calculation C11; calculation C15; Source readiness (CP-0)

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest contrary evidence is that no sponsor extracts value and no cash dividend has been paid: Post sold its stake in 2022, related-party service fees were only USD 3.0 million in fiscal 2025 and ended with the services agreement in March 2026, internal control over financial reporting was audited as effective at 30 September 2025, and no shares were repurchased in May or June 2026 after the April average price of USD 15.66 per share. The brand business also still grows, at 2.28% in the nine months, with Dymatize sales up 14%.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_brbr_brbr-20250930.htm.jsonl, pages 10, 53, 87, 102; 10-Q_2026-03-31_brbr_brbr-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 32; 10-Q_2026-06-30_brbr_brbr-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 7, 22, 33; calculation C2

The view would improve if the proxy statement shows a majority of independent directors and no large holder, if the new chief executive adopts a leverage target that caps repurchases, or if repurchases stay paused while free cash flow recovers. It would worsen if repurchases resume under the USD 506.9 million of authorisation remaining while the revolver is drawn, if a major retailer cuts distribution, or if the partial denial of the motion to dismiss leads to a settlement that is large against free cash flow of USD 56.9 million in the nine months.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_brbr_brbr-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 105; 10-Q_2026-06-30_brbr_brbr-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 17, 33–34; calculation C8

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

The only transaction in the supplied filings is the March 2022 spin-off from Post, and it set the capital structure that still stands. BellRing issued USD 840.0 million of 7.00% senior notes due 2030 at par on 10 March 2022 with USD 10.2 million of issuance costs and entered a USD 250.0 million revolving credit facility the same day, while Post contributed its units and USD 550.4 million of cash in exchange for the right to receive the notes and equity interests in BellRing. Post distributed 80.1% of its stake to its own shareholders and the dual-class structure of the former listed company was eliminated.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_brbr_brbr-20250930.htm.jsonl, pages 8–9, 93

The one later change to the debt documents is the first amendment of 22 August 2025. It raised the revolving commitments to USD 500.0 million, extended maturity to 22 August 2030 (14 December 2029 if the notes are not redeemed or refinanced by then), lowered the margin and broadened exceptions to the covenants that restrict activities such as stock repurchases. Revolver borrowing was USD 700.0 million and repayment USD 450.0 million in fiscal 2025, and USD 325.0 million and USD 275.0 million in the nine months to 30 June 2026, so principal debt rose from USD 840.0 million at 30 September 2024 to USD 1,140.0 million at 30 June 2026.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_brbr_brbr-20250930.htm.jsonl, pages 65, 93–95; 10-Q_2026-06-30_brbr_brbr-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 3, 10; calculation C11; calculation C21

No acquisition, sale of a business or new debt issue is described after the spin-off, apart from the discontinuance of PowerBar in North America, and no offering document, sources and uses or sponsor transaction is supplied, so entry valuation and sponsor economics are not established. Management says further long-term financing needs depend on growth opportunities that include acquisition activity, which is management language and not a plan.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_brbr_brbr-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 63; 10-Q_2026-06-30_brbr_brbr-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 25
