## Findings

### Bottom line

TTM Technologies is now a larger, more acquisitive and more data-center-dependent credit than its last annual report shows, and creditors rely on an unseen USD 1,100.0 million acquisition, Epiq, closed two days after quarter end and funded entirely from new notes and term loans.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-28_ttmi-20260928.htm.jsonl, page 24; 10-Q_2026-09-28_ttmi-20260928.htm.jsonl, page 18; calculation F8

Trading is strong on the evidence supplied: nine-month net sales rose 43.2% and operating margin reached 11.3%. The documents give no Epiq results, no holder percentages and no leverage after the new borrowings, and three further acquisitions are pending, so the combined effect on creditors is not established.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-28_ttmi-20260928.htm.jsonl, page 28; calculation F2; calculation F3; 10-Q_2026-09-28_ttmi-20260928.htm.jsonl, page 27

### Key findings

- Acquisitions now drive the risk profile more than operations do. Consideration announced or completed since 2026-06-17 totals USD 2,151.2 million: USD 401.2 million for STG (closed 2026-09-16), USD 1,100.0 million for Epiq (closed 2026-09-30) and USD 650.0 million for Ophir Optics (agreed 2026-10-19, closing expected in the second quarter of 2027). New debt of USD 1,600.0 million was raised between 2026-09-24 and 2026-09-30, which concentrates integration, leverage and refinancing risk in one quarter.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-28_ttmi-20260928.htm.jsonl, pages 23–24; 10-Q_2026-09-28_ttmi-20260928.htm.jsonl, page 18; calculation F7; calculation F8
- The earnings base has shifted toward AI-driven data center circuit boards, which makes cash flow more cyclical than the defense-heavy history suggests. Data center and networking was 39% of nine-month net sales against 30% a year earlier, while aerospace and defense fell to 37% from 45%; the Commercial segment was 62.3% of net sales against 53.3%. Management attributes the growth to continued build-out of AI data centers.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-28_ttmi-20260928.htm.jsonl, page 27; 10-Q_2026-09-28_ttmi-20260928.htm.jsonl, page 28; calculation F4; calculation F6
- Customer concentration is high and steady in sales but has risen in receivables. The five largest customers were 44% of nine-month net sales in both years, one customer was about 20% of third-quarter sales, and two customers held 30% of receivables at quarter end against one customer at 14% at the prior year-end.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-28_ttmi-20260928.htm.jsonl, page 27; 10-Q_2026-09-28_ttmi-20260928.htm.jsonl, page 11; 10-K_2025-12-29_ttmi-20251229.htm.jsonl, page 30

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a cautious view is earnings momentum and liquidity. Third-quarter segment operating margin was 18.5% in aerospace and defense and 21.1% in Commercial, against 16.3% and 14.7% a year earlier. Cash was USD 876.3 million, USD 733.9 million of revolver capacity was available, and management states compliance with every covenant at 2026-09-28. Epiq is described as adding critical technologies for defense customers, which, if its earnings are defense-linked and stable, would rebalance the mix toward aerospace and defense. Against that, the 75.3% fixed-rate share of debt predates the floating-rate term loans, and the cash balance appears to include the proceeds of the 2034 notes raised four days before quarter end, which were used after quarter end.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-28_ttmi-20260928.htm.jsonl, page 31; 10-Q_2026-09-28_ttmi-20260928.htm.jsonl, page 34; 10-Q_2026-09-28_ttmi-20260928.htm.jsonl, page 35; 10-Q_2026-09-28_ttmi-20260928.htm.jsonl, page 37; 10-Q_2026-09-28_ttmi-20260928.htm.jsonl, page 43

The view would improve if the fourth-quarter report shows Epiq earnings that comfortably service the USD 1,100.0 million price, if data center demand holds without a rise in customer concentration, and if the pending acquisitions are funded from cash flow rather than new debt. It would worsen if Epiq's earnings are thin or cyclical, if Ophir Optics is debt-funded on top of the September borrowings, or if share repurchases continue while capital spending runs ahead of operating cash flow.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-28_ttmi-20260928.htm.jsonl, page 24; 10-Q_2026-09-28_ttmi-20260928.htm.jsonl, page 34

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

TTM agreed on 2026-06-17 to buy two European companies, Swiss Technology Group AG (STG) and ILFA GmbH, in separate stock purchase agreements. The STG purchase closed on 2026-09-16 for cash of USD 401.2 million, with a deal-contingent cross-currency swap of USD 381.1 million against CHF 306.2 million to fix the Swiss franc cost, and the Q2 filing describes a planned revolver drawdown to finance it. STG makes rigid, rigid-flex and flex circuit boards and miniaturized microcircuits in Switzerland and Germany for surgical robotics, hearing aid, medical imaging and implantable-device customers and sits in the Commercial segment. Provisional amounts at quarter end were goodwill of USD 202.5 million, definite-lived intangibles of USD 111.0 million and property, plant and equipment of USD 44.4 million; no pro forma figures are shown because the filing calls the deal not material. The documents do not say whether the ILFA purchase closed.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-29_ttmi-20260629.htm.jsonl, page 21; 10-Q_2026-09-28_ttmi-20260928.htm.jsonl, page 23; 10-Q_2026-09-28_ttmi-20260928.htm.jsonl, page 22; 10-Q_2026-09-28_ttmi-20260928.htm.jsonl, page 26; 10-Q_2026-09-28_ttmi-20260928.htm.jsonl, page 14

On 2026-09-30, two days after the quarter ended, TTM bought all membership interests in EDS Intermediate Holding, LLC (Epiq) for cash of USD 1,100.0 million, subject to customary post-closing adjustments. The purchase agreement is dated 2026-08-15 and names EDS TopCo, LP, TTM Technologies North America, LLC and TTM Technologies, Inc. as parties besides Epiq, and a debt commitment letter of the same date from JPMorgan Chase Bank, Barclays Bank PLC and two Bank of America entities was filed alongside it. The filing says the deal is meant to provide critical technologies and innovative capabilities for aerospace and defense customers. It gives no Epiq revenue, earnings, assets, location or seller ownership and no purchase price allocation, because the information had not been received; preliminary accounting is promised in the fourth-quarter 2026 report.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-28_ttmi-20260928.htm.jsonl, page 24; 10-Q_2026-09-28_ttmi-20260928.htm.jsonl, page 46

Three financing steps shaped the debt. On 2026-06-01 the 2026 Credit Agreement replaced the term loan with a USD 400.0 million Amended Term Loan Facility (one-month Term SOFR plus 1.75%, due 2030-05-30) and replaced the USD 150.0 million U.S. and USD 150.0 million Asia asset-based facilities with a USD 1,000.0 million revolving facility due May 2031, of which USD 80.0 million was drawn to repay the Asia facility. On 2026-09-24 TTM issued USD 500.0 million of 6.750% senior unsecured notes due 2034-10-01 (7.05% effective rate), guaranteed by domestic subsidiaries and subject to special mandatory redemption had Epiq not closed. On 2026-09-30 the Third Amended and Restated Credit Agreement added a USD 300.0 million Incremental Term Loan A due 2031-05-30 and a USD 800.0 million Incremental Term Loan B due 2033-09-30, guaranteed and secured on the same basis as the existing loans, and USD 180.0 million of revolver borrowings was repaid after quarter end. Only the revolver carries maintenance covenants: minimum interest coverage of 2.50:1.00 and maximum leverage of 4.50:1.00, rising to 5.00:1.00 for the quarter of a qualifying material acquisition and the next three quarters.
