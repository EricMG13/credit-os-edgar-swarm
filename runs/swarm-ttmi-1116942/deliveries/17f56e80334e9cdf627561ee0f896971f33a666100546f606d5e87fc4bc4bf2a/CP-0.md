## Findings

### Bottom line

The five filings support a filings-only financial, business and credit view of TTM Technologies, but only to 2026-09-28: the Epiq acquisition and the USD 1,100.0 million of term loans that funded it closed two days later and sit outside the reported statements.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-28_ttmi-20260928.htm.jsonl, pages 17–18; 10-Q_2026-09-28_ttmi-20260928.htm.jsonl, page 24; 10-Q_2026-09-28_ttmi-20260928.htm.jsonl, page 27; calculation C9

Reported debt of USD 1,659.0 million at that date excludes those loans, so any pro forma leverage must be built from stated transaction amounts and labelled as an estimate. No executed debt documents, ratings, market quotes, peer data or Epiq results were supplied, so covenant capacity, recovery, relative value and security selection cannot be worked from this set.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-28_ttmi-20260928.htm.jsonl, page 15; calculation C8

### Key findings

Four audited fiscal years (FY2022 to FY2025) and every 2026 quarter are covered, and the statements extracted cleanly: all five balance sheets foot, the three 2026 quarters add exactly to the nine-month sales and operating income, and year-end figures agree between the two annual reports. The latest filing was signed 21 days before the as-of date, so later events are not captured.
Basis: calculation C1; calculation C2; calculation C3; calculation C4; calculation C5; calculation C6; calculation C7; calculation C10; calculation C11

The filings do not print a covenant ratio. The revolver carries two maintenance tests, a minimum interest cover of 2.50x and a maximum leverage of 4.50x, and management states compliance, so headroom cannot be established without the agreement's definitions. The executed credit agreements and indentures are listed as exhibits to earlier current reports but were not supplied.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-28_ttmi-20260928.htm.jsonl, page 17; 10-Q_2026-09-28_ttmi-20260928.htm.jsonl, page 35; 10-Q_2026-09-28_ttmi-20260928.htm.jsonl, page 46; 10-K_2025-12-29_ttmi-20251229.htm.jsonl, page 78

Three selected analyses, Financial foundation, Business & governance and Fundamental credit, are ready with limitations. Of the analyses outside this run, Earnings changes, Downside, Liquidity, Refinancing & LME, Rates, FX & hedging, Evidence validation, Committee decision and Short credit snapshot are also ready with limitations, while Peer benchmark, Earnings quality, Forward credit model, Ratings & triggers, Legal & covenants, Recovery & fulcrum, Security selection and Market-implied risk are conditional on documents the set does not carry.
