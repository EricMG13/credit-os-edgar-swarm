## Findings

### Bottom line

Leverage has risen from 2.42x total debt to EBITDA at FY2024 to 4.03x on a last-twelve-months basis at 2026-09-30 (net 2.57x, 2.61x pro forma for a USD 7 million Brazil payment in the fourth quarter of 2026), so creditors depend on earnings recovery. The filings state no EBITDA, so the measure is operating income plus depreciation and amortization: USD 181.7 million over the last twelve months after USD 48.7 million of impairment, and USD 230.4 million before it. Cash interest paid was USD 82.9 million over the same period, so cover is 2.19x; the 7.45x for FY2025 flatters because the first coupon on the 2031 senior secured notes fell in 2026.
Basis: calculation C21; calculation C22; calculation C46; calculation C49; 10-Q_2026-09-30_uis-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 5; 10-Q_2026-09-30_uis-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 22; 10-K_2025-12-31_uis-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 121

Total debt rose by USD 248.5 million in FY2025 to USD 741.7 million, and cash fell by USD 149.7 million between 2025-12-31 and 2026-09-30. Liquidity inputs total USD 353.3 million against debt of USD 731.4 million.
Basis: calculation C52; calculation C53; calculation C55; 10-Q_2026-09-30_uis-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 5; 10-Q_2026-09-30_uis-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 20

### Leverage and coverage

Gross leverage rose because debt rose while earnings fell. Total debt went from USD 493.2 million at FY2024 to USD 741.7 million at FY2025 when the June 2025 offering of the 2031 senior secured notes replaced the 2027 notes and also funded part of the US pension deficit, while derived EBITDA fell from USD 203.8 million to USD 173.7 million. The creditor consequence is that the same earnings now carry USD 248.5 million more debt at a higher coupon, and the interest line already shows it: interest expense was USD 53.4 million in FY2025 against USD 31.9 million in FY2024.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_uis-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 91–92; 10-K_2025-12-31_uis-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 70; calculation C52; calculation C3; calculation C2

The strongest contrary evidence is that last-twelve-months EBITDA of USD 181.7 million carries a non-cash impairment of USD 48.7 million; before it, leverage is 3.17x total and 2.03x net, and that view wins if no further impairment follows and earnings hold at the pre-impairment level. What would change the view the other way is a further year of cash interest at the current USD 82.9 million with no EBITDA recovery, because cover is already 2.19x on a last-twelve-months basis and only 1.17x after capital expenditure.
Basis: calculation C50; calculation C22; calculation C63; 10-Q_2026-06-30_uis-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 4

### Cash flow quality

Operating cash flow is negative because pension contributions absorb it: net cash from operating activities was an outflow of USD 140.0 million in FY2025 after pension and postretirement contributions of USD 345.3 million, USD 250 million of them a discretionary pension contribution, and an outflow of USD 66.5 million in the nine months of 2026 after USD 89.0 million of contributions. Free cash flow after marketable software and capital additions was an outflow of USD 46.4 million over the last twelve months, so debt service and pension funding both draw on cash rather than on cash generation.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_uis-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 92–93; 10-K_2025-12-31_uis-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 73; 10-Q_2026-09-30_uis-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 5–6; calculation C31

Working capital is a second drain: accounts payable and current liabilities used USD 79.3 million in the nine months of 2026, and deferred revenue, a non-debt funding float that clients prepay, fell from USD 329.2 million at 2025-12-31 to USD 253.6 million at 2026-09-30. The mechanism is that the float funded operations and its decline of USD 75.6 million is cash that must now come from the balance sheet; the implication is that a shrinking float adds to the cash drain from interest and pension funding.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_uis-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 5–6; calculation C51; 10-K_2025-12-31_uis-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 91–92

### Liquidity and maturities

Liquidity inputs are USD 264.2 million of cash (excluding USD 12.3 million of restricted cash) and USD 89.1 million of ABL availability, and the Brazil payment would lower cash by about USD 7 million, so pro forma cash is not established. Of the cash, USD 187.1 million is held abroad and about one-quarter of that may not be readily transferable. The ABL matures in June 2030, so no committed facility expires within twelve months, and availability is limited by a receivables borrowing base that the filings do not print: it is USD 29.4 million below the commitment net of letters of credit.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_uis-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 5; 10-Q_2026-09-30_uis-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 20; 10-Q_2026-09-30_uis-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 37; calculation C55

The maturity wall is a single bullet: at 2025-12-31 the only principal after FY2029 is the USD 700.0 million of 2031 senior secured notes, against USD 13.3 million of other debt falling due from FY2026 to FY2029. The consequence is that refinancing risk sits in January 2031 and in the ABL springing maturity, which brings the ABL forward to 91 days before any date on which US pension contributions above USD 100.0 million are required unless liquidity above USD 130.0 million and other conditions are met.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_uis-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 121; 10-K_2025-12-31_uis-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 72; calculation C44

### Definitions, data quality and conflicts

Every ratio here rests on a derived EBITDA, which is the main definition risk: the filings state none, so operating income plus the four depreciation and amortization lines is used without add-backs, and the issuer's own adjusted measure, if one exists, is not supplied. Interest is the second: the nine-month interest on the 2031 notes of USD 57.7 million exceeds the interest expense line of USD 55.8 million by USD 1.9 million, and FY2025 cash interest of USD 23.3 million omits the first coupon on the 2031 notes, so cover for FY2025 is not a run rate. The 2024-12-31 balance sheet and the 2023-12-31 capitalized contract costs were printed differently in the two annual reports, and the later report is used for 2024.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_uis-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 19; 10-Q_2026-09-30_uis-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-K_2025-12-31_uis-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 121; 10-K_2025-12-31_uis-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 91–92; 10-K_2024-12-31_uis-20241231.htm.jsonl, page 83; calculation C54

### Subsequent events and multi-figure items

One event after the balance-sheet date changes net debt: the 2026-09-30 report states that a Brazil tax reserve of about USD 7 million was paid in the fourth quarter of 2026, which raises net debt to USD 474.2 million and net leverage to 2.61x on the same EBITDA, and it is kept out of the period figures. The September 2026 pension annuity is a multi-figure event: about USD 200 million of obligations transferred, a pre-tax settlement loss of USD 151.2 million and USD 121.4 million reclassified out of accumulated other comprehensive loss, none of which enters EBITDA because the loss sits below operating income.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_uis-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 22; 10-Q_2026-09-30_uis-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 10; 10-Q_2026-09-30_uis-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 23; calculation C46

## Gaps

- No filing states an EBITDA or an adjusted EBITDA, so EBITDA (Reported) is derived for every period as operating income plus depreciation and amortization, and no adjusted figure or bridge to one is stated; this restricts comparison of leverage and cover with any issuer or agency measure. Severity medium. The action that would close it is the issuer's own adjusted EBITDA reconciliation, if it publishes one; until then the derived measure stays labelled.
- The cash flow statements print year-to-date amounts only, so discrete Q2 and Q3 depreciation and amortization, and with it discrete Q2 and Q3 EBITDA for 2025 and 2026, are derived by subtraction, and no discrete Q2 or Q3 cash flow is stated. Severity low. The derived quarters stay labelled as derived.
- No balance sheet is printed at 2025-03-31, 2025-06-30 or 2025-09-30 and no fourth-quarter 2025 statements are printed, so net debt and leverage at those dates and a discrete fourth quarter are not stated and the last-twelve-months series starts at 2026-03-31. Severity medium. Nothing in the five filings closes it; a fourth quarter can be derived by subtraction and labelled.
- The only debt maturity table and lease maturity table are at 2025-12-31, so the maturity ladder and the principal reconciliation are drawn there and no maturity profile is stated at 2026-09-30; the other debt of USD 8.3 million has no stated obligor, rate, maturity or security. Severity medium. A later maturity table, if published, would close it.
- The 2030 row of the 2025-12-31 maturity table is a dash. The row is left out of the debt maturities table rather than written as zero, and the printed principal total of USD 713.3 million equals the rows listed. Severity low.
- Finance leases of USD 35.6 million and other debt of USD 8.3 million are left out of the debt stack because no source states their legal rank, and the finance leases' security is not stated. Severity low. The lease and loan documents would close it.
- The ABL borrowing base is not printed, and reported availability of USD 89.1 million at 2026-09-30 is USD 29.4 million below the commitment net of letters of credit; undrawn committed facilities in the liquidity table is the reported availability. Severity medium. A borrowing base certificate would close it.
- FY2025 cash interest paid of USD 23.3 million is not a run rate because the first coupon on the 2031 senior secured notes was paid in January 2026, so FY2025 cover of 7.45x flatters; the 2026 rows use last-twelve-months cash interest. Severity medium. FY2025 cover should be read with that limit.
- The 2023-12-31 balance sheet column is on the FY2024 report's presentation: capitalized contract costs are printed as outsourcing assets of USD 31.6 million against USD 35.3 million in the FY2025 report, and prepaid and other long-term assets at 2023-12-31 are not re-presented. Totals, debt and cash are unaffected. Severity low.
- Dashes printed in the filings are recorded as not supplied and never as zero: goodwill impairment for FY2023, Q2 2025, H1 2025 and Q3 2026, loss before income taxes for Q2 2025, debt extinguishment, gain on sale of properties, sale proceeds, issuance costs and foreign exchange forward lines in several columns, and inventories for FY2023. Lines a report does not print, such as the impairment line in the Q1 reports, are recorded as not printed. Severity low.
- No guarantor financial information is printed for the three subsidiary guarantors, so the share of earnings, debt and cash at the guarantors is not established and structural subordination is described in text only. Severity medium. Summarized guarantor financial information, if it exists, would close it.
- Cash taxes paid for FY2024 and FY2023 are the amounts disclosed before the 2025 disclosure change, with no breakdown by jurisdiction. Severity low.
- The Brazil payment after the balance-sheet date is dated only to the fourth quarter of 2026 and its amount is approximate, so the pro forma net debt and net leverage are approximate and other fourth-quarter cash flows are not reflected. Severity medium. The fourth-quarter report would close it.
- The pension settlement events of 2025 and 2026 print a settlement loss, an amount of obligations transferred and a smaller amount reclassified out of accumulated other comprehensive loss, and the filings do not reconcile them; none of the three is in EBITDA. Severity low.
