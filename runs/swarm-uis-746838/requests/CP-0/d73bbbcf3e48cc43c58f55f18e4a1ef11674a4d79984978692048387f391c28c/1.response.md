## Findings

### Bottom line

The five Unisys filings are sufficient for a filings-only financial, business and fundamental credit review through 30 September 2026, but covenant, governance and issuer-defined EBITDA conclusions stay provisional because the documents that hold them are absent. The statements, debt notes, revolving-facility terms, pension funding and segment results needed for those modules are printed for FY2025 and each quarter of 2026, and the latest filing was signed 13 days before the 2026-11-18 cutoff. A discrete fourth quarter of 2025 and a maturity schedule at 30 September 2026 are not printed, so those inputs must be derived or taken from earlier dates.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_uis-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 45; 10-K_2025-12-31_uis-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 91–92; 10-Q_2026-03-31_uis-20260331.htm.jsonl, pages 4–6; 10-Q_2026-06-30_uis-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 4–6; 10-Q_2026-09-30_uis-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 4–6; 10-K_2025-12-31_uis-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 121; 10-Q_2026-09-30_uis-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 18; calculation C10; calculation C12

No credit agreement or indenture is supplied, so the level of the springing fixed-charge covenant, the company's reported ratio and the headroom are not established. The 2026 proxy statement that carries board, pay and ownership data is absent, and no filing states an adjusted EBITDA, so any leverage multiple would be an analyst construction. The gate therefore passes for the three requested modules with these limits attached.
