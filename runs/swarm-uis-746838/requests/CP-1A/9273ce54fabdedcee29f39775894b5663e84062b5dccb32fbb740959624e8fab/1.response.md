## Findings

### Bottom line

Unisys has no identified controlling shareholder or financial sponsor, so creditor risk lies in its own financial policy and a narrow earnings base, not in owner extraction, and its 2025 choice to borrow secured debt partly for pensions makes that base matter more. The June 2025 refinancing raised debt principal by USD 215.0 million and annual cash coupon by about USD 41.03 million, and about USD 200 million of the proceeds funded a USD 250 million discretionary pension contribution.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_uis-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 70–74; 10-K_2024-12-31_uis-20241231.htm.jsonl, page 113; calculation C13; calculation C14; calculation C12

Profit sits in one place. The enterprise computing segment, built on the ClearPath software franchise, earns 61.17% of segment gross profit on 33.63% of segment revenue, while the digital workplace segment earns a 14.54% gross margin and has had all of its goodwill written off. The filings show no dividend since 1990 and no share repurchases in 2025, so owner extraction is not in evidence.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_uis-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 143; 10-Q_2026-09-30_uis-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 17; 10-K_2025-12-31_uis-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 60; calculation C1; calculation C2; calculation C3

### Key findings

- Ownership gives creditors no sponsor to assess and no fee, distribution or recapitalisation pattern to price. The stock is listed in New York, held by a few thousand stockholders of record, and the aggregate market value held by non-affiliates was USD 300.4 million at 2025-06-30, small against debt of USD 741.7 million at 2025-12-31. The equity cushion is thin and there is no owner with a stated willingness or capacity to support the notes.
  Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_uis-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 1–2; 10-K_2025-12-31_uis-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 60; Source readiness (CP-0)
- Financial policy has been mixed and pension-led. The 2025 refinancing moved the maturity from 2027 to 2031 but at a higher coupon and a larger secured principal, and 80.00% of the 2025 discretionary pension contribution was borrowed. Annuity purchases in each year from 2023 to 2026 cut pension obligations at non-cash settlement losses of USD 857.2 million in total, and a further settlement is expected in 2027. Creditors gain a smaller pension claim, but at the price of a heavier secured debt stack.
  Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_uis-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 70–74; 10-K_2024-12-31_uis-20241231.htm.jsonl, page 54; 10-Q_2026-09-30_uis-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 10; 10-Q_2026-09-30_uis-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 39; calculation C12; calculation C15
- Earnings depend on the ClearPath franchise, whose revenue is lumpy rather than clearly weakening. ClearPath revenue fell 9.14% in the nine months to 2026-09-30 but rose 1.7% in the third quarter, and renewal contract value rose 33% over the nine months. The enterprise computing segment's share of segment gross profit held at 53.10% against 53.34% a year earlier; the 61.17% for FY2025 is higher because enterprise computing supplied an implied 74.75% of fourth-quarter segment gross profit. The filing says seasonality has not generally moved quarterly revenue materially, yet implied fourth-quarter 2025 revenue was 24.84% above the third quarter, so earnings and cash hinge on the fourth-quarter renewal season.

Revenue rose 2.17% in the nine months to 2026-09-30, but gross profit fell 28.05% and the gross margin was 11.17% against 15.86% a year earlier, after 14.54% for FY2025. Cumulative goodwill impairments reached USD 141.3 million with the second quarter 2026 charge, so no goodwill remains to impair, but weak segment profit leaves less to absorb cost-reduction cash.

Visibility is contract-based, not a commitment, and public-sector and sector concentrations inside enterprise computing remain.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against the view that owner behaviour is not a creditor risk is what the filings do not show: holder percentages, board seats, related-party dealings and director independence are all in a proxy statement that is not supplied, so a large holder or activist could exist without appearing here. Equity awards are also sizeable, with outstanding restricted stock and units equal to 9.07% of shares, which aligns employees with shareholders more than creditors. The strongest evidence against concern about the earnings base is stabilisation: nine-month revenue up 0.51%, backlog of USD 2.92 billion at 2026-09-30 above USD 2.83 billion a year earlier, third-quarter ClearPath revenue up 1.7% and nine-month ClearPath renewal contract value up 33%.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_uis-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 145; 10-K_2025-12-31_uis-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 130; 10-Q_2026-09-30_uis-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 26; 10-Q_2026-09-30_uis-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 30; 10-Q_2026-09-30_uis-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 36; 10-Q_2026-09-30_uis-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 37; calculation C8; calculation C10; calculation C17

The view would change in three ways. A large holder, or insider representation on the board, would turn the pension-led policy into a question of owner conduct. An indenture or credit agreement that permits large restricted payments would make the absence of distributions a choice rather than a limit. A fall in ClearPath revenue through the fourth-quarter renewal season, or the planned 2027 pension settlement funded with new debt, would move the assessment from mixed to adverse, while a strong renewal season would support the stabilisation case.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_uis-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 30; 10-Q_2026-09-30_uis-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 39; 10-K_2025-12-31_uis-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 70–71; calculation C8; calculation C21

### Limitations

- The proxy statement is not among the sources, so the conclusion that no controlling shareholder, sponsor or activist exists rests on the cover page, the stockholder count and the absence of any sponsor in the notes; a large holder would reverse it.
- No indenture or credit agreement is supplied, so what the documents let the company pay out, borrow or secure is not established, and the view that financial policy has been mixed rests on actions taken, not on capacity.
- Segments are reported only to gross profit, so the concentration of profit in the enterprise computing segment is a gross profit finding and not an operating profit one; allocated overhead could narrow or widen it.
- The 2024 and 2023 segment figures were re-presented after the January 2025 reorganisation, so segment trends before FY2025 rest on the later report's figures and not on the earlier report's.
