## Findings

### Bottom line

Axon is a listed public-safety technology issuer with no disclosed controlling holder, whose credit rests on recurring public-sector software contracts and a record of buying out stakes it already holds, so creditors depend on management's own financial policy.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_axon_axon-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 11–12; 10-K_2025-12-31_axon_axon-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 5–8; 10-K_2025-12-31_axon_axon-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 63; Source readiness (CP-0)

That policy is, on the evidence, cash-funded and free of payouts: no dividends and no program repurchases through 2025, USD 1,820,710 thousand of cash spent on acquisitions from 2024 to the first nine months of 2026, and an equity program that raised USD 489,554 thousand in 2025. The weak points are a governance record with two year-end control failures and a USD 1.2 billion Skydio commitment whose cash call is up to USD 720.0 million.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_axon_axon-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 63; 10-K_2025-12-31_axon_axon-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 87; 10-Q_2026-09-30_axon_axon-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 33; 10-K_2025-12-31_axon_axon-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 147–148; 10-K_2024-12-31_axon_axon-20241231.htm.jsonl, pages 167–168; calculation C11

### Key findings

Recurring software is now the larger and faster-growing half of the business, which gives creditors contracted visibility but ties it to public budgets. Software and Services made up 45.39% of net sales in the nine months of 2026 against 43.38% a year earlier and grew 38.28%, while Connected Devices grew 27.46%. Remaining performance obligations were USD 10.2 billion at 2026-09-30, and Axon expects 20% to 25% of that within 12 months, so most of the contracted value is realised over several years and depends on agency appropriations. Sales are concentrated by geography, with 82.47% in the United States, and the largest customer vertical is law enforcement, although no customer exceeded 10% of net sales in 2025, 2024 or 2023.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_axon_axon-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 11–12; 10-Q_2026-09-30_axon_axon-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 39–41; 10-K_2025-12-31_axon_axon-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 8; 10-K_2025-12-31_axon_axon-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 23; 10-K_2025-12-31_axon_axon-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 46; calculation C1; calculation C2; calculation C3; calculation C4; calculation C6

Ownership and control are only partly established: no sponsor or controlling holder is disclosed, but the filings give no holder percentages, board independence or related-party detail, because those items sit in a proxy statement that is not supplied. The 2025 annual report was signed by 8 directors, including the chief executive, against 10 a year earlier; three earlier signatories are absent and one new director appears, and the filings give no reason. The chief executive is also a director, the officer who reviews segment results and the holder of a shareholder-approved equity award, so board oversight of that role matters, and its independence cannot be tested without the proxy.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_axon_axon-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 149–150; 10-K_2025-12-31_axon_axon-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 154; 10-K_2024-12-31_axon_axon-20241231.htm.jsonl, pages 175–176; 10-Q_2026-09-30_axon_axon-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 9; 10-Q_2026-09-30_axon_axon-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 24; Source readiness (CP-0); calculation C32

Payout policy is creditor-friendly on the evidence, but equity pay is settled partly in cash. Axon has declared no dividends and says it does not intend to, and it repurchased no shares under its authorised program in 2023, 2024 or 2025, leaving USD 16.3 million of the authorisation unused. Instead, net-settling employee equity awards took cash payments of USD 350,981 thousand in 2025 and USD 167,156 thousand in the first nine months of 2026, equal to 55.34% and 38.42% of stock-based compensation expense, while an at-the-market equity program raised USD 489,554 thousand in 2025 and USD 100,259 thousand in 2026 to date, which adds to equity and not to debt. Shares outstanding rose 6.25% between 2024-12-31 and 2026-09-30.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_axon_axon-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 63; 10-K_2025-12-31_axon_axon-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 134; 10-K_2025-12-31_axon_axon-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 87; 10-Q_2026-09-30_axon_axon-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 7; 10-Q_2026-09-30_axon_axon-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 26–27; 10-Q_2026-09-30_axon_axon-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 4–6; calculation C16; calculation C17; calculation C18

Acquisitions follow a consistent pattern of building a minority stake and then buying the rest with cash, which concentrates integration and goodwill risk. Axon held 20.3%, 20.2%, 0.8% and 10.7% of Fusus, Dedrone, Prepared and Carbyne before buying the remaining interests in January 2024, October 2024, October 2025 and February 2026. Cash paid for business combinations was USD 1,820,710 thousand from 2024 to the first nine months of 2026, and goodwill and intangible assets reached USD 2,154,165 thousand, 24.39% of total assets at 2026-09-30. Remeasuring the stakes it already held produced non-taxable gains of USD 134.1 million across the four deals. This is an inference from four completed cases, and the filings do not say Axon intends to buy the rest of Skydio, in which it holds 15%.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_axon_axon-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 137–139; 10-Q_2026-09-30_axon_axon-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 31–33; 10-Q_2026-09-30_axon_axon-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-K_2025-12-31_axon_axon-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 87; 10-Q_2026-09-30_axon_axon-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 7; calculation C11; calculation C15; calculation C27

The Skydio commitment is the largest transaction still open, and it moves cash out of the company without moving control. On 2026-11-05 Axon agreed to non-voting minority investments of up to USD 1.2 billion that would lift its stake from 15% to about 37% on a fully diluted basis, settled with up to USD 720.0 million of cash and the rest in shares. USD 127.5 million was paid at the primary closing, which leaves up to USD 592.5 million of cash for the tender offer and secondary purchases expected in the fourth quarter of 2026 and the first quarter of 2027, equal to 36.12% of cash and short-term investments at 2026-09-30. Axon says it has no significant influence over Skydio, so the stake is accounted for under the measurement alternative and later price changes would pass through earnings without moving cash.

Strategic investments now carry a meaningful share of balance-sheet value and of reported earnings, so creditors cannot rely on them to service debt. Strategic investments were USD 924,934 thousand at 2026-09-30, 2.22x their USD 416,833 thousand at 2025-12-31 and 10.47% of total assets, and other income, net, which is mainly gains on these investments, was 82.01% of income before tax in the nine months of 2026. The filings attribute most of the gain to observable price changes at one investee without naming it, so whether it is Skydio is not established.

Creditor protection rests on filing summaries and on a structure with no subsidiary guarantees. The notes and the revolver are the parent's obligations, none of its subsidiaries guarantees the senior or convertible notes, and the non-guarantor subsidiaries produced 19% of nine-month net sales and held 17% of consolidated assets. The revolver is senior unsecured at USD 500.0 million after the September 2026 amendment, undrawn, with letters of credit of USD 9.1 million, and a change of control would oblige Axon to offer to repurchase the senior notes at 101%. The covenant levels after the amendment and every restricted-payment, investment and lien basket are not printed, so what the documents allow cannot be set against what Axon has done.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_axon_axon-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 23; 10-Q_2026-09-30_axon_axon-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 47; 10-Q_2026-09-30_axon_axon-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 54; 10-K_2025-12-31_axon_axon-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 117; 10-K_2025-12-31_axon_axon-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 130; Source readiness (CP-0)

The reporting record is the weakest governance evidence, although it is improving. Management reported a material weakness in revenue-recognition controls at both 2024-12-31 and 2025-12-31, an amended 2024 annual report restated the classification of the 2027 convertible notes, and earlier statements were revised for principal-versus-agent accounting; the weakness was reported remediated at 2026-06-30 and disclosure controls were effective at 2026-06-30 and 2026-09-30. The auditor changed in 2024. For creditors the consequence is lower comfort in covenant and leverage figures built from the 2024 and 2025 statements, not a doubt about the current quarter.
Basis: 10-K_2024-12-31_axon_axon-20241231.htm.jsonl, pages 167–168; 10-K_2025-12-31_axon_axon-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 44–45; 10-K_2025-12-31_axon_axon-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 144–148; 10-Q_2026-06-30_axon_axon-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 45–46; 10-Q_2026-09-30_axon_axon-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 52

Operating risk centres on margin mix and government budgets rather than on volume. In the nine months of 2026 gross margin fell to 58.72% from 60.36%, and Connected Devices adjusted gross margin to 49.6% from 52.0%, as Platform Solutions, driven by counter-drone equipment whose timing depends on large contracts, grew 108.57%. Operating income was USD 118,101 thousand, 4.51% of net sales, so debt service depends on sustaining subscription growth and on balance-sheet cash and not yet on operating margin.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest case against caution is that Axon behaves conservatively where creditors are most exposed: it pays no dividends, funds acquisitions from cash on hand, reports USD 1,640,404 thousand of cash and short-term investments at 2026-09-30 against USD 2,900,000 thousand of notes, and has USD 10.2 billion of contracted obligations behind its software revenue, and its revenue-recognition control weakness has been reported remediated. The view would move toward low governance risk if a proxy statement showed an independent board, no related-party dealings and a dispersed share register, and if the indentures showed tight limits on payments and investments. It would move the other way if the Skydio closings use cash near the USD 720.0 million ceiling and a stake of about 37% is followed by an agreement to take control, if the strategic investment gains reverse, if another control weakness is reported, or if acquisitions start to be funded with debt.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_axon_axon-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 16; 10-Q_2026-09-30_axon_axon-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 46; 10-Q_2026-09-30_axon_axon-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 33; 10-Q_2026-09-30_axon_axon-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 12; 10-Q_2026-06-30_axon_axon-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 45–46; 10-K_2025-12-31_axon_axon-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 63; calculation C26

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

Axon has completed four acquisitions since January 2024, each of an entity in which it already held a minority interest, and in September 2026 it raised and re-sized its debt ahead of a further minority investment. Fusus (2024-01-31) and Dedrone (2024-10-01) were followed by Prepared (2025-10-01) and Carbyne (2026-02-18); the Carbyne cash paid was USD 551.7 million net of cash acquired and of equity attributable to pre-combination service, against a base purchase price of USD 625.0 million stated in the 2025 annual report, and Axon funded it from cash on hand.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_axon_axon-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 137–140; 10-K_2025-12-31_axon_axon-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 73; 10-Q_2026-09-30_axon_axon-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 31–32

Two debt events frame the creditor position. In March 2025 Axon issued USD 1,000,000 thousand of 6.125% senior notes due 2030 and USD 750,000 thousand of 6.250% senior notes due 2033, and in September 2026 it issued USD 1,150,000 thousand of zero-coupon convertible senior notes due 2031 with a conversion price of USD 652.06, buying capped calls for USD 114,885 thousand. The same month it amended the revolver to USD 500.0 million, extending its maturity to the earliest of 2031-09-18 and dates 91 days before the 2031 notes' maturity or holder repurchase date. All 2027 convertible notes were exchanged, redeemed or converted by 2026-02-11.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_axon_axon-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 116–120; 10-Q_2026-09-30_axon_axon-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 16–23

The open transaction is Skydio: agreements signed on 2026-11-05 for non-voting minority investments of up to USD 1.2 billion, with up to USD 720.0 million in cash and the rest in shares, of which USD 127.5 million of cash was paid at the primary closing. Closings of the remaining tender offer and secondary purchases are expected in the fourth quarter of 2026 and the first quarter of 2027. This is a subsequent event and is not part of any period figure.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_axon_axon-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 33
