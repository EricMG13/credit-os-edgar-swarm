## Findings

### Bottom line

GFL is a scaled, price-led North American waste operator whose creditors now face an acquisition-transformed borrower and an unresolved take-private review, so governance risk is Medium and control is not established. Collection supplied 60.9% of gross revenue for nine months and price added 5.7% to third-quarter growth, so earnings depend on pricing more than on volume.

Equity funded 80.1% of the CAD 5,414.1 million SECURE consideration, which eased the debt load of the deal, but ownership percentages, voting control and the outcome of the review after the 9 November 2026 deadline are not established.
Basis: 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-10-30_tm2626649d1_ex99-1.htm.jsonl, page 14; 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-10-28_tm2626649d2_ex99-1.htm.jsonl, page 13; 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-10-30_tm2626649d1_ex99-1.htm.jsonl, pages 6–7; 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-10-28_tm2626649d2_ex99-1.htm.jsonl, page 1; calculation K1; calculation K5

### Key findings

- Growth is price-led and acquisition-fed. Organic growth was 7.4% in the third quarter, of which price contributed 5.7% and volume subtracted 0.1%, and acquisitions added a further 18.7%, so the reported 27.2% overstates the base business. Price is the mechanism that protects margin when volumes are flat, and the Adjusted EBITDA margin of 31.5% shows no erosion against 31.6% a year earlier, which supports debt service from the existing network.
Basis: 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-10-28_tm2626649d2_ex99-1.htm.jsonl, page 2; 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-10-28_tm2626649d2_ex99-1.htm.jsonl, page 13
- The SECURE acquisition changed scale, mix and the claims against the business. It closed on 1 September 2026 and brought industrial waste processing and disposal in Western Canada and North Dakota into the group; Canada rose to 35.9% of third-quarter revenue from 33.0% a year earlier, and 91.8% of the consideration is provisional goodwill, so the asset base that backs the claims can still be restated.
Basis: 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-10-30_tm2626649d1_ex99-1.htm.jsonl, pages 6–7; 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-10-30_tm2626649d1_ex99-1.htm.jsonl, page 15; calculation K4; calculation K12
- Equity funding makes the deal creditor-friendlier than its size suggests, but acquisitions overall were still debt-financed at the margin. Shares were 64.0% of nine-month acquisition consideration, while long-term debt issuance exceeded repayments by CAD 2,972.0 million and cash paid for acquisitions reached CAD 2,569.8 million, so leverage rose even though the largest deal was mostly paid in shares.
Basis: 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-10-30_tm2626649d1_ex99-1.htm.jsonl, page 4; 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-10-30_tm2626649d1_ex99-1.htm.jsonl, pages 6–7; calculation K6; calculation K13
- Control and ownership cannot be established from the sources. A class of multiple voting shares exists, 2.71% of basic shares by count, but its holders, votes per share and the voting weight of the preferred shares are not disclosed, so whether a founder, a holder group or a dispersed base decides a sale or a recapitalisation is unknown.
Basis: 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-10-28_tm2626649d2_ex99-1.htm.jsonl, page 14; 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-10-30_tm2626649d1_ex99-1.htm.jsonl, page 16; calculation K7
- Related-party and leakage items are small but recurring. Cash payments to the Founder and Chief Executive Officer of CAD 74.2 million for nine months, which replaced payment in restricted share units, are 4.2% of Adjusted EBITDA and are excluded from Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted Free Cash Flow, so adjusted measures overstate cash left for creditors by that amount.
Basis: 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-10-30_tm2626649d1_ex99-1.htm.jsonl, page 15; 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-10-28_tm2626649d2_ex99-1.htm.jsonl, page 18; calculation K11
- The take-private review is the main event risk. Unsolicited proposals led the board to form a special committee of independent directors that set a final deadline of 9 November 2026, and GFL says no transaction is assured. A leveraged buyout would raise debt and could trigger change-of-control terms that the sources do not describe, so creditors cannot size the exposure yet.
Basis: 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-07-29_tm2619744d2_ex99-1.htm.jsonl, page 2; 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-10-28_tm2626649d2_ex99-1.htm.jsonl, page 1; 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-10-28_tm2626649d2_ex99-1.htm.jsonl, page 6

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence for a better view is that equity paid most of the SECURE price, the third-quarter Adjusted EBITDA margin held at 31.5%, nine-month Adjusted EBITDA rose 18.4%, the issuer reports covenant compliance, and independent directors run the review. Share repurchases were also small in 2026, at CAD 71.0 million of cash for nine months against CAD 2,758.5 million in the same period of 2025. The view would improve if the voting control of the multiple voting shares were disclosed as dispersed or aligned with creditors, if the SECURE earnings and integration costs were shown in a full quarter, and if the review ended without a leveraged transaction. It would worsen if a bidder financed a purchase with new secured debt, if change-of-control terms were triggered, or if acquisition spending continued to be funded by net new debt rather than shares.
Basis: 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-10-30_tm2626649d1_ex99-1.htm.jsonl, page 4; 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-10-30_tm2626649d1_ex99-1.htm.jsonl, pages 6–7; 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-10-30_tm2626649d1_ex99-1.htm.jsonl, page 13; 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-10-28_tm2626649d2_ex99-1.htm.jsonl, page 2; calculation K5; calculation K13

### Limitations

- The annual information form and MD&A are not in the sources, so the governance view rests on interim filings and the annual report cover; board composition, committees and related-party policy cannot be tested.
- The SECURE arrangement agreement and SECURE standalone statements are not supplied, so exchange terms, assumed debt, termination rights and the earnings SECURE adds are not established.
- No executed debt documents are supplied, so legal capacity for distributions, investments and priming is not established and the behaviour flags rest on disclosed actions only.
