## Findings

### Bottom line
Element Solutions is a widely held, acquisitive specialty-chemicals group with no controlling sponsor identified, so governance risk is medium and rests on financial policy rather than extraction: debt-funded 2026 acquisitions and buybacks are the behaviours creditors should monitor.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_esi-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 51; 10-Q_2026-09-30_esi-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 12–13; 10-Q_2026-09-30_esi-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 47

The business sells consumable electronics and industrial materials with no customer at 10% of net sales, but Electronics is 77.5% of nine-month 2026 net sales and the two acquisitions closed in January and February 2026 added USD 389.3 million to nine-month net sales. A merger with Solstice was agreed on 2026-07-06 and terminated on 2026-08-27 with a mutual release and no payment stated.
Basis: calculation C01; calculation C02; 10-K_2025-12-31_esi-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 16; 10-Q_2026-09-30_esi-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 12; 10-Q_2026-09-30_esi-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 26

### Key findings
The operating model is a consumables franchise whose revenue visibility is short. Customers reorder products consumed in their production lines and none reaches 10% of net sales, which limits single-customer loss, but order backlog is minimal, so a fall in electronics demand reaches revenue within quarters rather than being cushioned by contracted volume. Electronics produced 76.2% of segment Adjusted EBITDA in nine months 2026, so creditors are exposed to one demand cycle, led by the AI infrastructure demand that management itself cites.
Basis: calculation C01; calculation C03; 10-K_2025-12-31_esi-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 8, 16; EX-99.1_2026-10-21_aearningsreleaseex99120269.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-09-30_esi-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 27

Acquisitions have changed the credit profile within one year. Micromax and EFC cost USD 861 million in cash, were funded by USD 450 million of add-on term loans plus cash and revolver borrowings, and brought USD 389.3 million of nine-month 2026 net sales, 13.8% of the total. The purchase accounting assigns USD 235.0 million and USD 172.0 million to identifiable intangibles and USD 128.9 million and USD 163.8 million to goodwill, USD 699.7 million of the USD 877.0 million total consideration, so most of the price is intangible value that depends on the acquired earnings arriving, and EFC executives hold performance units that vest on 2028 and 2030 EFC earnings, so the full cost stays open until then.
Basis: calculation C04; 10-Q_2026-09-30_esi-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 12–13; 10-K_2025-12-31_esi-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 126

Sponsor and governance risk is medium on the evidence available. No controlling holder or sponsor is identified, the company is listed, and the documented behaviours are mostly ordinary: a fixed quarterly dividend, USD 200 million of term loan prepayment funded by the 2025 sale of the printing plate business, and a related-party advisory fee of USD 2.0 million a year, which is 0.37% of FY2025 Adjusted EBITDA and was extended on 2026-05-05 to a three-year term. The softer signal is capital return while leverage was rising: dividends and buybacks of USD 97.0 million in nine months 2026 absorbed 50.5% of operating cash flow, with all of the USD 37.7 million of buybacks in the third quarter, almost all of it in September after the merger was terminated.
Basis: calculation C05; calculation C06; 10-Q_2026-09-30_esi-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 26; 10-K_2025-12-31_esi-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 113; 10-Q_2026-09-30_esi-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 47

The terminated Solstice merger shows willingness to pursue a transformative transaction. The agreement of 2026-07-06 would have made the company a wholly owned subsidiary of Solstice for half a Solstice share and USD 10.00 in cash per share, and carried a possible company termination fee of USD 376 million, which would have been paid from cash or the revolver. The termination on 2026-08-27 removed that contingency and the change of control, and no payment is stated, but the company has shown that a large combination is on its agenda, and the change-of-control terms of its notes and loans are not available to test.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_esi-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 12–13; 10-Q_2026-06-30_esi-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 50; 10-Q_2026-09-30_esi-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 12; 10-Q_2026-09-30_esi-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 33

Disclosure is adequate for monitoring but has three definitional soft spots. The adjusted EBITDA margin moved in 2026 to a denominator that excludes pass-through metals, prior-year amounts were reclassified without quantification, and annual-report purchase prices for both acquisitions differ from the later quarterly figures. None blocks monitoring, and each is logged in the conflict register, but together they mean year-on-year margin comparisons must be rebuilt rather than read across.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_esi-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 12; 10-Q_2026-09-30_esi-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 34; 10-K_2025-12-31_esi-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 126

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view
The strongest evidence against a medium risk reading is that the business is better protected than backlog and concentration suggest: products are consumed in manufacturing, the customer base is diversified, switching costs are described as high, and management reports third-quarter organic growth of 13% with record results. That supports a lower governance and business risk reading if growth proves durable and the acquisitions integrate as presented.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_esi-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 6, 16; EX-99.1_2026-10-21_aearningsreleaseex99120269.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 8

The view would move to high risk if capital returns were debt-funded while leverage stayed elevated, for instance buybacks continuing at the third-quarter pace of USD 37.7 million a quarter while revolver borrowings rise, or if a further large combination were announced before the 2026 acquisitions are absorbed. It would move to low risk if the proxy statement showed independent board control and no related-party leakage beyond the advisory fee, and if the credit agreement showed restricted-payment capacity that is tight relative to current leverage. Evidence that electronics demand is slowing, such as falling organic growth in the Electronics segment from 18% in nine months 2026, would raise business risk independently of governance.
Basis: calculation C06; EX-99.1_2026-10-21_aearningsreleaseex99120269.htm.jsonl, page 8; 10-Q_2026-09-30_esi-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 47

### Limitations
- The 2026 proxy statement was not supplied, so board independence, control rights, executive pay and share ownership are not established; this keeps governance risk at medium rather than low and prevents any statement that no holder controls the company.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_esi-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 75
- The executed credit agreement and indenture were not supplied, so restricted-payment, debt and lien capacity and the change-of-control terms are not established; financial policy is judged from behaviour only, and capacity is not separated from willingness.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_esi-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 76; 10-K_2025-12-31_esi-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 41
- Earnings of Micromax and EFC are not stated separately beyond net sales, so the conclusion that the acquisitions add earnings to cover their financing rests on segment growth rather than on acquired EBITDA.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-10-21_aearningsreleaseex99120269.htm.jsonl, page 4; EX-99.1_2026-10-21_aearningsreleaseex99120269.htm.jsonl, pages 17–18
- Merger and termination terms are known only from the quarterly summaries and no stand-alone agreement was supplied, so the statement that no termination payment was made rests on the absence of a stated payment.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_esi-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 12; 10-Q_2026-09-30_esi-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 33
