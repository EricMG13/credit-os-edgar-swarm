### Bottom line

Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_esi-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-Q_2026-09-30_esi-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 16–18; 10-K_2025-12-31_esi-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 75; 10-K_2025-12-31_esi-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 76
