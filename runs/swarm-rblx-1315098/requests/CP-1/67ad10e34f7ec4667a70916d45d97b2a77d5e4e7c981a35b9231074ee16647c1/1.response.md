## Findings

### Bottom line

Roblox's reported EBITDA is negative in every period, so leverage on reported earnings cannot be stated, and creditors rest on USD 6,238 million of cash and investments against USD 1,009 million of total debt at 2026-09-30.

On the issuer's adjusted measure, which adds back stock-based compensation, total debt is 2.58x adjusted EBITDA for the last twelve months and 8.08x for FY2025. Bookings fell to USD 1,697 million in the third quarter from USD 1,922 million a year earlier, the only 2026 quarter in which bookings fell year on year, which matters because advance payments fund operating cash flow.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_rblx.htm.jsonl, pages 9–10; 10-Q_2026-09-30_rblx.htm.jsonl, pages 38–39; 10-K_2025-12-31_rblx.htm.jsonl, pages 148–149; calculation K1; calculation K5

### Key findings

Leverage. The issuer states no unadjusted EBITDA, so reported EBITDA is built as operating loss plus depreciation and amortization; it was a loss of USD 710 million in the first nine months of 2025 and USD 613 million in the first nine months of 2026. The improvement is real but the figure stays negative because stock-based compensation of USD 855 million is expensed. The issuer's adjusted EBITDA, which adds that expense back, rose to USD 389 million from USD 122 million, which is why adjusted leverage fell from 8.08x at FY2025 to 2.58x on a last-twelve-months basis; leverage on reported EBITDA is not meaningful in any period.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_rblx.htm.jsonl, page 10; 10-Q_2026-09-30_rblx.htm.jsonl, page 39; calculation K1; calculation K5

Coverage. Cash interest on the only material borrowing is USD 38.75 million a year, so FY2025 adjusted EBITDA covered it 3.22x, while reported EBITDA was a deficit of 25.97 times cash interest. The 2026 quarterly reports state no cash interest paid, so cover for the last twelve months is not established.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_rblx.htm.jsonl, page 151; 10-Q_2026-09-30_rblx.htm.jsonl, page 12; calculation K5

Cash-flow quality. Operating cash flow of USD 1,342 million in the nine months to September 2026 exceeded the net loss of USD 663 million because stock-based compensation of USD 855 million is non-cash and working capital released cash: deferred revenue added USD 533 million and receivables fell USD 410 million. Free cash flow was USD 1,254 million after capital expenditure of USD 83 million, so cash generation rests on customers paying in advance and on collections rather than on earnings. The change in deferred revenue was USD 136 million in the third quarter against USD 573 million a year earlier, so that source of funding slowed sharply.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_rblx.htm.jsonl, pages 12–13; 10-Q_2026-09-30_rblx.htm.jsonl, page 38; 10-Q_2026-09-30_rblx.htm.jsonl, page 40; calculation K10

Liquidity. Cash and equivalents of USD 1,093 million exceed total debt of USD 1,009 million, a net cash position of USD 84 million on the method's narrow cash definition. Adding USD 5,145 million of short-term and long-term investments, cash and investments cover total debt 6.18 times. The filings describe no committed revolving facility, so there is no undrawn capacity to add, and share repurchases of USD 576 million were the only financing outflow in the nine months.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_rblx.htm.jsonl, page 9; 10-Q_2026-09-30_rblx.htm.jsonl, page 12; 10-Q_2026-09-30_rblx.htm.jsonl, page 17; 10-Q_2026-09-30_rblx.htm.jsonl, page 51; calculation K2; calculation K3

Non-debt funding float. Deferred revenue of USD 7,040 million at 2026-09-30 is an interest-free obligation funded by payments already collected, and it dwarfs total debt of USD 1,009 million; the developer exchange liability to creators adds USD 444 million. These obligations are settled in virtual items and creator payouts rather than interest, so they matter for liquidity when bookings weaken, not for leverage.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_rblx.htm.jsonl, page 9; calculation K4

Legal costs and later events. The nine-month result includes USD 140 million of legal settlement expense, USD 49 million of it in the third quarter; these are cash payments owed to states, added back in the issuer's adjusted EBITDA but expensed in reported EBITDA. The quarterly report describes developments to 2026-10-29, including a notice from the Federal Trade Commission in October 2026, but states no amount, so no adjustment to net debt is made for any event after the balance sheet date.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_rblx.htm.jsonl, pages 23–25; 10-Q_2026-09-30_rblx.htm.jsonl, page 39

Definitions. Three EBITDA measures differ: reported EBITDA is negative, the issuer's adjusted EBITDA is USD 389 million for nine months 2026, and the covenant measure in the notes indenture is USD 900 million for the same period because it also adds back the growth in deferred revenue, a gap of USD 511 million to adjusted EBITDA. A reader comparing leverage across sources must name the measure.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_rblx.htm.jsonl, page 52; calculation K6

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest contrary evidence is liquidity: cash and investments of USD 6,238 million are more than six times debt, the only sizeable maturity is the USD 1,000 million 2030 notes, and free cash flow of USD 1,254 million in nine months is positive, which points to a small refinancing need before 2030 whatever the EBITDA definition. The view that earnings cover is weak would be overturned if reported EBITDA turned positive, which needs operating profit to absorb the USD 855 million of stock-based compensation expensed in the nine months, or if bookings regained growth. It would be confirmed if bookings keep falling, third-quarter bookings being USD 225 million below a year earlier, and the quarterly change in deferred revenue stays near USD 136 million, which would cut operating cash flow and leave settlement and repurchase outflows to be met from the investment portfolio.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_rblx.htm.jsonl, page 9; 10-Q_2026-09-30_rblx.htm.jsonl, pages 19–21; 10-Q_2026-09-30_rblx.htm.jsonl, page 38; 10-Q_2026-09-30_rblx.htm.jsonl, page 40; calculation K7

## Gaps

Reported EBITDA is negative in every period, so total debt and net debt over reported EBITDA are not calculable for any period; this restricts every leverage statement to the issuer-adjusted measure, severity High, and the action is to name the measure whenever a leverage multiple is quoted.

Cash interest paid is stated only for the fiscal years in the annual report, so EBITDA over cash interest is not calculable for the three interim last-twelve-months dates; severity Medium, and the action is to obtain cash interest paid from the 2026 filings or to use the fiscal-year multiples.

No 2025 quarterly report is in the set, so Q4 2025 is not printed and every 2024 quarter, including Q4 2024, is not available; year-on-year quarterly comparisons for the fourth quarter are not calculable; severity Medium, and the action is to supply the 2025 quarterly reports.

The undrawn committed facilities column of the liquidity table is not supplied for any period because the filings describe no committed facility; severity Low, and the action is to confirm with the company that none exists.

The amount of letters of credit at 2026-09-30 is not stated in the quarterly reports, which give only the USD 9.3 million of FY2025; severity Low, and the action is to obtain the figure from the company.

The legal rank of the 2030 notes and of the joint venture notes is not stated beyond the instrument names, so the debt stack is not stated and no secured or senior subtotal is built; severity Medium, and the action is to supply the indenture.

Lines printed as a dash in the filings, such as the legal settlement add-back for the 2025 comparatives, payments for business combinations in some columns and the foreign exchange effect for nine months 2026, are carried as not supplied rather than zero; severity Low, and the action is none beyond reading the filing.

Lines that appear in only the annual or only the quarterly cash flow statements, such as amortization of debt issuance costs, impairment and other items against other adjustments, are carried as not supplied in the columns where the filing does not print them; severity Low, and the action is none beyond reading the filing.

Cash taxes paid are stated for fiscal years only and the 2026 quarterly reports state no income taxes paid; severity Low, and the action is to use the income tax provision as an indication.

Operating metric series such as hours engaged by quarter and the 2025 quarterly daily active users are not available as text; severity Medium, and the action is to obtain the key-metric data.

The 2025 comparative Adjusted EBITDA reconciliations in the quarterly reports print totals one lower than their lines add to, from rounding in the issuer's table; the printed totals are used and the difference is noted; severity Low, and the action is none.

The fiscal-year FY2022 statements printed in the FY2024 annual report are not extracted because the credit snapshot window starts at FY2023; severity Low, and the action is to extract them if a longer history is wanted.

The headquarters lease floor area differs between the Q1 and Q2 reports and the Q3 report, with the same rent; severity Low, and the action is to quote rent and term only.

Items without a stated date after the balance sheet date, including the notice from the Federal Trade Commission in October 2026 and the settlement negotiations with states, carry no stated amount, so no pro forma net debt row is built; severity Medium, and the action is to read the next quarterly report.
