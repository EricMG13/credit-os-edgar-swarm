## Findings

### Bottom line

Roblox is founder-controlled rather than sponsor-owned, so creditors depend on the founder's financial policy, which is cash-funded so far but unconstrained by any distribution limit the filings describe. Class B stock carries 56.94% of votes, and a USD 3.0 billion buyback authorization is three times the USD 1,000 million of notes.

Policy has not yet hurt creditors: no dividend has ever been paid, no debt was issued in 2026, and repurchases of USD 580 million in nine months 2026 were paid from cash and investments of USD 6.2 billion. The risks that could change this are state settlements, which cost USD 140 million over the same nine months with more claims pending, and an indenture that the filings summarise without any limit on distributions.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_rblx.htm.jsonl, pages 105–107; 10-Q_2026-09-30_rblx.htm.jsonl, pages 19–21; 10-Q_2026-09-30_rblx.htm.jsonl, pages 24–26; 10-Q_2026-09-30_rblx.htm.jsonl, pages 51–53; calculation V1; calculation V5

### Key findings

Sponsor and governance risk is Medium, not High, because documented conduct toward creditors is neutral so far, while the structure lets one holder change policy without any outside check. No dividend has been paid, buybacks are funded from cash, and no controlling sponsor, sponsor fee, dividend recapitalisation, or amendment, waiver or exchange of the notes appears in the filings read; against that, distribution capacity, board independence and related-party dealings cannot be tested.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_rblx.htm.jsonl, page 119; 10-K_2025-12-31_rblx.htm.jsonl, page 196; 10-Q_2026-09-30_rblx.htm.jsonl, pages 19–21

Voting control rests with the founder alone, so no outside holder can force a change of policy and a takeover is improbable. Class B stock carries 20 votes a share and is wholly owned by the founder and his affiliates; on share counts it was 56.94% of all votes at 2026-09-30, down from 58.74% at 2025-12-31 after the number of Class B shares fell in April 2026 for reasons the filings do not give. The company has elected out of Nevada's statute on business combinations with large holders, so its defences are the dual-class structure, a staggered board and a two-thirds vote for certain charter amendments. The notes' offer to repurchase at 101% of principal on a change of control therefore protects against an event the structure makes unlikely, and gives no check on the founder's own policy choices.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_rblx.htm.jsonl, pages 105–107; 10-Q_2026-03-31_rblx.htm.jsonl, page 1; 10-Q_2026-03-31_rblx.htm.jsonl, page 10; 10-Q_2026-09-30_rblx.htm.jsonl, page 9; 10-Q_2026-09-30_rblx.htm.jsonl, page 20; 10-Q_2026-09-30_rblx.htm.jsonl, page 129; calculation V1; calculation V2

Capital returns are funded from cash, but they are new and discretionary, and book equity is now a deficit. Roblox bought back no stock in FY2025; after a USD 3.0 billion authorization in May 2026 it repurchased USD 580 million by 2026-09-30, USD 380 million in the second quarter and USD 200 million in the third, and says it intends up to USD 1.0 billion in the first twelve months. Cash paid for repurchases was 42.92% of nine-month operating cash flow, and with the net loss it helped move total stockholders' equity from USD 375 million at 2025-12-31 to a deficit of USD 27 million. The program can be suspended at any time, and USD 2.4 billion of authority remains.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_rblx.htm.jsonl, page 119; 10-K_2025-12-31_rblx.htm.jsonl, page 151; 10-Q_2026-09-30_rblx.htm.jsonl, pages 10–13; 10-Q_2026-09-30_rblx.htm.jsonl, page 26; 10-Q_2026-09-30_rblx.htm.jsonl, page 53; 10-Q_2026-09-30_rblx.htm.jsonl, page 136; calculation V5; calculation V6; calculation V7; calculation V16

The covenant package as the company describes it limits liens, debt and mergers and names no limit on dividends or repurchases, so distribution capacity is a gap rather than a finding. The company says the limits apply up to the greater of USD 4.0 billion and 3.5 times Consolidated EBITDA, states compliance at 2026-09-30, and prices the notes at par for 2026 and thereafter, so the notes can be refinanced without a premium. No willingness to extract is inferred from capacity; the documented distributions are the buybacks above.

User-safety litigation is the main event risk and has already reached the income statement every quarter of 2026. Settlement expense was USD 57 million, USD 34 million and USD 49 million in the three quarters, USD 140 million in all, against an immaterial accrual at 2025-12-31. The number of states that had sued rose from seven at 2026-04-30 to ten at 2026-07-30, two California counties have also sued, six states have settled, and the Federal Trade Commission, Pennsylvania and Utah told the company in October 2026 that they are considering claims. Public-service campaigns, law-enforcement liaison staff, platform changes and liquidated damages under the settlements are not accrued and no loss estimate beyond the accrual is given, so USD 140 million is a floor.

Cash generation depends on advance payments from a thin paying base, so weaker bookings would reach liquidity before revenue. ### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against concern about financial policy is that nothing in the record yet shows creditors losing from it: no controlling sponsor is named, no dividend has ever been paid, repurchases took 42.92% of nine-month operating cash flow, cash and investments of USD 6.2 billion are well above the USD 1,000 million of notes, the company states compliance with its covenants, and no amendment, waiver or exchange is described. The view would soften further if the indenture proved to restrict distributions tightly. It would harden if repurchases ran above operating cash flow or above the USD 1.0 billion first-year intention, if any distribution were debt-funded, if new debt or liens were put in place under the USD 4.0 billion basket, if settlement costs rose well above USD 140 million, or if bookings kept falling from the USD 1,697 million of the third quarter while buybacks continued.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_rblx.htm.jsonl, page 119; 10-Q_2026-09-30_rblx.htm.jsonl, pages 12–13; 10-Q_2026-09-30_rblx.htm.jsonl, page 20; 10-Q_2026-09-30_rblx.htm.jsonl, page 38; 10-Q_2026-09-30_rblx.htm.jsonl, page 51; calculation V6

### Limitations

- The finding that distribution capacity is unmeasured rests on the company's own summary of the notes covenants; if the indenture holds a restricted-payments covenant the risk level would fall, and if its debt and lien baskets are wide it would rise.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_rblx.htm.jsonl, page 20; 10-K_2025-12-31_rblx.htm.jsonl, page 197
- The 56.94% vote share counts Class B stock only, so the founder's total voting power may be understated by his Class A shares and awards, and board independence and related-party dealings are not established; none of this changes majority control, but it would change the Medium rating if related-party payments proved material.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_rblx.htm.jsonl, page 119; 10-K_2025-12-31_rblx.htm.jsonl, page 196; calculation V1
- The second-quarter business acquisition is not named or described, so the view that acquisition appetite is low rests on USD 6 million of cash and USD 17 million of stock, restricted stock units and cash consideration payable; a larger or debt-funded purchase would change the capital-allocation view.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_rblx.htm.jsonl, pages 11–12
- The litigation notes give no loss estimate beyond amounts accrued, so the legal-cost finding is a floor; further settlements would reduce the cash available for repurchases and debt service.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_rblx.htm.jsonl, pages 24–25
- The hosting purchase commitments are printed as a total and a range that do not reconcile, so the finding on fixed commitments could be out by about USD 5 million in one annual period; the lease rent is a total over the term, not a schedule.

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

No debt was issued, repaid or refinanced by Roblox Corporation in 2026; the nine-month cash flow statement shows no debt proceeds or repayments. The only material borrowing remains the USD 1,000 million 3.875% unsecured senior notes due 2030-05-01, issued on 2021-10-29 for net proceeds of about USD 988 million that the company said it intended to use for general corporate purposes, including working capital, capital expenditures, share repurchases and acquisitions. The notes are redeemable at par for 2026 and thereafter, and holders may require repurchase at 101% of principal on a change of control triggering event. The only change to any debt in 2026 was the two-year extension, in the first quarter, of the USD 30 million joint venture notes of Roblox China Holding Corp. to 2028-05-10, which Roblox and an affiliate of Tencent funded equally.
