## Findings

### Bottom line

The supplied Entegris filings and releases support the requested financial, business and fundamental credit work through the quarter ended 2026-09-26, but covenant, lien and recovery questions stay open because no executed debt documents were supplied.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-26_entg-20260926.htm.jsonl, pages 11–12; 10-Q_2026-09-26_entg-20260926.htm.jsonl, pages 32–34

Creditors can read the debt stack, maturity ladder, liquidity and the reported covenant compliance statement from the latest quarterly filing, whose balance sheet date is 40 days before the as-of date. The annual report's note tables are captioned in thousands although their figures are in millions, as the later quarterly filings state, so those tables need unit care. Ratings, market prices, peer data, the executed credit agreement and indentures, and the level of revolver use that switches the covenant test on are not established.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-26_entg-20260926.htm.jsonl, pages 8–9; 10-Q_2026-09-26_entg-20260926.htm.jsonl, page 34; calculation TIE-10
