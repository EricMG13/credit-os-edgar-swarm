## Findings

### Bottom line

Novelis is a scaled, diversified aluminum producer owned entirely by Hindalco, which put USD 950 million of equity into it in fiscal 2026, so creditors have visible parent support, but it funds a USD 5 billion Bay Minette plant that has yet to produce cash.
Basis: 10-K_2026-03-31_nvl-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 142; 10-Q_2026-09-30_nvl-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 65; calculation C01

The support is shown by equity paid in, not by a guarantee of Novelis debt, and the filings say further contributions are not assured. Hindalco appoints every director, related-party dealings are small, and Ball alone is 18% of fiscal 2026 net sales. Sponsor and governance risk is assessed as Medium.
Basis: 10-K_2026-03-31_nvl-20260331.htm.jsonl, pages 190 and 188; 10-Q_2026-09-30_nvl-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 61

### Key findings

- Parent support is demonstrated but not committed. Hindalco subscribed USD 750 million of shares in December 2025 and USD 200 million in February 2026, USD 950 million in all, after the Oswego fires began, yet no guarantee, keepwell or support agreement for Novelis debt is stated, and the quarterly report warns that similar funding may not be made available in future. Equity paid in lowers the refinancing risk of the Bay Minette build; the absence of a commitment means that support is a pattern, not a claim.
  Basis: 10-K_2026-03-31_nvl-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 142; 10-Q_2026-09-30_nvl-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 61; calculation C01
- The business is diversified by region and end market but concentrated by customer. Beverage packaging was 54.45% of fiscal 2026 net sales and 56.14% of the first half of fiscal 2027, Ball was 18% of net sales, and the four regions contributed 26.43%, 23.03%, 18.89% and 31.65% of segment Adjusted EBITDA in fiscal 2026. Pricing passes aluminum costs through, so earnings follow conversion margins, but each price rise lifts working capital.
  Basis: 10-K_2026-03-31_nvl-20260331.htm.jsonl, pages 188 and 187; 10-Q_2026-09-30_nvl-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 44 and 49; calculation C02; calculation C04
- Earnings recovery rests on two projects. The Oswego hot mill restarted in June 2026 and USD 519 million of net insurance recoveries had been received by September 30, 2026, against an issuer estimate in May 2026 of about USD 1.7 billion of negative cash flow before recoveries. North America fell from 35.61% of segment Adjusted EBITDA in fiscal 2025 to 26.43% in fiscal 2026 and 25.02% in the first half of fiscal 2027, and Bay Minette, which produced its first cold mill coil in September 2026, expects commercial shipments in the first quarter of fiscal 2028.
  Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_nvl-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 36 and 48; 10-K_2026-03-31_nvl-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 70; EX-99.1_2026-11-05_novelisq2fy27results.htm.jsonl, pages 1–2 and 10; calculation C03
- Capital allocation is investment-led, with small and discretionary distributions. Capital expenditure guidance for fiscal 2027 is USD 2.1 billion to USD 2.4 billion, of which USD 1,543 million was spent in the first half; returns of capital to the shareholder were USD 35 million in the first quarter of fiscal 2026, USD 100 million in fiscal 2024 and none in fiscal 2025 or the first half of fiscal 2027. The framework aims to return 8% to 10% of post-maintenance adjusted free cash flow, at the Board's discretion and subject to the debt instruments.
  Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_nvl-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 48 and 65; 10-K_2026-03-31_nvl-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 142; 10-Q_2026-09-30_nvl-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 4–5 and 16
- Financing has been extended in size and tenor, but near-term liquidity still leans on the ABL Revolver. New funding since June 2025 includes USD 750 million of 2033 notes, USD 725 million of tax-exempt bonds, a USD 500 million term loan in July 2026 and USD 950 million of equity, and the ABL Revolver commitment rose by USD 1 billion in two steps to USD 3.0 billion with maturity moved to June 2031.
  Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_nvl-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 18–23; calculation C01; calculation C06; calculation C07; calculation C08
- Governance is simple and transparent but not independent of the parent. All eight directors were appointed by Hindalco, three have Hindalco ties that the company treats as related-party relationships, the two standing committees are Audit and Compensation, and the auditor has served since 2006. Related-party transactions are not material, and the new brand licence caps the royalty at USD 24 million for fiscal 2027.
  Basis: 10-K_2026-03-31_nvl-20260331.htm.jsonl, pages 190, 201, 204 and 109; 10-Q_2026-09-30_nvl-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 16
- About a fifth of sales sits outside the guarantor group. Non-guarantor subsidiaries were 20% of net sales and 18% of Adjusted EBITDA for the first half of fiscal 2027 and 11% of assets, with USD 2.2 billion of debt and other liabilities; the guarantor list itself is not supplied.
  Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_nvl-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 64–65

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against treating Hindalco's equity as a durable backstop is that a strategic parent has no stated obligation to Novelis creditors, took a USD 35 million return of capital in the first quarter of fiscal 2026, and that fiscal 2025 carried USD 10 million of costs for a postponed initial public offering, which shows a listing has been considered. Customer concentration is a second counterweight, with Ball at 18% of net sales and one supplier at 6% of metal purchases. The view would improve with a documented guarantee or support undertaking or a further equity injection, and it would worsen with a distribution or listing that takes capital out while net leverage of 4.7x is above the issuer's 3.5x target, or with commercial shipments from Bay Minette slipping past the first quarter of fiscal 2028.
Basis: 10-K_2026-03-31_nvl-20260331.htm.jsonl, pages 142, 173 and 188; 10-Q_2026-09-30_nvl-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 65; EX-99.1_2026-11-05_novelisq2fy27results.htm.jsonl, pages 2–3

### Limitations

- The executed indentures, credit agreements and guarantee documents were not supplied, so the restricted-payment and guarantee terms that show how much value the parent could take out are not established; this limits the Medium risk level, which rests on behaviour, not legal capacity.
  Basis: 10-K_2026-03-31_nvl-20260331.htm.jsonl, pages 206–208
- No agency rating, market price or support agreement exists in the set for this strategic parent, so willingness is read only from disclosed actions; a documented undertaking could move the level either way.
- The list of subsidiaries and the guarantor group are not supplied, so the guarantor perimeter rests on percentages and the structural reading of who owes what to whom is incomplete.
  Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_nvl-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 64–65
- No single transaction is declared in the run settings, so the transaction summary lists the financings and equity contributions the filings disclose, not one deal with sources and uses.

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

The run settings declare no single transaction, so this section lists the financings and equity contributions the filings disclose. New debt since June 2025 totals USD 1,975 million: USD 400 million of Series 2025A Bonds in June 2025, USD 750 million of 6.375% senior notes due 2033 on August 18, 2025, USD 100 million of Series 2025B Bonds in September 2025, USD 225 million of Series 2026A Bonds in March 2026 and a USD 500 million unsecured term loan on July 23, 2026. The three tax-exempt bond series, USD 725 million in all, were loaned to Novelis Corporation to finance Bay Minette and are guaranteed on a senior unsecured basis by Novelis Inc. and certain subsidiaries.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_nvl-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 18–23; calculation C06; calculation C08

The 2033 notes were issued with a cash tender offer for the 2026 notes, which bought USD 737 million of the USD 750 million outstanding and left USD 13 million to mature on November 15, 2026. The term loan was repriced on September 15, 2025, lowering its margin from 2.00% to 1.75%, and the ABL Revolver commitment was raised on February 19, 2026 and June 16, 2026 to USD 3.0 billion, with maturity moved to June 16, 2031. On the equity side, Hindalco paid USD 750 million on December 23, 2025 and USD 200 million on February 18, 2026 for new shares, which left ownership unchanged.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_nvl-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 18–21; 10-K_2026-03-31_nvl-20260331.htm.jsonl, pages 142 and 150; calculation C01

A new brand and intellectual property licence with Birla Group Holdings Private Limited was approved on August 4, 2026 and took effect on July 1, 2026, with fiscal 2027 royalties capped at USD 24 million and USD 11 million recognised in the second quarter.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_nvl-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 16
