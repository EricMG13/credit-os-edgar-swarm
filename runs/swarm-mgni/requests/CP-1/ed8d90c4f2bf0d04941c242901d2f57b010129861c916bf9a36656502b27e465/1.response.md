## Findings

### Bottom line

Magnite is lightly levered at September 30, 2026: total debt of USD 349.95 million is 1.76x LTM reported EBITDA, cash exceeds debt, and the term loan balloon is not due until February 2031, so the main creditor exposure is earnings quality and seller float.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_mgni-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 17; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mgni-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 2; calculation C07

Reported EBITDA is derived from the statements because the issuer states only an adjusted figure; on the adjusted basis leverage is 1.25x and interest cover is 10.98x, against 7.79x on the reported basis, and stock-based compensation is most of the difference. Operating cash flow includes a working-capital inflow and seller payables exceed receivables, so not all of the cash balance is free liquidity.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-11-04_a991-earningsq32026.htm.jsonl, page 13; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mgni-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 15; calculation C08; calculation C09; calculation C15

### Leverage and cover

Leverage has fallen sharply because debt was repaid and EBITDA grew. Total debt over reported EBITDA was 4.94x at the end of FY2024, 3.88x at the end of FY2025 and is 1.76x on the last twelve months to September 30, 2026; on issuer-adjusted EBITDA the ratios are 2.81x, 2.40x and 1.25x. Net debt of USD 70.525 million at the end of FY2024 became USD 2.75 million at the end of FY2025 and net cash of USD 42.912 million at September 30, 2026, because free cash flow exceeded share repurchases and the taxes paid on share settlement. The repayment of the USD 205.067 million convertible notes in March 2026 used cash and debt in equal amounts, so it lowered gross leverage without materially changing net debt, and the FY2025 total debt figure includes USD 204.815 million of notes that no longer exist. Interest cover on reported EBITDA improved from 3.04x in FY2024 to 5.09x in FY2025 and 7.79x on the last twelve months, as EBITDA grew and cash paid for interest fell after the term loan margin was cut in September 2024 and March 2025.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_mgni-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 120; 10-K_2025-12-31_mgni-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 148; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mgni-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mgni-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 17; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mgni-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 19; calculation C07; calculation C09; calculation C25; calculation C26; calculation C27

### Cash-flow quality and the seller float

Operating cash flow of USD 297.209 million over the last twelve months includes USD 63.491 million of working-capital inflow, so funds from operations before working capital are USD 233.718 million, and free cash flow is USD 160.593 million before working-capital changes against USD 224.084 million after them. The working-capital line is driven by seller payables, which are the largest liability on the balance sheet at USD 1,689,794 thousand at September 30, 2026 against receivables of USD 1,432,233 thousand, so cash less the excess of payables to sellers over receivables is USD 135,301 thousand. The excess was USD 217,734 thousand at the end of FY2024, USD 270,148 thousand at the end of FY2025 and USD 257,561 thousand at September 30, 2026, so the float is large and broadly stable; the issuer states that it may still have to pay sellers if a buyer defaults, so a buyer default or slow collection would draw on cash before it reduces revenue.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_mgni-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 15; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mgni-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 51; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mgni-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 5; calculation C13; calculation C18; calculation C20; calculation C21; calculation C19; calculation C37; calculation C36; calculation C35; calculation C38

### Liquidity and maturities

Liquidity is ample against near-term obligations: cash of USD 392.862 million and USD 170.9 million available under the USD 175.0 million revolver give USD 563.762 million against debt of USD 349.95 million, and the revolver was drawn and repaid for USD 60.0 million within the second quarter of 2026 without leaving a balance. Scheduled term loan amortization is USD 3.632 million a year, the balloon of USD 342.294 million is due in February 2031 and the revolver commitment ends in February 2029, so no facility expires within twelve months. The only financial covenant is a springing first-lien net leverage test that applies when more than 35% of the revolver is used; the ratio is not printed, so headroom is not calculated here.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_mgni-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mgni-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 17; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mgni-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 19; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mgni-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 20; 10-Q_2026-06-30_mgni-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 18; calculation C28

### Subsequent and multi-figure events

Two events sit outside the period figures. On October 7, 2026, after the balance sheet date, Amendment No. 3 cut the term loan margin by 50 bp to Term SOFR plus 2.50% and lowered the revolver margin range to 2.50% to 3.00%; it changes cash interest, not debt or cash, and is not blended into any period figure. The 0.25% convertible notes matured on March 15, 2026, and that repayment has several figures with different roles: principal paid of USD 205.067 million in the cash flow statement, net carrying value of USD 204.815 million at the prior year-end on the balance sheet, and an estimated fair value of USD 199.2 million at that date. Carrying value serves the leverage figures, principal serves the cash flow and the maturity history, and fair value serves no ratio.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_mgni-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 29; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mgni-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 30; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mgni-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 5; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mgni-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 18; 10-K_2025-12-31_mgni-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 148; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mgni-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 14

### Definitions and data quality

EBITDA is the main definitional risk. The issuer states only Adjusted EBITDA, so reported EBITDA is calculated from income before tax, net interest and depreciation and amortization; the issuer's add-backs total USD 81.541 million over the last twelve months, 90.31% of which is stock-based compensation, so the adjusted ratios are materially better than the reported ones. Total debt uses carrying value of USD 349.95 million, USD 7.78 million below principal, and USD 61.994 million of lease liabilities sit on their own lines outside debt. No pension, retiree, factoring or supplier finance obligation is disclosed in any source. Cash paid for interest of USD 25.549 million exceeds net interest expense of USD 19.696 million because net expense is after USD 9.251 million of interest income.

## Gaps

- Where a statement prints a dash, or a line exists in only one of the statements (merger costs and doubtful accounts in the annual statements, the revolver and convertible note lines in the 2026 cash flow statement, the loss on extinguishment of debt in the 2026 columns), the cell reads Not supplied rather than zero. This restricts only line-by-line arithmetic in those columns, not any total. Severity: Low. Action: none unless the issuer states the lines as zero.
- The cash flow statement for the third quarter alone is not presented in the quarterly report, so the cash flow table has nine-month columns only and quarterly cash generation cannot be separated. Severity: Low. Action: use the nine-month columns or the quarterly releases.
- The fourth quarter of 2025 is not presented on its own; its revenue and Adjusted EBITDA are calculated as the full year less nine months and labelled Calculated. Severity: Low. Action: obtain the fourth-quarter 2025 release.
- Balance sheets at March 31, 2026 and June 30, 2026 are in the quarterly reports but are not tabulated, so the debt and liquidity series has three balance-sheet dates. Severity: Low. Action: tabulate them if a quarterly net debt path is needed.
- The issuer states no unadjusted EBITDA, so EBITDA (Reported) is calculated for every period and every reported-basis ratio rests on that derivation. Severity: Low. Action: none; the components are audited or reported line items.
- Maintenance and growth capital expenditure are not split in any source; only total capital expenditure (property and equipment plus capitalized software) is stated. Severity: Low. Action: none unless the issuer discloses the split.
- The issuer's reported First Lien Net Leverage Ratio is not printed, and the covenant is worded as not greater than 3.25 to 1.00 in the risk factors and below 3.25 to 1.00 in the debt note, so covenant headroom is not calculable. - The issuer's Adjusted EBITDA add-backs have no stated realisation status and no cost-saving or run-rate items are disclosed; dashes in the 2026 reconciliations are taken as nil only in the EBITDA bridge, because the issuer's reconciliation foots to its stated total only with nil.
