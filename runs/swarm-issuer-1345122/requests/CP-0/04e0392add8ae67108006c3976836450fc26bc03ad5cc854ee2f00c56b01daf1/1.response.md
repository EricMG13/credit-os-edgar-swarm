## Findings

### Bottom line

The 12 supplied documents support a credit snapshot as of 2026-11-18 with limitations: the original 2024 annual report is superseded by a restatement, and no executed debt documents, ratings or market prices were supplied, so covenant conclusions rest on issuer summaries.

Current-period evidence is the strongest part of the set: the third-quarter 2026 quarterly report and earnings release, dated 14 days before the cutoff, give full statements, the debt note with its covenant test and the liquidity disclosure, and the totals checked agree with the 2025 annual report and the third-quarter 2025 report.

### Key findings

- The issuer is the operating company beneath a listed trust and files jointly with it. The trust only owns the company, so the consolidated statements printed under the trust's name are the issuer's consolidated statements. No company-only statements, parent-only schedule or subsidiary guarantor information were found, and the senior notes are described as unsecured obligations not guaranteed by subsidiaries.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_compass-group-diversified-holdings-llc.htm.jsonl, page 13; 10-K_2025-12-31_compass-group-diversified-holdings-llc.htm.jsonl, page 29; calculation C12
- The original 2024 annual report is not a safe source for 2024 values. It printed net revenues of USD 2,198.23 million and total assets of USD 4,052.42 million, against USD 1,788.01 million and USD 3,297.42 million in the restated comparatives of the 2025 annual report, so every 2024 value should come from the restated comparatives.
Basis: 10-K_2024-12-31_compass-group-diversified-holdings-llc.htm.jsonl, pages 226–227; 10-K_2025-12-31_compass-group-diversified-holdings-llc.htm.jsonl, pages 225–226; calculation C3; calculation C4
- The 2025 annual report carries an unqualified opinion on the financial statements and an adverse opinion on internal control, and management states that the control weaknesses were not fully remediated at the end of the third quarter of 2026, so the figures are usable but carry elevated reporting risk.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_compass-group-diversified-holdings-llc.htm.jsonl, pages 221–223; 10-Q_2026-09-30_compass-group-diversified-holdings-llc.htm.jsonl, page 112
- Quarterly headline lines for 2024 and 2025 (revenues, gross profit, operating income and net loss) are printed in the unaudited quarterly note of the 2025 annual report, and the fourth quarter of 2025 agrees with the full year less the nine months; first-half 2025 cash flows, balance sheets and segment detail are not available on their own because the first- and second-quarter 2025 reports and any fourth-quarter release are not in the set.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_compass-group-diversified-holdings-llc.htm.jsonl, pages 315–316; 10-K_2025-12-31_compass-group-diversified-holdings-llc.htm.jsonl, page 226; 10-Q_2025-09-30_compass-group-diversified-holdings-llc.htm.jsonl, page 7; calculation C6; calculation C14
- Debt amounts can be read from the debt note by instrument, and management's headline debt figure is the principal, not the balance-sheet carrying value, so the two must be kept apart when net debt is built.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_compass-group-diversified-holdings-llc.htm.jsonl, page 30; EX99.1_2026-09-30_compass-group-diversified-holdings-llc_exhibit991earningsreleaseq-htm.htm.jsonl, page 2; calculation C7; calculation C8; calculation C9
- Governance evidence is thin: directors, officers, pay and ownership are incorporated by reference to a proxy statement that is not in the set, so Business & governance must rest on the business description, the manager agreement summary and the related-party notes.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_compass-group-diversified-holdings-llc.htm.jsonl, page 209; 10-Q_2026-09-30_compass-group-diversified-holdings-llc.htm.jsonl, page 48
- None of the 12 documents names a rating agency, and the debt documents are described only in the issuer's own notes, so ratings triggers, security terms and covenant definitions cannot be checked against governing text.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_compass-group-diversified-holdings-llc.htm.jsonl, pages 27–30; calculation C11
