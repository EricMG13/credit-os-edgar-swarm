## Findings

### Bottom line

Net debt of USD 1,434.11 million at 2026-09-30 is 5.61x last-twelve-months reported EBITDA and 5.11x adjusted EBITDA, down from 8.48x adjusted EBITDA at 2025-12-31, so creditors still face a high multiple that is falling because disposal proceeds repaid debt and Lugano's losses left the perimeter.

The reported EBITDA denominator of USD 255.52 million is flattered by a USD 178.00 million gain on the Sterno food service sale and depressed by a USD 57.70 million fair-value loss, which the issuer's adjusted measure of USD 280.77 million removes. Interest cover cannot be calculated for the last twelve months because nine-month cash interest is not printed; on FY2025 adjusted EBITDA it was 1.59x.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_compass-group-diversified-holdings-llc.htm.jsonl, page 7; 10-Q_2026-09-30_compass-group-diversified-holdings-llc.htm.jsonl, page 8; calculation C7; calculation C12; calculation C13; calculation C24; calculation C30

### Key findings

- Net debt fell by USD 375.20 million between 2025-12-31 and 2026-09-30, from USD 1,809.30 million to USD 1,434.11 million. Carrying debt fell by USD 316.05 million to USD 1,561.27 million because USD 319.00 million of term loan was repaid from disposal proceeds, while cash rose to USD 127.17 million. The only secured debt left is the term loan of USD 233.50 million, so the unsecured notes now carry most of the capital structure.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_compass-group-diversified-holdings-llc.htm.jsonl, pages 7–11; 10-Q_2026-09-30_compass-group-diversified-holdings-llc.htm.jsonl, pages 29–30; calculation C8; calculation C9; calculation C10

The covenant EBITDA is defined in a credit agreement that was not supplied, so the gap between the two measures is not reconciled and covenant headroom is not calculated here.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_compass-group-diversified-holdings-llc.htm.jsonl, pages 29–30; calculation C1

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest contrary evidence is the issuer's own covenant ratio of 4.53x and senior secured leverage of 0.48x, which sit well inside the 5.75x and 2.50x limits and imply more headroom than the house multiple of 5.56x suggests. Sight of the credit agreement showing a covenant EBITDA that adds pro forma disposals or other items would move the view toward the issuer's figure, while a further drop in adjusted EBITDA or a failed disposal would move it the other way.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_compass-group-diversified-holdings-llc.htm.jsonl, page 30; calculation C1
