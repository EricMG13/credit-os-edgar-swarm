## Findings

### Bottom line

The seven supplied filings and releases support financial, business and fundamental credit work on Murphy Oil Corporation through the third quarter of 2026, with limitations; they cannot support covenant, structural or legal conclusions for creditors.

The evidence is current to the day before the as-of date, and the debt and liquidity inputs tie to each other across documents. Four source conflicts stay open for the next modules to carry rather than settle: the coupon of the 2042 notes, the label on total debt, the perimeter of the issuer's earnings measures, and the 2025 comparatives in the segment note. The executed indentures and credit agreement, any credit rating, and the 2026 proxy statement are not supplied, so the guarantee and security status of the notes, covenant definitions and headroom, and board independence are not established.
Basis: calculation C1; calculation C2; calculation C4; 10-K_2025-12-31_mur-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 83–85; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mur-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 14

### Key findings

Murphy Oil Corporation is both the registrant and the issuer of the notes, so its consolidated statements are the issuer's own and no parent or subsidiary perimeter has to be split out. The one perimeter difference is the noncontrolling interest in the Gulf of America joint venture: the issuer's earnings measures exclude it, while its consolidated balance sheet, cash and liquidity totals include it, so a ratio built from the issuer's earnings measure and its consolidated debt would mix two perimeters.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_mur-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 1, 4–5, 83–84, 93, 94; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mur-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 49; EX-99.1_2026-11-04_mur-2026q3xex991.htm.jsonl, pages 1–2

The evidence runs from the audited 2025 annual report to the quarterly report for the period ended September 30, 2026, signed on November 4, 2026, one day before the as-of date. Nothing supplied is dated after the as-of date. The post-quarter items that matter to creditors, a drillship agreement signed in October 2026, new exploration blocks, a Mauritania agreement and amended by-laws, are disclosed in documents signed on or before November 4, 2026.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_mur-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 32, 34, 52, 57; EX-99.1_2026-11-04_mur-2026q3xex991.htm.jsonl, page 2

Debt and liquidity inputs are printed in every filing and agree with the balance sheet: the debt note, counting the revolver line printed beneath its total, equals current plus long-term debt on the balance sheet at year-end 2025 and at September 30, 2026, the notes table sums to its printed total, and the issuer's liquidity statement reproduces from cash, the undrawn revolver and the letters of credit drawn on it. Series-level debt tables are printed only at those two dates; the March and June 2026 filings describe the same refinancing in narrative.
Basis: calculation C1; calculation C2; calculation C3; calculation C4; 10-K_2025-12-31_mur-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 112; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mur-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 13–14, 47

Four conflicts are open and are carried forward unsettled. The coupon of the 2042 notes is printed three ways, and the issuer's own weighted average coupons point to a change between the first and second quarters of 2026 that no document explains. The line labelled total debt in the debt note leaves out the revolver printed beneath it, so it differs from the balance sheet at year-end 2025. The 2025 comparatives in the segment note differ slightly from the statement of operations. The issuer's earnings measures and its debt use different perimeters.
Basis: calculation C5; calculation C6; calculation C8; 10-K_2025-12-31_mur-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 83, 112; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mur-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 13–14

The executed indentures and credit agreement, any credit rating, market prices, peer data, the 2026 proxy statement and the list of subsidiaries are not supplied. The guarantee and security status of the notes, covenant definitions and headroom, board independence and ownership concentration therefore cannot be established. The annual report summarises the revolver's covenant tests only for the earlier agreement and does not say whether the investment grade ratings date has occurred; the amended revolver is described as substantially similar.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_mur-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 81–85, 113; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mur-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 14

Legal & covenants, Covenant capacity, Structural priority, Intercreditor control, Recovery & fulcrum, Security selection and Market-implied risk depend on documents or prices the run does not hold and are not requested in this run.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest argument against ready with limitations is the notes table itself: two debt tables disagree on the coupon of one series, and a reader who took the note's total-debt line at face value would understate year-end 2025 debt. The balance-sheet ties and the issuer's printed weighted average coupons limit that risk, because they show which reading each table supports. The view would weaken if the executed indentures contradict the filings' description of the notes, and it would strengthen if a supplemental indenture or rating notice explains the 2042 coupon.
