## Findings

### Bottom line

Murphy Oil is a publicly listed producer with no sponsor or controlling holder named in the supplied documents, so creditors' risk lies in oil price exposure, offshore concentration and shareholder-return policy, not in owner conduct. Oil supplied 86.07% of sales revenue in the nine months to September 30, 2026 and offshore fields 58.97%, while dividends of USD 150.5 million took 75.40% of free cash flow and adjusted free cash flow, after dividends, minority distributions and acquisitions, was negative USD 32.2 million.
Basis: calculation C2; calculation C3; calculation C6; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mur-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 8–9, 50

Governance quality is thinly evidenced, because the proxy statement and the executed debt documents are not supplied, so board independence, large holders and any subsidiary guarantee of the notes are not established.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_mur-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 81–82, 84–85

### Key findings

The one ownership fact that moves creditor risk is the 20% minority in the Gulf of America venture, which shares in the venture's earnings and takes cash out of it. The minority's share of nine-month net income is 13.54%, and its distributions ran at 1.50x its earnings in 2024, 1.85x in 2025 and 0.30x in the first nine months of 2026, when they were USD 21.16 million. Murphy reports 100% of the venture, so consolidated cash and liquidity include value that belongs to the minority while the issuer's earnings measures exclude it, and the documents do not name the minority holder.
Basis: calculation C8; 10-K_2025-12-31_mur-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 83, 96–97; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mur-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 5, 37

The business is oil-weighted and concentrated, so cash flow follows one price and a few hubs. Oil was 86.07% of nine-month 2026 sales revenue against 82.81% in 2025, offshore fields supplied 58.97% against 61.21%, and ten fields give about 91% of Gulf of America output, which is about 65% of U.S. output. Chevron took 19% and ExxonMobil 12% of 2025 sales, no oil derivative was open at September 30, 2026, so price falls reach cash flow directly, and the Gulf of America storm season runs from June to November.
Basis: calculation C1; calculation C2; calculation C3; calculation C9; calculation C14; 10-K_2025-12-31_mur-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 5, 27, 140; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mur-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 8–9, 55

Earnings strength in 2026 comes from price, not volume. Third-quarter production was 7% below a year earlier on natural declines in the Gulf of America and the timing of new Eagle Ford wells, while WTI averaged USD 85.79 a barrel against USD 64.93 and the forward curve on November 2, 2026 stood at USD 96.46 for the rest of 2026 and USD 86.12 for 2027. Management raised full-year production guidance, but a return of oil toward the USD 66.70 average of the first nine months of 2025 would remove the cushion with lower volumes.
Basis: calculation C5; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mur-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 35, 38, 50, 52

Financial policy favours shareholders over debt reduction, and management says so. The dividend was raised 7.69% to USD 0.35 per share in January 2026, nothing has been repurchased since USD 100.0 million was bought in the first quarter of 2025 with USD 550 million still authorised, and the issuer states that adjusted free cash flow is more likely to go to repurchases than to bond repayment. Gross debt rose USD 249.27 million, or 18.00%, in nine months, to USD 1,634.35 million, because the 2034 notes added USD 272.51 million net of redemptions and the Vietnam finance lease USD 82.07 million, partly offset by the USD 100.0 million revolver repayment. Performance share awards vest 80% on relative shareholder return and 20% on return on capital employed, with no leverage measure, so leverage will fall through earnings and not through repayment.
Basis: calculation C6; calculation C7; 10-K_2025-12-31_mur-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 77, 121; 10-Q_2026-03-31_mur-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 24; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mur-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 27, 53

Fixed commitments are building ahead of cash flow. A finance lease for the Vietnam storage vessel added a USD 83.9 million liability running to 2039, a drillship agreement signed in October 2026 commits USD 225 million to USD 325 million gross from 2027 and is not yet on the balance sheet, and capitalised exploratory costs awaiting a reserves decision rose from USD 191.8 million to USD 494.1 million, an increase of 157.60% that carries dry-hole and impairment risk. Nine-month capital spending took 86.98% of operating cash flow before working capital, and full-year capital spending is guided at USD 1,500 million to USD 1,600 million.
Basis: calculation C10; calculation C12; calculation C13; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mur-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 11, 32, 50, 52

The notes' place in the structure cannot be read from the filings, and the evidence points to a possible gap. The revolver has three named borrowers and two subsidiary guarantors, while each indenture in the exhibit index is between Murphy Oil Corporation and a trustee alone. If the notes carry no subsidiary guarantee, noteholders would rank behind revolver lenders at the operating subsidiaries that hold the assets; this is an unverified reading that the executed documents would settle.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_mur-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 4, 84–85

Reported governance shows nothing adverse, but it is thinly evidenced. The audit committee is made up only of non-employee directors, internal control over financial reporting was effective at the end of 2025, no restatement required recoupment, and the chief executive in office since January 2025 was promoted from President and Chief Operating Officer. The Board amended its By-Laws on November 4, 2026 and the text is not supplied, so the effect on creditors is not established.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest case against a benign view of owner conduct is that distributions already exceed what the business keeps after investment: dividends of USD 150.5 million took 75.40% of free cash flow, adjusted free cash flow after distributions and acquisitions was negative USD 32.2 million, management prefers repurchases to bond repayment, and the Gulf of America minority drew more than it earned in 2024 and 2025. If oil fell back toward USD 66.70, those payments would come from cash or the revolver while exploration and drillship spending continue. The view would weaken if the proxy statement shows a large holder or an insider-heavy board, or if the debt documents allow wide restricted payments and the notes lack subsidiary guarantees. It would strengthen if the documents limit distributions as leverage rises, guarantee the notes alongside the revolver and show an independent board.
Basis: calculation C6; calculation C8; 10-K_2025-12-31_mur-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 84–85, 96; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mur-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 38, 50, 53

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

The only new debt issue in the period is USD 500.0 million of 6.50% senior notes due February 15, 2034, closed on January 23, 2026 with USD 8.3 million of issuance costs. The proceeds redeemed the remaining USD 78.9 million of 2027 notes and USD 148.6 million of 2028 notes, USD 227.5 million in all, repaid the USD 100.0 million drawn on the revolver and covered fees, with the rest for general purposes, so notes principal rose a net USD 272.51 million and the nearest note maturity moved to 2029.
Basis: calculation C7; 10-K_2025-12-31_mur-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 77, 114–115; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mur-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 15

The USD 2.00 billion senior unsecured revolving credit facility was amended on January 2, 2026, raising capacity from USD 1.35 billion and extending maturity from October 2029 to January 2031. Three Murphy companies are named as borrowers and two subsidiaries as guarantors in the exhibit index, the margin spread adjustment fell from 0.10% to zero, USD 12.3 million of costs are being amortised, and at September 30, 2026 nothing was drawn and USD 0.4 million of letters of credit reduced capacity. Covenant levels after the amendment are not established, because the executed credit agreement is not supplied.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_mur-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 85, 113–114; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mur-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 14; Source readiness (CP-0)

Two commitments sit outside the debt figure. The Vietnam storage vessel finance lease began in September 2026 and runs to October 2039, matching the production sharing contract, with a USD 83.9 million liability after USD 19.5 million was paid before commencement. A drillship agreement signed in October 2026 covers ten wells at USD 225 million to USD 325 million gross for the initial term, with options for up to five more, and starts in the first quarter of 2027, so nothing was recognised at September 30, 2026. In the first quarter of 2025 Murphy bought the Pioneer production vessel for USD 125.0 million, and the seller runs it under a five-year contract.
Basis: calculation C10; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mur-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 13–14, 32

Murphy signed a Petroleum Agreement in January 2026 for an operated 75% interest in a Moroccan deepwater block with no firm wells in its first three years, secured six Gulf of America blocks in the August federal lease sale, signed two Mauritania blocks after the quarter and is finalising four in Cameroon, with costs not stated. Acquisitions of oil and gas properties were USD 28.6 million in the nine months of 2026 against USD 24.4 million a year earlier, and the 2025 Eagle Ford purchases and minor sale carry no stated consideration. No acquisition of a business or change of control appears in the documents.
