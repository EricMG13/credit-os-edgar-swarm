## Findings

### Bottom line

The seven supplied documents support the requested credit snapshot of Service Properties Trust, with limitations: no executed debt agreements, ratings or earnings presentation were supplied, so creditors should treat covenant headroom beyond the issuer's printed indenture tests as unverified.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_svc-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 158–166; 10-Q_2026-09-30_svc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 51; EX-99.1_2026-11-04_svc_093026xexhibitx991.htm.jsonl, page 1

The 2025 annual report and the three 2026 quarterly reports give audited and interim statements, debt maturities, indenture covenant tests and liquidity disclosures through 30 September 2026, so the financial, business and fundamental credit work can proceed. The three earnings releases carry no financial tables. The USD 650 million revolving credit facility matures on June 29, 2027, 236 days after the as-of date, and the one-for-five reverse share split of July 2026 means per-share figures in the annual report and first quarter report are not comparable with the later filings.
