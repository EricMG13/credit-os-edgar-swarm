## Findings

### Bottom line

The seven supplied filings and results releases support a credit snapshot of Centene as at September 30, 2026, with limitations: no executed credit agreement or indenture was supplied, so creditor protections rest on the issuer's own summaries.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_cnc-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 129–132; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 8; 10-K_2025-12-31_cnc-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 179–181

In October 2026 the remaining 2027 senior notes of USD 569 million were redeemed, which the latest quarterly report states only as a later event, so debt at quarter end and after it must not be blended.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_cnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 21; EX-99.1_2026-10-27_a20261027ex991pressrelease.htm.jsonl, page 3

No agency rating, issuer EBITDA, market quote, peer set or portfolio limit appears in any source, so Financial foundation must derive earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortization from the statements, and ratings, relative-value and portfolio-fit work cannot rest on these documents.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_cnc-20251231.htm.jsonl; 10-Q_2026-03-31_cnc-20260331.htm.jsonl; 10-Q_2026-06-30_cnc-20260630.htm.jsonl; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cnc-20260930.htm.jsonl; EX-99.1_2026-04-28_a20260428ex991pressrelease.htm.jsonl; EX-99.1_2026-07-28_a20260728ex991pressrelease.htm.jsonl; EX-99.1_2026-10-27_a20261027ex991pressrelease.htm.jsonl

The annual report and the later quarterly reports state three December 31, 2025 balance sheet lines differently, each by USD 22 million; the quarterly reports say only that certain 2025 amounts were reclassified, with no effect on net earnings or equity.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_cnc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 129; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 8; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 12; calculation C4; calculation C5; calculation C6
