## Findings

### Bottom line

Centene is a large, government-dependent managed care franchise whose credit risk comes from state contract and federal rule changes rather than ownership, because no controlling shareholder is disclosed and 2026 financial policy has favoured debt reduction over distributions.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_cnc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 91; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 21

Medicaid is 52.49% and Medicare 24.34% of premium and service revenues for the nine months of 2026, and managed care membership has fallen 7.00% since year end 2025, with Marketplace membership down 38.58%, so state procurement outcomes and federal subsidy and Star ratings rules move revenue directly.
Basis: calculation K1; calculation K2; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 37–38

Directors, compensation and ownership sit in a proxy statement that was not supplied, so governance risk is rated Medium on the filings alone, and a securities class action over 2025 guidance and a change of principal accounting officer in 2026 are open questions the sources do not resolve.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_cnc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 178; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 27; 10-K_2025-12-31_cnc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 182; 10-Q_2026-03-31_cnc-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 42

### Key findings

- Revenue is concentrated in state and federal programs, and contract outcomes are moving against Centene in 2026 and 2027. Medicaid supplies 52.49% of premium and service revenues, the Florida children's medical services contract ended in September 2026, certain Hawaii Medicaid contracts were not renewed, protests in Georgia and Texas are unresolved and California moves its Medi-Cal UIS population out of managed care from January 2027, so each loss removes premium.
  Basis: calculation K1; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 37
- Marketplace volume has reset, which shifts the earnings mix toward Medicaid and Medicare. Marketplace membership fell 38.58% from year end 2025 to September 30, 2026, and the commercial business has the best health benefits ratio of the three programs for the nine months at 80.9%, so commercial premium is lower while the margin mix is better.
  Basis: calculation K2; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 38; EX-99.1_2026-10-27_a20261027ex991pressrelease.htm.jsonl, page 15
- Medicare Advantage Star ratings deteriorated, which lowers future premium. About 42% of Medicare Advantage members are in plans rated three and a half stars or higher in the October 2026 ratings against about 60% a year earlier, and the issuer says these ratings reach 2028 plan year revenues, so the revenue effect arrives in 2028.
  Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_cnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 38
- Financial policy in 2026 is creditor-aligned, but 2024 was not. Centene repurchased USD 1,847 million of par value senior notes in the nine months of 2026 and made only small stock repurchases, whereas in 2024 it repurchased USD 3,124 million of stock in a year when operating cash flow was USD 154 million and net proceeds from debt were USD 678 million, so the 2026 stance followed the 2025 loss and could reverse when earnings recover.
  Basis: calculation K3; calculation K4; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 21; 10-K_2025-12-31_cnc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 110
- Ownership gives creditors no sponsor to rely on or to fear. No controlling holder is disclosed, under one thousand holders of record held the shares in February 2026, and the proxy statement with director and ownership detail is not in the sources, so support from or extraction by a large owner cannot be assessed and the credit rests on the operating company.
  Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_cnc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 91; 10-K_2025-12-31_cnc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 178
- The receivable financing and divestiture programme outside the debt figure adds claims and liquidity dependence. The USD 1,000 million participating interest sold in March 2026 raised USD 970 million of cash, the purchase agreement can require Centene to repurchase sold receivables if certain events occur, and the remaining Magellan Health businesses with USD 312 million of held-for-sale assets are being sold, so liquidity depends on the CMS settlement expected in the fourth quarter of 2026 and on a sale that has not closed.
  Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_cnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 48; 10-K_2025-12-31_cnc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 177; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 13; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 49

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a Medium governance rating and a creditor-aligned policy is the 2024 buyback of USD 3,124 million that exceeded operating cash flow and was partly funded by net debt proceeds, together with a securities class action and five derivative suits over 2025 guidance and a different principal accounting officer signing the first quarterly report of 2026 than the annual report. A documented resumption of share repurchases or other distributions while leverage is above its 2024 level, an adverse ruling or large reserve in the securities litigation, or an explanation of the officer change that points to reporting problems would move the rating to High. Evidence that would move it to Low is a proxy statement showing independent oversight and no controlling holder, plus covenants in the executed documents that restrict distributions while debt is reduced.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_cnc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 110; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 27; 10-K_2025-12-31_cnc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 182; 10-Q_2026-03-31_cnc-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 42; calculation K3; calculation K4

### Limitations

- The proxy statement was not supplied, so the view that no controlling shareholder exists and the Medium governance rating rest on the holders-of-record count and cover data, and a disclosed large holder or board control right would change the ownership conclusion.
- No executed credit agreement or indenture was supplied, so the view that financial policy is creditor-aligned cannot be tested against the restricted payment and debt baskets, and a permissive basket would weaken it.
- The reasons for the change of principal accounting officer and the accruals for the securities and derivative litigation are not stated, so the contrary-evidence conclusion about reporting quality stays open.
- The quarterly figures are unaudited and the sale of the remaining Magellan Health businesses has no stated price or closing date, so the conclusion on the 2026 improvement in the health benefits ratio and on liquidity from disposals is provisional.
- Agency ratings, market prices and peer data were not supplied, so the business risk conclusions are not benchmarked against peers.
