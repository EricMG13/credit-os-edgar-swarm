## Findings

### Bottom line

Solaris has become a customer-concentrated power-leasing and services business whose growth is funded by acquisitions and debt, so creditors depend on a few data-center contracts starting on schedule and on integrating the services businesses it bought in 2026.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_sei.htm.jsonl, pages 14–15; 10-Q_2026-09-30_sei.htm.jsonl, page 58; 10-Q_2026-09-30_sei.htm.jsonl, page 63

Power Solutions made 75% of revenue and 80% of segment Adjusted EBITDA in the first nine months of 2026, one customer was 59% of third-quarter revenue and 67% of receivables, and no Stateline lease had started by September 30, 2026. Capital expenditure was USD 1,382.4 million in the period and the company sold USD 1.3 billion of senior notes in May and USD 1.25 billion in October 2026. The filings describe no controlling holder, and sponsor and governance risk is medium.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_sei.htm.jsonl, page 13; 10-Q_2026-09-30_sei.htm.jsonl, pages 14–15; 10-Q_2026-09-30_sei.htm.jsonl, page 42; 10-Q_2026-09-30_sei.htm.jsonl, page 58; 10-Q_2026-09-30_sei.htm.jsonl, page 63; 10-Q_2026-09-30_sei.htm.jsonl, page 69

### Key findings

- The credit is now mainly a power-leasing and services business, and its margin is thinning as the mix shifts. Power Solutions was 12% of revenue in FY2024, 54% in FY2025 and 75% in the first nine months of 2026, and its share of segment Adjusted EBITDA went from 22% to 68% to 80%. Its Adjusted EBITDA was 59% of its own revenue in the first nine months of 2025 and 52% in the same period of 2026, which fits the addition of services and construction businesses, though the filings do not split margin by business.
  Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_sei.htm.jsonl, page 67; 10-K_2025-12-31_sei.htm.jsonl, page 110; 10-Q_2026-09-30_sei.htm.jsonl, pages 14–15; 10-Q_2026-09-30_sei.htm.jsonl, page 69; calculation A10; calculation A12; calculation A16; calculation A17

- Customer concentration is the dominant operating risk, and the documents do not show how well it is protected. One customer was 59% of third-quarter revenue and 67% of receivables at September 30, 2026, and one data-center customer was 88% of Power Solutions revenue in FY2025. No Stateline lease had started by September 30, 2026 although the 2025 annual report expected revenue from that arrangement in 2026, and the USD 6,751.5 million of disclosed future lease payments includes leases that have not started. The first-signed Hatchbo contract let the customer end it on 30 days notice for a payment of 50% of remaining rent; a July 2026 amendment extended the tenor to up to 18 years, but the filings do not say whether the termination right remains. The credit agreement can force repayment or cut borrowing if certain Material Contracts end early, so a termination right matters for liquidity as well as revenue.
  Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_sei.htm.jsonl, page 13; 10-Q_2026-09-30_sei.htm.jsonl, page 38; 10-Q_2026-09-30_sei.htm.jsonl, page 44; 10-Q_2026-09-30_sei.htm.jsonl, page 58; 10-Q_2026-09-30_sei.htm.jsonl, page 68; 10-K_2025-12-31_sei.htm.jsonl, page 11; 10-K_2025-12-31_sei.htm.jsonl, page 138

- Acquisitions have changed the scale and risk of the company faster than the reporting can show. Reported nine-month revenue was USD 756.7 million, while the filing shows pro forma revenue of USD 1,249.4 million with GESA and Omega for the whole period, and no pro forma EBITDA or debt is printed. Employees rose to at least 4.49 times the year-end 2025 level, and the construction business Omega brought works predominantly under fixed-price contracts; GESA and Omega, 26% and 17% of third-quarter revenue, sit outside the management assessment of internal control, and a new Chief Accounting Officer starts on November 12, 2026.
  Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_sei.htm.jsonl, pages 14–15; 10-Q_2026-09-30_sei.htm.jsonl, page 22; 10-Q_2026-09-30_sei.htm.jsonl, pages 81–84; 10-K_2025-12-31_sei.htm.jsonl, page 12; calculation A2

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest case against a cautious view is that the largest customers are affiliates of investment-grade technology companies, 2026 added further large contracts in February, April and October, USD 6,751.5 million of future lease payments is disclosed, shares paid for much of each 2026 acquisition, and the company held USD 399.5 million of cash and USD 525.0 million of undrawn revolver availability at September 30, 2026, before the USD 1.25 billion issue. The view would improve if the Stateline leases start and the Hatchbo deployments begin in the first quarter of 2027 as planned, if pro forma EBITDA shows the acquired businesses earn their purchase prices, and if the executed agreements show limits that keep distributions and insider payments behind creditors. It would worsen if lease starts slip beyond that quarter, if a large customer uses a termination right, or if the acquired businesses show control weaknesses when they are first tested.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_sei.htm.jsonl, page 38; 10-Q_2026-09-30_sei.htm.jsonl, page 45; 10-Q_2026-09-30_sei.htm.jsonl, page 63; 10-Q_2026-09-30_sei.htm.jsonl, pages 67–68; 10-Q_2026-09-30_sei.htm.jsonl, page 77; 10-K_2025-12-31_sei.htm.jsonl, page 138

### Limitations

- The customer agreements are not in the documents read, so the finding that concentration is the dominant risk cannot be tested for termination rights, credit support or start dates; longer or better-protected contracts would lower that risk.
- The executed credit agreement and indentures were not supplied, so the medium governance rating rests on summaries and cannot separate what the company may do from what it has done; a tight restricted-payment limit would support a lower rating.
- Holder-level ownership and director independence sit in a proxy statement that was not supplied, and the Class B share calculation treats all Class B shares as one block, so the control finding could move once holder figures are known.
- No pro forma EBITDA is printed for Genco, GESA or Omega, so the finding that share funding limits the leverage added cannot be sized.
- Where the quarterly report is silent on the Hatchbo termination right after the July 2026 amendment, the contract durability finding stays open.
