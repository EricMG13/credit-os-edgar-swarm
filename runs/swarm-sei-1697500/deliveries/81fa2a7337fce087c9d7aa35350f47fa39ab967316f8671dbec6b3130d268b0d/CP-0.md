## Findings

### Bottom line

The five supplied SEC filings support the requested financial, business and fundamental-credit work, but not the instrument-selection and covenant questions in the full credit assessment, which need prices, executed documents and a portfolio the run does not carry.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_sei.htm.jsonl, pages 5–11; 10-K_2025-12-31_sei.htm.jsonl, pages 84–89

The issuer changed shape in 2026 through acquisitions in March, July and September. Debt at carrying value, excluding convertible notes, rose from USD 184.0 million at December 31, 2025 to USD 1,715.9 million at September 30, 2026, and the USD 1.25 billion senior notes issued on October 1, 2026 sit outside every statement, so each leverage or liquidity figure must state its date. No credit agreement, indenture, rating, market price or peer set was supplied, so the filings' own summaries are the only source for covenant levels and ranking.
