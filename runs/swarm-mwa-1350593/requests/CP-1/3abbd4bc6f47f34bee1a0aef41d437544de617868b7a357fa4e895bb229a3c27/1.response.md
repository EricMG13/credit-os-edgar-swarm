## Findings

### Bottom line

Mueller Water is in a net cash position at 2026-06-30, with USD 495.3 million of cash against USD 452.9 million of carrying-value debt, so creditors face low leverage and no material maturity until June 2029.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_mwa-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-06-30_mwa-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 15; calculation C5

On reported EBITDA of USD 337.2 million for the twelve months to that date, total debt is 1.34x and net debt is negative 0.13x; net debt is negative USD 42.4 million, or negative USD 31.5 million pro forma for the dividend declared on 2026-07-28. The issuer states only adjusted EBITDA, so reported EBITDA is calculated here, and cash interest paid is net of interest received.
Basis: calculation C29; calculation C5; calculation C39; EX-99.1_2026-08-05_exhibit991pressreleasefy26.htm.jsonl, page 5

### Key findings

Leverage has fallen because reported EBITDA grew while carrying-value debt stayed close to flat. Debt was USD 449.5 million at 2024-09-30 and USD 452.9 million at 2026-06-30, while reported EBITDA rose from USD 242.3 million in FY2024 to USD 307.7 million in FY2025 and USD 337.2 million for the twelve months to 2026-06-30. Total debt to reported EBITDA therefore fell from 1.86x to 1.47x and then 1.34x, and net debt to reported EBITDA fell from 0.58x to 0.07x and then negative 0.13x, as cash rose from USD 309.9 million to USD 431.5 million and then USD 495.3 million. On adjusted EBITDA of USD 368.5 million, built from the issuer's quarterly figures, the latest ratios are 1.23x and negative 0.12x, so reported EBITDA is the more conservative base. Pro forma for the dividend declared on 2026-07-28, net debt to reported EBITDA is negative 0.09x.
Basis: calculation C1; calculation C2; calculation C5; calculation C7; calculation C8; calculation C29; calculation C30; calculation C59

The capital structure is one USD 450.0 million 4.0% senior unsecured note due June 2029, guaranteed by substantially all U.S. subsidiaries and subordinate to an undrawn USD 175.0 million asset-based facility, plus small finance leases. Because 99.21% of scheduled principal falls in fiscal 2029, refinancing risk sits on one date, and the asset-based facility matures on 2029-03-16 while the notes remain outstanding, about three months earlier, so the liquidity backstop ends before the notes do. A refinancing therefore needs to be addressed before the facility expires, not only when the notes fall due.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_mwa-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 14; 10-Q_2026-06-30_mwa-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 15; 10-K_2025-09-30_mwa-20250930.htm.jsonl, pages 114–115; calculation C37; calculation C55

Cash interest paid is net of interest received, at USD 3.9 million for the twelve months to 2026-06-30, so the reported cover of 86.46x overstates protection. The notes carry USD 18.0 million of annual cash coupon, which reported EBITDA of USD 337.2 million covers 18.73x, the more useful measure of fixed-charge cover.
Basis: calculation C31; calculation C58; 10-Q_2026-06-30_mwa-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 36

Free cash flow, defined as operating cash flow less capital expenditures, was USD 172.0 million in FY2025 and USD 110.6 million in nine months 2026, but conversion of reported EBITDA fell to 43.42% from 45.74% a year earlier, because inventories absorbed USD 59.9 million and capital expenditures rose to USD 43.6 million from USD 32.8 million, mainly for iron foundries. Dividends and repurchases of USD 48.3 million in nine months 2026 were covered more than twice by free cash flow.
Basis: calculation C44; calculation C50; calculation C45; 10-Q_2026-06-30_mwa-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-Q_2026-06-30_mwa-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 35

Liquidity was USD 659.0 million at 2026-06-30: cash of USD 495.3 million plus USD 163.7 million of availability under the asset-based facility, which is net of USD 11.1 million of letters of credit. Of the cash, USD 116.6 million, or 23.54%, is held in Israel, Canada and China, where earnings are treated as permanently reinvested, so only part of the cash is readily available to the U.S. borrowers of the notes and the facility.
Basis: calculation C36; calculation C53; 10-Q_2026-06-30_mwa-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 32; 10-K_2025-09-30_mwa-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 60

Outside the debt figure sit operating lease liabilities of USD 28.4 million at 2025-09-30, letters of credit of USD 11.1 million, surety bonds of USD 13.2 million and a New Markets Tax Credit investor contribution of USD 4.8 million carried in other liabilities; the defined benefit pension plan, by contrast, is overfunded by USD 19.5 million at that date and is an asset. The environmental indemnity on the former U.S. Pipe site carries no accrual because its cost cannot be estimated, which leaves an unquantified claim outside debt.
Basis: calculation C57; calculation C56; 10-Q_2026-06-30_mwa-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 20; 10-Q_2026-06-30_mwa-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 21; 10-Q_2026-06-30_mwa-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 9–10

Two definition choices matter when comparing figures. The 2026-08-05 release quotes total debt of USD 452.9 million at carrying value while the debt note prints USD 454.9 million gross, a difference of USD 2.0 million of deferred financing costs, and the issuer's adjusted EBITDA adds back USD 22.0 million of nine-month charges that reported EBITDA keeps, so adjusted EBITDA runs higher than reported EBITDA by that amount in nine months 2026.
Basis: calculation C40; 10-Q_2026-06-30_mwa-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 13; EX-99.1_2026-08-05_exhibit991pressreleasefy26.htm.jsonl, page 5

The only post-balance-sheet event the documents state is a quarterly dividend declared on 2026-07-28 with an estimated cash outlay of USD 10.9 million, which moves net debt from negative USD 42.4 million to negative USD 31.5 million and net leverage from negative 0.13x to negative 0.09x; it is kept out of period figures.
Basis: calculation C39; 10-Q_2026-06-30_mwa-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 22; 10-Q_2026-06-30_mwa-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 36

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The net cash position is thinner than it looks: counting only cash held outside Israel, Canada and China, net debt is USD 74.2 million, or 0.22x reported EBITDA, because USD 116.6 million of the cash is held abroad. Free cash flow conversion also fell to 43.42% in nine months 2026 from 78.99% in FY2024. The view would weaken if the unnamed transaction behind the transaction-related expenses were funded with debt, if distributions rose above free cash flow, or if conversion kept falling while capital expenditures stayed near USD 43.6 million for nine months.
Basis: calculation C54; calculation C50; calculation C53
