## Findings

### Bottom line

Scotts Miracle-Gro's business is a seasonal branded franchise with two retailers at 52% of sales and about 23% family-partnership ownership, so creditor risk lies in concentration, the Roundup agency and governance transition rather than sponsor leverage.

Sponsor and governance risk is Medium: no extraction is documented and the company prepaid USD 250.0 million and USD 75.0 million of term loan in FY2024 and FY2025, but dividends of USD 116.3 million for nine months continue against thin free cash flow, the incoming chief executive is a general partner of the largest shareholder, and the Hawthorne exit left put and call options with partner-owned vehicles. Board, fee and related-party detail sits in a proxy statement that is not supplied.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_smg.htm.jsonl, pages 19, 49, 57, 136; 10-Q_2026-06-27_smg.htm.jsonl, pages 3, 12, 39

### Key findings

- Customer concentration and seasonality set the cash profile. The Home Depot and Lowe's together were 52% of FY2025 net sales and 25% of receivables at September 30, 2025, and more than three quarters of North American consumer sales fall in the second and third fiscal quarters, so receivables peak at USD 713.0 million in June and cash needs are financed by the revolver and by selling receivables.
  Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_smg.htm.jsonl, pages 9, 19; 10-Q_2026-06-27_smg.htm.jsonl, pages 4, 52
- The Roundup marketing agreement is a material earnings stream that depends on a third party. Net sales associated with it were USD 153.6 million for nine months against USD 134.6 million a year earlier, up 14.12%, for a USD 18.0 million annual contribution, and either party may terminate without a fee if the program's earnings before interest and taxes fall below USD 50.0 million. Those earnings are not printed, but the gross commission of USD 91.8 million in FY2025 is stated to be half of them, which implies about USD 183.6 million, 3.67x the termination floor, so the nearer risk is brand decommissioning or a change of control at Monsanto rather than a shortfall.
  Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-27_smg.htm.jsonl, page 18; 10-K_2025-09-30_smg.htm.jsonl, pages 32, 129; calculation R1; calculation R3; calculation R4
- The Hawthorne exit removed a weak segment but left contingent links to partner-owned vehicles. The nine-month loss on sale was USD 101.8 million, USD 35.0 million of cash left with the business, and the non-cash consideration was valued at USD 97.0 million at closing and USD 81.3 million at June 27, 2026; the company consolidates both vehicles and holds options over their assets, so a put exercise would require it to reacquire them against a note. It has also lent to strategic partners and recorded impairments of USD 64.6 million on convertible debt investments in FY2024, so credit losses on such balances are a pattern, not a one-off.
  Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_smg.htm.jsonl, page 42; 10-Q_2026-06-27_smg.htm.jsonl, pages 7, 11, 12, 33; calculation H1; calculation H2
- Control is concentrated and continuous but not sponsor-style. A family partnership holds about 23% of the shares, down from 24% a year earlier, and the chief executive and chairman who led the company since 2001 stepped down in June 2026, with the lead independent director becoming chairman; the successor is a general partner of the same partnership, so influence continues through the transition.
  Basis: 10-K_2024-09-30_smg.htm.jsonl, page 46; 10-K_2025-09-30_smg.htm.jsonl, pages 49, 57; 10-Q_2026-06-27_smg.htm.jsonl, page 39
- Financial policy is dividend-first with a record of debt reduction. The dividend of USD 0.66 a share each quarter has not changed since fiscal 2022 and absorbed 88.04% of nine-month free cash flow, leaving USD 15.8 million, while a USD 500.0 million repurchase authorisation of December 19, 2025 is unused; the credit agreement allows unlimited restricted payments while leverage after the payment is 4.00x or less, otherwise USD 225.0 million a year, and the reported covenant leverage is 3.78x.
  Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_smg.htm.jsonl, pages 48, 58; 10-Q_2026-06-27_smg.htm.jsonl, pages 3, 20, 21, 23, 51; calculation D2; calculation D3
- Noteholder guarantees are not permanent. The domestic subsidiary guarantees of the senior notes are released in listed circumstances, including designation of a guarantor as an unrestricted subsidiary and release from the credit agreement guarantee at the company's election, so structural support depends on indenture terms that are not supplied.
  Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_smg.htm.jsonl, page 81; 10-Q_2026-06-27_smg.htm.jsonl, page 22
- Litigation adds an unquantified claim on cash. Shareholder class and derivative suits allege misstatements about inventories and sales for May 2021 to August 2023, the court partly denied dismissal on April 22, 2026, and no accrual is recorded; asbestos claims are also unaccrued.
  Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-27_smg.htm.jsonl, pages 28, 29

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a Medium rating is the repayment record, an independent chair and audited, controls-effective reporting: voluntary term loan prepayments of USD 250.0 million and USD 75.0 million, a lead independent director now chairing the board, and unqualified audit opinions. The rating would rise if the proxy statement shows related-party payments or fees, if the USD 500.0 million repurchase authorisation is used while leverage is above 4.00x, or if a put option over a Hawthorne vehicle is exercised; it would fall if the proxy statement shows an independent board majority and no related-party payments.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_smg.htm.jsonl, pages 98, 100, 136; 10-Q_2026-06-27_smg.htm.jsonl, pages 12, 20, 39, 51

### Limitations

- The proxy statement is not supplied, so board independence, committee membership, related-party payments and pay are not established, and the Medium governance rating could move either way once they are.
  Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_smg.htm.jsonl, pages 92, 94
- Customer percentages are printed for FY2025 only, so the 52% concentration is not established for the nine months to June 27, 2026 and may differ in the seasonal peak.
  Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_smg.htm.jsonl, page 19
- The option terms of the Hawthorne vehicles, including the partner's identity, are not disclosed beyond the notes, so the contingent funding exposure to the company is not established.
  Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-27_smg.htm.jsonl, pages 7, 12
- Roundup program earnings are implied from the commission rate rather than printed, so the margin above the termination floor is an inference and the Roundup risk is rated by the agreement's terms, not by a reported cushion.
  Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_smg.htm.jsonl, page 129; 10-Q_2026-06-27_smg.htm.jsonl, page 18
- Nothing after August 5, 2026 is supplied, so the effect of the leadership change, any use of the repurchase authorisation and fiscal 2026 results are not reflected in these findings.
  Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-27_smg.htm.jsonl, pages 39, 51, 64

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

The company issued no new notes in fiscal 2025 or the nine months to June 27, 2026; its only new debt arrangement is the Seventh Amended and Restated Credit Agreement of November 21, 2025, a revolver of USD 1,500.0 million and a term loan of USD 500.0 million maturing November 21, 2030, which replaced the earlier agreement. The transactions that matter are disposals: the Hawthorne business in North America was sold on April 8, 2026 to Vireo for equity securities with an initial fair value of USD 97.0 million after USD 35.0 million of cash was transferred, the Hawthorne business in the Netherlands was sold on September 30, 2025 for USD 8.5 million financed by a seller loan, and the investment holding company that held cannabis-related investments was sold to a partner-owned vehicle on March 14, 2025 for a note of USD 39.0 million. Each sale left the company with a call option, and the vehicles' owner can put the assets back against cancelling the note, so the disposals reduce operating exposure without removing the economic link.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_smg.htm.jsonl, pages 67, 137; 10-Q_2026-06-27_smg.htm.jsonl, pages 7, 11, 12, 19, 20
