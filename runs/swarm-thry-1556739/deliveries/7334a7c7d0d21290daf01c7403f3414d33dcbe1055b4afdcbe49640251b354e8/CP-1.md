## Findings

### Bottom line

Thryv's net debt was USD 230,417 thousand at September 30, 2026, but no leverage ratio can be stated for that date on the business that remains, because the Print Business sale left no recast earnings history. On the earlier presentation, which still includes the print business, net leverage on reported EBITDA moved from 2.66x at the 2025 year-end to 3.61x at June 30, 2026, against 1.60x and 1.88x on the company's adjusted EBITDA, which adds back the restructuring, share-based pay and pension cost that reported EBITDA carries.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_thry.htm.jsonl, page 5; 10-Q_2026-06-30_thry.htm.jsonl, page 18; 10-Q_2026-09-30_thry.htm.jsonl, page 12; calculation C28; calculation C20; calculation C21

Cash of USD 8,752 thousand and ABL capacity of USD 11,000 thousand compare with USD 35,000 thousand of scheduled Term Loan amortization over the next 12 months, before operating cash flow and sale proceeds.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_thry.htm.jsonl, page 14; 10-Q_2026-09-30_thry.htm.jsonl, page 23; 10-Q_2026-09-30_thry.htm.jsonl, page 25; calculation C43

### Key findings

The earnings history needed for leverage is split by the sale. The third-quarter 2026 report restates the first nine months of 2026 and 2025 without the Print Business, while the annual reports and the first two quarterly reports include it, and no source states a recast full year 2025. Reported EBITDA for 2025 of USD 91,260 thousand therefore includes earnings the company has agreed to sell, and a last-twelve-months figure at September 30, 2026 would mix perimeters, so leverage and cash interest cover at that date are not supplied.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_thry.htm.jsonl, page 12; 10-Q_2026-09-30_thry.htm.jsonl, page 16; 10-K_2025-12-31_thry.htm.jsonl, page 116; calculation C2

Net debt fell from USD 268,026 thousand at the 2024 year-end to USD 242,735 thousand at the 2025 year-end and USD 230,417 thousand at September 30, 2026, mainly as the Term Loan was amortised. Over the same span reported EBITDA fell from USD 91,260 thousand in 2025 to USD 64,904 thousand for the twelve months to June 30, 2026, which is why net leverage rose from 2.66x to 3.61x even as debt fell.
Basis: calculation C17; calculation C18; calculation C21; calculation C28; 10-Q_2026-06-30_thry.htm.jsonl, page 18

Reported EBITDA is far below the company's adjusted measure because the company adds back large charges. In 2025 the gap was USD 60,586 thousand, and for the first nine months of 2026 the bridge from a reported loss to USD 11,934 thousand of adjusted EBITDA rests on a USD 94,800 thousand goodwill impairment, USD 17,815 thousand of restructuring and integration expense and USD 11,292 thousand of share-based pay. The company expects further restructuring costs through 2027, so the add-back behaves as a continuing cost rather than a one-off.
Basis: calculation C11; calculation C38; 10-Q_2026-09-30_thry.htm.jsonl, page 54

Operating cash flow shows the same split. For the first nine months of 2026 continuing operations used USD 37,867 thousand of operating cash and the print business supplied USD 77,453 thousand; with capital spending of USD 23,613 thousand, free cash flow before debt service was an outflow of USD 61,480 thousand on the continuing perimeter and an inflow of USD 15,973 thousand with the print business included. The print cash flow leaves with the sale.
Basis: calculation C42; calculation C41; 10-Q_2026-09-30_thry.htm.jsonl, page 9

Cash interest exceeds the continuing business's adjusted EBITDA. Adjusted EBITDA of USD 11,934 thousand for the first nine months of 2026 covered cash interest paid of USD 20,274 thousand only 0.59x, although that interest figure is stated for the whole company and may include interest of the print business.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_thry.htm.jsonl, page 9; 10-Q_2026-09-30_thry.htm.jsonl, page 54

Near-term liquidity is below scheduled amortization before operating cash flow. Cash and ABL capacity total USD 19,752 thousand against USD 35,000 thousand of Term Loan amortization due within 12 months, and the sale proceeds are earmarked for the ABL, USD 34.3 million of accrued tax liabilities and part of the Term Loan. The ABL is lent against print receivables only, so it ends with the sale.
Basis: calculation C43; calculation C44; 10-Q_2026-09-30_thry.htm.jsonl, page 10; 10-Q_2026-09-30_thry.htm.jsonl, page 63

Client prepayments and accrued taxes fund operations without interest. Contract liabilities of USD 24,789 thousand are services owed to clients, and accrued liabilities include USD 40,841 thousand of accrued taxes, of which the IRS matter is the main part; both are cash or service obligations that sit outside the debt figure. Finance leases, operating leases and pension obligations are also carried outside Total Debt.
Basis: calculation C45; calculation C46; 10-Q_2026-09-30_thry.htm.jsonl, page 23

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

On the company's own adjusted EBITDA leverage looks moderate: net debt was 1.60x adjusted EBITDA at the 2025 year-end and 1.88x at June 30, 2026, and the company expects USD 50.0 million to USD 55.0 million of gross annualized savings from its restructuring. The strongest reading in the issuer's favour is that the impairment and share-based pay are not cash costs and that restructuring will stop, so adjusted EBITDA is the better guide to debt service.
Basis: calculation C18; calculation C30; 10-Q_2026-09-30_thry.htm.jsonl, page 30

That reading would win if the company published a recast twelve-month adjusted EBITDA at September 30, 2026 above the earlier level, if the sale closed with net proceeds large enough to leave the remaining Term Loan covered, and if the lenders waived the leverage covenant. A recast twelve-month figure below the earlier one, a closing delayed by the monthly price reduction, or a refused waiver would confirm the weaker view.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_thry.htm.jsonl, page 11; 10-Q_2026-09-30_thry.htm.jsonl, page 63

### Limitations

The company states no unadjusted EBITDA, so reported EBITDA here is derived from earnings, interest, tax and depreciation and amortization; the covenant EBITDA in the loan agreement is a different measure and is not supplied.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_thry.htm.jsonl, page 24; calculation C2

The statement columns for the years, the first quarter and the half year include the print business and the nine-month and third-quarter columns do not, so no ratio or growth rate that crosses the two groups is stated.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_thry.htm.jsonl, page 12

The maturity ladder splits the Term Loan into equal quarterly installments inferred from the stated annual amount, because the agreement's schedule is not supplied, and the ABL availability is the company's own figure after restrictions with its commitment stated only in the 2024 annual report.
