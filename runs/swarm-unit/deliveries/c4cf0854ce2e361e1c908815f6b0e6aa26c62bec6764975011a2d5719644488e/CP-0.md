## Findings

### Bottom line

The seven supplied filings and releases support the financial foundation, business and governance, and fundamental credit work on Uniti Group Inc., with limitations, but only to the quarter ended June 30, 2026, so every creditor conclusion inherits that cutoff.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_unit-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 4–6; EX-99.1_2026-07-30_unit-ex9912q26.htm.jsonl, page 1
