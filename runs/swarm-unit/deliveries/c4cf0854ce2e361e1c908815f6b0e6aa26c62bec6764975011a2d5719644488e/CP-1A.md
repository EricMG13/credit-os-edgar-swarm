## Findings

### Bottom line

Uniti is a debt-heavy turnaround under concentrated but non-controlling ownership: creditor risk rests on a debt-funded fiber build and on board decisions over collateral and financing, not on a sponsor's distribution record.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_unit-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 42–44; 10-K_2025-12-31_unit-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 83; 10-Q_2026-06-30_unit-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 52–54

Elliott holds about 25.2% of the common stock with a right to designate two of the nine directors, the two largest holders and insiders hold about 36.1%, and preferred stock paying 11% with a liquidation preference of USD 575.0 million ranks ahead of the common. A July 2026 securitization moved assets out of guarantor subsidiaries into unrestricted subsidiaries, partly offset by up to USD 500.0 million of secured debt repayment offers.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_unit-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 42; 10-K_2025-12-31_unit-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 106; 10-Q_2026-06-30_unit-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 52–54

Compensation, related-party and board-independence detail sit in a proxy statement that is not in the sources, so the governance view rests on the annual report and quarterly filings.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_unit-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 205

### Key findings

The business changed in kind in August 2025. Old Uniti was a REIT whose tenant Windstream paid 68.3% of revenue; the combined company earns 56.14% of first-half 2026 revenue from Kinetic broadband, 24.27% from Fiber Infrastructure and 19.59% from Uniti Solutions, and no customer reached 10% of revenue after the merger, so single-tenant dependence has gone but legacy copper decline now drives the base.
Basis: calculation C2; 10-K_2025-12-31_unit-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 19; 10-K_2025-12-31_unit-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 5–6; 10-Q_2026-06-30_unit-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 47–48

The fiber build is the credit's central bet and it is not yet self-funding. Consumer fiber subscriber revenue was USD 195.4 million in FY2025 against USD 257.0 million of legacy DSL and other consumer revenue, and capital expenditures of USD 799.2 million in the first half of 2026 exceeded operating cash flow of USD 339.4 million, with the fiber build targeted to run to the end of 2029 (customer, household and passing counts are in the operating model table).
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_unit-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 161; 10-Q_2026-06-30_unit-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 6; EX-99.1_2026-03-02_unit-ex991202510xk.htm.jsonl, page 1

Fiber Infrastructure revenue is lumpy. A dark fiber IRU sold to a hyperscaler added USD 85.5 million of sales revenue in the first half of 2026, recognised up front as a sales-type lease, so first quarter 2026 revenue of USD 987.5 million, against USD 909.7 million in the second quarter, should not be read as a run rate.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_unit-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 63; 10-Q_2026-06-30_unit-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 13; 10-Q_2026-06-30_unit-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-Q_2026-03-31_unit-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 4

Ownership is concentrated but not controlling, and control rights are specific. Elliott may designate two directors for as long as it holds at least half of its original shares, the certificate waives corporate opportunities for significant holders, and affiliates of Elliott may require repurchase of the preferred stock after the tenth anniversary of issue at the liquidation preference of USD 575.0 million plus accrued dividends; none of this is evidence of how holders will behave toward creditors.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_unit-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 42–44; 10-K_2025-12-31_unit-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 106

Creditor claims sit in layers and one layer is growing. Credit facilities and secured notes share first-priority collateral under an intercreditor agreement, unsecured notes are effectively subordinated, and USD 1,799.1 million of ABS notes at unrestricted subsidiaries are ring-fenced from other creditors; the July 2026 Kinetic securitization added further assets to that pool.
Basis: calculation C6; 10-K_2025-12-31_unit-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 144; 10-K_2025-12-31_unit-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 201; 10-Q_2026-06-30_unit-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 20–21; 10-Q_2026-06-30_unit-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 52–54

Disclosure is audited and controls were reported effective, which supports monitoring. The 2025 annual report carries a PricewaterhouseCoopers opinion on the statements and on internal control, the auditor changed from KPMG for 2025, and common dividends are not expected while preferred dividends of USD 31.2 million were declared in the first half of 2026.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_unit-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 95; 10-K_2025-12-31_unit-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 99–100; 10-K_2025-12-31_unit-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 83; 10-Q_2026-06-30_unit-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 4

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence for a lower governance risk is that no distribution to shareholders is documented since the merger, the 10.50% secured notes were redeemed in full in October 2025 and replaced by longer-dated debt, and the July 2026 securitization carried a weighted average coupon of about 6.18% with USD 500.0 million of proceeds offered to repay secured debt. The view would improve if the offers retire that amount and the unrestricted pool stops growing. It would worsen if more guarantor-held assets move into unrestricted subsidiaries without matching repayment, if Elliott's 25.2% stake is used to alter the board or the preferred stock is put after the tenth anniversary, or if the executed debt documents show capacity for further transfers.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_unit-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 145–146; 10-K_2025-12-31_unit-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 151; EX-99.1_2026-07-30_unit-ex9912q26.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-06-30_unit-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 52–54

### Limitations

- The sources do not state the capacity in the debt documents for moving assets to unrestricted subsidiaries, so the July 2026 transfer is recorded as an action and not as evidence of intent; the executed indentures would settle it.
- Compensation, related-party transactions, board independence and holders below the two largest are in a proxy statement that is not in the sources, so the governance view could change on any of them.
- Results of the July 2026 repayment offers and any event after July 30, 2026 are not in the sources, so whether the securitization was creditor-neutral is not established.
- Segment and revenue figures are recast after the merger and earlier periods are Old Uniti only, so the 68.3% tenant share and the segment mix before August 2025 are not like-for-like with later periods.
- Customer and household counts are approximate figures stated by the issuer, so the fiber penetration trend is indicative only.

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

The Windstream merger closed on August 1, 2025 as a reverse merger: Old Uniti was the accounting acquirer and Windstream the legal acquirer, with pre-closing holders of Old Uniti and Windstream owning about 62% and 38% before convertible securities. Windstream's equityholders received about 90.1 million common shares, 35.42% of the common, about 0.6 million non-voting preferred shares with an 11% dividend and a liquidation preference of USD 575.0 million, about 17.6 million penny warrants and about USD 370.7 million of cash, the cash funded from Old Uniti's cash and revolver borrowings.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_unit-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 5–6; 10-K_2025-12-31_unit-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 106

On August 4, 2025 Uniti Group LP merged into Uniti Services LLC, so one borrower carries the debt of both legacy groups, each group's guarantors now guarantee the other's debt, the Windstream revolver became pari passu with the other first lien debt and the restrictive covenants that kept the two groups apart were effectively eliminated.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_unit-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 56; 10-K_2025-12-31_unit-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 139

In October 2025 Uniti Services issued USD 1.4 billion of 7.50% senior secured notes due 2033 and borrowed a new USD 1.0 billion seven-year term loan, using the proceeds to redeem the 10.50% secured notes in full at a redemption premium of USD 119.5 million, and the ABS notes issuers sold USD 250.0 million of secured fiber network revenue term notes. In 2026 the Kinetic ABS issuer sold USD 960.1 million of notes in January, and Uniti Services issued an additional USD 1.0 billion of 8.625% unsecured notes and repaid its USD 500.0 million Windstream term loan in February.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_unit-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 145–146; 10-K_2025-12-31_unit-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 151; 10-K_2025-12-31_unit-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 149; 10-K_2025-12-31_unit-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 195; 10-Q_2026-06-30_unit-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 24; 10-Q_2026-06-30_unit-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 30

After the June 30, 2026 balance sheet, the Kinetic ABS issuer sold about USD 1.1 billion of Series 2026-2 notes on July 15, 2026 to buy assets held by guarantor subsidiaries, and on July 23, 2026 Uniti Services offered to prepay up to USD 167.8 million of the 2025 term loan while the secured notes' issuers offered to buy up to USD 332.2 million of their notes, together USD 500.0 million; USD 19.8 million of the term loan was repaid on July 30, 2026 and the results of the other offers are not reported.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_unit-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 52–54; EX-99.1_2026-07-30_unit-ex9912q26.htm.jsonl, page 2
