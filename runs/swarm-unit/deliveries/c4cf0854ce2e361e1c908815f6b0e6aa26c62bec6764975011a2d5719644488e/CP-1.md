## Findings

### Bottom line

Uniti's reported EBITDA includes a USD 1,683.9 million non-cash merger gain, so reported total debt leverage of 3.64x is not decision-useful; on issuer-adjusted trailing EBITDA, leverage is 7.13x and cash interest cover 1.94x at June 30, 2026.
Basis: calculation C5; calculation C6; 10-K_2025-12-31_unit-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 100–101; 10-K_2025-12-31_unit-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 88; 10-Q_2026-06-30_unit-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 5; 10-Q_2026-06-30_unit-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 90–91

Net debt was USD 10,037.7 million at June 30, 2026 against cash of USD 608.9 million, and liquidity of USD 1,447.0 million includes USD 838.1 million of undrawn revolver capacity. The largest maturity year is 2031 at USD 3,420.1 million, built from instrument due dates because the second-quarter maturity table labels its years inconsistently.
Basis: calculation C1; calculation C7; calculation C8; 10-Q_2026-06-30_unit-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 20–21; 10-Q_2026-06-30_unit-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 23; 10-Q_2026-06-30_unit-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 24; 10-Q_2026-06-30_unit-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 30

The trailing figures mix one month of Old Uniti before the August 2025 merger with partial-year Windstream results, and net debt after the July 2026 financing transactions is not established.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_unit-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 5–6; 10-Q_2026-06-30_unit-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 52–54

### Analysis by dimension

Leverage is high, and the fall since year-end is in large part a denominator effect. On issuer-adjusted trailing EBITDA, total debt was 7.13x and net debt 6.73x on June 30, 2026, against 8.13x and 8.08x at December 31, 2025, yet carrying debt rose from USD 9,539.4 million to USD 10,646.6 million over the half year. Windstream's fuller contribution replaced a five-month year in the denominator, so creditors should expect little relief unless adjusted EBITDA grows or debt is repaid.
Basis: calculation C1; calculation C5; 10-K_2025-12-31_unit-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 101–102; 10-Q_2026-06-30_unit-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 5

Cash interest cover is thin, and the build is not self-funding. Trailing adjusted EBITDA covered trailing cash interest of USD 768.6 million 1.94x at June 30, 2026, against 1.78x for FY2025, while first-half capital expenditures of USD 799.2 million exceeded operating cash flow of USD 339.4 million. Free cash flow was negative USD 459.8 million, funded by debt issuance of USD 2,072.6 million against repayments of USD 930.0 million, so any slippage in the fiber build's payback falls on debt holders through further borrowing.
Basis: calculation C4; calculation C6; calculation C15; 10-Q_2026-06-30_unit-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 6

Liquidity covers the next two maturity years but leans on revolvers. Cash of USD 608.9 million and undrawn revolver capacity of USD 838.1 million, USD 1,447.0 million in all, compare with USD 316.5 million falling due in 2027 and USD 580.0 million in 2028. Cash fell by USD 373.7 million in the second quarter, letters of credit of USD 136.9 million already use revolver capacity, and the Windstream Revolver commitments step down on January 23, 2027.
Basis: calculation C7; 10-Q_2026-06-30_unit-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 23–24; 10-Q_2026-06-30_unit-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 83; 10-Q_2026-03-31_unit-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 5

Refinancing risk is concentrated in 2031 and 2032. Debt maturing is small to 2028, then USD 1,120.0 million in 2029 and USD 1,299.0 million in 2030, before USD 3,420.1 million in 2031 and USD 2,540.0 million in 2032, which include USD 2,200.0 million of 8.25% secured notes and USD 1,600.0 million of 8.625% unsecured notes. The ABS notes fall at anticipated repayment dates, so those amounts depend on refinancing the securitizations rather than on the issuer's cash.
Basis: calculation C8; 10-Q_2026-06-30_unit-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 20–21; 10-Q_2026-06-30_unit-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 30

Three data risks bound every ratio. Reported EBITDA keeps a non-cash merger gain of USD 1,683.9 million, trailing figures mix perimeters, and the second-quarter maturity table's year labels do not match the instruments' due dates; each is logged with the figure used and a twin row, and none is smoothed into one number.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_unit-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 88; 10-Q_2026-06-30_unit-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 30; calculation C5; calculation C6

Customer prepayments are funding float outside debt. Deferred revenue of USD 646.3 million at June 30, 2026, against USD 608.5 million at December 31, 2025, supports operations only while customers keep paying in advance. Events after June 30, 2026, a USD 1,140.7 million Kinetic securitization and secured debt repayment offers of up to USD 500.0 million, are kept out of the period figures, so pro forma net debt is not established.
Basis: calculation C12; calculation C16; 10-Q_2026-06-30_unit-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 5; 10-Q_2026-06-30_unit-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 52–54

The strongest evidence against a high-leverage reading is management's 2026 outlook for adjusted EBITDA of USD 1,450 million to USD 1,500 million, which brackets the trailing USD 1,492.2 million: if the outlook holds, partial-year Windstream results no longer understate the trailing figure and leverage near 7x is the run rate. The view would change if full-year pro forma adjusted EBITDA proved materially higher than the outlook, or if the securitization proceeds were used to repay debt.
Basis: calculation C3; EX-99.1_2026-07-30_unit-ex9912q26.htm.jsonl, page 2
