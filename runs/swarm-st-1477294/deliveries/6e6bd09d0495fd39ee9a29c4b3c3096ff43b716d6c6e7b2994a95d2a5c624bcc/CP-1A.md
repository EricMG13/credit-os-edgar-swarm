## Findings

### Bottom line

Sensata is a widely held public company with no sponsor or controlling shareholder identified, so creditors face moderate governance risk from shareholder returns and past acquisition write-offs rather than from sponsor extraction.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_st.pdf, page 30; 10-K_2025-12-31_st.pdf, page 108

In 2026 management put debt reduction ahead of shareholder returns, retiring debt with USD 431.8 million of cash in nine months against USD 77.5 million returned to shareholders, and the internal control weaknesses disclosed in the 2023 and 2024 annual reports were remediated by year-end 2025. Risk stays moderate because the credit agreement permits large distributions, the executed documents were not supplied, and USD 375.8 million of Dynapower goodwill was written off in 2024 and 2025.
Basis: calculation K10; calculation K12; 10-Q_2026-09-30_st.htm.jsonl, page 12; 10-K_2025-12-31_st.pdf, pages 105–107; 10-K_2025-12-31_st.pdf, pages 88–89

### Key findings

No sponsor or controlling shareholder is identified, which removes the sponsor-extraction mechanism but leaves public shareholders as the force behind distributions. Three holders of record, one of them the depository nominee, held the ordinary shares at 2026-01-29, and the proxy statement that would show beneficial holders, related-party dealings and pay incentives was not supplied, so the finding rests on the absence of any sponsor in the filings and not on a holder list.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_st.pdf, page 30; 10-K_2025-12-31_st.pdf, page 108

Shareholder returns have been subordinated to debt reduction since mid-2025 but remain a lever that can be pulled back. Dividends and repurchases took 41.31% of operating cash flow in the first nine months of 2025 and 30.73% in FY2025, against 13.89% in the first nine months of 2026, and repurchases fell to USD 25.1 million from USD 120.6 million a year earlier; USD 257.3 million of authorization remains with no expiry date, so a change of priority would not need lender consent within the distribution baskets.
Basis: calculation K9; 10-Q_2026-09-30_st.htm.jsonl, page 14; 10-K_2025-12-31_st.pdf, page 30

Liability management has so far favored creditors. Three voluntary purchases between November 2025 and the third quarter of 2026 retired USD 792.7 million of 4.0% note principal for USD 781.8 million of cash, with the revolver undrawn, and the documents describe no exchange, uptier, priming or drop-down; the company does reserve the right to retire or exchange debt in other ways, in amounts that may be material.
Basis: calculation K11; 10-K_2025-12-31_st.pdf, page 85; 10-Q_2026-06-30_st.htm.jsonl, page 12; 10-Q_2026-06-30_st.htm.jsonl, page 13; 10-Q_2026-09-30_st.htm.jsonl, page 12; 10-Q_2026-09-30_st.htm.jsonl, page 39

Legal capacity to move cash out of the borrowing group is wide, but no use beyond ordinary dividends and repurchases is documented, so capacity is a risk and not yet a behavior. As summarized in the annual report, distributions to the parent companies may reach USD 50.0 million a year plus unlimited amounts while senior secured net leverage is at or below 2.5x, 7.0% of market capitalization a year, and the greater of USD 150.0 million and 20.0% of covenant EBITDA, and the credit agreement lets unrestricted subsidiaries be designated, although none has been; these terms rest on the issuer's summary because the executed documents were not supplied.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_st.pdf, page 88; 10-K_2025-12-31_st.pdf, page 89

Reporting quality has been repaired, but the clean record is one year long. Material weaknesses in internal control, disclosed in the 2023 and 2024 annual reports and in quarterly reports through September 2025 and centered on inventory and account reconciliation processes in shared services in Mexico, were declared remediated at 2025-12-31, and the auditor's opinion on internal control was adverse for 2024 and unqualified for 2025.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_st.pdf, pages 105–107; 10-K_2024-12-31_st.pdf, page 66

The acquisition record is the main governance concern. Dynapower goodwill of USD 375.8 million was impaired in the third quarters of 2024 and 2025 with USD 4.1 million left, and the Insights Business, impaired by USD 321.7 million in 2023, was sold in 2024 for a purchase price of USD 165.0 million against net assets of about USD 263.4 million; the write-offs are non-cash, but they show that acquisition capital earned less than its cost while management still names acquisitions as an option.
Basis: calculation K12; 10-K_2025-12-31_st.pdf, page 96; 10-K_2024-12-31_st.pdf, page 121; 10-K_2025-12-31_st.pdf, page 7

The business is diversified across three segments with low single-customer concentration, yet it stays tied to the automotive cycle. Automotive was 57.0% of FY2025 revenue, Industrials 21.3% and Aerospace, Defense, and Commercial Equipment 21.7%, no customer exceeded 10% of revenue, and the top ten customers have averaged 36 years of relationship; in the third quarter of 2026 Industrials and Aerospace, Defense, and Commercial Equipment grew 12.6% and 13.4% against 3.7% for Automotive.
Basis: calculation K1; 10-K_2025-12-31_st.pdf, page 6; 10-K_2025-12-31_st.pdf, page 7; PRESENTATION_Q32026_st.pdf, page 17

Operating momentum supports debt service, but the third quarter was flattered by a one-time payment. Revenue grew 5.11% in the nine months to 2026-09-30 and segment operating income rose to USD 770.0 million from USD 680.3 million; the third quarter included USD 24.0 million from a customer for a cancelled electric vehicle program, so its growth was 7.68% reported and 5.11% without it.
Basis: calculation K5; calculation K6; 10-Q_2026-09-30_st.htm.jsonl, page 19; 10-Q_2026-09-30_st.htm.jsonl, page 20

Operations are weighted outside the United States, which adds tariff and trade-policy exposure. Subsidiaries outside the United States earned about 61% of FY2025 revenue, about 20% of it in China, and 92% of employees work outside the United States; the company incurred and passed through USD 40.8 million of tariffs in FY2025, and potential refunds after the February 2026 Supreme Court ruling are uncertain and excluded from fourth-quarter guidance.
Basis: calculation K7; 10-K_2025-12-31_st.pdf, page 19; 10-K_2025-12-31_st.pdf, page 102; PRESENTATION_Q32026_st.pdf, page 19

Guarantee coverage is thinner than the group's size suggests: the non-U.S. subsidiaries of the borrower were released as guarantors in August 2023, with a conditional duty to reinstate that the documents do not describe, while subsidiaries outside the United States generated about 61% of FY2025 revenue.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_st.pdf, page 88; 10-K_2025-12-31_st.pdf, page 19

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest case for a lower risk level is that management reports deleveraging ahead of schedule, ratings are stable at BB+ from S&P and Ba2 from Moody's, internal control now tests as effective, and nothing in the record shows value moving to shareholders at creditors' expense; the level would fall to Low if the executed documents show distribution baskets that tighten as leverage rises and a second clean year-end controls result follows.
Basis: PRESENTATION_Q32026_st.pdf, page 14; 10-Q_2026-09-30_st.htm.jsonl, page 40; 10-K_2025-12-31_st.pdf, pages 105–107

The strongest case for a higher level is that capacity is wide, shareholders received USD 173.5 million in the first nine months of 2025 before the change of priority, USD 257.3 million of repurchase authorization is unused, and Dynapower shows that acquisitions have lost money; the level would rise to High on a debt-funded acquisition, a return to heavy repurchases while leverage is elevated, the designation of an unrestricted subsidiary, any further guarantee release or non-pro-rata exchange, or a new material weakness in internal control.
Basis: calculation K9; 10-Q_2026-09-30_st.htm.jsonl, page 14; 10-K_2025-12-31_st.pdf, page 96; 10-K_2025-12-31_st.pdf, page 88

### Limitations

- The proxy statement was not supplied, so beneficial holders, board independence, related-party dealings and pay incentives are not established; the finding that no controlling shareholder exists would change if a large holder appears in it.
- The executed credit agreement, indentures and amendments were not supplied, so every statement about distribution, guarantee and unrestricted-subsidiary capacity rests on the issuer's own summary and could change when the baskets, definitions and the 2025 and 2026 amendments are read.
- The purchase price and funding of the Dynapower acquisition are not stated, so the acquisition finding rests on write-offs alone and cannot say whether the original deal was debt funded.
- No lender compliance certificate or basket-usage report was supplied, so the statement that covenants were met is management's, and the distribution-capacity finding cannot be tied to headroom.
- Segment figures are on different bases in the 2025 annual report and the 2026 quarterly reports, after products moved between segments, so a shift in business mix between FY2025 and 2026 is not established.
- Management's statement that deleveraging is its top capital allocation priority is an intention and not a commitment, so the moderate risk level would rise if returns to shareholders went back to their 2025 pace.
