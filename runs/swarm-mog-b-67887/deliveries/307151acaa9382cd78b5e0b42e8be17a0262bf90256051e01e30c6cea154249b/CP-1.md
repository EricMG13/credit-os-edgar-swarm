## Findings

### Bottom line

Moog's balance sheet leverage is modest and falling, at 1.36x net debt to LTM EBITDA at June 27, 2026 against 1.81x at fiscal 2025 year end, so lenders hold a wide earnings cushion today. That cushion is overstated by restated history, a one-off tariff refund and debt-like claims that sit outside reported debt.
Basis: calculation C30; calculation C28; 10-Q_2026-06-27_mog-20260627.htm.jsonl, pages 2–3

Counting finance lease liabilities as debt lifts net leverage to 1.63x, and the latest quarter includes a USD 30 million tariff recovery booked against cost of sales. An unremediated control weakness behind the restatement means the trend is less reliable than the ratios suggest.
Basis: calculation C37; calculation C40; 10-Q_2026-06-27_mog-20260627.htm.jsonl, pages 28–29; 10-K_2025-09-27_mog-20250927.htm.jsonl, pages 105–125

### Credit data analysis

Leverage has fallen in each period shown, from 2.14x net debt to EBITDA at fiscal 2023 year end to 1.88x at fiscal 2024 and 1.81x at fiscal 2025, and to 1.36x on a last-twelve-months basis at June 27, 2026. The driver is earnings growth rather than repayment: calculated EBITDA rose from USD 370.662 million in fiscal 2023 to USD 488.451 million in fiscal 2025 and USD 620.522 million over the last twelve months, while total debt stayed between USD 863.092 million and USD 945.686 million in the fiscal years and then fell to USD 907.989 million at June 27, 2026 as revolver borrowings were repaid. For creditors this means the headroom depends on sustaining the margin gain, not on a shrinking debt stack.
Basis: calculation C28; calculation C30; calculation C08; 10-K_2025-09-27_mog-20250927.htm.jsonl, pages 54–57; 10-Q_2026-06-27_mog-20260627.htm.jsonl, pages 2–5

Coverage is strong on cash interest in the fiscal years, at 6.23x in fiscal 2023, 6.61x in fiscal 2024 and 6.88x in fiscal 2025, and the interim reports show interest expense falling on lower debt and lower rates. A last-twelve-months cover on cash interest cannot be stated because the 2026 quarterly reports do not give interest paid; on income statement interest the last-twelve-months cover is 9.26x, which is a different and labelled measure.
Basis: calculation C33; calculation C10; 10-K_2025-09-27_mog-20250927.htm.jsonl, pages 56–57; 10-Q_2026-06-27_mog-20260627.htm.jsonl, pages 4–5

Cash flow quality is improving but uneven. Free cash flow, defined as operating cash flow less purchase of property, plant and equipment, was negative in fiscal 2023 and USD 128.355 million in fiscal 2025, and USD 350.228 million over the last twelve months. Working capital absorbed cash in each fiscal year, mainly inventories and unbilled receivables, so conversion of EBITDA to cash leans on customer advances. Contract advances and progress billings, a non-debt funding float, rose from USD 372.988 million at fiscal 2025 year end to USD 486.574 million at June 27, 2026; if that float reverses as deliveries are made, operating cash flow falls by the same amount and net debt rises.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-27_mog-20250927.htm.jsonl, pages 56–57; 10-Q_2026-06-27_mog-20260627.htm.jsonl, pages 2–5

Liquidity at June 27, 2026 is cash of USD 66.821 million plus about USD 932 million of undrawn revolving capacity after standby letters of credit, with the revolving loan and the term loan both maturing in 2031. At fiscal 2025 year end most cash was held outside the United States, so the undrawn revolver, not cash, is the usable buffer. The 2026 refinancing replaced the 4.25% notes due 2027 with 5.50% notes due 2034 and extended the revolver from 2027 to 2031, which removes the near-term maturity wall that the fiscal 2025 maturity schedule still shows.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-27_mog-20260627.htm.jsonl, pages 16–17; 10-Q_2026-06-27_mog-20260627.htm.jsonl, page 34; 10-K_2025-09-27_mog-20250927.htm.jsonl, page 46

Debt-like claims outside the Total Debt figure matter for creditors. Finance lease liabilities, carried inside accrued liabilities and other long-term liabilities, were USD 172.352 million at June 27, 2026 against USD 134.075 million at fiscal 2025 year end, and USD 125 million of receivables sold under the receivables purchase agreement is derecognised. Adding finance leases to net debt gives 1.63x and adding receivables sold as well gives 1.83x. Operating lease liabilities, pension obligations and purchase commitments are further claims that are not in debt.
Basis: calculation C37; calculation C38; 10-Q_2026-06-27_mog-20260627.htm.jsonl, page 13; 10-Q_2026-06-27_mog-20260627.htm.jsonl, page 15; 10-K_2025-09-27_mog-20250927.htm.jsonl, page 104

The data carry three quality risks. First, the 2025 annual report revised fiscal 2023, fiscal 2024 and the interim quarters, and management and the auditor state that internal control over financial reporting was not effective; the 2026 quarterly report says the weakness is not yet remediated, so the restated trend could move again. Second, the latest quarter benefits from a USD 30 million recovery of previously paid tariffs recorded as a reduction of cost of sales; excluding it, net leverage on the last twelve months would be 1.42x, shown as an analyst sensitivity and not applied. Third, the issuer reports no EBITDA, so every EBITDA here is calculated and differs from the loan agreement definition that governs the covenants.
Basis: calculation C40; 10-K_2025-09-27_mog-20250927.htm.jsonl, pages 105–125; 10-K_2025-09-27_mog-20250927.htm.jsonl, page 128; 10-Q_2026-06-27_mog-20260627.htm.jsonl, page 40; 10-Q_2026-06-27_mog-20260627.htm.jsonl, page 28

The 2026 financing events are dated and sized in the statements. The Eighth Amended and Restated Loan Agreement of February 26, 2026 extended the revolver and set a USD 250 million term loan to February 26, 2031, USD 500 million of 5.50% senior notes due October 15, 2034 were sold on March 24, 2026, and the USD 500 million of 4.25% notes were redeemed at par on April 3, 2026. After the balance sheet date, a quarterly dividend of USD 0.30 per share was declared on July 30, 2026; on the share count at July 24, 2026 that is an indicative net debt increase of USD 9.501 million, which lifts net leverage to 1.37x on a pro forma basis.
Basis: calculation C43; 10-Q_2026-06-27_mog-20260627.htm.jsonl, pages 16–17; 10-Q_2026-06-27_mog-20260627.htm.jsonl, page 25

Gaps that restrict conclusions are listed under Gaps, with the covenant calculation, the legal rank of each facility and the interim cash interest the most material.
