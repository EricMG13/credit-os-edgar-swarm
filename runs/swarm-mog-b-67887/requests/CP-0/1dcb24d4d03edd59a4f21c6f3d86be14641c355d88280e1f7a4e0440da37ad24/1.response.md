## Findings

### Bottom line

The eight supplied Moog filings and releases support the historical, business and fundamental credit work, but not covenant, legal-priority or ratings analysis. Creditors should treat every result as company-reported and restated, because the executed debt documents are missing and a reporting-control weakness is still open.
