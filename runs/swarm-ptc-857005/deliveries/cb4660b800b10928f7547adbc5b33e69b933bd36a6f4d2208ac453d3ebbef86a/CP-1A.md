## Findings

### Bottom line

Ptc Inc. is a widely held subscription software company with no sponsor or controlling shareholder identified in the filings, so creditor risk comes from management's capital-allocation policy, which has swung from debt repayment in FY2025 to debt-funded buybacks in FY2026.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_ptc-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 26; 10-Q_2026-06-30_ptc-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 10; 10-Q_2026-06-30_ptc-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 21

About 95% of revenue is recurring and no customer exceeds 10% of revenue, which supports cash flow, but the March 2026 sale of Kepware and ThingWorx removed part of the revenue base, and the ownership, board and related-party detail held in the proxy statement is not supplied.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_ptc-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 22; 10-K_2025-09-30_ptc-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 65; 10-Q_2026-06-30_ptc-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 26; 10-K_2025-09-30_ptc-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 51

### Key findings

The franchise is a single-segment design and product-data software business with broad diversification, which supports debt service. PLM was 62.27% and CAD 37.73% of FY2025 software revenue, the Americas, Europe and Asia Pacific generated 48.45%, 36.33% and 15.22% of FY2025 revenue, no customer exceeded 10% of revenue or receivables, and about 75% of sales are made directly.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_ptc-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 5; 10-K_2025-09-30_ptc-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 30; 10-K_2025-09-30_ptc-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 65; 10-K_2025-09-30_ptc-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 76; calculation C1; calculation C3

Ownership offers no sponsor-extraction channel, but control and board rights are not established. The shares are widely held, with a few hundred holders of record at 30 September 2025, and buybacks cut shares outstanding by 7.38% between 30 September 2025 and 30 June 2026; any holder of more than five percent, the board and related-party dealings sit in a proxy statement that is not supplied.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_ptc-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 26; 10-K_2025-09-30_ptc-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 51; 10-Q_2026-06-30_ptc-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 2; calculation C6

Management's stated policy is to return excess cash to shareholders, and in FY2026 that policy has been funded partly with debt. The sale brought in USD 523.3 million of cash and a USD 375 million accelerated repurchase was funded from the proceeds, open-market repurchases continued, and the July 2026 repurchase of USD 273.7 million was financed mainly by USD 301.3 million of net borrowings. Guided FY2026 repurchases of about USD 1,625 million exceed guided free cash flow plus the sale cash by USD 251.7 million.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_ptc-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 34; 10-K_2025-09-30_ptc-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 35; 10-Q_2026-06-30_ptc-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 10; 10-Q_2026-06-30_ptc-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 21; EX-99.1_2026-07-29_ptc-ex99_1.htm.jsonl, page 4; calculation C8

Acquisitions have been funded from bank debt that was later reduced, which links growth to bank capacity. ServiceMax cost USD 1,448.2 million net of cash in January 2023 and was funded with USD 630 million of revolver and USD 500 million of term loan borrowing, which also repaid the prior facility; pure-systems cost USD 93.5 million in October 2023 and was financed mainly by a revolver draw; the IncQuery purchase of USD 7.9 million in 2025 was small. The March 2026 sale of Kepware and ThingWorx is a disposal of part of the portfolio and not an acquisition.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_ptc-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 77; 10-K_2025-09-30_ptc-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 78; 10-K_2025-09-30_ptc-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 79

Cash flow is steady but reported revenue is lumpy. Under the revenue recognition standard the licence portion of an on-premises subscription is recognised when the contract starts, so Q3 2026 licence revenue fell by 18% on a single large renewal of shortened duration while support and cloud revenue held, and about 95% of revenue is recurring.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_ptc-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 22; 10-Q_2026-06-30_ptc-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 24; 10-Q_2026-06-30_ptc-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 25; calculation C9

Disclosure supports monitoring of results but not of covenants. The annual accounts carry an audit opinion from PricewaterhouseCoopers that the statements present fairly, and the issuer reports ARR, non-GAAP reconciliations and quarterly guidance, but covenant levels, compliance certificates and the credit agreement are not in the sources. Total deferred revenue also fell with the divestiture, by about USD 56 million, which explains part of the fall in the balance since 30 September 2025.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_ptc-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 58; 10-Q_2026-06-30_ptc-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 8; calculation C7

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against treating buybacks as a creditor risk is that management repaid debt before the repurchase wave and still holds large undrawn bank capacity: net debt repayments were USD 553 million in FY2025 and USD 757.5 million of the revolver was available to borrow at 30 June 2026. The view would soften if the fourth-quarter FY2026 report shows repurchases slowing to the level of free cash flow, and harden if borrowing keeps funding repurchases beyond the guided total.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_ptc-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 34; 10-Q_2026-06-30_ptc-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 19; EX-99.1_2026-07-29_ptc-ex99_1.htm.jsonl, page 4

### Limitations

- The proxy statement is not supplied, so the conclusion that no sponsor or controlling shareholder exists rests on the absence of any such disclosure in the filings and would change if a holder of more than five percent were found.
  Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_ptc-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 51
- The credit agreement and note indenture are not supplied, so the conclusion that debt-funded buybacks are a creditor risk rests on observed behaviour and not on legal capacity to distribute, which is not established.

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

No new debt instrument was issued in the period covered. The 2025 senior notes were redeemed in fiscal 2025 using a revolver draw and cash on hand, which left the 2028 senior notes and the bank facility as the only debt, and the bank facility was amended on 18 November 2025 to remove the asset-sale restriction for the Kepware and ThingWorx sale and to permit other asset sales of up to USD 250 million of book value in a fiscal year.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_ptc-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 87; 10-K_2025-09-30_ptc-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 102

The sale of Kepware and ThingWorx to Parrot US Buyer, L.P., an entity controlled by TPG-affiliated funds, was agreed on 5 November 2025 for USD 600 million in cash plus contingent consideration of up to USD 125 million, and closed on 13 March 2026 for total consideration of USD 530.8 million, of which USD 523.3 million was received in cash. The dates of the agreement, the closing and the financing events are recorded in the Source readiness events.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_ptc-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 102; 10-Q_2026-06-30_ptc-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 11; Source readiness (CP-0)

The sale proceeds funded an accelerated repurchase of USD 375 million agreed on 17 March 2026, and a July 2026 repurchase of USD 273.7 million was financed mainly by USD 301.3 million of net credit facility borrowings, so the transaction moved cash from the business to shareholders and, after the proceeds were spent, added to debt.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_ptc-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 10; 10-Q_2026-06-30_ptc-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 21
