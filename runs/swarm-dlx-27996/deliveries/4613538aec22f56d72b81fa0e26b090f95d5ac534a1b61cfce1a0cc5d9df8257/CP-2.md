## Findings

### Bottom line

Deluxe's credit profile is adequate but weaker since the Celero purchase: print cash flow still carries the debt, yet reported net leverage rose to 4.92x and creditors now rely on acquired earnings and synergies the documents do not show.

Free cash flow of USD 175.3 million in FY2025 and liquidity of USD 264.7 million cover the USD 235.0 million of principal due before 2029, and the bank debt runs to 2031, so the first real test is the 2029 notes. Reported net leverage is above the 4.75x covenant ceiling that applies to June 2027, yet compliance is stated, so headroom depends on an earnings definition that is not supplied and cannot be calculated. The net debt and leverage after the dividend payable on 1 December 2026 are not established.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); Business & governance (CP-1A); 10-Q_2026-09-30_dlx.htm.jsonl, pages 18–21; calculation C-1; calculation C-12

### Key findings

- Leverage jumped, and the 4.92x probably overstates the settled level because only two months of Celero earnings are in it. Reported net leverage rose from 3.28x at 30 June 2026 to 4.92x at 30 September 2026, and adjusted net leverage from 2.92x to 4.20x, because debt principal rose USD 660.5 million in one quarter while trailing earnings hold only two months of Celero. Against the issuer's full-year adjusted EBITDA outlook of USD 470 million to USD 480 million, net debt of USD 1,929.2 million is 4.10x to 4.02x, so a fuller-year reading is about 4x before any debt reduction. The net debt after the dividend payable on 1 December 2026 is not established.
  Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); Business & governance (CP-1A); EX99.1_2026-09-30_dlx_exhibit991110420268-k-htm.htm.jsonl, page 2; calculation C-5; calculation C-6
- Cash generation is the main support, and it improved. Free cash flow rose 75.3% to USD 175.3 million in FY2025, 46.3% of reported EBITDA, and was USD 134.9 million in the first nine months of 2026, on capital spending of 4.5% of revenue and dividends of 31.5% of free cash flow. After dividends of USD 56.6 million, trailing free cash flow of USD 157.7 million is only 8.2% of net debt, so deleveraging has to come from earnings growth as much as from repayment.
  Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_dlx.htm.jsonl, page 56; 10-Q_2026-09-30_dlx.htm.jsonl, pages 4, 38; Financial foundation (CP-1); calculation C-8; calculation C-13; calculation C-15
- Covenant headroom is the weakest-evidenced point. Reported net leverage of 4.92x is above the 4.75x total leverage ceiling that applies through 30 June 2027, and filed cash interest cover of 3.45x, on different definitions, is 0.45x above the 3.00x covenant minimum, yet the issuer states compliance at 30 September 2026. The covenant earnings and debt definitions sit in an agreement that is not supplied, so whether the gap reflects add-backs, pro forma Celero earnings or other terms is not established, and headroom is not calculable.
  Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_dlx.htm.jsonl, page 20; Financial foundation (CP-1); calculation C-12
- Refinancing risk sits in 2029, not before. Principal due before 2029 is USD 235.0 million against liquidity of USD 264.7 million, bank debt runs to 2031, and 88.4% of principal falls due from 2029; both note issues, USD 925.0 million together, mature in 2029 and the secured notes mature on 1 February 2029 if any unsecured notes remain outstanding. No rating, price or spread is in the documents, so market access is not established and refinancing capacity rests on how far leverage falls by then.
  Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_dlx.htm.jsonl, pages 18–19, 21; 10-K_2024-12-31_dlx.htm.jsonl, page 64; Financial foundation (CP-1); calculation C-1; calculation C-2; calculation C-3
- Liquidity is adequate for the near term but thinner. It fell by USD 151.8 million from FY2025 to USD 264.7 million, 13.2% of total debt, as the revolver draw reached USD 200.5 million, 50.1% of its commitment, and the securitization facility stands at its stated USD 100.0 million maximum, so it adds no headroom. The revolver is the only visible shock absorber.
  Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_dlx.htm.jsonl, pages 18, 21; Financial foundation (CP-1); calculation C-4
- Revenue durability is weak in Print and acceptable elsewhere, and reported growth overstates it. Print revenue declined 4.5% in FY2024 and 5.7% in FY2025 and provides 54.9% of segment adjusted EBITDA; payments and data revenue grew 14.6% in the nine months, but Merchant Services grew 3.7% in the third quarter without Celero against 37.9% as reported, so revenue of USD 2,126.4 million over the last twelve months is level with USD 2,133.2 million in FY2025.
  Basis: Business & governance (CP-1A); Financial foundation (CP-1); 10-Q_2026-09-30_dlx.htm.jsonl, pages 25–27; calculation C-14
- Margin resilience is average, because cost action is doing more than mix. Adjusted EBITDA margin rose to 22.2% in the nine months to 30 September 2026 from 20.4% as corporate costs fell USD 14.9 million and selling, general and administrative expense eased to 40.1% of revenue from 40.8%, yet revenue is moving toward lower-margin services and gross margin slipped to 51.9% from 53.3%. Further gains depend on more savings and on Celero cost synergies of more than USD 15 million, which have no stated phasing.
  Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_dlx.htm.jsonl, pages 3, 26, 31; Business & governance (CP-1A); EX99.1_2026-06-30_dlx_tm2621737d1-ex99-1-htm.htm.jsonl, page 1; calculation C-11
- Financial policy is a judgement call at medium risk. No sponsor or controlling holder is named, the quarterly dividend of USD 0.30 per share is modest and no extraction is documented, but management said in May 2026 that it had reached its 3x leverage target and then re-levered through a debt-funded purchase, and the amended agreement allows total leverage of 4.75x. The covenant, not the target, is the working limit on leverage.
  Basis: Business & governance (CP-1A); EX99.1_2026-03-31_dlx_exhibit991050620268-k-htm.htm.jsonl, page 1; 10-Q_2026-09-30_dlx.htm.jsonl, page 20
- The primary downside path is faster print erosion arriving while Celero is unproven. As an illustration and not a forecast, a further Print revenue decline equal to the FY2025 dollar decline of USD 68.3 million would remove about USD 22.0 million of EBITDA at the Print margin of 32.3% with no cost action, taking trailing reported EBITDA to USD 370.3 million, net leverage to 5.21x and cash interest cover to 3.25x, while free cash flow of USD 192.3 million would still cover dividends 3.40x. Liquidity would carry the near term, so the pressure would fall on the covenant tests and on refinancing the 2029 notes at higher leverage.
  Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_dlx.htm.jsonl, page 114; Financial foundation (CP-1); calculation C-9; calculation C-10

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest case against a cautious view is that the leverage spike may be transient: before Celero, reported net leverage had fallen from 4.02x at FY2024 to 3.28x and adjusted net leverage to 2.92x, management said it had reached its 3x target, the 2026 outlook for adjusted EBITDA was raised in August and again in November, and the outlook implies fourth-quarter adjusted EBITDA 9.6% to 19.1% above the prior-year quarter, a quarter that will hold three full months of Celero. On the outlook, net leverage would be about 4.02x to 4.10x before debt reduction, and cost synergies of more than USD 15 million are expected. The view would improve if year-end results show net debt falling, Celero earnings support the price and the covenant ratios show headroom. It would worsen if Print declines faster than the 5.7% of FY2025, if free cash flow falls below the outlook of about USD 200 million, or if payments to shareholders rise while leverage is high.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); EX99.1_2026-03-31_dlx_exhibit991050620268-k-htm.htm.jsonl, page 1; EX99.1_2026-06-30_dlx_exhibit991080520268-k-htm.htm.jsonl, page 2; EX99.1_2026-09-30_dlx_exhibit991110420268-k-htm.htm.jsonl, page 2; EX99.1_2026-06-30_dlx_tm2621737d1-ex99-1-htm.htm.jsonl, page 1; calculation C-6; calculation C-7

### Limitations

- The covenant ceilings cannot be tested because the agreement's earnings and debt definitions are not supplied; if its definition adds back more than the filed measure, headroom is wider than the filed ratios suggest, and if not, the leverage test would be tight, which would move the assessment of financial policy and refinancing.
  Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_dlx.htm.jsonl, page 20; calculation C-12
- The leverage and downside figures mix two months of Celero earnings with its full debt and rest on management's outlook and one labelled sensitivity, not on a forecast, so the 4.92x and the 5.21x illustration both overstate or understate the settled position by an amount the documents do not allow to be measured.
  Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); EX99.1_2026-09-30_dlx_exhibit991110420268-k-htm.htm.jsonl, page 2; calculation C-6; calculation C-10
- The documents contain no rating, bond price or spread, so ability to refinance rests on maturity structure and cash generation alone; market evidence of weak access would lower the refinancing view, and strong access would raise it.
  Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_dlx.htm.jsonl, pages 18–19, 21
- The ownership and governance reading is limited to the annual report and the quarterly reports, because the proxy statement and executed agreements are not supplied; a large holder or related-party leakage in them would change the financial policy judgement.
  Basis: Business & governance (CP-1A); 10-K_2025-12-31_dlx.htm.jsonl, page 41

### Strengths and weaknesses

A strength is cash generation on low capital needs. Free cash flow of USD 175.3 million in FY2025 came from a Print margin of 32.3% and capital spending of 4.5% of revenue, which funds dividends of 31.5% of free cash flow and leaves cash for debt service, so the debt is carried by operating cash flow and not by asset sales.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_dlx.htm.jsonl, pages 56, 114; Business & governance (CP-1A); calculation C-13; calculation C-15

A strength is the back-ended maturity profile. Bank debt runs to 2031, principal due before 2029 is USD 235.0 million against liquidity of USD 264.7 million, and the amended agreement removed the 2029 maturity of the bank facilities, so there is no near-term refinancing event.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_dlx.htm.jsonl, pages 18–19, 21; Financial foundation (CP-1); calculation C-1

A strength is improving margins from cost action and growth. Adjusted EBITDA margin of 22.2% against 20.4% and payments and data growth of 14.6% in the nine months support earnings that can lower leverage if they persist.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_dlx.htm.jsonl, pages 26, 31; Business & governance (CP-1A); calculation C-11; calculation C-14

A strength is policy without an owner to extract value: no sponsor or controlling holder is named, there is no buyback since the first quarter of 2020, and the dividend is capped at USD 75.0 million a year with FY2025 payments of USD 55.2 million.
Basis: Business & governance (CP-1A); 10-Q_2026-09-30_dlx.htm.jsonl, page 20; 10-K_2025-12-31_dlx.htm.jsonl, pages 56, 58

A weakness is leverage after the Celero purchase. Reported net leverage of 4.92x and adjusted net leverage of 4.20x rest on two months of acquired earnings, interest will carry the full debt, and covenant headroom cannot be calculated, so creditors bear execution risk on integration and synergies.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); 10-Q_2026-09-30_dlx.htm.jsonl, page 20; calculation C-5; calculation C-12

A weakness is the steady erosion of Print, which is 54.9% of segment adjusted EBITDA and declined 4.5% to 5.7% a year, while the replacement segments earn lower margins and Merchant Services grew only 3.7% in the third quarter without Celero.
Basis: Business & governance (CP-1A); 10-Q_2026-09-30_dlx.htm.jsonl, pages 25–26; calculation C-14

A weakness is the concentrated 2029 wall. USD 985.0 million is due in 2029, 88.4% of principal falls due from 2029, the secured notes spring to 1 February 2029 if unsecured notes remain, and no market evidence is supplied on access.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_dlx.htm.jsonl, pages 18–19, 21; 10-K_2024-12-31_dlx.htm.jsonl, page 64; calculation C-2; calculation C-3

A weakness is thinner liquidity. Liquidity fell USD 151.8 million to USD 264.7 million, the revolver is 50.1% drawn and the securitization facility has no headroom, so the revolver is the only visible buffer against a shock.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_dlx.htm.jsonl, pages 18, 21; Financial foundation (CP-1); calculation C-4
