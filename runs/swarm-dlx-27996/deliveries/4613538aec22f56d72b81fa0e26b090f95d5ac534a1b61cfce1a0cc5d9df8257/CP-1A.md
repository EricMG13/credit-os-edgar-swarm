## Findings

### Bottom line

Deluxe's credit risk comes from its own debt-funded shift from checks to payments, not from an owner, because no sponsor or controlling holder is named, and the Celero purchase lifted debt to USD 2,004.1 million before the new earnings are proven.

Print is still the cash engine, with 54.9% of segment adjusted EBITDA on 46.3% of revenue in the nine months to 30 September 2026, so the payments and data segments must replace shrinking print earnings while carrying the new debt. Celero added USD 33.5 million of revenue in two months, and its earnings are not in the documents, so the price paid cannot be tested against them. The quarterly dividend of USD 0.30 per share continues inside a USD 75.0 million annual cap on dividends and repurchases.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_dlx.htm.jsonl, pages 12–14, 18, 20, 25–27; EX99.1_2026-09-30_dlx_exhibit991110420268-k-htm.htm.jsonl, page 2; calculation C-3; calculation C-11

### Key findings

- The business is a shrinking print franchise that still earns most of the profit, beside three growing segments that earn lower margins. Print was 53.3% of FY2025 revenue and 60.8% of segment adjusted EBITDA at a 32.3% margin, against margins of 21.6% to 28.1% in the other three segments, so replacing print earnings takes more revenue per dollar of EBITDA and leaves cash flow dependent on how fast the growth segments scale.
  Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_dlx.htm.jsonl, pages 113–115; calculation C-1; calculation C-8; calculation C-11
- Print is eroding steadily rather than collapsing. Print revenue declined 4.5% in FY2024 and 5.7% in FY2025, and 4.7% in the nine months to 30 September 2026 once the Safeguard sale is removed, while Print adjusted EBITDA fell USD 18.7 million and the three other segments added USD 32.1 million. The loss of print cash flow is gradual enough to plan around but does not stop.
  Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_dlx.htm.jsonl, page 114; 10-Q_2026-09-30_dlx.htm.jsonl, pages 25, 27, 29; calculation C-12; calculation C-13; calculation C-15; calculation C-29
- Celero is large relative to the segment it joins, yet nothing in the documents shows its earnings. The pro forma statement implies about USD 148.6 million of Celero revenue in the nine months of 2025, 50.0% of the USD 297.2 million of existing Merchant Services revenue, and Merchant Services grew 3.7% in the third quarter without Celero against 37.9% as reported, so reported growth in the segment is mostly acquired.
  Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_dlx.htm.jsonl, pages 13, 26–27; calculation C-16; calculation C-17; calculation C-18
- The purchase was debt-funded and carries a heavy intangible component. Goodwill is USD 385.6 million, 58.0% of the USD 664.6 million consideration transferred, debt principal rose USD 660.5 million in the third quarter to USD 2,025.5 million, and a purchase multiple cannot be calculated because Celero earnings are not supplied, so creditors cannot test the price against earnings.
  Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_dlx.htm.jsonl, pages 12–13, 18; calculation C-19; calculation C-20
- No sponsor or controlling holder is named, which removes sponsor extraction as a documented risk and also removes any identified source of equity support. The common stock is listed with thousands of holders of record, but the proxy statement that would show large holders and related-person transactions is not supplied, so the absence is a limit of the documents and not proof of dispersed ownership.
  Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_dlx.htm.jsonl, page 1; 10-K_2025-12-31_dlx.htm.jsonl, pages 41, 117–118
- Distributions are modest and capped. Dividends paid were USD 55.2 million in FY2025, 31.5% of free cash flow, no shares have been repurchased since the first quarter of 2020, and the amended credit agreement caps dividends and repurchases at USD 75.0 million a year, up from a USD 60.0 million cap that applied above 2.75x leverage. The cap leaves USD 19.8 million of room above the FY2025 dividend before further payments allowed by leverage tests whose terms are not supplied.
  Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_dlx.htm.jsonl, pages 56, 58; 10-Q_2026-06-30_dlx.htm.jsonl, page 17; 10-Q_2026-09-30_dlx.htm.jsonl, page 20; calculation C-21; calculation C-22
- Management's leverage target gives way to strategy. The first-quarter release says the 3x leverage target was reached, the Celero purchase was announced on 18 June 2026, and the amended credit agreement allows total leverage of 4.75x through 30 June 2027, so the covenant and not the target is the working limit on how far leverage can rise.
  Basis: EX99.1_2026-03-31_dlx_exhibit991050620268-k-htm.htm.jsonl, page 1; EX99.1_2026-06-30_dlx_tm2621737d1-ex99-1-htm.htm.jsonl, page 1; 10-Q_2026-09-30_dlx.htm.jsonl, page 20
- Acquisition appetite is a pattern: large purchases are funded with debt and small ones with cash. First American Payment Systems in 2021, which the issuer called the largest acquisition in its history, was financed with USD 500.0 million of 8.0% senior unsecured notes, the 2025 CheckMatch purchase cost USD 24.6 million in cash and a residual commission buy-out cost USD 36.0 million, while disposals are small, such as USD 22.8 million of net proceeds for Safeguard.
  Basis: 10-K_2024-12-31_dlx.htm.jsonl, page 24; 10-K_2025-12-31_dlx.htm.jsonl, pages 56, 92; 10-Q_2026-09-30_dlx.htm.jsonl, pages 14, 21
- Disclosure supports monitoring of the base business but not of the acquired business or of legal capacity. The audit opinions are unqualified, internal control was effective at year-end 2025, covenant compliance is stated each quarter and segment results use the chief executive's measure, but Celero is excluded from the 2026 internal control assessment, covenant ratios are not printed and the executed agreements are not supplied.
  Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_dlx.htm.jsonl, pages 64, 66, 116; 10-Q_2026-09-30_dlx.htm.jsonl, pages 20, 25, 50
- Customer concentration is low but channel dependence is high. No customer exceeded 10% of consolidated revenue in the last three years, yet checks, 32.3% of FY2025 revenue, reach customers through financial institutions and banks are the largest customer segment of Data Solutions, so bank consolidation or in-house moves would hit Print and Data Solutions together.

- Sponsor and governance risk is Medium. The level is driven by a debt-funded acquisition that raised leverage while dividends continued, because that reduces the cash left for debt and leaves interest and covenant sensitivity higher, which implies weaker protection for creditors until deleveraging resumes; it is held at Medium and not High because no extraction, priming or sponsor behaviour is documented, and it cannot be firmer because the executed agreements and the proxy statement are missing.
  Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_dlx.htm.jsonl, pages 12, 18, 20; 10-K_2025-12-31_dlx.htm.jsonl, pages 56, 117–118; calculation C-20; calculation C-21

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest case against the view that the transition is being financed at creditors' risk is that the growth segments are already delivering: payments and data revenue rose 14.6% and their adjusted EBITDA rose 18.0% in the nine months, management raised the 2026 outlook for adjusted EBITDA to USD 470 million to USD 480 million after the closing, cost synergies of more than USD 15 million are expected, and free cash flow of USD 134.9 million was 3.14x the USD 43.0 million of dividends paid. The view would improve if year-end results show debt falling and Celero earnings supporting the price. It would worsen if Print declines faster than the 4.5% to 5.7% yearly pace seen so far, if payments to shareholders rise while leverage is high, or if the executed agreements show baskets that let value move outside the creditor group.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_dlx.htm.jsonl, pages 13, 25, 27, 29; EX99.1_2026-06-30_dlx_tm2621737d1-ex99-1-htm.htm.jsonl, page 1; EX99.1_2026-09-30_dlx_exhibit991110420268-k-htm.htm.jsonl, page 2; calculation C-14; calculation C-15; calculation C-12; calculation C-21

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

Deluxe bought Celero Commerce on 31 July 2026 for cash consideration of about USD 625.0 million plus seller transaction expenses and other adjustments, a purchase the issuer announced on 18 June 2026. The consideration transferred was USD 664.6 million, of which USD 385.6 million is goodwill and USD 258.0 million is intangible assets, and the allocation is still preliminary. Celero contributed USD 33.5 million of revenue and a net loss of USD 1.9 million in the two months it was owned, and management expects cost synergies of more than USD 15 million without giving a phasing.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_dlx.htm.jsonl, pages 12–13; EX99.1_2026-06-30_dlx_tm2621737d1-ex99-1-htm.htm.jsonl, page 1; calculation C-19

The documents state that the purchase was financed through the amended credit agreement with additional term loan borrowing and a revolver draw, and no equity issue or asset sale is described as a source. Debt principal rose USD 660.5 million in the third quarter to USD 2,025.5 million: the term loan rose USD 408.0 million to USD 800.0 million, revolver borrowings rose USD 194.5 million to USD 200.5 million and the securitization facility rose USD 58.0 million to USD 100.0 million. The amended and restated agreement of 31 July 2026 provides a USD 800.0 million first lien term loan and a USD 400.0 million first lien revolver, both due on 31 July 2031, with quarterly term loan instalments of USD 15.0 million from 31 December 2026 and debt issuance costs of USD 12.6 million.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_dlx.htm.jsonl, page 15; 10-Q_2026-09-30_dlx.htm.jsonl, pages 12, 18–19; calculation C-20

Maximum total leverage under the amended agreement is 4.75x through 30 June 2027, then 4.25x for the next two quarters and 4.00x from 31 March 2028, and the cap on dividends and repurchases is USD 75.0 million a year. In July 2026 the issuer also entered interest rate swaps with a notional of USD 600.0 million at a fixed rate of 4.13% that mature in July 2030, and USD 500.5 million of debt stayed variable after the swaps. No new notes were issued in the nine months to 30 September 2026; the 2029 senior unsecured notes and the 2029 senior secured notes are unchanged.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_dlx.htm.jsonl, pages 14, 19–21, 36

The executed credit agreement, the equity purchase agreement and the guarantor list are not supplied, so lien priority of the notes, baskets for further debt and distributions, and any guarantee from Celero are not established. The issuer's pro forma statement gives revenue and net income but no earnings before interest, so a purchase multiple is not calculable.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_dlx.htm.jsonl, pages 13, 20
