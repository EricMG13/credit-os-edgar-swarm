The source set does not include standalone executed Celero acquisition or credit-agreement documents. Those omissions prevent a source-readiness conclusion on complete contractual terms; adding the executed agreements and amendments would improve coverage of purchase-price mechanics, covenant definitions, baskets and other operative legal terms.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_dlx.htm.jsonl, pages 91–92; 10-Q_2026-09-30_dlx.htm.jsonl, pages 20–23, 49–50
