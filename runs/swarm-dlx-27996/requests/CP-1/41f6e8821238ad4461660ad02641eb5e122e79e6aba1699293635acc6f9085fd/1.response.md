## Findings

### Bottom line

Deluxe’s September 2026 credit profile reflects substantially higher debt following the Celero acquisition, while reported cash generation and adjusted EBITDA remain positive; creditors therefore face greater leverage and a concentrated 2029 refinancing wall despite meaningful committed revolver availability. Covenant compliance is stated, but actual covenant ratios and headroom are not disclosed.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_dlx.htm.jsonl, pages 19–21; EX99.1_2026-09-30_dlx_exhibit991110420268-k-htm.htm.jsonl, pages 4–7; calculation ND26; calculation LTM-EBITDA; calculation LTM-AEBITDA

### Key findings

The July acquisition and related amended financing took gross principal debt to USD 2025.5 million and carrying debt to USD 2004.1 million at September 30, 2026. Net debt was USD 1929.2 million after deducting only unrestricted cash; this basis excludes separately presented finance and operating lease liabilities and does not deduct restricted settlement cash. The acquisition therefore increased leverage even as issuer-adjusted LTM EBITDA reached USD 459.8 million.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_dlx.htm.jsonl, pages 12, 19–20; EX99.1_2026-09-30_dlx_exhibit991110420268-k-htm.htm.jsonl, page 5; calculation ND26; calculation LTM-AEBITDA

At September 30, 2026, cash of USD 74.9 million and undrawn committed revolving capacity of USD 189.8 million provide a USD 264.7 million liquidity pool before future operating needs. The facility availability is already net of USD 9.7 million of letters of credit. The debt ladder is weighted to 2029 and 2031, and the source does not provide the covenant-defined EBITDA needed to calculate headroom against the maintenance tests.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_dlx.htm.jsonl, pages 19–21; calculation LIQ26; calculation MAT26

Nine-month operating cash flow and free cash flow were USD 215.9 million and USD 134.9 million, respectively, but they include working-capital movements and precede the USD 642.9 million acquisition outflow. Settlement processing obligations declined by USD 256.9 million in financing cash flow; this is a non-debt operating float movement and should not be read as debt repayment. Restricted cash was USD 19.3 million at September 30, 2026 and is unavailable for net-debt offset under the issuer’s stated definition.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_dlx.htm.jsonl, pages 4–5, 10; EX99.1_2026-09-30_dlx_exhibit991110420268-k-htm.htm.jsonl, pages 5, 9; calculation FCF9M26; calculation WC9M26

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The company reported compliance with all debt covenants at September 30, 2026 and says cash generated from operations and revolver availability are expected to meet operational needs, contractual obligations and debt service over the next twelve months. That evidence moderates near-term liquidity concerns, but it does not quantify covenant cushion; disclosed thresholds step down over time, and the acquired debt remains exposed to refinancing well before the 2031 facility maturity. Actual agreement-defined ratios, permitted add-backs and lender calculations would be needed to show whether the reported compliance provides robust headroom.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_dlx.htm.jsonl, pages 20–21, 33, 46; 10-K_2025-12-31_dlx.htm.jsonl, pages 107–108

The strongest operating counterweight is growth in the payments and data businesses and higher consolidated adjusted EBITDA, including Celero from its July closing date. The contrary pressure is continued Print weakness, acquisition-related amortization and financing costs, and non-recurring adjustment categories that may recur. Evidence that post-acquisition cash conversion and debt reduction persist after transaction costs and settlement-float movements would strengthen the operating case; lower growth, ongoing exceptional costs or weaker working capital would make the leverage increase more consequential.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_dlx.htm.jsonl, pages 12–13, 42–43; EX99.1_2026-09-30_dlx_exhibit991110420268-k-htm.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 5–7; 10-K_2025-12-31_dlx.htm.jsonl, pages 45, 53–55

### Limitations

The issuer’s stated unadjusted EBITDA in the Q3 earnings release is derived from net income, non-controlling interest, depreciation and amortization, interest expense and income tax provision. I apply that issuer bridge consistently to annual periods; EBIT plus depreciation and amortization is a different operating measure and would produce lower leverage denominators. Adjusted EBITDA is a separate issuer non-GAAP measure with specified adjustments, not cash flow and not the covenant-defined figure.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_dlx.htm.jsonl, page 51; 10-Q_2026-09-30_dlx.htm.jsonl, page 39; EX99.1_2026-09-30_dlx_exhibit991110420268-k-htm.htm.jsonl, pages 4, 7

The acquisition purchase-price allocation remains preliminary. The public sources summarize, but do not include the complete operative amended credit agreement text, so clause-level definitions, exact floating spreads and floors, collateral exceptions and relative lien priority cannot be independently established here.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_dlx.htm.jsonl, pages 12–13, 19–23, 51

The gross debt figure follows the balance-sheet debt lines and includes the receivables securitization financing; it excludes finance and operating leases shown separately. Restricted cash remains in settlement-processing assets or other assets and is not netted against debt. The cash-flow statement includes restricted cash in its cash roll-forward, while the net-debt definition uses cash and cash equivalents only.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_dlx.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 10, 18–20; 10-K_2025-12-31_dlx.htm.jsonl, pages 51, 90, 110
