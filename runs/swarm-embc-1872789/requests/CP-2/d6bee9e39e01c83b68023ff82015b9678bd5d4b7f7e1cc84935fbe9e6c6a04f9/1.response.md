## Findings

### Bottom line

Embecta is a weak and weakening credit: cash generation is still sound, but United States pen needle earnings are eroding, leverage is rising and cash is going to equity holders, leaving creditors little cushion if the decline continues.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); Business & governance (CP-1A); calculation C1; calculation C9

Net leverage rose to 4.67x at 2026-06-30 from 4.12x at the 2025 year end, and pro forma for the dividend payable on 2026-09-15 net debt is USD 1,241.0 million and net leverage 4.67x. Both agencies cut their ratings in May 2026, to B3 from B1 at Moody's and to B from B+ at S&P. Free cash flow of USD 164.6 million over the last twelve months is the main support, but it was far lower in earlier years and the revolver covenants tighten from 2028. Headroom against them is not established because the definitions are not supplied.

### Key findings

The core business is losing price and volume in the United States, and that is where the earnings come from. United States revenue fell 20.6% in nine months 2026 and pen needle revenue fell 13.2%, while group revenue lost USD 60.0 million to volume and USD 27.4 million to price against nine-month revenue of USD 816.4 million a year earlier. Cost of products sold rose to USD 311.9 million from USD 298.1 million while revenue fell, so price concessions went straight to gross profit and gross margin dropped to 58.7% from 63.5%. Management attributes the shortfall to competition and soft market volumes and assumes both continue for the rest of the year, so the erosion should be read as the base case, not a one-quarter event.

### Strengths and weaknesses

The credit rests on four supports. Cash conversion is the strongest: free cash flow of USD 164.6 million is 61.9% of reported EBITDA with capital expenditure at 0.9% of revenue, so scheduled term loan amortisation of USD 9.5 million a year is covered 17.33 times by free cash flow after interest. The maturity runway is the second: apart from small scheduled amortisation and the revolver, no principal falls due before March 2029, and the senior secured notes of USD 700.0 million carry fixed rates, 47.6% of principal. Third, management has shown it will favour lenders when pressed, with USD 245.4 million of voluntary prepayments and a dividend cut. Fourth, international revenue rose 7.5% and the generic GLP-1 co-packaging pipeline gives a route to growth outside United States pen needles.

### Company description

Embecta sells pen needles, syringes and safety devices for insulin injection from three manufacturing sites, in one operating segment. Pen needles were 68.6% of nine-month 2026 revenue and the United States 46.0%, down from 53.6% in fiscal 2025. Demand follows insulin users and recurs, which supports cash flow visibility, but the business is concentrated in pen needles, with three distributors taking about 42% of gross sales in fiscal 2025, so a price or volume loss in United States pen needles reaches group cash flow directly and lenders have no second earnings engine yet. Owen Mumford, acquired on 2026-05-15, adds drug delivery devices but is small.

### Ownership and group structure

Embecta is a listed company with no identified sponsor or controlling holder and a few thousand holders of record, which removes sponsor extraction risk and also sponsor support, so creditors rely on internal cash flow and board policy. BD, the former parent, supplies all cannulas, leases the Holdrege plant and can end supply on a change of control, so a sale of the company cannot be assumed to benefit creditors. Beneficial ownership and board consent rights sit in a proxy statement that was not supplied, and the guarantor group is described only generically, so structural subordination of any creditor class is not assessed here.
Basis: Business & governance (CP-1A); 10-K_2025-09-30_embc.htm.jsonl, page 18; 10-K_2025-09-30_embc.htm.jsonl, page 77

### Revenue drivers and pricing power

Revenue is recurring and non-discretionary for insulin users, regulated and reimbursed by payers, concentrated in pen needles and the United States, exposed to substitution and structurally declining in the United States. Sales deductions took 34.9% of gross sales in fiscal 2025 against 26.8% in fiscal 2023, and in nine months 2026 price cost USD 27.4 million, 3.4% of prior-year revenue, and volume USD 60.0 million, 7.3%. Low-cost local providers, the shift from vials to pens, new entrants offering payers more choice and proposed competitive bidding for diabetes devices all act on price, so pricing power is weak and the first break in the credit is net price, which volume loss then compounds.

### Competitive forces

Rivalry is intense and takes the form of price. Novo Nordisk, MTD Group and Terumo Medical compete in diabetes injection, local low-cost providers have entered, and the shift from vials to pens has made the category highly competitive, so sales deductions rise and net price erodes; creditors face lower gross profit per unit and less pricing room to defend EBITDA.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_embc.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-K_2025-09-30_embc.htm.jsonl, page 80; calculation C9

Entry is easier than the brand suggests. Competitors new to pen needles and syringes have started to engage payers and give them more formulary choice, while Embecta's own market-appropriate pen needles are still under review by the United States FDA and BSI, so its lower-cost response is not yet available and pricing pressure continues in the meantime.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_embc.htm.jsonl, page 23; EX99.1_2026-06-30_embc_q32026ex-991-htm.htm.jsonl, page 2

Substitutes act on volume. Oral and once-weekly therapies, including GLP-1s and once-weekly insulin, have delayed insulin initiation and reduced demand, and insulin therapy in developed markets continues to move to infusion pumps. The same GLP-1 trend gives a co-packaging opening, with launches in India, Canada and Brazil and a United States small pack expected, but that revenue is not quantified, so it cannot yet offset lost pen needle volume.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_embc.htm.jsonl, page 80; EX99.1_2026-06-30_embc_q32026ex-991-htm.htm.jsonl, page 2

Buyer power sits with three distributors that took about 42% of gross sales and with payers that control formulary access; proposed competitive bidding by the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services would add a price-setting buyer. Buyer power is the direct route from payer decisions to the sales deductions that reached 34.9% of gross sales.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_embc.htm.jsonl, pages 23, 26; calculation C9

Supplier power is the force that can stop sales outright. BD is the sole source of cannulas and may end supply on a change of control, some resins and rubber stoppers are not always available from multiple sources, and tariffs may raise the cost of goods, so a supply interruption would stop sales while fixed costs and debt service continue.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_embc.htm.jsonl, pages 18–19; 10-K_2025-09-30_embc.htm.jsonl, page 80; Business & governance (CP-1A)

### Downside scenario

The initiating condition is that the nine-month 2026 pattern in United States pen needles continues into fiscal 2027: payers and distributors keep taking price and volume, and the receivable buyer does not increase purchases. The first operational break is net price, because concessions reach gross profit with no cost offset, and fixed manufacturing cost is then spread over fewer units. If reported EBITDA fell at the 23.9% rate that issuer-adjusted EBITDA fell in nine months 2026, last-twelve-months EBITDA would be about USD 202.3 million; on unchanged net debt of USD 1,240.4 million (USD 1,241.0 million pro forma for the dividend payable on 2026-09-15) net leverage would be 6.13x and cover on net interest expense 2.09x, against 4.67x and 2.75x now; 6.13x is above the first lien level of 4.75x, although that test is defined differently. This is an illustration at an observed rate, not a forecast, and covenant headroom under it is not established.
Basis: EX99.1_2026-06-30_embc_q32026ex-991-htm.htm.jsonl, pages 2–4; Financial foundation (CP-1); calculation C4; calculation C13

Liquidity would hold for a time: free cash flow would stay positive, cash and undrawn commitments are USD 584.8 million before the amendment and USD 394.8 million after it on an unchanged drawing, and the pressure points fall on known dates. The USD 100.0 million of commitments due on 2027-03-31 is the first refinancing event, the 2028 covenant steps are the first test of leverage, and the term loan in 2029 and notes in 2030 follow with debt already marked at 81.5% of carrying value. Management's levers are to stop buybacks, keep the dividend at USD 0.01 a share, direct cash to voluntary prepayments as it did in fiscal 2025, use the cost review and rely on receivable sales, but capital expenditure is already low, so little room remains there. Resilience looks sufficient to avoid a liquidity crisis within twelve months on the evidence supplied, and not sufficient to meet a first lien limit of 3.75x in 2028 without an EBITDA recovery or large debt reduction. Observable triggers are fourth-quarter fiscal 2026 results, the pace of the United States decline, revolver drawings and buyback pace.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_embc.htm.jsonl, pages 21–22; 10-Q_2026-06-30_embc.htm.jsonl, pages 52–53; EX99.1_2026-03-31_embc_q22026ex-991-htm.htm.jsonl, page 2; calculation C5; calculation C6; calculation C8

### Overall credit view

Overall, Embecta presents a weak business profile supported by a brand the company calls widely recognised, low capital intensity and cash conversion of 61.9% in the last twelve months, but credit quality is constrained by United States price and volume erosion, net leverage of 4.67x (4.67x pro forma on net debt of USD 1,241.0 million for the dividend payable on 2026-09-15) and a policy tilt toward buybacks and acquisition. Cash-flow durability appears adequate rather than strong because free cash flow was volatile before the last year and depends in part on receivable sales. Refinancing capacity is likely to depend on stabilising United States earnings before the revolver commitments of USD 100.0 million due on 2027-03-31 are addressed and before USD 1,318.0 million falls due in fiscal 2029 and 2030. The primary downside path is continued price and volume erosion that lifts leverage into tightening covenants while the debt is already marked at 81.5% of carrying value and rated B3 by Moody's and B by S&P. Further analysis would need the executed covenant definitions and compliance figures, the fiscal 2026 annual results, agency reports and outlooks, peer data and the split of the revolver drawing. Fundamental credit completed. Top risk: continued United States pen needle price and volume erosion while leverage rises.
