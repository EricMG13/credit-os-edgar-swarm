## Findings

### Bottom line

Embecta is a listed diabetes injection supplier with no identified sponsor whose core United States pen needle business is eroding on price and volume, so creditors depend on a shrinking cash engine and a former parent that supplies its key input.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_embc.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-K_2025-09-30_embc.htm.jsonl, page 5; 10-K_2025-09-30_embc.htm.jsonl, page 77; calculation C2

United States revenue fell 20.6% in nine months 2026 and the share of gross sales lost to rebates and discounts rose from 26.8% in fiscal 2023 to 34.9% in fiscal 2025. In May 2026 management cut the quarterly dividend to USD 0.01 a share and authorised a USD 100.0 million buyback, and it closed a revolver-funded acquisition, which moves financial policy from deleveraging toward shareholder returns and growth. Ownership and board detail are only partly documented, so governance risk is rated Medium.
Basis: EX99.1_2026-06-30_embc_q32026ex-991-htm.htm.jsonl, pages 3–4; 10-K_2025-09-30_embc.htm.jsonl, page 122; 10-Q_2026-06-30_embc.htm.jsonl, pages 23, 34; calculation C2; calculation C3

### Key findings

The core is concentrated and commoditising, and the damage is in the United States. Pen needles were 68.6% of nine-month revenue, United States revenue fell 20.6% and pen needles fell 13.2%, with volume costing USD 60.0 million and price USD 27.4 million of revenue. Rebates and discounts took 34.9% of gross sales in fiscal 2025 against 26.8% two years earlier, so competition is already reaching net price, and gross margin fell to 58.7% from 63.5%. Management blames increased competitive dynamics and soft market volumes and assumes they persist, which means the decline in earnings per unit of debt is not a one-quarter event.
Basis: EX99.1_2026-06-30_embc_q32026ex-991-htm.htm.jsonl, pages 2–4; 10-K_2025-09-30_embc.htm.jsonl, page 122; EX99.1_2026-03-31_embc_q22026ex-991-htm.htm.jsonl, page 2; calculation C1; calculation C2; calculation C3; calculation C11

Dependence on the former parent is structural and narrows strategic options. BD sells Embecta all of its cannulas, keeps the production and the intellectual property, leases the Holdrege plant to Embecta and may end cannula supply if Embecta changes control, while a tax agreement bars certain actions without a BD opinion or consent. A takeover, large equity issue or sale of the company is therefore not a clean source of creditor protection, and supply risk sits with a counterparty that cannot be replaced quickly; the annual purchase value from BD is not stated.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_embc.htm.jsonl, page 5; 10-K_2025-09-30_embc.htm.jsonl, page 18; 10-K_2025-09-30_embc.htm.jsonl, page 118

Financial policy was creditor-friendly in fiscal 2025 and has turned mixed in 2026. Embecta prepaid USD 175.1 million of term loan voluntarily in fiscal 2025 and USD 70.3 million in nine months 2026, and cut the quarterly dividend, which saves USD 8.1 million a quarter. In the same nine months it repaid USD 127.5 million of debt, drew USD 180.0 million on the revolver for the acquisition, so total principal rose by USD 52.5 million, and it began a USD 100.0 million buyback although management had said it expected to repay about USD 150 million of debt during 2026. Cash that could repay revolver debt is being split with equity holders while leverage is rising.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_embc.htm.jsonl, page 129; 10-Q_2026-06-30_embc.htm.jsonl, pages 22–23; EX99.1_2026-03-31_embc_q22026ex-991-htm.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-06-30_embc.htm.jsonl, page 34; calculation C5; calculation C6; calculation C12

The Owen Mumford acquisition diversifies the business but adds debt, an earn-out and integration risk before it adds earnings. Purchase consideration was USD 208.0 million, including contingent consideration with a fair value of USD 38.9 million at 2026-06-30 and a maximum of GBP 50.0 million, and the target contributed USD 13.8 million, 5.1% of third-quarter revenue, after closing. Other revenue was only 3.1% of nine-month revenue, so diversification does not yet offset the United States decline, and the 2026 integration and acquisition costs reduce free cash flow.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_embc.htm.jsonl, pages 8–10; 10-Q_2026-06-30_embc.htm.jsonl, page 28; 10-Q_2026-06-30_embc.htm.jsonl, page 34; calculation C7; calculation C10

Reporting control has improved but event risk has risen. A material weakness in internal control first reported for fiscal 2024 was concluded remediated at 2025-09-30, which supports confidence in reported figures, yet a securities class action over fiscal 2026 revenue guidance was filed on 2026-06-17 and the filing says no loss range can be estimated. The combined Chairman, President and Chief Executive Officer role concentrates authority over capital allocation, and an independent lead director is not stated.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_embc.htm.jsonl, page 144; 10-Q_2026-06-30_embc.htm.jsonl, page 14; EX99.1_2026-06-30_embc_q32026ex-991-htm.htm.jsonl, page 1

Ownership is the largest information gap. No sponsor or controlling holder is identified and only a few thousand holders of record are stated, which removes sponsor extraction risk and sponsor support alike; creditors rely on internal cash flow and board policy. Beneficial ownership, the remaining stake of the former parent and board consent rights are in a proxy statement that was not supplied.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_embc.htm.jsonl, page 77; 10-K_2025-09-30_embc.htm.jsonl, page 145

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest contrary evidence is that international revenue grew 7.5% in nine months 2026 to 54.0% of revenue, over 75% of revenue is already carried by products relaunched under the embecta label, about 40% of identified GLP-1 co-packaging partners are in negotiations or have signed, and management raised its adjusted margin and earnings guidance in August while reaffirming revenue guidance. Voluntary prepayments of USD 245.4 million over fiscal 2025 and nine months 2026 and the dividend cut show a willingness to favour lenders when pressed. The view would improve if United States revenue stabilised for two consecutive quarters, if regulators cleared the market-appropriate pen needles, or if the executed documents showed no change-of-control hazard, and it would worsen if buybacks accelerate or cannula supply is disrupted.
Basis: EX99.1_2026-06-30_embc_q32026ex-991-htm.htm.jsonl, pages 2–4; EX99.1_2026-03-31_embc_q22026ex-991-htm.htm.jsonl, page 4; calculation C2; calculation C12

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

The only completed transaction in the period is the acquisition of Owen Mumford, a UK medical device and drug delivery company founded in 1952, which closed on 2026-05-15. The upfront cash payment was GBP 126.0 million (USD 169.9 million) including estimated acquired cash, with up to GBP 50.0 million more payable on sales of its auto-injector platform; purchase consideration was USD 208.0 million including contingent consideration of USD 38.1 million at fair value, and the final price is subject to a working capital adjustment.
Basis: EX99.1_2026-03-31_embc_ss6329586-ex9901-htm.htm.jsonl, pages 1–2; 10-Q_2026-06-30_embc.htm.jsonl, pages 8–9; 10-Q_2026-06-30_embc.htm.jsonl, page 34

Embecta funded the closing by drawing about USD 180.0 million on its revolver and had repaid USD 50.1 million by 2026-06-30, leaving USD 129.9 million drawn. No new bonds or term debt were issued in fiscal 2026; the notes and the term loan date from the 2022 separation financing and are unchanged apart from term loan prepayments.
Basis: EX99.1_2026-06-30_embc_q32026ex-991-htm.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-06-30_embc.htm.jsonl, page 22; 10-K_2025-09-30_embc.htm.jsonl, page 128

After the period, on 2026-08-06, the first lien credit agreement was amended: revolver commitments fell from USD 500.0 million to USD 310.0 million, USD 210.0 million of them were extended to 2028-12-30 at SOFR plus 3.50% and USD 100.0 million still matures on 2027-03-31, and covenants and monthly liquidity reporting for revolving lenders were added. This is a subsequent event and is not folded into any period figure.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_embc.htm.jsonl, pages 52–53

Capital allocation actions around the deal were a three-year USD 100.0 million buyback authorisation, with USD 8.7 million repurchased, a cut in the quarterly dividend from USD 0.15 to USD 0.01 a share and the sale of patch pump intellectual property and assets for USD 10.0 million.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_embc.htm.jsonl, page 23; 10-Q_2026-06-30_embc.htm.jsonl, page 34; 10-K_2025-09-30_embc.htm.jsonl, page 141
