## Findings

### Bottom line
The eight supplied filings and earnings releases are sufficient for a credit snapshot of Oceaneering as of 2026-11-18, so the credit work can rest on issuer-reported figures; no executed legal document, rating or bond price is supplied, which limits covenant, rating and relative-value conclusions.

The evidence is current: the latest balance sheet is dated 2026-09-30 and the latest filing was signed 2026-10-22, which is 27 days before the cutoff. It already reflects the July 2026 refinancing, in which USD 500 million of senior notes due 2034 replaced the 2028 notes and the revolving facility was enlarged to USD 345 million, so current debt terms come from the third-quarter report and not from the 2025 annual report.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_oii-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-09-30_oii-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 16–19; calculation C7

### Key findings
- The financial record is continuous to the latest quarter and ties internally. The first three 2026 quarters add to the nine-month totals for revenue and adjusted EBITDA, and the fourth quarter of 2025, which has no filing of its own, is recoverable both from the first-quarter 2026 release and from the 2025 annual report less nine months.
Basis: calculation C1; calculation C2; calculation C3; 10-K_2025-12-31_oii-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 83; EX-99.1_2026-04-22_exhibit_991x8kx1qx2026.htm.jsonl, page 6; EX-99.1_2026-04-22_exhibit_991x8kx1qx2026.htm.jsonl, page 9
- Debt is disclosed instrument by instrument but only as filing summaries. At 2026-09-30 the only borrowing is the senior notes due 2034 with principal of USD 500 million, and the filings do not state whether those notes are guaranteed or secured; the revolving facility of USD 345 million is guaranteed by certain subsidiaries and secured by first-priority liens, and has no borrowings and no letters of credit issued under it.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_oii-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 16; 10-Q_2026-09-30_oii-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 18; 10-Q_2026-09-30_oii-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 19; calculation C5
- The third-quarter report controls debt terms. The 2025 annual report still shows the 2028 notes as the nearest maturity and a revolving facility of USD 215 million maturing in April 2027; the July 2026 notes offering, tender offer and amendment supersede both.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_oii-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 62; 10-K_2025-12-31_oii-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 64; 10-Q_2026-09-30_oii-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 17–19
- Letters of credit are the liquidity input the sources do not close. The 2025 annual report states USD 77 million outstanding at 2025-12-31 outside the revolving facility, no later total is printed, and the third-quarter report states only that USD 8.5 million of restricted cash backs letters of credit and corporate cards.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_oii-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 109; 10-Q_2026-09-30_oii-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 6; 10-Q_2026-09-30_oii-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 16; calculation C6
- Covenant terms are summarised, not documented. The revolving facility requires net leverage of no more than 3.25x and interest coverage of at least 3.00x, tested quarterly, and the issuer reports compliance, but neither the definitions nor the issuer's own ratio levels are printed, so any headroom is an estimate on statement figures.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_oii-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 20
- Two small disagreements inside the filings are kept visible. The tender offer is described as about USD 397 million of 2028 notes in the debt note and about USD 400 million in the liquidity discussion, and the 2034 notes proceeds are about USD 492 million in the second-quarter report and about USD 491 million in the third-quarter report, where the later figure agrees to the cash flow statement.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_oii-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-Q_2026-09-30_oii-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 17; 10-Q_2026-09-30_oii-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 18; 10-Q_2026-09-30_oii-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 41; 10-Q_2026-06-30_oii-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 17; 10-Q_2026-06-30_oii-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 27

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view
The strongest evidence against sufficiency is that a secured revolving facility ranking ahead of unsecured notes is normally judged from the executed documents, and none is supplied: the filing summaries could omit a lien basket, a definition or a guarantor that matters to creditors. Supplying the credit agreement with both amendments and the indenture for the 2034 notes would move the covenant and structural work from summary to document, and a filing after 2026-10-22 that changes the debt terms would reopen the current-terms finding.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_oii-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 18–20; 10-Q_2026-09-30_oii-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 45

### Limitations
- No executed credit agreement or indenture is supplied, so covenant definitions, lien baskets and guarantor scope rest on the issuer's summaries; this limits any covenant headroom conclusion to the issuer's statement that it complies.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_oii-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 19–20; 10-Q_2026-09-30_oii-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 45; 10-K_2025-12-31_oii-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 78
- No credit rating or bond price is supplied, and equity prices appear only as the issuer's average repurchase prices in August and September 2026 and a mid-2025 closing price, so rating and market-implied conclusions cannot be drawn from this evidence.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_oii-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 22; 10-Q_2026-09-30_oii-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 44; 10-K_2025-12-31_oii-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 2
- The proxy statement is not supplied, so board composition, share ownership and executive pay are not established and governance conclusions rest on the annual report's officer biographies.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_oii-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 21–24; 10-K_2025-12-31_oii-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 75
- The text conversion flattens tables and one row label is missing in the nine-month adjusted net income table of the third-quarter release, so adjusted net income lines must be tied to the statement totals before use.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-10-21_exhibit_991x8kx3qx2026.htm.jsonl, page 9
