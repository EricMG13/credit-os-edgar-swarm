## Findings

### Bottom line

Tenet is a listed hospital and surgery-center operator with no identified controlling holder, so creditor risk lies in payer mix and Medicaid policy, not ownership. The CommonSpirit agreement turns a recurring services contract into USD 1,900 million of installments.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_thc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-K_2025-12-31_thc-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 175–176; 10-Q_2026-09-30_thc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 5; 10-Q_2026-09-30_thc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 10

Hospitals produce about three quarters of revenue, yet Ambulatory Care earns 40.91% of nine-month Adjusted EBITDA and 39.35% of that belongs to minority partners, so parent debt service relies on subsidiary cash. Repurchases of USD 1,615 million in the nine months are the main shareholder-return behaviour, the refinancings were creditor-neutral to supportive, and sponsor and governance risk is medium because governance detail is incomplete, not because adverse conduct was found.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-10-29_thc-20260930ex991earningsr.htm.jsonl, page 3; EX-99.1_2026-10-29_thc-20260930ex991earningsr.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-K_2025-12-31_thc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 50; calculation C08; calculation C10

### Key findings

The business has two segments: acute care hospitals, physician practices and the revenue cycle business Conifer in Hospital Operations and Services, and a large network of surgery centers and surgical hospitals held through USPI in Ambulatory Care. At 2026-09-30 the hospital segment operated 51 hospitals in eight states, and about 74% of its outpatient centers were in Arizona and Texas at 2025-12-31, so regional payer, regulatory or weather events can hit a large share of cash flow at once.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_thc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-K_2025-12-31_thc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 6; 10-Q_2026-09-30_thc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 26

Ownership is public, and no sponsor, fund or controlling holder is named in the supplied filings, but board composition, executive detail and beneficial ownership sit in a proxy statement that was not supplied, so control cannot be confirmed. Repurchases cut shares outstanding by 8.48% between 2026-01-30 and 2026-10-23 and USD 1,875 million of authorization remained at 2026-09-30, which shows a policy of returning cash that creditors should monitor against leverage.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_thc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-K_2025-12-31_thc-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 177–178; 10-Q_2026-09-30_thc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 1; 10-Q_2026-09-30_thc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 50; calculation C16

On 2026-01-27 Tenet agreed with CommonSpirit Health to end the Conifer services contract early, to receive USD 1,900 million in annual installments over three years, of which USD 540 million was settled by offset against Conifer's redemption of CommonSpirit's minority interest, leaving a note of USD 1,360 million payable from January 2027. Tenet recognized USD 1,472 million of contract-termination revenue in the nine months while only the USD 540 million settled by offset has been received, so earnings run ahead of cash, and Tenet now owns all of Conifer. The issuer's Adjusted EBITDA excludes the contract-termination revenue but includes a USD 147 million net favorable transition impact in the third quarter.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_thc-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 175–176; 10-Q_2026-03-31_thc-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-06-30_thc-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-09-30_thc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-09-30_thc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 5; 10-Q_2026-09-30_thc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 10; EX-99.1_2026-10-29_thc-20260930ex991earningsr.htm.jsonl, page 2; EX-99.1_2026-10-29_thc-20260930ex991earningsr.htm.jsonl, page 8; calculation C17

Managed care supplies most hospital patient revenue, and the Medicaid share rose to 11.90% in the nine months from 11.19% a year earlier while exchange admissions fell, so payer mix is moving against Tenet, and the top ten managed care payers produced 69% of managed care patient revenue in 2025, so contract renewals matter. The One Big Beautiful Bill Act, whose most consequential provisions take effect from 2027, may materially reduce Medicaid payments and supplemental payment programs, which is the main policy risk to hospital margins.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_thc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 17; 10-Q_2026-09-30_thc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 28; 10-Q_2026-09-30_thc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 35–36; 10-K_2025-12-31_thc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 17; 10-K_2025-12-31_thc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 30; calculation C12; calculation C13

The two refinancings of the last year, in November 2025 and September 2026, extended maturities and retired secured notes without new debt-funded distributions, and the disclosed behaviour is repurchases funded from operating cash flow with senior notes outstanding unchanged at USD 12,662 million. The issuer's own summary says the secured notes' indentures place no limit on restricted payments, so legal capacity to return more cash is wide even though willingness shown so far is moderate.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_thc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 145; 10-K_2025-12-31_thc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 148; 10-Q_2026-09-30_thc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 13; 10-Q_2026-09-30_thc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 50; Source readiness (CP-0)

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest contrary evidence is that repurchases and distributions to noncontrolling interests absorbed 75.28% of nine-month free cash flow and 88.31% of adjusted free cash flow while the secured notes' indentures, as the issuer summarises them, restrict neither dividends nor repurchases. A debt-funded repurchase, a controlling holder or related-party payments shown in the proxy statement, or a payment restriction in the executed documents would move sponsor and governance risk to high or low, and a faster Medicaid supplemental payment cut would weaken the operating view.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_thc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 148; 10-K_2025-12-31_thc-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 177–178; 10-K_2025-12-31_thc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 17; EX-99.1_2026-10-29_thc-20260930ex991earningsr.htm.jsonl, page 14; calculation C26; calculation C25

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

The CommonSpirit Health arrangements are the defining transaction of 2026. On 2026-01-27 Tenet and CommonSpirit agreed to conclude the Conifer services contract, which had been scheduled to run to the end of 2032, by the end of 2026, and the transition of services is now expected to be substantially complete by 2026-10-30; CommonSpirit agreed to pay USD 1,900 million in annual installments over three years, of which USD 540 million was satisfied on the day by offsetting the USD 540 million Conifer owed for redeeming CommonSpirit's 23.8% minority interest in Conifer, effective 2026-01-01. The rest is a non-interest-bearing note of USD 1,360 million, with annual payments of about USD 453 million due in January 2027, January 2028 and January 2029, carried at USD 1,262 million of present value at 2026-09-30, USD 98 million below face value.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_thc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 6; 10-K_2025-12-31_thc-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 175–176; 10-Q_2026-09-30_thc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 5; 10-Q_2026-09-30_thc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 10; 10-Q_2026-09-30_thc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 17; calculation C24; calculation C22; calculation C23
