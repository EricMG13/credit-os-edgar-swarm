## Findings

### Bottom line

The supplied filings and earnings releases support a credit snapshot of TENET HEALTHCARE CORP as of 2026-11-05, so creditors can rest leverage, liquidity and maturity conclusions on primary issuer disclosures, but not on executed financing terms, ratings or note-level market prices.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_thc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 118; 10-Q_2026-09-30_thc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 2–3; EX-99.1_2026-10-29_thc-20260930ex991earningsr.htm.jsonl, page 1

Financial foundation, Business & governance and Fundamental credit are each ready with limitations. The main limits are the missing proxy statement for governance detail, the absence of the indentures and credit agreement behind the covenant summaries, and no document dated after 2026-10-29. No credit conclusion is drawn in this module.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_thc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 118; 10-K_2025-12-31_thc-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 177–178; 10-Q_2026-09-30_thc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 12–13; 10-Q_2026-09-30_thc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 54

### Key findings

The set holds one audited annual report for 2025, three unaudited quarterly reports for 2026 and an earnings release for each 2026 quarter, so the latest quarter, the nine-month comparatives and a last-twelve-months window to the third quarter of 2026 can all be read from supplied statements. The latest document is dated 2026-10-29, one week before the as-of date, so events in that week are not covered.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_thc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 184; 10-Q_2026-09-30_thc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 2–3; 10-Q_2026-09-30_thc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 54; calculation C3

The notes to the 2025 annual report and the latest quarterly report print each note series, the revolving credit facility, the letter of credit facility and the covenant descriptions, together with the September 2026 refinancing and the issuer's statement that it complied with its covenants. The indentures, the credit agreement and any guarantor financial split are not supplied, so covenant levels and guarantor terms rest on the issuer's own summaries and headroom cannot be tested from the documents.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_thc-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 144–149; 10-Q_2026-09-30_thc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 12–13; 10-Q_2026-09-30_thc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 50–51

The three earnings releases state a net debt to Adjusted EBITDA ratio without defining net debt, so that ratio cannot be reproduced from the sources. The third quarter of 2026 also carries contract-termination revenue and transition benefits from the CommonSpirit agreement: the termination revenue is reported on its own line and falls outside Adjusted EBITDA, while a catch-up adjustment and a transition benefit sit inside it, so reported and adjusted earnings have to be read together.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-10-29_thc-20260930ex991earningsr.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-09-30_thc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 2–3; 10-Q_2026-09-30_thc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 17; 10-Q_2026-09-30_thc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 24

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest case against readiness is the absence of the executed financing documents: if a credit view turns on covenant headroom, guarantor priority or collateral value, the issuer's summaries would not be enough and those questions would stay conditional on further documents. A published credit rating, note-level market prices or a filing dated after 2026-10-29 would also change the picture.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_thc-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 148–149; 10-Q_2026-09-30_thc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 53
