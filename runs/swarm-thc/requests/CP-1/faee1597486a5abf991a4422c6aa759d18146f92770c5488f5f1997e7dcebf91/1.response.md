## Findings

### Bottom line

Net leverage on issuer-adjusted EBITDA improved to 2.11x at 2026-09-30 from 2.25x at 2025-12-31, so the cushion against debt service widened, but net leverage of 1.68x on reported EBITDA is flattered by CommonSpirit termination revenue and is not a safe anchor.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_thc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 2–3; 10-Q_2026-09-30_thc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 12–13; 10-Q_2026-09-30_thc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 24–25; 10-K_2025-12-31_thc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 174; calculation C03; calculation C11

The September 2026 refinancing replaced the 2027 secured notes and part of the 2028 unsecured notes with 2034 notes, so the nearest large maturity is now 2028, and USD 2,674 million of cash plus an undrawn USD 1,900 million revolver stand ahead of it. Interest cover on adjusted EBITDA reached 6.03x over the last twelve months, but repurchases and distributions to noncontrolling interests absorbed USD 2,651 million of USD 3,328 million of free cash flow.

## Gaps

- EBITDA (Reported) is derived, not stated: the issuer prints no unadjusted EBITDA, so every reported-basis ratio rests on operating income plus depreciation and amortization, which includes the 2024 gain on facility sales and the 2026 contract-termination revenue; this restricts the leverage and interest cover charts for fiscal 2024 and the 2026 periods. The action that would close it is the issuer stating an unadjusted EBITDA; until then both bases stay labelled and the adjusted ratios are the better reading. Severity High.
