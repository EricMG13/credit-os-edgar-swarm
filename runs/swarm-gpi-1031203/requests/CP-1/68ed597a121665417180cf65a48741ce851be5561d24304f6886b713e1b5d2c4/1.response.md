## Findings

### Bottom line

Net debt was USD 3,004.7 million, 3.30x last-twelve-months EBITDA, at 30 September 2026, but the USD 1.3 billion Hennessy payment after quarter-end lifts it to about 4.73x before acquired earnings, so creditors should underwrite the higher figure.
Basis: calculation C10; calculation C35; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gpi-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 13

That EBITDA is unadjusted and carries USD 78.7 million of non-cash asset impairments in the last twelve months; adding them and restructuring charges back gives 3.00x, and the company reports its own covenant ratios, 3.26x leverage against a ceiling of 5.75x and 2.78x fixed charge coverage against a floor of 1.20x, as within their limits. Cash interest was covered 3.30x, and the new notes add about USD 80 million of annual interest.
Basis: calculation C18; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gpi-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 60; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gpi-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 67

### Key findings

- Net leverage rose from 2.82x at the end of 2024 to 4.29x at the end of 2025 because net debt grew by USD 788.2 million while EBITDA fell by USD 167.1 million to USD 855.1 million, with asset impairments of USD 192.8 million and selling, general and administrative expenses up from USD 2,179.2 million to USD 2,545.5 million; in the same year acquisitions and share repurchases absorbed USD 1,101.6 million of cash.
Basis: calculation C26; calculation C27; calculation C28; 10-K_2025-12-31_gpi-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 93–95
- The easing to 3.30x at 30 September 2026 came from the USD 123.9 million third-quarter 2025 impairment leaving the twelve-month window and from USD 481.8 million of disposal proceeds in 2026, not from lower borrowing: net debt fell by USD 193.8 million in the quarter, but total debt rose to USD 3,886.2 million, 4.27x EBITDA, because the new notes pre-funded the Hennessy payment; the cash of USD 881.5 million nets against that debt only until the payment is made.
Basis: calculation C44; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gpi-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 4–5; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gpi-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 7–8; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gpi-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 13
- Cash interest paid was USD 276.2 million in the last twelve months against USD 223.6 million in 2024, so EBITDA cover fell from 4.57x to 3.30x, and the company expects the two new note series to add about USD 80 million of annual interest expense, before any earnings from Hennessy.
Basis: calculation C43; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gpi-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 26; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gpi-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 67; 10-K_2024-12-31_gpi-20241231.htm.jsonl, page 129
- Free cash flow for the nine months of 2026 was USD 174.9 million against USD 373.1 million a year earlier as operating cash flow fell by USD 223.3 million, with payables and accruals absorbing USD 145.3 million against USD 24.5 million; share repurchases fell to USD 72.4 million from USD 249.8 million, so shareholder returns are not the pressure on cash this year.
Basis: calculation C32; calculation C31; calculation C34; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gpi-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 7–8
- Company-defined liquidity was USD 2,477.0 million at 30 September 2026 against USD 883.0 million at the end of 2025, but USD 881.5 million of it is cash that sat on the balance sheet before the Hennessy payment, and Acquisition Line availability of USD 1,442.4 million is USD 295.3 million below commitment less letters of credit because of a covenant limit the filing does not print, so liquidity after the payment is not established.
Basis: calculation C38; calculation C39; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gpi-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 55; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gpi-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 59–60; 10-K_2025-12-31_gpi-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 70
- Current maturities were USD 261.8 million at 30 September 2026 and the four senior note series total USD 2,500.0 million, with the 2032 and 2035 notes pushing unsecured maturities out; the Acquisition Line balance of USD 964.0 million at the end of 2025 was repaid, leaving only USD 12.3 million of letters of credit, and the only printed ladder, at 31 December 2025, put USD 1,575.6 million in 2030, so a ladder at 30 September 2026 is not established.
Basis: calculation C41; calculation C12; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gpi-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gpi-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 21–23; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gpi-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 25; 10-K_2025-12-31_gpi-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 128
- Floorplan notes payable of USD 2,123.9 million sit outside total debt; counting them net debt would be USD 5,128.6 million, 5.63x EBITDA, and floorplan interest of USD 90.8 million is a cost that headline EBITDA leaves out, so net leverage after floorplan interest is 3.67x.
Basis: calculation C24; calculation C21; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gpi-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gpi-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 24
- The U.K. segment moved from a loss before income taxes of USD 100.3 million, which included USD 126.5 million of impairments, in the first nine months of 2025 to a profit of USD 39.6 million in 2026, while the U.S. segment's income fell from USD 487.9 million to USD 396.8 million and group revenue was USD 802.7 million lower, so the group recovery rests on a U.K. rebound while U.S. earnings weaken.
Basis: calculation C46; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gpi-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 5; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gpi-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 16–17

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest case for a lower leverage reading is that unadjusted EBITDA is after non-cash impairments, so net leverage is 3.00x on the excluding basis, and the company reports covenant leverage of 3.26x against a 5.75x ceiling, which its definitions, not supplied here, make hard to compare; disposals could also cut debt, since three dealership sales brought about USD 235 million and would alone take net leverage to 3.04x. The view would change with Hennessy's earnings and purchase accounting, which the quarterly report does not give: enough acquired EBITDA would pull the 4.73x pro forma figure down, while a weaker U.S. segment would push it up.
Basis: calculation C18; calculation C36; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gpi-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 14; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gpi-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 60
