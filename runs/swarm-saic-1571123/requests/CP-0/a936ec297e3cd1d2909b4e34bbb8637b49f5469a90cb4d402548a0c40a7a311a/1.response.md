## Findings

### Bottom line

The four SAIC filings support the requested financial foundation, business profile and fundamental credit work as of the quarter ended July 31, 2026, but not covenant headroom, ratings or events after August 31, 2026. Creditors should therefore treat any conclusion on those three points as outside the evidence.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-07-31_saic.htm.jsonl, pages 1, 14, 39; 10-K_2026-01-30_saic.htm.jsonl, pages 64, 82–85, 131
