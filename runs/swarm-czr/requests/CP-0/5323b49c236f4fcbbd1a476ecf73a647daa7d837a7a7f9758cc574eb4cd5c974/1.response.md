## Findings

### Bottom line

The seven supplied Caesars Entertainment documents support a credit snapshot of the consolidated company, but creditor conclusions on covenants, ratings, valuation and the pending merger's effect on existing debt cannot be drawn from them.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_czr-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 6–9; 10-K_2025-12-31_czr-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 158–165

The annual report and the three quarterly reports give audited and unaudited statements, facility-level debt, liquidity and segment data to September 30, 2026, so Financial foundation, Business & governance and Fundamental credit can proceed with stated limitations. The fourth quarter of 2025 is not reported on its own, and the merger with Fertitta Gaming is described only in the company's own reports: how the buyer finances it and what happens to existing notes and loans at closing are not established. Ratings, peers, market prices and executed legal documents were not supplied, so the modules that need them cannot be assessed from these sources.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_czr-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 99–102; 10-Q_2026-09-30_czr-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 15–21; calculation C1
