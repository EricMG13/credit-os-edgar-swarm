## Findings

### Bottom line

Caesars is a diversified but rent-burdened casino operator whose creditors face an ownership change, and the supplied documents do not establish how the buyer finances it or what happens to existing debt.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_czr-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 6–9; 10-Q_2026-09-30_czr-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 56–57

Adjusted EBITDA for nine months 2026 was USD 2,672 million, down from USD 2,723 million, as Regional growth of 4.5% only partly offset Las Vegas at minus 8.0%. Cash interest paid on the VICI and GLPI leases took 38.55% of that Adjusted EBITDA and cash interest on debt a further 21.71%. Fertitta Gaming agreed on May 27, 2026 to buy the company for USD 31.00 per share in cash, stockholders approved on September 22, 2026, and regulatory approvals remain. Management itself lists increased leverage from the transaction as a risk.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_czr-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 31–32; 10-Q_2026-09-30_czr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 5; 10-Q_2026-09-30_czr-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 6–9; 10-Q_2026-09-30_czr-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 45–46; 10-Q_2026-09-30_czr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 57; EX-99.1_2026-10-27_ex991-2026q3ceiearningsrel.htm.jsonl, page 1; calculation B10; calculation B17

### Key findings

- Earnings are diversified by segment but concentrated in two of them. Regional and Las Vegas produced 49.37% and 47.68% of FY2025 Adjusted EBITDA, Caesars Digital 6.51% and Managed and Branded 1.85%, while corporate costs took 5.41%.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_czr-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 68; calculation B2
- Las Vegas is the segment under pressure and carries 44.12% of nine-month Adjusted EBITDA. Its Adjusted EBITDA fell 8.0% in the first nine months of 2026, to USD 1,179 million from USD 1,281 million, and its margin fell to 40.0% from 42.6%, on fewer city-wide conventions, lower leisure visitation and softer hotel occupancy and rates.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_czr-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 45–46; calculation B4
- Regional and digital growth is real but partly reflects perimeter change and hold. Regional Adjusted EBITDA rose 4.5% to USD 1,447 million, helped by consolidating Caesars Windsor from March 3, 2026. Caesars Digital rose 19.9% to USD 181 million, yet its quarterly Adjusted EBITDA swung from minus 15.0% in the second quarter to plus 57.1% in the third, which fits the company's own warning that hold and promotional spending move digital results. The Windsor contribution is not separated.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_czr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 10; 10-Q_2026-09-30_czr-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 46–47; EX-99.1_2026-07-28_ex991-2026q2ceiearningsrel.htm.jsonl, page 1; EX-99.1_2026-10-27_ex991-2026q3ceiearningsrel.htm.jsonl, page 1
- Rent to landlords is the largest fixed claim on cash flow and sits outside the debt table. Caesars leases 25 of its 47 casinos, 18 from VICI and six from GLPI, and cash interest paid on those leases was USD 1,030 million in nine months 2026 against USD 580 million of cash interest on debt. Financing obligations of USD 13,188 million exceed debt principal of USD 11,659 million, and a lease default could let a landlord terminate and trigger a default under the debt agreements.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_czr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-09-30_czr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 5; 10-Q_2026-09-30_czr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 36; 10-K_2025-12-31_czr-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 50; EX-99.1_2026-10-27_ex991-2026q3ceiearningsrel.htm.jsonl, page 2; calculation B7; calculation B10
- Control is about to change and creditor treatment is unestablished. The cash price is about USD 6,318 million on the shares outstanding, closing needs antitrust waiting-period expiry and gaming approvals, and a USD 450 million reverse termination fee is payable by the buyer if regulatory approvals fail. How the buyer finances the purchase, who owns the buyer and what happens to existing notes and loans are not stated.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_czr-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 6–9; 10-Q_2026-09-30_czr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 1; calculation B11; calculation B12

Eleven directors signed the annual report, an executive chairman among them, and an agreement with Icahn entities covers director appointment, but its terms and every holder's stake are not supplied. No sponsor fees or dividends appear, and the one related-party lease costs USD 0.6 million a year. Sponsor and governance risk is Medium on current evidence.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a cautious view is liquidity and a light near-term maturity profile. Cash was USD 864 million and cash plus revolver capacity totalled USD 2,947 million, no revolver was drawn, and only USD 28 million of principal falls due in the rest of 2026 and USD 114 million in 2027. The company reports compliance with its financial covenants, the leverage covenant being 6.50x, and the reverse termination fee gives the company a cash backstop if regulatory approvals fail.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-10-27_ex991-2026q3ceiearningsrel.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-09-30_czr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 16; 10-Q_2026-09-30_czr-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 20–21; 10-Q_2026-09-30_czr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 9; 10-Q_2026-09-30_czr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 54

The view would improve if the merger documents show existing notes and loans staying in place on unchanged terms or repaid from buyer equity, or if Las Vegas earnings stabilise. It would worsen if the transaction adds debt at the company or triggers repayment obligations on a change of control, or if the Las Vegas decline persists while rent escalates with consumer prices.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_czr-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 56–57; 10-Q_2026-09-30_czr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 51; 10-Q_2026-09-30_czr-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 45–46

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

The pending transaction is a cash merger in which Fertitta Gaming Holdco, LLC, through Empire Merger Sub, Inc., acquires Caesars Entertainment, Inc. for USD 31.00 per share, plus a daily per-share ticking fee if closing has not occurred by June 26, 2027. The agreement was signed on May 27, 2026, the board recommended it, stockholders approved it on September 22, 2026, and closing is subject to expiry of the antitrust waiting period and certain gaming approvals. Either side may terminate if closing has not occurred by May 27, 2027, an end date that extends automatically to August 27, 2027 and then November 27, 2027 when only those approvals are outstanding. The company owes a termination fee of USD 200 million in defined circumstances and the buyer owes a reverse termination fee of USD 450 million if regulatory approvals fail or the end date passes with only those approvals outstanding.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_czr-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 6–9

The supplied documents do not describe how the buyer finances the price, who owns Fertitta Gaming Holdco, LLC, who holds the rollover shares, or whether existing notes, term loans and leases are repaid, assumed or left in place. Management lists the risk that required financing is not obtained on anticipated terms and the risk of increased leverage from the transaction. The company reported merger costs of USD 22 million for nine months 2026.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_czr-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 6–9; 10-Q_2026-09-30_czr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 10; 10-Q_2026-09-30_czr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 57

No new notes or term loans were issued in the first nine months of 2026: the debt table shows the same instruments as at year-end 2025 with the revolver repaid, and the most recent issue was USD 1.1 billion of senior notes due 2032 on October 17, 2024. In July 2025 the company redeemed all USD 546 million of its notes due 2027 using revolver borrowings and USD 225 million of proceeds from the WSOP note receivable, with a loss on extinguishment of about USD 4 million.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_czr-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 15–16; 10-K_2025-12-31_czr-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 126
