## Findings

### Bottom line

Crane NXT is a specialised, acquisition-led technology group whose creditors depend on contract-driven security revenue and a payments business with shrinking organic sales, while the governance evidence supplied is too thin to confirm or rule out a controlling holder.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_cxt.htm.jsonl, page 16; EX99.1_2026-09-30_cxt_exhibit991-pressreleasexq3-htm.htm.jsonl, page 6; 10-K_2025-12-31_cxt.htm.jsonl, page 115

Debt-funded purchases of USD 1,076.4 million since May 2024 lifted goodwill and intangibles to 60.21% of assets, and one unnamed customer supplied 12.72% of FY2025 sales. Dividends are 19.67% of free cash flow and there are no buybacks, so sponsor and governance risk is rated Medium on incomplete evidence.
Basis: calculation K5; calculation K3; calculation K1; calculation K7

### Key findings

The business is specialised and technology-led, which supports retention but concentrates demand. Crane NXT sells proprietary security features, authentication and payment or inspection equipment to central banks, governments, brands and vending, retail and gaming operators, and describes itself as a principal competitor in most of its markets. One unnamed security customer supplied 12.72% of FY2025 net sales and banknote awards, notably from the U.S. government, vary in timing and size, so a delayed award would cut debt service capacity directly.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_cxt.htm.jsonl, page 9; 10-K_2025-12-31_cxt.htm.jsonl, page 14; 10-K_2025-12-31_cxt.htm.jsonl, page 91; calculation K1

Reported growth is acquisition-led while the payments and inspection segment is shrinking organically. In Q3 2026 the detection and traceability segment's organic sales fell 5.3% while acquisitions added 23.1%, and the security segment's organic sales rose 7.2%. Management lowered its full-year sales growth outlook to about 14% from a range of 15% to 17%, which is an untested management statement.
Basis: EX99.1_2026-09-30_cxt_exhibit991-pressreleasexq3-htm.htm.jsonl, page 2; EX99.1_2026-09-30_cxt_exhibit991-pressreleasexq3-htm.htm.jsonl, page 6

Acquisition appetite is the main behavioural signal and it adds debt faster than earnings. Consideration of USD 1,076.4 million across OpSec, De La Rue Authentication and Antares Vision was funded with revolver and term loan borrowings; balance-sheet debt rose by USD 737.6 million between 2023-12-31 and 2026-09-30 and goodwill and intangibles reached 60.21% of total assets, so an integration miss would appear as impairment as well as lower cash flow.
Basis: 10-K_2024-12-31_cxt.htm.jsonl, page 74; 10-K_2025-12-31_cxt.htm.jsonl, page 83; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cxt.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cxt.htm.jsonl, page 10; calculation K5; calculation K8; calculation K3

Distributions are modest and creditor-neutral on the evidence read. Dividends of USD 39.0 million were 19.67% of FY2025 free cash flow, the quarterly dividend rose by 5.88% to USD 0.18 per share, no open-market repurchases were made in any quarter of 2026, and no sponsor, management fee or related-party leakage appears in the filings read.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_cxt.htm.jsonl, page 54; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cxt.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cxt.htm.jsonl, page 34; 10-Q_2026-03-31_cxt.htm.jsonl, page 28; 10-Q_2026-06-30_cxt.htm.jsonl, page 34; calculation K7; calculation K9

Ownership and board detail cannot be established, so the risk level rests on behaviour rather than structure. No sponsor or controlling holder is named, but board composition, independence, compensation and beneficial ownership sit in a proxy statement that was not supplied. Management reinvestment in the Antares Vision acquisition vehicle adds minority claims of USD 20.6 million in ordinary shares and USD 36.1 million in Class B shares at fair value, with put and call terms not disclosed.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_cxt.htm.jsonl, page 115; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cxt.htm.jsonl, page 9

Reporting is adequate for monitoring but silent on covenants. Quarterly reports and releases are timely and audit opinions are unqualified, but revenue recognised over time on U.S. government contracts is a critical audit matter, management excluded De La Rue Authentication from its FY2025 internal-control assessment, and no covenant ratio or compliance statement is published.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a Medium risk view is the absence of any extraction: no sponsor, no fee stream, no buybacks, dividends at 19.67% of free cash flow, unqualified audits and a backlog of USD 715.5 million of which 94% is due within two years. Management also says it is on track for a year-end deleveraging target and reports falling company net leverage. That view would win if the proxy statement showed dispersed ownership and independent oversight, and if the target proved to be set at a conservative level.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_cxt.htm.jsonl, page 54; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cxt.htm.jsonl, page 16; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cxt.htm.jsonl, page 34; EX99.1_2026-09-30_cxt_exhibit991-pressreleasexq3-htm.htm.jsonl, page 1; calculation K7; calculation K23

The view would move to High if the proxy statement showed a controlling holder with a record of debt-funded distributions, if the credit agreement allowed wide restricted payments, or if a further debt-funded acquisition were announced before leverage fell. It would move to Low if the acquired Antares Vision business showed organic growth and the put and call terms were small and fixed.

### Limitations

- The 2026 proxy statement was not supplied, so the Medium rating changes to Low if it shows dispersed beneficial ownership and independent oversight, and to High if it shows a controlling holder with a distribution record.
- The credit agreement and the indentures were not supplied, so capital allocation is judged by what was done and not by what is permitted; broad restricted payment or acquisition capacity would raise the risk level.
- Results of Antares Vision before 2026-03-31 were not supplied, so the enlarged detection and traceability segment shows acquisition-driven growth without a like-for-like trend; a stable acquired base would soften the organic-decline finding.
- The significant customer is unnamed and no contract term is disclosed, so the concentration finding cannot be sized beyond its FY2025 share of sales.
- Put and call terms for management reinvestment are not disclosed, so the cash-call risk below the parent is directional only and could be larger or smaller.

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

The Antares Vision purchase is the central transaction. Crane NXT bought a 32.3% interest on 2025-12-16 for EUR 117.3 million and completed a tender offer and squeeze-out on 2026-03-31 for EUR 244.2 million, a total consideration of USD 416.9 million including the remeasured earlier interest. It was funded with the new EUR Term Loan B, and Antares Vision personnel reinvested through ordinary and Class B shares of the acquisition vehicle, so part of the acquired equity sits outside the parent. The purchase price allocation is still preliminary, with goodwill of USD 263.1 million and borrowings assumed of USD 132.3 million.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_cxt.htm.jsonl, page 8; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cxt.htm.jsonl, page 9; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cxt.htm.jsonl, page 10; calculation K22

The two earlier purchases were also debt-funded. OpSec Security, bought on 2024-05-03 for a USD 270 million base price, drew USD 210.0 million on the revolver. De La Rue Authentication, bought on 2025-05-01 for a GBP 300 million base price (net cash paid USD 391.1 million), was funded with the delayed-draw Term Loan A and brought a consolidated Ghana tax stamp joint venture in which the company owns 49% but controls the board.
Basis: 10-K_2024-12-31_cxt.htm.jsonl, page 74; 10-K_2025-12-31_cxt.htm.jsonl, page 83

New debt came only through bank facility amendments. The Fifth Amendment on 2025-12-15 added a EUR 430 million Term Loan B to 2032-12-15, lifted the revolver to USD 800.0 million and extended the revolver and Term Loan A to 2030-12-15. The Sixth Amendment on 2026-07-02 repriced Term Loan B and raised it to EUR 480.0 million at a margin of 2.0% to 2.25%, with the extra proceeds and cash on hand used to repay part of Term Loan A. A 364-day USD 602 million bridge and a USD 831 million backstop were committed in September 2025 and terminated once term debt was placed. No new notes were issued; the 2036 and 2048 notes are the only bonds outstanding.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_cxt.htm.jsonl, page 105; 10-K_2025-12-31_cxt.htm.jsonl, page 108; 10-Q_2026-06-30_cxt.htm.jsonl, page 24; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cxt.htm.jsonl, page 20; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cxt.htm.jsonl, page 21

Overall, Crane NXT presents Medium sponsor and governance risk. Countervailing evidence is modest dividends, no buybacks and no sponsor or fee leakage.
