## Findings

### Bottom line

Leverage on reported earnings rose after the Antares Vision purchase: net debt was 3.03x LTM EBITDA at 2026-09-30 against 2.56x at year-end 2025, so creditors carry more debt against earnings that include the acquired business only from March 2026.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_cxt.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cxt.htm.jsonl, page 20; 10-K_2025-12-31_cxt.htm.jsonl, page 53; calculation C4; calculation C10; calculation C38

EBITDA covered cash interest 5.62x over the last twelve months against 6.35x for FY2025. The company's own net leverage of 2.5x is lower because it adds pre-acquisition earnings of the acquired business and issuer add-backs; the two bases cannot be reconciled from the documents supplied.
Basis: EX99.1_2026-09-30_cxt_exhibit991-pressreleasexq3-htm.htm.jsonl, page 9; calculation C39; calculation C31

### Leverage and coverage

Reported leverage rose at each year-end and again at the latest quarter because debt grew faster than earnings. Net debt over reported EBITDA moved from 1.15x at FY2023 to 1.64x, 2.56x and 3.03x at FY2024, FY2025 and 2026-09-30, while EBITDA stayed between USD 353.3 million and USD 373.4 million. The 2025 De La Rue Authentication purchase and the 2026 Antares Vision purchase were funded mainly with term loans, so debt carries both in full while earnings carry the first from May 2025 and the second from March 2026 only.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_cxt.htm.jsonl, page 53; 10-K_2025-12-31_cxt.htm.jsonl, page 83; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cxt.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cxt.htm.jsonl, page 8; calculation C3; calculation C4; calculation C10

Interest cover shows the same shift. Reported EBITDA covered cash interest 8.08x in FY2023 and 8.06x in FY2024, then 6.35x in FY2025 and 5.62x over the last twelve months, because cash interest paid rose from USD 44.1 million in FY2024 to USD 66.4 million as the debt stack grew. Cover remains above 5x, so interest servicing is not the constraint these figures show; whether a covenant ceiling is a constraint cannot be established because no covenant ratio is reported.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_cxt.htm.jsonl, page 54; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cxt.htm.jsonl, page 5; calculation C39; calculation C11

The strongest evidence against a rising-leverage reading is the company's own series: 2.9x, 2.7x and 2.5x at the three 2026 quarter-ends, with company net debt falling by USD 150.0 million to USD 1,161.5 million while its TTM Adjusted EBITDA rose from USD 448.9 million to USD 460.3 million. That series counts Antares Vision earnings from before it was owned and issuer add-backs; it would prevail if the pre-acquisition amounts were supplied and confirmed on a consistent definition.
Basis: EX99.1_2026-03-31_cxt_exhibit991-pressreleasexq1-htm.htm.jsonl, page 8; EX99.1_2026-06-30_cxt_exhibit991-pressreleasexq2-htm.htm.jsonl, page 9; EX99.1_2026-09-30_cxt_exhibit991-pressreleasexq3-htm.htm.jsonl, page 9; calculation C30

### Cash flow and liquidity

Free cash flow was USD 235.5 million over the last twelve months, after operating cash flow of USD 290.3 million and capital expenditure of USD 54.8 million, or 0.63 of reported EBITDA against 0.56 in FY2025. Acquisition payments of USD 225.4 million net of cash acquired and dividends of USD 31.1 million in the nine months of 2026 were paid from that cash flow and from term loan and revolver borrowings, so net debt rose even as free cash flow stayed positive.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_cxt.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-K_2025-12-31_cxt.htm.jsonl, page 54; calculation C13; calculation C14; calculation C15; calculation C37

Liquidity at 2026-09-30 was cash of USD 251.1 million plus an undrawn revolver of USD 570.1 million, USD 821.2 million in total, against USD 231.1 million of borrowings classified as short-term. Letters of credit usage of the revolver is not stated, so availability may be lower. The only maturity ladder is at year-end 2025: USD 9.1 million falls due in 2026 and USD 27.2 million in 2027 to 2028, with USD 326.3 million in 2029 to 2030. It appears to omit the revolver and predates the 2026 refinancing steps, so the maturity wall after 2026 is not established.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_cxt.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cxt.htm.jsonl, page 20; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cxt.htm.jsonl, page 21; 10-K_2025-12-31_cxt.htm.jsonl, page 39; calculation C23; calculation C16

Contract liabilities, interest-free customer funding that is not debt, were USD 158.4 million at 2026-09-30, USD 71.1 million above 2025-12-31 on the 2026 presentation, of which USD 46.9 million came with Antares Vision. They fund operations at no interest cost; a fall without matching deliveries would draw on cash, and the filings give no customer deposit terms.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_cxt.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cxt.htm.jsonl, page 10; calculation C34

### Data quality and definition risks

Debt appears on three bases in the documents: carrying value on the balance sheet, principal before deferred financing costs in the company's net leverage table (USD 30.1 million higher at 2026-09-30), and the contractual obligations table, which sits USD 95.3 million below carrying value at 2025-12-31 and appears to leave out the revolver. This module uses carrying value and shows the others beside it. Reported EBITDA here is operating profit plus depreciation and amortization; the issuer's Adjusted EBITDA is USD 52.5 million higher for the nine months of 2026 because it adds back transaction, acquisition and restructuring items and starts from net income, so it also holds non-operating income.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_cxt.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cxt.htm.jsonl, page 20; EX99.1_2026-09-30_cxt_exhibit991-pressreleasexq3-htm.htm.jsonl, page 7; EX99.1_2026-09-30_cxt_exhibit991-pressreleasexq3-htm.htm.jsonl, page 9; 10-K_2025-12-31_cxt.htm.jsonl, page 39; 10-K_2025-12-31_cxt.htm.jsonl, page 40; calculation C35; calculation C32; calculation C17
