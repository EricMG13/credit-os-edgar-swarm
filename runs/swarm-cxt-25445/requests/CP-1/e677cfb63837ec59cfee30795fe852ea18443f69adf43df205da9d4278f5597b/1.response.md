## Findings

### Bottom line
Crane NXT’s reported cash flow supports ongoing debt service, but acquisition funding pushed carrying-value net debt higher and leaves reported LTM leverage at 3.03x. Creditors should distinguish issuer-adjusted leverage from carrying-value leverage and treat the current maturity ladder as incomplete.
Basis: calculation C5; calculation C6; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cxt.htm.jsonl, pages 3–5; EX99.1_2026-09-30_cxt_exhibit991-pressreleasexq3-htm.htm.jsonl, pages 7–9

The cash flow statement records USD 184.5 million of operating cash flow and USD 139.5 million of free cash flow for 9M 2026, while acquisition and financing activity left carrying-value net debt at USD 1,131.4 million at September 30, 2026. Management reports 2.5x net leverage using net debt before deferred financing costs and TTM Adjusted EBITDA; the comparable ratio using balance-sheet carrying debt and reported LTM EBITDA is 3.03x. Missing instrument-level principal schedules limit exact maturity-wall and secured-debt calculations.
Basis: calculation C4; calculation C5; calculation C6; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cxt.htm.jsonl, pages 3–5, 20–21; EX99.1_2026-09-30_cxt_exhibit991-pressreleasexq3-htm.htm.jsonl, pages 7–9

Leverage rose between FY2025 and September 2026 because carrying debt increased faster than cash and reported LTM EBITDA. The primary cause disclosed is acquisition financing: 9M 2026 included USD 225.4 million of acquisition cash outflow, USD 424.5 million of Term Loan B proceeds and USD 277.3 million of Term Loan A repayments. A stronger counterpoint is that 9M 2026 operating cash flow exceeded the prior-year period and free cash flow was positive; it would carry more weight if the acquired businesses sustain cash generation and management directs it to debt reduction rather than further acquisitions or distributions.
Basis: calculation C5; calculation C7; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cxt.htm.jsonl, pages 4, 20–21; 10-K_2025-12-31_cxt.htm.jsonl, page 83

Reported interest coverage remains positive, but FFO-to-debt is not calculable because the issuer does not report FFO or enough components to define it consistently. The adjusted measures exclude special items and include Antares Vision for periods before acquisition; they are useful as management’s supplemental view, not a substitute for GAAP cash flow or carrying-value debt.
Basis: calculation C6; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cxt.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 4–5; EX99.1_2026-09-30_cxt_exhibit991-pressreleasexq3-htm.htm.jsonl, pages 7–10
