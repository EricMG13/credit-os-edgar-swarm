## Findings

### Bottom line

The five periodic filings support a snapshot of Avient's consolidated credit profile to 30 September 2026, but not covenant, security, guarantor, rating or market-price conclusions, which stay not established for lack of debt agreements and market inputs. For creditors this means leverage, liquidity and maturities can be analysed from audited and interim statements, while lien priority and covenant headroom cannot.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_avnt_avnt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 10; 10-K_2025-12-31_avnt_avnt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 24; 10-K_2025-12-31_avnt_avnt-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 89–91

The latest filing was signed 14 days before the as-of date and nothing later is supplied. Liquidity of USD 932.4 million at 30 September 2026 foots to cash and revolver availability, and the debt table foots to the balance sheet, so the headline debt and liquidity figures are usable. Two items need care: the names of the chief executive and chief financial officer changed during 2026 without any filing in the set explaining it, and management now assumes the Calvert City environmental clean-up will finish in 2032, two years later than before.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_avnt_avnt-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 28–29; 10-Q_2026-09-30_avnt_avnt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 16; 10-Q_2026-09-30_avnt_avnt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 22; calculation CALC-02; calculation CALC-03; calculation CALC-06
