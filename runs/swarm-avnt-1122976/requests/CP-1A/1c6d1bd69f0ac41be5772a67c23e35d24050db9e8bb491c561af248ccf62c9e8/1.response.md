### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a Low-risk reading is behavioural uncertainty, not a documented adverse act. Both senior officers named in the 2026 filings differ from the annual report with no explanation, a buyback authorisation equal to 5.40% of shares outstanding remains open and share repurchases are listed among the expected uses of cash for the rest of 2026, dividends exceeded fiscal 2025 earnings, and the unreserved tax claims and the open-ended Calvert City accrual could absorb the cash that now goes to term-loan prepayment. The view would worsen to Medium if new management announced debt-funded repurchases or an acquisition, if the proxy statement showed a large holder pressing for change, or if the executed debt documents showed wide restricted-payment capacity. It would firm up if the proxy statement showed dispersed holdings and an independent board, if the leadership changes were explained as planned succession, and if prepayments continued.
