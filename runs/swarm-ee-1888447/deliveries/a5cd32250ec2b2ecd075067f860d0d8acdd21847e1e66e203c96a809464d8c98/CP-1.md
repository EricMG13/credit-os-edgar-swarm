## Findings

### Bottom line
Excelerate's leverage is moderate but has risen since the 2025 Jamaica acquisition, with net debt at 1.53x reported EBITDA at 2026-09-30 after net cash at the end of FY2024, so creditors depend on contracted terminal income to carry USD 1,075.91 million of debt.

Cash is the pressure point: free cash flow was negative USD 59.10 million in the nine months to 2026-09-30 as growth spending outran operating cash flow, cash fell to USD 356.74 million, and the undrawn USD 500 million revolver is now the main buffer. Cash interest paid is still covered 4.45x by derived EBITDA. Covenant ratios are not printed, so headroom is not established.

### Financial interpretation

The balance sheet moved from net cash at the end of FY2024 to net debt after the 2025 financing: the senior unsecured 2030 notes and new equity funded the Jamaica acquisition and replaced the term loan, lifting carrying debt from 504.448 to 1098.261 (US$m) between the two year-ends. Net debt has since grown to 719.167 at 2026-09-30 while carrying debt eased to 1075.909, because cash fell by 181.492 as capital spending, buybacks and distributions outran operating cash flow. On derived last-twelve-months EBITDA of 470.901, total leverage is 2.28x and net leverage 1.53x; on the issuer's adjusted EBITDA of 488.193 they are 2.20x and 1.47x. Neither figure is a covenant calculation.
