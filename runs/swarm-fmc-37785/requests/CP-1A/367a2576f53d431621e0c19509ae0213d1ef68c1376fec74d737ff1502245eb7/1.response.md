## Findings

### Bottom line

FMC is a listed agricultural chemicals producer whose creditors now depend on a new 20% strategic shareholder, pending asset sales and secured borrowing, because third-quarter 2026 gross margin was negative USD 401.6 million and the patents on its main diamide franchise have expired.
Basis: calculation C1A-1; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fmc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fmc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 30; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fmc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 45; 10-K_2025-12-31_fmc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 31

In 2026 the company sold shares to Tessenderlo Group for USD 403.2 million, received a USD 200.0 million prepayment from Corteva under a supply and licence agreement, issued USD 1,200.0 million of secured notes and agreed to sell its India business for USD 252 million, each directed to debt reduction or liquidity. These steps buy time but do not repair earnings: third-quarter revenue was USD 105.9 million after about USD 672 million of pricing and rebate charges, and total equity is USD 9.5 million.

### Key findings

The filings name no sponsor and no controlling shareholder, so the usual sponsor-extraction risk is not evidenced, but a new holder of about 20% arrived in September 2026 on terms the filings do not describe. Tessenderlo Group bought shares issued from treasury at USD 13.30 each, and the proxy statement that would list other large holders and the board's independence is not supplied. Creditors cannot yet say whether this holder is a stabiliser with board or consent rights or a passive investor, because the investor agreement is not supplied.
Basis: calculation C1A-1; calculation C1A-2; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fmc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 30; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fmc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 45; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fmc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 85; 10-K_2025-12-31_fmc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 186

Management has acted in creditors' favour on distributions and funding. The quarterly dividend fell from USD 0.58 to USD 0.08 per share, repurchases are barred until 2028-12-31, and the Tessenderlo equity, the Corteva prepayment and the secured notes proceeds went to repay the revolver and redeem notes. Cash dividends paid fell by 86.1% in the first nine months of 2026, which supports liquidity but does not restore earnings.
Basis: calculation C1A-10; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fmc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 23; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fmc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 31; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fmc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 45; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fmc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 69

Claims on assets now favour lien holders. The USD 1,200.0 million of notes due 2031 carry first-priority liens on substantially all assets of the company and its main guarantors, and the revolver is guaranteed and secured over certain assets and equity, partly released in June 2026. The filings do not state security for the existing senior and subordinated notes or how the liens rank against them, so the effect on those holders is not established here.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_fmc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 22–24

The business is broad by region and product, but its revenue rests on a diamide franchise whose composition-of-matter patents have expired in every major market. The two diamide families produced about USD 1.2 billion, roughly 34.6% of FY2025 revenue, and 82.2% of the foreign granted diamide patents expire by the end of 2030, so generic price pressure is a continuing mechanism and not a one-time event.
Basis: calculation C1A-6; calculation C1A-11; 10-K_2025-12-31_fmc-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 8–9; 10-K_2025-12-31_fmc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 11; 10-K_2025-12-31_fmc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 31

Revenue is concentrated by geography and input supply. Brazil and the United States together supplied 58.8% of FY2025 revenue, and critical intermediates come largely from outside the United States, principally China and India, so a regional channel shock or a trade disruption reaches earnings directly. The third quarter showed the channel mechanism: customers holding too much FMC inventory cut replenishment orders, and the company took about USD 672 million of pricing and rebate charges, of which USD 639 million reduced revenue.
Basis: calculation C1A-5; calculation C1A-14; 10-K_2025-12-31_fmc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 25; 10-K_2025-12-31_fmc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 114; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fmc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 45; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fmc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 58

The revenue base and the book equity cushion have both been sharply reduced. Third-quarter revenue fell 80.5% to USD 105.9 million, and total equity fell from USD 2,098.9 million to USD 9.5 million after the pricing charges, impairments and valuation allowances on deferred tax assets, which leaves almost no book equity beneath the debt. Recovery value therefore depends on asset sales and an earnings recovery, not on accounting capital.
Basis: calculation C1A-7; calculation C1A-9; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fmc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fmc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 5; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fmc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 21; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fmc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 33

Two sales are the remaining named debt-reduction sources and both are conditional. The India commercial business is agreed at USD 252 million, subject to regulatory approvals and adjustments, and the property at the Newark, Delaware research headquarters is framed at about USD 114 million gross, subject to due diligence. Completed 2026 sources total USD 603.2 million and the pending sales would add USD 366 million gross.
Basis: calculation C1A-8; 10-Q_2026-06-30_fmc-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 45; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fmc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 10; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fmc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 49

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a cautious reading is that outside capital arrived at the point of stress and the product pipeline is large. Tessenderlo Group paid USD 13.30 per share for about 20% of the company in September 2026, Corteva prepaid USD 200.0 million for rimisoxafen supply under a licence on a molecule whose ownership FMC keeps, and management reports 19 new active ingredients in development and four actives being commercialised, among them a herbicide mode of action that management calls the first new one in over three decades. If the two pending sales close, 2026 sources of cash outside operations would reach USD 969.2 million gross.
Basis: calculation C1A-8; 10-K_2025-12-31_fmc-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 9–10; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fmc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 14; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fmc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 30

The view would improve if the investor agreement shows the new holder has committed to support the company, if the India and Newark proceeds repay debt as planned, or if the new actives start to replace diamide revenue. It would worsen if either sale slips, if channel customers keep destocking so that further pricing charges follow, or if the investor agreement gives the holder rights that limit the board's room to protect creditors.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_fmc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 49; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fmc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 85

### Limitations

- The proxy statement that lists beneficial owners, directors and related-party dealings is not supplied, so the ownership conclusion cannot name any holder other than Tessenderlo Group or state the board's independence.
- The investor agreement and registration rights agreement with Tessenderlo Group are not supplied, so the conclusion that the new holder's influence is unknown cannot be narrowed to a stabilising or a controlling role.
- FY2025 and nine-month 2026 revenue mixes are distorted by about USD 422 million of India charges in FY2025 and about USD 639 million of pricing charges in 2026, so the mix conclusions describe reported revenue and not underlying demand.
- The diamide revenue figures are rounded management statements of about USD 0.8 billion and USD 0.4 billion, so the share of revenue exposed to generic competition is approximate to within a few percentage points.
- Customer concentration and distributor terms are not disclosed in the filings, so the diversification conclusion rests on regional and product mix only.
- No document dated after 2026-10-30 is supplied, so the status of the India and Newark sales and any later change in the shareholder base are not established beyond that date.

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

Three transactions in 2026 repaired liquidity and two sales remain open. Tessenderlo Group bought 30,319,166 shares issued from treasury at USD 13.30 each, for cash proceeds of USD 403.2 million after announcing the agreement on 2026-06-30; the company paid USD 8.0 million of fees, used the proceeds to repay revolver borrowings and for general purposes, and called the investment the conclusion of the strategic review begun in February 2026. In June 2026 Corteva prepaid USD 200.0 million for rimisoxafen to be supplied by FMC under a co-exclusive supply and licence agreement, which the company records as deferred revenue and so as an obligation to deliver product.
Basis: calculation C1A-2; calculation C1A-3; 10-Q_2026-06-30_fmc-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 42; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fmc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 14; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fmc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 30; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fmc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 45

The new debt is USD 1,200.0 million of senior secured notes due 2031, completed on 2026-06-05 and used to redeem USD 500 million of senior notes due 2026-10-01, repay revolver borrowings and meet general corporate needs. The notes are guaranteed by subsidiaries in the United States, Switzerland, the Netherlands, Canada and Singapore and secured by first-priority liens, and the covenants on the notes limit dividends and further indebtedness. The revolver was amended in December 2025, April 2026, June 2026 and, after the balance sheet date, on 2026-10-21, each time changing covenant levels, security or the use of sale proceeds.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_fmc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 22–24; 10-K_2025-12-31_fmc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 45

Two sales remain open. The India commercial business is agreed for USD 252 million with Crystal Crop Protection Limited, subject to regulatory approvals and adjustments for cash, debt and working capital, with closing expected in 2026; its carrying value is USD 260.0 million, USD 8.0 million above the stated consideration before adjustments. The framework agreement for the Newark property, about USD 114 million gross with a leaseback, was expected to close in the fourth quarter of 2026 but the latest report says only that it is subject to due diligence. Events after 2026-09-30 are a dividend of USD 12.6 million paid on 2026-10-15 and the eighth revolver amendment, which requires net India proceeds to repay revolver debt or fund general or working capital needs and conditionally releases some guarantors and security; the dividend payment is not in September cash and the amendment is not in the terms reported at that date.
