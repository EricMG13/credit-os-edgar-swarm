## Findings

### Bottom line

The five FMC filings supply the statements, notes and covenant disclosures that the requested financial, business and fundamental credit work needs, so that work can proceed with stated limits. Creditors should treat covenant definitions, collateral terms, guarantor detail and per-series debt terms as unverified, because no executed credit agreement, indenture or rating report is in the set.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_fmc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 22–24; 10-K_2025-12-31_fmc-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 188–189; 10-K_2025-12-31_fmc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 45

The evidence ends with the quarterly report signed 2026-10-30, 19 days before the cutoff. The October 2026 credit agreement amendment therefore appears only as a subsequent event, and the sale of the India commercial business is reported as pending. Conclusions that rest on rating levels, market pricing or management outlook for revenue and earnings cannot be drawn from this set.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_fmc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 10, 24, 85; calculation C0-1
