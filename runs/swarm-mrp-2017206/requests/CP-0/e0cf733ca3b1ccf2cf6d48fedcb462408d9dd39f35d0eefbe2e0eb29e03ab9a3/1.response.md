## Findings

### Bottom line

The eight supplied filings and releases are enough to run Financial foundation, Business & governance and Fundamental credit, but the executed debt documents, covenant levels, ratings and market prices are missing, so covenant headroom and relative value cannot be assessed.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_ck0002017206-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 31; 10-K_2025-12-31_ck0002017206-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 189

The latest evidence is the quarter ended September 30, 2026, in a quarterly report signed November 4, 2026. Two events after quarter end sit outside the September 30, 2026 balance sheet: USD 1.0 billion of senior notes closed on October 6, 2026, and funding to Dream Finders of about USD 1.1 billion was completed on October 20, 2026. Reported debt and cash therefore do not yet show them, and a pro forma position is not established from these documents.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_ck0002017206-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 40–41; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ck0002017206-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 77

Two further limits apply to period comparisons. The 2025 annual report and the latest quarterly report disagree on when USD 33 million of purchase money mortgages fall due, and the 2024 statements are those of the pre-spin predecessor, so growth from 2024 into 2025 is not like for like.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_ck0002017206-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 180; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ck0002017206-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 33; 10-K_2024-12-31_d931976d10k.htm.jsonl, pages 217–218
