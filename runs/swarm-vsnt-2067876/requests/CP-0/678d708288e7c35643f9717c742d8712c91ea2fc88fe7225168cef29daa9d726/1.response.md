## Findings

### Bottom line

The four filings support a credit snapshot of Versant Media Group, Inc. as of 2026-11-18, but covenant, guarantee and lien terms rest on the filings' summaries because no credit agreement or indenture was supplied, so headroom cannot be tested against the contracts. Covenant interpretation is blocked until those documents are supplied; the three requested analyses can run on the filings with the limits below.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_vsnt-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 99–102; 10-K_2025-12-31_vsnt-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 111–113; 10-Q_2026-09-30_vsnt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 15

The 2025 year and the 2025 quarters are carve-out statements built from Comcast's records with allocated costs, so they describe the business inside Comcast, not the standalone issuer, and comparisons with 2026 need that qualification. Ratings, market prices, peer data, a portfolio and any earnings release or management bridge were also not supplied, so rating-based, relative-value and portfolio conclusions cannot rest on this source set.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_vsnt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 61; 10-K_2025-12-31_vsnt-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 83–84; 10-Q_2026-09-30_vsnt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 8
