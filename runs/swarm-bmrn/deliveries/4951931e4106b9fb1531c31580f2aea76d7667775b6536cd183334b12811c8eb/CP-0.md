## Findings

### Bottom line

The seven BioMarin filings and releases support the requested credit snapshot, but with no executed financing documents supplied, covenant, guarantee and lien terms can only be read as the company summarises them, not tested against the contracts.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-03-31_bmrn-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 111; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bmrn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 50

The September 2026 quarterly report, dated two days before the as-of date, is the controlling source for debt, cash and covenant status. The annual report's balance sheet predates the April 2026 acquisition of Amicus Therapeutics and the senior secured term loans and senior unsecured notes that financed it, so year-end debt and liquidity are not comparable with September 2026. Amicus is consolidated only from April 27, 2026 and its purchase accounting is still preliminary. No credit rating, market price, peer set or portfolio input was supplied.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_bmrn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 14–16; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bmrn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 22–25; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bmrn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 125; 10-K_2025-12-31_bmrn-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 182; calculation C9
