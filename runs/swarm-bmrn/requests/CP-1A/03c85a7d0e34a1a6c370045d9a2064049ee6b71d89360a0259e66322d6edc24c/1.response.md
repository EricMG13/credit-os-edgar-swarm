## Findings

### Bottom line

BioMarin is a publicly held rare-disease drug maker with no controlling owner or sponsor in the sources, so creditors face operating, acquisition and financing risk rather than owner extraction risk, and the Amicus purchase has made that risk larger.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_bmrn-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 148; 10-K_2025-12-31_bmrn-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bmrn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 14–15

The largest product, VOXZOGO, was 28.78% of 2025 revenues and three customers bought 37% of net product revenues. The company counts four transactions in 2026 that added development programs, and the largest was paid for mostly with new secured debt; the board, holder and covenant detail that would limit further deals sits in documents that are not supplied. Owner behaviour is therefore rated medium risk on incomplete evidence, not low.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_bmrn-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 9; 10-K_2025-12-31_bmrn-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 18; EX-99.1_2026-11-02_earningsrelease-2nov26ex991.htm.jsonl, page 1; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bmrn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 22–25; calculation C1

### Key findings

No controlling owner or sponsor appears in the sources, which removes dividend recapitalisation and fee leakage as documented risks but leaves control rights unestablished. The company states that it has never paid a cash dividend, made no issuer repurchases in the fourth quarter of 2025 and shows none in its statements of equity, and it had 28 holders of record. Holders above five percent, board independence and related-party dealings are in a proxy statement that is not supplied.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_bmrn-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 148; 10-K_2025-12-31_bmrn-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 172; 10-K_2025-12-31_bmrn-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 183; Source readiness (CP-0)

Cash flow rests on one growth product and a few buyers. VOXZOGO produced USD 926.92 million, 28.78% of 2025 revenues, grew 26.10% in the year and earns more than 75% of its revenue outside the United States; three customers took 37% of net product revenues, and Sanofi is the only customer for ALDURAZYME (6.47% of revenues). VOXZOGO has United States orphan exclusivity that expires in 2028 for older children and 2030 for younger ones and a composition patent extended to 2035, but named clinical-stage competitors are developing rival treatments for achondroplasia.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_bmrn-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 9; 10-K_2025-12-31_bmrn-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 18–19; 10-K_2025-12-31_bmrn-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 23–25; 10-K_2025-12-31_bmrn-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 209; EX-99.1_2026-11-02_earningsrelease-2nov26ex991.htm.jsonl, page 3; calculation C1; calculation C3

Acquisition is now the main use of cash and it is mostly debt funded. The company bought Inozyme in July 2025 for USD 285.00 million net of cash acquired, closed Amicus in April 2026 for USD 5,323.56 million of total consideration, bought Alesta in August 2026 for USD 303.44 million and agreed a licence in October 2026 with an upfront payment of USD 70.00 million. New borrowings of USD 3,650.00 million and about $1.7bn of cash paid for Amicus, so about two thirds of the price was borrowed. The company counts four transactions in 2026 that added programmes, and its appetite for further deals is the main thing the supplied documents cannot bound.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_bmrn-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 225–226; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bmrn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 14–19; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bmrn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 34; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bmrn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 47; EX-99.1_2026-11-02_earningsrelease-2nov26ex991.htm.jsonl, page 1; calculation C4

The financing moved ranking against the existing creditors. The 2027 convertible notes are senior subordinated, unsecured and expressly effectively subordinated to secured debt, and the new secured term loans carry first-priority liens on substantially all assets of the company and its guarantors; the unsecured revolver that existed at the 2025 year end was terminated in April 2026 and replaced by a secured one. That was disclosed and expected in the notes' terms, so it is a ranking fact and not evidence of coercive treatment, but the convertible holders now sit behind a much larger secured claim.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_bmrn-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 206–207; 10-Q_2026-03-31_bmrn-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 72; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bmrn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 23–25; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bmrn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 50

Pre-funding was structured to protect noteholders. The senior unsecured notes were issued in February 2026 into escrow and had to be redeemed at par if the Amicus acquisition did not close by 2026-12-19, and the bridge commitment fell from $3.7bn to $2.8bn and then to zero as permanent debt replaced it. No dividend, buyback or related-party payment is documented in the supplied pages.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_bmrn-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 158; 10-K_2025-12-31_bmrn-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 226; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bmrn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 25; 10-K_2025-12-31_bmrn-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 148

Acquired pipeline spending has already produced a write-off. The lead Inozyme asset cost an acquired research expense of USD 220.96 million in 2025 and its development was discontinued in August 2026, about 13 months after the purchase, and the 2025 decision to withdraw ROCTAVIAN carried about USD 240.00 million of restructuring charges. Both were charged to earnings in the year and left no asset behind, which shows that purchased programmes can fail quickly.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_bmrn-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 8–9; 10-K_2025-12-31_bmrn-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 225; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bmrn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 38; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bmrn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 42–43; calculation C5

Reporting is regular and audited, with gaps around the new business. The auditor, KPMG, has served since 2002 and gave an unqualified opinion on the 2025 statements and on internal control, with one critical audit matter on ALDURAZYME variable consideration; a clawback policy dates from October 2023. Controls at Amicus are still being integrated and the Amicus purchase accounting is preliminary, so reported results for the acquired business carry more estimation risk than the legacy business.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_bmrn-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 177–181; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bmrn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 54; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bmrn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 15–16

The operating model is high-margin but heavy on discretionary spending. In 2025 research and development was 28.62% of revenues, 21.76% before the acquired research expense, selling, general and administrative expense was 35.79% of revenues and capital spending was 3.20%. Much of that spending is discretionary, which gives management room to cut if earnings fall while interest is contractual, but large international and government orders arrive unevenly: a big VOXZOGO order moved from the third to the fourth quarter of 2026 and the company cut its 2026 revenue guidance to USD 3,800.00 million to USD 3,900.00 million from USD 3,875.00 million to USD 3,925.00 million.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_bmrn-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 181–182; 10-K_2025-12-31_bmrn-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 183–184; 10-K_2025-12-31_bmrn-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 18; EX-99.1_2026-08-06_earningsrelease-6aug26ex991.htm.jsonl, page 5; EX-99.1_2026-11-02_earningsrelease-2nov26ex991.htm.jsonl, pages 3–5; calculation C3

The competing product now pays a royalty. The settlement agreed with Ascendis in late August 2026 licenses BioMarin's patents for Ascendis' Yuviwel in exchange for 20% of United States net sales and 18% in the European Union, Brazil and South Korea until May 2030. That reduces patent-litigation uncertainty and gives BioMarin income from a rival, but it confirms that a competing product is on the market.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-11-02_earningsrelease-2nov26ex991.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bmrn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 99; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bmrn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 124

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence for a lower risk rating is the record of financial policy. The company has never paid a dividend, made no buybacks in 2025, settled the 2024 convertible notes in cash at maturity, entered 2026 with net cash, kept the new revolver undrawn and states a target of gross leverage below 2.50x by mid-2027, with about USD 280.00 million of GAAP cost synergies expected by 2028. Annual reporting is audited and internal control was effective at the 2025 year end.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_bmrn-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 148; 10-K_2025-12-31_bmrn-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 161; 10-K_2025-12-31_bmrn-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 179; 10-K_2025-12-31_bmrn-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 182; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bmrn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 25; EX-99.1_2026-08-06_earningsrelease-6aug26ex991.htm.jsonl, page 3; EX-99.1_2026-11-02_earningsrelease-2nov26ex991.htm.jsonl, page 3

Against a lower rating, four facts weigh more. The acquisition replaced a single convertible note with secured term loans and new unsecured notes within five months; the credit agreement that limits further deals, distributions and extra debt is not supplied; management says it will keep expanding the pipeline through business development; and Amicus integration and controls are unfinished. The view would move to low risk on evidence of restrictive covenants and a second reporting period of falling leverage with no further large debt-funded deal, and to high risk on a debt-funded distribution, a large further debt-funded acquisition, or a change in the holder base that put control in one party's hands.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_bmrn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 22–25; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bmrn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 54; EX-99.1_2026-11-02_earningsrelease-2nov26ex991.htm.jsonl, page 1; Source readiness (CP-0)

### Limitations

- Board independence, holders above five percent and related-party dealings are not established because the proxy statement is not supplied; a controlling or activist holder would reverse the finding that owner extraction risk is low.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_bmrn-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 172; Source readiness (CP-0)

- The credit agreement and the indentures are not supplied, so capacity for distributions, acquisitions, further debt and liens cannot be assessed; the medium risk rating could move either way once the negative covenants are read.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-03-31_bmrn-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 111; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bmrn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 50; Source readiness (CP-0)

- The acquired GALAFOLD and POMBILITI + OPFOLDA business, 12.10% of nine-month 2026 revenues, has about five months of history and no patent or exclusivity detail in the supplied annual report, so its durability is judged on current sales only.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_bmrn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 17–28; 10-K_2025-12-31_bmrn-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 23–25; calculation C6

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

The Amicus acquisition was agreed on 2025-12-19 as an all-cash merger at USD 14.50 per share, and completed on 2026-04-27. Total consideration was USD 5,323.561 million, including USD 432.920 million to repay Amicus' own debt, accounted for as a business combination with preliminary allocations of USD 4,750.000 million to intangible assets and USD 469.786 million to goodwill. The price was funded by new non-convertible debt and about $1.7bn of cash.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_bmrn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 14–16; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bmrn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 47; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bmrn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 123; 10-Q_2026-03-31_bmrn-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 24

The new debt is USD 850.0 million of 5.5% senior unsecured notes due 2034, a USD 800.0 million senior secured term loan A due 2031-04-27, a USD 2.0 billion senior secured term loan B due 2033-04-27 and a USD 600.0 million senior secured revolver due 2031-04-27 that is undrawn. The credit agreement of 2026-04-27 names Citibank, N.A. as administrative and collateral agent and lists issuing banks, which points to a letter-of-credit facility whose size is not printed. A supplemental indenture of the same date lists guarantors, so the 2034 notes may carry subsidiary guarantees that the filings do not describe.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_bmrn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 22–25; 10-Q_2026-03-31_bmrn-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 111; 10-K_2025-12-31_bmrn-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 226

Smaller transactions are Inozyme (2025-07-01, asset acquisition, USD 285.0 million net of cash acquired), Alesta (2026-08-31, asset acquisition, USD 303.4 million, with up to USD 215 million of milestones), a Fabry licence in October 2026 (USD 70.0 million upfront, up to USD 405.0 million of milestones) and the Ascendis settlement. BioMarin also assumed a licence with Dimerix with milestones of up to USD 510.0 million. Pro forma nine-month revenue as if Amicus had been owned from 2025-01-01 is USD 3,009.554 million against USD 2,780.562 million reported; no pro forma earnings are printed.
