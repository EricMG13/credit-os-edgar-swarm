## Findings

### Bottom line

Fundamental credit quality is weak but improving: total debt of 6.47x last-twelve-month EBITDA, interest cover of 2.05x on accrued expense and control by Bausch Health leave creditors reliant on one year of earnings recovery holding until the 2028 maturities.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); Business & governance (CP-1A); calculation C8

The business is a three-segment eye-health franchise with revenue of USD 5,418 million over the last twelve months, and net pricing supplied 40.38% of the nine-month revenue increase. Liquidity of USD 1,179 million and no maturity beyond amortization before 2028 buy time, but net leverage of 5.82x at 2026-09-30 becomes 5.93x pro forma for the acquisition agreed on 2026-10-08 and 5.92x pro forma for the executive payment under the separation agreement dated 2026-10-11. The main downside is earnings falling back, through rebates, generic competition or supply problems, before the 2028 notes are refinanced.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); 10-Q_2026-09-30_blco_blco-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 79; calculation C39

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest case against a weak view is that the improvement is broad and cash-backed. EBITDA rose 47.38%, price and volume both contributed in Vision Care and Pharmaceuticals, accrued interest fell 11.76% in nine months, last-twelve-month free cash flow is USD 252 million after three years of deficits, total debt to EBITDA fell from 9.52x to 6.47x, liquidity covers the fourth-quarter needs by 3.30x, and nothing but amortization falls due before 2028. The parent has received no dividend since the listing, and the only large transfer to it in the filings, USD 2,429 million in 2022, is a dated event.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); Business & governance (CP-1A); 10-Q_2026-09-30_blco_blco-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 79; 10-Q_2026-09-30_blco_blco-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 82; calculation C11; calculation C6; calculation C45

The view would move toward adequate if four quarters keep last-twelve-month EBITDA at or above USD 787 million, if cash interest paid confirms cover near the 2.05x proxy, if the acquisition and the executive payment are met from cash without a revolver draw above USD 180 million, and if the 2028 notes are refinanced ahead of maturity. It would weaken if rebates return toward the 2025 level, if Lumify generic entry is accelerated, if the revolver is drawn past the 35% line, or if the parent takes cash out or its pledged shares are foreclosed.

### Company description

Bausch + Lomb sells contact lenses and lens care, over-the-counter eye drops, prescription eye medicines and surgical equipment, implants and consumables. In 2025 Vision Care was 57.30% of revenue at USD 2,923 million, Pharmaceuticals 25.17% at USD 1,284 million and Surgical 17.53% at USD 894 million, and the United States and Puerto Rico supplied 49.91%. ### Ownership and group structure

Bausch Health holds about 87% of the shares, can elect the board and generally decides mergers, financing and dividend policy, and the issuer needs its written consent for certain significant actions until it holds less than 50% of the votes; the issuer qualifies for controlled-company exemptions from board-independence rules. Creditors therefore cannot count on the board to resist the parent on leverage, acquisitions or distributions, and financial policy is set by a shareholder whose own finances are not in the filings.

The credit facilities and notes are secured by substantially all assets of the issuer and its material wholly owned Canadian, U.S., Dutch and Irish subsidiaries, the notes are guaranteed by the subsidiaries that guarantee the credit agreement and rank equally with the facilities, and they are structurally behind liabilities of subsidiaries that do not guarantee them. Noncontrolling interests of USD 71 million can rank ahead of the issuer's creditors on the assets they own.

### Revenue durability and pricing power

The analysis reads revenue as mostly repeat consumer and clinician demand, and it is growing. In the nine months of 2026 over-the-counter products were USD 1,418 million and devices USD 1,521 million of USD 4,013 million, prescription pharmaceuticals USD 885 million and branded and other generics USD 175 million, and revenue rose 8.58%, or 7.28% excluding foreign exchange. Revenue also grew in each of the last three years, by 10.03% in 2023, 15.56% in 2024 and 6.47% in 2025, which supports the view that debt service rests on a growing base.

### Overall credit view

Overall, Bausch + Lomb presents an adequate business profile supported by a three-segment product range, repeat consumer and clinician demand and growth in each of the last three years, but credit quality is constrained by total debt of 6.47x EBITDA, thin interest cover, a mostly floating debt structure and control by a parent with a pledged stake. Cash-flow durability appears adequate but unproven: free cash flow of USD 252 million is one year old and rests on pricing and a depressed 2025 base. Refinancing capacity is likely to depend on earnings holding above USD 787 million, on rebates staying contained and on the parent not taking cash or losing control before the 2028 notes mature. Business quality compared with typical leveraged-credit issuers in eye health is not established, because no peer data were supplied. Further analysis would require cash interest paid for 2026, the credit agreement and covenant definitions, the size of the pledge and Lumify revenue.
