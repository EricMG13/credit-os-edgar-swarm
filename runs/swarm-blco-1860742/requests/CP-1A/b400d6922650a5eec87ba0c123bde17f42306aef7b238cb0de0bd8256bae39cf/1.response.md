## Findings

### Bottom line

Control by Bausch Health, which holds about 87% of the shares and supplies the chairman and interim chief executive, is the main non-financial risk for creditors, because ownership can change through a distribution, sale or foreclosure. The business is diversified across three segments, the issuer has paid no dividend since 2022, and the only debt-funded transfer to the parent in the filings is the USD 2,429 million paid at the listing, funded by new term debt and cash.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_blco_blco-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 68; 10-K_2025-12-31_blco_blco-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 157–158; 10-K_2024-12-31_blco_blco-20241231.htm.jsonl, page 258; 10-Q_2026-09-30_blco_blco-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 24; 10-Q_2026-09-30_blco_blco-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 103; calculation C1; calculation C33

Operating risk is secondary: Surgical earned USD 18 million of segment profit in 2025 against USD 50 million in 2023, and rebates took 39.5% of gross product sales. The view worsens if the parent draws cash out or control passes to an unknown holder.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_blco_blco-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 180–181; 10-K_2025-12-31_blco_blco-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 322; calculation C37

### Key findings

Bausch Health holds about 87% of the shares, can elect the board, generally decides mergers, financing and dividend policy, and under the separation agreement the issuer needs its written consent for certain significant actions until the parent holds less than 50% of the votes; the issuer qualifies for controlled-company exemptions from board-independence rules. Creditors therefore cannot count on the board to resist the parent on strategy, distributions or acquisitions while control lasts, although related-party dealings need independent-director approval.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest case against putting control first is the parent's restraint since 2022. Its holding has not changed since 2025-02, no dividend has been paid, related-party dealings are small, the issuer refinanced on 2025-06-26 and 2026-01-02 to move maturities to January 2031, and the board has nine named independent directors with an auditor reporting effective controls. The debt is guaranteed by the issuer's own subsidiaries and the filings name no guarantee by the parent, so no contractual channel from the parent's debt to the issuer's is named in the filings, although ratings can move with the parent.
Basis: 10-K_2024-12-31_blco_blco-20241231.htm.jsonl, page 15; 10-K_2025-12-31_blco_blco-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 17; 10-K_2025-12-31_blco_blco-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 70; 10-K_2025-12-31_blco_blco-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 158; 10-K_2025-12-31_blco_blco-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 69; 10-K_2025-12-31_blco_blco-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 263–264; 10-K_2025-12-31_blco_blco-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 191–192; 10-K_2025-12-31_blco_blco-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 232; 10-Q_2026-09-30_blco_blco-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 33–34

The view would improve if a distribution left no controlling shareholder, if a permanent chief executive independent of the parent were appointed, or if the pledge size and the parent's leverage target were disclosed and proved benign. It would worsen on a dividend, an intercompany loan or any other cash call by the parent, on a foreclosure on pledged shares, or on a sale to a holder whose plans differ, and on any rating action driven by the parent.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_blco_blco-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 62–63; 10-K_2025-12-31_blco_blco-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 69; 10-Q_2026-09-30_blco_blco-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 103–104

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

No single transaction sits at the centre of this credit. The open transaction is the separation from Bausch Health, announced on 2020-08-06 and not completed: a distribution depends on the parent reaching leverage targets that the filings do not state, the parent can end the arrangement at its sole discretion, the agreement's outside date of 2024-12-31 passed without termination, and a sale or a transfer to the parent's shareholders remains possible. No date is given.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_blco_blco-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 17; 10-K_2025-12-31_blco_blco-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 62–63; 10-Q_2026-09-30_blco_blco-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 58

The one large acquisition since the listing was XIIDRA from Novartis, signed on 2023-06-30 and completed on 2023-09-29 for USD 1,750 million upfront plus potential milestones of up to USD 750 million, paid from USD 1,400 million of 8.375% secured notes due October 2028 and a new term loan. Later deals were small: Whitecap Biosciences for about USD 28 million on 2025-01-03, two acquisitions for about USD 33 million in November 2025 and Mexican manufacturing assets for about USD 75 million on 2025-12-09.
