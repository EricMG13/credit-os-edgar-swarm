## Findings

### Bottom line

Skeena is a single-project gold and silver developer whose creditors depend on completing Eskay Creek, where management targets first production in the second quarter of 2027, so execution and funding release, not governance, drive creditor outcomes.
Basis: 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-08-13_ske-20260813xex99d1.htm.jsonl, pages 1–3; 6-K-EX-99.2_2026-08-13_ske-20260813xex99d2.htm.jsonl, pages 4, 12

The company has no revenue and discloses no sponsor or controlling holder, while the April 2026 financing placed CAD 683,168 thousand of note proceeds in restricted accounts and a gold stream and royalty liabilities already claim part of future production. Creditor cash-flow protection therefore begins only after commissioning, and the evidence on control and legal capacity is absent rather than reassuring.
Basis: 6-K-EX-99.2_2026-08-13_ske-20260813xex99d2.htm.jsonl, pages 4, 12–15; 40-F_2025-12-31_ske-20251231x40f.htm.jsonl, page 1; calculation C3

### Key findings

- Construction and commissioning risk on one project dominates the credit. Eskay Creek is described as fully permitted and under construction, management expects initial production and cash flow in the second quarter of 2027 and commercial production in 2027, and until then there is no revenue. The August 2026 risk language names construction delays, cost overruns, commissioning or ramp-up failure and inability to access restricted funds, so a slip extends the period over which the notes are serviced from restricted funds and cash on hand, and creditors are underwriting a timetable rather than current earnings.
  Basis: 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-08-13_ske-20260813xex99d1.htm.jsonl, pages 1–3; 6-K-EX-99.2_2026-08-13_ske-20260813xex99d2.htm.jsonl, pages 4, 12
- The 2026 financing protects construction but makes the release of restricted funds a condition of credit support. The US$750,000,000 senior secured notes issued on April 10, 2026 carry an interest reserve of US$94,208,000 for three semi-annual payments and a construction disbursement account of US$400,000,000, and the statements say further financing is needed if the funds cannot be released or the notes prove insufficient. A delay in release would force a raise during construction, which weakens the creditors' position.
  Basis: 6-K-EX-99.2_2026-08-13_ske-20260813xex99d2.htm.jsonl, pages 4, 12
- Part of future production is already sold or burdened, which narrows the cash flow available to creditors. After the April 2026 buy-down the gold stream delivers 3.52% of payable gold for the life of the mine at 10% of the market price, and a 1% net smelter royalty and an option on a further royalty or shares were granted in 2026, while the March 2026 statements describe the project as subject to royalties of 4% to 5.5% without saying whether that range includes the new grants. These claims reduce revenue per ounce before the notes are paid, and the ranking of the royalty liabilities is not stated.
  Basis: 6-K-EX-99.2_2026-08-13_ske-20260813xex99d2.htm.jsonl, pages 14–15; 6-K-EX-99.2_2026-05-15_ske-20260515xex99d2.htm.jsonl, pages 9, 11
- Governance disclosure is adequate for monitoring but silent on who controls the company. The audit committee has three independent members, KPMG LLP audits the annual statements and internal control over financial reporting was effective at December 31, 2025, but no holder list, sponsor or board composition beyond the committee is supplied, and the share count rose by 3.16% between December 31, 2025 and June 30, 2026. With no sponsor there is no sponsor support or extraction evidence either way, so control risk is not established.
  Basis: 40-F_2025-12-31_ske-20251231x40f.htm.jsonl, pages 1, 5, 7; 6-K-EX-99.2_2026-08-13_ske-20260813xex99d2.htm.jsonl, page 2; calculation C1
- No distribution, acquisition or amendment history appears, so there is no evidence of extraction, but the history is short. The cash flow statements show no dividend line in any period supplied, and the only creditor-relevant actions in the statements are the 2026 financing, the stream buy-down, the royalty grants and a prepayment of royalty obligations.
  Basis: 6-K-EX-99.2_2026-08-13_ske-20260813xex99d2.htm.jsonl, pages 2–3, 14–15
- Management pay is largely share-based and tied to milestones the statements do not define. Share-based payments were CAD 21,690 thousand of CAD 43,418 thousand of general and administration expenses in the first half of 2026, or 49.96%, and a grant of performance share units to officers on February 9, 2026 vests on a company milestone. Outstanding awards equal 6.15% of shares outstanding; the cost is non-cash but adds dilution, and whether the milestones include construction completion is not stated.
  Basis: 6-K-EX-99.2_2026-08-13_ske-20260813xex99d2.htm.jsonl, pages 1, 17–18; calculation C2; calculation C4

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against the view that execution and funding release dominate is the way the 2026 financing is built: the construction disbursement account was funded at issue, the interest reserve covers the first three coupons, the project is described as fully permitted, and the buy-down cut the gold stream to 3.52% of payable gold. The valuation inputs in the statements also use a gold price of USD 4,017 per ounce at June 30, 2026, which, if it holds, favours the project economics, although costs are not supplied. These facts make a funding failure before commissioning less likely than the risk language alone suggests.
Basis: 6-K-EX-99.2_2026-08-13_ske-20260813xex99d2.htm.jsonl, pages 8, 12, 14; 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-08-13_ske-20260813xex99d1.htm.jsonl, page 1

The view would soften if restricted funds were released on schedule, ore mining and stockpiling began on the timetable management gives, the stream completion test were met before September 30, 2027 and the updated technical report confirmed the production profile and mine life. It would harden on a delay in grid energization or commissioning, a cost overrun that exhausts the construction account, a successful permit challenge or evidence that the restricted funds cannot be released when required.
Basis: 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-08-13_ske-20260813xex99d1.htm.jsonl, pages 2–3; 6-K-EX-99.2_2026-08-13_ske-20260813xex99d2.htm.jsonl, pages 4, 14

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

The senior secured notes offering of US$750,000,000 completed on April 10, 2026 is the one new debt issue in the supplied filings. The notes bear 8.50% a year paid semi-annually, mature on April 1, 2031, are non-callable for two years and rank as senior secured obligations pari passu with the Gold Stream obligations and senior to subordinated debt. Proceeds of US$184,000,000 repurchased 66.67% of the Gold Stream, US$94,208,000 went to an interest reserve covering three semi-annual payments, US$400,000,000 went to a construction disbursement account and US$71,792,000 covered general and administrative expenses and issuance costs.
Basis: 6-K-EX-99.2_2026-08-13_ske-20260813xex99d2.htm.jsonl, pages 4, 12–13

The offering replaced the undrawn US$350,000,000 Senior Secured Term Loan, which was terminated on April 10, 2026 with no draws made, and the buy-down followed an amending agreement of March 31, 2026 that also cancelled the option to draw an additional stream deposit. Change of control triggers an offer at 101% of principal, and the notes carry call prices of 104.25%, 102.125% and 100% in successive years from April 1, 2028.
Basis: 6-K-EX-99.2_2026-05-15_ske-20260515xex99d2.htm.jsonl, page 11; 6-K-EX-99.2_2026-08-13_ske-20260813xex99d2.htm.jsonl, pages 13–14

In the first quarter of 2026 the company also granted third parties a 1% net smelter royalty and an option to elect a further 1.5% royalty, shares or a mix, in exchange for rights described only as certain rights for the duration of the project; the liabilities stand at CAD 78,709 thousand and CAD 136,009 thousand and an equity component of CAD 108,575 thousand was recorded. The cash received, if any, is not stated, and CAD 25,000 thousand of royalty obligations was prepaid in the second quarter.
Basis: 6-K-EX-99.2_2026-08-13_ske-20260813xex99d2.htm.jsonl, pages 3, 15; 6-K-EX-99.2_2026-05-15_ske-20260515xex99d2.htm.jsonl, pages 11–12
