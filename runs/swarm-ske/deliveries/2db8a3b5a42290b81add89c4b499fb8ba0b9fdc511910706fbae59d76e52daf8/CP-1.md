## Findings

### Bottom line

The senior secured notes issued on April 10, 2026 are the only debt on Skeena's long-term debt lines, carried at CAD 1,055,969 thousand; with no revenue and negative EBITDA, repayment depends on construction funding and commissioning that management expects in 2027.
Basis: 6-K-EX-99.2_2026-08-13_ske-20260813xex99d2.htm.jsonl, pages 1, 13; calculation C2

Unrestricted cash at June 30, 2026 was CAD 134,808 thousand, with CAD 500,853 thousand more restricted to construction spending and interest on the notes; net debt on unrestricted cash is CAD 921,161 thousand. Six-month capital additions of CAD 203,600 thousand were met from cash on hand and note proceeds, while operations used CAD 30,235 thousand.
Basis: 6-K-EX-99.2_2026-08-13_ske-20260813xex99d2.htm.jsonl, pages 1, 3, 7, 12; calculation C1; calculation C5

### Analysis by dimension

The balance sheets print no debt line at December 31, 2024, September 30, 2025 or March 31, 2026 and a dash at December 31, 2025, so funded debt first appears with the US$750,000,000 senior secured notes issued on April 10, 2026, carried at CAD 1,055,969 thousand on June 30, 2026 against CAD 1,036,761 thousand of principal translated at issuance. The statements show no revenue and the derived EBITDA is negative in every period, CAD 22,065 thousand negative in the second quarter of 2026, so debt over EBITDA is not a usable measure and creditors rely on construction funding and, after commissioning, mine cash flow to carry the 8.50% coupon. The consequence is that leverage cannot rank this credit and the liquidity and funding analysis has to carry the judgement.
Basis: 6-K-EX-99.2_2026-08-13_ske-20260813xex99d2.htm.jsonl, pages 1, 12–13; calculation C2; calculation C4; calculation C12

Cash interest paid is not stated in any period, and the first interest payments on the notes are covered by an interest reserve of CAD 130,228 thousand deposited on April 10, 2026 for three semi-annual payments, so early coupons do not draw on operating cash. Interest and finance fee expense of CAD 19,851 thousand for the six months to June 30, 2026 is only the part not capitalised, because CAD 18,730 thousand of borrowing cost net of interest income went into mineral property and construction in progress. Interest cover is therefore not stated, and the reserve moves the first real coupon test to about eighteen months after issue, which on management's timetable is after the first production.
Basis: 6-K-EX-99.2_2026-08-13_ske-20260813xex99d2.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 11–12; 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-08-13_ske-20260813xex99d1.htm.jsonl, page 1; calculation C21

Operating activities used CAD 30,235 thousand in the six months to June 30, 2026 against CAD 38,699 thousand a year earlier, so the cash cost of running the company is small beside construction: additions to mineral property, plant and equipment were CAD 203,600 thousand against CAD 93,412 thousand a year earlier, with a further CAD 9,144 thousand of construction prepayments. Working capital moved little except accounts payable, which absorbed CAD 6,675 thousand. Capital spend, not operations, sets the funding need, and it was met from note proceeds and from CAD 199,176 thousand of restricted cash released, so release of the remaining restricted funds is the dependency that matters.
Basis: 6-K-EX-99.2_2026-08-13_ske-20260813xex99d2.htm.jsonl, pages 2–3; calculation C23

Unrestricted cash was CAD 134,808 thousand at June 30, 2026 against undiscounted obligations due within one year of CAD 316,513 thousand, which include CAD 138,109 thousand of contractual commitments and CAD 88,581 thousand of long-term debt payments; restricted cash of CAD 500,853 thousand sits in the construction disbursement account and the interest reserve. The statements say further financing is needed if the construction funds cannot be released or the notes prove insufficient, and the statements name no committed facility or letter of credit after the term loan was terminated. Liquidity therefore turns on the release mechanics of restricted funds, and a creditor should read the CAD 635,661 thousand of total cash as conditional rather than free.
Basis: 6-K-EX-99.2_2026-08-13_ske-20260813xex99d2.htm.jsonl, pages 1, 4, 10–12, 14; calculation C5; calculation C26; calculation C29

The statements are unaudited interim statements, the full-year 2025 statements are not supplied, the notes' principal is translated only at issuance, and several lines are printed as dashes that this module carries as not supplied rather than zero. These limits leave the June 30, 2026 balance sheet intact, because it foots to its printed totals, but they remove the trend and last-twelve-months measures a credit committee would normally rely on.
Basis: 6-K-EX-99.2_2026-08-13_ske-20260813xex99d2.htm.jsonl, page 1; calculation C27

The notes appear at principal, carrying value and fair value of CAD 1,036,761 thousand, CAD 1,055,969 thousand and CAD 1,119,655 thousand, and the claims outside Total Debt amount to CAD 424,004 thousand: the Gold Stream derivative liability of CAD 209,286 thousand, which ranks pari passu with the notes, and royalty liabilities of CAD 214,718 thousand whose ranking is not stated. A creditor who counts them as debt faces claims equal to 40.15% of the notes' carrying value on top of the notes, which is why Total Debt stays on carrying value and the other claims are listed beside it.
Basis: 6-K-EX-99.2_2026-08-13_ske-20260813xex99d2.htm.jsonl, pages 8, 12–15; calculation C17; calculation C20; calculation C28

The gaps that matter most are the missing full-year 2025 statements, the absence of any positive earnings measure and the unseen indenture and stream agreement. The first two stop every multiple and every trend from being stated, and the third leaves ranking, guarantees and covenants resting on a summary in the notes; each is logged under Gaps with its severity.
Basis: 40-F_2025-12-31_ske-20251231x40f.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 8; Source readiness (CP-0)
