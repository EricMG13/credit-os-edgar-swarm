## Findings

### Bottom line

Creditors face medium governance and structure risk at Lumen: debt sits in four borrowing groups with different guarantees and liability management since 2024 changed rankings, but no sponsor is named and no common dividend has been paid since 2022.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_lumn-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 43, 119–120; 10-Q_2026-09-30_lumn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 22–23, 24–26; calculation C1

The 2026 Mass Markets sale and USD 4,765 million of debt repurchases cut balance-sheet debt from USD 17,441 million at year-end 2025 to USD 13,193 million, and Lumen is now issuer or guarantor of 71.23% of debt principal. Executed financing documents are not supplied, so lien, guarantee-release and covenant terms are not established.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_lumn-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 114; 10-Q_2026-09-30_lumn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 23, 28; calculation C3; calculation C4; calculation C22; Source readiness (CP-0)

### Key findings

- Ownership risk is low on the evidence but unproven: no sponsor or controlling holder is named, no common dividend has been paid since 2022 and most of the sale proceeds repaid debt, yet the proxy statement that would show large holders and director independence is not supplied.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_lumn-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 43, 162; 10-Q_2026-09-30_lumn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 28
- Debt is concentrated in the Level 3 group, so recovery and refinancing turn on Level 3's assets and cash flow: Level 3 Financing carries 74.22% of debt principal, Qwest Corporation 13.00%, Lumen itself 9.67% and Qwest Capital Funding 1.24%, and Level 3 Parent's revenue from outside affiliates was 56.85% of group revenue in nine months 2026 before eliminations.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_lumn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 22–23, 61; calculation C1; calculation C8
- Guarantees now run to the parent: Lumen is issuer or guarantor of 71.23% of debt principal against the 9.67% that is its own notes, so holders of Lumen's unsecured notes share the parent's assets with guaranteed Level 3 Financing and Qwest creditors. The filings say the guarantees simplify reporting and do not discuss the effect on Lumen's own noteholders.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_lumn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 24, 26, 30; calculation C1; calculation C3
- Liability management is a pattern, not a single event, and it re-ranked claims each time. The 2024 transaction support agreement exchanged USD 10,244 million and repaid USD 2,258 million of debt into superpriority, first lien and second lien instruments, the 2024 exchange offers swapped USD 848 million of unsecured notes for USD 788 million of secured notes, second lien notes later became unsecured, and the 2026 Qwest exchange removed covenants from USD 254 million of notes that stayed out. The filings do not say what holders outside each deal received, so the size of the value transfer is not established.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_lumn-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 118–120, 125; 10-Q_2026-03-31_lumn-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 21; 10-Q_2026-09-30_lumn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 22, 24–25; calculation C5; calculation C6; calculation C17
- Capital allocation favors creditors on balance. USD 4,765 million of superpriority and secured debt was repurchased on 2026-02-02, the 2025 refinancings replaced the highest-coupon first lien notes with lower-coupon notes, and the revolver has no borrowings. The cost is that the sale also removed earnings, and USD 729 million of the cash was classified as operating cash flow against credits owed to the buyer.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_lumn-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 116; 10-Q_2026-09-30_lumn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 14–15, 28; calculation C4
- The business mix is improving but not fast enough to offset what was sold or is declining: Strategic revenue rose 10% in Q3 2026, Legacy revenue fell 17% and Mass Markets revenue fell 42%, leaving group revenue 12% lower, and no customer exceeds 10% of revenue.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_lumn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 19, 49; 10-K_2025-12-31_lumn-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 149; calculation C11
- Reporting is sound at group level but thinner for creditors of Level 3 and Qwest. KPMG LLP audited the FY2025 statements and internal control was effective, although the auditor flagged the new finance system as a critical audit matter, and Level 3 Parent and Qwest have stopped separate filings, so those creditors now rely on summaries inside Lumen's reports.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_lumn-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 75–76, 161; 10-Q_2026-09-30_lumn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 25–26, 61–62, 64
- The Alkira purchase adds little asset cover: Level 3 Communications paid USD 485 million in cash on 2026-07-01 and 99.18% of the price is goodwill and intangible assets. It is small against debt and cash-funded, so it does not signal a debt-funded roll-up.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_lumn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 15–16; calculation C12
- Protection for tax losses ends in late 2026: the rights agreement that protects USD 982 million of federal tax losses is scheduled to lapse, after which a large share purchase could limit use of the losses. Change of control offers at 101% protect holders of Lumen and Level 3 Financing notes, but the filing describes no such offer for Qwest's older notes and does not describe the terms of the new Qwest notes.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_lumn-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 40, 63, 91; 10-Q_2026-09-30_lumn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 24, 30–31

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence for lower risk is that owners have not taken value out and financial policy has turned toward creditors. USD 4,765 million of superpriority and secured debt was repaid from sale proceeds and cash, no common dividend has been paid since 2022, Moody's and Fitch each raised the corporate rating in February 2026, and liquidity of USD 1,655 million compares with USD 34 million of principal due by the end of 2027, with 92.07% of principal due in 2031 or later.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_lumn-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 43; 10-Q_2026-09-30_lumn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 23, 28, 53, 58; calculation C4; calculation C9; calculation C10

Management's own record points the other way on growth: it says Strategic sales rose 30% year on year and cumulative signed private connectivity fabric agreements exceed USD 13.5 billion. These are company statements, the contracts are not supplied and the cash arrives as advance payments, so they do not by themselves lower the risk level.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-11-04_lumnq320268-kexhibit991.htm.jsonl, page 1; 10-Q_2026-09-30_lumn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 55

The view would move to high risk on executed documents showing wide capacity for added debt, liens, investments or asset transfers outside the guarantor groups, evidence that holders outside the 2024 and 2026 exchanges lost priority or guarantees, a further exchange with covenant removal, or a controlling holder emerging after the rights agreement lapses. It would move to low risk if the documents show tight restrictions, a full year passes without another exchange or covenant removal, and deleveraging continues from earnings rather than asset sales.
Basis: Source readiness (CP-0)

### Limitations

- The proxy statement is not supplied, so the reading that no sponsor or controlling holder exists rests on silence; a large holder or a board with limited independence would move the risk level up.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_lumn-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 162
- The executed indentures and credit agreements are not supplied, so the lien, guarantee-release and capacity findings rest on the quarterly report summaries; different release or priority terms would change the medium rating.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_lumn-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 163–175; Source readiness (CP-0)
- The filings do not say what holders outside each exchange received or how many took part, so the finding that rankings changed cannot be sized; a poor outcome for those holders would move the rating toward high.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_lumn-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 118–120; 10-Q_2026-09-30_lumn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 24–25
- Level 3 Parent and Qwest figures are summarized before eliminations, so their shares of group revenue and debt are indicative and could overstate the Level 3 concentration.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_lumn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 61–62; calculation C8
- Private connectivity fabric terms and customer identities are not supplied and the signed-agreement total is management's, so the view of cash-flow quality could be wrong in either direction.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-11-04_lumnq320268-kexhibit991.htm.jsonl, page 1
- Nothing dated after 2026-11-04 is covered, so a financing, rating action or ownership filing in the 14 days to the as-of date could change the medium rating.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_lumn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 70

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

On 2026-02-02 Lumen completed the sale of its Mass Markets Fiber-to-the-Home business in 11 states to AT&T for pre-tax cash proceeds of USD 5.72 billion, subject to post-closing adjustments; the annual report gave the headline as USD 5.75 billion. Lumen applied USD 4.8 billion of the proceeds and cash on hand to repurchase USD 4,765 million of superpriority and secured debt. It recorded a net pre-tax gain of USD 569 million and USD 729 million of liabilities for contractual credits and commercial agreements with the buyer.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_lumn-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 5, 98; 10-Q_2026-09-30_lumn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 14–15, 28; calculation C4; calculation C14

New debt in 2026 was issued by Level 3 Financing and Qwest. Level 3 Financing issued a further USD 650 million of 8.500% Senior Notes due 2036 on 2026-01-09 and USD 1.0 billion of 7.500% Senior Notes due 2037 on 2026-05-21, using the proceeds to buy USD 607 million of second lien notes and USD 766 million of unsecured notes. On 2026-06-11 Qwest issued USD 1,002 million of 6.500% Notes due 2051 and USD 382 million of 6.750% Notes due 2052 in exchange offers; Lumen guarantees them, and substantially all restrictive covenants were removed from USD 254 million of old notes.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_lumn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 24–25, 27; calculation C17

Lumen entered a USD 825 million Revolving Credit Agreement on 2026-04-14, with no borrowings at 2026-09-30, and Level 3 Financing repriced its USD 2.4 billion Term Loan B-5 on 2026-05-13. On 2026-04-30 Lumen guaranteed Level 3 Financing's first lien notes and credit agreement, USD 6,825 million of secured debt, on an unsecured basis.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_lumn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 26–28; calculation C2

Level 3 Communications acquired Alkira, Inc. for USD 485 million in cash on 2026-07-01. Earlier transactions of the same kind include the 2024 transaction support agreement, the 2024 exchange offers and tender offers, and the 2025 refinancing of first lien notes and term loans; the transaction register lists each with its amounts.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_lumn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 15–16; 10-K_2025-12-31_lumn-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 114–120
