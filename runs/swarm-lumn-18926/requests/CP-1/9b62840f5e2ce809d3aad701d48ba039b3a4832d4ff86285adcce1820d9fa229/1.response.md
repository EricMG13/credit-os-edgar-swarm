## Findings

### Bottom line

Lumen cut debt to USD 13,193 million at 2026-09-30 after the Mass Markets sale, but earnings and cash flow lean on a one-off gain and customer prepayments, so reported leverage of 4.67x overstates how much stronger creditors are.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_lumn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 7, 22–23; 10-K_2025-12-31_lumn-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 78–79; calculation C4; calculation C6; calculation C14

Excluding the USD 569 million gain on sale, last-twelve-month reported EBITDA is USD 2,254 million and total debt is 5.85x that figure. Liquidity of USD 1,655 million covers the USD 34 million of debt due by the end of 2027, but the covenant definitions are not printed, so no headroom is inferred.
Basis: calculation C8; calculation C11; calculation C9; 10-Q_2026-09-30_lumn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 23, 30

### Leverage and debt

Balance-sheet debt fell by USD 4,248 million, or 24.36%, between 2025-12-31 and 2026-09-30, and net debt fell by USD 4,223 million to USD 12,215 million. The cause was the 2026-02-02 sale of the Mass Markets Fiber-to-the-Home business, whose proceeds, together with cash on hand, funded the repurchase of USD 4,765 million of superpriority notes and term loans, so the deleveraging came from an asset sale and not from earnings growth.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_lumn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 7, 28; calculation C14; calculation C5

Reported leverage is distorted in both directions. Total debt was 9.00x reported EBITDA at year-end 2025 because operating income carried a USD 628 million goodwill impairment, and it is 4.67x for the last twelve months because operating income carries the USD 569 million gain on sale. Removing the gain gives 5.85x on reported EBITDA and 5.70x on the issuer-defined Adjusted EBITDA, against 6.67x on that measure at year-end 2025.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_lumn-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 78; 10-Q_2026-09-30_lumn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 6, 14; calculation C1; calculation C2; calculation C6; calculation C8

Underlying earnings fell while debt fell: normalised last-twelve-month Adjusted EBITDA of USD 2,316 million is USD 297 million below the USD 2,613 million of FY2025, so lower earnings offset part of the debt reduction. Revenue fell 5.39% in FY2025 and the sold business no longer contributes, which is why the normalised leverage of 5.70x is the fairer starting point.
Basis: calculation C18; calculation C8; 10-K_2025-12-31_lumn-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 78

The strongest evidence for a better view is the outlook reiterated on 2026-11-04 for FY2026 Adjusted EBITDA excluding Special Items of USD 3.1 billion to USD 3.3 billion, against USD 2,366 million for the first nine months of 2026 on that basis, together with lower cash interest and the roughly USD 1 billion of annual Mass Markets fiber capital expenditure that leaves with the sold business. That measure is not the one used above, and the view wins only if fourth-quarter free cash flow excluding Special Items reaches the USD 893 million to USD 1,093 million the outlook implies, against negative USD 76 million in Q3 2026; it loses if the fourth quarter repeats the third.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-11-04_lumnq320268-kexhibit991.htm.jsonl, pages 3, 16–17; 10-Q_2026-09-30_lumn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 54; calculation C16

### Coverage and cash flow

Cash interest paid fell from USD 1,219 million in FY2025 to USD 840 million over the last twelve months as debt was repaid, lifting reported EBITDA cover from 1.59x to 3.36x; without the gain on sale, cover is 2.68x.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_lumn-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 79–80; 10-Q_2026-09-30_lumn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 7–8; calculation C6; calculation C8

Operating cash flow of USD 2,812 million in the first nine months of 2026 is not a clean measure of the business: it includes USD 729 million of divestiture proceeds classified as operating and a USD 1,832 million rise in deferred revenue. The filing does not say how much of the 729 sits inside the deferred revenue line, so the two are not added. After capital expenditure of USD 2,717 million, free cash flow was USD 95 million, against USD 1,453 million a year earlier.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_lumn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 7–8, 15; EX-99.1_2026-11-04_lumnq320268-kexhibit991.htm.jsonl, page 16; calculation C7

Q3 2026 alone was negative: operating cash flow of USD 518 million did not cover capital expenditure of USD 872 million, so free cash flow was negative USD 354 million. Capital expenditure absorbed 38.05% of last-twelve-month revenue against 35.21% in FY2025, and management expects USD 3.2 billion to USD 3.4 billion over the next twelve months, so free cash flow depends on customer prepayments continuing.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-11-04_lumnq320268-kexhibit991.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 16; 10-Q_2026-09-30_lumn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 54; calculation C17; calculation C6; calculation C19

### Liquidity and maturities

Liquidity at 2026-09-30 was USD 1,655 million: USD 978 million of cash and USD 677 million of availability under the USD 825 million revolving credit facility after USD 148 million of undrawn letters of credit. USD 92 million of the cash is held outside the United States.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_lumn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 28, 52, 53, 57; calculation C11; calculation C19

Debt due by the end of 2027 is only USD 34 million, the revolving facility matures on 2029-04-14, and 92.07% of principal falls due in 2031 or later, so near-term refinancing risk is small and the exposure is to the long-dated stack. The pressure on liquidity is cash use, not maturities: capital expenditure and the sign of free cash flow decide how long the cash and the revolver last.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_lumn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 23, 27; calculation C9

### Non-debt funding float

Deferred revenue, which funds operations without interest, rose to USD 9,258 million at 2026-09-30 from USD 7,411 million at 2025-12-31 and USD 4,594 million at 2024-12-31. The filing says advance payments under Private Connectivity Fabric agreements have historically increased operating cash flow and deferred revenue, vary by quarter and are applied to network expansion, which raises capital expenditure.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_lumn-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 78–79; 10-Q_2026-09-30_lumn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 7, 55; calculation C15

The credit implication is that reported operating cash flow rests on prepayments that can slow while the obligation to deliver the network capacity and the related capital expenditure remain. The float is not debt and is not in the debt figures, but it is a liability that must be served; the backlog figure of USD 6.1 billion excludes contracts classified as leasing arrangements, so it should not be read as covering the whole balance.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_lumn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 21, 55

### Subsequent and multi-figure events

One event after 2026-09-30 is disclosed, the 2026-10-06 transfer of the common stock and two Qwest note series to Nasdaq; it changes neither debt nor cash, so no figure is adjusted for it. Events after the 2025-12-31 balance sheet that change net debt are kept out of the year-end figures and listed separately: the divestiture cash of USD 5,706 million and the USD 470 million paid for Alkira net of cash acquired. The 2026-09-30 balance sheet already reflects both.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_lumn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 13–14, 15; calculation C12; calculation C13

The divestiture carries several figures for one event: pre-tax cash proceeds of USD 5.72 billion before post-closing adjustments, USD 4,977 million classified as investing cash flow, USD 729 million classified as operating cash flow for contractual credits and commercial agreements, and a USD 569 million net gain. The cash flow figures serve the net debt effect because they are cash received; the headline is not used because it is rounded and before adjustments.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_lumn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 8, 14–15; calculation C20

### Data quality and definitions

EBITDA is not printed in the statements. Reported EBITDA is calculated as operating income plus depreciation and amortization, so impairments and gains stay inside it. The issuer's Adjusted EBITDA adds back goodwill impairment and stock-based compensation but, unlike its Adjusted EBITDA excluding Special Items, keeps one-off gains and costs; only the second is the issuer's headline and it is not available for FY2025 because no fourth-quarter release was supplied.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_lumn-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 149; EX-99.1_2026-11-04_lumnq320268-kexhibit991.htm.jsonl, pages 16–17; calculation C1; calculation C2

The debt note foots to the balance sheet and the maturity ladder, and the Q3 2026 release ties to the quarterly report on revenue, net loss and cash. Segment reporting changed from two segments to one in 2026 and revenue categories were relabelled, so segment history comes from the annual report and is not spliced with 2026 category data. A new finance system went live in Q4 2025 and was substantially completed in Q3 2026; the auditor reported evidence over it as a critical audit matter, so the statements were prepared while controls were changing.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_lumn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 10, 35–36, 64; 10-K_2025-12-31_lumn-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 75–76; Source readiness (CP-0)

## Gaps

Adjusted EBITDA excluding Special Items, the issuer's headline measure, is not available for FY2025 or FY2024 because no fourth-quarter or annual release was supplied; this restricts a like-for-like last-twelve-month leverage on that basis, and the action that would close it is the fourth-quarter 2025 results release. Severity: High.

Reported EBITDA is a derived figure, not printed by the issuer, and it keeps a USD 628 million goodwill impairment in FY2025 and a USD 569 million gain on sale in the last twelve months; this restricts the reported leverage ratios to a labelled, unadjusted reading, and the normalised rows partly close it. Severity: Medium.

Covenant ratio definitions, the covenant EBITDA and the reported covenant ratios are not printed in any source, so no headroom against the 5.25x total net leverage and 2.00x interest coverage tests is calculated; the action is the revolving credit agreement and the latest compliance certificate. Severity: Medium.

Carrying value by instrument and by seniority is not disclosed, so senior secured debt to EBITDA and the facility-level carrying values are Not calculable; the debt table gives principal by line and one aggregate financing cost adjustment. Severity: Medium.

No legal ranking across Lumen, Level 3 Financing and Qwest debt is stated, so the debt stack table is left without a ranked stack; the action is the security and intercreditor documents. Severity: Medium.

Funds from operations is not defined in any source, so FFO to debt is Not calculable; the issuer's Unlevered Cash Flow and Free Cash Flow are the nearest measures and are not substitutes. Severity: Low.

A dash in a filing is carried as Not supplied and never as zero: net loss on sale of businesses in FY2025, 9M 2025 and Q3 2025, goodwill impairment in FY2024, 9M 2026, Q3 2025 and Q3 2026, goodwill at 2025-12-31 and the other dash lines of the statements; these restrict nothing, because the statements foot without them. Severity: Low.

Lines that the quarterly reports do not print separately are Not supplied for the interim columns: impairment of long-lived assets, debt modification costs, gain on sale of investment, unrealized loss on investments, revolving line of credit and dividends paid; the totals foot, and the lines affect no ratio. Severity: Low.

Assets held for sale and liabilities held for sale are not separated in the FY2023 balance sheet, because the FY2024 report prints a single Other line; the FY2023 other current assets figure therefore includes any such assets. Severity: Low.

The FY2024 and FY2025 segment data use two segments and the Grow, Nurture, Harvest and Other categories, and 2026 uses one segment and Strategic and Legacy; the release channel revenue for Q3 2025 and Q3 2026 is a customer-group view, and segment adjusted EBITDA is not stated for 2026. Severity: Low.

The release states Q4 2025 revenue by channel only, so Q4 2025 statements and the discrete fourth-quarter cash flow are not supplied; no quarterly series is built for 2025 other than Q3. Severity: Low.

The KPI dashboard cells marked Not supplied are periods for which the issuer states no value for that measure; Network-as-a-Service customers is stated only as more than 4,000. Severity: Low.

The USD 5.72 billion headline divestiture proceeds are rounded and before post-closing adjustments, and the cash taxes paid on the gain are not netted in the post-balance-sheet table; the pro forma leverage divides by unchanged FY2025 EBITDA and so overstates leverage after the sale. The 2024-12-31 revolver availability is stated by the issuer as approximate, and the fiscal-year balance sheet for 2023 supports no liquidity figure; the liquidity table starts at 2024-12-31. Severity: Low.

The finance system change, with an auditor critical audit matter over its implementation, means the FY2025 and 2026 statements were prepared while controls were changing; this lowers reliance on small line-level differences and restricts nothing in the totals. Severity: Low.
