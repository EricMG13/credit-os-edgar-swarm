## Findings

### Bottom line

The seven supplied filings and releases support a credit snapshot of Allison Transmission Holdings Inc as of 5 November 2026, so creditors can rely on the filed debt, liquidity and covenant figures but not on pro forma leverage or legal-document terms.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_alsn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 19–22; EX-99.1_2026-11-02_d187329dex991.htm.jsonl, page 3; calculation C-01; calculation C-02
