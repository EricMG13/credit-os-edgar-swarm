## Findings

### Bottom line

XPO's credit profile strengthened through Q3 2026: last-twelve-months reported net leverage is 2.03x and interest cover is 6.54x, so creditors hold a better-covered claim than at FY2025.

Total debt at carrying value is USD 3,124 million against USD 3,313 million at FY2025, after USD 1,085 million of term debt was repaid and USD 885 million of new term loans was raised. Apart from finance leases, only a USD 75 million term loan prepayment falls before the USD 830 million secured notes due in 2028.

The issuer states only an adjusted EBITDA, so reported EBITDA is derived here; on the adjusted USD 1,498 million basis total leverage is 2.09x. Covenant levels and the revolver drawn balance are not printed.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_xpo-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 13; 10-Q_2026-09-30_xpo-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 15; 10-Q_2026-09-30_xpo-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-K_2025-12-31_xpo-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 102; calculation C5; calculation C6; calculation C7; calculation C11; calculation C23; calculation C24

### Leverage and coverage

Reported leverage fell because earnings rose faster than debt fell. Last-twelve-months reported EBITDA is USD 1,412 million against USD 1,177 million for FY2025, driven by North American LTL, where nine-month adjusted EBITDA rose from USD 857 million to USD 1,070 million on higher yield, tonnage and fuel surcharge revenue. Net debt fell by USD 139 million over the nine months to USD 2,864 million, and cash interest paid is USD 216 million on the last-twelve-months basis, so reported EBITDA covers cash interest 6.54x against 5.14x for FY2025. The consequence for creditors is that the reduction in leverage depends mainly on the freight cycle and on pricing, not on a lower debt stock, which fell only 5.7% over the nine months.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_xpo-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 9–10; 10-Q_2026-09-30_xpo-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 31; calculation C5; calculation C7; calculation C11; calculation C23

Fuel is the main distortion in the revenue trend. North American LTL fuel surcharge revenue was USD 838 million for nine months 2026 against USD 555 million a year earlier, so part of the 11.0% revenue growth is a pass-through that also raises fuel cost. The European Transportation segment earns nine-month adjusted EBITDA of USD 129 million on USD 2,694 million of revenue, a thin margin, and the board authorisation to divest it remains in effect with no stated terms.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_xpo-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 9–10; 10-Q_2026-09-30_xpo-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 25; 10-Q_2026-09-30_xpo-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 26; 10-Q_2026-09-30_xpo-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 31

### Cash flow quality and liquidity

Nine-month operating cash flow was USD 906 million against USD 760 million a year earlier, and capital expenditure fell to USD 369 million from USD 551 million, a planned reduction against a full-year 2026 range of USD 500 million to USD 600 million. Free cash flow before financing (operating cash flow less capital expenditure) was therefore USD 537 million for the nine months. Management directed it to term debt repayment of USD 1,085 million, share repurchases of USD 250 million and USD 124 million of tax withholdings on vested awards. A capex rebound towards the guided range, or repurchases continuing at the nine-month pace until the USD 375 million remaining authorisation is used, would slow the deleveraging.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_xpo-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-Q_2026-09-30_xpo-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 39; calculation C26

Liquidity is cash of USD 260 million plus about USD 600 million of undrawn revolver, USD 860 million in all, and the revolver matures in 2030. The USD 131 million of letters of credit sit on a separate uncommitted facility, so they do not reduce revolver availability, but the facility can be withdrawn.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_xpo-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 15; 10-Q_2026-09-30_xpo-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 33; calculation C27

### Debt structure and funding outside debt

The May 2026 refinancing replaced the 2028 term loan B with a USD 500 million term loan A due 2029 and a USD 385 million term loan B due 2031, which cut the 2028 principal payments from USD 1,415 million at FY2025 to the USD 830 million secured notes. Apart from finance leases, the only debt falling due in the next twelve months is the USD 75 million of term loan B prepaid in October 2026. The 6.70% debentures carry a USD 66 million discount to principal from acquisition accounting, which is most of the USD 83 million gap between carrying value and principal.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_xpo-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 13–17; 10-K_2025-12-31_xpo-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 102; calculation C18; calculation C19

The European receivables securitisation is funding that sits outside debt. The programme maximum of EUR 200 million, about USD 227 million, was fully used at 2026-09-30, and the receivables are sold rather than pledged as debt, so it adds to the funding the business relies on without appearing in gross debt or leverage. Operating lease liabilities of USD 797 million (USD 170 million short-term plus USD 627 million long-term) are also outside debt.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_xpo-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 6–7; 10-Q_2026-09-30_xpo-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 2

### Data quality and definitions

The filings print rounded millions, so four statement identities differ by one unit: the cash roll-forward for FY2024, FY2025 and nine months 2025, and total liabilities plus equity against total assets for FY2024. Each affected total is stated as not supplied below rather than forced. Reported EBITDA is calculated (net income plus income tax plus interest expense plus depreciation and amortisation) because the issuer states only adjusted EBITDA; the difference between the two is the debt extinguishment loss, the environmental matter, legal matters, transaction and integration costs and restructuring costs.

- Total liabilities at FY2024 is not supplied because the printed subtotals and equity are one unit below total assets. Severity Low.
- The balance sheet at 2025-09-30 is not in the sources, so there is no nine months 2025 balance sheet and no interim leverage at that date. Severity Low.
