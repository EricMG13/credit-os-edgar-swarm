### Financial interpretation

Leverage rose in 2025 because debt grew faster than earnings: total debt increased by USD 5,530 million to fund the Snai, NSX and Boyd transactions, taking adjusted net leverage from 2.21x to 3.67x. In 2026 debt stayed close to that level, so the move to 4.55x at 2026-09-30 comes from the earnings side, with nine-month adjusted EBITDA down to USD 1,499 million from USD 2,013 million, which the company attributes to the UK remote gaming duty increase and investment in its US prediction markets offer.
Basis: calculation C18; calculation C06; calculation C08; EX-99.1_2026-11-04_d141532dex991.htm.jsonl, page 1; EX-99.1_2026-11-04_d141532dex991.htm.jsonl, page 11; 10-K_2025-12-31_flut-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 190; 10-Q_2026-09-30_flut-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 25

Cash interest paid was USD 636 million over the last twelve months against USD 531 million in 2025, which the company links to higher debt balances. Adjusted EBITDA covers that interest 3.67x against 5.36x in 2025, while reported EBITDA covers it only 1.03x; the reported measure carries USD 873 million of Brazil impairments, so the adjusted cover is the better guide to debt service capacity but rests on add-backs the filings do not allow a reader to test.
Basis: calculation C06; calculation C08; 10-Q_2026-09-30_flut-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 76

Operating cash flow of USD 904 million for nine months was higher than the USD 756 million a year earlier, but the working-capital lines added USD 343 million against a USD 559 million outflow, helped by accrued expenses that include UK gambling duty and legal provisions the company has not yet paid. Free cash flow after USD 527 million of capital expenditure was USD 377 million; a reversal of accruals or a customer-balance outflow would reduce it before debt service on the larger post-2025 debt stack.
Basis: calculation C21; calculation C15; 10-Q_2026-09-30_flut-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 76

Liquidity at 2026-09-30 was USD 1,708 million of unrestricted cash plus USD 782 million of undrawn revolver, USD 2,490 million in all. Player deposit cash of USD 2,117 million sits beside a customer liability of USD 1,987 million, is held in segregated accounts and is not used for general corporate purposes, so it is excluded from both cash and net debt. The amendment of 2026-10-30 extends the revolver and term A maturities to 2032 and raises revolver commitments, but the effect on undrawn capacity and on the maturity table is not stated.
Basis: calculation C17; calculation C22; 10-Q_2026-09-30_flut-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 5–6; 10-Q_2026-09-30_flut-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 42; 10-K_2025-12-31_flut-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 152

The strongest contrary evidence is the issuer's statement that it was in compliance with all debt covenants at 2026-09-30, together with its target range of 2.0x to 2.5x and the pause in share repurchases to delever. Compliance is the issuer's own statement and the covenant ratio cannot be reproduced here because its definitions sit in the unsupplied credit agreement, so it does not establish headroom; further adjusted EBITDA erosion from the full-year effect of the UK duty increase would show first in the adjusted leverage ratio and the interest cover.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-11-04_d141532dex991.htm.jsonl, pages 11–12; 10-Q_2026-09-30_flut-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 27; 10-K_2025-12-31_flut-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 189; calculation C08
