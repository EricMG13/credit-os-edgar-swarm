## Findings

### Bottom line
Baytex is well covered for creditors today because it holds net cash and an undrawn revolver, but its cash flow follows the oil price without flat-price hedges, so creditors rely on management keeping shareholder returns within free cash flow. At 2026-06-30 net debt was negative CAD 631.1 million, or negative CAD 615.0 million pro forma after the CAD 16.1 million dividend paid on 2026-07-02, against CAD 745.6 million of undrawn revolving capacity.

### Company description
Baytex is a Calgary-based oil and natural gas producer in the Western Canadian Sedimentary Basin, with heavy oil assets at Peavine, Peace River and Lloydminster and light oil in the Viking and the Duvernay. Production was 71,243 boe/d in the second quarter of 2026, 88% oil and natural gas liquids, after the sale of the Eagle Ford assets for net proceeds of US$2.2 billion on 2025-12-19, which repaid most debt and left one small note.
