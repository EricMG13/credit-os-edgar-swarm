## Findings

### Bottom line
Baytex has no sponsor or controlling owner stated in the documents, so governance risk to creditors rests on its own financial policy, which is to return cash to shareholders while keeping net cash, and nothing in the filings shows creditor-adverse action. Sponsor and governance risk is medium on documented behaviour.
Basis: 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-06-30_a991-q22026fs.htm.jsonl, pages 10–11; 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-08-05_tm2619297d2_ex99-1.htm.jsonl, pages 8–9; Source readiness (CP-0)

The business is a Western Canadian oil producer weighted to heavy oil, at 65% of production, so cash flow follows the oil price and the heavy-oil discount rather than a contracted income stream. First-half 2026 repurchases and dividends of CAD 349.1 million exceeded issuer-defined free cash flow of CAD 129.8 million, so the behaviour to monitor is the pace of distributions against cash created by the Eagle Ford sale. Ownership percentages, board control rights and the debt documents are not established.
Basis: 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-08-05_tm2619297d2_ex99-1.htm.jsonl, page 3; 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-08-05_tm2619297d2_ex99-1.htm.jsonl, page 25; 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-06-30_a991-q22026fs.htm.jsonl, page 3; calculation C03

### Key findings
- No sponsor exists to assess, so conduct is read from the issuer's own actions. The documents describe a publicly traded company whose common shares carry one vote each and rank equally, with no preferred shares issued, and name no controlling shareholder; the holders and their percentages are not established because the annual information form is not supplied.
Basis: 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-06-30_a991-q22026fs.htm.jsonl, page 10; 40-F_2025-12-31_bte-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 1–2; 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-03-31_tm2613640d2_ex99-1.htm.jsonl, page 1; Source readiness (CP-0)
- Creditor treatment has been favourable since the Eagle Ford sale. Net proceeds of US$2.2 billion closed on 2025-12-19 were followed by repayment of most of the credit facilities, redemption of the 8.5% senior notes and partial redemption of the 2032 notes, and the issuer later repurchased US$5.8 million of the 2032 notes at a premium to par.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view
The strongest evidence against concern is that creditor protection rests on a policy rather than on any document in the set. No indenture or credit agreement is supplied, so the restricted-payment and debt-incurrence limits that the notes summary calls standard cannot be read, and leverage could rise if buybacks or the Gemini thermal project, which may reach a final investment decision in 2027, were funded with borrowing.
Basis: 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-06-30_a991-q22026fs.htm.jsonl, page 10; 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-03-31_tm2613640d2_ex99-1.htm.jsonl, page 2; 6-K-EX-99.1_2026-03-31_tm2613640d2_ex99-1.htm.jsonl, page 5

The view would change if the revolver were drawn to fund distributions, if the net cash commitment were withdrawn, or if an annual information form disclosed a shareholder with control or consent rights; steady quarterly reporting, no covenant waiver or exchange offer in the set, and cash that exceeds debt many times over would argue for keeping the current view.
