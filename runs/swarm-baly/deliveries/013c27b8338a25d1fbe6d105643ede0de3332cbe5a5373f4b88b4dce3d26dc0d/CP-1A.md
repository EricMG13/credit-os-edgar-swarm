## Findings

### Bottom line

Creditors are exposed to a controlled company whose growth programme has outrun its funded liquidity: no dividends or fee leakage are documented, but no sponsor equity support is either, so sponsor and governance risk is medium and protection rests on documents, not alignment.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_baly-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 66; 10-K_2025-12-31_baly-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 72; 10-Q_2026-06-30_baly-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 11; 10-Q_2026-06-30_baly-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 63

Half of revenue comes from casinos, most of them leased from one landlord, and 41.14% from Bally's Intralot, a separate obligor group; Chicago, Bronx and Las Vegas commitments, and a controlling holder whose managing partner chairs the board, mean creditor protection rests on the executed documents and lender waivers, not on board independence.
Basis: calculation A02; calculation A03; 10-K_2025-12-31_baly-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 8; 10-K_2025-12-31_baly-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 66; 10-Q_2026-06-30_baly-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 45

### Key findings

One holder controls every creditor-relevant decision, so the board is not an independent check. Standard General beneficially owned 67.1% of the common stock at 2026-02-28, down from 73.8% at the February 2025 merger, the company is a controlled company exempt from independent-board requirements, and Standard General's managing partner is Executive Chairman. Financing, acquisition and distribution decisions therefore reach creditors without a minority check, which makes the legal documents and the Rhode Island regulatory agreement, which restricts distributions and new debt above a leverage of 5.50x on its own definition, the real constraints.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_baly-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 66; 10-K_2025-12-31_baly-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 72; 10-K_2025-12-31_baly-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 112; 10-K_2025-12-31_baly-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 16

The 2025 deals changed what creditors own: Casinos & Resorts fell from 56.75% of Successor-period revenue in FY2025 to 50.44% in the first half of 2026, while Bally's Intralot rose from 34.90% to 41.14%. Bally's Intralot brings lottery contracts and interactive operations in a subsidiary with its own lenders and minority holders, so part of group revenue growth sits behind other claims, and the UK tax increase hit its consumer segment in the second quarter of 2026.
Basis: calculation A01; calculation A02; calculation A03; calculation A12; 10-Q_2026-06-30_baly-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 11; EX-99.1_2026-08-14_ex991q22026earningsrelease.htm.jsonl, page 3

Landlord claims sit ahead of lenders in practice and are growing. Triple-net rent takes 29.06% of Adjusted EBITDAR over the last twelve months, the Twin River sale-leaseback added USD 56.0 million of annual rent in February 2026, and the Chicago ground lease adds 8.5% of every GLPI development advance to rent. After any refinancing, extension or majority amendment of the credit agreement the Chicago ground lease cross-defaults to the two GLPI master leases, so a default under the master leases would then also put the Chicago site and its landlord-funded construction at risk.
Basis: calculation A06; calculation A11; calculation A09; 10-K_2025-12-31_baly-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 48; 10-K_2025-12-31_baly-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 49; 10-Q_2026-06-30_baly-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 39

The development programme is the main discretionary claim on cash and is only partly funded. Chicago must spend at least USD 1,340.0 million under its host community agreement with about USD 400.0 million remaining and an expected overrun that the filing cannot yet estimate, GLPI advances of up to USD 940.0 million are conditional on the company showing unrestricted access to funds, and the USD 4.0 billion Bronx project has only a non-binding term sheet and a letter of intent behind it after a USD 500.0 million licence fee and USD 115 million of contingent consideration were paid in the first quarter of 2026. If the Bronx financing is not completed, the remaining project obligations would fall on the same liquidity that supports the revolver.
Basis: calculation A09; calculation A10; 10-Q_2026-06-30_baly-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 11; 10-Q_2026-06-30_baly-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 44; 10-Q_2026-06-30_baly-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 45; 10-Q_2026-06-30_baly-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 72; EX-99.1_2026-08-14_ex991q22026earningsrelease.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-K_2025-12-31_baly-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 49

Sponsor and company behaviour in the sources is mixed rather than extractive. No cash dividend was paid in either period and the filings state no intention to pay one, and no management or monitoring fee to Standard General appears in the documents read; against that, the company paid USD 416,180 thousand of cash to non-rolling shareholders at USD 18.25 a share in the first quarter of 2025, bought more Bally's Intralot shares for USD 24,359 thousand in the first half of 2026 while liquidity was falling, and entered a total return swap on Bally's Intralot shares of up to EUR 50.0 million in June 2026 ahead of the evoke offer.
Basis: calculation A05; 10-K_2025-12-31_baly-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 84; 10-Q_2026-06-30_baly-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 63; 10-Q_2026-06-30_baly-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-Q_2026-06-30_baly-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 5; 10-Q_2026-06-30_baly-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 28; 10-K_2025-12-31_baly-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 113

Disclosure quality limits how well creditors can monitor the group. A material weakness over income-tax review remains open at the June 2026 report, disclosure controls were not effective in both the annual and the June report, the annual report was filed under an extension, and the June quarterly report omits market-risk disclosure; the 2026 proxy statement that would carry board composition and compensation is not in the sources.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_baly-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 191; 10-K_2025-12-31_baly-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 193; 10-Q_2026-06-30_baly-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 73; EX-99.1_2026-03-16_ex991q42025earningsrelease.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-K_2025-12-31_baly-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 197

Overall, the evidence supports a medium sponsor and governance risk level. The supported behaviour is a controlling holder presiding over an acquisitive and development-heavy strategy that rests on landlord funding and lender waivers, which matters because it leaves creditors without equity support or board checks and implies refinancing and liquidity risk rather than leakage risk. Countervailing evidence is the absence of dividends or documented fee extraction and the willingness of lenders to waive; further analysis requires the proxy statement, the executed credit and lease documents, and any sponsor equity commitment.
Basis: calculation A04; 10-Q_2026-06-30_baly-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 11; 10-K_2025-12-31_baly-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 66; 10-K_2025-12-31_baly-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 49; Source readiness (CP-0)

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a cautious view is that nothing in the sources shows value leaving the group: no dividend, no related-party fee and no priming transaction appear, Standard General's reported holding is lower at 67.1% than the 73.8% at the merger closing, lenders granted a conditional waiver and amendments instead of enforcing, and GLPI is advancing Chicago construction costs. A binding Bronx loan with third-party equity, a sponsor equity cure or a disclosed absence of fees would move the view toward low risk; a distribution, a related-party transaction funded with debt, a further waiver without deleveraging or an uptier or priming step would move it to high.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_baly-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 112; 10-K_2025-12-31_baly-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 72; 10-Q_2026-06-30_baly-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 11; 10-Q_2026-06-30_baly-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 40; 10-Q_2026-06-30_baly-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 63

### Limitations

- Standard General's 67.1% holding is dated 2026-02-28 and no later holding is supplied, so the control finding would change only if the holding fell below a majority of votes, which no document indicates.
- No sponsor fund vintage, remaining fund life, management or monitoring fee, or equity-contribution amount is stated, so the medium risk level could rise if fees or exit pressure were disclosed and cannot fall to low without a documented equity commitment.
- Segment definitions were re-cut in the fourth quarter of 2025 and prior periods recast, so the shift in revenue mix between casinos and Bally's Intralot depends on the recast boundaries and is directional only.
- Only issuer-level conduct is assessed, so the development-risk finding for Chicago, the Bronx and Las Vegas rests on disclosed commitments and costs rather than delivery evidence, and would change if budget or milestone documents were supplied.
- The ownership map covers the entities the filings name; intermediate holding companies and any parent support of Bally's Intralot obligations are not described, so the finding that the two obligor groups are separate holds only for the documents read.

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

The 2025 merger of Bally's Corporation with Standard General's Queen Casino & Entertainment closed on 2025-02-07: Standard General contributed its Queen shares in exchange for new Bally's common stock, public holders not electing to roll received USD 18.25 in cash per share, and Standard General's group ended with 73.8% of the common stock. The Queen merger was accounted for as a transaction between entities under common control, and the company settled Queen's pre-existing debt and issued USD 500.0 million of 11% first-lien notes due 2028 at closing, which it repaid in full on 2025-10-08.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_baly-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 112; 10-K_2025-12-31_baly-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 113; 10-K_2025-12-31_baly-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 104; 10-K_2025-12-31_baly-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 163

On 2025-10-08 the company closed the Bally's Intralot transaction: Bally's Intralot paid USD 1.8 billion of cash and issued new shares for Bally's International Interactive, lifting the company's stake to a controlling 57.9% and creating a 42.1% non-controlling interest, and the company assumed Bally's Intralot debt of USD 1.97 billion at a fair value of USD 1.98 billion. New debt in this group since then comprises EUR 600 million of 6.75% senior secured notes and EUR 300 million of floating-rate notes due 2031, a GBP 400.0 million term loan, a euro term loan and a euro revolver, and on 2026-07-27 Bally's Intralot signed a further GBP 261.8 million of senior secured term facilities.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_baly-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 7; 10-K_2025-12-31_baly-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 114; 10-K_2025-12-31_baly-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 166; 10-K_2025-12-31_baly-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 168; 10-Q_2026-06-30_baly-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 37

At Bally's Corporation the new debt is the USD 1.1 billion of 2026 term loans signed on 2026-02-11, priced at 750 bp over term SOFR with a 300 bp floor and up to 3.50% payable in kind, which repaid the previous term loan and mature in 2031 unless the 2029 notes remain outstanding on 2029-03-01. The Twin River sale-leaseback to GLPI closed the same day for USD 700.0 million, and the credit agreement amendments of January and May 2026 split the revolver's maturities and raised its margins, while a further amendment dated 2026-07-29 is not described.
