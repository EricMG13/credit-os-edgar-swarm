## Findings

### Bottom line

Credit metrics are improving and the facility is being repaid from cash flow: net debt to trailing-twelve-month reported EBITDA fell to 3.91x at 30 September 2026 from 4.84x at the end of 2025, which favours lenders. Cash interest cover rose to 3.17x from 2.35x as earnings grew and USD 95.0 million of voluntary prepayments and the 2025 repricing cut cash interest.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_fa-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 2–4; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fa-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 12; 10-K_2025-12-31_fa-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 132–134; calculation C05; calculation C08; calculation C09

The acquisition effective 4 November 2026 is not in those figures: its USD 125.0 million cash consideration lifts pro forma net leverage to 4.19x before any acquired earnings, with USD 45.0 million borrowed on the revolver. Reported EBITDA is derived because the issuer states none, and fiscal 2024 multiples are distorted by Sterling transaction charges and two months of Sterling earnings, so only the latest two periods are comparable.

### Financial interpretation

Leverage is falling because earnings rose and debt was repaid, not because of any refinancing. Net debt over last-twelve-months reported EBITDA was 3.91x at 30 September 2026 against 4.84x at the end of 2025, and total debt over the same earnings was 4.45x against 5.47x. Cash stayed close to its year-end level while USD 95.0 million of voluntary prepayments reduced the term loan, so the improvement in the multiple comes from both a smaller numerator and a larger denominator. On the issuer's own adjusted EBITDA the corresponding net multiples are 3.59x and 4.17x, a labelled comparison that depends on add-backs whose realisation status the issuer does not state.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_fa-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 2–4; 10-K_2025-12-31_fa-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 132–134; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fa-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 12; calculation C08; calculation C10; calculation C07

Cash interest cover has improved to 3.17x on a trailing basis from 2.35x for 2025 because cash interest paid fell while earnings rose. The nine-month cash interest paid was lower than in the prior nine months because the term loan was repriced in July 2025 and reduced by prepayments, and the issuer attributes the stronger nine-month operating cash flow partly to lower interest payments. Free cash flow after cash interest, tax, working capital, capitalized software and property purchases was positive and rose, at 34.62% of Adjusted EBITDA for the nine months of 2026 against 31.92% for 2025, even though receivables absorbed more cash as revenue grew. Capitalized software is the main capital outlay, so the free cash flow depends on continued software investment rather than on a low capital base.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_fa-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 3–4; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fa-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 48; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fa-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 12; 10-K_2025-12-31_fa-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 133–134; calculation C09; calculation C13; calculation C04

The liquidity cushion is adequate and no scheduled principal falls due before 2028 on the year-end ladder, but that ladder pre-dates the 2026 prepayments and is the latest maturity table printed. Cash and the undrawn revolver gave USD 490.202 million of liquidity at 30 September 2026, before the acquisition of 4 November 2026, which carries USD 125.0 million of cash consideration and a USD 45.0 million revolver draw; how the rest of the consideration was funded is not stated, so liquidity after the event is not established. The term loan matures in 2031 and the revolver in 2029, so neither facility matures within twelve months of the as-of date.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_fa-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 11–12; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fa-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 45; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fa-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 22–23; 10-K_2025-12-31_fa-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 157; calculation C12; calculation C06; calculation C11

The main pressure point to monitor is the floating-rate exposure. Interest rate swaps with notional of USD 985.0 million cover 48.77% of term loan principal today, but three swaps totalling USD 460.0 million mature on 31 December 2026, which leaves USD 525.0 million, or 26.00%, from 2027. The 2025 annual report states that a change of 100 bp in the rate would move annual interest expense by about USD 21.1 million. Part of the improvement in cash interest cover therefore rests on hedges that expire, and cash interest on the expired notional will rise if floating rates are above the fixed swap rates of 3.56% to 3.94%.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_fa-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 12–13; 10-K_2025-12-31_fa-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 118; calculation C16

Contrary evidence to the improving trend is concentrated in one segment and in definitions. First Advantage International earned an Adjusted EBITDA margin of 5.9% in the nine months of 2026 against 16.1% a year earlier on lower revenue, although it contributes only a small part of consolidated Adjusted EBITDA; Americas margin was broadly flat at 29.9% against 30.1%, and Sterling improved to 29.6% from 27.7%. Reported EBITDA is derived and carries loss on extinguishment of debt, and the issuer's add-backs of USD 39.692 million over the last twelve months separate reported from adjusted EBITDA; the add-backs include integration and restructuring charges, part of which the issuer attributes to Sterling.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_fa-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 41; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fa-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 40–41; 10-K_2025-12-31_fa-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 102–103; calculation C17; calculation C02; calculation C04

A supported downside sequence starts with weaker employer hiring volumes, which the issuer lists among its macroeconomic risks and which drive its revenue from existing customers. Cost of services, which includes third-party data and internal fulfillment costs, runs at 54.6% of revenue for the nine months and rose with volumes, so revenue declines would be partly offset, while depreciation, technology and software costs are less variable in the short term, which is an analyst judgement. A fall in EBITDA would then raise leverage from 3.91x at the same time as expiring swaps raise cash interest, with cash and the undrawn revolver as the liquidity sources. Observable signals are revenue growth from existing customers, the International margin, the swap roll on 31 December 2026 and any revolver use above the 40.0% threshold at which the springing first lien leverage test applies. No stress figure is established here.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_fa-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 29; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fa-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 34; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fa-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 12; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fa-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 12–13; calculation C16

Deferred revenues are the only non-debt funding float and are small, at USD 4.767 million, with no supplier finance disclosed, so no float risk is carried forward. The legal accrual of USD 8.5 million at 30 September 2026 and the lease liabilities sit outside debt and outside net debt.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_fa-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fa-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 19
