## Findings

### Bottom line

Net debt is 3.91x last-twelve-months reported EBITDA at 30 September 2026 and cash interest is covered 3.17x, but a USD 125.0 million cash acquisition on 4 November 2026 lifts net leverage to 4.19x, so deleveraging now depends on earnings growth.
Basis: calculation C09; calculation C10; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fa-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 23

The FY2024 ratios are not comparable, because year-end debt carries the full Sterling financing while reported EBITDA holds two months of Sterling and USD 125.7 million of its transaction charges. All funded debt is one first lien bank facility with no scheduled principal due before 2028 at the end of 2025, and cash plus the undrawn revolver of USD 490.2 million is 24.62% of total debt.
Basis: calculation C08; calculation C19; 10-K_2025-12-31_fa-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 102; 10-K_2025-12-31_fa-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 157; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fa-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 45

### Financial interpretation

Leverage has fallen because earnings rose and debt was repaid. Total debt to reported EBITDA improved from 5.47x at the end of 2025 to 4.45x on a last-twelve-months basis at 30 September 2026, and net debt to reported EBITDA from 4.84x to 3.91x. Of the improvement in total leverage, 77.10% came from higher EBITDA and 22.90% from lower debt, chiefly USD 95.0 million of voluntary term loan prepayments. On the issuer's adjusted measure total leverage moved from 4.71x to 4.09x, a smaller step, because reported EBITDA grew 17.76% against 10.37% for adjusted EBITDA as the charges the issuer adds back shrank. The adjusted ratios are therefore the better guide to underlying progress and the reported ratios the better guide to the cost actually incurred.
Basis: calculation C09; calculation C18; calculation C19; 10-K_2025-12-31_fa-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 102; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fa-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 12; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fa-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 40

The FY2024 ratios of 25.77x total debt and 23.75x net debt to reported EBITDA and cover of 1.26x should not be read as a trend point. Sterling Check was acquired on 31 October 2024, so the year-end balance sheet carries all of the acquisition debt while the income statement holds only two months of the acquired earnings and transaction charges of USD 125.7 million. FY2025 is the first full year of the combined business, and the 9M 2025 and 9M 2026 stubs both include Sterling for nine months, which is why the last-twelve-months construction is comparable. The issuer gives pro forma revenue and net loss for FY2024 and FY2023 but no pro forma EBITDA, so a like-for-like FY2024 leverage cannot be built from the filings.
Basis: calculation C05; calculation C09; calculation C19; 10-K_2024-12-31_fa-20241231.htm.jsonl, page 136; 10-K_2024-12-31_fa-20241231.htm.jsonl, page 155; 10-K_2025-12-31_fa-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 102; 10-K_2025-12-31_fa-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 153

Cash interest cover rose from 2.35x in FY2025 to 3.17x on a last-twelve-months basis, and from 2.73x to 3.45x on adjusted EBITDA. Cash interest paid is 12.72% lower than in FY2025, which fits the issuer's explanation that term loan interest fell after the margin was reduced on 30 July 2025 to 2.50% to 2.75% over SOFR and after voluntary prepayments of USD 95.0 million in 2026. Interest expense, net understates the cash cost in 2026 because it is net of unrealised swap gains and interest income, so cover should be read on cash interest paid. Cover would fall again if SOFR rose, because the hedges are partial: swaps cover 48.77% of principal today and 26.00% after the three swaps maturing on 31 December 2026 expire, leaving 74.00% of the principal exposed to SOFR.
Basis: calculation C09; calculation C16; calculation C23; 10-K_2025-12-31_fa-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 156; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fa-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 12–13

Cash generation is adequate but thinner than adjusted EBITDA suggests. Last-twelve-months operating cash flow is 51.89% of adjusted EBITDA and levered free cash flow is 36.39%, after cash interest, cash taxes that rose 67.50% in the nine months, working capital absorption and capital expenditure that rose 56.85% on higher capitalized software. Levered free cash flow equals 8.90% of total debt. In the nine months to 30 September 2026, term loan prepayments absorbed 74.11% of levered free cash flow and share repurchases a further 29.94%, so together they used 104.05% and cash stayed flat. The USD 100.0 million repurchase programme authorised on 25 February 2026 remains partly unused, so shareholder distributions compete directly with deleveraging for the same cash.
Basis: calculation C13; calculation C19; calculation C20; calculation C21; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fa-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 3–4; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fa-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 48; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fa-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 50

Liquidity is comfortable against near-term obligations. Cash of USD 240.9 million and an undrawn revolver of USD 249.3 million give USD 490.2 million. The year-end ladder shows no scheduled principal in 2026 or 2027, the first instalment falls in 2028, the undrawn revolver matures on 31 October 2029 and the term loan on 31 October 2031, so no funded principal is due before 2028 and the final maturity is five years away. The one large cash call outside the ladder is discretionary: the acquisition of 4 November 2026, of which USD 45.0 million was drawn on the revolver and USD 80.0 million came from sources the filing does not state.
Basis: calculation C06; calculation C08; calculation C19; 10-K_2025-12-31_fa-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 157; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fa-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 11–12; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fa-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 23; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fa-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 45

The acquisition raises net debt by up to the full cash consideration of USD 125.0 million. On unchanged last-twelve-months EBITDA this lifts net leverage from 3.91x to 4.19x, which gives back part of the improvement since the end of 2025 until the acquired earnings, which the filing does not state, are counted. The figure is an upper bound because cash acquired and the working-capital settlement are not stated, and it is not cumulative with any later event.
Basis: calculation C10; calculation C19; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fa-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 23

Claims outside funded debt are small. Operating lease liabilities are USD 8,763 thousand and the accrued legal liability is USD 8.5 million at 30 September 2026, and deferred revenues of USD 4.8 million are too small to act as a funding float. No pension, retiree medical, factoring or supply-chain finance obligation was found. Two items have no stated amount: the minimum-volume purchase commitment under a twelve-month service contract, which management expects to exceed in the ordinary course, and the compliance duties under consent decrees with the US Federal Trade Commission inherited from a 2013 acquisition.
Basis: calculation C19; calculation C22; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fa-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fa-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 19; 10-K_2025-12-31_fa-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 33; 10-K_2025-12-31_fa-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 112

The strongest evidence against the improving trend is that earnings growth, not cash generation, explains most of it. Levered free cash flow converts only 36.39% of adjusted EBITDA, debt prepayments and share repurchases together took more than all of it in the nine months, and the acquisition adds debt before it adds reported earnings. The improvement would be confirmed if net leverage on reported EBITDA stayed below the 4.84x of the end of 2025 once the acquisition is consolidated and cover stayed above 2.35x. It would be reversed by a further debt-funded acquisition or a larger repurchase programme absorbing free cash flow, or by a rise in SOFR while 74.00% of principal is unhedged.
Basis: calculation C09; calculation C10; calculation C20; calculation C21; calculation C23

A hiring slowdown is the credible initiating condition, because most revenue is pre-onboarding screening that depends on hiring volumes. Lower volumes would first reduce revenue and the operating leverage that has lifted Adjusted EBITDA margin, then cut EBITDA faster than cash interest, which does not fall with volumes, so cover would narrow first, prepayments would shrink next and net leverage would rise last. The observable triggers are quarterly revenue growth, Adjusted EBITDA margin and whether the repurchase programme continues. No stress figure is established here.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_fa-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 32; 10-Q_2026-09-30_fa-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 40; calculation C09; calculation C21

Data quality is good where it can be tested. Every balance sheet, cash flow and income identity reconciles exactly across the periods, segment revenues and Adjusted EBITDA add to the totals, and the FY2024 figures agree between the two annual reports. The main definitional risk is that the issuer states no unadjusted EBITDA, so the reported ratios rest on a derived measure and are shown beside the issuer-adjusted ratios. Further limits are that security and guarantees are described only in the 2024 annual report, that the last printed maturity table is dated 31 December 2025, and that no discrete quarters are supplied, which limits any reading of seasonality.
Basis: calculation C03; calculation C04; calculation C11; calculation C12; calculation C14; 10-K_2024-12-31_fa-20241231.htm.jsonl, page 112; 10-K_2025-12-31_fa-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 157
