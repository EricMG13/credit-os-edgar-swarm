## Findings

### Bottom line

The seven supplied filings and releases support the Financial foundation, Business & governance and Fundamental credit work for GRAY MEDIA, INC, but only to the second quarter of 2026 and without executed debt documents, ratings or market data.

The latest balance sheet is dated 128 days before the as-of date, and a July 2026 revolver borrowing is not quantified, so liquidity and leverage conclusions carry that caveat. Covenant capacity, ratings, market-implied risk and committee sizing cannot be supported from these sources.
Basis: calculation C1; 10-Q_2026-06-30_gtn20260630_10q.htm.jsonl, page 25

### Key findings

The financial record supports a four-quarter view. The third and fourth quarters of 2025 and the first and second quarters of 2026 each have a filed report or release, and the quarterly revenue figures tie to the annual and year-to-date totals with no difference.
Basis: calculation C3; calculation C4

The instrument record is complete for the second quarter of 2026. The debt note lists every term loan and note series, with total principal of USD 5,867 million and maturities by year, and the credit agreement leverage table is printed, so Financial foundation can use the filed figures instead of rebuilding them.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_gtn20260630_10q.htm.jsonl, pages 15–16; 10-Q_2026-06-30_gtn20260630_10q.htm.jsonl, page 37

Two net debt figures are printed for the same date. The credit agreement table gives USD 5,696 million after letters of credit of USD 5 million and cash of USD 176 million, while the earnings release gives USD 5,691 million before letters of credit, so each analytical module must name the definition it uses.
Basis: calculation C5; 10-Q_2026-06-30_gtn20260630_10q.htm.jsonl, page 37; EX-99.1_2026-08-07_ex_998938.htm.jsonl, page 4

After the quarter end the company repurchased USD 120 million of notes on 2026-07-21, using cash on hand and borrowings under the revolving credit facility. The borrowed amount is not stated, so the USD 745 million of availability reported at 2026-06-30 cannot be carried forward as current liquidity.
Basis: calculation C6; 10-Q_2026-06-30_gtn20260630_10q.htm.jsonl, pages 25, 33–34

The proxy statement that carries board, compensation and ownership detail is incorporated by reference in the annual report and was not supplied, so Business & governance rests on the annual report's business, risk factor and legal proceedings sections.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_gtn20251231_10k.htm.jsonl, page 116
