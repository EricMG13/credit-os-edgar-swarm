## Findings

### Bottom line

Hertz's own Adjusted Corporate EBITDA was negative in both fiscal years and is not comparable for the last twelve months, so leverage multiples cannot be stated and creditors must judge the credit on liquidity, fleet collateral and cash flow.

Net debt rose from USD 15,744 million at FY2024 to USD 17,462 million at 2026-09-30 (USD 17,381 million pro forma for the cash received in the October 2026 Oro Mobility joint venture sale), while cash and available facilities fell from USD 1,842 million to USD 946 million. Vehicle debt of USD 11,958 million is matched to a fleet carried at USD 12,967 million; the earnings-dependent exposure is the non-vehicle debt of USD 6,035 million.
