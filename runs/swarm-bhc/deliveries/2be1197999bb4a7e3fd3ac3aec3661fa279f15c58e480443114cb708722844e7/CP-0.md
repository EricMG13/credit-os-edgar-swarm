## Findings

### Bottom line

The seven supplied filings and earnings releases support the requested Bausch Health credit snapshot with stated limits, but not a legal reading of covenants and liens, a market-value view or a position-size view, so those conclusions stay open for creditors. Coverage ends on 2026-10-28, which is 8 days before the as-of date, so later events are not covered.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_bhc-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 200–210; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bhc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 28–29; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bhc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 34; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bhc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 139; calculation C1.

The reporting entity is the consolidated group, which includes the approximately 87% owned Bausch + Lomb with its own debt, cash and revolver, so every balance and earnings figure needs a perimeter label. The third quarter quarterly report prints cash and debt for the two perimeters separately. The third quarter earnings release labels USD 20,168 million of principal as net of premiums, whereas the quarterly report shows USD 20,637 million as the net carrying value.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_bhc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 9; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bhc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 27; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bhc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 81; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bhc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 127–128; EX-99.1_2026-10-28_ex991-q32026.htm.jsonl, pages 26–27; calculation C4; calculation C9.
