## Findings

### Bottom line

Creditors rely on a concentrated franchise and a layered group: Xifaxan supplied 21.55% of FY2025 revenue with generic entry expected in 2028, and the Bausch + Lomb stake behind the newest secured debt sits outside the older notes' guarantor group.
Basis: calculation K1; 10-K_2025-12-31_bhc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 10; 10-K_2025-12-31_bhc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 26; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bhc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 39–40

No controlling shareholder or sponsor is disclosed, so outside support cannot be assumed. Management has repeatedly extended maturities through exchanges and refinancings that moved priority to creditors of the financing subsidiary 126NumberCo, yet its 2026 actions, a USD 643 million note redemption from cash and a USD 50 million buyback aimed at dilution, favour debt reduction over distributions.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_bhc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 137; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bhc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 76; EX-99.1_2026-10-28_ex991-q32026.htm.jsonl, page 1; 10-K_2025-12-31_bhc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 199

### Key findings

Profit comes from two engines of unequal quality. Salix earned 47.34% of nine-month 2026 segment profit on 26.79% of revenue, and Bausch + Lomb earned 27.04% on 48.25%, so group earnings rest on a US specialty franchise with a known loss of exclusivity and on a subsidiary whose cash is generally not available to other group entities.
Basis: calculation K4; calculation K5; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bhc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 75–76; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bhc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 127

Xifaxan is the largest single exposure and its protection is eroding. It earned USD 2,212 million, 21.55% of FY2025 revenue and 85.80% of Salix revenue, while the negotiated Medicare price applies from 2027-01-01, the issuer expects generic competition in 2028 and the FDA has given final approval to a Teva generic whose launch date is not stated. The failure of the follow-on formulation's Phase 3 trials in January 2026 removed the replacement product and led to a USD 1,426 million goodwill impairment, leaving USD 1,733 million of Salix goodwill on the balance sheet.
Basis: calculation K1; calculation K2; 10-K_2025-12-31_bhc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 10; 10-K_2025-12-31_bhc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 26; 10-K_2025-12-31_bhc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 153; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bhc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 26; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bhc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 58

Salix growth is price-led, so the profit gain depends on price holding. Nine-month 2026 Salix revenue rose USD 343 million, built from USD 504 million of net realized pricing and USD 12 million from discontinued products, less USD 173 million of lost volume, after the company exited high-rebate channels in the second half of 2025.
Basis: calculation K7; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bhc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 104; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bhc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 123

Customer concentration is high and so is dependence on one market. Three wholesalers took 48% of FY2025 revenue and the United States 59.79%, so a distribution dispute would reach receivables and rebate accruals quickly; product rebate accruals alone were USD 1,377 million at 2026-09-30.
Basis: calculation K10; calculation K11; 10-K_2025-12-31_bhc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 29; 10-K_2025-12-31_bhc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 343; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bhc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 27

No sponsor or controlling shareholder is disclosed, so support from an owner cannot be assumed. Ten directors signed the annual report, but ownership, independence and related-party detail is incorporated by reference to a proxy statement that is not supplied; letter agreements dated 2025-05-20 with two named shareholders are listed without their terms, a director nomination agreement ended on 2025-08-14, and a shareholder rights plan with a 20% threshold was adopted on 2025-04-14 and ratified on 2025-10-07.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_bhc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 199; 10-K_2025-12-31_bhc-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 206–207; 10-K_2025-12-31_bhc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 211; 10-K_2025-12-31_bhc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 319

Management overlap with Bausch + Lomb sharpens a conflict that matters to creditors. The chief executive became chairman of the Bausch + Lomb board in August 2026 and its interim chief executive on 2026-10-12, after the former Bausch + Lomb chief executive left with a separation payment of about USD 75 million, and the issuer itself flags the conflict risk. Because 68.28% of the Bausch + Lomb shares the group holds are pledged, and dividends above USD 50 million or sale proceeds must prepay the 126NumberCo secured debt, any separation or monetization of the stake would route value to those secured creditors first.
Basis: calculation K14; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bhc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 76; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bhc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 133; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bhc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 138; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bhc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 33; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bhc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 38

Management has used liability management repeatedly, and the record is mixed for creditors. The 2022 exchange cut principal by USD 2,469 million, or 44.14%, and was accounted for as a troubled debt restructuring; the 2025 refinancing retired USD 6,870 million of debt due between 2025 and 2028 into secured debt due 2030 to 2032; and the December 2025 exchange swapped USD 1,683 million of 2028 secured notes for USD 1,600 million of 2032 notes while moving more Bausch + Lomb shares into the borrower. Against that, the company repurchased USD 602 million of 2026 notes at close to par in August 2025 and announced redemption of USD 643 million of 2027 notes from cash. Non-guarantor subsidiaries produced 57.40% of nine-month revenue, so unsecured holders sit behind both secured claims and subsidiary liabilities.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest contrary evidence is operating momentum: Bausch Health excluding Bausch + Lomb reported fourteen consecutive quarters of revenue and adjusted EBITDA growth, the issuer raised 2026 adjusted EBITDA guidance to USD 4,315 million to USD 4,415 million, nine-month consolidated revenue rose USD 847 million, and cash covers the redemption of the 2027 notes. Bausch + Lomb revenue grew 7% in the third quarter and supplies 25.54% of guided adjusted EBITDA at the midpoint, so the stake is also a source of value. This view would be wrong if generic rifaximin entry slipped beyond 2028 or if Bausch + Lomb were separated with debt sized to each company. It would harden if a further exchange re-ranked claims, if pledged shares were released to other lenders or if generic entry came before 2028, and the proxy statement and executed indentures would settle control, independence and capacity.

### Limitations

- The proxy statement is not supplied, so board independence, shareholders with large stakes and related-party terms are not established, and the conclusion that no controlling holder exists could change if one is found.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_bhc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 199

- The two shareholder letter agreements and the rights plan are listed or summarized without detail, so whether either holder influences board seats or capital decisions is not established, and the control conclusion is limited to the absence of a disclosed controlling holder.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_bhc-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 206–207; 10-K_2025-12-31_bhc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 319

- Teva's settlement terms and any launch date for generic rifaximin are not supplied, so the 2028 date rests on the issuer's expectation, and an earlier launch would bring forward the loss of Xifaxan earnings.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_bhc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 26; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bhc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 58

- Nine-month Xifaxan revenue is not stated, so concentration is measured on FY2025 and could be higher or lower now.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_bhc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 10; EX-99.1_2026-10-28_ex991-q32026.htm.jsonl, page 2

- The reason the stake in Bausch + Lomb moved from about 88% to about 87% is not stated, and Bausch + Lomb's share count is not supplied, so ownership is shown as the issuer states it at each date.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_bhc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 8; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bhc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 81

- The executed indentures and credit agreement are not supplied, so the capacity to designate unrestricted subsidiaries, release the pledged shares or make restricted payments is read from the notes only, and the creditor-alignment view could harden or soften once the documents are read.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_bhc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 28; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bhc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 39–40

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

The group's largest recent debt transaction was the April 2025 refinancing at 126NumberCo, an indirect wholly-owned subsidiary: a USD 500 million revolver due 2030 that is undrawn, a USD 3,000 million term loan due October 2030 and USD 4,400 million of 10.00% secured notes due April 2032, secured on a first lien over 126NumberCo's assets including the pledged Bausch + Lomb shares. The proceeds repaid the 2027 term loan and redeemed USD 6,870 million of notes and term debt that matured between 2025 and 2028, which the issuer says extended maturities to the period 2030 to 2032.
Basis: calculation K18; 10-K_2025-12-31_bhc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 137; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bhc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 29; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bhc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 31

In December 2025 the company and 126NumberCo exchanged USD 797 million of June 2028 secured notes and USD 886 million of 11.00% notes due 2028 for USD 1,600 million of new 10.00% notes due 2032 that form one series with the April 2025 notes, and moved further Bausch + Lomb shares into 126NumberCo. The issuer treated the exchange as a modification, not a troubled debt restructuring, and USD 803 million and USD 888 million of the old notes remain outstanding.
Basis: calculation K17; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bhc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 30; 10-K_2025-12-31_bhc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 284

Bausch + Lomb refinanced on its own credit. On 2025-06-26 it added a USD 800 million revolver due 2030, a USD 2,325 million term loan due 2031 and EUR 675 million of floating-rate secured notes due 2031 to repay its 2027 debt, and in January 2026 it replaced its 2028 and 2031 term loans with a USD 2,802 million term loan due 2031, of which USD 2,788 million was outstanding at 2026-09-30. These facilities are secured by Bausch + Lomb assets, so its USD 5,065 million of debt ranks ahead of the shares the parent holds.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_bhc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 139; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bhc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 35–36; 10-Q_2026-09-30_bhc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 127
