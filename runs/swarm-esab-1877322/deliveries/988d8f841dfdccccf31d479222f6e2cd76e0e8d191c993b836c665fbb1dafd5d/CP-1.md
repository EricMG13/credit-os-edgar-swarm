## Findings

### Bottom line

Leverage roughly doubled with the June 2026 Eddyfi acquisition: total debt to reported EBITDA is 5.00x at the latest balance sheet against 2.49x at year-end 2025, and the 5.00x overstates the run-rate burden. The debt is fully loaded while last-twelve-months EBITDA holds only about 4 months of Eddyfi, so creditors should treat 3.95x on issuer-adjusted EBITDA as the better guide until pro forma EBITDA is disclosed.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-10-02_esab-20261002.htm.jsonl, pages 9–11; 10-Q_2026-10-02_esab-20261002.htm.jsonl, pages 15–17; calculation C3; calculation C4

Liquidity is adequate at the latest date: cash of USD 188,648 thousand and USD 760 million undrawn on the revolver, against USD 9,357 thousand of debt due within a year. Nine-month operating cash flow of USD 153,017 thousand covered little of the USD 1,469,372 thousand acquisition outflow, which was financed with new debt and equity. Covenant headroom and pro forma leverage are not calculable from the filings.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-10-02_esab-20261002.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-Q_2026-10-02_esab-20261002.htm.jsonl, page 5; 10-Q_2026-10-02_esab-20261002.htm.jsonl, pages 15–18

### Leverage trajectory and drivers

Total debt at carrying value rose from USD 1,234,952 thousand at year-end 2025 to USD 2,329,069 thousand at October 2, 2026, because ESAB issued USD 1,000,000 thousand of senior notes due 2031, drew on the revolver and paid USD 1,469,372 thousand for acquisitions. Net debt rose from USD 1,049,089 thousand to USD 2,140,421 thousand, so net debt to reported EBITDA moved from 2.11x to 4.59x and, on issuer-adjusted EBITDA, from 1.87x to 3.63x. Reported EBITDA for the last twelve months, USD 466,094 thousand, is below FY2025's USD 496,275 thousand, partly because nine-month restructuring charges of USD 39,807 thousand compare with USD 10,236 thousand a year earlier and acquisition costs and inventory step-up sit inside operating income, while the issuer adds them back to reach USD 589.7 million of Adjusted EBITDA.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-10-02_esab-20261002.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-Q_2026-10-02_esab-20261002.htm.jsonl, page 15; 10-Q_2026-10-02_esab-20261002.htm.jsonl, page 39; calculation C2; calculation C3; calculation C4

The interim ratios show the path. At April 3, 2026 gross leverage was 4.21x but net leverage only 2.13x because the proceeds of the USD 1,000,000 thousand notes issue, less a USD 240 million revolver paydown, sat in cash of USD 1,004,790 thousand; at July 3, 2026, after the closing, net leverage was 4.72x. The creditor consequence is that the net figure at April 3, 2026 is not a leverage reading at all, and the first clean post-closing reading is July 3, 2026.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-04-03_esab-20260403.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-Q_2026-10-02_esab-20261002.htm.jsonl, page 16; calculation C3; calculation C4

### Coverage and cash-flow quality

Cash interest paid was USD 66,569 thousand in FY2025 and USD 62,280 thousand in FY2024, giving reported EBITDA cover of 7.46x and 8.26x. The quarterly reports print no cash interest paid, so cover after the 2026 financing cannot be computed; the income statement line for interest expense and other, net, which is not the same measure, rose from USD 23,584 thousand in Q3 2025 to USD 36,511 thousand in Q3 2026, so cover is likely to fall from the FY2025 level before Eddyfi earnings are fully in the base.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_esab-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 80; 10-Q_2026-10-02_esab-20261002.htm.jsonl, page 2; calculation C5

Operating cash flow was USD 153,017 thousand in the nine months of 2026 against USD 163,514 thousand a year earlier, with purchases of property, plant and equipment of USD 49,700 thousand against USD 27,654 thousand, so cash flow after capital expenditure fell to USD 103,317 thousand from USD 135,860 thousand. Working capital absorbed cash: trade receivables used USD 45,481 thousand and inventories USD 47,321 thousand, and the filing separately reports USD 15.5 million of asbestos-related outflows from discontinued operations inside operating cash flow. The mechanism is that integration costs and working capital growth are absorbing cash just as interest costs rise.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-10-02_esab-20261002.htm.jsonl, page 5; 10-Q_2026-10-02_esab-20261002.htm.jsonl, page 52; calculation C6

### Liquidity adequacy

Liquidity at October 2, 2026 was cash of USD 188,648 thousand plus USD 760 million of revolver availability, which equals the USD 1,050 million facility less USD 290 million drawn and is available only while financial covenants are met. Of the cash, USD 176.5 million sat outside the United States, where repatriation can bear withholding taxes and minority-owner restrictions, so the usable domestic cash is small. Debt due within a year is USD 9,357 thousand, the nearest large maturities are the notes due April 2029 and the term loan and revolver due October 2030, and the revolver carries a springing maturity whose trigger the filing does not describe. Letters of credit of USD 53.6 million are outstanding under separate facilities with USD 116.8 million of capacity. The consequence is ample near-term liquidity but a dependence on the revolver, whose covenants cannot be tested from the filings.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-10-02_esab-20261002.htm.jsonl, pages 15–18; 10-Q_2026-10-02_esab-20261002.htm.jsonl, page 52; calculation C7

### Data-quality and definition risks

Three definitions need care. First, ESAB states no unadjusted EBITDA, so reported EBITDA here is operating income plus the cash flow statement's depreciation, amortization and other impairment charges, and it sits USD 123.606 million below the issuer's last-twelve-months Adjusted EBITDA of USD 589.7 million; the gap is restructuring, acquisition and integration costs, inventory step-up and a non-cash option award that the issuer adds back, and the reported measure is the primary denominator here with the adjusted measure shown beside it. Second, carrying value of debt differs from principal by USD 17.972 million of unamortized deferred financing fees at October 2, 2026. Third, the 2025 annual report re-presents the FY2024 cash flow statement, splitting the gain on sale of property from other operating items and issuance costs from other financing, with unchanged totals; the FY2024 column here uses the later presentation.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-10-02_esab-20261002.htm.jsonl, page 39; 10-Q_2026-10-02_esab-20261002.htm.jsonl, page 15; 10-K_2025-12-31_esab-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 79; 10-K_2024-12-31_esab-20241231.htm.jsonl, pages 77–78; calculation C1; calculation C8

The perimeter changes within the sample: Eddyfi contributed net sales of USD 84.3 million in the nine months of 2026 and is in the balance sheet from June 1, 2026, while the 2025 comparatives exclude it. Year-end 2025 debt, cash and the FY2025 ladder therefore describe a smaller group, and no table here blends them with the later balance sheets. No material non-debt funding float was found: contract liabilities were USD 19.7 million at year-end 2025 against USD 26.4 million a year earlier, they sit in accrued liabilities, and no supplier-finance or factoring programme is described.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-10-02_esab-20261002.htm.jsonl, page 9; 10-Q_2026-10-02_esab-20261002.htm.jsonl, page 12

### Subsequent events and multi-figure events

Three events after the balance sheet date are kept out of period figures. A common stock dividend of USD 7.5 million was paid on October 16, 2026 and the preferred stock dividend of USD 2.8 million on October 15, 2026, each of which was already accrued and lifts net debt when paid; in October 2026 the company settled all remaining obligations under its US pension plan and expects a non-cash charge of about USD 37 million in the fourth quarter, with no cash effect stated. The post-balance-sheet table shows the net debt effect of the two dividends.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-10-02_esab-20261002.htm.jsonl, pages 30–31; calculation C9

The Eddyfi price is a multi-figure event: about USD 1.45 billion at signing, about USD 1.5 billion of consideration net of cash received at closing, USD 1,487,883 thousand in the allocation before USD 24,673 thousand of cash acquired, and a nine-month acquisitions outflow of USD 1,469,372 thousand that covers all acquisitions. The cash flow figure serves cash paid and the allocation serves balance sheet purchase accounting; the definition register keeps all four.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_esab-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 52; 10-Q_2026-10-02_esab-20261002.htm.jsonl, page 5; 10-Q_2026-10-02_esab-20261002.htm.jsonl, page 9

## Gaps

- No pro forma or Eddyfi stand-alone EBITDA is printed for the enlarged business, so leverage at April 3, July 3 and October 2, 2026 puts the full acquisition debt over last-twelve-months EBITDA that holds about 4 months of Eddyfi at most; it restricts any statement of run-rate leverage. Severity: High. Action: obtain the acquisition-date presentation or an earnings release with pro forma EBITDA, or show leverage on both bases.
- Covenant levels, the credit agreement's EBITDA definition and the springing-maturity condition are not printed, although compliance at every date is stated; covenant headroom is not calculable for any period. Severity: High. Action: obtain the amended and restated credit agreement.
- No debt maturity ladder after the 2026 financing is printed; the only ladder is at December 31, 2025 and omits the 2031 senior notes and the 2026 revolver draw, so the maturity chart shows a pre-acquisition wall. Severity: Medium. Action: assemble the ladder from the instrument terms in the debt note and label it as derived.
- Cash interest paid is not printed in the quarterly reports, so EBITDA cover is calculable for FY2024 and FY2025 only and not after the 2026 financing. Severity: Medium. Action: obtain cash paid for interest for the nine months of 2026 or state cover on interest expense with that definition labelled.
- The legal rank and security of the term loan and revolver, and their rank against the senior notes, are not stated, so no debt stack is drawn and the instrument register shows security and ranking as not supplied for those facilities. Severity: Medium. Action: obtain the credit agreement and indentures.
- Cash flow statements are year-to-date only, so discrete Q2 and Q3 cash flows and the fourth quarter of 2025 are not reported; Q4 2025 net sales are derived by difference, the second-quarter income columns are not tabulated because they are carried in the six-month columns, and cash flows by quarter would be derived and labelled.
