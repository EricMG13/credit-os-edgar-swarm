## Findings

### Bottom line

PBF Holding is the six-refinery operating subsidiary of a listed parent, and sponsor and governance risk for its creditors is medium: the parent has put capital and assets into the issuer in 2026, but it can draw distributions and its executives run the issuer. Cash flow between issuer and parent was a net outflow of USD 306.0 million in FY2024, a loss year, and became a net inflow of USD 199.6 million in FY2025 and USD 29.6 million in the first nine months of 2026.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_pbfh-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 155-156; 10-Q_2026-09-30_pbf-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 9-10; calculation CA1

No executed debt documents were supplied, so the limits on distributions rest on the filings' summaries. The 2026 refinancings paid noteholders in full or at a premium, with no exchange, uptier or drop-down documented.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_pbf-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 21; 10-Q_2026-09-30_pbf-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 23; 10-Q_2026-09-30_pbf-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 33; calculation CA4

### Key findings

- Control is complete and internal, so creditors cannot count on an independent board or an outside sponsor to restrain distributions. PBF Energy Company LLC owns all of the issuer, PBF Energy Inc. holds about 99.3% of the economic interests in that company, and the issuer's three directors are executives of the parent who receive no separate pay. The documents name no financial sponsor, so fund life and exit pressure do not apply.
  Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_pbf-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-09-30_pbf-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-K_2025-12-31_pbfh-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 142-143; 10-K_2025-12-31_pbfh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 145

- Distributions to the parent are a recurring route for value to leave the issuer, and they continued through a loss year. Distributions less capital contributions were a net outflow of USD 424.0 million in FY2023, with USD 853.4 million of assets also distributed, and USD 306.0 million in FY2024, when the issuer lost USD 895.9 million. The flow reversed to a net contribution of USD 199.6 million in FY2025 and USD 29.6 million in the first nine months of 2026. In FY2025 the issuer distributed USD 273.1 million, of which USD 126.5 million was passed on as non-tax distributions to members, USD 125.6 million of it to the parent for its dividends, and the filing does not trace the other USD 146.6 million. USD 119.9 million of the USD 156.4 million distributed in the first nine months of 2026 fell in Q3. The indentures also let the issuer distribute enough for the parent to meet its tax receivable agreement payments, which the filing says could be significant.
  Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_pbfh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 80; 10-K_2025-12-31_pbfh-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 93-94; 10-K_2025-12-31_pbfh-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 154-156; 10-Q_2026-06-30_pbf-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 9; 10-Q_2026-09-30_pbf-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 9-10; calculation CA1; calculation CA2; calculation CA3; calculation CA13

  Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_pbf-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 26; 10-Q_2026-09-30_pbf-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 9-10; 10-Q_2026-09-30_pbf-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 13-14; 10-Q_2026-09-30_pbf-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 38; calculation CA3

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence for lower risk is that the parent has been a net provider of capital since FY2025, that the filings document no waiver, amendment of note terms or non-pro-rata transaction, that USD 800.0 million of 2030 notes were redeemed at a premium in October 2026 from cash, and that earnings recovered sharply, with net income of USD 1,906.0 million in Q3 2026. The assessment would move to low if the executed documents show tight restricted-payment capacity and the next two quarters show distributions no larger than the parent's dividend need.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_pbfh-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 155-156; 10-Q_2026-09-30_pbf-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 8; 10-Q_2026-09-30_pbf-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 33; calculation CA1

The strongest evidence for higher risk is the pattern of FY2023 and FY2024, with net outflows of USD 424.0 million and USD 306.0 million and assets of USD 853.4 million distributed, the stated practice of distributing to fund more than one year of the parent's dividends, the 18% dividend increase, and the fact that 76.7% of nine-month distributions fell in Q3 2026, the quarter with net income of USD 1,906.0 million. A distribution above the dividend need, a revolver draw that funds shareholder payments, or a call on the SBR loan guarantee would move the assessment to high.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_pbfh-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 155-156; 10-Q_2026-09-30_pbf-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 9; 10-Q_2026-09-30_pbf-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 25; 10-Q_2026-09-30_pbf-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 68-69; EX-99.1_2026-10-29_a991pressreleaseq3-26.htm.jsonl, page 1; calculation CA1; calculation CA12; calculation CA13

### Limitations

- The executed indentures and the amended revolving credit agreement are not supplied, so the conclusion that distributions are limited by incurrence tests and default blocks rests on the filings' own summaries; wider restricted-payment capacity in the documents would raise the sponsor risk assessment.
  Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_pbfh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 79; 10-K_2025-12-31_pbfh-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 93-94; 10-Q_2026-09-30_pbf-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 66; 10-Q_2026-09-30_pbf-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 83

- The parent's proxy statement, which carries its board, committees and holders, is incorporated by reference and not supplied, so the conclusion on control and board independence stops at the issuer's three directors; independent oversight at the parent could lower the risk assessment.
  Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_pbfh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-K_2025-12-31_pbfh-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 142-143

- The filings trace the use of distributions only for FY2025, so the reading of distributions as parent dividend funding is untested for FY2023, FY2024 and 2026; a use other than dividends and tax payments would raise the assessment.
  Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_pbfh-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 93-94; 10-Q_2026-09-30_pbf-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 68

- The hydrogen plants terms, the Chalmette fire effect and the Martinez penalties are not quantified, so the support and event-risk conclusions are bounded; a promissory note secured on the plants would cut the value of the parent's 2026 support.
  Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_pbf-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 26; 10-Q_2026-09-30_pbf-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 13-14; 10-Q_2026-09-30_pbf-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 75

- The difference between guarantor-group and consolidated earnings is not reconciled, so how much value sits behind the guarantees is not established and the ranking conclusion could be better or worse than stated.
  Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_pbf-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 69-70; calculation CA6

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

Between May and October 2026 the issuer issued USD 500.0 million of 7.25% senior unsecured notes due 2034 and USD 550.0 million of 0.00% exchangeable senior notes due 2032, and redeemed its 2028 notes and both 2030 note series. The 2034 notes were issued on 2026-05-28 for net proceeds of USD 492.1 million and, with cash, funded the redemption at par of USD 801.6 million of 6.00% notes on 2026-06-25. The 2032 exchangeable notes were issued on 2026-09-17 for net proceeds of USD 533.2 million, of which USD 27.7 million paid for capped call transactions; the remainder and cash redeemed USD 500.0 million of 7.875% notes on 2026-09-24 at 103.938% of principal.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_pbf-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 21-24; 10-Q_2026-09-30_pbf-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 37-38

The 2032 exchangeable notes carry no regular interest, mature on 2032-01-15 and are exchangeable into PBF Energy Class A shares at an initial exchange price of about USD 96.80, with settlement in cash up to principal. PBF Energy is a party to the indenture, the registration rights agreement and the capped calls, and the notes are guaranteed by certain issuer subsidiaries. The filing does not say whether PBF Energy guarantees the notes. The 2034 notes are senior unsecured, guaranteed by certain subsidiaries, rank equal in right of payment with the revolver and are effectively subordinated to secured debt including the revolver.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_pbf-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 18-22; 10-Q_2026-09-30_pbf-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 69; 10-Q_2026-09-30_pbf-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 83

On 2026-09-30 the issuer amended and restated its asset-based revolver, extending maturity to 2031-09-30, raising the commitment from USD 3.5 billion to USD 4.0 billion and cutting the commitment fee on the unused portion. On 2026-09-09 PBF Energy Company LLC closed the purchase of two hydrogen plants at the Torrance refinery from Air Products and contributed them to the issuer, which recognised USD 388.4 million of property, plant and equipment and removed an operating lease liability of USD 139.7 million and a right-of-use asset of USD 140.2 million.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_pbf-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 13-14; 10-Q_2026-09-30_pbf-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 24; 10-Q_2026-09-30_pbf-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 38
