## Findings

### Bottom line

The supplied filings support a credit snapshot of PBF Holding through Q3 2026 on the issuer's own statements, but creditor protections can be described only from filing summaries because the executed indentures and credit agreement are not supplied. The three earnings releases describe the indirect parent, PBF Energy Inc., and at the end of Q3 2026 its reported total debt exceeds the issuer's long-term debt by an amount the documents do not explain, so parent figures serve only as context.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_pbf-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-Q_2026-09-30_pbf-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 83; EX-99.1_2026-10-29_a991pressreleaseq3-26.htm.jsonl, page 8; 10-Q_2026-09-30_pbf-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 7; calculation C2

The latest financing events, including the full redemption of the last of the 2030 senior notes in October 2026, fall after the last balance sheet and must stay apart from period figures. The only rating statements pre-date the 2026 refinancings and concern notes that have since been redeemed, so current ratings are not established.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_pbf-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 33; 10-K_2025-12-31_pbfh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 77
