## Findings

### Bottom line

Leverage is moderate and improving, and nothing falls due before November 2028: carrying-value debt is 2.73x last-twelve-months reported EBITDA at 30 September 2026, down from 2.97x at year-end 2025, with interest covered 5.67x. Creditors therefore depend mainly on holding earnings through the 2028 refinancing of the USD 400.0 million notes.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_cars_cars-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 9, 25; 10-K_2025-12-31_cars_cars-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 63; calculation RT4; calculation RT13; calculation RT15

Reported EBITDA is calculated, not issuer-stated, and the issuer's own Total Net Leverage Ratio of 1.77x is lower than the 2.33x net leverage calculated here because the credit-agreement definitions are not supplied. Free cash flow converts 79.34% of EBITDA, but share repurchases absorbed 76.39% of nine-month free cash flow, so further deleveraging depends on EBITDA growth more than on debt repayment.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_cars_cars-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 10, 26; calculation RT14; calculation FC2; calculation FC3

### Leverage and coverage

Last-twelve-months reported EBITDA rose 6.53% from USD 152.092 million in FY2025 to USD 162.020 million to 30 September 2026, while carrying-value debt fell 2.01%, so most of the move in leverage from 2.97x to 2.73x comes from earnings and not from repayment. Over the first nine months of 2026 EBITDA rose USD 9.928 million, of which revenue added USD 2.704 million and lower cash operating costs USD 7.224 million, after a USD 8.5 million workforce-reduction expense in the first quarter. The gain is therefore cost-led on flat revenue, which makes it more exposed to reversal than a revenue-led gain would be.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_cars_cars-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 2–3, 9, 13; 10-K_2025-12-31_cars_cars-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 63–64; calculation EG1; calculation DG1; calculation EG2; calculation RT4; calculation RT13

Interest cover on cash interest paid has risen at every date shown, from 4.94x in FY2024 and 5.08x in FY2025 to 5.67x, as last-twelve-months cash interest paid fell to USD 28.553 million while EBITDA rose. The coupon on the notes alone costs USD 25.5 million a year, so cover is mostly a function of EBITDA and not of the revolving loan rate.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_cars_cars-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 5, 9; 10-K_2025-12-31_cars_cars-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 65; calculation RT3; calculation RT6; calculation RT15; calculation LI3; calculation CP1

The strongest contrary evidence is a flat top line and a shrinking audience: nine-month revenue grew 0.50%, OEM and National revenue fell 11.18% while Dealer revenue grew 2.11%, and average monthly unique visitors fell 3% in the third quarter. If the cost savings are exhausted and these trends continue, EBITDA could slip back towards its FY2025 level, which would return leverage to the year-end 2025 ratio and make the 2028 refinancing less comfortable. The issuer's reported Total Net Leverage Ratio of 1.77x is lower than the calculated 2.33x, so the lenders' own measure is the more favourable one; this report relies on the calculated measure because the agreement is not supplied.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_cars_cars-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 10, 19, 26; calculation OE1; calculation DL1; calculation RT14

### Cash flow and capital allocation

Operating cash flow was USD 151.784 million over the last twelve months and free cash flow USD 128.547 million after capital expenditure, which is 79.34% of EBITDA; capital expenditure is only 3.20% of revenue, so most EBITDA reaches cash after interest and tax. In the first nine months of 2026 the company spent USD 74.331 million on share repurchases, 76.39% of free cash flow, repaid USD 10.0 million of the revolving loan and paid the final USD 10.9 million D2C Media earnout in April 2026. Repurchases are discretionary and USD 100.2 million of the programme remains, so a weaker year would let management redirect cash to debt, and the same discretion lets management accelerate returns instead.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_cars_cars-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 4–5, 10, 26; calculation LC1; calculation LX1; calculation FC1; calculation FC2; calculation FC3; calculation CX1

Cash is seasonal. It fell to USD 33.287 million at 30 June 2026 after payments of accrued compensation (the line fell from USD 38.074 million at year-end 2025 to USD 15.238 million), the May coupon and repurchases, which lifted net leverage to 2.64x at that date before it returned to 2.33x at 30 September. Net leverage at interim quarter-ends therefore needs care when compared across dates.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_cars_cars-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 4; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cars_cars-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 2; calculation ND4; calculation RT11; calculation RT14

### Liquidity and maturity profile

Liquidity was USD 369.553 million at 30 September 2026: cash of USD 64.553 million plus USD 305.0 million undrawn under the USD 350.0 million revolving loan due in 2029, with no letter of credit printed against it. No debt matures until the USD 400.0 million notes fall due in November 2028, which are 89.89% of principal, so refinancing that single payment, not near-term liquidity, is the main event risk. The notes' coupon costs USD 25.5 million a year, and the filings put the debt's fair value at USD 427.3 million against USD 445.0 million of principal, which indicates the market values it below par but gives no price or yield.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_cars_cars-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 9, 25; calculation LQ1; calculation RV1; calculation NT1; calculation CP1

### Data quality and definitions

Three definitions shape every ratio in this report. EBITDA is calculated as operating income plus depreciation and amortization because the filings print none; debt is balance-sheet carrying value, USD 2.566 million below principal at 30 September 2026; and interest cover uses cash interest paid, which is uneven by quarter because the notes pay on 1 May and 1 November. Fourth-quarter 2025 is derived, not reported, and the 2025 annual report recast the 2024 and 2023 expense lines between functions without changing totals. None of these changes the direction of the leverage trend, but each limits comparison with the issuer's covenant ratios, which are quoted only as printed.

## Gaps

- No adjusted EBITDA or add-back schedule is printed in any of the five filings, so adjusted leverage, adjusted coverage and the adjusted EBITDA bridge are not stated; the filings name adjusted EBITDA only as a performance-share target. Severity: Medium. Action: obtain the earnings release or investor presentation that reconciles it.
- Reported EBITDA is calculated as operating income plus depreciation and amortization for every period because no filing prints EBITDA, and it may differ from the credit-agreement measure. Severity: Medium. Action: obtain the agreement's definition.
- The credit agreement is not supplied, so the issuer's Senior Secured Net Leverage Ratio, Consolidated Interest Coverage Ratio and Total Net Leverage Ratio cannot be recomputed and covenant headroom is not calculated. Severity: High. Action: obtain the executed credit agreement and a compliance certificate.
- The second-quarter report dates the Total Net Leverage Ratio of 1.95x as 30 June 2026 in the debt note and 31 March 2026 in the discussion, while the first-quarter report prints 1.84x for 31 March 2026; neither date is chosen here. Severity: Low. Action: confirm against the compliance certificate.
- The 2025 annual report prints no covenant ratio values at 31 December 2025, so the covenant trend has no year-end 2025 point; the 2024 annual report printed 0.04x and 6.5x for 31 December 2024. Severity: Low. Action: obtain the year-end compliance figures.
- Fourth quarter 2025 is not printed as a quarter; revenue, EBITDA and operating cash flow are derived as the fiscal year less nine months and labelled calculated, and other lines are not derived. Severity: Low. Action: none needed for the credit metrics.
- Printed dashes in the cash flow statement and balance sheet, such as acquisitions in the first quarter of 2026 or the current portion of debt, are carried as not supplied and never as zero, and lines that exist in some filings and not others, such as the convertible note purchase, are marked as absent from that filing's statement. Severity: Low. Action: none, because none of these cells is an operand of a ratio.
- Interest income is not disclosed separately from interest expense, net, so the gross interest cost cannot be separated and cover uses cash interest paid. Severity: Low. Action: none needed for the cover ratio.
- Gross profit is not presented and the issuer's cost of revenue and operations line is not a complete cost of sales, so no gross margin is stated. Severity: Low. Action: none.
- Maintenance and growth capital expenditure are not split; total capital expenditure is capitalised internally developed technology plus purchase of property and equipment. Severity: Low. Action: none needed for free cash flow.
- Funds from operations is not printed and the method gives no formula, so ratios based on it are not calculable. Severity: Low. Action: none.
- Debt carrying values by instrument are not disclosed, so the financing cost adjustment of USD 2.566 million at 30 September 2026 is derived as principal less the balance-sheet carrying value; part of the issuance costs is held as a deferred asset, so the adjustment is not the total cost. Severity: Low. Action: none.
- Security, ranking and guarantors rest on the annual report's lien wording for the debt agreements and on the instrument titles, and the quarterly reports omit that wording; the indenture and credit agreement are not supplied. Severity: Medium. Action: obtain both documents.
- The revolving loan margin, the reference rate now in force and any commitment fee are not stated; the rate of 6.1% in effect at 30 September 2026 is the only rate figure. Severity: Low. Action: obtain the credit agreement pricing grid.
- Per-share, share-count and comprehensive income lines are not carried in the statement tables because they do not enter any credit metric. Severity: Low. Action: none.
- No balance after 30 September 2026 is supplied. The notes pay interest on 1 May and 1 November, so the 1 November 2026 payment falls after the balance-sheet date and is not reflected. Severity: Low. Action: add post-quarter balances if they become available.
- Credit metrics for FY2023 are not shown because the 2024 annual report prints no 31 December 2023 debt composition or revolver availability; the FY2023 statements are carried in the statement tables only. Severity: Low. Action: none.
