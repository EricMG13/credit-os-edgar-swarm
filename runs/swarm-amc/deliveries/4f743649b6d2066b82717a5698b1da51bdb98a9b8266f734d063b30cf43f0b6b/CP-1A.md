## Findings

### Bottom line

AMC's credit rests on theatre attendance, continued equity-market access and a debt structure reshaped three times since 2024, so creditors should assess priority by entity and collateral pool rather than by headline rank.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_amc-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 100; 10-K_2025-12-31_amc-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, pages 144–147; 10-K_2025-12-31_amc-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 156; 10-Q_2026-09-30_amc-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, pages 33–38; calculation C01

The company is publicly held, with no sponsor or controlling holder identified and no cash dividend currently paid, and it describes itself as the world's largest theatrical exhibitor with USD 4,848.9 million of 2025 revenue, of which admissions were 54.71%. Equity issuance has funded debt reduction, and the share count rose 74.02% in the nine months to 30 September 2026. The 5 October 2026 refinancing placed USD 3,970.0 million of new debt and lifted principal by USD 303.5 million, with maturities moved to 2031 and 2033.

### Key findings

- Revenue depends on a small group of film suppliers and on attendance, so cash flow is volatile and seasonal. The seven largest studio distributors supplied about 83% of U.S. admissions revenue in 2025 and the five largest supplied about 76% of European box office; film rental is a share of admissions, but rent is largely fixed, with fixed theatre lease cost of USD 771.1 million against variable lease cost of USD 116.2 million. A weak release slate therefore reduces admissions and food and beverage sales while most occupancy cost remains, which is why attendance is the first variable to watch.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_amc-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 19; 10-K_2025-12-31_amc-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 22; 10-K_2025-12-31_amc-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 113; 10-K_2025-12-31_amc-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 139; calculation C01

- No sponsor or controlling holder is identified, so equity-market access is the only evidenced backstop, and it is finite. The documents identify no sponsor, describe no management or monitoring fees, state that no cash dividend is currently paid, and describe a sizable retail shareholder base. Gross equity raised through the June 2026 offering and the at-the-market programme was USD 350.0 million in the nine months, and the share count rose 74.02%, while only a limited number of authorized shares remain unissued and unreserved. Each further raise dilutes holders and is easier when the share price is high, so the lower-risk path of equity-funded deleveraging depends on the market rather than on a sponsor.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_amc-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 13; 10-K_2025-12-31_amc-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 37; 10-Q_2026-09-30_amc-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 1; 10-Q_2026-09-30_amc-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 18; 10-Q_2026-09-30_amc-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, pages 90–91; calculation C02; calculation C04

- Liability management has repeatedly moved collateral and priority between creditor groups, so a claim has to be read by entity and collateral pool. In July 2024 the net assets of a block of theatres and certain intellectual property were moved into subsidiaries that are unrestricted under the 7.5% notes indenture and that became co-borrowers of new term loans; in July 2025 consenting holders exchanged part of the 7.5% notes into new Muvico notes ranking behind the term loans on the Muvico assets, and many covenants of the remaining 7.5% notes were removed. Creditors outside the Muvico Group had less access to its theatres, and claims in a proceeding the company calls the Intercreditor Litigation were released as part of the 2025 transaction.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_amc-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, pages 100–101; 10-K_2025-12-31_amc-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, pages 144–147; 10-K_2025-12-31_amc-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 156; 10-K_2025-12-31_amc-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 162

- The October 2026 refinancing removed the 2029 maturity wall at the cost of a larger, partly second-lien stack. New first lien notes, first lien term loans and second lien term loans total USD 3,970.0 million, repaid the Muvico and Odeon term loans and the 2029 secured notes, and moved maturities to 2031 and 2033; gross principal rose from USD 3,782.6 million to USD 4,086.1 million. The new covenants limit debt, liens and restricted payments, but no maintenance test level and no margin on the new first lien term loans are printed, so capacity and cost cannot yet be assessed.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_amc-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 12; 10-Q_2026-09-30_amc-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, pages 33–38; calculation C03; calculation C05

- Disclosure is adequate for monitoring, but control sits with a combined chairman and chief executive and key governance detail is outside the documents read. The auditor gave unqualified opinions on the 2025 statements and on internal control, restricted-group information is published for the Muvico Group, and related-party dealings are limited to equity-method investees; board composition and holder concentration are incorporated by reference to a proxy statement that is not supplied.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_amc-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 26; 10-K_2025-12-31_amc-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, pages 109–112; 10-K_2025-12-31_amc-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 124; 10-K_2025-12-31_amc-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 192

- The footprint is contracting and almost entirely leased, so owned real estate adds little to creditor protection. Theatre closures exceeded new builds and acquisitions over 2021 to 2025, and 95.83% of consolidated theatres are leased, which makes rent a large fixed claim on revenue and leaves little owned property as security.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_amc-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 17; 10-K_2025-12-31_amc-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 63; calculation C06

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against caution is the breadth of the 2026 recovery and the way creditors were treated in October 2026: attendance rose 18.85% in the nine months, the company reports record revenue and Adjusted EBITDA in the third quarter, the 2029 secured notes were redeemed at par plus a make-whole premium of USD 144.3 million, and equity rather than extraction funded deleveraging. If that behaviour continues and the exchangeable notes are converted or repaid, the priority concerns above shrink to the split between first lien and second lien debt. The view would turn more adverse if a further transfer of collateral to unrestricted subsidiaries, a non-pro-rata exchange or a distribution to shareholders were disclosed, or if equity issuance stopped while principal stayed at USD 4,086.1 million.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_amc-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 38; 10-Q_2026-09-30_amc-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, pages 49–50; 10-Q_2026-09-30_amc-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 84; EX-99.1_2026-11-04_amc-ex99d1.htm.jsonl, page 2; calculation C03; calculation C07

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

On 5 October 2026, after the 30 September 2026 balance sheet date, AMC completed its 2026 refinancing. It issued first lien notes due 15 October 2031 with a principal of USD 2,000.0 million, borrowed USD 850.0 million of new first lien term loans maturing on 5 October 2031 and USD 1,120.0 million of new second lien term loans maturing on 5 October 2033, together USD 3,970.0 million. The first lien notes and first lien term loans share first-priority liens over substantially all assets of Holdings and the guarantors, and the second lien term loans rank behind them on the same collateral. The first lien term loans were issued at a 1.50% discount, amortize at 0.25% of the original principal each quarter from 31 March 2027 and carry Term SOFR or a base rate plus an applicable rate that the third-quarter report does not print; the second lien term loans were issued at a 1.00% discount and bear a fixed 11.25%.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_amc-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, pages 33–36; calculation C03

The proceeds repaid the Muvico term loans and the Odeon term loans in full, bought USD 355.5 million of the 7.5% first lien notes in a tender offer, discharged the remainder of those notes for redemption on or about 15 February 2027, and redeemed all USD 903.4 million of the 2029 secured notes at par plus a make-whole premium of USD 144.3 million. About USD 32.7 million of balance sheet cash was also used, and the company estimates a loss on extinguishment of about USD 182.7 million in the fourth quarter of 2026. The exchangeable notes of USD 116.1 million remain outstanding; principal after the transactions is USD 4,086.1 million against USD 3,782.6 million before, with USD 3,236.1 million or 79.20% at fixed rates. The new debt is guaranteed by wholly-owned subsidiaries, intercreditor agreements govern the AMC, Muvico and Odeon collateral pools, and the covenants limit new debt, liens, distributions, investments and asset sales.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_amc-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, pages 34–38; 10-Q_2026-09-30_amc-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 85; calculation C03; calculation C05

Earlier transactions explain the structure. The 2024 transactions created the Muvico Group, borrowed USD 2,024.3 million of term loans due 2029 and issued USD 414.4 million of exchangeable notes at Muvico; the 2025 transactions exchanged USD 590.0 million of 7.5% notes with consenting holders into USD 857.0 million of Muvico 2029 notes with USD 244.4 million of new money; and in 2026 the Odeon notes were refinanced with USD 425.0 million of term loans and the new exchangeable notes were converted into shares. The sources describe the 2026 debt only through the company's own summaries; executed documents are not supplied.
