### Bottom line

The supplied filings through 2026-10-04 support a short credit snapshot of EnerSys, but covenant terms, guarantor detail and most fiscal 2026 full-year results are missing, so creditor protections and headroom can only be stated from the company's own summaries.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-10-04_ens.htm.jsonl, pages 1–5; EX99.1_2026-10-04_ens_ex991-earningsx2qfy27-htm.htm.jsonl, page 5
