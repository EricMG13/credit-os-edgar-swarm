## Findings

### Bottom line
Leverage is falling but stays high, helped more by fading one-off costs and a cash build than by repayment: net debt was 3.87x reported last-twelve-months EBITDA at 2026-09-30 against 4.42x at the 2025 year end. Gross debt is flat at USD 5,162.1 million and liquidity is USD 1,064.0 million of cash and undrawn revolver.
Basis: calculation C70; calculation C67; calculation C05; calculation C26; 10-Q_2026-09-30_prmb-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-Q_2026-09-30_prmb-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 51

Creditors should anchor on reported EBITDA rather than management's 3.32x, which uses adjusted EBITDA and principal: USD 227.0 million of add-backs separate the two measures, the issuer states no realisation status for them, and the covenant definitions are not supplied. The term loans now run to 2031, so the next large maturities are the 2028 and 2029 notes.
Basis: calculation C60; calculation C66; EX-99.1_2026-11-05_ex991.htm.jsonl, page 14; 10-Q_2026-09-30_prmb-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 16–17; 10-K_2025-12-31_prmb-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 160–161
