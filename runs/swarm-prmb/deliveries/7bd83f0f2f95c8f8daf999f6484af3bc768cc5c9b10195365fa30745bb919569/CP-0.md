## Findings

### Bottom line
The seven supplied filings and releases are enough to support a creditor snapshot of Primo Brands Corp as of 2026-11-05, with covenant, legal-priority and governance conclusions limited by the absence of executed financing documents and the proxy statement. Creditors should treat any such conclusion as resting on the company's own summaries of its debt agreements, not on the agreements themselves.
