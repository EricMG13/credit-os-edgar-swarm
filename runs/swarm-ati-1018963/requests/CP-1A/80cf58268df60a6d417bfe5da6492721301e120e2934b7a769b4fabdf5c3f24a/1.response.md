## Findings

### Bottom line

Ati Inc is a technically qualified but end-market-concentrated aerospace and defense materials supplier, with large-holder ownership not established and buybacks as the main way cash leaves the company, so creditors depend on engine demand and board restraint on payouts.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-28_ati_ati-20251228.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-K_2025-12-28_ati_ati-20251228.htm.jsonl, page 9; calculation C7; 10-K_2025-12-28_ati_ati-20251228.htm.jsonl, page 76

Aerospace and defense is about 68% of sales and commercial jet engines alone were 42% of sales in Q3 2026, but long-term agreements, confirmed orders of about USD 3.7 billion and no customer above 10% of sales give visibility. Treasury stock purchases of USD 470.0 million in FY2025 were 140.85% of free cash flow, and no dividend is paid.

### Key findings

Business risk is concentrated in aerospace and defense but backed by contracts. The sector was about 68% of FY2025 sales and 69% of Q3 2026 sales, with commercial jet engines at 42% of Q3 2026 sales; about 92% of High Performance Materials & Components revenue and 41% of Advanced Alloys & Solutions revenue come from it. Most major engine and airframe makers have long-term agreements with the company, no customer exceeded 10% of sales, and confirmed orders were about USD 3.7 billion at 2025-12-28, of which about 70% were due to ship in FY2026. For creditors that means a year of covered volume against a cycle the company itself calls hard to predict.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-28_ati_ati-20251228.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-K_2025-12-28_ati_ati-20251228.htm.jsonl, page 9; 10-K_2025-12-28_ati_ati-20251228.htm.jsonl, page 11; 10-K_2025-12-28_ati_ati-20251228.htm.jsonl, page 12; 10-K_2025-12-28_ati_ati-20251228.htm.jsonl, page 78; 10-Q_2026-09-27_ati_ati-20260927.htm.jsonl, page 36

Growth is strong and driven by volume and pricing, which supports near-term cash flow, but one segment's quarterly margin eased. Sales rose 26.49% in Q3 2026 against Q3 2025 and 12.49% over 9M 2026; Advanced Alloys & Solutions earned segment EBITDA of USD 181.3 million against USD 90.4 million, while High Performance Materials & Components earned USD 167.3 million against USD 145.8 million and its quarterly margin eased to 23.2% from 24.2% on the cost of moving a new nickel-based superalloy into production, although its nine-month margin rose to 24.0% from 23.4%. That cost is the main operating item to watch because the engine-component segment carries most of the growth case.
Basis: calculation C1; calculation C2; 10-Q_2026-09-27_ati_ati-20260927.htm.jsonl, page 39; 10-Q_2026-09-27_ati_ati-20260927.htm.jsonl, page 41; 10-Q_2026-09-27_ati_ati-20260927.htm.jsonl, page 42; 10-Q_2026-09-27_ati_ati-20260927.htm.jsonl, page 43

Ownership does not read as a sponsor story, but it is not established. The company has a broad record-holder base, pays no dividend, and the filings read name no controlling or large holder; holdings above reporting thresholds, board composition and related-party dealings sit in a proxy statement that is not supplied. The only named outside owner is China Baowu Steel Group with 40% of the Chinese strip joint venture, whose cash and dividends belong partly to that minority. The consequence is no evidence of sponsor extraction or support in either direction, and payout risk comes from the board and public holders rather than a financial owner.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-27_ati_ati-20260927.htm.jsonl, page 1; 10-Q_2026-09-27_ati_ati-20260927.htm.jsonl, page 10; 10-K_2025-12-28_ati_ati-20251228.htm.jsonl, page 41; 10-K_2025-12-28_ati_ati-20251228.htm.jsonl, page 150

Financial policy is mixed: debt is refinanced early and lengthened, but cash goes to buybacks faster than it is generated. In 2026 the company issued USD 450.0 million of 2033 notes and redeemed USD 350.0 million of 2027 notes ahead of maturity, which removes the nearest maturity, yet treasury stock purchases were USD 470.0 million in FY2025, 140.85% of free cash flow of USD 333.7 million, and USD 195.0 million in 9M 2026, 98.48% of free cash flow. The credit facility sets no limit on dividends while undrawn availability stays above the greater of USD 120 million and 20% of the facility, and the filings do not say whether repurchases face the same test, so creditor protection rests on that availability and on board policy.
Basis: calculation C7; calculation C8; calculation C4; 10-Q_2026-09-27_ati_ati-20260927.htm.jsonl, page 15; 10-K_2025-12-28_ati_ati-20251228.htm.jsonl, pages 123–124; 10-K_2025-12-28_ati_ati-20251228.htm.jsonl, page 76; 10-Q_2026-09-27_ati_ati-20260927.htm.jsonl, pages 3–4

Capital spending is rising to fund aerospace growth, and it competes with debt reduction for cash. Capital expenditures were USD 239.1 million in FY2024, USD 280.6 million in FY2025 and USD 216.6 million in 9M 2026 against USD 187.9 million in 9M 2025, mainly growth projects, and restructuring and transformation charges recur. Free cash flow after this spending, not EBITDA, is what is available for debt reduction.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-28_ati_ati-20251228.htm.jsonl, page 76; 10-Q_2026-09-27_ati_ati-20260927.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-Q_2026-09-27_ati_ati-20260927.htm.jsonl, page 49; 10-Q_2026-09-27_ati_ati-20260927.htm.jsonl, pages 24–25

Claims outside debt are modest against earnings but not zero. Pension and retiree benefit liabilities were USD 187.9 million at 2026-09-27, environmental reserves are about USD 15 million with up to USD 21 million more reasonably possible, and a pension class action over the 2023 annuity purchase is pending with a recommendation to dismiss. These claims sit outside reported debt and would matter mainly in a downturn.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a stable reading is concentration and cyclicality: the company says commercial aerospace has been cyclical and hard to predict, confirmed orders fell to about USD 3.7 billion from about USD 3.9 billion while sales rose, about 35% of the workforce is under collective agreements, international sales were about 42% of FY2025 sales and nickel hedges cover only about 7% of a year's purchases, so trade policy and metal prices can hit margins faster than surcharges recover them. Buybacks that exceed free cash flow add to this by keeping net debt from falling when earnings are strong. The view would weaken if engine build rates or the order backlog fall, if the quarterly margin of the engine-component segment keeps easing, or if buybacks are funded from the revolving facility. It would strengthen if the proxy statement shows no dominant holder with payout influence, or if the board caps payouts at free cash flow.

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

On 2026-06-03 ATI Inc. issued USD 450 million of senior notes due 2033, unsecured and unsubordinated, with net proceeds of USD 443.1 million after USD 6.9 million of fees, and on 2026-07-08 it used about USD 350 million of the proceeds to redeem the senior notes due 2027 in full, recording a debt extinguishment charge of USD 1.2 million. The refinancing added no secured debt and moved the next maturity to the fourth quarter of FY2029.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-27_ati_ati-20260927.htm.jsonl, page 15; 10-Q_2026-09-27_ati_ati-20260927.htm.jsonl, page 47

The asset-based credit facility was amended on 2025-06-13 and now runs to June 2030, with a USD 600 million revolver, a USD 200 million term loan and a letter of credit sub-limit of USD 200 million; USD 29.3 million of letters of credit were used and nothing was drawn on the revolver at 2026-09-27. A receivables facility of up to USD 125.0 million, entered on 2025-09-19, had USD 120 million of funding used at 2026-09-27; receivables sold under it leave the balance sheet and so sit outside reported debt.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-28_ati_ati-20251228.htm.jsonl, page 98; 10-K_2025-12-28_ati_ati-20251228.htm.jsonl, page 104; 10-Q_2026-09-27_ati_ati-20260927.htm.jsonl, pages 12–13; 10-Q_2026-09-27_ati_ati-20260927.htm.jsonl, pages 15–16
