## Findings

### Bottom line
Brightstar's reported net leverage rose to 4.72x at June 2026 from 3.94x at year-end 2025 after the final Italian Lotto license payment, and management's 3.24x measure depends on adding back license amortization, so creditors should anchor on the higher figure.
Basis: 6-K_2026-06-30_brightstar-6302026xform6k.htm.jsonl, pages 3, 14; 20-F_2025-12-31_igt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 137; calculation C03; calculation C05; calculation C06

Cash interest cover on reported EBITDA is 4.19x for the last twelve months, and liquidity of USD 1,742 million is well above the USD 228 million that falls due in 2027, so the pressure point is cash generation after distributions to minority partners rather than refinancing.
Basis: 6-K_2026-06-30_brightstar-6302026xform6k.htm.jsonl, pages 14, 27; calculation C07; calculation C11

### Financial interpretation

Net leverage on reported EBITDA was 3.94x at the end of 2025 and 4.72x at June 2026, while the issuer's adjusted measure moved from 2.44x to 3.24x. The increase comes from net debt rising by USD 1,064 million over the half year as the final Italian Lotto instalment of USD 1,675 million was paid from operating cash flow, with consortium partners contributing USD 650 million of capital; the year-end net debt excluded the USD 1,680 million payable to the Italian regulator, an obligation that consortium partners were to fund in part.
Basis: 6-K_2026-06-30_brightstar-6302026xform6k.htm.jsonl, pages 3–5, 12; 20-F_2025-12-31_igt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 137; calculation C03; calculation C05; calculation C06; calculation C10

Most of the distance between the reported and adjusted denominators is the amortization of upfront license fees, which management adds back as non-cash although the cash was paid in advance. It is USD 201 million of the USD 573 million first-half adjusted EBITDA, or 35%, and its share was 20% in 2025 because amortization of the new Italian Lotto license only began in December 2025, so the adjusted measure now excludes a larger charge that already cost cash. Excluding the Lotto payments, operating cash flow was USD 733 million in 2025 and USD 501 million in the first half of 2026.
Basis: 6-K_2026-06-30_brightstar-6302026xform6k.htm.jsonl, pages 4–5; 6-K-EX-99.2_2026-06-30_q226earningsslidedeck-fi.htm.jsonl, page 10; 20-F_2025-12-31_igt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 139; calculation C12; calculation C20

Cash interest cover on reported EBITDA improved to 4.19x for the last twelve months from 3.25x in 2025, on higher reported EBITDA and lower cash interest paid, with first-half interest paid at USD 68 million against USD 89 million. Free cash flow excluding the Lotto payment was USD 269 million in the half year, while dividends and buybacks took USD 130 million and distributions to minority partners USD 194 million, so shareholder returns were not covered by cash after minority distributions.
Basis: 6-K_2026-06-30_brightstar-6302026xform6k.htm.jsonl, pages 5–6; calculation C07; calculation C13; calculation C14; calculation C15

Liquidity fell to USD 1,742 million from USD 3,036 million as cash dropped to USD 558 million, and 50% of the cash is in euros. No maturity falls due in the rest of 2026, USD 228 million falls due in 2027 and USD 798 million in 2028, so near-term refinancing risk is limited; the drawn revolver of USD 604 million is due in 2031. Floating-rate debt rose to 40% of the total from 36% and 25% at the two previous year-ends, and no interest rate swaps were held at the end of 2025, so interest cost is more sensitive to euro rates.
Basis: 6-K_2026-06-30_brightstar-6302026xform6k.htm.jsonl, pages 14, 27; 20-F_2025-12-31_igt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 116; calculation C08; calculation C11

Receivable factoring is a funding source outside reported debt: USD 264 million was factored in the first half of 2026 and USD 440 million in 2025, and the balances collected on behalf of banks fell to USD 78 million from USD 149 million. A pause in factoring would lower operating cash flow and raise reported receivables and debt needs, so factoring volumes and the collected-on-behalf balance are worth tracking beside leverage.
Basis: 6-K_2026-06-30_brightstar-6302026xform6k.htm.jsonl, page 12; 20-F_2025-12-31_igt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 162

The strongest contrary evidence is that cover and liquidity remain adequate, no maturity falls due before 2027, and management reaffirms its full-year outlook and expects free cash flow to rise after the 2025 to 2028 capital expenditure cycle. That view would win if reported net leverage falls back toward the 3.94x of year-end 2025 as the Lotto cash cost drops out of operating cash flow and minority partners keep funding their share; it would lose if the next report shows net debt above USD 3,848 million, the June figure plus the July repurchases and the September dividend, with no earnings growth.
Basis: 6-K-EX-99.2_2026-06-30_q226earningsslidedeck-fi.htm.jsonl, pages 5, 7; calculation C05; calculation C10; calculation C16
