## Findings

### Bottom line

Magnera is thinly covered but slowly deleveraging: debt is 5.11x trailing adjusted EBITDA at June 27, 2026 against 5.51x at the fiscal 2025 year end, yet 7.60x on reported EBITDA, so creditors should underwrite on cash generation, not on adjusted earnings. Net debt fell by only USD 26 million over three quarters, from USD 1,647 million to USD 1,621 million, while nine-month free cash flow was only USD 32 million.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-27_form10q.htm.jsonl, page 8; 10-Q_2026-06-27_form10q.htm.jsonl, page 18; EX-99.1_2026-08-06_ef20079553_ex99-1.htm.jsonl, page 6; calculation C01; calculation C04; calculation C06; calculation C07; calculation C13

Cash interest of USD 124 million absorbed 35.03% of fiscal 2025 adjusted EBITDA, the full-year free cash flow outlook of USD 90 million to USD 110 million needs a fourth quarter far stronger than the third, and all USD 1,966 million of principal falls due in 2029 and 2031.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-27_form10k.htm.jsonl, page 34; 10-Q_2026-06-27_form10q.htm.jsonl, page 13; 10-Q_2026-06-27_form10q.htm.jsonl, page 8; calculation C05; calculation C10; calculation C11; calculation C12

### Analysis by dimension

Leverage is falling because debt is being repaid, not because earnings are rising fast. Principal fell by USD 65 million between the fiscal 2025 year end and June 27, 2026, equal to the nine-month repayments, while trailing adjusted EBITDA rose from USD 354 million to USD 372 million. The same debt is 7.60x trailing reported EBITDA of USD 250 million, because USD 122 million of add-backs separate reported from adjusted EBITDA, namely restructuring, business optimization and other charges and non-cash items that the issuer does not count as ongoing operations. If those charges persist, the adjusted multiple overstates debt capacity and creditors are closer to the reported multiple.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-27_form10q.htm.jsonl, page 8; EX-99.1_2026-08-06_ef20079553_ex99-1.htm.jsonl, page 6; calculation C05; calculation C06; calculation C07

Coverage is the thinnest point of the credit. Net interest expense was USD 112 million over nine months against adjusted EBITDA of USD 282 million, and the only reported cash interest figure, USD 124 million for fiscal 2025, gives adjusted cover of 2.85x and reported cover of 1.70x. The floating-rate term loan prices at 4.25% over SOFR, so a 0.25% rate move changes annual interest by about USD 2 million; interim cash interest is not disclosed, so interim cover is not calculable.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-27_form10k.htm.jsonl, page 34; 10-Q_2026-06-27_form10q.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-Q_2026-06-27_form10q.htm.jsonl, page 19

Cash-flow quality is weak relative to the earnings reported. Fiscal 2025 operating cash flow of USD 103 million less capital expenditure of USD 67 million is USD 36 million, not the USD 126 million the issuer prints, because the issuer adds USD 90 million of pre-merger cash flow. In the nine months to June 2026 operating cash flow was USD 76 million and free cash flow USD 32 million, and the third quarter alone used USD 13 million of operating cash, so the outlook of USD 90 million to USD 110 million for the year depends on a fourth quarter of USD 58 million to USD 78 million. The issuer lowered its operating cash flow outlook to USD 150 million to USD 170 million while keeping the free cash flow range by cutting planned capital spending to USD 60 million.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-27_form10k.htm.jsonl, page 17; 10-Q_2026-06-27_form10q.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-Q_2026-06-27_form10q.htm.jsonl, page 13; 10-Q_2026-03-28_form10q.htm.jsonl, page 12; calculation C10; calculation C11; calculation C12

Liquidity is cash of USD 280 million, about 58% of it outside the U.S., plus an undrawn USD 350 million asset-based revolver due November 2029, more than twelve months away. Availability under that revolver depends on a borrowing base and letters of credit that are not disclosed, so undrawn capacity is a ceiling, not a measure, and no debt matures before 2029. The term loan and secured notes carry no financial maintenance covenants, only negative covenants, and the revolver's covenants are not described, so covenant headroom is not established. The refinancing risk is therefore later and larger: USD 500 million in 2029 and USD 1,466 million in 2031 on a business that generated USD 32 million of free cash flow in nine months.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-27_form10q.htm.jsonl, page 8; 10-Q_2026-06-27_form10q.htm.jsonl, page 9; 10-Q_2026-06-27_form10q.htm.jsonl, page 18; 10-Q_2026-06-27_form10q.htm.jsonl, page 19; calculation C05

Debt repayment is slowing as cash generation weakens: the issuer repaid USD 27 million in the first quarter and USD 36 million in the second, but only USD 2 million in the third. Outside the debt figure, creditors also face operating lease liabilities of USD 60 million, a USD 78 million cross-currency swap liability, an unfunded USD 27 million pension obligation, an environmental liability of USD 17 million and Brazilian tax claims that may reach USD 66 million, and the size of customer receivable programs is not disclosed.
Basis: 10-Q_2025-12-27_form10q.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-Q_2026-03-28_form10q.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-Q_2026-06-27_form10q.htm.jsonl, page 4; EX-99.1_2026-05-07_ef20072634_ex99-1.htm.jsonl, page 1; 10-Q_2026-06-27_form10q.htm.jsonl, page 8; 10-Q_2026-06-27_form10q.htm.jsonl, page 9; 10-Q_2026-06-27_form10q.htm.jsonl, page 12; 10-K_2025-09-27_form10k.htm.jsonl, page 39; calculation C15

Data quality limits how far these figures can be pushed. Internal control over financial reporting was not effective at the fiscal 2025 year end and disclosure controls were not effective at June 27, 2026, the fiscal 2024 comparatives are carve-out statements with no debt, and amounts sold under customer receivable programs are not disclosed, so working capital and operating cash flow may be flattered by an unknown amount.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-27_form10k.htm.jsonl, page 22; 10-Q_2026-06-27_form10q.htm.jsonl, page 20; 10-K_2025-09-27_form10k.htm.jsonl, page 27

No subsequent event after the latest balance-sheet date changes net debt in the documents read, and no fiscal fourth-quarter 2026 figure is supplied, so every balance and ratio is as at June 27, 2026.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-27_form10q.htm.jsonl, page 6; 10-Q_2026-06-27_form10q.htm.jsonl, page 21

## Gaps

- Fiscal fourth-quarter and full-year 2026 results are not supplied: the fiscal year ended on September 26, 2026, before the as-of date, so every balance and trailing ratio is as at June 27, 2026 and year-end net debt and leverage cannot be stated. Severity High. The fiscal 2026 annual report would close it.
- Cash interest paid is not disclosed for any interim period or for fiscal 2024, so interim interest cover is not calculable and the one cover figure rests on fiscal 2025, a year that includes pre-merger months. Severity High. The next annual report's cash interest note would close it.
- Revolver borrowing base, availability and letters of credit are not stated, so undrawn capacity of USD 350 million is the commitment less nil borrowings and not a measure of available liquidity. Severity Medium. A borrowing-base certificate would close it.
- Amounts sold under customer receivable programs are not disclosed, so working capital and operating cash flow cannot be adjusted for receivables sold without recourse. Severity Medium. Disclosure of program balances would close it.
- The issuer states no unadjusted EBITDA, so reported EBITDA is calculated from operating income and depreciation and amortization and excludes other expense, net, which was USD 30 million in fiscal 2025 and included a USD 15 million debt prepayment charge. Severity Medium.
- No source states the legal rank, collateral or guarantor scope of the term loan, the revolver or the two secured notes, so the debt stack is not established and all four instruments have no ranking in the instrument register. Severity Medium. The indentures and credit agreements would close it.
- Carrying value is not disclosed by instrument, so the instrument register shows principal in the reporting-currency column and one financing cost adjustment of USD 65 million, and no interest-rate floor is stated for the floating-rate facilities. Severity Low.
- Operating indicators that the issuer states only annually or only for some periods, such as customer concentration and the consolidated price-cost spread effect, are shown as not supplied in the periods that do not print them. Severity Low.
- The balance sheet and debt note print a dash for long-term debt at September 28, 2024, so total debt for fiscal 2024 is carried as not supplied rather than zero, although liabilities and equity sum without any debt. Severity Low.
- Lines printed as a dash in a filing, and lines printed in one filing format only (the annual cash flow working-capital detail and the quarterly working-capital totals, the corporate expense allocation and goodwill impairment lines, and the pre-merger parent investment), are carried as not supplied in the periods that do not print them. Severity Low.
- Discrete second-quarter and third-quarter cash flows are not printed; the third-quarter operating cash flow of USD -13 million is derived from nine-month and six-month figures. Severity Low.
