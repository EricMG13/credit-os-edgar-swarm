## Findings

### Bottom line

Cash generation weakened through Q3 2026: nine-month operating cash flow was negative USD 29.3 million and free cash flow was negative USD 68.5 million, while carrying-value net debt rose to USD 764.4 million. The USD 454.8 million of unrestricted cash and net committed revolver availability cushions near-term needs, but working-capital and tax outflows constrain that buffer.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_dbd.htm.jsonl, pages 9–13; calculation C06; calculation C11

The reported notes mature in March 2030, with no borrowing drawn under the committed revolver; however, the Q3 filing does not identify the instrument behind USD 26.4 million of other long-term debt or its maturity. The notes and revolver share first-priority collateral, and complete covenant thresholds cannot be established without the executed agreements.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_dbd.htm.jsonl, pages 17–18; 10-K_2025-12-31_dbd.htm.jsonl, pages 78–80

### Credit analysis

Services gross margin benefited from VAT credits and execution, but those credits are not a repeatable substitute for durable product economics.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_dbd.htm.jsonl, pages 6–8; calculation C09
