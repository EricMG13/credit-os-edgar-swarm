## Findings

### Bottom line

Corporate creditors face leverage of 3.41x adjusted EBITDA at September 30, 2026, but most of this year's fall in reported leverage comes from construction accounting timing, so credit quality has not improved as much as the headline suggests.

Consolidated debt including non-recourse securitisation funding was USD 7,776 million, or 6.52x reported EBITDA against 9.55x at the end of 2025, and USD 2,891 million of it is payable from pledged receivables rather than from corporate cash flow. Last-twelve-months adjusted EBITDA includes a net construction recognition of USD 218 million, against net deferrals of USD 202 million in 2025. Excluding that timing, corporate leverage is 3.88x, close to the 3.9x the company reports.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_hgv-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 22, 24; EX-99.1_2026-10-29_hgvq32026earningsreleaseex.htm.jsonl, pages 6, 7; 10-K_2025-12-31_hgv-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 97; calculation C28; calculation C45

### Key findings

Two different claims sit in the consolidated balance sheet. Corporate debt, net was USD 4,885 million at September 30, 2026, which is 3.41x last-twelve-months adjusted EBITDA and 3.25x after unrestricted cash, and it is the revolver, term loans and senior notes that rank against the issuer. Non-recourse debt, net was a further USD 2,891 million, equal to 78.65% of the USD 3,676 million of timeshare financing receivables and payable only from the receivables pledged to it, so the consolidated 6.52x and 6.33x net describe a mixed pool. A corporate creditor should therefore read the corporate ratios and treat the non-recourse pool as a funding line whose commitment periods end in 2028 and must be renewed or replaced.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_hgv-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 22, 24, 25; calculation C5

The earnings recovery in reported figures is mostly timing. Deferred revenue, which holds customer sales on projects still under construction, fell from USD 637 million at the end of 2025 to USD 263 million at September 30, 2026 as a Hawaii project was completed, and the third quarter recognised a net construction benefit of USD 296 million, lifting adjusted EBITDA for the quarter to USD 617 million. Operating cash flow in the same quarter was USD 87 million, so the earnings came from sales booked in earlier periods rather than from cash earned in the quarter. The float had grown from USD 252 million at the end of 2024, and last-twelve-months adjusted EBITDA of USD 1,434 million carries USD 218 million of net recognition that is not a quarterly run rate.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_hgv-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 112; 10-Q_2026-09-30_hgv-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 4; EX-99.1_2026-10-29_hgvq32026earningsreleaseex.htm.jsonl, pages 4, 6, 7; calculation C28

Cash interest cover on reported EBITDA improved to 4.22x from 2.56x at the end of 2025, and on adjusted EBITDA to 5.07x from 3.26x, while cash interest on corporate debt fell to USD 283 million over the last twelve months from USD 297 million in 2025. Lower interest helped, but the numerator moved far more than the denominator, and free cash flow of USD 398 million over the last twelve months against adjusted EBITDA of USD 1,434 million shows that cash conversion is well below the earnings figure.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_hgv-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 163; 10-Q_2026-09-30_hgv-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 24; calculation C21; calculation C33

Liquidity has been used while earnings looked stronger. Revolver borrowings rose from USD 130 million at the end of 2025 to USD 490 million at September 30, 2026 in the nine months in which the issuer repurchased USD 410 million of shares and paid USD 103 million for Elara, and remaining revolver capacity fell from USD 809 million to USD 457 million. Cash plus remaining revolver capacity was USD 684 million, against USD 1,048 million at year-end, and the USD 450 million and USD 175 million of warehouse capacity is non-recourse receivables funding rather than corporate liquidity.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_hgv-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 100, 143; 10-Q_2026-09-30_hgv-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 4, 22, 23, 25; calculation C37; calculation C36

The corporate maturity wall is back-ended but concentrated in 2029. Corporate principal due is USD 6 million in the last three months of 2026 and USD 22 million in 2027, then USD 430 million in 2028 and USD 878 million in 2029, when the senior notes due 2029 fall due, followed by USD 515 million in 2030 and USD 3,095 million after that. Refinancing therefore depends on earnings holding up, which is why the construction-timing distortion matters to creditors.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_hgv-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 26; calculation C24

The strongest contrary evidence is the issuer's own figure: total net leverage of 3.9x, 3.8x and 3.9x at the three 2026 quarter-ends, and a deferral-excluded calculation that gives 3.73x at the end of 2025 and 3.88x now, so corporate leverage on its own basis is broadly flat, neither improving nor worsening. Resort and club management earned USD 440 million of segment adjusted EBITDA in nine months, level with the prior year, which supports the recurring base. The view would change if covenant levels in the credit agreement show thin headroom on the issuer's definition, if contract sales keep falling (USD 807 million in the quarter against USD 907 million), or if the net leverage definition proves to exclude items counted here.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-04-30_hgvq12026earningsreleaseex.htm.jsonl, page 4; EX-99.1_2026-07-30_hgvq22026earningsreleaseex.htm.jsonl, page 3; EX-99.1_2026-10-29_hgvq32026earningsreleaseex.htm.jsonl, pages 6, 18, 19; calculation C44; calculation C45

Three data points need care. Opening cash for the first quarter of 2025 differs by USD 1 million between filings, the net leverage definition is not printed, and the last-twelve-months denominators combine periods with different perimeters because Elara has been consolidated since April 29, 2026.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-03-31_hgv-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-K_2025-12-31_hgv-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 114; 10-Q_2026-09-30_hgv-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 8
