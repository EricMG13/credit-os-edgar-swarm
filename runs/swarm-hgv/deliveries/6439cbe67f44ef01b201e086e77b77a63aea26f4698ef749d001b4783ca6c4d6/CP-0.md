## Findings

### Bottom line

The seven supplied documents support a source-based credit snapshot of Hilton Grand Vacations Inc. to its September 30, 2026 balance sheet, but covenant headroom and the guarantor perimeter cannot be established without executed debt documents, and governance is only partly covered.

Income statements, and through the releases cash flow statements, cover every quarter from the first quarter of 2025 to the third quarter of 2026, with the fourth quarter of 2025 by subtraction. Third-quarter 2026 earnings include a $296 million net construction recognition and a $55 million allowance reduction booked as a change in estimate, so quarters are not comparable without the company's reconciling table. The 3.9x total net leverage the company reports is not defined or reconciled in any supplied document.
