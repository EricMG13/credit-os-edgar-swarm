## Findings

### Bottom line

The supplied filings and releases support the financial, business and fundamental credit work for FAIR ISAAC CORP through June 2026, but covenant headroom cannot be tested because no executed credit agreement or indenture was supplied, so creditors cannot confirm the covenant cushion. The leverage covenant steps down in stages through 2027, so the missing definition of its earnings measure bears directly on that cushion.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_fico-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 46; 10-Q_2026-06-30_fico-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 11–12

The latest evidence is the third-quarter fiscal 2026 report, 99 days before the as-of date, so the fiscal year-end position, the settlement of the accelerated share repurchase and any later financing are not established. The 2026 debt additions also make the annual report's obligations table out of date, and ratings, debt prices, peer data and the proxy statement with board and ownership detail were not supplied, which limits governance and relative-value work rather than the core credit analysis.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_fico-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 13; 10-Q_2026-06-30_fico-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 10–12; 10-K_2025-09-30_fico-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 76; 10-K_2025-09-30_fico-20250930.htm.jsonl, pages 131–132; calculation CALC-1
