## Findings

### Bottom line

Debt-funded repurchases lifted derived gross leverage to 4.46x last-twelve-months EBITDA from 3.25x at the 2025 year-end, so creditors now rely on Scores earnings to service USD 2,526.70 million more debt. A USD 1,500.00 million accelerated share repurchase in June 2026 was funded by a new term loan of the same size.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_fico-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 10–12; 10-Q_2026-06-30_fico-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 13; calculation CALC-01; calculation CALC-05; calculation CALC-06; calculation CALC-12

Operating strength is intact, with last-twelve-months EBITDA of USD 1,252.44 million and cash interest cover of 7.60x, but that cover rests on interest paid before the new term loan and notes had run for a full year. The issuer reports no EBITDA, so every EBITDA figure here is derived from operating income and depreciation and amortization, and the covenant's own definition, which is not printed, may give a different ratio.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_fico-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 2–5; 10-Q_2026-06-30_fico-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 11–12; calculation CALC-02; calculation CALC-03; calculation CALC-07

### Leverage and coverage

Derived gross leverage was 2.96x at the 2024 year-end, 3.25x at the 2025 year-end and 4.46x at 2026-06-30, with net leverage of 2.75x, 3.11x and 4.26x. The step-up in the third quarter of fiscal 2026 comes from the debt side: gross debt at carrying value rose by USD 2,526.70 million between the 2025 year-end and 2026-06-30 while last-twelve-months EBITDA rose by USD 312.64 million, so earnings growth absorbed only part of the added borrowing.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_fico-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 10–12; calculation CALC-01; calculation CALC-05; calculation CALC-06

Scores supplied USD 1,117.22 million of the USD 1,293.46 million of total segment operating income in the first nine months of fiscal 2026, so the debt service capacity rests mainly on one segment whose revenue depends on the three consumer reporting agencies for 60% of total revenues.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_fico-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 15; 10-Q_2026-06-30_fico-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 19; 10-Q_2026-06-30_fico-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 32

Cash interest cover, EBITDA over interest actually paid, was 7.03x in fiscal 2024, 9.07x in fiscal 2025 and 7.60x on a last-twelve-months basis at 2026-06-30. Paid interest lags the new borrowing: the term loan was drawn on 2026-06-05, the 2026 senior notes were issued on 2026-03-20 with a 6.25% coupon, and the filing states weighted-average rates on the revolver and term loan that sit above the 4.00% coupon of the 2028 senior notes, so the cash cost of the larger stack is only partly in the paid figure.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_fico-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 4–5; 10-Q_2026-06-30_fico-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 10–12; calculation CALC-03; calculation CALC-07

### Cash flow quality

Operating cash flow was USD 777.88 million in the first nine months of fiscal 2026 against USD 555.14 million a year earlier, while derived EBITDA rose to USD 1,011.27 million from USD 698.63 million, so the conversion of EBITDA into operating cash flow slipped as cash interest paid rose to USD 162.42 million from USD 101.11 million and cash taxes rose to USD 162.88 million from USD 129.08 million. Capital expenditures are small, USD 27.85 million in nine months, so nearly all operating cash flow is free cash flow.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_fico-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 4–5; 10-K_2025-09-30_fico-20250930.htm.jsonl, pages 90–91; calculation CALC-02; calculation CALC-10

Repurchases of common stock were USD 3,045.99 million in cash in nine months against free cash flow of USD 750.04 million as the issuer states it, so the difference was funded largely by net borrowing: proceeds of USD 2,950.00 million from the revolver and term loans and USD 1,000.00 million from senior notes, less USD 1,015.00 million and USD 400.00 million repaid. The filing states that the term loan funded the accelerated repurchase and that the 2026 senior notes repaid the 2018 senior notes.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_fico-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 4–5; 10-Q_2026-06-30_fico-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 12; 10-Q_2026-06-30_fico-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 13; 10-Q_2026-06-30_fico-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 22; EX-99.1_2026-07-29_exhibit991erq32026.htm.jsonl, page 5

### Liquidity and debt structure

Cash was USD 248.44 million at 2026-06-30 and the revolver had USD 290.00 million of commitment not drawn, before any letters of credit, none of which the filings disclose, so liquidity on the filings' figures is USD 538.44 million against USD 859.14 million at the 2025 year-end. Scheduled term loan installments of USD 300.00 million fall due within twelve months, and the issuer states that cash, available revolver borrowings and operating cash flow are expected to cover them.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_fico-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 10–11; 10-Q_2026-06-30_fico-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 33; calculation CALC-08; calculation CALC-09

Principal of USD 5,610.00 million at 2026-06-30 matures from fiscal 2026 to fiscal 2034, with USD 1,987.50 million due in fiscal 2028 when the remaining term loan balance and the 2028 senior notes fall together and USD 710.00 million of revolver borrowings falling due in fiscal 2030. USD 2,210.00 million of principal, the revolver and the term loan, is floating-rate. The revolver and term loan are described as unsecured and the note indentures as carrying covenants typical of unsecured obligations, but no source states legal ranking, guarantors or co-issuers.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_fico-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 10–12; calculation CALC-11; calculation CALC-04

### Definitions, funding float and later events

Deferred revenue of USD 205.42 million at 2026-06-30, USD 187.37 million at 2025-09-30 and USD 156.90 million at 2024-09-30 is billed ahead of recognition and funds operations without interest, so it is working-capital float rather than ordinary payables, and a slowdown in new software bookings could reverse it.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_fico-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-K_2025-09-30_fico-20250930.htm.jsonl, pages 88–89

The sources state no subsequent event after 2026-06-30 that changes net debt. The settlement of the accelerated share repurchase is expected in the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026 and delivers shares against the USD 300.00 million already prepaid, so it does not change debt, and the first term loan installment of USD 75.00 million is due on 2026-09-30. The nine-month repurchase amount has two figures, USD 3,045.99 million paid in cash and USD 3,052.89 million recorded in equity, and each is used for its own purpose.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_fico-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 3–4; 10-Q_2026-06-30_fico-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 11; 10-Q_2026-06-30_fico-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 13; 10-Q_2026-06-30_fico-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 33

### Contrary evidence and monitoring

The strongest contrary evidence is earnings quality at the Scores segment, where segment operating income was 90% of segment revenue in nine months, and free cash flow of USD 750.04 million in the same period, which together would support debt reduction if repurchases slowed. The covenant definition could also add back share-based compensation of USD 174.29 million over twelve months and report a lower ratio than the derived one. The view would change on the executed credit agreement's definition, on the fiscal 2026 results and on the interest cost of the new borrowings once a full period is reported.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_fico-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 19; 10-Q_2026-06-30_fico-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 32; 10-Q_2026-06-30_fico-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 4–5; 10-Q_2026-06-30_fico-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 11–12; calculation CALC-13
