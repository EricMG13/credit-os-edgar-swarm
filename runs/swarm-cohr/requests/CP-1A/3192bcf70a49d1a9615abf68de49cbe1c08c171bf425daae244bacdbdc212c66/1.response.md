## Findings

### Bottom line

Creditors face operating concentration risk, not owner-behaviour risk: no controlling holder is disclosed and no common dividend or buyback took cash out, but earnings depend on AI datacenter demand and two unnamed customers. The Datacenter & Communications segment supplied 74.10% of fiscal 2026 revenue and 82.34% of the latest quarter, and one customer alone took 20% of fiscal 2026 revenue.
Basis: 10-K_2026-06-30_iivi-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 128–129; 10-Q_2026-09-30_iivi-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 25; calculation C01

Governance and sponsor risk is low on conduct visible in the filings: the preferred stock converted into common, NVIDIA added USD 2 billion of equity and no shares were repurchased. That rating is provisional because the proxy statement, board data and holder-level ownership were not supplied.
Basis: 10-K_2026-06-30_iivi-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 59, 114–115, 136

### Key findings

No controlling shareholder or sponsor is disclosed, and the one sponsor-style claim, the Series B convertible preferred stock, no longer exists. The two Series B issues carried an aggregate issue price of USD 2.15 billion. Their holder, who is not named in the filings, waived dividends from 2025-11-20, and all of the stock converted in the quarter ended 2025-12-31 into common shares equal to about 15.3% of the shares outstanding on 2026-11-02. That ended a claim ranking ahead of the common equity, on which dividends recognised were USD 123.69 million in fiscal 2025 and USD 31.75 million in fiscal 2026, with cash dividends of USD 11.44 million in each year. It also created a large common holding whose owner, current stake and selling intent are not established. The annual report's market-value calculation treats only directors and executive officers as affiliates, which fits a widely held company but does not prove that no large holder has influence.
Basis: 10-K_2026-06-30_iivi-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 59, 88, 114–115; 10-Q_2026-09-30_iivi-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 2; calculation C09; calculation C10

NVIDIA is at once a shareholder, a customer and a capacity counterparty, which strengthens funding but ties Coherent's growth to one AI supply chain. On 2026-03-02 NVIDIA bought common stock for USD 2 billion at USD 256.80 a share, equal to 3.95% of shares outstanding on 2026-11-02 if all are still held. The six-month price-protection clause expired in September 2026 without being triggered, so no extra shares or cash were owed. Management states that the agreement is non-exclusive, includes a multi-billion-dollar purchase commitment with NVIDIA and future access and capacity rights for NVIDIA, and may require incremental investment in equipment, labour and working capital through 2030, with possible effects on revenue concentration, gross margin and capital expenditure. The filings do not say which party commits to buy, or the size, minimum volumes or term of the commitment, or whether NVIDIA is one of the two customers above 10% of revenue.
Basis: 10-K_2026-06-30_iivi-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 60, 61, 79, 114; 10-Q_2026-09-30_iivi-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 19, 42; calculation C08

The group is becoming a datacenter optics company with a shrinking industrial tail, so the diversification that once cushioned creditors is eroding. Datacenter & Communications was 55.90% of revenue in fiscal 2024, 64.63% in fiscal 2025, 74.10% in fiscal 2026 and 82.34% in the quarter to 2026-09-30. Its revenue grew 40.46% in fiscal 2026 and 79.06% in the latest quarter, while Industrial revenue fell 10.29% and 14.81%, mainly because of the two disposals. Quarterly segment revenue rose from USD 1,018.3 million in the quarter to 2025-06-30 to USD 1,951.7 million in the quarter to 2026-09-30, and the sequential increase widened from 7.04% to 20.85%, so any pause in AI datacenter orders would be sharper against a larger and faster-growing base.
Basis: 10-K_2026-06-30_iivi-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 128; 10-Q_2026-09-30_iivi-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 25, 38–39; EX-99.1_2026-08-12_d128030dex991.htm.jsonl, pages 7–8; EX-99.1_2026-11-04_d176836dex991.htm.jsonl, page 7; calculation C01; calculation C03; calculation C04

Customer concentration is high, rising and unattributed. One customer took 20% of fiscal 2026 revenue after 10% in each of the two prior years, and a second took 12%, together about 32%, mostly in the Datacenter & Communications segment. The customers are not named, the issuer warns that losing, cancelling or delaying their orders could harm the business, and customer prepayments are small at USD 63 million of contract liabilities, so there is no advance-payment cushion if orders slow.
Basis: 10-K_2026-06-30_iivi-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 17, 28, 101, 129; calculation C13

Growth is absorbing cash faster than the business generates it, so debt and equity funding, not operations, are paying for the build-out. Operating cash flow fell to USD 79.51 million in fiscal 2026 from USD 633.60 million because the company built inventory, which rose 79.53% to 2026-06-30 and a further 29.46% in the next quarter to USD 3.34 billion. Capital expenditure was USD 1,102.91 million in fiscal 2026, 2.50x the prior year's, and USD 775.55 million in the latest quarter alone, in which the company also drew USD 785 million of new borrowings and realised USD 625 million from sales and maturities of short-term investments. Vendor purchase commitments stood at USD 11.8 billion at 2026-06-30, so leverage depends on earnings growth continuing while commitments run ahead of revenue.
Basis: 10-K_2026-06-30_iivi-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 76, 85, 88, 113; 10-Q_2026-09-30_iivi-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 4, 39; calculation C11

A large non-cash exposure sits in the shrinking Industrial segment, which holds 73.71% of the USD 4.38 billion of goodwill but supplied only 25.90% of fiscal 2026 revenue and 17.66% of the latest quarter. The segment lost revenue to the sale of the aerospace and defense business for about USD 400 million and of the Munich division at a loss of USD 96 million, and one more entity is still held for sale. The auditor's critical audit matter is the goodwill of the Lasers reporting unit, USD 3.1 billion, whose fair value depends on forecast revenue and the discount rate, and the filings do not state how that unit maps to the two segments. An impairment would not use cash but would cut equity and, where a lender definition starts from reported earnings, could matter; those definitions were not supplied.
Basis: 10-K_2026-06-30_iivi-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 83, 101, 103, 128; 10-Q_2026-09-30_iivi-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 25, 34; calculation C07; calculation C01

Part of the liquidity and part of the debt sit in subsidiaries where the parent's creditors have limited reach. Silicon Carbide LLC is owned about 75% by Coherent, with Denso and Mitsubishi Electric each holding about 12.5% after paying USD 1 billion in total in December 2023, and USD 581 million of restricted cash at 2026-09-30 can be used only by that subsidiary. Separately, foreign subsidiaries had USD 803 million outstanding under local credit lines and the August 2026 facility, equal to 20.07% of total debt obligations, and neither the parent nor any other subsidiary guarantees them, so those lenders' recourse is to the borrowers. The filings do not state the borrowers' assets, whether they guarantee the secured credit facilities, or the minority holders' rights in Silicon Carbide LLC.
Basis: 10-K_2026-06-30_iivi-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 116; 10-Q_2026-09-30_iivi-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 14, 41–42; calculation C12; calculation C14

Capital allocation shows no extraction. The company repurchased no shares in fiscal 2026 and has no repurchase programme, has not historically paid a cash dividend on its common stock, and paid USD 509 million on its term facilities in fiscal 2026, of which USD 502 million was voluntary. Cash costs of equity compensation are rising, with payments for employees' minimum tax obligations up 43.39% in fiscal 2026 to USD 77.42 million and at USD 129 million in the latest quarter, 3.49x the same quarter a year earlier. An export-control inquiry into past sales to Huawei has no estimated loss, and the company states it was in compliance with its credit agreement and indenture covenants at 2026-06-30 and 2026-09-30.
Basis: 10-K_2026-06-30_iivi-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 59, 78, 88, 107, 108, 113–114; 10-Q_2026-09-30_iivi-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 13, 14, 18, 39; calculation C15

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a concentration-led view is that the concentration is with some of the most capable buyers in the market and is becoming contractual: NVIDIA invested USD 2 billion and signed a multi-year supply agreement with a multi-billion-dollar purchase commitment, the Datacenter & Communications segment earned a profit margin of 25.21% in fiscal 2026 against 19.00% two years earlier, and shareholders' equity rose from USD 5.64 billion to USD 10.90 billion, a much larger buffer beneath the debt. The strongest evidence against a low governance reading is what is missing: the proxy statement, the board's composition and the owner of the shares issued when the preferred stock converted.
Basis: 10-K_2026-06-30_iivi-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 60, 61, 86, 114, 128; calculation C02

The view would worsen if customer disclosure showed that the 20% customer and the NVIDIA commitment are one counterparty on short or cancellable terms, if revenue fell below management's second-quarter outlook of USD 2.55 billion to USD 2.75 billion, if capital expenditure kept outrunning operating cash flow after the quarter to 2026-09-30, if the export-control inquiry ended in a material penalty, or if the proxy statement or credit agreement showed a holder with board rights or capacity for large distributions. It would improve if the largest customers were named with minimum purchase terms, if operating cash flow turned positive after capital expenditure while inventory stopped rising, and if the former preferred holder were shown to have sold its shares without conflict.
Basis: 10-K_2026-06-30_iivi-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 88, 113, 114, 129; 10-Q_2026-09-30_iivi-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 4, 18; EX-99.1_2026-11-04_d176836dex991.htm.jsonl, page 2

### Limitations

- The proxy statement was not supplied, so board composition, director independence, executive pay, holder-level ownership and related-party dealings are not established; the low governance and sponsor rating would change if they showed a controlling holder, board rights for NVIDIA or the former preferred holder, or fee arrangements.
Basis: 10-K_2026-06-30_iivi-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 3, 136

- The former Series B holder is not named and its holding after conversion is not stated, so the finding that no controlling holder is disclosed cannot exclude that this holder owns a stake of about 15.3% of shares outstanding with influence over the board or over sales into the market.
Basis: 10-K_2026-06-30_iivi-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 114–115; 10-Q_2026-09-30_iivi-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 2; calculation C09

- The credit agreement and indenture text were not supplied, so the capacity to pay dividends, repurchase shares, add subsidiary debt or release guarantees is not established; the sponsor conclusion rests on behaviour, not on legal capacity.
Basis: 10-K_2026-06-30_iivi-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 51, 55, 105, 106, 107

- The customers above 10% of revenue are unnamed and the purchase commitment with NVIDIA is neither sized nor assigned to a party, so the concentration findings give the shares but not the counterparty, the contract term or the minimum volumes that would show how much revenue is committed.
Basis: 10-K_2026-06-30_iivi-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 61, 129

- Segment disclosure splits revenue between Datacenter & Communications and Industrial only, with no separate datacenter and communications revenue and no volume, price, backlog or order data, so growth cannot be divided between price and volume and revenue visibility beyond the next-quarter outlook cannot be measured.
Basis: 10-K_2026-06-30_iivi-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 100–101; EX-99.1_2026-11-04_d176836dex991.htm.jsonl, page 2

- The export-control inquiry has no determination and no stated loss range, so the regulatory-conduct element of the governance assessment is held open rather than scored.
Basis: 10-K_2026-06-30_iivi-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 113–114; 10-Q_2026-09-30_iivi-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 18

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

The period holds no sponsor buyout, recapitalisation or large acquisition. The transactions that matter are one equity placement, the conversion of the preferred stock, two disposals, an amendment of the secured credit facilities and a new layer of subsidiary debt that the parent does not guarantee.
Basis: 10-K_2026-06-30_iivi-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 61, 103, 105–106, 114–115; 10-Q_2026-09-30_iivi-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 14

NVIDIA bought 7,788,161 newly issued common shares at USD 256.80 a share on 2026-03-02 for USD 2 billion gross, a private placement the issuer says will fund research, capacity and operations. The shares carried a six-month lock-up and a six-month price-protection clause, which expired in September 2026 without being triggered. A separate multi-year, non-exclusive agreement includes a multi-billion-dollar purchase commitment with NVIDIA, without saying which party commits, and capacity rights for NVIDIA, and the issuer says it may require Coherent to invest in equipment, labour and working capital through 2030.
Basis: 10-K_2026-06-30_iivi-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 60, 61, 79, 114; 10-Q_2026-09-30_iivi-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 19, 42; calculation C08

Series B-1, issued in March 2021 for USD 750 million, and Series B-2, issued on 2022-07-01 for USD 1.4 billion, converted into 30.1 million common shares during the quarter ended 2025-12-31 at a conversion price of USD 85 a share, most by the holder's election and the rest by the company's. The holder waived dividends from 2025-11-20, no Series B stock remained at 2026-06-30 or 2026-09-30, and USD 2.5 billion moved from mezzanine equity to common equity.
Basis: 10-K_2026-06-30_iivi-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 114–115; 10-Q_2026-09-30_iivi-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 19; calculation C09; calculation C10

The aerospace and defense business was sold on 2025-09-02 for about USD 400 million with a gain of USD 115 million, and the Munich, Germany materials-processing tools division was sold on 2026-01-30 at a loss of USD 96 million, largely taken earlier as impairment charges. One further entity classified as held for sale in the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026 was still held for sale at 2026-09-30, so a third disposal is possible but no agreement is stated.
Basis: 10-K_2026-06-30_iivi-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 103; 10-Q_2026-09-30_iivi-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 9, 34

On 2025-09-26 the Credit Agreement was amended twice. Amendment No. 4 replaced the revolver with USD 700 million of senior secured commitments, added a USD 1,250 million senior secured Term A tranche whose proceeds repaid the existing Term A loans, and set both to mature on the earlier of 2030-09-26 and a springing maturity 91 days before the 2029 notes or term B loans if liquidity is below USD 250 million plus the principal still outstanding. Amendment No. 5 replaced the term B loans with Term B-3 loans at a SOFR-based rate with a 0.50% floor plus 1.75%, maturing on 2029-07-01. The facilities are guaranteed by the borrower and certain subsidiaries and secured on substantially all their assets, and no revolver borrowings were outstanding at 2026-09-30.
Basis: 10-K_2026-06-30_iivi-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 105–106; 10-Q_2026-09-30_iivi-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 41

In June, July and August 2026 two foreign subsidiaries signed unsecured credit lines of up to USD 475 million that mature in 2029 and had USD 205 million outstanding at 2026-09-30. On 2026-08-12 another foreign subsidiary signed the August 2026 Facility with local lenders, first used on 2026-08-17 and drawn at USD 598 million at 2026-09-30, at three-month Term SOFR plus 20 basis points for US dollar loans, to fund working capital and repay intercompany loans. The annual report states its commitments as approximately USD 945 million and the quarterly report as approximately USD 950 million, a difference the filings do not reconcile. Neither the parent nor any other subsidiary guarantees these facilities, so on an analyst reading their lenders have first call on the borrowers' own assets and cash flow; the filings do not state those assets.
Basis: 10-K_2026-06-30_iivi-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 78, 134; 10-Q_2026-09-30_iivi-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 14, 41; calculation C12

The filings disclose no new bond or term loan issue other than the Term A tranche above and the foreign facilities; the only notes outstanding are the 2029 senior notes issued in December 2021.
