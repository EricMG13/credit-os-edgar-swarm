## Findings

### Bottom line

ESCO carries very little leverage on reported figures: net debt was USD 11.76 million, or 0.04x last-twelve-months EBITDA of USD 305.68 million at 2026-06-30, so existing creditors are well covered until the pending Megger acquisition closes. Pro forma for Megger's cash consideration of about USD 0.9 billion, net debt would be about USD 911.76 million, or 2.98x ESCO's own EBITDA; the closing date is not stated beyond the first quarter of fiscal 2027.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_ese-20260630x10q.htm.jsonl, page 10; 10-Q_2026-06-30_ese-20260630x10q.htm.jsonl, page 19; calculation C13; calculation C20; calculation C71

Cash interest cover was 20.42x and undrawn revolver capacity was USD 442.3 million against total debt of USD 85 million, with USD 20 million classified as due within twelve months and the rest due by 2028-08-30. The company states adjusted EBITDA only for interim periods, so leverage here is on reported EBITDA, and nothing after the June 2026 quarter is supplied.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_ese-20260630x10q.htm.jsonl, pages 10–11; EX-99.1_2026-08-06_tm2621646d1_ex99-1.htm.jsonl, page 7; calculation C52; calculation C36; Source readiness (CP-0)

### Key findings

Reported leverage has fallen because debt was repaid while earnings grew. Total debt fell from USD 186 million at 2025-09-30 to USD 85 million at 2026-06-30, and last-twelve-months EBITDA rose from USD 245.38 million to USD 305.68 million, which took total debt to EBITDA from 0.76x to 0.28x and net debt to EBITDA from 0.34x to 0.04x. The repayment came from operating cash flow of USD 193.38 million from continuing operations in the nine months to 2026-06-30 and from a USD 28.11 million fall in cash, and the Maritime acquisition, owned since 2025-04-25, lifted the earnings base.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_ese-20250930x10k.htm.jsonl, page 80; 10-Q_2026-06-30_ese-20260630x10q.htm.jsonl, page 10; calculation C20; calculation C50; calculation C51

Interest cover is strong and improving. Last-twelve-months EBITDA of USD 305.68 million against cash interest paid of USD 14.97 million gives 20.42x, up from 14.09x in fiscal 2025, because debt fell and the weighted average rate on the revolver eased to 4.98% in the quarter to 2026-06-30. Cash interest paid, not the interest expense line, is the denominator, since the June 2026 quarter's expense includes about USD 7 million of financing costs for the pending acquisition.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_ese-20260630x10q.htm.jsonl, page 11; 10-Q_2026-06-30_ese-20260630x10q.htm.jsonl, page 23; EX-99.1_2026-08-06_tm2621646d1_ex99-1.htm.jsonl, page 7; calculation C52; calculation C21

Cash flow quality is good but flattered by customer advances. Operating cash flow from continuing operations was USD 193.38 million in the nine months to 2026-06-30 against USD 88.30 million a year earlier, and free cash flow after capital expenditure and capitalized software was USD 160.94 million. Including discontinued operations the figure is USD 134.04 million, because discontinued operations used USD 59.34 million, which the company ties to about USD 59 million of cash taxes on the VACCO gain paid in the second quarter.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_ese-20260630x10q.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-Q_2026-06-30_ese-20260630x10q.htm.jsonl, page 25; calculation C32

Contract liabilities, customer advances that fund working capital, rose from USD 80.84 million at 2024-09-30 to USD 216.59 million at 2025-09-30, mainly through the Maritime acquisition and growth at Globe and Doble, and to USD 288.14 million at 2026-06-30, which the company attributes to the timing of customer payments at Globe and Maritime. That float supports cash generation and would unwind if deliveries outpace new advances, so part of the strong cash flow is timing, not earnings.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_ese-20250930x10k.htm.jsonl, page 39; 10-K_2025-09-30_ese-20250930x10k.htm.jsonl, page 58; 10-Q_2026-06-30_ese-20260630x10q.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-Q_2026-06-30_ese-20260630x10q.htm.jsonl, page 24

Liquidity is ample against the debt. At 2026-06-30 cash was USD 73.24 million and the undrawn part of the USD 500 million revolver was USD 442.3 million after USD 45 million of borrowings and USD 12.7 million of letters of credit. The revolver and the incremental term loan both mature on 2028-08-30, so no facility expires within twelve months, although USD 20 million of term loan principal is classified as current.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_ese-20260630x10q.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-06-30_ese-20260630x10q.htm.jsonl, pages 10–11; calculation C36

The strongest contrary evidence is the pending Megger acquisition, so these figures describe the company before closing. About USD 0.9 billion of the USD 2.35 billion price is cash; if paid from cash or new debt, net debt would be about USD 911.76 million, or 2.98x ESCO's own EBITDA before any Megger earnings, and new bank facilities of USD 500 million each for a revolver, a term loan A and a term loan B replace the existing facility on closing. Megger's own EBITDA, debt and cash are not supplied, so a closing inside the first quarter of fiscal 2027 with offsetting earnings would change the reading; so would any news that the deal did not close.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_ese-20260630x10q.htm.jsonl, page 12; 10-Q_2026-06-30_ese-20260630x10q.htm.jsonl, page 19; EX-99.1_2026-08-06_tm2621646d1_ex99-1.htm.jsonl, page 3; calculation C71

Three definition points limit comparisons. Annual EBITDA is calculated as EBIT plus depreciation and amortization on the issuer's own construction because the company states EBITDA only for interim periods; debt excludes USD 50.26 million of operating and USD 15.40 million of finance lease liabilities that sit on other balance sheet lines; and the USD 20 million current portion is described as having no contractual repayment obligation in the annual report and the December 2025 report but as obligated in the March and June 2026 reports.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_ese-20250930x10k.htm.jsonl, page 81; 10-Q_2025-12-31_ese-20251231x10q.htm.jsonl, page 9; 10-Q_2026-03-31_ese-20260331x10q.htm.jsonl, page 11; 10-Q_2026-06-30_ese-20260630x10q.htm.jsonl, page 11; 10-Q_2026-06-30_ese-20260630x10q.htm.jsonl, page 17; Source readiness (CP-0)

The VACCO sale shows how one event carries several figures with different roles: net proceeds of about USD 270 million, an investing inflow of USD 268.38 million in discontinued operations, an after-tax gain of USD 172.64 million, and about USD 59 million of cash taxes paid in the second quarter of fiscal 2026. Each is carried on its own line and none is netted against another.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_ese-20250930x10k.htm.jsonl, page 39; 10-K_2025-09-30_ese-20250930x10k.htm.jsonl, page 57; 10-K_2025-09-30_ese-20250930x10k.htm.jsonl, page 59; 10-Q_2026-06-30_ese-20260630x10q.htm.jsonl, page 25

Events after the balance sheet date are kept out of period figures: a dividend of USD 0.08 per share paid on 2026-07-17, a further dividend of the same amount payable on 2026-10-15, and the Megger closing expected in the first quarter of fiscal 2027. The table of post-balance-sheet adjustments states each effect on net debt and leverage.
