## Findings

### Bottom line

ESCO, a listed aerospace, utility-testing and RF-testing group, has no sponsor or controlling shareholder named in the sources, so creditors rely on bank documents and disclosure, and the pending Megger acquisition will test that by adding secured bank debt and a new shareholder.

Closing was expected in the first quarter of fiscal 2027 and no document after 2026-08-10 is supplied, so ownership and debt at the 2026-11-05 cutoff are not established.

### Key findings

No controlling shareholder or sponsor is evidenced, so there is no owner whose support or extraction can be assessed, and creditor protection rests on the bank documents and public reporting. ESCO is a Missouri corporation listed on the New York Stock Exchange; the sources name no controlling holder, and directors, officers and holders of five percent or more appear only in a proxy statement that is not supplied. The articles also provide a classified board and a two-thirds vote for mergers under Missouri law, which make a change of control harder. The absence of a named sponsor removes dividend recapitalization and sponsor fees as documented risks, but the control position stays unproven until the proxy statement is read.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_ese-20250930x10k.htm.jsonl, page 1; 10-K_2025-09-30_ese-20250930x10k.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-K_2025-09-30_ese-20250930x10k.htm.jsonl, page 28; 10-K_2025-09-30_ese-20250930x10k.htm.jsonl, page 31; 10-K_2025-09-30_ese-20250930x10k.htm.jsonl, page 48

The Megger purchase is the credit event: it is large against the balance sheet and changes both the debt and the shareholder base. The agreed price is about USD 2.35 billion, 97.11% of total assets and 1.45x shareholders' equity at 2026-06-30, and 4.98x the cash price paid for Maritime, the largest earlier deal. About USD 0.9 billion is cash, funded from existing cash and incremental debt with committed financing, and about USD 1.4 billion is ESCO equity. The stated cash and equity parts add to USD 50 million less than the stated total, which the filings repeat without reconciling.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a governance concern is ESCO's conduct so far: it has no sponsor, pays a flat dividend of USD 0.32 per share a year, has bought no shares since fiscal 2024, used the VACCO proceeds primarily to pay down debt, and arranged the Maritime financing through a lender-approved amendment. Before Megger its funded debt was only USD 85 million, so the new debt starts from a low base and no creditor-adverse action is documented.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_ese-20250930x10k.htm.jsonl, page 32; 10-K_2025-09-30_ese-20250930x10k.htm.jsonl, page 40; 10-K_2025-09-30_ese-20250930x10k.htm.jsonl, page 80; 10-Q_2026-06-30_ese-20260630x10q.htm.jsonl, page 11; 10-Q_2026-06-30_ese-20260630x10q.htm.jsonl, page 25; calculation C68

The view would turn adverse if closing gave TBG AG a large holding with board, consent or sale rights, if the new term loans were drawn in full without Megger earnings to match, if the credit agreements allowed distributions or buybacks that conduct has not yet used, or if the proxy statement named a large holder with interests apart from creditors. It would turn more favorable if the Shareholder Agreement limited TBG AG's rights and transfers, Megger's earnings and debt proved modest against the price, and the covenant levels left wide headroom. A termination of the Megger agreement would return the analysis to the stable position before the deal.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_ese-20260630x10q.htm.jsonl, page 12; 10-Q_2026-06-30_ese-20260630x10q.htm.jsonl, page 19; 10-Q_2026-06-30_ese-20260630x10q.htm.jsonl, page 30; EX-99.1_2026-08-06_tm2621646d1_ex99-1.htm.jsonl, page 3

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

ESCO signed a share purchase agreement on 2026-04-15 to buy Megger Group Limited, a testing and monitoring supplier to utilities, from TBG AG for about USD 2.35 billion, of which about USD 0.9 billion is cash and about USD 1.4 billion is ESCO equity. The cash is to come from existing cash and incremental debt with committed financing in place, Megger will join the Utility Solutions Group, regulatory filings are under way, and closing was expected in the first quarter of fiscal 2027. The annual report names Megger as a significant competitor of that segment.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-03-31_ese-20260331x10q.htm.jsonl, page 24; 10-Q_2026-06-30_ese-20260630x10q.htm.jsonl, page 19; EX-99.1_2026-05-07_tm2613615d1_ex99-1.htm.jsonl, page 3; EX-99.1_2026-08-06_tm2621646d1_ex99-1.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-K_2025-09-30_ese-20250930x10k.htm.jsonl, page 13

On 2026-05-29 ESCO and certain subsidiaries signed a credit agreement with JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A. as administrative agent and Bank of America, N.A. as syndication agent, to finance the purchase. It provides a USD 500 million senior secured revolving facility, a USD 500 million senior secured term loan A and a USD 500 million senior secured term loan B. The revolver and term loan A mature five years after the effective date and term loan B seven years after it. An expansion option allows more revolver or term loans up to the greater of USD 451 million or 100% of Consolidated EBITDA plus further amounts subject to leverage tests. The facility becomes effective on conditions that include the Megger closing, and it replaces the existing facility on that date.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_ese-20260630x10q.htm.jsonl, page 12; 10-Q_2026-06-30_ese-20260630x10q.htm.jsonl, page 30

The existing facility is a USD 500 million revolver and an incremental term loan, both maturing on 2028-08-30, secured by guaranties of material U.S. subsidiaries and a pledge of the equity of material foreign subsidiaries; USD 85 million was drawn at 2026-06-30. No bonds, notes or other debt securities are described in the seven documents. The credit agreements themselves are not supplied, so covenant levels, baskets and guarantor lists are not established. Nothing in the sources states Megger's revenue, earnings, debt or cash, the number or issue price of the ESCO shares, the terms of the Shareholder Agreement, or whether the deal has closed, so pro forma leverage and the size of TBG AG's holding are not established.
