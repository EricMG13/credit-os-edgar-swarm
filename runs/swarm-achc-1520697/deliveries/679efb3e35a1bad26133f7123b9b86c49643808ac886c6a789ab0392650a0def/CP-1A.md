## Findings

### Bottom line

Acadia has no sponsor in the filings, so creditor risk comes from payor concentration and legal exposure, not owner extraction: Medicaid supplied 61.8% of revenue in nine months 2026 and a federal criminal investigation of the acute care line is open.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_achc_achc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 10; 10-Q_2026-09-30_achc_achc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 18; 10-K_2025-12-31_achc_achc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 74

No dividend has ever been paid and share repurchases stopped at USD 50.4 million in 2025, but 2025 capital spending was debt-funded and the chief executive and chief financial officer changed in 2026. Governance risk is assessed as Medium because the evidence is mixed and the proxy statement with board and holder detail was not supplied.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_achc_achc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 78; 10-K_2025-12-31_achc_achc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 79; 10-Q_2026-09-30_achc_achc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 54; 10-Q_2026-03-31_achc_achc-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 47; 10-Q_2026-06-30_achc_achc-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 54; calculation N1; calculation N3

### Key findings

The filings identify no controlling holder or sponsor, so creditors face no owner-extraction mechanism but also no sponsor equity to cure a shortfall. The company is a NASDAQ-listed holding company, the only investor named is Waud Capital Partners and it appears only in a charter clause that exempts it and its funds from the business-combination limit, with no stake stated, and the holder list sits in a proxy statement that was not supplied.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_achc_achc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 74; 10-K_2025-12-31_achc_achc-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 108–110; 10-Q_2026-09-30_achc_achc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 1

Payor concentration is the dominant operating risk and it is rising. Medicaid was 57.7% of 2025 revenue and 61.8% of the first nine months of 2026, up from 53.9% in 2023, and Medicare and Medicaid together were about 72% of 2025 revenue. The federal 2025 budget act cuts Medicaid spending, bars new provider taxes and caps state directed payments, with grandfathered programs stepping down by ten percentage points a year from 2028; the company expects little near-term effect from the work requirements because of exemptions, but the payment caps would reduce the rate base behind its largest payor.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_achc_achc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 9; 10-K_2025-12-31_achc_achc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 42; 10-K_2025-12-31_achc_achc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 134; 10-Q_2026-09-30_achc_achc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 10; 10-Q_2026-09-30_achc_achc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 36–37

Legal and regulatory exposure is the main event risk and it sits on the largest line. A federal grand jury investigation and SEC subpoenas concern the acute care line, which was 55% of 2025 revenue and 57.53% of nine-month 2026 revenue, covering admissions, length of stay and billing; the company reports no findings and cannot estimate a liability. Settled matters show the cash scale: USD 400.0 million for Desert Hills, USD 179.0 million for the 2019 securities class action with USD 31.5 million insured, and a 2026 jury award of USD 35.0 million compensatory and USD 70.0 million punitive damages against an indirect subsidiary that the subsidiary is contesting.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_achc_achc-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 10–11; 10-Q_2026-09-30_achc_achc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 9; 10-Q_2026-09-30_achc_achc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 15; 10-Q_2026-09-30_achc_achc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 16; 10-Q_2026-09-30_achc_achc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 18; 10-Q_2026-09-30_achc_achc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 20; calculation C3

Insurance does not absorb all of this, which turns verdicts into cash calls. Coverage for the relevant policy year was exhausted by prior losses for the sixth Desert Hills lawsuit and for the excess portion of the Sandoval settlement, and the self-insured professional and general liability reserve rose to USD 214.7 million from USD 181.8 million after a USD 28.6 million unfavourable adjustment in the second quarter of 2026.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_achc_achc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 13; 10-Q_2026-09-30_achc_achc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 15; 10-Q_2026-09-30_achc_achc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 19

Leadership is in transition during the investigations. The chief executive agreement dated 2026-01-19 and the former chief executive's separation dated 2026-01-20 were followed by an interim chief financial officer who signs the second and third-quarter reports, and the returning chief executive holds a one-time option award that vests on share-price hurdles rather than credit measures. Finance continuity is therefore a monitoring point, not a finding of weakness.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-03-31_achc_achc-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 47; 10-Q_2026-03-31_achc_achc-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 48; 10-Q_2026-06-30_achc_achc-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 54; 10-Q_2026-09-30_achc_achc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 27

Capital allocation has not favoured shareholders at creditors' expense, but growth has been debt-funded. The company has never paid a dividend, used USD 50.4 million of a USD 300.0 million repurchase program in 2025 and none in 2026, leaving USD 250.0 million unused, while 2025 capital spending of USD 571.81 million far exceeded operating cash flow and total debt rose by USD 543.06 million; spending then fell to USD 165.86 million in the nine months of 2026.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_achc_achc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 78; 10-K_2025-12-31_achc_achc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 79; 10-K_2025-12-31_achc_achc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 123; 10-Q_2026-09-30_achc_achc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-Q_2026-09-30_achc_achc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 54; calculation N1; calculation N3

Growth is concentrated in acute inpatient care, which is also the line under investigation: acute revenue grew 8.05% in the nine months of 2026 while specialty treatment fell 7.72% and comprehensive treatment centers were flat, so the mix is moving toward the line with the largest regulatory overhang.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_achc_achc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 9; calculation C3

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a Medium rating is that no behaviour in the filings extracts value from creditors: no dividend, a paused repurchase, cut capital spending, USD 147.61 million less debt than at the end of 2025 and no state above 14% of revenue or facility above 4%. The strongest evidence for a higher rating is the open criminal investigation, the rising Medicaid share and uninsured verdicts.
The view would move to Low if the investigations close without charges or a material penalty and Medicaid rate caps are absorbed without margin loss. It would move to High if the investigations produce charges, exclusion risk or a settlement near the scale of the Desert Hills payment, if the contested 2026 jury award is upheld without relief, or if buybacks or debt-funded growth resume while leverage is above its recent range.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_achc_achc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 9; 10-K_2025-12-31_achc_achc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 79; 10-Q_2026-09-30_achc_achc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 18; 10-Q_2026-09-30_achc_achc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 20; calculation N4

### Limitations

- The proxy statement was not supplied, so board composition, executive pay and holder concentration are not established; the ownership finding is limited to the absence of an identified controlling holder in the filings.
- Neither the credit agreement nor the indentures were supplied, so capacity for shareholder payments is not assessed and the no-extraction view rests on observed behaviour alone.
- The investigations and the contested jury award are unresolved with no liability range, so the legal figures here are settled amounts and not an estimate of future cost, which caps how far the Medium rating can be tightened.
- The finance leadership change is evidenced only by signature blocks and exhibit titles, so the date of the chief financial officer change is not established.
