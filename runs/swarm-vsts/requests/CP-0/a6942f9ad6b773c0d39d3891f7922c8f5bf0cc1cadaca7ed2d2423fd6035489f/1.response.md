### Bottom line

The seven supplied documents are ready, with limitations, for the credit snapshot's financial, business and fundamental credit work through the quarter ended July 3, 2026, but nothing reaches the fiscal year-end or the 2026-11-05 as-of date.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-07-03_vsts-20260703.htm.jsonl, pages 1, 20; EX-99.1_2026-08-11_vstsq3fy26earningsrelease-.htm.jsonl, page 1; calculation C2
