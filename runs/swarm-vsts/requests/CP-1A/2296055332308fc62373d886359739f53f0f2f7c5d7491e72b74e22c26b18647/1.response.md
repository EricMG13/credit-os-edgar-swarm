## Findings

### Bottom line

Vestis presents medium governance and business risk for creditors: the supplied documents identify no sponsor or controlling holder and revenue is very diversified, but customer losses, executive turnover, shareholder litigation and a rising restructuring cost estimate show execution risk.
Basis: 10-K_2025-10-03_vsts-20251003.htm.jsonl, pages 11, 59, 68; 10-Q_2026-07-03_vsts-20260703.htm.jsonl, page 42

The main protection against value leakage is contractual, not governance: since May 2025 the credit agreement restricts dividends and share repurchases, and no dividend has been paid since the first half of fiscal 2025, but the restriction can end after the quarter ending October 2, 2026 once covenants are met, before leverage reaches 4.50x. The restructuring plan is the key test: its cost estimate rose from a range of USD 25 million to USD 30 million to a range of USD 35 million to USD 40 million, with USD 31.2 million already recognized.
Basis: 10-K_2025-10-03_vsts-20251003.htm.jsonl, pages 59, 67; 10-Q_2026-07-03_vsts-20260703.htm.jsonl, pages 17, 20, 42; calculation P3; calculation P6

### Key findings

Revenue durability is strong in structure but weakening in practice. Recurring rental is 95% of revenue and the 10 largest customers are under 10%, yet revenue fell 2.53% in fiscal 2025, or 4.37% without the extra week, mostly because lost business exceeded new business, and fell 1.90% in the nine months to July 3, 2026, with net volumes down 4.5% in the third quarter. Creditors are exposed to customer retention, not to end-market demand.
Basis: 10-K_2025-10-03_vsts-20251003.htm.jsonl, pages 10, 11, 68; 10-Q_2026-07-03_vsts-20260703.htm.jsonl, page 44; calculation R1; calculation R2; calculation S10

Operating profit is thin and is recovering through cost action, not growth. The fiscal 2025 operating margin was 2.36%, and the United States segment margin fell from 11.79% in fiscal 2023 to 6.19% in fiscal 2025 before recovering to 9.24% in the third quarter of 2026 from 6.63% a year earlier, while uniforms revenue fell 7.57% in fiscal 2025 and workplace supplies revenue rose 0.89%.
Basis: 10-K_2025-10-03_vsts-20251003.htm.jsonl, pages 89, 118, 132; 10-Q_2026-07-03_vsts-20260703.htm.jsonl, pages 21, 33, 34; calculation D7; calculation S2; calculation S4; calculation S6; calculation S7; calculation R6; calculation R7

No sponsor or controlling holder is identified, which removes the usual extraction channel but also any identified source of equity support. The shares were distributed to Aramark stockholders in September 2023, holders of record are in the hundreds, and a letter agreement with Corvex Management LP and a director, amended in May 2025, is known only by its title.
Basis: 10-K_2025-10-03_vsts-20251003.htm.jsonl, pages 59, 145, 154

Distribution risk is low today because the lenders hold a lock, but the lock is time-limited. Dividends paid in fiscal 2025 were 239.21% of free cash flow, none has been paid since, and the May 2025 amendment restricts dividends and repurchases until the earlier of a compliant quarter after October 2, 2026 or two consecutive quarters at 4.50x or lower, so the first test needs covenant compliance but not lower leverage.
Basis: 10-K_2025-10-03_vsts-20251003.htm.jsonl, pages 59, 91, 143; 10-Q_2026-07-03_vsts-20260703.htm.jsonl, pages 20, 38; calculation F2

Management continuity is a weakness that the cost plan depends on. Four executive separation agreements are dated in 2025, fiscal 2025 severance rose USD 13.9 million, and the three quarterly reports since the annual report are signed by an interim chief financial officer, whose offer letters are dated December 15, 2025 and June 12, 2026; no permanent appointment is stated, the documents give no reasons for the departures, and no individual is assessed.
Basis: 10-K_2025-10-03_vsts-20251003.htm.jsonl, pages 68, 154, 155; 10-Q_2026-01-02_vsts-20260102.htm.jsonl, page 52; 10-Q_2026-07-03_vsts-20260703.htm.jsonl, page 56

The restructuring plan costs more than first stated and has not yet been shown to pay. The estimate midpoint rose 36.36% from the annual report to the third-quarter report, consulting recognized of USD 23.2 million already exceeds the latest consulting estimate by USD 8.2 million, and USD 31.2 million, 78.00% of the high end, has been recognized in nine months. Management says about USD 30 million of an expected USD 50 million in-year benefit, 60.00%, is realized, which is its own claim.
Basis: 10-K_2025-10-03_vsts-20251003.htm.jsonl, page 67; 10-Q_2026-07-03_vsts-20260703.htm.jsonl, pages 17, 42; EX-99.1_2026-08-11_vstsq3fy26earningsrelease-.htm.jsonl, page 2; calculation P4; calculation P5; calculation P6; calculation P7

Shareholder litigation is an unquantified contingent claim ahead of equity. Two securities class actions are pending, in Georgia and New York, with a Delaware bylaw challenge and derivative suits; the Georgia motion to dismiss was denied on September 30, 2025, the New York motion was granted in part and denied in part on July 31, 2026, and a class certification motion was filed in Georgia on April 10, 2026. The company says it cannot predict outcomes and gives no loss estimate.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-07-03_vsts-20260703.htm.jsonl, pages 28–32

Labor and pension costs are structural. About 59.23% of employees are union-represented under a large number of collective bargaining agreements, multiemployer plan contributions rose 10.68% from fiscal 2023 to fiscal 2025, and some plans are in critical status, so a withdrawal could become a debt-like claim. Finance leases, operating leases and insurance reserves are further claims outside term debt.
Basis: 10-K_2025-10-03_vsts-20251003.htm.jsonl, pages 17, 32, 123; 10-Q_2026-07-03_vsts-20260703.htm.jsonl, pages 6, 15; calculation L1; calculation D2; calculation Z1; calculation Z2; calculation Z3

Creditor recourse rests on guarantees and liens from wholly owned domestic material subsidiaries, so the Canadian business, 8.98% of revenue and 5.49% of segment operating income, may sit outside the package; this is not established because the subsidiary list is not supplied.
Basis: 10-K_2025-10-03_vsts-20251003.htm.jsonl, pages 72, 155; calculation M8; calculation R8

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest case for lower risk is that nothing in the documents shows value extraction or creditor-adverse conduct: there is no sponsor, no acquisition debt, no priming or exchange transaction, the annual audit was unqualified and internal control effective, the revolver was undrawn with USD 294.2 million available, covenants were met at July 3, 2026 and the United States segment margin has recovered to 9.24%. It would win if the class action is dismissed or settled within insurance, finance leadership is made permanent, and the plan delivers its savings at or below the current estimate.
Basis: 10-K_2025-10-03_vsts-20251003.htm.jsonl, pages 84, 87, 148; 10-Q_2026-07-03_vsts-20260703.htm.jsonl, pages 20–21; calculation F7; calculation S6

The strongest case for higher risk is that the lock lifts before leverage is low and the plan overruns. Risk would move to high if the plan estimate rises again, if consulting keeps outrunning its estimate, if class certification is granted without insurance cover, if another executive departure or a large customer loss follows, or if dividends or repurchases resume while leverage is above 4.50x. The proxy statement or the letter agreement could move the view either way by showing who controls votes and board seats.
Basis: 10-K_2025-10-03_vsts-20251003.htm.jsonl, page 59; 10-Q_2026-07-03_vsts-20260703.htm.jsonl, page 29; calculation P5

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

Vestis was created by a spin-off, not a sponsor transaction. Aramark distributed the shares to its stockholders on September 30, 2023, and the company paid about USD 1,457 million in cash to Aramark at the separation, the day the senior secured credit agreement was signed; the documents do not itemise the funding.
Basis: 10-K_2025-10-03_vsts-20251003.htm.jsonl, pages 72, 145; 10-Q_2026-07-03_vsts-20260703.htm.jsonl, page 20

No new term loan tranche has been added since the first refinancing. Term Loan A-1 was refinanced in February 2024 with a USD 800 million Term Loan B-1 due February 2031, a USD 250 million receivables securitization facility was added in August 2024 and its proceeds repaid term loans, and the May 2025 amendment raised the covenant ceiling to 5.25x while restricting dividends, repurchases and receivables facilities.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-04-03_vsts-20260403.htm.jsonl, page 20; 10-Q_2026-07-03_vsts-20260703.htm.jsonl, pages 20, 37; 10-K_2025-10-03_vsts-20251003.htm.jsonl, page 41

Other transactions were small. A 39% equity interest in a Japanese uniform company was sold for USD 36.8 million in October 2024 with the proceeds applied to debt, a substitute insurance collateral facility of about USD 24.8 million replaced bank and surety letters of credit, and no significant acquisitions were made in fiscal 2023, 2024 or 2025.
Basis: 10-K_2025-10-03_vsts-20251003.htm.jsonl, pages 99, 103; 10-Q_2026-07-03_vsts-20260703.htm.jsonl, page 27

- The fiscal fourth quarter ended October 2, 2026, 34 days before the as-of date, and nothing in the documents covers it, so the covenant test of that quarter and any change to the distribution lock are not established.
  Basis: 10-Q_2026-07-03_vsts-20260703.htm.jsonl, page 20; calculation Y1

  Basis: 10-K_2025-10-03_vsts-20251003.htm.jsonl, pages 154–155; 10-Q_2026-01-02_vsts-20260102.htm.jsonl, page 52
- The plan's savings baseline and the reasons for the two cost estimate increases are not disclosed, so the benefit claimed cannot be tested against a base.
  Basis: 10-K_2025-10-03_vsts-20251003.htm.jsonl, page 67; 10-Q_2026-07-03_vsts-20260703.htm.jsonl, page 42; EX-99.1_2026-08-11_vstsq3fy26earningsrelease-.htm.jsonl, page 2

  Basis: 10-K_2025-10-03_vsts-20251003.htm.jsonl, pages 11, 118
- Withdrawal liability amounts for multiemployer plans and the outcome of the agreement that was due to expire on May 22, 2026 are not stated.
  Basis: 10-K_2025-10-03_vsts-20251003.htm.jsonl, page 123
