## Findings

### Bottom line

Orion's creditors depend on a fixed-price, project-based contractor whose cash flow follows backlog conversion and working-capital timing, and the debt-funded 2026 acquisition of J.E. McAmis has raised the cost of an execution miss while the documents name no controlling owner or sponsor.

Marine was 63.93% of FY2025 revenue and Concrete 36.07%, but Concrete drove the 2026 growth and carries 42.08% of nine-month 2026 revenue, so credit outcomes now lean on a data-center-led concrete business alongside Navy-linked marine projects. Board independence and shareholder concentration are not established because the proxy statement is not supplied.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_orn-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, pages 4–8; 10-Q_2026-09-30_orn-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, pages 10–11; calculation CALC-01; calculation CALC-02; calculation CALC-03

### Key findings

The mix is moving toward concrete, which raises dependence on private demand. Concrete revenue rose 36.76% to USD 292,166 thousand in the nine months of 2026 while marine revenue fell 0.80% to USD 402,165 thousand, and private companies supplied 48.08% of nine-month revenue against 45.29% a year earlier; a slowdown in data-center or commercial building would reach the segment now doing most of the growth first.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_orn-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, pages 10–11; 10-K_2025-12-31_orn-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, pages 6–8; calculation CALC-02; calculation CALC-03; calculation CALC-04

Revenue quality is that of a competitive fixed-price bidder, so execution and working capital, not contracted cash flow, drive repayment capacity. Most revenue comes from fixed-price contracts awarded by competitive bidding, contract assets rose by USD 53,122 thousand in nine months to USD 84,205 thousand, and claims and unapproved change orders in contract balances reached USD 17.5 million against USD 7.1 million at the 2025 year-end; remaining performance obligations of USD 645 million convert over about 18 to 24 months in marine and six to twelve months in concrete.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_orn-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, pages 5, 12, 26; EX-99.1_2026-10-27_orn-ex99d1.htm.jsonl, pages 2–3; calculation CALC-05; calculation CALC-12

Navy and joint-venture concentration is falling but still material. The U.S. Navy was 14% of FY2025 contract revenue against 25% in FY2024, and revenue from the Pearl Harbor dry dock subcontract fell to USD 47.4 million in nine months from USD 100.4 million, so replacement work matters; positions on a USD 9 billion Navy contract vehicle and a USD 700 million waterfront vehicle widen access but do not guarantee task orders.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_orn-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, pages 9, 84; 10-Q_2026-09-30_orn-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, pages 10–11; EX-99.1_2026-10-27_orn-ex99d1.htm.jsonl, page 2; calculation CALC-06; calculation CALC-13

The February 2026 acquisition added marine capacity but also claims that behave like debt. Beyond the bank borrowings and the USD 12.0 million subordinated seller note, contingent payments were carried at USD 16,267 thousand at 2026-09-30, goodwill of USD 45,076 thousand equals 26.03% of equity, and measurement-period adjustments have already added about USD 12.3 million to goodwill, so the value paid is not yet fixed.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_orn-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, pages 7–10, 13, 16; 10-K_2025-12-31_orn-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, pages 100–101; calculation CALC-07; calculation CALC-08

Control and governance cannot be assessed beyond board composition. The run names no sponsor, a dispersed base of holders of record including beneficial holders is reported, no controlling shareholder is stated, and vessel-owning entities must meet United States citizenship ownership rules, but ownership concentration, board independence and compensation sit in a proxy statement that is not supplied, and the chief financial officer and one director joined in 2025 while executive employment agreements were amended in September 2026 and a new chief financial officer agreement was signed in October 2026.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_orn-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 12, 16, 43, 60–61; 10-Q_2026-09-30_orn-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, pages 33–34

Financial policy was supportive of creditors until the 2026 acquisition. The 2024 equity offering raised about USD 26.4 million of net proceeds that were used partly to repay bank debt, no dividend or repurchase appears in any cash flow statement or share record, and the 2025 refinancing replaced a facility that carried a 10.70% weighted average rate with a bank facility priced at 2.50% to 3.00% over the reference rate; the 2026 acquisition then reversed the deleveraging with borrowing.
Basis: 10-K_2024-12-31_orn-20241231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 53; 10-K_2025-12-31_orn-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, pages 43, 73–74, 87–88; 10-Q_2026-09-30_orn-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, pages 14–15

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against caution is the order book and end markets: backlog was USD 645 million at 2026-09-30, third-quarter awards were USD 179 million, the company reports GAAP net income of USD 5,473 thousand for the quarter, and no sponsor or shareholder has extracted cash. The view would soften if backlog conversion shows contract assets reversing and claims settling at their booked value, and would harden if a marine project overrun, a data-center slowdown or an unfavourable measurement-period change to the contingent payments emerged.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_orn-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, pages 3, 12, 27; EX-99.1_2026-10-27_orn-ex99d1.htm.jsonl, pages 2–3; calculation CALC-05

### Limitations

- Control and board independence are not established, so the conclusion that no controlling owner or sponsor stands behind the credit would change if a holder of a large stake appears in the proxy statement.
- Stand-alone results of the acquired businesses are not stated, so the contribution of the acquisition to marine revenue and margin cannot be separated from organic change.
- Surety capacity is not quantified after 2024-12-31, so the conclusion that bonding does not limit growth rests on management's qualitative statement.
- Segment operating income appears on two bases, so any margin conclusion by segment holds only on the 2026 basis.
