## Findings

### Bottom line

Debt rose from USD 7,874 thousand at the 2025 year-end to USD 108,768 thousand at 2026-09-30, lifting total debt to last-twelve-month EBITDA from 0.23x to 3.13x, and cash interest cover of 4.10x still reflects interest paid before most of that debt was drawn.

The increase paid for the February 2026 acquisition of J.E. McAmis, which accounts for USD 52,234 thousand of the USD 96,811 thousand rise in net debt, and for cash absorbed by operations: nine-month operating cash flow was an outflow of USD 10,722 thousand and capital expenditure was USD 28,109 thousand. The ratios flatter leverage because finance lease liabilities of USD 16,754 thousand sit outside debt while their costs sit outside EBITDA; counting them would put total debt to EBITDA at 3.62x.

### Key findings

Total debt to reported EBITDA moved from 2.07x at the 2023 year-end to 0.23x at the 2025 year-end as the older term loan was repaid and cash was spent down, and then to 3.13x at 2026-09-30; net of cash it was 0.18x at the 2025 year-end, 1.72x pro forma for the acquisition of 2026-02-03, and 2.97x at 2026-09-30. The UMB bank facility entered in December 2025 supplies USD 83,926 thousand of the debt, the seller note USD 10,388 thousand and lessor-financed and sale-leaseback obligations USD 15,866 thousand, less USD 1,412 thousand of issuance costs.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_orn-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, pages 71–72; 10-Q_2026-09-30_orn-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, pages 2–3; 10-Q_2026-09-30_orn-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, pages 14–15; calculation CALC-03; calculation CALC-06; calculation CALC-11; calculation CALC-14; calculation CALC-15; calculation CALC-16; calculation CALC-17; calculation CALC-19

Operating cash flow was an outflow of USD 10,722 thousand in the nine months of 2026 against an inflow of USD 14,260 thousand a year earlier, because contract assets absorbed USD 52,029 thousand as work ran ahead of billing and payables and contract liabilities also fell, only partly offset by a USD 32,638 thousand release of receivables. Capital expenditure of USD 28,109 thousand and the acquisition outflow of USD 42,871 thousand took investing outflow to USD 70,213 thousand, and a financing inflow of USD 83,321 thousand funded the gap.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_orn-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, pages 4–5; calculation CALC-08

Reported EBITDA for the nine months of 2026 was USD 27,063 thousand against USD 26,381 thousand a year earlier, and issuer-adjusted EBITDA was USD 34,527 thousand against USD 32,276 thousand; add-backs of USD 7,464 thousand, mostly non-cash share-based compensation, lift reported EBITDA by 27.58%. The second quarter of 2026 was the weak point, with EBITDA of USD 5,782 thousand against USD 8,687 thousand a year earlier and a net loss of USD 4,145 thousand, after which the third quarter rebounded to EBITDA of USD 15,615 thousand.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-07-28_orn-20260728xex99d1.htm.jsonl, pages 7–8; EX-99.1_2026-10-27_orn-ex99d1.htm.jsonl, pages 7–8; 10-Q_2026-06-30_orn-20260630x10q.htm.jsonl, page 3; calculation CALC-22; calculation CALC-26

Last-twelve-month cash interest of USD 8,466 thousand gives cover of 4.10x, but it predates most of the new borrowing: reported interest expense in the third quarter of 2026 alone was USD 2,950 thousand against USD 2,120 thousand a year earlier, so cover will fall as the larger debt is carried for a full year.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_orn-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-Q_2026-09-30_orn-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 5; calculation CALC-07

Unrestricted cash was USD 5,671 thousand at 2026-09-30 and has ranged from USD 1,588 thousand to USD 6,254 thousand since the 2025 year-end, so the revolver is the working liquidity: availability was about USD 28.0 million at 2026-09-30 against USD 60.0 million at the 2025 year-end, before the facility was drawn to fund the acquisition and working capital.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_orn-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 14; 10-K_2025-12-31_orn-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, pages 51, 87; 10-Q_2026-03-31_orn-20260331x10q.htm.jsonl, page 13; 10-Q_2026-06-30_orn-20260630x10q.htm.jsonl, page 14

The statements tie: each balance sheet balances, each cash flow statement adds to its net change and rolls from opening to closing cash, and the issuer's own EBITDA reconciliations add up in every period. Fiscal-year EBITDA is calculated because the issuer states none, the annual and 2026 segment operating income sit on different bases, and the three quarters add to the nine-month revenue and EBITDA exactly.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest case against reading 3.13x as stretched is that cash interest cover is 4.10x, about USD 28.0 million of revolver availability remains, backlog is USD 645 million, and the company guides full-year adjusted EBITDA to USD 50 million to USD 54 million against USD 34,527 thousand earned in nine months, which would lower leverage if delivered. The view would improve if the fourth quarter delivered that guided EBITDA with working capital reversing, and would worsen if a quarter like the second quarter of 2026 recurred while the facility stays fully drawn.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-10-27_orn-ex99d1.htm.jsonl, page 2; EX-99.1_2026-10-27_orn-ex99d1.htm.jsonl, page 11; 10-Q_2026-09-30_orn-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 14; calculation CALC-06; calculation CALC-15

### Limitations

Fiscal-year EBITDA is calculated from the statements and does not net interest income, so it can differ slightly from the issuer definition used for the 2025 and 2026 interim periods, and the last-twelve-month figures inherit that mix.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_orn-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 72

The acquired businesses contribute EBITDA only from 2026-02-03 and their stand-alone EBITDA is not stated, so last-twelve-month leverage is higher than a full-year pro forma figure would be.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_orn-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, pages 7–8

No repayment schedule by year is disclosed for the term loans, so no maturity wall is drawn and contractual principal is not stated.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_orn-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, pages 14–15

Interim statements are unaudited and no discrete third-quarter cash flow is printed, so cash flow metrics use year-to-date figures.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_orn-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, pages 4–5
