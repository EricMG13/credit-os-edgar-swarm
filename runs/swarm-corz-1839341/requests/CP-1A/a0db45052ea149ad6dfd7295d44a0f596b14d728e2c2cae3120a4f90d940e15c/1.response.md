## Findings

### Bottom line

Core Scientific has become a single-customer data-center landlord, so creditors depend on CoreWeave paying and on contracted capacity being built and funded on time; no sponsor or controlling shareholder is named in the filings. One customer supplied 93% of third-quarter 2026 revenue and 84% of nine-month revenue.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_corz.htm.jsonl, page 43; 10-Q_2026-09-30_corz.htm.jsonl, page 49; 10-K_2025-12-31_corz.htm.jsonl, page 74

The AMD and Neocloud leases signed in July 2026 are the only contracted diversification, and none had commenced by 2026-09-30. About $4.4bn of build commitments are only partly funded by customers, and an accounting control weakness that forced a restatement is still open.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_corz.htm.jsonl, page 21; 10-Q_2026-09-30_corz.htm.jsonl, page 32; 10-Q_2026-09-30_corz.htm.jsonl, page 73

### Key findings

Customer concentration is the dominant business risk, and it is still rising. Colocation was 84% of nine-month 2026 revenue against 14% a year earlier, and CoreWeave is the only colocation customer. CoreWeave has assigned the Dalton 1 and Denton North license agreements to a special purpose subsidiary, which the company understands received customer commitments sufficient for investment-grade debt, while CoreWeave remains a primary obligor; the company still says its ability to enforce recourse would depend on CoreWeave's financial condition at the time of a default.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-03-31_corz.htm.jsonl, page 39; 10-Q_2026-09-30_corz.htm.jsonl, page 43; 10-Q_2026-09-30_corz.htm.jsonl, page 49; calculation M10; calculation M7

Delivery, not demand, is the nearer-term risk. Billing capacity covers 82.20% of the CoreWeave contract, the rest is still in construction, and the AMD and Neocloud leases would take contracted capacity to 1.90x the CoreWeave contract once built from 2027. Build commitments of $4.4bn are substantially all due within 24 months, and management expects project-level bond financing, warrant exercise proceeds and the release of restricted cash to cover what customers do not fund.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_corz.htm.jsonl, page 32; 10-Q_2026-09-30_corz.htm.jsonl, page 46; 10-Q_2026-09-30_corz.htm.jsonl, page 48; 10-Q_2026-09-30_corz.htm.jsonl, page 66; calculation B1; calculation B3

Diversification is real but partly paid for in equity and unproven on terms. AMD signed directly for 71.27% of the new capacity and has credit support agreements on the Neocloud leases that let it cure Neocloud defaults but do not oblige it to. AMD also received a warrant over 9.31% of current shares, valued at $296.4 million on issue and booked as a lease incentive that reduces recognized revenue over the lease terms, together with a right to reserve a further large block of capacity until 2028-12-28; the rent and termination rights of these leases are not disclosed.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_corz.htm.jsonl, page 38; 10-Q_2026-06-30_corz.htm.jsonl, page 39; 10-Q_2026-09-30_corz.htm.jsonl, page 21; 10-Q_2026-09-30_corz.htm.jsonl, page 27; calculation B9; calculation B16

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest case against caution is contract quality and early delivery. Current billing capacity supports, by management's own measure, over $700 million of annual colocation revenue, third-quarter Adjusted EBITDA reached $133.6 million at a 57% margin, and CoreWeave has prepaid more than $570 million of deferred revenue, funded $180.9 million of 2026 capital spending and signed contracts of ten years or more. Contracted lease payments still to be received exceed $7bn on leases already commenced, with a further $1.8bn on CoreWeave leases not yet commenced. The company also holds more than $800 million of unrestricted cash, a $347.9 million reserve account and $600 million of new undrawn facilities, which gives time to deliver the remaining capacity.
Basis: EX99.1_2026-09-30_corz_q32026corescientificinc-ea-htm.htm.jsonl, page 1; 10-Q_2026-09-30_corz.htm.jsonl, page 5; 10-Q_2026-09-30_corz.htm.jsonl, page 21; 10-Q_2026-09-30_corz.htm.jsonl, page 23; 10-Q_2026-09-30_corz.htm.jsonl, page 25; 10-Q_2026-09-30_corz.htm.jsonl, page 46; 10-Q_2026-09-30_corz.htm.jsonl, page 48; 10-Q_2026-09-30_corz.htm.jsonl, page 67; calculation B18

The view would improve if the AMD and Neocloud leases commence on schedule from 2027 with their terms and credit support disclosed, if the project-level bond financing is completed, and if CoreWeave's share of revenue falls as the new leases bill. It would worsen on slippage in converting contracted capacity to billing, a late payment by or financial deterioration of CoreWeave, a further restatement, the Tranche 1 warrants lapsing unexercised, or a share price low enough to close the revolver.

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

The company says the net proceeds funded a debt service reserve account and a distribution to the parent, which repaid and ended a $1.0bn 364-day term loan drawn in March 2026 in two steps of $500.0 million, the second after an amendment on 2026-03-18; that loan was secured on substantially all assets of the parent and its guarantors. $347.9 million remained in the reserve account on 2026-09-30. The notes are guaranteed by five subsidiaries that were the issuer's only subsidiaries at issue, secured by first-priority liens on substantially all assets of the issuer and those guarantors and on the issuer's equity, and covered by an uncapped completion guarantee from Core Scientific, Inc. for specified data-center projects. Principal amortization begins only after rent commencement and the end of revenue credits to the tenant, at an initial 11.50% of original principal a year, and a first coupon was due on 2026-11-15.
