### Key findings

Ownership is dispersed and no controlling shareholder or sponsor is identified, so there is no sponsor support or extraction to underwrite and creditor protection rests on the debt agreements and on management's policy. The spin-off distributed all shares to BorgWarner's stockholders on 3 July 2023, and BorgWarner has been a counterparty rather than an owner since. Beneficial-ownership and board-independence disclosure sits in a proxy statement that is not supplied, so a large holder cannot be ruled out.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest case against Medium is that Phinia looks conservatively run: no related-party transactions since the spin-off, no exchange, priming or asset transfer that moved value away from creditors in any supplied filing, unqualified audit opinions, and a third quarter in which management says capital returned was 104% of adjusted free cash flow, close to what the business generated. On the adjusted measure the nine-month excess was only USD 9 million, so Low would be defensible. Medium stays because the net leverage covenant ceiling was raised from 3.00x to 3.25x in 2024 when the notes replaced the term loans, a debt-assisted acquisition came while the buyback programme was running, and the documents that would show what the company may pay out or borrow are absent.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_phin-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 119; 10-K_2025-12-31_phin-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 70; EX-99.1_2026-10-29_q320268kex991pressrelease.htm.jsonl, page 1; 10-K_2025-12-31_phin-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 101; calculation K11; calculation K18

The view would move to Low if the executed agreements show tight restricted-payment baskets and a leverage covenant with real headroom after stoba. It would move to High if buybacks continue above free cash flow while the revolver stays drawn, or if the company used unrestricted-subsidiary, priming or exchange mechanics, none of which any supplied source reports.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_phin-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 15; 10-Q_2026-09-30_phin-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 41

### Limitations

- The proxy statement is not supplied, so board independence, committee roles, beneficial owners and pay design are not established; the Medium rating therefore rests on disclosed conduct only, and an independent board or a named large holder would lower or raise it.
- The executed credit agreement and indentures are not supplied, so legal capacity for restricted payments, investments and liens is not established; the separation of capacity from willingness stays open and loose baskets would raise the rating.
- Stoba's own debt, earnings and cash are not stated, so the acquisition-appetite judgement cannot be sized beyond its USD 136 million price and may be too benign if stoba carries debt.
- The reported-line comparison treats USD 29 million of separation-related payments in the nine months of 2026 as ordinary outflows; on the issuer's adjusted measure the gap shrinks to USD 9 million, so the returns-driven part of the Medium rating could fall away if returns settle at or below adjusted free cash flow.
- The rating statement dates from the February 2026 annual report and no agency report is supplied, so the view of financial policy cannot be checked against a current agency opinion.

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

Phinia became independent on 3 July 2023, when BorgWarner distributed all of its shares to BorgWarner holders, one for every five held on 23 June 2023, and the company entered a USD 1,225 million credit agreement the same day. That agreement combined a USD 500 million revolver, a USD 300 million Term Loan A and a USD 425 million Term Loan B, and matures on 3 July 2028.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_phin-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 7; 10-K_2025-12-31_phin-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 101

The only new debt issues in the supplied filings are the two 2024 notes. On 4 April 2024 the company issued USD 525 million of senior secured notes due 2029, which are guaranteed by certain domestic restricted subsidiaries and secured by first-priority liens on substantially all assets, ranking equally with the credit facilities, and used the proceeds to repay the Term Loan B and revolver borrowings. On 17 September 2024 it issued USD 450 million of senior unsecured notes due 2032 and repaid the Term Loan A. The same two dates brought Amendment No. 1, which raised the net leverage covenant from 3.00x to 3.25x with a step-up to 3.75x after a qualifying acquisition, and Amendment No. 2, which cut the revolver margin.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_phin-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 102; 10-K_2025-12-31_phin-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 102; 10-K_2025-12-31_phin-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 103; 10-K_2025-12-31_phin-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 101; calculation K16
