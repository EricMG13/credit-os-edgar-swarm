## Findings

### Bottom line

Sealed Air is a diversified packaging supplier that changed hands on April 9, 2026, and for creditors the open question is the new owner's capital structure, which the sources do not disclose, rather than the business itself.
Basis: STATUS_15-12G_2026-04-20_see.htm.jsonl, page 1; Source readiness (CP-0)

The company has two segments, Food at 67.1% of 2025 net sales and Protective at 32.9%, and no customer above 10% of segment revenue, so customer concentration is low. The CD&R-affiliated buyer agreed a total purchase price of USD 10.3 billion, 9.08x FY2025 Adjusted EBITDA, funded in part by a debt commitment whose terms are not given. Public reporting ended on April 20, 2026, so later governance and financing facts are not established.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_sealed-air-corp.htm.jsonl, pages 7, 8, 14, 137; calculation C1

### Key findings

- The business is diversified but the mix is shifting toward Food: Food net sales rose by USD 75.0 million to USD 3,594.7 million between 2023 and 2025 at a 23.1% Adjusted EBITDA margin, while Protective fell by USD 204.1 million, or 10.36%, to USD 1,765.1 million at 17.6%. Food is the larger and higher-margin segment, so Protective softness matters less to creditors than it would if the segments were equal.
  Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_sealed-air-corp.htm.jsonl, pages 77, 137; calculation C5; calculation C6; calculation C7; calculation C8
- Concentration is low by customer and moderate by channel: no customer or affiliated group exceeded 10% of segment revenue in 2025, and distributors sold about 60% of Protective sales, so the exposure sits with the distribution channel rather than any single account. 51.36% of net sales came from the U.S. and 49% from outside it.
  Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_sealed-air-corp.htm.jsonl, pages 8, 13, 14, 139; calculation C9
- Margins depend on resin cost pass-through and volume: raw materials are about one-third of cost of sales, which is 70.21% of net sales, and in 2025 price was flat (USD -0.1 million) while volume fell USD 65.4 million, whereas in 2024 price fell USD 118.8 million and volume rose USD 34.7 million, so earnings are exposed to a repeat of 2024 pricing pressure.
  Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_sealed-air-corp.htm.jsonl, pages 14, 66, 109; calculation C12
- The purchase price is high against earnings and its funding is undisclosed: USD 10.3 billion is 9.08x FY2025 Adjusted EBITDA and implies USD 6,211.6 million for the equity at the February 2026 share count, leaving USD 4,088.4 million that the sources do not allocate between assumed debt, refinanced debt and other items. The debt commitment letter is referred to without terms, so post-closing leverage, security and ranking are not established.
  Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_sealed-air-corp.htm.jsonl, pages 7, 20, 233; EX-99.1_2025-12-31_see.htm.jsonl, page 1; calculation C1; calculation C2; calculation C3
- Control moved from a widely held public shareholder base, where the largest holders had 10.9%, 10.1% and 9.2%, to a single owner, and public reporting ended with the Form 15; the annual report states no board, fee, distribution or financial-policy terms for the new owner, so creditor alignment under sponsor ownership is not established.
  Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_sealed-air-corp.htm.jsonl, pages 234, 236; STATUS_15-12G_2026-04-20_see.htm.jsonl, page 1
- Senior leadership turned over heavily around the sale: the chief executive who started on July 1, 2024 left on February 12, 2025, 226 days later, and the chief financial officer succeeded him; the chief operating officer left on April 1, 2025, a new chief financial officer started on August 25, 2025 and the Food president changed on November 20 and December 8, 2025. This raises execution risk through the change of ownership but is not evidence of creditor-adverse conduct.
  Basis: 10-K_2024-12-31_sealed-air-corp.htm.jsonl, pages 180, 186; 10-K_2025-12-31_sealed-air-corp.htm.jsonl, pages 52, 199–200, 203; calculation C11
- Pre-sale financial policy was creditor-aligned on the evidence: dividends held at USD 0.80 per share a year, USD 119.2 million paid in 2025, which is 18.98% of operating cash flow, no share repurchases are printed for 2024 or 2025, long-term debt repayments exceeded new long-term borrowing by USD 367.6 million in 2025, and the October 2025 credit agreement extended maturities to 2030, although it also loosened covenants to allow more debt.
  Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_sealed-air-corp.htm.jsonl, pages 111–112, 150; calculation C13; calculation C15

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence for a more comfortable view is that the filings document no extraction, creditor-adverse transaction or distribution beyond the regular dividend, the customer base is diversified, and the buyer is described by management as an investor with industrial and packaging experience, which is a management characterization and not evidence of how it will treat creditors. The view would improve if the financing documents show a large equity contribution and no priming or disclosure restrictions, and it would worsen if they show debt-funded distributions or reduced reporting to creditors.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_sealed-air-corp.htm.jsonl, pages 7, 21, 150

### Limitations

- The acquisition financing is not described, so conclusions about post-closing leverage, security, ranking and the equity cheque are not established.
- Sponsor behavior can only be assessed from actions toward this issuer, and the filings document only the purchase, so the sponsor and governance risk level is not established, which is different from low.
- Governance facts for the board, officers and holders are as of early 2026 before closing; the post-closing board, management and creditor reporting are not established.
- Segment margins are the issuer's own Adjusted EBITDA measures, which exclude special items, so they are not comparable with reported operating profit.

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

On November 16, 2025 Sealed Air agreed to be acquired by Sword Purchaser, LLC, a Delaware company affiliated with CD&R, through the merger of Sword Merger Sub, Inc. into the company, for a total purchase price of USD 10.3 billion in cash and USD 42.15 per share. Stockholders adopted the agreement on February 25, 2026, the antitrust waiting period ended early on December 23, 2025, and the merger completed on April 9, 2026, 144 days after signing, leaving the company a wholly owned subsidiary of Parent. The annual report calls the figure a total purchase price and the results release calls it an enterprise value; both state USD 10.3 billion.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_sealed-air-corp.htm.jsonl, pages 7, 8, 19; EX-99.1_2025-12-31_see.htm.jsonl, page 1; STATUS_15-12G_2026-04-20_see.htm.jsonl, page 1; calculation C10

The financing of the acquisition is not described: the annual report names a Debt Commitment Letter and warns that Parent could fail to obtain the debt financing on its terms, but gives no amount, ranking, security or pricing, and no equity contribution is stated. A termination fee of USD 205.1 million, 1.99% of the purchase price, was payable by the company in stated circumstances. While the merger was pending the company could not issue securities, incur certain debt, declare certain dividends or make certain capital expenditures without Parent's approval.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_sealed-air-corp.htm.jsonl, pages 7, 20, 21; calculation C4

New debt before the acquisition was raised to buy Liquibox and to refinance shorter notes: a USD 650.0 million incremental term loan used on February 1, 2023 and USD 775.0 million of 6.125% notes due 2028 issued on January 31, 2023 funded Liquibox with cash on hand and repurchased the 4.500% notes due 2023, USD 425.0 million of 7.250% notes due 2031 on November 20, 2023 repurchased the 5.125% notes due 2024, and USD 400.0 million of 6.500% notes due 2032 on June 28, 2024 repurchased the 5.500% notes due 2025. On October 31, 2025 an amended and restated credit agreement extended maturities to October 2030, added a USD 600 million delayed draw term loan available to October 15, 2026 and relaxed certain covenants. No debt issued after December 31, 2025 is described in the sources.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_sealed-air-corp.htm.jsonl, pages 150–153
