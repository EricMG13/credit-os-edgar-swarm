## Findings

### Bottom line

The five supplied filings support the requested financial, business and fundamental credit work, so creditors can rely on them for the issuer's statements, debt and liquidity, but not for covenant headroom, ratings or market pricing.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-10-03_bgs_bgs-20261003x10q.htm.jsonl, pages 5–9; 10-Q_2026-10-03_bgs_bgs-20261003x10q.htm.jsonl, pages 19–21

The latest filing, for the quarter ended 3 October 2026, was filed on 10 November 2026, eight days before the as-of date, and already reflects the refinancing completed in the third quarter, in which the 2031 notes issued in June replaced the 2027 notes. The filings print the covenant levels and a compliance statement but not the measured ratios, and the credit agreement, the indentures and any ratings are absent. Governance and ownership detail sits in a proxy statement that was not supplied. Any twelve-month window built from these filings spans 53 weeks, because the fourth quarter of 2025 had 14 weeks.
