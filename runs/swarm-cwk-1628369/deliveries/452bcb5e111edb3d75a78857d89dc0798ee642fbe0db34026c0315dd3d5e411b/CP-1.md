## Findings

### Bottom line

Leverage has improved but still depends on issuer add-backs: net debt was 2.96x issuer-adjusted EBITDA at September 30, 2026, down from 4.35x at end-2023, yet 4.63x on unadjusted EBITDA, so creditors need those add-backs to recur.
Basis: calculation C8; calculation C1; calculation C4

Liquidity is lower than at year-end but exceeds the USD 835.9 million due in 2030, the nearest maturity once the remaining 2028 secured notes were redeemed on October 21, 2026, after the balance sheet date, with an effect on net debt that is not established. Cash fell to USD 489.0 million from USD 784.2 million while the USD 1,000.0 million revolver stayed undrawn, and cash interest cover is 3.54x on adjusted and 2.26x on unadjusted EBITDA.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_cwk.htm.jsonl, pages 22–24; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cwk.htm.jsonl, page 38; EX99.1_2026-09-30_cwk_exhibit991-october212026re-htm.htm.jsonl, page 2; calculation C6; calculation C8; calculation C10

### Credit analysis

Gross debt fell at every balance sheet date while adjusted EBITDA grew, so adjusted net leverage fell from 4.35x at end-2023 to 2.96x at September 30, 2026; on unadjusted EBITDA the improvement is smaller and uneven, with net leverage of 6.25x, 4.26x, 4.52x and 4.63x over the same four dates, because a non-cash USD 177.0 million impairment of an equity-method investment in 2025 and other add-backs sit between the two measures. Both sides of the ratio moved: net debt fell by USD 358.6 million between end-2023 and September 30, 2026 and adjusted EBITDA rose by USD 147.2 million from 2023 to the latest twelve months, with USD 300.0 million of debt prepayments in 2025 and net repayments of USD 151.7 million in the nine months of 2026 behind the debt side.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_cwk.htm.jsonl, pages 80–82; 10-K_2025-12-31_cwk.htm.jsonl, page 112; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cwk.htm.jsonl, page 6; calculation C2; calculation C3; calculation C8; calculation C11

Cash interest cover is thin on unadjusted EBITDA and comfortable only on adjusted EBITDA, so the gap between the two measures decides how much cushion creditors have: cover was 1.70x, 2.00x and 2.01x on unadjusted EBITDA for 2023 to 2025 and 2.26x for the latest twelve months, against 2.44x, 2.21x, 3.03x and 3.54x on adjusted EBITDA. Cash interest paid was USD 202.4 million over the latest twelve months against USD 263.9 million in 2024, helped by repeated repricing of the term loans and by principal prepayments.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_cwk.htm.jsonl, page 136; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cwk.htm.jsonl, page 38; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cwk.htm.jsonl, page 66; calculation C4; calculation C8

Operating cash flow is seasonal and was weak in the nine months, so cash fell by more than debt and net debt rose in 2026: operating cash flow of USD 24.4 million, against USD 83.1 million a year earlier, did not cover capital spending of USD 58.5 million and net debt repayments of USD 151.7 million, with outflows in receivables, contract assets and other non-current assets, and free cash flow was negative USD 34.1 million for the nine months but positive USD 200.2 million for the latest twelve months. The company states that its operating cash flow is lowest in the first quarter and greatest in the fourth, so net debt at September 30 should be read against that pattern and not as a year-end level.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_cwk.htm.jsonl, pages 5–6; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cwk.htm.jsonl, page 66; EX99.1_2026-09-30_cwk_cwk-ex991q32026-htm.htm.jsonl, page 20; calculation C4; calculation C9; calculation C11

Liquidity of USD 1,489.0 million at September 30, 2026, made of USD 489.0 million of cash and the undrawn USD 1,000.0 million revolver, compares with USD 1,784.2 million at year-end. The receivables securitization adds unused funding capacity of USD 225.0 million that is not counted here because the receivables are sold off the balance sheet, and letters of credit outstanding under the revolver at September 30 are not stated, against USD 6.6 million at year-end.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_cwk.htm.jsonl, page 67; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cwk.htm.jsonl, page 38; 10-K_2025-12-31_cwk.htm.jsonl, page 112; calculation C10

Reported EBITDA is not stated by the issuer and had to be built from net income, taxes, net interest and depreciation and amortization, so it carries equity-method earnings and other income and expense that the issuer's own measure removes; the 2025 figure absorbs a USD 168.3 million loss from equity-method investments. Creditors should treat unadjusted and adjusted leverage as a range, not as alternatives to choose between.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_cwk.htm.jsonl, pages 80–81; 10-K_2025-12-31_cwk.htm.jsonl, page 101; calculation C1; calculation C7

Three debt measures are in use and none should be mixed with another: balance sheet debt including finance leases of USD 2,609.3 million, the issuer's presentation total of USD 2,553.9 million and contractual principal of term loans and notes of USD 2,585.9 million. The covenant is a springing net leverage test of 5.00x that applies only when revolver borrowings exceed a threshold the filings do not state, and its definition sits in an agreement that is not supplied, so covenant headroom is not inferred.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_cwk.htm.jsonl, pages 21–24; IR_Third-Quarter-2026_presentation.pdf, page 14; calculation C6; calculation C11

The most material gaps are the unstated rank of the term loans against the notes, the missing maturity ladder at September 30, 2026 (the ladder drawn here uses final maturity years and leaves scheduled quarterly amortization in the final year) and the unstated price of the October 21, 2026 redemption; each limits one conclusion but none reverses the direction of the leverage trend.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_cwk.htm.jsonl, pages 22–24; EX99.1_2026-09-30_cwk_exhibit991-october212026re-htm.htm.jsonl, page 2; calculation C6

## Gaps

- The filings print a dash, meaning nil, for several lines, which are stated as not supplied rather than zero: restructuring, impairment and related charges and impairment charges in the nine months of 2026; loss on debt extinguishment in 2024; impairment of equity method investment in 2023 and 2024; loss on disposition of business in 2025; acquisition of business in 2023, 2024 and the nine months of 2026; proceeds from disposition in 2023; proceeds from borrowings and debt issuance costs in 2024, 2025 and the nine months of 2025. This limits no ratio. Severity: Low. Action: none unless a later filing states an amount.
- Lines of the annual cash flow statement that the nine-month statements do not present (impairment of equity method investment, loss on disposition of business and proceeds from disposition of business or other investments) are not supplied for the nine-month columns. This limits comparison of the nine months with the full year only. Severity: Low. Action: none.
- The issuer states no unadjusted EBITDA, so reported EBITDA for every period is derived from net income, taxes, net interest and depreciation and amortization, and for the latest twelve months it is a built figure; it includes a USD 168.3 million equity-method loss in 2025. This limits unadjusted leverage and cover in every period. Severity: Medium. Action: read it beside the issuer-adjusted measure.
- No discrete fourth quarter of 2025 and no 2025 quarter-end balance sheets are supplied, and the third quarter report prints only the prior year-end balance sheet, so trends between year-ends cannot be drawn from statements. This limits intra-year leverage trend. Severity: Medium. Action: derive the fourth quarter from the full year less nine months and label it derived.
- No maturity ladder is printed at September 30, 2026, so the debt maturities table uses final maturity years and leaves the 0.25% quarterly term loan amortization in the final year; the ladder in the 2025 annual report predates the June 2026 refinancing. This limits near-term maturity conclusions. Severity: Medium. Action: obtain the schedule of maturities from the next annual report.
- The sources do not state how the term loans rank against the senior secured notes, so no facility is placed on the debt stack and ranking is not supplied for any instrument. This limits any priority conclusion. Severity: Medium. Action: obtain the executed security and intercreditor documents.
- The price of the October 21, 2026 redemption, any premium, accrued interest and the funding source are not stated, so the effect on net debt and pro forma leverage is not established. This limits the post-balance-sheet adjustment only. Severity: Medium. Action: obtain the redemption notice.
- No borrower-group, guarantor-group or restricted-group financial statements are supplied, only consolidated statements and a parent-only schedule that shows no debt at the parent. This limits structural analysis of which entity owes the debt. Severity: Medium. Action: obtain guarantor disclosures.
- The covenant is a springing net leverage test of 5.00x tested only when revolver borrowings exceed a threshold the filings do not state, defined in a credit agreement that is not supplied; headroom is therefore not calculable and none is inferred. Severity: High. Action: obtain the credit agreement.
- Revolver letters of credit outstanding at September 30, 2026 are not stated in the third quarter report; the year-end 2025 figure is USD 6.6 million and the revolver capacity for letters of credit is the lesser of USD 220.0 million and undrawn capacity. This limits precision of undrawn availability only. Severity: Low. Action: use the stated undrawn revolver and label the letters of credit as not supplied.
- The receivables securitization is off balance sheet, with USD 75.0 million of capital outstanding at September 30, 2026 against USD 120.0 million at year-end 2025, and is excluded from debt and from liquidity. This limits comparability with issuers that carry such funding as debt. Severity: Medium. Action: carry it as a named funding item beside debt.
- Segment Adjusted EBITDA before 2026 and from 2026 sit on different corporate cost allocations, and the issuer no longer reports service line fee revenue or its Adjusted EBITDA margin, so segment margins cannot be traced across the change. This limits segment trend conclusions. Severity: Medium. Action: keep the two series apart.
- Annual cash interest paid is taken from a supplemental note because the cash flow statement does not print it, and cash paid can differ from interest expense net of interest income (USD 216.6 million against USD 216.2 million in 2025). This limits cover precision slightly. Severity: Low. Action: none.
- Instrument terms not stated in the sources read are marked not supplied: security and terms for finance lease liabilities and short-term borrowings, the revolver floor, and annual amortization as an amount. This limits the instrument register only. Severity: Low. Action: obtain the credit agreement.
