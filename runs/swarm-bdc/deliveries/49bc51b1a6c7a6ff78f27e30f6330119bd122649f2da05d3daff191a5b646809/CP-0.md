## Findings

### Bottom line

The seven supplied filings and releases are enough for a credit snapshot of Belden at year-end 2025 and through the third quarter of 2026, but creditors cannot verify covenant terms, security or ratings because no credit agreement, indenture or rating report was supplied.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_bdc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 114; 10-Q_2026-09-27_bdc-20260927.htm.jsonl, pages 17–20

The year-end 2025 balance sheet is no guide to current debt: the Ruckus Networks acquisition closed on July 1, 2026 and was funded by a new term loan, purchase accounting is still preliminary and no company-stated net leverage for the enlarged group was supplied. Work on debt, liquidity and earnings can proceed from the filings, while covenant, recovery and security conclusions cannot be drawn until the agreements are supplied.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-27_bdc-20260927.htm.jsonl, pages 11–13; 10-Q_2026-09-27_bdc-20260927.htm.jsonl, pages 17–20; EX-99.1_2026-10-29_companynewsreleasedatedoct.htm.jsonl, page 7
