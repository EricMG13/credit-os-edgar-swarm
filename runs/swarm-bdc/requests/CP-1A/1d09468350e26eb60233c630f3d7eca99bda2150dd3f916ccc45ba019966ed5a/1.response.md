## Findings

### Bottom line

Belden is a listed manufacturer with no sponsor or controlling holder named in its filings, so creditors rely on management's own financial policy, and the filings after the debt-funded Ruckus acquisition no longer state the net leverage goal of about 1.5x.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_bdc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 37; 10-Q_2026-03-29_bdc-20260329.htm.jsonl, page 16; 10-Q_2026-06-28_bdc-20260628.htm.jsonl, page 19; 10-Q_2026-09-27_bdc-20260927.htm.jsonl, page 23

The operating risk sits in a distributor-led channel, where the ten largest customers, eight of them distributors, were 47% of 2025 revenue, and in a newly acquired Ruckus business whose purchase accounting is still preliminary. Management funded the acquisition with a USD 1,850.0 million term loan and has not repurchased shares since the first quarter, which is consistent with debt reduction but is not a stated policy.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_bdc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 106; 10-Q_2026-09-27_bdc-20260927.htm.jsonl, pages 11, 12; 10-Q_2026-09-27_bdc-20260927.htm.jsonl, page 20; 10-Q_2026-09-27_bdc-20260927.htm.jsonl, page 22

### Key findings

- Sponsor risk is absent but financial-policy risk has risen. The filings name no sponsor, fund or controlling holder, so there is no documented extraction pathway; the exposure is instead management's willingness to hold the balance sheet after a debt-funded purchase, and the documents give no replacement for the 1.5x net leverage goal that the annual report and the first-quarter report printed and the later reports omit.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_bdc-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 35, 37; 10-Q_2026-03-29_bdc-20260329.htm.jsonl, page 16; 10-Q_2026-06-28_bdc-20260628.htm.jsonl, page 19; 10-Q_2026-09-27_bdc-20260927.htm.jsonl, page 23
- The Ruckus acquisition changes the credit more than any other fact. Belden agreed it on 2026-04-29 for about USD 1.85 billion, closed on 2026-07-01, paid USD 1,869.6 million net of cash acquired and funded it with the term loan, so the new loan alone is larger than all the senior subordinated notes outstanding at the start of the year, while the allocation of the price, including in-process research and development, is unfinished, the business lost USD 63.1 million before tax in the quarter and the internal control assessment excludes it.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-03-29_bdc-20260329.htm.jsonl, page 16; EX-99.1_2026-04-30_companynewsreleasedatedmar.htm.jsonl, page 1; 10-Q_2026-09-27_bdc-20260927.htm.jsonl, pages 11, 12, 13, 17, 35
- Repurchases were large against free cash flow before the deal and have paused since. Cash paid under the repurchase program was 89.36% of 2025 free cash flow as the issuer defines it, dividends stayed at USD 0.05 per share a quarter, and no shares were repurchased in the second or third quarter of 2026 with USD 115.0 million of authorization remaining.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_bdc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 60; 10-Q_2026-09-27_bdc-20260927.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-Q_2026-09-27_bdc-20260927.htm.jsonl, page 22; EX-99.1_2026-10-29_companynewsreleasedatedoct.htm.jsonl, page 6; calculation C08
- Revenue is concentrated in channel partners and commodity-linked pricing. The largest distributor was 14% of 2025 revenue, copper price changes reach cost of sales about two months late, and tariff refunds of USD 17.9 million on 2025 transactions reduced the nine-month 2026 cost of sales, so reported margins carry one-offs that need separating.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_bdc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 24; 10-K_2025-12-31_bdc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 10; 10-Q_2026-09-27_bdc-20260927.htm.jsonl, page 25
- The Ruckus balance sheet brings a funding float that is not debt. Deferred revenue rose to USD 250.61 million from USD 49.73 million at the start of 2026, USD 201.68 million of it acquired, so part of the cash flowing through the combined company is customer prepayment for services still to be delivered.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-27_bdc-20260927.htm.jsonl, page 10; calculation C11

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a cautious reading is demand and discipline: orders were a record USD 1,017 million in the third quarter, up 16% organically, with a book-to-bill of 1.05x; backlog grew before the deal; the Revolver was repaid within the quarter it was drawn; and earlier acquisitions were funded with cash on hand. A removed leverage goal may reflect wording only. A stated post-deal leverage target, a dated deleveraging plan or a proxy statement showing incentives tied to net debt would move the view toward lower governance risk; a resumed buyback or a larger dividend before leverage falls would move it the other way.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-10-29_companynewsreleasedatedoct.htm.jsonl, page 1; 10-K_2025-12-31_bdc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 10; 10-Q_2026-09-27_bdc-20260927.htm.jsonl, page 18; 10-K_2025-12-31_bdc-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 77, 80

### Limitations

- The proxy statement is not supplied, so ownership above 5%, board independence, related-party dealings and incentive metrics are not established, and the view that no controlling holder exists rests on what the filings omit.
- The executed credit agreements are not supplied, so legal capacity for distributions, debt and asset transfers is not assessed and capacity cannot be separated from willingness, which holds the governance level at medium rather than low.
- No Ruckus synergy case, earnings before one-off items or customer data is supplied, so how much of the operating model now depends on Ruckus is shown only by its USD 167.8 million of third-quarter revenue, a USD 63.1 million loss before tax and pro forma revenue and net income.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_bdc-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 112, 113; 10-K_2025-12-31_bdc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 114; 10-Q_2026-09-27_bdc-20260927.htm.jsonl, page 13

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

The new debt serves one purpose: Belden borrowed a USD 1,850.0 million Term Loan B on 2026-07-01 to pay about USD 1,869.6 million for Ruckus Networks, a deal it announced with first-quarter results on 2026-04-30 at about USD 1.85 billion from Vistance Networks. The loan is floating-rate at term SOFR plus 2.25%, amortizes 0.25% a quarter and matures on 2033-07-01, so creditors hold a much larger and more rate-sensitive structure than the year-end 2025 one.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-04-30_companynewsreleasedatedmar.htm.jsonl, page 1; 10-Q_2026-03-29_bdc-20260329.htm.jsonl, page 16; 10-Q_2026-09-27_bdc-20260927.htm.jsonl, page 11; 10-Q_2026-09-27_bdc-20260927.htm.jsonl, page 20

The earlier 2026 refinancing was a maturity extension rather than new money: a EUR 450 million issue of senior subordinated notes due 2033 on 2026-01-28 repaid the EUR 450.0 million notes due 2027 on 2026-02-11, so the nearest note maturity moved from 2027 to 2028. The 2033 notes rank equally with the 2028 and 2031 notes and below senior debt including the Revolver; the Revolver was refinanced on 2025-07-18 to a USD 400.0 million commitment maturing 2030-07-18.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_bdc-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 92, 93; 10-Q_2026-09-27_bdc-20260927.htm.jsonl, pages 18, 20

The financing amounts are not reconciled in the filings: the cost of repurchasing the 2027 notes appears as 537.3 million USD in the annual report and 535.9 million USD in the 2026 quarterly reports, and the Ruckus price is stated four different ways. No amendment, waiver, exchange or liability management transaction is stated in the sources read.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_bdc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 93; 10-Q_2026-03-29_bdc-20260329.htm.jsonl, pages 13, 16; 10-Q_2026-06-28_bdc-20260628.htm.jsonl, pages 15, 18; 10-Q_2026-09-27_bdc-20260927.htm.jsonl, pages 11, 18, 26
