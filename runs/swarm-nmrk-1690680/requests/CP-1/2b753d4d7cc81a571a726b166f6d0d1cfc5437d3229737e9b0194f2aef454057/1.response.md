## Findings

### Bottom line

Newmark's corporate debt is moderate against reported earnings, with net leverage of 1.38x on reported EBITDA and 1.00x on the issuer's adjusted figure, so the reported measure is the prudent basis for sizing credit risk.

Interest cover on reported EBITDA is 8.17x, undrawn revolver capacity is USD 620.0 million, and the nearest corporate maturity is 2029. Reported EBITDA is derived because the filings state none, and it is far below the issuer's adjusted measure because it carries equity-based compensation as a cost and counts non-cash servicing-right gains as revenue. The third-quarter release and quarterly report print different nine-month cash-flow totals, so the quarterly report is used. The warehouse facilities are excluded from debt as matched funding of loans held for sale; with them, total debt would be much higher.

### Leverage and coverage
Corporate debt at carrying value rose from USD 671.746 million at year-end 2025 to USD 877.508 million at 2026-09-30 as the revolver draw went from USD 75.000 million to USD 280.000 million, while cash moved from USD 229.109 million to USD 239.983 million, so net debt rose by USD 194.888 million to USD 637.525 million. Over the same nine months the group spent USD 210.528 million on share repurchases and USD 72.100 million on convertible debt and other contract assets, which is consistent with the extra debt funding capital return and investment. Net debt over last-twelve-month reported EBITDA moved from 1.07x at year-end 2025 to 1.38x, still below 1.40x at year-end 2024, because last-twelve-month reported EBITDA grew from USD 415.305 million to USD 460.319 million. The senior notes principal of USD 600.000 million is unchanged and matures in 2029, and the revolver now matures in 2030, so the added debt sits in the instrument with the later maturity.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_nmrk-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 19–20; 10-Q_2026-09-30_nmrk-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 23; 10-K_2025-12-31_nmrk-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 173–174; calculation C19; calculation C22; calculation C40; calculation C11

The strongest contrary evidence is the issuer's own framing: on adjusted EBITDA net debt is 1.00x, because the adjusted measure adds back USD 196.892 million of equity-based compensation and partner allocations and removes USD 92.456 million of non-cash servicing-right gains in the nine months.
