## Findings

### Bottom line

VSE is an acquisition-built aviation aftermarket company with no disclosed sponsor or controlling holder, so creditors rely on management's equity-funded acquisition record and PAG integration rather than owner support, and governance evidence is thin.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_vsec-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 9–11; 10-Q_2026-09-30_vsec-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 29; 10-K_2025-12-31_vsec-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 78; calculation C11

Nine-month 2026 revenue was USD 1,314.7 million, up 62.09%, and PAG supplied USD 278.2 million of it after closing on 2026-05-05. Equity and equity-linked units raised about USD 1.3 billion, which limited debt-funded growth, yet debt still rose to USD 959.9 million. One customer group was 20% of FY2025 revenue and inventory is USD 893.0 million. The proxy statement is not supplied, so board, ownership and pay facts are not established.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_vsec-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-Q_2026-09-30_vsec-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 11; 10-Q_2026-09-30_vsec-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 17; 10-K_2025-12-31_vsec-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 11; calculation C1; calculation C15

### Key findings

The business is a single-segment aviation aftermarket platform in which repair has overtaken distribution. Repair revenue was USD 318.0 million and distribution USD 223.0 million in Q3 2026, against distribution at 63.29% of FY2025 revenue, mainly because of the PAG acquisition. Customers are almost entirely commercial, with government customers at 1.10% of Q3 revenue.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_vsec-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 60; 10-Q_2026-09-30_vsec-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 16–17; 10-Q_2026-09-30_vsec-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 30; calculation C3; calculation C4

Concentration is moderate but real. One affiliated customer group was 20% of FY2025 revenue and the United States was 56.61% and Canada 13.40%, so one relationship could remove a fifth of revenue while facility and inventory carrying costs stay. The 2026 reports do not update the customer share.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_vsec-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 11; 10-K_2025-12-31_vsec-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 70; calculation C5; calculation C15

Growth is acquisition-led and funded with a high share of equity, which supports creditors, but integration is the main business risk. Six businesses were acquired between 2025-05-01 and 2026-10-05, including PAG at USD 2,020.0 million of purchase consideration, and about USD 1.3 billion of equity and equity-linked units covered most of the USD 1.75 billion cash price. Reported growth of 62.09% includes acquired revenue; pro forma growth as if PAG had been owned throughout 2025 was 22.81% and the issuer puts Q3 organic growth at about 17%.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_vsec-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 9–12; 10-Q_2026-09-30_vsec-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 11; 10-Q_2026-09-30_vsec-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 27; EX-99.1_2026-11-04_vse-prxq3x2026earningsrele.htm.jsonl, page 2; calculation C1; calculation C7; calculation C11; calculation C12; calculation C13; calculation C15

No sponsor or controlling holder is disclosed, so stress support would have to come from public equity markets rather than an owner. The PAG seller will hold about 4.80% of the share count once its exchangeable subsidiary shares convert, automatically by 2029-05-05, and tangible equity unit holders receive shares from 2029-02-01; the sources state no board or veto rights for either, and the proxy statement that would show them is not supplied.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_vsec-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 9–10; 10-Q_2026-09-30_vsec-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 26–27; 10-Q_2026-06-30_vsec-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 33; 10-K_2025-12-31_vsec-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 78; calculation C6

Financial policy is acquisitive but shareholder payouts are small. Dividends were USD 8.0 million in nine months 2026, 3.20% of Adjusted EBITDA, there were no repurchases beyond shares forfeited to cover employee taxes, and the credit agreement limits dividends annually, so leakage to equity is not the concern; repeated debt-supported deals are.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_vsec-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 33; 10-Q_2026-09-30_vsec-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 35–36; 10-K_2025-12-31_vsec-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 26; calculation C9

Asset quality depends on judgements a creditor cannot test. Inventory is 19.93% of total assets and goodwill and intangibles are 62.59%, the FY2025 audit named slow-moving inventory write-downs as a critical audit matter, and purchase price allocations for the acquired businesses remain preliminary, so a write-down would cut equity and earnings without adding cash.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_vsec-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-K_2025-12-31_vsec-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 39–40; 10-Q_2026-09-30_vsec-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 9–12; calculation C8

Governance reporting is adequate for monitoring but incomplete for control. The sources show regular quarterly reporting, effective disclosure controls and a fair-presentation audit opinion, while controls at four acquired businesses are still being integrated, and board composition, independence, ownership and pay are not established.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_vsec-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 75; 10-Q_2026-09-30_vsec-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 35; 10-K_2025-12-31_vsec-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 39–41; 10-K_2025-12-31_vsec-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 78

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a cautious view is the funding record: about USD 1.3 billion of equity and equity-linked units covered most of the USD 1.75 billion PAG cash price, no related-party fees, distributions beyond a small dividend or creditor-adverse actions appear in the sources, and the chief executive's disclosed sale plan covers about 0.12% of shares outstanding. A proxy statement that shows a holder with board or veto rights, a credit agreement with wide restricted-payment and investment baskets, or pre-acquisition earnings well below management's adjusted measure would push governance risk toward High. Disclosure of the acquired businesses' earnings that matches management's view and a clean fourth-quarter integration would move it toward Low.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_vsec-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 29; 10-Q_2026-06-30_vsec-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 32; 10-Q_2026-09-30_vsec-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 1; 10-K_2025-12-31_vsec-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 26; calculation C11; calculation C12; calculation C16

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

The PAG acquisition closed on 2026-05-05 with purchase consideration of USD 2,020.0 million, made up of cash net of cash acquired of USD 1.75 billion, equity in a VSE subsidiary and an earn-out of up to USD 125.000 million recorded at fair value. The headline of about USD 2.025 billion in the annual report and releases is a different definition of the same price.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_vsec-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 9–10; 10-K_2025-12-31_vsec-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 4; calculation C12

Funding came in three layers. Common stock and tangible equity units raised about USD 1.3 billion in February 2026. The amended senior secured credit agreement added a USD 900.000 million term loan B due 2033-05-05, repaid the prior term loan and raised the revolver commitment from USD 400.000 million to USD 500.000 million due 2030-05-02. The tangible equity units also carry senior unsecured amortizing notes at 5.93% that amortize to 2029-02-01. Debt carrying value rose USD 667.1 million to USD 959.9 million by 2026-09-30.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_vsec-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 18; 10-Q_2026-09-30_vsec-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 19; 10-Q_2026-09-30_vsec-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 26–29; calculation C10; calculation C11

Three smaller acquisitions bracket PAG: NorthStar on 2026-04-01 before it, and Atech on 2026-08-31, paid partly from the revolver, and Test Logic on 2026-10-05 after it; the Test Logic price is not stated and the deal is not in any period figure.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_vsec-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 9; 10-Q_2026-09-30_vsec-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 12; 10-Q_2026-09-30_vsec-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 27
