## Findings

### Bottom line

PROG is still a concentrated consumer-payments credit: Progressive Leasing earned 91.73% of nine-month 2026 pre-tax earnings, so the Purchasing Power acquisition has diversified revenue far more than profit. Customers of its top three retail partners generated 54.8% of 2025 consolidated revenue, and creditors hold USD 600.0 million of senior unsecured notes beside USD 272.0 million of non-recourse asset-backed debt.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_prg-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 61; 10-K_2025-12-31_prg-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 27; 10-Q_2026-09-30_prg-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 38; calculation C1

Governance risk is medium on behavior alone: PROG repaid its acquisition borrowings within six months, but it funded a USD 425.0 million tender offer with note proceeds in 2022 and has announced a USD 100.0 million accelerated share repurchase to be paid from cash and revolver borrowings, while board and ownership detail were not supplied.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_prg-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 132; 10-Q_2026-09-30_prg-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 25; 10-Q_2026-09-30_prg-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 41; 10-Q_2026-06-30_prg-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 38; 10-K_2025-12-31_prg-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 161

### Key findings

- Partner concentration is the main business risk and has already cost volume. Customers of the top three retail partners generated 54.8% of consolidated 2025 revenue and those of the top ten 77.0%, two partners each accounted for more than 10%, and two retail partners have failed since 2024 (Big Lots in September 2024 and American Signature in November 2025), so store closures cut Progressive Leasing volume and earnings independent of customer credit quality.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_prg-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 14; 10-K_2025-12-31_prg-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 27; 10-K_2024-12-31_prg-20241231.htm.jsonl, page 26; 10-Q_2026-09-30_prg-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 54
- Credit performance at the core is weakening and management is trading volume for quality. Lease write-off provisions rose to 8.3% of Progressive Leasing revenue in Q3 2026 from 7.4%, above the top of the 6% to 8% annual target range, decisioning was tightened late in Q2 and again in Q3, and gross merchandise volume grew only 2.3% while revenue fell 0.7%, so tighter approvals protect losses at the cost of future revenue.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_prg-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 54–55; 10-Q_2026-09-30_prg-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 61; 10-Q_2026-09-30_prg-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 63; 10-K_2025-12-31_prg-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 11
- Purchasing Power diversifies revenue but has not yet added profit. It supplied 16.88% of nine-month 2026 revenue but a pre-tax loss of USD 4.8 million, even after a USD 9.0 million fair-value gain on its receivables, and federal employee receivables, which employees may stop paying by allotment, are 9.80% of its post-acquisition accounts receivable with a past-due share of 17.35% against 12.25% for non-federal receivables.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_prg-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 13; 10-Q_2026-09-30_prg-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 33; 10-Q_2026-09-30_prg-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 35; 10-Q_2026-09-30_prg-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 50; 10-Q_2026-09-30_prg-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 67; calculation C3; calculation C8; calculation C9
- The acquisition was funded with recourse debt that was repaid quickly, which is the clearest evidence in the documents of creditor-aligned policy. A USD 125.0 million term loan, a USD 135.0 million revolver draw and cash paid for the deal; revolver borrowings were repaid in Q1 and the term loan by June 30, 2026, and company-defined net leverage fell from about 2.5x after closing to 2.0x, 1.7x and 1.5x at the end of Q1, Q2 and Q3, at or inside the stated target range of 1.5x to 2.0x, although that measure excludes non-recourse asset-backed debt.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_prg-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 31; 10-Q_2026-09-30_prg-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 41; 10-Q_2026-06-30_prg-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 38; EX-99.2_2026-04-29_ex-992q12026earningssupp.htm.jsonl, page 4; EX-99.2_2026-07-29_ex-992q22026earningssupp.htm.jsonl, page 4; EX-99.3_2026-10-28_ex-993q32026earningssupp.htm.jsonl, page 5; calculation C19
- Shareholder returns have resumed, with one debt-funded precedent. A 2022 tender offer for USD 425.0 million was funded by note proceeds, repurchases resumed in Q2 2026 (USD 46.9 million in nine months) and the announced USD 100.0 million accelerated repurchase will use cash and revolver borrowings, but buybacks plus dividends were only 16.47% of nine-month 2026 operating cash flow, so deleveraging gains can be spent on equity if the revolver share is large.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a cautious reading is that diversification is real in growth and the new funding has performed as designed. Four grew nine-month pre-tax earnings by 359.5%, consolidated gross merchandise volume rose 58.9% in Q3, the asset-backed vehicles report covenant compliance and no early amortization event, and management says federal employee delinquencies have improved from the elevated 2025 level.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_prg-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 61; 10-Q_2026-09-30_prg-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 57; 10-Q_2026-09-30_prg-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 40; 10-Q_2026-09-30_prg-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 55; calculation C15; calculation C20

The view would soften if Progressive Leasing write-offs returned inside the 6% to 8% range while volume recovered, if Purchasing Power reached a sustained pre-tax profit without fair-value gains, or if the proxy statement and legal documents showed strong creditor protections. It would worsen if a top three partner left, if write-offs stayed above 8%, or if revolver-funded repurchases pushed company-defined net leverage above 2.0x.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_prg-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 11; 10-K_2025-12-31_prg-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 27; EX-99.2_2026-07-29_ex-992q22026earningssupp.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-Q_2026-09-30_prg-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 54–55

### Limitations

- Partner names, contract terms and individual revenue shares are not supplied; only the top three and top ten shares and two unnamed partners above 10% are stated, so the concentration finding cannot be turned into an estimate of what the loss of one named partner would cost.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_prg-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 14; 10-K_2025-12-31_prg-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 27
- The proxy statement was not supplied, so board composition, control rights, executive pay and ownership percentages are not established and the medium governance rating rests on observed behavior; a controlling or activist holder would change it.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_prg-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 161–162
- The executed credit agreement, indenture and asset-backed documents were not supplied and the notes describe covenants without dollar baskets, so the legal room for new distributions or debt is not established and the behavior flags cannot be tied to a permitted amount.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_prg-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 134; 10-Q_2026-09-30_prg-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 39
- Purchasing Power results before January 2, 2026 are available only in a pro forma note and the purchase price allocation is preliminary, so the finding that it adds revenue but not yet profit cannot be tested against its own history.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_prg-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 29–31; calculation C10

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

The only completed transaction that reshaped the group is the acquisition of Purchasing Power on January 2, 2026, made by a wholly owned subsidiary under a unit purchase agreement signed on December 1, 2025. Total consideration was USD 424.227 million in cash: USD 216.220 million to the seller, USD 199.283 million of seller debt settled at closing, USD 6.724 million of seller costs and USD 2.000 million of escrow, equal to a USD 420 million negotiated price plus USD 4.2 million of seller asset-backed debt repaid. USD 338.608 million of Purchasing Power non-recourse funding debt stayed in place, and net cash used after cash acquired was USD 391.8 million.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_prg-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 27–31; 10-K_2025-12-31_prg-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 157; calculation C7

New debt in 2026 was a Fourth Amendment to the credit facility that added a USD 125.0 million term loan, revolver borrowings of USD 185.0 million (USD 135.0 million for the acquisition and USD 50.0 million for working capital), and one new asset-backed issue: USD 220.0 million of Series 2026-A notes issued by a securitization entity on February 26, 2026 at coupons of 4.37% to 7.54%, maturing August 2030. No new corporate bonds were issued. About USD 224.0 million of assumed warehouse and securitization debt was repaid in February 2026, the revolver borrowings were repaid in Q1, and the term loan was prepaid in full by June 30, 2026, leaving the revolver undrawn at September 30, 2026.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_prg-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 31–41; 10-K_2025-12-31_prg-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 157; 10-Q_2026-03-31_prg-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 39; 10-Q_2026-06-30_prg-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 38; calculation C16

After quarter end, the company announced on October 28, 2026 an intention to enter an accelerated share repurchase of about USD 100.0 million, to be funded with cash and revolver borrowings and completed across Q4 2026 and Q1 2027, and on October 26, 2026 the Board added USD 237.3 million to the repurchase authorization, restoring it to USD 500.0 million.
