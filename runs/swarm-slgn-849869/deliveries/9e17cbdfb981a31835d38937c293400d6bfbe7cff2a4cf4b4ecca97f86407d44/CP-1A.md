## Findings

### Bottom line

Silgan is a scaled, contract-anchored packaging issuer, but the announced partnership would move its largest segment by sales into a separately financed structure that outside investors part-own, so creditors cannot yet size claim priority or leverage. Metal Containers produced 48.4% of 2025 net sales and 36.0% of adjusted EBIT, and Silgan would keep a 60% controlling interest and receive about USD 2.3 billion of cash that it intends to use to retire certain existing debt.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_slgn.htm.jsonl, pages 4–5; 10-Q_2026-09-30_slgn.htm.jsonl, page 16; calculation A2

Governance is conventional for a listed company with no sponsor: the supplied filings document no related-party payments, dividends took 29.74% of 2025 net income and the USD 500.0 million buyback authorization was untouched through September 2026. The gap is control: who the larger holders are, and what the founders' stockholders agreement says, sit in documents that are not in the source set.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_slgn.htm.jsonl, pages 55, 90, 98; 10-Q_2026-09-30_slgn.htm.jsonl, page 13; calculation A7

### Key findings

The business is diversified across three packaging segments and anchored by contracts that pass raw-material cost through, which gives creditors more cash-flow visibility than a single-product packager would. Metal Containers was 48.4% of 2025 net sales, Dispensing and Specialty Closures 41.8% and Custom Containers 9.8%; about 90% of projected 2026 metal container sales sit under multi-year arrangements, metal costs pass through by contract and resin costs pass through with a lag in many cases. The other side is a thin metal margin: Metal Containers earned an adjusted EBIT margin of 8.3% in 2025 against 15.5% in Dispensing and Specialty Closures.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_slgn.htm.jsonl, pages 4–5, 17, 21–22, 66–68; calculation A1

Nestlé is the one material customer concentration, and it is protected by contract rather than removed. Nestlé bought 14% of 2025 consolidated net sales and 13% in 2024 and 2023, and no other customer passed 10%; metal food containers sold to Nestlé were USD 799.8 million, or 12.3% of group sales, under long-term supply agreements that run through 2032 and price by formula. A loss or repricing would fall mainly on Metal Containers, the segment the announced partnership would hold.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_slgn.htm.jsonl, pages 23, 25, 145; calculation A6

Underlying 2026 operating momentum is weaker than the sales line suggests. Nine-month net sales rose 4.6%, mostly from cost pass-through and currency, while unit volumes fell in all three segments and adjusted EBIT was flat at USD 572.2 million against USD 572.6 million. An inventory program liquidated older LIFO layers and cut cost of goods sold by USD 34.0 million in the nine months, USD 26.8 million of it in the third quarter; if the whole benefit sits in adjusted EBIT, the measure would have been 6.01% below the prior-year nine months and 5.97% below the prior-year quarter without it.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_slgn.htm.jsonl, pages 8, 15–16, 18–20, 22–23; calculation A13; calculation A14

The announced perimeter takes the largest segment by sales, and a large share of earnings, out of the direct reach of Silgan's existing creditors. Metal Containers contributed 36.0% of 2025 adjusted EBIT and 38.60% in the first nine months of 2026, and the perimeter also includes a flat cap closures business whose size is not stated. Silgan would own 60% of the partnership and consolidate it, but the partnership's USD 4.9 billion of new senior secured term loans and USD 1.0 billion secured revolver would be secured by the partnership's assets only, and the other 40% would belong to an investment vehicle affiliated with American Industrial Partners that contributes about USD 1.0 billion of cash. Unless existing guarantees and share pledges continue to cover the contributed subsidiaries, which the note does not say, existing creditors of the parent would reach those assets only through the 60% interest, after the partnership's own lenders. The partnership would also buy Trivium, which the 2025 annual report names among the most significant metal container competitors, so the combination adds scale in the segment that already carries the largest customer concentration and depends on regulatory approval.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_slgn.htm.jsonl, page 16; 10-K_2025-12-31_slgn.htm.jsonl, page 27; calculation A2; calculation A3

The senior notes now rest on the parent alone while the bank debt keeps subsidiary support. The U.S. subsidiaries' guarantees of the three unsecured senior note series, USD 1,849.38 million of principal and 40.54% of total debt at September 30, 2026, fell away automatically on March 31, 2026 when the secured 1.4% notes were repaid, and the filings print no replacement guarantor. The Credit Agreement, 57.51% of debt principal, stays guaranteed by the U.S. subsidiaries and a Dutch borrower subsidiary and is supported by pledges of subsidiary shares, so noteholders sit behind subsidiary-level claims, and the partnership's secured lenders would add another layer ahead of them.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_slgn.htm.jsonl, pages 8–9; 10-K_2025-12-31_slgn.htm.jsonl, pages 118, 122–124; calculation A10

Management states that it uses leverage to fund growth and shareholder returns, and the 2024 to 2026 record matches that policy. Weener Packaging cost USD 921.6 million net of cash acquired in October 2024 and was financed with euro loans and cash; dividends have risen every year since 2004; cash returned through dividends and repurchases was 80.65% of net income in 2023, 33.06% in 2024, 55.71% in 2025 and 30.82% in the first nine months of 2026; interest and other debt expense absorbed 31.7% of 2025 income before interest and taxes. The planned USD 2.3 billion debt retirement would be creditor-supportive if completed, but the same announcement adds about USD 3.1 billion of Trivium debt to be assumed, so the net effect on consolidated debt is not established.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_slgn.htm.jsonl, pages 7, 10, 55, 59–60, 75, 109; 10-Q_2026-09-30_slgn.htm.jsonl, pages 4, 16; calculation A8; calculation A9; calculation A12

Working capital leans on bank-run programs, which is funding that is not counted as debt. Customer-based supply chain finance programs covered about 16% of net sales in 2025 and 2024 and improved days sales outstanding by about 28 days, and supplier invoices in the supplier program were USD 482.0 million at September 30, 2026 against USD 385.8 million a year earlier, up 24.94%. If the programs ended, net working capital would rise and the revolver would fund it.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_slgn.htm.jsonl, pages 74–75; 10-Q_2026-09-30_slgn.htm.jsonl, page 12; calculation A16

Governance is conventional and the audit record is clean, but the control picture is incomplete. No sponsor, parent or controlling holder is named; the company had 20 holders of record at January 31, 2026; the board is classified and fixed at seven seats subject to a temporary increase, while nine directors signed the annual report; the chair and the chief executive are different people; the auditor has served since 1987 and gave unqualified opinions, including on internal control; and no restatement, waiver or going-concern statement appears. Director independence, compensation, beneficial ownership and related-party transactions are incorporated from a proxy statement that is not supplied, and the 2022 founders' stockholders agreement is listed but not supplied, so the sponsor and governance risk read is medium and provisional.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_slgn.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 51, 55, 88–90, 98–101; calculation A17

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a cautious reading is that the announced transaction, if completed as described, would pay down about half of the group's debt: the USD 2.3 billion cash distribution equals 50.42% of total debt principal at September 30, 2026, and Silgan would keep control and full consolidation of the segment. The operating base also has a resilient record: multi-year contracts covered about 90% of projected 2026 metal container sales, the USD 50 million cost program was completed with about USD 20 million of savings in 2024 and about USD 30 million in 2025, adjusted EBIT rose to USD 723.3 million in 2025 from USD 658.2 million, and the supplied filings show no covenant waiver, restatement, related-party payment or exchange offer.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_slgn.htm.jsonl, pages 8, 16; 10-K_2025-12-31_slgn.htm.jsonl, pages 9–10, 24, 66; calculation A11

Executed agreements and a pro forma capital structure would settle the largest open question: which debt is retired, whether the Trivium debt is refinanced or stays in place, and whether the partnership's lenders have any recourse to Silgan. Which instruments are retired decides whether the remaining parent claims are mostly secured bank debt or unsecured notes. Evidence of a large founder or fund holder, or terms in the 2022 stockholders agreement that bind the board, would move the governance read from conventional to influenced. A debt-funded dividend or repurchase after the transaction, loss or repricing of the Nestlé agreement, or a second year of volume declines without pass-through offsets would move the view the other way.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_slgn.htm.jsonl, page 16; 10-K_2025-12-31_slgn.htm.jsonl, pages 25, 98

### Limitations

- The October 2026 announcement is described in one note with no executed agreements, sources and uses or pro forma balance sheet, so the conclusions on claim priority, perimeter and debt after closing are directional and rest on pre-transaction figures.
  Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_slgn.htm.jsonl, page 16
- The proxy statement, the Schedule 13G holder names and the 2022 founders' stockholders agreement are not supplied, so the conclusion that no controlling holder exists rests on the absence of a named sponsor and would change if a founder or fund holds a large block.
  Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_slgn.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 90, 98
- Only segment sales, adjusted EBIT, depreciation, assets and capital expenditure are printed by segment, so the share of group cash flow that would sit in the partnership is approximated by its adjusted EBIT share and could differ materially.
  Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_slgn.htm.jsonl, pages 143–144; 10-Q_2026-09-30_slgn.htm.jsonl, page 15
- Customer contracts are not supplied, so the revenue durability conclusion rests on management's statements of contract coverage and the Nestlé end date, and renewal, termination or volume commitments could be weaker than stated.
  Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_slgn.htm.jsonl, pages 17, 25
- Statements that the company is a leader or has more than half of the U.S. metal food container market are management's and are not tested against independent market data, so the competitive position conclusion is management's view.
  Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_slgn.htm.jsonl, pages 4, 17
- The nine-month 2026 figures are unaudited and the inventory benefit is stated only for the group, so the underlying earnings estimate changes if part of that benefit sits outside adjusted EBIT.

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

On October 19, 2026 Silgan announced agreements to create a partnership, Silgan MCC, with an investment vehicle affiliated with funds managed by American Industrial Partners. Silgan would contribute its Metal Containers segment and its food and beverage flat cap closures business and would receive about USD 2.3 billion of cash, which it intends to use to retire certain existing debt, and a 60% controlling ownership interest; the investment vehicle would contribute about USD 1.0 billion of cash for 40%. The partnership would have an independent balance sheet that is fully consolidated into Silgan's statements and would be financed by about USD 4.9 billion of new senior secured term loans and a new senior secured revolving facility of USD 1.0 billion, which the company describes as fully committed and secured by assets of the partnership only.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_slgn.htm.jsonl, page 16

The partnership would also acquire Trivium Packaging B.V., a Netherlands supplier of metal packaging, for a cash payment of about USD 327.5 million and the assumption of about USD 3.1 billion of Trivium's debt. Trivium is one of the competitors that the 2025 annual report names among the most significant in metal containers. Closing is expected in mid-2027 and is subject to regulatory approvals, works council consultation and customary conditions. Costs attributed to announced acquisitions were USD 10.3 million in the third quarter of 2026, against USD 1.1 million in the first nine months of 2025, and adjusted EBIT excludes them; the filing does not tie them to a named transaction. The note gives no agreement terms, no sources and uses, no pro forma balance sheet and no separate figures for the flat cap closures business; the stated cash sources of USD 5.9 billion exceed the stated cash uses of USD 2.3 billion to Silgan and USD 327.5 million to Trivium's sellers by about USD 3.27 billion, close to the USD 3.1 billion of Trivium debt, which is consistent with refinancing that debt but does not establish it.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_slgn.htm.jsonl, pages 15–16, 19–20; 10-K_2025-12-31_slgn.htm.jsonl, page 27; calculation A12

Silgan issued no notes or term loans in the first nine months of 2026. The last new issue was EUR 600.0 million of senior notes due 2031, sold at par on September 12, 2025 and used to repay euro revolving loans that had funded the March 15, 2025 repayment of EUR 650.0 million of senior notes. In 2026 Silgan prepaid USD 42.5 million of U.S. term loans on February 3, amended the Credit Agreement on March 6 to improve term loan pricing, and on March 31 repaid all USD 500.0 million of its 1.4% secured notes with revolving loans and cash, which released the subsidiary guarantees of the other three note series.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_slgn.htm.jsonl, pages 119–120, 125; 10-Q_2026-09-30_slgn.htm.jsonl, pages 3–4, 9

## Gaps

The announcement of October 19, 2026 is described in a single note, so the executed agreements, sources and uses, the debt to be retired, whether the Trivium debt stays in place, any recourse to Silgan, the partner's governance rights and the size of the flat cap closures business are all missing. The stated cash sources exceed the stated cash uses by about USD 3.27 billion, which is close to the Trivium debt but is not explained. This is a material gap for the corporate structure and claim priority conclusions.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_slgn.htm.jsonl, page 16; calculation A12

Ownership detail is missing. The proxy statement, the names of holders of 10% or more, director and officer ownership and the 2022 founders' stockholders agreement are not in the source set, so the conclusion that the company has no controlling holder rests on the absence of a named sponsor. This is a material gap for the governance read.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_slgn.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 90, 98

Customer contracts, including the Nestlé agreements that run through 2032, are not supplied, so price formulas, termination rights and volume commitments cannot be read. This is a minor gap for the revenue durability conclusion.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_slgn.htm.jsonl, page 25

Weener Packaging results are not shown separately after the October 2024 acquisition, so its contribution to the 17.5% growth in dispensing and specialty closures sales in 2025 cannot be separated from organic growth. This is a minor gap for the acquisition integration evidence.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_slgn.htm.jsonl, pages 56, 67

The ownership percentages of the three joint ventures and the lists of subsidiaries and guarantors after the March 31, 2026 release are exhibits that are not supplied. This is a minor gap for the ownership map.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_slgn.htm.jsonl, pages 23–24, 98; 10-Q_2026-09-30_slgn.htm.jsonl, page 9

No agency credit rating or outlook is stated in any supplied filing or in the run settings. This is a minor gap.

Segment-level debt, cash flow and working capital are not printed, so the cash flow that would sit in the partnership is approximated by adjusted EBIT share only. This is a minor gap for the perimeter conclusion.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_slgn.htm.jsonl, pages 143–144; 10-Q_2026-09-30_slgn.htm.jsonl, page 15

The company's statements that it is a leader in each business and holds more than half of the U.S. metal food container market are not tested against independent market data, and the estimate of the market still served by self-manufacturers moved from about 6% in the 2024 annual report to about 7% in the 2025 annual report. This is a minor gap for the competitive position conclusion.
