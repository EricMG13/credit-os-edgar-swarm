## Findings

### Bottom line

Net leverage swings with the packing season: 4.67x on last-twelve-months EBITDA at September 30, 2026 and 4.93x at June 30, against 3.56x at year-end 2025, so creditors rely on revolver access until cash rebuilds at year-end.
Basis: calculation C83; calculation C80; calculation C74; 10-Q_2026-09-30_slgn.htm.jsonl, page 2

Cash interest cover is 5.24x on the same last-twelve-months basis, up from 4.95x in FY2025, but the maturity ladder is concentrated in 2028 at 31.89% of principal, and the transaction announced on October 19, 2026 changes the perimeter and debt in ways the filings do not quantify, so its pro forma effect on net debt and leverage is not established.
Basis: calculation C84; calculation C75; calculation C102; 10-Q_2026-09-30_slgn.htm.jsonl, page 16

### Leverage and coverage

Leverage stepped up with the October 2024 purchase of Weener Packaging, paid for with USD 921.563 million of cash and funded by euro term loans and revolver borrowings, and eased in FY2025 as reported EBITDA grew 15.94% to USD 917.030 million. Total debt over EBITDA moved from 3.99x in FY2023 to 5.23x in FY2024 and 4.74x in FY2025, and net debt over EBITDA from 3.24x to 4.19x and 3.56x, so the FY2025 improvement came from earnings growth while total debt itself rose from USD 4,136.853 million to USD 4,346.848 million.
Basis: calculation C67; calculation C70; calculation C73; calculation C68; calculation C71; calculation C74; calculation C85; 10-K_2025-12-31_slgn.htm.jsonl, page 40; 10-K_2025-12-31_slgn.htm.jsonl, page 114; 10-K_2025-12-31_slgn.htm.jsonl, page 103

On the last-twelve-months basis total debt over EBITDA was 5.07x, 5.31x and 5.03x at the three 2026 quarter ends, and net debt over EBITDA 4.60x, 4.93x and 4.67x. Net debt at September 30, 2026 was USD 335.037 million below net debt at September 30, 2025; the same comparison on leverage is not calculable because nine months of 2024 are not supplied.
Basis: calculation C76; calculation C79; calculation C82; calculation C77; calculation C80; calculation C83; calculation C90; calculation C91; 10-Q_2026-03-31_slgn.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-06-30_slgn.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-09-30_slgn.htm.jsonl, page 2

EBITDA covered cash interest 5.53x in FY2023, 4.94x in FY2024 and 4.95x in FY2025, and 5.24x on the last-twelve-months basis at September 30, 2026. Cash interest paid in the first nine months of 2026 fell 8.61% against the prior-year period, and interest expense fell on lower average borrowings and lower weighted average rates.
Basis: calculation C69; calculation C72; calculation C75; calculation C84; calculation C89; 10-Q_2026-09-30_slgn.htm.jsonl, pages 3–4; 10-Q_2026-09-30_slgn.htm.jsonl, page 20

### Cash flow quality

Operating cash flow in FY2025 was USD 729.841 million, 79.59% of reported EBITDA, and free cash flow after capital expenditures of USD 307.092 million was USD 422.749 million, 46.10% of EBITDA. Dividends and share repurchases of USD 160.675 million used 38.01% of that free cash flow, so shareholder returns were covered by free cash flow in FY2025 but not by the interim seasonal cash flows.
Basis: calculation C96; calculation C94; calculation C95; calculation C97; calculation C98; 10-K_2025-12-31_slgn.htm.jsonl, pages 103–104

The interim picture is dominated by working capital. Operating cash flow was an outflow of USD 646.363 million in the first nine months of 2026, USD 82.398 million worse than the prior-year period, because receivables built by USD 188.902 million more, partly offset by inventory releasing cash. Part of the inventory release came from liquidations of older, lower-cost last-in, first-out layers that lowered cost of goods sold by USD 34.0 million in the nine months, 3.76% of last-twelve-months reported EBITDA.
Basis: calculation C92; calculation C93; calculation C108; 10-Q_2026-09-30_slgn.htm.jsonl, pages 3–4; 10-Q_2026-09-30_slgn.htm.jsonl, page 8

### Liquidity and maturities

At September 30, 2026 cash of USD 324.989 million and the undrawn revolver of USD 665.6 million gave liquidity of USD 990.589 million, 21.78% of total debt, with USD 812.7 million of revolving loans and USD 21.7 million of letters of credit outstanding against a USD 1,500.0 million commitment that matures in November 2029. The issuer says peak seasonal working-capital needs have averaged about USD 600 million, so revolver availability, not cash, is the buffer from summer into autumn.
Basis: calculation C99; calculation C100; calculation C60; 10-Q_2026-09-30_slgn.htm.jsonl, page 27; 10-K_2025-12-31_slgn.htm.jsonl, page 72

The ladder drawn at December 31, 2025 puts USD 1,379.544 million, 31.89%, in 2028 when the two 2028 senior note series fall due, and USD 1,221.976 million, 28.25%, in 2030 when the term loans mature, while 14.52% fell due in 2026, chiefly the USD 500.0 million secured notes that the company repaid on March 31, 2026 with revolver borrowings and cash, so part of the 2026 maturity moved into the revolver. The ladder predates those events and no ladder at September 30, 2026 is printed.
Basis: calculation C61; calculation C102; calculation C103; calculation C101; calculation C115; 10-K_2025-12-31_slgn.htm.jsonl, page 114; 10-Q_2026-09-30_slgn.htm.jsonl, page 9

### Funding float and subsequent event

Supply chain finance is a non-debt funding source carried in accounts payable: supplier invoices in the programme were USD 482.0 million at September 30, 2026, up 24.94% from USD 385.8 million a year earlier, equal to 10.60% of total debt and 54.54% of trade accounts payable. The bank may end the programme on ten days' notice, so a withdrawal would turn payables into a draw on the revolver.
Basis: calculation C104; calculation C105; calculation C106; calculation C107; 10-Q_2026-09-30_slgn.htm.jsonl, page 12; 10-Q_2026-09-30_slgn.htm.jsonl, pages 27–28

The transaction announced on October 19, 2026 is a subsequent event and is excluded from every period figure in this analysis. It would move the Metal Containers segment and the food and beverage flat cap closures business into a partnership 60% owned by the company, with about USD 4.9 billion of new secured term loans and a USD 1.0 billion secured revolver at the partnership, a cash distribution of about USD 2.3 billion to the company intended to retire debt, and about USD 3.1 billion of Trivium debt assumed; the effect on consolidated net debt, leverage and the notes' position is not established.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_slgn.htm.jsonl, page 16

### Data quality and definitions

Reported EBITDA is calculated as income before interest and income taxes plus depreciation and amortization because the issuer states no EBITDA, only adjusted EBIT. It therefore sits after USD 76.773 million of last-twelve-months rationalization charges (8.49% of EBITDA) and after USD 10.333 million of Q3 2026 costs attributed to announced acquisitions (1.14% of EBITDA), and it includes the inventory benefit above; none of these is normalised here and the covenant definition of EBITDA is not available.
Basis: calculation C109; calculation C110; calculation C111; calculation C108; 10-K_2025-12-31_slgn.htm.jsonl, pages 65–66; 10-Q_2026-09-30_slgn.htm.jsonl, pages 15–16

The derived fourth quarter of 2025 shows a provision for income taxes of USD 35.849 million on income before income taxes of USD 53.385 million, a 67.15% rate, which is calculated rather than reported and not explained in the filings supplied. Debt in every ratio is carrying value, USD 14.072 million below principal at September 30, 2026, and the interim statements are unaudited.
Basis: calculation C112; calculation C113; calculation C114; 10-Q_2026-09-30_slgn.htm.jsonl, page 8

### Contrary evidence

The strongest evidence against reading leverage as stretched is that FY2025 EBITDA covered cash interest 4.95x, net debt at the September 2026 balance sheet date was USD 335.037 million below a year earlier, and liquidity of USD 990.589 million exceeds the seasonal working-capital need the issuer describes. That reading would win if year-end 2026 net leverage returns to the 3.56x of year-end 2025 or lower. It would lose if net leverage stays above 4.67x at year-end 2026, if EBITDA without the inventory benefit and acquisition costs falls materially, or if the announced transaction adds secured debt ahead of the notes; covenant levels, which would settle the question, are not printed.
Basis: calculation C75; calculation C91; calculation C99; 10-Q_2026-09-30_slgn.htm.jsonl, page 27
