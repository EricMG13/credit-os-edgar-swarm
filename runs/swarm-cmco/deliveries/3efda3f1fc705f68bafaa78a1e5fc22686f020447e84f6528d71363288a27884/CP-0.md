## Findings

### Bottom line

The supplied filings support credit work on Columbus McKinnon through the September 2026 quarter, but covenant, structural and market work cannot rest on them because no executed debt documents, ratings or market prices were supplied.
Basis: 10-K_2026-03-31_cmco-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 163; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cmco-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 59

For creditors, the audited March 2026 statements and the two later quarterly reports give a usable view of the post-acquisition balance sheet and liquidity, yet any trend crossing the February 2026 acquisition of Kito Crosby compares two different perimeters. Covenant tests, guarantor coverage and board rights are known only from the issuer's own summaries, and the revolver maturity is stated two ways in the annual report. These are limits on the evidence, not findings about credit quality.
Basis: 10-K_2026-03-31_cmco-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 57; 10-K_2026-03-31_cmco-20260331.htm.jsonl, pages 111–113; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cmco-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 28–29
