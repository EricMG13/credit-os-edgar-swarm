## Findings

### Bottom line

Trinet is a co-employment HR outsourcer that earns an insurance spread on a shrinking client base, with a concentrated largest stockholder and a 2023 debt-funded buyback, so creditor governance risk is medium and rests on board discretion, not on terms we can read.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_tnet.htm.jsonl, pages 28–29; 10-K_2025-12-31_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 62; 10-K_2024-12-31_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 88; Source readiness (CP-0)

Insurance service revenues were 84.31% of FY2025 revenue, and had the insurance cost ratio stayed at its FY2023 level, FY2025 insurance costs would have been USD 273.09 million lower, close to the USD 272 million fall in Adjusted EBITDA since FY2023. Shareholder payments of USD 235 million in FY2025 equalled free cash flow of USD 234 million. Legal capacity to distribute more is not established because the debt documents were not supplied.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 93; 10-K_2025-12-31_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 71; 10-K_2025-12-31_tnet.htm.jsonl, pages 94–95; calculation C01; calculation C02; calculation C11; calculation C03

### Key findings

Earnings are an insurance-underwriting spread, so claims costs, not service fees, decide creditor cash flow. Insurance service revenues were USD 4,224 million in FY2025 against insurance costs of USD 3,835 million, and fees are set in advance, so each percentage point of the insurance cost ratio is worth USD 42.24 million of earnings. The ratio rose from 84% in FY2023 to 91% in FY2025 and was 86% in the nine months to 2026-09-30, helped by repricing and a one-time USD 21 million recovery of prior-year costs.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 74; 10-K_2025-12-31_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 31; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 16; calculation C08; calculation C02

The client base is shrinking and can leave quickly, so revenue visibility is short. Total worksite employees at 2026-09-30 were 10.99% below a year earlier, mainly in technology and professional services clients and partly because of health benefit repricing. PEO clients may cancel on 30 days' notice, and five states account for about 64% of paid wages, which also concentrates insurance pricing. Revenue per co-employed worksite employee rose 8%, which has so far only partly offset lost volume.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 10; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 13; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 16; 10-K_2025-12-31_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 16; 10-K_2025-12-31_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 39; calculation C06

Client funds give the business liquidity and a yield but also a contingent exposure. Clients prefund payroll, payroll taxes and benefits, which are held as restricted funds, and interest on those funds was USD 67 million of FY2025 revenue, partly from interest on payroll tax refunds. In nine months 2026 interest income was USD 39 million against USD 53 million, because of lower refund interest and lower average cash balances. In some states a PEO must pay wages and taxes even when a client has not remitted, so the prefunding discipline is a creditor protection worth monitoring.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 76; 10-K_2025-12-31_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 31; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 18; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 35

Governance risk is medium because the one documented extraction-style act is old and the current pattern is cash-funded, while capacity is unreadable. The largest stockholder, an affiliate of Atairos, held about 38% at 2026-01-31, may nominate a director while it holds 15% or more, and with directors and officers controls about 39%. In 2023 the company bought USD 360 million of shares from Atairos and USD 640 million through a tender offer, funded with USD 400 million of 2031 notes and a USD 200 million revolver draw, which lifted debt by USD 597 million in a year.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 62; 10-K_2024-12-31_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 88; 10-K_2024-12-31_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 126; 10-K_2024-12-31_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 74; calculation C07; calculation C05

Current capital allocation is funded from cash and returned all of free cash flow in FY2025. Buybacks and dividends were USD 235 million against free cash flow of USD 234 million that year, and USD 134 million against USD 243 million in the nine months of 2026. The board added USD 336 million of repurchase authority in February 2026, USD 311 million remained at 2026-09-30, and the dividend has risen from USD 0.25 to USD 0.29 per share. The revolver's negative covenants restrict payments to stockholders, but their size is not readable, so willingness is evidenced and capacity is not.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 83; 10-K_2025-12-31_tnet.htm.jsonl, pages 94–95; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 24; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 45; 10-K_2024-12-31_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 89; calculation C03

Disclosure supports monitoring of earnings but not of covenants. The 2025 financial statements and internal controls received unqualified audit opinions, with a critical audit matter on accrued workers' compensation and health insurance costs, which are judgemental estimates. The chief financial officer changed under agreements dated in October 2025, the reason is not stated, and the proxy statement with director and pay detail was not supplied.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_tnet.htm.jsonl, pages 89–90; 10-K_2025-12-31_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 135; 10-K_2025-12-31_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 2

Acquisition appetite is not established as a pattern: the 2022 acquisitions of subsidiaries, including Zenefits, cost USD 229 million of net cash and the 2026 Cocoon purchase USD 22 million, with a restructuring that wound down the software-only HRIS product in between, so two deals in five years of different size show no consistent funding or integration behavior.
Basis: 10-K_2024-12-31_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 101; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 43; 10-K_2025-12-31_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 129; calculation C10

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest case for lower risk is that the 2023 episode has been unwound in substance: the revolver was repaid in full by July 2025 with USD 110 million in 2024 and USD 90 million in 2025, all remaining debt is two fixed-rate notes, no sponsor fees or related-party leakage are stated, related-party revenue is USD 4 million, and buybacks since then came from cash flow. A medium level would fall to low if the debt documents showed tight limits on distributions, and would rise to high if another debt-funded repurchase, a large special dividend or a stockholder fee appeared, or if the largest stockholder sold or bought a block.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 83; 10-K_2025-12-31_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 115; 10-K_2025-12-31_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 119; 10-K_2025-12-31_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 129; 10-K_2024-12-31_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 88

### Limitations

- The proxy statement was not supplied, so the board's composition, pay design and the beneficial ownership table are not established; this keeps the governance level at medium on documented behavior and prevents a view on board independence from creditors' standpoint.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 2
- The credit agreement and indentures were not supplied, so what the documents let the company pay out or invest is unknown; the willingness conclusion therefore cannot be tested against capacity, and the level stays medium instead of moving either way.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_tnet.htm.jsonl, pages 133–134
- Revenue by vertical and by client size is not disclosed, so the shrinking-client-base conclusion rests on worksite employee counts and the five-state concentration alone and cannot say whether attrition is broad or concentrated in a few large accounts.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 16; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 13
- The reasons for the October 2025 chief financial officer transition and the vintage and remaining life of Atairos' investment vehicle are not stated, so no conclusion on management continuity or exit pressure is drawn.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 135; 10-K_2025-12-31_tnet.htm.jsonl, page 62

### Overall governance view

Overall, Trinet presents medium sponsor and governance risk. The key supported behavior is a debt-funded repurchase of USD 1,000 million in 2023, including USD 360 million bought from the largest stockholder, which matters because it raised leverage and set the 2031 maturity and implies that the board will lever for repurchases when it judges the price right. Governance review completed with limitations; the risk level is medium and the key gaps are the debt documents and the proxy statement.
