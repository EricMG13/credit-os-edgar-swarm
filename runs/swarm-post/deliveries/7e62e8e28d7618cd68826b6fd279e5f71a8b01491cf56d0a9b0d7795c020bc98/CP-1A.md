## Findings

### Bottom line

Post Holdings is a publicly owned food group with no disclosed controlling holder, so creditors rely on board discipline over buybacks and acquisitions, and repurchases of USD 916.1 million in nine months to June 2026 ran at 2.28x free cash flow of USD 401.5 million.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_post-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-K_2025-09-30_post-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 161; calculation C9; calculation C10

The operating base is spread over four segments and no customer exceeds 17.4% of sales, but nine-month pet food sales fell 15.0% and management treats about USD 60 million of Foodservice earnings as above a normal run rate. Sponsor and governance risk is Medium.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_post-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 16; 10-Q_2026-06-30_post-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 27, 33; EX-99.1_2026-08-06_ex99-1q32026er.htm.jsonl, page 7; calculation C8

### Key findings

No controlling shareholder or sponsor is disclosed, which removes both a source of equity support and a source of extraction, and leaves the board and the debt covenants as the only limits on shareholder returns. The share count fell 15.7% between November 2025 and August 2026. Two board appointments were announced in February 2026, taking the board to nine, one of them the former Chief Operating Officer, and the chief executive became chairman in December 2025. Beneficial ownership, board independence and pay sit in the proxy statement, which is not supplied.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_post-20250930.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 20, 161; 10-Q_2026-06-30_post-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 1; EX-99.1_2026-02-03_ex99-1q12026er.htm.jsonl, page 8; calculation C14

Shareholder returns have outgrown the cash the business generates, and this is the main creditor-relevant behaviour. Cash paid for repurchases was 0.87x free cash flow in fiscal 2023, 0.60x in fiscal 2024, 1.45x in fiscal 2025 and 2.28x in the nine months to June 2026, a period in which principal debt rose USD 223.8 million and book equity fell USD 674.6 million, or 17.9%. The board approved four repurchase authorizations between August 2025 and May 2026, and the two most recent replaced programmes that still had USD 122.1 million and USD 236.6 million unused. The documents do not say how the repurchases were funded.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_post-20250930.htm.jsonl, pages 70, 96; 10-Q_2025-12-31_post-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 41; 10-Q_2026-06-30_post-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 3, 4, 17, 48; EX-99.1_2026-02-03_ex99-1q12026er.htm.jsonl, page 6; EX-99.1_2026-05-05_ex99-1q22026er.htm.jsonl, page 7; calculation C9; calculation C10; calculation C13; calculation C15

Portfolio changes are large against the earnings base and make reported history hard to compare. Post bought the rest of 8th Avenue for USD 798.8 million in July 2025, sold its pasta business five months later for a preliminary USD 375.0 million, and sold Crystal Farms in May 2026 for a preliminary USD 50.0 million after a USD 43.3 million loss on the held-for-sale value. Disposal proceeds of USD 424.2 million in nine months compare with USD 920.3 million spent on acquisitions in fiscal 2025, and goodwill and intangibles are 59.3% of total assets, so the carrying value of the balance sheet rests heavily on past acquisitions.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_post-20250930.htm.jsonl, pages 96, 117–118; 10-Q_2026-06-30_post-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 3, 4, 10, 12; calculation C16; calculation C17; calculation C18; calculation C28

Earnings are steady in total but lean on one segment and one price cycle. Foodservice produced 38.5% of nine-month segment profit, up USD 56.6 million on USD 198.0 million of lower raw material costs, mainly eggs, and management says about USD 60 million of its fiscal 2026 earnings sits above a normal run rate of USD 500 million, with preliminary fiscal 2027 Adjusted EBITDA generally flat at about USD 1.48 billion on a comparable basis. Post Consumer Brands grew 9.0% only because 8th Avenue added USD 504.0 million; without it, segment net sales fell 8.6%, with pet food down 15.0%.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_post-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 26, 33–34; EX-99.1_2026-08-06_ex99-1q32026er.htm.jsonl, pages 4, 7; calculation C6; calculation C8; calculation C24

Customer and category concentration is moderate for the group and high inside each segment. Walmart is 17.4% of net sales and no other customer exceeds 10%, but the largest customers are 29.9% of Post Consumer Brands, 33.0% of Weetabix, 42.0% of Foodservice and 34.9% of Refrigerated Retail, and cereal, eggs and pet food together were 81.3% of fiscal 2025 net sales. Management describes the ready-to-eat cereal category as weak and expects that to continue.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_post-20250930.htm.jsonl, pages 11, 16; calculation C7

Disclosure is good enough for monitoring but leaves the legal limits on creditor protection untested. The auditor's opinions on the statements and on internal controls were unqualified, results are released with each filing and non-GAAP measures are reconciled. Against that, 8th Avenue was left out of the controls assessment, the audit matter on Weetabix goodwill records fair value only 8.7% above carrying value, and the indentures and credit agreement that set the limits on distributions are not supplied.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_post-20250930.htm.jsonl, pages 47, 91, 93, 159; EX-99.1_2026-08-06_ex99-1q32026er.htm.jsonl, page 22

Related-party dealings are small. BellRing, which shares officers and directors with Post, bought USD 57.5 million of protein shakes in fiscal 2025, purchases from the former affiliate 8th Avenue ended when it was acquired, and no sponsor, monitoring or advisory fee is disclosed.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_post-20250930.htm.jsonl, pages 115, 117

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest case against caution is that the board's choices have so far kept the balance sheet orderly: no dividend is paid, no sponsor fee is disclosed, the 2028 and 2029 maturities were refinanced early in October 2024 and December 2025, and repurchases are discretionary and can stop. Risk would move to Low if repurchases fell to the level of free cash flow, USD 401.5 million over nine months, and principal debt began to fall, or if the proxy statement showed an independent board and a stated leverage target. It would move to High if the board again outspent free cash flow in the fourth quarter while USD 575.0 million of convertible notes fall due in August 2027, or if egg earnings fell back to the USD 500 million run rate before replacement earnings arrived.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_post-20250930.htm.jsonl, pages 69, 136; 10-Q_2026-06-30_post-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 17, 19–20; EX-99.1_2026-08-06_ex99-1q32026er.htm.jsonl, page 7; calculation C9

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

Post reshaped its portfolio in both directions over the last two years. It bought the pet food business of J. M. Smucker in April 2023 for USD 1,207.5 million, partly paid in newly issued Post shares valued at USD 492.3 million, bought Perfection Pet Foods for USD 235.0 million in December 2023, and in fiscal 2025 bought Potato Products of Idaho for USD 120.0 million and the rest of 8th Avenue for USD 798.8 million, which included retiring its debt and was funded with cash and revolver borrowings. It then sold 8th Avenue's pasta business on December 1, 2025 for a preliminary USD 375.0 million, receiving USD 378.5 million at closing while the buyer took on USD 78.2 million of leaseback liabilities, and sold Crystal Farms on May 1, 2026 for a preliminary USD 50.0 million, receiving USD 45.7 million at closing.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_post-20250930.htm.jsonl, pages 117–119; 10-Q_2026-06-30_post-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 10, 12; calculation C17; calculation C16

Three note issues since October 2024 pushed maturities out and raised either coupons or principal. On October 9, 2024 Post issued USD 600.0 million of senior notes due 2034 at par and used part of the proceeds to redeem its notes due 2028. On December 15, 2025 it issued USD 1,300.0 million of 6.50% notes due 2036 at par and redeemed all USD 1,235.0 million of its 5.50% notes due 2029 for a premium of USD 22.6 million, a coupon 1.00 percentage points higher. On March 13, 2026 it issued a further USD 600.0 million of the 2034 notes at 100.75% of par and used the proceeds to repay the revolver. The notes are guaranteed by the domestic subsidiaries other than immaterial, excluded and unrestricted ones.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_post-20250930.htm.jsonl, pages 79, 136; 10-Q_2026-06-30_post-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 19–20, 22; calculation C19

Two events after the quarter end are kept outside the period figures. Post repurchased shares for USD 39.3 million between July 1 and August 5, 2026, leaving USD 490.7 million of authorization, and it expects to sell its Cobourg and Ashton-under-Lyne facilities in the second half of calendar 2026, with no proceeds stated.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-08-06_ex99-1q32026er.htm.jsonl, page 6; 10-Q_2026-06-30_post-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 12
