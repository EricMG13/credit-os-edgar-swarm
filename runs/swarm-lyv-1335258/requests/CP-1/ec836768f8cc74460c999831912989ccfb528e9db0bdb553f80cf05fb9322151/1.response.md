## Findings

### Bottom line

Leverage on reported EBITDA rose from 4.34x at year-end 2025 to 6.07x at September 30, 2026, and the 0.93x net figure is flattered by client and event cash, so creditors should read leverage nearer the gross figure.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_lyv-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 15; 10-Q_2026-09-30_lyv-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 2–3; EX-99.1_2026-11-05_lyv-2026q3xex991er.htm.jsonl, page 6; calculation C52; calculation C53; calculation C22

The rise has two causes: the carrying value of debt grew by USD 1.55 billion in nine months, and twelve-month EBITDA fell to USD 1.61 billion after a USD 450 million litigation charge that the issuer's adjusted measure excludes. On that adjusted measure leverage is 3.81x. The October 2026 refinancing is not reflected, and its effect on net debt is not established.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_lyv-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 20; 10-Q_2026-09-30_lyv-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 26–27; 10-Q_2026-09-30_lyv-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 36–37; calculation C23; calculation C24; calculation C14; calculation C15; calculation C53

Cash interest cover can be stated only for year-ends, because the quarterly reports print no cash interest and the annual figure is net of interest income; cover on interest expense is 4.30x for the latest twelve months.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_lyv-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 93; 10-Q_2026-09-30_lyv-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-Q_2026-09-30_lyv-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 6–7; calculation C56; calculation C19

### Leverage and coverage

Gross leverage on reported EBITDA moved from 4.68x at year-end 2024 to 4.34x at year-end 2025 and then to 6.07x at September 30, 2026. The latest step comes from new borrowing, including the VenueCo notes and a revolver draw to fund an arena purchase, and from a smaller EBITDA base after the litigation reserve; net leverage stays low only because balance-sheet cash is large.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_lyv-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 14; 10-Q_2026-09-30_lyv-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 16–17; 10-Q_2026-09-30_lyv-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 20; calculation C51; calculation C52; calculation C53

On the issuer's adjusted operating income the same debt is 3.47x at year-end 2025 and 3.81x at September 30, 2026. The issuer adds the USD 450 million reserve back in its free cash flow reconciliation, which indicates an accrual rather than a payment; creditors should treat it as a claim on future cash until the litigation is resolved.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_lyv-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 20; EX-99.1_2026-11-05_lyv-2026q3xex991er.htm.jsonl, page 5; calculation C52; calculation C53

Cash interest paid is printed only in the annual statements and is net of interest income and capitalized interest, giving cover of 10.35x for FY2024 and 15.93x for FY2025. Measured on interest expense, cover is 4.22x and 5.98x for those years and 4.30x for the twelve months to September 30, 2026, which is the more cautious reading.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_lyv-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 93; 10-K_2025-12-31_lyv-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 89; calculation C54; calculation C55; calculation C56

### Cash flow and liquidity

Operating cash flow in the nine months of 2026 was higher than a year earlier mainly because of working-capital timing from event sales, not because of earnings; cash used in investing rose with acquisitions and venue capital spending, and financing inflows funded the difference. Purchases of property, plant and equipment are the largest investing outflow, and the issuer guides to about USD 1.1 billion of capital spending for the year.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_lyv-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 7; 10-Q_2026-09-30_lyv-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 50; EX-99.1_2026-11-05_lyv-2026q3xex991er.htm.jsonl, page 3

Undrawn revolving commitments fell from USD 1,679.500 million at year-end 2025 to USD 1,234.544 million at September 30, 2026 after the arena-related revolver draw; the USD 700 million delayed draw term loan A commitment is outside that figure.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_lyv-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 114; 10-Q_2026-09-30_lyv-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 46–47; calculation C29; calculation C30

### Float and cash definitions

Deferred revenue, client payables and accrued artist fees fund operations without bearing interest, and they are the reason balance-sheet cash is not free cash. At September 30, 2026 the issuer's own free cash measure is USD 2,306.6 million against cash and equivalents of USD 8,257.468 million, so net debt measured against free cash would be much higher than the balance-sheet figure.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_lyv-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 44–45; EX-99.1_2026-11-05_lyv-2026q3xex991er.htm.jsonl, page 6; calculation C44; calculation C45

### Subsequent events and data quality

The October 15, 2026 issue of dollar and euro senior notes due 2032, the redemption of the 6.500% secured notes due 2027 and the credit agreement amendment all fall after the September 30, 2026 balance sheet and are kept out of every period figure; the sources give no pro forma debt.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_lyv-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 26–27; 10-Q_2026-09-30_lyv-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 47–49

## Gaps

- Cash interest paid for the nine-month periods is not stated: the interim cash flow statements and notes print no interest paid, so cover on cash interest cannot be built for the twelve months to September 30, 2026. Severity Medium. The quarterly cash interest paid would close it.
- Gross cash interest paid is not stated: the annual figure is interest net of interest income and capitalized interest, so cash cover for FY2024 and FY2025 is overstated against gross cash interest. Severity Medium. A gross cash interest figure would close it.
- No by-year debt maturity table is printed after December 31, 2025, so the ladder and the principal reconciliation are as of that date and omit the VenueCo notes, the September 2026 revolver draw and the October 2026 refinancing. Severity High. A maturity table at a later balance-sheet date would close it.
- The October 15, 2026 notes issue, the redemption of the 6.500% secured notes due 2027 and the credit agreement amendment are not reflected in any period figure, and the euro notes' US dollar value, the redemption price and the fees are not stated, so pro forma net debt and leverage are not established. Severity High. An issuer pro forma debt schedule would close it.
- The current portion of debt at September 30, 2026 is smaller than the 2029 convertible notes plus the 6.500% secured notes counted there at June 30, 2026, and the third-quarter report prints no explanation; the conflict is preserved and no reclassification is made. Severity Medium. An issuer explanation would close it.
- Other debt of 1,241.107 million USD at September 30, 2026 is itemised only for a cash pool revolving facility of 533.6 million USD and finance leases of 90.5 million USD, leaving about 617.0 million USD without terms. Severity Medium. An itemised other-debt schedule would close it.

- The issuer states AOI rather than EBITDA, so reported EBITDA is derived as operating income plus depreciation and amortization and AOI is mapped as the issuer-adjusted measure; no add-back is tested for realisation. Severity Low.
- Statement lines printed in only one of the annual and interim statements, and lines the statements print as a dash, are shown as Not supplied in the other columns: the loss on extinguishment of debt, the forward currency exchange contracts line, purchases of intangible assets against acquisition of intangible rights, proceeds from sale of intangible assets, common stock repurchases, and cash flow lines printed as a dash in a single period.
