## Findings

### Bottom line

Business risk is concentrated in one partner and customer, Saudi Aramco, and governance risk is medium: no holder controls Nabors and 2026 cash went to debt reduction, but related-party dealings, a combined chairman and chief executive, and a joint venture backstop limit creditor protection.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_nbr-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 21; 10-K_2025-12-31_nbr-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 113; 10-K_2025-12-31_nbr-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 114; calculation A6; calculation A11

Saudi Aramco supplied about 30% of 2025 operating revenues and holds USD 502.72 million of redeemable interests in SANAD, the consolidated joint venture that holds 38.02% of group cash (USD 180.64 million), so creditors cannot assume that consolidated cash and earnings are fully available to them.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_nbr-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 21; 10-Q_2026-09-30_nbr-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 13; calculation A5

Ownership, board and pay detail sits in a proxy statement that is not supplied, so the medium level rests on the filings, not on governance documents.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_nbr-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 132; Source readiness (CP-0)

### Key findings

- Earnings are anchored in International Drilling and in Saudi Arabia, not spread evenly across four segments. International Drilling produced 50.17% of FY2025 operating revenues and 53.05% in the first nine months of 2026, and Saudi Arabia alone produced about USD 1.0 billion of 2025 revenue, so a Saudi activity cut would move cash flow faster than a change in the U.S. rig count.
Basis: calculation A1; calculation A2; 10-K_2025-12-31_nbr-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 122; 10-K_2025-12-31_nbr-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 123
- Saudi Aramco is customer, joint venture partner and claimant at once, which concentrates counterparty risk on one name. It accounted for approximately 30% of 2025 operating revenues, 31% in 2024 and 26% in 2023, and holds USD 502.72 million of redeemable interests in SANAD outside equity; Nabors must also backstop its share of the first 25 SANAD rig purchases if the venture lacks cash or financing, and a change of control could trigger a call option to purchase its SANAD interest. A dispute or contract cut with this one counterparty would reach revenue, cash and the joint venture at the same time.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_nbr-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 9; 10-K_2025-12-31_nbr-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 47; 10-K_2025-12-31_nbr-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 112; 10-K_2025-12-31_nbr-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 114; 10-Q_2026-09-30_nbr-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 13
- Contract terms give limited protection to revenue. Most contracts are daywork contracts at a dayrate, term contracts last from six months to five years, single-well contracts can be ended on short notice, and early termination payments may not fully compensate; no backlog or rig-level contract schedule is supplied, so durability is judged from rig counts and margins only.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_nbr-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 8; EX-99.1_2026-10-27_tm2628332d1_ex99-1.htm.jsonl, page 7
- Capital allocation has favoured creditors in 2026, not clearly in 2025. In the first nine months of 2026 principal fell by USD 479.15 million, paid from cash on hand, while in 2025 the USD 902.68 million of debt repaid was largely refinanced with USD 700.0 million of new notes and revolver borrowings, leaving principal only USD 10.46 million lower. The issuer paid no common dividend in the cash flow statements for 2023 to 2025, bought Parker with shares and USD 0.6 million of cash, and sold Quail Tools for net consideration of USD 600.0 million. The restraint is a choice, not a limit: the September 2025 amendment lets the issuer repurchase up to USD 100.0 million of equity each fiscal year, so capacity to distribute exists although none has been used.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_nbr-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 83; 10-K_2025-12-31_nbr-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 84; 10-K_2025-12-31_nbr-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 103; 10-K_2025-12-31_nbr-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 13; 10-Q_2026-09-30_nbr-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 15; 10-Q_2026-09-30_nbr-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 16; 10-Q_2026-09-30_nbr-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 46; calculation A6
- Governance leaves authority concentrated and related parties active, and the proxy statement that would show board independence is not supplied. One person is chairman, president and chief executive and the chief operating decision maker; a company he owns and manages is a member of Hexegen LLC, which received energy transition assets from Nabors and would share profits with Nabors above a threshold, up to a 90:10 split; a director's group billed USD 14.8 million for services in 2025; and managers and directors co-sponsor Nabors' SPAC. None of these items is quantified as leakage, but together they weaken the independent check on capital allocation.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_nbr-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 121; 10-K_2025-12-31_nbr-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 113; 10-K_2025-12-31_nbr-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 114; 10-Q_2026-09-30_nbr-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 30
- Cash is not freely upstreamed to note holders. Nabors is a holding company that relies on its subsidiaries and investments, its debt instruments do not forbid contractual limits on upstream payments, and 38.02% of consolidated cash (USD 180.64 million) sits in SANAD, whose liabilities carry no recourse to other Nabors assets, so group cash overstates what is available to creditors of the borrower.

SANAD newbuild capital spending is guided at USD 295 million to USD 305 million for 2026 with about USD 20 million of cash consumed at SANAD, one further newbuild is due in 2026 and two in the first quarter of 2027, and five SANAD rigs have been suspended, so some of that spending adds capacity while other rigs are idle (analyst interpretation).

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence for a lower governance risk is the debt reduction and the absence of a sponsor or common dividend: cash that could have gone to shareholders cut principal by USD 479.15 million in the first nine months of 2026, the auditor's opinion on the annual statements and on internal control is unqualified, and the issuer reports compliance with its credit agreement covenants. The strongest evidence for a higher risk is the concentration in Saudi Aramco and the open joint venture backstop. A proxy statement showing an independent board majority and a separate chair, or indentures showing guarantee and restricted payment terms no wider than the credit agreement's, would move the level toward low; a renegotiation or cut of Saudi Aramco work, use of the USD 100.0 million annual repurchase capacity, or a call on the backstop would move it toward high.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_nbr-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 76; 10-K_2025-12-31_nbr-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 131; 10-Q_2026-09-30_nbr-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 40; 10-K_2025-12-31_nbr-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 114; 10-Q_2026-09-30_nbr-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 16; calculation A6

- The proxy statement is not supplied, so board independence, committee structure, holdings above 5% and executive pay are not established; this holds the governance level at medium and prevents any statement about director independence.

- The joint venture agreement, the contracts with Saudi Aramco, the indentures and the credit agreement are not supplied, so the call option, the backstop, restricted payment capacity and guarantor coverage are known only from filing summaries; conclusions about the partner's leverage over Nabors and about distribution capacity are bounded accordingly.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_nbr-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 135; 10-K_2025-12-31_nbr-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 136; 10-K_2025-12-31_nbr-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 112
