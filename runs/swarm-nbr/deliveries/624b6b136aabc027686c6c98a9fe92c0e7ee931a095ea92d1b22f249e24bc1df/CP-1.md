## Findings

### Bottom line

Leverage is moderate and lower than at year-end (2.26x total debt to adjusted EBITDA at 2026-09-30), and nothing falls due before 2029, so creditors' near-term exposure is liquidity, not refinancing.
Basis: calculation C10; calculation C14; 10-Q_2026-09-30_nbr-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 15

That exposure has grown: cash fell by USD 465.64 million in nine months because note redemptions were paid from cash, leaving USD 475.07 million, of which 38.02% sits in the consolidated Saudi joint venture, plus an undrawn USD 350 million revolver.
Basis: calculation C13; calculation C17; calculation C19; 10-Q_2026-09-30_nbr-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 13; 10-Q_2026-09-30_nbr-20260930x10q.htm.jsonl, page 15

Reported-EBITDA ratios (1.78x at year-end, 2.22x now) are not comparable, because USD 527.62 million of 2025 disposal and acquisition gains sit in FY2025. Cash interest cover can be stated only for FY2025 (4.36x adjusted); interim reports print no cash interest, and the ranking and security of the notes and the covenant definitions are not established.
Basis: calculation C9; calculation C11; calculation C18; 10-K_2025-12-31_nbr-20251231x10k.htm.jsonl, page 121

### Leverage trajectory and drivers
