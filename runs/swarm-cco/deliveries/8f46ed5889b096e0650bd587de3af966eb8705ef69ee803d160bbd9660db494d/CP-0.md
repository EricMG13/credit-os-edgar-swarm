## Findings

### Bottom line

Creditors can rely on these documents for a financial, debt and liquidity read of Clear Channel Outdoor Holdings, Inc. through 30 June 2026, with limitations, but not for covenant, legal-priority, market or portfolio conclusions, and nothing is dated after 5 August 2026. That gap matters because the pending take-private merger has an outside date of 9 November 2026, four days after the as-of date, and the debt amendments and conditional note redemptions tied to it take effect only on completion, so the capital structure may already differ from the one described.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_cco-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 24; 10-Q_2026-06-30_cco-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 16–17; calculation C7; calculation C8

Within those limits the supplied set is strong where credit work starts: four consecutive quarters on one perimeter of continuing operations, a full debt table with maturities, facility availability net of letters of credit, and the company's own net debt and Adjusted EBITDA reconciliations. Missing are the executed debt documents, agency ratings, market prices and portfolio inputs, so legal and covenant questions rest on the filings' summaries.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_cco-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 2–6; 10-Q_2026-06-30_cco-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 15; 10-Q_2026-06-30_cco-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 33–34; EX-99.1_2026-08-05_exhibit991-ccohearningsrel.htm.jsonl, page 6; EX-99.1_2026-08-05_exhibit991-ccohearningsrel.htm.jsonl, pages 8–9; 10-K_2025-12-31_cco-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 106–109; calculation C4
