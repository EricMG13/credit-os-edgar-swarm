## Findings

### Bottom line
Kinetik owed USD 3,929.02 million of debt at carrying value on 2026-09-30, equal to 3.70x last-twelve-months issuer-adjusted EBITDA, and distributions to equity holders exceed free cash flow, so net borrowing funds the gap.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_kntk-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 6–8; 10-Q_2026-09-30_kntk-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 18; calculation K02; calculation K05; calculation R1

The 3.08x ratio on reported EBITDA is flattered by a one-time USD 415.41 million gain on an equity-method sale; without it the ratio is 4.57x, so creditors should read leverage between 3.70x and 4.57x.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_kntk-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 94; 10-K_2025-12-31_kntk-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 123; calculation K03; calculation R2

Liquidity is USD 1,069.42 million, yet the fully drawn receivables facility ends within twelve months and USD 2,200.00 million of principal falls due in 2028.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_kntk-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 47; 10-Q_2026-09-30_kntk-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 17–18; calculation K11; calculation R3; calculation R4
