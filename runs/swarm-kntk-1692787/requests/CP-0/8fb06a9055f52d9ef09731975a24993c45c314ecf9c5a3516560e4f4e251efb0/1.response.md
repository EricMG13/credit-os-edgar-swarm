## Findings

### Bottom line
The five Kinetik filings are sufficient for financial, business and fundamental credit work through the quarter ended 2026-09-30, but they cannot support conclusions on covenants, ratings, market pricing or recovery because no governing documents, ratings or quotes were supplied.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_kntk-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 5–8; 10-K_2025-12-31_kntk-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 84–85; 10-Q_2026-09-30_kntk-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 19

Readiness is limited rather than clean. Every statement is consolidated at the parent level, while the term loan, the revolver and the notes sit with its operating partnership, and the filings print only a compliance statement for covenants, never their levels. The latest report was signed 13 days before the as-of date and reports balance sheet figures that are 49 days older than it, so later financing, legal or ownership events are not covered.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_kntk-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 11; 10-K_2025-12-31_kntk-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 125–128; 10-K_2025-12-31_kntk-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 84; calculation C5; calculation C6

Source readiness is therefore ready with limitations for Financial foundation, Business & governance and Fundamental credit, and blocked for Legal & covenants and Covenant capacity should those be requested.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_kntk-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 84–85; 10-Q_2026-09-30_kntk-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 19
