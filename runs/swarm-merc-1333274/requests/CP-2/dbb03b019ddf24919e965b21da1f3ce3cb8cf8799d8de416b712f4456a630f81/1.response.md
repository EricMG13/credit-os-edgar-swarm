## Findings

### Bottom line

Mercer is a distressed credit whose debt can no longer be serviced from earnings, so creditor outcomes depend on the noteholder-led restructuring and not on the operating business.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); Business & governance (CP-1A)

Trailing twelve-month Operating EBITDA is negative USD 36,145 thousand against cash interest paid of USD 110,562 thousand, and net debt is USD 1,593,466 thousand at 2026-09-30, or USD 1,588,380 thousand pro forma for grant funds received in October 2026. Liquidity of USD 143,531 thousand compares with USD 340,431 thousand of facility maturities in 2027 and a covenant test on 2026-12-31 that management said in the second-quarter report it was probable the company would fail. The issuer describes its mills as modern and they sell surplus energy, and noteholders holding about 87% of the notes have backed a plan with USD 400,000 thousand of new cash, but the plan is unconfirmed and nothing after 2026-11-05 is established.
Basis: calculation B1; calculation B2; calculation B3; calculation B12; Financial foundation (CP-1); 10-Q_2026-06-30_Q2-2026-Quarterly-Report.pdf, page 11; 10-Q_2026-09-30_Q3-2026-Quarterly-Report.pdf, pages 8, 12, 13

### Key findings

Liquidity and refinancing are the weakest dimension and set the timetable. Cash plus undrawn facilities fell to USD 143,531 thousand from USD 488,582 thousand at FY2024, and USD 72,524 thousand of that is undrawn capacity that includes a letters-of-credit-only line and German availability that exists only under the waiver's utilization cap. The Canadian Facility, USD 72,594 thousand drawn, matures in January 2027 and the German Facility, USD 267,837 thousand drawn, in September 2027, together USD 340,431 thousand, and the German leverage covenant is tested again at 2026-12-31. A miss could let the German lenders accelerate and cross-default the notes, so the bank facilities cannot be assumed to stay available to maturity.
Basis: calculation B3; Financial foundation (CP-1); 10-Q_2026-09-30_Q3-2026-Quarterly-Report.pdf, pages 11, 13, 44

Earnings are negative and the third-quarter improvement is not durable. Operating EBITDA was negative USD 36,145 thousand over the last twelve months, so cash interest of USD 110,562 thousand is uncovered, with cover at negative 0.33x against 2.31x in FY2024. Third-quarter Operating EBITDA improved by USD 25,226 thousand year on year, but a grant of USD 12,968 thousand and a smaller inventory write-down explain it; like for like, the quarter was negative USD 7,819 thousand against negative USD 7,677 thousand a year earlier (Analyst Judgement).
Basis: calculation B1; calculation B5; Financial foundation (CP-1)

The operating franchise is the main support, but it is not low-cost enough to carry the debt at current prices. Mercer has four kraft mills that the issuer describes as modern, surplus energy sales and no customer above 10% of revenue, and the pulp segment returned to a Segment Operating EBITDA of USD 5,890 thousand in the third quarter. Pulp products were 69.85% of FY2025 revenue and each USD 10 per tonne of realization is worth about USD 18,299 thousand a year on FY2025 volumes, about half of the trailing loss, so the franchise supports recovery only if prices or fiber costs turn.
Basis: calculation B7; calculation B8; calculation B11; Business & governance (CP-1A); 10-K_2025-12-31_2025-Annual-Report-on-Form-10-K.pdf, pages 8, 128; 10-Q_2026-09-30_Q3-2026-Quarterly-Report.pdf, page 31

The restructuring plan is the strongest near-term support and also the largest unknown. Noteholders holding about 87% of the notes and holders of about half the equity back a prepackaged plan that would repay the German Facility, add an asset-based facility of up to USD 300,000 thousand and raise USD 400,000 thousand of new cash through a backstopped rights offering. The plan is not confirmed, the chapter 11 cases had not started at 2026-11-05, and its effect on leverage is not established because no pro forma net debt is stated.
Basis: calculation B12; Business & governance (CP-1A); 10-Q_2026-09-30_Q3-2026-Quarterly-Report.pdf, pages 7, 8, 12, 51

Cash conversion is negative even after capital spending was cut. Operating cash flow after interest was negative USD 74,618 thousand over the last twelve months and free cash flow was negative USD 127,445 thousand, with nine-month capital spending of USD 38,674 thousand against USD 74,430 thousand and inventories absorbing USD 85,201 thousand. The cash burn is therefore operational and not only a capital-spending choice.
Basis: calculation B6; Financial foundation (CP-1); 10-Q_2026-09-30_Q3-2026-Quarterly-Report.pdf, page 6

Governance and financial policy are a medium risk that does not change the view. Mercer has no sponsor, paid dividends of USD 10,039 thousand in 2025 before suspending the dividend on 2025-07-31, and no priming, uptier or fee leakage is documented. Board and holder detail was not supplied, so this dimension stays provisional.
Basis: calculation B13; Business & governance (CP-1A)

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a distressed view is operating stabilisation. Third-quarter revenue fell only 3.80%, the pulp segment returned to USD 5,890 thousand of Segment Operating EBITDA from negative USD 12,686 thousand, solid wood revenue rose 9.73%, per unit fiber costs at the Canadian mills are expected to fall after Celgar's supply contracts were renegotiated, and the issuer expects NBSK prices to rise modestly in the fourth quarter. Liquidity of USD 143,531 thousand also exceeds the waiver's minimum of USD 30,000 thousand by USD 113,531 thousand, so cash is not yet exhausted. The view would soften if the plan is confirmed on the stated terms and removes the 2027 bank maturities, or if two quarters of positive Operating EBITDA without grants follow; it would harden if the covenant test at 2026-12-31 is missed without relief, if the plan terms change after 2026-11-05, or if German fiber costs rise while pulp prices stay flat.
Basis: calculation B3; calculation B7; calculation B11; 10-Q_2026-09-30_Q3-2026-Quarterly-Report.pdf, pages 3, 29, 30, 31, 44

### Company description

Mercer International Inc. is a Washington corporation headquartered in Vancouver that runs two segments from sites in Germany, Canada and the United States: pulp, with four kraft mills of about 2.1 million tonnes a year of capacity, 86% of it softwood grade, and solid wood, with sawmills, pallet and biofuel plants and mass timber plants. In FY2025 pulp products were 69.85% of revenue, lumber 13.25%, energy and chemicals 5.81% and pallets 5.36%, and Germany, China and the United States took 25.44%, 29.45% and 16.00% of sales. Mercer is a price-taker with dollar revenue and euro and Canadian dollar costs; the model gives low customer concentration but high cash-flow volatility and little revenue visibility, so debt service capacity depends on the price cycle and not on contracts.
Basis: calculation B7; calculation B16; Business & governance (CP-1A); 10-K_2025-12-31_2025-Annual-Report-on-Form-10-K.pdf, pages 7, 8, 124

### Ownership and group structure

Mercer is a listed company with no sponsor, a dispersed shareholder base and a few unnamed significant holders. The senior notes are unsecured parent obligations that the operating subsidiaries do not guarantee, while the subsidiaries that own the mills carry the secured Canadian Facility and the German Facility, so noteholders rank behind subsidiary and secured debt in the current structure and the waiver barred cash distributions from the German borrowers to the parent until 2026-09-30. A sponsor-free structure removes extraction risk but also any equity cure: dividends of USD 10,039 thousand were paid in 2025 and suspended on 2025-07-31, and the only new money now offered comes from the noteholders. Board composition and holder percentages are not established.
Basis: calculation B13; Business & governance (CP-1A); 10-K_2025-12-31_2025-Annual-Report-on-Form-10-K.pdf, pages 34, 57; 10-Q_2026-09-30_Q3-2026-Quarterly-Report.pdf, page 13

### Revenue drivers and pricing power

Revenue is cyclical and price-driven. NBSK realization fell to USD 671 per tonne in the third quarter from USD 728, down 7.83%, nine-month NBSK sales volume fell to 1,096.8 thousand tonnes from 1,135.4 thousand, and third-quarter pulp segment revenue fell 8.52% while solid wood revenue rose 9.73% on higher lumber and mass timber realizations. Pricing power is weak in pulp, where competition is on price; surplus energy and chemicals, 4.38% of FY2025 revenue, are the only stable stream. Revenue is neither contracted nor recurring, so a price fall passes straight to EBITDA.
Basis: calculation B7; 10-K_2025-12-31_2025-Annual-Report-on-Form-10-K.pdf, pages 9, 15; 10-Q_2026-09-30_Q3-2026-Quarterly-Report.pdf, pages 31, 32, 35, 36

### Cost structure and margin resilience

Fiber is about 55% of pulp cash production cost and about 75% of lumber cash production cost, and German fiber costs rose in 2025 on reduced harvest of beetle-infested wood, so margins depend on a cost Mercer cannot control. Pulp segment fiber expense was USD 507,842 thousand in the nine months of 2026, so a 5% rise adds USD 25,392.1 thousand of cost for nine months (arithmetic, not a forecast). Operating leverage is high because mills carry fixed costs, maintenance is expensed under US GAAP, planned downtime cost about USD 34,100 thousand of Segment Operating EBITDA in the third quarter, and a stronger dollar helped by about USD 5.0 million at the operating level in that quarter. Margins cannot be defended against a price fall without a cost offset; the identified levers are lower Canadian fiber costs and the Torgau cost actions.
Basis: calculation B9; calculation B16; 10-K_2025-12-31_2025-Annual-Report-on-Form-10-K.pdf, pages 19, 21; 10-Q_2026-09-30_Q3-2026-Quarterly-Report.pdf, pages 23, 33, 34

### Capital intensity and cash conversion

EBITDA is not converting to cash. Operating cash flow after interest was negative USD 74,618 thousand over the last twelve months and free cash flow negative USD 127,445 thousand, with capital spending of USD 52,827 thousand and inventories absorbing USD 85,201 thousand in the nine months of 2026. Capital spending was cut to USD 38,674 thousand from USD 74,430 thousand, which shows flexibility, but the maintenance share is not stated. Cash interest of USD 110,562 thousand is the largest fixed cash call and the senior notes alone carry coupons of USD 96,343.75 thousand a year, so EBITDA would have to rise by USD 146,707 thousand for cash interest to be covered once, and by USD 199,534 thousand to cover cash interest and capital spending together.
Basis: calculation B1; calculation B6; calculation B10; calculation B17; Financial foundation (CP-1); 10-Q_2026-09-30_Q3-2026-Quarterly-Report.pdf, page 6

### Competitive forces

Rivalry is intense and price-based. Mercer's NBSK competitors are large producers in Northern Europe and Canada, pulp is a commodity, and about 2.6 million tonnes of net new hardwood capacity is announced for 2026, which pressures prices and lengthens a downturn for a price-taker. Buyer power is high because customers can blend hardwood into their pulp mix, although strength requirements in tissue and lightweight paper limit substitution of softwood. Supplier power sits with fiber owners and is rising in Germany, where wood is also in demand as an energy source. Entry barriers are high because a pulp mill costs a great deal to build, Stendal having cost about USD 1.1 billion, and substitutes are digital media for printing paper, offset by tissue growth. The net effect is weak pricing power and persistent margin pressure, which is why debt capacity should be sized on trough and not peak earnings.
Basis: calculation B16; 10-K_2025-12-31_2025-Annual-Report-on-Form-10-K.pdf, pages 8, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 20

### Macro, currency and regulation

Three outside factors move credit quality. Currency matters because pulp is priced in dollars while many costs are in euros and Canadian dollars, and no derivatives were outstanding at FY2025, so a weaker dollar raises costs relative to revenue. German energy rules matter because surplus power is sold at regulated rates that expire between 2029 and 2034, which will lower a stable revenue stream. Trade policy matters because weak lumber markets and incremental government duties and tariffs led to sawmill curtailments and closures in 2025, which cut wood chip supply and raised per unit fiber costs at the Canadian pulp mills. Interest rates matter chiefly for lumber and mass timber, whose demand is tied to housing and construction finance.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_2025-Annual-Report-on-Form-10-K.pdf, pages 15, 16, 19, 22, 81; 10-Q_2026-09-30_Q3-2026-Quarterly-Report.pdf, pages 30, 45

### Strengths, weaknesses, opportunities and threats

Strengths that support credit: four kraft mills, described by the issuer as modern, with energy cogeneration, which lowers unit cost and adds a stable revenue line; low customer concentration; a pulp segment that earned positive Segment Operating EBITDA in the third quarter; and a noteholder group with a backstopped plan. Weaknesses that constrain it: negative Operating EBITDA and uncovered interest; liquidity below the 2027 bank maturities; pulp products at about seventy percent of revenue with prices outside Mercer's control; and unsecured parent notes behind subsidiary debt.
Basis: calculation B3; calculation B7; calculation B11; Financial foundation (CP-1); Business & governance (CP-1A)

Opportunities that could improve credit: confirmation of the plan with new money and a new asset-based facility; a modest NBSK price rise expected in the fourth quarter of 2026; lower Canadian fiber costs; and public support at the Peace River mill. Threats that could weaken it: a missed covenant test at 2026-12-31; failure to confirm the plan, which the issuer says could lead to a non-prepackaged chapter 11 case or liquidation; higher German fiber costs; and new hardwood capacity.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_Q3-2026-Quarterly-Report.pdf, pages 8, 9, 13, 30, 44; 10-K_2025-12-31_2025-Annual-Report-on-Form-10-K.pdf, page 14

### Strengths and weaknesses

Mercer's first strength is an energy-integrated pulp system that the issuer describes as modern. Four kraft mills with cogeneration sold surplus energy and chemicals worth USD 81,857 thousand in FY2025, which lowers unit cost against older mills and gives a revenue line unrelated to pulp prices; the weakness of this support is its size, about 4.38% of revenue.
Basis: calculation B7; calculation B16; 10-K_2025-12-31_2025-Annual-Report-on-Form-10-K.pdf, pages 8, 9

A second strength is breadth of customers and geography: no customer exceeded 10% of revenue and sales are split across Germany, China and the United States, so a single customer loss is unlikely to threaten debt service, although all regions are exposed to the same global price.
Basis: calculation B7; 10-K_2025-12-31_2025-Annual-Report-on-Form-10-K.pdf, pages 124, 128

A third strength is creditor support for the restructuring: noteholders holding about 87% of the notes have agreed to a plan that would put USD 400,000 thousand of new cash into the company and replace the 2027 bank maturities, which makes a negotiated outcome more likely than a disorderly default, but only if the court confirms the plan.
Basis: calculation B12; 10-Q_2026-09-30_Q3-2026-Quarterly-Report.pdf, pages 7, 8, 12

The first weakness is liquidity against maturities: USD 143,531 thousand of liquidity faces USD 340,431 thousand of facility maturities in 2027 and a covenant test at 2026-12-31, so refinancing cannot be done from internal resources and depends on the plan or a new waiver.
Basis: calculation B3; Financial foundation (CP-1); 10-Q_2026-09-30_Q3-2026-Quarterly-Report.pdf, pages 13, 43, 44

The second weakness is negative earnings and cash flow: Operating EBITDA of negative USD 36,145 thousand and free cash flow of negative USD 127,445 thousand over twelve months mean interest is paid from the cash balance and revolver, which cannot continue for long; the third-quarter gain does not change this.
Basis: calculation B1; calculation B5; calculation B6; Financial foundation (CP-1)

The third weakness is exposure to prices and fiber costs Mercer does not set: about 55% of pulp cash cost is fiber and each USD 10 per tonne of realization is worth about USD 18,299 thousand a year, so a small price move changes the debt service picture materially.
Basis: calculation B8; calculation B16; 10-K_2025-12-31_2025-Annual-Report-on-Form-10-K.pdf, pages 8, 19

### Outlook, tailwinds and headwinds

In the next 12 to 24 months the issuer expects to complete the restructuring by the end of 2026, NBSK prices to rise modestly in the fourth quarter, lumber prices to be flat, German fiber costs to rise slightly and Canadian fiber costs to fall. Tailwinds are supply curtailments and closures in the industry, a stronger dollar and the Canadian fiber contracts; headwinds are weak Chinese and European demand, the covenant test at 2026-12-31, the Rosenthal mill outage in the fourth quarter and the Torgau staged workforce reduction to about the second quarter of 2027. If the plan is confirmed, the 2027 bank maturities fall away and the benefit of the tailwinds accrues partly to the new owners; if it is not, the headwinds hit a company with cash of USD 71,007 thousand.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_Q3-2026-Quarterly-Report.pdf, pages 7, 29, 30, 33, 34, 44

Over three to five years the sector headwinds are new hardwood capacity of about 2.6 million tonnes announced for 2026, hardwood substitution in market pulp, and the expiry of German regulated energy rates between 2029 and 2034; the tailwinds are tissue demand in emerging markets and constrained softwood supply. Medium-term credit depends on whether prices recover enough to cover a reduced debt load, which cannot be assessed because the post-plan capital structure is not stated.
Basis: calculation B16; 10-K_2025-12-31_2025-Annual-Report-on-Form-10-K.pdf, pages 13, 14, 19

### Downside scenario

The issuer-specific downside is a failed bridge to the plan. The initiating condition is a covenant miss at 2026-12-31 or a delay in plan confirmation beyond the extended coupon grace period, which on a count from the due date runs to 2026-12-30, while NBSK prices stay near USD 1,625 per tonne in Europe and USD 645 per tonne in China and German fiber costs rise. The first operational break is at the German mills, where the waiver cap limits availability to USD 48,655 thousand and the German lenders could accelerate USD 267,837 thousand of borrowings. The financial transmission is that acceleration triggers cross-defaults on the Canadian Facility and the notes, USD 1,275,000 thousand of principal, while cash of USD 71,007 thousand covers only 0.74x of one year of note coupons of USD 96,343.75 thousand. A further USD 10 per tonne fall in pulp realization would cost about USD 18,299 thousand a year and a 5% rise in pulp fiber cost about USD 25,392.1 thousand over nine months, both labelled arithmetic and not a forecast. Management levers are already partly used: the dividend is suspended, capital spending is cut, the coupon grace period is extended and the Torgau cost actions are under way; the plan's USD 400,000 thousand of new money is the remaining lever. Resilience appears insufficient to avoid a liquidity event without the plan or a new waiver. The observable triggers are a covenant notice, a lender default notice, a petition filing and a change in plan terms.
Basis: calculation B3; calculation B8; calculation B9; calculation B10; 10-Q_2026-09-30_Q3-2026-Quarterly-Report.pdf, pages 11, 13, 29, 44

### Overall credit view

Overall, Mercer presents a weak business and financial profile that is not fully assessable beyond the restructuring, supported by energy-integrated mills that the issuer describes as modern, broad customers and noteholder backing for a plan, but constrained by negative earnings, uncovered interest and liquidity below the 2027 bank maturities. Cash-flow durability appears weak because free cash flow was negative USD 127,445 thousand over twelve months and the third-quarter gain came from a grant and a smaller write-down. Refinancing capacity is likely to depend on court confirmation of the plan and on the 2026-12-31 covenant test. The primary downside path is a missed covenant test or a delayed plan that lets the German lenders accelerate and cross-default the notes. Further analysis would require the executed plan documents, post-emergence financial projections, covenant calculations and any filing after 2026-11-05.
