## Findings

### Bottom line

Mercer's leverage and interest cover cannot be measured on current earnings: trailing twelve-month Operating EBITDA to 2026-09-30 is negative USD 36,145 thousand, so creditors are relying on the planned chapter 11 restructuring and not on cash generation.
Basis: calculation K7; 10-Q_2026-09-30_Q3-2026-Quarterly-Report.pdf, pages 8, 31

Net debt was USD 1,593,466 thousand at 2026-09-30, or USD 1,588,380 thousand pro forma for grant funds received in October 2026, against USD 1,298,476 thousand at the end of FY2024, when leverage was 6.09x on Operating EBITDA of USD 243,722 thousand. Cash fell to USD 71,007 thousand and undrawn facilities were USD 72,524 thousand, against USD 340,431 thousand of facility maturities in 2027. The Q3 gain in Operating EBITDA to negative USD 2,851 thousand includes a government grant of USD 12,968 thousand and is not a recovery.
Basis: calculation K1; calculation K3; calculation K14; calculation K15; calculation K20; calculation K22; 10-K_2024-12-31_2024-Annual-Report-on-Form-10-K.pdf, pages 61, 91; 10-Q_2026-09-30_Q3-2026-Quarterly-Report.pdf, pages 4, 9, 11, 13, 31, 43

### Leverage and coverage

Leverage was already elevated when earnings were positive. At FY2024 carrying debt of USD 1,483,401 thousand was 6.09x Operating EBITDA of USD 243,722 thousand, and net leverage was 5.33x after cash of USD 184,925 thousand. The issuer's definition adds back the goodwill write-off and the loss on the joint venture disposal, USD 57,922 thousand together; on operating income plus depreciation alone the FY2024 ratio would have been 7.98x, so 6.09x is the favourable reading. From FY2025 the measure fails: Operating EBITDA was negative USD 22,019 thousand and the twelve months to 2026-09-30 were negative USD 36,145 thousand, while debt rose to USD 1,664,473 thousand. Creditors can no longer size the debt against earnings, and it is being carried by the cash balance, revolver draws and now the restructuring.
Basis: calculation K3; calculation K4; calculation K6; calculation K7; calculation K17; calculation K16; 10-K_2024-12-31_2024-Annual-Report-on-Form-10-K.pdf, pages 61, 91, 108; 10-K_2025-12-31_2025-Annual-Report-on-Form-10-K.pdf, pages 63, 92; 10-Q_2026-09-30_Q3-2026-Quarterly-Report.pdf, pages 4, 11, 31

Cash interest paid was USD 110,562 thousand over the twelve months to 2026-09-30 against negative Operating EBITDA, so EBITDA cover was negative 0.33x, compared with 2.31x in FY2024 and negative 0.20x in FY2025. The senior notes alone carry cash coupons of USD 96,343.75 thousand a year, and cash of USD 71,007 thousand covers 0.74x of one year of those coupons. The 2028 notes coupon of about USD 25,800 thousand due 2026-10-01 was not paid, and on 2026-10-30 the grace period was lengthened from 30 days to 90 days, which on a count from the due date runs to 2026-12-30. Interest is therefore being paid or deferred out of liquidity and not out of earnings, and the extended grace period makes late December the first dated test of the restructuring timetable.
Basis: calculation K8; calculation K16; calculation K21; 10-K_2024-12-31_2024-Annual-Report-on-Form-10-K.pdf, page 94; 10-K_2025-12-31_2025-Annual-Report-on-Form-10-K.pdf, page 95; 10-Q_2026-09-30_Q3-2026-Quarterly-Report.pdf, pages 6, 12, 13

The strongest contrary evidence is the third quarter: Operating EBITDA improved by USD 25,226 thousand year on year to negative USD 2,851 thousand, and capital spending was cut. The improvement is explained by two items and not by operations: a government grant of USD 12,968 thousand credited to cost of sales, and a smaller inventory write-down, USD 8,000 thousand against USD 20,400 thousand a year earlier. Adding back the write-downs and removing the grant gives negative USD 7,819 thousand against negative USD 7,677 thousand a year earlier, an unchanged run rate (Analyst Judgement, not an issuer measure; inventory write-downs recurred in every year shown, so the reported figure stays the denominator). The view would improve with two consecutive quarters of positive Operating EBITDA that include no grant and write-downs at or below the third-quarter level, and with the German Facility, whose margin was raised to a range of 2.50% to 4.25% under the waiver, repaid under the plan.
Basis: calculation K22; 10-Q_2026-09-30_Q3-2026-Quarterly-Report.pdf, pages 9, 10, 12, 13, 31, 33

### Cash flow quality and the latest quarter

Cash flow shows that the earnings problem is a cash problem. Operating cash flow, which is after cash interest, was negative USD 74,618 thousand over the twelve months to 2026-09-30, and after capital spending of USD 52,827 thousand free cash flow was negative USD 127,445 thousand; in FY2024 it was positive USD 5,886 thousand. FY2025 operating cash flow of USD 8,587 thousand was positive only after a receivables release of USD 52,588 thousand, and in the nine months of 2026 the inventory build absorbed USD 85,201 thousand even though USD 59,000 thousand of inventory was written down. Nine-month capital spending was cut 48.04% to USD 38,674 thousand, which flatters free cash flow: the sources give no maintenance and growth split, so how much of the cut is deferral is not established.
Basis: calculation K10; calculation K25; 10-K_2024-12-31_2024-Annual-Report-on-Form-10-K.pdf, page 94; 10-K_2025-12-31_2025-Annual-Report-on-Form-10-K.pdf, page 95; 10-Q_2026-09-30_Q3-2026-Quarterly-Report.pdf, pages 6, 10

The nine-month outflow was met from cash and borrowing. Cash and restricted cash fell by USD 110,798 thousand, revolving facilities were drawn for USD 55,675 thousand net and the new repayable government contribution added USD 6,034 thousand; dividends stopped after USD 10,039 thousand was paid in 2025, when Operating EBITDA was already negative. Customer deposits for mass timber contracts, a small non-debt funding source, fell from USD 16,434 thousand at FY2025 to USD 10,446 thousand, a drop of 36.44%, which removes a further source of working capital as projects complete. The pattern is a business drawing down its remaining facility headroom to fund working capital and interest, which is why the maturity profile in the next section matters.
Basis: calculation K25; 10-K_2025-12-31_2025-Annual-Report-on-Form-10-K.pdf, pages 95, 106; 10-Q_2026-09-30_Q3-2026-Quarterly-Report.pdf, pages 6, 10

In the third quarter revenue fell 3.80% to USD 440,668 thousand on lower pulp volumes and realizations, with softwood pulp realizations at USD 671 per tonne against USD 728 a year earlier. Interest expense rose 13.39% to USD 32,323 thousand, reflecting higher borrowings and the higher German Facility margin under the waiver, and operating cash flow was negative USD 19,992 thousand. The fourth quarter opened with a further cost: the Rosenthal mill lost 24 days to unplanned downtime after its annual maintenance, which the quarterly report states without quantifying the earnings effect.
Basis: calculation K25; 10-Q_2026-09-30_Q3-2026-Quarterly-Report.pdf, pages 3, 6, 32, 34

### Liquidity and maturity wall

Liquidity is the binding constraint. Cash plus undrawn facilities fell from USD 488,582 thousand at FY2024 to USD 430,364 thousand at FY2025 and USD 143,531 thousand at 2026-09-30, which is 8.62% of carrying debt against 32.94% at FY2024. The USD 72,524 thousand of undrawn capacity is flattered in three ways: USD 6,962 thousand is a letters-of-credit-only line that cannot fund cash needs, the German Facility availability of USD 48,655 thousand exists only after the waiver caps utilization at EUR 300.0 million while German leverage exceeds 2.00x, and USD 5,000 thousand of cash is restricted collateral and is excluded from the cash figure. The waiver also requires average liquidity of at least USD 30,000 thousand, tested monthly over a rolling 13-week period, so the headroom over that floor is USD 113,531 thousand at the quarter end (Analyst Judgement).
Basis: calculation K13; calculation K14; 10-K_2024-12-31_2024-Annual-Report-on-Form-10-K.pdf, page 69; 10-K_2025-12-31_2025-Annual-Report-on-Form-10-K.pdf, page 72; 10-Q_2026-09-30_Q3-2026-Quarterly-Report.pdf, pages 13, 43, 44

The 2027 maturities exceed liquidity. The Canadian Facility, USD 72,594 thousand drawn, falls due in January 2027 and the German Facility, USD 267,837 thousand drawn, in September 2027, together USD 340,431 thousand against liquidity of USD 143,531 thousand, a shortfall of USD 196,900 thousand; both were classified as current at 2026-09-30, lifting current debt to USD 354,703 thousand. The senior notes then fall due in October 2028, USD 400,000 thousand, and February 2029, USD 875,000 thousand. Management stated in the second-quarter report that it was probable the German leverage covenant would not be met at 2026-12-31, when testing resumes, and an unwaived breach could lead to acceleration and cross-defaults, so the facilities cannot be assumed to stay available to their maturity. Under the restructuring support agreements the German Facility would be repaid and terminated, the Canadian Facility reduced by at least USD 41,000 thousand, a new asset-based facility of up to USD 300,000 thousand put in place and USD 400,000 thousand of new money raised through the rights offering, none of which is in the 2026-09-30 balance sheet.
Basis: calculation K15; calculation K26; 10-Q_2026-06-30_Q2-2026-Quarterly-Report.pdf, page 11; 10-Q_2026-09-30_Q3-2026-Quarterly-Report.pdf, pages 4, 11, 12, 13, 44

Contrary evidence is that the plan leaves general unsecured creditors unimpaired and holders of about 87% of the senior notes principal have signed support agreements, so the near-term outcome points to a negotiated deleveraging and not a disorderly default, while cash of USD 71,007 thousand and undrawn capacity of USD 72,524 thousand cover ordinary-course obligations on the issuer's own statement. What would change the view is the filing of the chapter 11 cases and court approval of the rights offering and the new asset-based facility; until then the sources do not establish the petition date, any debtor-in-possession financing or the outcome of the extended interest grace period.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_Q3-2026-Quarterly-Report.pdf, pages 7, 8, 12, 43, 44

### Perimeter, definitions and subsequent events

The statements are consolidated for Mercer International Inc. and all its subsidiaries, but the senior notes are the parent's unsecured obligations and are not guaranteed by the operating subsidiaries that own the mills. The notes therefore rank behind secured debt and all subsidiary debt and liabilities, including the Canadian Facility, which has a first-priority charge over substantially all borrower assets, and the German Facility, whose waiver provides for security over certain German assets. Consolidated cash and facility figures overstate what is available at the parent, because the waiver barred distributions from the German borrowers to the parent until 2026-09-30 with limited exceptions, and no parent-only statements were supplied to size the gap.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_2025-Annual-Report-on-Form-10-K.pdf, pages 34, 35; 10-Q_2026-09-30_Q3-2026-Quarterly-Report.pdf, pages 13, 44

Three definitions drive the figures. Operating EBITDA is the issuer's own non-GAAP measure and the only EBITDA it states; it adds back long-lived asset impairments, USD 215,682 thousand in FY2025 and USD 57,922 thousand of impairment and disposal items in FY2024, but not inventory write-downs, which were USD 54,400 thousand in FY2025 and USD 59,000 thousand in the nine months of 2026 and stay in the figure. Total debt is carrying value including the finance lease liability of USD 48,203 thousand that the issuer carries in its debt lines, while operating lease liabilities of USD 5,731 thousand are separate and excluded. The issuer's available-capacity figure of USD 72,524 thousand is used as stated for liquidity even though it includes a letters-of-credit-only line.
Basis: calculation K13; calculation K17; 10-K_2025-12-31_2025-Annual-Report-on-Form-10-K.pdf, pages 63, 105; 10-Q_2026-09-30_Q3-2026-Quarterly-Report.pdf, pages 4, 10, 11, 28

Five events after 2026-09-30 are shown beside and not inside the period figures: the 2028 notes coupon not paid on 2026-10-01, the second supplemental indenture of 2026-10-30 extending the grace period, receipt in October 2026 of USD 5,086 thousand of the Peace River grant, the restructuring support agreements of 2026-11-02, and an unplanned 24-day outage at the Rosenthal mill in the fourth quarter. Only the grant receipt changes net debt on a stated amount, giving pro forma net debt of USD 1,588,380 thousand; the support agreements are conditional on court confirmation, so no pro forma figure is stated for them.
Basis: calculation K20; 10-Q_2026-09-30_Q3-2026-Quarterly-Report.pdf, pages 7, 9, 12, 13, 34

### Data quality and limits

Four presentation differences need care. The current finance lease liability sits in accounts payable and other in the annual reports and in current debt in the quarterly reports; the FY2024 joint venture investment is folded into other long-term assets in the FY2025 report; the German Facility is non-current at 2026-03-31 and current at the two later quarter ends; and cash in the interim cash flow statements includes restricted cash. Each is shown as filed with the other presentation beside it, and none changes a total. Items the sources do not state, namely interest income, a maintenance and growth capex split, an adjusted EBITDA, parent-only statements and the discrete first and second quarter flows, are listed under Gaps and are not estimated.
