## Findings

### Bottom line

Elanco is an adequate but stretched credit that is improving: adjusted net leverage has fallen to 3.36x mainly on higher earnings, yet liquidity of USD 1,280 million only just covers the USD 1,236 million due in 2028, so the 2027 refinancing is the test. A competitor has sold a generic Seresto collar in the United States since January 2026, so revenue durability, not near-term debt service, is the main business risk.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_elan-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 6–7; 10-Q_2026-06-30_elan-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 12; 10-Q_2026-06-30_elan-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 36; 10-K_2025-12-31_elan-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 28; Financial foundation (CP-1); calculation C01; calculation C02; calculation C18

The primary downside path is generic price pressure on parasiticides and a fade in launch volume while restructuring and acquisition cash arrive before savings, stalling deleveraging ahead of the 2028 maturities. Covenant headroom, legal priority and any rating are not established, and this review assigns no rating.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against caution is momentum and the pace of deleveraging. Half-year adjusted EBITDA rose 21.01%, the issuer reports share gains for Zenrelia and Credelio Quattro, and its year-end target of about 3.0x needs only about USD 91.50 million less issuer net debt at the guidance midpoint, against USD 173 million of free cash flow in the second half of 2025. Guidance has been raised twice since February: the 2026 adjusted EBITDA range was USD 955 million to USD 985 million in February, USD 975 million to USD 1,005 million in May and is now USD 1,010 million to USD 1,035 million, so the slower second-half growth it implies may simply be conservative. Cash taxes rose to USD 225 million in 2025 mainly because of tax payments on the aqua sale gain, so FY2025 free cash flow likely understated the run rate, although the size of the one-off part is not stated.

### Business model and ownership

Elanco sells animal health products in more than 90 countries through one reportable segment, with Pet Health at 48.78% of FY2025 revenue (parasiticides, dermatology, vaccines and pain) and Farm Animal at 50.10% (feed additives, antibiotics, vaccines and enzymes for cattle, poultry, swine and sheep). Sales go through veterinary distributors, retailers and directly to producers, and the sections read give no contract duration or renewal rates, so revenue visibility rests on prescribing habits, brands and seasonal demand rather than on contracted backlog. For creditors, balanced end markets and a regulated animal-health product set support cash flow, while first-half seasonality, with 51.62% of FY2025 revenue arriving in the first half, makes cash and working capital swing within the year and makes the first half the season that decides the full-year debt service picture.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_elan-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 8; 10-K_2025-12-31_elan-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 12–13; Business & governance (CP-1A); calculation C23

Elanco is a listed Indiana corporation separated from Eli Lilly in 2019, with no sponsor and no controlling holder identified; holders above five percent, board independence and related-person dealings sit in a proxy statement that is not supplied. The credit facilities are secured by a significant part of the group's assets and the 2028 notes are senior unsecured, so the structure ranks the notes behind secured lenders to the extent of the collateral, and the annual report says a default under the credit facilities could stop payments on the notes through a cash sweep; guarantor scope and the priority among secured facilities are not established. Financial policy so far has favoured creditors: no dividends are anticipated, although the policy may change at any time, the facilities restrict dividends, debt and liens, and the aqua proceeds repaid term loans. The offsetting signals are the issuer's statement that leverage below 3x unlocks greater capital allocation flexibility, the growth in claims outside the debt figure and the annual report's reference to activist shareholders as a risk to strategy; the governance risk level from Business & governance is medium because the evidence is incomplete rather than adverse.

### Revenue durability and pricing power

Revenue is recurring and seasonal, moderately cyclical and concentrated, with no disclosed contract backlog. Price added 2% and volume 5% to FY2025 revenue growth of 6% after a 2% drag from the aqua sale, and in the half-year to 2026-06-30 volume added 7% and price 2%, led by Zenrelia, Credelio Quattro and AdTab with part of the volume from new customers. Growth that depends on launches is only as durable as clinic adoption of each product, so the credit implication is that earnings rest on the pipeline continuing to replace franchises that are losing protection. Concentration adds fragility: five products and families supply about 38% of revenue, the largest customer, an affiliate of Cencora, about 12% and the second distributor about 7%, and the same largest customer owed USD 131 million, 12.16% of receivables, at 2026-06-30, so a change in one distributor's ordering or payment terms would move revenue and cash quickly.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_elan-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 9; 10-K_2025-12-31_elan-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 13; 10-K_2025-12-31_elan-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 78; 10-Q_2026-06-30_elan-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 10; 10-Q_2026-06-30_elan-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 32; Business & governance (CP-1A); calculation C08; calculation C07

Pricing power is modest. Price added 2% in both periods, and the annual report warns that generic competition may force lower prices, discounts or rebates; sales rebates and discounts were a liability of USD 450 million at 2026-06-30 and the auditor treats United States rebate estimates as a critical audit matter, so a change in accruals would move revenue and cash. Farm Animal revenue is exposed to regulation and consumer sentiment: shared-class antibiotics were 9% and animal-only antibiotics 15% of FY2025 revenue, with 89% of the animal-only amount from ionophores, and the European Union has restricted preventative antibiotic use since 2022. Revenue is not structurally declining on the evidence, with reported half-year growth of 12.53% and second-quarter organic constant-currency growth of 8%, but the Seresto exposure described in the key findings is the clearest case of a franchise that is already facing substitution.

### Margin resilience and cost flexibility

Margins can be defended against moderate volume pressure but not against a sharp loss of parasiticide revenue. Gross margin was 55.0% in FY2025 against 54.9% in FY2024, with price and productivity offset by inflation and higher manufacturing costs, and 57.79% in the half-year against 57.40%, helped by mix and price despite higher inventory costs; the issuer guides adjusted gross margin to 55.2% to 55.6% for 2026. In the half-year 35.41% of the extra revenue reached adjusted EBITDA, which is operating leverage on the way up. The reverse follows on the way down: gross profit is 57.79% of revenue, so each USD 100 million of lost revenue removes about USD 57.79 million of gross profit, while selling costs of USD 830 million grew 12.01%, nearly as fast as revenue, partly to support launches and higher pay.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_elan-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 78; 10-Q_2026-06-30_elan-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 32–33; EX-99.1_2026-08-05_elanco-20260630exhibit991.htm.jsonl, page 3; EX-99.1_2026-08-05_elanco-20260630exhibit991.htm.jsonl, page 4; calculation C09

### Overall credit view

Overall, Elanco presents an adequate business profile supported by balanced pet and livestock revenue, launch-led volume growth and creditor-aligned behaviour, but credit quality is constrained by adjusted net leverage of 3.36x, concentration in a few products and one distributor, and claims outside the debt figure. Cash-flow durability appears adequate due to free cash flow of USD 379 million over the last twelve months and light scheduled principal to 2028, although interest cover is flat. Refinancing capacity is likely to depend on holding parasiticide earnings through the 2027 generic season and on market access for USD 1,236 million due in 2028. The primary downside path is generic erosion of Seresto and Advantage Family earnings coinciding with that refinancing. Further analysis would require the credit agreement and indenture, the proxy statement, rating reports, peer figures and the third-quarter 2026 report. Whether the business profile is better or worse than typical leveraged-credit issuers in the sector is not established because no peers are supplied, and the financial profile trend is improving.
