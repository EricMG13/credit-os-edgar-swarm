## Findings

### Bottom line

Terex's creditor risk lies in management's financial policy, not in a controlling owner: no controlling shareholder is disclosed, yet two large acquisitions since October 2024 and a new USD 500 million buyback authorisation test that policy.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_tex.htm.jsonl, page 83; 10-K_2024-12-31_tex.htm.jsonl, page 114; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tex.htm.jsonl, page 19; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tex.htm.jsonl, page 34

Creditors are protected by funding mix and diversification: 83.6% of the REV consideration was paid in shares, no customer exceeds 10% of sales and the largest is below 6%. They remain exposed to an unresolved review of Aerials, which is 30.0% of nine-month 2026 sales, and to buybacks that resumed in Q3 2026.
Basis: calculation B5; calculation B1; 10-K_2025-12-31_tex.htm.jsonl, page 18; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tex.htm.jsonl, page 49

### Key findings

No controlling owner is disclosed in the supplied filings, so creditors face a public-company board and management rather than a sponsor, and the usual tests of sponsor support or extraction do not apply. The REV merger gave former REV holders shares equal to 42.1% of those outstanding on 2026-02-10. No block holder is named in the supplied filings, and holders above 5%, board independence and related-party dealings sit in a proxy statement that was not supplied.
Basis: calculation B6; 10-K_2025-12-31_tex.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-K_2025-12-31_tex.htm.jsonl, page 83; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tex.htm.jsonl, page 19; Source readiness (CP-0)

The business is more diversified by product and customer than by region, and REV has made it more North American. Four segments each carry 20.5% to 30.0% of nine-month 2026 net sales, no customer exceeds 10% and the largest is below 6%, yet North America produced 81.1% of nine-month 2026 sales against 72.3% in FY2025. The customer figures predate REV.
Basis: calculation B1; calculation B4; 10-K_2025-12-31_tex.htm.jsonl, page 18; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tex.htm.jsonl, pages 15–17

Aerials is the weak earner and the asset under review. It supplies 30.0% of nine-month 2026 sales but only 12.3% of segment Adjusted EBITDA, at a 5.4% margin against 15.0% to 17.8% elsewhere, and its Adjusted EBITDA fell USD 31 million year on year, which the company attributes to tariff costs, unfavourable mix and inflation. Without Aerials the nine-month Adjusted EBITDA margin would be 14.5% against 11.8%, before any reallocation of corporate costs, but a sale would also remove the largest sales base, and no timetable, price or use of proceeds has been disclosed a year after the review was announced alongside the REV merger.
Basis: calculation B1; calculation B3; calculation B11; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tex.htm.jsonl, pages 41–42; 10-K_2025-12-31_tex.htm.jsonl, page 26; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tex.htm.jsonl, page 52

The two acquisitions were funded very differently, and REV was the more creditor-friendly one on funding mix. The Environmental Solutions Group cost USD 2,010 million in cash and was financed with USD 2,000 million of new notes and term loan plus cash on hand, while 83.6% of the REV consideration was paid in shares and the cash element was USD 548 million, including USD 122 million of REV bank debt repaid at closing. The first-quarter 2026 cash flow shows cash falling and new borrowing, but no sentence names the source of that cash.
Basis: calculation B7; calculation B5; 10-K_2024-12-31_tex.htm.jsonl, page 27; 10-K_2024-12-31_tex.htm.jsonl, page 114; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tex.htm.jsonl, page 19; 10-Q_2026-03-31_tex.htm.jsonl, page 6; 10-Q_2026-03-31_tex.htm.jsonl, page 37

Shareholder returns are resuming while the REV purchase accounting is still provisional. Nine-month 2026 dividends of USD 58 million and buybacks of USD 75 million took 52.6% of free cash flow of USD 253 million, buybacks restarted in Q3 after none in the first half, and an October 2026 authorisation of up to USD 500 million lifts repurchase capacity to USD 610.8 million. The debt agreements restrict dividends, but their limits were not supplied.
Basis: calculation B8; calculation B9; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tex.htm.jsonl, page 7; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tex.htm.jsonl, page 34; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tex.htm.jsonl, page 48; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tex.htm.jsonl, page 49; 10-K_2025-12-31_tex.htm.jsonl, page 54; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tex.htm.jsonl, page 27

Governance and reporting are conventional and clean on the evidence available, but untestable for creditor protections. The chairman is non-executive and separate from the chief executive, the auditor gave unqualified opinions on the FY2025 statements and internal control with one critical audit matter on inventory quantities in Aerials and Materials Processing, and the 2026 reports add REV controls from closing. Related-party dealings, director independence, pay and the executed debt documents were not supplied.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_tex.htm.jsonl, page 83; 10-K_2025-12-31_tex.htm.jsonl, page 90; 10-K_2025-12-31_tex.htm.jsonl, page 91; 10-K_2025-12-31_tex.htm.jsonl, page 93; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tex.htm.jsonl, page 52; Source readiness (CP-0)

Management's claims on REV are not yet evidence. The second-quarter report says integration is progressing as planned and the FY2025 report says ESG integration is complete and ahead of its USD 25 million synergy commitment, but no REV synergy figure is given, the 2.5x net leverage target of the FY2024 report is no longer stated, and the 2026 outlook of USD 930 million to USD 1 billion of EBITDA predates three quarters of results.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_tex.htm.jsonl, page 32; 10-K_2025-12-31_tex.htm.jsonl, page 57; 10-K_2024-12-31_tex.htm.jsonl, page 11; 10-K_2025-12-31_tex.htm.jsonl, page 11; 10-K_2025-12-31_tex.htm.jsonl, page 59

Overall governance and financial-policy risk is medium. The supplied filings show no extraction, related-party payment or creditor-adverse transaction and a mostly share-funded merger, set against debt-funded growth in 2024, resumed buybacks, an open portfolio review and legal limits that cannot be tested.
Basis: calculation B5; calculation B7; calculation B8; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tex.htm.jsonl, page 34

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest case for a more positive view is that the REV merger was paid mostly in shares, the revolver was undrawn at 2026-09-30, no single customer dominates and no distribution is shown to have been debt-funded. Management also reports Q3 free cash flow of USD 209 million and a backlog of USD 7.0 billion, and the auditor found internal control effective at 2025-12-31. On that evidence the risk could be judged low.
Basis: calculation B5; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tex.htm.jsonl, page 25; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tex.htm.jsonl, page 35; 10-K_2025-12-31_tex.htm.jsonl, page 91

The view would worsen if the executed credit agreement shows wide capacity for restricted payments, if buybacks are funded from revolver drawings or run ahead of free cash flow, if proceeds of an Aerials sale go to shareholders rather than to debt, or if the REV purchase accounting moves materially again. It would improve if the company restates a numeric leverage target, commits sale proceeds to debt reduction or discloses covenant headroom.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_tex.htm.jsonl, page 27; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tex.htm.jsonl, page 34; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tex.htm.jsonl, page 20; 10-K_2024-12-31_tex.htm.jsonl, page 11

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

Two large acquisitions and several disposals have shaped the group since 2024. On 2024-10-08 Terex bought the Environmental Solutions Group from Dover Corporation for USD 2,010 million in cash, financed with USD 750 million of 6.25% senior notes due 2032 issued at par, a new USD 1,250 million term loan due 2031-10-08 and cash on hand; the revolver was enlarged to USD 800 million and extended to 2029-10-08 at the same time.
Basis: 10-K_2024-12-31_tex.htm.jsonl, page 27; 10-K_2024-12-31_tex.htm.jsonl, page 114; 10-Q_2026-03-31_tex.htm.jsonl, page 23; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tex.htm.jsonl, page 26

On 2025-10-29 Terex agreed to acquire REV Group, Inc. in a stock-and-cash merger, which closed on 2026-02-02 with Terex taking 100% of REV's stock. Provisional consideration is USD 3,384 million in the 2026 reports and USD 3,262 million in the FY2025 annual report: USD 2,828 million in Terex shares valued at USD 58.99 each, USD 426 million of cash at USD 8.71 per REV share, USD 122 million of REV bank debt repaid at closing and USD 8 million for converted awards. REV added USD 1,744 million of net sales and USD 17 million of net income to 2026-09-30, goodwill of USD 1,428 million was assigned to Specialty Vehicles, and the purchase accounting remains provisional.
Basis: calculation B5; calculation B10; 10-K_2025-12-31_tex.htm.jsonl, page 137; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tex.htm.jsonl, page 8; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tex.htm.jsonl, page 19; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tex.htm.jsonl, page 20; 10-Q_2026-09-30_tex.htm.jsonl, page 21
