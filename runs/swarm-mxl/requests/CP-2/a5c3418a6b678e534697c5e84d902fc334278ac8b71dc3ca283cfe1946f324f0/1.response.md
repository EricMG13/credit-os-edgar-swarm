## Findings

### Bottom line

MaxLinear's credit profile is improving but unproven: data center demand has turned last-twelve-month EBITDA positive on one quarter, while cash flow is negative and a litigation claim is unpriced, so creditors should not yet treat the credit as stable.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); Business & governance (CP-1A); calculation CALC-01; calculation CALC-02

At September 2026 total debt was 4.43x calculated last-twelve-months EBITDA, net debt 2.24x and cash interest cover 4.35x, with all USD 165.0 million of debt due in 2028, but that EBITDA is calculated rather than stated and the fourth-quarter outlook of USD 240 million to USD 260 million of revenue has to be met to confirm it.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); 10-Q_2026-09-30_mxl-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 19–23; EX-99.1_2026-10-22_a09302026exhibit991.htm.jsonl, page 2; calculation CALC-01; calculation CALC-09

### Key findings

Revenue durability is average: growth is fast but rests on a narrow group of data center programs and on customers who buy without long-term commitments. Revenue rose 58.24% to USD 524.1 million in the first nine months of 2026, infrastructure became 52% of sales from 31% a year earlier, and the ten largest customers were 56% of revenue, yet revenue fell 48.00% in 2024 before it recovered, so the cycle can reverse as fast as it rose. Management itself lists the sustainability of hyperscale capital spending as a risk to its growth.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_mxl-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 34–36, 46–47, 50; EX-99.1_2026-10-22_a09302026exhibit991.htm.jsonl, page 3; Financial foundation (CP-1); calculation CALC-03

Margin resilience is average: gross margin is high and steady, but operating profit depends on revenue staying above a large fixed cost base. Gross margin was 58.12% in the nine months against 54.03% in 2024, yet research and development alone is 32.41% of revenue, and at the third-quarter cost base operating break-even revenue is about USD 166,591 thousand a quarter, 23.60% below third-quarter revenue. A larger fall would return the company to operating losses, as in 2025 when the operating margin was -27.13%.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_mxl-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 4–5, 51–53; Financial foundation (CP-1); calculation CALC-04; calculation CALC-05

The revolver, drawn by USD 40.0 million, is financing the build.

The strongest evidence against caution is that the third quarter may be the run rate, not a spike. Revenue of USD 168.8 million in the second quarter and USD 218.1 million in the third landed inside the outlooks of USD 160 million to USD 170 million and USD 210 million to USD 220 million that management gave a quarter earlier, the fourth-quarter outlook is 10.06% to 19.23% above third-quarter revenue, and if fourth-quarter EBITDA only equalled the third quarter's, last-twelve-months EBITDA would reach USD 81,953 thousand and total debt would be about 2.00x it. The term loan is valued at 98.72% of par by the company's own estimate and no debt is due before 2028.

### Business, ownership and revenue durability

MaxLinear designs communications chips and outsources all manufacturing, so its revenue depends on winning design slots at a few large customers rather than on owned capacity. The infrastructure line, which management ties to optical products for data centers, high-performance analog and wireless backhaul, was 57% of third-quarter revenue, broadband fell as customers moved to a new standard, and the company reports one operating segment. Product life cycles of 24 to 60 months give some visibility once a design is won, but customers buy on purchase orders, distributors were 48% of third-quarter revenue, the company carries accrued price protection of USD 30,367 thousand, and price changes had no material effect on revenue. Revenue is therefore cyclical and concentrated, not contractual, and supports debt service only while data center demand lasts.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_mxl-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 8, 34–36, 46–47, 50–51; Business & governance (CP-1A)

Ownership is public with no declared sponsor, so the credit does not carry sponsor leverage, fee or exit-pressure risk, but holder percentages and board independence are not supplied. The annual report describes a classified board and Delaware takeover provisions that make a hostile leveraged transaction harder, one person holds the chairman and chief executive roles with a lead director named, and the company has never paid a dividend. The main ownership-related exposure is the claim from the terminated Silicon Motion merger and the stockholder suits that followed it.
Basis: Business & governance (CP-1A); 10-K_2025-12-31_mxl-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 87–88, 103, 133

### Margin resilience and cash conversion

Cost of revenue is largely variable, because wafers, assembly and test are bought from third parties, but operating expenses are fixed in the short run: research and development of USD 60,642 thousand and selling, general and administrative expense of USD 36,947 thousand in the third quarter total USD 97,843 thousand with restructuring, against gross profit of USD 128,070 thousand. That fixed base gives strong operating leverage on the way up, as the third-quarter operating margin of 13.86% against 9M 1.69% shows, and equal leverage on the way down. Margins can be defended against modest volume pressure but not against a fall of more than about a quarter in revenue, and inventory write-downs from oversupply would hit gross margin first, as the company warns.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_mxl-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 4–5, 51–54; 10-K_2025-12-31_mxl-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 82; calculation CALC-04; calculation CALC-05

Capital intensity is low, with property and equipment and intangible purchases of USD 15,035 thousand in the nine months, but working capital is the swing factor: inventory rose from USD 78,104 thousand to USD 113,722 thousand and prepaid and other current assets from USD 50,390 thousand to USD 121,648 thousand between December 2025 and September 2026, so free cash flow was an outflow of USD 32,688 thousand despite positive EBITDA. Cash interest of USD 8,508 thousand over the last twelve months is small against EBITDA, so the cash constraint is the supply build, not interest. Lease liabilities of USD 21,943 thousand and restructuring cash payments of USD 12,800 thousand in the nine months are further, smaller calls on cash.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_mxl-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 4, 6–7, 14–15, 37, 62; Financial foundation (CP-1); calculation CALC-01; calculation CALC-06

### Competitive and macro forces

Competitive rivalry is intense: the annual report names thirteen merchant semiconductor competitors including Broadcom, Marvell, Credo and MediaTek, several far larger, and notes that some module makers supply components in house. Buyer power is high, with the ten largest customers at 56% of revenue and one customer at 10%; supplier power is high too, with one supplier at 54% of inventory purchases, key foundries in Taiwan and prepayments that tie cash to wafer capacity. Entry and substitution threats are lower in the near term because design wins take long to move, but customers can develop their own products or adopt a competitor's. Each force bears on the same creditor outcome: gross margin and cash conversion in a downturn.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_mxl-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 25–26, 51, 82; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mxl-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 33–34, 46

Trade and geopolitical factors are material, so the PEST view applies. Export controls have already led the company to restrict or curtail business with certain customers and partners in China, demand in China has weakened, tariffs could pressure average selling prices as customers offset their own costs, and Taiwan-China developments could affect supply from Taiwan, where key foundry capacity sits. Shipments to Asia were 85% of nine-month revenue, so a trade or export-control shock would hit volume and supply together; the Singapore tax incentive runs only to March 2027, a modest cash tax risk.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_mxl-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 28, 34, 47; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mxl-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 33–34, 56

In credit terms, the opportunities are the ramp of 800G and 1.6T optical and electrical products for AI data centers, which management says is converging with several growth drivers over the next two years, and diversification of infrastructure revenue beyond one product family. The threats are a hyperscale capital spending pullback, a competitor win at a key customer, export-control or tariff escalation and an inventory write-down; the strengths and weaknesses are ranked in the next section.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-07-23_a06302026exhibit991.htm.jsonl, page 1; EX-99.1_2026-10-22_a09302026exhibit991.htm.jsonl, pages 1, 3

### Financial profile and credit quality

Business diversification is weak: there are four end markets, but customers, one supplier and one region each carry a concentration. Cost and capex flexibility is average, with low capex offset by fixed research spending and non-cancellable commitments; margin stability is average, since gross margin stayed within a 54.03% to 58.12% band across 2023 to 2026 while operating margin swung between -61.95% and 13.86%.

Credit supports are the growth inflection, low capital intensity, no sponsor and 2028 maturities; constraints are cash conversion, concentration and one-quarter earnings; the trend is improving; missing datapoints are the credit agreement, ratings and customer identity.

### Outlook, tailwinds and headwinds

For the next 12 to 24 months the company guides fourth-quarter revenue of USD 240 million to USD 260 million, so the near-term trend is growth, with margin expansion as fixed costs are spread, and EBITDA likely to stay positive if the outlook is met; tailwinds are data center optical demand and a larger revolver, headwinds are working-capital build, commitments that rose 65.84% in nine months and the unresolved litigation. Over three to five years management describes a multi-year growth phase, which is management language and not evidenced beyond the next two years; the sector is cyclical, as the 48.00% revenue fall in 2024 shows, so a medium-term credit view should assume at least one down cycle before the 2028 maturities are refinanced.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-04-22_a03312026exhibit991.htm.jsonl, page 2; EX-99.1_2026-10-22_a09302026exhibit991.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-K_2025-12-31_mxl-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 32; calculation CALC-03; calculation CALC-08

### Downside scenario

The scenario starts with a hyperscale customer pausing or re-sequencing optical data center orders, which management names as a risk. The first operational break is inventory and commitments: USD 272,967 thousand of inventory purchase obligations, USD 113,722 thousand of inventory and USD 121,648 thousand of prepaid and other current assets are already placed against forecast demand, and firm orders go to suppliers up to 26 weeks ahead. Revenue then falls, gross margin falls on write-downs and the third-quarter cost base of USD 97,843 thousand is not cut quickly, so operating income turns negative below about USD 166,591 thousand of quarterly revenue and cash is consumed by commitments. The liquidity consequence is a draw on the revolver: headroom of USD 91,085 thousand over the USD 80.0 million covenant minimum would last about 5.30 quarters at the 2024 burn rate and about 8.36 quarters at the 2026 nine-month burn rate, before any Silicon Motion payment. The observable triggers are quarterly revenue below USD 166,591 thousand, a rising price protection liability, further revolver drawings and cash plus availability approaching the covenant minimum. Resilience appears sufficient to avoid liquidity distress within 12 to 24 months absent a large claim payment, but not to refinance comfortably in 2028 without a recovery in EBITDA.

### Overall credit view

Overall, MAXLINEAR, INC presents an adequate but unproven business profile supported by a data center-led growth inflection, low capital intensity, no sponsor and no debt due before 2028, but credit quality is constrained by negative cash conversion, customer and supplier concentration and an unpriced Silicon Motion claim. Cash-flow durability appears weak until working capital releases and EBITDA is repeated. Refinancing capacity is likely to depend on sustaining positive EBITDA through 2027, on the revolver remaining available and on the claim not requiring debt. The primary downside path is a hyperscale order pause that strands supply commitments while the fixed cost base stays. Further analysis would require the executed credit agreement, the proxy statement, ratings or dealer prices and the outcome of the arbitration. Fundamental credit analysis completed. Top risk: cash conversion under a demand pause.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); Business & governance (CP-1A); Source readiness (CP-0); calculation CALC-01; calculation CALC-06; calculation CALC-11

### Strengths and weaknesses

The ranked strengths are as follows. First, the data center-led revenue inflection: revenue rose 58.24% in the nine months and infrastructure reached 57% of the third quarter, which lifts EBITDA and cover and supports debt service while demand lasts. Second, liquidity and maturities: USD 171,085 thousand of cash plus undrawn revolver against a covenant minimum of USD 80.0 million, and no principal due before 2028, which gives time to refinance. Third, a low-capital fabless model with a high gross margin of 58.12%, which keeps capital needs small and lets cash be directed to working capital. Fourth, conservative distribution policy with no sponsor, no dividend and a paused repurchase, which keeps cash in the business.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_mxl-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 4–6, 19–24, 34–35, 44, 50; Business & governance (CP-1A); calculation CALC-03; calculation CALC-09

The ranked weaknesses are as follows. First, negative cash conversion: operating cash flow was an outflow of USD 17.7 million and commitments reached USD 347.6 million, so growth is financed by the revolver. Second, concentration: ten customers at 56% of revenue, one supplier at 54% of purchases and 85% of shipments to Asia, which makes revenue and supply fragile to one program or trade shock. Third, recovery rests on one quarter and on calculated EBITDA, so leverage of 4.43x could look very different after one more quarter. Fourth, the Silicon Motion claim is unquantified and could compete with debt service. Fifth, covenant and document opacity: the credit agreement is known only through summaries that differ between filings, so covenant headroom and letters of credit cannot be tested.
