## Findings

### Bottom line

MaxLinear has moved from a deep loss to a levered recovery: last-twelve-months EBITDA turned positive only at September 2026, leaving total debt at 4.43x EBITDA, net debt at 2.24x and interest cover at 4.35x. The company states no EBITDA, so the measure is calculated here from operating income and amortization and depreciation.
Basis: calculation CALC-03; calculation CALC-06

Operating cash flow is still negative, so the recovery is being financed by revolver borrowing rather than by internal cash generation, and two secured facilities fall due in 2028 with USD 90 million of revolver commitments undrawn. Covenant headroom cannot be established because the credit agreement's EBITDA definition is not supplied.
Basis: calculation CALC-08; calculation CALC-09; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mxl-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 6, 19–24

### Credit analysis by dimension

Leverage is a story about the denominator. Calculated reported EBITDA was negative in FY2024 (negative USD 169.212 million) and FY2025 (negative USD 82.898 million) and on a last-twelve-months basis to March and June 2026, so no leverage ratio can be stated for those dates. Last-twelve-months EBITDA at September 2026 was USD 37.048 million; the third quarter alone contributed USD 40.801 million, while the other three quarters of the window sum to negative USD 3.753 million. Debt rose by USD 40.136 million in the third quarter to USD 164.062 million of carrying value as the revolver was drawn, which gives total debt of 4.43x and net debt of 2.24x EBITDA. A creditor should read these ratios as resting on one quarter, not on a trend.
Basis: calculation CALC-03; calculation CALC-06; calculation CALC-10; calculation CALC-13; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mxl-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 4–5, 19

Cover is strong on the latest quarter and moderate on a trailing basis. Last-twelve-months cash interest paid was USD 8.508 million, giving cover of 4.35x, and third-quarter EBITDA covered that quarter's cash interest of USD 2.441 million 16.71 times. Both facilities carry floating rates, with a weighted average effective rate on aggregate debt of 7.0% at September 2026, so cover is sensitive to rates and to the revolver balance. Cover was negative at every earlier date in the table because EBITDA was negative, not because interest went unpaid.
Basis: calculation CALC-04; calculation CALC-06; calculation CALC-10; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mxl-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 19

Cash generation lags earnings. Operating cash flow was negative USD 17.653 million in the first nine months of 2026 against positive USD 9.213 million a year earlier, although operating income swung from a loss of USD 111.998 million to a profit of USD 8.833 million. The company attributes about USD 137.6 million of working-capital investment to wafer prepayments and inventory; prepaid expenses and other current assets rose from USD 50.390 million to USD 121.648 million and inventory from USD 78.104 million to USD 113.722 million between December 2025 and September 2026, and inventory purchase and other contractual obligations rose from USD 209.6 million to USD 347.6 million. After capital expenditure of USD 15.035 million, free cash flow for the nine months was negative USD 32.688 million. Growth is being pre-funded: if orders slowed, the company would hold inventory and prepayments it has committed to pay for while the revolver carried the gap.
Basis: calculation CALC-08; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mxl-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 4, 6, 59, 62

Liquidity is adequate at the reporting date. Cash of USD 81.085 million and undrawn revolver commitments of USD 90.000 million total USD 171.085 million at September 2026, against a covenant minimum of USD 80 million that the October 2026 amendment cut to USD 60 million while adding USD 70 million of commitments. Both facilities mature in 2028, so no principal falls due before March 2028. Restricted cash of USD 28.779 million is excluded, and letters of credit outstanding are not stated, so availability under the revolver may be lower than shown.
Basis: calculation CALC-01; calculation CALC-09; calculation CALC-14; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mxl-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 4, 22–24, 44–45

Obligations to customers act as a non-debt funding float: the accrued price protection liability was USD 30.367 million at September 2026, against USD 26.493 million at December 2025 and USD 43.401 million at December 2024, and distributor price adjustments of USD 14.4 million sit in accrued expenses. Two items outside total debt behave like borrowing: USD 8.0 million of proceeds from selling an interest in certain claims, carried as a long-term liability, and USD 15.0 million of jointly funded research and development deferred in other long-term liabilities because repayment conditions are unresolved. Lease liabilities are carried on their own lines. None of these is added to total debt, and no adjusted figure is built.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_mxl-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 136; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mxl-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 4, 19, 24, 36–38

The amendment of 16 October 2026 is kept out of every September 2026 figure. It adds USD 70 million of revolving commitments, taking total commitments to USD 200 million, and lowers the liquidity covenant minimum from USD 80 million to USD 60 million, but the filings state no borrowing under the new commitments, so net debt is unchanged. The third-quarter liquidity discussion describes the revolver as USD 200.0 million at the quarter end while the debt note puts commitments at USD 130 million at that date; this analysis uses the note's USD 130 million and treats the larger figure as the post-amendment size.
Basis: calculation CALC-14; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mxl-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 22–23, 44–45, 60

EBITDA is the weakest figure in this foundation. The company states none, the calculated measure includes right-of-use amortization of USD 5.336 million for the nine months of 2026 (USD 5.632 million a year earlier) because the annual report and first-quarter report print one combined line, and the credit agreement's Consolidated EBITDA, which governs the 3.50x total net leverage covenant, is not supplied. Net leverage of 2.24x on the calculated measure is therefore not covenant headroom. The liquidity covenant is described three ways across the 2026 filings: a minimum of USD 80 million in the first-quarter and third-quarter reports, a ceiling of 100% of Consolidated EBITDA in the second-quarter debt note and a minimum of USD 80 million again in the second-quarter liquidity discussion, while the annual report describes only a springing secured net leverage test.
Basis: calculation CALC-02; calculation CALC-12; 10-Q_2026-03-31_mxl-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 40; 10-Q_2026-06-30_mxl-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 22–24, 60; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mxl-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 23–24; 10-K_2025-12-31_mxl-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 167

The strongest case against caution is breadth. Revenue of USD 218.057 million in the third quarter was 72.43% above the prior-year quarter, gross margin was 58.73%, operating margin was 13.86%, infrastructure revenue reached 57.28% of the total, no customer reached 10% of revenue in the quarter, and management guided fourth-quarter revenue to USD 240 million to USD 260 million. If the fourth quarter delivers that range, last-twelve-months EBITDA rises and leverage falls without any change in debt. The view would change in favour of creditors after two more quarters of EBITDA near the third-quarter level together with positive operating cash flow, and against them on a revenue miss, an inventory write-down or a further revolver draw to meet purchase commitments.
Basis: calculation CALC-11; 10-Q_2026-09-30_mxl-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 33–35; EX-99.1_2026-10-22_a09302026exhibit991.htm.jsonl, page 2

The most material gaps are the missing credit agreement and amendments, which stop any covenant test, and the unquantified Silicon Motion arbitration and related suits, for which the company records no accrual and states that no loss estimate can be made. An adverse award would be an unsecured claim against the company competing for the same cash and revolver availability as debt service. Dash cells in the statements, instrument-level carrying values and the quarterly cash interest derivations are smaller limits recorded in the gap table.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_mxl-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 38–43
