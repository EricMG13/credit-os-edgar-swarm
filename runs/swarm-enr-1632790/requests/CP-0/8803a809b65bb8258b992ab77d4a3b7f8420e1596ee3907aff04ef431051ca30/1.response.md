## Findings

### Bottom line

The supplied Energizer filings and releases support a reported-financials credit snapshot through the quarter ended 2026-06-30, but not covenant, collateral, recovery or market-pricing conclusions, and nothing after 2026-08-04 is supplied.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_enr.htm.jsonl, page 1; EX99.1_2026-06-30_enr_enrpr63026-htm.htm.jsonl, page 1

A creditor cannot read its protections from these documents. The credit agreement and the note indentures appear only as exhibit references, the debt note describes the covenants as customary and says the company was in compliance, and no rating, price or portfolio input is supplied. The latest quarter-end covered is 141 days before the as-of date, so the fiscal 2026 year-end position is not established.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_enr.htm.jsonl, pages 17–18; 10-K_2025-09-30_enr.htm.jsonl, pages 157–160; calculation C02

### Key findings

Coverage is strong for reported financials. The set holds the audited annual reports for fiscal 2024 and fiscal 2025, the three quarterly reports of fiscal 2026 and the three matching earnings releases, so statements, segment results, the debt note and the company's non-GAAP reconciliations can each be traced to a supplied document. The quarterly reports carry prior-year quarter and year-to-date comparatives, but not prior-year interim balance sheets.
Basis: 10-K_2024-09-30_enr.htm.jsonl, pages 104–106; 10-K_2025-09-30_enr.htm.jsonl, pages 99–101; 10-Q_2026-06-30_enr.htm.jsonl, pages 2–4; EX99.1_2026-06-30_enr_enrpr63026-htm.htm.jsonl, pages 16–17

The debt picture is complete enough to rebuild the capital structure from the filings. At the latest quarter-end the debt note lists a secured term loan due 2032, four series of senior notes and capital leases, and the revolving facility due 2030 is described separately. The note's balances add to USD 3,327.3 million, and to USD 3,278.6 million once USD 48.7 million of capital leases is removed, which agrees to the stated schedule of maturities. Balance-sheet debt of USD 3,335.6 million agrees to the earnings release.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_enr.htm.jsonl, pages 17–19; EX99.1_2026-06-30_enr_enrpr63026-htm.htm.jsonl, page 17; calculation C03; calculation C04

One liquidity input is contradicted between filings. The latest quarterly report says USD 30.0 million was drawn on the revolving facility at both 2026-06-30 and 2025-09-30, while the 2025 annual report and the two earlier quarterly reports say nothing was drawn at 2025-09-30, and the same quarterly report's own notes-payable paragraph attributes the 2025-09-30 balance to other borrowings. The conflict is carried forward unresolved. Availability of USD 462.4 million at 2026-06-30 is consistent with a USD 500.0 million facility, USD 30.0 million drawn and USD 7.6 million of letters of credit.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_enr.htm.jsonl, page 18; 10-K_2025-09-30_enr.htm.jsonl, page 137; 10-Q_2025-12-31_enr.htm.jsonl, page 14; 10-Q_2026-03-31_enr.htm.jsonl, page 16; calculation C05

The legal and structural modules cannot run on this set. Legal & covenants, Covenant capacity, Structural priority, Intercreditor control and Recovery & fulcrum all need executed documents, and the filings list the credit agreement, five indentures and four supplemental indentures dated 2026-01-02 only as exhibits incorporated by reference. Ratings & triggers, Security selection, Market-implied risk, Peer benchmark and Committee decision need a rating, quotes, peers or a portfolio, none of which is supplied.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_enr.htm.jsonl, pages 157–160; 10-Q_2026-03-31_enr.htm.jsonl, page 55

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest contrary point is that the filings are largely self-consistent: the debt note foots, the balance sheet agrees to the earnings release, and the revolver wording is the only difference found in the checks made here. That makes a reported-financials snapshot reliable as far as it goes. It does not make it a lender's view, because the company's own measures may differ from covenant definitions. The view would improve if the executed documents or the fiscal 2026 annual report were supplied, and would weaken if further differences between the annual and quarterly reports surfaced when the statements are read in full.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_enr.htm.jsonl, pages 17–19; EX99.1_2026-06-30_enr_enrpr63026-htm.htm.jsonl, pages 16–17

### Limitations

- Nothing dated after 2026-08-04 is supplied, so the fiscal 2026 year-end position, fourth-quarter results and any later financing are not established, and the company's own fiscal 2026 outlook cannot be tested against actual results.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_enr.htm.jsonl, page 56; EX99.1_2026-06-30_enr_enrpr63026-htm.htm.jsonl, page 3; calculation C06
- No executed credit agreement or indenture is supplied, so covenant levels, guarantors, collateral and the permitted receivables financing cannot be read, and any statement about creditor protection rests on the filings' summaries.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_enr.htm.jsonl, pages 157–160; 10-Q_2026-06-30_enr.htm.jsonl, page 18
- EBITDA and adjusted EBITDA figures are the company's own non-GAAP measures, printed only in the earnings releases and not in the filed reports, so any leverage or coverage ratio built on them follows the company's definition, not a lender's.
Basis: EX99.1_2026-06-30_enr_enrpr63026-htm.htm.jsonl, pages 16–17; EX99.1_2025-12-31_enr_enrpr123125-htm.htm.jsonl, page 14
