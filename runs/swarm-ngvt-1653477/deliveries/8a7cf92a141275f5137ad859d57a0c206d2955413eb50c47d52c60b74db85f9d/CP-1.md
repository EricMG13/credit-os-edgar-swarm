## Findings

### Bottom line

Ingevity has cut debt including finance leases to USD 1,145.5 million, with net debt at 2.75x the issuer's adjusted EBITDA but 5.35x unadjusted EBITDA, so creditor comfort depends on whether the add-backs recur.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_ngvt-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 3–4; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ngvt-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 45–46; calculation C01; calculation C02; calculation C06; calculation C08

Debt including finance leases is USD 255.5 million lower than at December 2024, but free cash flow in the nine months of 2026 was only USD 49.1 million after a USD 113.2 million litigation payment, while disposal proceeds of USD 156.3 million exceeded share repurchases of USD 125.3 million, so the reduction leans on sales that will not recur. The gap between the two EBITDA measures is impairment, restructuring and litigation charges that the issuer excludes.

### Financial interpretation

## Gaps

Unadjusted EBITDA is not stated by the company, so it was derived from net income, interest, tax and depreciation and amortization for every period; it restricts the leverage and cover charts to a calculated measure, severity Medium, and would be closed by the issuer stating one or by an agreed definition.

The cash flow statements include discontinued operations and are not segregated, so cash interest, capital expenditures and operating cash flow cannot be restated to continuing operations; severity Medium, closed by the discontinued operations cash flow detail, which the annual report gives only for selected items.

No debt maturity schedule is printed, and the maturity of the receivables securitization and the timing of the smaller finance leases are not stated; the ladder therefore uses the balance sheet classification and is severity Medium, closed by the notes or the facility agreements.

Instrument carrying values are not disclosed, so the instrument table carries principal and one aggregate financing cost adjustment; severity Low, closed by the allocation of issuance costs by instrument.

The security, guarantee and legal rank of the revolving credit facility, the receivables securitization and the finance leases are not stated, and the guarantors of the 2028 notes are not named, so the debt stack is not drawn and cumulative leverage is not stated; severity High, closed by the credit agreement, the indenture and the securitization agreement.

Covenant net leverage and interest coverage are stated only on credit agreement definitions, so headroom against the 4.0x and 3.0x tests is not calculable; severity High, closed by the credit agreement and the covenant compliance certificate definitions.

The road markings line sold on April 15, 2026 stays in continuing results and in the last twelve months, and its sales and earnings are not separately stated for the periods to its sale except for net sales of USD 12.9 million in the nine months of 2026; severity Medium, closed by segment detail or pro forma figures from the issuer.

Leverage and cover ratios are built for the last twelve months to September 30, 2026 only; the quarter ends of March and June 2026 have balance sheet figures but no twelve-month ratio, severity Low, closed by constructing them from the first and second quarter reports.
