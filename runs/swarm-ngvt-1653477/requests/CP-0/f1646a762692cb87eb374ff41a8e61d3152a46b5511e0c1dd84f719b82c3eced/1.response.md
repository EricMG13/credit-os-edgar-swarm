## Findings

### Bottom line

The five Ingevity filings are sufficient for a credit snapshot, but guarantor, security, covenant-definition and board facts rest on filing summaries because the financing agreements, proxy statement and any rating are not supplied. Creditors should treat those four areas as unverified until the documents arrive.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_ngvt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 119; 10-K_2025-12-31_ngvt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 155; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ngvt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 18

The latest filing was signed 13 days before the as-of date, so the balance sheet at September 30, 2026 is the freshest evidence and nothing dated after November 5, 2026 was supplied. FY2024 figures must be taken from the 2025 annual report, which recasts them for the industrial specialties disposal.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_ngvt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ngvt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 59; 10-K_2025-12-31_ngvt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 84; calculation C7

### Key findings

The reporting entity is single and clear: Ingevity Corporation, a Delaware company listed in New York, files consolidated statements with no parent company named and no separate restricted-group figures. The revolving credit agreement also names Ingevity Holdings SRL and Ingevity UK Ltd as parties, so the borrowers extend beyond the listed parent, but no filing names the subsidiary guarantors of the 2028 senior notes.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_ngvt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 1; 10-K_2025-12-31_ngvt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 119; 10-K_2025-12-31_ngvt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 159; 10-Q_2026-03-31_ngvt-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 52

Period coverage is sufficient for the run's current and prior periods. The three 2026 quarterly reports give consecutive quarter-end balance sheets with year-to-date results, and the audited 2025 annual report gives FY2025 with FY2024 and FY2023 recast, so Q3 2026 and FY2025 can be built from the filings alone. The September 30, 2025 balance sheet is not in any supplied document, so prior-year interim comparisons are limited to income statement, cash flow and equity items.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_ngvt-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 2–5; 10-K_2025-12-31_ngvt-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 84–87; 10-Q_2026-06-30_ngvt-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-Q_2026-03-31_ngvt-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 3

Comparability needs care because the reporting perimeter changed twice. The industrial specialties business, sold on January 1, 2026, is reported as discontinued operations, while the road markings product line stayed in continuing results until its sale on April 15, 2026, so FY2025 and the first months of 2026 include road markings revenue that the company no longer has. FY2024 net sales are USD 1,406.4 million in the 2024 annual report and USD 1,200.1 million recast in the 2025 annual report, and the USD 206.3 million difference equals the discontinued business's FY2024 net sales.
Basis: 10-K_2024-12-31_ngvt-20241231.htm.jsonl, page 83; 10-K_2025-12-31_ngvt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 84; 10-K_2025-12-31_ngvt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 150; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ngvt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 9; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ngvt-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 32–34; calculation C5

At September 30, 2026 debt including finance leases was USD 1,147.8 million, undrawn revolver capacity was USD 312.8 million and cash was USD 85.4 million, of which USD 81.1 million was held by foreign subsidiaries.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The main evidence against calling the sources ready is the unfinished sale process for the Advanced Polymer Technologies segment, which contributed USD 30.5 million of nine-month 2026 segment EBITDA. A sale agreement signed after November 5, 2026 would change the reporting perimeter and every pro forma figure, and none of the supplied documents could show it. The view would move to conditional for Financial foundation if any filing or announcement dated between November 5 and November 18, 2026 reports such an agreement.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_ngvt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 13; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ngvt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 27; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ngvt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 36

A second counterargument is that the missing documents are public and already summarised in the filings, so their absence may change little. The indenture and credit agreement are listed as exhibits and covenant compliance is reported with the issuer's own ratios. That argument would win if the agreements confirm the guarantor group, security and definitions the filings summarise, which would close both material gaps and let Fundamental credit state structure with source support.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-03-31_ngvt-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 52; 10-K_2025-12-31_ngvt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 157; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ngvt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 18

Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_ngvt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 119; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ngvt-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 17–18

Cash flow statements include discontinued operations and cover year-to-date periods, so discrete-quarter and continuing-operations cash flows are not stated and need differencing or remain not established.
