## Findings

### Bottom line

Ingevity is a widely held listed company with no controlling owner or sponsor named in the filings, so creditors rely on management's own financial policy, which in 2026 cut debt but returned more cash to shareholders than free cash flow covered. Debt including finance leases fell USD 63.0 million to USD 1,145.5 million in the nine months to September 30, 2026, while share repurchases of USD 125.3 million exceeded free cash flow of USD 49.1 million after the USD 113.2 million BASF settlement, and the company does not say whether sale proceeds of USD 156.3 million paid for them.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_ngvt-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 4–5; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ngvt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 16; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ngvt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 21; calculation A6; calculation A8

Earnings rest on one business: Performance Materials, which sells activated carbon for vehicle fuel-vapor systems, produced 77.9% of FY2025 segment EBITDA. Two disposals completed in 2026 and a third segment for sale mean the company creditors lend to is still changing shape.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_ngvt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 8; 10-K_2025-12-31_ngvt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 145; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ngvt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 32; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ngvt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 34; calculation A3

### Key findings

The filings give no owner or sponsor behaviour to assess and no owner support to count on. They name no controlling shareholder, just over four thousand holders of record held the shares in February 2026, and the only outside investor named, Vision One Fund, signed a cooperation agreement on 2025-03-30 that settled a board contest and carried a standstill until the nomination window for the 2026 annual meeting opened. Its stake is not stated, and who holds the largest positions is not established because the 2025 annual report takes ownership and board information from a proxy statement that is not supplied, so a creditor cannot judge whether an equity cure or a holder-driven shareholder return is likely.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_ngvt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-K_2025-12-31_ngvt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 41; 10-K_2025-12-31_ngvt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 47; 10-K_2025-12-31_ngvt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 155

Earnings are concentrated in a business tied to combustion and hybrid vehicle output. Performance Materials supplied 52.0% of FY2025 net sales and 77.9% of segment EBITDA at a 53.8% margin, against margins of 15.1% in Performance Chemicals and 20.0% in Advanced Polymer Technologies, so a fall in vehicle production, a weaker emissions standard or the loss of one of about 65 automotive customers moves group cash flow far more than any other segment could. The annual report puts the ten largest customers at about 90% of sales in this business, around half of its sales are in North America and about 40% in Asia Pacific, and sealed fuel tanks and electric vehicles are named as substitutes.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_ngvt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 5; 10-K_2025-12-31_ngvt-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 15–17; 10-K_2025-12-31_ngvt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 27; 10-K_2025-12-31_ngvt-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 145; calculation A1; calculation A3

Portfolio change has been fast and is not finished. The sale of the industrial specialties business and the crude tall oil refinery closed on 2026-01-01 for cash proceeds of USD 93.1 million and the road markings sale closed on 2026-04-15 for USD 63.2 million, so nine-month net sales of USD 877.2 million, below USD 912.5 million a year earlier, include road markings only to mid-April. Advanced Polymer Technologies, which earned USD 30.5 million of nine-month segment EBITDA, remains under review for sale after buyer feedback led to a USD 32.1 million impairment, and each sale removes earnings faster than it removes debt unless the proceeds repay borrowings, which the company does not state.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a cautious reading of financial policy is that borrowing fell and covenant headroom is stated as wide: the issuer reports net leverage of 2.4x against a 4.0x maximum and interest coverage of 7.0x against a 3.0x minimum, revolver borrowings fell USD 92.0 million, and the road markings assets were written down before their sale closed. Management also describes North American automotive demand as resilient on hybrid vehicle preference, and Performance Materials held a nine-month margin of 55.6%. The cautious reading would lose if proceeds of an Advanced Polymer Technologies sale repay debt, the unused USD 172.2 million of repurchase authorization is largely left unused, and the 2028 notes are refinanced before the revolver maturity springs forward.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_ngvt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 18; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ngvt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 17; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ngvt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 42; 10-K_2025-12-31_ngvt-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 45–47; calculation A4; calculation A7; calculation A8

The strongest evidence against treating Performance Materials concentration as a weakness is its stability: net sales of USD 606.9 million in FY2025 were close to USD 609.6 million in FY2024, and nine-month sales rose to USD 470.1 million from USD 455.7 million on pricing, with the margin up to 55.6% from 54.5%. The concentration view would strengthen if volumes keep falling, as they did by USD 6.7 million, or four percent, in the third quarter on softness in China and Europe, and it would soften if hybrid vehicle share keeps offsetting lower combustion output. A named controlling holder, or a proxy statement showing large holders with creditor-aligned positions, would change the ownership view.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_ngvt-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 15–16; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ngvt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 42; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ngvt-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 8; calculation A1; calculation A2; calculation A4

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

No new notes or term debt were issued in 2025 or in 2026 to September 30 in the filings supplied; the senior unsecured notes due 2028, USD 550.0 million issued on 2020-10-28, remain the only bond. The one financing transaction in the period is the second amendment and restatement of the revolving credit facility on 2026-03-26, which cut commitments from USD 1,000 million to USD 750 million, moved maturity to 2031-03-31 and cost fees of USD 4.0 million. The new maturity advances to 91 days before the 2028 notes mature if more than USD 250 million of them remain outstanding, and the margin on benchmark-rate loans is 1.00% to 1.75%. Receivables securitization borrowings rose from USD 43.9 million to USD 75.0 million within a facility of up to USD 100.0 million.
