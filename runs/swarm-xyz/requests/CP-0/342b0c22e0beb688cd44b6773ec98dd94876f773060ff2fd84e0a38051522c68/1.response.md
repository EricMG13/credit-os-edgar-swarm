## Findings

### Bottom line

The supplied filings support a consolidated credit snapshot of Block, Inc., but creditors cannot rely on them for covenant headroom, legal priority or market pricing because no financing document, covenant level or market price is supplied.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_xyz-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 28, 55; 10-K_2025-12-31_xyz-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 215–217

The evidence runs from the audited 2025 annual report to the third-quarter 2026 report signed on November 3, 2026, two days before the as-of date, and it covers the 2026 note repayments, the January 2026 revolver amendment and the October 2026 agreement in principle with the Department of Justice.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_xyz-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 140; 10-Q_2026-09-30_xyz-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 27–28, 34, 144

Three open points limit the work: the level of the revolver's leverage covenant is printed nowhere, the Department of Justice resolution is not final and its payment timing is not stated, and governance detail sits in a proxy statement that was not supplied.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_xyz-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 28, 34–35; 10-K_2025-12-31_xyz-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 71, 197, 214

Financial foundation, Business & governance, Earnings changes, Liquidity and Fundamental credit can proceed, most with stated limitations; Legal & covenants and Market-implied risk are blocked, and Peer benchmark and Security selection are conditional on inputs the run did not supply.
