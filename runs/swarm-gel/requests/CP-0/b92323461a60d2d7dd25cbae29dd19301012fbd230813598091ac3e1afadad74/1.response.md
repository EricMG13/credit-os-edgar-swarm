## Findings

### Bottom line

The supplied filings and releases are sufficient for a financial, business and capital-structure read of Genesis Energy through 30 September 2026, but not for covenant definitions, legal ranking or relative value, because no executed debt documents, ratings or prices were supplied. The three 2026 quarterly results add up exactly to the nine-month totals for Adjusted EBITDA, attributable net income and operating cash flow, so the quarterly series can be used without adjustment.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_gel-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gel-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 5; EX-99.1_2026-11-05_a90ex9918kearnings_q32026.htm.jsonl, page 10; calculation C1; calculation C2; calculation C3

Four points need care before later analysis relies on the set. Adjusted EBITDA and the bank leverage ratio are printed only in the earnings releases; the covenant levels in the annual report were replaced by a new credit agreement in March 2026; none of the 2026 quarterly filings states compliance with the credit agreement covenants; and the early-October hurricane disruption and the widened warning on full-year Adjusted EBITDA appear only in the November release, with no stated dollar effect.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_gel-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 198; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gel-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 16–17; EX-99.1_2026-11-05_a90ex9918kearnings_q32026.htm.jsonl, page 4; EX-99.1_2026-11-05_a90ex9918kearnings_q32026.htm.jsonl, page 11
