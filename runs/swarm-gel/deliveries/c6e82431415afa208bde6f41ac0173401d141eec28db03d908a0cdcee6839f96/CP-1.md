## Findings

### Bottom line

Genesis Energy's leverage fell to 5.00x last-twelve-months EBITDA at 30 September 2026 from 5.77x at the end of 2025, but cash interest cover of 2.41x remains thin.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_gel-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-K_2025-12-31_gel-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 166; calculation K31; calculation K32; calculation K36

The improvement came from higher earnings, not repayment: carrying-value debt rose by USD 159.34 million in the nine months because a receivables facility and a larger note issue helped fund the redemption of preferred units. Revolver availability of USD 894.4 million and the absence of any maturity before 2029 limit near-term refinancing risk. The company's own bank leverage ratio of 5.03x uses a narrower debt definition and a different EBITDA, so it is not comparable with these figures.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_gel-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gel-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 14–18; EX-99.1_2026-11-05_a90ex9918kearnings_q32026.htm.jsonl, page 11; calculation K6; calculation K17; calculation K19; calculation K25; calculation K32; calculation K36; Source readiness (CP-0)

### Leverage and coverage

Leverage on carrying-value debt fell from 8.89x at the end of 2024 to 5.77x at the end of 2025 and 5.00x at 30 September 2026. The 2024 ratio is not like-for-like: that debt also funded the Alkali business, sold in February 2025 and shown as discontinued, while the EBITDA covers continuing operations only. Gross debt rose in 2026, so the fall to the latest ratio rests on higher earnings over the last twelve months, which creditors need to see sustained, rather than on repayment.
Cash interest cover rose from 1.43x in FY2024 to 1.87x in FY2025 and 2.41x on the last twelve months, against a minimum covenant of 2.00x that is measured on the credit agreement's own definitions and therefore cannot be tested from these figures.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_gel-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 166–167; 10-K_2025-12-31_gel-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 210; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gel-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gel-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 17; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gel-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 29; calculation K6; calculation K7; calculation K30; calculation K31; calculation K32; calculation K33; calculation K34; calculation K35; calculation K36; calculation K37

### Cash flow quality

Operating cash flow for the nine months of 2026 was helped by a working-capital inflow that reversed the outflow of the nine months of 2025, so it is not a clean run-rate. Cash paid for fixed and intangible assets fell sharply after the completion of the offshore growth projects in 2025, which is why earnings less capital expenditure covers cash interest 2.00x on the last twelve months against 1.21x in FY2025. Cash flow statements include the discontinued Alkali business until its sale on 2025-02-28, so annual and nine-month 2025 figures are on a total-company basis.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_gel-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 5; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gel-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 28; 10-K_2025-12-31_gel-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 168–169; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gel-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 52–53; calculation K8; calculation K9; calculation K10; calculation K38; calculation K39

### Liquidity and maturities

The USD 99.5 million receivables facility is fully drawn and is carried as a current borrowing although it expires in 2029. No note matures before January 2029, the revolver matures in March 2031 with an earlier springing date if more than USD 150 million of the 2029 notes or 2030 notes remain outstanding at the dates stated, and the revolver is undrawn. Liquidity is cash of USD 41,477 thousand plus revolver availability of USD 894.4 million after letters of credit.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_gel-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 16–18; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gel-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 49; calculation K19; calculation K20

### Definitions and data quality

The statements-based EBITDA includes the share of noncontrolling interests and equity earnings, while the release's EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA are net to Genesis' ownership interest; the two bases differ by the noncontrolling interest and accretion lines. Adjusted EBITDA for the nine months of 2026 also keeps a gain of USD 17,436 thousand on the sale of offshore gas assets, which the cash flow reconciliation adds back and the Select Items do not remove. Total debt includes the receivables facility, which the issuer's Adjusted Debt excludes, and the issuer's Adjusted Debt also deducts an inventory financing amount although the revolver is undrawn.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-11-05_a90ex9918kearnings_q32026.htm.jsonl, pages 9–11; 10-Q_2026-09-30_gel-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 3; calculation K26; calculation K27
