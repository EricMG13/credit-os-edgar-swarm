## Findings

### Bottom line

Beazer's fundamental credit profile is weak and deteriorating: earnings have fallen below the level that supports its debt, with net debt to EBITDA at 23.10x for the twelve months to June 30, 2026 against 5.86x at FY2025 and EBITDA covering cash interest only 0.63x.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); 10-Q_2026-06-30_issuer_bzh-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 41

Support comes from a maturity profile with no debt due before October 2029, an unsecured structure, an inventory base that exceeds total debt, and a pending all-cash acquisition whose effect on the notes is not established. The primary downside is continued cash burn: nine months of inventory growth left free cash flow negative and liquidity covers only 0.67x of that outflow. The pro forma effect of the August 6, 2026 merger on net debt and leverage is not established.
Basis: calculation C4; calculation C1; Financial foundation (CP-1); Business & governance (CP-1A)

### Key findings

Earnings, not the debt schedule, is the central credit problem. Revenue for the first nine months of fiscal 2026 fell 18.36% as closings dropped 22.1%, and homebuilding gross margin before impairments, abandonments and interest slid to 15.6% from 18.3%, while general and administrative costs did not fall, so the company moved from an operating profit to an operating loss. The issuer's EBITDA also adds back interest amortized to cost of sales; on operating income plus depreciation and amortization the twelve months to June 30, 2026 are negative and leverage on that basis is not calculable. Interest is therefore being paid from liquidity and borrowing, not from earnings.
Basis: calculation C6; Financial foundation (CP-1); 10-Q_2026-06-30_issuer_bzh-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 40; 10-Q_2026-06-30_issuer_bzh-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 44

Liquidity is adequate for now but thin against the pace of cash use. Operating cash outflow plus capital expenditures over nine months was large because inventory was built ahead of closings, the revolver went from undrawn at fiscal 2025 year end to a 73.49% utilisation including letters of credit, and cash plus undrawn capacity covers only 0.67x of the nine-month outflow. Seasonality and backlog conversion could reverse part of this, but the covenant levels that govern revolver access are not printed, so headroom is not established. Net debt to EBITDA of 23.10x and cover of 0.63x are on the issuer's EBITDA; pro forma for the August 6, 2026 merger they are not established.
Basis: calculation C7; calculation C4; Financial foundation (CP-1); 10-Q_2026-06-30_issuer_bzh-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 17; 10-Q_2026-06-30_issuer_bzh-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 55–56

Refinancing risk is low until the end of the decade and market access remains open at a higher price. The remaining 2027 notes were redeemed with the proceeds of $400.0 million of 2032 notes sold at par in June 2026, and the nearest maturity is now the 2029 notes in October 2029, with the revolver terminating in March 2030. The new notes carry a higher coupon than the notes they replaced and S&P lowered its rating to B- with a negative outlook, while Moody's kept B2 and a stable outlook, so access is open but increasingly expensive.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); Business & governance (CP-1A); 10-K_2025-09-30_issuer_bzh-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 91; 10-Q_2026-06-30_issuer_bzh-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 18–19; 10-Q_2026-06-30_issuer_bzh-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 57

The asset base is the main support but it is a recovery question for another module. Inventory is 1.68x total debt at carrying value and 60.0% of active lots are held under options, which limits owned land, yet inventory includes capitalized interest and land held for sale, and the value of that inventory in a downturn, and the ranking and guarantee terms that decide who is paid from it, are not assessed here.
Basis: calculation C5; 10-Q_2026-06-30_issuer_bzh-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 9; 10-Q_2026-06-30_issuer_bzh-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 58

Financial policy is at odds with the stated deleveraging goal. The company repurchased $66.2 million of stock in nine months while net debt rose, and net debt to net capitalization is 52.8% against a goal of the low 30% range by the end of fiscal 2027. The pending cash acquisition at $33.50 per share changes who sets financial policy after closing, and nothing in the filings says how the notes would be treated.
Basis: 10-K_2025-09-30_issuer_bzh-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 8; 10-Q_2026-06-30_issuer_bzh-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 41; 10-Q_2026-06-30_issuer_bzh-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 33; 10-Q_2026-06-30_issuer_bzh-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 57–58; Business & governance (CP-1A)

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest case against a deteriorating view is that the decline looks cyclical and affordability-driven rather than structural. Net new orders rose 4.5% in the third quarter, the cancellation rate fell to 15.9% from 19.8%, backlog value rose 2.2% and the average price in backlog is higher, while the company attributes soft demand to mortgage rates near 52-week highs and consumer sentiment near all-time lows. The company also pushed out its maturities and enlarged its revolver before the acquisition was announced. The view would soften if quarterly closings recover and operating cash flow turns positive as inventory converts, if the revolver is repaid, or if the acquirer's financing leaves the notes with a stronger parent. It would worsen if closings and margins keep falling, if the revolver is drawn further, if covenant levels prove tight, or if repurchases continue.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_issuer_bzh-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 37; 10-Q_2026-06-30_issuer_bzh-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 42–44; 10-Q_2026-06-30_issuer_bzh-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 17; 10-Q_2026-06-30_issuer_bzh-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 57

### Strengths and weaknesses

Strengths: the company has no debt maturing before October 2029, its notes and revolver are unsecured and guaranteed by substantially all significant subsidiaries, it carries no mortgage-subsidiary debt, inventory exceeds total debt, and its option-based land model limits owned land. Audited statements, effective internal control and an extended revolver add to transparency and flexibility.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); Business & governance (CP-1A); 10-Q_2026-06-30_issuer_bzh-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 17–18; 10-Q_2026-06-30_issuer_bzh-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 58

Weaknesses: earnings have turned to an operating loss, leverage on the issuer's own EBITDA has risen from 5.86x to 23.10x and cash interest cover is 0.63x, cash flow is negative while inventory grows, the revolver is heavily used, shareholder returns have continued, and the company faces a change of control whose debt treatment is not stated. Pro forma net debt and leverage for the merger are not established.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); Business & governance (CP-1A); 10-Q_2026-06-30_issuer_bzh-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 3–4; 10-Q_2026-06-30_issuer_bzh-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 41
