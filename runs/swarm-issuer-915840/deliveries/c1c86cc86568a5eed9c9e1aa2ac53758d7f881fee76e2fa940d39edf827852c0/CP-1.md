## Findings

### Bottom line

Beazer's leverage on its own EBITDA definition has deteriorated sharply, which leaves creditors with little earnings cover: net debt to EBITDA reached 23.10x on the twelve months to June 30, 2026 against 5.86x at FY2025, and EBITDA covered cash interest only 0.63x.
Basis: calculation C44; calculation C45; calculation C46; 10-Q_2026-06-30_issuer_bzh-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 41

That EBITDA adds back interest amortized to cost of sales, so it flatters the position: operating income plus depreciation and amortization is negative for the same twelve months and leverage on that basis is not calculable. Nine months of inventory growth and share repurchases were funded by net new borrowing while earnings fell, and the pro forma effect of the pending Dream Finders merger on net debt is not established.
Basis: calculation C27; 10-Q_2026-06-30_issuer_bzh-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-Q_2026-06-30_issuer_bzh-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 15–19; 10-Q_2026-06-30_issuer_bzh-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 33; 10-Q_2026-06-30_issuer_bzh-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 41
