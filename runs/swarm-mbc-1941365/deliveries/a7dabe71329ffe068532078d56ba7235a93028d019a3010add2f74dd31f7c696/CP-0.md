## Findings

### Bottom line

The eight supplied filings and releases are enough to run the credit snapshot for MasterBrand, Inc. as of 2026-11-18, but they cannot support covenant, structural-priority or valuation conclusions because the governing legal documents, ratings and market data were not supplied.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-27_mbc-20260927.htm.jsonl, pages 19–22; 10-K_2025-12-28_mbc-20251228.htm.jsonl, pages 102–106
