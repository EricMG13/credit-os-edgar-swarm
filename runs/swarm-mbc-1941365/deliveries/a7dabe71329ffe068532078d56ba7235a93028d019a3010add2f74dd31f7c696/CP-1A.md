## Findings

### Bottom line

MasterBrand is a widely held cabinet manufacturer with no named sponsor, and its May 2026 merger with American Woodmark added debt and integration risk to a business reliant on two retailers for a third of sales, so creditors depend on cost savings, not owner support.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-28_mbc-20251228.htm.jsonl, pages 3, 8, 17; 10-Q_2026-09-27_mbc-20260927.htm.jsonl, pages 8–9

Third-quarter net sales rose 48.3% to USD 1,036.8 million only because American Woodmark added USD 355.7 million, while legacy sales fell 2.5%. Governance evidence is thin: the proxy statement and the credit documents are not supplied, and the chief financial officer's separation was agreed on 2026-10-23.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-11-05_q32026earningspressrelease.htm.jsonl, page 1; 10-Q_2026-09-27_mbc-20260927.htm.jsonl, pages 37, 51

### Key findings

Two retailers, Lowe's and Home Depot, took 33% of fiscal 2025 net sales and the ten largest customers about 50%, so one retailer's volume cut or reset moves fixed-cost absorption quickly. Gross margin fell to 24.6% in the third quarter from 31.2% a year earlier on lower volume, fixed-cost under-absorption, inflation ahead of pricing and American Woodmark's lower margin, which leaves the cost base little room for further volume loss. The merger changed the mix: builders were 22.63% of third-quarter net sales against 13.65% a year earlier, and retailers 35.73% against 30.32%.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-28_mbc-20251228.htm.jsonl, pages 8, 17; EX-99.1_2026-11-05_q32026earningspressrelease.htm.jsonl, page 1; 10-Q_2026-09-27_mbc-20260927.htm.jsonl, pages 13–14; calculation calc-1

The merger was an all-stock transaction for American Woodmark's holders, who took about 37% of the combined company, while USD 375.0 million of Term Loan A repaid USD 367.5 million of American Woodmark's debt. Equity therefore grew with the business, but debt came with it, and the acquired business has to deliver the savings management targets because the 3.75x net leverage test for the merger quarter and the three after it is measured on a definition in the credit agreement that is not supplied.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-28_mbc-20251228.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-Q_2026-09-27_mbc-20260927.htm.jsonl, pages 8–9, 20; calculation calc-4; calculation calc-5

Management reports USD 11 million of cost synergies realised, actions under way for more than USD 50 million, and a target of over USD 100 million of annual run-rate savings by the end of year three, beside a separate USD 30 million cost reduction programme announced in February 2026. These are management statements, not results, and the cash cost of reaching them is visible: restructuring charges were USD 11.1 million in the third quarter and acquisition-related costs USD 48.4 million in the 39 weeks.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-11-05_q32026earningspressrelease.htm.jsonl, pages 1–2; 10-Q_2026-09-27_mbc-20260927.htm.jsonl, pages 23, 33; calculation calc-7

Tariffs add a dated risk: the federal tariff on kitchen cabinets and vanities is 25% and scheduled to rise to 50% on 2027-01-01. Gross tariff costs run at about 5% to 6% of 2026 net sales and management expects to offset them in full on a run-rate basis by year end, which is an expectation, while refunds of earlier duties added USD 10.9 million to third-quarter gross profit and will not recur.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-11-05_q32026earningspressrelease.htm.jsonl, pages 1, 3; 10-Q_2026-09-27_mbc-20260927.htm.jsonl, page 34

No dividend is expected, share repurchases stopped in 2026 after USD 18.1 million in fiscal 2025, and no controlling shareholder or sponsor appears in the filings. The auditor gave clean opinions and effective internal control at the 2025 year end. Against that, the chief financial officer role is held on an interim basis after the separation agreement, American Woodmark is excluded from the 2026 internal control assessment, and the proxy statement that would show directors and holders is not supplied.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-28_mbc-20251228.htm.jsonl, pages 49, 121; 10-Q_2026-09-27_mbc-20260927.htm.jsonl, pages 29, 48, 51; calculation calc-8

The sponsor and governance risk level is Medium. Acquisitive growth, a covenant reset in March 2026 and a leverage step-up weigh against it, while the absence of distributions and an equity-funded merger weigh for it; legal capacity for distributions, new debt and collateral moves is not established because the credit agreement and the indenture are not supplied. This is an analyst reading of the evidence, not a score.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-27_mbc-20260927.htm.jsonl, pages 20–21; EX-99.1_2026-05-05_q12026earningspressrelease.htm.jsonl, page 2

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest contrary evidence is that integration is reported ahead of schedule, the merger was funded mainly with shares, no distribution is planned and the issuer states that all debt covenants were met at 2026-09-27, which would argue for lower risk. Cost savings realised well above USD 11 million, or legacy sales stabilising, would strengthen that case. A further fall in legacy volumes, a loss of volume at either large retailer, or the tariff on cabinets rising to 50% without offset would weaken it.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-11-05_q32026earningspressrelease.htm.jsonl, pages 1–2; 10-Q_2026-09-27_mbc-20260927.htm.jsonl, pages 21, 34

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

The merger with American Woodmark closed on 2026-05-28. Each American Woodmark share was converted into 5.15 MasterBrand shares, which made the former American Woodmark holders about 37% of the combined company, and the preliminary total consideration was USD 1,059.8 million, which includes the USD 367.5 million settlement of American Woodmark's existing debt. Purchase accounting recognised USD 197.2 million of goodwill and remains preliminary, so the acquired balances can still change.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-27_mbc-20260927.htm.jsonl, pages 8–11

The new debt for the merger is a USD 375.0 million delayed-draw Term Loan A, committed under a First Amendment dated 2025-11-03 and drawn in full on 2026-05-28. It repaid American Woodmark's debt and covered fees, matures in June 2029 with the revolver, and requires quarterly payments from September 2026 set as a share of the commitment. It sits beside the USD 750.0 million revolver and the USD 700.0 million 7.00% senior notes due 2032 that were issued on 2024-06-27.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-27_mbc-20260927.htm.jsonl, pages 9, 19–22

A Second Amendment dated 2026-03-26 raised the maximum net leverage ratio to 3.75x, 4.00x and 3.75x for the quarters to the end of 2026 and lowered the minimum interest coverage ratio to 2.75x until the earlier of 2027-01-01 or the merger becoming effective. The amendment's thresholds ended when the merger became effective, and the original 3.75x test for a material acquisition then applied for the merger quarter and the three after it.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-27_mbc-20260927.htm.jsonl, pages 20–21; EX-99.1_2026-05-05_q12026earningspressrelease.htm.jsonl, page 2

Earlier, the July 2024 purchase of Supreme Cabinetry Brands cost USD 520.0 million in cash from GHK Capital Partners LP and was funded with cash and USD 430.0 million of borrowings under the revolver of the credit agreement signed with the June 2024 notes. The separation from Fortune Brands on 2022-12-14 left the issuer with no named parent or sponsor.
Basis: 10-K_2024-12-29_mbc-20241229.htm.jsonl, page 100; 10-Q_2026-09-27_mbc-20260927.htm.jsonl, page 43; 10-K_2025-12-28_mbc-20251228.htm.jsonl, page 3
