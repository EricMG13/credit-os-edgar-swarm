## Findings

Qnity's fundamental credit profile is solid and improving: volume-led growth, a 30.87% adjusted EBITDA margin and total leverage of 2.72x reported EBITDA leave creditors a cushion, but dependence on a few Asian customers and cash conversion that fell in 2026 are the main threats. Liquidity of USD 2,362 million covers principal and interest due within twelve months 9.12x, and 97.07% of principal falls due in 2032 and 2033.

### Company description

Qnity sells consumable materials that chipmakers and circuit-board makers qualify into their production roadmaps: planarization pads and slurries, photoresists and cleaning chemistries in Semiconductor Technologies, and plating, packaging, thermal and flexible-circuit materials in Interconnect Solutions. Over 90% of 2025 net sales came from products consumed in manufacturing or built into devices, so revenue follows customers' unit output and not one-off projects, which supports cash-flow visibility without giving contractual certainty, because top customers give no long-term purchase commitments.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_q-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 7; 10-K_2025-12-31_q-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 8; 10-K_2025-12-31_q-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 11; 10-K_2025-12-31_q-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 17; calculation FC19

The business was part of DuPont until 2025-11-01, so statements for 2025 and earlier are carve-out statements and year-on-year comparisons rely on pro forma measures, and since the separation Qnity carries USD 4,100 million of principal debt that the carve-out did not, which turned an unlevered unit into a leveraged issuer with creditors ranking ahead of equity.

### Ownership and group structure

Ownership is public with no sponsor identified, and the credit-relevant structure sits in the separation terms: DuPont holds sole authority over legacy claims that Qnity funds at 44%, one preferred share held by a trust can block charter changes that protect that authority, and the sponsor and governance risk level is Medium. For creditors this means no sponsor exit pressure or fee leakage, but a perpetual outflow risk that the board cannot contest, and shareholder returns of 35.01% of nine-month free cash flow with the capacity limits of the executed documents not supplied.
Basis: Business & governance (CP-1A); calculation FC18

### Revenue drivers and pricing power

Revenue growth is volume, not price: nine-month net sales rose 20.65% on volume of 21% with local price and mix flat, against volume of 11% and price and mix of minus 1% in 2025 and volume of 10% and price and mix of minus 2% in 2024. Management attributes demand to artificial intelligence, advanced nodes and packaging that raise material content per wafer, and Interconnect Solutions volume rose 27% against 16% in Semiconductor Technologies, so Interconnect Solutions supplied 69.34% of the growth in segment Adjusted Operating EBITDA. The mechanism is repeat consumption qualified into customer roadmaps, which supports volume but gives Qnity little pricing power, so earnings follow customers' output and creditors should expect them to fall when output does.

### Cost structure and margin resilience

Margins are widening but the cost base is semi-fixed: cost of sales fell to 53% of net sales and gross margin rose to 47.14% from 46.18%, yet research and development plus selling, general and administrative costs of USD 870 million are 20.23% of net sales and selling, general and administrative costs grew 26.49% against sales growth of 20.65%. The split between fixed and variable cost is not disclosed, so operating leverage is read from the incremental margin of 34.51%, which implies that a volume decline would take back margin at a similar rate. Metals pass-through and local sourcing limit input shocks, which supports margin stability in a normal year but not in a volume shock.

### Porter's five forces

Competitive rivalry is moderate: the named rivals are Entegris, Merck KGA, Resonac, Element Solutions and MKS Instruments, competition is on performance, quality and reliability, and price and mix of minus 1% in 2025 and minus 2% in 2024 show some price give, yet an adjusted EBITDA margin of 30.87% shows Qnity holds its position. Buyer power is high, with the top ten customers at 34% of sales and no purchase commitments. Supplier power is low, with about 70% of raw materials sourced locally and no supplier above 10% of raw material spend, which limits input-cost shocks and supports margin stability.
