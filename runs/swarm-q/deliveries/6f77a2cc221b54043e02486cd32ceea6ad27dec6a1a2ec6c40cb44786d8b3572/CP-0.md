## Findings

### Bottom line

The seven filed documents support a credit snapshot of Qnity Electronics, Inc. as of 2026-11-05, but because no executed credit agreement or indenture was supplied, creditor views on covenant headroom and call protection can rest only on the filings' own summaries. Covenant headroom and security-level terms are therefore not established.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_q-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 85–87; 10-Q_2026-09-30_q-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 46

Financial statement coverage is otherwise sufficient: annual statements for FY2023 to FY2025, three 2026 quarterly reports and three results releases give four discrete quarters from Q4 2025 to Q3 2026, with total debt of USD 4,019 million and cash of USD 1,124 million at 2026-09-30. The main comparability limit is that periods before the separation from DuPont on 2025-11-01 are carve-out statements, so like-for-like prior-year comparisons depend on the company's pro forma measures.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_q-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 66, 91; 10-Q_2026-09-30_q-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 5, 6, 8, 9; EX-99.1_2026-11-04_exhibit991supplementalsche.htm.jsonl, page 10; calculation C4; calculation C8
