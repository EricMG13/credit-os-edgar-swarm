## Findings

### Bottom line

The seven supplied filings and releases are enough for a credit snapshot of Valaris Ltd, but not for any view that depends on the executed debt documents, which were not supplied. Creditors can rely on the reported statements, debt terms and liquidity; covenant headroom, ratings triggers and recovery cannot be established from these documents.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_val-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 127–129; 10-Q_2026-09-30_val-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 21–22

The evidence is current: the latest report was signed two days before the as-of date and the latest balance sheet is 36 days old. It describes the capital structure before the pending Transocean combination, which is scheduled to close after the as-of date and which may let noteholders put their notes at a premium to par if a change of control triggering event occurs.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_val-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 11–12; 10-K_2025-12-31_val-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 127; calculation C2; calculation C3
