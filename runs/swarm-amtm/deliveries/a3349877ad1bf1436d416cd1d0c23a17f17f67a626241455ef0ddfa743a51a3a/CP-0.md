## Findings

### Bottom line

The sources support a credit snapshot of Amentum Holdings, Inc. with limits: figures are usable to 3 July 2026, but covenant headroom after the April 2026 credit agreement amendment, governance detail and any later event cannot be confirmed.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-07-03_amtm-20260703.htm.jsonl, pages 2–5, 11–13; 10-K_2025-10-03_amtm-20251003.htm.jsonl, pages 164–165

Fiscal 2025 includes the merged Critical Mission Solutions business for the full year while fiscal 2024 does not, and fiscal 2025 has 53 weeks, so growth and margin trends across the two years are not like for like. The run settings call the second quarter current, but the newest filing covers the third quarter ended 3 July 2026, so each figure must carry the period its filing states.
Basis: 10-K_2025-10-03_amtm-20251003.htm.jsonl, pages 117–118; 10-Q_2026-07-03_amtm-20260703.htm.jsonl, pages 1–3
