## Findings

### Bottom line

Net debt was 9.45x reported LTM EBITDA and 4.51x issuer-adjusted EBITDA at 30 June 2026, so creditor leverage depends on accepting add-backs that are 52.30% of adjusted EBITDA, and both ratios exclude TopBuild.
Basis: calculation C26; calculation C29; calculation C32

Gross debt at carrying value was USD 6,040 million against unrestricted cash of USD 2,774 million, so net debt was USD 3,266 million; that counts USD 3,000 million of escrowed notes as debt while their cash sits outside the cash figure, and it excludes the USD 3,000 million incremental term loan drawn on 1 July 2026. Cash interest paid over the twelve months covered reported EBITDA 1.56x and adjusted EBITDA 3.28x.
Basis: calculation C16; calculation C27; calculation C30; 10-Q_2026-06-30_qxo-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 44; 10-Q_2026-06-30_qxo-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 49

The earnings base is uneven by quarter: adjusted EBITDA was USD 301.9 million in the third quarter of 2025 and USD 1.2 million in the first quarter of 2026, so a single quarter should not be annualised.
Basis: 10-Q_2025-09-30_qxo-20250930.htm.jsonl, page 50; EX-99.1_2026-05-12_qxoq1-26earningsrelease.htm.jsonl, page 8; 10-Q_2026-06-30_qxo-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 62

### Financial interpretation

The leverage picture turns on three choices the reader must make. Net debt of USD 3,266 million at 30 June 2026 is 9.45x reported EBITDA and 4.51x adjusted EBITDA, and USD 3,000 million of it is the escrowed notes counted as debt while the matching cash is restricted and outside the cash figure; with the escrow counted, net debt would be USD 266 million. Excluding the escrow, net debt rose from USD 12.3 million at 31 March 2026 to USD 266 million at 30 June 2026. The real step-up is the incremental term loan of USD 3,000 million drawn on 1 July 2026, which lifts standalone net debt to USD 6,266 million before any TopBuild earnings are counted, so creditors should treat the reported ratios as a floor on post-closing leverage, not a measure of it.
Basis: calculation C15; calculation C16; calculation C42; calculation C40; 10-Q_2026-06-30_qxo-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 44; 10-Q_2026-06-30_qxo-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 49; 10-Q_2026-06-30_qxo-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 54; EX-99.1_2026-08-13_qxoq2-26earningsrelease.htm.jsonl, page 8

Coverage is thin on reported earnings and adequate on adjusted earnings. Cash interest paid over the twelve months was USD 221.0 million, which covers reported EBITDA of USD 345.7 million 1.56x and adjusted EBITDA of USD 724.8 million 3.28x.
