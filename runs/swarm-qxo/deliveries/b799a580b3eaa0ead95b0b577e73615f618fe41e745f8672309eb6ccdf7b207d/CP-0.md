## Findings

### Bottom line

The supplied documents support a credit snapshot of QXO as of 30 June 2026, not of the company after the TopBuild acquisition closed on 1 July 2026, because no later financial statements are supplied. Creditors should read every leverage, liquidity and coverage figure the next analyses produce as pre-TopBuild: the incremental term loan and the preferred equity raised at closing, and the acquired business itself, appear in no supplied balance sheet.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_qxo-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 25–26; 10-Q_2026-06-30_qxo-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 49; EX-99.1_2026-08-13_qxoq2-26earningsrelease.htm.jsonl, page 8

The latest quarter ended 128 days before the as-of date, and the purchase price allocation for TopBuild is stated to be incomplete. Debt terms are available only as the notes summarise them, because no indenture or credit agreement is supplied.
