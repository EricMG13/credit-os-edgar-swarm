## Findings

### Bottom line

Leverage is high but easing: net debt was 6.87x last-twelve-months reported EBITDA at 30 September 2026, down from 7.88x at the end of 2025, so creditors depend on continued EBITDA growth to keep refinancing risk contained. Reported EBITDA covered cash interest 2.83x over the same twelve months, or 3.16x on the issuer's Adjusted EBITDA.
Basis: calculation CAL-33; 10-Q_2026-09-30_irm-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 2–4; 10-K_2025-12-31_irm-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 96–99

Liquidity at 30 September 2026 was cash of USD 188.16 million plus USD 2.22 billion of undrawn revolver, against USD 1.23 billion of debt due within a year, while capital spending still exceeds operating cash flow. The quarterly reports print no year-by-year maturity schedule, so the wall at that date is not established.
Basis: calculation CAL-35; calculation CAL-36; 10-Q_2026-09-30_irm-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-09-30_irm-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 20; 10-Q_2026-09-30_irm-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 6–7; 10-K_2025-12-31_irm-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 98–99

### Leverage and coverage

Net debt over last-twelve-months reported EBITDA fell from 7.88x at the end of 2025 to 6.87x at 30 September 2026. The improvement came from EBITDA growth, not repayment: carrying-value debt rose by USD 1,540.243 million over the nine months while last-twelve-months reported EBITDA rose by USD 524.128 million.
Basis: calculation CAL-30; calculation CAL-31; calculation CAL-33; 10-Q_2026-09-30_irm-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 2–4

Part of the reported gain is relief from items that did not recur: the issuer booked USD 152.432 million of restructuring and other transformation in the first nine months of 2025 and prints a dash for it in 2026, and other expense (income), net fell from USD 106.379 million to USD 32.910 million. On the issuer's Adjusted EBITDA, which adds those items back, net leverage eased by only 0.16x against 1.01x on reported EBITDA, so the headline deleveraging overstates the operating improvement.
Basis: calculation CAL-34; calculation CAL-33; calculation CAL-32; 10-Q_2026-09-30_irm-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 3–4; 10-Q_2026-09-30_irm-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 32

The strongest contrary evidence is operating momentum and covenant room: Adjusted EBITDA rose from USD 660.379 million in the third quarter of 2025 to USD 747.958 million in the third quarter of 2026, and the leverage ratio the issuer reports under its Credit Agreement sits inside its limit. That reading would win if reported EBITDA keeps converging on Adjusted EBITDA as restructuring ends, narrowing the gap from USD 508.992 million in FY2025 to USD 299.106 million in the last twelve months. Interest cover moved the same way, from 2.50x to 2.83x on reported EBITDA, but remains thin for a borrower whose capital spending exceeds its operating cash flow.
