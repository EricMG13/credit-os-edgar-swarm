## Findings

### Bottom line

The five filings support a credit snapshot of Wyndham Hotels & Resorts, Inc. through the quarter ended 2026-09-30, but they cannot support an independent test of covenants, ranking or recovery, so creditors must take the covenant position from management's own reported ratio.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_wh-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 40; 10-K_2025-12-31_wh-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 124–125

Current statements, the debt schedule, liquidity, the reported first-lien leverage ratio and the issuer's own statement of its agency ratings are all printed in the latest quarterly report. The executed credit agreement and note indentures, the proxy statement, rating agency reports, market prices and any filing after 2026-10-22 were not supplied, so board and ownership detail, covenant definitions and events in the 27 days before the as-of date are not established.
