## Findings

### Bottom line

Wyndham has no disclosed controlling holder and a diversified franchisee base, so creditor risk lies in management choices: shareholder returns have exceeded free cash flow, finance leadership turned over in 2026, and the large franchisee Revo became insolvent.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_wh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 17; 10-K_2025-12-31_wh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 9; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wh-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 45; calculation C7

Dividends and buybacks were 148.28% of free cash flow from 2023 to 2025 and 151.55% in the nine months to 2026-09-30, with net borrowing filling the gap, and the board added USD 400 million of repurchase capacity in September 2026. The Revo failure cost USD 160 million, 81.22% of the USD 197 million book exposure, and the former parent, Travel + Leisure, supplies licence fees equal to 7.07% of FY2025 net revenues. Sponsor and governance risk is Medium: no sponsor or controlling holder is disclosed that could extract value, but board composition, the size of the restricted-payment baskets and the notes' change-of-control terms were not supplied.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_wh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 118; 10-K_2025-12-31_wh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 121; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wh-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 45; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wh-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 40; calculation C6; calculation C7; calculation C10; Source readiness (CP-0)

### Key findings

The franchise system is diversified and fee-based, but the diversification is not complete. More than six thousand franchisees pay the fees, none is above 2% of hotels on the issuer's count, and the United States supplies 77% of royalty contribution, so no single owner decides the cash flow. The Revo insolvency shows the limit: the rooms implied for Revo were 2.60% of system rooms at 2025-09-30, above the 2% the issuer cites by hotel count, and the two measures are not comparable. The creditor consequence is that fee income is spread widely, yet one regional operator was large enough to cost USD 160 million.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_wh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 9; 10-K_2025-12-31_wh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 17; 10-K_2025-12-31_wh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 6; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wh-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 27; calculation C3; calculation C10

Reported revenue overstates the fee base, because 39.33% of FY2025 net revenues is marketing, reservation and loyalty income that the issuer is generally obliged to spend for the system and that cost USD 3 million more than it raised that year. The profit-bearing streams are royalties and franchise fees at 37.86%, licence and other fees at 8.82% and partnership fees at 6.72%, together 53.39%, and the royalty rate is not rising: 4.0% globally and 4.7% in the United States in Q3 2026, against 4.0% and 4.8% a year earlier. The creditor consequence is that debt service rests on a narrower income base than the revenue line suggests.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_wh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 80; 10-K_2025-12-31_wh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 95; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wh-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 27; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wh-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 11; calculation C5

The former parent is a material related-party dependency. Travel + Leisure paid licence fees of USD 101 million in FY2025, 7.07% of net revenues and 12.22% above 2023, and two Wyndham directors sit on its board. Wyndham also carries one-third of the former parent's pre-spin contingent liabilities and guarantees of USD 127 million, with a fair value of USD 61 million, given on the former parent's sale of its European vacation rentals business, of which the former parent bears two-thirds of any loss, with a USD 41 million receivable from it. The creditor consequence is that weaker timeshare sales would cut fee income with no change in Wyndham's own hotels, and the contingent claims depend on the former parent paying its share; the quarterly reports repeat only the separation guarantee, so the current status of the credit support is not established.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_wh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 121; 10-K_2025-12-31_wh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 114; 10-K_2025-12-31_wh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 38; 10-K_2025-12-31_wh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 28; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wh-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 20; calculation C6

Shareholder returns are the clearest creditor-adverse behaviour, because they exceeded free cash flow in every period reviewed and were largely funded by net borrowing. Dividends and repurchases were 150.74% of free cash flow in 2023, 179.25% in 2024, 122.43% in 2025 and 151.55% in the nine months to 2026-09-30, a cumulative USD 1,336 million against USD 901 million of free cash flow over 2023 to 2025, with net borrowing of USD 522 million. Stockholders' equity fell 57.02% from USD 1,089 million at 2021 year-end to USD 468 million at 2025 year-end while total debt rose 22.84%, and in September 2026 the board added USD 400 million of repurchase capacity when USD 118.33 million remained at the end of August. The creditor consequence is that leverage is a board choice with no controlling holder to impose discipline, and the only restraint is the board's own policy and the restricted-payment covenant in the credit facilities, whose basket sizes were not supplied.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_wh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 82; 10-K_2025-12-31_wh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 49; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wh-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wh-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 45; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wh-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 40; calculation C7; calculation C8; calculation C9

The Revo insolvency shows that franchisee financing is a real credit exposure and that recoveries are slow. Revo carried USD 197 million of book value, of which USD 160 million, or 81.22%, was written off in FY2025, equal to 22.28% of FY2025 Adjusted EBITDA. In 2026 collateral enforcement took two European hotels with net assets of USD 23 million, equal to the loans and development advance notes they replaced, and about USD 19 million of restricted cash awaits court approval over months to years; Revo revenue is removed from the 2026 outlook and Revo rooms are excluded from the issuer's growth metrics. Wyndham keeps funding franchisees, with USD 105 million of development advance notes net of repayments in 2025 and about USD 110 million planned for 2026, and the notes stood at USD 383 million at 2026-09-30, 80.63% of stockholders' equity of USD 475 million. The creditor consequence is that growth funded by advances turns a franchisee failure into a balance-sheet loss, and because the notes are forgiven over the franchise term the cash is recovered only through fees.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_wh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 118; 10-K_2025-12-31_wh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 60; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wh-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 10; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wh-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 12; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wh-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 27; calculation C10; calculation C11

Finance leadership has changed within the period covered, and the credit case rests on continuity of reporting. The 2024 annual report names Michele Allen as chief financial officer; the 2025 annual report, signed on 2026-02-19, names Kurt Albert as interim chief financial officer; an employment letter for Amit Sripathi is dated 2026-02-27 and he signed the three 2026 quarterly reports as chief financial officer; Mr. Albert's separation agreement is dated 2026-03-06. The 2026-07-23 report is signed by Nicola Rossi as chief accounting officer and the 2026-10-22 report by Christopher Androski, and the filings give no reason for the changes. The chief executive officer, Geoffrey Ballotti, is the same in the 2024 and 2025 annual reports, internal control over financial reporting is stated effective at FY2025 year-end and the auditor, Deloitte, has served since 2017. The creditor consequence is that covenant reporting and add-back policy, which carry the adjusted leverage figures, are now signed off by a new chief financial officer and chief accounting officer, with no control weakness reported.
Basis: 10-K_2024-12-31_wh-20241231.htm.jsonl, page 18; 10-K_2025-12-31_wh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 19; 10-Q_2026-03-31_wh-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 40; 10-Q_2026-06-30_wh-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 44; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wh-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 45; 10-K_2025-12-31_wh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 72; 10-K_2025-12-31_wh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 80

Ownership and takeover risk cannot be tested from the filings: no controlling holder is named, but holders of 5% or more were not supplied. The company defended an unsuccessful hostile takeover in 2024, with transaction-related costs of USD 47 million in 2024 against USD 11 million in 2023, Delaware law restricts combinations with a holder of 15% or more, and the governance documents add advance-notice rules. Shares outstanding fell 2.99% between 2026-01-31 and 2026-10-15 as buybacks outran issuance. The creditor consequence is that a leveraged takeover is the clearest discontinuity risk: a change of control is an event of default under the credit facilities, so a bid would need a refinancing or waiver of the secured debt, while whether the notes give holders a change-of-control right is not established because the indentures were not supplied.
Basis: 10-K_2024-12-31_wh-20241231.htm.jsonl, page 54; 10-K_2024-12-31_wh-20241231.htm.jsonl, page 49; 10-K_2025-12-31_wh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 60; 10-K_2025-12-31_wh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 40; 10-K_2025-12-31_wh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wh-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 1; 10-K_2025-12-31_wh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 73; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wh-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 40; calculation C8; calculation C12

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a Medium rating is the absence of any creditor-adverse structure. There is no controlling shareholder or sponsor, the sources show no dividend recapitalisation, exchange offer, priming, unrestricted-subsidiary transfer or covenant waiver, dividends of USD 127 million were 39.56% of FY2025 free cash flow, the October 2025 revolver extension and the February 2026 notes moved maturities out to 2030 and 2033, internal control is stated effective with an audit by Deloitte, and the Revo loss came from one franchisee in a base of more than six thousand. The view would improve to Low if the proxy statement showed an independent board with no holder of strategic concern, if the credit agreement's restricted-payment baskets proved tight, and if shareholder returns stayed at or below free cash flow for two consecutive years. It would worsen to High if a second large franchisee failed, if licence fees from the former parent fell materially, if a bidder or the board proposed a debt-funded transaction, or if buybacks continued above free cash flow after leverage rose.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_wh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 62; 10-K_2025-12-31_wh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 58; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wh-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 14; 10-K_2025-12-31_wh-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 72; calculation C7

### Limitations

- The proxy statement was not supplied, so board size, independence, committee structure, executive pay and holders of 5% or more are not established; this limits the Medium governance rating, which could move either way once the board and holders are known.
- The credit agreement and note indentures were not supplied, so the size of the restricted-payment baskets, the definition of change of control and the guarantor list are not established; this limits the conclusion on shareholder returns, which may be tightly capped or effectively unconstrained.
- No filing after 2026-10-22 was supplied, so a further management change, financing or Revo settlement in the 27 days to the as-of date, and the reasons for the 2026 finance changes, are not established; they limit the leadership finding.
- The Revo exposure of USD 197 million is book value before the charge, not the amount contractually owed, so the 81.22% write-off measures a loss of carrying value and not of principal; the USD 19 million of restricted cash could still change the recovery.
- Licence fees from the former parent are stated only for full years, so the 2026 size of that dependency is not established and the 7.07% share is a FY2025 figure.
- Rooms and properties are stated on the issuer's recast basis (Super 8 China excluded from 2025, Revo excluded from growth metrics), so room-growth comparisons with the 2024 annual report are not like for like and the diversification finding rests on the recast figures.

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

The only new debt issue in the period is the February 2026 sale of USD 650 million of senior unsecured notes due 2033, with net proceeds of USD 642 million, a first call date of 2029-03-01 and semi-annual interest from 2026-09-01. The proceeds repaid revolver borrowings and term loan A, which removed the 2027 term loan maturity and left the August 2028 notes as the next large maturity, and added fixed interest of about USD 36.56 million a year. Wyndham also assumed USD 15 million of mortgages on the two European hotels it took by enforcing collateral against Revo in the first quarter of 2026.
