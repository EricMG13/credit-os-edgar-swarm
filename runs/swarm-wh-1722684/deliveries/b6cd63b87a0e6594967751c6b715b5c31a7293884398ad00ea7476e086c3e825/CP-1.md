### Analysis: leverage trajectory and drivers

Net leverage on unadjusted EBITDA rose from 4.17x at FY2024 to 5.38x at FY2025 and 5.48x on the last twelve months to 2026-09-30, while net leverage on the issuer's adjusted EBITDA moved from 3.40x to 3.48x and 3.59x. The gap between the two is the issuer's add-backs, USD 254 million in FY2025 and USD 255 million on the last twelve months, mostly stock-based compensation, development advance note amortization and the Revo charges. FY2025 deteriorated because Revo charges of USD 160 million cut unadjusted EBITDA while net debt rose USD 136 million; since then net debt has risen another USD 160 million as cash returned to shareholders exceeded free cash flow, and unadjusted net leverage has stayed between 5.32x and 5.51x at the three 2026 quarter ends as last-twelve-months unadjusted EBITDA recovered to USD 485 million. The creditor consequence is that leverage on unadjusted EBITDA stepped up in FY2025 and has not come back down, while the adjusted figure looks stable, so the add-backs carry the credit case.

### Analysis: covenant position

The credit facilities carry one financial maintenance covenant, a maximum first-lien leverage ratio of 5.0 times tested quarterly, and the sources state no step-down.
