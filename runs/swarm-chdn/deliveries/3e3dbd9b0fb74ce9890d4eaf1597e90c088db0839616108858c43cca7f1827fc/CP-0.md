## Findings

### Bottom line

The seven supplied filings and releases support the financial foundation, business and governance, and fundamental credit work on Churchill Downs Inc through the third quarter of 2026, but covenant, legal-priority, ratings and market-implied work lack executed documents, rating reports and quotes.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_chdn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 13; 10-Q_2026-09-30_chdn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 27–28; EX-99.1_2026-10-28_ex991pressrelease10-28x26.htm.jsonl, page 1
