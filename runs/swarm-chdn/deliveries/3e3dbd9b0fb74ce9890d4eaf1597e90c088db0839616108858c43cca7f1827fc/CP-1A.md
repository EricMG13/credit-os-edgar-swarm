## Findings

### Bottom line

Churchill Downs is a listed, widely held company with no sponsor or controlling owner identified in the documents, so creditors rely on management's capital allocation, which turned from heavy buybacks in 2025 to debt repayment in 2026, rather than on owner support.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_chdn-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 52; 10-K_2025-12-31_chdn-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 133–134; 10-Q_2026-09-30_chdn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 1; 10-Q_2026-09-30_chdn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 4; calculation C03

Earnings are concentrated in the Kentucky Derby quarter and in historical racing machine venues, and 14.78% of FY2025 Adjusted EBITDA is equity income from two unconsolidated casinos whose cash arrives as distributions. Sponsor and governance risk is assessed as medium: shareholder returns absorbed 92.67% of free cash flow in FY2025 but have been restrained in 2026, and no creditor-adverse action is documented, while ownership percentages and the financing documents were not supplied.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_chdn-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 125–126; EX-99.1_2026-07-29_ex991pressrelease07-29x26.htm.jsonl, page 1; 10-K_2025-12-31_chdn-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 78–79; calculation C02; calculation C03; calculation C04

### Key findings

The business is a three-segment racing, wagering and casino operator led by Live and Historical Racing, which was 47.67% of FY2025 net revenue and 49.10% of FY2025 segment Adjusted EBITDA, with Gaming at 35.64% of revenue and 37.23% of segment earnings. That mix ties earnings to one flagship event and to historical racing machine regulation in Kentucky and Virginia, so a regulatory or competitive change in a few states moves consolidated cash flow.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_chdn-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 6; 10-K_2025-12-31_chdn-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 125–126; calculation C01

Earnings are strongly seasonal: the second quarter, which holds the Kentucky Derby, produced USD 451 million of USD 1,205.3 million of FY2025 Adjusted EBITDA, or 37.42%, so any single quarter-end balance sheet or leverage figure misrepresents the year and leverage is best read on a last-twelve-months basis.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_chdn-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 125–126; 10-K_2025-12-31_chdn-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 16; EX-99.1_2026-07-29_ex991pressrelease07-29x26.htm.jsonl, page 1; calculation C02

Equity investees are a material but cash-lagged part of earnings: Gaming segment Adjusted EBITDA includes USD 178.1 million of equity income from the 61.3% interest in Midwest Gaming (Rivers Des Plaines) and the 50% interest in Miami Valley Gaming, equal to 14.78% of consolidated Adjusted EBITDA, while distributions received in FY2025 were USD 116.0 million against equity income of USD 139.4 million. Creditors reach that cash only as distributions, and the investees' own debt is outside the consolidated debt table.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_chdn-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 14; 10-K_2025-12-31_chdn-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 125–126; 10-K_2025-12-31_chdn-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 77–79; calculation C04

Capital allocation is the main ownership-related risk. In FY2025 dividends of USD 30.8 million and buybacks of USD 427.8 million were 92.67% of free cash flow of USD 494.9 million, revolver borrowings rose by USD 279.5 million and the company bought 90% of Casino Salem for USD 180 million; in nine months 2026 dividends and buybacks were USD 75 million against free cash flow of USD 484 million and revolver borrowings fell by USD 578 million. Restraint in 2026 lowers risk now, but the new USD 750 million repurchase authorization shows that capacity to resume larger returns exists.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_chdn-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 78–79; 10-K_2025-12-31_chdn-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 66; 10-K_2025-12-31_chdn-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 67; 10-Q_2026-09-30_chdn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 2–4; 10-Q_2026-09-30_chdn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 13; 10-Q_2026-09-30_chdn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 28; 10-Q_2026-09-30_chdn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 8; calculation C03; calculation C05

Expansion is debt-funded before it earns: Casino Salem is being redeveloped as Rockingham Grand Casino with USD 180 million to USD 200 million of planned investment for a mid-2027 opening, a USD 280 million to USD 300 million building at Churchill Downs Racetrack is planned by the 2028 Kentucky Derby, and a USD 85 million Preakness purchase was agreed in April 2026 with its closing not stated. These commitments compete with debt reduction in 2027 and 2028.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_chdn-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 7; 10-K_2025-12-31_chdn-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 11; EX-99.1_2026-04-22_ex991pressrelease04-22x26.htm.jsonl, page 1; 10-Q_2026-03-31_chdn-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 20

No sponsor, equity support, drop-down, priming or amendment-and-extension behaviour against creditors is documented. The September 2026 bank amendment extended maturities, and the largest related-party style transaction, the repurchase of one million shares from an affiliate of The Duchossois Group in 2023, was approved by the board separately and funded with cash and credit facility borrowings. Legal capacity for restricted payments and debt cannot be tested because no executed financing documents were supplied.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_chdn-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 104; 10-Q_2026-09-30_chdn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 14

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a cautious view is the 2026 record: buybacks fell to USD 44 million in nine months from USD 396 million a year earlier, revolver borrowings fell by USD 578 million, the bank facilities were extended to September 2031, and the dividend has risen for sixteen consecutive years and is small against free cash flow. A diversified base of ten owned casinos, many racing and historical racing venues and a flagship event also limits single-asset risk. The view would turn adverse if buybacks under the new USD 750 million authorization were funded with revolver draws, if the USD 85 million Preakness purchase or the Rockingham investment were financed by borrowing without offsetting repayment, or if the proxy statement or financing documents showed a concentrated holder or loose restricted-payment baskets. It would improve if covenant ratios and indenture terms confirmed tight limits on distributions.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_chdn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-Q_2026-09-30_chdn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 28; 10-Q_2026-09-30_chdn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 43; 10-K_2025-12-31_chdn-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 12–13; calculation C03

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

Casino Salem was the last completed acquisition: on August 27, 2025 the company bought 90% of the equity of Casino Salem for a base price of USD 180 million, recorded as an asset acquisition with a USD 197 million gaming rights intangible, and funded it with revolver borrowings. The company then redevelops the site as Rockingham Grand Casino, with USD 180 million to USD 200 million planned and a mid-2027 opening.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_chdn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 8; 10-K_2025-12-31_chdn-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 66; EX-99.1_2026-04-22_ex991pressrelease04-22x26.htm.jsonl, page 1

The financing changed materially in September and October 2026. On September 25, 2026 the company closed the eighth amendment to the Credit Agreement, extending the revolver and term loan A to September 2031 with a springing maturity, and entered a new USD 500 million term loan B due 2033 at 99.875% priced at SOFR plus 175 basis points; proceeds repaid the 2028 term loan B and part of the revolver. A conditional redemption notice for the 2027 notes was issued on September 18, 2026 and the notes were redeemed on October 19, 2026 with revolver borrowings, so the debt table at 2026-09-30 still shows them.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_chdn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 14; 10-Q_2026-09-30_chdn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 15; 10-Q_2026-09-30_chdn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 16; 10-Q_2026-09-30_chdn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 28
