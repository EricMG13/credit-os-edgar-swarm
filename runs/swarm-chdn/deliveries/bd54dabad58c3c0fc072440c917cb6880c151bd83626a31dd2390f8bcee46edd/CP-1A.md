## Findings

### Bottom line

Churchill Downs is a company with no sponsor identified, so creditors depend on board capital-allocation choices, while a seasonal Derby profit pool, legally contingent machine revenue and a possible sale of nine gaming properties make the earnings base less certain than leverage suggests.

The second quarter, which holds Kentucky Derby week, produced 47.80% of nine-month Adjusted EBITDA, historical racing machines in Kentucky and Virginia supplied 33.35% of nine-month revenue, and the company says nine properties carrying USD 280 million to USD 300 million of annual Adjusted EBITDA are under review for sale. Governance risk is Medium because shareholder returns, which reached 92.67% of free cash flow in FY2025, can reverse the 2026 deleveraging, and the evidence on control and legal capacity is incomplete.

### Key findings

No sponsor or controlling holder is identified, so creditors have no equity backstop to rely on and no sponsor extraction pattern to price, and the capital-allocation record is the best evidence of how the board treats lenders. The company is widely held and its filings defer director, ownership and related-party detail to a proxy statement that is not supplied, so the absence of a controlling holder is the reading of the filings rather than a verified fact.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_chdn-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-K_2025-12-31_chdn-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 52; 10-K_2025-12-31_chdn-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 134; Source readiness (CP-0)

Earnings are concentrated in the quarter that holds Kentucky Derby week. Second-quarter Adjusted EBITDA of USD 477 million was 47.80% of the nine-month total and 38.30% of the last twelve months, and second-quarter revenue was 41.90% of nine-month revenue, so one event week largely decides each year's cash generation, and quarter-end debt readings should be set against last-twelve-months earnings rather than a single quarter.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-04-22_ex991pressrelease04-22x26.htm.jsonl, page 1; EX-99.1_2026-07-29_ex991pressrelease07-29x26.htm.jsonl, page 1; EX-99.1_2026-10-28_ex991pressrelease10-28x26.htm.jsonl, page 1; calculation C3

Machine revenue is legally contingent and sits in two states. Historical racing machines produced 33.35% of nine-month revenue, with 52.55% of the machines in Kentucky and 47.45% in Virginia, and the Louisiana Supreme Court's ruling ended the company's machines in Louisiana on 2025-05-08 and sent them mainly to Virginia. In Q3 2026 Central Virginia earnings fell by USD 10 million, including USD 5 million of new tax under a structure effective 2026-07-01 and the effect of competition, so the redeployed capacity is facing a tougher market while one adverse ruling or tax change in either state would cut revenue directly.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The best evidence for lower governance risk is the record: audited reporting, an effective control report, no documented extraction, cure request or liability management, a dividend that has risen for sixteen consecutive years at a small cash cost, and buybacks that paused while the revolver fell by USD 578 million in 2026. The supplied legal proceedings disclosure describes only ordinary routine litigation, with no pending ruling against machines in Kentucky or Virginia. A proxy statement showing a controlling holder or related-party arrangements, or buybacks resuming with revolver funding, would raise the risk level, while sale proceeds applied to debt and a defined perimeter would lower it.

### Limitations

- Because the proxy statement is not supplied, the finding that no sponsor or controlling holder exists rests on the annual report cover and its deferral of ownership detail, and a disclosed large holder would turn the governance reading from capital-allocation discipline into a control question.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_chdn-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-K_2025-12-31_chdn-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 134
- The sale exploration is described by one sentence, so the perimeter view cannot say which properties would leave or whether proceeds reduce debt, and a signed sale agreement would replace the open perimeter with a defined one.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_chdn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 32
- The credit agreement and indentures are not supplied, so legal capacity for distributions, asset sales and priming is separated from willingness only by documented behaviour, and the Medium risk level would move if the baskets prove tight or wide.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_chdn-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 135; 10-K_2025-12-31_chdn-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 140

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

The company issued no new notes in 2026 and entered one new debt instrument, a USD 500 million term loan B due 2033 issued at 99.875% of principal at 175 bp over SOFR on 2026-09-25, alongside an eighth amendment that extended the revolver and term loan A to September 2031 with a springing maturity. The proceeds repaid the term loan B-1 and revolver borrowings, so the transaction extended maturities rather than adding debt, and the secured share of principal then rose when the USD 600 million 2027 notes were redeemed with revolver borrowings on 2026-10-19.

The re-acquisition removed a put right and a redeemable noncontrolling interest, while the sale exploration has no stated terms.
