## Findings

### Bottom line

The seven filings support a credit snapshot of Churchill Downs Incorporated through Q3 2026, but creditors cannot yet verify debt terms, covenant headroom or the effect of the possible sale of nine regional gaming properties. Statements, the debt note, segment results and the Adjusted EBITDA reconciliation are present for FY2025 and for each 2026 quarter, and their totals tie. The debt agreements, proxy statement, ratings and market prices are not in the set. Two points need care: the filing's net debt line does not deduct cash, and USD 600 million of 2027 notes was redeemed on 2026-10-19, after the balance sheet date.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_chdn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 13–16; 10-Q_2026-09-30_chdn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 28; calculation C1; calculation C2

### Key findings

Every period the later analyses need is printed in the set: the audited FY2025 year, the three 2026 quarterly reports and the three matching earnings releases, which together give FY2025, nine months 2026 and nine months 2025 for a last-twelve-months build. The latest document is dated 2026-10-28, eight days before the cut-off of 2026-11-05, and nothing in the set is dated after the cut-off, so no later fact enters the analysis.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_chdn-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 77; 10-Q_2026-09-30_chdn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 2–4; EX-99.1_2026-10-28_ex991pressrelease10-28x26.htm.jsonl, page 1; calculation C8

The reporting entity is Churchill Downs Incorporated, one consolidated registrant, so debt, earnings and cash flow are group figures and no subsidiary perimeter has to be separated. The 2030 senior notes were issued by a wholly owned subsidiary, and the indentures and the credit agreement are described as having guarantors that the supplied text does not name, so the legal obligor and guarantor structure is not established.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_chdn-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 111; 10-K_2025-12-31_chdn-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 135–140

Debt evidence is consistent across the debt note, the management discussion table and the balance sheet: total debt was USD 4,748 million at 2026-09-30 against USD 5,155 million at 2025-12-31, and the balance sheet lines reconcile to the debt note at both dates. The fall reflects the September 2026 refinancing, which added a USD 500 million term loan due 2033, moved the revolver and term loan A maturity from July 2029 to September 2031 and repaid the older term loan B-1 and revolver borrowings. The extension carries a springing maturity that applies if notes maturing before 2031-09-25 are not refinanced at least 91 days before they fall due.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_chdn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 13–15; 10-Q_2026-09-30_chdn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 43–44; calculation C1; calculation C2; calculation C4

USD 600 million of 2027 senior notes was redeemed on 2026-10-19 with revolver borrowings, after the balance sheet date. The September balance sheet shows current maturities of USD 659 million against USD 63 million at year-end, and the supplied text does not itemise those current maturities, so Financial foundation has to carry the redemption as a dated event with its own pro forma line and never blend it into September amounts.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_chdn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-Q_2026-09-30_chdn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 16; 10-Q_2026-09-30_chdn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 28; EX-99.1_2026-10-28_ex991pressrelease10-28x26.htm.jsonl, page 3

Three definitions can mislead a reader who has not seen the filings. The quarterly report's net debt line of USD 4,063 million is debt net of current maturities and issuance costs, not debt net of cash, because cash and cash equivalents of USD 167 million are not deducted in it. Adjusted EBITDA includes the company's share of equity-investee EBITDA and the portion attributable to noncontrolling interests, and segment revenue is stated before intercompany eliminations, so Q3 2026 revenue of USD 714 million across the three segments reconciles to consolidated net revenue of USD 696 million only after All Other revenue of USD 2 million and USD 20 million of eliminations. The releases also quote net bank leverage of 3.8x, 3.7x and 3.8x for Q1, Q2 and Q3 2026 with no definition or calculation printed.

Covenant evidence is dated. The annual report prints interest coverage of 3.9x against a required level above 2.5x and a consolidated total secured net leverage ratio of 1.4x against a maximum below 4.0x at 2025-12-31, with a statement that the company complied with all applicable covenants at that date. The 2026 quarterly reports print neither the tests nor a compliance statement, so covenant headroom at 2026-09-30 is not established from the set.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_chdn-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 69–70; 10-K_2025-12-31_chdn-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 109–110; 10-Q_2026-09-30_chdn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 14

On 2026-07-29 the company announced that it is exploring a sale of nine wholly owned regional gaming properties, which it says represent USD 280 million to USD 300 million of annual Adjusted EBITDA including retail sportsbook and market access. The only statement of this in the set is one sentence in the Q3 2026 quarterly report, and the earlier Q2 documents mention strategic alternatives only in their risk language, so the announcement, the property list, any buyer, price, timetable or use of proceeds are not established. Until they are, the perimeter that Business & governance and Fundamental credit describe stays open.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_chdn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 32; 10-Q_2026-06-30_chdn-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 26; EX-99.1_2026-07-29_ex991pressrelease07-29x26.htm.jsonl, pages 7–8

The debt documents, the proxy statement and any rating or market price are the main absent sources. The annual report lists the indentures, supplemental indentures and credit agreement among its exhibits but they are not supplied, and directors, ownership and compensation are incorporated by reference to a proxy statement that is also absent. Debt terms therefore rest on the filings' own summaries, and legal and covenant analysis cannot be completed from this set.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest case against calling the set ready is that the terms creditors rely on most sit in executed documents that are missing: the springing maturity, the guarantor list and the covenant definitions are known only from summaries, and the 2026 reports print no covenant test. Against that, the summaries agree with each other and with the balance sheets, every total tied in the checks, and the company states compliance at year-end, so the figures look reliable and only the legal reading is open. Supplying the eighth amendment to the credit agreement, the indentures and the 2026-07-29 announcement would let the legal and perimeter questions be answered, and a later filing that restates 2026 figures or confirms a sale agreement would require the analyses to be rerun.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_chdn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 14; 10-Q_2026-09-30_chdn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 32; calculation C1; calculation C2; calculation C3; calculation C5; calculation C6
