## Findings

### Bottom line

Churchill Downs has substantial reported revolver availability and positive free cash flow, but carrying-value net leverage remains high and the 2027 refinancing shifts debt into the secured revolver. At September 30, 2026, calculated net debt was USD 4,555 million, or 4.55x trailing reported EBITDA; management separately reported 3.8x net bank leverage. Creditors should treat liquidity as adequate on the balance-sheet date while retaining refinancing and definition risk.
Basis: calculation C01; calculation C03; 10-Q_2026-09-30_chdn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 44; EX-99.1_2026-10-28_ex991pressrelease10-28x26.htm.jsonl, page 1

Q3 reported EBITDA was USD 204 million and issuer-adjusted EBITDA was USD 264 million; trailing adjusted EBITDA was USD 1,245.3 million. Trailing levered free cash flow was USD 529.9 million, but cash generation remains exposed to seasonal deferred revenue, investment spending and floating-rate debt. The October 19 redemption of the 2027 notes was funded with revolver borrowings; the amount drawn and resulting pro forma net debt are not established in the supplied disclosures. The amended facility also has a springing maturity tied to earlier-dated notes, leaving execution risk around the 2028 and 2030 maturities.
Basis: calculation C04; 10-Q_2026-09-30_chdn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 14, 16, 28; EX-99.1_2026-10-28_ex991pressrelease10-28x26.htm.jsonl, pages 1–4

### Leverage

At the latest balance-sheet date, carrying debt less cash equalled USD 4,555 million. That is 4.55x trailing reported EBITDA and 3.66x issuer-adjusted EBITDA, using the respective trailing measures. The difference between reported and adjusted EBITDA is material: FY2025 reported EBITDA calculated from operating income plus depreciation and amortisation was USD 916.9 million, versus issuer-adjusted EBITDA of USD 1,205.3 million. The adjusted measure includes equity-investment income and excludes selected items, so it should not replace the reported measure without a bridge to the leverage covenant calculation.
Basis: calculation C01; calculation C02; calculation C03; 10-K_2025-12-31_chdn-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 64–65; 10-Q_2026-09-30_chdn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 2–3, 44

Management's net bank leverage was 3.8x at Q3 2026, 3.7x at Q2 and 3.8x at Q1, against calculated net debt to trailing reported EBITDA of 4.55x at September 30. The issuer's bank measure is not reconciled to balance-sheet carrying debt, cash eligibility, restricted cash, EBITDA adjustments or the applicable covenant perimeter in the supplied material. Use the disclosed bank ratio as a management measure and the calculated ratio as a separate carrying-value indicator; do not treat them as interchangeable.
Basis: calculation C03; EX-99.1_2026-04-22_ex991pressrelease04-22x26.htm.jsonl, page 1; EX-99.1_2026-07-29_ex991pressrelease07-29x26.htm.jsonl, page 1; EX-99.1_2026-10-28_ex991pressrelease10-28x26.htm.jsonl, page 1

### Earnings and EBITDA

FY2025 consolidated revenue was USD 2,925.9 million and operating income was USD 683.8 million. Calculated reported EBITDA was USD 916.9 million. Adjusted EBITDA was USD 1,205.3 million, including USD 819.8 million of adjustments from net income under the issuer's reconciliation. This reconciliation is not a bridge from calculated reported EBITDA: it begins with net income and includes equity-investment expense adjustments and other exclusions. No directly comparable bridge from reported EBITDA to adjusted EBITDA is supplied.
Basis: calculation C02; 10-K_2025-12-31_chdn-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 64–65, 77

Trailing twelve-month revenue at September 30, 2026 was USD 3,004.9 million and adjusted EBITDA was USD 1,245.3 million. The Q3 release shows adjusted EBITDA of USD 264 million, up USD 2 million year on year. Q2 adjusted EBITDA was USD 477 million and Q1 was USD 257 million. The quarter-to-quarter profile reflects meaningful racing seasonality, so the nine-month and trailing measures are more representative than annualising a single quarter.
Basis: calculation C02; EX-99.1_2026-04-22_ex991pressrelease04-22x26.htm.jsonl, pages 1–2; EX-99.1_2026-07-29_ex991pressrelease07-29x26.htm.jsonl, pages 1–3; EX-99.1_2026-10-28_ex991pressrelease10-28x26.htm.jsonl, pages 1–4

### Cash flow and conversion

Operating cash flow was USD 660 million in 9M 2026, down USD 14 million year on year. The issuer attributes the decrease mainly to higher cash taxes, partly offset by higher distributions from equity-method investments and lower cash interest payments. Cash taxes paid rose to USD 65 million from USD 17 million, while cash interest paid declined to USD 173 million from USD 193 million. Cash taxes therefore absorbed more of the period's operating cash generation than in the prior-year comparison.
Basis: calculation C04; 10-Q_2026-09-30_chdn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 4, 42–43

Calculated trailing operating cash flow was USD 755.8 million and maintenance plus project capital expenditure was USD 225.9 million, producing USD 529.9 million of levered free cash flow before dividends and repurchases. This definition is operating cash flow less cash capital expenditure and includes working-capital movements; it is not an issuer-defined free-cash-flow measure. The company expects significant 2026 project capital expenditure, but timing can vary. Working-capital movements were positive USD 128 million in Q1, positive USD 14 million in H1 and negative USD 6 million in 9M, primarily reflecting swings in income taxes and deferred revenue rather than a uniform recurring cash contribution.
Basis: calculation C04; 10-Q_2026-03-31_chdn-20260331.htm.jsonl, pages 4, 10; 10-Q_2026-06-30_chdn-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-Q_2026-09-30_chdn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 4, 43

### Liquidity and debt service

At September 30, 2026, reported cash was USD 167 million and available revolver capacity was USD 1,100 million after letters of credit, for USD 1,267 million of stated cash plus committed availability. The capacity was USD 722 million at March 31 and USD 861 million at June 30, reflecting changes in revolver borrowings and letters of credit. Cash excludes restricted cash, which was USD 92 million at September 30. The disclosed availability supports near-term liquidity, but the October redemption was financed by the revolver and no post-redemption capacity or current drawn amount is supplied.
Basis: calculation C05; 10-Q_2026-03-31_chdn-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 10; 10-Q_2026-06-30_chdn-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 12; 10-Q_2026-09-30_chdn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 3, 14, 28

At September 30, principal due by final instrument maturity was USD 600 million in 2027, USD 700 million in 2028, USD 1,200 million in 2030, USD 1,748 million in 2031 and USD 500 million in 2033. The 2027 notes were redeemed after the balance-sheet date using revolver borrowings. This maturity view groups each instrument's outstanding principal by final stated maturity and does not allocate scheduled amortisation to earlier years; it is therefore not an annual contractual cash debt-service schedule. The Term Loan A and Term Loan B also require quarterly amortisation.
Basis: calculation C06; 10-Q_2026-09-30_chdn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 13–16, 28

The September 2026 amendment extended the revolver and Term Loan A final maturities to September 2031, subject to a springing maturity if earlier-dated notes or other material debt are not refinanced sufficiently ahead of their stated maturity. The 2028 and 2030 notes therefore remain relevant to the facility's maturity condition. The 2031 Revolver and Term Loan A have a 150 bp applicable margin over SOFR at September 30; the new 2033 Term Loan B has a 175 bp spread over SOFR. Variable-rate debt was USD 1.6 billion, and the issuer estimates a 100 bp SOFR increase would reduce net income and operating cash flow by USD 12 million if balances were unchanged.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_chdn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 14, 16, 45

### Working capital and other obligations

Deferred revenue is an operating funding float rather than financial debt. Current plus non-current deferred revenue rose from USD 70.1 million at FY2025 to USD 173 million at March 31, 2026, then fell to USD 39 million at June 30 and USD 48 million at September 30. The timing of Kentucky Derby receipts and revenue recognition can therefore support cash temporarily, but it reverses as performance obligations are satisfied; it should not be treated as permanent debt capacity.
Basis: calculation C07; 10-K_2025-12-31_chdn-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 77; 10-Q_2026-03-31_chdn-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-Q_2026-06-30_chdn-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-Q_2026-09-30_chdn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 3, 17

Lease obligations are outside the reported financial-debt lines. At September 30, the company disclosed USD 52 million of operating-lease minimum rent, with USD 7 million due in the next twelve months, and USD 31 million of financing-obligation rent, with USD 4 million due in the next twelve months. These contractual obligations compete for cash even though the balance sheet's financial debt total does not include them.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_chdn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 48

### Post-balance-sheet events and limitations

On October 19, 2026, the company redeemed USD 600 million of 2027 Senior Notes with borrowings from the 2031 Revolver. The event replaces unsecured notes with secured bank debt; because the exact draw, fees, accrued interest and post-event cash balance are not disclosed, post-event net debt and leverage are not established. The event is excluded from all balance-sheet-date charts and ratios.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_chdn-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 16, 28

On October 27, 2026, the board declared a USD 0.469 per-share annual dividend payable January 5, 2027 and approved a repurchase authorization of up to USD 750 million that includes, rather than adds to, USD 306 million remaining under the prior authorization. These announcements are not treated as debt or cash outflows at September 30; the aggregate dividend and future repurchases are not established from the supplied information.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_chdn-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 28; EX-99.1_2026-10-28_ex991pressrelease10-28x26.htm.jsonl, pages 3–4
