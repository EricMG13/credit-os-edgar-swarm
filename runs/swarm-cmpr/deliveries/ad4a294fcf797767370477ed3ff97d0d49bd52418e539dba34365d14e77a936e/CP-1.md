## Findings

### Bottom line

Leverage rose and liquidity fell in the September 2026 quarter, with net debt at 3.93x trailing reported EBITDA, because the Saxoprint acquisition was funded from cash and the revolver while trailing earnings slipped, so creditors hold a thinner cushion going into the seasonal peak.

Net debt rose 11.80% in the quarter, and undrawn revolver capacity fell from USD 232.7 million to USD 197.7 million as USD 35.0 million was drawn. On the issuer's own adjusted EBITDA net leverage is 3.39x and cash interest cover 4.11x, but that measure keeps USD 14.4 million of one-off tariff refunds.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_cmpr-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 20–21, 33, 46; calculation C35; calculation C41

### Key findings

- Leverage improved through FY2026 and gave much of it back in the September 2026 quarter. Net debt to reported EBITDA was 3.68x at June 2025, 3.38x at June 2026 and 3.93x on a trailing basis at 30 September 2026, and on adjusted EBITDA it was 3.12x, 2.97x and 3.39x. Reported EBITDA rose in FY2026 while net debt barely moved, but the Saxoprint purchase and a seasonal inventory build then cut cash while trailing EBITDA slipped, so creditors enter the December peak with less margin.
Basis: 10-K_2026-06-30_cmpr-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 87, 117; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cmpr-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 3, 18, 43–44; calculation C01; calculation C04; calculation C05; calculation C15; calculation C16
- Cash interest cover is steady rather than improving. EBITDA over cash interest paid was 3.34x, 3.67x and 3.54x at the same dates, and 4.11x on adjusted EBITDA at 30 September 2026. Cash interest paid has been flat to slightly lower, so cover moves with earnings, and 67.73% of principal floats on Term SOFR: a 100 bp rise in rates would add USD 7.4 million to annual interest after swaps.
Basis: 10-K_2026-06-30_cmpr-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 90; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cmpr-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 5, 21, 50; calculation C17; calculation C36; calculation C43
- Cash conversion is weakening as capital spending rises. Adjusted free cash flow was 55.70% of adjusted EBITDA in FY2024, 34.17% in FY2025 and 26.70% in FY2026, while capex including capitalised software rose from 3.44% of revenue to 4.48%, mostly for manufacturing and automation equipment in the North American network. The latest quarter was an adjusted free cash outflow of USD 9.25 million, so less internally generated cash is available for debt reduction while acquisitions and share purchases compete for the same cash.
Basis: 10-K_2026-06-30_cmpr-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 69, 75, 89; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cmpr-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 44–45, 49
- Liquidity thinned in the quarter. Cash plus undrawn revolver capacity was 0.19x of total debt at 30 September 2026 against 0.30x at 30 June 2026. The leverage covenant applies only if drawings exceed 20% of the USD 250.0 million commitment, which is USD 50.0 million, and drawings were USD 35.0 million, so USD 15.0 million of further drawings would bring the test into play; the lender-defined ratio against its 4.50x limit is not printed, so headroom is not established.
Basis: 10-K_2026-06-30_cmpr-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 72, 119; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cmpr-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 21, 46; calculation C37; calculation C38; calculation C39
- Maturities are distant but concentrated. The USD 525.0 million of 2032 notes and the USD 1,094.5 million term loan are 97.11% of principal and fall due in 2032 or 2033, with the term loan due in 2032 if the notes are not repaid, refinanced or extended, and the revolver matures in 2031. Apart from scheduled amortisation and a small amount of other debt, nothing falls due before 2031, so the credit case rests on refinancing the 2032 notes ahead of mid-2032.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_cmpr-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 21–22, 45–47; calculation C40; calculation C26
- The adjusted measure flatters the picture. Adjusted EBITDA exceeds reported EBITDA by 13.81% in FY2026, the largest add-back is share-based compensation, and USD 14.4 million of tariff refunds recognised in the last two quarters stays inside the measure; without it, net leverage on adjusted EBITDA would be 3.50x rather than 3.39x.
Basis: 10-K_2026-06-30_cmpr-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 55, 57, 75; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cmpr-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 33, 35, 49; calculation C41; calculation C42
- Reported growth overstates underlying growth, and margins are lower. Revenue grew 9.80% in FY2026 and 8.20% in the September 2026 quarter, but organic constant-currency growth was 4% and 2%, with currency and acquisitions supplying the rest, while the adjusted EBITDA margin fell from 14.24% in FY2024 to 12.27% in FY2026 and 11.81% on a trailing basis, so growth is not yet converting into earnings.
Basis: 10-K_2026-06-30_cmpr-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 58–59, 75, 87; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cmpr-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 3, 35–36, 49; calculation C44

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest support is the June 2026 refinancing, which extended the term loan to 2033 and the revolver to 2031 and leaves no large maturity before 2031, together with steady cash interest cover and a seasonal pattern in which the December quarter carries the highest earnings: adjusted EBITDA in the December 2025 quarter was 1.41x the September 2025 quarter. A completed sale-leaseback of the Saxoprint facility, now held for sale, would cut net debt, and net leverage returning towards the 3.38x seen at June 2026 would reverse the concern. Further revolver drawings above USD 50.0 million, or a fall in trailing EBITDA, would strengthen it.
Basis: 10-K_2026-06-30_cmpr-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 118; 10-Q_2025-12-31_cmpr-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 54; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cmpr-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 19, 49; calculation C45; calculation C37

### Limitations

- The fourth quarter of FY2026 is not reported in any readable source and is derived as the full year less nine months; the earnings release for that quarter holds no text.
- No balance sheet is supplied for June 2024 or September 2025, so leverage before June 2025 and a year-ago comparison of the latest quarter are not established.
- The term loan amortisation schedule is not stated, so the maturity ladder shows amortisation after the first year at final maturity and understates near-term repayments.
- The lender definition of EBITDA and the reported covenant ratio are not printed, so covenant headroom is not established, and the test definition changed on 4 June 2026.
- Reported EBITDA is calculated because the company prints only adjusted EBITDA, and the amount of letters of credit under the revolver is not stated separately.
