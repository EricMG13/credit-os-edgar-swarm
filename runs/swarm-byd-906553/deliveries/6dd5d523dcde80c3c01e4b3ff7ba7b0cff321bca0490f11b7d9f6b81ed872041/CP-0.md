## Findings

### Bottom line

The five Boyd Gaming filings are enough to support a credit snapshot of the consolidated company through the September 30, 2026 quarter, but not covenant headroom, legal priority or rating context, which remain open for creditors.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_byd_bgc20260930-10q.htm.jsonl, pages 2–3, 22–24

The latest filing is dated 21 days before the as-of date, the statements, debt note and liquidity discussion agree with one another, and a trailing twelve-month view can be built from filed figures. The earliest maturity date for creditors is the September 1, 2027 springing maturity, which moved USD 800.9 million of credit facility borrowings into current maturities while the USD 1,000.0 million senior notes due 2027 remain outstanding.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_byd_bgc20260930-10q.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 21, 69; calculation C1; calculation C3; calculation C6

Legal, ratings, peer and market questions are conditional on documents the filings only summarise or omit.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_byd_bgc20251121-10k.htm.jsonl, pages 154–156; 10-Q_2026-09-30_byd_bgc20260930-10q.htm.jsonl, pages 22–24

### Key findings

Every filing reports Boyd Gaming Corporation on a consolidated basis, with the senior notes guaranteed by certain wholly owned domestic restricted subsidiaries, so debt, earnings and liquidity describe one reporting entity across all periods. No subsidiary-only statements are supplied; each filing carries summarised combined information for the parent and the guarantor group, which is the only separate financial view of that group.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_byd_bgc20260930-10q.htm.jsonl, pages 7–8, 62–63; 10-K_2025-12-31_byd_bgc20251121-10k.htm.jsonl, page 63

The filings cover fiscal 2024 and fiscal 2025 and the first three quarters of 2026 with 2025 comparatives, so a trailing twelve-month position to September 30, 2026 can be built from filed figures; a stand-alone fourth quarter of 2025 is not printed and would have to be derived. The perimeter moved over the period, with 28 wholly owned properties in the 2024 annual report, 27 in the 2025 annual report and 26 after the August 1, 2026 sale of Sam's Town Shreveport, so period comparisons must use the perimeter changes the filings disclose.
Basis: 10-K_2024-12-31_byd_bgc20241008-10k.htm.jsonl, page 41; 10-K_2025-12-31_byd_bgc20251121-10k.htm.jsonl, page 42; 10-Q_2026-09-30_byd_bgc20260930-10q.htm.jsonl, page 34

The latest debt note lists the credit facility and both senior note issues with principal, rate, issuance costs and carrying value, and the liquidity discussion repeats the same totals, so the debt stack can be read without outside data. Principal totals USD 2,700.9 million at September 30, 2026, of which USD 800.9 million was reclassified to current maturities because the springing maturity applies while the notes due 2027 are neither retired nor refinanced.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_byd_bgc20260930-10q.htm.jsonl, pages 19–21, 59; calculation C3

The filings print contractual availability of USD 1,834.9 million, and the printed inputs reproduce that total exactly, so liquidity can be rebuilt from the filing rather than taken on trust.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_byd_bgc20260930-10q.htm.jsonl, page 21; calculation C7

No executed credit agreement or indenture is supplied, although both are listed as exhibits to the 2025 annual report. No credit rating appears in any filing, and no earnings release, lender presentation or management projection was supplied. Legal and covenant questions therefore rest on the issuer's own summaries, and rating context must come from outside the supplied documents.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_byd_bgc20251121-10k.htm.jsonl, pages 154–156

The latest filing states a maximum leverage ratio of 4.50x and that the company was in compliance at September 30, 2026, but it gives neither the reported ratio nor the minimum interest coverage level under the January 2026 agreement; the 2025 annual report stated a minimum of 2.50x under the earlier agreement. Covenant headroom is therefore not calculable from the filings.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_byd_bgc20260930-10q.htm.jsonl, pages 23–24; 10-K_2025-12-31_byd_bgc20251121-10k.htm.jsonl, page 115

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest case that the filings already suffice for covenant and priority work is that they print the financing structure in detail: the maximum leverage test of 4.50x, the leverage-based pricing grid, the guarantee and collateral package, the springing-maturity mechanics and a compliance statement at each reporting date. That case wins only where a described covenant is enough, because the filings state neither the reported leverage ratio nor the lender-defined earnings measure, so headroom cannot be computed without the executed agreement.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_byd_bgc20260930-10q.htm.jsonl, pages 21–24

Two developments would change this view. Receipt of the amended credit agreement, or an issuer-reported covenant ratio, would make covenant capacity calculable, and retirement of the notes due 2027, or their refinancing with debt that matures late enough, would remove the springing maturity and ease the near-term liquidity question.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_byd_bgc20260930-10q.htm.jsonl, page 21; 10-K_2025-12-31_byd_bgc20251121-10k.htm.jsonl, page 156

Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_byd_bgc20260930-10q.htm.jsonl, pages 39, 69; calculation C1

The 2026 quarterly statements are unaudited, so only the 2024 and 2025 annual figures carry an audit opinion.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_byd_bgc20260930-10q.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-K_2025-12-31_byd_bgc20251121-10k.htm.jsonl, page 81
