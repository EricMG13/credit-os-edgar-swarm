## Findings

### Bottom line

Governance risk to creditors is medium: no sponsor and an unqualified audit, but a family-influenced board returned more cash to shareholders in the first nine months of 2026 than operations generated, and the filings do not show the covenant room left.

The consequence is a credit whose protection rests on board restraint and on restricted-payment limits that cannot be tested here. Shareholder returns of USD 518.4 million, made up of USD 475.0 million of repurchases and USD 43.4 million of dividends, were 1.65x operating cash flow of USD 313.4 million, which a USD 364.2 million tax-credit payment had reduced, while the USD 1,758.0 million sale of the FanDuel stake in 2025 first repaid the credit facility. Business risk is moderate: 26 properties in 11 states, with earnings tilting toward Midwest and South properties as Las Vegas locals and online earnings fall.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_byd_bgc20260930-10q.htm.jsonl, pages 6, 24–28, 45, 48; 10-K_2025-12-31_byd_bgc20251121-10k.htm.jsonl, pages 3, 34, 79, 145; calculation A1; calculation A2; calculation A6; calculation A7

### Key findings

Control risk is moderate and rests with the board, not a sponsor. The Chairman and her immediate family beneficially owned about 30% of the shares at the end of 2025 and directors and executive officers about 31% in February 2026, enough to influence director elections and any merger or asset sale, yet the filings state no dealings with the family beyond compensation and director fees in 2023, 2024 and 2025. With no sponsor there are no sponsor fees, no fund-life pressure and no sponsor support to rely on, so creditors depend on the board's own capital allocation and on the debt covenants. Board independence, committee oversight and the table of principal stockholders sit in the proxy statement, which was not supplied.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_byd_bgc20251121-10k.htm.jsonl, pages 34, 39, 145, 150–152

Shareholder returns are the behaviour that most changes creditor risk. In the first nine months of 2026 the company paid USD 475.0 million for share repurchases and USD 43.4 million in dividends, USD 518.4 million in all, which is 1.65x operating cash flow of USD 313.4 million. That comparison overstates the strain, because operating cash flow bore a USD 364.2 million payment for tax credits bought in 2025 and used mainly against tax on the FanDuel sale; before it, operating cash flow would have been USD 677.6 million and returns 0.77x of it. The shares outstanding fell 6.7% from the end of 2025 to the end of September, and the weighted average share count fell 19.4% from 2023 to 2025, so repurchases are a standing policy rather than a one-off. The board raised the quarterly dividend to USD 0.20 per share from USD 0.18 in February 2026 and added USD 500.0 million to the repurchase authorization in April 2026, leaving USD 387.1 million. Both are discretionary and limited only by restricted-payment terms in the credit facility and the notes, whose capacity the filings do not quantify.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_byd_bgc20260930-10q.htm.jsonl, pages 3, 6, 27–28, 58, 63; 10-K_2025-12-31_byd_bgc20251121-10k.htm.jsonl, page 82; calculation A6; calculation A7; calculation A8; calculation A9

The use of the 2025 stake sale was creditor-friendly, but the effect has partly reversed. The company sold its five percent stake in FanDuel on 2025-07-31 for USD 1,758.0 million and used it to repay USD 1,680.9 million outstanding under the prior credit facility in the third quarter of 2025. The facility has since been redrawn: principal outstanding rose from USD 160.7 million at the end of 2025 to USD 800.9 million at 2026-09-30, a rise of USD 640.2 million, in a period when repurchases, dividends and capital spending exceeded operating cash flow. The deleveraging was real but did not become a lower-leverage policy.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_byd_bgc20251121-10k.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-Q_2026-09-30_byd_bgc20260930-10q.htm.jsonl, pages 20, 45; calculation A10

Business risk is diversified by geography but weakening where margins are highest. The company operates 26 properties in 11 states, down from 28 at the end of 2024, and gaming provides 65.8% of revenues. Midwest and South properties produce 53.7% of revenues and 56.7% of segment earnings before corporate expense, up from 53.9% a year earlier, while Las Vegas Locals earnings fell 5.7% to USD 293.8 million and Downtown Las Vegas earnings fell 13.8%. The mix is shifting from Las Vegas Locals, the highest-margin property segment at 45.2% of revenue, toward Midwest and South at 37.2%, and total Adjusted EBITDAR of USD 987.5 million, after corporate expense, is 2.9% lower than a year earlier.
Basis: 10-K_2024-12-31_byd_bgc20241008-10k.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-K_2025-12-31_byd_bgc20251121-10k.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-Q_2026-09-30_byd_bgc20260930-10q.htm.jsonl, pages 7, 45, 48; calculation A3; calculation A4; calculation A5; calculation A15

Online earnings are far smaller than online revenue suggests, and they have fallen sharply. The Online segment is 15.7% of revenues but 2.8% of segment earnings, because USD 383.0 million of its USD 474.3 million of revenue is reimbursement of gaming taxes and other costs paid for online partners at no margin. Market access fee revenue fell USD 28.9 million after the FanDuel agreements were terminated and in some cases replaced at lower rates, and management says it expects the lower margins to continue. Online Adjusted EBITDAR fell 45.7% to USD 29.9 million. The stake sale therefore monetised a high-margin income stream that is not being replaced at the same level, although technology revenue rose USD 14.2 million, helped by the Design Works acquisition.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_byd_bgc20260930-10q.htm.jsonl, pages 8, 43, 45, 47, 48; calculation A3; calculation A4; calculation A11

Management fee income is a growing but contract-bound earnings stream. The Sky River management fee was USD 82.3 million in nine months, 13.3% higher, and the Managed and Other category contributed 8.4% of segment earnings. The management agreement was signed in 2012 with Wilton Rancheria and runs for seven years from the casino's opening on 2022-08-15, so it ends in 2029 unless extended; the filings state no extension terms. Creditors should treat the income as finite and as dependent on a counterparty that is not part of the borrower group.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_byd_bgc20260930-10q.htm.jsonl, pages 24, 43, 47; calculation A3; calculation A12

Norfolk is the largest single funding and execution commitment. The company expects to spend USD 750.0 million on the project, with USD 275.0 million in 2026, and its management agreement makes it responsible for funding the operating losses of the consolidated entity that holds the license. The transitional casino opened on 2025-11-07 and the full resort is expected to open in November 2027 pending final regulatory approval. The company exercised its option on membership interests of that entity on 2026-06-01 but the filings do not give the percentage. Capital spending of USD 456.0 million in nine months is already 70.2% of the USD 650.0 million planned for 2026.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_byd_bgc20260930-10q.htm.jsonl, pages 25–26, 64; calculation A13

Portfolio actions are small against the balance sheet and show no distress. The company sold Sam's Town Shreveport on 2026-08-01 and the land of the closed Eastside Cannery in the third quarter, received USD 56.8 million from asset sales net of cash transferred in nine months, opened Cadence Crossing on 2026-03-25 to replace Jokers Wild and closed Sam's Town Tunica on 2025-11-09. The acquisition of Design Works for USD 53.3 million closed on 2026-04-01. The pattern is pruning and reinvestment funded from cash and the facility; the filings describe no covenant waiver or lender-driven disposal.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_byd_bgc20251121-10k.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-Q_2026-09-30_byd_bgc20260930-10q.htm.jsonl, pages 6, 8, 34, 44

Four properties are leased from a real estate investment trust under master leases, a claim that ranks ahead of the debt in operating cash flow. The leases were extended to 2031-04-30 and master lease rent was USD 86.4 million in nine months. The filings do not state lease coverage, escalators or cross-default terms, so the leases are a fixed charge whose creditor impact cannot be sized from the supplied documents.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_byd_bgc20260930-10q.htm.jsonl, pages 3–4, 25

Disclosure quality is adequate for monitoring operations but not for testing covenants. The 2025 financial statements carry an unqualified audit opinion with effective internal control and a critical audit matter on goodwill and gaming license impairment, the third-quarter report followed 28 days after the quarter end, and the company states that it complied with its financial covenants. Neither the covenant ratios nor a compliance certificate are printed, and the director, committee and compensation disclosures are incorporated from the unsupplied proxy statement.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_byd_bgc20251121-10k.htm.jsonl, pages 79–81, 149–152; 10-Q_2026-09-30_byd_bgc20260930-10q.htm.jsonl, pages 24, 69; calculation A14

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest case for lower risk is that Boyd Gaming is a widely held public company with an unqualified audit opinion, effective internal control, no dealings with the founding family beyond compensation and director fees, a dividend of only USD 43.4 million in nine months and a stake-sale windfall that first repaid USD 1,680.9 million of facility debt. Repurchases can be stopped at any time, and most of the 2026 cash gap is a one-off tax payment, since returns were 0.91x operating cash flow in the first nine months of 2025. That case wins if repurchases slow once the notes due 2027 are addressed and if the proxy statement confirms an independent board and no related-person transactions.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_byd_bgc20251121-10k.htm.jsonl, pages 79, 145–149; 10-Q_2026-09-30_byd_bgc20260930-10q.htm.jsonl, pages 6, 45, 58; calculation A6; calculation A7; calculation A8

The case for higher risk wins if repurchases continue at the 2026 pace while the facility stays drawn, if Norfolk costs exceed USD 750.0 million, or if the credit agreement and indentures show restricted-payment capacity far larger than the USD 387.1 million left under the repurchase authorization. Evidence that would settle the question is the executed credit agreement, the indentures and the proxy statement, together with the next quarterly report's repurchase figures.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_byd_bgc20260930-10q.htm.jsonl, pages 26–27, 63

### Limitations

- The proxy statement was not supplied, so board independence, committee oversight, the principal-stockholder table and related-person transactions are not established; independent oversight with no dealings would lower the medium control rating, and material related-party arrangements would raise it.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_byd_bgc20251121-10k.htm.jsonl, pages 150–152

- The credit agreement and the indentures were not supplied, so the capacity to pay dividends and repurchase shares is not established; the conclusion on shareholder returns is therefore a judgement about behaviour, not a test of permission, and a capacity much larger than the repurchase authorization would raise the risk.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_byd_bgc20260930-10q.htm.jsonl, pages 23, 63; 10-K_2025-12-31_byd_bgc20251121-10k.htm.jsonl, page 116

- Ownership is stated at 2025-12-31 for the family and at 2026-02-16 for directors and officers, and the share count has fallen since, so the current holding is not established and may be higher than the stated percentages.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_byd_bgc20251121-10k.htm.jsonl, pages 34, 39; 10-Q_2026-09-30_byd_bgc20260930-10q.htm.jsonl, page 2

- The Sky River, Norfolk, master lease and market access agreements are summarised in the notes but were not supplied, so the durability of management fee income and the cost of Norfolk to the borrower rest on summary statements; an extension or a cost overrun would change the judgement on those two items.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_byd_bgc20260930-10q.htm.jsonl, pages 24–26, 47

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

The debt note lists the same two senior note issues at 2026-09-30 as at 2025-12-31, so no new bonds were issued in 2026. The only new debt contract is the amended and restated credit agreement signed on 2026-01-21, which replaced the earlier facility with a USD 1,450.0 million revolving credit facility and a USD 1,200.0 million delayed-draw term A loan. USD 400.0 million of the term loan is drawn, USD 800.0 million is undrawn and can be drawn until 2027-07-01, and its maturity springs to 2027-09-01 unless the USD 1,000.0 million of notes due 2027 are retired or refinanced, which is why all USD 800.9 million drawn is classified as current. The facility is secured on substantially all assets other than gaming licenses, carries a maximum net leverage covenant of 4.50x and restricts investments, dividends and other payments.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_byd_bgc20260930-10q.htm.jsonl, pages 19–23; calculation A10

The completed transactions are the 2025 sale of the five percent FanDuel stake for USD 1,758.0 million, the 2026-04-01 acquisition of Design Works for USD 53.3 million, the 2026-08-01 sale of Sam's Town Shreveport and the third-quarter sale of the Eastside Cannery land. The stake sale ended a shareholding that had run since 2018 and replaced the strategic partnership with market access agreements at lower fees. None of the completed transactions was financed with new bonds, and the filings state no sale price for Sam's Town Shreveport.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_byd_bgc20251121-10k.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-Q_2026-09-30_byd_bgc20260930-10q.htm.jsonl, pages 8, 34, 47

Two commitments remain open: the Norfolk resort, expected to open in November 2027 at a total cost of about USD 750.0 million, and the master leases for four properties, extended to 2031-04-30. The company intends to fund its capital requirements, which include Norfolk, from cash, the credit facility and operating cash flow, and shareholder returns of USD 518.4 million compete with that spending for the same cash. The third-quarter report states that no subsequent event required disclosure.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_byd_bgc20260930-10q.htm.jsonl, pages 25–26, 39, 64; calculation A6
