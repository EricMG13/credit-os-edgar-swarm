## Findings

### Bottom line

Leverage is easing but remains high: net debt is 4.21x issuer-adjusted EBITDA for the twelve months to September 30, 2026, against 4.67x at year-end 2025, so creditors still depend on earnings recovery and refinancing.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_cc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 3–4; EX-99.1_2026-11-02_cc-ex99_1.htm.jsonl, page 13; 10-K_2025-12-31_cc-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 161–162; calculation C39; calculation C38

Net debt fell by USD 336 million while adjusted EBITDA stayed at USD 743 million and USD 744 million; the fall is close to the USD 342 million of asset-sale proceeds, so it mostly reflects a one-time disposal. Reported EBITDA, which carries litigation charges and a USD 316 million asset-sale gain, puts net leverage at 4.31x. Liquidity was USD 698 million of cash and USD 953 million of revolver availability at quarter end, but USD 220 million of those commitments matured on October 7, 2026, and cash interest cover is not printed for 2026.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_cc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 5–6; 10-Q_2026-09-30_cc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 149; calculation C36; calculation C40; calculation C44
