### Analytical findings

Acquisition funding changed the balance sheet faster than the earnings base could absorb. Carrying-value debt increased from USD 1,354.295 million at FY2025 year-end to USD 3,400.485 million at Q3 FY2026, while unrestricted cash declined from USD 1,043.252 million to USD 559.759 million. LTM net debt is USD 2,840.726 million, or 2.47x reported EBITDA; the acquisition period includes USD 2,516.122 million of net acquisition cash outflow and USD 1,985.000 million of debt proceeds. This shifts creditor risk toward acquired-operations cash generation and integration.
Basis: calculation C2; calculation C3; 10-Q_2026-05-31_cmc.htm.jsonl, pages 3–4

Cash generation is positive but leaves less room after investment. LTM operating cash flow of USD 918.228 million less USD 513.190 million of capex yields USD 405.038 million of levered free cash flow. Nine-month working capital used USD 195.098 million, and maintenance versus growth capex is not disclosed. Continued working-capital absorption alongside integration spending would slow deleveraging.
Basis: calculation C3; 10-Q_2026-05-31_cmc.htm.jsonl, page 4

Liquidity remains meaningful relative to disclosed near-term commitments. At Q3 FY2026, cash plus undrawn committed facilities was USD 1,768.475 million after letters of credit; the filing lists USD 780 million of purchase obligations due within twelve months and USD 170.9 million of debt interest payable over that period. The revolver matures in 2030, while latest expiries for the Polish credit and receivables facilities are not stated.
Basis: calculation C1; 10-Q_2026-05-31_cmc.htm.jsonl, pages 22, 48–49

Restricted cash materially changes the cash-flow presentation but not available liquidity. Q1 FY2026 closing cash, restricted cash and equivalents were USD 3,032.097 million, including USD 2,009.059 million restricted; Q3 restricted cash was USD 3.458 million. The liquidity and net-debt calculations therefore use unrestricted cash.
Basis: 10-Q_2025-11-30_cmc.htm.jsonl, pages 3–4; 10-Q_2026-05-31_cmc.htm.jsonl, pages 3–4, 48

The issuer reports current covenant compliance. The annual report provides a 2.50x minimum interest-coverage test and 0.60x maximum debt-to-capitalization test; reported FY2024 ratios were 20.37x and 0.22x. The Q3 filing does not state current ratios or headroom, and the covenant definitions differ from the EBITDA and cash-interest measures calculated here.
