## Findings

### Bottom line

CMC remains a cyclical, vertically integrated steel and construction-products group, but its credit profile now rests on integrating two debt-funded precast acquisitions that changed the company more than any operating shift did. Precast delivered adjusted EBITDA of USD 86.5 million in nine months, and the issuer's 2.1x net leverage reproduces at 2.12x from its own inputs, which corrects the Financial foundation statement that it could not be reproduced.
Basis: calculation K2; calculation K3; 10-Q_2026-05-31_cmc.htm.jsonl, page 45; EX99.1_2026-05-31_cmc_cmc-5312026xearningsreleas-htm.htm.jsonl, page 4

The shareholder base is listed and widely held with no controlling holder identified, and nine of ten directors are independent; the proxy statement that would confirm control rights is not supplied. Dividends rose 11% in March 2026 and buybacks of USD 76.1 million continued alongside USD 2,000 million of new notes, so creditor alignment rests on the stated aim of leverage below 2x by mid-2027.
Basis: 10-K_2025-08-31_cmc.htm.jsonl, page 58; EX99.1_2026-02-28_cmc_d117730dex991-htm.htm.jsonl, page 1; 10-Q_2026-05-31_cmc.htm.jsonl, page 48; EX99.1_2026-05-31_cmc_cmc-5312026xearningsreleas-htm.htm.jsonl, page 1

### Key findings

- The precast acquisitions are the dominant credit event, and they were mostly debt-funded: Foley, the larger purchase, was paid from the 2033 and 2035 senior unsecured notes, whose net proceeds equal 78.9% of the USD 2,516,122 thousand of cash paid net of cash acquired for both purchases, and Concrete Pipe and Precast was paid from cash on hand. Goodwill and intangibles make up 81.9% of the allocated price, so little tangible asset value stands behind the new debt, and the businesses added net sales of USD 320.3 million and pre-tax earnings of USD 24,625 thousand since closing.
Basis: calculation K2; calculation K4; calculation K5; 10-Q_2026-05-31_cmc.htm.jsonl, pages 10–15

- The legacy base is cyclical but integrated and, for now, supported by trade policy. North America Steel Group supplies 75.1% of net sales and 77.7% of segment adjusted EBITDA in nine months, its metal margin per ton rose 27%, and a 50% tariff on most steel imports props up domestic prices; relaxation of that tariff is the main external risk to the margin.
Basis: calculation K1; calculation K6; 10-Q_2026-05-31_cmc.htm.jsonl, page 37; 10-Q_2026-05-31_cmc.htm.jsonl, page 44

- The issuer's leverage measure is reproducible, which corrects the earlier Financial foundation statement. Net debt of USD 2,865,703 thousand on the issuer's definition over adjusted EBITDA adjusted for expected annualized precast EBITDA of USD 245 million gives 2.12x, so the 2.1x depends on that management expectation rather than on reported precast results.
Basis: calculation K3; EX99.1_2026-05-31_cmc_cmc-5312026xearningsreleas-htm.htm.jsonl, page 4

- Ownership and governance look conventional for a listed company but control rights are not established. The board has ten directors with nine independent and a separate chairman, and the auditor has served for decades, yet the proxy statement, the credit agreement and the indentures are not supplied, so holders above five percent, restricted payment capacity and guarantor scope are not known; the sponsor and governance risk level is medium on that evidence.
Basis: 10-K_2025-08-31_cmc.htm.jsonl, page 58; 10-K_2025-08-31_cmc.htm.jsonl, page 139; EX99.1_2026-02-28_cmc_d117730dex991-htm.htm.jsonl, page 1

- Shareholder returns continued while debt rose. Buybacks and dividends took USD 138,234 thousand in nine months, 22.9% of operating cash flow, and the dividend was raised 11% in March 2026; this is moderate in size but is discretionary cash that could repay debt after a debt-funded acquisition.
Basis: calculation K8; 10-Q_2026-05-31_cmc.htm.jsonl, page 4

- Litigation is the main event risk outside debt. A jury verdict of USD 110.0 million for Pacific Steel Group, trebled by the court, is accrued at USD 373,476 thousand in current liabilities with payment timing uncertain, and a second suit seeking about USD 29 million has no accrual; the company is appealing.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-05-31_cmc.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-Q_2026-05-31_cmc.htm.jsonl, pages 29–30

- Disclosure is frequent and reconciled, with one correction. The latest quarterly report corrected pro forma net sales that the second-quarter report overstated by USD 75.1 million, which the company judged not material; reported statements were not affected.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-05-31_cmc.htm.jsonl, page 14

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a more levered, integration-dependent view is operating momentum and the issuer's own path: core EBITDA margin of 14.2% in the latest quarter, precast adjusted EBITDA of USD 86.5 million in nine months against a management expectation of USD 245 million annualized, and a stated aim of leverage below 2x ahead of mid-2027. That view would win if fiscal 2026 results show a full year of precast earnings near the expectation, free cash flow applied to debt rather than buybacks, and the tariff intact. It would lose if the Pacific Steel Group accrual of USD 373,476 thousand is paid in cash, if the tariff is relaxed and margins narrow, or if integration costs and capital spending for the West Virginia mill stay high.
Basis: calculation K3; 10-Q_2026-05-31_cmc.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-Q_2026-05-31_cmc.htm.jsonl, page 37; 10-Q_2026-05-31_cmc.htm.jsonl, page 45; EX99.1_2026-05-31_cmc_cmc-5312026xearningsreleas-htm.htm.jsonl, page 1; EX99.1_2026-05-31_cmc_cmc-5312026xearningsreleas-htm.htm.jsonl, page 4

### Limitations

- The proxy statement is not supplied, so the conclusion that control is dispersed rests on holders of record and board independence only; a concentrated holder above five percent, or related-party dealings, would change the governance view.
- The credit agreement, indentures and guarantor lists are not supplied, so the conclusion on creditor alignment cannot be tied to restricted payment or debt capacity; a basket that permits larger distributions would worsen it.
- No document after 2026-06-29 is supplied, so whether the fourth quarter and the stated deleveraging aim were delivered is not established; the leverage path conclusion is provisional until fiscal 2026 results are read.
- Precast adjusted EBITDA is stated only as a contribution to the change in segment results and as management's expectation, so the reproduced 2.12x leverage is issuer-defined and would move with a different annualized figure.

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

CMC completed two cash acquisitions in December 2025 that created a precast platform: Concrete Pipe and Precast, LLC on 2025-12-01 for about USD 675 million from cash on hand, and the holding companies that own Foley Products Company, LLC on 2025-12-15 for about USD 1.84 billion. Foley was funded from the proceeds of the 2033 and 2035 senior unsecured notes, USD 1,000 million each, issued in November 2025, which raised net proceeds of USD 1,985,000 thousand. The purchase price allocations are preliminary and goodwill and intangibles are 81.9% of the allocated price.
Basis: calculation K4; calculation K5; 10-Q_2026-05-31_cmc.htm.jsonl, pages 10–15; 10-Q_2026-05-31_cmc.htm.jsonl, page 22

The notes add an annual coupon of USD 117,500 thousand, which is 68.8% of the USD 170.9 million of interest payable on long-term debt in the next twelve months, and the first coupons were paid on 2026-05-15 and 2026-06-15. In December 2025 the revolver was raised from USD 600.0 million to USD 1.0 billion and extended to 2030-12-17; a bridge facility of up to USD 1.85 billion and a backstop revolver of USD 600.0 million were committed in October 2025, and the sources do not state that either was drawn.
Basis: calculation K7; 10-Q_2026-05-31_cmc.htm.jsonl, page 22; 10-Q_2026-05-31_cmc.htm.jsonl, page 36; 10-Q_2026-05-31_cmc.htm.jsonl, page 50; 10-K_2025-08-31_cmc.htm.jsonl, page 116
