## Findings

### Bottom line

The eight supplied filings and releases support the financial foundation, business profile and credit view through the third quarter of 2026, but creditors cannot test covenant headroom, guarantors or ratings from them.

The latest statements cover the nine months to 30 September 2026 and were signed 21 days before the cutoff. ### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest case against a limited-readiness view is that the statements, debt notes and liquidity table cover every reported period through the third quarter of 2026. It weakens because USD 1,226.7 million of the USD 1,256.5 million of available credit sits on revolving lines whose availability depends on borrowing-base calculations that change monthly, so the covenant and borrowing-base terms decide how much of the reported funds can be used. Receipt of the credit agreement and the note indentures would make covenant headroom testable and would lift that limit; a further revision of reported figures beyond the 2024 correction would lower trust in the statements.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_lad-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 45; 10-Q_2026-09-30_lad-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 46; calculation CALC-7
