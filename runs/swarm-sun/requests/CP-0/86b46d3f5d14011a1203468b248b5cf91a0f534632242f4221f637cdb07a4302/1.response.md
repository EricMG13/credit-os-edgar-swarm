## Findings

### Bottom line

The seven Sunoco LP sources support a credit snapshot as of 2026-11-05, but only on the issuer's own debt summaries, because no credit agreement, indenture, rating or market price was supplied. Covenant headroom, legal priority and relative value therefore cannot be verified here, and any conclusion on them rests on the filings' descriptions.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_sun-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 13–15; 10-K_2025-12-31_sun-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 250–252

The latest balance sheet is also not the current position. The all-cash purchase of Offen Petroleum for approximately USD 600 million closed on 2026-10-27, after the September 30, 2026 balance sheet date, so quarter-end debt, leverage and liquidity exclude it, and how it was funded is not established. Quarterly and nine-month 2025 comparatives contain none of Parkland, which closed on 2025-10-31, so growth against them mixes acquisition effects with underlying change.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_sun-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 3, 10; EX-99.1_2026-11-03_ex991sunocoandsunococorper.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-K_2025-12-31_sun-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 7
