## Findings

### Bottom line

Leverage is 4.25x trailing reported EBITDA on USD 5,381.6 million of debt at 2026-09-30, overstated because Leggett & Platt's debt is set against only 36 days of its earnings, so creditors should rely on cash generation and liquidity until full-period earnings are printed.

The issuer's covenant leverage of 2.69x is lower by construction: it divides by USD 1,864.8 million of covenant EBITDA, which is USD 475.5 million above adjusted EBITDA and includes USD 145.0 million of expected cost savings. Free cash flow of USD 657.6 million in nine months 2026, cash interest cover of 5.01x and liquidity of USD 1,794.8 million support the credit, but net sales excluding Leggett & Platt fell 6.80% in the third quarter.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_sgi.htm.jsonl, pages 6–7, 19, 35, 47, 52–53; calculation C3; calculation C4; calculation C6; calculation C18; calculation C20

### Key findings

Leverage on reported earnings is high but overstated at the latest date, while the issuer's own ratio is understated by construction, so the credible range is wide. Total debt at carrying value rose from USD 3,809.9 million at 2024-12-31 to USD 4,685.7 million at 2025-12-31 and USD 5,381.6 million at 2026-09-30, as the Mattress Firm and Leggett & Platt acquisitions were financed with borrowing and new shares. Total debt to reported EBITDA was 4.53x, 4.51x and 4.25x at those dates, and net debt to EBITDA was 4.39x, 4.38x and 3.77x. The latest figures use trailing EBITDA of USD 1,264.9 million, up from USD 1,039.3 million, but that figure holds 36 days of Leggett & Platt against all of its debt, so a full-period ratio would be lower and no filing prints the earnings needed to say by how much. The USD 200.0 million Term B prepayment of 2026-10-01 lowers debt and cash equally, so pro forma net debt stays at USD 4,769.5 million and pro forma net leverage at 3.77x.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_sgi.htm.jsonl, pages 6–7, 19, 21, 52; 10-K_2025-12-31_sgi.htm.jsonl, pages 63, 73; calculation C1; calculation C3; calculation C16

The year-end 2024 ratios are not like for like either: debt includes the USD 1,600.0 million Term B Loan held in escrow ahead of the Mattress Firm closing, while EBITDA excludes Mattress Firm. The issuer's 2.31x covenant ratio for that date nets USD 1,592.3 million of the escrow cash and gives net debt of USD 2,134.8 million against USD 3,692.5 million on unrestricted cash, so the two year-end series should not be compared with the 2026 figures without that adjustment.
Basis: 10-K_2024-12-31_sgi.htm.jsonl, page 62; 10-K_2025-12-31_sgi.htm.jsonl, pages 63, 73; calculation C3; calculation C18

The mix of claims has shifted toward unsecured debt. Senior unsecured principal is USD 3,100.0 million at 2026-09-30, 55.09% of principal of USD 5,627.0 million against 33.92% at 2025-12-31, because the three Leggett & Platt notes of USD 500.0 million each came with the acquisition and carry no guarantee from Somnigroup's subsidiaries, while secured principal fell from USD 2,908.4 million to USD 2,329.3 million after the Amendment No. 5 refinancing and a USD 700.0 million Term B prepayment. Secured principal is 1.84x trailing reported EBITDA, down from 2.80x at 2025-12-31, which strengthens the secured lenders and leaves unsecured noteholders relying on cash flow rather than collateral; the Leggett & Platt notes are claims on that company alone. The earlier source review quoted total debt of USD 5,627.0 million, which is principal; the ratios in this module use carrying value of USD 5,381.6 million.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_sgi.htm.jsonl, pages 19–21; 10-K_2025-12-31_sgi.htm.jsonl, page 96; calculation C8; calculation C17; Source readiness (CP-0)

Interest cover is adequate but will fall from its latest level. Reported EBITDA covered cash interest 5.34x in FY2024, 3.73x in FY2025 and 5.01x over the trailing twelve months; the FY2025 low reflects cash interest of USD 278.3 million, up from USD 157.7 million, on the acquisition debt, and USD 266.7 million of acquisition-related charges that hold reported EBITDA below adjusted EBITDA, which covered cash interest 4.69x. Trailing cash interest of USD 252.7 million holds 36 days of interest on the Leggett & Platt notes, and the issuer's guide of about USD 240 million of cash interest for 2026, raised from about USD 225 million in the annual report, implies USD 68.5 million in the fourth quarter against an average of USD 57.17 million in the first three, the first quarter to carry those notes for a full period, so run-rate cover will be below 5.01x even allowing for the uneven timing of semi-annual note interest.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_sgi.htm.jsonl, pages 56, 61–62, 75; 10-Q_2026-09-30_sgi.htm.jsonl, pages 9, 47, 52; calculation C4; calculation C19

Cash generation is the strongest part of the credit. Operating cash flow was USD 831.9 million in nine months 2026 against USD 700.7 million a year earlier and, after USD 174.3 million of capital expenditure, free cash flow was USD 657.6 million, or USD 698.7 million over the trailing twelve months, which is 0.55x trailing EBITDA. Its quality is mixed: a USD 135.5 million release from working capital supplied 16.29% of operating cash flow, deferred taxes added USD 49.4 million without cash, and capital expenditure is guided to about USD 255 million for 2026 including USD 75 million of one-time Mattress Firm store refresh. After dividends of USD 108.1 million, free cash flow of USD 549.5 million funded most of the USD 583.9 million of net debt repayment, while the cash balance rose to USD 612.1 million mainly because Leggett & Platt brought USD 560.2 million of cash.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_sgi.htm.jsonl, pages 74–75; 10-Q_2026-09-30_sgi.htm.jsonl, pages 9, 44; calculation C4; calculation C13; calculation C19

Liquidity is ample for the debt falling due soon. It was USD 1,794.8 million at 2026-09-30, made of USD 612.1 million of cash and USD 1,182.7 million of revolver availability, compared with USD 773.6 million at 2025-12-31. The revolver is the only undrawn facility because the securitization is fully drawn at USD 100.5 million, and USD 200.0 million of the cash went to the Term B prepayment the next day. By final maturity, debt falls due USD 600.5 million in 2027, USD 1,300.0 million in 2029 and USD 3,028.8 million in 2031; the 2027 amount is the securitization due 2027-10-07 and the USD 500.0 million Leggett & Platt note due 2027-11-15, and liquidity covers it 2.99 times. The ladder uses final maturities only, so term loan amortisation, which the filings do not quantify, would bring part of the 2031 amount forward.
Basis: 10-K_2024-12-31_sgi.htm.jsonl, page 56; 10-K_2025-12-31_sgi.htm.jsonl, pages 55, 100; 10-Q_2026-09-30_sgi.htm.jsonl, pages 19–21, 23, 47; calculation C6; calculation C7

Obligations outside total debt are material. Operating lease obligations are USD 2,143.2 million, pension payments of USD 33.0 million and letters of credit of USD 34.8 million were scheduled at 2025-12-31 with no purchase commitments listed, and about USD 110.0 million of accounts payable, 12.67% of the USD 868.4 million total, is settled through third-party supplier payment programmes that the company uses following the Leggett & Platt acquisition. The filing states that the company pays the provider on the original invoice due date and cannot lengthen payment terms through the programmes, so the amount adds no obligation beyond ordinary payables, but it is a dependence on third-party finance whose size, with no earlier figure to compare, should be tracked. No lease-adjusted leverage is stated because operating lease cost is already deducted in EBITDA, and adding the lease obligations to debt without adding the rent back would count the lease twice.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_sgi.htm.jsonl, pages 7, 12; 10-K_2025-12-31_sgi.htm.jsonl, pages 56, 103; calculation C1; calculation C19

Underlying trading is soft, which weakens the earnings that must carry the debt. Net sales were USD 2,350.8 million in the third quarter of 2026, 10.75% above the third quarter of 2025, but USD 1,978.2 million without the USD 372.6 million from Leggett & Platt, down 6.80%, with Mattress Firm and North America each down 5.8% and International down 12.3% after an ERP implementation disrupted Dreams. Gross margin fell to 42.02% from 44.89% because Leggett & Platt's 17.7% margin dilutes the mix, and North America's margin gain of 6.3 percentage points includes about USD 20.0 million of tariff refunds that may not repeat. Add-backs were 16.16% of adjusted EBITDA in the quarter against 9.52% a year earlier, so adjusted EBITDA of USD 447.5 million flatters reported EBITDA of USD 375.2 million.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_sgi.htm.jsonl, pages 6, 35–36, 51–52; calculation C19; calculation C20

The covenant measure does not show that earnings support the debt. The issuer's covenant leverage was 2.31x at FY2024, 3.21x at FY2025 and 2.69x at 2026-09-30 against a 5.00x maximum, but the 2026 figure rests on EBITDA of USD 1,864.8 million: adjusted EBITDA of USD 1,389.3 million plus USD 330.5 million of Leggett & Platt earnings before acquisition and USD 145.0 million of expected cost synergies. Those additions are 34.23% of adjusted EBITDA. The filings print the level of only one of the three maintenance tests, so headroom on the interest coverage and secured net leverage tests is not assessed.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_sgi.htm.jsonl, pages 62–63, 99; 10-Q_2026-09-30_sgi.htm.jsonl, pages 45, 52–53; calculation C18; Source readiness (CP-0)

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a leverage concern is the issuer's covenant leverage of 2.69x against a 5.00x maximum, trailing free cash flow of USD 698.7 million, liquidity of USD 1,794.8 million and no note or facility final maturity before 2027. If Leggett & Platt earns close to the USD 330.5 million the issuer adds back for the part of the year it did not own, full-period leverage would move toward the issuer's target range of 2.0x to 3.0x and the comfort would rest on earnings as well as cash. The view would turn toward comfort on a printed full-period EBITDA for Leggett & Platt, two quarters of positive net sales growth excluding acquisitions, or debt repayment beyond the USD 200.0 million Term B prepayment. It would turn toward concern if the third-quarter softness at Mattress Firm and International persists as the 2027 maturities approach, or if add-backs keep rising as a share of adjusted EBITDA.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_sgi.htm.jsonl, pages 35, 45, 47, 52–53; calculation C4; calculation C18

### Limitations

- Trailing EBITDA, cash interest and cash flow hold only 36 days of Leggett & Platt, and no filing prints its full-period EBITDA, so no full-period leverage or cover ratio is stated.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_sgi.htm.jsonl, pages 16–18, 52–53

- Nine-month EBITDA and adjusted EBITDA are sums of three quarterly reports because no nine-month reconciliation is printed; they reproduce the issuer's trailing twelve-month figures exactly.
Basis: calculation C4; calculation C5

- Carrying values are printed only in aggregate, so debt by instrument is shown at principal with one financing cost adjustment, and the part of the USD 224.1 million Leggett & Platt note discount still unamortised is not established.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_sgi.htm.jsonl, pages 19, 21

- The maturity ladder uses final maturities, and scheduled term loan amortisation and any maturity table after Amendment No. 5 are not printed.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_sgi.htm.jsonl, pages 19–20; 10-K_2025-12-31_sgi.htm.jsonl, page 102

- Only the 5.00x maximum for total net leverage is printed among the maintenance covenants, so headroom is not computed, and the guarantors are not named.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_sgi.htm.jsonl, page 45; 10-K_2025-12-31_sgi.htm.jsonl, pages 99, 101

- Income taxes paid for nine months, a quarterly cash flow statement and total letters of credit at 2026-09-30 are not printed; the three quarterly reports are unaudited and the filings are text conversions whose tables were read from row order and checked against totals.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_sgi.htm.jsonl, pages 9, 21; Source readiness (CP-0)
