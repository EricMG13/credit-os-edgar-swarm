## Findings

### Bottom line

Control rests with CVR Energy and Icahn Enterprises through the general partner, and the board's policy is to pay out all available cash, so creditor protection depends on debt covenants that are not in the evidence rather than on independent governance.
The business is two nitrogen fertilizer plants selling mostly UAN and ammonia at commodity prices under contracts of less than one year, so cash flow follows prices and plant uptime. Distributions of USD 125.99 million in FY2025 exceeded free cash flow of USD 98.84 million, and the owners are weighing strategic options that could involve the Partnership; a sale of the Icahn interest in CVR Energy could trigger a repurchase offer on the notes.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_uan.htm.jsonl, page 9; 10-K_2025-12-31_uan.htm.jsonl, pages 47–53; calculation A1; calculation A5

### Key findings

Control is concentrated and creditors have no governance voice. CVR Energy holds about 37% of the units and all of the general partner, and Icahn Enterprises owns about 71% of CVR Energy and so is treated by the issuer as holding about 40% of the units beneficially. Unitholders do not elect the general partner or its directors, the partnership agreement replaces default fiduciary duties with narrower contractual ones, and the board rests on exemptions from the exchange's independence rules, so the only checks on owner decisions that creditors can rely on are the debt documents.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_uan.htm.jsonl, page 9; 10-K_2025-12-31_uan.htm.jsonl, pages 52–53; 10-K_2025-12-31_uan.htm.jsonl, page 121

The board changed shape in October 2025, which tightens owner control. Four directors, three of them independent, were removed that month, the board is chaired by a current Icahn Enterprises employee, and at the end of 2025 the board of six held three independent directors, short of a majority, while a two-person special committee of the chair and the chief executive could approve urgent matters between board meetings at the date of the annual report. The documents give no reason for the removals.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_uan.htm.jsonl, pages 121–122; 10-K_2025-12-31_uan.htm.jsonl, pages 127–128

Related-party arrangements are a dependency as well as a cost. CVR Energy's refinery supplied about 36% of the pet coke used in 2025, the services agreement that provides management and administration can be ended on 90 days' notice, and the Coffeyville feedstock and hydrogen agreement ends automatically if the two parties fall out of common control. Charges to the Partnership were about USD 42.32 million in FY2025, 8.89% of cost of sales and administrative expense, and the CVR Energy subsidiary line rose from USD 8.9 million to USD 14.9 million in the first nine months of 2026, so a change of control would reach supply and services as well as the notes.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_uan.htm.jsonl, page 16; 10-K_2025-12-31_uan.htm.jsonl, page 53; 10-K_2025-12-31_uan.htm.jsonl, pages 115–116; 10-Q_2026-09-30_uan.htm.jsonl, page 16; calculation A4

Distribution policy leaks cash in weak years but has not been debt funded. The board distributes available cash after reserves it sets in its sole discretion, and about 39% goes to CVR Energy and Icahn Enterprises. Distributions exceeded free cash flow in FY2023 and FY2025 and were met from cash, while borrowed debt stayed at the notes alone; in the first nine months of 2026 free cash flow of USD 193.88 million covered distributions of USD 110.45 million. The declared third-quarter distribution of USD 18.9 million follows a quarter in which USD 64.26 million was paid.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_uan.htm.jsonl, page 80; 10-Q_2026-09-30_uan.htm.jsonl, page 16; 10-Q_2026-09-30_uan.htm.jsonl, page 17; calculation A1; calculation A2; calculation A7

Event risk comes from the owners' strategic review and the change-of-control terms, not from any disclosed transaction. CVR Energy and Icahn Enterprises say they are considering strategic transactions that may include options involving the Partnership, with no terms or timetable. A sale or transfer of some or all of the Icahn interest in CVR Energy could be a change of control under the notes, with a repurchase offer at 101%, and an event of default under the asset-based facility; the owners' general partner can also buy out public unitholders if it and its affiliates ever hold more than 80% of the units, which is far from the 40% held.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_uan.htm.jsonl, pages 18–19; 10-K_2025-12-31_uan.htm.jsonl, page 47; 10-K_2025-12-31_uan.htm.jsonl, pages 52–53; calculation A5

Management is in transition. The chief executive named in the first-quarter release was succeeded by the chief financial officer by the second-quarter release, the second and third quarterly reports are signed by an interim chief financial officer, and executives are employed through CVR Energy, which sets the pay of every executive officer except the chief executive. The partnership bears these costs through service fees and the filings do not show a cost of the transition.
Basis: EX99.1_2026-03-31_uan_exhibit991-uanq12026earnin-htm.htm.jsonl, page 2; EX99.1_2026-06-30_uan_exhibit991-uanq22026earnin-htm.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-06-30_uan.htm.jsonl, page 35; 10-Q_2026-09-30_uan.htm.jsonl, page 37; 10-K_2025-12-31_uan.htm.jsonl, page 52

The operating model is a commodity price-taker with feedstock and plant concentration. Product sales are mostly UAN and ammonia under wholesale contracts of less than one year, the top two customers took 28% of FY2025 net sales, sales are heavier in the first half of each year, and each plant is a single site, so a turnaround or outage hits EBITDA in the quarter. Customers also prepay, which lifted deferred revenue to USD 83.93 million at September 30, 2026 from USD 43.6 million at the end of 2025.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_uan.htm.jsonl, pages 14–16; 10-K_2025-12-31_uan.htm.jsonl, page 107; 10-Q_2026-09-30_uan.htm.jsonl, page 6; calculation A3; calculation A8

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against concern is conduct: the documents read report no debt-funded distribution, no amendment or exchange of the notes, no drop-down of assets to an unrestricted subsidiary and no payment to the owners beyond their pro rata share of distributions and the charges under the service agreements, borrowed debt has been the one note issue since 2021, and the board sets reserves. The chain of ownership is also stable, with the shares of the holders unchanged to the level the filings print. That view would win if the owners' review ends without a transaction and the executed debt documents show tight restricted-payment and affiliate-transaction limits.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_uan.htm.jsonl, page 104; 10-Q_2026-09-30_uan.htm.jsonl, page 9; 10-K_2025-12-31_uan.htm.jsonl, page 106; calculation A6; calculation A7

The view would turn negative on any disclosed transaction that moves control of CVR Energy or the general partner, on a distribution that exceeds free cash flow while liquidity is falling, or on a further change to the board that removes independent directors. Each would act through the 101% repurchase offer of about USD 555.5 million against liquidity of USD 203.4 million, the termination rights in the services and feedstock agreements, and the loss of the cash buffer.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_uan.htm.jsonl, page 47; 10-K_2025-12-31_uan.htm.jsonl, page 53; 10-Q_2026-09-30_uan.htm.jsonl, page 31; calculation A1; calculation A5

### Limitations

- The partnership agreement, the indenture and the asset-based facility agreement are not in the evidence, so restricted-payment, affiliate-transaction and change-of-control limits cannot be read, which stops any view on whether the owners' capacity to extract cash is constrained.
  Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_uan.htm.jsonl, pages 172–175; 10-K_2025-12-31_uan.htm.jsonl, page 106
- The documents give no reason for the October 2025 board changes and no terms of the strategic review, so the view that control has tightened and the event-risk finding rest on disclosed facts only and say nothing on owner intent.
  Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_uan.htm.jsonl, page 121; 10-Q_2026-09-30_uan.htm.jsonl, page 19
- Ownership percentages after 2025 are printed to whole numbers, so small changes in the Icahn Enterprises holding cannot be seen, which limits the finding that the holdings are stable.
  Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_uan.htm.jsonl, page 9; calculation A6
- Related-party charges are shown in total only, with no breakdown of the Corporate service fee by service and no market comparison, so the finding that the cost is modest cannot be tested against arm's-length terms.
  Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_uan.htm.jsonl, page 116; 10-Q_2026-09-30_uan.htm.jsonl, page 16
