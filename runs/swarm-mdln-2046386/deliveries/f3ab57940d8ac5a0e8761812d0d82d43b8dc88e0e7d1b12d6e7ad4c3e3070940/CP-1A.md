## Findings

### Bottom line

Medline's business supports creditors, but owner claims on group cash are the main risk: sponsors and the Mills family held about 67.0% of the votes in February 2026, and a USD 4,415 million tax receivable agreement is payable from group cash. IPO equity repaid the euro term loans and most of the 2028 dollar term loans in December 2025, although USD 1,970 million of the proceeds went to pre-IPO owners. Sponsor affiliates and an early investor sold a further USD 6.2 billion of shares in 2026, and the company received none of it.

### Key findings

The business earns its profit in the brand segment and grows its volume in a thin-margin distribution segment, so creditors depend on Medline Brand. Medline Brand gave 82.84% of segment Adjusted EBITDA on 46.49% of net sales in nine months 2026, while Supply Chain Solutions grew its sales 15.1% and saw its margin fall to 4.7% from 5.5%, which the issuer links to lower-margin Prime Vendor customers. Management says its own brand earns higher margins as customers convert to it; the filings give the average Prime Vendor mix of 35% Medline Brand, which sizes the room left to convert, but not how fast conversion has proceeded.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against the view that owner claims are the main risk is that creditors are being repaid and the owners are leaving without taking company cash. Total debt fell by USD 3,933 million in FY2025, sponsor-affiliated holdings of the company's debt are down to USD 41 million, no dividend is planned, the 2026 sell-downs were paid for by public investors, the audit committee at the offering had no owner designee, and at the September 25, 2026 price the equity is worth about USD 45.6 billion against total debt of USD 12,580 million, so a very large equity cushion sits beneath the debt. The view would soften if the executed debt documents show tight limits on distributions, or if a proxy statement shows an independent board majority, and it would harden if payments under the tax receivable agreement begin to exceed the cash tax savings, if repurchases are used to buy Medline Holdings units from the owners, or if a new owner distribution is paid while operating costs from the fire, recall and warning letters are still being incurred.

### Limitations

- The executed credit agreement, indentures and security documents are not supplied, so the room for distributions and the guarantee structure rest on filing summaries; if payment baskets prove wide the owner risk level would move up, if tight it would move down.
- The proxy statement is not supplied, so the current board, committees, owner stakes and pay are taken from the November 2025 registration statement and February 2026 voting figures; the control conclusion could be out of date after the two sell-downs.
- Fund vintage, entry price, equity invested and exit plans of the sponsors are not stated, so sponsor willingness to support creditors is not assessed and none is assumed.
- Owner ownership percentages are blank in the registration statement tables, so the split of the 67.0% between the sponsors and the Mills family is not established and sponsor concentration cannot be refined.
- Supply Chain Solutions has no product split and no segment assets are reported, so conclusions on distribution margins rest on segment totals.
- The share of Prime Vendor sales under agreements that can be ended for convenience is not stated, so contract durability is described but not measured.
