## Findings

### Bottom line

WABASH NATIONAL Corp is a publicly listed trailer and truck-body manufacturer with no identified controlling owner or sponsor, so creditors rely on a cyclical operating business and on a board that kept paying dividends through a loss-making 2026.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_wnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 1; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 3–4; 10-K_2025-12-31_wnc-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 144–145

Sponsor and governance risk is Medium. Repurchases fell from USD 76.21 million in FY2023 to USD 33.77 million in FY2025 and none were made in the first nine months of 2026, and the audit opinion is unqualified. Against that, dividends of USD 10.03 million were still paid in nine months 2026 against an operating loss of USD 97.68 million, USD 150.0 million of convertible debt was added in July 2026 and the revolver was cut from USD 350.0 million to USD 300.0 million.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_wnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 3–4; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 19–23; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 54; 10-K_2025-12-31_wnc-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 94–98; calculation C10

### Key findings

No sponsor or controlling shareholder is identified, which removes leveraged-buyout style extraction risk but also means no sponsor equity support is documented. The issuer is a Delaware company listed on the New York Stock Exchange with one class of common stock, and beneficial ownership percentages and board control rights sit in a proxy statement that was not supplied, so concentration of control is not established.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_wnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 1; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-K_2025-12-31_wnc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 63; 10-K_2025-12-31_wnc-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 144–145

The operating model is cyclical equipment manufacturing, and the Parts & Services business that management positions as less cyclical is still too small to offset the trailer loss. New trailers were 73.9% of nine-month 2026 net sales and the Transportation Solutions segment lost USD 58.79 million at the operating line, while Parts & Services earned USD 15.52 million on 15.53% of external sales.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_wnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 39–41; calculation C02; calculation C03; calculation C10

Order intake has turned sharply upward but is not yet earnings. Total backlog was USD 1,198 million at 2026-09-30 against USD 705 million at 2025 year end, and management attributes the rise to orders exceeding shipments and an early 2027 dry van order cycle. The filing adds that some orders may reflect customers ordering ahead of tariff-related price increases and that backlog can change or be cancelled.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_wnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 66–67

Customer concentration is moderate. The five largest customers were 35% of FY2025 net sales against 42% in FY2024, no customer exceeded 10% in FY2025 after one reached 15% in FY2024, and sales go through factory-direct accounts and independent dealers with international sales under 10%.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_wnc-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 26–28; 10-K_2025-12-31_wnc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 137

Shareholder returns have been scaled back but not stopped, which is mixed behaviour for creditors. In FY2025 dividends and repurchases together of USD 47.55 million exceeded operating cash flow of USD 11.67 million while cash fell by USD 83.56 million, and in nine months 2026 dividends of USD 10.03 million were paid while operating cash flow was negative USD 65.82 million. USD 93.2 million of repurchase authorisation remains until February 2027, and the filings do not state the funding source of either payment.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_wnc-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 97–98; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 37; calculation C07; calculation C10

The July and August 2026 transactions extended maturities but added debt and tightened the revolver. The issuer sold USD 150.0 million of 4.00% convertible senior unsecured notes due 2032 and repaid revolver borrowings, then cut revolver commitments from USD 350.0 million to USD 300.0 million and moved the stated maturity from 2027 to 2031. The revolver's stated maturity is the earliest of several dates, one of which is 91 days before the USD 400 million 4.50% Senior Notes mature on 2028-10-15, which is 2028-07-16, and no final decision on those notes is disclosed.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_wnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 18–23; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 54; calculation C06

Affiliate and venture exposure is small today but carries contingent claims. The issuer now owns Linq outright after funding it through a note, holds 20% stakes in two venture-lab companies, guarantees a counterparty's equipment lease for 24 months, and has maximum exposure of USD 12.4 million on the UpLabs arrangement and USD 5.8 million on FreightVana. The partner in the Wabash Parts joint venture holds a put right that would require the issuer to buy its 50% interest at a valuation that is not disclosed.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_wnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 9–16; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 31; 10-K_2025-12-31_wnc-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 109–110; calculation C01

Reporting quality is adequate and creditor-adverse transactions are not found in the five filings. The auditor gave an unqualified opinion on the FY2025 statements and on internal control, disclosure controls were reported effective at 2026-09-30, and no exchange, consent solicitation, covenant waiver, priming or sponsor fee appears in them. The 2024 jury award of USD 450 million, settled in 2025 with a USD 30 million company contribution, shows that litigation can create a very large claim before insurance and appeals.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_wnc-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 94–95; 10-K_2025-12-31_wnc-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 124–125; 10-K_2025-12-31_wnc-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 141–143; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 70

Senior management continuity is a watch item. A new chief financial officer was appointed on 2024-09-01, new chief operating and chief commercial officers on 2025-01-15, and a transition agreement dated 2026-04-08 with the executive listed as chief growth officer in the annual report has terms that were not supplied, so continuity of the team that must deal with the 2028 notes is not established.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_wnc-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 31–34; 10-Q_2026-03-31_wnc-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 50

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a Medium rating is the order book. Total backlog of USD 1,198 million at 2026-09-30 is 70% above year end 2025, 2027 dry van order books opened early and trailer shipments rose 13.4% in nine months 2026 on higher dry van and platform shipments, so earnings could recover fast enough to make the dividend and any restored buyback unremarkable and support a Low rating. Against that, truck body shipments fell 50.4% in nine months 2026, the trailer segment still lost money, and part of the order surge may be tariff-driven pull-forward. The view would move to High if the proxy statement showed a concentrated holder or entrenched board control, if dividends or buybacks were funded from the revolver while the 2028 maturity approaches, or if the executed credit documents showed wide restricted-payment capacity that was then used. It would move to Low if backlog converted into positive operating cash flow, the 2028 notes were refinanced and no further support to affiliates appeared.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_wnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 45–50; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 66–67

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

The main new debt issue is the USD 150.0 million of 4.00% convertible senior unsecured notes due 2032, completed on 2026-07-20 after the initial purchasers exercised a USD 20.0 million option on 2026-07-16. The notes are guaranteed by the subsidiaries that guarantee the 4.50% Senior Notes, carry an initial conversion price of about USD 16.75 per share, a 32.50% premium to the USD 12.64 closing price on 2026-07-15, cannot be redeemed before 2029-08-06 except in a cleanup redemption, and convert into about 21.98% of the shares now outstanding if every holder converted at the initial rate. The net proceeds were used primarily to repay revolver borrowings, and the revolver had no amounts outstanding at 2026-09-30.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_wnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 18; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 21–23; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 54; calculation C05

The revolving credit agreement was amended twice. The Fifth Amendment on 2026-07-14 allowed up to USD 150.0 million of convertible notes as additional permitted debt. The Sixth Amendment on 2026-08-12 cut commitments from USD 350.0 million to USD 300.0 million, moved the scheduled maturity from 2027-09-23 to 2031-08-12 subject to earlier tests tied to other debt, kept a USD 175 million accordion and raised the margin range over term SOFR from 1.25% to 1.75% to 1.50% to 2.00%. The amended agreement also carries a USD 40 million availability block until fixed charge coverage exceeds 1.0x and a minimum liquidity of USD 90 million, neither of which the previous quarter's description of the agreement mentions. The Senior Notes of USD 400 million remain outstanding to 2028-10-15, and the issuer says it may evaluate alternatives for that maturity without a final decision.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_wnc-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 19–20; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 18–21; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 54; calculation C06

Other transactions were small. Linq was bought out on 2026-01-01 for USD 6.4 million cash and USD 18.6 million of a forgiven note, TrailerHawk.ai assets were bought on 2025-02-03, trailers were sold to a third party on 2026-02-26 with a 24-month limited guaranty of the lessee, and the idled Goshen plant was sold in Q3 2026 for about USD 17.8 million. No sponsor, buyout, merger or change of control transaction is disclosed, and the company states no pro forma figures for any of these events.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_wnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 9–10; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 31; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 41; 10-K_2025-12-31_wnc-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 105
