## Findings

### Bottom line

The five supplied filings are enough to run a credit snapshot of WABASH NATIONAL Corp to Q3 2026, but not to test covenant definitions, ratings or market pricing, because no executed debt agreements, agency ratings or market quotes were supplied. Creditor protections can therefore be described only as the issuer summarises them.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_wnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 18, 91

Audited annual statements for FY2023 to FY2025 and quarterly statements through the quarter ended 2026-09-30 let net debt, liquidity and trailing twelve month figures be built from primary documents. Nothing dated after the latest filing on 2026-10-29 was supplied, so events up to the 2026-11-18 cutoff are not established.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_wnc-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 95–98; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 2–4, 92

### Key findings

Period coverage fits the pathway. Two annual reports supply FY2023 to FY2025, three quarterly reports supply Q1, Q2 and Q3 of 2026 with 2025 comparatives, and the FY2025 annual report prints quarterly sales and profit for 2025, so Q4 2025 and the twelve months to Q3 2026 can be derived without a further document. The quarterly cash flow statements are year to date, so discrete Q2 and Q3 cash flows need subtraction.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_wnc-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 95–98, 138; 10-Q_2026-03-31_wnc-20260331.htm.jsonl, pages 2–4; 10-Q_2026-06-30_wnc-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 2–4; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 2–4

Debt disclosure is instrument level for all three debt instruments: the 4.50% unsecured senior notes due 2028, the 4.00% convertible notes due 2032 issued in July 2026, and the secured revolving credit agreement, which was amended in August 2026 to cut commitments from USD 350.0 million to USD 300.0 million and to extend maturity to 2031. The revolver carries a minimum liquidity test of USD 90 million until a springing fixed charge coverage test takes over, and the issuer reports compliance with all covenants at the latest quarter end.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_wnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 18–23, 54

Period comparability needs care before any trend is read. The 2024 results carry a USD 450 million product liability charge in general and administrative expense, and the 2025 results book a USD 418.6 million gain against it after a settlement that insurance covered apart from a USD 30 million company contribution. The Linq business has been consolidated since 2026-01-01 with purchase accounting still provisional, although the filing states that its revenue and earnings are not material.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_wnc-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 125, 139; 10-Q_2026-09-30_wnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 9–10

Several inputs that a longer pathway would use are absent: no agency rating, no trading level for the notes, no company earnings guidance, no peer set and no portfolio were supplied. The only price indication is the fair value of the notes in the debt fair value note, which rests on third-party pricing that the issuer says generally does not represent daily market activity.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_wnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 18, 30, 67

Board composition, committee structure, beneficial ownership and executive pay are incorporated by reference from proxy statements that were not supplied, so Business & governance rests on the annual report's business description and executive officer list.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_wnc-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 31–34, 144–145

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest case against the ready view is that covenant tests, guarantee and security terms and the springing maturities of the revolver rest on the issuer's own summaries, so a definition in the executed agreements could change the headroom reading. The credit agreement and both indentures would move Legal & covenants from conditional to ready, and a rating report or note quotes would do the same for Ratings & triggers and Market-implied risk. A filing after the 2026-10-29 report that changes debt, liquidity or litigation would also change the latest-quarter view.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_wnc-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 19–21, 91
