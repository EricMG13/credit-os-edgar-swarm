## Findings

### Bottom line

The five Snap filings are enough to build the financial, business and fundamental-credit view now requested, with stated limitations, but not to verify creditors' legal protections, covenant terms or market pricing.

The latest report was signed on 4 November 2026, so facts stop at the quarter ended 30 September 2026 and the two weeks to the as-of date are uncovered. Debt by instrument, the revolving facility, letters of credit, the minimum-liquidity test, purchase commitments and litigation are all disclosed, and the notes' carrying amounts agree to the balance sheets. The executed indentures and credit agreement are listed in the annual report but not supplied; agency ratings, guarantor detail, peer data, quotes and portfolio limits are also absent, and regional user metrics could not be extracted.
