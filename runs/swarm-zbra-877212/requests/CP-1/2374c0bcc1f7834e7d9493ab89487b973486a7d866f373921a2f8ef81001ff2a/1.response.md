## Findings

### Bottom line

Zebra's leverage on trailing unadjusted EBITDA fell to 2.25x total debt and 2.09x net debt at October 3, 2026, but USD 2,077 million of debt falls due within a year, so creditors rely on the planned refinancing more than on the ratio.
Trailing EBITDA rose from USD 885 million in FY2025 to USD 1,141 million, helped by the Elo Touch acquisition and a tariff refund benefit of about USD 73 million in the second quarter of 2026, while debt carried on the balance sheet rose from USD 2,502 million to USD 2,571 million. Zebra states no EBITDA of its own, so every ratio uses operating income plus depreciation and amortization with no add-backs, and interest cover is 7.66x on USD 149 million of trailing cash interest.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-10-03_zbra-20261003.htm.jsonl, pages 2–5, 13–15, 16; 10-K_2025-12-31_zbra-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 73–76; calculation C082; calculation C083; calculation C084; calculation C033; calculation C039; calculation C051; calculation C054; calculation C105

### Leverage and coverage

Leverage has fallen from 3.38x total debt at FY2023 to 2.38x at FY2024, risen to 2.83x at FY2025 and fallen again to 2.25x on a trailing basis at October 3, 2026. The FY2025 rise follows the acquisitions of Elo Touch and Photoneo, which took USD 1,365 million of cash and were funded from cash that had built up to USD 901 million at FY2024 and from revolver borrowings, so net leverage moved from 1.39x to 2.69x. The 2026 fall comes from earnings, not repayment: total debt is USD 69 million higher than at FY2025, while trailing EBITDA is USD 256 million higher, so deleveraging depends on the earnings base holding.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_zbra-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 73–76; 10-Q_2026-10-03_zbra-20261003.htm.jsonl, pages 2–5; calculation C067; calculation C070; calculation C073; calculation C071; calculation C074; calculation C111; calculation C112

Reported EBITDA contains items that flatter or depress it. The trailing figure includes a tariff refund benefit of about USD 73 million recognized in cost of sales in the second quarter of 2026; without it trailing EBITDA would be USD 1,068 million and leverage 2.41x total debt and 2.24x net debt (an Analyst Judgement sensitivity, not used in any ratio). FY2025 EBITDA is after exit and restructuring costs of USD 76 million, including USD 45 million of impairments, so the FY2025 base is also not clean. Zebra states no adjusted EBITDA, so no bridge is drawn and none of these items is added back.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-10-03_zbra-20261003.htm.jsonl, page 16; 10-K_2025-12-31_zbra-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 59, 74; calculation C105; calculation C106; calculation C107

Interest cover is 5.92x at FY2023, 16.62x at FY2024, 6.86x at FY2025 and 7.66x on a trailing basis. The FY2024 figure is not comparable: cash interest paid of USD 55 million in FY2024 is described as inclusive or net of forward interest rate swaps, and that year also shows USD 86 million of cash receipts on forward swaps, while net interest expense was USD 98 million. Cash interest rose to USD 99 million in the first nine months of 2026 from USD 79 million a year earlier as borrowings grew, and a refinancing at current market rates could raise it further, because the term loan and revolver carry floating rates near 5.25% and the notes 6.5%.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_zbra-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 74–76, 95; 10-Q_2026-10-03_zbra-20261003.htm.jsonl, pages 3, 5, 13–15; calculation C069; calculation C072; calculation C075; calculation C084

### Cash flow quality

Operating cash flow of USD 711 million in the first nine months of 2026 against EBITDA of USD 943 million converts well, and free cash flow after USD 50 million of capital expenditure was USD 661 million; the conversion is helped by about USD 73 million of tariff refunds, of which USD 46 million has been received in cash and USD 27 million sits in receivables. Working capital absorbed cash: receivables rose to USD 1,025 million from USD 801 million and inventories to USD 851 million from USD 729 million as sales grew, partly offset by higher payables. The consequence for creditors is that cash generation is strong but is being returned to shareholders: repurchases of USD 643 million equal 97.28% of free cash flow, and the shortfall was met by net borrowing of USD 67 million, so free cash flow has not reduced debt.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-10-03_zbra-20261003.htm.jsonl, pages 3, 5, 16, 26; calculation C103; calculation C104; calculation C110; calculation C113; calculation C115

### Liquidity and refinancing

Cash of USD 182 million and revolver availability of USD 1,045 million after letters of credit give liquidity of USD 1,227 million at October 3, 2026, but every bank facility matures within about eight months: the receivables facility on March 19, 2027 and the term loan and the revolver on May 25, 2027. The balance sheet therefore classes USD 2,077 million of debt as current, against USD 141 million at FY2025, and the 2027 maturity of USD 2,056 million is the term loan balance after one more installment plus the revolver and receivables borrowings. The revolver availability disappears when the revolver matures, so committed liquidity beyond May 2027 is cash alone until a refinancing is signed. Management states it intends to refinance the credit facility in the fourth quarter of 2026, after saying in its second quarter report that it would do so in the second half of 2026; the sources hold nothing dated after November 3, 2026, so the outcome is not established.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-10-03_zbra-20261003.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 13–15, 26; 10-Q_2026-07-04_zbra-20260704.htm.jsonl, page 27; calculation C108; calculation C109; calculation C054

### Data quality and definitions

Several definition points limit comparability. EBITDA is derived because Zebra states none, as operating income plus depreciation and amortization, and quarterly depreciation for the second and third quarters is obtained by difference because the cash flow statements are cumulative. Debt facility amounts in the debt note are called carrying value but are before USD 6 million of issuance costs and USD 1 million of discounts that the note deducts in total, so the balance sheet total of USD 2,571 million is used as Total Debt and the facility total of USD 2,578 million is kept as principal. Segments were realigned in the fourth quarter of 2025 to Connected Frontline and Asset Visibility & Automation and share-based compensation was removed from segment income, so FY2022 segment data are on the former basis and are not shown. The 2026 reports state that prior-period operating cash flow amounts were reclassified without giving amounts, and the cover of the FY2025 annual report checks the box for correction of an error without describing one, although the key FY2024 totals agree between the two annual reports.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-10-03_zbra-20261003.htm.jsonl, pages 5, 13; 10-K_2025-12-31_zbra-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 58, 108; 10-K_2024-12-31_zbra-20241231.htm.jsonl, page 2

Two items are credit-relevant funding that is not debt. Deferred revenue of USD 852 million (current USD 438 million and long-term USD 414 million) is stable against USD 842 million at FY2025 and is a prepayment obligation to customers rather than payables. Receivables factoring of up to EUR 150 million removes receivables from the balance sheet, with USD 22 million of sold receivables uncollected and USD 63 million of collections not yet remitted to the bank at October 3, 2026, so the off-balance-sheet funding is small relative to debt but the remittance liability falls inside accrued liabilities.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-10-03_zbra-20261003.htm.jsonl, pages 2–3, 8, 15, 18; calculation C116

Subsequent events are flagged and kept out of period figures. After FY2025 the board authorized repurchases of up to USD 1 billion on February 4, 2026 and about USD 100 million of shares were bought by February 5, 2026; the first quarter report adds about USD 200 million bought after its quarter end. All of these fall inside the 2026 interim statements and are not applied to FY2025 net debt, so no pro forma figure is established for FY2025. For the latest balance sheet the share count on the cover fell between October 3 and October 27, 2026 without an amount being stated, so no adjustment is drawn.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_zbra-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 57, 65, 110; 10-Q_2026-04-04_zbra-20260404.htm.jsonl, page 26; 10-Q_2026-10-03_zbra-20261003.htm.jsonl, pages 1, 4
