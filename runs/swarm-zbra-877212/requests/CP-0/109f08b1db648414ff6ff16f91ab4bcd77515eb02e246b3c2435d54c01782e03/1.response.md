## Findings

### Bottom line

The five supplied filings support the financial, business and credit-view work on Zebra through October 3, 2026, but creditors cannot test covenants, guarantor coverage, ratings or market pricing from them because the credit documents and market data are absent. The debt, maturity and revolver figures foot to the balance sheet and to each other, so the printed statements can be used as they stand.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-10-03_zbra-20261003.htm.jsonl, pages 13–15; 10-K_2025-12-31_zbra-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 116–118; calculation CALC-1; calculation CALC-2; calculation CALC-3; calculation CALC-4

The sharpest evidence gap is at the maturity wall. Debt of USD 2,077 million was classed as current at October 3, 2026 because the term loan and revolver mature on May 25, 2027, and management states an intention to refinance in the fourth quarter of 2026. Nothing dated after November 3, 2026 was supplied, so whether that refinancing has progressed is not established.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-10-03_zbra-20261003.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 14, 26, 32
