## Findings

### Bottom line

Emergent is now a government-dependent credit: U.S. government biodefence orders, not naloxone, carry the cash flow that services its debt, so order timing and a finance-chief handover matter more to creditors than any sponsor behaviour, because no sponsor or controlling shareholder is identified.
Basis: calculation C03; 10-K_2025-12-31_ebs.htm.jsonl, pages 156–157; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ebs.htm.jsonl, page 28

The government supplied 55.36% of revenue in nine months 2026 against 45.37% a year earlier, while naloxone revenue fell 26.05% and led to a USD 191.3 million impairment. Management cut debt principal by USD 185.7 million since 2024-12-31 and spent USD 18.1 million on share repurchases in nine months 2026, and the issuer describes the April 2026 refinancing as bringing less restrictive covenants.
Basis: calculation C03; calculation C04; calculation C07; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ebs.htm.jsonl, pages 15–16; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ebs.htm.jsonl, pages 18–20; EX99.1_2026-03-31_ebs_a04162026refinancingpr-htm.htm.jsonl, page 1

### Key findings

- Government concentration is the central business risk, and it is rising. The U.S. government supplied 55.36% of revenue in nine months 2026 against 45.37% in nine months 2025; its long-term contracts expire between 2026 and 2036 and can be terminated or modified unilaterally, and orders arrive through annual options, so total revenue swung from USD 234.3 million in Q2 2026 to USD 110.0 million in Q3 2026. Creditors are exposed to the timing of option exercises more than to end demand.
Basis: calculation C03; 10-K_2025-12-31_ebs.htm.jsonl, pages 156–157; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ebs.htm.jsonl, pages 27–28; EX99.1_2026-06-30_ebs_ebs202606-30ex99er-htm.htm.jsonl, page 1; EX99.1_2026-09-30_ebs_ebs202609-30ex99er-htm.htm.jsonl, page 1
- The naloxone franchise has stopped diversifying the business. Naloxone revenue fell 43.32% in FY2025 and 26.05% in nine months 2026 as generic competitors and an unfavourable price and volume mix in U.S. public-interest channels cut sales, which led to the USD 191.3 million impairment of the NARCAN intangible asset; the franchise is now 27.74% of revenue, so the commercial engine no longer offsets government timing.
Basis: calculation C02; calculation C03; calculation C05; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ebs.htm.jsonl, pages 15–16; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ebs.htm.jsonl, pages 49–50
- Financial policy is creditor-aligned on debt but mixed on equity. Debt principal fell from USD 700.0 million at 2024-12-31 to USD 514.3 million at 2026-09-30 through a USD 100.0 million voluntary prepayment and USD 85.3 million of note repurchases, yet the company also repurchased USD 24.9 million of shares in 2025 and USD 18.1 million in nine months 2026 while earnings weakened, and the term loan carries an uncommitted incremental facility of the greater of USD 200.0 million and 80% of covenant EBITDA, which the issuer says increases its ability to incur more debt.
Basis: calculation C06; calculation C07; 10-K_2025-12-31_ebs.htm.jsonl, pages 187–189; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ebs.htm.jsonl, pages 20–22; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ebs.htm.jsonl, page 26
- There is no sponsor, so creditors face neither sponsor extraction nor a sponsor equity backstop, and the filings identify no controlling shareholder. The company is listed on the New York Stock Exchange, ownership percentages sit in a proxy statement that was not supplied, and the only lender link to the equity is warrants equal to 4.86% of shares outstanding, whose exercise prices sit above the USD 8.44 average paid for shares in July 2026.
Basis: calculation C08; 10-K_2025-12-31_ebs.htm.jsonl, page 217; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ebs.htm.jsonl, page 1; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ebs.htm.jsonl, pages 26–27
- Management is in the middle of a turnaround with a leadership handover, and the legal overhang is mostly resolved. The finance chief retires on 2026-12-31 with an interim successor from 2027-01-01, while the August 2026 plan cuts about 90 employees, around a tenth of the workforce, for targeted annualized savings of about USD 40.0 million; the securities class action settled for USD 40.0 million with insurers funding USD 30.0 million, the derivative case brought USD 15.0 million from insurers, and a Department of Justice discussion continues.
Basis: calculation C09; 10-K_2025-12-31_ebs.htm.jsonl, pages 208–211; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ebs.htm.jsonl, pages 13–14; EX99.1_2026-06-30_ebs_ebs202606-30ex99er-htm.htm.jsonl, page 1; EX99.1_2026-09-30_ebs_ebs202609-30ex99er-htm.htm.jsonl, page 2
- The manufacturing footprint is being rationalised, which lowers fixed cost but raises reliance on third parties. The Baltimore-Bayview plant was sold for about USD 36.5 million at closing, the Camden drug product plant went to Bora, Rockville was closed and the services business is no longer a reportable segment, while most products depend wholly or partly on third-party manufacturers, some as sole source.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_ebs.htm.jsonl, pages 9–10; 10-K_2025-12-31_ebs.htm.jsonl, page 27; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ebs.htm.jsonl, page 62

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a cautious view is the durability of government demand and the pace of debt reduction. Remaining performance obligations were USD 241.9 million at 2026-09-30, USD 222.7 million of it recognizable within 24 months, new orders continued (a CYFENDUS modification of about USD 24 million in August 2026 and a BioThrax order of up to USD 22.3 million on 2026-11-04), and principal debt is USD 185.7 million lower than at 2024-12-31.
Basis: calculation C07; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ebs.htm.jsonl, page 28; EX99.1_2026-09-30_ebs_a1142026biothraxmodpr-htm.htm.jsonl, page 1

The view would soften if naloxone revenue stopped falling for consecutive quarters, if government orders were spread more evenly through the year, or if the share repurchase authorization of USD 37.3 million remaining were left unused while the 2028 notes stay outstanding. It would harden on the non-exercise of a major government option, a further impairment, a covenant waiver or amendment, or a debt-funded distribution.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_ebs.htm.jsonl, page 26

### Limitations

- The proxy statement was not supplied, so ownership percentages, board independence and executive pay are not established; the finding that no controlling shareholder exists rests only on what the annual and quarterly reports state and could change if a large holder is disclosed.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_ebs.htm.jsonl, page 217
- The executed financing agreements were not supplied, so how much creditor protection the April 2026 refinancing removed cannot be measured; the loosened-covenant finding rests on the issuer's own description of the new terms.
Basis: EX99.1_2026-03-31_ebs_a04162026refinancingpr-htm.htm.jsonl, page 1; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ebs.htm.jsonl, pages 20–25
- Contract values and expiry dates are disclosed for only some government contracts, so revenue visibility beyond the remaining performance obligations is not established and the concentration trend may reflect option timing rather than a lasting shift.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_ebs.htm.jsonl, pages 14–19
- The restructuring headcount and savings are management estimates revised between the second and third quarter reports, so the cost-cutting offset to the naloxone decline is unproven until reported results show it.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-06-30_ebs.htm.jsonl, page 32; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ebs.htm.jsonl, page 14

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

The April 2026 refinancing replaced the prior term loan with a USD 150.0 million term loan from OrbiMed, adds a USD 75.0 million delayed draw term loan available for 24 months subject to a secured leverage condition, and cuts the asset-based revolving commitment from USD 100.0 million to USD 50.0 million; both facilities now mature on 2031-04-16, subject to springing earlier maturities. The issuer describes the terms as less restrictive covenants with more capacity to incur incremental debt, and the term loan allows an uncommitted incremental facility of the greater of USD 200.0 million and 80% of covenant EBITDA. For creditors this extends the maturity wall past the 2028 notes but loosens the protection around them.
Basis: EX99.1_2026-03-31_ebs_a04162026refinancingpr-htm.htm.jsonl, page 1; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ebs.htm.jsonl, pages 20–23

Alongside the refinancing, the company repurchased USD 75.0 million principal of its 3.875% Senior Unsecured Notes for USD 68.1 million of cash in the third quarter of 2026, leaving USD 364.7 million due on 2028-08-15, after repurchasing USD 10.3 million in 2025 and prepaying USD 100.0 million of the prior term loan in December 2025. No new notes were issued in the period covered by the documents.
Basis: calculation C06; calculation C07; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ebs.htm.jsonl, pages 19–20; 10-K_2025-12-31_ebs.htm.jsonl, pages 187–189
