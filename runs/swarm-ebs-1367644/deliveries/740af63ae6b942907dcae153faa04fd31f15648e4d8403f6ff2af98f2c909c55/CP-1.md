### Bottom line

Trailing earnings no longer cover Emergent's debt: reported EBITDA for the twelve months to 2026-09-30 is USD -106.6 million, so leverage is not calculable and creditors rely on cash, liquidity and the 2028 maturity date rather than earnings cover.
Basis: calculation C03; calculation C04; 10-K_2025-12-31_ebs.htm.jsonl, pages 148–149; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ebs.htm.jsonl, pages 6–7

Before the impairment, trailing EBITDA is USD 84.7 million and net debt of USD 332.0 million is 3.92x that figure, an analyst measure the issuer does not report.
Basis: calculation C04; calculation C10; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ebs.htm.jsonl, pages 6–7; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ebs.htm.jsonl, page 16

Cash of USD 175.7 million and an undrawn USD 50.0 million revolver give liquidity of USD 225.7 million, and the USD 364.7 million 2028 notes are the next maturity.
Basis: calculation C01; calculation C09; calculation C14; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ebs.htm.jsonl, pages 18–20; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ebs.htm.jsonl, page 55

### Financial interpretation

Earnings, not the balance sheet, are the weak point. Revenue for the twelve months to 2026-09-30 is USD 649.1 million against USD 1,043.6 million in FY2024, the NARCAN franchise is losing price and volume to generic and branded competitors, and that deterioration triggered the second-quarter impairment that turns trailing reported EBITDA negative. Leverage on that measure is therefore not calculable, and the issuer's own Adjusted EBITDA of USD 127.5 million for nine months 2026 is 38% below the USD 204.8 million of a year earlier.
Basis: calculation C04; calculation C13; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ebs.htm.jsonl, pages 15–16; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ebs.htm.jsonl, pages 43–44; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ebs.htm.jsonl, pages 63–64

The strongest offset is cash. Operating cash flow was USD 200.1 million over the last twelve months and free cash flow USD 188.4 million, helped by a USD 62.6 million inventory release that will not repeat, and the issuer retired USD 75.0 million of notes and USD 150.0 million of prior term loan in 2026. If trailing EBITDA excluding the impairment of USD 84.7 million is the right earnings base, net debt is 3.92x, which is high for an issuer whose quarterly Adjusted EBITDA was negative in Q3 2026; the next quarters of revenue and margin are the test.
Basis: calculation C04; calculation C10; calculation C14; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ebs.htm.jsonl, page 7

Liquidity is adequate but narrower than a year ago. Cash of USD 175.7 million plus the undrawn USD 50.0 million revolver is USD 225.7 million, down from USD 305.4 million at 2025-12-31 because the revolver commitment was halved and cash fell after the milestone payment, note repurchases and refinancing costs. The USD 75.0 million delayed draw term loan is not counted because drawing it needs secured leverage no higher than 1.75x under a definition that is not supplied, so availability cannot be tested.
Basis: calculation C09; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ebs.htm.jsonl, pages 20–23; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ebs.htm.jsonl, page 55

Maturities are concentrated in 2028. The USD 364.7 million notes fall due on 2028-08-15 and, on the issuer's own earlier presentation of a similar clause, the USD 149.6 million term loan could spring forward to 2028 if more than USD 75.0 million of notes remain and a liquidity test is unmet; the revolver already springs to 2028-05-17 while notes remain. Each note repurchase therefore shortens the exposure as well as reducing debt.
Basis: calculation C07; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ebs.htm.jsonl, pages 20–23; 10-K_2025-12-31_ebs.htm.jsonl, page 191

Deferred revenue is a small but rising non-debt funding source: the balance of USD 14.3 million at 2026-09-30 compares with USD 5.0 million at 2025-12-31 in the re-presented comparative, while inventories fell from USD 343.4 million to USD 280.9 million. Customer advances fund operations until the associated deliveries are made, so lower advance receipts would remove a source of cash at the same time as the inventory release runs out.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_ebs.htm.jsonl, pages 5–6; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ebs.htm.jsonl, pages 28–29

A supported downside sequence starts with further erosion of NARCAN pricing or volume in U.S. public-interest channels, which cuts Commercial Products margin first, then reported EBITDA and trailing cover, and finally the ability to meet the term loan's total leverage covenant of 5.25x tested from the quarter ended 2026-09-30. The issuer states compliance at 2026-09-30; the covenant is not computed here because its EBITDA definition is not supplied. Observable triggers are quarterly Naloxone revenue, government order timing and the next compliance statement.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_ebs.htm.jsonl, page 21; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ebs.htm.jsonl, page 24; 10-Q_2026-09-30_ebs.htm.jsonl, pages 43–44
