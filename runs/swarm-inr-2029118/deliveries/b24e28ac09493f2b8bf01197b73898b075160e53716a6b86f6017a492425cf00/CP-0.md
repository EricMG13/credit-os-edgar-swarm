## Findings

### Bottom line

The five SEC filings support a financial and capital-structure credit snapshot of Infinity Natural Resources, Inc. as of 2026-11-18, but without the financing agreements covenant headroom and creditor protections cannot be confirmed.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_inr-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 26–29; 10-K_2025-12-31_inr-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 194–195

Anchor the work on the 2026-09-30 balance sheet: year-end 2025 statements pre-date the Antero acquisition (USD 681.7 million), the Series A preferred issue (USD 350.0 million) and the senior notes issue (USD 550.0 million), so they understate today's capital structure. Management also reports unremediated material weaknesses in internal control, so every reported figure carries a reliability qualification.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_inr-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 13, 182–183, 190–192; 10-Q_2026-09-30_inr-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 21, 27, 37, 72

### Key findings

The filings give income and cash-flow statements from FY2022 and balance sheets from 2023-12-31 through 2026-09-30, in two annual reports and three 2026 quarterly reports, the latest signed 2026-11-09. Discrete quarters for 2025 and the first three quarters of 2026 and a trailing twelve-month view to 2026-09-30 can be rebuilt from them, but quarterly cash flows must be derived by subtracting year-to-date figures, and no filing reports EBITDA, adjusted EBITDA or free cash flow, so those measures will be constructions rather than reported figures.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_inr-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 7–11; 10-K_2025-12-31_inr-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 144–146; 10-K_2024-12-31_inr-20241231.htm.jsonl, pages 153–155; calculation calc-1; calculation calc-2

The reporting entity changes between the two annual reports. The 2024 annual report carries audited statements of the operating company, Infinity Natural Resources, LLC, known as INR Holdings, while the listed parent presents only nil balance sheets because it had no operations before its February 2025 IPO; from FY2025 the statements consolidate the parent with INR Holdings and show the other owners of INR Holdings as redeemable non-controlling interest. INR Holdings is the borrower under the credit facility and the issuer of the senior notes due 2031, while the Series A preferred stock was issued by the parent, so every debt, cash and leverage figure needs a stated perimeter.
Basis: 10-K_2024-12-31_inr-20241231.htm.jsonl, pages 149–150, 153–155; 10-K_2025-12-31_inr-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 14, 144, 183; 10-Q_2026-09-30_inr-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 12, 26–27

Year-end 2025 statements describe the issuer before the Antero acquisition (closed 2026-02-23 for USD 681.7 million), the Series A preferred issue (USD 350.0 million, 2026-02-23) and the senior notes issue (USD 550.0 million, 2026-03-20). The latest quarterly report states no credit facility borrowings at 2026-09-30 against USD 875.0 million of commitments. The third-quarter report gives pro forma revenue and net income for the acquisition but no pro forma EBITDA or net debt, so any run-rate leverage is an analyst construction and must be labelled as one.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_inr-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 13, 182–183; 10-Q_2026-09-30_inr-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 21–22, 26–28, 37; calculation calc-10

Management reports that disclosure controls were not effective at 2024-12-31, at 2025-12-31 and at each 2026 quarter end because material weaknesses in internal control over financial reporting remained open, including segregation of duties, risk assessment, control activities and formal accounting policies; management reports significant progress toward remediation but gives no completion date. The auditor opined on the statements but not on internal control, so reported figures are usable but carry a reliability qualification that every analysis should carry forward.
Basis: 10-K_2024-12-31_inr-20241231.htm.jsonl, page 187; 10-K_2025-12-31_inr-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 143, 190–192; 10-Q_2026-03-31_inr-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 64; 10-Q_2026-06-30_inr-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 68; 10-Q_2026-09-30_inr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 72

No credit agreement, amendment, indenture, certificate of designation or purchase agreement was supplied, and the run settings declare none, so the filings' summaries are the only evidence of financing terms. The summaries give two maintenance tests, a current ratio of at least 1.0x and a leverage ratio of no more than 3.0x, but no filing reports the ratio or covenant EBITDA, and the 2025 annual report defines leverage as total funded debt to EBITDAX while the second- and third-quarter notes call it a consolidated total net leverage ratio. Covenant headroom is therefore not calculable from this set.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_inr-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 132, 194–195; 10-Q_2026-03-31_inr-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 66; 10-Q_2026-06-30_inr-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 27; 10-Q_2026-09-30_inr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 27

Directors, pay and ownership for FY2025 are incorporated from a proxy statement that is not in the set, so the latest ownership table is as of 2025-03-21. The third-quarter exhibit index lists a transition and severance agreement dated 2026-08-09 with the chief financial officer who signed the first two 2026 quarterly reports, and a different officer signs the third-quarter report; neither the terms nor the successor's appointment are described in the filings supplied. The third-quarter report states no subsequent events, and the set ends 9 days before the as-of date.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_inr-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 193; 10-K_2024-12-31_inr-20241231.htm.jsonl, page 211; 10-Q_2026-03-31_inr-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 67; 10-Q_2026-09-30_inr-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 44, 74; calculation calc-9

The latest reserve estimate is as of 2025-12-31, and the annual report states that its operating information does not give effect to the Antero acquisition unless it says otherwise, so asset coverage for the enlarged asset base is not established.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_inr-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 13, 18
