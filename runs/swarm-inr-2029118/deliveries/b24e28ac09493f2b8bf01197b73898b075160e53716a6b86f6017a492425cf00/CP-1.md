## Findings

### Bottom line

Reported leverage is moderate at 1.24x total debt to trailing EBITDA at 2026-09-30 and liquidity is ample, but the EBITDA is constructed and holds the Antero assets for only about seven months, so run-rate leverage is probably lower but is not established.
Basis: calculation calc-ratios-20260930; calculation calc-ltm-ebitda-0930; 10-Q_2026-09-30_inr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 21

Total debt is USD 538.8 million of senior notes due 2031 against USD 26.2 million of cash and USD 875.0 million undrawn under the secured facility, with USD 336.5 million of Series A preferred stock and USD 706.3 million of redeemable non-controlling interest outside total debt. Free cash flow was negative at USD 61.6 million over the trailing twelve months, and the 54.51x cash interest cover is overstated because the first note coupon was payable after the quarter end.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_inr-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 7–8, 11, 27, 38; calculation calc-fcf; calculation calc-ratios-20260930

### Key findings

Total debt to EBITDA moved from 1.55x at 2024-12-31 to 1.30x at 2025-12-31 and 1.24x at 2026-09-30, but the three readings describe different companies: year-end 2025 debt of USD 150.9 million was revolver borrowings before the Antero acquisition, while the latest USD 538.8 million is the 2031 notes after it. The trailing EBITDA of USD 435.4 million no longer carries the one-time IPO share-based charge that depressed FY2025 and holds Antero only from 2026-02-23, so a full-period ratio would probably be lower, but no filing states pro forma EBITDA and the amount is not established. Net debt to EBITDA is 1.18x.
Basis: calculation calc-ltm-ebitda-0930; calculation calc-ratios-20260930; 10-K_2025-12-31_inr-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 144–145; 10-Q_2026-09-30_inr-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 7–9, 21–22

Cash interest cover of 54.51x says little about capacity: cash interest paid over the trailing twelve months was only USD 8.0 million because the first coupon on the 2031 notes was payable on 2026-10-01, so the denominator omits the interest the notes now carry. EBITDA to net interest expense on the accrual basis is 11.78x and is the better indicator, although it too includes the notes only from their March 2026 issue date.
Basis: calculation calc-ltm-interest-0930; calculation calc-ltm-interest-net-0930; 10-Q_2026-09-30_inr-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 8, 27, 43

Operating cash flow of USD 408.6 million over the trailing twelve months fell short of capital expenditure of USD 470.1 million, so free cash flow was negative at USD 61.6 million before the USD 620.5 million paid for Antero, and capital expenditure ran at 103.52% of revenue in FY2025 and 72.68% in the nine months of 2026. The gap is financed with debt and preferred stock, so leverage rises unless capital spending slows or EBITDA catches up; the issuer states that it operates the vast majority of its acreage and can defer or accelerate part of its programme, which is the main mitigant. Cash settlements on hedges turned from an inflow of USD 12.2 million in FY2025 to a net outflow of USD 19.0 million over the trailing twelve months; they sit below operating income, so EBITDA excludes them while operating cash flow carries them. Working capital was a modest inflow, mostly from royalties payable, which more than doubled over the nine months partly through royalties assumed with Antero, and from trade payables; these are interest-free balances that reverse if payment timing normalises, so part of the cash flow is timing.
Basis: calculation calc-fcf; calculation calc-capex; calculation calc-ltm-hedge-0930; 10-K_2025-12-31_inr-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 146; 10-Q_2026-09-30_inr-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 11, 60

Liquidity is USD 901.2 million, being USD 26.2 million of cash plus USD 875.0 million undrawn, and the maturity ladder drawn from the filings shows no principal due before the 2031 notes, so near-term refinancing risk is low on the figures. The commitment is only as firm as the borrowing base, which is redetermined twice a year against reserve values, and the filings do not restate the facility maturity after the 2026 amendments (the 2025 annual report gives September 2028); the credit agreement that would settle both points is not in the set.
Basis: calculation calc-liquidity; calculation calc-ladder; 10-Q_2026-09-30_inr-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 26–27; 10-K_2025-12-31_inr-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 164

Two claims sit between liabilities and equity and are outside total debt: USD 336.5 million of Series A preferred stock, which carries an 8.0% cumulative dividend paid in cash since the second quarter and ranks ahead of the common stock, and USD 706.3 million of redeemable non-controlling interest held by the other owners of INR Holdings. Neither is a creditor claim, but the preferred dividend is a fixed cash draw ahead of equity and a change of control gives its holders a put, so the leverage ratios understate the fixed claims on cash flow. Operating leases are small and sit on their own balance sheet lines outside debt; the firm transportation and drilling rig commitments are disclosed in the notes but are not in any figure here.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_inr-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 8, 37–38, 43

Three data issues limit how far the figures can be pushed. Management reports unremediated material weaknesses in internal control, so every reported line carries a reliability qualification. Small differences stay unexplained in the filings, such as a USD 24 thousand gap in nine-month 2026 depreciation, depletion, and amortization between the income statement and the cash flow statement and USD 1 thousand of rounding in quarterly sums, none of which moves a ratio at two decimals. And the definitions differ from the covenant: EBITDA here is operating income plus depreciation, depletion, and amortization, while the facility tests total funded debt to a consolidated EBITDAX, or a net leverage ratio, defined in a document that is not supplied, so covenant headroom is not calculable.
Basis: Source readiness (CP-0); 10-K_2025-12-31_inr-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 132, 190–192; 10-Q_2026-09-30_inr-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 8, 11, 27, 72

The reporting perimeter changes in FY2025: FY2023 and FY2024 are the operating company's own results, audited in the 2024 annual report and carried as comparatives in the 2025 annual report, while FY2025 onward consolidate the listed parent, so the fiscal-year ratios share an operating business but not an owner structure. No subsequent event after 2026-09-30 changes net debt in the sources read, and the 2025-12-31 net debt of USD 148.0 million should not be quoted as a current figure because the Antero acquisition, the preferred issue and the notes issue all followed it.
Basis: 10-K_2024-12-31_inr-20241231.htm.jsonl, pages 149–150, 153–155; 10-K_2025-12-31_inr-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 13, 144, 182–183; 10-Q_2026-09-30_inr-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 44
