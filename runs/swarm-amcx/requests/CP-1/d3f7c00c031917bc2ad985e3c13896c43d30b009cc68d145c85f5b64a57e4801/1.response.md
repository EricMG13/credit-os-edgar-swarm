## Findings

### Bottom line

AMC Global Media's adjusted earnings are shrinking faster than its debt is falling, so net debt has risen to 3.82x adjusted operating income from 3.03x at the end of 2025, and reaches 8.73x on reported EBITDA.

Adjusted operating income fell 46.21% in the first half of 2026 on weaker affiliate and advertising revenue, while net debt fell only USD 52.9 million to USD 1,197.0 million. No maturity falls due before 2029 and all debt is fixed rate, but the revolver was cancelled in May 2026, leaving cash of USD 464.0 million as the only committed liquidity.
Basis: calculation C41; calculation C35; calculation C38; calculation C43; calculation C83; 10-Q_2026-06-30_amcx-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 16–17; EX-99.1_2026-07-30_amcx-73026ex991.htm.jsonl, page 11

### Key findings

Leverage is only as good as the earnings measure behind it, and the issuer's own headline sits at the flattering end. The issuer reports 4.1x for 2026-06-30 on principal debt, finance leases and adjusted operating income; net debt at carrying value over the same earnings is 3.82x, and over reported EBITDA it is 8.73x. Reported EBITDA is USD 176.0 million below adjusted operating income over the last twelve months because the issuer adds back share-based compensation, restructuring, an impairment of USD 97.78 million, cloud computing amortization and its share of equity investees. Adding back only the impairment and the restructuring charges, an Analyst Judgement, gives 4.63x, which is the figure a creditor should weigh against the issuer's 4.1x.
Basis: calculation C41; calculation C38; calculation C61; calculation C63; calculation C86; EX-99.1_2026-07-30_amcx-73026ex991.htm.jsonl, pages 10–11

Earnings are deteriorating in revenue and in margin, and the second quarter was the weakest point. Adjusted operating income fell 57.88% in the quarter and 46.21% in the half, revenue fell 5.68% in the half, and the last twelve months margin is 13.94% against 23.23% in 2024. Operating income fell USD 48.6 million in the second quarter on a revenue decline of USD 52.5 million, so almost every lost revenue dollar fell through to earnings. The drivers are a shrinking affiliate fee base as subscribers leave basic cable, lower advertising from weaker ratings and pricing, and lighter content licensing deliveries, which streaming price rises have not offset.
Basis: calculation C44; calculation C43; calculation C46; calculation C51; calculation C49; calculation C88; calculation C89; EX-99.1_2026-07-30_amcx-73026ex991.htm.jsonl, pages 2–3; 10-Q_2026-06-30_amcx-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 43–47

The debt stack is long-dated but expensive and heavily secured: the 10.50% secured notes due 2032 are USD 1,315.1 million, which is 75.77% of principal, the two notes due 2029 together are USD 420.5 million, and nothing else is outstanding. The annual cash coupon on this stack is USD 156.0 million, against cash interest paid of USD 151.15 million over the last twelve months, so interest cover is 2.07x on adjusted operating income but only 0.91x on reported EBITDA. The March 2026 exchange swapped nearly all of the 2029 secured notes for additional notes due 2032, and the credit agreement, with its leverage and interest cover tests, was then repaid and cancelled; that ended the tests but also removed the committed backstop.
Basis: calculation C57; calculation C68; calculation C70; calculation C42; calculation C39; 10-Q_2026-06-30_amcx-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 16–19; 10-K_2025-12-31_amcx-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 171

Liquidity rests on cash alone, and part of it is not freely available. Cash of USD 464.0 million at 2026-06-30 includes USD 136.5 million held by foreign subsidiaries, and the USD 175.0 million revolver is gone; the annual report describes a receivables sale facility with a limit of USD 125.0 million, under which nothing had been sold, but it is not a committed credit line and its status at 2026-06-30 is not restated. Free cash flow, which is struck after cash interest, was USD 190.5 million over the last twelve months, equal to 60.86% of adjusted operating income, and leaves room for buybacks, but it is falling with earnings: it was USD 108.1 million in the first half of 2026.
Basis: calculation C87; calculation C20; calculation C53; calculation C24; 10-Q_2026-06-30_amcx-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 55–58; 10-K_2025-12-31_amcx-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 106

Content spending is a funding float that has recently helped cash flow. Program rights obligations, which are interest-free amounts owed to content suppliers, were USD 381.7 million at 2026-06-30, USD 58.3 million lower than at the end of 2025, and amortization of program rights exceeded program payments by USD 72.4 million in the first half. A reversal of that gap, for example higher production payments for the slate the issuer has announced, would reduce operating cash flow without any change in reported earnings.
Basis: calculation C76; calculation C77; calculation C81; 10-Q_2026-06-30_amcx-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-06-30_amcx-20260630.htm.jsonl, page 5

Four events at or after the balance sheet date are flagged and kept out of period figures. The licence agreement with Netflix, announced on 2026-07-30, provides USD 500 million of fees over five years, with about USD 25 million expected in 2026 and about USD 100 million a year from 2027 to 2030. One domestic customer filed for bankruptcy protection on 2026-06-30 and the court allowed payment of prior vendor claims on 2026-07-23, and the issuer expects to collect its receivables in full; the filings do not say whether this is the customer that accounts for about 20% of revenue. The accelerated share repurchase settles in the fourth quarter of 2026. A trial of contract claims for more than USD 200 million was scheduled for 2026-10-27 and its outcome is not stated. None changes net debt at 2026-06-30.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a cautious reading is that the debt is spread over time, fixed in cost and already serviced from cash flow: nothing matures until 2029, free cash flow after interest was USD 190.5 million over twelve months, and the Netflix licence adds about USD 100 million a year of cash from 2027 to 2030, which is 64.12% of the annual coupon. The last covenant test under the cancelled credit agreement, at 2026-03-31, showed net leverage of 5.11x against a ceiling of 5.75x and interest cover of 1.85x against a floor of 1.50x. The view would improve if adjusted operating income stabilised in the second half of 2026 against easier comparisons, if affiliate renewals held price, or if buybacks were set aside for debt reduction. It would worsen if adjusted operating income kept falling at the first-half pace, which would push net debt over adjusted operating income through the issuer's own 4.1x, or if cash fell below USD 464.0 million through buybacks or content payments.
Basis: calculation C20; calculation C90; calculation C70; calculation C63; EX-99.1_2026-05-08_amcx-50826ex991.htm.jsonl, page 10; 10-Q_2026-06-30_amcx-20260630.htm.jsonl, pages 57–58; EX-99.1_2026-07-30_amcx-73026ex991.htm.jsonl, page 4

### Limitations

- Reported EBITDA is derived by adding depreciation and amortization to operating income because the issuer states no EBITDA; the USD 97.78 million impairment sits inside it, so the reported-basis ratios overstate stress relative to cash earnings and the leverage conclusion on that basis is an upper bound.
- The balance sheet date is 2026-06-30 and no later quarter is available, so the liquidity and net debt conclusions do not reflect cash movements after that date.
- Last-twelve-months earnings, cash interest and free cash flow are built from year-to-date disclosures, so quarterly timing differences can distort them and the interest cover conclusion carries that risk.
- Discount and financing costs are not given by instrument, so instrument-level carrying values are used where disclosed and the effective cost of each instrument is not established.
