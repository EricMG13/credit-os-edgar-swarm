## Findings

### Bottom line

NCR Atleos has an adequate credit profile that is improving on earnings and debt reduction, but a flat revenue base, a heavy 2028 and 2029 maturity wall and a change of control with undisclosed financing make event and refinancing risk the main concerns. Net debt fell to USD 2,233 million at 2026-09-30, or 2.59x on derived reported EBITDA and 2.47x on the issuer's adjusted measure for the trailing year, from 3.50x and 3.21x at 2024-12-31, although the pro forma effect of the consent fee on the 2029 secured notes is not established.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); calculation D1; calculation D2

Liquidity of USD 882 million covers scheduled principal through 2027 about five times, so a liquidity squeeze is unlikely before 2028, but USD 2,619 million of the USD 2,792 million of principal at year-end 2025 falls due in 2028 and 2029, and FY2025 interest cover was a modest 2.75x on derived reported EBITDA.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_ncr-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 170; 10-K_2025-12-31_ncr-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 138; Financial foundation (CP-1); calculation D4; calculation D5; calculation D12

The business profile is average and the earnings base is steadier than it is growing. Revenue was USD 4,354 million in FY2025 and USD 4,397 million for the twelve months to 2026-09-30, 70.6% of FY2025 revenue was recurring, no customer exceeds 10% of revenue and 55% of revenue is earned outside the United States, which gives lenders visible cash flow, but recurring revenue fell 1.6% in FY2025 and rose only 0.7% in nine months of 2026 while remaining contracted revenue fell to about USD 1.8 billion from USD 2.3 billion at 2026-03-31. Self-Service Banking and Network together are 95.3% of revenue and 97.3% of segment Adjusted EBITDA, so softness in either segment reaches EBITDA directly.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest case against an adequate, improving profile is that the improvement has run ahead of the business. Recurring revenue is flat, remaining contracted revenue has fallen for two quarters to below its FY2024 level, hardware revenue fell 20% in Q3 2026, Network managed units are shrinking and 2026 earnings include items that will not repeat, so trailing leverage of 2.47x may flatter the position, before any pro forma effect of the consent fee, which is not established; if Adjusted EBITDA slipped back to its FY2024 level with net debt unchanged, net leverage would be 2.84x, and at the FY2023 level 3.03x, still below the 3.21x of 2024-12-31 because debt has fallen. Interest is also a larger drain than leverage suggests, since interest expense absorbed 32.5% of Adjusted EBITDA in FY2025.
Basis: Financial foundation (CP-1); Business & governance (CP-1A); calculation D1; calculation D13; calculation D10; calculation D23; calculation D24

The strongest case for a better view is that lenders already price the debt above principal, liquidity covers the two nearest years of maturities 5.1x, and Brink's may refinance the 2028 and 2029 maturities on its own terms. Publication of the acquirer's financing that repays or refinances the credit facilities, a Q4 2026 revenue recovery with remaining contracted revenue above USD 1.8 billion, or a refinancing of the 2028 maturities before closing would strengthen the view. A revolver draw to pay the consent fee, a failed merger that triggers the termination fee, remaining contracted revenue below USD 1.8 billion, or trailing Adjusted EBITDA below USD 903 million would weaken it.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_natl-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 21; 10-Q_2026-03-31_natl-20260331.htm.jsonl, page 51; 10-Q_2026-09-30_natl-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 12; calculation D15; calculation D20

### Business model, ownership and revenue drivers

The issuer is a global ATM technology, services and network business, and its model gives lenders visible cash flow rather than growth. It sells ATM hardware, software, maintenance and ATM as a Service to banks and retailers, runs the Allpoint surcharge-free network and bank branding for banks and fintechs, and provides managed network services to enterprises; 70.6% of FY2025 revenue was recurring and no customer exceeds 10% of revenue, which supports debt service as long as contracts renew, but renewal rates and contract terms are not supplied and Self-Service Banking and Network are 97.3% of segment Adjusted EBITDA.
