## Findings

### Bottom line

Reported leverage is extreme, at 14.32x total debt to last-twelve-months EBITDA at 2026-09-30, because impairments sit inside reported EBITDA, while the issuer’s normalized basis gives 4.70x, so the credit view depends on how much of the add-backs recurs.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_nwl-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 17; EX-99.1_2026-10-30_exhibit991_q3xseptemberx20.htm.jsonl, pages 17, 18; calculation C5; calculation C8

Total debt is USD 4,841 million and net debt USD 4,592 million at 2026-09-30 after the July and August 2026 refinancing, and the issuer’s own net debt agrees. Cash interest cover is stated for fiscal years only, at 0.94x on reported EBITDA and 2.49x on normalized EBITDA for FY2025, because the quarterly reports print no cash interest. Operating cash flow of USD 264 million in FY2025 left USD 17 million of free cash flow after capital expenditure, before dividends of USD 120 million. The only maturity table printed pre-dates the refinancing, so the current maturity wall is not established.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_nwl-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 17; 10-K_2025-12-31_nwl-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 81, 82, 107; calculation C4; calculation C8; calculation C12

### Leverage and coverage

Reported leverage moved from 12.84x at FY2024 to 14.12x at FY2025 and 14.32x at 2026-09-30, with a dip to 10.67x at 2026-06-30 when second-quarter results carried about USD 126 million of tariff recoveries. On the issuer’s normalized EBITDA the same debt is 5.30x at FY2025 and 4.70x at 2026-09-30. The gap between the two bases is USD 692 million of add-backs over the last twelve months, of which USD 441 million is impairment of acquired intangible assets. Cash interest paid exceeded reported EBITDA in FY2025, with cover of 0.94x.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_nwl-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 17; EX-99.1_2026-10-30_exhibit991_q3xseptemberx20.htm.jsonl, pages 17, 18; 10-K_2025-12-31_nwl-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 81, 82; calculation C8; calculation C10

### Cash flow quality

Cash conversion is thin: operating cash flow was USD 264 million in FY2025 and USD 93 million for the nine months to 2026-09-30, and free cash flow for the twelve months to that date is USD 53 million before dividends. Nine-month 2026 operating cash flow absorbed an inventory build of USD 320 million, which the quarterly report attributes to anticipated sales.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_nwl-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 81, 82; 10-Q_2026-09-30_nwl-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 3, 49; calculation C7; calculation C12

### Liquidity and refinancing

Liquidity at 2026-09-30 is cash of USD 249 million and net availability of USD 480 million under the asset-based facility, USD 729 million in all, which is 15.06% of total debt, and the facility is limited by a borrowing base. The July and August 2026 refinancing replaced the 2027 revolver with an asset-based facility due 2031 and the 2027 notes with notes due 2031, so none of the series printed matures before 2028, although the facility can mature 91 days before the earliest maturity of indebtedness of USD 125 million or more.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_nwl-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 17, 18, 19, 20; calculation C11

### Data quality and definitions

Reported EBITDA is a house calculation because the issuer prints none, and the issuer’s normalized EBITDA is a separate, labelled basis. Debt is carried at USD 4,673 million against USD 4,718 million of principal at FY2025, and USD 435 million of sold receivables sit outside it. Printed dashes are read as missing, not zero, and the gaps list the affected lines.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_nwl-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 105, 107; 10-Q_2026-09-30_nwl-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 9, 10; calculation C9; calculation C13
