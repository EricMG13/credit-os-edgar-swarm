## Findings

### Bottom line

The seven supplied filings support the financial foundation, business and governance, and fundamental credit work on Newell Brands' consolidated accounts through the third quarter of 2026, but covenant headroom, current ratings and market pricing cannot be assessed from them. Creditors can rely on the reported statements and the refinancing record; any view on covenant cushion rests only on the issuer's statement of compliance.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_nwl-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 80–82; 10-Q_2026-09-30_nwl-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 2–4, 50

The accounts are those of one registrant with no separate subsidiary issuer. The latest period ended 36 days before the as-of date and nothing supplied is dated after it. Net sales for the three 2026 quarters add to the nine-month figure, and the issuer's net debt of USD 4,592 million and trailing normalized EBITDA of USD 1,030 million both recompute from printed inputs. The August 2026 refinancing, the new asset-based facility and the receivables amendments of September and October 2026 are all documented, but ownership and board detail sits in a proxy statement that was not supplied.
