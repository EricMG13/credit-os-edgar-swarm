## Findings

### Bottom line

The five filings support a credit snapshot of Asbury Automotive Group as of September 30, 2026, but covenant headroom, ratings and market pricing cannot be assessed from them. Creditors can rely on the consolidated statements, the full debt schedule and the stated liquidity; they cannot rely on covenant levels, which no document prints.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_abg_abg-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 18–19, 55–56; 10-K_2025-12-31_abg_abg-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 79–80

The set runs from the 2024 annual report to the quarterly report signed on October 30, 2026, which is 19 days before the as-of date. Every document is a primary filing by the registrant. No executed credit agreement, indenture, rating report, lender presentation or market quote was supplied, and the filings give no EBITDA figure of the company's own, so earnings power has to be rebuilt from statement lines. The 2021 real estate facility matures in December 2026, after the latest evidence, and its refinancing is not documented in the set.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_abg_abg-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 56, 66; calculation C1
