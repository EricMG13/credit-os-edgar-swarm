## Findings

### Bottom line

Total debt is 5.07x reported EBITDA at the July 2026 year end, down from 7.63x only because FY2025 earnings carried a litigation charge, while cash interest cover has fallen to 2.41x from 3.45x in FY2024, so creditors have less cushion than the leverage trend suggests.
Basis: calculation C06; 10-K_2026-07-31_fgpr-20260731.htm.jsonl, pages 59–60

On the issuer's adjusted EBITDA, which excludes that charge, leverage rose from 4.44x to 4.81x as debt grew and adjusted earnings fell. Liquidity was USD 195.1 million at year end against USD 259.7 million a year earlier, with USD 87.5 million drawn on the revolver and a further USD 20.0 million drawn on August 27, 2026. The latest audited balance sheet is later than the quarter the run settings call current, so net debt and cover are also shown at April 30, 2026.
Basis: calculation C06; calculation C08; calculation C09; 10-K_2026-07-31_fgpr-20260731.htm.jsonl, page 64; 10-K_2026-07-31_fgpr-20260731.htm.jsonl, page 143; Source readiness (CP-0)

### Financial interpretation

Reported leverage looks better than it is. Total debt at the July 2026 year end is 5.07x reported EBITDA and net debt 4.91x, against 7.63x and 7.13x a year earlier, but the FY2025 base carried USD 130.635 million of legal fees and settlements outside the core business. On the issuer's adjusted EBITDA, which removes that charge, total debt rose from 4.44x to 4.81x because carrying debt grew by USD 77.599 million while adjusted EBITDA fell by USD 9.388 million. On reported EBITDA the FY2024 level of 4.76x is the cleaner comparison, and FY2026 sits above it.
Basis: calculation C01; calculation C04; calculation C06; 10-K_2026-07-31_fgpr-20260731.htm.jsonl, pages 59–60

Cash interest cover is the weaker side. Cash interest paid rose to USD 126.465 million from USD 96.072 million, so reported EBITDA covers it 2.41x against 2.00x in FY2025 and 3.45x in FY2024. The October 2025 refinancing replaced USD 650.000 million of senior notes due 2026 with senior notes due 2031 at a much higher coupon; at the stated coupons that principal alone costs USD 25.1875 million more a year, and the two senior note issues together carry USD 108.594 million of coupons in fiscal 2027, the amount the issuer's own table of fixed-rate interest obligations shows, before the revolver, lease interest and fees. The new notes were outstanding for about nine of the twelve months of fiscal 2026, so a full year of the higher coupon is not yet in the fiscal 2026 figures. The last-twelve-months cover of 3.64x at April 30, 2026 is not comparable with fiscal years because that period excludes the first payment on the new notes.
Basis: calculation C05; calculation C06; calculation C11; 10-K_2026-07-31_fgpr-20260731.htm.jsonl, page 145; 10-K_2026-07-31_fgpr-20260731.htm.jsonl, page 141; 10-K_2026-07-31_fgpr-20260731.htm.jsonl, page 74; 10-Q_2026-04-30_fgpr-20260430.htm.jsonl, page 18

Cash generation covers the preferred units but not the Class B distribution. Operating cash flow of USD 152.660 million less capital expenditures of USD 77.315 million leaves free cash flow of USD 75.345 million, against USD 56.345 million in FY2025 and USD 174.714 million in FY2024. Cash distributions to the senior preferred units took USD 65.729 million, leaving USD 9.616 million, and the USD 107.016 million Class B distribution in March 2026 turned free cash flow after both distributions negative at USD 97.400 million; the revolver was drawn by USD 87.500 million in the same year and cash fell by USD 48.461 million. Operating cash flow was also held down by a USD 47.635 million outflow in other current liabilities; the note on that line shows accrued payroll falling from USD 31.072 million to USD 7.307 million and accrued miscellaneous items from USD 47.578 million to USD 10.988 million.
Basis: calculation C10; 10-K_2026-07-31_fgpr-20260731.htm.jsonl, page 118; 10-K_2026-07-31_fgpr-20260731.htm.jsonl, page 154; 10-K_2026-07-31_fgpr-20260731.htm.jsonl, pages 140–141

Liquidity is thinner than a year ago and depends on a borrowing base. Cash of USD 48.422 million and stated facility availability of USD 146.7 million give USD 195.1 million at July 31, 2026, against USD 259.7 million at July 31, 2025 and USD 219.7 million at April 30, 2026. Availability is the lesser of the USD 350.0 million commitment and a borrowing base of USD 250.0 million plus 80% of eligible receivables and 70% of eligible propane inventory, less borrowings and letters of credit of USD 115.8 million. A further USD 20.0 million was drawn on August 27, 2026, which leaves net debt unchanged at drawdown but uses availability. Scheduled principal of USD 1.310 million is due in fiscal 2027, the revolver matures on October 27, 2028, and the first large maturity is USD 825.550 million in fiscal 2029, followed by USD 650.200 million in fiscal 2031.

A non-debt funding float and several claims sit outside debt. The senior preferred units carry USD 651.349 million in mezzanine equity and accrued distributions of USD 65.231 million a year, which rank ahead of equity but are in no ratio here.

The downside chain starts with weather and volumes.
