## Findings

### Bottom line

The ten filings and releases support a credit snapshot of Diversified Healthcare Trust as of 2026-11-18 with limitations: statements, debt, liquidity and segments are sourced to 2026-09-30, but covenant levels and executed debt documents are absent, and EBITDA appears only as management non-GAAP figures.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_dhc.htm.jsonl, pages 2–5, 13–16, 49–50, 60–62

Creditors can rely on the audited 2024 and 2025 statements and the three 2026 quarterly reports for debt, maturities and revolver availability. Covenant headroom, recovery, market pricing and peer comparison cannot be assessed from this set. Quarterly earnings measures such as EBITDAre come only from the investor presentations, and their definition changed in the third quarter of 2026. The November 2026 presentation also carries two sets of liquidity figures, and the set that matches the quarterly report is the one used.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_dhc.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 16, 44; EX99.1_2026-09-30_dhc_tm2629915d1-ex99-1-htm.htm.jsonl, pages 8, 20–21; calculation C1; calculation C7
