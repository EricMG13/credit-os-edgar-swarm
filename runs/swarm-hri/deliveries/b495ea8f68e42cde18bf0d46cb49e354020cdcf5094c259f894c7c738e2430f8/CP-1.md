## Findings

### Bottom line

Herc's net debt is 4.12x last-twelve-months reported EBITDA at September 30, 2026, above the 3.90x the issuer reports because its figure rests on adjusted, pro forma earnings, so creditors should size leverage on the higher reported measure.
Basis: calculation C27; EX-99.1_2026-10-27_herc2026q3-pressrelease.htm.jsonl, page 4

Leverage has fallen from 4.79x at March 31 to 4.27x at June 30 and 4.12x at September 30, but the fall comes from earnings, not repayment: net debt is broadly flat at USD 7,942 million, USD 7,869 million and USD 7,965 million while last-twelve-months EBITDA rose from USD 1,658 million to USD 1,932 million as H&E rolled into the base.
Basis: calculation C21; calculation C24; calculation C27; calculation C8; calculation C12

Liquidity is USD 2,100 million, almost all undrawn asset-based revolver, and the USD 550 million AR Facility is fully drawn and falls due on August 31, 2027, inside twelve months.
Basis: calculation C30; 10-Q_2026-09-30_hri-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 18

### Analysis by dimension

The H&E acquisition, completed on June 2, 2025, roughly doubled carrying debt from USD 4,086 million at year-end 2024 to USD 8,047 million at year-end 2025, which lifted net debt over fiscal-year EBITDA from 2.95x to 5.34x. The 5.34x overstates run-rate leverage because it sets full year-end debt against seven months of acquired earnings, so only the last-twelve-months ratios at June 30 and September 30, 2026 are comparable with each other. Within them the improvement to 4.12x is earnings-led: net debt moved from USD 7,942 million at March 31 to USD 7,965 million at September 30.
Basis: calculation C15; calculation C18; calculation C21; calculation C27; 10-Q_2026-09-30_hri-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 15

EBITDA covered cash interest 3.65x over the last twelve months to September 30, 2026, after 3.99x at March 31 and 3.47x at June 30; the dip in June reflects the H&E financing entering the trailing cash-interest window in full before earnings caught up. Cash interest paid is USD 67 million below interest expense, net, in nine months 2026, and cover here uses cash paid.
Basis: calculation C22; calculation C25; calculation C28; calculation C45

Operating cash flow of USD 951 million in nine months 2026 did not cover USD 1,056 million of rental equipment expenditure without USD 319 million of disposal proceeds. Before disposals, operating cash flow less capital expenditure was negative USD 212 million, and the issuer's free cash flow of USD 133 million depends on USD 345 million of disposal proceeds. Dividends paid of USD 71 million took more than half of that free cash flow, and receivables absorbed USD 260 million as revenue grew.
Basis: calculation C36; calculation C37; calculation C38; 10-Q_2026-09-30_hri-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 6

Liquidity at September 30, 2026 was USD 66 million of cash and USD 2,034 million available under the asset-based revolver, which matures on June 2, 2030. The AR Facility is fully drawn, so its extension in August 2026 to August 31, 2027 is the nearest refinancing event. The issuer states no ongoing financial maintenance covenant; a fixed charge coverage test applies only if liquidity falls below levels that are not printed.
Basis: calculation C30; 10-Q_2026-09-30_hri-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 18–19; 10-Q_2026-09-30_hri-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 33

Three definition choices move the numbers and each is carried both ways. Net debt on carrying value is USD 7,965 million against USD 8,014 million on principal; financing obligations of USD 97 million sit outside Total Debt; and the issuer's 3.90x net leverage uses pro forma adjusted EBITDA that is not printed, so its 0.22x gap to the reported 4.12x cannot be decomposed. The ladder of maturities is dated December 31, 2025 and does not say where the AR Facility sits.
Basis: calculation C47; calculation C48; calculation C40; EX-99.1_2026-10-27_herc2026q3-pressrelease.htm.jsonl, page 4; 10-K_2025-12-31_hri-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 103–104

The strongest evidence against a cautious reading is the earnings trend: adjusted EBITDA reached USD 603 million in the third quarter and the issuer raised 2026 guidance for the second time this year, which, if sustained with flat net debt, lowers reported leverage further. The view would weaken if net rental capital expenditure, guided to USD 1.0 billion to USD 1.1 billion for the year against USD 737 million spent in nine months, is funded by borrowing rather than disposals, or if the utilization gains reverse.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-10-27_herc2026q3-pressrelease.htm.jsonl, page 1; EX-99.1_2026-10-27_herc2026q3-pressrelease.htm.jsonl, pages 3–4
