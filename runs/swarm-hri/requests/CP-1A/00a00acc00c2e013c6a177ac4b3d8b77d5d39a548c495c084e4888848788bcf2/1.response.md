## Findings

### Bottom line

Herc is a diversified, scaled equipment-rental business whose credit risk now rests on integrating the debt-funded H&E acquisition rather than on its market position; no controlling holder is disclosed and the dividend continues, so creditor protection comes from performance, not sponsor support.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_hri-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 97; 10-K_2025-12-31_hri-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 53

The acquisition, completed on June 2, 2025 for a total purchase price of USD 4.8 billion, nearly doubled carrying debt in 2025 and added branches in over 30 U.S. states; management says integration finished in the first quarter of 2026 but publishes no integration cost or synergy amount. Demand is spread: no customer exceeds 3% of equipment rental revenue and national accounts are 49%.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_hri-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 97; 10-K_2025-12-31_hri-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 7; 10-K_2025-12-31_hri-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 11; EX-99.1_2026-04-28_herc2026q1-pressrelease.htm.jsonl, page 1; calculation C17

The USD 0.70 quarterly dividend continued through the acquisition year, and the ownership and compensation detail that would show who controls the company sits in a proxy statement that is not supplied.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_hri-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 53; 10-K_2025-12-31_hri-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 134–135

### Key findings

- The business is scaled and diversified, which supports cash-flow resilience through the cycle. Contractors, industrial, infrastructure and government, commercial facilities and other customers account for 38%, 25%, 18%, 14% and 5% of equipment rental revenue, national accounts for 49%, and no customer exceeds 3%. Equipment rental is 86.15% of FY2025 revenue and the only segment, so fleet utilization and rental rates drive almost all earnings.
  Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_hri-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 7; 10-K_2025-12-31_hri-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 11; 10-K_2025-12-31_hri-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 79; 10-Q_2026-09-30_hri-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 23; calculation C1
- The H&E acquisition is the dominant credit event, and its delivery matters more than business quality. The USD 4.8 billion purchase was funded by USD 2.8 billion of new senior unsecured notes, a USD 750 million term loan and USD 2.5 billion drawn on a new revolver, and new Herc shares valued at USD 584 million went to H&E holders. Management says integration finished in the first quarter of 2026 and synergies are tracking to plan, but integration cost and synergy amounts are not disclosed.
  Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_hri-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 97; EX-99.1_2026-04-28_herc2026q1-pressrelease.htm.jsonl, page 1; EX-99.1_2026-07-28_herc2026q2-pressrelease.htm.jsonl, page 1
- Ownership and control give creditors neither a sponsor to lean on nor a sponsor to fear. No holder above reporting thresholds is named, the chairman is non-executive and the annual report incorporates holdings, compensation and related-party detail by reference to a proxy statement that is not supplied, so a controlling holder is not ruled out.
  Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_hri-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 53; 10-K_2025-12-31_hri-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 134–135; 10-K_2025-12-31_hri-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 142
- Capital allocation is mixed rather than extractive. The dividend of USD 0.70 per share each quarter continued while debt rose, but there were no repurchases in 2025, disposal and refinancing proceeds went to debt, and the 2027 notes were redeemed at par. Dividends paid of USD 71 million in the first nine months of 2026 equal 53.38% of the issuer's free cash flow.
  Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_hri-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 53; 10-K_2025-12-31_hri-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 57; 10-K_2025-12-31_hri-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 104; 10-Q_2026-09-30_hri-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 6; EX-99.1_2026-10-27_herc2026q3-pressrelease.htm.jsonl, page 10; calculation C12
- Leased branches and a fixed cost base amplify cyclical risk. About 94% of locations are leased, operating lease liabilities of USD 1,502 million sit outside debt, rental contracts can be cancelled within a day, and rent, insurance and overhead do not fall in the winter, so a demand slowdown reaches cash flow faster than the revenue mix suggests.
  Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_hri-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 13; 10-K_2025-12-31_hri-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 50; 10-Q_2026-09-30_hri-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 3; 10-Q_2026-09-30_hri-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 11; calculation C10; calculation C11
- Operating momentum is strong but largely acquired. Equipment rental revenue rose 21.09% in the first nine months of 2026 and dollar utilization reached 43.5% in the third quarter against 39.9%, yet direct operating costs rose to 44.6% of equipment rental revenue in the nine months, and organic growth is not separated from acquired growth.
  Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_hri-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 4; EX-99.1_2026-10-27_herc2026q3-pressrelease.htm.jsonl, pages 2–3; calculation C19
- Legal exposure looks ordinary-course on the evidence supplied: claims arise from operating rented equipment and workers' compensation, and management does not expect a material adverse effect.
  Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_hri-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 127

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against treating integration as the main risk is operating: utilization, rental revenue growth and twice-raised 2026 guidance suggest the combined fleet is earning more per dollar of cost, and the near-term notes were replaced at par rather than through an exchange. The view would soften if cross-selling and branch optimization lift margins as management says and synergy amounts are disclosed; it would harden if integration costs recur, organic growth proves weak once acquired revenue is removed, or the dividend and fleet investment are funded by borrowing.
Basis: EX-99.1_2026-10-27_herc2026q3-pressrelease.htm.jsonl, page 1; EX-99.1_2026-10-27_herc2026q3-pressrelease.htm.jsonl, pages 2–4; 10-K_2025-12-31_hri-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 104

  Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_hri-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 134–135
- The integration conclusion rests on management statements; disclosed integration costs or a synergy shortfall would raise or lower the weight given to delivery risk.
  Basis: EX-99.1_2026-04-28_herc2026q1-pressrelease.htm.jsonl, page 1; EX-99.1_2026-07-28_herc2026q2-pressrelease.htm.jsonl, page 1; EX-99.1_2026-10-27_herc2026q3-pressrelease.htm.jsonl, page 1

  Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_hri-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 114–118; 10-Q_2026-09-30_hri-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 33

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

On June 2, 2025 Herc completed its acquisition of H&E Equipment Services, a full-service rental company with branches in over 30 U.S. states, for a total purchase price of USD 4.8 billion, made up of USD 2.9 billion of cash and new Herc shares valued at USD 584 million, and paid a further USD 1.4 billion in cash to extinguish H&E debt that was not assumed.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_hri-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 97; calculation C16

Herc funded the deal with USD 2.8 billion of new senior unsecured notes due 2030 and 2033, a USD 750 million term loan and USD 2.5 billion drawn on a new asset-based revolver of up to USD 4.0 billion, of which about USD 1.6 billion repaid the prior revolver, so carrying debt nearly doubled in 2025.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_hri-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 97; 10-K_2025-12-31_hri-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 114–116; calculation C17

In December 2025 Herc redeemed the USD 1.2 billion 2027 notes at par using the proceeds of USD 600 million each of new 2031 and 2034 senior notes, and it cut the term loan margin; the AR Facility was amended four times between August 2025 and August 2026 and now stands at USD 550 million, due on August 31, 2027.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_hri-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 57; 10-K_2025-12-31_hri-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 104; 10-K_2025-12-31_hri-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 116; 10-Q_2026-09-30_hri-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 18

Herc sold its Cinelease studio entertainment business on July 31, 2025 for USD 100 million of initial cash plus earnouts and used the proceeds to repay debt, and in 2024 it bought the assets of Otay Mesa Sales for about USD 273 million. The documents describe no pending acquisition and no new debt issue after the third quarter of 2026.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_hri-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 57; 10-K_2025-12-31_hri-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 98; 10-Q_2026-09-30_hri-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 18
