## Findings

### Bottom line

Insulet is a listed, single-platform medical device maker with no sponsor or controlling shareholder identified, so creditor risk lies in product safety, customer concentration and cash returned to shareholders rather than in sponsor behaviour.

Omnipod products were 99.40% of nine-month 2026 revenue and three distributors supplied 66% of it, so a safety action or the loss of one distributor would reach cash flow quickly; two voluntary device corrections in 2026 and a securities class action over product quality show that exposure is live. Against that, the company pays no dividend, cut debt in 2025, and the USD 300 million repurchase completed by March 2026 exceeded nine-month free cash flow of USD 211.7 million. Board, ownership and pay detail are not established because the proxy statement was not supplied.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_podd-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 8, 10, 14, 17, 31; 10-K_2025-12-31_podd-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 49, 107; calculation C1; calculation C6; calculation C9

### Key findings

Revenue concentration is high but easing. Almost all revenue comes from the Omnipod platform, and three distributors supplied 66% of nine-month 2026 revenue against 77% a year earlier, with the same three holding 67% of trade receivables at 2026-09-30. The mechanism is that a distributor's ordering pattern moves receivables and cash conversion, and a loss would hit collections before earnings.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_podd-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 8; calculation C1; calculation C6; calculation C7; calculation C8

Product safety has become the main event risk. The company issued two voluntary device corrections in 2026 for manufacturing issues that tore the cannula of certain products and estimates their total cost at about USD 50 million, and a putative securities class action filed on 2026-07-02 alleges misleading statements about product quality and safety; no loss has been accrued for the lawsuit. For creditors the cost so far is small against cash, but a further correction or a regulatory action would test the single-platform model.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_podd-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 10, 14, 20

Governance risk from owners is not evident, but it cannot be tested. The filings identify no sponsor, controlling shareholder or management fee, the company has never paid a cash dividend, and an earlier related-party distributor relationship was ended as a related-party matter on 2025-10-01 with terms described as arm's length. Ownership percentages, board independence and pay are in a proxy statement that was not supplied, so this finding rests on absence of disclosure, not on a positive test.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_podd-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 48, 49, 83, 107; 10-Q_2026-09-30_podd-20260930.htm.jsonl, page 6

Capital allocation turned from deleveraging in 2025 to shareholder returns in 2026. Total debt fell by USD 430.7 million in 2025 as the convertible notes were retired, then the company spent USD 300 million on accelerated repurchases by March 2026, which exceeded nine-month free cash flow of USD 211.7 million by USD 88.3 million, and the board authorised a further USD 1 billion in October 2026 with about USD 400 million expected to be spent in 2026. Cash, not new debt, paid for it, so creditors are protected today, but the legal limit on distributions is not established.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_podd-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 69; 10-Q_2026-09-30_podd-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 17, 31, 33; calculation C9; calculation C10

The operating model is high-margin and capital-hungry. Gross margin was 71.7% for the nine months of 2026 and revenue grew 25.0%, but capital expenditure doubled to USD 113.9 million for the Costa Rica plant and other capacity, and the company has guaranteed up to USD 97 million of the seller's loan for that plant, which is a contingent claim outside reported debt.
Basis: 10-Q_2026-09-30_podd-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 11, 24, 30

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest case against a cautious view is the scale and momentum of the business: revenue grew 25.0% in the nine months of 2026 with 71.7% gross margin, 31.63% of nine-month revenue is already international, the non-affiliate equity was worth about USD 22.1 billion at 2025-06-30, and the revolver was raised to USD 750 million in September 2026. That equity cushion and liquidity would absorb a further correction of the size seen in 2026. The view would turn more positive if the proxy statement showed dispersed ownership and independent board control, if the credit agreement limited restricted payments, and if the 2026 repurchases stayed within free cash flow. It would turn more negative if a third correction, a regulatory action or the loss of a large distributor appeared, if repurchases ran well ahead of free cash flow, or if the guarantee of the Costa Rica loan were called.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_podd-20251231.htm.jsonl, page 2; 10-Q_2026-09-30_podd-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 8, 11, 21, 24; calculation C3

Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_podd-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 2, 107

Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_podd-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 108, 111

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

No acquisition, leveraged buyout or sponsor transaction appears in the filings. The financing events that matter are a March 2025 issue of USD 450 million of 6.5% senior unsecured notes due 2033, used with cash to repurchase and redeem the convertible notes that were due in 2026, and a September 2026 amendment of the credit agreement that refinanced the USD 475 million term loan at SOFR plus 1.75%, down from SOFR plus 2.00%, and raised the revolver to USD 750 million. In April 2026 the company also guaranteed up to USD 97 million of the loan the seller used to build the Costa Rica plant. For creditors the notes are the only unsecured debt, they rank behind the secured credit agreement, and no new debt was raised in 2026 to fund the repurchases.
Basis: 10-K_2025-12-31_podd-20251231.htm.jsonl, pages 92, 93; 10-Q_2026-09-30_podd-20260930.htm.jsonl, pages 10, 11, 17
